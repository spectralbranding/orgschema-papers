## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Lennar Corporation acquiring via a newly-formed Delaware Merger Sub, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lennar. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-003465; SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878560/000119312518042625/d454806d8k.htm]`
- Target: CalAtlantic Group, Inc. (a Delaware corporation), a U.S. homebuilder. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-003465; SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878560/000119312518042625/d454806d8k.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated October 29, 2017; merger completed February 12, 2018 (effective 11:59 PM ET). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-003465; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
- Disclosed deal value: Each CalAtlantic share converted into 0.885 Lennar Class A share and 0.0177 Lennar Class B share, with a cash-election alternative of $48.26 per share subject to an aggregate cash cap of $1,162,250,000. Total consideration was approximately $6.06 billion (roughly $1.16 billion cash plus $4.9 billion in Lennar stock); the transaction was valued at about $9.3 billion including approximately $3.6 billion of assumed debt. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-003465; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html; https://www.cnbc.com/2017/10/30/lennar-to-buy-calatlantic-group-in-about-9-point-3-billion-deal.html]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Statutory merger — CalAtlantic merged with and into a Lennar Merger Sub, with the Merger Sub continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Lennar; CalAtlantic was delisted (Form 15-12B filed 2018-02-26). `[SRC: SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878560/000119312518042625/d454806d8k.htm; SEC 15-12B accession 0001193125-18-058264]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — CalAtlantic's independent-public-company controlling intent exited; Lennar's imprint replaced it (stated aim: create the largest U.S. homebuilder and generate operating and administrative cost savings). Partial leadership continuity: CalAtlantic Executive Chairman Scott Stowell joined Lennar's board and CFO Jeff McCall became a Lennar Senior Vice President. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-003465; https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The homebuilding value model (land acquisition, construction, home sales) transferred and was described as compatible with Lennar's; the two firms had "compatible product lines" in "familiar markets." `[SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal entity was extinguished into the acquirer's vehicle: CalAtlantic merged with and into Lennar's Merger Sub (Merger Sub surviving as a Lennar subsidiary) and CalAtlantic's public listing was terminated. `[SRC: SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/878560/000119312518042625/d454806d8k.htm; SEC 15-12B accession 0001193125-18-058264]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-acceptance layer transferred whole: the combined company controlled approximately 250,000 homesites and was actively selling in about 1,300 communities across 21 states. `[SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
- **T5 Process** — Operations were slated for integration to capture direct cost savings, production efficiencies, technology improvement, consistent land-acquisition underwriting, and overhead reduction. `[SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
- **T6 Organization** — People transferred with partial senior-leadership retention (Stowell to the board, McCall as SVP of corporate services). `[SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lennar-completes-strategic-combination-with-calatlantic-300597384.html]`
