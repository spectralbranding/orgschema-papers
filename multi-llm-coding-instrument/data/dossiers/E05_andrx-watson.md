## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Watson Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (a Nevada corporation, NYSE: WPI), a specialty pharmaceutical company that develops, manufactures, markets and distributes branded and generic pharmaceutical products, acting through its wholly owned Delaware subsidiary Water Delaware, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), formed solely to effect the merger. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0000950144-06-005153, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123337/000095014406005153/g01070dfdefm14a.htm]`
- Target: Andrx Corporation, a Delaware corporation (Nasdaq: ADRX), a pharmaceutical company that develops and commercializes controlled-release generics, oral contraceptives and immediate-release products; distributes pharmaceutical products (primarily generics) through its Anda distribution business; and develops/manufactures products for other pharmaceutical companies. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0000950144-06-005153, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123337/000095014406005153/g01070dfdefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement entered March 12, 2006 (per proxy); special meeting held June 28, 2006; acquisition completed November 6, 2006. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Approximately $1.9 billion; $25.00 per share in cash for each share of Andrx common stock. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular cash merger — Merger Sub (Water Delaware, Inc.) merged with and into Andrx, with Andrx continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Watson. Andrx subsequently deregistered its common stock (Form 15-12G filed November 6, 2006). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0000950144-06-005153, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123337/000095014406005153/g01070dfdefm14a.htm; Form 15-12G filed 2006-11-06, CIK 1123337]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Andrx's prior controlling principal (its public board/shareholders) exited; the asset came under Watson's strategic intent, stated as expanding Watson's product portfolio, pipeline and drug-delivery technology base and making Watson the third-largest generic pharmaceutical company in the U.S. by total prescriptions dispensed. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The generic-development/manufacturing value model transferred and merged into Watson's; the Anda distribution business (quality generic products from global manufacturers) was retained and established as a new operating segment within Watson, described by Watson's CEO as providing "added financial stability and solid cash flow." `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Legal entity survived as a subsidiary: Andrx continued as the surviving corporation and became a wholly owned subsidiary of Watson via the reverse triangular merger; it ceased to be an independent public company (Form 15-12G deregistration November 6, 2006). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0000950144-06-005153, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123337/000095014406005153/g01070dfdefm14a.htm; Form 15-12G filed 2006-11-06, CIK 1123337]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-facing product franchise transferred whole: controlled-release generics, oral contraceptives, selective immediate-release products, contract development/manufacturing for other pharmaceutical companies, and the Anda distribution catalog; Andrx's pipeline brought Watson's on-file ANDA count to 66 with the FDA. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Formulation and manufacturing operations transferred; Watson stated it had "completed detailed integration planning and is proceeding with plans for a rapid transition to a combined company," describing the assets as "highly complementary" and "likely to facilitate a smooth integration." `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — People transferred as part of the going-concern acquisition; the proxy addressed continuation of employee benefits and executive change-in-control arrangements on completion. Specific transferred-headcount figure not located in the filings fetched this session. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0000950144-06-005153, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123337/000095014406005153/g01070dfdefm14a.htm]` `[UNVERIFIED: exact transferred employee count]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture of the acquired core business; the Anda distribution unit was retained as a Watson operating segment. Watson stated the combined company expected to realize $30 million in annual synergies (majority from corporate/administrative overhead) and that the transaction was expected to be accretive to earnings. Independent 3-5yr CAR / impairment figures were not retrieved from a primary source this session. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]` `[UNVERIFIED: 3-5yr CAR / any goodwill impairment]`

Watson Pharmaceuticals was later renamed Actavis, Inc. `[SRC: Watson news release, 8-K accession 0001106459-06-071725, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/884629/000110465906071725/a06-19943_1ex99d3.htm]` `[UNVERIFIED: independent assessment of long-run integration success]`
