## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Kinder Morgan, Inc. ("Kinder Morgan" / "KMI") acquired El Paso Corporation ("El Paso" / "EP") through a series of transactions (the "first merger," an LLC conversion, and a "second merger" and "third merger"), after which El Paso became an indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Kinder Morgan. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]`
- Target: El Paso Corporation, a publicly traded Delaware corporation (NYSE: EP) operating primarily in natural gas transmission, and exploration and production (E&P). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated October 16, 2011; special meeting of El Paso stockholders held March 6, 2012; acquisition of all outstanding El Paso shares completed effective May 25, 2012. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]` `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Per-share election of (1) Kinder Morgan Class P stock plus 0.640 of a warrant, (2) $25.91 cash plus 0.640 of a warrant, or (3) 0.4187 KMI share, $14.65 cash and 0.640 of a warrant. On completion KMI issued ~330 million KMI Class P shares (fair value ~$10.6 billion as of May 24, 2012), ~505 million warrants (fair value ~$863 million; $40 exercise price, 5-year term), and paid ~$11.6 billion cash to former EP stockholders and equity-award holders — total equity consideration ~$23 billion. `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]` The transaction was widely reported at ~$38 billion including ~$17 billion of assumed El Paso debt. `[SRC: web-search-surfaced trade press, https://www.naturalgasintel.com/news/kinder-morgan-completes-38b-el-paso-mega-acquisition/]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Multi-step statutory merger of a public C-corporation; El Paso became an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Kinder Morgan (share/entity acquisition, not an asset purchase). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — El Paso's prior controlling-principal imprint exited; the natural-gas assets came under Kinder Morgan's stated strategic intent (build the largest midstream and natural-gas network operator in North America). The upstream (E&P) intent was deliberately separated out and divested at close. `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The natural-gas pipeline/midstream value model transferred into Kinder Morgan; the exploration-and-production (upstream) model was carved out — El Paso sold its EP Energy E&P subsidiary for $7.2 billion effective at close, a deliberate model separation. `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — El Paso became an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Kinder Morgan via a series of statutory mergers and an LLC conversion; El Paso Pipeline Partners, L.P. (EPB) continued as a separate publicly traded MLP under Kinder Morgan control. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-facing franchise transferred: interstate natural-gas transmission and storage services (and related pipeline assets). The upstream E&P product line was divested to the Apollo-led group. `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Pipeline operating infrastructure transferred and was integrated into Kinder Morgan's midstream operating platform; both companies were Houston-headquartered. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-12-032399, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1066107/000119312512032399/d252957ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Employees and operations of the retained natural-gas businesses transferred into Kinder Morgan; E&P staff followed the divested EP Energy to the Apollo-led buyer. Precise headcount transfer figures not located in the effort budget. `[UNVERIFIED]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): A major divestiture occurred simultaneously with close — El Paso's EP Energy E&P business was sold for $7.2 billion (to a group led by affiliates of Apollo Global Management per trade press) on May 24, 2012, one day before the acquisition's effective date; almost all proceeds were applied to reduce acquisition debt. `[SRC: SEC 10-Q (KMI, Q2 2012), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000150630712000096/kmi-2012630x10q.htm]` `[SRC: web-search-surfaced trade press, https://www.naturalgasintel.com/news/kinder-morgan-completes-38b-el-paso-mega-acquisition/]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Integration proceeded as designed — the natural-gas pipeline franchise was absorbed into Kinder Morgan (making KMI the largest midstream/natural-gas network operator in North America) while the upstream business was divested at close. The FTC required certain asset divestitures as an antitrust condition. `[SRC: web-search-surfaced FTC matter 1210014, https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/1210014-kinder-morgan-inc-matter]` Fuller 3-5-year financial outcomes (e.g., later corporate restructuring and dividend policy) beyond the sourced facts above are `[UNVERIFIED]`.
