## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Cavendish Global Limited ("Parent," incorporated in England and Wales) acquiring via Cavendish Acquisition Corporation ("Merger Sub," a New Jersey corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Parent); Parent is jointly owned by Burlingtown UK LTD (affiliate of the Cutrale Group) and Erichton Investments Ltd. (affiliate of the Safra Group), collectively "Cutrale-Safra." `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-14-062986, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_8k.htm]`
- Target: Chiquita Brands International, Inc. (a New Jersey corporation; NYSE: CQB), one of the leading fresh-produce companies (Chiquita bananas, Fresh Express salads). `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-14-062986; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated October 26, 2014 (announced October 27, 2014); tender offer followed by merger completed January 6, 2015. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-14-062986; SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-15-000880, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420415000880/v398165_8k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: All-cash, $14.50 per share, in a transaction valued at approximately $1.3 billion including the assumption of Chiquita's net debt. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-14-062986; https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm; https://www.fooddive.com/news/cutrale-safra-acquires-chiquita-in-13-billion-agreement/325877/]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Tender offer for all outstanding shares at $14.50 cash, followed by a statutory merger of Merger Sub with and into Chiquita, with Chiquita as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; Chiquita was delisted from the NYSE (Form 25 / trading suspended January 7, 2015). `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-14-062986; SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-15-000880]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Chiquita's public-company controlling intent (CEO Ed Lonergan / public board, NYSE-listed) exited; Cutrale-Safra's imprint replaced it as a private long-term investment, framed to leverage Cutrale's fruit-and-juice value-chain experience and the Safra Group's financial expertise. `[SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The branded fresh-produce value model transferred and was retained; the acquirers stated they would support Chiquita's Chiquita and Fresh Express franchises using Cutrale's farming, processing, sourcing, distribution, logistics and marketing capabilities. `[SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal entity was acquired and taken private: Cavendish Acquisition Corporation merged with and into Chiquita, with Chiquita surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Cavendish Global Limited and its NYSE listing terminated. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-15-000880]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-acceptance layer transferred whole: Chiquita's branded fresh produce — Chiquita bananas and Fresh Express packaged salads — described as among the most recognizable brands in the sector. `[SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Operations continued under new ownership, with stated access to the Cutrale Group's farming, processing, technology, sourcing, distribution and logistics capabilities. `[SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — People transferred into a privately-held, streamlined organization; the acquirer expressed intent to work with Chiquita employees, but the Charlotte, North Carolina headquarters was closed shortly after the deal closed (see section 3). `[SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/101063/000114420414062986/v392166_ex99-1.htm; https://www.fooddive.com/news/chiquita-to-shut-down-its-headquarters-in-north-carolina/352786/]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture identified; the company remained privately held under Cutrale-Safra. Structural change concentrated in the organization tier — Chiquita closed its Charlotte, NC headquarters in January 2015 (approximately 320 employees affected within 90 days), citing a transition to a "more simplified and streamlined business model" and from a public to a private enterprise. By 2019 the company's main offices had relocated to Étoy, Switzerland. `[SRC: https://www.fooddive.com/news/chiquita-to-shut-down-its-headquarters-in-north-carolina/352786/; https://wfae.org/post/mass-layoffs-chiquita-headquarters-begin-april]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Going-private transaction; the produce franchises and business model were retained under new private ownership, while the U.S. corporate headquarters was dismantled and later relocated abroad. A quantified financial performance figure at the 3-5 year horizon is not available (private company, no public filings after the January 2015 Form 15 deregistration). `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001144204-15-000880; https://www.fooddive.com/news/chiquita-to-shut-down-its-headquarters-in-north-carolina/352786/]`
