## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Bristol-Myers Squibb Company (acquirer), acting through Burgundy Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of Bristol-Myers Squibb. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- Target: Celgene Corporation, a Delaware corporation, an integrated global biopharmaceutical company (primary commercial products REVLIMID, POMALYST/IMNOVID, OTEZLA, ABRAXANE, VIDAZA). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Definitive merger agreement dated January 2, 2019; merger completed on the Closing Date, November 20, 2019. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001104659-19-065939, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000110465919065939/tm1923405d1_8k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Per Celgene share, $50.00 in cash + one share of Bristol-Myers Squibb common stock + one contingent value right (CVR); the cash-and-stock components represented approximately $99.37 per share based on the BMS closing price on January 31, 2019. BMS expected to issue up to ~701,024,507 shares and ~701,024,507 CVRs; former Celgene holders expected to own ~31% of the combined company. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular merger — Burgundy Merger Sub merged with and into Celgene, with Celgene surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bristol-Myers Squibb; an entity (share) acquisition, not an asset deal. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Celgene's prior controlling principal (its board/charter) exited; Celgene's strategic intent was replaced by Bristol-Myers Squibb's imprint, stated as combining the two companies' oncology, immunology/inflammation and cardiovascular franchises into one biopharmaceutical company. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The biopharmaceutical discovery/development/commercialization value model transferred and was combined with BMS's; a portion of the value (three near-term pipeline assets) was carved out into a contingent value right rather than paid at close. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001104659-19-065939, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000110465919065939/tm1923405d1_8k.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Entity acquired via reverse triangular merger; Celgene Corporation survived the merger as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Bristol-Myers Squibb. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001104659-19-065939, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000110465919065939/tm1923405d1_8k.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — The customer-facing commercial product franchise transferred whole: REVLIMID, POMALYST/IMNOVID, OTEZLA, ABRAXANE, VIDAZA and the late-stage pipeline (Ozanimod, JCAR017/liso-cel, bb2121/ide-cel). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Celgene's R&D, manufacturing and commercialization operations transferred with the entity as a going concern; the DEFM14A frames integration into the combined company. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Celgene's workforce transferred with the surviving subsidiary; treatment of Celgene equity awards (conversion into BMS awards) is specified in the merger agreement. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001140361-19-003696, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/816284/000114036119003696/s002620x7_defm14a.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture of the core Celgene business — it was retained and integrated into Bristol-Myers Squibb. The contingent value right (potential $9.00 per CVR; up to ~$6.3 billion nominal across ~701 million CVRs) terminated worthless: on January 1, 2021 BMS announced that because FDA approval of liso-cel (JCAR017) did not occur by December 31, 2020, one of the three required milestones was not met, the CVR Agreement terminated automatically, the CVRs became ineligible for payment, and they ceased trading on the NYSE. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001140361-21-000023, exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14272/000114036121000023/brhc10018575_ex99-1.htm]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Mixed on the sourced record — the entity and product franchise were fully absorbed as a going concern, but the CVR portion of the consideration lapsed unpaid when the pipeline-approval milestone was missed. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001140361-21-000023, exhibit 99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/14272/000114036121000023/brhc10018575_ex99-1.htm]`
