## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Barclays PLC / Barclays Bank PLC (acquiring into Barclays Capital) acquired Lehman Brothers' North American investment banking and capital markets businesses. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- Target: Lehman Brothers North American fixed income and equities sales, trading and research and investment banking businesses (a distressed subset of Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc., which had filed for bankruptcy). `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Agreement announced September 17, 2008; U.S. Bankruptcy Court (SDNY) approval received September 19, 2008; acquisition completed September 22, 2008. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001659, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001659/barc200809226k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Cash consideration of £0.14bn (US$0.25bn) for the businesses, plus the New York headquarters at 745 Seventh Avenue and two New Jersey data centres for close to market value, estimated at £0.8bn (US$1.5bn); combined consideration ~£1.0bn (US$1.75bn). Barclays acquired trading assets estimated at £40bn (US$72bn) and trading liabilities estimated at £38bn (US$68bn). `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Asset purchase out of Chapter 11 under a U.S. Bankruptcy Court Section 363 sale order — not a share/entity acquisition of Lehman Brothers Holdings. `[SRC: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=2fd1861b-10cb-4afb-9ccf-07678891a024]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Lehman Brothers Holdings (the bankrupt controlling principal) exited; the acquired businesses came under Barclays' strategic intent (stated aim: diversify Barclays earnings toward North America and build a top-3 US capital-markets position). `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The investment-banking / capital-markets value model transferred and was merged into Barclays Capital, Barclays' investment-banking arm. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Assets and businesses, not the legal entity: acquired via Section 363 sale; Lehman Brothers Holdings Inc. remained a debtor in bankruptcy and the acquired operations were folded into Barclays Capital. `[SRC: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=2fd1861b-10cb-4afb-9ccf-07678891a024]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-facing franchise transferred: North American fixed income and equities sales, trading and research, plus US M&A and equity-capital-markets and prime-brokerage/cash-equity franchises. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Supporting operating infrastructure transferred, including the 745 Seventh Avenue NY headquarters and two New Jersey data centres; Barclays stated it intended rapid integration into Barclays Capital. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Approximately 10,000 Lehman employees transferred; Barclays framed a "strong cultural fit" and welcomed the team into Barclays Capital under Robert E. Diamond Jr. `[SRC: SEC 6-K accession 0001191638-08-001637, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000312069/000119163808001637/barc200809176k.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture — the acquired businesses were retained and integrated into Barclays Capital. The Lehman estate later sued alleging Barclays reaped an ~$11 billion "windfall"; on February 22, 2011, Judge James M. Peck (SDNY Bankruptcy) denied Lehman's motion to modify the Section 363 sale order and ruled substantially in Barclays' favor, finding the sale process "may have been imperfect, but it was still adequate under the exceptional circumstances." `[SRC: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=2fd1861b-10cb-4afb-9ccf-07678891a024]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Succeeded from Barclays' standpoint — the going-concern brokerage sale preserved the franchise and jobs, and Barclays used it to build a top-3 US capital-markets position; the estate-vs-Barclays litigation over sale terms is the principal controversy. `[SRC: https://www.lexology.com/library/detail.aspx?g=2fd1861b-10cb-4afb-9ccf-07678891a024]`
