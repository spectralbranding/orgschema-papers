## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Reynolds American Inc. (RAI, acquirer), acting through Lantern Acquisition Co., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of RAI; British American Tobacco p.l.c. (BAT) participated via a Subscription and Support Agreement to maintain its ownership stake. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`
- Target: Lorillard, Inc., a Delaware corporation; the oldest continuously operating tobacco company in the United States, whose flagship premium cigarette brand Newport was the top-selling menthol and second-largest-selling cigarette brand overall in the U.S. (Newport accounted for ~85.4% of Lorillard's consolidated net sales for fiscal 2013). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-14-449963, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312514449963/d805736ddefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated July 15, 2014; merger completed (Effective Date) June 12, 2015. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Per Lorillard share, 0.2909 of a share of RAI common stock plus $50.50 in cash; based on RAI's closing price of $72.15 on June 11, 2015, the aggregate fair value of the merger consideration was approximately $25.8 billion. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular merger — Lantern Acquisition Co. merged with and into Lorillard, with Lorillard surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of RAI; cash-and-stock consideration, paired with a contemporaneous asset divestiture to Imperial Tobacco (see below). `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Lorillard's prior board/charter control exited; strategic intent was replaced by RAI's imprint, consolidating a horizontal tobacco competitor into RAI's portfolio (with BAT retaining an ownership stake in the enlarged RAI). `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The premium-cigarette (menthol-led) value model transferred into RAI; a horizontal combination of two U.S. tobacco manufacturers, partially reshaped by the required divestiture of certain brands to a third competitor. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-14-449963, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312514449963/d805736ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Entity acquired via reverse triangular merger; Lorillard, Inc. survived as a wholly-owned subsidiary of RAI. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001193125-15-221980, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312515221980/d941804d8k.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — The customer-acceptance layer transferred to RAI centered on Newport (top-selling U.S. menthol brand). Simultaneously, a defined set of transferred assets was carved out and sold to Imperial Sub (an Imperial Tobacco entity) under an asset purchase agreement and transfer agreement — a partial Tier-4 subset routed to a third party rather than retained by the acquirer. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-14-449963, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312514449963/d805736ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Lorillard's manufacturing and distribution operations transferred with the surviving entity, net of the operations attached to the divested assets that moved to Imperial Sub. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-14-449963, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312514449963/d805736ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Lorillard's workforce transferred with the surviving subsidiary, again net of personnel attached to the divested assets conveyed to Imperial Sub. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-14-449963, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1424847/000119312514449963/d805736ddefm14a.htm]`
