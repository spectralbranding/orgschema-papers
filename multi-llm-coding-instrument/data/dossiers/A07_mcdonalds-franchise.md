## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: McDonald's Corporation (franchisor; NYSE: MCD) and its independent franchisees (independent local business owners). This is not a one-time M&A transaction but a standing franchise/real-estate control structure. `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]` `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's]`
- Target: The individual McDonald's restaurant operation granted to a franchisee. The original restaurant business was founded by brothers Richard and Maurice McDonald (first McDonald's opened in San Bernardino, California, May 15, 1940); the corporate franchising vehicle, McDonald's Corporation, dates to Des Plaines, Illinois, April 15, 1955. `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's]`
- Announced / closed dates: Ray Kroc became a McDonald's franchise agent in 1955 and opened a restaurant in Des Plaines, Illinois; he purchased the McDonald brothers' ownership stake in the company in 1961 for $2.7 million. The system is ongoing to the present. `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's]`
- Disclosed deal value: No single transaction value (ongoing system). Kroc's 1961 buyout of the McDonald brothers' equity cost $2.7 million. For a new U.S. franchise today, McDonald's requires a minimum of $750,000 (net) of non-borrowed personal resources. `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's]` `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Franchise agreement plus real-estate arrangement — McDonald's Corporation licenses its brand/system and typically owns/develops the site; the franchisee operates the restaurant as an independent business. Approximately 95% of McDonald's restaurants worldwide are owned and operated by independent local business owners. `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction. (Here the "boundary" is the recurring franchise grant: which tiers McDonald's Corporation retains vs. which it hands to the franchisee.)
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Retained by McDonald's Corporation at the brand/system level; the franchisee holds independent owner intent only for the single unit and operates within McDonald's system (McDonald's selects "individuals with significant business experience" as owners but sets the overarching system). `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The value-flow model is set and retained by McDonald's Corporation: McDonald's manages site evaluation, acquires the property and constructs the building, then awards the franchise; the franchisee supplies capital and operates the unit. The parent holds the brand + real-estate + system layer while ~95% of restaurants are owned and operated by independent local business owners. `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Two separate legal entities persist: McDonald's Corporation (the franchisor, a public company, NYSE: MCD) and the franchisee's own independent business; no company is sold to the franchisee — a franchise is granted. `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]` `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonald's]`
- **T4 Product** — The product/brand specification (menu, brand standards) is owned and standardized by McDonald's Corporation; the franchisee delivers that standardized product to customers under the McDonald's brand (read-only to the operator, not modifiable). `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
- **T5 Process** — Operating standards are specified by McDonald's; day-to-day operation is executed by the franchisee, whose profitability "depends on many factors including operating and occupancy costs, financing terms and most important, your ability to operate the business effectively." `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
- **T6 Organization** — People/staff sit with the franchisee: restaurants are "owned and operated by independent local business owners" who employ and manage their own restaurant staff, not McDonald's Corporation. `[SRC: https://corporate.mcdonalds.com/corpmcd/franchising-overview.html]`
