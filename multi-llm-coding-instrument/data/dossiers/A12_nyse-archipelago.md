## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: The New York Stock Exchange, Inc. (a member-owned NY Type A not-for-profit corporation) and Archipelago Holdings, Inc. (a Chicago-based publicly-traded electronic exchange operator) combined into a newly formed for-profit parent, NYSE Group, Inc. (Delaware). `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`
- Target: Archipelago Holdings, Inc. (operator of the ArcaEx electronic trading platform; became NYSE Arca); Archipelago's CEO was Gerald D. Putnam, NYSE's CEO was John Thain. `[SRC: SEC Archipelago 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001107389/000110465906014686/a06-6347_28k.htm]` `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated April 20, 2005 (amended/restated July 20, 2005; further amended October 20 and November 2, 2005); Form S-4 declared effective November 3, 2005; NYSE members approved (>95%) December 6, 2005; combination completed March 7, 2006; NYSE Group (ticker NYX) first traded March 8, 2006. `[SRC: SEC Archipelago 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001107389/000110465906014686/a06-6347_28k.htm]` `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]`
- Disclosed deal value: Reported as a ~$9 billion transaction; each NYSE seat was valued at over $5 million (of which $300,000 was cash). `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): A series of mergers under which the NYSE and Archipelago each became wholly owned subsidiaries of the newly formed NYSE Group, Inc.; each Archipelago share converted 1-for-1 into an NYSE Group share (a reverse-merger-style transaction that took the member-owned NYSE public via combination with the already-public Archipelago). `[SRC: SEC Archipelago 8-K, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001107389/000110465906014686/a06-6347_28k.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Controlling principal transformed: the NYSE's member-owner mutual control was replaced by a for-profit public-shareholder board (NYSE Group), ending the exchange's 213-year member-owned governance; John Thain led the transformation. `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]`
- **T2 Business Model** — Business model changed via demutualization: from a member-owned not-for-profit exchange to a for-profit, publicly traded exchange operator, combined with Archipelago's electronic-trading model. `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — New legal vehicle created: NYSE Group, Inc. (Delaware for-profit corporation); the NYSE converted from a New York Type A not-for-profit corporation into New York Stock Exchange LLC, and both NYSE and Archipelago became wholly owned subsidiaries of NYSE Group. `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — Market/trading products combined: the NYSE floor auction market plus Archipelago's ArcaEx electronic platform (renamed NYSE Arca), giving a full-service venue across NYSE-listed, Nasdaq-listed and OTC securities. `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]` `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Operating/trading processes combined: the NYSE's floor-based auction operations with Archipelago's electronic trading technology, with a stated aim of enhancing the trading technology of both markets. `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Membership/ownership converted: each of the 1,366 NYSE members received $300,000 cash plus 80,177 NYSE Group shares plus a $70,571 cash dividend in exchange for its membership; NYSE members plus reserved employee shares held ~70% (~109.5 million shares), Archipelago stockholders ~30% (~46.0 million shares) of NYSE Group. `[SRC: SEC NYSE Group 10-Q FY2006, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001326579/000088237706001666/p06-0710_10qnew.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture or write-down; the combination succeeded and the for-profit entity expanded — roughly 13 months after close, on April 4, 2007, NYSE Group combined with Euronext N.V. to form NYSE Euronext, "the first global stock exchange." `[SRC: SEC NYSE Euronext 8-K FY2007, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001368007/000089882207000531/nyseeuronext8k.htm]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Succeeded — the deal accomplished the NYSE's demutualization and for-profit public listing (NYX) and positioned it for further global consolidation, consistent with Thain's stated expectation of more consolidation. `[SRC: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/nyse-archipelago-merger-approved/]`
