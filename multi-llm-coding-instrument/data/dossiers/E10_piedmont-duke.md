## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Duke Energy Corporation (acquirer), a Delaware corporation, acting through Forest Subsidiary, Inc., a newly formed North Carolina corporation and wholly-owned direct subsidiary of Duke Energy. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-402439, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000119312515402439/d49546ddefm14a.htm]`
- Target: Piedmont Natural Gas Company, Inc., a North Carolina corporation; an energy-services company whose principal business is the distribution of natural gas to over one million residential, commercial and industrial customers. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-402439, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000119312515402439/d49546ddefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated October 24, 2015; merger consummated (completed) on October 3, 2016. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001104659-16-148229, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000110465916148229/a16-19209_18k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: $60.00 per share in cash for each share of Piedmont common stock. Piedmont had 80,890,885 shares of common stock outstanding as of the special-meeting record date, implying an equity value of approximately $4.85 billion (80,890,885 x $60.00). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-402439, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000119312515402439/d49546ddefm14a.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular merger — Forest Subsidiary merged with and into Piedmont, with Piedmont surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Duke Energy; an all-cash entity (share) acquisition. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0001104659-16-148229, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/78460/000110465916148229/a16-19209_18k.htm]`
