## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Kinder Morgan, Inc. ("KMI"), a Delaware corporation (NYSE: KMI), acquired directly or indirectly all outstanding Kinder Morgan Energy Partners, L.P. ("KMP") common units that KMI and its subsidiaries did not already own. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- Target: Kinder Morgan Energy Partners, L.P. ("KMP"), a Delaware limited partnership (NYSE: KMP), one of the largest publicly traded pipeline MLPs in the United States; KMI was already KMP's controlling general partner (via Kinder Morgan G.P., Inc. and its delegate Kinder Morgan Management, LLC / "KMR"). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: KMP merger agreement dated August 9, 2014; special meeting of KMP unitholders held November 20, 2014; the acquisitions of KMP (and, under separate merger agreements, KMR and El Paso Pipeline Partners, L.P. / "EPB") were completed November 26, 2014. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]` `[SRC: SEC 8-K (KMI), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000110465914084673/a14-25356_28k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: Each outstanding public KMP common unit was converted, at unitholder election subject to proration, into (i) 2.4849 shares of KMI Class P common stock, (ii) $91.72 in cash without interest, or (iii) a combination of 2.1931 KMI shares plus $10.77 in cash. Former public KMP unitholders were expected to own ~31.4% of KMI post-close. On a pro forma basis after the combined Transactions, KMI's expected enterprise value was ~$140 billion. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]` The combined roll-up of KMP, KMR and EPB was reported at approximately $76 billion. `[SRC: web-search-surfaced trade press, https://www.thefreelibrary.com/KMP,+KMR,+EPB+shareholders+approve+acquisition+by+Kinder+Morgan.-a0390914272]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Statutory merger acquiring the public units of a controlled MLP into the C-corporation parent (part of a simultaneous three-way consolidation of KMP, KMR and EPB into KMI). `[SRC: SEC 8-K (KMI), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000110465914084673/a14-25356_28k.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — No change of ultimate controlling principal: KMI already controlled KMP as its general partner. The transaction consolidated the strategic-commitment layer at the KMI level, eliminating the separate public-MLP governance (the KMGP conflicts committee negotiated the deal on behalf of unaffiliated KMP unitholders). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — Value-flow model changed from a pass-through master limited partnership (with general-partner incentive distribution rights) to a single C-corporation; the underlying fee-based pipeline/midstream operating model was retained and consolidated under KMI. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal vehicle was consolidated: KMP (a Delaware LP) was rolled up into KMI (a Delaware C-corporation), which after the Transactions became the single publicly traded entity (NYSE: KMI); KMR and EPB were consolidated in parallel, and EPB later merged into KMP effective January 1, 2015. `[SRC: SEC 8-K (KMI), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000110465914084673/a14-25356_28k.htm]` `[SRC: web-search-surfaced trade press, https://www.thefreelibrary.com/KMP,+KMR,+EPB+shareholders+approve+acquisition+by+Kinder+Morgan.-a0390914272]`
- **T4 Product** — No change to the customer-facing franchise: the pipeline transportation, storage and terminal services already operated under KMI's control carried forward unchanged; the transaction altered ownership/financing structure, not the product. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — No operational disruption: KMP's pipeline operations were already managed under KMI's operating platform (via the general partner and its delegate KMR); the consolidation left the operating processes intact. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — No change of workforce ownership: KMP had no separate employees independent of the Kinder Morgan organization already directing it; the people/culture layer was already unified under KMI. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture; the consolidation produced a single C-corporation (KMI) described as the third-largest energy company and largest energy-infrastructure company in the United States, with ~$140 billion pro forma enterprise value. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-14-008474, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/888228/000104746914008474/a2221839zdefm14a.htm]` `[SRC: SEC 8-K (KMI), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000110465914084673/a14-25356_28k.htm]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): The structural consolidation completed as designed (single-C-corp structure achieved November 26, 2014; EPB merged into KMP January 1, 2015). Because the operating businesses were already under common control, there was no separate operational integration to succeed or fail; the change was to corporate/financing structure. Longer-horizon financial outcomes beyond the completed consolidation are not covered by the sourced materials. `[SRC: SEC 8-K (KMI), https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1506307/000110465914084673/a14-25356_28k.htm]`
