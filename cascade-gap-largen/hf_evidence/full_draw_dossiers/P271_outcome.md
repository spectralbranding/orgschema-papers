## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Kennedy-Wilson Holdings, Inc. ("KWH," CIK 1408100), the entity that completed the acquisition, remained an SEC reporting company and continued filing annual reports on Form 10-K after closing, including for fiscal years 2020 and 2021 (filed 2021-02-26 and 2022-02-25 respectively), both within a 3-5-year window measured from the October 2017 closing. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0001408100, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001408100&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40]
- Kennedy Wilson Europe Real Estate Plc ("KWE"), the Jersey company acquired in the transaction, has its own SEC filer record (CIK 0001704302). That record shows only Rule 425 business-combination-communication filings dated between April 24, 2017 and October 12, 2017, with no filing of any kind after October 12, 2017 and no Form 10-K, 20-F, or other periodic report ever filed under that CIK. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0001704302, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001704302&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100]
- Consequently, no standalone post-closing periodic SEC filing exists for KWE as a discrete reporting entity; the only periodic public record covering the combined business within the outcome window is KWH's own consolidated Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Consolidated realized record of the surviving registrant (KWH)
- KWH's consolidated net income attributable to common shareholders was $92.9 million for fiscal year 2020 and $313.2 million for fiscal year 2021, both years falling within the 3-5-year outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Income]
- KWH determined there was no impairment of goodwill as of December 31, 2021, 2020, or 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, Note re: Goodwill]
- As of December 31, 2021, KWH reported 220 employees in 12 offices located primarily in the United States, the United Kingdom, Ireland, and Spain, and $21.6 billion in real estate assets under management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 Business]
- KWH's FY2021 Form 10-K states that geographically its portfolio was concentrated in the Western United States (60%), the United Kingdom (18%), and Ireland (19%), based on Consolidated and joint-venture net operating income. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 Business]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K discloses that KWH "wholly-owns Kennedy Wilson Europe Real Estate Limited" (KWE), domiciled in the United Kingdom with GBP as its functional currency, and describes ongoing currency-hedging activity tied to KWE's GBP- and euro-denominated assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, foreign-currency-risk discussion]

## Limits on the acquired-business-specific record
- KWH's FY2021 segment reporting (ASC Topic 280) defines two reportable segments — "Consolidated Portfolio" and "Co-Investment Portfolio" — organized by investment structure, not by geography or by legacy acquired entity; the note does not present a discrete income statement, balance sheet, or NOI figure for the former KWE business as such. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 16 Segment Information]
- Beyond the geographic percentage-of-NOI disclosures and the ownership/currency statements above, no discrete post-closing financial statement specific to the KWE business (as distinct from KWH's combined consolidated results) was located in KWH's SEC filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- KWE's own SEC filer record shows no filing after its London listing was cancelled in October 2017, so there is no standalone, KWE-specific 3-5-year outcome record in the SEC filing system. The realized record that is traceable in the outcome window is that of the surviving combined registrant, KWH: consolidated net income attributable to common shareholders of $92.9 million (FY2020) and $313.2 million (FY2021), no goodwill impairment recorded for FY2019-FY2021, continued UK/Ireland real estate holdings equal to roughly 37% of portfolio NOI, and 220 employees across 12 offices spanning the US, UK, Ireland, and Spain as of year-end 2021 — figures that describe the whole combined enterprise rather than a discrete, separately reported record for the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-22-000053, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0001704302, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001704302&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100]
