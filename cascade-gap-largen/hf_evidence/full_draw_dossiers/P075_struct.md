# Structural sub-dossier — Skechers U.S.A. / HF Logistics I distribution-facility joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 30, 2010, Skechers U.S.A., Inc. (the "Company") entered into a Limited
  Liability Company Agreement with HF Logistics I, LLC ("HF"), through Skechers RB, LLC —
  a newly formed wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company — regarding the ownership and
  management of HF Logistics-SKX, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company (the "JV"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction was structured as an equal 50/50 partnership: the parties are equal
  fifty percent partners and the term of the JV is 50 (fifty) years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The stated purpose of the JV is to acquire and develop real property of approximately
  110 acres in Moreno Valley, California (the "Property") and to construct approximately
  1,820,000 square feet of buildings and improvements (the "Project") to lease to the
  Company as a distribution facility; the JV's objective is to operate the Project "for
  the production of income and profit." [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Legal vehicle and what each party contributed at closing
- The legal vehicle is a newly formed Delaware LLC (HF Logistics-SKX, LLC); the Company
  interposed a newly formed wholly-owned subsidiary, Skechers RB, LLC, to hold its 50%
  interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company, through Skechers RB, LLC, was to make an initial cash capital contribution
  of $30 million; HF was to make an initial capital contribution of land; any additional
  capital contributions would be made on an equal basis by the two partners. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- HF was deemed to have extended a loan to the JV as consideration for assigning the JV
  all of its interest in the entitlements (e.g., specifications, surveys, drawings)
  relating to ownership and development of the Property. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Allocation of management/operations at closing (authority split)
- Management authority was split by function rather than pooled: in general, Skechers RB,
  LLC was to have exclusive management over the Project's buildings and operations after
  completion of construction, while HF was to have exclusive management over landlord
  decisions under the Lease, over financing the Project or encumbering JV assets, and over
  matters pertaining to entitling the Property and developing the Project. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The customer-facing asset (the distribution facility) did not exist at closing — it was
  to be constructed by the JV — so no operating facility, workforce, or going-concern
  business crossed the ownership boundary; the transaction transferred capital, land, and
  entitlements into a new development vehicle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Alongside the JV agreement, on December 18, 2009 the Company and HF entered into an
  Amendment to Lease Agreement modifying a lease between the same parties dated September
  25, 2007; the Amendment would not become effective until the parties entered into the
  joint venture agreement, and that condition was met on January 30, 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Under the amended Lease, the Company will lease from the JV approximately 1,820,000
  square feet as a distribution facility; the lease term is 240 calendar months (twenty
  years) commencing when the JV obtains a certificate of occupancy, at a base rent of
  $933,894.44 per month. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Project financing was to be arranged at the JV level: the JV was "in the process of
  obtaining $55 million in construction financing," the closing of which was subject to
  conditions; if the construction loan was not finalized or construction did not begin by
  June 1, 2010, the JV would be null and void and each party would receive return of its
  initial capital contribution in the form contributed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction placed land (from HF) and $30 million of cash (from the Company via a
  newly formed subsidiary) into a new 50/50 Delaware LLC whose purpose was to build a
  ~1,820,000-square-foot distribution facility and lease it back to the Company for twenty
  years; operational control of the completed buildings was allocated to the Company's
  subsidiary while development, landlord, and financing control was allocated to HF, with a
  pre-existing lease (as amended) as the instrument bridging the two parties' roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-10-000470, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
