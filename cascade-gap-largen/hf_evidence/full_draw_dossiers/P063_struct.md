## Parties and vehicle at formation
- In October 2007 Apollo Group, Inc. and The Carlyle Group established Apollo Global, Inc.
  as a jointly owned entity ("a joint venture with The Carlyle Group") to pursue investments
  primarily in the international education-services industry. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950153-08-001796, Form 10-K FY2008]
- The vehicle is a distinct corporate entity, Apollo Global, Inc.; Carlyle is described as
  one of the world's largest private-equity firms, contributing as a minority financial
  partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950153-08-001796, Form 10-K FY2008]

## Ownership, governance and capital as structured
- At formation the parties agreed to commit up to $801 million (Apollo Group, for 80.1%
  ownership) and up to $199 million (Carlyle, for the remaining 19.9%), in cash or
  contributed assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950153-08-001796, Form 10-K FY2008]
- As of August 31, 2008, total cash contributions to Apollo Global were $61.0 million, of
  which $48.9 million was funded by Apollo Group; the entity was consolidated in Apollo
  Group's financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950153-08-001796, Form 10-K FY2008]
- As of August 31, 2009, Apollo Group owned 86.1% and Carlyle owned 13.9%; total cash
  contributions were approximately $511.8 million, of which $440.5 million was funded by
  Apollo Group; the entity remained consolidated (controlled) in Apollo Group's financial
  statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]

## What crossed the boundary — customer-facing product, processes, people
- The entity acquired operating, accredited institutions as going concerns, each retaining
  its accreditations, trademarks, campuses and local operations: UNIACC (Chile, purchased
  March 2008), Universidad Latinoamericana / ULA (Mexico, 65% August 2008, remaining
  interest July 2009), and BPP Holdings plc (United Kingdom, acquired July 30, 2009). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]
- BPP is described as a provider of professional education and training organized into three
  operating divisions (Professional Education; College of Professional Studies operating four
  law schools, a business school and HR training; and Mander Portman Woodward), delivering
  through UK schools and a European office network — i.e. the customer-facing product, its
  delivering operations and its organization transferred together as an intact institution. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]
- The acquired institutions were run as their own reportable segments (Apollo Global — BPP;
  Apollo Global — Other, which includes UNIACC, ULA and Apollo Global corporate operations),
  and BPP's operating results were consolidated from its acquisition date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]
- For BPP, the purchase consideration comprised $506.459 million cash paid, $84.306 million
  debt assumed and $10.821 million transaction-related costs; the acquisition recorded
  $425.6 million of goodwill and $191.294 million of acquired intangibles (including
  $134.068 million trademarks and $5.922 million accreditations and designations, both
  assigned indefinite lives). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]

## Bridging / shared-substrate arrangements at formation
- No transitional-services, shared-services or supply agreement bridging a shared operating
  substrate between Apollo Group and the acquired institutions is described in the drawn or
  contemporaneous filings reviewed; the acquired institutions are presented as self-standing
  operating segments rather than businesses dependent on a retained parent substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009]

## Structural summary (formation only)
- Two parties formed a jointly owned corporate vehicle (Apollo Group majority/controlling,
  Carlyle minority financial partner) capitalized by staged cash/asset commitments, through
  which they acquired operating accredited institutions as going concerns — each carrying its
  customer-facing programs together with its delivering operations, trademarks, accreditations
  and organization — run as distinct consolidated reportable segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-09-053647, Form 10-K FY2009] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950153-08-001796, Form 10-K FY2008]
