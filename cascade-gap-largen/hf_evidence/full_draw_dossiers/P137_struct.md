# Structural sub-dossier — DISH Network / L-Band Acquisition proposed acquisition of LightSquared LP assets (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured — what was proposed to cross the ownership boundary and what
> was not — as documented in the contemporaneous filings. It contains no post-transaction
> outcome information.

## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive
  Agreement): on July 23, 2013 L-Band Acquisition, LLC ("L-Band"/"LBAC"), a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of DISH Network Corporation ("DISH") "formed to make a bid to acquire assets
  of LightSquared LP," entered into a Plan Support Agreement (the "PSA") with certain senior
  secured lenders to LightSquared LP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- DISH itself is a party to the PSA "solely with respect to certain guaranty obligations";
  the operating bidder is the subsidiary L-Band Acquisition, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- DISH's Board of Directors approved entering into the PSA "based, among other things, on the
  recommendation of a special committee of the Board (the 'Special Committee') and a fairness
  opinion that was prepared by a financial advisory firm at the request of the Special
  Committee." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction type as structured
- Under the PSA, LBAC and the senior secured lenders agreed to "support and pursue
  confirmation of a plan of reorganization (the 'LightSquared LP Plan')" for LightSquared LP
  and certain debtor and debtor-in-possession subsidiaries in pending Chapter 11 bankruptcy
  cases in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York, jointly
  administered under the caption In re LightSquared Inc., et al., Case No. 12-12080 (SCC).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The LightSquared LP Plan contemplated "a sale of substantially all of the assets of the
  LightSquared LP Entities to L-Band," i.e., a distressed-asset purchase implemented through
  a court-confirmed Chapter 11 plan of reorganization / bankruptcy auction process.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What was proposed to cross the boundary — the asset
- The customer-facing asset proposed to be acquired is "substantially all of the assets of
  the LightSquared LP Entities" (LightSquared LP and certain of its subsidiaries), acquired
  by the DISH subsidiary L-Band. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Proposed consideration and legal vehicle
- Proposed purchase price: "$2.22 billion in cash, plus the assumption of certain
  liabilities," pursuant to the terms of a proposed asset purchase agreement (the "Proposed
  APA"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The acquiring legal vehicle is L-Band Acquisition, LLC, a wholly-owned DISH subsidiary
  formed for the bid; the PSA (dated July 23, 2013) is filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the 8-K and
  DISH's participation in the PSA is limited to Section 7.11 (guaranty obligations).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 9.01 / EX-10.1]

## Conditional / non-final character (as structured)
- The bid was expressly subject to competing offers: "L-Band's purchase offer under the
  LightSquared LP Plan is subject to the submission of higher and better offers in accordance
  with certain bid procedures to be proposed in connection with the LightSquared LP Plan."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The LightSquared LP Plan was "subject to confirmation by the Bankruptcy Court," and
  "consummation of the acquisition contemplated under the Proposed APA is subject to, among
  other things, Bankruptcy Court, Federal Communications Commission ('FCC') and Canadian
  federal Department of Industry ('Industry Canada') approvals"; funding of the purchase
  price was not conditioned on receipt of FCC or Industry Canada approvals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## People / processes provisions (as proposed)
- The Proposed APA "has not been negotiated with, or executed by, the LightSquared LP
  Entities" at the time of the filing; accordingly the drawn filing sets out no committed
  terms for transfer of the workforce, management, or operating organization of the target.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (as structured)
- The transaction was structured as a proposed distressed-asset acquisition: L-Band
  Acquisition, LLC (a wholly-owned DISH subsidiary formed to bid) agreed via a Plan Support
  Agreement with LightSquared LP's senior secured lenders to pursue a court-confirmed
  Chapter 11 plan under which L-Band would purchase substantially all assets of the
  LightSquared LP Entities for $2.22 billion cash plus assumption of certain liabilities,
  with DISH liable only for guaranty obligations, the offer expressly open to higher and
  better competing bids and conditioned on Bankruptcy Court, FCC, and Industry Canada
  approvals; no definitive asset purchase agreement had been negotiated or executed with the
  target and no workforce/organization transfer terms were set. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-055868, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-10.1]
