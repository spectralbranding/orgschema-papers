## The drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K furnishing the registrant's Second Quarter 2009 earnings-call presentation (call dated July 29, 2009); it is an earnings-release exhibit, not the acquisition's deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-046575, Form 8-K EX-99.1 Q2 2009 earnings-call presentation]
- The presentation states that the price-oriented leisure brand acquired in 2009 had "18 locations now in operation from four original locations when acquired," and lists planned expansion of "20 more locations in 2010" for the leisure brand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-046575, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The same presentation records two contemporaneous add-on acquisitions: the acquisition of Canada's Irving Industrial Rentals, described as "Part of HERC roll-up strategy" in the equipment-rental segment, and the acquisition of "Automoti," a virtual full-service used-car showroom, to support the registrant's Rent2Buy car-sales program. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-046575, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## The transaction and parties at closing (sourced from contemporaneous primary filings)
- In April 2009 the registrant completed the acquisition of certain assets of Advantage Rent A Car ("Advantage"), described as "a popular brand for price-oriented customers at key U.S. leisure travel destinations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]
- The structure was an asset acquisition, not a going-concern purchase: "The purchase agreement provided us with the rights to purchase certain rights, trademarks and copyrights to use the Advantage brand name, website and phone numbers." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]
- In addition to the brand rights, "the agreement provides us with the option to have assigned to us certain leases, fixed assets, airport concession agreements and other agreements associated with approximately 20 locations that Advantage is or previously was operating." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- Customer-facing product/asset that crossed: the Advantage brand identity — brand name, website, and phone numbers — plus intangible assets "primarily comprised of the tradename and concession agreements associated with the Advantage Rent A Car acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 (Item 1 Business; Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, intangible assets)]
- Operational processes/people did NOT cross as an intact going concern: only rights and an option to assume selected leases/fixed assets/concession agreements were acquired, and the acquired brand was operated using the registrant's own car-rental substrate — "As of December 31, 2009, we had 24 corporate Advantage brand rental locations in the United States." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]
- The transaction was accounted for using the acquisition method, with operating results of the acquired assets included from the date of acquisition, and total cash paid for intangible assets in FY2009 ($44.7 million) was blended across Advantage, reacquired franchise rights, and the Eileo technology acquisition (no Advantage-specific consideration figure is separately disclosed in this filing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]

## The broader consolidation footprint at closing
- Beyond Advantage, the registrant expanded by acquiring former franchisees: "During the year ended December 31, 2009, we added 32 locations by acquiring former franchisees in our domestic and international car rental operations, as well as approximately 20 locations associated with our acquisition of Advantage Rent A Car and one location related to an external acquisition done within our equipment rental operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- The acquired price-oriented brand was positioned to operate alongside the registrant's premium car-rental brand and its company-operated location network, rather than as a standalone entity retaining its own management and operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]

## Transitional / bridging arrangements at closing
- The transaction was an inbound acquisition of brand rights and an option on selected assets/agreements; the retrieved closing-era filings disclose no transition-services agreement or shared-services agreement of the kind used to bridge a carve-out (the acquired brand's operations were absorbed into the registrant's existing car-rental operating substrate). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction transferred a customer-facing brand asset (name, website, phone numbers, tradename, and concession/lease agreements for roughly 20 locations of a price-oriented leisure car-rental brand) to a large incumbent car-rental operator, which then ran the brand through its own company-operated locations rather than acquiring the target as an intact going concern with its own operating processes and workforce. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001542, Form 10-K FY2009 Item 1 Business] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-046575, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
