## What the acquirer (Virtu Financial, Inc.) separately discloses about the acquired business, ~3-4 years after closing
- Virtu Financial, Inc.'s Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed 2023-02-21) states that
  "[o]n March 1, 2019 (the 'ITG Closing Date'), we completed our acquisition of
  Investment Technology Group, Inc. ('ITG') in an all-cash transaction (the 'ITG
  Acquisition')," describing the acquired ITG as "a global financial technology company
  that offered a suite of trading and financial technology products to help leading
  brokers and asset managers improve returns for investors around the world." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- As of December 31, 2022, Virtu's execution-services trading activity outside the U.S.
  continued to be conducted through subsidiaries carrying the "Virtu ITG" name across
  multiple jurisdictions: Virtu ITG Europe Limited (Ireland), Virtu ITG UK Limited,
  Virtu ITG Australia Limited, Virtu ITG Hong Kong Limited, and Virtu ITG Singapore Pte.
  Limited, together with U.S. entities Virtu ITG Analytics LLC (pre- and post-trade
  analysis, fair value, and trade optimization services) and Virtu ITG Platforms LLC
  (workflow technology solutions and network connectivity services). [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- One acquired regional entity was subsequently renamed to drop the "ITG" identifier:
  the FY2022 10-K identifies "Virtu Canada Corp (f/k/a Virtu ITG Canada Corp.)" as the
  entity conducting Virtu's Canadian market-making and execution-services trading
  activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- "ITG Net," described as the global financial communications network integrated into
  Virtu's OMS/EMS platforms and supporting approximately 9,000 global billable
  connections to more than 600 unique execution destinations worldwide, continued to be
  offered as part of Virtu's product suite as of the FY2022 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Virtu reports two operating segments, "Market Making" and "Execution Services," plus a
  non-operating "Corporate" segment; as of both December 31, 2022 and December 31, 2021,
  the Execution Services segment carried $393,634 thousand of the Company's total
  $1,148.9 million of goodwill, and "No goodwill impairment was recognized during the
  years ended December 31, 2022 and 2021." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The FY2022 10-K states: "As a result of the ITG Acquisition, the Company has non-U.S.
  net operating losses at December 31, 2022, 2021... of $64.6 million and $67.2 million,
  respectively, and has recorded a related deferred tax asset of $12.4 million and
  $13.4 million, respectively," with "[a] valuation allowance of $12.4 million and
  $13.3 million... recorded against this deferred tax asset." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Financing tied to the acquisition: Virtu drew a $1,500.0 million Acquisition First
  Lien Term Loan Facility in its entirety on the ITG Closing Date, of which
  approximately $404.5 million repaid a pre-existing term loan facility and
  approximately $1,095.0 million financed the consideration and fees and expenses paid
  in connection with the ITG Acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]

## A specific divestiture of a business acquired via ITG
- In May 2020, Virtu entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement with Cboe Global
  Markets, Inc. to sell 100% of the outstanding interests in TriAct Canada Marketplace
  LP and TCM Corp, which operated the Canadian equities alternative trading system
  "MATCHNow" — a business that had come to Virtu through the ITG Acquisition. The sale
  closed August 4, 2020, for total gross proceeds of $60.6 million in cash plus
  contingent consideration of up to approximately $23.0 million, generating a $58.7
  million gain on sale recognized in the year ended December 31, 2020; the SPA also
  provided for a transition-services arrangement and a licensing agreement covering
  MATCHNow software and intellectual property. Contingent-consideration payments were
  assessed quarterly through December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Following the MATCHNow sale, Virtu assessed impairment of the long-lived intangible
  assets acquired in the ITG Acquisition (of which MATCHNow technology had been a
  component); no impairment was recognized for the year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired company itself deregistered under the Exchange Act within two weeks of
  closing (Form 15-12B, 2019-03-14) and has filed no subsequent periodic report under
  its own CIK, so no stand-alone public financial record exists for it in the ~2021-2023
  window. The acquirer's own FY2022 Form 10-K, however, separately discloses several
  facts tied specifically to the acquired business roughly three to four years after
  closing: most "Virtu ITG"-branded operating subsidiaries continued in place (one,
  the Canadian entity, was renamed to drop "ITG"); the Execution Services segment
  carrying the associated goodwill recognized no impairment in FY2022 or FY2021;
  ITG-Acquisition-linked non-U.S. net operating losses and related valuation allowances
  persisted through FY2022; and a discrete piece of the acquired business (the
  MATCHNow Canadian trading venue) was divested to Cboe Global Markets in August 2020
  for $60.6 million cash plus contingent consideration, with no related intangible-asset
  impairment recorded that year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-014831, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-23-000025, Form 10-K FY2022]
