# Structural sub-dossier — Bear State Financial, Inc. acquisition by Arvest Bank (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Bear State Financial, Inc. is an Arkansas bank holding company whose sole subsidiary
  is Bear State Bank (42 branches in Arkansas, Missouri and Oklahoma; $2.24 billion
  consolidated assets at June 30, 2017). On August 22, 2017, Bear State and Arvest Bank
  (an Arkansas state-chartered bank with $17.30 billion in consolidated assets and 254
  branches) entered an Agreement and Plan of Reorganization under which Arvest Acquisition
  Sub, Inc., a wholly-owned merger subsidiary of Arvest, would merge with and into Bear
  State, with Bear State surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Arvest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A]
- The agreement contemplated a two-step structure: (1) the holding-company merger,
  after which Bear State would dissolve/liquidate or merge into Arvest, terminating its
  separate corporate existence; then (2) a "Bank Merger" of Bear State Bank with and into
  Arvest under a separate Bank Merger Agreement, with the Bank's offices becoming Arvest
  branch offices. Consideration was $10.28 cash per share (~12% premium to the
  pre-announcement price). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing and what did not
- Customer-facing product: Bear State Bank's deposit/lending business, delivered through
  its branch network, was the operating entity acquired; the Bank's offices were
  structured to become Arvest branch offices directly rather than continuing under a
  separate brand or subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: neither the parent holding company nor the bank charter was structured
  to survive as a separate going legal vehicle — the holding company was to be
  dissolved/liquidated or merged out, and the bank was to be merged directly into Arvest
  as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A]
- People/organization: at the effective time each Bear State executive officer and each
  board member ceased to hold office; the top governance layer did not carry over into
  the surviving structure. Certain named executives held change-in-control agreements
  (cash severance of one-to-two times salary plus accelerated equity vesting) and the
  proxy stated only that "it is expected that certain members of Bear State's senior
  management team may continue as employees of Arvest" — continuity of specific
  individuals was contemplated but not guaranteed organization-wide. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-007338, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Bear State was required, upon Arvest's request, to terminate its 401(k) plan and any
  other Arvest-designated employee benefit plan at or before closing, and designated "key
  individuals" were bound by three-year covenants not to solicit Arvest's (or former Bear
  State's) employees or customers — mechanisms protecting the acquirer's post-close
  operations rather than transferring Bear State's benefits/process substrate intact. No
  transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a shared operating
  substrate was identified in the reviewed filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A]

## Regulatory conditions and process/branding sequencing at closing
- To satisfy a Federal Reserve approval condition, Bear State agreed (pre-closing) to
  sell two Bear State Bank branches (Mena, Arkansas; ~$58 million deposits, ~$30 million
  loans as of February 28, 2018) to a third-party bank, First Financial Bank of El
  Dorado, with that sale expected to close within 180 days after the merger's own
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-006071, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]
- The parties' pre-closing announcement stated "customers of Bear State Bank and Arvest
  will not notice any immediate changes" following the merger, and that "[a]t a later
  date, Bear State Bank's branding will change to Arvest Bank, with the full conversion
  of systems expected to occur later in 2018" — the customer-facing product/brand and
  underlying operating systems were structured to continue unchanged at the moment of
  legal closing, with system/brand conversion deferred rather than completed at closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-006071, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]

## Closing-time listing and control events
- The transaction closed April 20, 2018; each outstanding Bear State share converted
  into the right to receive $10.28 cash, and outstanding options/warrants/restricted
  stock units were cancelled and converted into cash-equivalent amounts. In connection
  with closing, Bear State notified NASDAQ of completion and requested that NASDAQ file
  a Form 25 to remove Bear State common stock from listing and suspend trading before
  the opening of trading on April 23, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-007338, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.01]
- At the effective time, shareholders ceased to have any rights as shareholders (other
  than the right to receive merger consideration), and a change of control of the
  registrant occurred, after which the registrant's separate existence ceased. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-007338, Form 8-K Items 3.03, 5.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- This was a whole-company cash acquisition of a state bank holding company and its bank
  subsidiary by a substantially larger, privately held state bank, structured as a
  two-step merger in which the target's branch network was absorbed directly into the
  acquirer's own branch system, the target's legal entities were structured for
  dissolution/merger-out rather than preservation, its board and named executive officers
  ceased to hold office at closing, workforce/benefit-plan continuity was addressed
  through individual change-in-control/retention terms and plan-termination provisions
  rather than a company-wide transition-services bridge, and the customer-facing
  brand/systems conversion was explicitly deferred to "later in 2018" rather than
  completed at the legal closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-17-016831, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-007338, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-006071, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
