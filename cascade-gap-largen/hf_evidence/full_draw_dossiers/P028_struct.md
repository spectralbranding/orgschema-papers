# Structural sub-dossier — Keysight Technologies separation from Agilent Technologies (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- In September 2013 Agilent Technologies, Inc. announced plans to separate its electronic
  measurement business from its life sciences, chemical analysis, diagnostics and genomics
  businesses; the separation was effected as a spin-off of a newly formed company,
  Keysight Technologies, Inc., created to hold the electronic measurement business, with
  the remaining businesses staying in the existing public company Agilent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The separation took the form of a pro rata distribution of 100% of the outstanding shares
  of Keysight common stock to Agilent shareholders, at a ratio of one Keysight share for
  every two Agilent shares held on the October 22, 2014 record date, distributed on
  November 1, 2014; no shareholder vote or consideration was required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Keysight, a Delaware corporation and previously a wholly owned subsidiary of Agilent,
  was formed to hold, directly or indirectly, the assets and liabilities associated with
  Agilent's electronic measurement business; it applied to list its common stock on the
  NYSE under the symbol "KEYS." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing product line that crossed comprised electronic measurement
  instruments and systems and related software, software design tools and related services
  used in the design, development, manufacture, installation, deployment and operation of
  electronics. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The producing/delivering processes moved with the product: the business's primary
  research-and-development and manufacturing sites — in California and Colorado (U.S.) and
  in China, Germany, India, Japan, Malaysia, Singapore and Spain, with the Penang,
  Malaysia facility described as the largest manufacturing facility — were part of the
  electronic measurement business transferred to Keysight, along with its direct sales
  force, distributor, reseller and manufacturer's-representative sales channels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People crossed with the operations: as of October 31, 2013 the business's headcount was
  approximately 8,500, and prior to the separation approximately 1,200 additional employees
  of Agilent's corporate and shared services were to be transferred to the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Certain shared corporate functions historically provided by Agilent did NOT cross as an
  intact substrate: the information statement records that Keysight was "in the process of
  creating its own, or engaging third parties to provide, systems and services to replace
  many of the systems and services that Agilent currently provides," including transitioning
  data from Agilent's systems to Keysight's, and that Keysight might incur higher costs for
  functions previously performed by Agilent such as accounting, tax, legal, human resources
  and other general administrative functions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- To effect the separation and frame the ongoing relationship, Keysight entered into a
  separation and distribution agreement with Agilent plus a services agreement, a tax
  matters agreement, an employee matters agreement, an intellectual property matters
  agreement, a trademark license agreement and a real estate matters agreement, which
  allocate assets, employees, liabilities and obligations between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The services agreement, effective upon distribution, provided for each company to supply
  the other with various services — including information technology, accounts payable,
  payroll and other financial functions and administrative services — with charges
  generally set to recover out-of-pocket costs plus a predetermined profit, and each
  service terminable by the recipient on 30 days' notice up to the last service's
  expiration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The information statement states Keysight "will rely on Agilent to satisfy its
  performance and payment obligations under these agreements," and that if Keysight did not
  put in place its own systems and services or secure other providers once the transaction
  agreements expired, it might not be able to operate its business effectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A customer-facing product operation (electronic measurement instruments, systems and
  related software/services) crossed the boundary together with its R&D and manufacturing
  sites, sales channels, its approximately 8,500-person workforce and approximately 1,200
  transferred Agilent corporate/shared-services employees, while Agilent's shared
  administrative substrate (IT, accounts payable, payroll, financial and administrative
  functions) was bridged at closing by a services agreement and the related separation
  agreements rather than transferred intact, with Keysight tasked to stand up or procure
  replacement systems. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-007795, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
