# Outcome sub-dossier — Jonah Gas Gathering System joint venture, realized ~3-5-year record (Enterprise Products Partners, FY2009-FY2010)

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-closing record. The outcome window is fixed as Enterprise Products Partners L.P.'s
> FY2009-FY2010 annual reports (the 3-4-year horizon after the 2006 joint venture). It

## Whether the venture was consummated
- The joint venture to further expand the Jonah system was entered into by TEPPCO and
  Enterprise in August 2006. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- Enterprise and TEPPCO were joint venture partners in Jonah prior to the later merger of
  the two registrants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 7 / Notes]

## Realized operating record of the asset (in-window)
- In mid-2009 an expansion of the portion of the system serving the Pinedale field was
  completed, increasing total capacity of the Jonah Gathering System from 2.35 Bcf/d to
  2.55 Bcf/d. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 1 / Item 2]
- For fiscal 2009, the Jonah Gathering System contributed a $17.0 million year-to-year
  increase in gross operating margin, primarily due to higher natural gas gathering
  volumes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-11-000009, Form 10-K (FY2010) Item 7]
- For fiscal 2010, gross operating margin from the Jonah, Val Verde and Carlsbad gathering
  systems collectively decreased $21.2 million year-to-year, primarily due to lower
  natural gas gathering volumes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-11-000009, Form 10-K (FY2010) Item 7]
- The FY2009 10-K's segment reconciliation disclosed revenues from Jonah of $232.8 million
  (2008) and $204.1 million (2007), and gross operating margin from Jonah of $157.6 million
  (2008) and $125.4 million (2007). [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 7 "Other Items"]

## Realized structural resolution of the joint venture (in-window)
- On October 26, 2009, the mergers of Enterprise's wholly-owned subsidiaries with TEPPCO
  Partners, L.P. and TEPPCO GP were completed; TEPPCO and TEPPCO GP became wholly-owned
  subsidiaries of Enterprise, each TEPPCO unitholder (other than a privately held EPCO
  affiliate) receiving 1.24 Enterprise common units per TEPPCO unit, and TEPPCO's units
  (NYSE: TPP) were delisted. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 7]
- The merger was accounted for at historical cost as a reorganization of entities under
  the common control of EPCO and its affiliates, in a manner similar to a pooling of
  interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 7]
- As a result of the merger, Jonah — in which Enterprise and TEPPCO had been joint venture
  partners — became a consolidated subsidiary; the equity earnings from Jonah recorded by
  each partner prior to the merger were thereafter consolidated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Item 7 "Other Items"]
- The asset was retained, not divested: at December 31, 2010 the Jonah natural gas
  gathering-contract intangible assets carried a value of $113.1 million, amortized using a
  units-of-production method on Jonah system throughput estimated to extend through 2041.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-11-000009, Form 10-K (FY2010) Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]

## Documented disputes bearing on the venture (in-window)
- A putative class and derivative action (Brinckerhoff) alleged, among other things, that
  the August 2006 joint venture to further expand the Jonah system was unfair to TEPPCO —
  specifically that TEPPCO did not receive fair value for allowing Enterprise to
  participate in the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Notes / Commitments and Contingencies]
- Separate putative class actions concerning the TEPPCO Merger were consolidated on May 11,
  2009 under the caption Texas Eastern Products Pipeline Company, LLC Merger Litigation,
  C.A. No. 4548-VCL, naming Enterprise, EPGP, TEPPCO GP, the directors of TEPPCO GP, EPCO,
  and Dan L. Duncan as defendants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009) Notes / Commitments and Contingencies]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2009-FY2010 window the venture was consummated (August 2006),
  operated and physically expanded (Pinedale capacity 2.35 to 2.55 Bcf/d in mid-2009,
  with the asset retained and gathering-contract intangibles of $113.1 million at
  year-end 2010), and its co-ownership structure was then dissolved into single ownership
  when the two commonly-controlled co-owners merged on October 26, 2009, after which Jonah
  became a wholly-consolidated subsidiary; the fairness of both the original venture to
  TEPPCO's unitholders and the subsequent merger was contested in litigation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-10-000011, Form 10-K (FY2009)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-11-000009, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
