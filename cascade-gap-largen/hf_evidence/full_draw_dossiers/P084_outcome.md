# Outcome sub-dossier — Marathon Oil Corporation realized record, ~2014-2016 horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-separation record of the retained entity (Marathon Oil Corporation, CIK 101778)
> within a fixed 3-5-year window after the ~2011 closing. It contains no information
> about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Outcome window
- Marathon Oil Corporation remained a separately reporting public company after the
  2011 separation and continued to file annual reports on Form 10-K; the window fixed
  for this dossier is the fiscal year 2015 Form 10-K (filed 2016-02-25), covering the
  entity's realized record roughly 3.5-4.5 years after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Marathon Oil reported a consolidated net loss of $2,204 million for fiscal year 2015,
  compared with consolidated net income of $3,046 million for fiscal year 2014 — a swing
  driven by a decline in segment income from $1,496 million (2014) to a segment loss of
  $487 million (2015) plus $1,717 million of items not allocated to segments (2015) net
  of income taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- Each of the three reporting segments posted a year-over-year decline in segment
  income in 2015: North America E&P moved from income of $693 million (2014) to a loss
  of $486 million (2015); International E&P declined from $568 million to $112 million;
  Oil Sands Mining moved from income of $235 million to a loss of $113 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The company recorded total impairments of proved properties of $752 million in 2015
  (versus $132 million in 2014), comprising a $340 million goodwill impairment of the
  North America E&P reporting unit, $335 million of proved-property impairments
  (primarily Colorado and Gulf of Mexico) attributed to lower forecasted commodity
  prices, and $44 million associated with the disposition of natural gas assets in East
  Texas, North Louisiana, and Wilburton, Oklahoma. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The 2014 net income figure included after-tax gains of $532 million and $976 million
  recorded within discontinued operations related to the dispositions of the company's
  Angola assets and Norway business, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Documented divestitures and capital-program response within the window
- In August 2015 the company closed the sale of its East Texas, North Louisiana, and
  Wilburton, Oklahoma natural gas assets, and in the fourth quarter of 2015 it closed
  the sale of part of its Gulf of Mexico business, as part of a stated divestiture
  program with a goal of $750 million to $1 billion in proceeds. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The company reduced its 2015 Capital Program by approximately 50% versus the prior
  year to $3 billion, and set its 2016 Capital Program lower still, citing continued
  capital discipline. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The company decreased its quarterly common-stock dividend from $0.21 to $0.05 per
  share, a reduction stated to save approximately $425 million of cash on an annualized
  basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Documented organizational response within the window
- General and administrative expense declined $64 million year-over-year, attributed
  primarily to cost savings from workforce reductions that occurred during 2015,
  partially offset by $55 million of severance expense associated with those same
  workforce reductions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The company reported 2,611 active, full-time employees as of December 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 3.5-4.5-year post-separation window, the retained upstream entity's
  realized record shows a swing from net income of $3,046 million (FY2014, aided by
  one-time discontinued-operations gains on the Angola and Norway asset sales) to a net
  loss of $2,204 million (FY2015), accompanied by a $752 million impairment charge
  (including a $340 million goodwill impairment), a roughly 50% cut to the capital
  program, a 76% cut to the quarterly dividend, and workforce reductions carrying
  $55 million of severance cost, alongside continued portfolio divestitures (East
  Texas/North Louisiana/Wilburton gas assets; a portion of the Gulf of Mexico business). [SRC: SEC accession 0000101778-16-000047, Form 10-K FY2015]
