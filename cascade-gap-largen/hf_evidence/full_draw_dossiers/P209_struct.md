# Structural sub-dossier — Rice Energy Inc. merger with EQT Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- EQT Corporation ("EQT"), Eagle Merger Sub I, Inc., an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of EQT ("Merger Sub"), and Rice Energy Inc. ("Rice") entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 19, 2017 (amended by Amendment No. 1 dated
  October 26, 2017), providing for Merger Sub to merge with and into Rice, with Rice
  surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately following the effective time, the surviving Rice corporation was to be
  merged with and into an indirect, wholly owned limited liability company subsidiary
  of EQT, with that LLC surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of EQT (a
  two-step, forward-then-downstream merger structure). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was documented as a joint proxy statement/prospectus (Form DEFM14A)
  forming part of EQT's registration statement on Form S-4 (File No. 333-219508),
  declared effective October 12, 2017; EQT's shareholder vote to approve the required
  share issuance and Rice's stockholder vote to adopt the merger agreement were each
  scheduled for November 9, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger consideration: each share of Rice common stock was converted into the right
  to receive 0.37 of a share of EQT common stock plus $5.30 in cash; based on EQT's
  closing price the day before announcement this represented approximately $27.04 in
  value per Rice share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- On a pro forma basis following the transaction, existing EQT shareholders were
  expected to hold approximately 65% and former Rice stockholders approximately 35% of
  outstanding EQT common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on November 13, 2017: Merger Sub merged with and into Rice (Rice
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of EQT), and immediately thereafter the
  surviving corporation was merged with and into EQT RE, LLC, another wholly owned
  indirect EQT subsidiary, which became successor in interest to Rice. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]
- Aggregate merger consideration issued at closing consisted of approximately 91.90
  million shares of EQT common stock and approximately $1.3 billion in cash (inclusive
  of amounts payable to Rice employees not continuing with EQT). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]

## What Rice comprised at closing (the acquired product/asset tier)
- Rice operated as an independent natural gas, oil and NGL producer in the Appalachian
  Basin across three managed segments: Exploration and Production (acquisition,
  exploration and development of natural gas, oil and NGLs), Rice Midstream Holdings
  (gathering/compression in Belmont and Monroe Counties, Ohio), and Rice Midstream
  Partners (gathering/compression in Washington and Greene Counties, Pennsylvania).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and financing changes at closing
- In connection with closing, all outstanding obligations under Rice's Fourth Amended
  and Restated Credit Agreement and Rice Midstream Holdings LLC's separate credit
  agreement were repaid and those agreements were terminated; Rice's outstanding 6.25%
  Senior Notes due 2022 and 7.25% Senior Notes due 2023 were called for redemption and
  were redeemed and cancelled at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the merger, Rice requested that the New York Stock Exchange
  suspend trading in and remove the listing of Rice common stock (before the opening
  of trading on November 13, 2017), with a subsequent Form 15 planned to terminate
  Rice's Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]

## People/management tier at closing
- The Rice board considered, among factors bearing on its recommendation, "the
  expectation that many Rice employees will not be retained by EQT following
  completion of the merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement required the EQT board to appoint Rice's Chief Executive
  Officer and the chairman of the Rice board to the combined company's board of
  directors (Daniel J. Rice IV and Robert F. Vagt were the named Rice designees),
  contingent on shareholder approval of a related charter amendment enlarging the EQT
  board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- Concurrent with signing, Rice entered into amendments to each executive officer's
  employment agreement and a separate confidentiality, non-solicitation and
  non-competition agreement with Daniel J. Rice III, restricting competitive activity
  within the Appalachian Basin for three years following the end of employment (three
  years from closing for Mr. Rice III). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A]
- Rice equity awards held by employees continuing with EQT were converted into EQT
  restricted stock unit awards at closing; awards held by employees identified as not
  continuing vested and were settled for the merger consideration at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]
- A Separation and Release Agreement dated November 13, 2017 among EQT, EQT RE, LLC
  and Daniel J. Rice IV is listed as an exhibit incorporated by reference in EQT's
  later annual report. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K Exhibit Index]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company stock-and-cash merger (via a forward merger
  into Rice followed immediately by a downstream merger into an EQT LLC subsidiary)
  with a closing-day removal of Rice's separate capital structure (debt repaid/
  redeemed, common stock removed from NYSE listing) and a bifurcated treatment of
  Rice's workforce: two named Rice principals were slated for EQT board seats while
  the Rice board itself anticipated that a substantial portion of Rice's employees
  would not be retained by EQT after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006341, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K]
