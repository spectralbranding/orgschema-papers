# Outcome sub-dossier — Swift Energy Company realized record, ~2014-2016 window

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier contains no information about how
> any transaction was structured at closing. The outcome window is fixed at Swift Energy
> Company's own reported record for fiscal years 2014-2015 (the 3-5-year horizon after
> ~2011), traced through its subsequent SEC filings.

## Realized financial scalar (best-documented)
- For fiscal year 2015 the company reported a net loss of $1.7 billion, "primarily due to
  the $1.6 billion non-cash write-down of our oil and gas properties" (the full-cost
  ceiling-test write-down). [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The company disclosed that if commodity prices remained at or below the levels used in
  the fourth-quarter 2015 ceiling test, it anticipated being "required to record
  additional non-cash write-downs of oil and gas properties in the first quarter of
  2016." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Public-listing and going-concern status
- The company's common stock, traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol
  "SFY," received an NYSE notice of delisting for non-compliance with continued-listing
  standards and was delisted on December 18, 2015; it subsequently traded on the OTC Pink
  marketplace under "SFYWQ." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The FY2015 Form 10-K states that the company's condition "raises substantial doubt
  about the Company's ability to continue as a going concern." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Insolvency event
- On December 31, 2015 (the "Petition Date") Swift Energy Company and eight subsidiaries
  filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code in
  the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, seeking joint administration
  under the caption In re Swift Energy Company, et al (Case No. 15-12670). [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- In connection with the filing the company reached a Restructuring Support Agreement with
  holders of a majority of its senior notes "to convert all senior notes to equity";
  existing equity holders were to retain four percent of the company's equity on a
  fully diluted basis, plus warrants for up to 30% of the post-petition equity exercisable
  on reaching certain benchmarks. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The company filed a motion for debtor-in-possession financing of a multi-draw term loan
  of up to $75,000,000, with $15,000,000 sought on an interim basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Commencement of the Chapter 11 cases constituted an event of default that accelerated
  obligations under the company's senior-note indentures (7.125% notes due 2017, 8.875%
  notes due 2020, 7.875% notes due 2022) and its Second Amended and Restated Credit
  Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 2.04]

## Disposition of operating assets (interface record over time)
- On December 31, 2015 the company entered into a purchase and sale agreement with Texegy
  LLC to sell a 75% interest in an approximately 65,132 net-acre position in the South
  Bearhead Creek and Burr Ferry fields (central Louisiana) for $48,750,000 in cash,
  subject to Bankruptcy Court approval; the FY2015 10-K notes the Court approved the sale
  on February 2, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Under the associated joint-development and joint-operating agreements to be entered at
  closing, an affiliate of Texegy (SV Energy Company, LLC) was to serve as operator of the
  properties, "conducting all drilling, completion and production operations," and future
  development plans were to be "mutually agreed upon by the Company and Texegy." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 2014-2015 outcome window the company's realized record was a 2015 net
  loss of $1.7 billion (driven by a $1.6 billion non-cash property write-down), a
  going-concern qualification, delisting from the NYSE on December 18, 2015, and a
  December 31, 2015 Chapter 11 filing accompanied by a noteholder restructuring support
  agreement, a $75 million DIP facility, and a Court-approved sale of a 75% interest in
  its South Bearhead Creek and Burr Ferry Louisiana acreage to Texegy LLC with a
  Texegy affiliate taking over operatorship. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000042, Form 10-K (FY2015)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-16-000036, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
