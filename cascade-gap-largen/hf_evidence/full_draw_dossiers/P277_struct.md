# Structural sub-dossier — Corporate Property Associates 17 – Global Incorporated merger into W. P. Carey Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- W. P. Carey Inc. ("W. P. Carey") and Corporate Property Associates 17 – Global
  Incorporated ("CPA:17 – Global") proposed a merger of CPA:17 – Global with and into
  CPA17 Merger Sub LLC, an indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey ("Merger Sub"), with
  Merger Sub surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of W. P. Carey, pursuant to
  a definitive Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 17, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- As of the Effective Time, each share of CPA:17 common stock would be cancelled and
  converted into the right to receive 0.160 shares of W. P. Carey common stock (the
  "Exchange Ratio"); W. P. Carey expected to issue approximately 53.9 million shares of
  its common stock in connection with the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- CPA:17 – Global is described as "an externally managed, publicly owned non-traded REIT
  that invests in a diversified portfolio of income producing commercial properties
  leased to companies, both domestically and internationally." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]

## Pre-existing advisory/management relationship
- W. P. Carey, the ultimate parent of CPA:17 – Global's external manager, already
  managed CPA:17 – Global through certain of its wholly owned subsidiaries pursuant to
  the "CPA:17 Advisory Agreements" prior to the Merger; CPA:17 – Global paid the advisor
  asset-management fees, transactional fees, and expense reimbursements, including for
  "personnel provided for administration of CPA:17 – Global's operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- "As a result, CPA:17 – Global has no employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- The proxy discloses that "W. P. Carey and its affiliates serve as the advisor for
  CPA:17 – Global, the companies share common management, and the officers and directors
  of W. P. Carey and CPA:17 – Global may have certain interests in the proposed
  transactions that are different from or in addition to" those of the two companies'
  respective stockholders; CPA:17 – Global's board formed a special committee of
  independent directors that retained its own independent legal and financial advisors
  (Morgan Stanley) in response. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]

## Customer-facing asset that crossed
- As of June 30, 2018, CPA:17 – Global owned full or partial ownership interests in 411
  net-leased commercial properties (99.7% occupancy, 11.3-year weighted average lease
  term), plus 37 self-storage properties and one hotel property, for an aggregate of
  approximately 47 million square feet. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and listing status
- CPA:17 – Global would merge with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub continuing as
  the surviving entity and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of W. P. Carey, and "the
  separate existence of CPA:17 – Global will cease." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- CPA:17 – Global's own common stock was not listed on a national securities exchange
  (it is described throughout as a non-traded REIT); at the Effective Time its shares
  would simply be cancelled and converted into the right to receive shares of W. P.
  Carey common stock, which the parties anticipated would trade on the NYSE under the
  symbol "WPC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]

## Process/organization continuity asserted by both boards
- Among the reasons W. P. Carey's board cited for the Merger: "the Merger will have
  limited integration risk due to W. P. Carey's experience managing CPA:17 – Global's
  assets and operations," and, "given that W. P. Carey and its affiliates act as CPA:17
  – Global's advisor and manage the day-to-day activities of CPA:17 – Global, the Merger
  would require less real estate due diligence than would otherwise occur with an
  unrelated third party and significantly reduces integration risks"; anticipated cost
  savings were not quantified "since they were expected to be relatively immaterial ...
  given that CPA:17 – Global is managed by W. P. Carey, does not have any employees, and
  has very little in terms of operational costs or overhead aside from the advisory fees
  paid to W. P. Carey." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- Among liquidity alternatives considered and rejected by the CPA:17 Special Committee:
  pursuing an independent public listing as an internally managed REIT, on the view that
  "it could be challenging to retain a management team in order to pursue a listing as
  an internally managed REIT." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- Following completion, W. P. Carey's chief executive stated in a press release that,
  "having assembled and managed the assets on behalf of CPA:17," the company expected "a
  seamless transition." [SRC: https://www.insideselfstorage.com/acquisitions-buying/w-p-carey-acquires-44-self-storage-properties-in-5-9b-merger-with-cpa-17-fund]

## Contractual settlement of the pre-existing advisory relationship at closing
- The Merger Agreement provided that W. P. Carey and its affiliates would continue to
  receive fees and distributions accrued under the CPA:17 Advisory Agreements and the
  CPA:17 LP Agreement prior to closing (approximately $9.5 million accrued and unpaid at
  June 30, 2018), and separately defines "Advisor Closing Amounts" payable to W. P.
  Carey in connection with the Merger to settle amounts otherwise owed to it as advisor,
  which could be credited against, or increased by, a termination fee depending on the
  circumstances of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction merged a portfolio-holding REIT that had no employees of its own and
  was already managed day-to-day by the acquirer as its external advisor into a
  subsidiary of that same acquirer; both boards' stated rationale rested explicitly on
  the pre-existing advisory/management relationship as reducing integration risk, and
  the pre-existing advisory-fee arrangement was settled contractually through defined
  "Advisor Closing Amounts" rather than through a newly created transition-services
  agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
