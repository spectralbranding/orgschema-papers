# Structural sub-dossier — Select Income REIT merger into Government Properties Income Trust (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Government Properties Income Trust ("GOV") and Select Income REIT ("SIR"), each a
  Maryland real estate investment trust, entered into an agreement and plan of merger
  dated September 14, 2018 under which SIR would merge with and into GOV MS REIT, a
  wholly owned subsidiary of GOV ("GOV Merger Sub"), with GOV Merger Sub as the
  surviving entity; immediately following that merger, GOV Merger Sub was expected to
  merge with and into GOV, with GOV as the surviving entity, and GOV would change its
  name to "Office Properties Income Trust" ("OPI"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("SIR has agreed to merge with and into GOV Merger Sub... GOV will change its name to \"Office Properties Income Trust\"")]
- Consideration was a fixed exchange ratio: each SIR common share (other than excluded
  shares) would convert into 1.04 newly issued GOV common shares, not adjusted for
  market-price fluctuation prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("SIR shareholders will be entitled to receive 1.04... newly issued GOV Common Shares, for each SIR Common Share... The exchange ratio is fixed and will not be adjusted")]
- GOV was to account for the merger as a business combination under GAAP, with GOV
  treated as the acquirer of SIR for accounting purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV will account for the Merger as a business combination with GOV treated as the acquirer of SIR")]
- Existing SIR shareholders were expected to hold approximately 48.4% of the combined
  company (OPI) immediately following the merger through their receipt of newly issued
  GOV common shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("The GOV board of trustees... believes... shareholders will also participate directly in the growth of the combined company through their continued ownership of approximately 48.4%")]

## Pre-closing carve-out of a SIR asset (product-tier separation before the merger)
- As a condition to closing, SIR was required to declare and, at least one business day
  before the closing date, pay a pro rata distribution to its own shareholders of all
  45,000,000 common shares of Industrial Logistics Properties Trust ("ILPT") — a
  majority-owned (approximately 69.2%), separately traded consolidated subsidiary of
  SIR holding an industrial/logistics property portfolio (269 buildings/parcels,
  approximately 29.2 million rentable square feet as of September 30, 2018) — the
  "ILPT Distribution." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("ILPT Distribution refers to the pro rata distribution by SIR to its shareholders of all of the 45,000,000 ILPT Common Shares owned by SIR"; ILPT portfolio detail)]
- Completion of the ILPT Distribution was not conditioned on completion of the merger,
  so the industrial-property asset could separate from SIR's shareholder base
  independently of whether the merger with GOV closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("Completion of the ILPT Distribution is not conditioned upon consummation of the Merger.")]
- Separately, GOV was required by the merger agreement to sell, prior to the merger's
  effective time, all 24,918,421 SIR common shares GOV already owned (approximately
  27.8% of SIR's outstanding shares) — the "Secondary Sale" — completed October 9, 2018
  in an underwritten public offering at $18.25 per share, net proceeds approximately
  $434.7 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV sold all 24,918,421 SIR Common Shares owned by GOV... raising net proceeds of approximately $434.7 million")]
- GOV also separately expected, prior to closing, to complete the sale of 20 properties
  (50 buildings) referred to as the "Other Asset Dispositions," excluded from the
  portfolio contributed to the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("Prior to the closing of the Merger, GOV expects to complete the sale of the Other Asset Dispositions. These 20 properties (50 buildings)...")]

## Business model and legal vehicle at the tier level
- GOV's pre-merger business consisted of properties majority leased to government
  tenants and Washington, D.C.-area office properties leased to government and private
  tenants, wholly owning 105 properties (164 buildings) as of September 30, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV primarily owns properties... leased to government tenants..."; "GOV wholly owned 105 properties (164 buildings)")]
- SIR's pre-merger business consisted of properties primarily net leased to single
  tenants, with a consolidated portfolio (including ILPT) of 368 buildings/parcels and
  approximately 45.8 million rentable square feet as of September 30, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("SIR owns properties that are primarily net leased to single tenants"; portfolio detail)]
- Both GOV and SIR are structured as externally managed REITs with no employees of
  their own; personnel and services required to operate each business were instead
  provided under business and property management agreements with The RMR Group LLC
  ("RMR LLC"), which has successive 20-year terms with each entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV has no employees. Services which would otherwise be provided to GOV by employees are provided by... RMR LLC"; "SIR has no employees. Services... are provided by RMR LLC"; "management agreements with RMR LLC, which have successive 20 year terms")]

## What crossed at closing versus what was structurally re-routed (process/organization tier)
- Contemporaneously with the merger agreement, RMR LLC and SIR entered into a letter
  agreement under which SIR's business and property management agreements with RMR LLC
  would be terminated "for convenience" effective upon consummation of the merger, and
  RMR LLC agreed to waive the termination fee otherwise payable on that termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("RMR LLC and SIR entered into the RMR LLC Letter Agreement, pursuant to which... SIR shall have terminated its business and property management agreements with RMR LLC for convenience, and RMR LLC shall have waived its right to receive payment of the termination fee")]
- GOV's own business and property management agreements with RMR LLC were expected to
  remain in effect and apply to the combined company after the merger, rather than a
  new or SIR-derived management arrangement being adopted. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("If the Merger is consummated, GOV's business management and property management agreements will remain in effect and apply to the combined company.")]
- The disclosure explains that GOV was determined to be the acquirer for accounting
  purposes in part because both companies rely on RMR LLC for personnel and services,
  and GOV's own management agreements with RMR LLC would be in place both before and
  after the transactions, so GOV was considered to acquire "a substantive process" for
  accounting purposes through that continuity — i.e., the operating process/personnel
  substrate for the combined company was the one already attached to GOV via RMR LLC,
  not one that moved over intact from SIR. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV will acquire a substantive process, for accounting purposes, because the business management and property management agreements with RMR LLC would be in place before and after the Transactions are completed.")]
- Adam D. Portnoy, a managing trustee of both GOV and SIR, was also an officer of the
  entity controlling RMR LLC, and other GOV/SIR managing trustees and executive
  officers were also employees of RMR LLC — a controlling-principal linkage that ran
  through the shared external manager rather than through either REIT's own payroll. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("Adam D. Portnoy... is the sole trustee, an officer and the controlling shareholder of ABP Trust, which is the controlling shareholder of RMR Inc.... GOV's, SIR's and ILPT's other managing trustee and each of their executive officers also serve as executive officers, and are employees, of RMR LLC.")]

## Governance and listing at closing
- Following the merger, GOV's two managing trustees were expected to continue as the
  managing trustees of OPI's board; the OPI board was to expand from six to eight
  members, adding two SIR independent trustees (Donna D. Fraiche and William A.
  Lamkin) alongside four continuing GOV/SIR independent trustees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("GOV expects that... Messrs. Adam D. Portnoy and David M. Blackman will serve as the managing trustees... The GOV board of trustees plans... to increase the size of the board of trustees of OPI from six members to eight members, and to invite Donna D. Fraiche and William A. Lamkin...")]
- Under Section 7.10 of the merger agreement (the provision governing removal of the
  Company's shares from exchange listing and deregistration), GOV and the surviving
  entity agreed to use reasonable best efforts to have SIR's common shares removed
  from listing on Nasdaq and to have them deregistered under the Exchange Act promptly
  following the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Section 7.10]
- GOV common shares issued in the merger were to continue trading on Nasdaq, with GOV
  itself expected to trade thereafter under the new ticker symbol "OPI." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A ("subsequent to the Effective Time, for the Parent Common Shares to be traded on Nasdaq under the symbol \"OPI.\"")]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, SIR's shareholder base separated from an industrial-property asset (the
  ILPT stake, distributed pro rata before the merger) and SIR itself then merged into a
  GOV subsidiary and disappeared as a listed entity, with GOV surviving under the new
  name Office Properties Income Trust; the personnel/process substrate for the combined
  company was the one already running through GOV's continuing RMR LLC management
  agreements, while SIR's parallel management agreements with the same manager were
  terminated for convenience concurrent with closing rather than carried forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007290, DEFM14A]
