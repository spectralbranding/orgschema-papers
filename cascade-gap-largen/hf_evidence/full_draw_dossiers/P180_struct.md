# Structural sub-dossier — ClubCorp Holdings, Inc. acquisition by affiliates of Apollo Global Management (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- ClubCorp Holdings, Inc. ("ClubCorp"), a Nevada corporation, entered into an Agreement
  and Plan of Merger dated July 9, 2017 with Constellation Club Parent, Inc. ("Parent"),
  a Delaware corporation, and Constellation Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a Nevada
  corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; Parent and Merger Sub were formed by
  funds managed by affiliates of Apollo Management VIII, L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into ClubCorp, with ClubCorp
  surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share of ClubCorp common stock was converted into the right to
  receive $17.12 in cash, a premium of approximately 30.7% over the July 7, 2017 closing
  price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on September 18, 2017 (the "Closing Date"); the aggregate
  consideration paid by Parent to ClubCorp's stockholders was approximately $1,123
  million, funded through equity contributions from Apollo-affiliated funds and other
  investors together with debt financing proceeds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K Item 2.01/5.01]
- ClubCorp requested that the NYSE remove its common stock from listing effective
  September 19, 2017 and intended to file a Form 15 to terminate Exchange Act
  registration and reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K Item 3.01]

## Business acquired and legal vehicle
- At signing, ClubCorp described itself as a membership-based leisure business and a
  leading owner-operator of private golf and country clubs and business, sports and
  alumni clubs in North America, with a portfolio of 204 owned or operated clubs across
  27 states, the District of Columbia and two foreign countries, serving over 430,000
  individual members. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- The transaction acquired ClubCorp's entire existing corporate structure via a
  whole-company merger vehicle (Merger Sub merging into ClubCorp); the merger agreement
  is not structured as a spin-off, carve-out, or asset-only purchase of a discrete
  business line. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- Financing of the merger consideration and related refinancing needs (expected to total
  approximately $2.4 billion) was structured as debt financing in an aggregate principal
  amount of $1.6 billion plus a $175 million revolving facility, together with equity
  financing of approximately $1.1 billion committed by the Apollo Funds under an equity
  commitment letter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]

## People / management continuity at the closing boundary
- Following consummation of the merger, all directors then serving on ClubCorp's Board
  were removed without cause, and Parent, as sole stockholder, elected Mark A. Burnett
  (ClubCorp's President since July 2016 and Chief Operating Officer since October 2013)
  and Ingrid J. Keiser (ClubCorp's General Counsel, Secretary and EVP of People Strategy
  since July 2008) as the new directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K Item 5.01/5.02]
- The Closing-Date Form 8-K was signed on ClubCorp's behalf by Curtis D. McClellan,
  Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer, indicating continuity of the pre-merger finance
  officer into the immediate post-closing entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K signature block]
- At the effective time, ClubCorp's articles of incorporation and bylaws were amended
  and restated per forms attached to the merger agreement, and ClubCorp's fiscal year
  provisions were subject to amendment under the restated bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K Item 5.03]
- ClubCorp's pre-merger executive officers named in the proxy (Eric L. Affeldt, CEO;
  Mark A. Burnett, President/COO; Curtis D. McClellan, CFO; Ingrid J. Keiser, General
  Counsel/EVP People Strategy; Charles H. Feddersen; Patrick A. Droesch; Andrew V.
  Miller; Todd M. Dupuis) held outstanding equity awards and change-in-control severance
  agreements that would be triggered by the merger and, for certain executives, by a
  qualifying termination of employment thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]

## Interim operating covenants (closing-time bridge)
- Pending the merger, the merger agreement required ClubCorp and its subsidiaries to
  conduct their businesses in the ordinary course of business in all material respects
  consistent with past practice and to maintain their existence in good standing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement noted that ClubCorp retained sufficient operating flexibility to
  conduct its business in the ordinary course between signing and closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, going-private cash merger in which ClubCorp's
  entire existing operating business, brand, and a substantial portion of its incumbent
  senior operating management (President/COO, General Counsel, CFO) crossed into the
  new Apollo-affiliated ownership structure via a single merger vehicle, funded by a
  combination of Apollo-affiliate equity and new debt financing, with the pre-merger
  Board replaced by Parent-designated directors at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005072, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001577095-17-000161, 8-K]
