## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction was a distribution ("spin-off"): General Growth Properties, Inc. ("GGP") furnished the information statement in connection with the distribution to its stockholders of substantially all of the outstanding common stock of Rouse Properties, Inc. ("Rouse"), a newly-formed subsidiary of GGP that would hold, directly or indirectly, the assets and liabilities associated with 30 regional shopping malls formerly owned and operated by GGP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- GGP distributed as a dividend all shares of Rouse Common Stock on a pro rata basis to GGP's stockholders in a taxable transaction; holders of record as of December 30, 2011 would receive approximately 0.0375 shares of Rouse Common Stock per GGP share, with cash in lieu of fractional shares, and the distribution was to occur on January 12, 2012. Approximately 35.54 million shares of Common Stock were to be distributed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- No vote of GGP's stockholders was required, and GGP stockholders were not required to pay consideration or surrender existing GGP shares to receive Rouse shares; following the distribution GGP would not own any of Rouse's Common Stock (a subsidiary of GGP would hold non-voting Class B Common Stock representing approximately 1.0% of aggregate shares). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The separation was registered on a Registration Statement on Form 10 under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934; Rouse Common Stock was expected to trade on the NYSE under the symbol "RSE." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset: the Rouse Portfolio comprised 30 regional malls in 19 states totaling over 21 million square feet of retail and ancillary space; the company described itself as the 8th largest publicly-traded regional mall owner in the United States based on total square footage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / organization: following the distribution the company expected to have approximately 275 employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Management: Andrew Silberfein was expected to serve as President, Chief Executive Officer and a director; Michael McNaughton, expected to serve as Chief Operating Officer, had previously served as GGP's Executive Vice President of Asset Management, and the operational management team included experienced members of GGP's former organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared substrate that did NOT transfer as an intact operating whole
- Prior to the separation the business was operated by GGP as part of its broader corporate organization rather than as a separate company; GGP or its affiliates performed various corporate functions for the business, including property management, human resources, security, payroll and benefits, legal, corporate communications, information services, and restructuring and reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The business's historical results reflected allocations of these corporate costs; the company stated it had "no experience operating as an independent company and performing various corporate functions," and effective with the separation it would assume responsibility for all of these functions and anticipated its costs as a stand-alone entity would be higher than those allocated from GGP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The business had historically shared economies of scope and scale in costs, employees, vendor relationships and certain customer relationships with the other businesses of GGP; the company cautioned that its contractual arrangements after separation might not capture those benefits and that transition services would be provided only for a limited period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- Separation Agreement: pursuant to the separation agreement GGP would transfer the Rouse Portfolio to the company, which would assume all liabilities relating to the Rouse Portfolio (except certain liabilities arising from intentional fraud, misrepresentation or a knowing violation of law by a GGP officer, director or employee); the parties would also enter into transitional agreements allocating certain assets, liabilities, employees and obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Transition Services Agreement with GGP: provided for various services to be provided to the company by GGP — including accounting, asset management, development, human resources, information technology, leasing, legal, marketing, public reporting and tax — charged on an hourly-fee basis with pass-through of out-of-pocket costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Services agreement with Brookfield: the company expected to enter into an agreement with Brookfield Asset Management or its affiliates providing for the lease of two of the company's executive officers on an interim basis, plus a lease agreement at market rates for corporate office space in New York. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Financing arrangements with Brookfield at separation: a backstop agreement under which Brookfield was contractually obligated to support a $200 million rights offering, and an expected $100 million revolving subordinated credit facility from a wholly-owned Brookfield subsidiary (Trilon (Luxembourg) S.a.r.l.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction distributed a portfolio of 30 regional malls (over 21 million square feet) and approximately 275 employees from GGP to GGP's stockholders as a newly-formed stand-alone company, while the parent's shared corporate service substrate (property management, HR, IT, legal, accounting, leasing, tax, treasury and related functions) did NOT transfer as an intact operating whole and was instead bridged at closing by a Separation Agreement, a Transition Services Agreement with GGP, and a services agreement plus financing arrangements with Brookfield. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-010146, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
