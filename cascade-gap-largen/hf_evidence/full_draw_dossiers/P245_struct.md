## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 10, 2017, Alexander & Baldwin, Inc. ("A&B"), a Hawaii corporation, entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Alexander & Baldwin REIT Holdings, Inc.
  ("A&B REIT Holdings"), a Hawaii corporation and direct, wholly owned subsidiary of
  A&B, and A&B REIT Merger Corporation ("Merger Sub"), a Hawaii corporation and direct,
  wholly owned subsidiary of A&B REIT Holdings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the merger, A&B REIT Holdings had no assets other than the stock of Merger
  Sub, and Merger Sub had no assets or operations, other than those incident to
  formation and participation in the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a holding company merger: Merger Sub merged with
  and into A&B, with A&B continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a direct,
  wholly owned subsidiary of A&B REIT Holdings; each outstanding share of A&B common
  stock automatically converted into one share of A&B REIT Holdings common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- The stated purpose of the merger was to facilitate A&B's ongoing compliance with
  requirements for qualification and taxation as a real estate investment trust
  ("REIT") for U.S. federal income tax purposes, following the board's July 10, 2017
  approval of a plan for A&B to be taxed as a REIT commencing with the taxable year
  ending December 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- Promptly following consummation of the merger, (i) A&B (the surviving corporation)
  would be converted into a limited liability company and change its name to
  "Alexander & Baldwin Investments, LLC," and (ii) A&B REIT Holdings would change its
  name to "Alexander & Baldwin, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- At a special meeting held October 27, 2017, A&B shareholders approved the holding
  company merger proposal by a vote of 40,395,733 for to 35,497 against (81,426
  abstain). [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-17-000039, Form 8-K (Item 5.07, special meeting vote results)]
- The merger became effective on November 8, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-17-000067, Form 15-12B]

## What crossed / did not cross at the tier level
- The customer-facing asset base and business model did not change hands to an outside
  party: A&B REIT Holdings (renamed Alexander & Baldwin, Inc.) was described as
  replacing A&B as "the Hawaii-based, publicly held corporation through which our
  operations are conducted," and would (through its subsidiaries) "conduct all of the
  operations conducted by A&B immediately prior to the merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- A&B's operating business at the time comprised three reportable segments —
  Commercial Real Estate, Land Operations, and Materials & Construction — none of
  which is described as being separated, sold, or excluded from the surviving REIT
  structure by the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- For accounting purposes, the merger was to be treated as a merger of entities under
  common control, with A&B's financial position and results of operations included in
  A&B REIT Holdings' consolidated financial statements "on the same basis as currently
  presented." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement/prospectus states an expectation that the directors and executive
  officers of A&B REIT Holdings following the merger "will be the same as those of A&B
  immediately prior to the merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, employee-matters
  agreement, or comparable bridging arrangement between A&B and A&B REIT Holdings is
  described in the drawn filing; none of these instruments appears in the document.
  [UNVERIFIED — absence noted, not affirmatively documented elsewhere]
- No employment-agreement amendments, change-in-control payments, or management
  re-investment provisions tied to this merger are described in the drawn filing (unlike
  a third-party acquisition); the document instead frames anti-takeover-style ownership
  and transfer restrictions in A&B REIT Holdings' charter as REIT-compliance measures.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing status at closing
- A&B REIT Holdings' registration statement (of which the proxy statement/prospectus
  forms a part) and NYSE listing approval for A&B REIT Holdings common stock were
  conditions to completing the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- The filing anticipated that A&B REIT Holdings common stock would trade on the NYSE
  under A&B's existing ticker symbol "ALEX," while "A&B's common stock will no longer
  be quoted on the NYSE or be registered under the Exchange Act, and A&B will cease to
  be a reporting company under the Exchange Act." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the November 8, 2017 effective time, the predecessor registrant (A&B, the
  Hawaii corporation) converted into a Delaware limited liability company named
  "Alexander & Baldwin Investments, LLC," and the successor registrant (A&B REIT
  Holdings) changed its name to "Alexander & Baldwin, Inc."; each outstanding share of
  the predecessor's common stock had been automatically converted into one share of the
  successor's common stock at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-17-000067, Form 15-12B]
- On November 21, 2017, the successor registrant, as successor issuer to the
  predecessor registrant, filed a Form 15-12B terminating registration of the
  predecessor's common stock and suspending the predecessor's duty to file reports,
  recording approximately one holder of record of the predecessor's stock as of that
  date; the successor's own common stock and its own duty to file reports were
  unaffected by that filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-17-000067, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was an internal holding-company reorganization undertaken to
  facilitate REIT-compliance ownership and transfer restrictions, not a change of
  control to an outside party: a newly formed, previously dormant subsidiary chain
  (A&B REIT Holdings / Merger Sub) was inserted above the existing operating
  corporation via a merger of Merger Sub into A&B, with A&B's shareholders converted
  1-for-1 into shareholders of the new top entity, which then took A&B's name and NYSE
  ticker while the original operating corporation was converted into an LLC subsidiary
  and its own class of stock removed from registration and reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-17-000067, Form 15-12B]
- The filing's own framing and the accounting treatment (merger of entities under
  common control) indicate continuity of business, management, and operations across
  the transaction, with no third-party acquirer, no transition-services bridge, and no
  segment carved out at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-285196, Form DEFM14A]
