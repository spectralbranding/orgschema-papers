# Structural sub-dossier — Legacy Reserves LP corporate reorganization into Legacy Reserves Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Legacy Reserves LP (the "Partnership"), a Delaware limited partnership focused on the
  development of oil and natural gas properties in the Permian Basin, East Texas, Rocky
  Mountain and Mid-Continent regions, reorganized from a master limited partnership into
  a corporation, Legacy Reserves Inc. ("New Legacy"), a newly formed Delaware corporation
  incorporated March 22, 2018 that had conducted no business operations of its own before
  the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- The reorganization was structured as (i) a merger of Legacy Reserves Merger Sub LLC (a
  wholly owned subsidiary of New Legacy) with and into the Partnership, with the
  Partnership surviving as a subsidiary of New Legacy, under which outstanding units,
  Series A Preferred Units and Series B Preferred Units were converted into the right to
  receive New Legacy common stock, and (ii) a separate purchase by New Legacy of all of
  the limited liability company interests in Legacy Reserves GP, LLC (the Partnership's
  general partner) from Lion GP Interests, LLC for $3.0 million in cash (the "GP
  Purchase"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- The fixed Exchange Ratios were: one share of New Legacy common stock for each
  outstanding unit; 2.92033118 shares for each Series A Preferred Unit; and 2.90650421
  shares for each Series B Preferred Unit (the Series A/B ratios set pursuant to a
  litigation settlement agreement); incentive distribution units were cancelled for no
  consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately after closing, former unitholders (excluding GP/founder/management
  affiliates) were expected to own approximately 60.95%, GP/Founding Investor/management
  affiliates approximately 13.35%, former Series A holders approximately 6.24%, and
  former Series B holders approximately 19.45% of New Legacy common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger and GP Purchase were completed on September 20, 2018, following unitholder
  approval at a special meeting held September 19, 2018; upon completion, New Legacy
  issued approximately 106.1 million shares of common stock as merger consideration and
  paid $3.0 million in cash (plus expenses) for the GP Interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278769, Form 8-K]

## What crossed at closing versus what did not (tier level)
- Legal vehicle: the limited partnership form was replaced by a Delaware corporation;
  the Partnership and its general partner became wholly owned subsidiaries of New
  Legacy, and the General Partner's own limited liability company agreement was amended
  to reflect New Legacy as its sole member. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278769, Form 8-K]
- Controlling intent: before closing, the general partner's interests (carrying
  "negative control rights") were held by Lion GP Interests, LLC, itself linked through
  an assignment/membership-interest-purchase chain to Moriah Properties, Ltd. and other
  GP members; the GP Board stated the reorganization was intended to remove "the
  Partnership GP members' negative control rights." At closing New Legacy purchased the
  GP outright, folding that previously separately held controlling interest into the
  reorganized entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- Business/value-capture model and customer-facing asset: on a pro forma basis, "the
  business of New Legacy will be the business of the Partnership," and New Legacy's only
  assets at closing were its ownership interests in the Partnership and the General
  Partner; the underlying oil and natural gas producing properties were not separated,
  divided, or reassigned as part of the reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- People/management/organization: the Partnership and New Legacy stated they "expect
  that the existing management team will stay in place after the Corporate
  Reorganization"; Paul T. Horne (Chairman and CEO of the General Partner) was
  designated to chair the New Legacy Board, which was to be classified into three
  staggered classes (Horne and Cary D. Brown as Class I, others as Class II/III).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]
- Named executive officers' outstanding phantom-unit awards under the Partnership's
  long-term incentive plan automatically vested at the Effective Time, with certain
  officers electing to reinvest cash settlement proceeds into New Legacy common stock
  (capped in aggregate at $30 million), and the Merger Agreement provided for six years
  of post-closing indemnification of the Partnership's directors, officers and
  employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278769, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- In connection with the consummation, the Partnership requested that the NASDAQ Global
  Select Market file a Form 25 notification of removal from listing with respect to the
  Partnership's outstanding units, Series A Preferred Units and Series B Preferred
  Units, with trading suspended before the opening of trading on September 21, 2018; the
  Partnership also intended to file a Form 15 to deregister those interests under the
  Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278769, Form 8-K]
- It was a condition of closing that New Legacy common stock be approved for listing on
  a national securities exchange; New Legacy common stock was expected to be, and was,
  listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the same ticker symbol, "LGCY,"
  previously used by the Partnership's units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction converted the legal vehicle from a master limited partnership to a
  Delaware corporation and eliminated the general partner's separately held controlling
  interest (purchased for $3.0 million cash from Lion GP Interests, LLC), while the
  underlying oil and gas asset base, business operations, and — per the Partnership's
  own stated expectation — the existing management team were represented as continuing
  unchanged inside the reorganized corporate entity; the prior units were removed from
  Nasdaq listing and slated for deregistration, with New Legacy common stock taking
  their place on Nasdaq under the same ticker. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278769, Form 8-K]
