# Structural sub-dossier — HSN, Inc. separation from IAC/InterActiveCorp (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The Board of Directors of IAC/InterActiveCorp approved the spin-offs of HSN, Inc.
  ("HSNi"), Interval Leisure Group, Inc., Ticketmaster and Tree.com, Inc. (each a
  "Spinco"), separating IAC into five separate, publicly traded companies via the
  distribution of all outstanding shares of common stock of each Spinco to IAC's
  stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B information statement]
- The separation was effected as a pro rata dividend to IAC stockholders, at a
  distribution ratio of one-fifth of a share of HSNi common stock for every share of IAC
  common stock and/or Class B common stock held on the record date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- No vote or approval of IAC stockholders was required; stockholders paid no
  consideration and were not required to surrender or exchange their IAC shares to
  receive Spinco shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- Each Spinco, including HSNi, was a newly formed entity that had not conducted any
  operations prior to the spin-offs; the relevant businesses were transferred to them
  prior to the spin-offs. Immediately after the distribution, IAC would have no
  continuing stock ownership in the Spincos. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing businesses transferred into HSNi were HSN (a retailer and
  interactive lifestyle network) delivered through the HSN television network and
  HSN.com, together with the Cornerstone Brands portfolio of catalogs and related
  websites — Frontgate, Ballard Designs, Garnet Hill, Smith+Noble, The Territory Ahead,
  TravelSmith and Improvements — and a limited number of retail stores. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- These were operating businesses conducted as part of IAC's Retailing segment prior to
  the spin-offs; the historical combined financial information reflected those businesses
  as they were conducted within IAC before the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- Following the spin-offs, IAC would have no obligation to provide financial, operational
  or organizational assistance to the Spincos, other than limited services pursuant to a
  transition services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]

## Contractual arrangements governing the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Prior to the spin-offs each Spinco was to enter into a Separation and Distribution
  Agreement and several other agreements with IAC and the other Spincos to effect the
  spin-offs and provide a framework for the relationships going forward, including a Tax
  Sharing Agreement, an Employee Matters Agreement and a Transition Services Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- The information statement states these spin-off agreements "were not the result of
  arm's length negotiations" and were established by IAC, in consultation with the
  Spincos, "with the intention of maximizing the value to current IAC's shareholders,"
  so terms for any particular Spinco might not be as favorable as arm's-length terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- Under the Tax Sharing Agreement, each Spinco generally would indemnify IAC and the
  other Spincos for taxes resulting from the spin-off, subject to restrictions on certain
  actions for a period of 25 months after the distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]

## Financing / value-capture structure at closing
- In connection with the spin-offs, HSNi was to transfer to IAC approximately
  $324 million in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
- HSNi was the borrower under new senior secured credit facilities providing financing of
  up to $300.0 million — $150.0 million in term loans and a $150.0 million revolving
  credit facility, each with a five-year maturity; the net proceeds of the term loan,
  together with the net proceeds of notes, were to be used to fund a distribution to IAC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction spun off an operating retail business (HSN television/HSN.com plus the
  Cornerstone catalog brands) from IAC into a newly formed standalone company (HSNi) by
  pro rata dividend, with IAC's shared corporate substrate bridged at closing by a
  Transition Services Agreement and related Tax Sharing / Employee Matters / Separation
  and Distribution agreements, and with HSNi capitalized through new senior secured debt
  and a ~$324 million cash transfer plus a debt-funded distribution to the parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-08-008292, Form 10-12B]
