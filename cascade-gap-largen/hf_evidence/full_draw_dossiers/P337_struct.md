# Structural sub-dossier — Green Bancorp, Inc. acquisition by Veritex Holdings, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Veritex Holdings, Inc. ("Veritex"), a Texas corporation and parent holding company of
  Veritex Community Bank, and Green Bancorp, Inc. ("Green"), a Texas corporation and
  parent holding company of Green Bank, N.A., entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Reorganization dated July 23, 2018, together with merger subsidiary MustMS, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Veritex is headquartered in Dallas, Texas; Green is a Texas-focused bank holding company
  headquartered in Houston, Texas, whose subsidiary Green Bank, N.A. provided commercial
  and private banking services through 21 full-service branches in the Houston and Dallas
  metropolitan statistical areas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]

## Structure of the transaction (what crossed and the legal mechanics)
- The transaction was structured as a three-step reorganization: (i) MustMS, Inc., a
  wholly owned Veritex subsidiary formed solely to facilitate the transaction, merged with
  and into Green, with Green surviving as a wholly owned Veritex subsidiary (the
  "merger"); (ii) immediately thereafter Green merged with and into Veritex, with Veritex
  surviving (together with the merger, the "holdco mergers"); and (iii) immediately after
  the holdco mergers, Green Bank, N.A. merged with and into Veritex Community Bank, with
  Veritex Community Bank continuing as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Per the closing report, effective January 1, 2019, as a result of the merger
  transactions Green ceased to exist as a separate corporation and Green Bank ceased to
  exist as a separate bank; the articles of incorporation and bylaws of Veritex in effect
  immediately before completion became those of the surviving corporation without change,
  and Green's articles and bylaws ceased to be in effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-19-000005, Form 8-K Item 2.01/5.03]
- The whole company — Green Bank's customer-facing banking business together with its
  branch network, operations, and personnel — transferred as a going concern via the
  holdco/bank merger sequence rather than being separated into distinct product, process,
  or people components. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]

## Consideration and post-closing ownership split
- Each share of Green common stock (other than certain shares held by Green, Veritex, or
  their subsidiaries) was converted into the right to receive 0.79 shares of Veritex
  common stock (the exchange ratio), with cash paid in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Based on Veritex's closing price on July 23, 2018 (the last trading day before
  announcement), the implied value of the per-share merger consideration was $25.89; based
  on Veritex's closing price of $29.99 on October 9, 2018, Veritex expected to issue
  approximately 31,283,848 shares of its common stock in the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- An illustrative contribution analysis prepared for the joint proxy statement estimated
  that, following consummation, former Green shareholders would hold approximately 55% and
  former Veritex shareholders approximately 45% of the fully diluted shares of the pro
  forma combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties intended the holdco mergers, taken together, to qualify as a
  "reorganization" within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- An illustrative, unaudited pro forma purchase-accounting table included in the joint
  proxy statement reflected total assets acquired of approximately $4,292,542 thousand and
  assumed deposits of approximately $3,433,029 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]

## Governance and management continuity at closing
- The combined company's board of directors was to consist of nine members: six members
  drawn from the pre-merger Veritex board and three members of the pre-merger Green board
  (Manuel Mehos, Steve Lerner, and Don Ellis). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Malcolm Holland, the Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Veritex before the merger,
  was to continue as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of the combined company; Terry
  Earley, Green's Chief Financial Officer, was to become the Chief Financial Officer of
  the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Per the closing report, effective upon completion, all directors and executive officers
  of Green and all directors and officers of Green Bank ceased serving in those roles,
  including Green CEO Manuel J. Mehos, Green CFO Terry S. Earley, Green Chief Credit
  Officer Donald Perschbacher, and Green Bank CEO Geoffrey Greenwade; Earley, Perschbacher,
  and Greenwade had entered employment agreements with Veritex effective upon completion
  of the merger transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-19-000005, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Contractual arrangements bridging shared substrate at closing
- For the one-year period following the effective time, Veritex agreed to provide Green
  employees who continued employment with Veritex or the surviving corporation with
  employee benefits under Veritex benefit plans that are, in the aggregate, substantially
  comparable to those provided to similarly situated Veritex employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Veritex agreed to maintain Green's existing directors' and officers' liability insurance
  policy (or a comparable policy, capped at 250% of the annual premium paid on Green's
  policy) for a six-year period following the effective time, and to indemnify present and
  former Green directors, officers, and employees to the fullest extent permitted by
  applicable law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- Green agreed to pay Veritex a $40,000,000 termination fee under specified conditions,
  and Veritex agreed to pay Green the same fee under specified conditions, if the merger
  agreement were terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction required regulatory approvals from, among others, the Federal Reserve
  and the Texas Department of Banking, plus notice to the Office of the Comptroller of the
  Currency for the bank-level merger; Veritex filed the required applications in
  August–September 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Veritex acquired the whole of Green Bancorp, Inc. via a three-step holdco/bank merger
  sequence completed January 1, 2019, with Green and Green Bank ceasing to exist as
  separate legal entities and Veritex's charter documents continuing unchanged as those of
  the surviving corporation; Green's banking business, branches, operations, and personnel
  transferred together as a going concern for stock consideration (0.79 Veritex share per
  Green share, implied ~$25.89–$29.99 range), leaving former Green holders with roughly
  55% of the pro forma combined shares. Management/board continuity was partial: Veritex's
  CEO continued leading the combined company and three Green directors joined its board,
  while Green's CEO and other Green Bank officers ceased their roles, with three (not the
  CEO) receiving new employment agreements with Veritex; the shared substrate (employee
  benefits, D&O coverage) was bridged at closing by time-limited contractual commitments
  (one-year benefits parity; six-year D&O tail). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006708, Form DEFM14A]
