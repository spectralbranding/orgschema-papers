## Public-reporting continuity and successor identity (window-fixing)
- The registrant that continued under CIK 1326428 after emergence, "Old Linn" (Linn
  Energy, Inc.), later undertook an internal reorganization in which Old Linn contributed
  certain assets to Riviera Resources, Inc. (the "Riviera Separation," excluding its 50%
  equity interest in Roan LLC) and Old Linn merged with and into a wholly owned subsidiary,
  with Roan Resources, Inc. becoming the surviving public registrant under CIK 1326428. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K (definitions; "Old Linn," "Riviera," "Reorganization")]
- The reorganization proceeded under a Linn Merger Agreement dated September 24, 2018 among
  Linn Energy, Inc., Roan Resources, Inc. and Linn Merger Sub #2, LLC, and a companion Roan
  Merger Agreement of the same date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K (Exhibits 2.1, 2.2)]

## Realized financial record (best-documented scalar)
- For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018 the registrant (Roan Resources, Inc.) reported
  a net loss of $140.7 million (net loss of $140,671 thousand), versus net income of $18,457
  thousand in 2017 and $6,947 thousand in 2016 on a predecessor basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K (consolidated statements of operations)]
- The 2018 net loss arose despite positive pre-tax income ($216.2 million before income taxes)
  and was driven primarily by income tax expense of $356.9 million, including $304.5 million
  related to the initial recording of the deferred tax liability recognized as a result of the
  Reorganization (conversion to a taxable corporation). [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K (results of operations / income taxes)]
- The registrant stated it did not incur impairment charges of proved properties during the
  year ended December 31, 2018; it recorded impairment on unproved property of approximately
  $4.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K (properties / impairment discussion)]

## Terminal event within the window
- On December 6, 2019, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated October 1, 2019,
  Roan Resources, Inc. completed a merger in which Citizen Energy Pressburg Inc. (Merger Sub)
  merged with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Citizen Operating, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-308171, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- At the effective time, each outstanding share of Class A common stock was cancelled and
  converted into the right to receive $1.52 in cash, without interest; the shares were suspended
  from NYSE trading before the opening on December 6, 2019 and the registrant requested delisting
  (Form 25) and termination of registration (Form 15). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-308171, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Concurrent with the merger the registrant, Roan Resources, Inc., converted into a Delaware
  limited liability company named "Pressburg, LLC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-308171, Form 8-K Introductory Note]
- The registrant filed a Form 15-12B on December 18, 2019, terminating registration of its
  common stock and suspending its Exchange Act reporting obligations; no subsequent public
  annual report was filed under CIK 1326428. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-316571, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant that continued under the drawn CIK reorganized in 2018 (contributing assets
  to a separately spun-off Riviera Resources and surviving as Roan Resources, Inc.), reported a
  fiscal-2018 net loss of $140.7 million driven primarily by a $356.9 million income tax expense
  (including a $304.5 million deferred tax liability recognized on the Reorganization), did not
  record proved-property impairments in 2018, and was then acquired for cash at $1.52 per share by
  Citizen Operating, LLC on December 6, 2019, converting to Pressburg, LLC, delisting from the NYSE,
  and deregistering via Form 15-12B — ending the drawn registrant's separate public record within the
  fixed window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326428-19-000006, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-308171, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-316571, Form 15-12B]
