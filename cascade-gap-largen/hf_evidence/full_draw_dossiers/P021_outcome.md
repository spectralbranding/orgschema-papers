## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant remained an SEC-reporting public company throughout the window and filed an annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2016, with its common stock and 6.25% Series A Mandatory Convertible Preferred Stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended December 31, 2016)]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Net income (loss) attributable to WPX Energy, Inc. was $(601) million in 2016, $(1,727) million in 2015, $164 million in 2014, $(1,185) million in 2013 and $(223) million in 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016, Selected Financial Data]
- Total revenues were $693 million in 2016, $1,366 million in 2015 and $2,523 million in 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016, Selected Financial Data]
- The 2015 loss was driven substantially by discontinued operations, which included a $2,334 million (approximately $2.3 billion) pre-tax impairment of the Piceance Basin operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements — discontinued operations]

## Portfolio repositioning realized in the window (natural-gas to oil)
- The registrant entered the Delaware Basin (a subset of the Permian Basin) in August 2015 upon closing its acquisition of RKI Exploration & Production, LLC ("RKI"), for which it paid approximately $1.2 billion in cash, net of cash acquired and net of certain distributions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]
- As of the FY2016 filing the registrant's principal areas of operation were the Delaware Basin, the Williston Basin and the San Juan Basin, and it held approximately 98,000 net acres in the Delaware Basin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The registrant completed the sale of its Piceance Basin operations for $910 million (the properties were sold in April 2016 and represented approximately 52 percent of its reserves as of December 31, 2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]
- On March 9, 2016 the registrant completed the sale of its San Juan Basin gathering system for consideration of approximately $309 million to a portfolio company of ISQ Global Infrastructure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The registrant's discontinued operations comprised its holdings in the Piceance Basin, Powder River Basin, Barnett Shale and Arkoma Basin; it also completed the sale of Apco in the first quarter of 2015 and recorded a $15 million loss on the sale of the Powder River Basin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Workforce in the window
- As of the FY2016 filing the registrant had approximately 650 full-time employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~2015-2016 window the registrant remained an independent NYSE-listed public company but reported net losses attributable to it of $(1,727) million (2015) and $(601) million (2016) on declining total revenues ($2,523 million in 2014 to $693 million in 2016), the 2015 result including an approximately $2.3 billion pre-tax impairment of its Piceance Basin operations; over the same period it repositioned its asset base by acquiring RKI Exploration & Production for approximately $1.2 billion in cash to enter the Delaware Basin (August 2015), selling the Piceance Basin operations for $910 million (April 2016) and the San Juan Basin gathering system for approximately $309 million (March 2016), and reducing its full-time workforce to approximately 650. [SRC: SEC accession 0001518832-17-000004, Form 10-K FY2016]
