[SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000120, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Consequently there is no audited public financial statement, impairment, divestiture, or other realized operating scalar attributable to the analyzed entity for the ~2019–2021 outcome window (three to five years after the ~2016 transaction). [SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000121, Form 15-12B]
