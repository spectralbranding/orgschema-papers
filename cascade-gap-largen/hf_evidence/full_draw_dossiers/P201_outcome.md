# Outcome sub-dossier — Cornerstone Building Brands, Inc. realized record, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> record after closing. It contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing.

## Continuing registrant identity
- The registrant (SEC CIK 0000883902) continued reporting under the name Cornerstone
  Building Brands, Inc., stating that it "became a newly formed company on November
  16, 2018 as the result of a merger" between NCI Building Systems, Inc. and Ply Gem
  Parent, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]
- Cornerstone Building Brands describes itself as the largest manufacturer of exterior
  building products in North America, serving residential and commercial customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]

## Realized financial trajectory, FY2019-FY2021
- Net income (loss) reported in the FY2021 Form 10-K: $(15,390) thousand for fiscal
  2019; $(482,778) thousand for fiscal 2020; $665,859 thousand for fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]
- FY2020 results included a goodwill impairment of $503.2 million; management
  attributed the year-over-year swing to net income in FY2021 in part to the absence
  of that impairment and to gains on 2021 divestitures. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]
- In 2021 the company divested its IMP and DBCI businesses (August 2021) for a
  reported gain on sale of $831.3 million, while also acquiring Prime Windows LLC
  (April 2021), Cascade Windows, Inc. (August 2021), and Union Corrugating Company
  Holding, Inc. (December 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]
- FY2021 net income was reported as $665.9 million, or $5.19 per diluted share. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K]

## 2022 change-of-control transaction and its effect on reporting
- On March 5, 2022, the Company entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Camelot
  Return Intermediate Holdings, LLC ("Parent") and Camelot Return Merger Sub, Inc.,
  subsidiaries of investment funds managed by Clayton, Dubilier & Rice, LLC ("CD&R"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-082274, Form 8-K dated 2022-07-25]
- Prior to this merger, CD&R and its affiliates already owned approximately 49% of
  Company common stock (a position originating from the 2018 combination); the July
  25, 2022 merger converted CD&R's funds into indirect owners of all remaining
  outstanding Company common stock, at $24.65 in cash per share for unaffiliated
  holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-082274, Form 8-K dated 2022-07-25]
- The merger was funded in part by a new $300 million senior secured term loan
  facility, $710 million of 8.750% senior secured notes due 2028, and $464.4 million
  of 2.99% senior payment-in-kind notes due 2029. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-082274, Form 8-K dated 2022-07-25]
- On July 25, 2022 the Company notified the NYSE that the merger had completed and
  requested that the NYSE suspend trading of Company common stock prior to the market
  open that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-082274, Form 8-K dated 2022-07-25]
- Despite the change of control, the Company continued filing annual reports on Form
  10-K under CIK 0000883902 in each year through the FY2025 report (filed 2026-03-12);
  the FY2022 Form 10-K's cover page indicates the registrant checked "not required to
  file reports" under Exchange Act Section 13/15(d) yet filed the report regardless. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-23-000006, FY2022 Form 10-K]

## Realized financial trajectory, FY2022 (split accounting periods due to the 2022 change of control)
- The FY2022 Form 10-K presents results in Successor/Predecessor periods split at the
  July 25, 2022 change-of-control date: net loss of $(63,496) thousand for the
  Successor period (July 25-December 31, 2022); net income of $483,786 thousand for
  the Predecessor period (January 1-July 24, 2022); combined full-year 2022 net income
  of $420,290 thousand, compared with FY2021 net income of $665,859 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-23-000006, FY2022 Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window, the surviving registrant (renamed
  Cornerstone Building Brands, Inc.) reported net income of $665.9 million for FY2021,
  supported by large 2021 divestiture gains, following a FY2020 net loss that had
  included a $503.2 million goodwill impairment; the registrant then underwent a
  second change-of-control transaction on July 25, 2022 in which its existing minority
  CD&R shareholder group acquired full ownership for $24.65/share in cash, common
  stock trading was suspended on the NYSE, and combined FY2022 net income was $420.3
  million (a Predecessor-period income of $483.8 million partly offset by a
  Successor-period net loss of $63.5 million after the ownership change). [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-22-000014, FY2021 Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-23-000006, FY2022 Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-082274, Form 8-K dated 2022-07-25]
- The registrant remained an SEC filer after this second transaction (annual 10-Ks
  continued at least through the FY2025 report), so the realized post-2018 record
  remains traceable in primary filings across the full outcome window rather than
  disappearing behind a going-private deregistration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883902-23-000006, FY2022 Form 10-K]
