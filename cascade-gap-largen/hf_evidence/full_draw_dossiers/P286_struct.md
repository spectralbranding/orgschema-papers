# Structural sub-dossier — LaSalle Hotel Properties merger into Pebblebrook Hotel Trust (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- LaSalle Hotel Properties, a Maryland REIT, and LaSalle Hotel Operating Partnership,
  L.P. (together the "LaSalle parties"), agreed to combine with Pebblebrook Hotel Trust,
  Pebblebrook Hotel, L.P., Ping Merger Sub, LLC and Ping Merger OP, LP (together the
  "Pebblebrook parties") through (i) a merger of LaSalle with and into Ping Merger Sub,
  LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Pebblebrook), with Merger Sub surviving, and (ii) a
  merger of Merger OP with and into LaSalle OP, with LaSalle OP surviving as a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Pebblebrook OP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Structure"]
- The combined company retains the name "Pebblebrook Hotel Trust" and its shares continue
  to trade on the NYSE under symbol "PEB"; it was currently expected that the mergers
  would be completed on November 30, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]
- LaSalle Hotel Properties was organized in 1998 as a self-administered Maryland REIT
  that "primarily buys, owns, redevelops and leases upscale and luxury full-service
  hotels." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and what crossed the ownership boundary
- Each outstanding LaSalle common share converts, at the holder's election, into either
  0.92 Pebblebrook common shares (the "share consideration") or $37.80 in cash (the
  "cash consideration"), with the cash consideration capped at approximately 30% of
  outstanding LaSalle common shares (increasing to roughly 33% after excluding shares
  held by Pebblebrook itself). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]
- After the company merger, LaSalle common shares, Series I preferred shares and Series
  J preferred shares currently listed on the NYSE will cease to be listed on the NYSE and
  will be deregistered under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A, NYSE listing/deregistration provisions]
- The resulting combined portfolio was expected to comprise 66-69 hotel properties,
  assuming completion of a then-pending contracted sale of three LaSalle hotels, with a
  pro forma enterprise value of approximately $7.6 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]

## Governance, management, and controlling-intent fusion
- Immediately after the company merger, the board of trustees of the combined company
  will consist solely of the seven then-current Pebblebrook trustees (Jon E. Bortz,
  Cydney C. Donnell, Ron E. Jackson, Phillip M. Miller, Michael J. Schall, Earl E. Webb
  and Laura H. Wright); the executive officers of Pebblebrook immediately before the
  merger continue as the combined company's executive officers, with Jon E. Bortz
  continuing as President, CEO and Chairman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "Trustees and Management of the Combined Company After the Mergers"]
- Deal-negotiation disclosure records that Pebblebrook repeatedly stated it was
  "unwilling to add any LaSalle trustees to the board of trustees of the proposed
  combined company," a position that remained unresolved through much of the
  negotiation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "Background of the Mergers"]
- Jon E. Bortz, Pebblebrook's President, CEO and Chairman, founded LaSalle in 1998 and
  served as its president, CEO and a trustee until his 2009 retirement, and as chairman
  of the LaSalle board from 2001 to 2009; during his LaSalle tenure LaSalle purchased 22
  of the 41 hotel properties it owned at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "Background of the Mergers"]
- The merger agreement's completion is deemed a "change in control" under the existing
  severance agreements of LaSalle's senior officers (Barnello, Fuller, Young and Gaum),
  entitling each to severance payments/benefits regardless of whether they are actually
  terminated; separately, Pebblebrook agreed to cause the surviving entity to pay other
  LaSalle employees (excluding the senior officers) pro-rated 2018 bonuses and payments
  under an employee retention bonus plan valued at approximately $7,500,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]

## Product/process/people structural note
- LaSalle, like Pebblebrook, owns hotel real estate but leases its hotels to taxable
  REIT subsidiary ("TRS") lessees, which in turn engage independent third-party hotel
  management companies (qualifying as "eligible independent contractors") to operate the
  hotels; this separation of the owned real-estate asset from day-to-day hotel operating
  management predates and is unchanged in form by the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "Federal Income Tax Considerations"]
- The joint proxy's risk factors flag "the additional complexities of combining two
  companies with different histories, cultures, markets and third-party hotel management
  companies," and identify the combined company's ability "to maintain relationships
  with third-party hotel management companies, vendors and employees" as a named risk to
  realizing the mergers' anticipated benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A "Risk Factors"]
- The filing does not describe any transition-services or shared-services agreement
  bridging LaSalle's corporate functions into the combined company (this is a
  whole-company merger of a going concern, not a carve-out with a bridging substrate).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two REITs of the same asset class (upscale/luxury full-service
  hotels operated through independent third-party managers under a TRS-lessee structure
  common to both) via a whole-company merger, with LaSalle's shares ceasing to be listed
  and deregistered; the surviving entity's board and executive management were drawn
  entirely from the acquirer, and the acquirer's CEO was himself LaSalle's founding
  president/CEO/chairman, while LaSalle's own senior officers were treated as departing
  under change-in-control severance triggers rather than continuing to run the combined
  company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006913, DEFM14A]
