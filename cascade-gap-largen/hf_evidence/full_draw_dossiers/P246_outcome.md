# Outcome sub-dossier — Avista Corporation realized record, ~3-5-year horizon (~2020-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier contains no information about
> how the transaction was structured as signed; it records only what was subsequently
> realized in public filings.

## Consummation status
- The proposed transaction was not consummated. On January 23, 2019, Avista
  Corporation, Hydro One Limited and certain subsidiaries entered into a Termination
  Agreement terminating the July 19, 2017 Agreement and Plan of Merger, effective
  immediately, by mutual agreement of the parties. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000104918-19-000022, Form 8-K]
- The termination followed denial of the proposed acquisition by state regulators: on
  December 5, 2018, Avista and Hydro One received a decision from the Washington
  Utilities and Transportation Commission (WUTC) denying the proposed acquisition. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0000104918-18-000119, Form 8-K]
- Avista's later Form 10-K states that the Termination Agreement followed "the denial of
  the proposed merger by certain of the Company's regulatory commissions," and that
  Hydro One is "Ontario's largest electricity transmission and distribution provider."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Note 25]
- Pursuant to the Termination Agreement, Hydro One paid Avista a $103 million
  termination fee in January 2019, which after 2019 transaction costs of $19.7 million
  pre-tax and applicable income taxes was applied to reimbursing transaction costs
  incurred from 2017-2019, with any remainder used for general corporate purposes and to
  reduce the Company's need for external financing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Note 25]

## Public-reporting and listing status after termination
- Following termination, Avista continued to exist and file as an independent SEC
  registrant; its common stock remained listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the
  ticker symbol "AVA," with 6,841 registered shareholders as of January 31, 2021. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]
- Avista continued filing periodic reports after termination, including a Form 10-K for
  fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-02-24). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form
  10-K]

## Realized financial record within the outcome window (fiscal year 2020)
- Net income attributable to Avista Corporation shareholders was $129.5 million for
  2020, a decrease from $197.0 million for 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774,
  Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]
- The decrease was attributed primarily to the non-recurrence of the $103 million
  Hydro One termination fee received in the first quarter of 2019 (partially offset in
  2019 by associated expenses of $19.7 million pre-tax), together with higher operating
  expenses in 2020 partially offset by higher utility margin from lower power-supply
  costs, rate relief in Washington and Oregon, and customer growth; utility margin was
  also affected by lower commercial and industrial loads associated with COVID-19. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]
- Net cash provided by operating activities was $331.0 million for 2020 compared with
  $398.2 million for 2019, a decrease the filing attributes primarily to the
  non-recurrence of the 2019 termination-fee receipt (net of related transaction costs
  and taxes) and to other working-capital and derivative-settlement items. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]
- There were no additional merger-transaction costs or termination-fee amounts recorded
  in 2020 relating to the terminated Hydro One matter. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The proposed acquisition of Avista by Hydro One was blocked by state regulatory
  denial and terminated by mutual agreement in January 2019, with Hydro One paying
  Avista a $103 million termination fee. [SRC: SEC accession 0000104918-19-000022, Form
  8-K; SEC accession 0000104918-18-000119, Form 8-K]
- Avista continued to operate and report as an independent, NYSE-listed public company
  through the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window, with FY2020 net income attributable to
  shareholders of $129.5 million (down from $197.0 million in FY2019, a year whose
  results were elevated by the one-time termination-fee receipt) and no residual
  transaction costs recorded in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-007774, Form 10-K
  (fiscal year 2020)]
