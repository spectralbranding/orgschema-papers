# Structural sub-dossier — Esterline Technologies Corporation acquisition by TransDigm Group (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Esterline Technologies Corporation ("ESL"), a Delaware corporation formed in 1967 and
  headquartered in Bellevue, Washington, agreed to be acquired by TransDigm Group
  Incorporated ("TransDigm"), a Delaware corporation, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan
  of Merger dated October 9, 2018 and amended October 10, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A pp. 1, 30]
- The legal vehicle was a reverse triangular merger: Thunderbird Merger Sub Inc., a
  Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of TransDigm formed solely to effect
  the transaction, merged with and into ESL, with ESL continuing as the surviving
  corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of TransDigm; Merger Sub ceased to
  exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A pp. 1, 30-31]
- ESL's shareholders were to receive $122.50 in cash, without interest, for each share
  of ESL common stock, representing an all-cash consideration structure with an
  aggregate transaction value disclosed for fee-calculation purposes of approximately
  $3.68 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A cover fee table, p. 62]
- ESL described itself as a specialized manufacturing company principally serving
  aerospace and defense customers, operating three segments organized around technical
  capabilities: Avionics & Controls (avionics, control and communication systems,
  human-machine interface products), Sensors & Systems (power distribution products,
  connection technologies, advanced sensors), and Advanced Materials (engineered
  materials and defense technologies). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A p. 1]
- TransDigm described itself, through its wholly-owned subsidiaries, as a global
  designer, producer and supplier of highly engineered aircraft components for
  commercial and military aircraft. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A p. 1]

## What crossed at closing / legal and financing structure
- ESL as a whole corporate entity — its stock, and therefore its subsidiaries and
  operating segments as structured — became a wholly owned subsidiary of TransDigm at
  the merger's effective time; the merger agreement did not carve out or separate
  individual ESL segments at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A p. 31]
- TransDigm expected to finance the merger consideration through debt financing (a
  commitment letter for a $3.7 billion senior secured term facility from named lenders)
  together with cash on hand; the merger itself was not conditioned on TransDigm
  obtaining this financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A p. 44]
- The proxy statement's summary and full merger-agreement text do not describe any
  transition services agreement, shared-services agreement, or similar bridging
  arrangement between ESL and TransDigm for post-closing operations; no such instrument
  is named among the transaction documents. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A (transaction-documents description, pp. 1-2, 30-31)]
- On the closing date, ESL terminated and repaid all outstanding indebtedness under its
  pre-existing 2011 credit agreement and effected satisfaction/discharge of its 2015
  indenture governing EUR330.0 million of 3.625% senior notes due 2023, replacing its
  standalone financing structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 1.02]

## Governance and management at closing (people/organization tier)
- At the merger's effective time, all nine incumbent ESL directors (Curtis C. Reusser,
  Michael J. Cave, Michael J. Covey, Delores M. Etter, Anthony P. Franceschini, Paul V.
  Haack, Mary L. Howell, Scott E. Kuechle, and Nils E. Larsen) ceased to be directors,
  and eight incumbent officers (Reusser, Paul P. Benson, Stephen M. Nolan, Brian R.
  Reid, Roger A. Ross, Donald E. Walther, Amy L. Watson, and Albert S. Yost) ceased to
  be officers of the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 5.02]
- The two directors of Merger Sub immediately prior to the effective time, Michael J.
  Lisman and Halle F. Terrion, became the directors of the surviving company; Robert
  Henderson became President and Chief Executive Officer, Halle F. Terrion became
  Secretary, and Jonathan D. Crandall became Treasurer of the surviving company at
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 5.02]
- The merger agreement required TransDigm to maintain, for one year following closing,
  base salary/wage rates, target incentive-compensation opportunities, and severance
  levels for continuing ESL employees at least as favorable as those in effect
  immediately before closing, and to recognize prior ESL service for eligibility,
  vesting, and benefit-accrual purposes under post-closing benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A pp. 77-78]
- Pending closing, the merger agreement obligated ESL to conduct business "in the
  ordinary course of business consistent with past practice" and use commercially
  reasonable efforts to "preserve intact its present business organizations, goodwill
  and ongoing businesses" and "preserve its present relationships with customers,
  suppliers, vendors, licensors, licensees, governmental authorities and other persons
  with whom it and they have material business relations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A p. 71]
- The Restated Certificate of Incorporation and Bylaws of ESL were amended and restated
  in their entirety at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 5.03]

## Public-market status change at closing
- Trading of ESL common stock on the NYSE was halted prior to the opening of trading on
  the closing date; ESL requested that the NYSE file a Form 25 to remove the common
  stock from listing and registration, with ESL intending to follow with a Form 15 to
  deregister the stock and suspend periodic reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a reverse triangular merger in which the whole ESL
  corporate entity, including its three technical-capability segments, was acquired
  intact for all-cash consideration and became a wholly owned subsidiary of TransDigm,
  financed by a committed debt facility plus cash on hand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A pp. 1, 30-31, 44]
- At the governance/people tier, closing brought a full turnover of ESL's board and
  most named officers, replaced by TransDigm/Merger-Sub-designated directors and a new
  President/CEO, while the merger agreement contractually bound TransDigm to preserve
  continuing employees' compensation, benefits, and service credit for a defined
  one-year transition period; no transition-services or shared-services agreement
  bridging ESL and TransDigm operations is described in the closing-era filings
  reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000003, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-14, Item 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339843, DEFM14A pp. 77-78]
