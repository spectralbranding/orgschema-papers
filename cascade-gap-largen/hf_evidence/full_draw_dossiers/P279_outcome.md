# Outcome sub-dossier — FGL Holdings realized record, ~3-5 years after the 2017 closing

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-closing record; it contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The renamed surviving company (FGL Holdings) continued as an SEC-reporting registrant
  under CIK 0001668428 and filed annual reports on Form 10-K for fiscal years 2017,
  2018, and 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-18-000049, 0001668428-19-000032, 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K filings]
- On February 7, 2020, FGL Holdings entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with
  Fidelity National Financial, Inc. ("FNF"), F I Corp. and F II Corp. (as amended April
  24, 2020), and on June 1, 2020 FNF completed its acquisition of FGL Holdings; FGL
  Holdings became a wholly owned subsidiary of FNF as a result of the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-157208, Form 8-K]
- In the June 1, 2020 transaction, each FGL Holdings ordinary share was canceled and
  converted into the right to receive $12.50 in cash or 0.2558 shares of FNF common
  stock; FNF issued approximately 27 million shares of FNF common stock and paid
  approximately $1.8 billion in cash to former holders of FGL Holdings ordinary and
  preferred shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-157208, Form 8-K]
- On June 1, 2020, FGL Holdings notified the New York Stock Exchange of the completion
  of the mergers and requested that its ordinary shares and warrants be withdrawn from
  listing; the NYSE filed a notification of removal from listing on Form 25 and trading
  was suspended prior to the opening of trading on June 2, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-157208, Form 8-K]
- FGL Holdings filed a Form 15-12B on 2020-06-15, terminating registration of its
  ordinary shares and warrants and suspending its reporting obligations under Sections
  13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act; no further Form 10-K has been filed by FGL Holdings
  since. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-073397, Form 15-12B]
- At the effective time of the mergers, seven of the nine then-current directors of FGL
  Holdings (William P. Foley II, Keith W. Abell, Chinh E. Chu, James A. Quella, Patrick
  S. Baird, Menes O. Chee and Timothy M. Walsh) resigned from its board of directors;
  Richard N. Massey, Raymond R. Quirk, Michael J. Nolan and Christopher O. Blunt became
  directors after the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-157208, Form 8-K]

## Realized financial record while independently reporting (through fiscal year 2019)
- FGL Holdings reported total revenues of $2,113 million and net income of $507 million
  for the year ended December 31, 2019, compared with total revenues of $711 million and
  net income of $13 million for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K FY2019]
- After deducting preferred stock dividends of $31 million (2019) and $29 million
  (2018), net income available to common shareholders was $476 million for 2019 versus a
  loss of $16 million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The year-over-year swing was driven substantially by net investment gains of $674
  million in 2019 versus net investment losses of $629 million in 2018, as reported in
  the FY2019 Form 10-K's results-of-operations table. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Total assets reported by FGL Holdings grew from $30,945 million at year-end 2018 to
  $36,714 million at year-end 2019, and total equity grew from $890 million to $2,743
  million over the same period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Realized record after absorption into Fidelity National Financial (segment disclosure)
- Following the June 1, 2020 acquisition, FNF's insurance subsidiaries formerly
  comprising FGL Holdings (FGL Insurance and FGL NY Insurance) were disclosed by FNF as
  a separate reportable "F&G" segment in FNF's own SEC filings, rather than being merged
  into an existing FNF segment without separate disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-21-000015, FNF Form 10-K FY2020]
- FNF disclosed that F&G's revenues and net earnings for the period from June 1, 2020
  through December 31, 2020 were $1,233 million and $136 million, respectively, as
  included in FNF's consolidated statement of earnings for fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-21-000015, FNF Form 10-K FY2020]
- As of December 31, 2021, FNF's F&G segment was reported under the name "F&G Annuities
  & Life," described as a wholly owned subsidiary issuing a broad portfolio of deferred
  annuities (fixed indexed and fixed rate), indexed universal life insurance, immediate
  annuities, funding agreements and pension risk transfer solutions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-22-000024, FNF Form 10-K FY2021]
- FNF reported that the F&G segment's employee base increased approximately 40% during
  2021 "as our F&G business continues to grow." [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-22-000024, FNF Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- For approximately the first two and a half years after the 2017 closing, the combined
  entity (FGL Holdings) continued as an independent SEC-reporting company, filing three
  annual reports and showing a large year-over-year swing in reported net income between
  fiscal 2018 ($13 million) and fiscal 2019 ($507 million), attributed in its own filing
  substantially to net investment gains versus losses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001668428-20-000009, Form 10-K FY2019]
- FGL Holdings was then itself acquired by Fidelity National Financial effective June 1,
  2020, at which point it ceased to be separately publicly traded, deregistered with the
  SEC, and its pre-existing board was substantially replaced. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-157208, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-073397, Form 15-12B]
- No further stand-alone public financial record exists for the entity after
  deregistration; however, the acquiring company (FNF) separately discloses the
  business's results as its own "F&G" reportable segment, showing $1,233 million in
  segment revenue and $136 million in segment net earnings for the roughly seven months
  of 2020 following the acquisition, and continued operation and workforce growth
  (employee base up approximately 40%) through 2021 under the renamed "F&G Annuities &
  Life" brand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-21-000015, FNF Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001331875-22-000024, FNF Form 10-K FY2021]
