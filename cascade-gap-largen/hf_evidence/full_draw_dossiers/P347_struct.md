# Structural sub-dossier — Vectren Corporation acquisition by CenterPoint Energy, Inc. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- Under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 21, 2018 among Vectren Corporation
  (the "Company"), CenterPoint Energy, Inc. ("CenterPoint Energy"), and Pacer Merger
  Sub, Inc., an Indiana corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of CenterPoint Energy
  ("Merger Sub"), Merger Sub would merge with and into the Company, with the Company
  continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of
  CenterPoint Energy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Proposed Merger"]
- Each outstanding share of Company common stock would be cancelled and converted into
  the right to receive $72.00 in cash, without interest — a whole-company, all-cash
  going-private acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Effects of the Merger; Merger Consideration"]
- The Company was described as "an energy holding company headquartered in Evansville,"
  Indiana, whose wholly owned subsidiary Vectren Utility Holdings, Inc. ("VUHI") served
  as intermediate holding company for three public utilities: Indiana Gas Company, Inc.,
  Southern Indiana Gas and Electric Company ("SIGECO"), and Vectren Energy Delivery of
  Ohio, Inc. ("VEDO"); the Company also conducted nonutility activities through Vectren
  Enterprises, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- CenterPoint Energy was described as a Texas public utility holding company with
  reportable segments including Electric Transmission & Distribution, Natural Gas
  Distribution, Energy Services, Midstream Investments, and Other Operations, serving
  more than five million metered customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]

## What would cross at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity — not a carved-out business unit — was the
  transaction's subject; Merger Sub merged into the Company, which survived as a wholly
  owned subsidiary of CenterPoint Energy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Proposed Merger"]
- If completed, the Company's common stock would be removed from listing on the NYSE and
  its Exchange Act reporting obligations would terminate through deregistration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Proposed Merger"]
- Controlling intent / governance: CenterPoint Energy committed that, upon completion,
  it would establish the headquarters for the combined companies' gas operations in
  Evansville for a minimum of three years, maintain the headquarters of Indiana electric
  operations in Evansville, and retain key operational activities in support of the
  utilities in Evansville; the current headquarters locations were intended to remain
  for the Company's MVerge (Miller Pipeline and Minnesota Limited) and Energy Systems
  Group subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- People / organization: under the merger agreement's Employee Matters provisions,
  CenterPoint Energy agreed that, for two years following the effective time, it would
  provide each Company employee with the same or better responsibilities in the same
  location (or within 50 miles), base pay/short-term incentive targets no less
  favorable in the aggregate, and substantially comparable employee benefits, plus
  defined severance protections for terminations without cause; the agreement also
  reflected commitments to honor existing union contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Employee Matters"]
- Directors/officers: the Company's directors and executive officers were disclosed as
  having interests in the merger different from, or in addition to, stockholders
  generally (e.g., treatment of equity awards, tail D&O/fiduciary liability insurance to
  be maintained by the surviving company). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Interests of Vectren's Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger"]

## Financing structure (what would fund the closing)
- Completion of the merger was not conditioned on CenterPoint Energy's or Merger Sub's
  receipt of financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Financing and Financing Cooperation"]
- CenterPoint Energy planned to fund the merger consideration through a combination of
  cash on hand, commercial paper, borrowings under an existing revolving credit facility
  (amended to increase total commitment to $3.3 billion under specified circumstances),
  and other equity and debt financing, including a commitment letter with Goldman Sachs
  Bank USA and Morgan Stanley Senior Funding, Inc. for a 364-day senior unsecured bridge
  term loan facility of up to $5.0 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger Consideration"]

## Closing conditions, regulatory process, and termination-fee structure
- Consummation required, among other things: expiration or early termination of the
  waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act; authorization by the Federal Energy
  Regulatory Commission under Section 203 of the Federal Power Act; Federal
  Communications Commission authorization to transfer FCC registrations; and receipt of
  an order approving, denying, or dismissing an application relating to money-pool and
  corporate-services/affiliate arrangements filed with the Indiana Utility Regulatory
  Commission ("IURC") and the Public Utilities Commission of Ohio ("PUCO"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Completion of the Merger is Subject to Regulatory Clearance"]
  HSR waiting-period early termination was granted by the FTC on June 26, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Completion of the Merger is Subject to Regulatory Clearance"]
- If the merger agreement were terminated under specified circumstances relating to
  regulatory-approval failure or CenterPoint Energy's failure to close once all
  conditions were satisfied, CenterPoint Energy would owe the Company a termination fee
  of $210 million; if terminated under specified circumstances involving a Company
  superior proposal, the Company would owe CenterPoint Energy a termination fee of $150
  million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Termination Fees and Expenses"]
- Approval required the affirmative vote of holders of a majority of all outstanding
  shares of Company common stock at a special meeting scheduled for August 28, 2018;
  under Indiana law, shareholders were not entitled to dissenters' rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Approvals Required by Shareholders"]
- Company financial advisor Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated ("BofA
  Merrill Lynch") delivered a written fairness opinion, dated April 21, 2018, to the
  Board regarding the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A "Opinion of the Company's Financial Advisor"]

## Consummation
- On February 1, 2019 (the "Closing Date"), CenterPoint Energy completed the previously
  announced merger: each outstanding share of Company common stock was cancelled and
  converted into the right to receive $72.00 in cash, without interest, and the Company
  and its subsidiaries, including VUHI, became wholly owned subsidiaries of CenterPoint
  Energy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-025987, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the effective time, all existing directors of the Company and of VUHI ceased to
  serve, and Scott M. Prochazka (the sole director of Merger Sub immediately before the
  effective time) became the sole director of both the Company and VUHI. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-025987, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- The 8-K reporting completion also gave notice under Exchange Act Rule 12g-3(a)
  regarding transfer of listing status and stated that the Company's common stock would
  cease trading and its Exchange Act reporting obligations would terminate through
  deregistration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-025987, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A regulated public utility holding company (Vectren, parent of the Indiana Gas,
  SIGECO, and VEDO utilities through intermediate holding company VUHI) was acquired
  whole, for $72.00/share in cash, by a larger public utility holding company
  (CenterPoint Energy) via reverse triangular merger; the acquirer committed
  contractually to two years of employee compensation/benefit continuity and to
  multi-year headquarters and community-involvement commitments in Evansville, and the
  deal closed as structured on February 1, 2019 with the target's board and management
  replaced by the acquirer's designee and the target's shares removed from public
  trading. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-218284, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-025987, Form 8-K]
