# Structural sub-dossier — Fortive Corporation separation from Danaher Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Danaher Corporation announced its intention to separate into two independent, publicly
  traded companies; the separation was to occur "by means of a spin-off of Fortive to
  Danaher shareholders." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution was structured as "a pro rata distribution of 100% of the outstanding
  shares of Fortive common stock to holders of Danaher common stock," with no consideration,
  exchange, or surrender of Danaher shares required and no shareholder vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Fortive Corporation is "a newly formed Delaware corporation that has been organized
  specifically for the purpose of effecting the separation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The distribution was "intended to be tax-free to Danaher shareholders for U.S. Federal
  income tax purposes," except for cash in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary (asset / product tier)
- The business transferred to Fortive comprised Danaher's "existing Test & Measurement
  segment, Industrial Technologies segment (excluding the Product Identification platform)
  and Retail/Commercial Petroleum platform," organized post-separation into two segments:
  Professional Instrumentation and Industrial Technologies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- These industrial-technology and professional-instrumentation product lines are the
  customer-facing asset separated into the standalone company; in the year ended
  December 31, 2015 the business "delivered revenues of $6.2 billion." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The separation agreement provided that "Fortive Assets," including assets "primarily
  (or in the case of intellectual property, exclusively) relating to Fortive's business,"
  would be retained by or transferred to Fortive. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## People / management tier (what moved with the business)
- The management team moved with the business: "Led by James A. Lico, who will be
  Fortive's President and Chief Executive Officer after the separation," and described as
  "a management team with an extensive background in the industrial growth businesses."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The workforce moved with the business: "As of December 31, 2015, we employed
  approximately 22,000 persons, of whom approximately 12,000 were employed in the United
  States." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- An employee matters agreement governs "the assignment and transfer of employees," and
  provides that following the distribution "Fortive's employees generally will no longer
  participate in benefit plans sponsored or maintained by Danaher and will commence
  participation in Fortive's benefit plans." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Operational-process tier (the process substrate did NOT cross as owned property)
- The operating management system — the "DANAHER BUSINESS SYSTEM ('DBS')," described as
  "a set of growth, lean and leadership tools and processes" — was retained by Danaher and
  LICENSED to Fortive rather than transferred: "our rights to the DBS tools and processes
  will continue pursuant to the DBS license agreement... and we will continue using and
  developing these tools and processes under the name 'Fortive Business System,' or 'FBS.'"
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under that agreement "Danaher will grant a non-exclusive, worldwide, non-transferable,
  perpetual license to Fortive to use DBS solely in support of its businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Shared back-office processes were not yet standalone at closing: "Fortive is in the
  process of creating its own, or engaging third parties to provide, systems and services
  to replace many of the systems and services that Danaher currently provides to Fortive."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- At separation Fortive and Danaher were to enter into "a separation and distribution
  agreement... a transition services agreement, an employee matters agreement, a tax
  matters agreement, an intellectual property matters agreement and a DBS license
  agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transition services agreement, "effective upon the distribution," provides that the
  two companies "will provide to each other various services... including information
  technology, facilities, certain accounting and other financial functions, and
  administrative services," at cost "generally without profit," defaulting to termination
  "on the second anniversary of the effective date." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The agreements "were prepared in the context of Fortive's separation from Danaher while
  Fortive was still a wholly-owned subsidiary of Danaher," so their terms "may not reflect
  terms that would have resulted from arm's-length negotiations between unaffiliated third
  parties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Fortive "expects to have approximately $3.25 billion of indebtedness" immediately
  following the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A diversified industrial-instrumentation product operation, together with its management
  team and ~22,000 employees, was separated into a newly formed Delaware corporation by
  pro rata spin-off; the operating-process substrate (the DBS management system) was
  retained by the parent and conveyed to the separated company by perpetual non-exclusive
  LICENSE rather than by ownership transfer, and shared back-office processes (IT,
  facilities, accounting, administrative) were bridged by a transition services agreement
  at cost for a transitional period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-533838, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
