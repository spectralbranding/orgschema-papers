## Nature of the drawn filing
- The drawn filing is a Current Report on Form 8-K furnished under Items 2.02 (Results of
  Operations and Financial Condition) and 7.01 (Regulation FD Disclosure), attaching a
  press release (Exhibit 99.1); it is a Regulation FD disclosure announcing the
  transaction, not itself a merger/purchase agreement or its exhibits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Items 2.02/7.01/9.01]
- The transaction is documented by the announcement itself; no separate acquisition
  agreement was located as an exhibit to this filing, and the structural facts below are
  taken from the drawn 8-K and its Exhibit 99.1 press release. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The registrant is Deerfield Capital Corp. ("DFR," NYSE: DFR), a Maryland corporation and
  real estate investment trust, principal offices at 6250 North River Road, Rosemont,
  Illinois. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K cover]
- On July 21, 2008, DFR announced the acquisition of the management contract for Robeco
  CDO II Limited — a collateralized debt obligation previously managed by Robeco
  Investment Management, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / Exhibit 99.1]
- The company's chief executive officer, Jonathan Trutter, stated: "This is the first
  transaction in our previously announced strategy to acquire CDO management contracts...
  We see significant revenue and earnings growth potential in the CDO management contract
  roll-up strategy and believe Deerfield is well positioned to execute additional
  transactions going forward." [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (by tier)
- Customer-facing asset acquired: the management contract for the CDO — i.e., the right to
  serve as investment manager and, per the release, "the majority of the ongoing
  management fee stream from the CDO." The underlying collateral pool (a CDO collateralized
  primarily by high-yield corporate bonds; aggregate principal balance approximately $201
  million as of June 17, 2008; stated maturity August 20, 2013, subject to customary early
  amortization) is held by the CDO vehicle and was not itself acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Consideration / value-capture model: DFR stated it acquired the majority of the ongoing
  management fee stream "without the payment of any purchase price to the prior manager";
  value capture is the recurring investment-management fee stream, one contract at a time,
  under a stated strategy to acquire further CDO management contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Legal vehicle / operating substrate: DFR conducts third-party asset management through
  its subsidiary Deerfield Capital Management LLC (DCM); the release describes DFR as a
  REIT that, "through its subsidiary, Deerfield Capital Management LLC... manages assets for
  third party clients." The Exhibit 99.1 forward-looking factors also reference an earlier
  "Merger" through which DFR acquired Deerfield & Company LLC / DCM (consideration described
  as approximately $74 million of senior secured notes and 14,999,992 shares of Series A
  Preferred Stock), establishing DCM as the operating platform that performs the
  management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Platform scope after the acquisition: including this contract, DFR reported it then
  managed 31 CDOs and structured products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Processes and people (what did NOT move alongside the contract)
- The filing describes the transfer of the management contract and fee stream only; DFR,
  through DCM, assumes the manager role going forward. The press release does not describe
  any transfer of the prior manager's (Robeco Investment Management's) portfolio-management
  personnel, staff, systems, or operational processes to the acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- No transitional-services agreement, sub-advisory agreement, operating agreement, or
  shared-services arrangement bridging the two managers' operations is described in the
  drawn filing or its exhibit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction moved a customer-facing contractual asset — the right to manage a
  specific CDO and receive its ongoing management fees — onto DFR's existing management
  platform (DCM), with no purchase price paid to the prior manager and no described
  transfer of that prior manager's people, processes, or a transitional/sub-advisory
  bridge; it was announced as the first in a stated series of CDO management-contract
  acquisitions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-08-003494, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
