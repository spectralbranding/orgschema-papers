## Best-documented realized outcome scalar
- FY2016 net earnings attributable to Eastman Kodak Company were $15 million (total net earnings $16 million including noncontrolling interests), equal to 1% of revenue — a return to positive net earnings after net loss attributable to the Company of $(80) million in FY2015 and $(123) million in FY2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Total net revenues declined across the window: $2,046 million (2014), $1,709 million (2015), and $1,543 million (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- At the end of 2016 the Company employed the full-time equivalent of approximately 6,100 people globally, of whom approximately 2,700 were in the United States. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Based on its December 31, 2016 analysis, the Company concluded that no impairment of goodwill was indicated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Realized record at the divested product / operations interface over time
- The sale of the Personalized Imaging and Document Imaging ("PI/DI") businesses to the pension-plan purchasing parties was consummated for net cash consideration of $325 million; certain jurisdictions were not transferred at the initial closing but at a series of deferred closings, with the final deferred closing occurring in September 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Until each deferred closing, the Company operated the PI/DI Business in the applicable jurisdictions and reported its results as Loss from discontinued operations, net of income taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Up to $35 million of the purchase price was subject to repayment if the PI/DI Business did not meet certain annual adjusted-EBITDA targets over the four-year period ending December 31, 2018; the PI/DI Business did not achieve the target for the year ended December 31, 2015, and $4 million (the maximum amount for 2015) was repaid to the purchasing parties in 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Post-reference balance-sheet management
- The second-lien term credit agreement entered at emergence was prepaid and terminated on November 15, 2016, and the asset-based revolving credit agreement was amended and restated on May 26, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed window the reorganized company continued as a public SEC registrant and, by FY2016, returned to a small positive net result ($15 million attributable to the Company) after consecutive prior-year losses, on declining revenues ($2,046M in 2014 to $1,543M in 2016) and a reduced workforce (~6,100 full-time-equivalent employees); the divested Personalized and Document Imaging product businesses completed their staged transfer to the pension-plan purchasing parties by September 2015, missed their first post-sale adjusted-EBITDA target (triggering a $4 million purchase-price repayment in 2016), and no goodwill impairment was indicated at December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-003570, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
