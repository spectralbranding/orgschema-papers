# Structural sub-dossier — Caesars Acquisition Company / Caesars Entertainment Corporation merger (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction, parties, and closing

- Caesars Acquisition Company ("CAC") and Caesars Entertainment Corporation ("CEC")
  entered into an Amended and Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 9, 2016,
  as amended February 20, 2017, under which CAC would merge with and into CEC, with
  CEC as the surviving company; each share of CAC common stock outstanding immediately
  before the effective time was converted into 1.625 shares of CEC common stock (the
  "Exchange Ratio"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus]
- The merger became effective on October 6, 2017, on which date CAC merged with and
  into CEC (CEC surviving), resulting in CEC issuing approximately 226 million shares
  of CEC common stock under the Exchange Ratio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-305845, Form 8-K dated October 6, 2017]
- CAC's separate corporate existence ceased upon consummation of the merger; CAC filed
  a Form 15-12B on October 16, 2017 to deregister its common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310641, Form 15-12B]

## Relationship between the parties before the merger

- CAC was formed on February 25, 2013 to make an equity investment in Caesars Growth
  Partners, LLC ("CGP"), a joint venture between CAC and certain subsidiaries of CEC;
  as of December 31, 2016, CAC held 39% and CEC held 61% of the economic interests in
  CGP. CAC directly owned 100% of CGP's voting membership units and served as its
  managing member, while CEC subsidiaries held CGP's non-voting membership units.
  CAC did not own any other material assets or operate any business apart from its
  CGP interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]
- Both CAC and CEC were "controlled companies" under NASDAQ governance standards: a
  majority of the voting stock of each was beneficially owned by Hamlet Holdings LLC,
  whose members comprised individuals affiliated with Apollo Global Management, LLC
  and TPG Global, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]

## Tie to the CEOC Chapter 11 plan of reorganization

- Completion of the merger was conditioned on, among other things, a plan of
  reorganization for Caesars Entertainment Operating Company, Inc. and certain of its
  subsidiaries (the "Debtors") being confirmed by the Bankruptcy Court (Confirmation
  Order entered January 17, 2017) and the plan's effective time occurring
  contemporaneously with the merger's effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]
- Stockholders were separately asked to approve the issuance of CEC common stock to
  creditors of the Debtors in connection with their Chapter 11 emergence, and the
  issuance of CEC common stock under approximately $1.1 billion of convertible notes
  to be issued to Debtor creditors in connection with that emergence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]

## Governance at closing

- Under the plan of reorganization, the surviving company's initial post-merger board
  was to consist of eleven directors, a composition described as "substantially
  different" from the pre-merger CEC board; seats were allocated among appointees of
  CEC and CAC, the Second Lien Committee, and holders of CEOC first-lien claims, rather
  than being selected solely by CEC or CAC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]
- Among the individuals designated as initial directors under the plan, Mark Frissora
  was designated President and Chief Executive Officer of the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]

## Shared operating substrate at closing

- The CAC Special Committee's evaluation noted that CAC's interest in CGP — CAC's
  principal asset since its formation — was dependent in large part on CGP's access to
  the Total Rewards customer loyalty program and property management services provided
  by CEC, Caesars Enterprise Services, LLC ("CES"), and other CEC affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]
- CES was a services joint venture formed May 20, 2014 among CEC, Caesars Entertainment
  Resort Properties, LLC, and Caesars Growth Properties Holdings, LLC; it manages
  certain enterprise assets and employs certain employees who provide services across
  those affiliated properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]
- Under the CGP Operating Agreement, CGP's purpose was limited to businesses and assets
  contributed by CEC or acquired in conjunction with CEC; any business opportunity
  identified by CGP had to be offered to CEC first; CAC was restricted from
  transferring its CGP interests to a third party without CEC's consent (until October
  2018) and thereafter while it remained managing member; CEC held a right, effective
  October 2016, to purchase CAC's CGP interests at a pre-determined price; and CGP was
  required to liquidate and wind up its affairs no later than October 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- At closing, a minority joint-venture partner (CAC), whose sole material asset was a
  39% economic interest in a venture contractually bound to its majority partner (CEC)
  for opportunity sourcing, transfer restrictions, and buy-out rights, and dependent on
  services and a loyalty program supplied by CEC and a jointly formed services entity
  (CES), was absorbed by all-stock merger into CEC, with CAC's separate corporate
  existence ending and the surviving company's board reconstituted under terms set by a
  concurrent Chapter 11 plan of reorganization rather than by either merging company
  alone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-211288, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-305845, Form 8-K dated October 6, 2017]
