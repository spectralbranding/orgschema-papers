# Outcome sub-dossier — San Juan Silver Mining Joint Venture, realized record ~2011-2013

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at the registrant's
> ~3-5-year post-closing record; the realized facts below are drawn from the registrant's
> Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012 (filed 2013-02-25). This
> sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Realized ownership trajectory (within the outcome window)
- On December 12, 2011, the registrant's wholly-owned subsidiary Rio Grande Silver Inc.
  ("Rio") entered into a Purchase and Sale Agreement with Emerald Mining & Leasing, LLC
  ("EML"), Golden 8 Mining, LLC ("G8"), and AgX Resources, Inc. ("AgX") to purchase the
  Sellers' "remaining 30% undivided interest in certain properties that had been governed
  by a Joint Venture Agreement ('2008 JVA') entered into between EML, G8, and Rio on
  February 21, 2008." [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Note 16]
- Consideration for the remaining 30% was the issuance of 5,395,683 shares of Hecla common
  stock valued at $33.8 million on the completion date, priced at 90% of the 20-trading-day
  volume-weighted average price preceding and including December 2, 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Note 16]
- The original 2008 JVA "was terminated and a new Joint Venture Agreement" for the same
  Mineral County, Colorado properties was entered into, again operating under the name San
  Juan Silver Mining Joint Venture with Rio as the initial operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Note 16]
- The registrant classified the $33.8 million issued for the remaining 30% interest as a
  2011 capital expenditure item ("During 2011 we had capital expenditures of $33.8 million
  related to the acquisition of the remaining 30% interest in the San Juan Silver
  project"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Item 7]

## Realized project status at the product/process interface (within the window)
- As of December 31, 2012, the San Juan Silver / Creede properties (including the historic
  Equity and Bulldog mines) remained in the exploration / pre-development stage rather than
  production: the registrant reported "advancing pre-development projects ... at the
  historic Equity and Bulldog mines in Creede, Colorado." [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Item 7]
- Costs at the project were expensed, not capitalized, "due to the lack of proven and
  probable reserves," and the registrant stated "establishing proven and probable reserves
  would indicate future recovery of these expenses" — i.e., no reserves had been
  established at the project through FY2012. Company-wide exploration and pre-development
  expense (which includes the San Juan Silver project) rose to $49.7 million in 2012 from
  $31.4 million in 2011 and $21.6 million in 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Item 7]

## Realized status of the closing-era guaranty / bridging arrangement
- The Hecla guaranty of certain EML environmental-remediation-related obligations,
  capped at a $2.5 million maximum liability, remained outstanding but had not been drawn:
  "As of December 31, 2012, we have not been required to make any payments pursuant to the
  guaranty," and no related liability had been recorded. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) — Commitments/Contingencies, "Rio Grande Silver Guaranty"]
- The FY2012 filing describes Rio as "a party to a joint venture with Emerald Mining &
  Leasing, LLC ('EML') and certain other parties" in the Creede Mining District and states
  "Rio holds a 70% interest in the joint venture." [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) — "Rio Grande Silver Guaranty"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~3-5-year window the registrant consolidated full ownership of the venture
  properties — Rio bought the counterparties' remaining 30% interest on December 12, 2011
  for 5,395,683 Hecla shares valued at $33.8 million, terminated the 2008 agreement, and
  entered a new joint-venture agreement over the same Mineral County, Colorado properties —
  while, as of December 31, 2012, the Creede (Equity and Bulldog) properties remained in
  exploration/pre-development with no proven and probable reserves and no production, and
  the $2.5 million conditional guaranty remained outstanding and undrawn. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-13-001916, Form 10-K (FY2012) Item 7, Note 16, "Rio Grande Silver Guaranty"]
