## What the drawn filing establishes
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K dated October 15, 2010 (accession 0001104659-10-052516)
  reporting: (Item 5.02) board-approved increases to the base salaries of four named
  officers effective January 1, 2011, and a change in the bonus arrangement to pay
  bonuses in company stock rather than cash; (Item 5.03) a bylaws amendment permitting
  designation of several vice-president levels and deleting a stockholder-records-access
  provision; (Item 5.07) results of the October 12, 2010 annual stockholders' meeting
  (director election, charter amendment and restatement, independent-director stock
  option plan, all approved); and (Item 7.01) furnishing a related press release.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-10-052516, Form 8-K Items 5.02/5.03/5.07/7.01/9.01]
- This filing reports governance, compensation, and annual-meeting matters; it does not
  report or reference a merger, acquisition, or other business-combination closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-10-052516, Form 8-K]
- The furnished Exhibit 99.1 press release states that, as of June 30, 2010, the entity
  is a "self-managed real estate investment trust" with a portfolio exceeding 46 million
  square feet across 294 consolidated operating properties, 11 unconsolidated operating
  properties, and 8 properties under development. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-10-052516, Form 8-K Ex-99.1]
- The same press release reports year-to-date items including refinancing of 83% of 2010
  debt maturities, a new definitive joint-venture agreement (initial $123 million) with
  RioCan REIT under which it had closed on three of eight initial properties, and the
  sale of five assets for $80 million (extinguishing $61 million of debt).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-10-052516, Form 8-K Ex-99.1]

## Entity structure and history (from the FY2009 Form 10-K, the closing-era annual report)
- The entity describes itself as "a self-managed real estate investment trust (REIT)
  that acquires, manages, and develops a diversified portfolio of real estate, primarily
  multi-tenant shopping centers." As of December 31, 2009 its portfolio consisted of 299
  consolidated operating properties (242 wholly owned, 57 in three majority-owned joint
  ventures), plus interests in 11 further unconsolidated joint-venture properties and
  several development properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-10-000004, Form 10-K FY2009]
- The entity was formed in 2003 with no employees of its own; it had agreements under
  which related parties (a business manager/advisor and property managers) provided all
  services and staff for fees. On November 15, 2007 the entity acquired those related
  parties in exchange for 37,500 newly issued shares of its stock (with 55% escrowed),
  making the former business manager/advisor and property managers its subsidiaries — an
  "internalization" that predates the drawn filing by roughly three years.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-10-000004, Form 10-K FY2009]

## Structural summary (closing-era only)
- The drawn filing and its immediate contemporaneous 8-Ks establish routine governance,
  compensation, distribution, and (in one instance) a rejected unsolicited partial tender
  offer — none of them a business-combination or roll-up closing. The entity's own
  closing-era annual report describes an ongoing, multi-property, joint-venture-based
  acquisition and financing program (hundreds of properties accumulated over several
  years, plus a newly entered joint venture in 2010) rather than a single discrete
  acquisition transaction dated to this filing. No S-4/DEFM14A merger document was found
  for 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-10-052516, Form 8-K Ex-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-10-000004, Form 10-K FY2009]
