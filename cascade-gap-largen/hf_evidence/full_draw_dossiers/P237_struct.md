# Structural sub-dossier — Tallgrass Energy Partners, LP merger into Tallgrass Energy GP, LP (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On March 26, 2018, Tallgrass Energy GP, LP ("TEGP"), Tallgrass Equity, LLC ("TE"),
  Tallgrass Energy Partners, LP ("TEP"), Tallgrass MLP GP, LLC ("TEP GP," the general
  partner of TEP), and Razor Merger Sub, LLC ("Merger Sub," a wholly owned subsidiary of
  TEP formed solely to effect the merger) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the Merger Agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into TEP, with TEP surviving
  the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of TE and its subsidiaries; each outstanding
  TEP common unit (other than units already held by TE, its subsidiary Tallgrass Equity
  Investments, LLC, or TEP itself) was converted into the right to receive 2.0 TEGP
  Class A Shares, a fixed exchange ratio with no cash component apart from fractional
  rounding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]
- TEP unitholders approved the merger at a special meeting on June 26, 2018 (62,168,943
  votes for vs. 120,753 against); the merger became effective June 30, 2018 at 11:59
  p.m. Central Daylight Time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]
- At and after closing, TEGP changed its name to "Tallgrass Energy, LP" (ticker changed
  from "TEGP" to "TGE" on the NYSE), and TEGP's general partner, TEGP Management, LLC,
  changed its name to "Tallgrass Energy GP, LLC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]
- Before the merger, TEGP and TEP were already described as "effectively under common
  control": TE (a TEGP subsidiary) was the sole member of TEP GP, and TEP's officers and
  a majority of TEP GP's directors were also officers/directors of TE, TEGP GP, and
  Holdings, the entity (controlled by Tallgrass KC, EMG, and Kelso) that appointed both
  boards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]

## What TEP comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- TEP was "a publicly traded, growth-oriented limited partnership that owns, operates,
  acquires and develops midstream energy assets in North America," with segments in
  Natural Gas Transportation (FERC-regulated interstate pipelines and storage), Crude
  Oil Transportation (a FERC-regulated pipeline system), and Gathering, Processing &
  Terminalling (gathering/processing, crude storage/terminalling, water services, NGL
  transport and marketing), serving the Denver-Julesburg, Powder River, Wind River,
  Permian, and Hugoton-Anadarko basins and several shale formations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]
- These midstream assets were not divested to an outside party; TEP itself "continued
  without dissolution" as the operating partnership after the merger, now wholly owned
  by TE/TGE rather than partly public. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]

## Controlling-intent, management, and legal-vehicle continuity at closing
- "The directors and executive officers of TEGP GP prior to the Merger will continue as
  directors and executive officers of TEGP GP after the Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]
- Officers of TEGP GP and Holdings who were also officers of TEP GP "are expected to
  continue to serve as officers of TEGP GP and Holdings following the Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]
- Separately, at the effective time, nine individuals (including David G. Dehaemers,
  Jr., William R. Moler, Frank J. Loverro, Stanley de J. Osborne, Jeffrey A. Ball, John
  T. Raymond, Terrance D. Towner, Roy N. Cook, and Jeffrey R. Armstrong) resigned as
  directors of TEP GP specifically; the filing states this was "not the result of any
  disagreement with TEP GP or the Partnership on any matter relating to the operations,
  policies or practices of TEP GP or the Partnership." Dehaemers continued to sign for
  Tallgrass MLP GP, LLC as its President and Chief Executive Officer on the closing
  Form 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]
- The Partnership Agreement was amended at the effective time so that TEP GP's 1.13%
  general partner interest ceased to carry distribution/economic rights, while "TEP GP
  continued and shall continue to be entitled to any management rights associated with
  the Partnership General Partner Interest." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]
- Following closing, TEGP planned "a series of additional transactions to simplify its
  organizational structure," including MLP Operations acquiring REX Holdings,
  Development Holdco merging into TE, two subsidiaries merging into MLP Operations, REX
  Holdings merging into a new TEP REX Holdings, LLC, and Tallgrass Management becoming a
  wholly owned subsidiary of TE. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Upon completion, the TEP common units were removed from listing on the NYSE: TEP
  notified the NYSE on June 29, 2018 that the certificate of merger had been filed and
  requested a Form 25 notification of removal from listing; trading in the units was
  suspended before the opening of trading on July 2, 2018, ahead of subsequent
  deregistration under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined an MLP (TEP, the customer-facing pipeline/midstream
  business) into its already-affiliated general-partner-side entity (TEGP, renamed
  Tallgrass Energy, LP), eliminating the separate public unit listing and the general
  partner's economic (though not management) interest, in a unit-for-share
  exchange rather than a cash acquisition. Controlling intent, senior management, and
  the operating legal vehicle (TEP itself) continued largely unchanged; the only
  personnel change disclosed was the resignation of TEP GP's own director slate (not
  its officers), attributed to the elimination of TEP GP's need for an independent
  board once TEP became wholly owned. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-168177, Form DEFM14A; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-210364, Form 8-K]
