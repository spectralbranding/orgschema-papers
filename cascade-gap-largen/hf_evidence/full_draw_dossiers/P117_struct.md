## The drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn Form 8-K is a Regulation FD disclosure (Item 7.01), not a deal-terms
  document: it furnishes as Exhibit 99.1 the registrant's Monthly Operating Report for
  May 2009 and is dated June 16, 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-09-032752, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- It records that, "on March 18, 2009 Chemtura Corporation ... and 26 U.S. affiliates
  filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 ... in the United States
  Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York," consolidated for joint
  administration under case number 09-11233 (REG); certain non-U.S. and U.S.
  subsidiaries were not included in the filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-09-032752, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The registrant cautioned that the furnished Monthly Operating Report "was not audited
  or reviewed by independent accountants" and was prepared as prescribed by bankruptcy
  law, not for investment-decision purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-09-032752, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## How the entity was assembled (successive combinations)
- The registrant is the successor to Crompton & Knowles Corporation (incorporated in
  Massachusetts in 1900), which "expanded [its] specialty chemical business through
  acquisitions in the United States and Europe, including the 1996 acquisition of
  Uniroyal Chemical Company, Inc. ..., the 1999 merger with Witco Corporation ... and the
  2005 merger with Great Lakes Chemical Corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-013557, Form 10-K (FY2008), General Development of Business]
- The most recent combination closed July 1, 2005: Copernicus Merger Corporation, a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of the registrant, "merged ... with and into Great Lakes, with
  Great Lakes being the surviving corporation of the Merger and a wholly-owned subsidiary
  of Chemtura. The Registrant changed its name to 'Chemtura Corporation' in connection
  with the Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The transaction terms were set by an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 8, 2005,
  described in the registrant's proxy statement/prospectus dated June 3, 2005
  (SEC File No. 333-123857). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the boundary at the 2005 combination
- Legal vehicle: a reverse-triangular merger — the merger subsidiary (Copernicus Merger
  Corporation) was absorbed into Great Lakes, which survived as a wholly-owned subsidiary
  of the renamed parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Controlling intent / governance (people): the parent's board was increased to 11
  directors, and five of them (Messrs. Andrews, Crownover, Hale, Nichols and Proops) "were
  ... member[s] of Great Lakes' board of directors prior to the effective time of the
  Merger," while one prior director (Patricia K. Woolf) retired as required by the Merger
  Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Business/value-capture model: the combined entity is "a global diversified producer of
  specialty chemicals and polymer products," selling most products to industrial
  manufacturing customers as additives/ingredients/intermediates, with pool and spa
  chemicals sold through dealers and retailers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-013557, Form 10-K (FY2008), General Development of Business]
- Customer-facing product (assets): the combination brought Great Lakes' product lines
  under the same parent as the legacy Crompton/Witco/Uniroyal product lines, forming the
  combined specialty-chemical portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-013557, Form 10-K (FY2008), General Development of Business]

## Bridging of the shared financial/legal substrate at closing
- Concurrent with consummation of the Merger on July 1, 2005, the parent, Great Lakes and
  certain subsidiaries entered into a new credit agreement and executed supplemental
  indentures under which the combined parent and its wholly-owned subsidiaries (including
  Great Lakes and certain Great Lakes subsidiaries) became bound with respect to
  Great Lakes' outstanding notes (including Great Lakes' 7% Notes due 2009), consolidating
  the two firms' financing obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 2.01]
- Integration of the combined operations continued to generate separately identified,
  non-capitalizable "merger costs ... associated with the merger of the Company and Great
  Lakes" reported in periods after the 2005 combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-013557, Form 10-K (FY2008), Results of Operations]

## Structural summary (closing / combination only)
- The registrant was assembled through a succession of mergers and acquisitions
  (Uniroyal 1996, Witco 1999, Great Lakes 2005); the 2005 combination was effected as a
  reverse-triangular merger that folded Great Lakes' product lines and five of its
  directors into the renamed parent, with the two firms' credit and note obligations
  consolidated under a new credit agreement and supplemental indentures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-05-030993, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 2.01 and 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-013557, Form 10-K (FY2008), General Development of Business]
- At the June 2009 drawn-filing anchor the registrant was operating under Chapter 11
  protection (petitions filed March 18, 2009). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-09-032752, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
