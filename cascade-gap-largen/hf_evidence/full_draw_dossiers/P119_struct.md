## What the drawn filing is
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K "Current Report," dated June 10, 2014 (date of
  earliest event reported), filed by Expeditors International of Washington, Inc. under
  Item 7.01 (Regulation FD Disclosure); it is a question-and-answer document responding
  to "SELECTED INQUIRIES RECEIVED THROUGH JUNE 9, 2014" from shareholders, not a merger
  agreement, tender offer, or deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- No discrete acquisition, merger, spin-off, or asset transfer is executed or documented
  in the drawn filing; it references, but does not effectuate, any transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The report is signed by Jeffrey S. Musser (President, Chief Executive Officer and
  Director) and R. Jordan Gates (President and Chief Operating Officer). [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Controlling intent / imprint at the time
- The filing states a "newly appointed President and CEO, Jeff Musser," and describes a
  "strategic assessment" then underway framed as "as much about what will not change as
  it is about what we will change and at the top of the things that will not change is
  our culture." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Senior management describes the company's culture as what "defines" the firm and is
  "completely responsible" for how it "got to be what we are." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The filing describes Peter J. Rose as a "retiring founder" and 25-year CEO awarded an
  ~$8 million retirement bonus stated to be borne "by management, not by the
  shareholders" and paid "from an executive bonus pool formula" in effect since the
  company began trading publicly. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Business / value-capture model and the product
- The business is a freight-forwarding / global logistics operation; the customer-facing
  service is freight consolidation and forwarding (airfreight, ocean, and an
  intra-continental "Transcon" time-definite service). [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The entity uses independent agents in markets that will not "sustain one of our
  offices"; its 2013 Form 10-K is stated to report "relationships with 44 independent
  agents," and agent economics run on "a variable model" where "We split net freight
  profits with each other and each pay for their own respective cost of operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## How units are added to the organization (people/process/product alignment)
- When the entity opens its own office it "typically approach[es] our existing agent and
  try to have a discussion about purchasing them or barring that at least forming a joint
  venture," and "In the preponderance of the cases, we have acquired the agent outright
  on a deferred payout basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- On converting an agency into a company office the entity assumes "all the
  responsibilities, financial and otherwise, of financing that office," so the acquired
  local operation is folded into the entity's own offices rather than run at arm's
  length. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Management characterizes such additions as happening "in an evolutionary manner" and
  "naturally," and frames future growth as being in "optimizing our existing network,
  augmenting it with naturally evolving agent acquisition opportunities as they present
  themselves," rather than in a program of consolidating acquisitions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a transferred

## Structural summary (closing-era only)
- The drawn filing is a Regulation FD investor-relations Q&A, not a deal-terms document;
  it documents no discrete acquisition. The contemporaneous structure is a non-asset,
  variable-margin freight-forwarding network that grows predominantly by internal office
  development, occasionally purchasing individual local agents outright (deferred payout)
  or via joint venture and folding them into wholly operated company offices, under a
  founder-imprinted culture the newly appointed CEO commits to preserving, and with no
  long-term debt. [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-14-000023, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
