## Public-reporting status after the transaction

- CU Bancorp filed a Form 15-12B on November 2, 2017 to deregister its common stock
  under Section 12 of the Exchange Act; it filed no Form 10-K or other periodic report
  as an independent registrant after that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-331156,
  Form 15-12B]

## Whether the acquired business is separately observable in the acquirer's later filings

- PacWest Bancorp (the surviving corporation, CIK 0001102112) continued as an
  independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company throughout the outcome window,
  filing its own Forms 10-K for fiscal years 2020 and 2021. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-21-003477, Form 10-K FY2020 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-22-004301, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page]
- PacWest's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 states, in its "Business Segments"
  accounting-policy note: "As of December 31, 2020 and since December 31, 2015, we
  operated as one reportable segment." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003477, Form
  10-K FY2020, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, "Business Segments"]
- PacWest's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states the same in substance: "As of December
  31, 2021 and since December 31, 2015, we have operated as one reportable segment,"
  identifying only a separately acquired 2021 business (Civic) as a distinct operating
  segment not meeting quantitative disclosure thresholds. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-22-004301, Form 10-K FY2021, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements,
  "Business Segments"]
- Neither the FY2020 nor the FY2021 Form 10-K narrative text contains the terms "CU
  Bancorp" or "California United Bank" — i.e., PacWest's public filings within the
  outcome window do not separately name, segment, or disclose a distinct trajectory for
  the businesses acquired from CU Bancorp. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003477,
  Form 10-K FY2020, full-text review] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004301, Form
  10-K FY2021, full-text review]

- CU Bancorp ceased to exist as a separate legal entity and as an SEC registrant at the
  October 20, 2017 closing (Form 15-12B filed November 2, 2017), and its acquirer,
  PacWest Bancorp, has reported as a single consolidated reportable segment continuously

  the outcome window, with no separate segment, name reference, or disclosed trajectory

  FY2021 Forms 10-K. There is no public 3-5-year outcome record specific to the acquired
