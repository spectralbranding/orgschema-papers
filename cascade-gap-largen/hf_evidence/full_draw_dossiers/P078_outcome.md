## Public-reporting status across the outcome window
- The registrant filed no annual report on Form 10-K (or 20-F) for any period in the fixed
  ~2019-2021 outcome window; its last annual report on Form 10-K covers fiscal year 2017
  and was filed 2018-03-27. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000007, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2017)]
- On November 16, 2018 (before the outcome window opened), the registrant merged with and
  into REIT Merger Sub, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan
  of Merger dated July 17, 2018 with Phillips Edison & Company, Inc. (PECO); the separate
  corporate existence of the Company ceased and the merger sub continued as the surviving
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the merger, each issued and outstanding share of the Company's common stock was
  canceled and converted into the right to receive 2.04 shares of PECO common stock; each
  qualifying operating-partnership unit was converted into 2.04 PECO operating-partnership
  units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The registrant terminated its registration under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act by
  filing a Form 15 on November 16, 2018, certifying "None" holders of record as the
  successor and stating that the separate corporate existence of the Company ended on that
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000044, Form 15-12G]
- Consequently no separate audited public financial statements exist for the registrant
  (or for its interest in the venture as a stand-alone reporting matter) within the fixed
  ~2019-2021 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000044, Form 15-12G]

## Realized record at the process/organization interface (documented)

  Agreement (dated September 1, 2017), the Master Property Management Agreement Addenda
  (dated October 4, 2017), and the Master Services Agreement Addenda (dated October 4,
  2017) were terminated effective immediately prior to the merger; PECO waived disposition
  fees and no fees were payable on account of the terminations other than unpaid fees

  interest is available within the fixed window, so the realized outcome is not publicly
