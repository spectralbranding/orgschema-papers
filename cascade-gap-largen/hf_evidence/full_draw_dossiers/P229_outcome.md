## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On April 15, 2019, the registrant (by then renamed "Arris International Limited")
  filed a Form 15-12B certifying termination/suspension of its Exchange Act reporting
  obligations, stating an approximate number of holders of record of one (1) and that,
  "effective as of April 4, 2019, CommScope Holding Company, Inc. acquired all of the
  issued and outstanding shares of Arris International Limited." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-106618, Form 15-12B]
- No annual report (Form 10-K or 20-F) has been filed under CIK 1645494 since that
  deregistration; consequently there is no ARRIS-specific audited public financial
  record for the ~2022-2024 outcome window, and the facts below rely on the acquirer's
  (CommScope's, CIK 1517228 — a registrant that has also since undergone a name change,
  now filing as "Vistance Networks, Inc.") own periodic disclosures to the extent they
  separately identify the acquired business. [UNVERIFIED]

## Acquirer's disclosed integration/restructuring record naming the acquired business
- CommScope's fiscal-year 2022 Form 10-K states: "we have not fully achieved the
  expected growth prospects associated with the ARRIS acquisition and that has had
  adverse effects on our financial condition, results of operations, cash flows and
  stock price"; the same statement recurs verbatim in CommScope's fiscal-year 2023 Form
  10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003856, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-24-022519, Form 10-K FY2023]
- CommScope's FY2022 10-K discloses that its "transaction, transformation and
  integration" cost line "In 2022, primarily reflects transformation costs related to
  CommScope NEXT and integration costs related to the ARRIS acquisition. In 2021,
  primarily reflects transaction separation costs related to the planned separation of
  the Home segment from CommScope, transformation costs related to CommScope NEXT and
  integration costs related to the ARRIS acquisition. In 2020, primarily reflects
  integration costs related to the ARRIS acquisition" — i.e., CommScope reports
  ARRIS-integration costs as a named line item across fiscal years 2020-2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003856, Form 10-K FY2022]
- In its FY2022 10-K goodwill note, CommScope discloses that during Q4 2022 it recorded
  a $1,119.6 million goodwill impairment charge in its Access Network Solutions (ANS)
  reporting unit, attributed to "technology shifts affecting hardware sales, trends
  affecting bandwidth growth and other operational challenges" plus a higher cost of
  capital; the note separately states the Home Networks reporting unit's goodwill was
  reduced to zero as of December 31, 2020 following a $206.7 million impairment in Q2
  2020 (the year the ARRIS-acquisition goodwill was finalized), with a further $13.7
  million Home Networks goodwill impairment in Q4 2021 attributed to "continued decline
  in customer demand for video products." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003856, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Divestiture of the Home segment (within the outcome window)
- CommScope's FY2023 10-K states that in 2021, "as a step in our CommScope NEXT
  transformation plan, we announced a plan to separate the Home Networks (Home)
  segment," and that on October 2, 2023 it entered a Call Option Agreement with Vantiva
  SA (a French société anonyme) granting Vantiva a binding call option to acquire the
  Home segment and substantially all associated assets/liabilities; the option was
  subsequently exercised, a Purchase Agreement was signed December 7, 2023, and "the
  transaction closed on January 9, 2024" — roughly 4 years and 9 months after the ARRIS
  acquisition closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-24-022519, Form 10-K FY2023]
- The same filing states the divested Home business met "held for sale" and
  "discontinued operations" accounting criteria, and that CommScope's remaining
  operating segments following the divestiture are Connectivity and Cable Solutions
  (CCS), Outdoor Wireless Networks (OWN), Networking, Intelligent Cellular and Security
  Solutions (NICS), and Access Network Solutions (ANS) — i.e., the Home segment (which
  had absorbed certain ARRIS-heritage product lines shifted from ANS in Q2 2021) was
  removed from CommScope's continuing-operations segment set within the outcome window.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-24-022519, Form 10-K FY2023]
- CommScope's FY2023 10-K further notes that, going forward, certain corporate costs
  formerly allocated to the divested Home segment will "be at least partially offset by
  income from our transition services agreement with Vantiva" — a post-divestiture
  services bridge between CommScope and the buyer of the former Home business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-24-022519, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because ARRIS International plc was acquired whole and deregistered in April 2019,
  there is no ARRIS-specific public 10-K for the ~2022-2024 window; however, the
  acquirer (CommScope) separately discloses reporting-unit and segment-level facts
  naming the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-106618, Form 15-12B]
- Within the fixed ~2022-2024 window, CommScope's own filings document: (1) a
  reporting-unit-level goodwill impairment of $1,119.6 million in the ANS reporting
  unit, recorded Q4 2022; (2) a company statement (repeated in both the FY2022 and
  FY2023 10-Ks) that expected growth prospects from the ARRIS acquisition were "not
  fully achieved," with adverse effects on financial condition, results of operations,
  cash flows and stock price; and (3) the divestiture of the Home segment (which had
  absorbed realigned former-ARRIS product lines) to Vantiva SA, closing January 9, 2024.
  The Home Networks reporting unit's own goodwill had already been written down to zero
  by year-end 2020 and impaired further in Q4 2021 (both dates preceding the fixed
  window), which is reported here only as background to the FY2022/FY2023 facts above.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003856, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-24-022519, Form 10-K FY2023]
