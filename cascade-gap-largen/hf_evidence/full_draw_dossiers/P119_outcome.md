- The entity reported "no long-term debt at December 31, 2018." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Scale and organizational record
- The entity had "approximately 17,500 employees" and provided "a complete range of
  global logistics services to a diversified group of customers." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The entity leased and maintained "approximately 490 locations worldwide, of which
  approximately 100 are in the United States." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Product/process and process/organization interface record
- The entity stated it "operates the same transportation and accounting systems, running
  on a common hardware platform, in all of our full-service locations," and contrasted
  this with "competitors, who have tended to grow by merger and acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- On growth mode, the entity stated it "often concluded over the course of our history
  that it is better to grow organically rather than by acquisition," and that when
  acquisitions were made "it has generally been to obtain technology, geographic coverage
  or specialized industry expertise that could be leveraged to benefit our entire
  network," while remaining "not opposed to acquisitions." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Goodwill was carried at $7,927 thousand, unchanged year over year; the entity performed
  "goodwill annual impairment test during the fourth quarter and determined that no
  impairment had occurred." [SRC: SEC accession 0000746515-19-000004, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
