## Public-reporting status through the outcome window

- The entity remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company: it filed a
  Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 on March 8, 2023 under the registrant name "Earthstone
  Energy, Inc.," with Class A Common Stock (ticker ESTE) continuing to trade on the New
  York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized financial trajectory

- Total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022 were $1,695,154 thousand, versus
  $419,643 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2021 — a 304% increase. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income for the year ended December 31, 2022 was $650,617 thousand, versus
  $61,506 thousand for 2021 and a net loss of $(29,434) thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statement of Operations]
- Net income attributable to Earthstone Energy, Inc. (after deducting the portion
  attributable to noncontrolling interest) was $452,485 thousand for 2022, versus
  $35,484 thousand for 2021 and a net loss of $(13,547) thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statement of Operations]
- Oil revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022 increased by $817.2 million (275%)
  versus 2021, of which $702.9 million was attributed to a volume increase and $114.3
  million to a realized-price increase (average realized oil price rose from $67.83/Bbl
  in 2021 to $93.91/Bbl in 2022, up 38%); management attributed the results primarily to
  the impact of the Titus, Bighorn, and Chisholm acquisitions together with improved
  commodity prices. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Interest expense, net, was $(66,821) thousand for 2022 versus $(10,796) thousand for
  2021 (up 519%), and income tax expense was $(124,416) thousand for 2022 versus
  $(1,859) thousand for 2021 (up 6,593%). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]

## Documented events over time (unrelated to the terminated 2018 transaction)

- On February 15, 2022, EEH consummated the "Chisholm Acquisition," acquiring interests
  in oil and gas leases and related property in Lea County and Eddy County, New Mexico,
  under an agreement previously reported on Form 8-K filed December 17, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Note re: Acquisitions]
- On April 14, 2022, EEH consummated the "Bighorn Acquisition," acquiring interests in
  oil and gas leases and related property of Bighorn Asset Company, LLC located in the
  Midland Basin, Texas, under a Purchase and Sale Agreement dated January 30, 2022
  (previously reported on Form 8-K filed February 2, 2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Note re: Acquisitions]
- On August 10, 2022, EEH consummated the "Titus Acquisition," acquiring interests in
  oil and gas leases and related property located in the Delaware Basin, New Mexico,
  from Titus Oil & Gas Production, LLC and related sellers, under a Purchase and Sale
  Agreement dated June 27, 2022 (previously reported on Form 8-K filed June 29, 2022),
  for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $567 million, comprising cash
  consideration and 3,857,015 shares of Class A Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Note re: Acquisitions]
- As of December 31, 2022, the company reported approximately 167,000 net acres in the
  Midland Basin and approximately 45,000 net acres in the Delaware Basin, New Mexico,
  and interests in a combined 263 gross/206 net vertical and 998 gross/855 net
  horizontal producing wells (Midland Basin) plus 265 gross/94 net vertical and 265
  gross/144 net horizontal producing wells (Delaware Basin). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, "Properties"]
- As of December 31, 2022, the company had 219 full-time employees, comprising 13
  management, 62 technical, 37 administrative, and 107 field-operations personnel. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, "Human Capital"]
- The company also repurchased treasury shares during the year ended December 31, 2022
  (3,000,000 shares of Class B common stock repurchased, per the Consolidated Statement
  of Changes in Equity). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statement of Changes in Equity]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- The entity continued to operate and report publicly under its own name (Earthstone
  Energy, Inc., ticker ESTE, NYSE) through fiscal year 2022 rather than being acquired,
  removed from listing, or dissolved. Its realized trajectory across the outcome window
  shows total revenues rising 304% year-over-year in 2022 ($1,695,154 thousand versus
  $419,643 thousand in 2021) and net income attributable to the company rising from a
  net loss of $(13,547) thousand in 2020 to $35,484 thousand in 2021 to $452,485
  thousand in 2022, a trajectory management attributed primarily to three oil and gas
  property acquisitions completed in 2022 (Chisholm, Bighorn, and Titus, none involving
  the 2018 counterparties) together with higher realized commodity prices, alongside
  materially higher interest expense and income tax expense in the same period. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-23-000058, Form 10-K FY2022]
