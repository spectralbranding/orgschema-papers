# Structural sub-dossier — Tidewater Inc. / Sonatide Angola joint venture (closing structure, ~2011)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> arrangement was structured at the closing horizon — what sat inside the ownership
> boundary and what did not, and how a shared operating substrate was bridged. It
> contains no post-closing outcome information.

## Registrant and its business at the closing horizon
- Tidewater Inc. is a Delaware corporation (NYSE: TDW), incorporated in 1956, that
  provides offshore service vessels and marine support services to the global offshore
  energy industry through a diversified fleet of marine service vessels, conducting its
  operations through wholly owned U.S. and international subsidiaries and joint ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended March 31, 2011)]
- Services span all phases of offshore exploration, field development and production
  (towing/anchor-handling for mobile offshore drilling units; transport of supplies and
  personnel; offshore construction and seismic support; pipe/cable laying). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- At March 31, 2011 the company had 378 vessels available to serve the global energy
  industry, of which 10 were operated through joint ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]

## The drawn filing (described for what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K, Item 8.01 "Other Events," reporting that as of
  February 25, 2011 Tidewater and its joint-venture partner Sonangol agreed to further
  extend the date of the existing joint venture agreement from January 31, 2011 to
  April 30, 2011, "to give the parties additional time to negotiate the terms of a new,
  more permanent joint venture agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047707, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The 8-K references, but is not itself, the joint venture agreement; it points back to
  the quarterly Form 10-Q filed February 4, 2011 for the underlying disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047707, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## The transaction as structured (parties, vehicle, tiers)
- The arrangement is a joint venture: Tidewater holds a 49% ownership interest in
  Sonatide (Sonatide Marine Ltd.), a joint venture company located in Luanda, Angola,
  that owns vessels serving the Angolan offshore energy industry; the joint-venture
  partner is the Angolan entity Sonangol. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- The company states that in some international operating environments local customs or
  laws may require it to form joint ventures with local owners or use local agents — the
  stated reason the local vehicle exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- Legal vehicle / value-capture model: Sonatide is an unconsolidated affiliate accounted
  for by the equity method; the assets and liabilities of the joint-venture company are
  NOT consolidated on Tidewater's balance sheet, and Tidewater's return is recognized as
  equity in net earnings of unconsolidated companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- Customer-facing asset vs. its operating substrate: the vessels that serve Angolan
  customers comprise both vessels owned by the joint venture and Tidewater-owned vessels
  operating in Angola; the joint venture agreement is the instrument under which Sonatide
  "is managed and operated," i.e., the vehicle through which the vessels are placed under
  charterparty agreements with customers in Angola. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- Concentration inside the boundary: more Tidewater vessels are deployed in Angola, and
  more revenue is derived from Angolan operations, than in or from any other country of
  operation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]

## The bridging arrangement at the closing horizon
- The underlying joint venture agreement was originally scheduled to expire by its terms
  on July 31, 2010; representatives of Sonangol and Tidewater agreed from time to time to
  extend the expiration date (the drawn 8-K records the extension to April 30, 2011; the
  contemporaneous 10-K records a most-recent extension to July 31, 2011), pending
  negotiation of a new, more permanent agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047707, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
- As a transitional bridge, Sonangol and Tidewater agreed to continue the Sonatide joint
  venture "on a charter by charter basis" to fulfill several new or renewed charterparty
  agreements and the remaining terms of existing charters for both the joint-venture-owned
  vessels and the Tidewater-owned vessels operating in Angola — i.e., the shared operating
  substrate was held together contract-by-contract while a permanent vehicle was
  negotiated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]

## Structural summary (closing horizon only)
- A customer-facing vessel-services operation in Angola sat inside a 49%-owned local
  joint venture (Sonatide, with Sonangol), accounted for off-balance-sheet by the equity
  method; the operating vehicle (the joint venture agreement under which the vessels are
  chartered to customers) had lapsed its original term and, at the closing horizon, was
  being held together by repeated short extensions and a charter-by-charter continuation
  while the parties negotiated a new, more permanent agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047707, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-145426, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2011)]
