## Public-reporting status (feasibility-relevant)
- Ford Motor Company continued filing annual reports on Form 10-K throughout the

- The outcome window is fixed to Ford's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016 (filed

  comparisons to 2015 and 2014 — approximately 3 to 4 years after the November 30,

## Realized scale of the venture
- By year-end 2016, Changan Ford Automobile Corporation, Ltd. ("CAF") "currently
  operates five assembly plants, an engine plant, and a transmission plant in China,"
  up from the single retained Chongqing facility (plus two assembly plants, an engine
  plant, and a transmission plant reported as under construction at the time of the
  2012 restructuring). [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Ford's carrying value of its equity investment in CAF (50% ownership) was $1,307
  million at year-end 2015 and $1,315 million at year-end 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The related three-way Changan Ford Mazda Engine Company, Ltd. ("CFME") joint venture
  (Ford 25%, Mazda 25%, Changan 50%) continued unchanged, with Ford's investment
  carrying value of $77 million (2015) and $80 million (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Realized earnings contribution and trend
- Ford's China joint ventures (including CAF) contributed $1.5 billion to the Asia
  Pacific segment's pre-tax profit in 2015, described as "$234 million higher than"
  2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Those same China joint ventures contributed $1.4 billion to Asia Pacific's pre-tax
  profit in 2016, "$75 million lower than in 2015"; their net income margin was 14.6%
  in 2016, "a reduction of 1 percentage point from 2015," which the filing attributes
  to "negative industry pricing in China and a higher mix of vehicles with engine
  displacement of 1.6 liters or lower." [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- At the Asia Pacific segment level, full-year pre-tax profit was a record $765 million
  in 2015 (up 29% from 2014), then $627 million in 2016 (down $138 million from 2015),
  which the filing attributes to "lower net pricing, adverse exchange effects (mainly a
  weaker Chinese renminbi), and unfavorable cost performance." [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 3-4-year outcome window, the joint venture's disclosed record shows
  physical and financial growth (one retained plant to five assembly plants plus
  engine and transmission plants; investment carrying value up from $1,307 million to
  $1,315 million) alongside a documented one-percentage-point margin decline (to 14.6%)
  and a $75 million year-over-year drop in pre-tax profit contribution in 2016,
  attributed by the filing to negative China industry pricing; the wider Asia Pacific
  segment's pre-tax profit likewise declined from a record $765 million (2015) to $627
  million (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
