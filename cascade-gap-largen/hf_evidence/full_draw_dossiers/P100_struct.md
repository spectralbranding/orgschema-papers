## Ownership and controlling intent at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- At December 1, 2015, the registrant's common equity consisted of membership interests, 99.5% of which were held by TPG Accolade, L.P., and the remainder by management of Nexeo Solutions Holdings, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015 (fiscal year ended September 30, 2015)]
- The registrant is "controlled by TPG," which "beneficially owns the substantial majority of our equity" and "has significant influence over our operations"; "TPG" refers to investment funds controlled by affiliates of TPG Global, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- Related parties including TPG were paid recurring minimum management and consulting fees under management and consulting services agreements for providing management and strategic consulting services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]

## The legal vehicle and the origin of the platform (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant was formed on November 4, 2010, and on March 31, 2011 acquired the global distribution business ("the Distribution Business," also the "Predecessor") from Ashland Inc. — the "Ashland Distribution Acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- The entity is a Delaware limited liability company holding structure: Nexeo Solutions Holdings, LLC ("Holdings") is a holding company that conducts substantially all operations through Nexeo Solutions, LLC ("Solutions"); remaining membership interests are held via Nexeo Solutions Sub Holding Corp. ("Sub Holding"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Holdings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]

## The customer-facing asset and the operating processes that deliver it (closing era)
- The company is "a global distributor of chemicals products in North America and Asia, and plastics products in North America, EMEA and Asia"; in connection with distribution it provides value-added services such as custom blending, packaging and re-packaging, private-label manufacturing and product testing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- During fiscal year 2015 the company distributed approximately 23,000 products into over 80 countries for approximately 1,300 suppliers to approximately 27,500 customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- Operations run on a "hub-and-spoke" distribution model in North America, processing large volumes of inventory at main regional hubs and shipping to smaller local warehouses on a demand-driven basis to achieve economies of scale and route optimization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- People/organization: at September 30, 2015 the company had approximately 2,450 employees worldwide (approximately 2,000 in the United States), including approximately 500 sales professionals; several U.S. facilities are represented by local unions (Teamsters, United Steelworkers). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]

## Successive acquisitions folded into the platform (closing era)
- The platform grew through a series of acquisitions of distribution businesses: Beijing Plaschem (a joint venture formed in fiscal 2012 whose operations were acquired November 1, 2012), the CSD Acquisition (100% of Chemical Specialists and Development, Inc., a chemical distribution company and packaged-chemicals provider headquartered in Conroe, Texas, plus STX Freight Company and ST Laboratories Group, LLC, effective December 1, 2013) and the Archway Acquisition (100% of Archway Sales, Inc., a St. Louis, Missouri chemicals blending and distribution business, April 1, 2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
- Goodwill of $373.7 million was recorded across the Ashland Distribution Acquisition (April 2011), the Beijing Plaschem acquisition (November 2012), the CSD Acquisition (December 2013) and the Archway Acquisition (April 2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- At the original carve-out from Ashland, the company and Ashland entered into a Transition Services Agreement dated March 31, 2011 (Exhibit 10.6, by and between Ashland Inc. and Nexeo Solutions, LLC). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015 (Exhibit Index)]
- Under the purchase agreement for the Distribution Business, Ashland retained liability for known remediation obligations arising before the closing date and agreed to indemnify the company for such losses (subject to limitations, and for conditions of which Ashland received notice prior to the fifth anniversary of the closing); Ashland also agreed to continue performing certain ongoing corrective actions and to indemnify the company for their costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession is a Regulation FD (Item 7.01) furnishing that announces the execution of a merger agreement under which Nexeo Solutions agreed to be acquired by WL Ross Holding Corp.; it is an announcement/disclosure filing, not the merger agreement or a deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-106466, 8-K Item 7.01]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is a TPG-controlled (99.5% TPG Accolade, L.P.) Delaware LLC holding structure operating a chemicals-and-plastics distribution platform originally carved out of Ashland's distribution business (March 31, 2011) and expanded by successive add-on acquisitions of distribution businesses (Beijing Plaschem 2012, CSD 2013, Archway 2014); the customer-facing asset (approximately 23,000 distributed products from approximately 1,300 suppliers to approximately 27,500 customers) is delivered through a hub-and-spoke logistics network and value-added services (blending, re-packaging, testing) run by approximately 2,450 employees, with a March 31, 2011 Transition Services Agreement having bridged the shared operating substrate at the original Ashland carve-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-15-009287, 10-K FY2015]
