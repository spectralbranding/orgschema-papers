## Realized financial trajectory (fixed outcome window)
- Net income was $15.0 million for 2018, compared to net income of $6.9 million for 2017; net income was $52.7 million for 2016 — a marked decline across the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY ended 2018-12-31]
- Total operating revenues were $2.9 billion in 2018, a decrease of 7% from 2017 ($3,146,480 thousand), which in turn was below 2016 ($3,047,474 thousand); the 2018 decrease was attributed primarily to continued softness in publishing-segment advertising and marketing-services revenues. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Digital revenues were $1.1 billion in 2018, or 36% of total company revenues, with print advertising described as remaining challenged due to market pressures and the range of digital advertising choices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Documented disruption at the product/operations interface over time
- The company reports print advertising is challenged due to the range of advertising choices across digital products and platforms and the large available digital inventory, and states it is focused on driving deeper penetration of its digital products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Total company revenue declined year over year across the period while the company shifted its revenue mix toward digital; the USA TODAY NETWORK is reported with more than 3,200 journalists averaging approximately 126 million monthly unique visitors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Documented disruption at the operations/organization interface over time
- The company recorded restructuring costs of $67.9 million in 2018, $44.3 million in 2017, and $45.8 million in 2016, describing a "series of individual restructuring programs designed to right size our employee base, consolidate facilities, and improve operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- The company recorded asset impairment charges of $50.5 million in 2018, $46.8 million in 2017, and $55.9 million in 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Loss of independence within the outcome window
- Effective in November 2019, the company (then Gannett Co., Inc.) was acquired and became a subsidiary renamed Gannett Media Corp., with New Media Investment Group Inc. — renamed Gannett Co., Inc. — as its parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-295996, Form 8-K dated 2019-11-14]
- In the transaction, each share of the company's common stock outstanding immediately prior to the effective time was converted (subject to limited exceptions) into merger consideration comprising 0.5427 of a share of the parent's common stock and $6.25 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-295996, Form 8-K dated 2019-11-14]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net income fell from $52.7 million (2016) to $6.9 million (2017) to $15.0 million (2018) on total operating revenues declining to $2.9 billion (down 7% in 2018), amid sustained restructuring ($67.9 million in 2018) and asset-impairment charges ($50.5 million in 2018) tied to right-sizing the employee base and consolidating facilities; within the outcome window the standalone company lost its independence, becoming a subsidiary (renamed Gannett Media Corp.) of an acquirer in November 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635718-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018; SEC accession 0001193125-19-295996, Form 8-K dated 2019-11-14]
