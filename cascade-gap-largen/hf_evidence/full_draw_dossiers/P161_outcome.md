> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at the company's

## Public-reporting status (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports on EDGAR after the transaction;
  the record used here is the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020, filed
  February 23, 2021 by Peabody Energy Corporation (CIK 1064728). [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Best-documented realized scalar
- For the year ended December 31, 2020, net loss attributable to common stockholders was
  $(1,870.3) million, compared with $(211.3) million for the prior year — a decrease of
  $1,659.0 million (785.1%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A "Net Loss Attributable to Common Stockholders"]
- Diluted loss per share attributable to common stockholders was $(19.14) for 2020, versus
  $(2.04) for the prior year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A]
- Total revenues were $2,881.1 million for 2020, compared with $4,623.4 million for the
  prior year — a decrease of $1,742.3 million (37.7%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A]
- Asset impairment charges of $1,487.4 million were recorded in 2020, compared with
  $270.2 million in the prior year, described in Note 3 "Asset Impairment" to the
  consolidated financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, Note 3]

## Documented disruption at the operating (product/process) interface over time
- The company's North Goonyella Mine in Queensland, Australia experienced a fire in a
  portion of the mine during September 2018 and mining operations remained suspended
  thereafter; in 2020 the company commenced an ongoing review of strategic alternatives
  for North Goonyella. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, "North Goonyella Mine"]
- Several operations were wound down within the window: the Kayenta Mine closed in August
  2019 upon termination of its coal supply agreement, and the Millennium mine ceased
  production in December 2020 (with sales continuing into January 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, mine tables notes]
- As of December 31, 2020 the company had approximately 4,600 employees worldwide,
  including approximately 3,500 hourly employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, "Human Capital"]

## Documented capital-structure/organization action within the window
- During the fourth quarter of 2020 the company entered into transaction support
  agreements with its surety bond providers that prohibit the payment of dividends on its
  stock and repurchases through December 31, 2024. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A]
- In January 2021 the company entered into a series of interrelated transactions with its
  surety bond providers and revolving lenders; it reached a surety transaction support
  agreement with the providers of 99% of its surety bond portfolio to resolve
  approximately $800 million in additional collateral demands. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A / Subsequent Events]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2020 window the registrant continued to operate the same coal-mining
  business but reported a sharply widened net loss of $(1,870.3) million on revenues of
  $2,881.1 million (down 37.7%), with asset impairment charges of $1,487.4 million,
  suspension of the North Goonyella operation and closure of other mines, and a
  fourth-quarter-2020 / January-2021 series of surety- and lender-support transactions
  addressing collateral demands and restricting dividends through 2024. [SRC: SEC accession 0001064728-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
