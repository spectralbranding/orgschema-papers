## Realized scale of the portfolio (outcome window)
- As of December 31, 2018 the company owned 890 properties (including nine properties held through a consolidated joint venture arrangement), comprising 26.5 million rentable square feet of commercial space located in 45 states. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]
- As of December 31, 2018 the gross rentable square feet of the portfolio was 96.2% leased, including any month-to-month agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]
- Over the life of the offering the company raised gross offering proceeds of $3.5 billion (including 50.5 million shares issued in the distribution-reinvestment offerings), before organization and offering costs, selling commissions and dealer-manager fees of $306.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]

## Realized financial record (best-documented outcome scalars, in-window)
- Reported net income attributable to the Company was $71,842 thousand for fiscal 2016, $79,420 thousand for fiscal 2017, and $37,278 thousand for fiscal 2018 (a decline of approximately 53% from 2017 to 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018 selected financial data]
- Total assets were $4,617,371 thousand as of December 31, 2018, versus $4,728,689 thousand as of December 31, 2017; notes payable and credit facility, net were $2,516,914 thousand as of December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018 selected financial data]
- The Board's estimated per-share net asset value (NAV) declined over the window: $10.08 (December 31, 2016 valuation), $9.37 (December 31, 2017 valuation), and $8.65 (December 31, 2018 valuation) — each below the $10.00 original offering price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]

## Change of control of the external advisor (realized event in-window)
- On February 1, 2018, CIM Group, LLC ("CIM") acquired CCO Group, LLC and its subsidiaries ("CCO Group") from VEREIT Operating Partnership, L.P., a subsidiary of VEREIT, Inc. (the "Transaction"); CCO Group owns and controls the Company's advisor (CR IV Management) and is the indirect owner of the Company's dealer manager, CCO Capital, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]
- As of the FY2018 report the Company's advisor (styled CR IV Management / Cole REIT Advisors IV, LLC) is described as "an affiliate of CIM Group, LLC ('CIM'), a vertically-integrated owner and operator of real assets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]
- The registrant continued to be named Cole Credit Property Trust IV, Inc. in its FY2018 Form 10-K; the FY2018 filing does not record a change of the registrant's own name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018 cover]

## Realized operating/management structure (outcome window)
- The company continued to operate without its own workforce: "We have no paid employees and rely upon our advisor and its affiliates to provide substantially all of our day-to-day management." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]

## Items noted but not primary-verifiable in this window

[SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-20-000015, 10-K FY2019 (filing name)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed fiscal 2016-2018 window the program reached large realized scale (890 properties, 26.5 million square feet, 45 states, 96.2% leased; $3.5 billion of gross offering proceeds raised) while its per-share NAV fell across the window ($10.08 → $9.37 → $8.65, below the $10.00 offering price) and net income attributable to the Company dropped from $79.4 million (2017) to $37.3 million (2018); over the same period control of the entity's external advisor and dealer manager passed to CIM Group via the February 1, 2018 acquisition of CCO Group, while the entity itself retained its name in the FY2018 report and continued to have no paid employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-19-000008, 10-K FY2018]
