## Public-reporting status of the acquired entity after the transaction
- HCBF Holding Company, Inc. filed a Form 15-15D on 2018-01-11 (shortly after the merger
  became effective on January 1, 2018), certifying termination/suspension of its duty to
  file periodic reports under Sections 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act. No further
  periodic reports (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K) by HCBF appear in EDGAR after this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-001805, Form 15-15D]
- At the effective time of the merger, HCBF's directors and executive officers ceased
  serving in those capacities; HCBF ceased to exist as a separate legal entity, merged
  into CenterState. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-000005, Form 8-K (HCBF), Items 2.01/5.02]

## Public-reporting status of the direct acquirer within the outcome window
- CenterState Bank Corporation (the direct acquirer of HCBF, CIK 0001102266), continued
  to file its own periodic reports for roughly two years after the HCBF merger, with its
  last annual report on Form 10-K covering fiscal year 2019, filed 2020-02-27. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007443, Form 10-K FY2019]
- On June 7, 2020, CenterState itself completed a "merger of equals" with South State
  Corporation, with South State as the surviving public company; at the effective time
  CenterState's own directors and executive officers ceased serving in those
  capacities. CenterState subsequently filed a Form 25-NSE (2020-06-08) and a Form
  15-12G (2020-06-18) terminating its own Exchange Act registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-028550, Form 8-K (CenterState), completion of merger of equals]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-171864, Form 15-12G]
- Consequently, within the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window (approximately 3-5 years
  after the HCBF combination closed on January 1, 2018), neither HCBF nor its direct
  acquirer CenterState remained a distinct SEC-reporting entity; any subsequent
  consolidated reporting is that of South State Corporation (later SouthState Bank
  Corp, CIK 0000764038), a company formed through a further, separate combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-028550, Form 8-K (CenterState)]
