# Structural sub-dossier — Dynegy Holdings, LLC Chapter 11 restructuring (closing structure)

## Note on the drawn filing

The drawn accession, a Form 8-K dated March 16, 2012, is a Regulation FD disclosure
furnishing the Chapter 11 examiner's report and the registrants' preliminary response
to it — a dispute over the propriety of an earlier intercompany asset transfer (below),
not the plan-of-reorganization terms themselves. [SRC: SEC accession
0001104659-12-018950, Form 8-K Item 7.01] The closing structure below is drawn from the
registrants' contemporaneous primary filings documenting the actual plan and its
consummation.

## Parties, prior intercompany transfer, and plan formation

- On November 7, 2011, Dynegy Holdings, LLC ("DH") and four wholly-owned subsidiaries
  (Dynegy Northeast Generation, Inc.; Hudson Power, L.L.C.; Dynegy Danskammer, L.L.C.;
  Dynegy Roseton, L.L.C.) filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the Southern District of New York; DH's direct parent, Dynegy Inc., and its
  other subsidiaries did not file and continued operating in the ordinary course at
  that time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-018950, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Effective September 1, 2011 (before the Chapter 11 filing), DH transferred its Coal
  segment (approximately 3,100 MW of generating capacity) to parent Dynegy Inc.; DH's
  consolidated results included the Coal segment only through August 31, 2011. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001445305-12-002901, Form 10-K (FY2011) Item 7]
- Dynegy Inc., DH, certain DH subsidiaries, and certain beneficial owners of DH's
  senior notes entered into a Settlement Agreement dated May 1, 2012 (filed as an
  exhibit to a Form 8-K). On its June 5, 2012 effective date, DH reacquired the entity
  holding the Coal segment ("Coal Holdco") from Dynegy Inc. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001445305-12-002901, Form 10-K (FY2011) Item 7 / Exhibit Index item 10.84]
- On May 30, 2012, Dynegy Inc.; Dynegy Gas Investments, LLC; Dynegy Coal Holdco, LLC;
  the DH debtor entities; consenting senior noteholders; the Roseton/Danskammer lessor
  and lease-certificate parties; and other named claimholders entered an Amended and
  Restated Plan Support Agreement amending DH's plan of reorganization. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001104659-12-053113, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On July 6, 2012, Dynegy Inc. itself filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition; on July 12,
  2012, DH and Dynegy Inc. (together, the "Plan Proponents") filed a Joint Chapter 11
  Plan of Reorganization and Joint Disclosure Statement covering both as debtors. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001104659-12-060272, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- After a September 5, 2012 confirmation hearing, the Bankruptcy Court confirmed the
  Joint Plan and entered a Confirmation Order on September 10, 2012, setting the stage
  for the reorganization to become effective on or before October 1, 2012. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001104659-12-063317, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## What crossed the ownership/legal-vehicle boundary at closing

- The confirmed Plan contemplated the merger of DH with and into Dynegy Inc., Dynegy
  Inc. as surviving legal entity ("Surviving Entity"); all DH equity interests
  outstanding immediately before the merger's effectiveness were to be cancelled, as
  were all then-outstanding shares of Dynegy Inc. common stock. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001104659-12-063317, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Holders of Allowed General Unsecured Claims were to receive their pro rata share of
  99% of Reorganized Dynegy's common stock, a $200 million cash payment, and proceeds
  from the sale of the Roseton and Danskammer generation facilities allocated to DH
  under the Settlement Agreement; the Dynegy Administrative Claim holder(s) were to
  receive 1% of Reorganized Dynegy's common stock plus warrants for 13.5% of fully
  diluted shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-063317, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Except as specified in the Plan, DH's, Dynegy's, and the Surviving Entity's executory
  contracts and unexpired leases were to be deemed rejected as of the Effective Date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-063317, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- A new board of directors for the Surviving Entity was selected under a creditor-agreed
  process (summary filed August 14, 2012) to serve from the Effective Date; on August
  27, 2012, Dynegy announced that creditors holding approximately 99% of voting claims
  (about 87% of voting creditors by number) had approved the Joint Plan and the
  proposed board slate (Pat Wood III as Chairman, plus six other named directors). [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001104659-12-060272, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Effective October 1, 2012, DH's Form 15 certified termination of its Exchange Act
  registration for its outstanding notes and lease-related guarantees, reporting
  approximately zero holders of record as of the certification date — consistent with
  DH's merger into Dynegy Inc. and cessation as a separately reporting legal entity.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-066793, Form 15-12G]
- Coal purchased by DH under existing supply contracts (approximately $433 million of
  $449 million in remaining contracted purchases through 2015) was to be sold onward to
  Dynegy Midwest Generation, LLC, an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Dynegy Inc. —
  an intercompany supply arrangement spanning the corporate structure at closing. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001445305-12-002901, Form 10-K (FY2011) Item 7A]
- DH's own periodic reporting (10-K/10-Qs for periods through June 30, 2012, plus
  monthly operating reports filed with the Bankruptcy Court) continued under DH's own
  registrant CIK through late September 2012, immediately preceding the effective date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-002901, Form 10-K, filed 2012-09-18]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- The drawn 8-K documented an interim dispute (the bankruptcy examiner's report and
  Dynegy's response) over a prior intercompany asset-segment transfer, not the deal
  terms themselves; the actual closing structure — confirmed roughly six months later —
  merged the debtor subsidiary (DH) into its parent (Dynegy Inc.), cancelled DH's prior
  equity, gave unsecured creditors the substantial majority of the surviving company's
  new equity plus cash and asset-sale proceeds, installed a creditor-selected board,
  and ended DH's separate SEC registration on the effective date — preceded by the
  Coal-segment transfer between DH and Dynegy Inc. that the same Settlement Agreement
  unwound. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-063317, Form 8-K Item 1.03] [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001445305-12-002901, Form 10-K (FY2011) Item 7]
