## Public-reporting status after the transaction (outcome-observability)
- The registrant's ordinary shares ceased trading on the NASDAQ Global Select Market prior
  to the opening of trading on December 2, 2016, and NASDAQ filed a Form 25 the same day to
  effect delisting and deregistration of the shares under Section 12(b) of the Exchange
  Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- The registrant filed a Form 15-12B on December 5, 2016 to terminate registration and
  suspend its reporting obligations under Sections 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act,
  reporting an approximate number of holders of record of 1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784713, Form 15-12B]
- No annual report (Form 10-K), Form 20-F, or other periodic report was filed by the
  registrant on EDGAR after deregistration; its last annual report was the Form 10-K for
  the fiscal year ended December 31, 2015 (filed March 8, 2016). Consequently no audited
  public financial statements exist for the ~2019-2021 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784713, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001585854-16-000093, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

  discloses the acquired business's standalone post-close trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Introductory Note]
