## Transaction that terminated the public record
- On December 14, 2015 the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with DRA Growth and Income Fund VIII, LLC ("Parent"), an affiliate of DRA Advisors LLC, and a merger subsidiary; under the agreement each outstanding common share would convert into the right to receive $10.60 in cash at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0000923284-16-000069, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- DRA Advisors LLC completed the acquisition effective March 30, 2016; Inland Real Estate Corporation survived as a wholly owned subsidiary and was renamed IRC Retail Centers, and common stock was converted into the $10.60-per-share cash consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000923284-16-000088, Form 8-K (filed 2016-03-30)]
- In connection with the closing, the common and preferred stock were delisted from the New York Stock Exchange via Form 25 and the company deregistered under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-16-000890, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-110722, Form 15-12G]
