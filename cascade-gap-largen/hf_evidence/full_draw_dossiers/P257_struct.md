# Structural sub-dossier — South State Corporation merger with Park Sterling Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On April 26, 2017, Park Sterling Corporation, a North Carolina corporation and bank
  holding company, and South State Corporation, a South Carolina corporation and bank
  holding company, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which Park
  Sterling would merge with and into South State, with South State continuing as the
  surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately following the corporate merger, Park Sterling Bank (Park Sterling's
  wholly owned bank subsidiary) was to merge with and into South State Bank (South
  State's wholly owned bank subsidiary), with South State Bank continuing as the
  surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of Park Sterling common stock was to be converted into the right to
  receive 0.14 shares of South State common stock (no separate merger-sub entity was
  used; South State itself was the direct surviving corporation). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- As of June 30, 2017, South State reported approximately $11.2 billion in total assets
  and Park Sterling operated 55 full-service branches across North Carolina, South
  Carolina, Virginia and North Georgia with 532 employees (511 full-time equivalents).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- South State's shareholders approved the merger proposal at a special meeting on
  October 25, 2017 (98.56% of shares voted in favor). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-064063, Form 8-K]
- The Merger was completed on November 30, 2017: Park Sterling merged with and into
  South State, with South State as the surviving entity, and Park Sterling Bank
  immediately thereafter merged with and into South State Bank, with South State Bank
  surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071278, Form 8-K]
- The merger was structured to qualify as a tax-free "reorganization" within the
  meaning of Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing (asset/legal-vehicle tier)
- Park Sterling's business — a full array of retail and commercial banking, wealth
  management and capital-market services delivered through its branch network — passed
  into South State as the surviving corporation; South State assumed Park Sterling's
  obligations under indentures for Park Sterling's outstanding trust preferred
  securities (aggregate principal $43.1 million), instruments Park Sterling itself had
  previously assumed from several prior acquisitions (Community Capital Corporation,
  Citizens South Banking Corporation, Provident Community Bancshares, and First Capital
  Bancorp). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071278, Form 8-K]
- Upon completion of the merger, Park Sterling common stock was to no longer be listed
  for trading on the NASDAQ; South State common stock was to continue trading on the
  NASDAQ under "SSB." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]

## Governance / management tier crossing
- At the effective time, South State appointed James C. Cherry (Park Sterling's Chief
  Executive Officer) and Jean E. Davis (a Park Sterling director) as new directors of
  South State, increasing the board to 15 members; Mr. Cherry was also appointed to
  South State's Executive Committee, and Mr. Robert R. Horger continued as Chairman of
  South State's board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071278, Form 8-K]
- Rather than continuing as an executive, Mr. Cherry's prior Employment Agreement with
  Park Sterling was replaced at closing by a three-year Consulting and Noncompetition
  Agreement between Mr. Cherry and South State Bank (an annual retainer of $333,334 and
  scheduled payments including $1,633,854 shortly after closing, $1,080,000 paid in
  installments over 24 months, and $3,000,000 contingent on completion of a later
  systems conversion). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- South State Bank entered closing-contingent Employment and Noncompetition Agreements
  ("Replacement Agreements") with Donald K. Truslow and Bryan F. Kennedy, III, and an
  Employment Agreement with Mark Ladnier, each of whom continued with South State Bank
  after closing; Mr. Ladnier's agreement likewise included a one-time payment tied to
  completion of the systems conversion (paid no later than 90 days after that
  completion) alongside a restricted-stock-unit grant vesting on the third anniversary
  of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- Ms. Nancy J. Foster remained subject to her existing Employment Agreement with Park
  Sterling, which provided severance if her employment were terminated without cause or
  she resigned for good reason in connection with the change of control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]

## Bridging mechanism for the process/systems tier
- Several of the closing-contingent executive agreements (Cherry's Consulting Agreement
  and Ladnier's Employment Agreement) tied specific one-time cash payments ($3,000,000
  and $100,000, respectively) to the completion of a "systems conversion" occurring
  after closing, rather than to closing itself — the merger agreement's compensation
  structure treated the operational/systems integration of the two banks as a distinct,
  later-completing milestone from the legal closing of the corporate and bank mergers.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided for a termination fee payable by either party under
  specified circumstances if the agreement were terminated before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a direct statutory merger (no merger-sub vehicle) of the
  legal/corporate and bank-subsidiary tiers, completed November 30, 2017, with South
  State as sole surviving corporation and South State Bank as sole surviving bank; the
  customer-facing product/branch network and the trust-preferred legal obligations
  crossed with the merged entity, while the management/process tier crossed only
  partially and on different terms — two Park Sterling directors joined the South State
  board and select executives received multi-year consulting/employment agreements, but
  Park Sterling's CEO moved from an employment to a consulting role, and a material
  slice of executive compensation (including a $3,000,000 payment) was explicitly
  deferred to and contingent on a later, separately timed "systems conversion" rather
  than being resolved at the legal closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005767, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071278, Form 8-K]
