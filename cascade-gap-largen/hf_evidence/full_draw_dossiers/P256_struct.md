# Structural sub-dossier — Tenneco Inc. acquisition of Federal-Mogul LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On April 10, 2018, Tenneco Inc., Federal-Mogul LLC, American Entertainment Properties
  Corp. ("AEP"), and Icahn Enterprises L.P. ("IEP") entered into a Membership Interest
  Purchase Agreement under which Tenneco agreed to acquire from AEP all of the
  membership interests of Federal-Mogul. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- As consideration, Tenneco agreed to pay AEP $800 million in cash and to issue and
  deliver an aggregate of 29,444,846 shares of Common Stock, comprised of a number of
  shares of newly created Class A Voting Common Stock equal to 9.9% of Class A shares
  outstanding immediately after closing, with the balance in newly created Class B
  Non-Voting Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- To issue this consideration, Tenneco was required to amend its certificate of
  incorporation to reclassify its existing common stock as "Class A Voting Common
  Stock" and to authorize a new "Class B Non-Voting Common Stock"; stockholder adoption
  of this amendment and approval of the share issuance (required under NYSE rules
  because the issuance exceeded 20% of outstanding shares) were each conditions to
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was subject to clearance under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act and approval
  by the European Commission's Directorate General for Competition under the EUMR;
  applicable HSR waiting periods expired as of 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time on May 24, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties negotiated a termination fee of $200 million payable under specified
  circumstances if the transaction was not consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction closed on October 1, 2018: Federal-Mogul was merged with and into
  Tenneco, with Tenneco continuing as the surviving company; Tenneco paid AEP $800
  million in cash and issued 5,651,177 shares of Class A Voting Common Stock and
  23,793,669 shares of Class B Non-Voting Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-289580, Form 8-K Item 2.01/Introductory Note]

## What Federal-Mogul comprised at signing (the acquired product/process tier)
- Federal-Mogul operated two independent business divisions, each with its own chief
  executive officer reporting to Federal-Mogul's board of directors: Federal-Mogul
  Motorparts (aftermarket products sold under more than 20 brands, including ANCO,
  Beck/Arnley, and others, plus original-equipment-service parts) and Federal-Mogul
  Powertrain (OE-facing powertrain products). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]

## Management and control arrangements at closing
- Upon closing, and prior to any later separation, Brian J. Kesseler (Tenneco's then
  Chief Executive Officer) and Roger J. Wood (a member of Tenneco's board of directors)
  were designated to serve as co-Chief Executive Officers of the combined Tenneco, with
  Kesseler leading the Aftermarket & Ride Performance business and Wood leading the
  Powertrain Technology business, both reporting to Tenneco's board of directors — i.e.,
  incumbent Tenneco leadership, not Federal-Mogul's pre-closing executives, was placed
  atop the combined organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- On October 1, 2018, concurrent with closing, Tenneco, AEP, IEP, and Icahn Enterprises
  Holdings L.P. entered into a Shareholders Agreement giving IEP the right to have its
  designee nominated to Tenneco's board (one seat) until an intended later separation or
  loss of a 10% ownership threshold, together with standstill and lock-up provisions and
  registration/preemptive rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-289580, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Planned post-closing reorganization disclosed at signing
- Subject to the fiduciary duties of Tenneco's board, Tenneco agreed to use reasonable
  best efforts to pursue a reorganization of the combined business into two separate
  businesses — "Powertrain Technology" and "Aftermarket & Ride Performance" — and to
  distribute the equity of one such business to Tenneco stockholders (the "Spin-Off")
  within 18 months of closing; Tenneco expected the Aftermarket & Ride Performance
  business to be separated while the Powertrain Technology business would remain at
  Tenneco and retain the "Tenneco Inc." name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement further disclosed that, following any such separation, Kesseler
  was designated to become Chief Executive Officer of the separated Aftermarket & Ride
  Performance company and Wood was designated to become Chief Executive Officer of the
  Powertrain Technology company, with IEP holding board-designation rights at Powertrain
  Technology (up to two seats depending on ownership thresholds) but no board-designation
  rights at Aftermarket & Ride Performance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-entity acquisition of all of Federal-Mogul's
  membership interests for cash plus newly created dual-class stock, with Federal-Mogul
  then merged into Tenneco at closing (no carve-out or transition-services bridge was
  identified in the reviewed sources); at signing, Tenneco simultaneously committed to a
  contingent, board-fiduciary-duty-qualified 18-month plan to re-divide the just-combined
  businesses into two separate public companies along Federal-Mogul's own pre-existing
  Motorparts/Powertrain divisional lines, with combined-company leadership at closing
  drawn from Tenneco's incumbent management rather than Federal-Mogul's. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-236739, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-289580, Form 8-K]
