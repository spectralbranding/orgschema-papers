## Public-reporting status across the outcome window
- The registrant's last annual report on Form 10-K covers fiscal year 2015 and was filed
  March 24, 2016; no later periodic report was filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-16-004332, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The registrant filed a Form 15-12B on August 11, 2016 to terminate the registration of its
  common stock under the Securities Exchange Act; consequently no public audited financial
  statements exist for the ~2018–2020 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-138948, Form 15-12B]

## Realized post-close trajectory (as reported)
- On May 20, 2015 the registrant entered a Forbearance Agreement and Third Amendment to its
  December 14, 2012 Credit Agreement, under which the lenders agreed to forbear from
  exercising remedies (until the earlier of specified events or June 30, 2015) with respect
  to two anticipated events of default: a "going concern" qualification in the registrant's
  2014 audited financial statements, and the failure to make the April 2015 interest payment
  due under the credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-040093, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On July 15, 2015 the registrant and nine subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for
  reorganization under Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of
  New York (jointly administered as In re Sabine Oil & Gas Corporation, et al, Case No.
  15-11835), continuing to operate as debtors-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-051260, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Chapter 11 filing constituted an event of default that accelerated the registrant's
  obligations under its Second Amended and Restated Credit Agreement (Dec 16, 2014), its
  December 14, 2012 Credit Agreement, and three series of senior notes (9.75% due 2017, 7.25%
  due 2019, 7.5% due 2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-051260, Form 8-K Item 2.04]

## Best-documented realized scalars (from the FY2015 Form 10-K)
- For the year ended December 31, 2015 the registrant recorded a non-cash impairment charge
  for oil and gas properties of $1.6 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-16-004332, Form 10-K FY2015, MD&A]
- All of the registrant's debt was classified as "Liabilities Subject to Compromise" on the
  consolidated balance sheet at December 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-16-004332, Form 10-K FY2015]
- On January 26, 2016 the debtors filed a joint plan of reorganization and disclosure
  statement; as of the FY2015 Form 10-K the disclosure statement had not yet been approved by
  the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-16-004332, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Resolution of the reorganization
- On July 27, 2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered a Confirmation Order confirming the Second
  Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization; the Plan provided for a debt-for-debt
  exchange, a debt-to-equity conversion, and the issuance of warrants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-136612, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Under the Plan, all of the registrant's existing equity interests — 202,859,849 shares of
  common stock outstanding as of June 30, 2016 — were to be automatically cancelled on the
  effective date, and the reorganized company would become a wholly owned subsidiary of a new
  parent, Sabine Oil & Gas Holdings, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-136612, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Items not primary-verifiable in the fixed outcome window
- No public audited financial or operating record exists for the reorganized private company

  the outcome in that window is therefore coded uncertain (no public record). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-138948, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly seven months of the December 2014 closing the registrant entered lender
  forbearance over a going-concern qualification and a missed interest payment (May 2015) and
  then filed Chapter 11 (July 2015); it recorded a $1.6 billion oil-and-gas property
  impairment for FY2015, classified all debt as liabilities subject to compromise, and had a
  reorganization plan confirmed July 27, 2016 that cancelled all existing equity and moved the
  business under a new private parent, after which it deregistered (August 2016), leaving no
  public audited record for the fixed ~2018–2020 window.
