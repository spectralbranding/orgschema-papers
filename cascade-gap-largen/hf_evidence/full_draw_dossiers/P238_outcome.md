## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On January 9, 2018, Union Bankshares Corporation, as successor by merger to Xenith
  Bankshares, Inc., filed a Form 15-12B certifying termination of Xenith's registration
  under Section 12(g) and suspension of its duty to file reports under Sections
  13(a)/15(d); the filing recorded the approximate number of holders of record as
  "None." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-001529, Form 15-12B]
- A second Form 15-12B for Xenith was filed on February 1, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-005177, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed under Xenith's own CIK
  (0001143155) since its pre-merger FY2016 10-K; no periodic report covering fiscal
  years within the ~2021-2023 outcome window exists for Xenith as a standalone
  registrant. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001143155&type=10-K]

## Acquirer's identity and continuity
- Union Bankshares Corporation is a continuing SEC filer (CIK 0000883948); it was
  renamed Atlantic Union Bankshares Corp effective May 13, 2019, per its EDGAR
  former-names record, and its stock traded on NASDAQ (later transferred, per its own
  recent-developments disclosure, effective January 18, 2023).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-002183, Form 10-K (FY2021); SEC accession 0000883948-23-000027, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## What the acquirer's later Form 10-Ks disclose about the acquired business
- Both the FY2021 Form 10-K (filed 2022-02-25) and the FY2022 Form 10-K (filed
  2023-02-24) list "Xenith Bank" in a table of constituent banking institutions with
  the notations "1987" (founded), "2018" (acquired), and "2018" (merged into the Bank)
  — i.e., the acquired bank was consolidated into the surviving bank subsidiary in the
  same year it was acquired, with no distinct operating identity retained afterward.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-002183, Form 10-K (FY2021); SEC accession 0000883948-23-000027, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Both 10-Ks name Xenith Bankshares, Inc. only as one of the company's "three most
  recent acquisitions" in narrative discussion of overall growth (alongside StellarOne
  Corporation in 2014 and Access National Corporation in 2019); neither 10-K contains a
  segment, reporting-unit, or scalar disclosure isolating Xenith's post-close financial
  or operating trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0000883948-23-000027, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The only Xenith-specific dollar figure found in either 10-K is a note, in the FY2021
  Form 10-K's discussion of the FY2019-vs-FY2020 comparison (FY2019, therefore outside
  the fixed ~2021-2023 window), that FY2019 noninterest income included approximately
  $9.3-9.8 million in life insurance proceeds "related to a Xenith-acquired loan that
  had been charged off prior to the Company's acquisition of Xenith"; this is a legacy
  credit-related item, not an in-window outcome scalar, and is reported here only to
  document that no other Xenith-specific figure was located. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-002183, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- A subordinated trust-preferred capital-notes line item in both 10-Ks is jointly
  attributed to "StellarOne Bank and Xenith" (combined, not separated by acquisition).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-002183, Form 10-K (FY2021); SEC accession 0000883948-23-000027, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Xenith Bankshares, Inc. deregistered from SEC reporting in January 2018 and has filed
  no periodic report since; the acquirer (Union Bankshares Corporation, later Atlantic
  Union Bankshares Corp) is a continuing SEC filer, but its FY2021 and FY2022 Form
  10-Ks disclose only that the acquired bank was fully merged into the surviving bank
  subsidiary in 2018 and name Xenith solely as a completed past acquisition, without a
  segment, reporting-unit, or scalar figure isolating its subsequent trajectory.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-001529, Form 15-12B; SEC accession 0001558370-22-002183, Form 10-K (FY2021); SEC accession 0000883948-23-000027, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Per the no-record convention, this case is coded **uncertain** on the outcome: the
  acquirer does not separately disclose the acquired business's realized 3-5-year
  trajectory, and no public record permits a sourced determination of it.
