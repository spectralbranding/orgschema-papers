## Fixed outcome window and registrant
- The outcome window is fixed to the successor registrant's fiscal years 2020 through
  2022, drawn from its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-02-25) and its Form
  10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed 2023-02-14). [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026,
  Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-23-000044, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The registrant describes itself as "an energy technology company with a diversified
  portfolio of technologies and services that span the energy and industrial value
  chain," conducting business in more than 120 countries, organized into four operating
  segments/product companies: Oilfield Services, Oilfield Equipment, Turbomachinery &
  Process Solutions, and Digital Solutions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026,
  Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1 Business]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Fiscal year 2020: revenue of $20.7 billion (down from $23.8 billion in 2019, with
  declines across all four segments); a goodwill impairment charge of $14,773 million
  ($14.8 billion) recognized in the first quarter of 2020, following an interim
  quantitative impairment test triggered by a significant market-capitalization decline
  attributed to the COVID-19 pandemic and the collapse of oil prices; restructuring,
  impairment and other charges of $1,866 million; and a consolidated net loss of $15,761
  million (of which $9,940 million was attributable to Baker Hughes Company after
  noncontrolling interests). [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026, Form 10-K FY2020,
  Item 7 MD&A and Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- Fiscal year 2021: consolidated net loss of $330 million (of which $219 million was
  attributable to Baker Hughes Company); no goodwill impairment recorded. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001701605-23-000044, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Income
  (Loss), comparative 2021/2022/2020 columns]
- Fiscal year 2022: consolidated net loss of $578 million (of which $601 million was
  attributable to Baker Hughes Company, exceeding the consolidated loss due to
  noncontrolling-interest income); no goodwill impairment recorded. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001701605-23-000044, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Income (Loss)]
- Separation-related costs tied to the ongoing separation from GE were recorded in each
  year of the window: $134 million (2020), $60 million (2021), and $23 million (2022).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-23-000044, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of
  Income (Loss)]

## GE ownership sell-down and corporate-identity change (documented within the window)
- Effective September 16, 2019, following a secondary offering and a repurchase of Class
  B common stock and associated LLC units, GE's ownership interest was reduced from
  approximately 50.3% to approximately 36.8%, and GE ceased to be the company's
  controlling shareholder (the "Trigger Date"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026,
  Form 10-K FY2020, Note "Transactions with GE"]
- On October 17, 2019, the company changed its name from "Baker Hughes, a GE company" to
  "Baker Hughes Company," and on October 18, 2019 began trading under the ticker "BKR" on
  the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026, Form 10-K
  FY2020, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, Basis of Presentation]
- In July 2020, GE launched a program to fully divest its ownership in the company over
  approximately three years. As of December 31, 2020, GE's economic interest in Baker
  Hughes Holdings LLC (the company's operating subsidiary) was 30.1%. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001701605-21-000026, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1 Business]
- As of December 31, 2021, GE's economic interest in Baker Hughes Holdings LLC had fallen
  to 11.4%; as of December 31, 2022, GE no longer held any economic interest in Baker
  Hughes Holdings LLC and no longer held any of the company's Class B common stock or
  associated LLC units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-23-000044, Form 10-K FY2022,
  "Description of the Business" and Note on equity/Class B common stock]
- The company disclosed continuing involvement with GE during the window through GE's
  remaining ownership interest, ongoing purchases and sales of products and services,
  transition services GE provided, and an aeroderivative joint venture ("Aero JV") formed
  with GE in the fourth quarter of 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-21-000026, Form
  10-K FY2020, Note "Transactions with GE"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the 2020-2022 window, the successor registrant recorded a large first-year net
  loss dominated by a $14.8 billion goodwill impairment and a COVID/oil-price-driven
  revenue decline (2020), followed by two subsequent years of much smaller net losses
  ($330 million in 2021, $578 million in 2022) with no further goodwill impairment;
  separation-related costs persisted but shrank across all three years ($134M to $60M to
  $23M); and GE's ownership interest, already reduced below majority control before the
  window began, was fully divested by the end of calendar year 2022. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001701605-21-000026, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001701605-23-000044, Form
  10-K FY2022]
