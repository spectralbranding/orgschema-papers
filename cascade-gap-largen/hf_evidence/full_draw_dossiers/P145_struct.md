## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement): on August 28, 2009, Huntsman Corporation ("the Company," a Delaware corporation) entered into an Asset and Equity Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Sellers are Tronox Incorporated (a Delaware corporation) and affiliated entities: Tronox LLC, Tronox Pigments (Savannah) Inc., Tronox Worldwide LLC, Tronox Western Australia Pty. Ltd., and Tronox Pigments (Netherlands) B.V. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-10.1 (preamble)]
- Buyers are Huntsman Pigments LLC (a Delaware limited liability company, "U.S. Buyer") and Huntsman Australia R&D Company Pty. Ltd. (an Australian company, "Australia Buyer"); Huntsman Corporation is the Guarantor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (preamble)]
- The Sellers' U.S. entities and certain affiliates filed for relief under chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code on January 12, 2009; the cases are pending in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York, jointly administered as In re Tronox Incorporated, et al., Case No. 09-10156 (ALG). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-10.1 (recitals)]

## Transaction type as structured
- The Agreement is a purchase of substantially all of the assets relating to the Sellers' titanium dioxide and electrolytics businesses, including related working capital, together with the equity interests of certain of Tronox Incorporated's subsidiaries, to be implemented in a court-approved sale under sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code, subject to approval by the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 (recitals, Section 5(c))]
- The Buyers entered into the Agreement as a "stalking horse" bidder; the purchase was subject to the Sellers' solicitation of higher or otherwise better offers pursuant to specified bidding procedures and an auction process conducted under supervision of the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## The business proposed to cross the boundary — product/asset
- The Business is described as, worldwide, developing, researching, processing, manufacturing, distributing, marketing and selling the Products (titanium dioxide pigments and electrolytics), and, in Australia, the mining of and exploration for raw materials required to produce the Products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (recitals)]
- The equity interests to be acquired ("Target Interests") are of the Target Companies: Tronox Pigments (Holland) B.V., Tronox Pigments Ltd. (Bahamas), and Tronox Pigments (Singapore) Pte Ltd. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (recitals)]
- The Australia Buyer agreed to purchase a 50% joint venture interest in a titanium dioxide facility in Australia and associated mining operations (the Tiwest Joint Venture); Tronox's JV partner, Exxaro Resources Limited, agreed to waive contractual restrictions (right of first refusal and change-of-control rights) on transfer of the JV interests, contingent on the Bankruptcy Court approving Huntsman as buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Consideration and legal vehicle
- The base consideration ("Sales Proceeds") is $415,000,000, subject to specified adjustments; the Buyers also agreed to assume certain liabilities associated with the ongoing operations of the businesses being acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 Section 2(e), Section 1 (definition of "Sales Proceeds")]
- If Sellers fail to obtain specified consents, the U.S. Buyer may elect not to purchase certain assets related to the Sellers' Henderson, Nevada site and reduce the purchase price by $32,500,000 (the "Henderson Amount"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 Section 1 (definition of "Henderson Amount")]
- The Buyers made a $12,450,000 deposit toward the purchase price at execution, held under a Deposit Escrow Agreement with Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. as escrow agent; Huntsman Corporation as Guarantor guaranteed payment of the purchase price and certain other payment obligations of the Buyers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 Section 2(e)(ii)]
- Stalking-horse protection: if the Bankruptcy Court approves an alternative transaction (including a reorganization), the Sellers would pay the Buyers a break-up fee of $12,450,000 (three percent of the Sales Proceeds) plus reimbursement of up to $3,000,000 of transaction-related expenses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 Section 7 (Break-Up Fee)]

## People / processes provisions (as structured)
- Buyers agreed to offer employment, effective as of the Closing Date, to substantially all of the Covered Employees (other than Seller Retained Employees) at substantially the same base salaries or hourly wages and with substantially similar bonus opportunities and benefit-plan coverage in the aggregate; Buyers were under no obligation to assume any defined benefit pension plan or post-retirement welfare benefits unless required by law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 Section 6(g)]
- The Excluded Assets, Excluded Liabilities and Retained Intercompany Balances were carved out and expressly not assumed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 Section 1 (definition of "Excluded Assets"); Item 1.01]

## Bridging / remedy arrangement over a shared substrate
- As an antitrust remedy contingency, to the extent required to resolve objections of a Governmental Entity under applicable Competition/Investment Law, the parties agreed to divest their interest in Louisiana Pigment Company, L.P. ("LPC") and to enter into reasonable long-term supply agreements or tolling agreements for the Buyers'/Guarantor's portion of the titanium dioxide produced by LPC with an independent third party, with information-barrier protections. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K EX-10.1 Section 5(b)]

## Closing conditions
- Closing was subject to termination or expiration of applicable waiting periods under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, Bankruptcy Court approval (entry of a Sale Order), and absence of a Material Adverse Effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01; EX-10.1 Section 1 (definition of "Sale Order")]

## Structural summary (as structured)
- The transaction was structured as a distressed-asset (and equity) acquisition: Huntsman subsidiaries would purchase, out of Tronox Incorporated's Chapter 11 estate under sections 105/363/365 of the Bankruptcy Code, substantially all assets of Tronox's titanium dioxide and electrolytics businesses plus related working capital, the equity of three foreign Target Companies, and a 50% Tiwest JV interest, for $415 million (less a possible $32.5 million Henderson adjustment) plus assumption of certain operating liabilities, with Huntsman Corporation guaranteeing payment; the Buyers were the "stalking horse" subject to a Bankruptcy Court auction, with a $12.45 million deposit, a $12.45 million break-up fee and up to $3.0 million expense reimbursement as bid protection, and a commitment to offer employment to substantially all Covered Employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-052428, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-10.1 (preamble/recitals, Sections 2(e), 6(g), 7)]
