# Structural sub-dossier — General Cable Corporation acquisition by Prysmian S.p.A. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- General Cable Corporation, a Delaware corporation headquartered in Highland Heights,
  Kentucky, was "a global leader in the development, design, manufacture, marketing and
  distribution of copper, aluminum and fiber optic wire and cable products," also engaged
  in design/integration/installation of high and extra-high voltage terrestrial and
  submarine systems. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A]
- Prysmian S.p.A. (Republic of Italy, shares listed on Borsa Italiana under "PRY")
  entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated December 3, 2017 to acquire General
  Cable, using Alisea Corp. — a Delaware corporation and wholly owned Prysmian subsidiary
  formed solely for the transaction — as Merger Sub. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A]
- Alisea Corp. merged with and into General Cable; General Cable survived as the surviving
  corporation and became a wholly owned subsidiary of Prysmian. Stockholders approved the
  merger agreement, and it closed and became effective June 6, 2018 upon filing the
  certificate of merger with the Delaware Secretary of State. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Item 2.01/Introductory Note]

## Consideration and closing-date financial mechanics
- Each share of common stock converted into the right to receive $30.00 cash (an
  approximately 81% premium to the pre-announcement price); aggregate consideration to
  stockholders was approximately $1.5 billion in cash, funded from Prysmian's existing
  cash plus new indebtedness under binding financing commitments of €2.435 billion not
  conditioned on financing availability. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Item 2.01]
- At closing, General Cable's existing Credit Agreement was repaid and terminated, its
  5.750% Senior Notes due 2022 were called for redemption, a First Supplemental Indenture
  was executed on its Subordinated Convertible Notes due 2029, and outstanding equity
  awards were cancelled and converted into cash-equivalent rights tied to the $30.00
  consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Items 1.01/1.02/3.03]

## What transferred as the legal/operating entity
- The transaction was a whole-entity merger: the surviving corporation is General Cable
  itself, not a subset of its assets, carrying its pre-existing subsidiaries into single
  ownership by Prysmian; its certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and
  restated in their entirety at the Effective Time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Items 5.01/5.03]
- The merger agreement committed Parent to keep continuing employees (outside collective
  bargaining agreements) at no-less-favorable base pay/benefits through the first
  anniversary of closing, target bonus opportunities through December 31, 2018, and
  severance no less favorable than the Company's existing plans through the second
  anniversary of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A]

## Governance and management at closing
- At the Effective Time, all seven then-serving directors (John E. Welsh III, Sallie B.
  Bailey, Edward Childs Hall III, Gregory E. Lawton, Michael T. McDonnell, Craig P.
  Omtvedt, Patrick M. Prevost) and six named executive officers (including President/CEO
  Michael T. McDonnell, Matti M. Masanovich, Shruti Singhal, Roberto A. Sacasa, Emerson C.
  Moser, Leah S. Stark) ceased serving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Item 5.02]
- Prysmian, as sole stockholder, elected three new directors and appointed new officers of
  the surviving corporation, including Massimo Battaini as President/CEO and Fabio
  Bocchio as VP/Treasurer/CFO, while two prior administrative officers (Ramon J. Ceron,
  Ryan Green) continued in officer roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Item 5.02]

## Public-market status at closing
- General Cable common stock had traded on the NYSE under "BGC." At closing the Company
  notified the NYSE and requested a Form 25 Notification of Removal from Listing and/or
  Registration; trading was suspended ~9:00 a.m. EST June 6, 2018, and Prysmian stated
  its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Item 3.01]
- Closing was conditioned on customary terms including stockholder approval, antitrust
  clearance (including under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Act), and continued effectiveness of
  the Company's pre-existing DOJ non-prosecution agreement dated December 22, 2016; a
  termination fee of $47 million was payable by General Cable under specified
  circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The deal was a cash merger of the entire General Cable corporate entity into a wholly
  owned Prysmian subsidiary, with General Cable surviving as that subsidiary: legal
  vehicle, subsidiaries, and customer-facing wire/cable product business all crossed
  together at the June 6, 2018 closing, while the Company's entire pre-existing board and
  named-executive-officer slate was concurrently replaced by Prysmian-appointed directors
  and officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011306, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185029, 8-K Items 5.01/5.02]
