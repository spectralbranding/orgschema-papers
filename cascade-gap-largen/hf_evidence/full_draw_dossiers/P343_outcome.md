## Closing and deregistration (feasibility-relevant)
- The merger of Beneficial Bancorp, Inc. into WSFS Financial Corporation (with the
  simultaneous merger of Beneficial Bank into WSFS Bank) was completed on March 1,
  2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-011959, 8-K Item 2.01]
- Upon completion, Beneficial's common stock was withdrawn from quotation on the Nasdaq
  Global Select Market as of close of business February 28, 2019, and price quotations
  for Beneficial common stock ceased to be available in the public market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-011959, 8-K Item 3.01]
- On March 11, 2019, a Form 15 was filed on behalf of Beneficial Bancorp, Inc.
  (executed by WSFS Financial Corporation "as successor to Beneficial Bancorp, Inc."),
  certifying termination of registration of Beneficial's common stock under Section
  12(g)/12(h) of the Exchange Act, with the approximate number of holders of record
  listed as "None." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-013839, Form 15-12B]
- No further periodic reports (10-K, 10-Q) were filed under Beneficial Bancorp, Inc.'s
  own SEC registrant record (CIK 0001615418) after its final FY2018 Form 10-K filed
  February 26, 2019; the entity's EDGAR filing history ends with the March 2019
  deregistration filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-013839, Form 15-12B]

## Surviving registrant's subsequent public disclosure of the acquired business
- WSFS Financial Corporation (CIK 0000828944), the surviving corporation, continued to
  file its own periodic reports after the merger, including Form 10-K for fiscal year
  2021 (filed 2022) and fiscal year 2022 (filed 2023). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004404, 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005573, 10-K FY2022]
- In both the FY2021 and FY2022 Form 10-K, WSFS discloses only two reportable operating
  segments for goodwill-impairment and segment-reporting purposes — "WSFS Bank" and
  "Wealth Management" — with goodwill presented on a consolidated/segment basis; neither
  filing presents a separate reporting line, segment, or disclosed financial result
  specifically isolating the former Beneficial Bank franchise. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004404, 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005573, 10-K FY2022]
- In both years, WSFS states that, based on its qualitative assessment, no goodwill
  impairment existed for the year then ended (December 31, 2021; December 31, 2022),
  reported for the consolidated "WSFS Bank" and "Wealth Management" reporting units as a
  whole rather than for Beneficial specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004404, 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005573, 10-K FY2022]
- The only remaining references to "Beneficial Bank" by name in the FY2021 and FY2022
  Form 10-K are administrative/legacy items — a Beneficial Bank other-postretirement
  benefit plan discount-rate disclosure and exhibit-index references to legacy
  Beneficial Bank compensation-plan documents (e.g., a Supplemental Pension and
  Retirement Plan, a Stock-Based Deferral Plan, and an Elective Deferred Compensation
  Plan) originally filed in 2019 — not a disclosed post-close operating or financial
  trajectory for the acquired franchise. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004404, 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005573, 10-K FY2022]
