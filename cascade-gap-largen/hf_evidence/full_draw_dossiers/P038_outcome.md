  filed thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-040013, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-067566, Form 10-K]

## Realized disposition of the separated entity (best-documented scalar)
- On May 22, 2012, Google Inc. completed the acquisition of Motorola Mobility: Merger Sub
  merged into Motorola Mobility, which became a wholly owned subsidiary of Google; each
  outstanding common share (with customary exceptions) was cancelled and converted into the
  right to receive $40.00 in cash, for a total purchase price of approximately
  $12.9 billion, financed with cash on hand. [SRC: SEC accession 0000903423-12-000298, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the closing, the common shares were delisted: on May 22, 2012 the NYSE
  filed a Form 25 to remove the shares from listing, and the registrant stated it would file
  a Form 15 to deregister and suspend reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0000903423-12-000298, Form 8-K]

## Documented change at the organization/management interface at absorption
- Upon the merger, the entire pre-existing board resigned (including Dr. Sanjay K. Jha,
  Anthony J. Vinciquerra, Jon E. Barfield, Jeanne P. Jackson, Keith A. Meister, Daniel A.
  Ninivaggi, Thomas J. Meredith, James R. Stengel, and Dr. Andrew J. Viterbi), and the
  Merger Sub directors (Donald Harrison, Patrick Pichette, Kent Walker) became the
  directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0000903423-12-000298, Form 8-K]
- Immediately following the effective time, Dennis Woodside was appointed CEO, President and
  Secretary and Vanessa Wittman was appointed CFO and Treasurer — i.e., the senior
  management that had run the business was replaced by acquirer-installed leadership.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000903423-12-000298, Form 8-K]

## Within-window product-business divestiture (~2014)
- On January 29, 2014, Google Inc. entered an Acquisition Agreement with Lenovo Group
  Limited to sell Google's Motorola Mobility mobile-devices business, including certain
  related assets, IP rights and contracts, for total consideration of $660 million in cash,
  $750 million in Lenovo ordinary shares, and a $1.5 billion promissory note payable at the
  third anniversary of closing; Google retained the vast majority of the patent portfolio,
  licensed back to the business for its continued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001288776-14-000008, Form 8-K (Google Inc.)]
- On October 30, 2014, Lenovo announced it had completed the acquisition of Motorola
  Mobility from Google for a total purchase price of approximately US$2.91 billion at close
  (approximately US$660 million in cash and 519,107,215 newly issued Lenovo shares valued at
  approximately US$750 million), adding a company with nearly 3,500 employees.
  [SRC: https://news.lenovo.com/pressroom/press-releases/lenovo-completes-full-acquisition-motorola-mobility-from-google/]
