## Public-reporting continuity and identity within the outcome window
- The registrant continued to file annual reports on Form 10-K under the same CIK; its financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2013 are captioned "Columbia Property Trust, Inc. (formerly Wells Real Estate Investment Trust II, Inc.) and subsidiaries." [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- The FY2013 annual report is filed by "COLUMBIA PROPERTY TRUST, INC." with common stock registered on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 cover page]

## Realized change at the operating/organization interface (self-management transition)
- "From inception through February 27, 2013, we operated as an externally advised REIT" under an advisory agreement pursuant to which a subsidiary of Wells Real Estate Funds ("WREF"), Columbia Property Trust Advisory Services, LLC, performed key functions on the company's behalf. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Item 1]
- "On February 28, 2013, we became a self-managed company by terminating the above-mentioned advisory agreement and property management agreement, and acquiring Columbia Property Trust Advisory Services" and the affiliated property manager. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Item 1]
- In preparation for listing, "we transitioned to a self-managed platform in February 2013 by acquiring, or hiring, the employees necessary to perform the functions previously performed by our external advisor and property manager upon termination" of those agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Item 1]
- The company disclosed limited experience operating in the new configuration: "We recently transitioned to a self-managed REIT and have limited operating experience being self-managed," and that "excluding the effect of the eliminated asset management fees, our direct overhead, on a consolidated basis, increased as a result of becoming self-managed." [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Item 1A, Risk Factors]

## Realized change in listing status and control
- "On October 10, 2013, Columbia Property Trust, Inc. ('Columbia Property Trust') listed its shares on the New York Stock Exchange (the 'NYSE') under the ticker symbol 'CXP.'" Prior to October 10, 2013, none of the company's common stock was listed on a national securities exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, Note 1 Organization; Item 5]
- On that same date the company commenced a modified "Dutch-auction" tender offer for its shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Item 1]

## Realized financial and portfolio record (2013)
- Net income attributable to Columbia Property Trust for the year ended December 31, 2013 was $15.7 million, or $0.12 per share (basic and diluted); total revenues for 2013 were $526,578 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Selected Financial Data / MD&A, Net Income]
- Portfolio dispositions were substantial: on November 5, 2013 the company closed the "18 Property Sale" to an unaffiliated third party for $521.5 million (exclusive of closing costs); during 2013 it sold 19 properties for net proceeds of $565.9 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Note 12, Dispositions]
- In connection with the disposition the company "recognized aggregate impairment losses of $29.7 million in 2013" related to the properties sold. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 MD&A / Note 12]
- On March 21, 2013 the company closed on the sale of the Dvintsev Business Center — Tower B building in Moscow, Russia, and its related holding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Note 12, Dispositions]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the 2012-2014 window the registrant remained a public reporting company under a changed name (Columbia Property Trust, Inc., formerly Wells Real Estate Investment Trust II, Inc.), terminated its external advisory and property-management agreements and became self-managed on February 28, 2013 by acquiring the former advisor entities and hiring the necessary employees, and listed on the NYSE under ticker "CXP" on October 10, 2013; the realized 2013 record shows net income attributable to the company of $15.7 million on total revenues of $526,578 thousand, alongside large-scale dispositions (19 properties for $565.9 million net proceeds, including an 18-property $521.5 million sale) that carried aggregate impairment losses of $29.7 million, and management disclosed limited experience and increased direct overhead operating on the new self-managed platform. [SRC: SEC accession 0001252849-14-000036, 10-K FY2013 Items 1, 1A, 5, MD&A, Notes 1 and 12]
