# Structural sub-dossier — Validus Holdings, Ltd. acquisition by American International Group, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured and what became of that structure through the deal window
> (2018). It contains no information about the entity's subsequent operating or
> financial performance.

## Transaction and parties
- On January 21, 2018, Validus Holdings, Ltd. (a Bermuda exempted company, "Validus")
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with American International Group, Inc.
  ("AIG") and Venus Holdings Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of AIG ("Merger Sub"),
  under which Merger Sub would merge with and into Validus, with Validus surviving as a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of AIG, pursuant to the merger agreement and a statutory
  merger agreement required under Section 105 of the Bermuda Companies Act 1981. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Validus was described as "a leading global provider of reinsurance, insurance, and
  asset management services," operating through four diversified operating companies:
  Validus Reinsurance, Ltd. (global treaty reinsurance); Talbot Underwriting Ltd. (a
  specialty (re)insurance group in the Lloyd's market via Syndicate 1183); Western World
  Insurance Group, Inc. (U.S. specialty and excess and surplus lines); and AlphaCat
  Managers, Ltd. (a Bermuda-based investment advisor managing third-party and Validus
  capital in insurance-linked securities and property catastrophe/specialty reinsurance
  investments). [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Parties to the Merger"]
- Each outstanding common share (other than treasury/AIG-owned/dissenting shares) would
  be converted into the right to receive $68.00 in cash without interest — a
  whole-company, all-cash acquisition by a strategic (non-financial-sponsor) acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The $68.00 merger consideration represented a 45.6% premium to the closing price of
  common shares on January 19, 2018, the last full trading day before announcement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Questions and Answers"]
- Total funds needed to complete the merger were anticipated at approximately $5.6
  billion; AIG informed Validus it expected to use cash on hand to fund the aggregate
  merger consideration, and the merger was not conditioned on receipt of financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Financing"]
- Validus's outstanding 5.875% non-cumulative preferred shares, series A, and 5.800%
  non-cumulative preferred shares, series B, would continue as preferred shares of the
  surviving company with unchanged rights, terms and conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A cover letter]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity was the subject of the transaction — Merger
  Sub merged with and into Validus under the Bermuda statutory merger procedure, with
  Validus continuing as the surviving company and a wholly-owned subsidiary of AIG; this
  was not a carve-out of a business unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Board of directors: per the merger agreement, the directors of Merger Sub immediately
  prior to the effective time would become the initial directors of the surviving
  company, while the officers of Validus immediately prior to the effective time would
  become the initial officers of the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Board of Directors and Management of Validus Following Completion of the Merger"]
- Workforce/compensation: for a continuation period running from the effective time
  through the end of the following calendar year, AIG agreed to provide continuing
  employees base salary/wage no less than pre-close, target incentive-compensation
  opportunity "substantially similar" in the aggregate to pre-close, other compensation
  and benefits no less favorable in the year of closing and "substantially comparable"
  thereafter, and severance no less favorable than similarly situated AIG employees;
  AIG also agreed to honor and continue Validus's existing employment, severance,
  retention, termination and change-in-control arrangements as in effect at the
  effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Employee Matters"]
- Pre-signing, on January 17, 2018, AIG representatives contacted Kean Driscoll,
  Validus's President and Global Head of Reinsurance, to discuss terms of an ongoing
  employment arrangement with AIG in advance of any announcement; the parties (per
  discussion between Validus's then-CEO and Peter Zaffino, AIG's General Insurance CEO)
  agreed to delay such employment discussions with Validus personnel so as not to
  interfere with finalizing and announcing the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- The proxy disclosed, among directors'/officers' interests in the merger, a transaction
  bonus of $2,850,961 to a Validus executive officer vesting and payable upon closing, a
  separate equity award valued at $1,608,317, and "new and potential employment and
  retention arrangements between certain executive officers of Validus and AIG." [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Interests of Certain Persons in the Merger"]
- Indemnification and D&O insurance for Validus's then-current officers and directors
  were contractually continued for a six-year tail period following completion of the
  transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Interests of Certain Persons in the Merger"]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a shared
  operating substrate was disclosed in the proxy statement; the structure was a direct
  whole-company merger rather than a separation requiring such a bridge. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A]

## Regulatory process and deal protections
- Consummation required expiration/termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period,
  clearance or expiration of relevant waiting periods under the EU Merger Regulation,
  and approvals from the New Hampshire Department of Insurance, the Bermuda Monetary
  Authority, the UK Prudential Regulation Authority, the UK Financial Conduct Authority,
  Lloyd's of London, the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority, and other
  jurisdictions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]
- If Validus made an adverse recommendation change, AIG could terminate the agreement
  and Validus would owe a termination fee of $162 million (approximately 3.0% of the
  estimated aggregate merger consideration), or a reduced $81 million fee under certain
  specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A "Termination Fee"]

## Consummation and immediate post-signing events
- The merger closed July 18, 2018: Merger Sub merged with and into Validus pursuant to a
  statutory merger agreement dated July 18, 2018, with Validus continuing as the
  surviving corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of AIG; a change in control of the
  registrant was reported as occurring on that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 5.01]
- At the effective time, all twelve of Validus's pre-merger directors (Edward J. Noonan,
  Mahmoud Abdallah, Michael E. A. Carpenter, Matthew J. Grayson, Jeffrey Greenberg,
  Karin Hirtler-Garvey, John J. Hendrickson, Jean-Marie Nessi, Mandakini Puri, Gail Ross,
  Therese M. Vaughan and Christopher Watson) ceased to be directors, and the three
  directors of Merger Sub (Charles Fry, Elias Habayeb, David Friston) became the
  Company's directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The employment of the Company's Chief Executive Officer (Edward J. Noonan), Executive
  Vice President and Chief Financial Officer (Jeffrey D. Sangster), and Executive Vice
  President and General Counsel (Robert Kuzloski) was reported as terminating, with each
  ceasing to be an officer of the Company within a 31-day window following the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- On the closing date, the Company notified the NYSE of completion of the Merger and
  requested that trading in the common shares be withdrawn from listing; the NYSE filed
  a Form 25 notification of removal from listing and trading in the common shares was
  suspended before the opening of trading on July 18, 2018. The Company stated its
  intent to file a Form 15 to deregister the common shares. The Series A and Series B
  Preference Shares (depositary shares) continued to be listed on the NYSE and
  registered under the Exchange Act as of the filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K Items 3.01, 3.03]
- In connection with closing, AIG was added as a guarantor under the Company's existing
  secured letter-of-credit facility agreement (amended), and separate credit agreements
  with Barclays Bank PLC and HSBC Bank USA were repaid and their associated obligations
  terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 1.02]
- The Company filed a Form 15-12B on July 30, 2018 to terminate registration of its
  Common Shares ($0.175 par value) under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, reporting
  an approximate number of holders of record of "One" as of that date; the Company filed
  two further Form 15-12B notices, on August 16, 2018 and November 13, 2018, addressing
  its other registered securities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-040820, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-045153, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-058953, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A strategic acquirer (AIG) acquired the entire corporate entity of Validus Holdings,
  Ltd. in an all-cash, whole-company merger ($68.00/share, ~$5.6 billion, funded from
  cash on hand with no financing condition), with the merged entity's four operating
  insurance/reinsurance businesses (Validus Re, Talbot, Western World, AlphaCat)
  transferring intact as the acquired corporate vehicle. The legal vehicle and workforce
  (under contractual continuation terms) crossed the ownership boundary together, but
  governance and top leadership did not: all twelve incumbent directors and the
  Company's CEO, CFO and General Counsel departed at or within a short window of
  closing, replaced by AIG-designated directors, while retention discussions with at
  least one other senior executive were held separately and deliberately delayed until
  after signing. The common shares ceased to trade on the NYSE and were promptly
  deregistered following closing; no transitional-services or shared-services agreement
  bridging a shared operating substrate was disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000019, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000410, Form 8-K]
