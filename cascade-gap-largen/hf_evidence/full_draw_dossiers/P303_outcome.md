## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- Reynolds American Inc. (RAI) filed its final annual report on Form 10-K on
  2017-02-09 (for fiscal year 2016); no Form 10-K was filed by RAI thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-001245, Form 10-K]
- RAI filed a Form 15-12B on 2017-08-04 to terminate the registration of its common
  stock under the Securities Exchange Act; consequently no subsequent free-standing,
  audited public financial statements exist for Reynolds American Inc. as an
  independent registrant in the ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-248557, Form 15-12B]

## Separate disclosure of the acquired business within the acquirer's later filing
- The acquirer, British American Tobacco p.l.c. (BAT, CIK 1303523), a foreign private
  issuer, files annual reports on Form 20-F; its Form 20-F for fiscal year 2020, filed
  2021-03-09, falls within the ~3-5-year post-closing window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- That Form 20-F states that financial statements and information provided "with
  respect to the US business or RAI (and/or RAI and its subsidiaries (collectively, the
  'Reynolds Group'))" are prepared on a US GAAP basis and constitute "the primary
  financial statements or financial information of the US business or RAI," converted
  to IFRS solely for consolidation into the BAT Group's results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- Numerous RAI-related legal entities are individually named in the Form 20-F's
  subsidiary listing, including R.J. Reynolds Tobacco Company, Lorillard, LLC, RAI
  Services Company, RAI International, Inc., and Reynolds American Inc. itself, several
  addressed at 401 North Main Street, Winston-Salem, NC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- The Form 20-F reports "United States" as a distinct geographic revenue segment, with
  revenue (current rates) of £11,473 million in 2020 versus £10,373 million in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- The Form 20-F's management listing names Guy Meldrum as "President and CEO (Reynolds
  American Inc.)" — a title distinct from the BAT Group's own chief executive role,
  addressed at the Winston-Salem, NC location. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]

## Documented items relevant to interface disruption or continuity
- The Form 20-F reports Group-level litigation charges "(mainly in the US)" of £487
  million in 2020, compared with £236 million in 2019; the passages read do not provide
  a Reynolds-Group-only breakout of this figure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- The Form 20-F's legal-proceedings disclosure references ongoing "Engle progeny"
  litigation (a recurring category of US tobacco litigation) multiple times as a
  continuing matter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
- The Form 20-F reports Group-level goodwill impairment charges of £209 million in
  2020 (2019: £172 million), stated as relating largely to Malaysia (2020) and
  Indonesia (2019) respectively; the passages read do not attribute any goodwill
  impairment to the Reynolds Group or the United States segment specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Reynolds American Inc. deregistered its common stock under the Exchange Act on
  2017-08-04 and filed no subsequent Form 10-K, so no independent, free-standing
  audited outcome record exists for the entity itself; the traceable realized record
  instead comes from the acquirer's Form 20-F for fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-03-09,
  within the ~3-5-year window), which separately names the Reynolds Group/RAI business,
  its subsidiaries, and its own "President and CEO," reports a "United States" segment
  revenue increase from £10,373 million (2019) to £11,473 million (2020), and discloses
  continuing US-related litigation charges and impairments at the BAT Group level
  without a Reynolds-Group-only breakout. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-248557, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-073992, Form 20-F (FY2020)]
