## Realized event within the outcome window (acquirer's primary disclosure)
- On October 15, 2018 Penn National Gaming, Inc. (NASDAQ: PENN) announced it "completed its
  previously announced acquisition of Pinnacle Entertainment, Inc. (PNK: Nasdaq) … as well as
  the related divestitures to Boyd Gaming Corporation … and the real estate transactions with
  Gaming and Leisure Properties, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061976, Penn National Gaming Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Following the acquisition the acquirer stated it would operate "40 facilities in 18
  jurisdictions," "more than 49,000 gaming machines, 1,200 table games and nearly 9,000 hotel
  rooms," and "more than 30,000 team members," absorbing the acquired operations into its
  consolidated regional-gaming portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061976, Penn National Gaming Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Press coverage corroborates the October 15, 2018 completion of the Penn National acquisition
  of Pinnacle. [SRC: https://www.gamingintelligence.com/finance/manda/41997-penn-national-gaming-completes-pinnacle-entertainment-acquisition/]
