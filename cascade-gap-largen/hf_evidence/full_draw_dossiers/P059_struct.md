# Structural sub-dossier — Costco Wholesale Corporation Mexico joint venture (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Costco Wholesale Corporation's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  August 28, 2011 (filed 2011-10-14); it is an annual report that DESCRIBES the company's
  50%-owned Mexico joint venture rather than being the venture-formation or buyout
  document. The venture agreement and the subsequent buyout terms are therefore sourced
  additionally from a contemporaneous exhibit and from the company's fiscal-2012 Form
  10-K, each cited separately. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Parties and transaction type
- The company describes operating "a 50% owned joint venture in Mexico (Mexico)," alongside
  its wholly and majority-owned operations elsewhere. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The governing vehicle is identified in the FY2011 exhibit index as the "Restated
  Corporate Joint Venture Agreement between The Price Company, Price Venture Mexico and
  Controladora Comercial Mexicana S.A. de C.V. dated March 1995" (incorporated by
  reference). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011) exhibit index]
- The joint venture partner is named in the FY2012 Form 10-K as Controladora Comercial
  Mexicana. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## What was inside the venture at the JV stage (tier level)
- Customer-facing product/asset: the venture entity ("Costco Mexico") operated 32
  membership warehouses in Mexico "similar to Costco warehouses operated elsewhere."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- People/organization: approximately 9,000 individuals were employed by Mexico at the end
  of the prior fiscal years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Management integration: an executive officer (Dennis R. Zook) served as Executive Vice
  President, Chief Operating Officer, Southwest and Mexico Divisions, indicating the Mexico
  operation was run within the company's divisional management structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Prior to fiscal 2011 the company accounted for its 50% interest under the equity method;
  its equity-method investment in Mexico was $357 (million) as of August 29, 2010, which
  was derecognized on the initial consolidation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Change to the control boundary at the JV stage
- Effective August 30, 2010 the company began consolidating the 50%-owned venture on a
  prospective basis (upon adopting a new accounting standard), recording the partner's 50%
  share as a noncontrolling interest of $357 in equity; the initial consolidation
  increased total assets, liabilities, and revenue by approximately 3% with no impact on
  net income. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- In December 2010 the company and its 50% joint venture partner amended the Mexico joint
  venture agreements; as a result the company "obtained, subject to certain continuing
  conditions, majority-voting control of the joint venture," which did not change the
  consolidated financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Buyout that converted the venture to wholly-owned (contemporaneous primary)
- On July 10, 2012 the company acquired the remaining 50% interest in Mexico from its
  joint venture partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Consideration: the company purchased its former partner Controladora Comercial
  Mexicana's 50% equity interest in Costco Mexico for $789 (million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Bridging cash mechanism: Mexico declared a cash dividend of $366, 50% payable to the
  company and 50% payable to the former joint venture partner; the company used the
  dividend proceeds together with existing cash and investment balances to fund the
  purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Legal instrument: the buyout was effected under a "Stock Purchase Agreement, dated June
  13, 2012, among Costco Venture Mexico, Controladora Comercial Mexicana, S.A.B. de C.V.
  and other parties named therein" (filed as an exhibit to the FY2012 10-K). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012) exhibit index]

## What did and did not move across the boundary at the buyout (tier level)
- The buyout transferred the partner's 50% equity interest in the existing venture entity;
  the operating substrate (the Mexico warehouses, workforce, and management) remained
  within that same operating entity rather than being reconstituted, as the company had
  already consolidated the operation before the equity purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The retrieved filings disclose no transfer of employees, management, or organization to
  or from a third party as part of the buyout; the transaction is described as an equity
  purchase of the remaining interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)] [UNVERIFIED: any personnel or operational restructuring accompanying the buyout]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The Mexico operation was held from March 1995 through a 50/50 corporate joint venture
  (originally The Price Company / Price Venture Mexico with Controladora Comercial
  Mexicana), consolidated by the company from August 30, 2010 with a December 2010
  amendment granting majority-voting control, and converted to wholly-owned on July 10,
  2012 when the company bought the remaining 50% equity interest for $789 (million) under a
  June 13, 2012 Stock Purchase Agreement, funded partly by a $366 venture dividend; across
  these steps the venture's warehouses, workforce, and divisional management remained
  within the same operating entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-271844, Form 10-K (FY2011)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-428890, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
