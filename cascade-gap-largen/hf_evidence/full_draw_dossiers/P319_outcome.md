# Outcome sub-dossier — CalAtlantic Group, Inc. business under Lennar Corporation ownership, ~3-5-year horizon (fixed window ~2021-2023)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. CalAtlantic was absorbed into its acquirer and
> deregistered; there is no subsequent CalAtlantic-specific 10-K, so this sub-dossier

## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- Following the Merger's completion, CalAtlantic Group, Inc. filed a Form 15-12B
  ("Certification and Notice of Termination of Registration") on February 26, 2018,
  certifying termination/suspension of its duty to file reports on its common stock and
  6.25% Senior Notes due 2021, and listing the approximate number of holders of record
  of common stock as 1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058264, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed under CalAtlantic's own CIK after
  that Form 15-12B; the entity's own EDGAR filing history ends there, so no
  CalAtlantic-specific audited financial statements exist for a ~2021-2023 outcome
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058264, Form 15-12B]

## What the acquirer's later Forms 10-K do and do not disclose about the acquired business
- Lennar Corporation's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November 30, 2021 (filed
  January 28, 2022) mentions the 2018 acquisition of CalAtlantic exactly once, in a
  single sentence within its history-of-acquisitions narrative in Item 1 Business:
  "In 2018, we acquired CalAtlantic Group, Inc. ('CalAtlantic'), a major homebuilder
  which was building homes across the homebuilding spectrum, from entry level to
  luxury, in 43 metropolitan statistical areas spanning 19 states, and providing
  mortgage, title and escrow services." No CalAtlantic-specific revenue, earnings, unit
  count, or other financial or operating scalar accompanies that sentence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 Business]
- Lennar's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November 30, 2022 (filed January 26,
  2023) contains the same single-sentence historical mention of the CalAtlantic
  acquisition, plus two further CalAtlantic references: one noting that goodwill was
  "recorded in connection with the 2018 acquisition of CalAtlantic" without a
  CalAtlantic-specific carrying value, and one noting that certain outstanding senior
  notes "were obligations of CalAtlantic when it was acquired, and were subsequently
  exchanged in part for notes of the Company." Neither reference attaches a
  CalAtlantic-specific revenue, earnings, or unit figure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 1 Business / Critical Accounting Policies / Notes to Financial Statements]
- Both the FY2021 and FY2022 Forms 10-K organize Lennar's homebuilding operations into
  geographic segments (Homebuilding East, Central, West, Houston and Other) rather than
  by acquired-company origin; no segment, product line, or geography in either filing is
  labeled as attributable specifically to the former CalAtlantic business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Lennar's FY2022 Form 10-K states that its annual goodwill impairment analysis,
  performed as of September 30, 2022 across its reporting units on a consolidated
  basis, resulted in no impairment recorded; this is a consolidated Lennar-wide
  determination not broken out by acquired business and so is not treated here as a
  CalAtlantic-specific outcome figure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, Critical Accounting Policies]

## Documented personnel continuity
- Lennar's FY2022 Form 10-K executive-officer disclosure states that Jeff J. McCall,
  listed as Lennar's Executive Vice President, "served as Executive Vice President and
  Chief Financial Officer of CalAtlantic Group, Inc., or a predecessor" from June 2011
  to February 2018 (the closing month), then served as Lennar's Senior Vice President
  from February 2018 to January 2020, and as Lennar's Executive Vice President from
  January 2020 onward — i.e., one of CalAtlantic's former named executive officers
  continued in progressively senior roles at the acquirer through the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, "Information about our Executive Officers"]
- No comparable continuity disclosure was located for CalAtlantic's former Chief
  Executive Officer, Larry Nicholson, or Executive Chairman, Scott Stowell, in either
  the FY2021 or FY2022 Form 10-K text reviewed. [UNVERIFIED]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because CalAtlantic Group, Inc. deregistered under the Exchange Act on February 26,
  2018 and filed no further periodic reports, there is no publicly available
  CalAtlantic-specific 3-5-year outcome record. The acquirer, Lennar Corporation,
  continued to report as a public company throughout the window, but its FY2021 and
  FY2022 Forms 10-K disclose the CalAtlantic acquisition only in brief historical
  references without any separate segment, revenue, earnings, or unit-level breakout
  for the acquired business, and organize homebuilding operations geographically rather
  than by acquired-company origin. Under the no-record protocol for a whole-company
  acquisition with no separately disclosed acquired-business trajectory, this case's
  outcome is coded **uncertain**: no CalAtlantic-specific financial or operating
  outcome can be sourced to a primary filing. The one documented realized fact that is
  CalAtlantic-specific is personnel continuity — a former CalAtlantic Executive Vice
  President and CFO held increasingly senior roles at Lennar through the window — which
  does not by itself establish a broader outcome trajectory for the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058264, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022]
