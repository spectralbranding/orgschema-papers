# Structural sub-dossier — Albany Molecular Research, Inc. merger with affiliates of The Carlyle Group and GTCR LLC (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Albany Molecular Research, Inc. ("AMRI"), a Delaware corporation, agreed to a merger
  under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 5, 2017 among AMRI, UIC Parent
  Corporation, a Delaware corporation ("Parent"), and UIC Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware
  corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent ("Merger Sub"); Parent and Merger
  Sub were formed by affiliates of The Carlyle Group and affiliates of GTCR LLC to
  facilitate the participation of investment funds advised by both firms in the
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties structured the transaction as a forward merger: Merger Sub merged with and
  into AMRI, with AMRI surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent; Parent and
  Merger Sub had not engaged in any business except activities incidental to their
  formation and the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of AMRI common stock were entitled to receive $21.75 in cash per share, without
  interest and less applicable withholding taxes; AMRI and Parent estimated the total
  funds required to complete the merger and related transactions at approximately $1.955
  billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- Each of GTCR and Carlyle committed, under equity commitment letters dated June 5, 2017,
  to capitalize Parent with an aggregate equity contribution of $995 million; the
  Investors/Guarantors also executed limited guarantees in favor of AMRI covering
  Parent's termination-fee and related obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed (effective) on August 31, 2017, pursuant to a Certificate of
  Merger filed with the Delaware Secretary of State. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
- On the closing date, the surviving company (successor by merger to Merger Sub) and
  certain subsidiaries entered into a First Lien Credit Agreement (a seven-year $655
  million term loan and a five-year $100 million multi-currency revolving facility) and
  a Second Lien Credit Agreement (an eight-year $205 million term loan), the proceeds of
  which funded a portion of the merger consideration and refinanced AMRI's prior credit
  agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]

## What AMRI comprised at closing (the acquired business/product tier)
- AMRI was described as "a leading global contract research and manufacturing
  organization providing customers fully integrated drug discovery, development, and
  manufacturing services," supplying services and technologies supporting pharmaceutical
  discovery and development, API and fine-chemical manufacture, and drug-product
  development and manufacture for new and generic drugs, plus analytical/testing
  services to the medical device and personal care industry, with locations in the
  United States, Europe, and Asia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the sale process, AMRI's board and management periodically reviewed long-term
  strategy including organic growth, possible acquisitions, and potential business
  combinations, and the company had outstanding convertible notes maturing in 2018 that
  would require refinancing or restructuring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- During the sale process one bidder's non-binding proposal expressly noted "opportunities
  for management to participate ... in the growth and success of" the company, while
  another bidder's counsel separately confirmed that an equity rollover by AMRI's
  management team or other stockholders was not a condition to its proposal and that it
  did not intend to discuss management's future compensation or transaction
  participation at that stage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]

## Management, employees, and governance at closing
- Pursuant to the merger agreement, the members of AMRI's board of directors resigned
  effective immediately prior to the effective time; the directors of Merger Sub
  immediately prior to the effective time continued as the board of directors of the
  surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
- The officers of AMRI immediately prior to the effective time continued as officers of
  the surviving company immediately following the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
- For 12 months after closing, Parent agreed to cause the surviving corporation to
  provide continuing employees with base salary/wage and cash-incentive target
  opportunities at least equal to pre-closing levels, substantially comparable employee
  benefits (excluding equity awards), and to honor AMRI's 2017 annual cash incentive
  bonus plan and existing severance agreements/policies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- AMRI's executive officers were parties to employment agreements providing for
  pro-rated bonus payments and severance benefits upon certain qualifying terminations in
  connection with or following the merger, with enhanced ("Change of Control") severance
  benefits if a qualifying termination occurred within two years of the closing; the
  proxy separately disclosed "golden parachute" compensation estimates for AMRI's named
  executive officers under Item 402(t) of Regulation S-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]
- In connection with the merger, William S. Marth, AMRI's President and Chief Executive
  Officer, elected to forfeit, for no consideration, a portion of his performance-based
  restricted stock unit awards that would otherwise have entitled him to cash on
  conversion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
- The Special Committee of AMRI's board that evaluated the merger consisted of David
  Deming (chairman), Kenneth Hagen, Kevin O'Connor, and Anthony Maddaluna; each received
  a one-time special bonus payment for the additional time commitment, not contingent on
  the merger closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- At the effective time, AMRI's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and
  restated in the form of Merger Sub's certificate of incorporation and bylaws, and AMRI
  became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent (a change of control of the registrant).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
- On the closing date, AMRI notified the Nasdaq Global Market of its intent to remove its
  common stock from listing and requested that Nasdaq file a Form 25 with the SEC to
  report the shares no longer listed; trading of AMRI common stock on Nasdaq was
  suspended as of the opening of trading that day, and AMRI stated its intent to
  separately file a Form 15 to terminate its Exchange Act registration and reporting
  obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company cash merger (AMRI's contract-research/
  contract-manufacturing business, valued at an estimated $1.955 billion in total
  transaction funds) into a newly formed Carlyle/GTCR acquisition vehicle, funded by a
  combined $995 million equity commitment from the two sponsors and approximately $960
  million of new secured term/revolving debt, with AMRI's pre-existing officer group
  retained in place immediately after closing and its pre-existing board replaced by the
  acquisition vehicle's designees; common stock was removed from Nasdaq trading and
  registration termination was announced as of the closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-036873, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-046115, Form 8-K]
