## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- MainSource Financial Group filed a Form 15-12G (deregistering its common stock under
  Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act) and a Form 15-15D (suspending its duty to file
  reports under Section 15(d)) on 2018-04-13, consistent with the merger's completion.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-023900, Form 15-12G]; [SRC: SEC accession
  0001104659-18-023902, Form 15-15D]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed by MainSource after that date; the
  surviving corporation, First Financial Bancorp., continued as the SEC-reporting entity.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020, filed
  2021-02-19)]
- In its fiscal-year-2020 Form 10-K, First Financial states that the company is "managed
  and financial performance is evaluated on a consolidated basis," and that "all of the
  Company's operations are considered by management to be aggregated in one reportable
  operating segment." No segment-level or business-unit-level disclosure isolates the
  post-merger performance of the business acquired in this transaction. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K]
- Because the acquired entity does not itself report post-closing and the surviving
  corporation discloses results only on a consolidated, single-segment basis, no
  separately disclosed 3-5-year financial trajectory exists in the public record for the
  specific business acquired in this transaction; the consolidated results of the
  surviving corporation are not used here as a substitute for a business-specific record.

## What is separately traceable in the surviving corporation's later filings
- Three named individuals identified in the closing-time filing as continuing officers of
  the acquired company are listed as executive officers of the surviving corporation in
  its fiscal-year-2020 Form 10-K, roughly three years after the merger closed: Archie M.
  Brown (President and Chief Executive Officer, appointed to that role April 1, 2018
  following the acquisition), James M. Anderson (Executive Vice President and Chief
  Financial Officer since the same date), and Karen B. Woods (Executive Vice President,
  General Counsel, and Chief Risk Officer, who joined in April 2018 following the merger).
  Claude E. Davis (the pre-merger CEO of the surviving corporation) is listed as Chairman
  of the Board. [SRC: SEC accession 0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K]
- As of fiscal year 2020, the surviving corporation reports operating 63 banking centers
  in Ohio, three in Illinois, 63 in Indiana, and 14 in Kentucky — the same four states in
  which the acquired bank's branch network was located at closing — though this is a
  combined, consolidated figure for the surviving corporation and is not disaggregated by
  legacy franchise. [SRC: SEC accession 0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the acquired entity deregistered under the Exchange Act shortly after closing
  and the surviving corporation reports its results only on a consolidated, single-segment
  basis with no disclosed business-unit breakout, there is no public 3-5-year financial
  outcome record specific to the acquired business. The traceable realized record is
  limited to reporting-status facts and named-individual continuity: the acquired
  company's chief executive, chief financial officer, and general counsel are each
  reported, roughly three years after closing, as continuing to hold senior executive
  roles at the surviving corporation, and the surviving corporation's branch network still
  spans the acquired company's four-state footprint. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K]
