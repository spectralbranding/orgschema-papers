# Structural sub-dossier — Verso Corporation acquisition of NewPage Holdings Inc. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn accession (0001193125-16-440657) is a Form 8-K/A whose definitional recitals
  confirm the corporate ownership chain established by the acquisition: NewPage Holdings
  Inc. is a direct, wholly owned subsidiary of Verso Paper Holdings LLC; NewPage
  Investment Company LLC ("NewPage LLC") is a direct subsidiary of NewPage Holdings; and
  NewPage Corporation is a direct subsidiary of NewPage LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-440657, Form 8-K/A definitional recitals]
- Because the drawn 8-K/A is not itself the deal-terms document, the closing structure
  below is sourced from the contemporaneous closing-era primary filing (the January 7,
  2015 Form 8-K reporting completion of the acquisition), each fact with its own tag.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 7, 2015 (the "Closing Date"), Verso Corporation, formerly named Verso Paper
  Corp., consummated its acquisition of NewPage Holdings Inc. through the merger of Verso
  Merger Sub Inc., an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Verso, with and into NewPage;
  Merger Sub's separate corporate existence ceased and NewPage continued as the surviving
  corporation as an indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Verso. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01/2.01]
- The acquisition was consummated pursuant to the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated as of
  January 3, 2014, among Verso, Merger Sub and NewPage; that Merger Agreement was filed as
  an exhibit to Verso's Form 8-K filed January 6, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-002326, Form 8-K]
- Both parties were coated-paper producers; NewPage was acquired and retained intact as a
  distinct, wholly owned subsidiary corporation rather than dissolved into the acquirer at
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Legal vehicle / value-capture: partial merger consideration to NewPage stockholders took
  the form of $650 million aggregate principal amount of 11.75% Senior Secured Notes due
  2019, issued by Verso Paper Holdings LLC and Verso Paper Inc. under a new indenture, plus
  NewPage's addition as a guarantor under Verso's existing credit facilities, secured notes
  and adjustable subordinated notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Controlling intent / control: as a result of the acquisition, Verso Paper Management LP
  (a controlled affiliate of Apollo Management Holdings GP, LLC), which owned approximately
  68% of Verso common stock immediately before, owned approximately 44% immediately after,
  and no longer owned a majority. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- People / organization: Verso's board was increased from 9 to 10 directors and Robert M.
  Amen, a director of NewPage up to the acquisition, was elected to fill the new seat; the
  same day Verso's CEO (David J. Paterson) and CFO (Robert P. Mundy) were elected directors
  of Verso Holdings after removal of its existing directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Product crossing without its producing/delivering processes moving whole
- At closing Verso, NewPage and NewPage Corporation entered into a Shared Services
  Agreement under which Verso "may provide or cause to be provided to NewPage certain
  services from and after the closing," described as "a broad array of potential services,
  including operating and 'back office' or corporate-type services," with payment monthly
  and quarterly true-ups at an all-in cost basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Shared Services Agreement]
- Under that agreement, synergy-implementation costs were allocated one-third to Verso and
  two-thirds to NewPage, 100% of realized synergies and cost-savings inured to Verso, a
  Steering Committee equally represented by each was to meet at least quarterly to
  determine services and cost, and the agreement had an initial three-year term with
  automatic one-year renewals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Shared Services Agreement]
- The acquired coated-paper product operations therefore crossed the boundary while the
  operating and back-office processes serving NewPage were bridged by this contractual
  arrangement (with NewPage retained as a separate corporate entity) rather than merged
  into the acquirer's operations at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Shared Services Agreement]

## Assets removed from the perimeter at closing
- Immediately prior to and in connection with the acquisition, NewPage subsidiaries
  (NewPage Corporation, NewPage Wisconsin System Inc., and Rumford Paper Company) completed
  the divestiture of the paper mill in Biron, Wisconsin and the pulp-and-paper mill in
  Rumford, Maine to Catalyst Paper Operations Inc.; the divestiture was undertaken pursuant
  to a settlement with the U.S. Department of Justice that enabled the acquisition to
  proceed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Item 2.01 Divestiture of Certain Mill Assets]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A coated-paper producer was acquired whole and retained as a distinct wholly owned
  subsidiary; its product operations crossed the boundary while its operating and
  back-office processes were bridged by a three-year Shared Services Agreement and a
  guarantor/indenture structure, and two producing mills were carved out to a third party
  under a DOJ antitrust settlement as a condition of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-004117, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.01/5.01]
