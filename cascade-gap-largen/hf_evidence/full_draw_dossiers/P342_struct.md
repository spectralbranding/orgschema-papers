# Structural sub-dossier — OneBeacon Insurance Group, Ltd. acquisition by Intact Financial Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- OneBeacon Insurance Group, Ltd. is a Bermuda exempted limited liability company with
  U.S.-based underwriting operating subsidiaries that are property and casualty insurance
  writers, and a Bermuda-based reinsurance company subsidiary, Split Rock Insurance, Ltd.;
  it offers a wide range of specialty insurance products and services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- OneBeacon, Intact Financial Corporation (a Canadian corporation, "Intact"), Intact
  Bermuda Holdings Ltd. ("Holdco," an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Intact), and
  Intact Acquisition Co. Ltd. ("Merger Sub," a Bermuda company formed April 27, 2017
  solely to effect the transaction and a wholly owned subsidiary of Holdco) entered into
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub would merge with and into OneBeacon, with
  OneBeacon surviving as a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Intact; Merger Sub would
  cease to exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- Each OneBeacon Class A and Class B common share would be converted into the right to
  receive $18.10 in cash, without interest, less applicable withholding taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The whole of OneBeacon — its Bermuda holding structure, U.S. underwriting operating
  subsidiaries, and the Bermuda reinsurance subsidiary Split Rock Insurance, Ltd. —
  crossed into Intact's ownership as a single going-concern legal and operating entity;
  the merger structure did not carve out or separate the customer-facing insurance
  product lines from the underwriting/claims processes or from the organization that runs
  them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- OneBeacon's majority shareholder, White Mountains Insurance Group, Ltd. ("White
  Mountains"), and its wholly owned subsidiaries Lone Tree Holdings Ltd. and Bridge
  Holdings (Bermuda) Ltd. (together, the "WTM shareholders"), entered into a voting
  agreement with Intact obligating them to vote their OneBeacon shares in favor of the
  merger, subject to limited exceptions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- Retention arrangements for certain executives (including a named officer, Mr. Miller)
  provided for retention bonuses vesting in stages contingent on the closing of the
  merger and the executive's continued employment through specified vesting dates,
  bridging management continuity across the ownership change. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements governing the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Concurrently with the closing, OneBeacon and White Mountains agreed to enter into a
  transition services agreement (superseding a prior 2006 agreement) under which, for a
  limited period after closing, White Mountains subsidiaries and OneBeacon would provide
  or continue to provide each other certain services related to software, employee
  benefits, payroll, business insurance policies, payment agents, and insurance
  consulting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- Concurrently with the closing, OneBeacon and White Mountains Advisors LLC (a White
  Mountains subsidiary) agreed to enter into an arrangement under which White Mountains
  Advisors LLC would continue to provide investment management services to OneBeacon
  through January 31, 2018 or an earlier date to be determined by the parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]

## Closing conditions and consummation
- The merger was subject to shareholder approval, expiration/termination of the
  Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, and receipt of required insurance-regulatory and
  antitrust approvals in applicable jurisdictions; OneBeacon would owe Intact a
  termination fee of $85.1 million and expense reimbursement up to $22.0 million in
  certain termination circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
- On September 25, 2017, OneBeacon announced that all regulatory approvals required to
  complete the merger had been obtained. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-17-001319, Form 8-K dated 2017-09-25]
- On September 28, 2017, in connection with the consummation of the merger, OneBeacon
  dismissed PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP as its independent registered public accounting
  firm, reflecting completion of the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-17-001343, Form 8-K dated 2017-09-28]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a cash merger in which OneBeacon, as a whole
  going-concern insurance holding company (product lines, underwriting/claims processes,
  and organization together), became a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of Intact for
  $18.10 per share; the shared substrate bridged at closing was limited to former-parent
  (White Mountains) corporate services — transition services and interim investment
  management — rather than any separation of OneBeacon's own product/process/people
  structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003896, DEFM14A]
