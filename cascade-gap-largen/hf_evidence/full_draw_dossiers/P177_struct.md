# Structural sub-dossier — Pinnacle Foods Inc. acquisition by Conagra Brands, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 26, 2018, Pinnacle Foods Inc. ("Pinnacle") entered into a merger agreement
  with Conagra Brands, Inc. ("Conagra") and Patriot Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a
  Delaware corporation and direct wholly owned subsidiary of Conagra formed on June 22,
  2018 solely to effect the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a merger of Merger Sub with and into Pinnacle, with
  Pinnacle surviving the merger as a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Conagra — i.e. the
  acquisition of the whole going-concern business by means of a reverse triangular
  merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed on October 26, 2018 (the "Closing Date"); at the effective
  time the separate corporate existence of Merger Sub ceased and Pinnacle Foods became a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of Conagra. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-308612, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-26]
- Each issued and outstanding share of Pinnacle common stock was converted into the
  right to receive $43.11 in cash and 0.6494 shares of Conagra common stock (the "merger
  consideration"); Conagra issued approximately 77.4 million shares of its common stock
  to Pinnacle stockholders, who were expected to hold approximately 16.5% of Conagra's
  outstanding shares immediately after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]

## What the acquired entity comprised at closing
- Pinnacle is described as a manufacturer, marketer and distributor of branded food
  products in North America, with products found in over 85% of U.S. households, sold
  through supermarkets, grocery wholesalers/distributors, mass merchandisers,
  convenience and dollar stores, e-commerce, and military/foodservice channels in the
  U.S. and Canada. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- Pinnacle's business was organized into four reportable segments at closing: the Frozen
  segment, the Grocery segment, the Boulder segment, and the Specialty segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- Pinnacle Foods Inc. was itself a holding company whose sole asset was 100% ownership of
  Peak Finance Holdings LLC, which in turn wholly owned Pinnacle Foods Finance LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and post-merger officers/directors of the surviving entity
- Under the merger agreement, the directors of Merger Sub at the effective time became
  the directors of the surviving corporation (Pinnacle), and the officers of Merger Sub
  immediately prior to the effective time continued as the officers of the surviving
  corporation, in each case until successors were duly elected/appointed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A, Article III of the merger agreement annex]
- The certificate of incorporation of the surviving corporation was amended and restated
  in its entirety at the effective time as set forth in the merger agreement's charter
  exhibit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]

## Documented uncertainty about post-closing integration and retention (stated in the drawn filing)
- The filing's risk factors state that Conagra's and Pinnacle's success depended in part
  on the ability to retain key management personnel and other key employees, and that
  current and prospective employees of both companies "may experience uncertainty about
  their roles within Conagra following the merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- The filing states Conagra expected to incur one-time cash costs of approximately $355
  million to achieve anticipated cost synergies, and identifies "a large number of
  processes, policies, procedures, operations, technologies and systems" — including
  purchasing, accounting and finance, sales, payroll, pricing and benefits — that would
  need to be integrated post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
- The filing does not describe a transitional-services, shared-services, or
  supply-bridging agreement separating a retained parent substrate from the acquired
  business; the transaction as structured transferred the whole operating business (all
  four segments) into Conagra at closing, consistent with a full going-concern
  acquisition rather than a partial carve-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction transferred the entire Pinnacle operating business — its four
  reportable segments, branded product portfolio, and underlying corporate legal
  entities — to Conagra as a wholly owned subsidiary via a reverse triangular merger,
  with cash-plus-stock consideration; the surviving corporation's post-closing officers
  and directors were, by the merger agreement's mechanics, those of the acquirer's
  merger vehicle rather than a continuation automatically specified for Pinnacle's
  pre-merger executive slate, while the filing itself flags employee-retention and
  process-integration as unresolved risks at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-275423, DEFM14A]
