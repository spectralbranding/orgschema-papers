## Financial trajectory (fiscal 2016-2019, as restated in the FY2019 10-K)
- Net sales rose from $22,919 million (fiscal 2016) to $24,147 million (2017), $24,175
  million (2018), and $25,939 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012,
  Form 10-K for fiscal year ended 2019-12-28, Selected Financial Data]
- Net income over the same span was $210 million (2016), $444 million (2017), $407
  million (2018), and $385 million (2019) — a declining trend in net income across fiscal
  2017-2019 despite rising net sales. [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K,
  Selected Financial Data]
- No goodwill or indefinite-lived intangible asset impairment was recorded: the fiscal
  2019 annual impairment assessment (performed as of June 30, 2019, the first day of the
  third fiscal quarter) concluded "with no impairments noted," and management stated it
  was "more likely than not that the fair value of goodwill exceeded its carrying value."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K, Note on Goodwill and Other
  Intangible Assets]

## Continued acquisition activity within the window
- On September 13, 2019, the company's operating subsidiary USF completed a $1.8 billion
  all-cash acquisition of five foodservice companies (the "Food Group") from Services
  Group of America, Inc. — Food Services of America, Inc., Systems Services of America,
  Inc., Amerifresh, Inc., Ameristar Meats, Inc., and GAMPAC Express, Inc. — expanding the
  company's network in the western/northwestern U.S. and financed with a new $1.5 billion
  incremental senior secured term loan plus revolving-credit borrowings. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K, Note 5 Business Acquisitions]
- As a condition of Federal Trade Commission clearance for the acquisition, USF divested
  three of the acquired Food Group distribution facilities (the "Divested Assets") on
  October 11, 2019, for $94 million in proceeds plus approximately $20 million in holdback
  funds and working-capital adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form
  10-K, Note 5 Business Acquisitions]
- The filing states that "[n]o effect has been given to potential synergies, operating
  efficiencies or costs arising from the integration of the Food Group" in the reported
  pro forma financial information — i.e., integration costs/benefits had not yet been
  separately quantified as of this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form
  10-K, Note 5 Business Acquisitions]

## Organizational continuity
- Pietro Satriano continued as Chief Executive Officer and director through this filing
  (in the role since July 2015); he was additionally elected Chairman of the Board of
  Directors in December 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K, Item 1
  Information about our Executive Officers]
- The company continued to report its business in a single business/reportable segment,
  described as marketing and distributing fresh, frozen, and dry food and non-food
  products to foodservice customers throughout the U.S. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K, Note 25 Business Information / Business Description]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly three to four years of the entity's 2016 registration-era filing, the
  publicly reporting entity showed rising net sales but a declining year-over-year net
  income trend (2017-2019), no goodwill/intangible impairment, continuation of the
  company's long-standing acquisition-driven growth pattern (a further $1.8 billion
  debt-financed acquisition in September 2019, subject to an FTC-mandated partial
  divestiture), unquantified integration synergies/costs as of the filing, and continuity
  of both the single-segment operating structure and the CEO in place since 2015. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001665918-20-000012, Form 10-K]
