## Public-reporting status within the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant's last public annual report is the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2016, filed 2017-03-10. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The registrant filed a Form 15-12B on 2017-07-18 to terminate the registration of its
  ordinary shares under the Exchange Act; no standalone public Form 10-K was filed for
  fiscal years 2017, 2018 or 2019, so there is no primary audited public financial record
  for the later part of the ~2017-2019 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-045469, Form 15-12B]

## Best-documented realized outcome scalars (from the in-window 10-K)
- For fiscal 2016 the Company reported operating revenues of $636 million, a net loss of
  $338 million (a loss of $3.87 per diluted share), and a pre-tax impairment charge of
  $130 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The Company reported a net loss of $999,643 thousand ($1.0 billion) for 2015 and a net
  loss of $646,746 thousand for 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The financial statements were prepared with a disclosure of substantial doubt about the
  Company's ability to continue as a going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Chapter 11 and reorganization trajectory (from the in-window 10-K)
- On February 12, 2016 the Debtors entered into a plan support agreement relating to a
  plan of reorganization; on February 14, 2016 they commenced their chapter 11 cases by
  filing voluntary petitions for relief in the Bankruptcy Court, after which the Company
  began trading on the OTC market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Certain subsidiaries (the "Non-Filing entities") were not party to the chapter 11
  filing and continued to operate in the ordinary course. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- On February 7, 2017 the Company filed a new plan of reorganization ("New Plan"). It
  provided for, among other things, elimination of approximately $2.4 billion of existing
  debt in exchange for a combination of cash, debt and new equity, and stated that
  "existing shareholders of the Company will not receive a recovery under the New Plan."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Documented interface disruption over time (from the in-window 10-K)
- At the process/asset level, a portion of the drilling fleet was cold stacked, and the
  filing noted that "our cold stacked rigs will require significant capital investment
  prior to re-entering service"; the FY2016 fleet was described as including 34 jackups
  and additional units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Residual entanglement with the former parent persisted: tax audit claims in Mexico
  assessed against Noble were "subject to indemnity by us as a result of the Spin-Off,"
  which the Company contested or intended to contest, including through litigation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2017-2019 window the sole in-window public annual report (FY2016 Form
  10-K, filed 2017-03-10) documents a distressed realized record: a $338 million FY2016
  net loss on $636 million revenue, a $130 million impairment charge, going-concern doubt,
  a February 2016 chapter 11 filing, and a February 2017 New Plan that would eliminate
  ~$2.4 billion of debt with existing shareholders receiving no recovery; the Company then
  deregistered (Form 15-12B, 2017-07-18), so no primary public financial record exists for
  the remainder of the window (2018-2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001594590-17-000030, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-045469, Form 15-12B]
