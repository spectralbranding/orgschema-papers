# Structural sub-dossier — SunPower / AU Optronics solar-cell manufacturing joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties as structured
- On May 27, 2010, SunPower Technology, Ltd. ("SPTL"), an indirect Cayman Islands subsidiary of SunPower Corporation (a Delaware corporation), and SunPower Malaysia Manufacturing SDN.BHD (a wholly-owned Malaysian subsidiary of SPTL, the "JVC") entered into a Joint Venture Agreement with AU Optronics Singapore Pte. Ltd. ("AUO") and its ultimate parent AU Optronics Corporation of Taiwan ("AUO Taiwan"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction is a jointly owned manufacturing venture: SPTL and AUO will each hold a 50% equity ownership interest in the JVC and jointly own the GW-scale Fab 3 facility then under construction in Malaysia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Fab 3 is described as SunPower's 1.4 gigawatt third solar-cell fabrication facility, under construction in Malaysia; SunPower is characterized as a manufacturer of high-efficiency solar cells, panels and systems and AUO as a global manufacturer of TFT-LCD displays based in Taiwan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- Closing was subject to customary conditions (including governmental and third-party consents) and expected during the third quarter of 2010; the agreement would terminate automatically if Closing did not occur by September 30, 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product: the JVC will manufacture and sell solar cells, selling them on a "cost-plus" basis to SunPower Systems Sarl ("SPSW," a Switzerland subsidiary of SPTL) and to AUO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Producing processes are contributed rather than transferred whole: the venture is to deploy SunPower's solar-cell technology and process know-how together with AUO's manufacturing expertise to install and operate Fab 3. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Intellectual property is licensed into the venture, not owned outright by it: SPTL and AUO each license to the JVC, on a non-exclusive, royalty-free basis, certain background IP — solar-cell manufacturing IP (SPTL) and manufacturing processes (AUO); upon dissolution IP primarily relating to AUO's field of use reverts to AUO and all other IP to SPTL. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Controlling intent / governance is shared 50/50: the JVC has a six-person board with SPTL and AUO each nominating three directors; certain fundamental actions require holders of at least 75% of shares; the agreement provides a deadlock-resolution mechanism and default remedies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Operating leadership and finance leadership are split across the two contributors: AUO has the right to nominate the Chief Executive Officer of the JVC and SPTL has the right to nominate the Chief Financial Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Capital vehicle: at Closing SPTL and AUO each subscribe for JVC shares of ~$350 million par value and each contribute cash totaling ~$350 million in the aggregate over time; each may be required to add up to $50 million more if further equity financing is requested. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Interface observations (neutral, sourced)
- The party contributing the solar-cell technology and process know-how (SPTL) is not the party that nominates the operating chief executive of the manufacturing entity (AUO nominates the CEO; SPTL nominates the CFO), so the cell-technology contributor and the operating-management nominator are different parties at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The customer-facing product (solar cells) will be produced by an entity that neither ultimate parent controls unilaterally, with the underlying cell technology licensed into that entity rather than owned by it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Seven-year supply agreements: at Closing SPSW and AUO each enter a seven-year supply agreement (renewable) committing to purchase JVC-manufactured solar cells; the JVC's annual output allocated to SPSW moves from 95% (FY2010) to 80% (FY2013 and after) and to AUO from 5% (FY2010) to 20% (FY2013 and after). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Licensing and joint-development agreements govern improvements and step-modifications, with approved SPTL step modifications triggering a joint development program among the parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Financing bridges: SPTL agreed the JVC will retain the existing Malaysian Government loan for Fab 3 of RM1.0 billion, and AUO agreed to arrange (or, on an interim basis, provide) additional third-party debt financing for the JVC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Prior to Closing the JVC must assume a portion of SunPower's existing polysilicon purchase obligations, or enter a commercial arrangement to purchase a portion of them on the same terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Geographic sales restrictions and a ten-year first-look provision constrain AUO's supply and its pursuit of crystalline-cell production above 20% efficiency outside the JVC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction forms a 50/50 jointly owned Malaysian manufacturing entity to produce a customer-facing product (solar cells) using cell technology and process know-how contributed and licensed by one party (SunPower/SPTL) and manufacturing expertise plus operating-CEO nomination held by the other (AUO), with the two owners' commercial relationship to the entity bridged at closing by cost-plus seven-year supply agreements, royalty-free cross-licenses, a shared board, and allocated debt (Malaysian Government loan retained; third-party debt to be arranged by AUO). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-10-129898, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
