## Transaction and parties
- Silver Run Acquisition Corporation II ("Silver Run") was "a blank check company formed for the purpose of effecting a merger, capital stock exchange, asset acquisition, stock purchase, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses," listed on NASDAQ under "SRUN"/"SRUNW"/"SRUNU," with 129,375,000 shares of Class A/Class B Common Stock outstanding (103,500,000 public shares, 25,875,000 founder shares) held ahead of the vote on the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A]
- Under three Contribution Agreements each dated August 16, 2017, Silver Run agreed to acquire: (i) from High Mesa Holdings, LP ("Alta Mesa Contributor"), all limited-partner interests in Alta Mesa Holdings, LP ("Alta Mesa") and 100% of the economic / 90% of the voting interests in Alta Mesa Holdings GP, LLC; (ii) from KFM Holdco, LLC ("Kingfisher Contributor"), 100% of the outstanding membership interests in Kingfisher Midstream, LLC ("Kingfisher"); and (iii) from Riverstone VI Alta Mesa Holdings, L.P. ("Riverstone Contributor"), the balance of the limited-partner interests in Alta Mesa. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Business Combination Proposal"]
- Alta Mesa, founded in 1987, was described as "an independent exploration and production company" holding ~130,000 net acres in the STACK play (Anadarko Basin, Oklahoma); Kingfisher, formed in 2015, was "engaged in the acquisition, development and operation of midstream oil and gas assets" (gathering, processing, marketing) with a network including ~308 miles of pipelines and a 60 MMcf/d cryogenic gas processing plant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Summary of the Proxy Statement"]
- The board's 80%-asset-test analysis implied a combined enterprise value of approximately $3.55 billion for Alta Mesa and Kingfisher (approximately $2.75 billion of equity consideration plus approximately $800 million of cash consideration to the Kingfisher Contributor plus assumed closing indebtedness). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "The Business Combination Proposal"]
- The transaction was structured as an "Up-C": the Contributors received Class C Common Stock paired with common units of a newly formed pass-through partnership, SRII Opco, LP, redeemable in the future for Class A Common Stock, "to (i) provide the owners of Alta Mesa with interests in a pass-through entity that would defer their tax liability ... and (ii) provide Silver Run with a step up in tax basis." Alta Mesa and Kingfisher were structured as separate wholly owned subsidiaries of SRII Opco. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Background of the Business Combination"]
- Upon Closing, Silver Run intended to (and did) change its name to "Alta Mesa Resources, Inc." and continue its Class A Common Stock and warrant listing on NASDAQ under new symbols "AMR" and "AMRRW." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A cover letter]
- On February 9, 2018 (the "Closing Date"), Alta Mesa Resources, Inc. (formerly Silver Run Acquisition Corporation II) "consummated the previously announced acquisition" of the Alta Mesa and Kingfisher interests under the three Contribution Agreements; a wholly owned subsidiary, SRII Opco GP, LLC, became sole general partner of SRII Opco, LP, and the registrant thereafter operated its business through SRII Opco and its subsidiaries, including Alta Mesa and Kingfisher. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038241, Form 8-K "Introductory Note"]

## People / management tier — what crossed with the acquired businesses
- Messrs. Ellis, Chappelle, McCabe and Murrell (Alta Mesa's named executive officers) were "parties to employment agreements with Alta Mesa Services, L.P.," a wholly owned Alta Mesa subsidiary, that pre-dated the transaction (amended and restated March 25, 2014) and set minimum base salaries and bonus terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Executive Compensation of Alta Mesa"]
- "After completion of the business combination, James T. Hackett will serve as Chairman of the board of directors of Silver Run and Chief Operating Officer—Midstream, while Harlan H. Chappelle, Michael E. Ellis, and Michael A. McCabe will serve as Chief Executive Officer, Chief Operating Officer—Upstream, and Chief Financial Officer, respectively, under substantially the same terms as are provided in their current employment agreements with Alta Mesa Services or under the terms of new employment agreements to be entered into in connection with the Closing." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Compensation of Executive Officers and Directors after the Business Combination"]
- "Kingfisher has no employees. Under the Operator Agreement, ARM operates, maintains and administers Kingfisher's operations, and ARM also provides management services to Kingfisher." ARM (an affiliate of one of the three co-owners of the Kingfisher Contributor) exclusively dedicated certain of its own employees to Kingfisher and was reimbursed for their fully burdened cost; Kingfisher itself incurred no direct executive-officer compensation cost. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Business of Kingfisher" / "Compensation Committee Interlocks and Insider Participation"]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the operational substrate at closing
- "At the Closing, the Kingfisher Contributor will terminate effective upon the Closing the Operator Agreement with no further liability to Kingfisher, as provided in the Transition Services Agreement described below." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Kingfisher Contribution Agreement — Conduct of Kingfisher's Business"]
- "Upon the Closing, Kingfisher will enter into an operating transition services agreement (the 'Transition Services Agreement') with ARM. Under the Transition Services Agreement, during the six-month period following the Closing, ARM will provide certain operational services with respect to certain gas gathering and processing systems," with ARM liable only for its own gross negligence, willful misconduct or fraud, and subject to customary audit rights and monthly reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A "Agreements Related to the Kingfisher Contribution Agreement — Transition Services Agreement"]
- On the Closing Date, Alta Mesa (as a subsidiary of the combined company) separately entered into an amended and restated senior secured revolving credit facility with Wells Fargo Bank, National Association as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038241, Form 8-K "Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A NASDAQ-listed blank-check company with no pre-existing product, operating process, or employee base combined, via an Up-C structure, with two operating businesses under common upstream ownership (E&P and midstream) whose customer-facing product lines and business models moved into the new structure along with each business's existing organizational tier: Alta Mesa's executive team continued under its pre-existing (or newly negotiated) employment agreements, while Kingfisher — which itself employed no one and whose operations and management had been performed by a third-party operator (ARM) under an Operator Agreement — had that agreement terminated at Closing and its operational continuity bridged, not by an internalized workforce transfer, but by a fixed six-month Transition Services Agreement with the same third-party operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-014904, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038241, Form 8-K]
