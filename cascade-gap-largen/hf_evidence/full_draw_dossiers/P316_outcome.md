## Realized financial record (fiscal year 2021)
- For fiscal year 2021, the successor company reported total revenues of $366,106 thousand and net earnings attributable to shareholders of $35,478 thousand ($111.83 per equivalent Class A share), compared with a net loss of $37,989 thousand the prior fiscal year and net earnings of $45,380 thousand the fiscal year before that. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Net earnings attributable to shareholders for fiscal year 2021 were disaggregated as: restaurant operations $11,235 thousand, insurance $11,290 thousand, oil and gas $7,528 thousand, brand licensing $2,364 thousand, interest expense $(841) thousand, and corporate and other $(9,829) thousand (total operating businesses $21,747 thousand), plus investment partnership gains of $8,899 thousand and investment gains of $4,832 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]
- At fiscal year-end 2021, total assets were $894,807 thousand and total shareholders' equity was $587,696 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- The company employed 2,633 persons as of December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]

## Realized operating record — restaurant segment
- As of December 31, 2021, the restaurant subsidiaries operated a combined 577 units; the larger chain had 199 company-operated restaurants (of which 42 were closed, with the company stating an intent to refranchise a majority of them), 159 franchise-partner units, and 178 traditional franchise units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]
- The Form 10-K attributes elevated asset impairments in fiscal year 2020 primarily to "dining room closures and uncertainties caused by the pandemic," with asset impairments decreasing by $19,011 thousand in fiscal year 2021 compared with fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001726173-22-000004, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]
