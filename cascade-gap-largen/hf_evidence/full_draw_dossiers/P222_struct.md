# Structural sub-dossier — CYS Investments, Inc. merger with Two Harbors Investment Corp. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Two Harbors Investment Corp. ("Two Harbors"), Eiger Merger Subsidiary LLC ("Merger
  Sub," a newly formed Maryland limited liability company that had conducted no
  activities other than those incidental to its formation), and CYS Investments, Inc.
  ("CYS") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 25, 2018 (the "Merger
  Agreement"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub merged with
  and into CYS, with CYS continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming an
  indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Two Harbors. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- CYS common stockholders received merger consideration consisting of Two Harbors common
  stock (determined by a formula dividing CYS's adjusted book value per share,
  multiplied by 96.75%, by Two Harbors' adjusted book value per share, multiplied by
  94.20%) plus a pro rata share of a $15,000,000 cash pool; CYS Series A and Series B
  preferred stock were each converted into newly classified Two Harbors Series D and
  Series E preferred stock, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form
  DEFM14A]
- CYS common stockholders were expected to own approximately 30% of the combined
  company's fully diluted equity, with Two Harbors stockholders owning approximately
  70%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger closed on July 31, 2018: Merger Sub merged with and into CYS at 12:00 p.m.
  Eastern Time, with CYS continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming an
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Two Harbors. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-232852, Form 8-K dated 2018-07-31]
- The combined company retained the name "Two Harbors Investment Corp." and continued
  trading on the NYSE under the symbol "TWO." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715,
  Form DEFM14A]

## Business model / product tier at closing
- Two Harbors described itself as a REIT focused on investing in, financing and
  managing Agency residential mortgage-backed securities (Agency RMBS), non-Agency
  securities, mortgage servicing rights (MSR), and other financial assets. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- CYS was described as a specialty finance company investing, on a leveraged basis, in
  Agency RMBS (residential mortgage pass-through securities guaranteed by Fannie Mae,
  Freddie Mac, or Ginnie Mae) and related instruments (CMOs, U.S. Treasury securities,
  U.S. Agency debentures, and credit-risk-transfer securities). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- The combined company was described as continuing to invest in, finance, and manage
  Agency RMBS, non-Agency securities, MSR, and other financial assets, i.e., the same
  asset-class business model as Two Harbors pre-merger. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and manager tier at closing
- Following completion of the Merger, the combined company continued to be externally
  managed by PRCM Advisers LLC ("PRCM Advisers"), a subsidiary of Pine River Capital
  Management L.P. — the same external manager Two Harbors used pre-merger; the DEFM14A
  contains no comparable external-manager description for CYS. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- In connection with the Merger, PRCM Advisers agreed (via a "Fourth Amendment to the
  Management Agreement") to reduce its base management fee on the additional equity
  under management resulting from the Merger from 1.5% to 0.75% of stockholders' equity
  through the first anniversary of closing, plus a one-time $15,000,000 downward fee
  adjustment for the quarter of closing and up to a further $3.3 million adjustment for
  transaction-related expenses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent / governance / management (people) tier at closing
- At the effective time, the directors and officers of Merger Sub (rather than any
  continuing CYS directors or officers) became the directors and officers of the
  surviving corporation (CYS as a subsidiary of Two Harbors); each member of CYS's board
  (Kevin E. Grant, Tanya S. Beder, Dale A. Reiss, Karen Hammond, Raymond A.
  Redlingshafer, and James A. Stern) ceased to be a director of CYS, and CYS's four
  named officers (Kevin E. Grant, Jack DeCicco, Thomas A. Rosenbloom, and Richard E.
  Cleary) each ceased to be an officer of CYS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715,
  Form DEFM14A; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-232852, Form 8-K]
- Two of CYS's former independent directors, James A. Stern and Karen Hammond, were
  appointed to the Two Harbors board upon closing, with the Two Harbors board size
  increased by two members to accommodate them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715,
  Form DEFM14A]
- CYS maintained individual employment agreements with four named executive officers
  (Grant, DeCicco, Cleary, Rosenbloom) providing lump-sum severance (2.5x for Grant, 1.0x
  for the others, times average base salary plus bonus), pro rata bonus, and benefit
  continuation upon a qualifying termination not for cause; the CYS board approved an
  amendment to these agreements, in connection with signing the Merger Agreement, to
  clarify severance payment mechanics and timing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]
- The pro forma combined-company disclosure noted that net book value per share did not
  reflect approximately $9.0 million of compensation-related expenses (described as
  "non-executive severance, retention, etc.") expected to be recognized after closing in
  accordance with GAAP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On July 31, 2018, CYS notified the NYSE that its common stock, Series A preferred
  stock, and Series B preferred stock (each cancelled and converted into Two Harbors
  securities) should be removed from listing, and requested NYSE file a Form 25
  notification of removal from listing with respect to all three securities; CYS's
  8-K states this removal would terminate registration of those securities under
  Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act, and that CYS "intends to file a Form 15" to
  terminate registration under Section 12(g) and suspend its Section 13/15(d) reporting
  obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-232852, Form 8-K]
- The NYSE filed Form 25 notifications removing CYS common stock, Series A preferred
  stock, and Series B preferred stock from listing on 2018-08-01 (three separate Form
  25/25-NSE filings under CYS's CIK). [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001396446&type=25&owner=include&count=40]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined CYS whole (as a going-concern, same-asset-class REIT) into
  Two Harbors by reverse triangular merger, with CYS's own governance/management tier
  (its full board and four named executive officers) replaced at the effective time by
  Merger Sub's directors/officers, two former CYS independent directors added to the
  parent board, and the combined entity's external-manager relationship (PRCM Advisers)
  continued and only fee-adjusted rather than restructured. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004715, Form DEFM14A; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-232852, Form 8-K]
