## The November 2016 separation into two public companies
- Effective 5:00 p.m. New York time on November 30, 2016, the Company completed the
  separation of its business into two independent publicly traded companies through
  the spin-off of International Seaways, Inc. ("INSW"); the Company retained the U.S.
  Flag business and INSW received the entities and assets/liabilities that had formed
  the Company's former International Flag business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-013619, Form 10-K, Item 1]
- The spin-off was effected as a pro rata distribution of INSW common stock to holders
  of the Company's common stock and warrants of record as of November 18, 2016 (0.3333
  INSW shares per Company share held), completed pursuant to a Separation and
  Distribution Agreement together with a Transition Services Agreement and an Employee
  Matters Agreement between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-013619, Form 10-K, Item 1]
- As a result of the spin-off, the Company distributed $895,650 thousand in net assets
  of INSW, recorded as a reduction to additional paid-in capital and accumulated other
  comprehensive loss. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-013619, Form 10-K, Item 1]
- Because of the spin-off, results of INSW's operations and its assets and liabilities
  were reported as discontinued operations for all periods presented in the Company's
  subsequent annual reports; a held-for-sale basis charge of $332,562 thousand was
  recognized for the excess of the carrying value of the INSW disposal group over its
  fair value, included within the results of discontinued operations for fiscal year
  2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Notes to Financial Statements, Note 5]

## Realized financial trajectory of the continuing (U.S. Flag) business
- Selected financial data reported in the fiscal-2017 10-K show income/(loss) from
  continuing operations of $80,565 thousand for 2015, $(1,059) thousand for 2016, and
  $55,978 thousand for 2017, on shipping revenues of $466,872 thousand (2015), $462,420
  thousand (2016), and $390,426 thousand (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Selected Financial Data]
- The fiscal-2018 10-K reports income from continuing operations of $13,489 thousand
  for 2018 on shipping revenues of $366,163 thousand, continuing the year-over-year
  revenue decline (2017: $390,426 thousand; 2016: $462,420 thousand). [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-19-000020, Form 10-K, Selected Financial Data]
- Total equity of continuing operations was reported at $1,580,488 thousand for 2015
  (prior to the spin-off distribution), $254,332 thousand for 2016, $313,238 thousand
  for 2017, and $329,468 thousand for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Selected Financial Data] [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-19-000020, Form 10-K, Selected Financial Data]

## Documented vessel-level impairment and disposal activity, continuing operations
- The fiscal-2017 10-K discloses vessel impairment charges of $97,782 thousand
  (including $3,873 thousand recorded as a reduction in deferred drydock costs)
  recognized as of September 30, 2016 on seven rebuilt articulated tug-barges, and a
  further $6,623 thousand impairment charge recorded in December 2016 on one additional
  rebuilt articulated tug-barge, based on discounted-cash-flow tests below carrying
  value. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Notes to Financial Statements, Note 10]
- The fiscal-2017 10-K discloses a further vessel impairment charge of $5,878 thousand
  recorded in the fourth quarter of 2017, writing down the carrying values of four
  rebuilt articulated tug-barges to their estimated fair values, following
  discounted-cash-flow tests showing carrying values in excess of undiscounted future
  cash flows for seven of the Company's rebuilt tug-barges as of year-end 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Notes to Financial Statements, Note 10]
- At December 31, 2017 the Company's owned vessel fleet had a weighted average age of
  10.1 years, following disposal(s) of vessels including one sale that produced a
  recognized loss of $7,322 thousand within the year. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021, Form 10-K, Notes to Financial Statements, Note 6]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- In the roughly two-plus years following the August 2014 emergence, the reorganized
  entity split into two separately traded companies (November 2016), retaining the
  U.S. Flag (Jones Act) business while distributing the International Flag business
  and recognizing a $332,562 thousand held-for-sale impairment charge on the
  distributed disposal group; over the following fiscal-2017 and fiscal-2018 reporting
  years the retained continuing business recorded declining shipping revenues
  ($462,420 thousand in 2016 to $366,163 thousand in 2018), swung from a loss from
  continuing operations of $(1,059) thousand in 2016 to income of $55,978 thousand in
  2017 and $13,489 thousand in 2018, and recognized several further vessel-specific
  impairment charges on its articulated tug-barge fleet in both 2016 and 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-013619] [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-18-000021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-19-000020]
