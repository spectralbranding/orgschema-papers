# Structural sub-dossier — Schweitzer-Mauduit International, Inc. acquisition of DelStar, Inc. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K under Item 7.01 (Regulation FD Disclosure): on
  November 18, 2013 the Company issued a press release announcing it had entered into a
  definitive agreement to acquire DelStar, Inc. for a purchase price of $231.5 million in
  cash (subject to customary post-closing adjustments), and it furnished the investor-call
  slides as Exhibit 99.1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000116, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The drawn filing announces the signed agreement; it does not itself report a completed
  transaction. The closing structure below is sourced from the Company's contemporaneous
  closing-era primaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000116, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On December 12, 2013 the Company completed the acquisition of DelStar, Inc. (a Delaware
  corporation) through a merger of SWM Acquisition Corp. II (a Delaware corporation and
  indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company) with and into DelStar, pursuant to an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger dated November 18, 2013 among the Company, DelStar, SWM
  Acquisition Corp. I, SWM Acquisition Corp. II, certain DelStar security holders, and
  American Capital, Ltd. (the seller). [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- As a result of the merger DelStar became a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of the
  Company; the purchase price to acquire DelStar and its subsidiaries was $231.5 million
  in cash, subject to customary post-closing adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The purchase price was funded from borrowings under the Company's Revolving Credit
  Facility; on December 11, 2013 the Company drew $235 million on that facility to fund in
  part the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Items 2.01 and 2.03]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Controlling interest: 100% ownership and control of DelStar and its subsidiaries crossed
  to the Company via the reverse-triangular merger; a Delaware holding company owns 100% of
  DelStar, Inc., which in turn directly owns 100% of DelStar Holding Corp. and further
  operating entities (including U.S. Netting Inc., Delstar Technologies Inc., and two
  Chinese operating entities such as DelStar Technologies (Shanghai) Trading Corp.).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Customer-facing product/asset: DelStar manufactures thermoplastic nets, nonwovens,
  laminates, and extruded components (plastic and metal), used across filtration,
  automotive, healthcare, industrial, food, electronics, and textiles; the products carry
  established trade names including DELSTAR, NALTEX, DELNET, DELPORE, STRATEX and CORETEC.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Operating processes: DelStar was acquired as an operating going concern; it is
  headquartered in Middletown, Delaware, and its manufacturing operations and subsidiaries
  transferred inside the acquired corporate vehicle rather than as a stand-alone asset
  purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
- People/organization: the acquired DelStar facilities are non-union; because the merger
  transferred the DelStar legal entities as a whole, the workforce and management running
  those operations moved with the vehicle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Segment placement: at the closing-era annual report the Company reported three segments
  based on product lines — Paper, Reconstituted Tobacco, and Filtration — with the
  DelStar-branded business constituting the newly established Filtration segment.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Stated rationale and contractual arrangements at closing
- The Company described the transaction within its diversification initiatives to diversify
  and expand its product portfolio beyond its historical paper and tobacco-related lines.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Concurrent with closing the Company entered an Amended and Restated Credit Agreement
  (December 11, 2013) providing a $500 million senior unsecured revolving credit facility
  maturing December 11, 2018 (replacing a prior $225 million facility), used in part to fund
  the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The material agreements filed with the closing 8-K are the Agreement and Plan of Merger
  (Exhibit 2.1) and the Amended and Restated Credit Agreement (Exhibit 10.1); the closing
  8-K describes no transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement with the seller
  (consistent with a whole-company going-concern acquisition from a financial owner).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 9.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The Company acquired the entire DelStar corporate group for $231.5 million in cash via a
  reverse-triangular merger funded by a revolving-credit draw, transferring DelStar's
  customer-facing resin-based product lines, its manufacturing processes, and the workforce
  and management running them together inside one going-concern vehicle, and placing that
  business as the Company's newly established Filtration segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-13-000124, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-14-000035, Form 10-K FY2013]
