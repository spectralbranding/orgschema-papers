# Outcome sub-dossier — Sun Bancorp, Inc. realized record after merger into OceanFirst Financial Corp., ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Because Sun Bancorp's banking charter and
> holding-company registrant were absorbed into the acquirer at closing, no
> Sun-specific post-closing 10-K exists; this sub-dossier traces what, if anything,
> the surviving registrant's own periodic filings separately disclose about the
> acquired Sun business in the 2021-2023 window. This sub-dossier contains no
> information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Completion and public-reporting status after the transaction
- The first-step merger and second-step merger described in the June 30, 2017 merger
  agreement were completed effective January 31, 2018, with OceanFirst Financial Corp.
  continuing as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038064, Form 15-12G footnote]
- On January 31, 2018, The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC filed a Form 25-NSE with respect to
  Sun Bancorp, Inc., the notification instrument by which a national securities
  exchange reports removal of a security from its listing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-18-000019, Form 25-NSE]
- On February 9, 2018, OceanFirst Financial Corp., as successor to Sun Bancorp, Inc.,
  filed a Form 15-12G certifying termination of Sun Bancorp's registration under
  Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, ending Sun Bancorp's stand-alone SEC periodic
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038064, Form 15-12G]
- Consequently, no Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by Sun Bancorp,
  Inc. for any period after the merger; there is no Sun-specific audited annual report
  covering the 2021-2023 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038064, Form 15-12G]

## Sun-specific disclosures within the acquirer's consolidated filings (2021-2023 window)
- OceanFirst Financial Corp. does not report Sun Bancorp / Sun National Bank as a
  separate segment; the acquired business is folded into OceanFirst's single
  consolidated set of financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-22-000022, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2021]
- OceanFirst's FY2021 Form 10-K discloses, in its income-tax footnote, federal net
  operating losses carried over "from the acquisitions of ... Sun Bancorp, Inc."; the
  balance attributable to Sun was $129.4 million as of December 31, 2021, down from
  $152.6 million as of December 31, 2020, subject to an annual Section 382 limitation
  of approximately $23.3 million (expiring in 2022) and $9.3 million (expiring
  2029-2036). [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-22-000022, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2021]
- OceanFirst's FY2022 Form 10-K discloses the same Sun-attributed net-operating-loss
  balance had declined further to $106.1 million as of December 31, 2022 (from $129.4
  million as of December 31, 2021), under the same Section 382 annual limitation and
  expiration terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-23-000017, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2022]
- Both the FY2021 and FY2022 Form 10-Ks disclose that OceanFirst held $2.3 million of
  Alternative Minimum Tax credits "that were part of the Sun acquisition," subject to
  the same Section 382 limitation but not subject to expiration, unchanged across
  December 31, 2020, 2021, and 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-22-000022, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-23-000017, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2022]
- OceanFirst's FY2021 Form 10-K lists among its statutory business trusts (used for
  trust-preferred securities) entities named "Sun Statutory Trust VII," "Sun Capital
  Trust VII," and "Sun Capital Trust VIII," alongside OceanFirst's own trusts,
  indicating these Sun-originated financing vehicles remained on OceanFirst's books
  into the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-22-000022, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Sun Bancorp, Inc. ceased independent SEC reporting on completion of the merger
  (effective January 31, 2018) and deregistered on February 9, 2018; its common stock
  was removed from Nasdaq listing on January 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038064, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-18-000019, Form 25-NSE]
- The acquiring registrant, OceanFirst Financial Corp., does not disclose a separate
  segment or standalone operating/financial result for the former Sun business in its
  2021-2023 filings; the only Sun-specific facts traceable in this window are
  tax-attribute footnote disclosures (declining acquired net operating losses,
  $152.6 million at year-end 2020 to $129.4 million at year-end 2021 to $106.1 million
  at year-end 2022; a static $2.3 million AMT credit balance) and the continued
  presence of Sun-originated trust-preferred financing vehicles on OceanFirst's
  balance sheet. [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-22-000022, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001004702-23-000017, OceanFirst Financial Corp. Form 10-K for FY2022]
