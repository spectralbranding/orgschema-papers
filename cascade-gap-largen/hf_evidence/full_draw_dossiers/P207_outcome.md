## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- A search of the acquired company's EDGAR filing history (CIK 0000057528) finds no
  Form 10-K filed after the pre-merger fiscal-2017 10-K (filed 2018-02-28); a Form
  25-NSE (removal from listing) was filed March 26, 2018 and a Form 15-12G
  (deregistration) was filed April 5, 2018, and no periodic report was filed under this
  CIK afterward. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0000057528, accessions 0001354457-18-000066 and 0001174947-18-000568]
- The acquired business is not reported as its own segment: the acquirer's (Campbell
  Soup Company, CIK 0000016732) reportable segments are "Meals & Beverages" and
  "Snacks," with the acquired brands (Snyder's of Hanover, Lance, Cape Cod, Kettle
  Brand, Late July, Snack Factory Pretzel Crisps, Pop Secret) reported together with
  Pepperidge Farm and Goldfish inside the "Snacks" segment; no standalone financial
  results for the acquired business are separately reported. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Documented post-close integration record (acquirer's own disclosure, specifically naming the acquired business)
- The acquirer's fiscal-2021 and fiscal-2022 10-Ks each describe a "Snyder's-Lance Cost
  Transformation Program and Integration" as part of its multi-year cost-savings
  initiatives, stating that the acquired company had itself launched a cost
  transformation program before the acquisition and that the acquirer "continued to
  implement this program" and identified additional cost synergies "as we integrated
  Snyder's-Lance." [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-21-000103, Form 10-K (FY2021)][SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- As of fiscal 2021 (year ended August 1, 2021) the acquirer targeted $850 million in
  annualized cost savings from continuing operations by the end of 2022, including $295
  million in synergies and run-rate cost savings attributed specifically to the
  Snyder's-Lance acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-21-000103, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Capital-expenditure integration projects specifically tied to the acquired business
  continued through fiscal 2022 (year ended July 31, 2022) — more than four years after
  the March 2018 closing — including implementation of the acquirer's existing SAP
  enterprise-resource-planning system for the acquired business, improvement of quality/
  safety/cost structure across its manufacturing network, transition of production from
  a Toronto manufacturing facility to the acquirer's U.S. thermal plants, and
  optimization of its warehouse and distribution network. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- In the second quarter of fiscal 2021, the acquirer recorded a $19 million ($.06 per
  share) deferred tax charge "in connection with a legal entity reorganization as part
  of the continued integration of Snyder's-Lance." [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-21-000103, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- In connection with the acquisition, the acquirer had issued Senior Notes; a subsequent
  tender offer/redemption of Senior Notes connected to the Snyder's-Lance acquisition
  (excluding one unrelated note series) involved consideration of $1,765 million,
  including $65 million of premium, and the acquirer recognized a $75 million loss
  (including the premium) on the redemption. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-21-000103, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Realized asset-value record (no impairment as of the outcome window)
- As of July 31, 2022, indefinite-lived trademarks specifically associated with the
  acquisition of Snyder's-Lance, Inc. were carried at $620 million (Snyder's of Hanover),
  $350 million (Lance), $318 million (Kettle Brand), and $292 million (Pace), out of a
  total indefinite-lived-trademark carrying value of $2,549 million across the
  acquirer's portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The acquirer's fiscal-2021 and fiscal-2022 annual impairment testing found that all
  reporting units, including those carrying the acquired trademarks, had fair values
  that "significantly exceeded" carrying values; no goodwill or trademark impairment
  charge tied to the Snyder's-Lance-associated brands was recorded in either year. Only
  Pacific Foods and certain other Snacks-segment trademarks (not the Snyder's-Lance
  brands) were flagged as having 10% or less of fair-value headroom over carrying value
  as of the 2022 test. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-21-000103, Form 10-K (FY2021)][SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the acquired company deregistered in April 2018, there is no independent
  public 10-K record for the ~2021-2022 window under its own name. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0000057528, accession 0001174947-18-000568]
- The acquirer does separately name and quantify the acquired business's post-close
  trajectory through its integration and cost-transformation disclosures: as of four-
  plus years after closing (fiscal 2022), systems integration (SAP implementation),
  manufacturing-network consolidation (the Toronto facility transition), and legal-
  entity reorganization tied to the acquired business were still being completed, and a
  dedicated cost-synergy target ($295 million) tied to the acquisition was in effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- No impairment of the goodwill or trademarks associated with the acquired brands was
  recorded within the window; their carrying values were reported as fully supported by
  fair value in both the fiscal-2021 and fiscal-2022 annual impairment tests. [SRC: SEC accession 0000016732-22-000093, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
