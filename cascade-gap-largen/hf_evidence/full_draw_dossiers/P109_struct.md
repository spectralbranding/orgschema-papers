# Structural sub-dossier — Kratos Defense & Security Solutions acquisition of Herley Industries (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing and the transaction it establishes
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K of Kratos Defense & Security Solutions, Inc. dated
  February 7, 2011, reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive
  Agreement) that on February 7, 2011 the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger with Lanza Acquisition Co. (a Delaware corporation and indirect wholly-owned
  subsidiary of the Company, the "Merger Sub") and Herley Industries, Inc. The 8-K is the
  deal-terms document for this acquisition; the Merger Agreement is attached to the
  related Prospectus Supplement by reference. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Parties and transaction type as structured
- The transaction is structured as a cash tender offer followed by a merger: Merger Sub is
  to commence a tender offer to purchase all issued and outstanding shares of Herley common
  stock at $19.00 per share in cash, and upon completion Merger Sub will be merged with and
  into Herley, with Herley surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Merger Agreement provides a "Top-Up Option" and short-form-merger mechanics under
  Delaware law to complete the merger without a Herley stockholder meeting if Merger Sub
  holds at least 90% of shares after the offer; a termination fee of $9,440,000 is payable
  by Herley under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The acquisition price for all Herley shares was described as approximately $270 million in
  cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: Herley is described as a leading provider of microwave-based
  products and technologies for Command and Control Systems, Weapons Systems, Electronic
  Warfare Systems, and Radar and Communications Systems, with established positions in defense
  platforms including manned and unmanned aircraft, tactical and ballistic missiles, and
  sensor-based and radar platforms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]
- Legal vehicle and operating substrate: the entire issued and outstanding capital of Herley
  (an operating company with its own subsidiaries) is acquired, with Herley continuing as a
  wholly-owned subsidiary; the operating business — its producing/delivering processes as an
  ongoing enterprise — transfers intact within that subsidiary rather than as a carved-out
  product line. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- People/management: the announcement states that "key Herley operational management" is
  "expected to continue their employment with Kratos." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]
- Value-capture model as stated: the transaction is described as expected to be accretive to
  the acquirer's earnings per share, margins, and cash flow, excluding transaction and
  financing costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]

## Financing and contractual bridging at closing
- Financing was structured with a debt commitment of up to $307.5 million from Jefferies
  Group, Inc., Key Capital Corporation and OPY Credit Corp., reducible (up to $40 million) by
  net proceeds of a proposed public equity offering; the Company intended to issue up to $325
  million of senior secured notes due 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- To permit the acquisition, the Company obtained a supplemental indenture consent from a
  majority of holders of its existing 10% Senior Secured Notes due 2017 and an amendment to
  its existing senior secured credit agreement with KeyBank National Association waiving
  restrictions on the acquisition and note issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply arrangement between the parties is
  described, consistent with acquisition of the whole operating entity (as opposed to a
  carve-out requiring a bridged shared substrate). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Acquirer context at closing (concurrent acquisitions)
- The 8-K records that, as an acquirer active in successive transactions, the Company had
  acquired Gichner Holdings, Inc. on May 19, 2010 and Henry Bros. Electronics, Inc. on
  December 15, 2010, and includes pro forma combined financial statements showing the effect
  of the Herley, Gichner and HBE acquisitions together. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Item 9.01]
- The acquirer described itself as a specialized National Security business with a primarily
  engineering and technical workforce of approximately 2,800. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction is a cash tender-offer-and-merger acquisition of the entire outstanding
  stock of an operating defense-electronics company ($19.00/share, ~$270 million), in which
  the target's product line, its producing operations, and its key operational management were
  structured to transfer together as a wholly-owned subsidiary, financed by committed debt and
  a senior-secured-note issuance, and layered onto a sequence of prior 2010 acquisitions by the
  same acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-11-000539, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 9.01 and EX-99.2 press release]
