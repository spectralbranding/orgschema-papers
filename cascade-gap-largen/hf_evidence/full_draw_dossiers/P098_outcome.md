## Outcome window and source
- The outcome window is fixed at fiscal year 2021 (the ~5-year point after the ~2016-2017 closing era); the realized record is taken from the registrant's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- For the year ended December 31, 2021 the registrant reported net income of $56.9 million ($56,854 thousand), compared with $52.4 million ($52,373 thousand) in 2020 and $226.4 million ($226,357 thousand) in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- Net income attributable to common shareholders for 2021 was $26.0 million ($25,963 thousand), compared with $27.4 million ($27,435 thousand) in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- Consolidated net income for 2021 increased by $4.5 million versus 2020, reflecting improved fair-value performance of CRT-related investments, partially offset by decreases in the correspondent production and interest-rate-sensitive strategies results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The trajectory was not smooth within 2021: "in the third and fourth quarters of fiscal year 2021, we reported net losses attributable to common shareholders of $43.9 million and $27.3 million, respectively." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- Total assets at year end grew to $13,772,708 thousand ($13.77 billion) at December 31, 2021 from $11,492,011 thousand at December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- At December 31, 2021, $13.2 billion, or 96%, of total assets were carried at fair value on a recurring basis, of which $4.7 billion (34% of total assets) were measured using Level 3 inputs "which are difficult to observe and require significant management judgment." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]

## Documented disruption over the window
- Consolidated net income in 2020 had decreased by $174.0 million versus 2019, "reflecting the effect of the COVID-19 pandemic on the fair value of our CRT-related investments and a $63.1 million increase in provision for income taxes." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The 2020 shift from a net gain to a net loss on investments "reflects the effect of the disruption in the credit markets during 2020 on our CRT investments including expectations for increased losses to be absorbed by those investments as a result of the COVID-19 pandemic." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The registrant cautioned there could be no assurance of generating sufficient cash to pay operating expenses and sustain distributions, citing the 2021 third- and fourth-quarter net losses attributable to common shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]

## Continuity at the manager/servicer interface over the window
- The registrant remained externally managed at the end of the window: it continued to be managed by PNMAC Capital Management, LLC ("PCM"), described as a wholly-owned subsidiary of PFSI, with PCM making all or substantially all of its investment, financing and risk management decisions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- Servicing continued to sit with PLS under the loan servicing and mortgage banking services agreements; the registrant reiterated it relies on PCM and PLS "because we have limited in-house capabilities and employees to perform the activities independently." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The registrant's own workforce contracted over the window: "We have one employee. All of our officers are employees of PFSI or its affiliates," and its long-term growth is stated to be highly dependent upon PFSI's employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The registrant disclosed continuing dependence risk: it "may not find suitable replacements if any of our service agreements with PCM or PLS are terminated," and PLS's own covenant compliance under the registrant's repurchase agreements remained a condition of the registrant's access to liquidity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2021 outcome window, the registrant remained a going concern with total assets of $13.77 billion (up from $11.49 billion a year earlier), reporting full-year net income of $56.9 million ($26.0 million attributable to common shareholders) that nonetheless included consecutive third- and fourth-quarter 2021 net losses to common shareholders of $43.9 million and $27.3 million; the period also carried the earlier COVID-19-driven $174.0 million year-over-year decline in 2020 consolidated net income tied to fair-value losses on CRT investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
- The external-management and affiliate-servicing structure persisted through the window (managed by PCM, serviced by PLS, both PFSI affiliates), with the registrant employing one person by year-end 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-007180, 10-K FY2021]
