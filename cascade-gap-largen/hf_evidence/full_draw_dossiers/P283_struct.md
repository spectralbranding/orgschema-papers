# Structural sub-dossier — NewStar Financial, Inc. acquisition by First Eagle (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- NewStar Financial, Inc. ("NewStar"), a Delaware corporation headquartered in Boston,
  Massachusetts, was a commercial finance company engaged in direct lending activities
  and asset management, with common stock listed on NASDAQ under the symbol "NEWS."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Transactions"]
- On October 16, 2017, NewStar entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with First
  Eagle Holdings, Inc., FE Holdco, LLC, and FE Merger Sub, Inc. (all Delaware entities and
  affiliates of First Eagle Investment Management, LLC, a privately-owned investment
  manager with approximately $116 billion in assets under management as of September 30,
  2017, majority-owned by an entity jointly owned by funds advised by Blackstone and
  Corsair). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Transactions"]
- Concurrently, on October 16, 2017, NewStar entered into a separate Asset Purchase
  Agreement with GSO Diamond Portfolio Holdco LLC, a Delaware entity wholly owned by a
  newly formed investment fund advised by GSO Capital Partners LP (the global credit
  investment platform of Blackstone). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Transactions"]
- The merger and the asset sale were cross-conditioned: completion of one required
  completion of the other, and the merger closing was conditioned upon prior completion
  of the asset sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A summary]
- Both transactions closed on December 22, 2017: FE Merger Sub, Inc. merged with and
  into NewStar, with NewStar surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of FE Holdco, LLC;
  and GSO Diamond Portfolio Holdco LLC completed its purchase of a portfolio of
  investment assets from NewStar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary, by tier
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity, NewStar Financial, Inc., became an
  indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of First Eagle; its certificate of incorporation and
  bylaws were amended and restated to those of the (acquirer-formed) Merger Sub.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- Controlling intent / governance: all seven then-sitting NewStar directors resigned
  effective at the merger's Effective Time (December 22, 2017), and three new directors
  affiliated with the transaction were elected in their place. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Business/value-capture model and customer-facing product: First Eagle's stated primary
  purpose was to acquire NewStar's asset-management platform and expand its own
  investment platform "to include fundamental capabilities in corporate and middle
  market credit investing," and to provide "NewStar's credit strategies to institutional
  and retail investors" — i.e., the platform/capability, not the loan portfolio itself,
  was First Eagle's stated object. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A "Purpose and Reasons of First Eagle and Corsair for the Merger"]
- Product/asset carve-out: immediately prior to and conditioned on the merger, NewStar
  sold its portfolio of investment assets — loans and other credit investments — to GSO
  Diamond Portfolio Holdco LLC for a purchase price of approximately $2.3 billion,
  separately from the entity that First Eagle acquired via merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Use of proceeds: the net proceeds of the asset sale were used to repay outstanding
  NewStar debt (including discharge of $380 million of 7.25% Senior Notes due 2020) and,
  together with First Eagle's own cash and credit-facility borrowings, to fund the
  merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- People/organization: employees who remained employed by the surviving company as of
  the Effective Time ("Continuing Employees") were contractually assured continuity of
  base salary/wages, 2018 benefit-plan participation on terms comparable to First Eagle's
  own employees, service credit for eligibility/vesting, and specified severance
  protections for a defined post-closing period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A merger agreement summary, Employee Matters]
- Named executive officers (Timothy Conway, John Bray, Patrick McAuliffe, Mark du Four,
  and Daniel McCready) held pre-existing individual employment agreements providing for
  change-in-control severance benefits, including cash payments and other benefits upon
  qualifying termination within two years of the closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A "Change in Control and Employment and Severance Arrangements"]

## Consideration
- NewStar common stockholders were entitled to receive, per share, $11.44 in cash plus
  one contractual contingent value right (CVR) representing a pro-rata share of certain
  post-closing tax refunds attributable to losses recognized on the asset sale, estimated
  by NewStar at up to approximately $1.00 per CVR if closing occurred in 2017.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A summary of merger consideration]
- The aggregate merger consideration for outstanding common stock was calculated at
  $517,035,129.76. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A Schedule 14A fee-calculation table]

## Public-market status change at closing
- In connection with the closing, NewStar notified the Nasdaq Stock Market that the
  merger had been consummated and that trading of its common stock be suspended, and
  requested that Nasdaq file a Form 25 application reporting that the common stock was no
  longer listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction bifurcated NewStar at closing along the product/legal-vehicle line:
  the corporate entity, its asset-management platform, its Continuing Employees, and (per
  First Eagle's stated purpose) its investment-management capability passed intact into
  First Eagle's ownership via a reverse merger, while the customer-facing loan and credit
  investment portfolio — the assets the business had originated and managed — was
  carved out and sold separately, immediately pre-merger, to a Blackstone/GSO-sponsored
  buyer entity unrelated to First Eagle's chain of ownership. Governing control changed
  hands cleanly (full board turnover at the Effective Time); continuity of the
  operating workforce was addressed contractually via Continuing Employee provisions in
  the merger agreement rather than a separate transitional-services arrangement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-355921, DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-377704, Form 8-K]
