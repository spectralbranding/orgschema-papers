## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- Effective July 12, 2021, the company changed its name from Albany Molecular Research,
  Inc. ("AMRI") to Curia, introducing a new brand identity and website; at that time the
  company was reported to employ "more than 3,000 people in 21 locations around the
  world," including more than 600 chemists, 70 biologists, 225 senior scientists, and
  approximately 400 quality and regulatory specialists, with 564 active patents. [SRC: https://www.contractpharma.com/breaking-news/amri-becomes-curia/]
- The company's chairman and CEO at the time of the rename was John Ratliff, who
  described the new name as reflecting "the assembled deep expertise of our people, the
  breadth of our products, services and solutions." [SRC: https://www.contractpharma.com/breaking-news/amri-becomes-curia/]
- In March 2021 the company (still named AMRI) was reported to have approximately 3,100
  employees and to have been named to a Forbes list of best employers for 2021,
  notwithstanding "the profound challenges the world faced with the COVID-19 pandemic."
  [SRC: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20210309005021/en/AMRI-Earns-Spot-on-Forbes-America's-Best-Employers-2021-List]
- Concurrent with the rebrand, the company pursued expansion into biologics: it
  announced an agreement in July 2021 to acquire biologics formulation and fill-finish
  company Integrity Bio (expected to close by year-end 2021), and it completed an
  acquisition of LakePharma Inc. in early September 2021; industry press dated February
  2022 reported that Integrity Bio and LakePharma were being brought under the unified
  Curia brand. [SRC: https://xtalks.com/global-cdmo-amri-rebrands-as-curia-and-sets-sights-on-biologics-2838/]

  31, 2017 (the closing date), there is no public audited outcome record (10-K/10-Q) for
