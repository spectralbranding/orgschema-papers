# Structural sub-dossier — Forest City Realty Trust, Inc. acquisition (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Forest City Realty Trust, Inc., a Maryland corporation, entered into a definitive
  Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 30, 2018 with Antlia Holdings LLC ("Parent"),
  a newly formed Delaware LLC affiliated with a Brookfield Asset Management Inc. real
  estate investment fund, and Antlia Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a Maryland
  corporation newly formed by Parent solely for the transaction. Structured as a reverse
  triangular merger, Merger Sub would merge into Forest City, which would survive as a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; Merger Sub itself "has not engaged in any business
  except for activities incidental to its formation" and the merger transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- Holders of Forest City Class A common stock would receive $25.35 per share in cash, an
  approximate 26.6% premium over the closing price on June 15, 2018, the last trading
  day before a press report of the discussions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- Forest City's Board approved the merger agreement by a non-unanimous vote of seven to
  five, and the company's non-executive chairman, James Ratner (a member of the
  company's founding family), informed the company he intended to vote his own shares
  against the Merger Proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- The customer-facing product/asset acquired was Forest City's real estate portfolio and
  operating platform: the proxy describes the company as principally engaged in "the
  operation, development, management and acquisition of office, apartment and retail
  real estate and land throughout the United States," with approximately $7.9 billion of
  consolidated assets in 17 states and the District of Columbia as of June 30, 2018,
  core markets in Boston, Chicago, Dallas, Denver, Los Angeles, Philadelphia, and the
  greater New York, San Francisco, and Washington, D.C. metro areas, regional offices in
  those markets, and corporate headquarters in Cleveland, Ohio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- As a whole-company, 100% cash-out merger rather than an asset carve-out, the legal
  vehicle, portfolio, and operating entity structure crossed the boundary together as a
  single surviving corporation; the proxy describes no separation of the product/asset
  base from the operating entity at signing. Day-to-day operating processes ran partly
  through pre-existing third-party arrangements: the merger agreement's financing
  covenants reference obtaining consents "from third parties to existing joint-venture
  agreements, financing documents, property management agreements, ground leases, tax
  credit agreements, and purchase and sale agreements" as needed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- The proxy documents no stated commitment by Parent to retain Forest City's existing
  senior management, employees, or organization after closing; certain named executive
  officers instead held employment or change-of-control agreements providing
  "double-trigger" cash severance and accelerated equity/incentive vesting upon a
  qualifying termination tied to the change in control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- At the Effective Time (December 7, 2018) all twelve incumbent directors ceased serving
  — the 8-K states this was "in connection with the Merger and not due to any
  disagreement with the Company on any matter" — and were replaced by Merger Sub's own
  four director designees; effective after that date, CEO David LaRue and three other
  named executives (Robert G. O'Brien, Duane F. Bishop, and Brian Ratner, of the founding
  family) ceased employment with the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-345012, Form 8-K dated December 7, 2018]
- The joint closing press release described the acquired portfolio (6.3M sq ft office,
  2.3M sq ft life-sciences, 2.2M sq ft retail, 18,500 multifamily units, five large-scale
  development projects) as featuring "many natural synergies with Brookfield's existing
  real estate operating business" — language pointing to integration with the acquirer's
  pre-existing platform rather than standalone continuation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-345012, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]

## Financing and deal-protection terms bridging the transaction at closing
- Parent's obligations were backed by debt financing commitments of up to $4.25 billion
  and equity financing commitments of up to $3.4 billion (with the equity sources also
  entering a limited guaranty in Forest City's favor); completion was not conditioned on
  obtaining this financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]
- Deal-protection terms: Forest City would owe Parent a $261 million termination fee
  (plus, in certain circumstances, up to $70 million expense reimbursement) if it
  terminated under specified circumstances; Forest City's exclusive remedy if closing
  conditions were satisfied and the Brookfield parties failed to close was a reverse
  termination fee payable by Parent, referenced in the proxy's fairness discussion at
  $488 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A]

## Listing status at closing
- The proxy stated that upon completion, Forest City's stock would be wholly owned by
  Parent, no longer quoted on the NYSE, and deregistered under the Exchange Act. On the
  December 7, 2018 closing date, Forest City notified the NYSE, requested suspension of
  trading, and all shares were removed from trading at the close of that day; the
  company stated it intended to file a Form 15 to terminate its reporting obligations.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-345012, Form 8-K dated December 7, 2018]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, all-cash reverse-triangular merger in which a
  newly formed Brookfield-affiliated vehicle acquired 100% of Forest City's outstanding
  stock; Forest City survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, carrying its
  portfolio, legal entity structure, and existing third-party joint-venture/property-
  management arrangements across the boundary intact. At the same Effective Time, the
  entire incumbent board and the four most senior named executives departed and were
  replaced by Merger Sub's designees, and the acquirer's own closing statement framed the
  portfolio as complementary to its pre-existing operating platform rather than as a
  standalone business — so the customer-facing product/asset and its legal vehicle
  crossed together, while the incumbent people/management layer that had operated it did
  not. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298438, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-345012, Form 8-K dated December 7, 2018]
