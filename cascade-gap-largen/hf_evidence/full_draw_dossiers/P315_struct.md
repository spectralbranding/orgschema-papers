# Structural sub-dossier — Earthstone Energy, Inc. proposed acquisition of Sabalo Energy / Shad Permian interests (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the proposed
> transaction structure and what became of that structure through the deal window

> financial performance.

## Transaction and parties

- On October 17, 2018, Earthstone Energy, Inc. ("Earthstone"), Earthstone Energy
  Holdings, LLC ("EEH"), and Sabalo Holdings, LLC ("Sabalo Holdings") entered into a
  Contribution Agreement under which EEH would acquire the issued and outstanding
  interests of Sabalo Energy, Inc. and Sabalo Energy, LLC ("Sabalo") from Sabalo
  Holdings (the "Sabalo Acquisition"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Immediately following the Sabalo Acquisition, Sabalo would separately acquire the
  wellbore interests owned by Shad Permian, LLC ("Shad") under a Farmout and Development
  Agreement between Sabalo and Shad, pursuant to a Letter Agreement dated October 17,
  2018 (the "Shad Acquisition"); the Sabalo Acquisition and Shad Acquisition together
  were defined as the "Transaction." [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- The underlying properties comprised proved reserves, well equipment/infrastructure,
  and undeveloped drilling locations in the Midland Basin of Texas, with an effective
  date of May 1, 2018 for both the Sabalo and Shad components. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Sabalo Holdings is an affiliate of an investment entity controlled by EnCap
  Investments L.P. ("EnCap"); EnCap through its investment entities indirectly owned
  approximately 61.2% of the outstanding voting securities of Earthstone at the time of
  signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- EnCap through an affiliate owned approximately 98.9% of Sabalo Holdings, which
  directly and indirectly owned 100% of Sabalo; because Earthstone and Sabalo were
  under the common control of EnCap, Earthstone's management stated GAAP required the
  acquisition to be recorded on Earthstone's books at Sabalo's historical cost
  (retrospectively, similar to pooling-of-interests treatment under APB 16) rather than
  at fair value as would apply to a transaction with an unrelated third party. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]

## Proposed consideration (what would have crossed at closing)

- For the Sabalo Acquisition, Sabalo Holdings was to receive: (i) $480,000,000 in cash
  (the "Sabalo Cash Purchase Price") and (ii) 32,315,695 shares of Class B common stock
  together with 32,315,695 corresponding EEH Units. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Total consideration for the Sabalo Acquisition component was disclosed (in the
  historical-cost pro forma financial statements) as approximately $780 million,
  subject to customary post-closing adjustments reflecting the May 1, 2018 effective
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Total consideration for the separate Shad Acquisition component (100% of Shad's
  well-bore interests and related proved reserves/equipment) was disclosed as
  approximately $170 million, subject to post-closing adjustments on the same May 1,
  2018 effective date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement's fee-calculation cover page stated the aggregate consideration
  to be paid across the Transaction was (i) approximately $650 million in cash and (ii)
  32,315,695 shares of Class B common stock, part of which Earthstone intended to
  partially fund from the proceeds of a separate Series A Preferred Stock sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]

## Financing and related preferred-stock issuance

- Earthstone had entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement with EIG ESTE Equity
  Aggregator, L.P. ("EIG") for the sale of $225 million of Series A Redeemable
  Convertible Preferred Stock and up to $30 million of Class A common stock (the
  "Preferred Stock Financing"), proceeds of which were intended to partially fund the
  Transaction's cash consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Because the Series A Preferred Stock was convertible into more than 20% of
  Earthstone's outstanding voting power, Rule 312.03 of the NYSE Listed Company Manual
  required a separate stockholder vote (the "NYSE Preferred Stock Proposal") to approve
  that issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- A separate debt commitment letter among EEH and a bank group (Wells Fargo Bank,
  National Association; Royal Bank of Canada; SunTrust Bank; BOKF, NA; and PNC Bank
  National Association) was also in place to help fund the Transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]

## Closing conditions and stockholder vote mechanics

- Because EnCap's affiliation with both Earthstone and Sabalo Holdings made the
  Transaction a related-party matter, Earthstone's Special Committee retained Stephens
  Inc. as financial advisor in connection with the Sabalo Acquisition and Shad
  Acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- Closing of the Transaction was conditioned on stockholder approval, at a special
  meeting, of five linked proposals: the Contribution Agreement Proposal, the NYSE
  Transaction Proposal (share issuance under NYSE listing rules), the Authorized Share
  Increase Proposal, the Authorized Director Proposal, and the NYSE Preferred Stock
  Proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]
- The special meeting of stockholders was called for January 3, 2019 at Earthstone's
  principal offices in The Woodlands, Texas, with a proxy-submission deadline of 11:59
  p.m. Eastern Time on January 2, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A]

## Deal-window resolution: mutual termination before closing

- On December 21, 2018, Earthstone, EEH, and Sabalo Holdings entered into a Termination
  Agreement pursuant to which the parties mutually terminated the Contribution
  Agreement; the parties also agreed to release each other from certain claims and
  liabilities related to the Contribution Agreement and to certain mutual non-compete
  provisions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- Under the Termination Agreement, EEH agreed to reimburse Sabalo Holdings for
  transaction-related expenses estimated at not less than $1.6 million (potentially
  increased by up to $500,000), and to reimburse Sabalo Holdings for a separate $1.0
  million payment to Shad to terminate the related Letter Agreement dated October 17,
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- As a direct consequence of the Contribution Agreement's termination, two related
  agreements terminated in accordance with their own terms on the same date: the
  Support and Standstill Agreement dated October 17, 2018 among Earthstone, EEH, Sabalo
  Holdings, and EnCap; and the Securities Purchase Agreement dated October 17, 2018
  between Earthstone and EIG (the Preferred Stock Financing agreement). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- Under the terms of the terminated Securities Purchase Agreement, Earthstone agreed to
  pay EIG a termination fee of $3.4 million plus reimbursement of EIG's expenses up to
  $750,000, and agreed that for the 12 months following termination it would not
  acquire Sabalo assets, sell preferred stock, or agree to do either. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- The debt commitment letter among EEH and the bank group (Wells Fargo, Royal Bank of
  Canada, SunTrust, BOKF, and PNC) also terminated as a result of the Contribution
  Agreement's termination; EEH agreed to reimburse the banks approximately $600,000 in
  expenses. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- On December 21, 2018, Earthstone's Board of Directors determined to cancel the
  special meeting of stockholders that had been scheduled for January 3, 2019, on the
  basis that, the Contribution Agreement having been terminated, the meeting was no
  longer necessary; a press release announcing both the Transaction's termination and
  the meeting's cancellation was issued the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K Items 7.01, 8.01]

## Structural summary (deal-window only)

- Earthstone proposed to acquire, via its subsidiary EEH, the Midland Basin oil and gas
  interests of Sabalo (owned by an EnCap-controlled affiliate that also indirectly held
  approximately 61.2% of Earthstone's voting stock) and, in a linked step, Shad's
  related wellbore interests, for combined consideration of roughly $950 million in
  cash and Class B stock, financed in part through a related preferred-stock issuance
  to EIG and a bank debt-commitment letter — but the Contribution Agreement was
  mutually terminated by the parties on December 21, 2018, before the scheduled
  stockholder vote, all related financing and standstill agreements terminated
  alongside it (including a $3.4 million termination fee owed to EIG), and the special
  meeting was cancelled; no consideration was paid and no interests were contributed or
  acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000071, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0000010254-18-000095, Form 8-K]
