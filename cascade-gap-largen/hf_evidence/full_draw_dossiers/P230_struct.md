# Structural sub-dossier — USG Corporation merger with Gebr. Knauf KG (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- USG Corporation, a Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated June 10, 2018 with Gebr. Knauf KG, a German limited partnership
  (Kommanditgesellschaft), and World Cup Acquisition Corporation, a Delaware corporation
  and indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of Knauf ("Merger Sub"), under which Merger Sub
  would merge with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving
  corporation and becoming an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of Knauf. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Knauf is described as the ultimate parent of the Knauf Group, headquartered in
  Iphofen, Germany, a manufacturer of building materials operating more than 220
  factories worldwide, with 2017 global revenues in excess of $8 billion and more than
  27,000 employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of USG common stock (other than Knauf, its subsidiaries, the Company, and
  excluded holders) were entitled to receive $43.50 per share in cash at closing (the
  "closing consideration"), plus a conditional special cash dividend of $0.50 per share
  paid October 2, 2018, for total consideration of $44.00 per share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Berkshire Hathaway and affiliated entities, holding approximately 31.1% of USG common
  stock, entered into a voting agreement dated June 10, 2018 with Knauf and Merger Sub
  to vote in favor of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Knauf's outreach began as a partnership inquiry regarding its North American
  fiberglass insulation business; after the Company found no strategic value in such a
  combination, Knauf's representatives stated an intention to make a proposal to acquire
  the Company outright, and pursued the acquisition over roughly a year of negotiation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Completion of the merger was conditioned on antitrust/foreign-investment approvals
  under the HSR Act and other competition laws, which Knauf was obligated to pursue
  under a "hell or high water" standard; the merger was not conditioned on Knauf
  obtaining financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on April 24, 2019; Knauf financed the closing consideration and
  related payments — totaling approximately $5.6 billion — through external credit
  facilities arranged by UniCredit Bank AG and Commerzbank Aktiengesellschaft together
  with Knauf's own funds. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]

## What USG comprised at closing (product/asset tier)
- USG described itself as an industry-leading manufacturer of building products serving
  construction markets worldwide through its Gypsum, Performance Materials, Ceilings,
  and USG Boral divisions, with wall, ceiling, flooring, sheathing and roofing products;
  its USG Boral Building Products joint venture was described as a leading plasterboard
  and ceilings producer across Asia, Australasia and the Middle East. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]

## People/management tier at closing
- Immediately upon closing of the merger, USG's then-current named executive officers —
  Jennifer F. Scanlon (President and CEO), Matthew F. Hilzinger (EVP and CFO), Brian J.
  Cook (EVP and Chief Administrative Officer), Dominic A. Dannessa (EVP and Chief
  Customer and Innovation Officer), Gregory D. Salah (SVP and President, Gypsum), and
  Michelle M. Warner (SVP, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary) — ceased acting in
  their roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
- All nine incumbent USG directors ceased to be directors upon closing; a new board
  (Alexander Knauf, Martin Stürmer, Dr. Enno Henze, and Christopher R. Griffin) was
  installed, and the new board appointed Christopher R. Griffin — concurrently — as
  President, Chief Executive Officer, Chief Financial Officer and Treasurer of the
  surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
- The merger agreement provided that, for the period from closing through December 31
  of the following calendar year, Knauf would provide "Continuing USG Employees" base
  compensation, incentive opportunities, retirement benefits, and severance benefits
  each no less favorable than immediately pre-closing, and would recognize pre-closing
  service for eligibility/vesting purposes — a fixed-term compensation/benefits
  continuity covenant rather than a permanent retention commitment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]
- Separately, change-in-control severance agreements with named executive officers
  (Cook, Dannessa, Hilzinger, Holmes, Macey, Martin, Reale, Salah, Scanlon, Warner)
  provided cash severance, pro-rated bonus, and equity cash-out upon a qualifying
  termination in connection with the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-255504, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and capital-structure changes at closing
- USG's Fifth Amended and Restated Credit Agreement (revolving facility with JPMorgan
  Chase Bank as administrative agent) was terminated at closing, along with associated
  subsidiary guarantees and liens; outstanding letters of credit were cash-collateralized
  rather than carried forward under that facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
- The merger constituted a "Change of Control" under USG's 5.50% Senior Notes due 2025
  and 4.875% Senior Notes due 2027, triggering a required repurchase offer for
  approximately $350 million and $500 million outstanding principal, respectively, at
  101% of principal plus accrued interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
- USG's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
- On April 24, 2019, in connection with closing, USG notified the New York Stock
  Exchange that the merger had completed and requested withdrawal of its common stock
  and preferred stock purchase rights from listing on the NYSE and Chicago Stock
  Exchange; the NYSE filed Form 25 with the SEC that day, and USG's common stock was
  removed from listing and ceased trading. USG intended to file a Form 15 to terminate
  Exchange Act registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company cash merger (Merger Sub into USG, with USG
  surviving as an indirect, wholly-owned Knauf subsidiary) with an immediate, same-day
  replacement of USG's entire pre-closing board and named executive team by a small
  Knauf-affiliated slate, one member of which (a former 18-year USG employee who by 2019
  led Knauf's North American insulation business) was appointed to hold the CEO, CFO,
  and Treasurer roles simultaneously; USG's own credit facility was terminated and
  replaced by Knauf-arranged acquisition financing, and continuity of the acquired
  workforce's compensation/benefits was bridged only through a fixed-term (through the
  following December 31) contractual covenant rather than an open-ended retention
  commitment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000757011-19-000030, Form 8-K dated 2019-04-24]
