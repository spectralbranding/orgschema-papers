## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Whole Foods Market, Inc. filed a Form 25-NSE on 2017-08-28 to remove its common stock
  from listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-17-000163, Form 25-NSE]
- Whole Foods Market, Inc. filed two Form 15-12G notices on 2017-09-07 to deregister
  classes of securities under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, and a Form 15-15D on
  2017-09-27 to suspend its duty to file reports under Section 15(d). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-278601, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-278607, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-294950, Form 15-15D]
- The registrant's last periodic report was a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  September 24, 2017, filed 2017-11-17; no 10-K, 10-Q, or other periodic report has been
  filed by CIK 0000865436 since that date, so there is no company-specific audited public
  financial record covering the ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865436-17-000238, Form 10-K]

## Acquirer's consolidated disclosures touching the acquired business
- Amazon.com's reportable segments are North America, International, and Amazon Web
  Services (AWS); Amazon.com does not report Whole Foods Market as a separate reportable
  segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001018724-22-000005, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1 segment description]
- Amazon.com's FY2021 Form 10-K discloses consolidated net sales by category including a
  "Physical stores" line of $17,192 million (2019), $16,227 million (2020), and $17,075
  million (2021), defined as sales where "customers physically select items in a store"
  (as distinct from online orders fulfilled from physical stores, which are counted in
  "Online stores"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001018724-22-000005, Form 10-K (FY2021), Note on segment/net-sales information]
- This "Physical stores" category is not identified in the filing as specific to Whole
  Foods Market; it aggregates all of Amazon.com's physical retail formats, and Amazon.com
  does not separately disclose Whole Foods Market's individual revenue, profitability, or
  employee count in this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001018724-22-000005, Form 10-K (FY2021), Note on segment/net-sales information]

## Leadership and operating facts (press-sourced)
- On September 30, 2021, Whole Foods Market announced that John Mackey, its co-founder
  and Chief Executive Officer (who had remained CEO through the 2017 acquisition), would
  retire effective September 1, 2022. [SRC: https://www.supermarketnews.com/executive-moves/whole-foods-market-co-founder-and-ceo-john-mackey-to-retire-in-2022]
- Mackey's successor as CEO was Jason Buechel, who had joined Whole Foods Market in 2013,
  was promoted to Executive Vice President in 2015, and had served as Chief Operating
  Officer since 2019. [SRC: https://www.supermarketnews.com/executive-moves/whole-foods-market-co-founder-and-ceo-john-mackey-to-retire-in-2022]
- At the time of the September 2021 announcement, press reporting described Whole Foods
  Market as operating more than 500 stores nationwide. [SRC: https://www.supermarketnews.com/executive-moves/whole-foods-market-co-founder-and-ceo-john-mackey-to-retire-in-2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the acquired entity deregistered from SEC reporting within weeks of closing and
  filed no further periodic reports, there is no company-specific audited public outcome
  record for Whole Foods Market covering the ~2020-2022 window; the acquirer's
  consolidated 10-K discloses an aggregate "Physical stores" net-sales category but does
  not separately name or attribute that figure to Whole Foods Market, and does not report
  the acquired business as a distinct segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865436-17-000238, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001018724-22-000005, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The traceable realized record in this window is limited to a press-reported leadership
  transition: the founding CEO who had remained in place through closing announced his
  retirement in September 2021, effective September 2022, with an internal successor (the
  then-Chief Operating Officer) named at the same time. [SRC: https://www.supermarketnews.com/executive-moves/whole-foods-market-co-founder-and-ceo-john-mackey-to-retire-in-2022]
