## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is a Form 8-K, Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement),
  date of earliest event February 28, 2011, signed and filed March 1, 2011; it reports the
  entry into an Amended and Restated Joint Venture Agreement and attaches the full agreement
  text as Exhibit 10.1 and a related Amended and Restated Manufacturer's Repurchase Agreement
  as Exhibit 10.2. The drawn filing IS the deal-terms document for this transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01 and Item 9.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On February 28, 2011, Polaris Industries Inc., a Minnesota corporation, and GE Commercial
  Distribution Finance Corporation ("GECDF"), a Delaware corporation, entered into an Amended
  and Restated Joint Venture Agreement that "amends and restates in its entirety" the prior
  Joint Venture Agreement dated February 7, 1996, as amended. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The venture is conducted through Polaris Acceptance, an Illinois general partnership formed
  between Polaris Industries' direct subsidiary Polaris Acceptance Inc. ("PAI") and GECDF's
  direct subsidiary CDF Joint Ventures, Inc. ("CDFJV"); each subsidiary holds a 50%
  partnership interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.1, §1.5]
- Stated purpose: ownership and operation of "a commercial finance business and related
  finance businesses" providing commercial inventory (floor-plan) financing to support (i)
  domestic sales of Polaris products to dealers and distributors, (ii) domestic sales of other
  manufacturers' products (e.g., Alcom, Inc.) to Polaris dealers, and (iii) other businesses
  the parties may agree, the latter two subject to unanimous management-committee approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.1]
- The restatement extends the partnership term to February 28, 2017, subject to additional
  five-year Extended Terms upon proper notice/agreement and to unlimited automatic one-year
  extensions absent a non-renewal notice. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — and what did not (tier level)
- Legal vehicle: the customer-facing financing operation runs through a distinct standalone
  entity — Polaris Acceptance, an Illinois general partnership with principal place of
  business in Hoffman Estates, Illinois and an operations office in Minneapolis, Minnesota —
  not inside either parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.2, §1.3]
- The producing/delivering PROCESSES were not contributed into the partnership but retained by
  the parents and supplied back under service contracts: the Definitive Agreements include a
  "Services Agreement — CDF to PA," a "Services Agreement — Polaris to PA," and a "Sub
  Services Agreement — Polaris to CDF" (dated September 6, 2006), under which the partners
  provide the operating services to Polaris Acceptance. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.6]
- The operating TECHNOLOGY did not transfer with the business: §6.18 provides that any
  technology owned or used by either party "shall be and at all times shall remain the
  property of such Party," and that the partnership "shall not have any interest in such
  Technology"; the defined "System Technology" (CDF's hardware/software for invoice
  purchasing, cash application, credit underwriting, portfolio control, floorcheck
  verifications, etc.) remains CDF's, with only a contingent license to the partnership/PAI on
  buyout or dissolution. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §6.18]
- The trade name is held contingently: upon dissolution PAI succeeds to the name "Polaris
  Acceptance" and CDF/CDFJV retain no rights (except limited run-off/liquidation use). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §6.19]
- Origin of the financing portfolio (background from the agreement): on March 1, 1996 CDF
  contributed its then-existing portfolio of commercial finance business supporting Polaris to
  Polaris Acceptance under a Contribution Agreement, encumbered by an equalization payment;
  PAI later exercised its option to raise its equity share to 50% with a corresponding capital
  contribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.5, §1.9]

## Bridging / operating and supply arrangements at closing
- The shared substrate is allocated by a set of Definitive Agreements executed alongside the
  JV Agreement: the Partnership Agreement, a Credit and Security Agreement (Polaris Acceptance
  and CDF), the three Services Agreements, the Manufacturer's Repurchase Agreement, a
  Contribution Agreement, a Program Letter, and a License Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.6]
- Exclusivity/referral: during the term Polaris agrees to recommend that all inventory
  (floor-plan) financing for Polaris products be provided by Polaris Acceptance and covenants
  not to arrange a competing inventory-finance venture (§1.10, §6.17). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.10, §6.17]
- The concurrently executed Amended and Restated Manufacturer's Repurchase Agreement
  (February 28, 2011), among Polaris Industries Inc. (Minnesota), Polaris Industries Inc.
  (Delaware, "Manufacturer"), Polaris Sales Inc., and Polaris Acceptance, obligates the
  Polaris entities to repurchase repossessed and financed trade-in inventory, limited to an
  annual maximum of not more than 15 percent of the prior calendar year's average month-end
  balances of inventory financed by Polaris Acceptance. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.2 Repurchase Agreement, preamble and §1]
- The filing states Polaris will continue to account for its investment in Polaris Acceptance
  under the equity method. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction re-executed a 50/50 general partnership (Polaris Acceptance) between a
  Polaris subsidiary and a GE-affiliated finance subsidiary to provide floor-plan financing
  for Polaris products, extending the term to February 28, 2017; the financing operation sits
  in a separate legal vehicle while the operating processes and technology that run it remain
  owned by the two parents and are supplied to the partnership under Services, Credit and
  Security, and Manufacturer's Repurchase agreements rather than transferred into it. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-020591, Form 8-K EX-10.1 JV Agreement §1.1-§1.6, §6.18-§6.19]
