# Structural sub-dossier — Gores Holdings II, Inc. business combination with Greenlight Holding II Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Gores Holdings II, Inc., a Delaware corporation, describes itself in its own proxy
  as "a special purpose acquisition company formed for the purpose of effecting a
  merger, capital stock exchange, asset acquisition, stock purchase, reorganization
  or similar business combination with one or more businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- On June 21, 2018, the Company and two of its wholly-owned merger subsidiaries
  entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Greenlight Holding II Corporation
  ("Greenlight") and PE Greenlight Holdings, LLC (the "Platinum Stockholder"),
  structured as two sequential mergers (First Merger Sub into Greenlight, then
  Greenlight into Second Merger Sub, surviving as "Verra Mobility Holdings, LLC"),
  with aggregate consideration of approximately $2.3 billion subject to
  working-capital/indebtedness adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- The Company describes itself as changing its own name to "Verra Mobility
  Corporation" in connection with closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- Greenlight's operating business, Verra Mobility, is described as "a leading
  provider of smart mobility technology solutions and services throughout the United
  States, Canada and Europe," offering toll and violations management, title and
  registration, and automated safety solutions to rental-car companies, fleet
  management companies, municipalities, and violation-issuing authorities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- Verra Mobility (formerly ATS Consolidated Inc.) had itself been acquired by
  Platinum Equity in a separate, earlier merger on May 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent and post-closing governance
- Alec Gores (the Company's Chairman) is the brother of Tom Gores (CEO/Chairman of
  Platinum Equity); Platinum Equity is described as already "currently" holding "a
  controlling stake in Verra Mobility through the Platinum Stockholder" before
  closing. Post-closing, the Platinum Stockholder was set to retain ~34% of voting
  power (up to 47% under maximum redemptions) and the right to nominate up to three
  directors, one being Verra Mobility's then-CEO and two Platinum representatives
  (one to chair the board). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- Post-combination ownership was structured as: public stockholders ~26%, Private
  Placement Investors ~28%, Initial Stockholders (Sponsor) ~4%, and Greenlight
  Stockholders ~42%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- For financial-reporting purposes, the proxy's pro forma disclosure identifies Verra
  Mobility (Greenlight) — not the Company — as the accounting acquirer, citing the
  Greenlight Stockholders' ~42% retained equity/voting rights and that "the ongoing
  senior management of the Company will be entirely comprised of Verra Mobility
  employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing — product, processes, and people
- The customer-facing product/service lines (toll and violations management, title
  and registration, automated safety solutions) crossed intact as Verra Mobility's
  pre-existing operating business, layered under the Company's public legal shell
  rather than being newly assembled at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- Management continuity: "We anticipate that all of the executive officers of Verra
  Mobility will remain with the post-combination company," with several individuals
  including Verra Mobility's then-CEO nominated as directors of the post-combination
  company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- The proxy states the most significant factor in the accounting-acquirer
  determination was "the fact that management of Verra Mobility will continue to
  manage the company upon completion of the merger" — i.e., the operating-personnel/
  management tier was retained together with the product/process tier rather than
  separated from it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- A Tax Receivable Agreement was entered into at closing between the post-combination
  company and the Platinum Stockholder, providing for payment to the Platinum
  Stockholder of 50% of certain net cash tax savings realized after closing from the
  increased tax basis of intangible assets from a pre-closing acquisition (Highway
  Toll Administration, LLC). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
- Closing was conditioned on stockholder approval of the Business Combination
  Proposal, a Nasdaq Proposal (required because the share issuance exceeded 20% of
  outstanding common stock under Nasdaq Listing Rules), and a Charter Approval
  Proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- This was a whole-business combination structured as a two-step reverse merger in
  which a special purpose acquisition company (the legal/public vehicle) absorbed an
  already-operating business (Verra Mobility, via its parent Greenlight), renamed
  itself after the target, and — per the proxy's own accounting-acquirer analysis —
  treated the target's management, employees, and controlling equity/voting position
  as the tier that continued, with the SPAC contributing primarily its public
  listing, cash trust, and legal-entity shell. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006535, DEFM14A]
