## Public-reporting status of the seller after closing (feasibility-relevant)
- The seller (ATP) filed no Form 10-K after its fiscal-year-2011 annual report (filed
  March 15, 2012); no audited annual financial statements exist covering the ~2016-2018
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-117694, 10-K (FY2011)]
- The seller's final SEC filings are two Forms 8-K in January 2014 reporting the
  resignation of the entire outside board of directors and of the President (Leland E.
  Tate) and Chief Financial Officer (Albert L. Reese, Jr.), who continued only as
  consultants from February 1, 2014 — a wind-down of the registrant's governance and
  management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-031095, 8-K Item 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-022137, 8-K Item 5.02]
- The seller's Securities Exchange Act registration was administratively REVOKED by
  Commission order on December 4, 2014 under Section 12(j). [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1123647/999999999714015631/9999999997-14-015631-index.htm]

## Realized record of the transferred assets (no SEC primary record → uncertain)
- The buyer of the assets, Bennu Oil & Gas, LLC, was not an SEC-reporting registrant;
  consequently no public SEC periodic report documents the operating trajectory of the
  transferred Gulf of Mexico properties during the ~2016-2018 outcome window, and the
  realized operating outcome of the transaction is UNCERTAIN on the primary record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Reputable trade press (secondary) reports that Bennu Oil & Gas, LLC filed Chapter 7
  bankruptcy in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court in Houston in December 2016 and had shut down
  operations ("Everything has been shut down in an environmentally responsible way"), and
  describes the company as "originally founded to buy ATP Oil and Gas Corp.'s drilling
  assets." [SRC: https://www.okenergytoday.com/2016/12/texas-based-bennu-oil-gas-goes-belly/]
