## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- HighPoint Resources Corporation (CIK 0001725526) continued as an independent,
  NYSE-listed (ticker "HPR"), Exchange Act-reporting public company from the 2018
  closing through early 2021, filing Forms 10-K for fiscal years 2018, 2019 and 2020.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-19-000062, Form 10-K FY2018] [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-20-000031, Form 10-K FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Consolidated net income for the year ended December 31, 2018 was $121,220 thousand;
  the company then reported a consolidated net loss of $(134,830) thousand for fiscal
  year 2019, followed by a consolidated net loss of $(1,163,981) thousand for fiscal
  year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The fiscal year 2020 net loss was driven substantially by a $1,285,085 thousand
  impairment and abandonment expense, versus $9,642 thousand in fiscal year 2019 and
  $719 thousand in fiscal year 2018 — total operating expenses rose to $1,576,755
  thousand in 2020 against total operating revenues of only $250,347 thousand that year
  (down from $452,659 thousand in 2019 and $453,017 thousand in 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Basic and diluted net loss per common share for fiscal year 2020 was $(274.65),
  compared with $(32.04) per share in fiscal year 2019 and net income of $32.19/$32.03
  (basic/diluted) per share in fiscal year 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Operations]

## Documented events over time
- Effective October 30, 2020, HighPoint effected a 1-for-50 reverse stock split of its
  common stock and reduced authorized shares from 400,000,000 to 8,000,000, and filed a
  Form 8-K recasting its FY2019 Form 10-K and 2020 proxy statement to reflect the split.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-319337, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- HighPoint's independent auditor's report on its financial statements disclosed
  "substantial doubt about [HighPoint's] ability to continue as a going concern," tied
  to the company's potential inability to comply with financial covenants under its
  Credit Facility and insufficient cash on hand or available liquidity to repay
  outstanding debt in the event of default as of December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1A Risk Factors]
- On February 1, 2021, HighPoint and its subsidiary HighPoint Operating Corporation
  entered a Consent and Agreement with their credit-facility administrative agent
  providing that an anticipated "going concern" opinion in the FY2020 audit would not
  itself trigger a default under the credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000002, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On March 12, 2021, at a special stockholders meeting, HighPoint's stockholders
  approved an Agreement and Plan of Merger (dated November 9, 2020, as amended January
  29, 2021) providing for HighPoint's acquisition by Bonanza Creek Energy, Inc., and
  also approved a prepackaged Chapter 11 plan as an alternative path to the same merger
  and restructuring transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-080051, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- On March 14, 2021, HighPoint filed voluntary petitions under Chapter 11 of the
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware to
  effectuate its prepackaged plan of reorganization implementing the merger with
  Bonanza Creek; under the plan, holders of HighPoint Operating Corporation's senior
  notes were to receive approximately 30.4% of Bonanza Creek's fully diluted common
  stock plus $100 million of new Bonanza Creek senior notes, while holders of existing
  HighPoint equity interests were to receive approximately 1.6% of Bonanza Creek's fully
  diluted common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-080407, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed HighPoint's prepackaged plan of reorganization on March
  18, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-091807, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On April 1, 2021, Bonanza Creek Energy, Inc. completed its acquisition of HighPoint
  pursuant to the confirmed prepackaged plan: a merger subsidiary merged with and into
  HighPoint (HighPoint surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Bonanza Creek), each
  eligible HighPoint common share converted into the right to receive 0.11464 shares of
  Bonanza Creek common stock, and HighPoint's credit facility was terminated with
  outstanding obligations paid in full. HighPoint common stock was suspended from
  trading on the NYSE prior to market open on April 5, 2021 and ceased to be listed
  there. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-045683, Form 8-K Introductory Note / Item 1.02 / cover page]
- HighPoint filed a Form 15-12B to deregister its common stock under the Exchange Act on
  April 19, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-052036, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The surviving public registrant from the 2018 combination (HighPoint Resources
  Corporation) reported positive net income in its first post-closing fiscal year
  (2018: $121.2 million) but swung to a net loss in fiscal year 2019 ($(134.8) million)
  and a substantially larger net loss in fiscal year 2020 ($(1,164.0) million, driven by
  a $1,285.1 million impairment/abandonment charge), executed a 1-for-50 reverse stock
  split in October 2020, and disclosed a going-concern qualification tied to credit-
  facility covenant risk in its FY2020 Form 10-K. Approximately three years after the
  2018 closing, on March 14, 2021, the entity filed a prepackaged Chapter 11 case that
  was confirmed March 18, 2021 and consummated April 1, 2021 as a merger into Bonanza
  Creek Energy, Inc., under which former noteholders received roughly 30.4% and former
  equityholders roughly 1.6% of the acquirer's fully diluted common stock, and the
  entity's own NYSE listing and SEC registration terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001725526-21-000016, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-080407, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-045683, Form 8-K]
