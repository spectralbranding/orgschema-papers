## Legal-vehicle change over the outcome window
- By December 31, 2019, the registrant owned, and planned to continue to own, all or
  substantially all of its assets through JLLIPT Holdings LP, a Delaware limited
  partnership referred to as its "operating partnership," of which the registrant is the
  initial limited partner, with JLLIPT Holdings GP, LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary) as
  sole general partner; the Form 10-K describes this as an "Umbrella Partnership Real
  Estate Investment Trust" ("UPREIT") structure "intended to facilitate tax-free
  contributions of properties" to the operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-20-000018, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The registrant's own description of its management arrangement shifted in wording from
  "externally managed" (used in the FY2015 filing) to "externally advised" in the FY2019
  filing, with LaSalle Investment Management, Inc. continuing as Advisor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-20-000018, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Leadership at the outcome date
- The FY2019 Form 10-K is signed by C. Allan Swaringen in the capacity of President and
  Chief Executive Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-20-000018, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the roughly four years traceable through the FY2019 Form 10-K, the registrant's
  reported portfolio grew to 77 properties in 20 states, total assets grew from
  approximately $1.32 billion to approximately $2.53 billion, and net income showed
  volatility (a dip in FY2016 to $5,540 thousand from $18,351 thousand in FY2015) before
  rising to $100,002 thousand by FY2019. The registrant also documented a change in its
  legal holding structure, adopting an UPREIT operating-partnership arrangement, while
  retaining its external-advisory relationship with LaSalle Investment Management and
  the same signing Chief Executive Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-20-000018, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
