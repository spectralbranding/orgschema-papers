## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K attaching, as Exhibit EX-99.1, a United States Bankruptcy Court Official Form 26 (Form B26) periodic report under Bankruptcy Rule 2015.3, reporting the value, operations, and profitability of the non-debtor entities in which the estate held a substantial or controlling interest, as of September 30, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-12-000109, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Official Form 26), filed 2012-12-28]
- The Form 26 establishes that Eastman Kodak Company and affiliates ("the Debtors") were in a Chapter 11 case, In re Eastman Kodak Company, et al., Case No. 12-10202 (ALG), in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York; it was signed by the Chief Accounting Officer and Corporate Controller. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-12-000109, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Official Form 26)]
- Its Exhibit C legal-entity chart lists roughly 144 rows enumerating the combined enterprise: the New Jersey parent (Eastman Kodak Company) plus numerous wholly and partly owned foreign subsidiaries, holding companies, and minority venture investments across many countries. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-12-000109, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Official Form 26)]
- This drawn Form 26 is a periodic operations/valuation report, NOT the transaction-terms document; the closing structure of the reorganization and the concurrent imaging-business sale is sourced from the entity's contemporaneous plan and closing filings below. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-12-000109, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Official Form 26)]

## Transaction type and legal vehicle at closing
- On January 19, 2012, Eastman Kodak Company and certain subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11, Case No. 12-10202 (ALG), Southern District of New York. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On August 23, 2013, the Bankruptcy Court entered a Confirmation Order (Docket No. 4966) confirming the revised First Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of Eastman Kodak Company and its Debtor Affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## What did NOT cross to the new owners at closing (equity / debt)
- The Plan cancelled the Debtors' pre-existing equity interests: no holder of an Equity Interest received any distribution, and on the Effective Date all such Equity Interests were cancelled. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Second Lien Notes and Unsecured Notes (including the 7.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2017, 7.25% Senior Notes due 2013, 9.20% Debentures due 2021, 9.95% Debentures due 2018, 9.75% Senior Secured Notes due 2018, and 10.625% Senior Secured Notes due 2019) were cancelled upon effectiveness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-355259, Form 8-K Item 3.03]

## New ownership, capital, and funding structure at closing
- The Plan issued a new class of common stock; a total of 41,700,168 shares of New Common Stock were to be issued and outstanding to holders of allowed general unsecured claims, backstop parties, and rights-offering participants; former creditors and backstop parties thereby became the owners. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Sources of funds under the Plan: a $695 million emergence term loan facility ($420 million six-year first-lien term loan plus $275 million seven-year second-lien term loan); approximately $406 million in rights-offering proceeds; a $200 million five-year senior secured asset-based revolving credit facility; and $525 million of cash proceeds from the KPP Global Settlement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Plan required the Company to adopt fresh-start accounting as of the Effective Date, recording assets and liabilities at fair value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## People / management continuity across the ownership change
- On the Effective Date, nine incumbent directors ceased to serve and six new directors joined the board; Antonio M. Perez continued as Chief Executive Officer, Rebecca A. Roof continued as Chief Financial Officer, and Eric H. Samuels continued as Chief Accounting Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Items 1.03 / 5.02]

## Customer-facing product businesses sold, and the arrangement bridging their operation
- On August 30, 2013, the Company entered into an Amended and Restated Stock and Asset Purchase Agreement (amending the original agreement dated April 26, 2013) with KPP Trustees Limited ("KPP") transferring the Personalized Imaging and Document Imaging businesses (together, "the Business") to KPP subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-13-000065, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The customer-facing product lines (Personalized Imaging and Document Imaging) were the assets crossing the ownership boundary to KPP. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-13-000065, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Transitional operating arrangement bridging the substrate: the agreement provided for a series of deferred closings in certain foreign jurisdictions after the initial closing; the Company would continue to operate the Business in those deferred-closing jurisdictions, subject to covenants, delivering to (or receiving from) a KPP subsidiary at each deferred closing a true-up payment reflecting the actual economic benefit or detriment from the initial closing through the applicable deferred closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-13-000065, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At the initial closing, promissory notes issued by a Company subsidiary were to be delivered to KPP or its subsidiaries to fund local-currency payments required at the deferred closings. [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-13-000065, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Effectiveness
- On September 3, 2013, the Plan became effective and the Debtors emerged from their Chapter 11 cases; upon effectiveness all previously issued and outstanding common stock and other equity interests were cancelled and the new common stock was issued. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-355259, Form 8-K Item 3.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A court-supervised Chapter 11 reorganization in which the pre-existing equity and note obligations were cancelled and ownership passed to former creditors and backstop parties, while operating leadership (CEO, CFO, CAO) continued; concurrently the Personalized Imaging and Document Imaging product businesses were sold to a pension-plan trustee (KPP), with the seller continuing to operate those businesses under a deferred-closing and true-up arrangement until each local legal transfer completed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-352328, Form 8-K Item 1.03] [SRC: SEC accession 0000031235-13-000065, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
