# Structural sub-dossier — 8point3 Energy Partners LP merger with affiliates of Capital Dynamics (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- 8point3 Energy Partners LP, a Delaware limited partnership formed to own, operate and
  acquire solar energy generation projects, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  and Purchase Agreement dated February 5, 2018 with its General Partner, its operating
  company (OpCo), Holdings, and a group of newly formed acquisition entities affiliated
  with CD Clean Energy and Infrastructure V JV, LLC ("Capital Dynamics"), an investment
  vehicle managed by Capital Dynamics, Inc., an independent global asset manager
  investing in private equity, private credit and clean energy infrastructure.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a series of mergers: two merger subs formed by Parent
  merged successively into OpCo (the "OpCo Mergers"), and a separate merger sub merged
  into the Partnership itself (the "Partnership Merger"), with OpCo and the Partnership
  surviving each merger as the continuing entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the sale process, the Partnership's Class A shares traded on the NASDAQ Global
  Select Market under the symbol "CAFD"; the Partnership was 35.5%-owned (non-economic
  controlling interest) by itself through OpCo, with First Solar, Inc. and SunPower
  Corporation (together, the "Sponsors") collectively holding a 64.5% noncontrolling
  economic interest in OpCo and 100% of Holdings, the parent of the General Partner.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- At the Partnership Merger Effective Time, each outstanding Class A share and each
  outstanding OpCo Common/Subordinated Unit converted into the right to receive cash
  merger consideration; the deal was funded by debt financing from Mitsubishi UFJ
  Financial Group, Inc. and equity financing from investment funds managed by Capital
  Dynamics, Inc. and co-investors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]
- The mergers closed on June 19, 2018, at a final merger consideration of $12.48 per
  unit/share; on the same date OpCo's existing Credit and Guaranty Agreement and a
  Promissory Note owed to First Solar Asset Management, LLC were terminated and repaid in
  full with the new debt and equity proceeds, with no early-termination penalties.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]

## What the acquired entity comprised at closing (asset/product tier)
- OpCo, the operating company, directly or indirectly owned and controlled the
  Partnership's various subsidiaries, solar generation projects and assets; the
  Partnership's business was described as owning, operating and acquiring solar energy
  generation projects. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- In connection with the sale process, two projects (California Flats, under a First
  Solar right-of-first-offer, and Boulder Solar, under a SunPower right-of-first-offer)
  were sold by the Sponsors directly to third parties/Capital Dynamics affiliates at
  prices above what had been offered to the Partnership, rather than being contributed to
  or retained within the acquired Partnership Group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]

## Operational-process tier: what stayed with the Sponsors vs. what crossed
- The Partnership had entered into an Amended and Restated Omnibus Agreement with First
  Solar, SunPower, the General Partner, OpCo and Holdings, under which the Sponsors
  agreed to provide operation, maintenance and asset-management services (the "O&M
  Agreements") plus certain performance/availability guarantees for the underlying
  projects. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- Simultaneous with closing, the parties terminated the Omnibus Agreement itself,
  including the Sponsors' related indemnification obligations for ownership/operation of
  their contributed projects, and the Sponsors were fully released from credit-support
  obligations (performance/surety bonds, guaranty agreements) tied to those projects.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- Notwithstanding that termination, "the O&M Agreements will remain effective after the
  Closing under the same terms and obligations" — i.e., the day-to-day operating/
  maintenance process layer for the underlying solar projects continued to run on the
  pre-existing Sponsor-affiliated service contracts rather than being replaced or
  internalized at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- Separately, the Merger Agreement provided for the Partnership to negotiate in good
  faith one or more transition services agreement(s) with the Sponsors, under which the
  Sponsors or their affiliates would provide information technology, financial reporting,
  tax, treasury, insurance, legal and other general corporate support services to bridge
  the surviving entity's back-office substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]

## People/organization tier: what did not cross with the acquired entity
- No Partnership Group Entity (and, to the Partnership's knowledge, no Non-Controlled
  Partnership Group Entity) had ever had any employees since inception; none was party to
  any collective bargaining or other labor-related agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- The General Partner's directors and executive officers were designees/employees of the
  Sponsors (e.g., the CEO was also SunPower's EVP/CFO; other directors/officers held
  concurrent First Solar or SunPower executive roles); these directors and officers who
  were also Sponsor employees "are expected to continue their employment with the
  Sponsors," and none was entitled to severance, change-of-control payments, or golden
  parachute compensation in connection with the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A]
- At the Partnership Merger Effective Time on June 19, 2018, seven named directors
  (Boynton, Bradley, Jackson, O'Connor, Szydlowski, Widmar, Yackira) resigned from the
  General Partner's board and its committees, and five named officers (Boynton,
  Schumaker, Dymbort, Jackson, Gardner) resigned as officers of the General Partner.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- At the OpCo Merger 1 Effective Time, Holdings transferred 100% of the membership
  interests in the General Partner and 100% of the Incentive Distribution Rights in OpCo
  to 8point3 Solar (a Parent-affiliated entity) for no additional consideration.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]
- In connection with closing, the Partnership requested that NASDAQ file a Form 25 to
  remove the Class A shares from listing and to deregister them under Section 12(b) of
  the Exchange Act; the Class A shares were suspended from trading on NASDAQ prior to the
  opening of trading on June 20, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]
- The Partnership filed a Form 15 on June 28, 2018 certifying termination of registration
  of the Class A shares under Section 12(g) and suspension of its duty to file reports
  under Sections 13/15(d) of the Exchange Act, recording approximately 1 holder of record
  as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008779, Form 15-12B]
- On June 8, 2018, the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States (CFIUS)
  notified the Partnership that it had completed review of the merger and found no
  unresolved national security concerns, one of the customary closing conditions.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-007800, Form 8-K (2018-06-11)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-entity cash merger of the acquired asset/product tier
  (the Partnership's solar generation project portfolio, held through OpCo) into Capital
  Dynamics-affiliated acquisition vehicles with a same-closing termination of the
  Sponsors' overarching Omnibus Agreement (and its indemnification/credit-support
  obligations), while the underlying project-level O&M service contracts with the
  Sponsors were expressly carried forward unchanged and a new transition services
  agreement was negotiated to bridge back-office functions; separately, the Sponsor-
  affiliated directors and officers who had governed the General Partner (an entity that,
  like the rest of the Partnership Group, had never had its own employees) resigned at
  closing and were expected to remain employed by the Sponsors rather than move with the
  acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-110072, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-008042, Form 8-K (2018-06-20)]
