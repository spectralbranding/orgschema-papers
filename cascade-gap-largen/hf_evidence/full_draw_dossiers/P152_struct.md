# Structural sub-dossier — Bausch Health acquisition of certain assets of Synergy Pharmaceuticals Inc. (closing structure)

## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn Form 8-K reports that on December 11, 2018, Bausch Health Companies Inc. (the "Company") and its wholly-owned subsidiary Bausch Health Ireland Limited (an Irish private limited company, the "Purchaser") entered into a "stalking horse" Asset Purchase Agreement with Synergy Pharmaceuticals Inc. and its subsidiary Synergy Advanced Pharmaceuticals, Inc. (Delaware corporations, collectively the "Sellers"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The drawn 8-K is an announcement of a proposed acquisition, not a completed-deal record: the Sellers were debtors in Chapter 11 cases before the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York (commenced December 12, 2018), and the Purchaser was to be designated the "stalking horse" bidder in a court-supervised auction under Section 363 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code, with its offer being the standard against which any other qualifying offers would be evaluated. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The full text of the Asset Purchase Agreement was not attached to the drawn 8-K; the filing states the Agreement would be filed with the Company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction type and legal vehicle as structured
- The transaction was structured as an ASSET purchase (not a whole-company or stock acquisition): the Purchaser agreed to acquire "certain assets" of the Sellers and to assume only certain specified liabilities, with the assets acquired "free and clear of liabilities except certain expressly assumed liabilities." [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, EX-99.1 press release]
- The acquiring legal vehicle was Bausch Health Ireland Limited, a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Consideration and bid mechanics at closing (as agreed)
- The Purchaser agreed to acquire the Assets for approximately $200 million in cash (subject to adjustment for cure amounts and certain accrued liabilities) plus assumption of the specified Liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Purchaser was to deposit $18.5 million with an escrow agent, credited against the purchase price upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- If the Sellers accepted a higher or better competing bid at auction, the Sellers could owe the Company/Purchaser expense reimbursement up to $1.95 million and a break-up fee of $7 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Completion was conditioned on Bankruptcy Court approval of the Transaction, expiration/termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, and certain minimum inventory levels at closing, among other customary conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — tier by tier
- Customer-facing product/asset: the acquired assets included the worldwide rights to the Trulance (plecanatide) product (a once-daily tablet for chronic idiopathic constipation and IBS-with-constipation) and the investigational compound dolcanatide, together with intellectual property, customer and vendor contracts, accounts receivable, and goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, EX-99.1 press release]
- Producing/delivering processes: the acquirer stated the acquired product "complements our existing Salix products and allows us to effectively leverage our existing GI sales force" — i.e., the product was to be run through the acquirer's own pre-existing Salix commercial, supply, and distribution processes rather than Synergy's operating processes crossing intact. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019) business discussion]
- People/organization: the acquirer "committed to make employment offers to a number of the sales and commercial employees of Synergy" — a selective offer to certain personnel, not a transfer of the Sellers' whole organization or management. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, EX-99.1 press release]
- Controlling intent: the acquirer characterized the purchase as a "strategic bolt-on" to fold the acquired product into its existing GI/Salix portfolio and sales footprint. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, EX-99.1 press release]

## Composition of what crossed (purchase-consideration allocation)
- Total fair value of consideration transferred was $180 million, allocated to product brand intangible assets of $159 million (estimated 7-year useful life), goodwill of $3 million, and total identifiable net assets of $177 million (net of assumed accounts payable of $1 million and accrued expenses of $17 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019) acquisitions note]

## Transitional / bridging arrangements
- No transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement between the Company and the Sellers is described in the drawn 8-K, the EX-99.1 press release, or the FY2019 acquisitions note; the acquired product instead relied on the acquirer's pre-existing Salix sales, supply, and distribution channels. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019) business discussion]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a court-supervised Section 363 asset purchase in which a discrete customer-facing product (Trulance / plecanatide), its intellectual property, contracts, receivables, and goodwill crossed the ownership boundary into an acquirer subsidiary for ~$180 million, while the product was placed into the acquirer's own existing GI/Salix commercial and supply processes and only a selected subset of the Sellers' sales and commercial employees received employment offers — the producing/delivering processes and the selling organization did not cross intact. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-18-000074, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019) acquisitions note]
- CONSUMMATION: the transaction CLOSED (the ownership boundary was crossed). Bausch Health's agreement was designated the highest and best offer on February 26, 2019 because no party submitted a higher and better bid, so the scheduled auction did not proceed; the Bankruptcy Court was expected to approve the sale on March 1, 2019. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/synergy-pharmaceuticals-confirms-bausch-health-as-successful-bidder-for-its-business-assets-300802756.html] The acquiring registrant's subsequent annual report confirms the acquisition of the certain assets of Synergy was completed in March 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019) subsequent/acquisitions discussion]
