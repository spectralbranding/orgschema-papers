> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction record; the outcome window is fixed at the fiscal-year-2018 annual

> It contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status in the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- ONE Gas continued as an independent, NYSE-listed public company and filed an annual
  report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018, reporting as registrant
  ONE Gas, Inc. of Tulsa, Oklahoma. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The company continued to file annual reports on Form 10-K in every intervening year
  (FY2014 through FY2018), so a continuous public outcome record exists across the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Realized financial trajectory (primary-sourced scalar)
- Net income was $172.2 million ($3.25 per diluted share) for the year ended December 31,
  2018, compared with $163.0 million ($3.08 per diluted share) for 2017 and $140.1 million
  ($2.65 per diluted share) for 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018), Selected Financial Results]
- The five-year selected-financial-data net income series was $172.2 million (2018),
  $163.0 million (2017), $140.1 million (2016), $119.0 million (2015) and $109.8 million
  (2014), i.e., a monotonically rising figure across the post-separation period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018), Selected Financial Data]
- Total revenues for 2018 were $1,633 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Realized structural continuity of the business
- The company operated in one reportable business segment — regulated public utilities
  delivering natural gas — serving approximately 2.2 million customers across three states
  through three divisions — Oklahoma Natural Gas, Kansas Gas Service and Texas Gas
  Service. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The company remained the largest natural gas distributor in Oklahoma and Kansas and the
  third largest in Texas in terms of customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- At February 1, 2019, approximately 700 of an estimated 3,500 employees were represented
  by collective-bargaining units under collective-bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Approximately $158 million of goodwill was recorded on the Consolidated Balance Sheet;
  the FY2018 10-K's references to goodwill impairment appear as annual-testing accounting
  policy and risk-factor language, and no goodwill-impairment charge is stated as taken in
  the year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The 10-K's references to divestitures appear as forward-looking risk-factor language
  about acquisition/divestiture strategy generally; no divestiture of the distribution
  business or its divisions is reported in the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed FY2018 outcome window the separated entity remained an independent
  NYSE-listed public company operating three natural-gas distribution divisions
  (Oklahoma Natural Gas, Kansas Gas Service, Texas Gas Service), with approximately
  2.2 million customers, headcount
  at roughly 3,500, and net income rising each year from $109.8 million (2014) to
  $172.2 million (2018), with no divestiture of the core business and no goodwill
  impairment charge reported. [SRC: SEC accession 0001587732-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
