## Note on the drawn filing

[SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-219041, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- That drawn filing establishes the pendency of the Chapter 11 proceeding (jurisdiction held "since March 14, 2018") but is not the transaction-structuring document; the structure of the transaction is sourced below from the registrant's contemporaneous primary filings. ## Transaction and parties at closing
- On March 14, 2018, iHeartMedia, Inc. and certain direct and indirect subsidiaries (the "Debtors") filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions for reorganization in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, Houston Division, jointly administered as In re iHeartMedia, Inc., et al., Case No. 18-31274 (MI); the Debtors continued to operate as debtors-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-083055, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. ("CCOH") and its direct and indirect subsidiaries did NOT file petitions and were not Debtors in the cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-083055, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Concurrent with the petitions, the parties furnished proposed execution versions of a restructuring support agreement and related term sheet reflecting an agreement in principle between iHeartMedia and holders of more than $10 billion of its outstanding debt as well as its equityholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-083055, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- On January 22, 2019, the Bankruptcy Court entered the Confirmation Order approving the "Modified Fifth Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of iHeartMedia, Inc. and its Debtor Affiliates" (the "Plan"), attached as Exhibits 2.1 and 99.1 to the reporting 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, by tier
- Controlling intent / ownership: the Plan cancelled iHeartMedia's outstanding equity on the Effective Date and issued Reorganized iHeart Equity to creditors — senior creditors (2019 PGN and Term Loan Credit Agreement claims) receiving 60.82% and other PGN claimholders 33.18% of Reorganized iHeart Equity, with 2021/Legacy Notes claimholders 5.00% and existing iHeart interest-holders a pro rata share of 1%; control thus passed from the prior equity holders to the reorganized creditor body. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Business / value-capture model and legal vehicle: the Plan "will result in the separation (the 'Separation') of CCOH from the Company, creating two independent companies," splitting the radio/audio business (retained by Reorganized iHeart) from the outdoor-advertising business (CCOH). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Customer-facing product / operating assets: to effect the Separation the Plan provides for "the transfer of the iHeartMedia radio business to a newly formed subsidiary of iHeartCommunications," after which CCOH merges with and into Clear Channel Holdings, Inc. (a Debtor subsidiary), which survives and is renamed Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. ("New CCOH"); iHeartCommunications retains approximately 89.1% of New CCOH common stock (325,726,917 shares), which is then transferred to senior claimholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Capital-structure term of the deal: the Plan "contemplates a restructuring of the Debtors that will reduce the Debtors' debt from approximately $16.1 billion to $5.75 billion." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Shared operating substrate and the contractual bridges at closing
- Before the Separation the two businesses shared a corporate-services substrate governed by an existing Corporate Services Agreement between CCOH and iHeartCommunications, including a cash-sweep arrangement, and by agreements/licenses under which CCOH paid royalties to iHeartMedia and the Debtors for trademarks and other intellectual property. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- At the Effective Date that shared substrate is unwound and re-bridged: the cash-sweep arrangement under the Corporate Services Agreement "will be terminated," the royalty-bearing IP/trademark agreements "will be terminated," and "a new transition services agreement will supersede and replace the existing Corporate Services Agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Additional bridging arrangements between the two separated entities: iHeartMedia "will provide an unsecured revolving line of credit in an aggregate amount not to exceed $200 million to CCOH for a period of no more than three years following the Effective Date"; iHeartMedia will indemnify CCOH for 50% of certain Separation-related tax liabilities above $5.0 million (aggregate liability capped at $15.0 million); and the parties will provide mutual releases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction is a court-supervised reorganization that recapitalized the enterprise (equity cancelled and reissued to creditors; debt reduced per the Plan) and separated a previously shared two-business group into two independent companies — the retained radio/audio business and the outdoor-advertising business (New CCOH) — with the historically shared corporate-services, cash-management, and IP substrate terminated and re-bridged at closing by a new transition services agreement, a $200 million revolving credit line, and tax-indemnity and release provisions rather than carried across intact. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-019100, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
