# Structural sub-dossier — Impax Laboratories / Amneal Pharmaceuticals combination (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Impax Laboratories, Inc. (a Nasdaq-listed specialty pharmaceutical company) and
  Amneal Pharmaceuticals LLC (a privately held generic-drug manufacturer headquartered
  in Bridgewater, New Jersey, founded in 2002 by Chintu and Chirag Patel) agreed to
  combine their businesses pursuant to a Business Combination Agreement ("BCA") dated
  October 17, 2017 among Impax, Amneal, Atlas Holdings, Inc. (a direct wholly owned
  subsidiary of Impax, "Holdco") and K2 Merger Sub Corporation (a subsidiary of Holdco),
  as amended November 21, 2017 and December 16, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- The combination structure: (i) Merger Sub merged with and into Impax, with Impax
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Holdco; (ii) the surviving Impax entity then
  converted into a Delaware LLC ("Impax LLC"); (iii) Holdco contributed the equity
  interests of Impax LLC to Amneal in exchange for equity interests of Amneal; (iv)
  Holdco issued Class B common stock to the existing members of Amneal; and (v) Holdco
  (renamed "New Amneal") became the managing member of Amneal — an "Up-C" umbrella
  partnership structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the Combination, Holdco was renamed Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. ("New
  Amneal") and became a holding company whose principal asset is its economic interest
  in the combined Impax/Amneal business, conducted primarily through its direct
  subsidiary Amneal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration: each outstanding Impax share converted into one share of New Amneal
  Class A Common Stock; Impax stockholders collectively held approximately 25% and the
  Existing Amneal Members approximately 75% of voting/economic interests immediately
  post-closing; following closing and a concurrent PIPE investment from TPG Improv
  Holdings, L.P. and Fidelity-affiliated funds, the Existing Amneal Members were expected
  to hold approximately 60% of New Amneal voting power and the PIPE/other institutional
  investors approximately 15%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- New Amneal Class A Common Stock was to be listed on the NYSE under ticker "AMRX";
  Impax's existing common stock traded on the NASDAQ Global Select Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent / control tier
- Because the Existing Amneal Members would hold a majority of New Amneal's voting
  power, New Amneal qualifies and will operate as a "controlled company" under NYSE
  rules, exempting it from certain NYSE corporate-governance requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- Post-closing board: up to 13 directors — 7 designated by the Amneal Group
  (including Chirag Patel and Chintu Patel as Co-Chairmen), 5 designated by Impax
  (including Paul M. Bisaro as Executive Chairman and Robert L. Burr, the sitting Impax
  Chairman, as Lead Independent Director), and Robert A. Stewart as CEO and director
  (contingent on his continuing as President of Amneal through closing; absent that
  "Executive Event," Bisaro becomes CEO and the board shrinks to 11 with 6 Amneal
  directors). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- A Second Amended and Restated Stockholders Agreement (dated December 16, 2017) between
  Holdco and the Existing Amneal Members governs board composition, committee structure
  (Nominating, Compensation, Conflicts, and a dedicated Integration Committee), transfer
  restrictions, and termination triggers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- The Integration Committee — comprised of Chirag Patel, Chintu Patel, and Paul M.
  Bisaro — was to continue for a minimum of two years following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## Product / business-model tier
- Impax operated two segments: "Impax Generics" (bioequivalent generic pharmaceutical
  products) and "Impax Specialty Pharma" (branded products for CNS disorders and other
  select specialty segments). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- Amneal was described as one of the largest and fastest-growing U.S. generic
  pharmaceutical manufacturers, with approximately 5,000 employees across North America,
  Asia, and Europe, and a portfolio expanded into complex dosage forms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy frames a business-model rationale for combining centered on product-mix
  diversification: it describes the Combination as resulting in "significant revenue
  diversification" for the Combined Company relative to Impax's pre-combination
  concentration in a small number of top-selling generic and specialty products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## Process / operations tier
- Amneal's manufacturing footprint at signing comprised ten FDA-approved manufacturing
  facilities and seven co-located R&D centers across the United States (New York, New
  Jersey), India, and Ireland; Amneal's stated strategy was to co-locate R&D centers
  within manufacturing sites. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy states the Combined Company was "expected to manufacture and distribute its
  products from a strengthened global supply chain supporting capabilities across all
  dosage forms, with R&D and manufacturing sites in the United States, India and
  Ireland," and projected cost-saving opportunities of up to $200 million within three
  years of closing "through combined footprint and product transfer opportunities." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- A dedicated risk-factor section, "Following the Combination, the Combined Company may
  be unable to integrate operations successfully," states that Impax and Amneal "operated
  as independent public companies prior to the Combination" and warns of possible
  "diversion of ... management's attention," "disruption or interruption of ... ongoing
  businesses," and "inconsistencies in standards, control, procedures and policies"
  during integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- A further risk factor states the BCA's contemplated integration "will present
  challenges" because the two businesses "currently operate as independent businesses,"
  listing potential difficulties including inability to integrate "operations,
  technologies, products and services" and loss of customers unwilling to concentrate
  business with a single combined supplier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## People / management-continuity tier
- Immediately following closing (absent the "Executive Event"): Robert A. Stewart (an
  Amneal executive) becomes CEO of New Amneal; Paul M. Bisaro (Impax's then-President
  and CEO) becomes Executive Chairman; Bryan M. Reasons (Impax's then-CFO) becomes CFO of
  New Amneal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- For 18 months following closing, removal of Bisaro as Executive Chairman (or as CEO if
  the Executive Event occurs) without cause requires both a majority of the full New
  Amneal board and a majority of the Non-Amneal Directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- Impax's outstanding Restricted Shares and Options were to fully vest at closing and
  convert into New Amneal Class A equivalents; the BCA also provided continued
  indemnification and D&O insurance for current and former Impax directors/officers, and
  disclosed employment/severance agreements for Impax executives triggered upon
  qualifying termination following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Impax and Amneal agreed to use reasonable best efforts to cause Impax's shares to be
  removed from listing on the NASDAQ Global Select Market and deregistered under the
  Exchange Act as promptly as practicable following closing, while causing New Amneal's
  Class A Common Stock to be approved for listing on the NYSE under ticker "AMRX." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
- The BCA's Up-C structure (Holdco/New Amneal as a public parent holding an economic
  interest in the Amneal operating partnership) was structured, per the proxy, to allow
  New Amneal to realize tax benefits through an associated Tax Receivable Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined Impax's public listed corporate shell and specialty/generics
  product lines with Amneal's privately held generics manufacturing business via an
  Up-C holding-company structure (New Amneal), with the Existing Amneal Members taking
  majority economic and voting control (approximately 75%, later ~60% post-PIPE) while
  select Impax executives (CEO-designate contingency, Executive Chairman, CFO) and
  directors retained defined governance roles for a transitional period, and a dedicated
  two-year Integration Committee was created to manage the operational combination of
  two businesses the proxy repeatedly describes as still operating independently at
  signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-040279, Form DEFM14A]
