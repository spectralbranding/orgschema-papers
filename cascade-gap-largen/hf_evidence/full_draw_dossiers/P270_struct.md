# Structural sub-dossier — Blackhawk Network Holdings, Inc. acquisition (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Blackhawk Network Holdings, Inc. ("the Company"), a Delaware corporation, entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated January 15, 2018 with BHN Holdings, Inc.
  ("Parent") and BHN Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Parent, under which Merger Sub would merge with and into the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]
- Parent, Intermediate (BHN Intermediate Holdings, Inc.) and Merger Sub were each newly
  formed Delaware entities affiliated with Silver Lake Partners V, L.P. ("SLP Equity
  Investor") and P2 Capital Master Fund I, L.P. ("P2 Equity Investor"), formed solely to
  enter into and consummate the merger agreement, and none had engaged in any other
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time each outstanding share of Company common stock (other than
  specified excluded shares) was to be converted into the right to receive $45.25 per
  share in cash; upon completion the Company would be an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent and existing stockholders would cease to have an ownership
  interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on June 15, 2018: Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with
  the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, which is controlled by
  investment funds affiliated with Silver Lake Partners and P2 Capital Partners. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]

## Business model and legal vehicle
- At signing the Company described itself as a global financial technology company
  operating through two divisions: Hawk Commerce (technology solutions to businesses
  and direct to consumers) and Hawk Incentives (branded value products and rewards
  programs for enterprise, SMB and reseller partners), headquartered in Pleasanton,
  California and operating in 26 countries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company remained the surviving legal entity in the merger, continuing its
  corporate existence as a Delaware corporation and retaining the name "Blackhawk
  Network Holdings, Inc." as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, with its certificate
  of incorporation and bylaws amended and restated at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]

## People and organization crossing the boundary
- The officers of the Company immediately prior to the effective time continued as
  officers of the Company after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]
- The board composition changed at closing: Michael Bingle, Jason White and Chip
  Schroeder (directors of Merger Sub immediately before the effective time) became
  directors of the Company, and Talbott Roche (Chief Executive Officer and President),
  William Tauscher (Executive Chairman) and Alex Silver were appointed as directors
  immediately following the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]
- Restricted stock unit awards held by employees were treated differently by seniority:
  most employee awards converted into a cash right equal to the per-share merger
  consideration, while awards granted on or after January 1, 2018 to employees at
  vice-president level or higher converted into an RSU award of substantially
  equivalent value in Parent capital stock, retaining the original vesting schedule. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A]

## Public-market and disclosure obligations at closing
- Upon completion of the merger the Company common stock ceased to be publicly traded;
  the Company notified NASDAQ that the merger had been completed and requested removal
  of the Common Stock from listing on NASDAQ and its deregistration under Section 12(b)
  of the Exchange Act, effective June 15, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]
- The Company also delivered a Notice of Fundamental Change Repurchase Right and a
  Supplemental Indenture to holders of its 1.50% Convertible Senior Notes due 2022 as a
  result of the Make-Whole Fundamental Change triggered by consummation of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]
- The Company subsequently filed a Form 15-12B on 2018-06-25 to terminate registration
  of its common stock under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-029766, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- This was a whole-company going-private acquisition: a private-equity-affiliated
  acquisition vehicle acquired 100% of the Company's outstanding equity for cash, the
  Company's two-division commerce and incentives business, its legal entity, and its
  pre-existing officer group all continued forward intact into the post-closing
  structure, while board composition combined new Parent-designated directors with
  three continuing/appointed Company-side directors, and the Company's public-reporting
  obligations were removed at closing rather than any operating unit being carved out or
  left behind. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-011449, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-028701, Form 8-K]
