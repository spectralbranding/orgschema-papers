# Structural sub-dossier — Sears Holdings Corporation going-concern asset sale to Transform Holdco LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it actually establishes)
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K reporting that on November 29, 2018 the Company
  entered into Superpriority Senior and Junior Secured Debtor-in-Possession credit
  agreements (each effective November 30, 2018), following the Company's October 15, 2018
  Chapter 11 petitions; it is a financing/covenant and other-events filing, NOT the
  asset-sale terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341446, 8-K Items 1.01/2.03]
- That 8-K established the sale PROCESS, not its terms: it required the Debtors to obtain
  an acceptable "Qualified Stalking Horse Bid" by December 15, 2018 and to pursue bidding
  procedures for the sale of the "Go Forward Stores." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341446, 8-K Item 1.01]
- It also reported a November 28, 2018 sale of certain SRAC Medium Term Notes (principal
  $880,696,000) to Cyrus Capital Partners L.P. for a cash payment of $82,500,000, with net
  proceeds deposited to the Debtors' winddown account. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341446, 8-K Item 8.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Sellers = Sears Holdings Corporation and certain subsidiaries; Buyer = Transform Holdco
  LLC, an affiliate of ESL Investments, Inc. On January 17, 2019 the Sellers entered into
  an Asset Purchase Agreement under which Buyer agreed to acquire substantially all of the
  go-forward retail footprint and other assets and component businesses "as a going
  concern" (the "Going Concern Transaction"), as the successful bid under Bankruptcy-Court-
  approved Global Bidding Procedures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K Item 1.01]
- Controlling-principal fusion across both sides: Edward S. Lampert was Chairman (and CEO)
  of ESL and Chairman of the Sears Board; Kunal S. Kamlani was President of ESL and a Sears
  director; the transaction was approved on the Company's behalf by the independent
  Restructuring Committee of the Board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K Item 1.01]
- Legal vehicle: a sale under Chapter 11 (In re Sears Holdings Corporation, Case No.
  18-23538, U.S. Bankruptcy Court, S.D.N.Y.), authorized by a Sale Order entered
  February 8, 2019 and completed February 11, 2019 pursuant to the Purchase Agreement as
  amended by Amendment No. 1 (dated February 11, 2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-041193, 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary (tier level)
- Customer-facing product/assets: approximately 425 retail stores under the "Sears" and
  "Kmart" brands plus related real estate; the "Kenmore," "DieHard," "Sears Auto Centers,"
  "PartsDirect," "ServiceLive," "Sears Home Services," "Wally," "Monark Premium Appliance
  Co." and "Sears Home Improvement" businesses; the "Shop Your Way" membership program;
  various websites; and acquired inventory, receivables, equipment, intellectual property,
  data and goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"]
- Operating processes moved WITH the product: the businesses were sold "as a going
  concern," including a home delivery and retail installation business and the service/
  repair operations (Sears Home Services, Auto Centers, PartsDirect) and e-commerce
  websites — the producing/delivering operations transferred alongside the stores and
  brands rather than the product being detached from its operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"]
- People/organization moved WITH the processes: the Purchase Agreement provided that Buyer
  would offer ongoing employment to approximately 45,000 employees of the Company and its
  subsidiaries, with salary continuation, substantially comparable benefits, and
  reinstatement of the prepetition severance program through February 1, 2020 for employees
  accepting offers at close. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"]
- Consideration comprised approximately $1.4 billion in cash (plus up to $17 million of
  store cash and $35 million of credit-bid release consideration), plus Section 363(k)
  credit bids of Buyer/affiliate debt of approximately $231 million (IP/Ground Lease Term
  Loan), $125 million (FILO Facility), $544 million (Real Estate Loan 2020) and
  $433.45 million (Second Lien obligations), plus assumption of specified liabilities;
  Buyer paid a $120 million deposit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"]

## Bridging / shared-substrate arrangements at closing
- Financing bridge: obligations under the DIP Credit Agreement were to be refinanced with
  $850 million in cash from a new ABL facility, with a roll-over of approximately
  $621 million of indebtedness (including $350 million of the Junior DIP term loan and
  approximately $271 million of the Citi L/C Facility) into exit facilities of Buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Financing"]
- Specific shared assets bridged by conditions/agreements: transfer of certain KCD IP, LLC
  notes was subject to Bermuda Monetary Authority approval; assumed liabilities included
  amounts owed by Sears Re in respect of reinsurance of warranties and protection
  agreements; and a separate SHIP Purchase Agreement covered specified assets and
  liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"]
- Controlling-intent shift at closing: upon completion, Edward S. Lampert (Chairman) and
  Kunal S. Kamlani (director) each notified the Company of their decision to step down from
  the Sears Board effective February 12, 2019, their resignations relating to completion of
  the Going Concern Transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-041193, 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Structured as a Chapter 11 Section 363 going-concern asset sale, the transaction moved
  the customer-facing retail brands/stores, their producing-and-delivering service
  operations, and offers of ongoing employment to approximately 45,000 people together to
  Transform Holdco LLC — an affiliate of the Company's own Chairman and significant creditor
  (ESL/Lampert) — for approximately $1.4 billion cash plus Section 363(k) credit bids and
  assumed liabilities; it was consummated February 11, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-016016, 8-K "Assets and Consideration"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-041193, 8-K Item 2.01]
