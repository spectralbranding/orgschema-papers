## Transaction and parties at closing
- Great Plains Energy Incorporated ("Great Plains Energy"), a Missouri corporation
  incorporated in 2001 and headquartered in Kansas City, Missouri, and Westar Energy,
  Inc. ("Westar Energy"), headquartered in Topeka, Kansas, entered into an Amended and
  Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated July 9, 2017, describing the transaction
  as a "merger of equals," under which the two companies would combine under a new
  holding company, Monarch Energy Holding, Inc. ("Monarch Energy"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- The amended and restated agreement amends and restates in its entirety an original
  merger agreement among Great Plains Energy, Westar Energy and GP Star, Inc., dated
  May 29, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- The mergers were structured as: (1) Great Plains Energy merges with and into Monarch
  Energy, with Monarch Energy continuing as the surviving corporation, and (2) Westar
  Energy merges with and into King Energy, Inc., a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of
  Monarch Energy, with Westar Energy continuing as the surviving corporation; upon
  completion, Monarch Energy becomes the direct parent of Westar Energy and of Great
  Plains Energy's direct subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration: each share of Great Plains Energy common stock converts into 0.5981
  shares of Monarch Energy common stock; each share of Westar Energy common stock
  converts into one share of Monarch Energy common stock; the implied Westar Energy
  ownership split of the combined company was 52.5 percent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- The mergers were completed on June 4, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-182966, Form 8-K]
- On May 23, 2018, the registrant filed an amendment to its articles of incorporation
  changing its name from "Monarch Energy Holding, Inc." to "Evergy, Inc."; the parties
  had agreed in the merger agreement to use commercially reasonable efforts to agree on
  a new name and ticker for Monarch Energy prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-175457, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle, listing and headquarters at closing
- Upon completion of the mergers, Monarch Energy (renamed Evergy, Inc.) maintains
  corporate headquarters in Kansas City, Missouri, and utility operating headquarters in
  both Topeka, Kansas, and Kansas City, Missouri. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- If the mergers were completed, the common stock of both Great Plains Energy and
  Westar Energy would cease to be listed on the NYSE and would be deregistered under the
  Securities Exchange Act of 1934, while the new holding company's stock would be
  approved for listing on the NYSE. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- On June 4, 2018, in connection with the closing, Great Plains Energy requested that
  the NYSE withdraw its common stock from listing and file a Form 25; the shares were
  suspended from trading before the open on June 5, 2018, and the surviving holding
  company's stock began trading under the symbol "EVRG" on the NYSE that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-182966, Form 8-K]

## Governance and management continuity at closing
- Under the merger agreement, Great Plains Energy and Westar Energy each designate an
  equal number of directors to the Monarch Energy board; the bylaws provide for not
  less than seven nor more than 13 directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- Mark Ruelle, President and CEO of Westar Energy, becomes non-executive Chairman of the
  combined-company board for an initial three-year term; Terry Bassham, Chairman,
  President and CEO of Great Plains Energy (and its subsidiaries KCP&L and GMO),
  becomes President and CEO of the combined company and a board member; Charles Q.
  Chandler IV is expected to be designated lead independent director (a designation
  made by Westar Energy). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- Initial standing-committee chairs are split by legacy company (compensation, audit,
  and nuclear-operations/environmental committees chaired by Great Plains Energy
  designees; nominating/governance and finance committees chaired by Westar Energy
  designees), with each committee composed of an equal number of directors from each
  legacy company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- The senior leadership team named to serve the combined company at closing draws from
  the Great Plains Energy executive bench: Terry Bassham (President/CEO), Kevin E.
  Bryant (EVP/COO), Greg A. Greenwood (EVP-Strategy/Chief Administrative Officer),
  Anthony D. Somma (EVP/CFO), Jerl L. Banning (SVP/Chief People Officer), Charles A.
  Caisley (SVP-Marketing/Chief Customer Officer) and Heather A. Humphrey (SVP, General
  Counsel and Corporate Secretary). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed at the product/process/people tiers
- The customer-facing product/asset — regulated electric utility service in Kansas and
  Missouri — continued to be delivered through the parties' existing operating
  subsidiaries (Westar Energy and Great Plains Energy's KCP&L/GMO), which continued to
  exist as subsidiaries of the new parent rather than being combined into a single
  operating company at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- The boards' discussions and the agreed terms tie the operating tier (utility
  operating headquarters retained in both Topeka and Kansas City) and the people tier
  together: management represented to the boards its intent to maintain a minimum
  employment level at the Topeka operating headquarters for five years following
  closing and to avoid employee involuntary severances as a result of the transaction,
  as part of the regulatory-commitment package discussed by the Westar Energy board.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, Monarch Energy (and the surviving corporations) agreed to
  assume and honor, in accordance with their terms, all of Westar Energy's, Great
  Plains Energy's and their respective subsidiaries' existing employment, severance,
  retention, termination and change-in-control plans, policies, programs, agreements
  and arrangements (including any change-in-control severance agreements). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- Pre-existing change-in-control severance agreements for Great Plains Energy's
  executive officers required both a change in control and a qualifying termination of
  employment before severance (generally two times base salary plus other amounts)
  would be payable; these arrangements were disclosed as continuing obligations of the
  combined enterprise rather than newly created at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- Both companies committed under the merger agreement's ordinary-course covenants not
  to unilaterally increase, or enter into new, change-in-control/severance/retention
  arrangements for personnel outside the ordinary course pending closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services, transition-operating, or shared-services agreement bridging
  a carved-out substrate was identified in the reviewed sections of the filing; the
  transaction combines two already-complete, independently operating utility companies
  under a common new parent rather than separating a business unit from a single
  parent. [UNVERIFIED — absence of such an agreement could not be exhaustively confirmed
  across the full filing text]
- Great Plains Energy and Westar Energy each committed to continue charitable
  contributions and community support within their respective legacy service areas at
  levels substantially comparable to pre-merger levels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two complete, going-concern public utility holding companies
  under a newly formed parent (Monarch Energy, renamed Evergy, Inc. before closing),
  with each legacy company's operating subsidiary, headquarters location, and workforce
  retained rather than carved apart, and with governance (board seats, committee
  chairs) and senior leadership positions allocated between the two legacy
  managements under negotiated terms rather than one side's structure simply absorbing
  the other. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307158, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-182966, Form 8-K]
