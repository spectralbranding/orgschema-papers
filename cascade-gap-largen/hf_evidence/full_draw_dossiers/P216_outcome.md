## Realized business record under the acquirer (named "TerraForm Global" business)
- Brookfield Renewable's Form 20-F for fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-02-26) states that,
  "as part of the acquisition of TerraForm Global in December 2017," it acquired 294.4
  MW of wind facilities in the State of Bahia, Brazil, and that the December 2017
  acquisition "marked our entry into the wind and solar markets" in both India (66
  employees, none covered by collective agreements) and China (approximately 95
  employees, 13% covered by collective agreements). [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020]
- The same filing states that in March 2020 Brookfield Renewable "extended a $45
  million non-recourse revolving credit facility associated with our TerraForm Global
  business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020]

## Documented divestitures within the window (Note 4, Disposal of Assets)
- In March 2020, Brookfield Renewable, together with institutional partners, sold a 39
  MW solar portfolio in Thailand (part of the original 2017 acquired footprint) for
  total consideration of THB 3,079 million ($94 million); Brookfield Renewable's
  interest was approximately 31%, and the sale resulted in a loss on disposition of $12
  million ($4 million net to Brookfield Renewable). The portfolio had been classified
  as held-for-sale since 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020, Note 4]
- In September 2020, Brookfield Renewable sold its interest in a 33 MW solar facility in
  South Africa for gross cash consideration of ZAR 300 million ($18 million; $25 million
  total net proceeds after foreign-exchange contract settlements, $8 million net to
  Brookfield Renewable), resulting in a $4 million loss on disposition; this portfolio
  had also been classified as held-for-sale since 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020, Note 4]
- The FY2020 20-F states that "in 2020, Brookfield Renewable completed the sale of its
  non-core assets from the TerraForm Global portfolio in South Africa and Thailand,
  corresponding to 72 MW of solar assets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020]
- As of December 31, 2020, a remaining 19 MW solar portfolio in Malaysia (also part of
  the original acquired footprint) was classified as an asset held for sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020]

## Later-window status (FY2021 20-F, filed 2022-03-01)
- The FY2021 20-F states Brookfield Renewable "entered into an agreement to sell" the
  Malaysia solar portfolio, indicating the sale agreed but not yet completed as of that
  filing; it reports 6 employees in Malaysia, 5 in Uruguay, and 4 in Chile at that date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-22-000007, Form 20-F FY2021]
- The FY2021 20-F's narrative business descriptions of the India, China, and Brazil
  operations no longer name "TerraForm Global" as the acquired-business label in the
  way the FY2020 filing did; the term appears only in the defined-terms glossary and a
  debt-structure reference to a successor entity, "TerraForm Global Holdco LLC."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-22-000007, Form 20-F FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the acquired entity deregistered in January 2018, there is no periodic filing
  of its own for the ~2020-2022 window; the traceable realized record instead comes
  from the acquirer's (Brookfield Renewable Partners L.P.) own Form 20-F filings, which
  separately name and describe the acquired business through FY2020 and FY2021.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020; SEC accession 0001533232-22-000007, Form 20-F FY2021]
- Within the window, two of the original seven acquired geographic markets (Thailand
  and South Africa, together 72 MW) were sold at a combined disclosed loss on
  disposition of $16 million ($8 million net to Brookfield Renewable), and a third
  (Malaysia, 19 MW) was agreed to be sold but remained unclosed by the FY2021 filing;
  the remaining India, China, and Brazil operations continued to be reported, with
  declining standalone naming of the business as "TerraForm Global" in the parent's
  public narrative by FY2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533232-21-000009, Form 20-F FY2020; SEC accession 0001533232-22-000007, Form 20-F FY2021]
