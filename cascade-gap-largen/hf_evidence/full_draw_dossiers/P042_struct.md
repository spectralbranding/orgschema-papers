# Structural sub-dossier — Varex Imaging Corporation separation from Varian Medical Systems (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Varex Imaging Corporation was formed as a wholly owned subsidiary of Varian Medical Systems, Inc. to hold Varian's Imaging Components business; the separation was effected by a spin-off in which Varian distributed 100% of the outstanding Varex common stock pro rata to Varian stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution ratio was 0.4 of a share of Varex common stock for every one share of Varian common stock held on the January 20, 2017 record date; Varian and Varex expected the distribution to occur on January 28, 2017, and the distribution was intended to be tax-free to Varian stockholders for U.S. federal income tax purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- No vote of Varian stockholders was required and no consideration was to be paid or shares surrendered by stockholders to receive Varex shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Varex intended to list on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the symbol "VREX." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing product line that crossed was the Imaging Components business: X-ray tubes, flat panel (digital) detectors, imaging software, high voltage connectors, and high-energy inspection accelerators sold as components of X-ray imaging systems for medical, security and industrial applications. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- As of September 30, 2016 the separated business had approximately 1,400 full-time and part-time employees worldwide (approximately 960 in the United States, approximately 440 elsewhere). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Producing/delivering processes crossing with the product included owned manufacturing at Salt Lake City, Utah (approximately 494,000 sq ft of office and manufacturing space on approximately 37 acres) and Liverpool, New York, plus leased manufacturing space in Calamba, Philippines; industrial products were manufactured in approximately 94,000 sq ft within a shared Varian facility in Las Vegas, Nevada. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The information statement states that prior to the separation the business "has been operated by Varian as part of its broader corporate organization, rather than as an independent company," and that Varian or an affiliate had performed various corporate functions for it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Historically the business shared "economies of scope and scale in costs, employees, vendor relationships and customer relationships" with Varian, and its working capital and general corporate-purpose capital "have historically been satisfied as part of the corporate-wide cash management policies of Varian." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## People / management crossing with the operations
- Varian and Varex were to be separate companies with separate management teams and separate boards of directors after the distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Varex's management team was expected to be led by Sunny S. Sanyal, who was at the time Senior Vice President and President of Varian's Imaging Components business and had joined Varian in February 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the employee matters agreement, employees to be employed by Varex following the distribution ("Varex allocated employees") and associated benefit-plan assets, liabilities and account balances were to be transferred to Varex or Varex plans, with prior Varian service generally credited under Varex benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- The parties were to enter into a separation and distribution agreement plus ancillary agreements: a transition services agreement, a tax matters agreement, an employee matters agreement, an intellectual property matters agreement, a trademark license agreement, and one or more supply/distribution agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the transition services agreement, Varian and Varex were to provide each other interim, transitional services expected to include information technology, accounts payable, payroll, legal and other financial functions and administrative services, generally lasting between 60 days and up to 24 months following the distribution, on a cost-recovery basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The intellectual property matters agreement granted each party a non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free, worldwide license to the other's IP within its own field (Varex's field being medical, dental, veterinary, scientific, security and industrial applications other than oncology/radiation therapy), designed to let each party continue using IP used in its business prior to the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The trademark license agreement granted Varex a limited, royalty-free license to use certain Varian marks after the distribution for products that had been sold in the Varex business (e.g., products engraved or registered with Varian marks), for a term of three to eight years depending on product class. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Varian was expected to continue as a major customer of Varex, and the parties expected to enter into one or more supply/distribution agreements under which each would supply the other with products and services for manufacturing use, incorporation in OEM products, and resale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transfer of assets to Varex was described as primarily property, employee-benefit-plan assets of Varex allocated employees, and certain other fixed assets, with certain shared (e.g., environmental) liabilities allocated between the parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A customer-facing product operation (X-ray imaging components, together with its named manufacturing sites and its allocated workforce and management drawn from the business's existing leadership) was separated from a parent whose shared corporate service substrate (IT, accounts payable, payroll, legal, financial functions, cash management) did not transfer intact; that substrate, along with cross-field IP, trademark use, and an ongoing supply relationship, was bridged at closing by a transition services agreement and related ancillary agreements rather than transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-008082, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
