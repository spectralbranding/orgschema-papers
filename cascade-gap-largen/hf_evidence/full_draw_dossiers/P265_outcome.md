## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Bear State Financial, Inc. deregistered from SEC reporting on 2018-05-01, shortly
  after its April 20, 2018 acquisition closed, and filed no further periodic reports;
  the acquiring bank has never itself been subject to SEC periodic reporting and has
  filed no report separately disclosing the acquired business's post-close trajectory.
  No public 3-5-year financial or operating record specific to the acquired business was
  located for the 2018-2023 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-008191, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0001233767 (https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001233767)]
