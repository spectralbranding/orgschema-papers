# Outcome sub-dossier — Hines Global REIT, Inc. / HGR Liquidating Trust realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after ~2018

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The Plan of Liquidation and Dissolution was approved by the affirmative vote of the
  holders of at least a majority of the outstanding common stock at the annual meeting
  held July 17, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- The registrant continued filing periodic reports under the same SEC CIK after the
  vote: annual reports on Form 10-K were filed covering fiscal years 2018 (filed
  2019-03-28), 2019 (filed 2020-03-30), 2020 (filed 2021-03-31), and 2021 (filed
  2022-03-31); a Form 15-12G was also filed on 2020-07-07. [SRC: SEC EDGAR submissions history, CIK 0001453818]
- On June 30, 2020, the Company entered an Agreement and Declaration of Trust forming
  HGR Liquidating Trust, a Maryland statutory trust, transferred all of its assets and
  liabilities to the Trust, and distributed one Unit of beneficial interest in the
  Trust for each outstanding share of common stock, converting former stockholders into
  Trust unitholders; the registrant's SEC filings thereafter are made in the name of
  HGR Liquidating Trust under the same CIK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- The Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (period ended December 31, 2021, filed 2022-03-31)
  states the registrant remained subject to Exchange Act reporting requirements at that
  time (the cover-page checkbox for "not required to file reports" is marked "No").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), cover page]

## Realized disposition record (primary-sourced)
- The originally anticipated 24-month completion period (by July 17, 2020) was not met;
  the Form 10-K attributes the delay in the Plan's completion to "economic disruption
  and uncertainty resulting from the Coronavirus pandemic." [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- At the peak of its acquisition phase the entity had owned interests in 45 properties;
  40 of the 45 real estate investments had been sold prior to December 31, 2020, four of
  the five remaining properties were sold during 2021, and the final remaining property
  (Minneapolis Retail Center) was sold in March 2022 for $150.0 million excluding
  closing costs and prorations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1 and Item 7]
- In aggregate, interests in 39 properties were sold for an aggregate sale price of
  approximately $5.4 billion from 2017 through 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- Net assets in liquidation (the liquidation-basis measure of remaining unitholder
  value) were $549.7 million as of December 31, 2020 and declined to $330.6 million as
  of December 31, 2021, as sale proceeds continued to be distributed to unitholders.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 7]
- Per-Unit net asset value was determined at $2.09 as of December 31, 2020, declining to
  $0.66 as of March 11, 2022 and to $0.04 as of March 17, 2022, following special
  distributions and the sale of the remaining properties, including the final property
  sold March 10, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- Aggregate distributions paid to investors from inception through the FY2021 10-K
  totaled approximately $15.11 per share/Unit (comprising $6.09 per share of regular
  distributions declared through December 2018 and approximately $9.02 per share of
  special/liquidating distributions to date). [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- On October 4, 2022, the trustees declared a final special distribution of
  approximately $0.03 per Unit, payable around October 20, 2022; after that payment,
  aggregate distributions since inception were reported at approximately $15.14 per
  share/Unit ($9.50 of special/return-of-capital/liquidating distributions plus $5.64 of
  regular operating distributions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000010, Form 8-K, Items 7.01/8.01]
- The same October 4, 2022 filing states that, promptly following the final special
  distribution, ownership of the Operating Partnership (Hines Global REIT Properties,
  LP) was anticipated to be transferred to Hines Global REIT Investor Limited
  Partnership, an affiliate of the sponsor, after which winding down would be considered
  complete and the Trust would be terminated per the Trust Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000010, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]

## Management/organization continuity over the disposition period
- Throughout the period covered by the FY2021 10-K, the entity continued to have no
  employees; its business continued to be managed by the same Advisor, an affiliate of
  Hines, under an Advisory Agreement effective as of June 30, 2020 (following a Prior
  Advisory Agreement covering January-June 2020 under which the Advisor managed the
  predecessor company's operations in substantially the same manner). [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]
- The trustees of the Trust were drawn from the predecessor board of directors (Jeffrey
  C. Hines, Charles M. Baughn, Jack L. Farley, Thomas L. Mitchell, John S. Moody, Peter
  Shaper) together with the Company's Chief Investment Officer, David L. Steinbach.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The disposition process took materially longer than the 24-month period originally
  contemplated, with the last of 45 properties not sold until March 2022, roughly 3
  years and 8 months after the July 2018 stockholder vote; the entity's structure
  changed mid-process (assets and liabilities were transferred into HGR Liquidating
  Trust in June 2020), but the same external Advisor/Hines management continued
  operating and disposing of the portfolio throughout, and the same registrant CIK
  continued filing periodic reports (10-Ks through fiscal 2021, an 8-K in October 2022)
  documenting a declining net-asset-in-liquidation balance and a final special
  distribution as the Trust approached termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-22-000010, Form 8-K]
