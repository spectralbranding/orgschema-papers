## Realized record of the acquired membership interest
- The originally acquired interest was reassigned after closing: per the filing's exhibit
  index, membership interests were assigned under an Assignment of Membership Interests dated
  October 3, 2016 between Piedmont ACP Company, LLC and Dominion Atlantic Coast Pipeline, LLC
  (incorporated by reference to a registrant Form 8-K filed October 7, 2016, File No. 1-06196).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K exhibit index, item 10.5]
- By December 31, 2019 the disclosed ownership of ACP was Dominion 48%, Duke Energy 47%, and
  Southern Company Gas 5%; the registrant no longer appears among ACP's equity members and is
  described only as a prospective customer of the pipeline (with Duke Energy Carolinas, Duke
  Energy Progress and the registrant to purchase under 20-year supply contracts subject to
  state regulatory approval). [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K, Regulatory Matters]

## Realized record of the joint-venture project (best-documented scalars)
- Project cost: as of the FY2019 report, total project cost was approximately $8 billion,
  excluding financing costs, with the filing attributing the increase to delays resulting from
  legal challenges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K, Regulatory Matters]
- Schedule: as of the FY2019 report the project expected mechanical completion in late 2021
  with in-service likely in the first half of 2022.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K, Regulatory Matters]
- Operating/permitting disruption: the project was the subject of challenges in state and
  federal courts and agencies (including its biological opinion and incidental take statement,
  and crossings of the Blue Ridge Parkway, the Appalachian Trail, and the Monongahela and
  George Washington national forests), and a pending FERC Notice to Proceed for full
  construction in Virginia was held up by legal challenges as of the report date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K, Regulatory Matters]
- Impairment assessment: at December 31, 2019 the carrying value of Duke Energy's equity
  method investment in ACP was $1.2 billion; following several adverse court rulings the
  investment was evaluated for impairment using probability-weighted discounted-cash-flow
  scenarios, and management concluded fair value approximated carrying value and the
  investment was not impaired as of that date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001326160-20-000034, Form 10-K, Regulatory Matters]
