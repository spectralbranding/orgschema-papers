# Structural sub-dossier — Park Sterling Corporation merger with South State Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Park Sterling Corporation, a North Carolina corporation, and South State Corporation,
  a South Carolina corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  April 26, 2017, providing that Park Sterling would merge with and into South State,
  with South State continuing as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately following the merger, Park Sterling Bank, Park Sterling's wholly owned
  bank subsidiary, was to merge with and into South State Bank, South State's wholly
  owned bank subsidiary, with South State Bank continuing as the surviving bank (the
  "bank merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a direct statutory merger of Park Sterling into
  South State — no separate merger-sub/shell entity was used as the acquisition
  vehicle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of Park Sterling common stock was to convert into the right to receive
  0.14 shares of South State common stock; at announcement this represented
  approximately $12.87 per share and aggregate consideration of approximately $691
  million, and by September 8, 2017 (the last practicable date before mailing) it
  represented approximately $11.17 per share and approximately $600 million in
  aggregate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger and bank merger were completed on November 30, 2017, with South State as
  the surviving entity in each; South State assumed Park Sterling's obligations on
  outstanding trust preferred securities with an aggregate principal amount of $43.1
  million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071280, Form 8-K]

## What Park Sterling comprised at closing (the asset/product tier)
- At the time of the proxy statement, Park Sterling Bank served customers through 18
  full-service branches in North Carolina, 23 in South Carolina, nine in Virginia, and
  five in North Georgia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- Regulatory approvals required to complete the merger and bank merger included the
  Federal Reserve Board, the FDIC, the South Carolina State Board of Financial
  Institutions, and the North Carolina Commissioner of Banks. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]

## People/management tier: what crossed and what did not
- At the effective time, each of Park Sterling's directors and executive officers
  ceased serving in such capacities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071280, Form 8-K]
- Selected individuals were separately re-engaged by South State Bank effective at the
  effective time: a Consulting and Noncompetition Agreement with CEO James C. Cherry;
  Employment and Noncompetition ("Replacement") Agreements with Donald K. Truslow and
  Bryan F. Kennedy, III; and an Employment Agreement with Mark Ladnier — each providing
  payments/benefits for services or severance. Nancy J. Foster remained party to a
  separate Employment Agreement with Park Sterling providing severance rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately after the effective time, James C. Cherry and Jean E. Davis (a Park
  Sterling director) were appointed to South State's board of directors; Cherry was
  also appointed to South State's Executive Committee; Robert R. Horger continued as
  Chairman of South State's board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071280, Form 8-K]
- The officers of South State in office immediately prior to the effective time were to
  continue serving as the officers of the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]

## Process-tier bridging and integration mechanics at closing
- Certain named executives' agreements provided for a one-time payment of $3,000,000
  tied to completion of "systems conversion" (payable within 120 days of, or upon,
  completion of that conversion), indicating operational/systems integration was
  scheduled to occur after the legal closing rather than being completed at it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus disclosed integration risk factors, including
  the possibility of difficulty integrating Park Sterling's business, operations and
  workforce, potential loss of key employees, and that anticipated cost savings
  (including "branch consolidation cost" as a named expense category) might not be
  fully realized. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided for a $25 million termination fee payable by either
  party to the other under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On November 30, 2017, Park Sterling notified NASDAQ that its common stock would be
  cancelled/converted and requested that trading be suspended after the close of
  trading that day; Park Sterling requested NASDAQ file a Form 25 removing its common
  stock from listing and registration, and stated its intent to file a Form 15 to
  terminate registration and suspend reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071280, Form 8-K]
- Park Sterling filed a Form 15-12B on December 11, 2017, certifying termination of
  registration, signed by South State Corporation "as successor by merger to Park
  Sterling Corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-072745, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a direct whole-company stock-for-stock merger (no separate
  merger-sub vehicle) combining Park Sterling's branch-banking franchise and bank
  subsidiary into South State at the legal-entity and bank-charter levels
  simultaneously at closing, while the people/management tier crossed only selectively:
  all Park Sterling directors and executive officers ceased serving at the effective
  time, with a small subset re-engaged under new consulting/employment agreements and
  two individuals added to South State's board, and with operational systems
  integration ("systems conversion") explicitly contemplated to complete after, not at,
  the legal closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-071280, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005768, Form DEFM14A]
