## Public-reporting status of the acquired entity after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc. filed a Form 15-12B on March 16, 2018 certifying
  termination of registration of its Class A common shares under Section 12(g) of the
  Exchange Act and suspension of its duty to file reports under Sections 13(a)/15(d),
  recording approximately one holder of record as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-085949, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by Scripps Networks Interactive,
  Inc. under its own CIK after the pre-merger period; consequently no stand-alone
  audited financial statements exist for the acquired business under its own name for
  the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-085949, Form 15-12B]

## Discovery's separate disclosure of the acquired networks and legal entity (~2021)
- As of December 31, 2021, Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc. remained a distinct,
  wholly owned (indirect) legal subsidiary of Discovery, Inc., and continued to be named
  as a co-obligor/guarantor, alongside Discovery Communications, LLC ("DCL"), of certain
  of Discovery's outstanding senior notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 8]
- For the year ended December 31, 2021, DCL and Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc.
  issued notices for, and completed, the redemption in full of $168 million and $62
  million aggregate principal amounts (respectively) of their 3.300% and 3.500% Senior
  Notes due 2022, redeemed in July 2021 for an aggregate redemption price of $235
  million plus accrued interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 8]
- Discovery's FY2021 Form 10-K reports summarized combined financial information for
  "the Obligors" (Discovery, Inc., Scripps Networks, and DCL combined, intercompany
  eliminated) as of/for the year ended December 31, 2021: current assets $4,452 million,
  noncurrent assets $5,969 million, current liabilities $1,018 million, noncurrent
  liabilities $15,778 million, revenues $2,091 million, operating income $1,028 million,
  and net income $294 million; this figure is a combined guarantor-group disclosure and
  is not broken out for Scripps Networks alone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 8]
- Discovery's FY2021 Form 10-K organizes its operations into two reportable segments —
  "U.S. Networks" (domestic television networks and digital content services) and
  "International Networks" — and does not report a separate segment or line item for
  the acquired Scripps lifestyle networks; the segment presentation is stated to align
  with Discovery's management structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's business description lists HGTV and Food Network among
  Discovery's "prominent" brands alongside Discovery Channel, TLC, Animal Planet, and
  others, and reports, as of December 31, 2021: HGTV had approximately 82 million U.S.
  subscribers and approximately 176 million subscribers/viewers internationally; Food
  Network — described as "our most widely distributed ad-supported cable network in the
  U.S." — had approximately 82 million U.S. subscribers and approximately 112 million
  subscribers/viewers internationally. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The same filing states the Company "launched discovery+, its aggregated DTC product,
  in the U.S. across several streaming platforms" in January 2021, without isolating
  which legacy Scripps or Discovery brands' content specifically drove discovery+
  subscription or usage figures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc. deregistered its own SEC reporting on March 16,
  2018, so no stand-alone 10-K record exists for the acquired business under its own
  name; however, the acquirer, Discovery, Inc., does separately disclose the acquired
  networks in its FY2021 Form 10-K (filed February 2022, within the ~3-5-year window):
  Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc. persisted as a distinct legal subsidiary and debt
  co-obligor through 2021, its combined guarantor-group financials are reported jointly
  with another subsidiary rather than in isolation, and its flagship brands (HGTV, Food
  Network) are described individually with specific subscriber counts but are folded
  into Discovery's two-segment ("U.S. Networks"/"International Networks") reporting
  structure rather than tracked as a separate segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-085949, Form 15-12B]
- Because Discovery's own subsequent combination with WarnerMedia (a separate,
  later transaction) recast Discovery's segment and entity structure again starting in
  2022, this dossier fixes its outcome window to the FY2021 Form 10-K — the last annual
  report filed before that later combination — to avoid conflating the two transactions'
  effects. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437107-22-000031, Discovery Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]
