## Public-reporting status after the transactions

- The Advisory Board Company ("ABCO") terminated its own SEC registration shortly after
  closing: it filed a Form 25-NSE on November 17, 2017 and a Form 15-12G on November 27,
  2017, ending its status as an Exchange Act reporting company. No ABCO-specific Form
  10-K exists for any period after fiscal year 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-17-000234, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-352504, Form 15-12G]
- The education business acquirer entities named in the closing 8-K — Avatar Holdco,
  LLC and EAB Global, Inc. (an affiliate of Vista Equity Partners Fund VI, L.P.) — do
  not appear as registrants in the SEC EDGAR company database under those names (no
  filings of any type located), consistent with continued private-equity ownership and
  no obligation to make periodic public disclosure of that business's results.
  [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=EAB+Global&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40&output=atom] [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=Avatar+Holdco&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40&output=atom]
- The health care business acquirer, OptumInsight, Inc., is itself not a separate SEC
  registrant; its ultimate parent, UnitedHealth Group Incorporated (CIK 0000731766,
  NYSE: UNH), is the reporting entity and consolidates Optum's operations into its own
  periodic filings rather than filing separately for OptumInsight or for the acquired
  Advisory Board Company business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K "Item 2.01"]
- "The Advisory Board Company" continued to appear, by that legal name, in the list of
  UnitedHealth Group's subsidiaries in Exhibit 21.1 of UnitedHealth Group's Form 10-K
  for fiscal year 2020 (accession 0000731766-21-000013, filed March 1, 2021), listed
  as a Delaware subsidiary alongside hundreds of other UnitedHealth Group subsidiaries,
  with no accompanying financial or operating detail specific to that entity.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000731766-21-000013, Form 10-K Exhibit 21.1]
- A full-text search of UnitedHealth Group's Forms 10-K for fiscal years 2017 through
  2022 for the phrase "Advisory Board Company" returns matches only within each year's
  Exhibit 21.1 subsidiaries list, not within the Management's Discussion and Analysis,
  segment-reporting footnotes, or business-combination footnotes of any of those filings
  — i.e., no located UnitedHealth Group 10-K breaks out separate financial results,
  revenue, earnings, or an operating narrative specific to the acquired Advisory Board
  Company health care business as a standalone unit within the outcome window.
  [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar (EDGAR full-text search, query "Advisory Board Company", forms=10-K, ciks=0000731766)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Both businesses that had operated under The Advisory Board Company left the public
  reporting system at the November 2017 closing: ABCO itself deregistered from the SEC
  within days of closing, the education-business buyer entities are not SEC registrants
  at any point during the outcome window, and the health-care-business buyer's ultimate
  parent (UnitedHealth Group) consolidates the acquired business into its own filings
  without separate financial or operating disclosure — "The Advisory Board Company"
  appears only as a bare subsidiary-list entry in UnitedHealth Group's Exhibit 21.1 as
  of the FY2020 10-K, with no revenue, earnings, or narrative specific to it located.
  No public 3-5-year outcome record exists for either the health care business or the
  education business as separately identifiable entities; this case is coded
  **uncertain** on the outcome for lack of a public record.
