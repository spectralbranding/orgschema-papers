# Outcome sub-dossier — Vantiv/Worldpay, Inc. realized record, ~2018-2020 (3-5-year horizon from 2017 closing-era events)

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The combined entity's own public reporting ended

## Standalone public-reporting window of the combined entity
- Vantiv, Inc. changed its name to Worldpay, Inc. upon closing on January 16, 2018 and
  continued filing under the same registrant (CIK 1533932; Commission File No.
  001-35462); its last annual report as an independent registrant was the Form 10-K for
  fiscal year 2018, filed February 26, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- On July 31, 2019, pursuant to a Merger Agreement dated March 17, 2019, a subsidiary
  of Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. ("FIS") merged into Worldpay, Inc.,
  with Worldpay continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of FIS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-208255, Form 8-K Introductory Note]
- Worldpay, Inc. filed a Form 15-12B on August 12, 2019 to deregister its securities;
  its standalone public financial record therefore covers only fiscal year 2018 (the
  first full year including Legacy Worldpay operations from January 16, 2018) before
  reporting responsibility passed to FIS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-218602, Form 15-12B]

## Realized financial record, fiscal year 2018 (combined entity, own filing)
- For the year ended December 31, 2018, Worldpay, Inc. reported net income of $19.6
  million and net income attributable to Worldpay, Inc. of $12.8 million, down from net
  income of $182.7 million and net income attributable to Vantiv, Inc. of $130.1 million
  for the year ended December 31, 2017 (pre-merger Vantiv only). [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 6, Selected Financial Data]
- Total assets grew to $24,888.5 million as of December 31, 2018 from $8,667.0 million
  as of December 31, 2017, reflecting consolidation of the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 6, Selected Financial Data]
- The FY2018 10-K disclosed "Transition, Acquisition and Integration Costs" as a
  recurring non-GAAP adjustment category, describing costs "associated with
  acquisitions and related integration activities, consisting primarily of consulting
  fees for advisory, conversion and integration services and related personnel costs,"
  including "costs related to employee termination benefits and other transition
  activities." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 7, MD&A]
- As of the FY2018 10-K, the company described itself as processing over 40 billion
  transactions annually across more than 300 payment types in 146 countries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 7, MD&A]

## Management/organization discontinuity at the FIS acquisition (July 2019)
- Effective upon completion of the FIS merger, "all of the directors of Worldpay ceased
  serving as directors of Worldpay and all of the executive officers of Worldpay ceased
  serving in their capacity as executive officers of Worldpay," with limited named
  exceptions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-208255, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Mark Heimbouch (Worldpay's President and COO) agreed to serve as "President, Merchant
  Solutions"; Charles Drucker (Worldpay CEO) and Stephanie Ferris (Worldpay CFO) agreed
  to continue briefly in their roles before moving to serve as executive officers of
  FIS, with Drucker becoming an FIS executive and vice chairman of the FIS board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-208255, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Realized record under the acquirer's segment disclosure (FIS, post-2019)
- Following the acquisition, FIS "reorganized its reportable segments" and created a
  "Merchant Solutions" segment built around the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-20-000032, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2019) Item 1]
- FIS reported Merchant Solutions segment revenue of $2,013 million for 2019 (the
  segment's results include Worldpay only from July 31, 2019) versus $276 million for
  2018 and $261 million for 2017 (FIS's pre-existing, pre-Worldpay merchant business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-20-000032, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2019) Item 1]
- In the following year's 10-K, FIS reported Merchant Solutions segment revenue of
  $3,767 million for 2020 (a full year including Worldpay) versus $1,942 million for
  2019 and $208 million for 2018 under the restated segment presentation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-21-000025, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2020) Item 1]
- FIS's consolidated selling, general and administrative expenses for 2019 included
  "higher acquisition, integration and other costs of $704 million in 2019 as compared
  to $156 million in 2018," attributed in part to "incremental Worldpay corporate and
  infrastructure expenses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-20-000032, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2019) Item 7, MD&A]
- In its FY2020 10-K goodwill-impairment disclosure, FIS reported that $94 million of
  goodwill related to certain non-strategic businesses within its Corporate and Other
  segment was impaired in 2020, and that "for all other reporting units for all periods
  presented, goodwill was not impaired" (i.e., the Merchant Solutions reporting unit
  built around the acquired business was not impaired in 2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-21-000025, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2020) Item 7A/Notes, Goodwill]
- The same FY2020 10-K disclosed that a quantitative sensitivity assessment of the
  Merchant Solutions reporting unit found that "a hypothetical 100-basis-point increase
  in the discount rate yielded an estimated fair value for the Merchant Solutions
  reporting unit below its carrying amount," and stated the reporting unit was "at risk
  for future goodwill impairment" given, among other factors, the economic impact of
  the COVID-19 pandemic on the Merchant Solutions business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-21-000025, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2020) Notes, Goodwill]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The combined entity's own public record runs from the January 2018 closing to its
  July 2019 acquisition by FIS: its first full year (FY2018) showed sharply lower net
  income than the pre-merger prior year alongside a near-tripling of total assets and
  explicitly disclosed integration/transition costs. Eighteen months after closing, the
  entity was itself acquired, its board and (with a few named exceptions) its executive
  officers turned over, and it deregistered from the SEC. Under the acquirer's
  subsequent segment reporting, the acquired business (as the "Merchant Solutions"
  segment) grew in revenue year over year through 2020, generated materially higher
  consolidated acquisition/integration costs in the acquirer's own financials, was not
  itself goodwill-impaired in FY2020, but was flagged by the acquirer's own sensitivity
  analysis as being at risk of future goodwill impairment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-208255, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001136893-21-000025, Form 10-K (FIS, FY2020)]
