# Outcome sub-dossier — GCI Liberty, Inc. realized record, ~3-5 years after closing

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. GCI Liberty, Inc. itself ceased to be a separate
> SEC registrant before the fixed outcome window opened; the realized record below draws
> on (a) GCI Liberty's own subsequent public filings and (b) the segment-level disclosure
> of its acquirer once it was absorbed. This sub-dossier contains no information about
> how the original 2018 transaction was structured at closing.

## Continued independent public reporting, then a further change of hands
- GCI Liberty, Inc. continued to file its own annual reports on Form 10-K under CIK
  808461 after the closing-era transactions, including a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2019
  filed February 27, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000808461-20-000006, Form 10-K]
- On December 18, 2020, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated August 6, 2020,
  a merger subsidiary of Liberty Broadband Corporation merged with and into GCI Liberty,
  and immediately thereafter GCI Liberty (as the surviving corporation of that first
  merger) merged with and into another Liberty Broadband merger subsidiary, with GCI
  Liberty ceasing to exist as a separate corporation and its business continuing as a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Liberty Broadband Corporation. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-23-001533, Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 1]
- Effective December 31, 2020, GCI Liberty's successor by merger filed a Form 15-12B
  terminating GCI Liberty's SEC registration, certifying one holder of record of the
  post-merger equity interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-140760, Form 15-12B]
- This further change of hands and deregistration occurred within roughly three years of
  the 2018 closing-era transactions — before the fixed ~3-5-year outcome window for this
  case opens — so no Form 10-K exists under GCI Liberty's own CIK for the outcome window
  itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-140760, Form 15-12B]

## Segment-level trajectory of the continuing operating business (within the fixed window)
- Following the December 2020 combination, the Alaska communications operating business
  formerly conducted by GCI Liberty continued to operate as "GCI Holdings, LLC," a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Liberty Broadband Corporation, and Liberty Broadband reports it as a
  distinct reportable segment separate from its Charter Communications equity investment
  and its "Corporate and other" category. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-24-001302,
  Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2023), Note 15 Segment Information]
- GCI Holdings segment revenue was reported as $970 million for 2021, $969 million for
  2022, and $981 million for 2023; segment Adjusted OIBDA was reported as $354 million
  for 2021, $358 million for 2022, and $361 million for 2023. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-24-001302, Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2023), Note 15 Segment
  Information]
- GCI Holdings segment total assets were reported as $3,378 million at December 31, 2022
  and $3,340 million at December 31, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-24-001302,
  Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2023), Note 15 Segment Information]
- Liberty Broadband's FY2022 Form 10-K separately discloses that GCI Holdings recorded a
  $15 million additional estimated settlement expense during 2022 relating to a Rural
  Health Care Program compliance matter (for a total estimated liability of $27 million),
  and a further $14 million estimated settlement expense during 2022 relating to a
  Department of Justice inquiry into Rural Health Care Program claims covering 2010
  through 2019, with the ultimate outcome of both matters unable to be assessed as of
  that filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-001533, Liberty Broadband Corporation
  Form 10-K (FY2022), Note 14]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- GCI Liberty, Inc. continued filing its own 10-Ks through fiscal year 2019, then was
  itself absorbed by Liberty Broadband Corporation in a merger completed December 18,
  2020 and deregistered its securities effective December 31, 2020 — before the fixed
  ~3-5-year outcome window opens, so no standalone public record exists for GCI Liberty
  under its own CIK within that window. The specific operating business continued to be
  separately disclosed as the "GCI Holdings" reportable segment of Liberty Broadband
  throughout 2021-2023, with revenue essentially flat to modestly increasing ($970M to
  $981M) and Adjusted OIBDA modestly increasing ($354M to $361M) over that span, alongside
  disclosed regulatory settlement liabilities tied to a federal subsidy program. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001104659-20-140760, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-24-001302,
  Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2023)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-001533,
  Liberty Broadband Corporation Form 10-K (FY2022)]
