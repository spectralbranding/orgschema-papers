## Realized financial record (FY2013)
- For the year ended December 31, 2013, the company reported revenues of $4.5 billion and net income of $478.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
- As of December 31, 2013, shareholders' equity was $5.0 billion and total assets were $34.8 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
- Premium collections were $3.5 billion in 2013, $3.3 billion in 2012, and $3.6 billion in 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]

## Realized state of the assembled portfolio's legacy blocks
- Legacy blocks that were no longer actively marketed were carried in a separately managed "Other CNO Business" segment, consisting of blocks of interest-sensitive life insurance, traditional life insurance, annuities, long-term care insurance, and other supplemental health products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
- The Medicare supplement block in the Washington National segment was in run-off in the window: the company had discontinued new sales of that business in the fourth quarter of 2012, and premiums for that block declined accordingly. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
- In-force blocks continued to be ceded to third-party reinsurers; for example, $2,818.3 million of ceded life insurance in force was reinsured with Wilton Reassurance Company, alongside cessions to Swiss Re, Jackson National, Munich American, and others. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]

## Realized state of processes and organization
- The actively marketed operations continued to run through three distribution channels -- career agents, independent producers, and direct marketing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
- Management concluded that the company's internal control over financial reporting was effective as of December 31, 2013, evaluated under the COSO Internal Control -- Integrated Framework. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-14-000006, Form 10-K (FY2013)]
