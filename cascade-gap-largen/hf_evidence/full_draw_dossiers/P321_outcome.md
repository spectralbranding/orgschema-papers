## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The acquired entity does not file periodic reports on its own account: its last
  annual report on Form 10-K covered fiscal year 2017 (filed February 28, 2018,
  pre-closing); no subsequent Form 10-K, 10-K/A, or 20-F was filed under CIK 0001348259. [SRC: SEC accession 0001348259-18-000008, Form 10-K FY2017 (last annual filing on this CIK)]
- A search of the acquirer's (AIG, CIK 0000005272) Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021
  (filed February 17, 2022 — within the ~3-5-year outcome window) finds Validus
  referenced only as an operating-company name and a debt-schedule line item within
  AIG's consolidated "General Insurance" segment; no standalone revenue, earnings,

## What IS disclosed about the acquired business within AIG's consolidated filings
- AIG's FY2021 Form 10-K lists Validus Reinsurance, Ltd. (Validus Re), Talbot Holdings
  Ltd. (Talbot), and Western World Insurance Group, Inc. among the "major operating
  companies" comprising its General Insurance segment (alongside National Union,
  American Home, Lexington, and other legacy AIG entities), indicating the acquired
  businesses continued to operate under their pre-acquisition names as constituent
  units of AIG's General Insurance segment as of that filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-024701, AIG Form 10-K FY2021, "Business" — segment description]
- AIG's FY2021 Form 10-K discloses "Validus notes and bonds payable" as a distinct
  line item within AIG's general-borrowings debt schedule, at a carrying amount of
  $293 million as of December 31, 2021 versus $348 million as of December 31, 2020,
  bearing a stated rate of 8.88% and maturing in 2040; this is a debt-schedule
  disclosure, not an earnings or revenue disclosure for the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-024701, AIG Form 10-K FY2021, Note on Long-Term Debt / Enterprise Risk Management debt-maturity table]
- Validus Reinsurance, Ltd. is separately named in AIG's catastrophe-reinsurance risk
  disclosure (e.g., a described program of catastrophe protection including $400
  million of per-occurrence limit in excess of a $150 million retention for named
  windstorm outside North America), consistent with Validus Re continuing to operate
  as an active reinsurance underwriting entity within AIG's structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-024701, AIG Form 10-K FY2021, Item 7 "Enterprise Risk Management"]
- Financial-strength ratings for Validus Reinsurance, Ltd. are listed separately
  alongside AIG's other insurance operating subsidiaries (rated A / A+ / -- / A2 by the
  agencies shown), again without an accompanying standalone financial-performance
  disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-024701, AIG Form 10-K FY2021, "Ratings" table]
- AIG's goodwill/intangible-asset note states that "Other intangible assets and Value
  of distribution network acquired (VODA) resulted primarily from the acquisition of
  Validus," but the accompanying roll-forward table presents these balances at the
  consolidated segment level (not isolated to a Validus-only figure), so no
  Validus-specific dollar impairment or write-down figure can be extracted from this
  disclosure. [UNVERIFIED — figure not separately attributable to Validus]
