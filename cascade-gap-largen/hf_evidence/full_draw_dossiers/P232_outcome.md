# Outcome sub-dossier — Brookfield Property REIT Inc. (former GGP Inc.) realized record, ~3-5-year horizon (~2020-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The renamed surviving entity continued to file
> its own periodic reports for part of the outcome window before its own registration
> was later terminated; this sub-dossier draws only on those post-closing filings. It
> contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following the August 2018 closing, the surviving corporation (renamed Brookfield
  Property REIT Inc., "BPR," retaining CIK 0001496048) continued to file its own
  periodic reports under its newly listed Class A Stock and Series A Preferred Stock,
  including Form 10-K annual reports for fiscal years 2018, 2019, and 2020 (the last
  filed 2021-02-26) and Form 10-Q quarterly reports into 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001496048-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- BPR's own registration was later terminated: on August 30, 2021, BPR filed a Form
  15-12B certifying termination/suspension of its reporting duty for its Class A Stock
  and 6.375% Series A Preferred Stock, recording approximately 1 holder of record as of
  the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-111019, Form 15-12B]

## Realized business record within the FY2020 (in-window) 10-K
- The FY2020 10-K describes ongoing impact from "the novel coronavirus pandemic
  (\"COVID-19\" or \"the global economic shutdown\")" on the company's regional mall
  portfolio, including "an increased number of bankruptcies of national retailers, as
  well as store closures," downward pressure on rental rates and occupancy levels, and
  tenants that filed for bankruptcy, ceased operations, downsized their brick-and-mortar
  presence, or failed to comply with lease obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001496048-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Net income attributable to the Class A stock (BPYU) "decreased 264.4% from $432.9
  million for the year ended December 31, 2019 to $(711.5) million for the year ended
  December 31, 2020 primarily due to the increase in equity in loss of unconsolidated
  real estate affiliates and the decrease in gains on the sale of unconsolidated"
  investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001496048-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The FY2020 10-K's quarterly data table shows a loss from continuing operations in
  every quarter of 2020 (Q1 $(100.4) million; Q2 $(231.6) million; Q3 $(190.3) million;
  Q4 $(279.3) million) and a net loss attributable to Brookfield Property REIT Inc. in
  every quarter of 2020 (Q1 $(81.8) million; Q2 $(209.1) million; Q3 $(170.6) million;
  Q4 $(250.0) million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001496048-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Full absorption of the remaining public entity within the outcome window
- On April 1, 2021, Brookfield Asset Management Inc. ("BAM") and Brookfield Property
  Partners L.P. ("BPY") announced an agreement for BAM to acquire all outstanding
  limited partnership units of BPY. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-045187, Form 8-K]
- That transaction closed on July 26, 2021: each holder of BPR Class A Stock received,
  per share, $12.38 in cash, 0.0913 of a BAM class A limited voting share, and 0.0657 of
  a BPY preferred unit (liquidation preference $25.00 per unit) in an automatic exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-098240, Form 8-K]
- In connection with that closing, BPR notified the Nasdaq Global Select Market that its
  Class A Stock trading would be suspended before the opening of trading on July 27,
  2021, and Nasdaq filed a Form 25 removing the Class A Stock from listing and
  deregistering it under Section 12(b); the Class A Stock ceased trading on Nasdaq
  effective before the market opened that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-098240, Form 8-K]
- Also on July 26, 2021, an affiliate of BAM purchased newly issued BPR Class A Stock
  for $252,125,000, with proceeds used to redeem all outstanding shares of BPR's 6.375%
  Series A Preferred Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-098240, Form 8-K]
- On July 30, 2021, five BPR directors (Soon Young Chang, Omar Carneiro da Cunha,
  Michael J. Warren, Caroline Atkinson, and A. Douglas McGregor) resigned; the filing
  states none resigned due to any disagreement with the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-098240, Form 8-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~3-5-year outcome window, the renamed surviving entity's own periodic
  filings show a swing from FY2019 net income of $432.9 million to an FY2020 net loss of
  $(711.5) million, explicitly tied by the company to COVID-19-driven retailer
  bankruptcies, store closures, and rent/occupancy pressure on its retail portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001496048-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Approximately three years after the 2018 closing, on July 26, 2021, the remaining
  public minority stake in the surviving entity was fully absorbed via a cash-plus-stock
  exchange into Brookfield Asset Management, its Class A Stock was removed from Nasdaq
  listing, and the entity's SEC reporting obligations were subsequently terminated
  (Form 15-12B, August 30, 2021, ~1 holder of record), ending independent public
  disclosure of the acquired business earlier than the full 3-5-year window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-098240, Form 8-K][SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-111019, Form 15-12B]
