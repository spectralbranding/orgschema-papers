## Acquisition and deregistration (why the outcome window is truncated)
- Baxalta entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated January 11, 2016 among Shire plc (a Jersey public company), BearTracks, Inc., and Baxalta Incorporated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-125393, Form 8-K]
- The merger closed on June 3, 2016; at the effective time each outstanding share of Baxalta common stock was converted into the right to receive $18.00 in cash plus 0.1482 of a Shire American Depositary Share (or, if validly elected, 0.4446 of a Shire ordinary share in lieu of that fraction of an ADS). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-125393, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the merger, Baxalta requested that the New York Stock Exchange suspend trading of its shares, and the filing addressed the deregistration of the shares under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-125393, Form 8-K]
- Baxalta filed a Form 15-12B on 2016-06-13 — a "FORM 15 CERTIFICATION AND NOTICE OF TERMINATION OF REGISTRATION" — terminating the registration of its common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126858, Form 15-12B]
