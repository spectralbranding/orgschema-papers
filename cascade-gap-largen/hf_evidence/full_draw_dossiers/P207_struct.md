## Transaction and parties at closing
- Snyder's-Lance, Inc., a North Carolina corporation headquartered in Charlotte, North
  Carolina, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated December 18, 2017 with
  Campbell Soup Company, a New Jersey corporation, and Twist Merger Sub, Inc., a North
  Carolina corporation and indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Campbell, pursuant to
  which Campbell would acquire the Company by means of a merger of Merger Sub with and
  into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving entity and becoming an
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Campbell. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company cash merger: each outstanding share
  of Company common stock (other than shares held by Campbell, Merger Sub, or Company
  subsidiaries) was converted into the right to receive $50.00 in cash, without
  interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- The adjusted fully diluted enterprise value of the transaction was approximately
  $6.093 billion, representing a multiple of 20.9x last-twelve-months EBITDA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on March 26, 2018; Campbell financed the acquisition with net
  proceeds from previously disclosed debt financing (a $1.2 billion senior unsecured
  term loan facility, with a corresponding permanent reduction to a bridge facility
  originally committed at up to $6.2 billion). [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K][SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- Consummation was not conditioned on Campbell obtaining financing. The Hart-Scott-Rodino
  Act waiting period was satisfied February 2, 2018 with no antitrust-mandated
  divestiture, hold-separate, or disposition condition imposed on either party as a
  condition to closing. Termination fees were specified in the merger agreement: a
  $149,000,000 fee payable by the Company under specified circumstances, a $198,600,000
  reverse termination fee payable by Campbell, and a $50,000,000 regulatory termination
  fee if antitrust approvals were not obtained. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]

## What the Company comprised at closing (product/business tier)
- The Company was engaged in the manufacturing, distribution, marketing and sale of
  snack food products including pretzels, sandwich crackers, kettle-cooked chips,
  pretzel crackers, cookies, potato chips, tortilla chips, popcorn and nuts, under
  brands including Snyder's of Hanover, Lance, Cape Cod, Kettle Brand, Snack Factory
  Pretzel Crisps, Pop Secret, and Late July. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company's common stock was listed on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under the
  symbol "LNCE" prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- All of the Company's then-outstanding equity awards (stock options, restricted stock,
  and restricted stock units) vested (with performance awards pro-rated) and were
  cancelled in exchange for cash payments tied to the $50.00 merger consideration at the
  effective time; the whole of the business, rather than a carved-out product line or
  asset group, crossed to the acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K]

## Legal vehicle
- The parties used a reverse triangular merger structure: Merger Sub merged with and
  into the Company, with the Company surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  Campbell. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- As of the effective time, the Company's Restated Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws
  were amended and restated in forms attached to the closing Form 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K]

## Governance and management (people tier) at closing
- All members of the Company's board of directors immediately prior to the effective
  time ceased to be directors of the Company at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K]
- Eight named individuals — Brian Driscoll (the Company's chief executive officer),
  Alex Pease, Andrew Frohning, Gail Sharps-Meyers, Randy Chapman, Kirk Jensen, Nikhil
  Sawant, and Holly Mensch — ceased to be officers of the Company at the effective time
  in connection with consummation of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K]
- The Company's executive officers had entered into executive severance agreements
  providing enhanced severance and benefits upon an involuntary termination without
  cause or voluntary termination for good reason following a change in control such as
  this merger; cash severance and annual-bonus payments were "double-trigger" (payable
  upon a qualifying termination following the change in control) while long-term
  incentive cash performance awards were "single-trigger" (payable upon the change in
  control itself). [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided that, through July 31, 2019, Campbell would provide
  continuing Company employees compensation and benefits in the aggregate no less
  favorable than immediately before the effective time, and would recognize prior
  service for benefit-plan purposes; the merger itself was deemed a "change in control"
  under Company benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing and registration status at closing
- In connection with consummation of the merger, the Company requested that the NASDAQ
  Global Select Market suspend trading of its common stock after the market closed on
  March 26, 2018, remove the common stock from listing, and file a notification of
  removal from listing and registration (Form 25) with the SEC; the Company also
  intended to file a further form to terminate its Exchange Act registration and
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K]
- EDGAR records confirm a Form 25-NSE filed March 26, 2018 and a Form 15-12G filed
  April 5, 2018 under the Company's CIK. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0000057528, accessions 0001354457-18-000066 (Form 25-NSE) and 0001174947-18-000568 (Form 15-12G)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company, all-cash reverse triangular merger (no
  carve-out, no antitrust-mandated divestiture) with a same-day, board-and-senior-
  management turnover: the entire board and eight named executive officers, including
  the chief executive officer, left the Company at the effective time, while a
  contractual continuity commitment (comparable pay/benefits through July 31, 2019,
  service-recognition, and change-in-control severance) bridged the workforce below the
  senior-management tier rather than retaining the pre-merger leadership team intact.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000477, Form 8-K][SRC: SEC accession 0001174947-18-000230, Form DEFM14A]
