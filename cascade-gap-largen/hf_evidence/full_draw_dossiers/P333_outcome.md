# Outcome sub-dossier — FCB Financial Holdings, Inc. (Florida Community Bank) realized record after absorption into Synovus Financial Corp., ~3-5-year horizon

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier contains no information about

> exist within Synovus effective January 1, 2019; the fixed outcome window is

## Deregistration (end of separate public-reporting status)
- FCB filed a Form 15-12B on 2019-01-14, certifying termination of registration of its
  common stock under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act, with "Synovus Financial Corp.,
  as successor by merger to FCB Financial Holdings, Inc." as the signing filer and the
  registrant's address given as "c/o Synovus Financial Corp." in Columbus, Georgia;
  approximate number of holders of record as of the filing was 0. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-000863, Form 15-12B]
- FCB filed no Form 10-K or other periodic report after this deregistration; no
  separate audited financial statements exist for FCB for the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-000863, Form 15-12B]

## Confirmation and timing of closing (via the surviving entity's later disclosure)
- Synovus's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 defines the "FCB Merger" as "The January 1,
  2019 merger of FCB into Synovus, with Synovus as the surviving entity," confirming
  the merger's completion date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021, "Index of Defined Terms"]
- The same Form 10-K records that Synovus issued common stock, reissued treasury stock,
  and exchanged equity awards/warrants "to acquire FCB" as a non-cash financing
  activity, and separately notes Synovus assumed approximately 3.2 million outstanding
  employee and director stock options in the FCB Merger on January 1, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows / Stock Compensation note]

## Absence of separate post-close disclosure of the acquired business
- Synovus's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states that Synovus "identified three major
  reportable business segments: Community Banking, Wholesale Banking, and Financial
  Management Services (FMS)," with corporate functions grouped under "Treasury and
  Corporate Other" — a functional segmentation with no segment or reporting unit
  corresponding to FCB, Florida Community Bank, or a Florida-specific business line. [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021, "Segment Disclosures"]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's goodwill-by-reporting-unit table lists only "Community Banking
  Reporting Unit," "Wholesale Banking Reporting Unit," "Consumer Mortgage Reporting
  Unit," and "Wealth Management Reporting Unit" for both December 31, 2019 and December
  31, 2021 balances; no reporting unit tied to the acquired FCB business is separately
  disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 5 "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K discloses a $44.9 million non-cash goodwill impairment charge
  recorded in 2020, but attributes it entirely to "the Consumer Mortgage reporting unit"
  (within the FMS segment) — a pre-existing Synovus business line, not the acquired FCB
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 5 "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]
- No further search located a separate disclosure by Synovus, within the fixed outcome
  window, of the acquired FCB/Florida Community Bank business's standalone revenue,
  earnings, asset quality, or operational trajectory as distinct from Synovus's
  consolidated results. [UNVERIFIED]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- FCB ceased to exist as a separate legal entity and SEC registrant effective January
  1, 2019 (confirmed by Synovus's own later Form 10-K) and deregistered via a Form
  15-12B filed 2019-01-14 with zero holders of record; consequently there is no
  separate public 3-5-year (~2021-2023) outcome record for FCB or the acquired Florida
  Community Bank business. Synovus's subsequent Forms 10-K report only functional,
  company-wide reportable segments (Community Banking, Wholesale Banking, FMS) and
  goodwill-by-reporting-unit disclosures that do not isolate the acquired business, and
  a 2020 goodwill impairment charge is explicitly attributed to a different,
  pre-existing reporting unit (Consumer Mortgage) rather than to FCB. Absent any located
  separate disclosure of the acquired business's trajectory, the outcome over the fixed
  window is coded uncertain (no public record) rather than inferred from Synovus's
  consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-000863, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0000018349-22-000076, Form 10-K FY2021]
