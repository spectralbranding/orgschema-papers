# Structural sub-dossier — Cole Credit Property Trust III, Inc. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Registrant and portfolio assembled (closing-era)
- The registrant is Cole Credit Property Trust III, Inc., a Maryland corporation and real
  estate investment trust with principal offices in Phoenix, Arizona; the fiscal-year-2012
  Form 10-K was for the year ended December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K cover page]
- As of December 31, 2012 the registrant and its consolidated joint ventures owned 1,014
  commercial properties comprising 43.1 million rentable square feet located in 47 states,
  99% leased with an average remaining lease term of about 12 years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- The portfolio was assembled rapidly by acquisition: the registrant and its consolidated
  joint ventures owned 1,014 properties as of December 31, 2012 compared with 693 as of
  December 31, 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- As of December 31, 2012 the portfolio comprised 807 single-tenant retail properties, 120
  single-tenant freestanding commercial properties, 70 multi-tenant retail properties, 16
  office and industrial properties, and one land parcel, with a gross carrying amount of
  $5,914,529 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K Schedule III]

## Management structure at closing — what did NOT cross the boundary (externally managed)
- The registrant had no employees and relied on its external advisor and the advisor's
  affiliates for substantially all day-to-day management: "We have no employees and rely
  upon our advisor and its affiliates to provide substantially all of our day-to-day
  management." [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- CR III Advisors acted as advisor under an advisory agreement; the advisor and its
  affiliates provided property management, asset management, financing, marketing, investor
  relations, and other administrative services on the registrant's behalf. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- All of the registrant's properties were managed by an affiliated property manager, Cole
  Realty Advisors, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- The advisor was owned within a broader sponsor group: "Our sponsor, Cole Real Estate
  Investments, is a group of affiliated entities, which includes our advisor." The sponsor
  had "built a business of over 350 employees" experienced in acquiring, financing, and
  managing commercial real estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]

## Transaction as structured at closing (internalization of the manager)
- On March 5, 2013 the registrant entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Cole
  Holdings Corporation (an Arizona corporation that is the parent and indirect owner of the
  registrant's advisor and is wholly owned by Christopher H. Cole, the chairman, chief
  executive officer, and president of the registrant), CREInvestments, LLC (a wholly owned
  subsidiary of the registrant, "Merger Sub"), and the Holdings Stockholder; the agreement
  provides for the merger of Holdings into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub surviving as a wholly
  owned subsidiary of the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- The controlling principal was fused with the acquired business: Cole Holdings Corporation
  is wholly owned by Christopher H. Cole, who was also the registrant's chairman, CEO, and
  president. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- The acquired business is a going investment-management operation: the special committee
  noted that "Holdings is a full-scale real estate investment management firm that currently
  manages over $12.0 billion of real estate assets for over 160,000 individual investors."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- Under the agreement, Holdings common stock would convert into upfront consideration of
  $20.0 million in cash (subject to adjustment) plus 10,711,225 newly-issued shares of the
  registrant's common stock; a further 2,142,245 shares were payable upon a listing on the
  New York Stock Exchange, plus a contingent 2017 "earn-out" tied to the acquired business's
  EBITDA and to relative stock performance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]

## Contractual bridging of the transferred substrate (closing-time)
- The registrant intended, if the merger were completed, to operate "our acquired investment
  management and real estate advisory business through one or more TRSs" (taxable REIT
  subsidiaries). [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- Subsequent to December 31, 2012 the registrant and its operating partnership entered into
  employment agreements with key personnel (including Christopher H. Cole); the agreements
  compensate the executives for services rendered after the merger and "are not part of the
  Merger consideration." [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- The upfront stock consideration and listing consideration were subject to a three-year
  lockup releasing approximately one-third of the shares each year, and a customary escrow
  agreement would hold roughly one-third of the upfront stock consideration in part to
  satisfy the seller's indemnity obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing the registrant held a large multi-state single- and multi-tenant retail real
  estate portfolio (1,014 properties, 43.1M sq ft) that had been assembled by acquisition,
  but had no employees of its own and depended entirely on an external advisor and affiliated
  property manager owned by a founder-controlled sponsor; the documented transaction brought
  that external management/advisory business inside the registrant by merging the advisor's
  parent (Cole Holdings) into a wholly owned subsidiary, with the personnel bridged by
  employment agreements and the equity consideration bridged by lockups, escrow, and a
  future earn-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000003, Form 10-K]
