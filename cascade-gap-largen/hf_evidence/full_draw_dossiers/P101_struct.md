## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K, date of earliest event reported August 25, 2016 (filed August 30, 2016), reporting under Item 5.03 that on August 25, 2016 the Company filed a Second Certificate of Correction to its charter with the State Department of Assessments and Taxation of Maryland, upon the approval and at the direction of the board of directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-16-000021, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The Second Certificate of Correction restored the original definition of "Roll-Up Transaction" in the Company's charter as it stood before a First Certificate of Correction filed March 21, 2014; the restored definition reads that a "Roll-Up Transaction" means "a transaction involving the acquisition, merger, conversion or consolidation either directly or indirectly of the Corporation and the issuance of securities of a Roll-Up Entity to the Stockholders." [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-16-000021, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The definition mirrors the definition used in the Statement of Policy Regarding Real Estate Investment Trusts adopted by the North American Securities Administrators Association (NASAA); the filing notes that a majority of state securities examiners require an unlisted issuer conducting a public offering to include Statement-of-Policy provisions in its charter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-16-000021, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The 8-K is a charter/governance correction event, not a property-acquisition or deal-terms document; it was signed by M. Kirk Scott, Chief Financial Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-16-000021, 8-K signature block]

## Parties, vehicle, and management structure at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is "an externally managed REIT" that has "no employees"; its day-to-day activities are managed by Dividend Capital Total Advisors LLC (the "Advisor"), which is wholly owned by the Company's Sponsor, Dividend Capital Total Advisors Group LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The Advisor "assumes principal responsibility for managing our affairs," researching, identifying, reviewing and making investments in and dispositions of real property and real estate-related investments on the Company's behalf under an Advisory Agreement, acting as a fiduciary; the current term of the Advisory Agreement expires June 30, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The Company's officers and its two interested directors are all employees of an affiliate of the Advisor; the Company's executive officers, in their capacities as such, do not receive compensation from the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The legal vehicle is an umbrella-partnership REIT (UPREIT): the Company holds all or substantially all of its assets through its operating partnership, Dividend Capital Total Realty Operating Partnership, LP, and is the sole general partner of that Operating Partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The Company is a non-listed, NAV-based REIT (its shares are not listed on the NYSE); it commenced an ongoing public offering of up to $3,000,000,000 of common stock on July 12, 2012, with shares distributed on a "best efforts" basis by a Dealer Manager that is an entity related to the Advisor and a FINRA member. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The valuation of the real property portfolio (used in the daily NAV process) is managed by Altus Group U.S., Inc., an independent valuation firm approved by the board of directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]

## What was assembled at the ownership boundary (the customer-facing asset) and how
- The Company is invested in "a diverse portfolio of real properties and, to a lesser extent, real estate-related debt investments"; its real-property investment consists of office, industrial and retail properties located in the United States. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The portfolio is assembled by serial acquisition and reshaped by disposition: the Company acquired one property during the year ended December 31, 2016, eight properties in 2015 and three properties in 2014, and disposed of a portfolio of 12 office and industrial properties during 2015; through dispositions its industrial ownership had declined to less than 5% of the portfolio as of December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- As of December 31, 2016 the real property portfolio comprised 20 real properties (55 buildings) totaling approximately 8,971 thousand square feet, approximately 91.2% leased, geographically diversified across 20 markets, at an aggregate reported real property value of approximately $2,282,200 thousand (office: 3 properties / 16 buildings; industrial: 3 / 5; retail: 9 / 34). [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- Reported total assets were $1,783,728 thousand as of December 31, 2016 (versus $1,960,891 thousand as of December 31, 2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- The Company runs none of these processes with its own personnel: it has no employees, and the identification, acquisition, management and disposition functions are performed by the external Advisor and its affiliates under the Advisory Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- Property-level management was contractually separable from the Advisory Agreement: the Company states it "previously paid our Advisor a development management fee and we were previously subject to a property management agreement," and "may reinstate the development management fee or reenter into a property management agreement at any time with the agreement of our Advisor." [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
- The Company is subject to conflicts of interest arising out of its relationship with the Advisor and other affiliates, including conflicts related to the compensation arrangements between the Advisor, certain affiliates and the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents a charter correction (restoring the original NASAA-derived definition of "Roll-Up Transaction" in the charter), not a property or portfolio purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-16-000021, 8-K Item 5.03]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is structured as a non-listed, NAV-based UPREIT that assembles a diversified office/industrial/retail portfolio (20 properties, ~8,971 thousand square feet across 20 markets; total assets ~$1,783,728 thousand as of December 31, 2016) by serial acquisition and disposition, while the Company itself has no employees and the investing, acquiring, managing and disposition processes — and the people who run them — sit with the external Advisor (Dividend Capital Total Advisors LLC) and its affiliates under the Advisory Agreement rather than being employed or performed by the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001327978-17-000028, 10-K FY2016]
