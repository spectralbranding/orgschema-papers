# Structural sub-dossier — PharMerica Corporation acquisition (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- PharMerica Corporation, a Delaware corporation whose common stock traded on the NYSE
  under the symbol "PMC," is a national provider of institutional pharmacy, specialty
  infusion, and hospital pharmacy management services, servicing skilled nursing
  facilities, assisted living facilities, hospitals, in-home care patients, and cancer
  patients (including the only national oncology pharmacy in the United States). [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "The Parties to the Merger
  Agreement--PharMerica Corporation"]
- On August 1, 2017, PharMerica entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with
  Phoenix Parent Holdings Inc. ("Parent") and Phoenix Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub," a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Parent), both newly formed Delaware corporations affiliated
  with investment funds affiliated with Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. ("KKR"); at
  closing an affiliate of Walgreens Boots Alliance, Inc. ("WBA") acquired a minority
  ownership interest in Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A,
  "The Parties to the Merger Agreement--Phoenix Parent Holdings Inc. and Phoenix Merger
  Sub Inc."]
- The merger was structured so that Merger Sub would merge with and into PharMerica,
  with PharMerica continuing as the surviving corporation, wholly owned by Parent; each
  outstanding share of common stock (other than certain excluded shares) would convert
  into the right to receive $29.25 per share in cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "Certain Effects of the Merger"]
- The transaction closed December 7, 2017: Merger Sub merged with and into the Company,
  with the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-17-045327, Form 8-K dated December 7, 2017, Item 2.01]

## Post-closing public-reporting and listing status (closing-time process)
- Upon completion, the Company requested that the NYSE suspend trading and remove the
  common stock from listing prior to the open of trading on December 8, 2017, and stated
  its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate Exchange Act registration; PharMerica filed a
  Form 15-12B on December 18, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-17-045327, Form 8-K
  dated December 7, 2017, Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-17-046543, Form
  15-12B]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The transaction is structured as an acquisition of the entire company: PharMerica
  survives the merger intact as a single legal entity, becoming a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent, rather than a division, product line, or subset of assets being
  separated out; the proxy statement identifies no separation of the customer-facing
  pharmacy product or operating processes from the surviving legal entity, referencing
  PharMerica's existing Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016 rather than describing a
  carved-out or reorganized business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A,
  "Certain Effects of the Merger"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A,
  "The Parties to the Merger Agreement--PharMerica Corporation"]
- PharMerica's directors and executive officers held equity-based awards (restricted
  stock units and performance stock units) treated under the merger agreement as
  accelerated cash payments or converted into cash-settled awards continuing under
  existing vesting schedules; certain named executive officers held pre-existing
  employment agreements providing for accelerated or continued vesting and enhanced
  severance benefits tied to a change in control. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "Interests of the Company's Directors and Executive
  Officers in the Merger"]

## Financing and deal-mechanics structure at closing
- Funds required to complete the merger were structured as a combination of $450
  million equity financing ($315 million from KKR Americas Fund XII L.P. and affiliates,
  $135 million from WBA and affiliates) and borrowings under a $1,100 million senior
  secured credit facility ($815 million first lien term loan, $100 million revolving
  facility, $185 million second lien term loan); consummation was not conditioned on
  financing, and KKR Americas XII and WBA executed limited guarantees of Parent's
  termination-fee obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "The
  Merger (Proposal 1)--Financing"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A,
  "The Merger (Proposal 1)--Limited Guarantees"]
- The merger agreement specified a Company termination fee of $33 million payable to
  Parent under specified circumstances, and a reverse termination fee payable by Parent
  to the Company of either $56.6 million or $113.3 million depending on circumstances.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "The Merger Agreement--Termination
  Fees"]
- In connection with the merger agreement, the Company, KKR Americas XII, and WBA
  entered into an Antitrust Support Side Letter under which KKR and WBA affiliates
  agreed to use reasonable best efforts to help obtain required regulatory approvals,
  subject to limitations excluding any obligation to divest assets. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "Antitrust Support Side Letter"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a reverse-triangular merger in which a newly formed
  acquisition vehicle (Merger Sub) merged into PharMerica, leaving PharMerica intact as
  the surviving legal entity and wholly owned subsidiary of a newly formed KKR-affiliated
  holding company (with a minority WBA interest), funded by a combination of committed
  equity and debt financing, with no carve-out, spin-off, or separation of the
  customer-facing pharmacy business from its operating entity disclosed in the proxy
  materials. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002126, DEFM14A, "Certain Effects of the
  Merger"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-17-045327, Form 8-K dated December 7, 2017]
