# Structural sub-dossier — Kennedy-Wilson Holdings, Inc. combination with Kennedy Wilson Europe Real Estate Plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Kennedy-Wilson Holdings, Inc. ("KWH"), a Delaware corporation (SEC CIK 1408100), solicited its own stockholders' approval to issue KWH common stock to combine with Kennedy Wilson Europe Real Estate Plc ("KWE"), a public limited company registered in Jersey and listed on the London Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, cover page and notice of special meeting]
- At the date of the proxy statement, KWH already owned approximately 23.8% of KWE's issued and outstanding shares and was its largest shareholder, and also served (through a wholly owned subsidiary, the "Investment Manager") as KWE's external investment manager. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
- KWE shareholders (other than KWH and its subsidiaries) were entitled to receive, for each KWE ordinary share, either (i) 0.667 shares of KWH common stock, or (ii) a mixed consideration of 300 pence cash paid by KWH, 250 pence cash paid by KWE as a special distribution, and 0.3854 shares of KWH common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Overview of the Transaction"]
- The combination was structured as a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement between KWE and its shareholders under Article 125 of the Companies (Jersey) Law 1991 — not a US-style merger through a separately formed merger subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
- The scheme was sanctioned by the Royal Court of Jersey on October 18, 2017 and became effective October 20, 2017; completion consideration comprised approximately 37,226,695 new KWH shares plus approximately £528.57 million ($696.77 million) in cash, of which £288.32 million was paid by KWH and £240.25 million by KWE via special distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-17-000129, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]
- Following completion, KWE's ordinary shares were cancelled from listing on the premium segment of the UK Listing Authority's Official List and ceased trading on the London Stock Exchange main market, effective on or about October 23, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001408100-17-000140, Form 8-K, Item 7.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- Controlling intent / imprint: KWH states the transaction would be accounted for as an equity transaction under ASC 810 because "KWH controls KWE and will continue to control KWE after the Transaction," with no gain or loss recognized in KWH's consolidated income statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Accounting Treatment"]
- Legal vehicle: no new merger subsidiary was formed; the combination was effected directly between KWH and KWE via the Jersey scheme of arrangement, with KWH issuing its own shares and cash directly to KWE shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
- Business/value-capture model: before the transaction, all of KWE's investment and asset-management decisions were made by KWH's wholly owned Investment Manager subsidiary under a February 2014 investment management agreement (a 1% of adjusted net-asset-value management fee plus a performance fee); the transaction converts a pre-existing external-management relationship into full ownership rather than introducing a new operating model. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Investment Management Agreement"]
- People/management/organization: the proxy states "there will be no change to management or systems as a result of the Merger, which should minimise integration risk and disruption to the business," describing continuity of the existing leadership team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
- KWH's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer and other KWH executive officers/directors already served on KWE's board of directors and on the Investment Manager's investment committee before the transaction, and held KWE equity awards that would vest upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product/asset: KWE's real estate portfolio (assets located primarily in the United Kingdom and Ireland) became part of KWH's consolidated global portfolio at closing, continuing under the same investment-decision authority that had already managed it externally. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]

## Bridging / transitional arrangements at closing
- A separate manager's-powers agreement (dated April 10, 2017) between KWE and the Investment Manager governed cash-disbursement authority and certain restricted actions during the pendency of the transaction, subject to approval by KWE's Independent Committee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Separate Manager's Powers Agreement"]
- An indemnity agreement (dated June 23, 2017) among KWH, the Investment Manager, KWE, and the KWE independent directors allocated liability for inaccuracies or omissions in KWE information filed with, or communicated to, regulators. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Indemnity Agreement"]
- A transaction agreement between KWH and KWE allocated responsibility for obtaining regulatory approvals and imposed mutual notice/cooperation obligations through closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Transaction Agreement"]
- The investment management agreement's existing termination provisions (including a three-years'-fees termination payment triggered by certain change-of-control or governance events) were in force at the time of the transaction; the proxy statement describes these provisions without stating that the agreement was terminated as part of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A, "Investment Management Agreement"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined KWH with an already-affiliated company (a minority-owned, externally managed investee) into full ownership by means of a Jersey scheme of arrangement rather than a US merger-subsidiary structure, with KWH's pre-existing management personnel, board representation, and investment-decision authority over KWE continuing unchanged into the combined enterprise; no separate integration or transition-services agreement was needed to bridge management or operations because the controlling relationship and operating personnel predated the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-283232, DEFM14A]
