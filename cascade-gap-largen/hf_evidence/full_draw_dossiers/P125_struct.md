## Nature of the entity and how its asset base was actually assembled
- The registrant is a Maryland corporation formed May 4, 2009, electing REIT status,
  that used the proceeds of a public non-traded stock offering (terminated December 20,
  2013) to invest in a portfolio of real estate, primarily multifamily properties,
  located throughout the United States. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013) Item 1]
- The registrant's real estate portfolio was assembled asset-by-asset over several
  years rather than through a single discrete corporate transaction: it owned eight
  multifamily properties at December 31, 2011, thirty at December 31, 2012, and
  sixty-three (comprising 15,859 apartment homes and 30,125 square feet of commercial
  space) at December 31, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013) Item 2]
- This asset-by-asset acquisition pattern continued into the closing era: on June 3,
  2014, through a newly formed, indirect wholly owned subsidiary (SIR Oak Crossing,
  LLC), the Company acquired from an unaffiliated third-party seller a fee-simple
  interest in a 222-unit multifamily community in Fort Wayne, Indiana for $24,230,000,
  financed with remaining IPO proceeds plus a $15,762,000 loan from PNC Bank under a
  Fannie Mae structured ARM program. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000026, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or operating agreement with the property
  seller is described in connection with this acquisition; the acquired property was
  placed directly into the Company's own, already-standing external-management
  structure rather than bridged by an interim arrangement with the seller. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000026, Form 8-K]

## External advisor / property-manager tier and controlling-principal fusion
- The Company is externally managed: Steadfast Income Advisor, LLC (the "advisor")
  supervises and manages the Company's day-to-day operations and selects its real
  property investments, subject to board oversight. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013) Item 1]
- On March 12, 2014, the Company entered Amendment No. 5 to the Amended and Restated
  Advisory Agreement among the Company, its operating partnership, and the advisor,
  renewing the advisory relationship's term for one additional year (May 4, 2014 to
  May 3, 2015) — the same external-advisor structure was renewed, not replaced, going
  into the drawn filing's period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000016, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Acquired properties are operated by Steadfast Management Company, Inc. (the
  "property manager"), an affiliate of the Company's sponsor, under management
  agreements paying a percentage-of-revenue fee; a small number of properties were
  managed instead by an unaffiliated third-party manager. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013) Item 1 / Item 13]
- The Company's sponsor, Steadfast REIT Investments, LLC, purchased the Company's
  initial shares in 2009 and is majority owned and controlled indirectly by Rodney F.
  Emery, who is also the Company's chairman and chief executive officer — a documented
  fusion of the controlling sponsor principal with the officer tier of the managed
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013) Item 13]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- At the closing era, the entity's customer-facing product (leased multifamily real
  estate) crossed into the entity's ownership one asset at a time, from unaffiliated
  third-party sellers, financed by a mix of equity offering proceeds and asset-level
  mortgage debt; the operational processes and personnel that run each acquired asset
  were supplied throughout by the entity's own standing, sponsor-affiliated external
  advisor and property manager rather than inherited from the seller or bridged by a
  transitional-services agreement; and the controlling intent behind the entity was
  vested in a single natural person who was simultaneously the sponsor's controlling
  owner and the entity's chairman/CEO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000019, Form 10-K (FY2013)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-14-000026, Form 8-K]
