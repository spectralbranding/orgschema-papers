## The drawn filing, described truthfully
- The drawn accession, a Form 8-K dated August 22, 2017, reports only that stockholders
  approved amendments to the registrant's Maryland charter (Item 5.03) at the 2017 Annual
  Meeting, to comply with requests from a state securities examiner; the Charter
  Amendments became effective August 22, 2017. It contains no merger, acquisition, or
  disposition terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000124, Form 8-K, Item 5.03]
- This registrant does not have a single discrete "closing" transaction dated 2017: it is
  a non-traded, continuously-offered REIT that grows its portfolio through an ongoing
  series of individual property acquisitions funded by an open public offering, rather
  than through one identifiable merger or acquisition event. The facts below are drawn
  from the entity's contemporaneous closing-era primary filings (the FY2016 Form 10-K,
  filed March 2017, and an August 2017 Form 8-K) rather than from the drawn filing itself.

## Legal vehicle and organizational structure (closing-era)
- The registrant is a Maryland corporation that conducts all of its business through
  Carter Validus Operating Partnership II, LP, a Delaware limited partnership formed
  January 10, 2013; the registrant is the sole general partner, and Carter Validus
  Advisors II, LLC ("the Advisor") is the initial limited partner of the Operating
  Partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]
- Individual properties are held through single-purpose, wholly-owned subsidiaries of the
  Operating Partnership (e.g., "DCII-8700 Governors Hill Drive, LLC" and "HCII-9800 Levin
  Road NW, LLC"), which are periodically added as guarantors/collateral to a shared
  syndicated credit facility rather than financed individually. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000127, Form 8-K, Item 2.03]

## Controlling intent / management model — externally managed, no employees
- "We have no paid employees and we rely on our Advisor to provide substantially all of
  our services." The Advisor is managed by, and is a subsidiary of, the registrant's
  sponsor, Carter Validus REIT Management Company II, LLC ("the Sponsor"); a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of the Sponsor separately serves as Property Manager.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The Advisor and Property Manager receive acquisition- and operational-stage fees under
  an Advisory Agreement; the Sponsor's own equity investment in the registrant was
  limited to $200,000 (20,000 Class A shares at $10.00/share). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Business model / customer-facing asset (the product)
- The registrant was formed to invest in income-producing commercial real estate,
  focused on net-leased "mission critical" data center and healthcare properties leased
  to creditworthy tenants, operated through two reportable segments — data center and
  healthcare commercial real estate investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]
- As of December 31, 2016, the registrant owned 37 real estate investments comprising 51
  properties (~2,984,000 rentable square feet) across 29 metropolitan statistical areas
  and one micropolitan statistical area, 99.6% leased, for an aggregate purchase price of
  approximately $1,000,088,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Capital-raising / financing structure at closing-era
- The registrant funded portfolio growth through a continuous public offering (Form
  S-11) of up to $2,350,000,000 in shares (up to $2,250,000,000 primary offering plus up
  to $100,000,000 via distribution reinvestment plan); as of December 31, 2016 it had
  issued approximately 83,102,000 shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]
- Portfolio financing was pooled under a syndicated KeyBank credit facility (a
  $325,000,000 revolving line plus a $100,000,000 term loan, expandable to $550,000,000);
  on August 25, 2017 two additional wholly-owned property subsidiaries were joined as
  guarantors and their properties pledged as collateral, increasing pool availability by
  approximately $11,000,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000127, Form 8-K, Item 1.01/2.03]

## Structural summary (closing-era only)
- No single "roll-up" transaction with identifiable deal terms occurred in 2017: the
  registrant is an externally managed, non-traded REIT whose "product" (a diversified
  pool of net-leased data center and healthcare real estate) was built through ongoing
  individual property acquisitions and pooled financing, with the operating processes
  (property/asset management) and controlling intent supplied entirely by an external
  Advisor/Sponsor/Property Manager complex rather than by employees of the registrant
  itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-17-000028, Form 10-K FY2016]
