## Transaction and parties at closing
- The information statement describes DowDuPont Inc.'s ("DowDuPont") separation of its materials science business, "Dow," through the distribution of shares of common stock of its subsidiary, Dow Holdings Inc. (defined in the document as "Dow NewCo," with Dow NewCo's common stock the "Dow common stock"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- DowDuPont stated it intended to separate into three independent, publicly traded companies — one each for its agriculture, materials science, and specialty products businesses — and that this filing effected the first step: the separation of the materials science business, "Dow." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Prior to the completion of the Merger on August 31, 2017, Historical Dow was a standalone, publicly traded company; as a result of the Merger, Historical Dow became a subsidiary of DowDuPont, which then undertook a series of internal reorganization transactions to align its businesses into three subgroups: agriculture, materials science, and specialty products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- "Dow" refers to Dow NewCo, The Dow Chemical Company ("TDCC") and their consolidated subsidiaries after giving effect to the Internal Reorganization and Business Realignment, resulting in Dow NewCo holding the materials science business of DowDuPont. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Following the separation and distribution of Dow, DowDuPont was expected to be renamed (name shown as "[●]", "New DuPont") and to subsequently separate Corteva, Inc., the subsidiary holding the agriculture business, by a further pro rata distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Distribution mechanics at closing
- The distribution was to be effected by way of a pro rata dividend of Dow common stock to DowDuPont stockholders of record as of the record date; in the distribution DowDuPont would distribute to DowDuPont stockholders all of the then issued and outstanding shares of Dow common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The distribution ratio ("[●] shares of Dow common stock for every [●] shares of DowDuPont common stock"), the distribution date, the listing exchange, and the trading symbol were shown as unfilled placeholders ("[●]") in this preliminary information statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Stockholders would not receive fractional shares of Dow common stock and instead would receive a cash payment for any fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transaction, together with certain related transactions, was intended to qualify as a tax-free transaction for U.S. federal income tax purposes under Section 368(a)(1)(D) and Section 355 of the Code, such that no gain or loss would generally be recognized by DowDuPont stockholders on the receipt of Dow common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: at the separation the new Dow would be comprised of a portfolio of six global business units organized into three operating segments — Performance Materials & Coatings; Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure; and Packaging & Specialty Plastics. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operational assets/processes: Dow produces products at 114 manufacturing sites in 32 countries, utilizing proprietary technologies; the document also describes co-located sites across value chains providing greater ability to optimize asset utilization (a vertical integration model). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / organization: on a pro forma basis in 2017 Dow employed approximately 39,000 people and delivered pro forma sales of approximately $44.8 billion, pro forma operating EBIT of $5.5 billion, and pro forma operating EBITDA of $8.2 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- (Context on the predecessor headcount:) Historical Dow employed approximately 54,000 people at December 31, 2017, down from approximately 56,000 at December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Controlling intent / management: the information statement's stockholder letter is signed by Jim Fitterling, Chief Executive Officer, Dow Holdings Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- Separation agreement: Dow would enter into the separation agreement with DowDuPont and Corteva to effect the separation (including the Internal Reorganization and Business Realignment) and distribution of Dow and Corteva. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- In connection with the separation and distribution, Dow would also enter into various other agreements with New DuPont and Corteva, including a tax matters agreement, an employee matters agreement, intellectual property cross-license agreements, trademark license agreements, and certain services, manufacturing, supply and real estate-related agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- These agreements would collectively provide for the terms of the allocation among Dow, New DuPont and Corteva of the assets, liabilities and obligations of DowDuPont and its subsidiaries (including investments, property, employee benefits, and tax-related assets and liabilities). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The information statement cautioned that historical financial statements reflect only allocations of corporate expenses from Historical Dow and are not necessarily representative of the costs Dow will incur for similar services as an independent company following the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction separated the materials science business of DowDuPont into an independent public company (Dow Holdings Inc. / "Dow NewCo," holding TDCC and its subsidiaries after an Internal Reorganization and Business Realignment) by a pro rata distribution to DowDuPont stockholders of all outstanding Dow common stock, intended to be tax-free under Sections 368(a)(1)(D) and 355; the distributed entity held six global business units in three operating segments (Performance Materials & Coatings; Industrial Intermediates & Infrastructure; Packaging & Specialty Plastics), 114 manufacturing sites in 32 countries, and approximately 39,000 employees (2017 pro forma), with continuity of the incumbent chief executive (Jim Fitterling), while the assets, liabilities, obligations, and shared functions of the previously combined company were allocated among the three separating companies and bridged at closing by a separation agreement plus tax matters, employee matters, IP cross-license, trademark license, and services/manufacturing/supply/real estate agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-268647, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
