## Terminal event and change of control (realized record)
- Effective December 2, 2016, pursuant to an Implementation Agreement and a Scheme of
  Arrangement under Section 210 of the Singapore Companies Act, GWafers Singapore Pte. Ltd.
  (an entity of GlobalWafers Co., Ltd., a Republic of China / Taiwan company) "acquired all
  of the outstanding ordinary shares of the Company," and "the Company is an indirect wholly
  owned subsidiary of GWC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Consideration: shareholders (other than the acquiror group) received "the right to receive
  US$12.00 per share in cash." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Leadership/organization at the change of control: "each member of the Company's board of
  directors resigned from the board," and three GWC-designated directors (Doris Hsiu-Lan Hsu,
  Mark Lynn England and Woo Heng Thong) were appointed; on December 2, 2016 "the employment
  of Shaker Sadasivam, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Company, was terminated." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Financing at the change of control: the 2014 senior secured credit agreement (as amended
  December 29, 2015) "was repaid in full and terminated," the MEMC Korea term loans with
  Shinhan Bank, Hana Bank and Korea Development Bank were "repaid in full and terminated,"
  and a new facility of up to $200.0 million guaranteed by GWC was entered by the Company's
  subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 1.02]
