## Transaction and parties at closing
- ILG, Inc. ("ILG") and Marriott Vacations Worldwide Corporation ("MVW") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 30, 2018, together with Ignite Holdco, Inc. ("Holdco," a wholly-owned direct subsidiary of ILG), Ignite Holdco Subsidiary, Inc. ("Ignite Merger Sub," a wholly-owned direct subsidiary of Holdco), Volt Merger Sub, Inc. ("Volt Corporate Merger Sub"), and Volt Merger Sub, LLC ("Volt LLC Merger Sub"), the latter two wholly-owned direct subsidiaries of MVW. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus]
- The combination was structured as a four-step sequence: (1) Ignite Merger Sub merged with and into ILG, with ILG surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Holdco (the "ILG Merger"); (2) ILG converted from a corporation to an LLC ("ILG LLC"); (3) Volt Corporate Merger Sub merged with and into Holdco, with Holdco surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of MVW (the "Initial Holdco Merger"); and (4) Holdco merged with and into Volt LLC Merger Sub, with Volt LLC Merger Sub surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of MVW (the "Final Holdco Merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- ILG operated in two segments prior to the transaction: Vacation Ownership (development, marketing, sales and financing of vacation ownership interests and related management services, comprising Vistana Signature Experiences and Hyatt Vacation Ownership) and Exchange and Rental (comprising Interval International, the Vistana Signature Network, the Hyatt Residence Club, Trading Places International, and Aqua-Aston Holdings). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- MVW described itself as the exclusive worldwide developer, marketer, seller and manager of vacation ownership and related products, generating revenue from selling vacation ownership products, managing resorts, financing purchases, and renting inventory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and legal mechanics
- ILG stockholders were to receive, for each share of ILG common stock, 0.165 shares of MVW common stock (the fixed "exchange ratio," not adjusted for stock-price changes before closing) plus $14.75 in cash without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- Based on MVW's closing price the last trading day before announcement (April 27, 2018), the merger consideration represented an implied value of $36.93 per ILG share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was not subject to a financing condition; MVW arranged a bridge facility commitment for up to $2,450,000,000 to fund the cash consideration and fees to the extent not covered by balance-sheet cash or debt issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: ILG's vacation-ownership resort portfolio and brand licenses (Sheraton, Westin, Hyatt Vacation Ownership, Marriott Vacation Club and Ritz-Carlton Club vacation-ownership licenses held by MVW separately) and its exchange/rental network (Interval International, TPI, Aqua-Aston, VRI) were the assets brought into the combined company through the merger structure described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: ILG (as ILG LLC after conversion) became, through the sequential mergers, a wholly-owned subsidiary of MVW via intermediate Holdco/Volt Merger Sub entities formed solely for the transaction; Ignite Merger Sub and Volt Corporate Merger Sub ceased to exist upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- People/organization (board): eight of MVW's then-current directors were to remain on MVW's board following closing, with two members of ILG's then-current board to be added by mutual agreement, described in the proxy as intended to ensure "continuity of MVW's Board." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- Employee benefits continuity: the merger agreement required MVW to honor ILG's existing compensation and benefit plans and, for one year following closing, provide continuing ILG employees with base compensation, cash incentive opportunities, and (with enumerated exclusions such as defined-benefit pension and equity-based benefits) aggregate employee benefits no less favorable than those in place immediately before closing, plus severance protection and service-credit recognition for years worked at ILG. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or supply arrangement bridging a retained shared substrate was identified in the reviewed sections of the joint proxy statement/prospectus; the reviewed disclosure describes the employee-benefits continuity covenant above as the principal contractual mechanism addressing post-closing personnel treatment. [UNVERIFIED beyond the employee-benefits covenant cited]

## Risk disclosures on integration (as anticipated at signing, not realized outcome)
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus disclosed, as a risk factor, that the pursuit and preparation for integration "may place a significant burden on management and internal resources" and that the integration process "could take longer than anticipated," with no assurance that anticipated benefits would be realized. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A]

## Closing completion (per the deal 8-K)
- ILG completed its merger with MVW on September 1, 2018 pursuant to the April 30, 2018 merger agreement, with Volt LLC Merger Sub surviving the Final Holdco Merger as a wholly-owned subsidiary of MVW. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-266809, Form 8-K dated 2018-09-05, Item 2.01]
- On August 31, 2018, ILG notified NASDAQ that trading in ILG common stock should be suspended and its listing removed; trading was suspended prior to the opening of business on September 4, 2018, and ILG requested that NASDAQ file a Form 25 with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-266809, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]
- Upon closing, ILG's directors and executive officers ceased serving in those capacities and ILG's articles of incorporation and by-laws were amended and restated to conform to those of the merger subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-266809, Form 8-K, Items 5.01-5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- ILG (vacation-ownership development/management plus an exchange-and-rental network) was combined into MVW through a sequential four-entity merger structure for fixed per-share cash-and-stock consideration (0.165 MVW shares + $14.75 cash), with ILG's product/brand portfolio and its Interval International/TPI/Aqua-Aston/VRI exchange businesses becoming MVW subsidiaries, two ILG directors added to MVW's board, and continuing ILG employees covered by a one-year compensation/benefits-continuity covenant; the reviewed closing-era filings did not disclose a transition-services or shared-substrate bridging agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-223956, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-266809, Form 8-K]
