## Realized completion of the closing-era transaction (2013)
- The manager-internalization merger was completed effective April 5, 2013: Holdings merged
  into the registrant's wholly owned subsidiary (Merger Sub), and the selling executives
  received a total of $20.0 million in cash (subject to adjustment) and 10,711,225
  newly-issued shares of the registrant's common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000006, Form 8-K dated April 5, 2013]
- Leadership changed at completion: on April 5, 2013 Christopher H. Cole resigned as chief
  executive officer and president and the board appointed Marc T. Nemer to those positions;
  Cole remained a director and became executive chairman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000006, Form 8-K dated April 5, 2013]
- On April 11, 2013 the registrant issued a press release commenting that American Realty
  Capital Properties, Inc. had withdrawn its earlier unsolicited proposal to acquire 100% of
  the registrant's outstanding common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001425923-13-000006, Form 8-K dated April 5, 2013]

## Acquisition and end of independent public existence (2013–2014)
- The registrant subsequently listed its common stock on the New York Stock Exchange under
  the name Cole Real Estate Investments, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-040724, Form 8-K dated February 7, 2014]
- On October 22, 2013 Cole Real Estate Investments, Inc. entered into an Agreement and Plan
  of Merger with American Realty Capital Properties, Inc. and Clark Acquisition, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-040724, Form 8-K dated February 7, 2014]
- The merger was consummated on February 7, 2014: each outstanding share of Cole common
  stock (other than shares held by ARCP or affiliates) converted into the right to receive
  either 1.0929 shares of ARCP common stock or $13.82 in cash; the shares of Cole common
  stock were delisted and removed from trading on the NYSE prior to the open of trading on
  February 7, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-040724, Form 8-K dated February 7, 2014]
- Registration of the entity was terminated: a Form 15 filed February 18, 2014 by "ARC
  Properties Operating Partnership, L.P., successor to Clark Acquisition, LLC, successor to
  Cole Real Estate Investments, Inc." certified termination of registration under the
  Exchange Act, reporting an approximate number of holders of record as of the notice date
  of "One"; it was signed by Nicholas S. Schorsch. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-010247, Form 15-12B filed February 18, 2014]

  within about ten months the entity was itself acquired: it listed on the NYSE as Cole Real
  Estate Investments, Inc., agreed on October 22, 2013 to be acquired by American Realty
  Capital Properties, completed that merger on February 7, 2014 ($13.82 cash or 1.0929 ARCP

  February 18, 2014 with a single holder of record. Consequently there is no separate public
  audited record for this entity within the fixed ~2016–2018 outcome window, and the outcome
