## Realized record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- Under private ownership (styled "Transformco"), the acquired retail footprint continued
  to contract: a reputable trade-press account dated January 24, 2022 reported that
  research firm Coresight counted 46 announced Sears closures and 29 announced Kmart
  closures during 2021, with Transformco owning both banners. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/sears-gets-smaller-with-florida-store-closure/617572]
- The same account identifies Eddie Lampert as the last CEO of Sears Holdings and majority
  owner of the company in the years leading up to its 2018 bankruptcy filing. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/sears-gets-smaller-with-florida-store-closure/617572]
