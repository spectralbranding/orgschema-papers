## Transaction and parties at closing
- Quality Care Properties, Inc. ("QCP") is a Maryland corporation primarily engaged in the ownership and leasing of post-acute/skilled nursing properties and memory care/assisted living properties, and had elected REIT treatment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Welltower Inc. is a Delaware corporation, also REIT-elected, that owns interests in properties concentrated in major, high-growth U.S., Canadian and U.K. markets, consisting of seniors housing/post-acute communities and outpatient medical properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Potomac Acquisition LLC ("Merger Sub") is a Delaware LLC and wholly owned subsidiary of Welltower, formed in 2018 solely to engage in the transactions contemplated by the merger agreement and had not engaged in any other business activity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 25, 2018, certain QCP subsidiaries were merged with and into Merger Sub in a series of successive mergers, followed by the merger of QCP with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Welltower. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger consideration was $20.75 in cash per share (plus $0.006/day if closing occurred after August 25, 2018), representing a discount of approximately 0.24% to the pre-speculation closing price, a premium of approximately 64.7% to the price before QCP's announced plan to acquire HCR ManorCare, and a premium of approximately 17.3% to the 60-day volume-weighted average price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Total funds required to complete the merger were estimated at approximately $3.9 billion, funded by approximately $1.0 billion of debt financing and approximately $2.9 billion of cash on hand/credit facilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger's closing was conditioned on the concurrent closing of separate "HCR ManorCare transactions": under an Alternative Plan Sponsor Agreement dated April 25, 2018 among QCP, HCR ManorCare, Inc., ProMedica Health System, Inc./Suburban HealthCo, Inc. ("ProMedica"), and Meerkat I LLC (the "ProMedica-Welltower JV"), ProMedica agreed to acquire all newly issued common stock of HCR ManorCare pursuant to an amended plan of reorganization in HCR ManorCare's bankruptcy proceeding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on July 26, 2018, with Welltower completing its previously announced acquisition of QCP; QCP stockholders received the merger consideration in cash and Potomac survived as a wholly owned Welltower subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]

## What crossed at the tier level, and what did not
- Controlling intent: the QCP board unanimously approved and recommended the merger; as of the record date QCP's directors and executive officers together held only approximately 0.19% of the outstanding common stock entitled to vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Business/value-capture model: the post-acute/skilled nursing and memory care/assisted living real estate leasing business continued as a component of the surviving Welltower subsidiary rather than terminating. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: QCP's separate corporate existence ceased; Potomac's certificate of formation and amended/restated LLC operating agreement (in effect immediately before the merger) continued as the surviving entity's governing documents, replacing QCP's Articles of Amendment and Restatement and Bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K, Item 5.03]
- People/management: all officers of QCP and all members of QCP's board of directors ceased to hold those roles at the effective time; the surviving entity appointed a single new officer, Matthew McQueen, as Senior Vice President-General Counsel and Secretary, effective July 26, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- The merger agreement required each QCP director to deliver a resignation letter effective immediately prior to the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- QCP's executive officers were parties to employment agreements providing severance benefits upon qualifying terminations of employment in connection with or following the merger, and outstanding equity awards (options, restricted stock, RSUs) held by directors/officers were cashed out at the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing/registration change at closing
- In connection with the merger's consummation, the NYSE removed QCP's shares from listing and filed a Form 25 with the SEC on July 27, 2018 to report the removal under Exchange Act Section 12(b); QCP stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate registration of its shares and suspend its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## Contractual/financial arrangements bridging the closing
- QCP's Employee Matters covenant required Welltower to cause the surviving entity to honor QCP's existing benefit plans, and for one year following the effective time to maintain compensation/benefits and severance terms substantially comparable to those each employee had immediately before closing, along with full service credit under benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, QCP repaid and terminated its First Lien Credit and Guarantee Agreement (dated October 31, 2016), and Potomac redeemed $750,000,000 in aggregate principal of 8.125% Senior Secured Second Lien Notes due 2023 originally issued by four QCP REIT subsidiaries, discharging the related indenture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- The merger agreement provided for continued D&O indemnification and insurance coverage (including a "tail" policy option, capped at 600% of the current annual premium) for QCP's directors and officers for pre-closing acts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- QCP, a REIT owning and leasing post-acute/skilled nursing and memory-care/assisted-living real estate, merged whole into a newly formed Welltower merger subsidiary for all-cash consideration; the real estate/leasing business and its financing obligations continued forward through the surviving entity, but essentially the entire pre-closing officer and director group departed at the effective time (one new officer appointed), with the departing group's compensation/benefits and severance terms contractually bridged for a fixed one-year period rather than the organization moving forward intact. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004637, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-047710, Form 8-K]
