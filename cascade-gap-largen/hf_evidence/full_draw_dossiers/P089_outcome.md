## Outcome window
- The fixed outcome window is the registrant's own realized record 3–5 years after the
  closing-era filing; the primary source is its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Total revenue increased 13.4% ($82.4 million) to $696.4 million for the year ended
  December 31, 2018, compared to $614.0 million for 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- Net income was $217.6 million for 2018, compared to $65.6 million for 2017; net income
  attributable to common stockholders was $1.02 per diluted share ($213.5 million) versus
  $0.34 per diluted share ($63.9 million) for 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- Net Operating Income increased 12.8% ($54.0 million) to $475.8 million; Same-Property
  Cash NOI increased 2.5% ($7.5 million) to $308.9 million versus 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- The portfolio comprised approximately 23.2 million square feet of gross leasable area,
  with approximately $6.8 billion invested since inception, primarily in medical office
  buildings; approximately 68% of the portfolio was on the campuses of, or adjacent to,
  recognized healthcare systems as of December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]

## Realized integration of the acquired assets onto the operating platform
- The in-house asset management and leasing platform operated approximately 21.6 million
  square feet of GLA, or 93% of the total portfolio, described as a significant increase
  since the 2012 NYSE listing when it managed approximately 8.8 million square feet, or 70%,
  of GLA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- A further consolidating acquisition occurred within the window: the filing references
  "the Duke acquisition" in 2017, noting $4.6 million of non-incremental transaction costs
  related to it recorded in 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]

## Documented impairments and dispositions over the period
- Impairment charges of $8.9 million were recorded in 2018 (related to six MOBs in
  Tennessee, Texas and South Carolina) and $13.9 million in 2017 (two MOBs and a portfolio
  of MOBs in Massachusetts, South Carolina and Texas). [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- During 2018 the company completed the disposition of 20 medical office buildings for an
  aggregate gross sales price of $308.6 million (approximately 1.2 million square feet of
  GLA), generating net gains of approximately $166.0 million, primarily the third-quarter
  disposition of its Greenville, South Carolina MOB portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the outcome window the registrant grew revenue and net income (net income $217.6
  million in 2018 versus $65.6 million in 2017) while continuing to consolidate medical
  office buildings and moving the operating processes for acquired assets onto its in-house
  platform, which reached 93% of the portfolio (approximately 21.6 million square feet of
  GLA). [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net income of $217.6 million for FY2018 (up from $65.6
  million in FY2017), alongside modest asset-level impairments ($8.9 million in 2018;
  $13.9 million in 2017) and selective portfolio dispositions ($308.6 million gross across
  20 MOBs, net gains ~$166.0 million in 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-19-000023, 10-K FY2018]
