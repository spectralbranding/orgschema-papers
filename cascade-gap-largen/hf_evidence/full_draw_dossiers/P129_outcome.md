## Divestiture of the acquired agrochemical business
- On July 20, 2018, the registrant (by then reporting as Element Solutions Inc) agreed
  to sell to UPL Corporation Ltd., a wholly owned subsidiary of UPL Limited, 100% of the
  issued and outstanding shares of common stock of Arysta LifeScience Limited and its
  subsidiaries, pursuant to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated July 20, 2018, as amended
  January 25, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The sale (the "Arysta Sale") was completed on January 31, 2019 for an aggregate
  purchase price / net cash proceeds of $4.28 billion in cash, after post-closing
  adjustments relating to cash, indebtedness and working capital, which were finalized
  with UPL on May 17, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Following the sale, the former Agricultural Solutions segment (which had housed the
  Agriphar, CAS/Chemtura AgroSolutions and Arysta businesses) was reclassified as
  discontinued operations for all periods presented, on the stated basis that the Arysta
  Sale "represented a significant strategic shift" with "a major effect on the Company's
  operations and financial results." [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K
  FY2019]

## Corporate identity and capital-structure changes at the divestiture
- On the closing date of the Arysta Sale (January 31, 2019), the Company changed its
  name from "Platform Specialty Products Corporation" to "Element Solutions Inc," and
  effective February 1, 2019 its common stock began trading on the NYSE under the ticker
  symbol ESI (previously PAH). [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K
  FY2019]
- Sale proceeds were used to pay down approximately $4.60 billion of debt outstanding at
  closing; the Company entered a new Credit Agreement (dated January 31, 2019) providing
  $1.08 billion of senior secured credit facilities and borrowed a $750 million term
  loan under it. On February 1, 2019 the Company used a portion of the sale proceeds and
  new borrowings to redeem all outstanding "Prior Senior Notes" — including the 6.500%
  USD senior notes due 2022 that had been issued in February 2015 to help fund the
  original Arysta Acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K
  FY2019]

## Documented impairment / loss record
- In 2017, the Company recorded a goodwill impairment charge of $160 million within the
  former Agricultural Solutions segment's "Agro Business" reporting unit, attributed to
  the impact of a delayed agricultural market recovery. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Net loss from discontinued operations was $243 million (loss attributable to common
  stockholders of $245.9 million) for 2018, primarily due to an estimated asset
  impairment loss of $450 million recorded in connection with the pending Arysta Sale;
  the 10-K attributes this loss primarily to the reclassification of accumulated
  foreign-currency translation adjustments out of equity and into earnings. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Net income from discontinued operations was $13.3 million for 2019, primarily
  reflecting the settlement of post-closing adjustments with UPL. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- In connection with the Arysta Sale, the Company agreed to retain certain legal and tax
  liabilities, primarily related to an Arysta subsidiary in Brazil; the 10-K states the
  Company does not expect to incur a material loss from these matters but notes that
  their resolution "may take several years." [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023,
  Form 10-K FY2019]

## Organizational aftermath and remaining segment structure
- The 10-K's restructuring disclosures describe "the Company's reorganization efforts
  resulting from the Arysta Sale to consolidate its corporate function with those of its
  former Performance Solutions segment," including associated headcount reductions.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Following the divestiture, the Company's remaining reportable segments were
  Electronics and Industrial & Specialty, with 2019 reported Adjusted EBITDA of $252.9
  million and $163.8 million respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023,
  Form 10-K FY2019]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly four years of the February 2015 Arysta closing, the entire agrochemical
  cluster built up through the 2014-2015 acquisitions (Agriphar, CAS, Arysta) was
  divested in a single stock sale to UPL for $4.28 billion, preceded by a $160 million
  segment goodwill impairment in 2017 and a $450 million sale-related impairment in 2018,
  and accompanied by a corporate renaming (Platform Specialty Products Corporation to
  Element Solutions Inc), a new credit facility, redemption of the notes originally
  issued to fund the Arysta Acquisition, and a stated corporate reorganization to
  consolidate remaining functions into the surviving Electronics and Industrial &
  Specialty segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590714-20-000023, Form 10-K FY2019]
