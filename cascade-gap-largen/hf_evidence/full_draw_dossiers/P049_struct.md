# Structural sub-dossier — Goodyear / Sumitomo Rubber Industries global alliance tire joint ventures (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is The Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year
  ended December 31, 2007 (filed 2008-02-14); it is an annual report that DESCRIBES the
  standing global alliance joint ventures with Sumitomo Rubber Industries, Ltd. ("SRI")
  rather than being the venture-formation document. The venture formation terms are
  therefore sourced additionally from the contemporaneous 1999 primary in which the
  agreements were entered and reported. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-08-001092, Form 10-K (FY2007)]
- The formation agreements are the Joint Venture Agreement for Europe and the Shareholders
  Agreement for the Europe JVC, both dated June 14, 1999, incorporated by reference as
  Exhibits 10.1 and 10.2 to the Company's Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 1999
  (File No. 1-1927). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-08-001092, Form 10-K (FY2007)]

## Transaction and parties at formation (contemporaneous 1999 primary)
- On June 14, 1999 the Company entered into a definitive general agreement and various
  other agreements ("Alliance Agreements") with SRI relating to the formation and operation
  of a strategic global alliance providing for tire manufacturing and sales joint ventures;
  on September 1, 1999 the alliance was completed and the joint ventures commenced
  operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- Europe: the Company acquired 75%, and SRI 25%, of Goodyear Dunlop Tires Europe B.V.
  (a Netherlands holding company), which on September 1, 1999 acquired substantially all of
  SRI's tire businesses in Europe (including eight tire manufacturing plants in England,
  France and Germany and sales/distribution operations in 18 European countries) and most of
  the Company's tire businesses in Europe. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- North America: the Company acquired 75%, and SRI 25%, of Goodyear Dunlop Tires North
  America Ltd., a holding company that purchased SRI's tire manufacturing operations in North
  America and certain related tire sales and distribution operations; separately the Company
  acquired 100% of the balance of SRI's Dunlop tire distribution and sales operation in the
  United States and Canada. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- Japan and support: the Company acquired 25% (SRI 75%) of each of two Japanese tire
  companies (one replacement-market, one original-equipment); 51% (SRI 49%) of a company
  coordinating and disseminating tire technology among the parties and ventures; and 80%
  (SRI 20%) of a global purchasing company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at formation (tier level)
- The customer-facing product operations (tire manufacturing, sales and distribution
  businesses of both parties) were contributed into jointly owned holding companies rather
  than transferred whole to a single owner: each principal European and North American
  vehicle is 75% Company / 25% SRI. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- Certain of the Company's operations were EXCLUDED from the European joint venture and did
  not cross: its tire businesses in Poland (other than a sales company), Slovenia and Turkey
  (as well as Morocco and South Africa), its aircraft tire businesses, and its textile, steel
  tire cord and tire mold manufacturing plants and technical center in Luxembourg. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- The processes and people acquired had not yet been integrated at formation: the Company
  stated it was "formulating, but has not completed, plans to integrate the businesses,"
  contemplating "downsizing or consolidation of various manufacturing, distribution, sales,
  support and administrative operations," contingent on analysis of optimal integration of
  facilities, information systems, R&D and "appropriate staffing levels," with execution
  extending over several periods commencing Q4 1999. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]
- Associate-severance and lease costs for the acquired businesses were estimated at $80
  million to $120 million, recorded as an adjustment to acquisition cost. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]

## Consideration and accounting at formation
- In addition to the businesses contributed, the Company paid $915.5 million to SRI and its
  affiliates (financed by additional debt); the alliance was accounted for under the purchase
  method with acquired-business cost of approximately $1.22 billion (including the cash
  payment and the $307 million fair value of the 25% of Goodyear businesses contributed to the
  European joint venture) and $390 million of goodwill amortized straight-line over 40 years. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)]

## Governing / exit terms carried in the alliance agreements (closing-time)
- Under the Umbrella Agreement (dated June 14, 1999) SRI holds "exit rights": SRI has the
  right to require the Company to purchase SRI's ownership interests in the European and North
  American joint ventures in September 2009 if certain triggering events have occurred, and
  immediately upon certain other events (including certain bankruptcy events or a change of
  control of Goodyear); the Company's 1999 cost of acquiring an interest in these businesses
  was approximately $1.2 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-08-001092, Form 10-K (FY2007)]
- As of the FY2007 report the alliance remained in force and had been amended over time,
  including Amendment No. 4 to the Umbrella Agreement dated February 12, 2008 among the
  Company, SRI and their respective affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-08-001092, Form 10-K (FY2007)]

## Structural summary (formation only)
- The transaction pooled the two parties' tire manufacturing, sales and distribution
  operations into jointly owned holding companies (Goodyear Dunlop Tires Europe B.V. and
  Goodyear Dunlop Tires North America Ltd., each 75% Company / 25% SRI, plus 25/75 Japanese
  sales companies, a 51/49 technology company and an 80/20 purchasing company), with the
  Company paying $915.5 million plus contributing 25% of its European businesses; certain
  Company operations were expressly excluded and stayed outside the ventures; and integration
  of the acquired processes, facilities, information systems and staffing was explicitly not
  yet completed at closing, with severance and consolidation planned and SRI granted
  contractual exit rights exercisable from September 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-99-009016, Form 10-Q (Q3 1999)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950152-08-001092, Form 10-K (FY2007)]
