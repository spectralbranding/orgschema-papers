## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The car-rental company continued to file annual reports on Form 10-K under CIK 1657853; its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020 was filed February 26, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalar)
- Net income (loss) attributable to Hertz Global for the year ended December 31, 2020 was a net loss of $(1,714) million, compared with $(58) million for 2019 and $(225) million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The company attributed the increased loss primarily to the impact of COVID-19; cash flows from operating activities decreased by $2.0 billion year over year in 2020, driven by a $1.8 billion change in net loss. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- For the year ended December 31, 2021 the company returned to a net income attributable to Hertz Global of $366 million, compared with the $(1,714) million net loss in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-22-000012, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Court-supervised restructuring during the window
- On May 22, 2020, Hertz Global, Hertz and their direct and indirect subsidiaries in the U.S. and Canada (collectively "the Debtors") filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 in the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The Debtors entered into a $1.65 billion superpriority secured debtor-in-possession (DIP) credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The Plan of Reorganization became effective on June 30, 2021 (the "Effective Date"), on which date the Company emerged from Chapter 11. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-22-000012, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Documented operational and market disruption over time
- The company's common stock was delisted from the New York Stock Exchange and began trading on the over-the-counter market on October 30, 2020 under the symbol HTZGQ. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- COVID-19 reduced vehicle utilization; the company implemented strategies to reduce its fleet to align with demand, and, as authorized by the Bankruptcy Court, addressed its rental fleet in the U.S. and International Rental Car segments of approximately 515,700 vehicles and 131,500 vehicles, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered an Interim Lease Order on July 24, 2020 related to the vehicle Operating Lease, and entered Lease Rejection Orders in the Chapter 11 Cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001657853-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 2019-2021 outcome window, the car-rental registrant recorded a net loss attributable to Hertz Global of $(1,714) million in 2020, entered Chapter 11 on May 22, 2020 under a $1.65 billion DIP facility, was delisted from the NYSE to the over-the-counter market (symbol HTZGQ) on October 30, 2020, and undertook a court-supervised fleet reduction and vehicle-lease rejection process; it then emerged from Chapter 11 on June 30, 2021 and returned to a net income attributable to Hertz Global of $366 million for fiscal 2021.
