# Structural sub-dossier — NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust combination into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc. ("NorthStar I"), a Maryland corporation and
  publicly registered non-traded REIT, entered a master combination agreement (dated
  August 25, 2017, as amended and restated November 20, 2017) together with NorthStar
  Real Estate Income II, Inc. ("NorthStar II"), subsidiaries of Colony NorthStar, Inc.
  ("CLNS"), and a newly formed entity, Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. (the
  "Company"), under which CLNS subsidiaries contributed a select portfolio of assets and
  liabilities to the Company and NorthStar I and NorthStar II each merged, in an
  all-stock merger, into the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- The Company was a Maryland corporation and, at the time of the agreement, a wholly
  owned subsidiary of CLNS formed in August 2017 solely to facilitate the combination; it
  had not carried on any activities or operations prior to the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- At 11:56 p.m. Eastern Time on January 31, 2018, NorthStar I merged with and into the
  Company, with the Company surviving the merger; each outstanding share of NorthStar I
  common stock was cancelled and converted into the right to receive 0.3532 shares of
  Company Class A common stock, plus cash in lieu of fractional shares, and restricted
  stock outstanding under NorthStar I's Long Term Incentive Plan automatically vested and
  became entitled to the same consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029381, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]
- In connection with the Combination the Company issued approximately 42.1 million
  shares of Class A common stock to former NorthStar I stockholders (and approximately
  40.4 million Class A shares to former NorthStar II stockholders and approximately 44.4
  million Class B-3 shares to a CLNS subsidiary for its contributed portfolio). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029381, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing asset that crossed was NorthStar I's investment portfolio: a
  diversified book of commercial real estate (CRE) debt, equity and securities
  investments — first mortgage loans, subordinate mortgage and mezzanine loans and
  participations, preferred equity interests, direct and indirect real-estate equity
  positions, and CRE securities including commercial-mortgage-backed securities — held,
  directly or indirectly, through its operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- One NorthStar I asset — a specific loan referred to as the "NorthStar I excluded
  asset" — did NOT cross to the Company. Under the combination agreement, prior to
  closing NorthStar I could sell this asset to a third party (distributing net proceeds
  above $65 million to its stockholders); absent a sale, CLNS committed to purchase a
  $65.0 million senior participation interest at closing, NorthStar I retained an $85.15
  million junior participation interest, and that junior interest was transferred to a
  newly formed liquidating trust whose beneficial units were distributed pro rata to
  NorthStar I stockholders as a special dividend immediately before the merger — a
  carve-out mechanism that kept this single asset with legacy stockholders rather than
  passing it to the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- NorthStar I had no employees prior to closing; it was externally managed by CNI NSI
  Advisors, LLC, a CLNS subsidiary, under an advisory agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- At and after closing the combined Company likewise had no employees and was externally
  managed by CLNC Manager, LLC, a subsidiary of CLNS's operating partnership, under a new
  management agreement with an initial three-year term (automatically renewing annually
  thereafter absent termination); the Manager was responsible for selection, acquisition
  and sale of portfolio investments, financing activities, and the Company's day-to-day
  operations under the Board's supervision. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029381, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]
- Kevin P. Traenkle was expected to be Chief Executive Officer and Sujan S. Patel was
  expected to be Chief Financial Officer of the combined Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- The proxy disclosed integration risk explicitly: "the actual integration may result in
  additional and unforeseen expenses and the anticipated benefits of the integration may
  not be realized," that management would have to "dedicate substantial effort to
  integrating the businesses of the Contributed Entities, NorthStar I and NorthStar II,"
  and that difficulties "managing the Company's larger and more complex portfolio" could
  prevent realization of anticipated benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]

## Listing status at closing
- NorthStar I common stock was not, and had never been, listed on the New York Stock
  Exchange or any other national securities exchange prior to the transaction (it was a
  non-traded REIT). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A]
- The Company's Class A common stock was approved for listing and began trading on the
  New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "CLNC" effective the opening of trading on
  February 1, 2018, the day immediately following the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029381, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined substantially all of NorthStar I's CRE debt/equity/securities
  portfolio and its legal entity into a newly formed, previously non-operating company
  alongside a parallel merger of NorthStar II and a separate contribution of assets from
  CLNS; the customer-facing asset (the CRE investment portfolio, less one carved-out loan
  retained by legacy stockholders through a liquidating trust) and the legal vehicle
  changed hands, but the operating organization did not change in substance because both
  the pre- and post-closing external managers were CLNS-affiliated entities and NorthStar
  I itself carried no employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362938, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029381, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]
