## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The outcome window is the entity's realized record 3–5 years after the ~2015 closing; the record below is drawn from the annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 (filed February 28, 2020), which falls within that window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Public-reporting status (feasibility-relevant)
- The entity remained an independent, publicly traded SEC registrant filing annual reports on Form 10-K through the outcome window; it filed its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 on February 28, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- Revenues were $1,167,997 thousand for 2019, compared with $1,072,295 thousand for 2018 and $959,956 thousand for 2017 — an increase of $95.7 million, or 8.9%, in 2019 versus 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- Net income was $178,582 thousand for 2019, compared with $164,760 thousand for 2018 and $235,171 thousand for 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- The 2019 revenue increase was attributed to organic growth in the higher-margin residential data and business services lines, the acquired Fidelity and Clearwave operations, a residential video rate adjustment and the implementation of modem rental charges, partially offset by decreases in residential video and other revenues. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Realized operating scale (within the window)
- As of December 31, 2019 the company described itself as a fully integrated provider of data, video and voice services in 21 Western, Midwestern and Southern states, serving residential and business customers in more than 950 communities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- It provided service to approximately 907,000 residential and business customers out of approximately 2.3 million homes passed as of December 31, 2019; of these, approximately 773,000 subscribed to data services, 314,000 to video services and 139,000 to voice services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Expansion by acquisition (no divestiture / dissolution in the window)
- The company completed two acquisitions during 2019: Clearwave, which contributed revenues of $27.4 million and net income of $5.1 million from January 8, 2019 to December 31, 2019 (including $4.9 million of acquired-intangible amortization); and Fidelity, which contributed revenues of $32.0 million and net income of $4.7 million for the three months ended December 31, 2019 (including $2.4 million of acquired-intangible amortization). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Documented realignment activity at the product/process and process/organization interfaces
- The company began rebranding its business as Sparklight in 2019 (starting with legacy Cable One operations), streamlining residential internet plans and pricing; it stated it intended to complete rebranding of legacy Cable One and NewWave systems in 2020 and to rebrand Fidelity systems further in the future. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- The company disclosed that the rebranding "has required and will continue to require significant investment" and "may result in the diversion of senior management's attention from our ongoing operations," and that it "could also result in the loss of brand recognition, customer loyalty or reputation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- Selling, general and administrative expenses increased $22.9 million, or 10.3%, to $245.1 million in 2019, an increase attributed in part to acquisition-related and rebranding costs incurred during 2019; the company's non-GAAP reconciliation separately identified system conversion costs, rebranding costs and acquisition-related costs as add-back line items for the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
- The independent auditor and management excluded the newly acquired Clearwave and Fidelity operations from the assessment and audit of internal control over financial reporting as of December 31, 2019 because they were acquired in purchase business combinations during 2019 (Clearwave representing 4.5% of total assets and 2.3% of revenues; Fidelity 6.3% of total assets and 2.7% of revenues of the related consolidated amounts). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed outcome window the entity remained an independent public registrant that grew rather than contracted: 2019 revenues of $1.168 billion (up 8.9% year over year) and net income of $178.6 million, expanded operating footprint (21 states, 950+ communities, ~907,000 customers), and two completed 2019 acquisitions — with the realized record also documenting active product/process and organizational realignment through a company-wide rebranding to Sparklight (disclosed to require significant investment and diversion of management attention) and acquisition-integration, system-conversion and rebranding costs recognized during the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-20-003799, Form 10-K]
