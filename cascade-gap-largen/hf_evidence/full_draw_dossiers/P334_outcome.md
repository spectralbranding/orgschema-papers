## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- FNB Bancorp filed a Form 15-12G on 2018-08-03 to terminate its registration under the
  Exchange Act, stating an approximate number of holders of record of zero as of the
  certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000928, Form 15-12G]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by FNB Bancorp (CIK 1163199) since
  that deregistration; consequently no audited public financial statements specific to
  FNB Bancorp or First National Bank of Northern California exist for the FY2021-FY2022
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000928, Form 15-12G]
- The surviving corporation, TriCo Bancshares (CIK 356171), continued as an independent,
  Exchange Act-reporting public company throughout the outcome window, filing a Form 10-K
  for fiscal year 2021 (filed 2022-03-01) and a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed
  2023-03-01) under its own name and CIK. [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-22-000016, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022]

## What TriCo's later filings do and do not say about the acquired business
- TriCo's FY2021 and FY2022 Forms 10-K reference the 2018 FNB Bancorp transaction only in
  passing, historical terms (e.g., alongside its 2014 North Valley Bancorp and 2022
  Valley Republic Bank acquisitions, as a source of intangible assets and of federal/state
  net operating loss carryforwards); neither filing presents a separate segment,
  business-unit disclosure, or standalone financial trajectory for the businesses
  acquired from FNB Bancorp. [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-22-000016, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022]
- TriCo's consolidated goodwill balance was $220,872 thousand at both December 31, 2020
  and December 31, 2021 (no additions or reductions recorded in fiscal year 2021), and
  the FY2022 Form 10-K's goodwill-impairment testing narrative states no impairment
  resulted for each of the three years in the period ended December 31, 2022; the
  FY2022-end goodwill balance of $304,442 thousand reflects an $83,570 thousand addition
  attributed to the 2022 Valley Republic Bank acquisition, not to the 2018 FNB Bancorp
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-22-000016, Form 10-K FY2021, Goodwill note] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022, Goodwill note]
- These goodwill and impairment-testing figures are TriCo's company-wide consolidated
  totals (spanning multiple acquisitions across multiple years); none of TriCo's FY2021
  or FY2022 Forms 10-K isolate a goodwill balance, an impairment charge, or any other
  financial-performance scalar attributable specifically to the businesses acquired from
  FNB Bancorp. [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-22-000016, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because FNB Bancorp merged into TriCo Bancshares and deregistered in 2018, there is no
  public, audited 3-5-year outcome record specific to the acquired business (FNB Bancorp
  / First National Bank of Northern California) within the FY2021-FY2022 window; the
  surviving company, TriCo Bancshares, continued as an independent public reporting
  company and reports its consolidated goodwill (spanning several acquisitions) as
  unimpaired through the window, but TriCo does not separately disclose a trajectory for
  the specific businesses acquired from FNB Bancorp. This case's outcome is accordingly
  coded as uncertain for want of a business-specific public record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000928, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-22-000016, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000356171-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022]
