# Structural sub-dossier — Vantiv, Inc. acquisition of Worldpay Group plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Vantiv, Inc., a Delaware corporation whose Class A common stock traded on the New York
  Stock Exchange, released an announcement on August 9, 2017 under the UK Takeover Code
  setting out the terms of a recommended offer to acquire the entire issued and to-be-
  issued ordinary share capital of Worldpay Group plc, a public limited company
  registered in England and Wales whose ordinary shares traded on the London Stock
  Exchange under the symbol "WPG." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- The offer was to be made both directly by Vantiv and indirectly through Vantiv UK
  Limited ("Bidco"), a subsidiary of Vantiv, and was intended to be effected by means of
  a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement between Worldpay and its shareholders under
  the UK Companies Act 2006. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- Under the terms of the offer, Worldpay shareholders were to receive, for each Worldpay
  ordinary share, 55 pence in cash and 0.0672 new shares of Vantiv Class A common stock;
  Vantiv expected to issue approximately 134,400,000 shares of Class A common stock, after
  which Worldpay shareholders would hold approximately 43.0% of the combined company's
  fully diluted common shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- Vantiv LLC entered into an amendment to its existing credit facilities under which
  Morgan Stanley, Credit Suisse, and The Bank of Tokyo-Mitsubishi UFJ, Ltd. severally
  provided US$1.6 billion in term loan commitments to fund the cash consideration and
  refinance certain existing Worldpay indebtedness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Controlling intent / imprint: following completion, the combined company was to be
  named "Worldpay, Inc." (no separate Vantiv stockholder vote was required to change the
  registrant's name), with Cincinnati, Ohio becoming the global and corporate
  headquarters and London becoming the international headquarters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: Vantiv, Inc. (the Delaware registrant and SEC filer) was the entity that
  continued and was renamed; the filing states elsewhere that "Vantiv is a holding
  company that does not conduct any business operations of its own," with operations
  conducted through subsidiaries including Bidco and Vantiv LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product/asset: Worldpay's business was described as a global payments
  technology platform serving approximately 400,000 clients through three operating
  divisions — Global eCom, WPUK, and WPUS — processing on the order of 40 million
  transactions worldwide on an average day across more than 300 payment methods in 126
  currencies and 146 countries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- People / management / organization: the board of the combined company was set to
  comprise eight Vantiv directors and five Worldpay directors, with Charles Drucker as
  Chairman and Co-CEO, Philip Jansen as Co-CEO, Stephanie Ferris as CFO, Sir Michael Rake
  as lead independent director, and Jeffery Stiefler continuing in a non-executive board
  role; the filing states "Vantiv has high regard for the skills and experience of the
  existing management and employees of the Worldpay Group" and "confirms their existing
  employment rights, including pension rights, will be observed." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- Business/value-capture model and processes: the filing's stated reasons for the
  acquisition describe the U.S. and global technology platforms as being "harmonised" and
  the combined operations, technology infrastructure, and data/analytics capabilities as
  something the combined company would integrate and leverage going forward, rather than
  describing a single already-unified processing infrastructure in place at closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]

## Listing and share-registration changes at closing
- Vantiv intended to maintain its NYSE listing (with the combined company's stock trading
  under the symbol "WP") and to seek a secondary standard-segment listing on the London
  Stock Exchange for the enlarged Class A common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- In connection with the scheme of arrangement, applications were to be made to the
  London Stock Exchange to have Worldpay ordinary shares removed from trading on its
  market for listed securities, and to the UK Financial Conduct Authority / UK Listing
  Authority to cancel the listing of the Worldpay ordinary shares on the Official List.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]

## Transitional / process-continuity provisions noted at closing
- The filing states that appropriate proposals would be made in due course to
  participants in the Worldpay Share Schemes (Worldpay's employee equity plans), with
  details to follow in the Scheme Document and separate participant letters — i.e., a
  mechanism for carrying employee equity arrangements across the closing rather than
  extinguishing them outright. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply agreement between Vantiv and a
  divesting former parent of Worldpay is described in the reviewed sections of the
  filing (Worldpay is described as having operated as a standalone business since its
  2010 separation from RBS and its 2015 initial public offering). [UNVERIFIED — not
  located in the reviewed filing text]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company, cash-and-stock acquisition in which
  Vantiv, Inc. (via Bidco and a scheme of arrangement) acquired the entire share capital
  of the separately listed Worldpay Group plc; rather than the acquirer's name and
  listing simply continuing, the enlarged company was renamed "Worldpay, Inc.," retained
  Vantiv's NYSE listing while removing Worldpay's LSE listing, and combined the two
  boards and executive teams under a Co-CEO structure, with the filing framing the two
  companies' U.S. and global technology platforms as targets for future integration and
  harmonisation rather than already-unified processes at the closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-351649, DEFM14A]
