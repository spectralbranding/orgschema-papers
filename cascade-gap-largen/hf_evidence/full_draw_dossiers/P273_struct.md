# Structural sub-dossier — Gramercy Property Trust acquisition by Blackstone-affiliated entities (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Gramercy Property Trust, a Maryland REIT formed in March 2004, described itself as "a
  leading global investor and asset manager of commercial real estate" specializing in
  industrial commercial real estate leased to high-quality tenants in the US and Europe;
  its common shares traded on the NYSE ("GPT"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- GPT Operating Partnership LP, with Gramercy as sole general partner, indirectly held
  all consolidated real estate investments, unconsolidated-investment interests, and the
  entities conducting the company's third-party asset-management operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- The acquirer entities — BRE Glacier Parent L.P. ("Parent"), BRE Glacier L.P.
  ("Merger Sub I"), and BRE Glacier Acquisition L.P. ("Merger Sub II") — were each formed
  solely to facilitate the acquisition and had "not carried on any activities to date";
  Parent was an affiliate of Blackstone Real Estate Partners VIII L.P., itself an
  affiliate of The Blackstone Group L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- Under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated May 6, 2018, the transaction was a two-step
  merger: Gramercy merged with and into Merger Sub I (surviving as a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Parent), and Merger Sub II merged with and into GPT Operating
  Partnership LP (which continued as the surviving partnership). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- Consideration was $27.50 cash per common share and $25.00 cash per preferred share
  (plus accrued dividends), funded by a debt commitment of up to $3.0 billion plus a
  Sponsor equity contribution, with no financing condition to closing. The merger
  closed October 10, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061314, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- Product/asset: the industrial real estate portfolio and third-party asset-management
  business crossed intact into the surviving entities, becoming indirectly wholly owned
  by Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061314, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- Controlling intent/governance did not carry forward unchanged: a change in control of
  both the company and the Partnership occurred, and all members of the board of
  trustees ceased to serve at the merger effective time; the filing states these
  departures were "not a result of any disagreements" over operations or policies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061314, Form 8-K Items 5.01-5.02]
- People/management: Parent agreed to assume and honor, without amendment, the existing
  employment agreements of named executives (CEO Gordon F. DuGan and Messrs. Harris,
  Pell, and Clark). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- For the broader workforce, the merger agreement guaranteed for one year post-closing a
  base salary, bonus opportunity, and benefits no less favorable than pre-closing terms;
  a cash retention program (20% of base salary, capped at $100,000) covered employees
  without individual agreements. No employees were represented by a labor organization
  at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: the Maryland REIT entity did not survive as an independent registrant —
  it merged into Merger Sub I, a Delaware limited partnership; REIT tax-status mechanics
  were preserved via a consideration-adjustment clause. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]

## Bridging mechanism and listing status at closing
- A financing-cooperation clause permitted Parent, prior to closing, to share
  non-public information about "certain of our and our subsidiaries' properties or
  subsidiaries" with potential purchasers and their financing sources — a mechanism
  contemplating that individual assets could be marketed to third parties around the
  time of closing rather than the whole portfolio necessarily being retained intact. No
  separate transition-services agreement was described; workforce continuity instead
  rested on the assumed employment agreements and the one-year compensation floor above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]
- Effective October 10, 2018, all common and Series A preferred shares were removed from
  trading on the NYSE; Merger Sub I requested NYSE filing of a Form 25 to effect removal
  from listing and registration, and stated its intent (with the Partnership) to file
  Forms 15 to terminate registration and reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061314, Form 8-K Items 3.01, 3.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, the entire real estate portfolio and its asset-management business
  crossed as a single unit into wholly-owned Blackstone-affiliated successor entities,
  with executive employment agreements assumed and a one-year compensation floor
  extended to the workforce — but the pre-existing board of trustees was dissolved in
  full at the merger effective time, and the merger agreement's financing-cooperation
  clause explicitly contemplated individual properties being shopped to third-party
  purchasers around the time of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004781, DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061314, Form 8-K]
