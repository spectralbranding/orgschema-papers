## Drawn filing and what it establishes

- The drawn filing is Chevron Corporation's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2008 (filed
  2009-02-26). As of year-end 2008 it describes Chevron as operator of the Gorgon
  development with a 50 percent ownership interest across most of the Greater Gorgon
  Area, proceeding together with two unnamed joint-venture participants toward a
  combined development of the Gorgon and nearby natural-gas fields; environmental
  approvals were still in process, no proved reserves had been recognized (pending LNG
  sales agreements, including with three Japanese utility customers and Chevron
  affiliate GS Caltex, and other milestones), and a final investment decision (FID)
  was expected in the second half of 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0000891618-09-000054,
  Form 10-K]
- The drawn filing therefore pre-dates the transaction's actual closing (FID); the
  entity's next annual report documents the FID and the resulting ownership/contract
  structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0000891618-09-000054, Form 10-K]

## Closing structure (FY2009 Form 10-K, the contemporaneous closing-era primary)

- In September 2009, following an August 2009 environmental approval from the
  Australian Commonwealth Government, Chevron and its partners reached final
  investment decision to proceed with the Gorgon Project: a three-train,
  15-million-metric-ton-per-year LNG facility, a carbon sequestration project, and a
  domestic natural-gas plant, with total estimated first-phase project costs of $37
  billion (AU$43 billion). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]
- As of December 31, 2009, Chevron held a 47.3 percent-owned and operated interest in
  the project; it is designated the operator, i.e. the party managing project
  execution on behalf of the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-016846,
  Form 10-K]
- In 2009, long-term binding LNG sales agreements were finalized with four Asian
  customers; three of those customers also acquired an ownership interest in the
  project as part of the arrangement, which reduced Chevron's interest from its
  initial 50 percent to 47.3 percent by year-end 2009. Nonbinding Heads of Agreement
  for further volumes were signed with three additional Asian customers in 2009 and
  early 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]
- During 2009 the company recognized proved reserves for the Greater Gorgon Area
  fields included in the project (a change from the unrecognized status in the drawn
  filing); first natural-gas production was then expected in 2014, over an estimated
  economic life exceeding 40 years from start-up. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]

## Legal vehicle, governance, product/process, and bridging arrangements

- Chevron's stated accounting policy is to consolidate "undivided interests in oil and
  gas joint ventures ... on a proportionate basis," as distinct from equity-method
  treatment used for affiliates in which it holds a non-controlling minority interest
  with significant influence but not control; and its risk factors state generally
  that "these operations are conducted through joint ventures over which the company
  may have limited influence and control," notwithstanding an operator designation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000891618-09-000054, Form 10-K]
- The customer-facing product is LNG (and separately, domestic natural gas), marketed
  under long-term sales contracts to Asian utility customers; three of those customers
  additionally hold an equity stake in the producing project itself, linking offtake
  and ownership at closing. The operational process scope at closing comprised the LNG
  trains, a carbon sequestration facility, a domestic gas plant, and associated Western
  Australia infrastructure, to be executed by Chevron as operator. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]
- Neither the drawn filing nor the FY2009 Form 10-K describes a transitional-services,
  operating, or shared-services agreement bridging a pre-existing shared substrate —
  consistent with this being a new-build joint-venture project rather than a
  separation of an existing operating business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000891618-09-000054,
  Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- At closing (FID, September 2009), Chevron held a 47.3 percent operated interest in a
  proportionately-consolidated, unincorporated joint venture whose customer-facing LNG
  product was contractually pre-sold to Asian buyers, three of whom held direct equity
  stakes; the operational build-out (LNG trains, sequestration, domestic gas plant) was
  to be executed by Chevron as operator, with no transitional bridging agreement
  described. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-016846, Form 10-K]
