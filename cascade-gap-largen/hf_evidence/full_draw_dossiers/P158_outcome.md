## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- Caesars Entertainment Corporation continued as an SEC registrant trading under "CZR" on the
  NASDAQ Global Select Market and filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]
- The FY2019 10-K states the Debtors emerged from bankruptcy and consummated their reorganization
  on October 6, 2017, and that the registrant conducts operations primarily through its wholly
  owned subsidiaries CEOC, LLC and Caesars Resort Collection, LLC while leasing certain real
  property assets from third parties including VICI Properties Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]

## Best-documented realized scalar
- For fiscal year 2019 the registrant reported net revenues of $8,742 million and a net loss
  attributable to Caesars of $1,195 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019, Selected Financial Data]
- FY2019 results included an impairment of goodwill of $27 million and an impairment of tangible
  and other intangible assets of $441 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019, Selected Financial Data]
- The five-year selected-financial-data table reports net income/(loss) attributable to Caesars of
  $(1,195) million (2019), $303 million (2018), $(368) million (2017), $(3,049) million (2016),
  and $6,012 million (2015), with net revenues rising from $4,868 million (2017) to $8,391 million
  (2018) to $8,742 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019, Selected Financial Data]

## Realized state of the split-asset / leaseback structure over time
- As of the FY2019 report the leases from VICI and other landlords remained in force and are
  described as triple-net leases under which the tenants pay all operating costs associated with
  the facilities in addition to rent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]
- The registrant recorded the deconsolidation of Horseshoe Baltimore effective August 31, 2017
  (net revenues of $190 million and net loss attributable to Caesars of $7 million to the date of
  deconsolidation, with a $31 million gain on deconsolidation), thereafter accounting for it as an
  equity-method investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]
- On September 20, 2019 a subsidiary agreed to dispose of the Rio All-Suite Hotel & Casino. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]

## Terminal corporate event in the window
- On June 24, 2019 the registrant entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (amended
  August 15, 2019) with Eldorado Resorts, Inc. and a merger subsidiary, under which the merger
  subsidiary would merge into Caesars, with Caesars surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Eldorado; per-share consideration was $8.40 in cash (subject to a daily accretion if not closed
  by March 25, 2020) or 0.0899 of an Eldorado share, subject to election and pro-ration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]
- Caesars and Eldorado stockholders approved the merger on November 15, 2019, and the FY2019 10-K
  stated the transaction was expected to close in the first half of 2020, with Eldorado to be
  renamed Caesars Entertainment, Inc. upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019]
- On July 20, 2020, Caesars Entertainment, Inc. (formerly Eldorado Resorts, Inc.) announced it had
  completed its acquisition of Caesars Entertainment Corporation, describing the combined company as
  owning and operating more than 55 casino properties worldwide across 16 states. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-195568, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1, press release dated July 20, 2020 — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590895/000119312520195568/d39058dex991.htm]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2018-2020 window the reorganized registrant operated at large scale (net revenues
  of $8,742 million in FY2019) as an operator-tenant leasing its physical properties back from the
  creditor-owned property vehicle under triple-net leases, while recording a FY2019 net loss
  attributable to Caesars of $1,195 million (including $468 million of goodwill and asset
  impairments), and was acquired by Eldorado Resorts on July 20, 2020, at which point it ceased to
  exist as an independent registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K for FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-195568, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 — https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1590895/000119312520195568/d39058dex991.htm]
