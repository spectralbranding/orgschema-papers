# Structural sub-dossier — Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc. merger with Discovery Communications, Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Discovery Communications, Inc. ("Discovery"), Scripps Networks Interactive, Inc.
  ("Scripps"), an Ohio corporation, and Skylight Merger Sub, Inc., a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Discovery ("Merger Sub"), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated July 30, 2017, providing for the merger of Merger Sub with and into Scripps,
  with Scripps continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of
  Discovery. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, each outstanding Scripps common share and Class A common
  share was to be converted into the right to receive $63.00 in cash plus a number of
  shares of Discovery Series C common stock (subject to a collar and a per-share cash-
  or-stock election, with proration), described as the "merger consideration." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- The Mergers were completed on March 6, 2018: pursuant to the merger agreement, Merger
  Sub was merged with and into Scripps, with Scripps as the surviving corporation, and
  as a result Scripps became a wholly owned subsidiary of Discovery; as a result of
  Discovery's exercise of a cash top-up right, each converted share received $65.82 in
  cash plus 1.0584 shares of Discovery Series C common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]
- If the merger agreement were terminated in specified circumstances (e.g., a change of
  the Scripps board's recommendation, or a failure to obtain the required shareholder
  vote), a termination fee of $356 million or an expense-reimbursement payment of $25
  million, as applicable, would be payable between the parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent and business rationale as stated at signing
- Discovery described the transaction as bringing together "a suite of world-class
  linear, digital and short-form entertainment brands," with the combined company
  expected to produce approximately 8,000 hours of original programming annually and
  hold approximately 300,000 hours of library content. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Discovery's stated reasons for the merger included an expectation, as of signing, that
  it would achieve $350 million in annual cost synergies by the end of the first full
  year after completion of the merger, with potential upside in future years; this is a
  forward-looking synergy target disclosed in the proxy, not a realized outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties disclosed integration risk factors, including the challenges of
  "conforming standards, controls, procedures and policies, business cultures and
  compensation structures between Discovery and Scripps" and of "retaining key
  employees," noting that post-merger Discovery management would have to dedicate
  substantial effort to integrating the two businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]

## What Scripps comprised at closing (the customer-facing product/asset tier)
- Scripps' U.S. lifestyle portfolio comprised the television and internet brands HGTV,
  DIY Network, Food Network, Cooking Channel, Travel Channel and Great American Country,
  plus international assets including UKTV (a joint venture with BBC Worldwide), Asian
  Food Channel, and Fine Living Network. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Scripps was headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee, with its principal executive
  offices at 9721 Sherrill Boulevard. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and governance at closing (the controlling/organization tier)
- On March 6, 2018, all of Scripps' pre-merger directors ceased to be directors of the
  company effective as of the merger's effective time, and the directors of Merger Sub
  immediately prior to the effective time — David Zaslav, Gunnar Wiedenfels and Bruce
  Campbell, all Discovery officers — became the directors of Scripps as the surviving
  corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the merger, Kenneth Lowe resigned as President, Chairman and Chief
  Executive Officer of Scripps. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]
- At the effective time, the articles of incorporation and code of regulations of
  Merger Sub became those of Scripps as the surviving corporation (renamed to Scripps),
  and Scripps filed the certificate of merger with the Ohio Secretary of State. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]
- On March 6, 2018, in connection with the merger, Scripps notified NASDAQ (where its
  shares had traded under symbol "SNI") of the merger's consummation and requested that
  its shares cease to be traded and be removed from listing on NASDAQ effective March 7,
  2018, and that NASDAQ file the corresponding notification of removal from listing on
  Form 25; Scripps also stated its intention to separately file a Form 15 to terminate
  its Exchange Act registration and reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]

## People/process tier: retention and continuity arrangements bridging the boundary
- Scripps' five executive officers (Kenneth W. Lowe; Lori A. Hickok, EVP/CFO and Chief
  Development Officer; Burton F. Jablin, COO; Cynthia L. Gibson, EVP/Chief Legal and
  Business Affairs Officer; and Mark S. Hale, EVP/Global Operations and CTO) held
  individual employment agreements and were covered by a Scripps Executive Change in
  Control Plan providing enhanced, "double-trigger" severance (a lump-sum multiple of
  base salary and target bonus — 3x for Mr. Lowe, 2.5x for Mr. Jablin, 2x for the
  remaining three officers) payable if, within 24 months of the merger, the executive
  resigned for "good reason" or was terminated without cause. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Pursuant to the merger agreement, Discovery agreed to honor Scripps' existing
  employment agreements, severance plans and retention agreements following completion
  of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Under a "continuation period" running through December 31, 2018, each Scripps employee
  who continued to be employed was to receive base salary/wage and target annual cash
  bonus opportunity no less favorable than before the merger, plus substantially
  comparable pension, welfare and other benefits; separately, until the second
  anniversary of the merger's completion, continuing employees were to be provided
  severance benefits no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the
  merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Discovery agreed to credit Scripps employees for their prior service with Scripps for
  purposes of benefit eligibility, vesting, continuous service, and severance
  entitlements under Discovery's post-merger plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- Scripps shareholders were separately asked to cast a non-binding advisory vote on the
  "golden parachute" compensation payable to Scripps' named executive officers in
  connection with the merger, compensation the Scripps board unanimously recommended
  approving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or supply arrangement
  between Scripps and a retained/spun-off entity was identified in the reviewed
  documents; the transaction was structured as a whole-company merger rather than a
  carve-out, so the "bridge" mechanisms captured above run to Discovery honoring
  Scripps' pre-existing employee arrangements rather than a separate operating-services
  contract. [UNVERIFIED — absence noted, not affirmatively ruled out beyond the
  documents reviewed]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company, one-sided cash-and-stock merger:
  Scripps' customer-facing lifestyle-network portfolio (HGTV, Food Network, Travel
  Channel, and related brands) crossed into Discovery's ownership as a wholly-owned
  subsidiary, with Scripps' entire pre-merger board and its Chairman/CEO replaced at
  closing by Discovery-designated directors, while continuity of the operating/people
  tier below the top of the organization was handled contractually — Discovery agreed to
  honor existing Scripps employment agreements, severance plans and a fixed
  (through-2018) compensation-continuation period, and enhanced change-in-control
  severance was available to departing senior executives — rather than through a
  standalone transition-services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-314122, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-072063, Form 8-K]
