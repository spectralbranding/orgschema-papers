# Structural sub-dossier — Ensco plc / Rowan Companies plc combination (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On October 7, 2018, Ensco plc ("Ensco") and Rowan Companies plc ("Rowan") entered into a Transaction Agreement providing for the combination of the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus, cover letter]
- Ensco agreed to acquire the entire issued and to-be-issued ordinary share capital of Rowan, implemented by way of a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement under Part 26 of the UK Companies Act 2006, requiring sanction of the High Court of Justice of England and Wales. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter]
- Eligible Rowan shareholders were entitled to receive 2.215 Ensco Class A ordinary shares for each Rowan Class A ordinary share subject to the Scheme of Arrangement (the "exchange ratio"); it was anticipated that former Ensco and former Rowan shareholders would own approximately 61% and 39% of the combined company, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter]
- Subject to Ensco shareholder approval, Ensco also expected to effect a 4-for-1 consolidation (reverse stock split under English law) of Ensco ordinary shares, conditional upon and following the Scheme of Arrangement becoming effective. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter]
- In connection with closing, Ensco was to be renamed Ensco Rowan plc, with its shares to trade on the NYSE under the symbol "ERD" or another symbol selected before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter]
- Prior to the transaction, Rowan's registered office and principal executive offices were located at 2800 Post Oak Boulevard, Houston, Texas, with Rowan ordinary shares listed on the NYSE under "RDC"; Ensco's principal executive offices were at 6 Chesterfield Gardens, London, England, with Ensco ordinary shares listed on the NYSE under "ESV." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, pp. 1311 area / notice of meeting]
- Immediately following the Effective Time, Ensco was to have its registered office and global headquarters located in London, England. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A §1.7 "Headquarters"]

## Business described at closing
- Rowan was described as operating in three segments — Deepwater, Jack-ups, and Saudi Aramco Rowan Offshore Drilling Company ("ARO"), Rowan's 50/50 joint venture with Saudi Aramco Development Company — with a fleet focused on high-specification and harsh-environment jack-up rigs and ultra-deepwater drillships. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, business description of Rowan]
- The ARO joint venture pre-dated the transaction: Rowan and Aramco, through their subsidiaries, entered a Shareholders' Agreement on November 21, 2016 to create the 50/50 joint venture to own, manage, and operate offshore drilling units in Saudi Arabia and potentially elsewhere. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, background of the transaction]
- The transaction agreement and Scheme of Arrangement acquired Rowan's entire ordinary share capital (and thereby its consolidated businesses, including its interest in ARO) into the Ensco group; the filing does not describe any carve-out of Rowan's product lines, operating processes, or workforce from that combination — the parties are described as combining as whole, publicly traded companies rather than as a transfer of a discrete asset or business unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter and business description]

## Governance and management arrangements at closing
- Immediately following the Scheme Effective Time, the board of directors of Ensco Rowan plc (the "Ensco Rowan Board") was to consist of 11 members: six designated by Ensco (including Carl Trowell as Executive Chairman) and five designated by Rowan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, board and management section]
- For the first two Annual General Meetings after the Scheme Effective Time, the Ensco Rowan Board would renominate the directors designated by each of Ensco and Rowan who remained on the board, per an Ensco Rowan Corporate Governance Policy to be adopted; for two years following the Scheme Effective Time, the Nominating and Governance Committee would consist of two Ensco designees and two Rowan designees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, board and management section]
- At the Scheme Effective Time, Rowan's President and CEO, Dr. Burke, was to become President and CEO of the combined company, and Ensco's President and CEO, Dr. Trowell, was to become Executive Chairman of the combined company; the remaining executive management team was to be named later and comprise executives from both companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, interests of directors and officers section]

## Contractual / planning arrangements referenced at closing
- Management expected the transaction to result in cost savings and operational synergies, including anticipated annualized cost synergies of more than $150 million by the end of the year following consummation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, reasons for the transaction section]
- The filing identifies risks tied to successfully integrating the operations of the two organizations and achieving the anticipated synergies, describing this as requiring "the dedication of significant management resources," without detailing a specific transition-services or shared-services agreement bridging the two companies' operations at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, risk factors section]
- Completion of the transaction was conditioned on antitrust clearances in a number of jurisdictions, including the United States, Saudi Arabia (in connection with ARO), and the United Kingdom. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, conditions to closing section]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two entire, previously independent, publicly traded offshore-drilling companies (Ensco and Rowan) via a share-for-share scheme of arrangement, with Rowan's whole business — including its pre-existing 50/50 Saudi Arabia joint venture (ARO) — carried into the renamed combined entity (Ensco Rowan plc) rather than any single tier (product, process, or people) being separated from the others; governance was structured with negotiated board representation from both legacy companies and a combined-company headquarters in London. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007564, DEFM14A, cover letter, board and management section, §1.7]
