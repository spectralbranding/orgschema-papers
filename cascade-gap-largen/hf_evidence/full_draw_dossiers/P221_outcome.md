## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant continued as an SEC filer under the same CIK (0001698990) after closing,
  filing periodic reports under its new name; the source cited throughout this dossier is
  its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022, filed February 16, 2023, which presents comparative
  financial data for fiscal years 2020, 2021, and 2022 — a window falling roughly two to
  four years after the July 31, 2018 closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The entire disclosed business of the registrant in this filing is the asset base
  acquired in the 2018 transaction (the Karnes County and Giddings assets and, until
  divested, the Ironwood interest); there is no separate segment to consolidate away, so
  the figures below are the acquired business's own record, not an inference from a
  larger parent's consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Realized financial record
- Consolidated net income (loss): a net loss of approximately $1,868.983 million for
  fiscal year 2020, net income of approximately $559.716 million for fiscal year 2021,
  and net income of approximately $1,050.249 million for fiscal year 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The fiscal-year-2020 loss reflected a proved-property impairment of approximately $1.4
  billion (recorded in "Impairment of oil and natural gas properties") and an
  unproved-property impairment of approximately $0.6 billion (recorded in "Exploration
  expenses"), both described in the filing as recognized "as a result of a sharp decline
  in commodity prices." [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- As of December 31, 2022, the Company reported 213 employees: 87 at its field offices in
  Giddings and Gillett, Texas, and 126 at its corporate headquarters in Houston, Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Documented shift at the process/organization interface over time
- On August 1, 2020, the Company provided written notice to EVOC (the EnerVest-affiliated
  contract operator engaged at closing) of its intent to terminate the Services
  Agreement; EVOC continued providing services during a transition period that was
  completed June 30, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The related Non-Competition Agreement with EnerVest was likewise amended during the
  second quarter of 2021 to end its term on June 30, 2021, concurrent with the Services
  Agreement's termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The filing attributes part of the year-over-year decline in general and administrative
  expenses for fiscal year 2022 to "the reduction in costs related to the termination of
  the Services Agreement on June 30, 2021," partially offset by "higher corporate payroll
  expenses related to increased employee headcount and professional service fees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Partial divestiture within the window
- On October 23, 2020, the Company sold its 35% membership interest in Ironwood Eagle
  Ford Midstream, LLC (one of the three asset components acquired in 2018) for
  approximately $27.1 million in cash, recognizing a gain on sale of the equity-method
  investment of approximately $5.1 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The Company received an earnout payment in the second quarter of 2022 associated with
  that 2020 sale of the Ironwood interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Ownership continuity
- As of December 31, 2022, the Company owned approximately 89.8% of the interest in
  Magnolia LLC (the operating subsidiary holding the acquired assets), with the remaining
  approximately 10.2% held as a noncontrolling interest by remaining Magnolia LLC Unit
  Holders (EnerVest-affiliated entities that elected Class B stock/units at closing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window, the acquired business recorded a large
  impairment-driven net loss in the first year (fiscal 2020, ~$1.869 billion, driven by
  ~$2.0 billion combined proved/unproved property impairments amid a commodity-price
  decline) followed by a sharp recovery to net income of ~$559.7 million (2021) and
  ~$1,050.2 million (2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Over the same window, the closing-era contract-operator arrangement (the EVOC Services
  Agreement) was terminated by Company notice in August 2020 and wound down by June 30,
  2021, coinciding with growth in the Company's own reported headcount (213 employees by
  year-end 2022) and a partial divestiture (the October 2020 sale of the Ironwood
  interest). [SRC: SEC accession 0001698990-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
