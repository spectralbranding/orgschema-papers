# Outcome sub-dossier — Pinnacle Foods business realized record after acquisition, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The acquired entity deregistered and stopped
> filing its own periodic reports, so no standalone post-closing financial-performance
> record exists for it; the facts below are drawn from the acquirer's (Conagra Brands,
> Inc., CIK 0000023217) own separately-labeled disclosures about the acquisition, within
> its fiscal 2021-2023 annual reports (fiscal year ends in late May), covering
> approximately the 3rd-5th year after the October 26, 2018 closing. This sub-dossier
> contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Pinnacle Foods Inc. filed a Form 15-12B on 2018-11-05 to deregister its securities
  under the Exchange Act following the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-318395, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other annual report was filed under Pinnacle's own CIK (0001564822)
  after its final fiscal-2017 annual report (filed 2018-03-01); consequently there is no
  standalone, Pinnacle-only audited financial record for the ~2021-2023 outcome window.
  [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history for CIK 0001564822, accession 0001564822-18-000014 (last Pinnacle 10-K, FY2017) and 0001193125-18-318395 (Form 15-12B)]
- Conagra, the acquirer, does not report a distinct "Pinnacle" operating segment in its
  post-closing 10-Ks; the acquired business's brands were folded into Conagra's existing
  Grocery & Snacks, Refrigerated & Frozen, and International reporting segments, so no
  separate revenue, volume, or segment-profit figure isolates the legacy Pinnacle
  business within Conagra's consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]

## Acquirer-disclosed, acquisition-specific integration record (the "Pinnacle Integration Restructuring Plan")
- In December 2018 Conagra's Board approved a restructuring and integration plan tied
  specifically to the ongoing integration of the acquired Pinnacle operations (the
  "Pinnacle Integration Restructuring Plan"), for the purpose of achieving cost
  synergies between the companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]
- Conagra recognized pre-tax charges under this plan of $73.8 million in fiscal 2020,
  $31.7 million in fiscal 2021, $19.6 million in fiscal 2022, and $2.4 million in fiscal
  2023 (fiscal years ending in late May). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001437749-23-019879, Conagra FY2023 Form 10-K]
- Cumulative pre-tax expenses recognized under the plan from inception through May 28,
  2023 totaled $295.7 million ($267.6 million cash, $28.1 million non-cash); total
  expected lifetime expenses were $340.7 million ($280.1 million cash, $60.6 million
  non-cash), with the remainder attributed mainly to certain leased facilities not
  expected to be used in their current capacity through the lease term. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-23-019879, Conagra FY2023 Form 10-K]
- As of the end of the first quarter of fiscal 2023 (i.e. by roughly August 2022, within
  the outcome window), Conagra stated it had "substantially completed" the Pinnacle
  Integration Restructuring Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-23-019879, Conagra FY2023 Form 10-K]
- Conagra's FY2022 10-K describes the plan's implementation as having presented
  "significant organizational design and infrastructure challenges," in many cases
  requiring negotiations with third parties including labor organizations, suppliers,
  and business partners, while stating it had "generally been successful" implementing
  the plan without material interruption to date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]

## Segment-level charges within the window (not attributed by name to specific legacy brands)
- Conagra's Grocery & Snacks segment recognized brand-intangible impairment charges of
  $13.0 million in fiscal 2021 and $90.7 million in fiscal 2022; its Refrigerated &
  Frozen segment recognized $76.9 million in fiscal 2021 and $103.9 million in fiscal
  2022 in impairment of certain brand intangible assets. The filing does not attribute
  these segment-level impairments to specific brands. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]

## Litigation tied specifically to the acquisition
- Securities class actions (consolidated as West Palm Beach Firefighters' Pension Fund
  v. Conagra Brands, Inc., et al., filed February 22, 2019) alleged that statements
  around the Pinnacle acquisition caused the market to have an "unrealistically positive
  assessment" of Conagra's financial prospects; the consolidated action was dismissed
  with prejudice on December 23, 2020, and the Seventh Circuit affirmed dismissal on May
  9, 2022, closing the matter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]
- A related consolidated shareholder derivative action (Klein v. Arora, et al.) alleging
  breaches of fiduciary duty and mismanagement in connection with the Pinnacle
  acquisition was dismissed without prejudice by stipulation on June 9, 2022, and the
  FY2023 10-K confirms the lawsuit was dismissed and terminated in the first quarter of
  fiscal 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001437749-23-019879, Conagra FY2023 Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Pinnacle deregistered and filed no further periodic reports, there is no
  standalone financial-performance record (revenue, profit, or segment results) for the
  acquired business specifically within the ~2021-2023 window; Conagra does not report
  the legacy brands as a distinct segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-318395, Form 15-12B; SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K]
- What IS separately and specifically documented by the acquirer, by name, is an
  acquisition-tied integration-cost record spanning fiscal 2020-2023 (cumulative $295.7
  million recognized of an expected $340.7 million, substantially completed by early
  fiscal 2023) together with acquisition-specific securities and derivative litigation
  that was fully resolved (dismissed) by mid-calendar-2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-22-017530, Conagra FY2022 Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001437749-23-019879, Conagra FY2023 Form 10-K]
