## Documented factual events within the ~2022-2024 window (press-sourced)
- In a related joint-venture business associated with the transaction — USG Boral, the
  Asia-Pacific plasterboard and ceilings joint venture in which Knauf acquired full
  ownership in connection with the 2019 merger — the "USG Boral" brand name was
  reported to be gradually replaced by the "Knauf" brand in Australia during 2022,
  according to a distributor's news post dated February 28, 2022 quoting Knauf's
  Australian managing director; the post states this was a name/branding change only,
  with the business continuing to supply the same product range from the same
  manufacturing facilities. [SRC: https://www.nhs.trade/news/post/usg-boral-rebrands-as-knauf.html]
- This rebranding item concerns the Asia-Pacific joint-venture business, not USG's core
  U.S. gypsum/wallboard operations, and no source was found isolating a comparable
  documented event specifically for the U.S. business within the ~2022-2024 window. [UNVERIFIED]

## Items noted but outside the fixed outcome window
- Reputable press reported that USG (by then part of the Knauf Group since 2019) would
  permanently close its Southard, Oklahoma manufacturing facility in November 2026,
  affecting more than 250 employees, phasing out the majority of a specialized
  industrial-gypsum-products portfolio; the press report states the facility had
  continued operating under Knauf ownership since 2019 and attributes the closure to
  "escalating complexity and cost to mine and manufacture the specialized products in
  the portfolio." This event falls well outside the ~2022-2024 fixed outcome window
  (announced 2026) and is noted here only for completeness, not as an in-window outcome
  fact. [SRC: https://www.wconline.com/articles/98512-usg-to-close-oklahoma-plant-250-jobs-affected]
