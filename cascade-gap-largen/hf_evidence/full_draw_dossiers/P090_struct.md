## The drawn accession (what it is, and why the structure is sourced elsewhere)
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K dated May 24, 2016 (earliest event May 18, 2016)
  reporting, under Item 1.01, entry into an amendment to a senior secured credit
  agreement; it post-dates the acquisition activity described below and does not itself
  establish the closing-era acquisition structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-600567, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The closing-era acquisition structure is therefore sourced from the registrant's
  contemporaneous primary filings (2014-2015 deal 8-Ks, a merger-communication 425, and
  the FY2014 Form 10-K), each cited separately below. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]

## Registrant identity and value-capture vehicle (closing era)
- The registrant described itself as "a leading global solar energy services provider,"
  reporting a TerraForm Power segment and a Semiconductor Materials segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]
- The TerraForm Power segment "owns and operates clean power generation assets, both
  developed by the Solar Energy segment and acquired through third party acquisitions,
  that sell electricity" — i.e. a publicly listed holding vehicle (TerraForm Power, Inc.)
  into which developed and purchased operating projects were placed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]
- TerraForm Power, Inc. completed its initial public offering, closing July 24, 2014,
  establishing the listed vehicle used to hold operating generation assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000143, 8-K (TerraForm IPO closing)]

## Acquisition transactions crossing the ownership boundary at closing
- First Wind: on November 17, 2014, SunEdison, TerraForm Power, Inc. and TerraForm Power,
  LLC entered a purchase and sale agreement to acquire First Wind Holdings, LLC; SunEdison
  purchased the development platform, pipeline and projects in development (over 1.6 GW of
  pipeline and backlog), and TerraForm LLC purchased 521 MW of operating power assets (500
  MW wind, 21 MW solar) for an enterprise value of $862 million; total consideration up to
  $2.4 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000188, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The First Wind acquisition closed on January 29, 2015; the FY2014 10-K describes the
  split whereby TerraForm First Wind ACQ, LLC purchased the 521 MW operating portfolio and
  SunEdison acquired the development platform/backlog. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]
- Silver Ridge Power: the registrant entered an agreement with Silver Ridge Power
  Holdings, LLC, with The AES Corporation as Parent and SunEdison, Inc. as Buyer Guarantor
  (a purchase of solar assets from an AES/Riverstone joint venture). [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014] [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000117, 8-K (Silver Ridge Power LLC acquisition agreement)]
- Vivint Solar (attempted): on July 20, 2015 SunEdison entered a merger agreement to
  acquire Vivint Solar, Inc.; on July 24, 2015 the Company furnished merger-related
  communications (a Rule 425 filing including an introductory video to Vivint Solar
  employees by Vivint Solar's CEO Gregory Butterfield). [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000199, 8-K / Rule 425]

## What crossed vs. what was bridged/retained at closing
- Customer-facing asset that crossed: operating renewable-power projects (contracted wind
  and solar generation) placed into the TerraForm Power holding vehicle, plus the acquired
  development pipeline/backlog held at SunEdison. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000188, 8-K Item 1.01]
- Processes and people bridged, not simply transferred: in the First Wind acquisition
  SunEdison agreed to "continu[e] the employment of certain identified key employees to
  facilitate the development and construction of the earnout projects," with an earnout of
  approximately $510 million over roughly two-and-a-half years tied to Project Completion —
  a contractual mechanism binding acquired personnel and development processes to the buyer
  post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000188, 8-K Item 1.01]
- Cross-entity bridging substrate: SunEdison, TerraForm LLC and SunEdison Holdings
  Corporation entered an Intercompany Agreement allocating costs, indemnities, purchase-price
  adjustments and excess capex/O&M among affiliates, and granting TerraForm LLC call-right
  options over projects — i.e. a Support Agreement / call-right list under which the listed
  vehicle would purchase additional projects from the sponsor over time. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000188, 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- Across 2014-2015 the registrant assembled operating renewable-power assets and a
  development platform through a sequence of acquisitions (First Wind closed January 2015;
  Silver Ridge Power; an attempted Vivint Solar merger agreed July 2015), routing operating
  projects into a separately listed holding vehicle (TerraForm Power) via call-right/support
  arrangements while retaining development pipeline and key personnel at the parent through
  earnout and intercompany agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-14-000188, 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]
