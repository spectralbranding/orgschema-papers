# Structural sub-dossier — Legacy Reserves Inc. corporate reorganization (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction was a corporate reorganization in which Legacy Reserves LP (the
  "Partnership"), a Delaware master limited partnership, converted into a corporate
  form via a merger between the Partnership and Legacy Reserves Merger Sub LLC, a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Legacy Reserves Inc. ("New Legacy"), a newly formed
  Delaware corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Following the merger, the Partnership and its general partner became wholly owned
  subsidiaries of New Legacy; New Legacy common stock was expected to be listed on the
  Nasdaq Global Select Market under the symbol "LGCY." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Alongside the merger, New Legacy agreed to purchase all of the outstanding limited
  liability company interests in the Partnership's general partner from Lion GP
  Interests, LLC (the "GP Seller") for an aggregate cash purchase price of $3.0 million
  (the "GP Purchase"), together with the merger constituting the "Corporate
  Reorganization." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Common units of the Partnership were to be exchanged for New Legacy common stock at a
  fixed "Common Unit Exchange Ratio"; Series A Preferred Units were to receive
  2.92033118 shares of New Legacy common stock per unit and Series B Preferred Units a
  separate fixed ratio, pursuant to a Settlement Agreement submitted to a Delaware
  Chancery Court on July 6, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Legacy Reserves LP separately filed its own DEFM14A for the same special meeting under
  its own SEC registrant CIK (0001358831), dated 2018-08-03. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238546, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Legal vehicle: the operating business converted from a master limited partnership
  form to a Delaware corporation form; the merger was structured under Delaware law with
  the Partnership merging with Merger Sub, a New Legacy subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Business/value-capture model and controlling intent: the merger was to be "accounted
  for as an equity transaction among the owners of New Legacy using historical cost
  accounting with no gain or loss being recognized," indicating the transaction was
  structured as a continuity-of-ownership conversion rather than a change-of-control
  acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- People / management: "The Partnership and New Legacy expect that the existing
  management team will stay in place after the Corporate Reorganization." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- People / equity awards: outstanding unvested awards under the Partnership's long-term
  incentive plan, held by named executive officers of the Partnership's general partner
  and by Partnership employees, were to convert automatically at the effective time
  rather than being severed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Board / governance: designees identified in the proxy statement/prospectus were slated
  to become the New Legacy Board, either as a staggered three-class board (if a
  classification proposal passed) or as a single class of six directors (if it did not).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- Listing / registration: it was a condition to closing that New Legacy common stock be
  approved for listing on a national securities exchange (expected symbol "LGCY" on
  Nasdaq); upon consummation, the Partnership's units then listed on Nasdaq were to cease
  to be listed and be subsequently deregistered under the Exchange Act, with the former
  unitholders and Preferred Unitholders becoming New Legacy stockholders and New Legacy
  becoming the successor SEC registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]

## Continuity of product/process substrate
- No sourced fact in the drawn filing indicates the customer-facing product (oil,
  natural gas liquids, and natural gas production and sale) or the operating processes
  that produce and deliver it were separated from, or realigned independently of, the
  legal-vehicle conversion; the filing frames the reorganization as a change in
  corporate form of the same operating partnership rather than a transfer of assets to
  a different operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- A putative class action (the "Doppelt Action") was filed by a Preferred Unit holder in
  the Delaware Court of Chancery on March 28, 2018 challenging the merger, styled
  Doppelt v. Legacy Reserves LP, Legacy Reserves GP, LLC, and Legacy Reserves Inc., Case
  No. 2018-0225. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
- The Partnership and New Legacy determined the Corporate Reorganization was not subject
  to Hart-Scott-Rodino Act requirements and that no other governmental antitrust
  consents were required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as an entity-form conversion (master limited
  partnership to Delaware corporation) via merger, accounted for as an equity
  transaction with no gain or loss recognized, in which the existing management team was
  expected to stay in place and unvested employee equity awards converted rather than
  being severed; the Partnership's Nasdaq-listed units were to cease to be listed and be
  deregistered as New Legacy's common stock took their place as the Nasdaq-listed
  security, with New Legacy becoming the successor SEC registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-238437, DEFM14A]
