# Structural sub-dossier — Sprague Resources LP asset acquisitions (closing structure)

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction(s) were structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and
> what did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The registrant and its value-capture model at closing
- Sprague Resources LP is a Delaware limited partnership formed in June 2011 by Sprague
  Holdings and its General Partner "to engage in the purchase, storage, distribution and
  sale of refined products and natural gas, and to provide storage and handling services
  for a broad range of materials." [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000047, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- At the closing era the registrant "own[ed], operate[d] and/or control[led] a network of
  19 refined products and materials handling terminals strategically located throughout
  the Northeast United States and in Quebec, Canada." [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000047, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## The drawn filing, described truthfully
- The drawn current report (filed 2017-03-10) is furnished under Item 2.02 "Results of
  Operation and Financial Condition"; it attaches as Exhibit 99.1 a press release
  announcing fourth-quarter and full-year 2016 financial results, and is not a
  transaction-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000045, Form 8-K Item 2.02]

## Transaction type and what crossed the boundary (contemporaneous acquisitions)
- During the closing era the registrant, through wholly owned operating subsidiaries,
  entered into and completed a series of separate agreements to purchase petroleum-terminal
  and energy-marketing ASSETS from unaffiliated sellers (asset purchases, not whole-company
  acquisitions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000002, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000004, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- (Global) On December 30, 2016, Sprague Operating Resources LLC and Sprague Energy
  Solutions Inc. entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement with Global Montello Group Corp.
  and Global Energy Marketing LLC (subsidiaries of Global Partners LP) "to acquire the
  assets of Global's natural gas and electricity brokerage businesses," for $17,250,000
  plus valuation, inventory and security-deposit amounts, minus assumed customer credit
  balances and customer deposits, plus assumption of Assumed Liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000002, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- (L.E. Belcher) On January 23, 2017, Sprague Operating Resources LLC entered into a
  Terminal and Wholesale Fuels Asset Purchase Agreement with Leonard E. Belcher,
  Incorporated "for the purchase of refined product terminal assets in Springfield,
  Massachusetts," for $20,000,000 plus Inventory Value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000004, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- (Carbo) On March 13, 2017, Sprague Operating Resources LLC entered into an Asset Purchase
  Agreement to acquire Carbo's Inwood and Lawrence, New York refined product terminal
  assets from Carbo Industries, Inc., Carbo Realty, LLC, and Paul Hochhauser, for
  $70,000,000 plus seller inventory, Buyer Employee Obligations, assumed liabilities and
  Bonus Units; $38,184,000 payable in 120 equal monthly installments over ten years and
  $30,000,000 paid in Sprague Resources common units under a concurrent Unit Purchase
  Agreement (a Section 4(a)(2)/Regulation D unregistered issuance). [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01 and Item 3.02]
- (Carbo — completion) On April 19, 2017, the registrant announced completion of the
  acquisition of "substantially all of the operating assets of Carbo Industries and Carbo
  Realty"; consideration was paid as $10 million cash at closing, 1,131,551 common units
  issued at closing, and the balance in monthly installments over the ten-year period
  following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000054, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## People / processes crossing (or not) with the acquired assets
- Each acquisition current report flags, as a forward-looking risk, the possibility that
  the parties "may be unable to ... successfully integrate [the seller]'s operations into
  those of Sprague" and the question of "the ability ... to retain certain key employees"
  of the acquired business — i.e., the purchased assets and the people/processes that run
  them were not treated as an automatically intact unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000002, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000004, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Carbo consideration expressly included a "Buyer Employee Obligations" component,
  indicating that certain employee-related obligations were assumed by the buyer as part of
  the asset transfer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000054, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Transitional / bridging arrangement over a shared substrate (closing-time)
- A First Amendment dated April 18, 2017 provided that, at or prior to closing, Carbo
  Realty would execute and deliver to Sprague a license agreement for the exclusive use of
  a portion of the property at 30 Inip Drive, Inwood, NY (and non-exclusive use of
  additional portions) "until such time as an agreement granting to Sprague an easement to
  such portions of the property is properly recorded" — a transitional instrument bridging
  real-property access not fully conveyed at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000054, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Controlling-principal fusion at a tier
- One Carbo counterparty is a named natural person, Paul Hochhauser, party to the Asset
  Purchase Agreement; the corporate seller Carbo Industries, Inc. received Sprague Resources
  common units and represented itself an "accredited investor" acquiring the units for its
  own account. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01 and Item 3.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The registrant, an energy-terminaling and marketing limited partnership, expanded at
  closing by purchasing discrete petroleum-terminal and energy-marketing ASSET packages
  (Global brokerage books; L.E. Belcher, Springfield MA terminal; Carbo Inwood/Lawrence NY
  terminals) into wholly owned operating subsidiaries — funding one purchase partly with
  issued common units and deferred ten-year installments — with the acquired assets crossing
  the boundary while the associated people/processes were addressed through integration and
  key-employee-retention risk, an assumed employee-obligations component, and a transitional
  property license pending an easement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-17-000054, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
