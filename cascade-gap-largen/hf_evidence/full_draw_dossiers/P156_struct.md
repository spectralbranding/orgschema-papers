## Commencement of the transaction and the entities inside/outside the boundary
- On April 29, 2014, EFH Corp. and the substantial majority of its direct and indirect
  subsidiaries — including Energy Future Intermediate Holding Company LLC (EFIH), Energy
  Future Competitive Holdings Company LLC (EFCH) and Texas Competitive Electric Holdings
  Company LLC (TCEH), but excluding Oncor Electric Delivery Holdings Company LLC and its
  subsidiaries — filed voluntary petitions under Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court
  for the District of Delaware, and operated as debtors-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-164757, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The regulated transmission-and-distribution utility (Oncor) sat outside the filing
  perimeter: EFIH's wholly owned subsidiary Oncor Holdings owns approximately 80% of the
  equity interests of Oncor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-164757, Form 8-K financial-information footnotes]
- Purchase accounting intangibles on the competitive side originated in "EFH Corp.'s 2007
  merger," identifying the current group as the product of that prior transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-164757, Form 8-K TCEH Adjusted EBITDA footnote 1]

## How the reorganization closed (plan-based separation, effective October 3, 2016)
- On August 29, 2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered a Confirmation Order confirming the
  Third Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization solely as it pertains to EFCH, TCEH and
  TCEH's Debtor subsidiaries plus the EFH Shared Services Debtors (collectively the
  "T-Side Debtors"), with that confirmation and effective date structured to occur
  separate from and independent of a later plan for EFH Corp., EFIH and their remaining
  Debtor subsidiaries (the "EFH Debtors"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K background information]
- On October 3, 2016 the Plan for the T-Side Debtors became effective; the T-Side Debtors
  consummated their reorganization and emerged from Chapter 11 as a tax-free spin-off from
  EFH Corp. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K background information]
- What crossed the boundary: TCEH contributed its interests and certain TCEH-related
  assets/liabilities into a newly formed entity (TEX Energy LLC, converted to TCEH Corp.),
  through an IntermediateCo/OpCo/AssetCo chain; 427,500,000 shares of TCEH Corp. common
  stock and approximately $370,000,000 of cash were then distributed to the former
  first-lien creditors of TCEH in exchange for cancellation of their claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Distribution/Spin-Off description]
- New controlling owners of the separated competitive business were the former creditors:
  TCEH Corp. entered stockholders' agreements with affiliates of Apollo Management
  Holdings L.P., Brookfield Asset Management Private Institutional Capital Adviser
  (Canada), L.P., and Oaktree Capital Management, L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Stockholders' Agreements]

## People and operating substrate — what moved and what was bridged
- Operating-business management moved WITH the competitive assets: TCEH Corp. was staffed
  with Curtis A. Morgan (CEO), James A. Burke (COO, previously President and CEO of TXU
  Energy), Stephanie Zapata Moore (GC, previously GC of Luminant) and Terry L. Nutt
  (Controller) — i.e., leaders drawn from the underlying Luminant / TXU Energy operating
  units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Item 5.02 TCEH Corp. officers]
- On the remaining (EFH) side, as of the Effective Date all existing EFH Corp. officers
  were removed and a restructuring-focused slate was appointed (Paul M. Keglevic as CEO,
  President, Chief Restructuring Officer and Chief Accounting Officer). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Item 5.02 EFH Corp. officers]
- Shared corporate-service substrate was BRIDGED, not transferred whole: EFH Corp. and
  OpCo entered a Transition Services Agreement under which OpCo would provide the EFH
  Debtors business-services administration, accounting, corporate secretary, tax, human
  resources, information technology, internal audit and SOX compliance, physical
  facilities and security, treasury and legal services until the earlier of December 31,
  2017 or the closing of the EFH Debtors' own emergence transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Transition Services Agreement]
- Additional bridging instruments at closing: a Tax Matters Agreement preserving the
  spin-off's intended tax treatment, and an Amended and Restated Split Participant
  Agreement between OpCo and Oncor allocating pension and post-retirement benefits for
  employees whose service straddled the regulated and non-regulated sides. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Tax Matters Agreement and Split Participant Agreement]
- Financing crossed as exit debt: the $4.25 billion DIP Roll Facilities converted on
  October 3, 2016 into the senior secured OpCo exit credit facilities, assumed by OpCo and
  IntermediateCo. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Credit Agreement / Collateral Trust Agreement]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction separated the competitive electricity business (generation plus retail,
  under TCEH/Luminant/TXU Energy) from the regulated transmission-and-distribution utility
  (Oncor, held outside the bankruptcy perimeter) through a plan-based, tax-free spin-off
  in which the competitive operating assets and their operating-business management crossed
  to a new creditor-owned company, while the shared corporate-service substrate was held
  together at closing by a Transition Services Agreement, a Tax Matters Agreement and a
  Split Participant Agreement rather than moving as an intact unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-729877, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 5.02 and 7.01]
