# Outcome sub-dossier — Newell Brands realized record after the Jarden combination, ~3-year horizon (FY2018)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The acquired entity ceased to be a separate SEC
> registrant at closing, so the realized record below is traced through the surviving
> parent's (Newell Brands Inc., CIK 0000814453) subsequent periodic filings. This
> sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was structured at
> closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Jarden Corporation filed a Form 15-12B on April 28, 2016 certifying termination of its
  Exchange Act registration (one holder of record); no further separate periodic reports
  were filed by the legacy Jarden entity after that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-562349, Form 15-12B]
- The acquired business's results have since been reported only as part of the
  consolidated Newell Brands Inc. entity; Newell's segment structure (Food and
  Appliances; Home and Outdoor Living; Learning and Development; Connected Home and
  Security) mixes legacy-Newell and legacy-Jarden brands rather than reporting a
  standalone Jarden segment, so no fully separate financial record for the acquired
  business is publicly observable — the most granular acquired-business-specific data
  available is where Newell's filings separately attribute a figure to assets
  "acquired in the Jarden acquisition," as with the impairment detail below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]

## Realized financial trajectory (fixed outcome window: FY2018, filed 2019-03-04)
- For the year ended December 31, 2018, Newell Brands reported a net loss of
  approximately $6,917.9 million (net sales of $8,630.9 million, an operating loss of
  approximately $7,828.5 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018), Revenue Recognition footnote]
- During 2018 the Company recorded goodwill impairment charges of $3.9 billion to
  reduce certain reporting units to fair value, plus a further $4.4 billion of trade-
  name impairment charges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]
- Of the trade-name impairments, the Food and Appliances segment (trade names "primarily
  acquired in the Jarden acquisition") was impaired by $1.7 billion during 2018, and the
  Home and Outdoor Living segment (trade names "all acquired in the Jarden acquisition")
  was impaired by $2.4 billion during 2018; the Learning and Development segment (trade
  names "primarily acquired in the Jarden acquisition") was impaired by $0.2 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]
- The Company also recorded held-for-sale impairment charges in 2018 of $697 million on
  its Process Solutions disposal group, $131 million on its Rexair disposal group, and
  $80 million on its U.S. Playing Cards disposal group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]

## Integration program and divestitures
- The Company began "the Jarden Integration" restructuring in 2016, initially focused on
  cost synergies and "organizational changes designed to redefine the operating model of
  the Company from a holding company to an operating company"; in Q1 2018 it announced an
  "Accelerated Transformation Plan" to divest industrial/commercial and non-core consumer
  businesses, which continued elements of the Jarden Integration and realigned the
  Company's "management structure." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Ongoing Restructuring Initiatives"]
- During 2018 the Company sold, among other businesses, Rawlings/Team Sports (~$400M,
  pretax loss $128M), Waddington (~$2.3B, pretax gain $599M), Goody (~$109M, pretax gain
  $20.3M), Jostens (~$1.3B, pretax loss $32.1M), and Pure Fishing (~$1.3B, pretax gain
  $372M) — all businesses that had been part of the acquired Jarden portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Divestitures"]
- In February 2019 the Company signed a definitive agreement to sell its Rexair business
  — also a legacy-Jarden brand — for $235 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Divestitures"]

## Documented process/organization disruption
- Management disclosed that as of December 31, 2018, the Company's disclosure controls
  and internal control over financial reporting were not effective, due to a material
  weakness specifically in "the accounting for the impact of divestitures," including
  deferred-tax and asset/liability classification errors, which required adjustments to
  prior-quarter figures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Controls and Procedures" / risk factors]
- At closing (April 2016), William A. Burke III — a Newell Rubbermaid executive — was
  named "President, Jarden Group," a role he held only from April 2016 to January 2017,
  after which he became Executive Vice President, Chief Operating Officer for the
  combined Company; i.e., the acquired business was run as a distinct "Jarden Group"
  organizational unit for roughly nine months before that unit was folded into the
  combined Company's normal operating structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Executive Officers"]
- The Company's Chief Financial Officer position also turned over during the outcome
  window: Christopher H. Peterson became EVP, CFO in December 2018, arriving from an
  external company (Revlon, Inc.) rather than from either legacy organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Executive Officers"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2018 outcome window (filed 2019-03-04, roughly 2.5-3 years after
  the April 2016 closing), the surviving parent reported a net loss of approximately
  $6.9 billion, driven substantially by $3.9 billion of goodwill impairment and $4.4
  billion of trade-name impairment, with the largest trade-name impairments concentrated
  in segments whose names were "acquired in the Jarden acquisition"; the Company also
  divested the majority of the acquired brand businesses named at signing (Rawlings,
  Waddington, Goody, Jostens, Pure Fishing, and, in early 2019, Rexair) under an
  "Accelerated Transformation Plan," disclosed a material weakness in internal controls
  specific to divestiture accounting, and had disbanded, after roughly nine months, an
  interim organizational unit ("Jarden Group") that had been used to run the acquired
  business separately post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-061714, Form 10-K (FY2018) "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets" / "Divestitures" / "Ongoing Restructuring Initiatives" / "Controls and Procedures" / "Executive Officers"]
