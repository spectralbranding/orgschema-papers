## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K (date of earliest event April 27, 2017; signed May 3, 2017) reporting under Item 1.01 the entry, on April 28, 2017, into a Second Amended and Restated Master Repurchase Agreement referred to as the "Roll-Up Facility" with Credit Suisse First Boston Mortgage Capital LLC (administrative agent), Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch, and Alpine Securitization LTD (buyers). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The "Roll-Up Facility" here is a secured financing (master repurchase / warehouse) arrangement, not a portfolio-acquisition or company-purchase agreement: the registrant, through subsidiaries, may sell and later repurchase newly originated mortgage loans purchased from correspondent lenders and distressed mortgage loans / REO interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The facility is entered through four wholly-owned subsidiaries — PennyMac Operating Partnership, L.P. ("POP"), PennyMac Corp. ("PMC"), PennyMac Holdings, LLC ("PMH"), and PMC REO Financing Trust, LLC — with the obligations of POP, PMC and PMH fully guaranteed by the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The 8-K establishes that the mortgage loans are serviced and the REO properties are managed by PennyMac Loan Services, LLC ("PLS"), a controlled subsidiary of PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. (NYSE: PFSI). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Roll-Up Facility and companion Re-warehouse Facility together provide for a maximum combined purchase price of $1.5 billion (maximum combined committed purchase price $650 million), committed to April 27, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The 8-K also reports (Item 5.02) that on April 27, 2017 trustee Clay A. Halvorsen resigned from the Board, stated to be due to other commitments and not any disagreement with the Company. It was signed by Andrew S. Chang, Senior Managing Director and Chief Financial Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Item 5.02 and signature block]

## Parties, vehicle and value-capture model (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is a specialty finance company that invests primarily in residential mortgage loans and mortgage-related assets; it was organized in Maryland on May 18, 2009 and conducts operations through two segments, correspondent production and investment activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- Substantially all operations and investments are conducted through PennyMac Operating Partnership, L.P. (the Operating Partnership) and its subsidiaries; a wholly-owned subsidiary is the sole general partner and the registrant is the sole limited partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- The registrant has elected to be taxed as a REIT and intends to maintain its exclusion from the Investment Company Act, requiring it to invest a substantial majority of assets in loans secured by real estate and real estate-related assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]

## Where controlling intent, processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- The registrant is externally managed and advised by PNMAC Capital Management, LLC ("PCM"), an SEC-registered investment adviser that is an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of PennyMac (PFSI), pursuant to a management agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- PCM is responsible for administering the registrant's business activities and day-to-day operations, including developing its investment strategies and sourcing and acquiring mortgage loans and mortgage-related assets for its portfolio, and provides the senior management team, officers and support personnel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- The operating processes for the assembled assets are run by affiliates rather than in-house: under a loan servicing agreement, PLS (an indirect wholly-owned PennyMac subsidiary) provides primary and special servicing for the portfolio of residential mortgage loans and MSRs; commercial loans are master-serviced by Midland Loan Services (a division of PNC Bank) under a separate agreement, with PLS providing oversight. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- The people/organization tier is minimal at the registrant itself: "We have three employees. All of our officers are employees of PennyMac or its affiliates. We do not pay our officers any cash compensation under the terms of our management agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- Investment decisions sit with the external manager: PCM "makes all or substantially all of our investment, financing and risk management decisions," and the registrant states it relies on PCM and PLS "because we have limited employees or in-house capability to handle the services independently." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]

## How the portfolio was assembled at the ownership boundary (closing era)
- The portfolio is built by serial acquisition of mortgage assets: to date the registrant had invested in mortgage loans (a substantial portion distressed and acquired at discounts to unpaid principal balance), MSRs, excess servicing spread (ESS), mortgage-related securities, and small-balance commercial real estate loans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- Correspondent production assembles newly originated loans at scale: the registrant purchased approximately $66.1 billion at fair value of loans in 2016 ($23.9 billion conventional; $42.2 billion government-insured), up from $46.4 billion in 2015, and was the second-largest correspondent aggregator in the United States as ranked by Inside Mortgage Finance, purchasing from 522 approved sellers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- During the year ended December 31, 2016 the registrant received $275.1 million of MSRs as proceeds from the sale of mortgage loans, purchased $2.7 million of MSRs, and received $6.6 million of ESS and $1.6 million of MSRs under recapture agreements with PLS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- As of December 31, 2016, PLS acted as servicer for mortgage loans with an aggregate unpaid principal balance of approximately $194.2 billion, and the combined net assets of the entities managed by PCM (including the registrant's shareholders' equity) were approximately $1.5 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- The manager/servicer relationships are related-party service agreements, not arm's-length in-house functions: a management agreement with PCM (base management fee on shareholders' equity plus incentive compensation), a loan servicing agreement with PLS, and a mortgage banking services agreement with PLS providing fulfillment and disposition-related services for correspondent production. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- The management agreement is difficult and costly to terminate without cause (a significant termination fee is payable to PCM), and terminating the loan servicing or mortgage banking services agreements without cause can itself trigger PCM's right to terminate the management agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
- Investment opportunities are allocated across affiliated vehicles: opportunities consistent with the registrant's objectives are allocated between it and the PennyMac funds and other accounts managed by PCM, generally pro rata by available investment capital, with PCM retaining discretion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents entry into a secured master-repurchase financing arrangement (the "Roll-Up Facility") with Credit Suisse and a companion Re-warehouse Facility, executed through four wholly-owned subsidiaries and guaranteed by the Company — a financing event that references the servicing role of PLS/PFSI, not a portfolio or company purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-008429, 8-K Items 1.01/5.02]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is an externally managed Maryland REIT that assembles a portfolio of residential mortgage loans, MSRs, ESS and mortgage-related securities by serial acquisition (including ~$66.1 billion of correspondent-production loan purchases in 2016), while the controlling intent, investment/sourcing processes, servicing processes, and essentially all personnel sit with its external manager PCM and servicer PLS — affiliates of PennyMac (PFSI) — under related-party management, servicing and mortgage-banking-services agreements, the registrant itself having three employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-17-002631, 10-K FY2016]
