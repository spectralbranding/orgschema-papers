# Outcome sub-dossier — Rockwell Collins / Collins Aerospace realized record, ~3-5-year horizon

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The merger closed on November 26, 2018; Rockwell Collins became a wholly owned
  subsidiary of United Technologies Corporation ("UTC"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-334170, Rockwell Collins Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Rockwell Collins common stock was removed from New York Stock Exchange trading (NYSE:
  COL), and Rockwell Collins filed a Form 25-NSE on 2018-11-27 and a Form 15-12B on
  2018-12-07 to deregister its common stock under the Exchange Act; no Rockwell
  Collins-only annual report (Form 10-K) was filed thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-344256, Rockwell Collins Form 15-12B]
- Because Rockwell Collins ceased separate SEC reporting, no standalone Rockwell
  Collins-only realized-performance record exists for the ~2021-2023 outcome window;
  the record below is the acquirer's (subsequently Raytheon Technologies Corporation,
  "RTX," same SEC CIK as UTC) own disclosure of the "Collins Aerospace" reporting
  segment that comprises the acquired business together with UTC's pre-existing
  aerospace-systems business — segment-level, not Rockwell-Collins-only. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K]
- On April 3, 2020, UTC completed its own merger with Raytheon Company, forming Raytheon
  Technologies Corporation; the FY2020 Form 10-K explicitly references "the Rockwell
  Collins acquisition" and "the Raytheon Merger" as distinct prior corporate-history
  events within the combined company's disclosures, and describes the "Collins Aerospace
  Systems" segment as "a leading global provider of technologically advanced aerospace and
  defense products and aftermarket service solutions" spanning avionics, communications,
  navigation, electric power, environmental/flight control, and engine systems. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K]

## Collins Aerospace segment financial trajectory (realized, primary-filing sourced)
- FY2018 (year of the acquisition, ~5 weeks included): segment net sales $16,634 million,
  operating profit $2,397 million. FY2019 (first full year including Rockwell Collins):
  net sales $26,028 million, operating profit $4,508 million; of the $9,394 million total
  net-sales increase, the 10-K attributes $8,398 million to "Acquisitions/Divestitures,
  net" versus $1,068 million organic. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K, Segment Review]
- FY2020: segment net sales $19,288 million (-26% year over year); operating profit
  $1,466 million (-67%); operating profit margin 7.6%. The 10-K attributes the organic
  sales decline of $6.6 billion primarily to lower commercial aerospace OEM sales ($3.7
  billion) and lower commercial aerospace aftermarket sales ($3.4 billion), tied to the
  COVID-19 pandemic's effect on flight hours, fleet utilization, and commercial OEM
  deliveries. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K, Segment Review]
- FY2021: segment net sales $18,449 million (-4% vs 2020); operating profit $1,759
  million (+20% vs 2020); operating profit margin 9.5%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-23-000009, RTX FY2022 Form 10-K, Segment Review]
- FY2022 (end of outcome window): segment net sales $20,597 million (+12% vs 2021);
  operating profit $2,343 million (+33% vs 2021); operating profit margin 11.4%,
  attributed to the recovery of commercial air traffic and higher aftermarket and OEM
  sales volume. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-23-000009, RTX FY2022 Form 10-K, Segment Review]

## Documented impairment / disruption within the outcome window
- In the second quarter of 2020, the company concluded that the carrying values of two
  of the six Collins Aerospace reporting units exceeded their respective fair values and
  recorded a goodwill impairment charge of $3.2 billion (no further deterioration was
  identified when annual goodwill testing was completed as of October 1, 2020); it
  separately reports other non-goodwill impairment items at Collins Aerospace, including
  an impairment of a Collins Aerospace trade name and write-downs tied to increased
  estimated credit losses and impairments of contract assets reflecting lower estimated
  future customer activity on commercial contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K, Note 2]
- The FY2020 Form 10-K also references restructuring actions at the Collins Aerospace
  segment undertaken in connection with the Raytheon Merger (completed April 3, 2020) as
  a contributor to the period's results, alongside the pandemic-driven volume declines. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K, Segment Review]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Rockwell Collins deregistered as a standalone SEC filer within weeks of the
  November 26, 2018 closing, no Rockwell-Collins-only realized record exists for the
  ~2021-2023 window; the acquirer (UTC, subsequently Raytheon Technologies Corporation)
  separately discloses a "Collins Aerospace" segment combining the acquired business with
  its pre-existing aerospace-systems operations. That segment recorded a $3.2 billion
  goodwill impairment in Q2 2020 alongside a sharp net-sales and operating-profit decline
  (net sales -26%, operating profit -67% in FY2020 versus FY2019), followed by a
  multi-year recovery through FY2022 (net sales $20,597 million, operating profit $2,343
  million, margin 11.4%), which the filings attribute principally to pandemic-driven
  commercial-aerospace demand shocks and the subsequent recovery of air traffic, with
  restructuring tied to the concurrent Raytheon Merger cited as an additional factor in
  the 2020 results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-21-000008, RTX FY2020 Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000101829-23-000009, RTX FY2022 Form 10-K]
