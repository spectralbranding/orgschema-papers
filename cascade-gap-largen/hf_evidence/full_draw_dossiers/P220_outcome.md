## Public-reporting status and continuity after the transaction
- The registrant formerly known as Westar Energy, Inc. continued as an SEC filer after
  the transaction, renamed Evergy Kansas Central, Inc. (a Kansas corporation, Commission
  File No. 001-03523), and, as of the Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021, files jointly under
  a single combined report together with its parent, Evergy, Inc. (CIK 1711269), and
  affiliate Evergy Metro, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K
  (FY2021), cover page]
- Great Plains Energy's former principal Missouri utility subsidiaries are likewise
  identified in the same combined report as Evergy Metro, Inc. and Evergy Missouri West,
  Inc. (formerly KCP&L Greater Missouri Operations Company). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021), cover page / exhibit index]

## Realized financial record within the window
- For fiscal year 2021, Evergy Kansas Central, Inc. (formerly Westar Energy) reported
  net income attributable to Evergy Kansas Central, Inc. of $488.5 million, up from
  $224.1 million in fiscal year 2020, in its own separately presented statement of
  income within the combined 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K
  (FY2021), Evergy Kansas Central, Inc. consolidated statements of income]
- Parent Evergy, Inc. reported consolidated net income attributable to Evergy, Inc. of
  $879.7 million for fiscal year 2021, versus $618.3 million for fiscal year 2020, an
  increase the filing attributes in part to "non-regulated energy marketing margins
  related to the February 2021 winter weather event." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021), MD&A Earnings Overview]

## Continuing merger-order conditions and merger-related goodwill in the window
- As of the FY2021 Form 10-K, conditions in the Kansas Corporation Commission and
  Missouri Public Service Commission orders that authorized the merger continued to
  require Evergy Kansas Central, Evergy Metro, and Evergy Missouri West each to maintain
  consolidated common equity of at least 40% of total capitalization and to maintain
  investment-grade credit ratings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K
  (FY2021), Liquidity/dividend-restriction discussion]
- Evergy's balance sheet carries "a significant amount of goodwill... recorded... in
  connection with completion of the merger that resulted in the formation of Evergy,"
  which the filing states is assessed for impairment annually; the FY2021 Form 10-K does
  not report any impairment having been recognized against that goodwill. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021), Risk Factors]

## Labor and workforce continuity in the window
- At December 31, 2021, "the Evergy Companies" (the combined enterprise) reported 4,930
  employees, including 2,632 represented by five local unions of the International
  Brotherhood of Electrical Workers and one local of the United Government Security
  Officers of America, under labor agreements variously expiring in 2024 or operating
  under extension while under negotiation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008,
  Form 10-K (FY2021), Human Capital Resources]

## Governance friction documented within an in-window filing
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's exhibit index continues to list, as material contracts, a
  February 28, 2020 agreement and a March 25, 2020 amendment between Evergy, Inc. and
  Elliott Investment Management L.P. (together with related Elliott Associates and
  Elliott International entities), along with a related cooperation agreement. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021), Exhibit Index]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2021-2023 window, the surviving Westar-descended registrant (renamed
  Evergy Kansas Central, Inc.) and its parent Evergy, Inc. continued to file combined
  public periodic reports; Evergy Kansas Central's own net income roughly doubled
  year-over-year and Evergy's consolidated net income also grew, partly attributed to
  weather-related trading margins; no merger-related goodwill impairment or divestiture
  is reported; the merger orders' equity/credit-rating conditions remained in force; and
  an activist-shareholder cooperation agreement (Elliott, dated February-March 2020)
  continued to be listed among the enterprise's material contracts as of the in-window
  filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
