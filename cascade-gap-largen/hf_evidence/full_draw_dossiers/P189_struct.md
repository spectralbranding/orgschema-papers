# Structural sub-dossier — Rent-A-Center, Inc. proposed acquisition by Vintage Rodeo Parent, LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured and what became of that structure through the deal window
> (2018). It contains no information about the entity's subsequent operating or
> financial performance.

## Transaction and parties
- On June 17, 2018, Rent-A-Center, Inc. (the "Company") entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger with Vintage Rodeo Parent, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company
  ("Parent"), and Vintage Rodeo Acquisition, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly
  owned subsidiary of Parent ("Merger Sub"), under which Merger Sub would merge with and
  into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A p.1/cover letter]
- Parent is an affiliate of Vintage Capital Management, LLC, described as "a
  value-oriented, operations-focused, private and public equity investor specializing in
  the consumer, aerospace and defense, and manufacturing sectors"; Parent was formed on
  May 7, 2018 solely to engage in the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement
  and had no other business activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- Vintage Capital was separately disclosed as the controlling shareholder of Buddy's
  Newco, LLC d/b/a Buddy's Home Furnishings, "a privately-held rent-to-own company with
  over 300 locations across the U.S. and Guam," and as beneficial owner of approximately
  6% of the Company's common stock at the time of signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- Under the terms, each outstanding share of the Company's common stock (other than
  excluded and dissenting shares) would be converted into the right to receive $15.00 in
  cash, without interest — a whole-company, all-cash going-private acquisition structured
  as a reverse triangular merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Merger (Proposal 1)"]
- The $15.00 per-share price represented approximately a 25% premium to the closing
  price on June 15, 2018 (the last trading day before announcement) and approximately a
  49% premium to the closing price on October 30, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A cover letter]
- If completed, the Company would cease to be an independent public company, its common
  stock would no longer be listed on any stock exchange or quotation system, and its
  Exchange Act reporting obligations would terminate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Merger (Proposal 1)"]

## Financing structure (what would have funded the closing)
- Parent's obligations were backed by an equity commitment letter under which Vintage
  Rodeo, L.P. committed to contribute up to $610 million in cash (plus certain rollover
  interests) and B. Riley Financial, Inc. committed to contribute up to $429 million in
  cash to Vintage Rodeo, L.P., together constituting the equity financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]
- Debt commitment letters provided for an aggregate principal amount of $1,075 million
  in debt financing, consisting of two senior secured term loans of $800 million and
  $275 million respectively; the DEFM14A states completion of the Merger was "not
  subject to any financing condition," although funding of the debt and equity
  financing was itself subject to customary conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]

## What would have crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: a newly formed merger subsidiary (Vintage Rodeo Acquisition, Inc.)
  would merge into the Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary
  of Parent — the entire corporate entity, not a carved-out business unit, was the
  transaction's subject. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- People/organization: the Merger Agreement's Employee Matters provisions required that,
  for one year following closing, Company employees who remained employed by the
  surviving company would receive base pay and incentive-compensation opportunities "no
  less favorable in the aggregate" than immediately pre-closing, employee benefits "no
  less favorable in the aggregate," and severance protection for terminations within
  twelve months of closing — i.e., contractual continuity of the workforce and
  compensation structure was built into the deal rather than left silent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Employee Benefits"]
- Directors/officers: the Company's directors and executive officers were disclosed as
  having interests in the Merger "different from, or in addition to" stockholders
  generally (e.g., treatment of equity awards, tail D&O/fiduciary liability insurance to
  be maintained by the surviving company). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "Interests of Rent-A-Center's Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger"]

## Closing conditions and regulatory process
- Consummation was conditioned on, among other things, expiration or termination of the
  waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976 ("HSR
  Act") and receipt of other required antitrust consents. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals" / "Conditions to the Merger"]
- The Merger Agreement provided for a Company termination fee of $25.3 million payable
  to Parent under specified circumstances, and a reverse termination fee of $126.5
  million payable by Parent to the Company under specified circumstances, including
  financing-related failures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A "Termination Fees"]

## Stockholder vote and subsequent termination
- At a Special Meeting held September 18, 2018, the Company's stockholders approved the
  Merger Proposal (41,539,430 votes for versus 2,914,220 against) and the related
  advisory compensation proposal; the filing noted the Merger remained "subject to the
  satisfaction or waiver of certain closing conditions that have not yet been satisfied,
  including receipt of regulatory approvals." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-276693, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- The parties had received a "Second Request" from the Federal Trade Commission
  extending the HSR waiting period; the Merger Agreement set a deadline of 11:59 p.m.
  Eastern Time on December 17, 2018 for either party to notify the other of an election
  to extend the agreement's end date to March 17, 2019 due to the continued pendency of
  that waiting period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-354508, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- Rent-A-Center did not receive an extension notice from Vintage Capital by that
  deadline; the Company's Board determined not to exercise its own extension right and
  instead elected to exercise the Company's contractual right to terminate. On December
  18, 2018 Rent-A-Center terminated the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 17,
  2018, and notified Vintage Capital of the termination, noting Vintage Capital's
  obligation to pay the Company a reverse breakup fee of $126,500,000 within three
  business days. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-354508, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- The transaction did not close: the merger agreement was terminated before the
  effective time, and no merger consideration was ever paid or share conversion
  effected. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-354508, Form 8-K Item 1.02]

## Structural summary (deal-window only)
- A financial sponsor (Vintage Capital Management, via newly formed Parent and Merger
  Sub entities) agreed to acquire the entire Company for $15.00/share cash in a
  whole-company going-private merger, backed by committed equity and debt financing and
  with contractual employee-continuity terms; the deal was approved by stockholders in
  September 2018 but was never consummated — Rent-A-Center terminated the Merger
  Agreement in December 2018 after the acquirer did not seek to extend the deadline tied
  to an unresolved HSR antitrust review, triggering a $126.5 million reverse termination
  fee obligation on the acquirer's side. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-249885, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-354508, Form 8-K]
