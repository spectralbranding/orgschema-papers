# Structural sub-dossier — Capital Bank Financial Corp. combination with First Horizon National Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no information about the entity's subsequent operating or
> financial performance.

## Transaction and parties
- On May 3, 2017, First Horizon National Corporation ("First Horizon"), Capital Bank
  Financial Corp. ("Capital Bank Financial"), and Firestone Sub, Inc., a Delaware
  corporation and direct, wholly-owned subsidiary of First Horizon ("Merger Sub"),
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger providing for a two-step combination: a
  first-step merger of Merger Sub with and into Capital Bank Financial (with Capital
  Bank Financial surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of First Horizon), immediately
  followed, as part of a single integrated transaction, by a second-step merger of
  Capital Bank Financial with and into First Horizon (with First Horizon as the
  surviving corporation). [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A pp.1-2]
- Merger Sub was a Delaware corporation incorporated April 27, 2017 solely to effect the
  merger and, as of the DEFM14A date, had conducted no activities other than those
  incidental to its formation and the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Mergers"]
- Immediately following completion of the second-step merger (or such later time as
  First Horizon determined), Capital Bank Corporation, Capital Bank Financial's
  wholly-owned North Carolina state-chartered bank subsidiary, was to merge with and
  into First Tennessee Bank National Association, First Horizon's national-bank
  subsidiary, with First Tennessee Bank National Association as the surviving bank (the
  "bank merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A p.1]
- Consideration: each outstanding share of Capital Bank Financial common stock (other
  than specified excluded/dissenting shares) would be converted, at the holder's
  election subject to proration, into either cash or a number of First Horizon common
  shares equal to the per-share cash consideration divided by the First Horizon share
  closing price, i.e. a whole-company stock-and-cash reverse triangular merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A pp.1-2]
- Based on the July 25, 2017 closing price used in the joint proxy statement, the deal
  implied approximately 28.03% of First Horizon's post-merger outstanding common stock
  would be held by former Capital Bank Financial stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "Comparative Per Share Data"]
- The transaction required stockholder votes at separate special meetings of Capital
  Bank Financial (to adopt the merger agreement) and First Horizon (to approve issuance
  of First Horizon shares), and was conditioned on regulatory approvals from the Federal
  Reserve Board and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency, for which First
  Horizon and First Tennessee Bank National Association filed applications on June 16,
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]
- The merger agreement provided for a termination fee of $85 million payable by Capital
  Bank Financial to First Horizon under specified circumstances, including a change of
  board recommendation or pursuit of a competing acquisition proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "Termination Fee"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity, not a carved-out business unit, was the
  transaction's subject — Merger Sub merged into Capital Bank Financial, which then
  merged into First Horizon, so Capital Bank Financial ceased to exist as a separate
  legal entity and First Horizon became successor by operation of law. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A pp.1-2]
- Operating/legal-entity (bank) tier: Capital Bank Corporation (the operating bank
  subsidiary) was merged into First Tennessee Bank National Association, consolidating
  the acquired bank's charter and operations into the acquirer's existing bank subsidiary
  rather than continuing it as a standalone charter. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A p.1]
- Governance/people tier: the merger agreement provided that First Horizon's board of
  directors would be increased by two seats immediately after closing, one to be filled
  by R. Eugene Taylor (Capital Bank Financial's Chairman and CEO) and the other by Peter
  N. Foss (a Capital Bank Financial director) — a mechanism carrying a portion of the
  acquired company's board leadership across the boundary. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "The First Horizon Board of Directors After the Mergers"]
- Executive employment: First Horizon negotiated a new employment agreement with R.
  Eugene Taylor to supersede his existing Capital Bank Financial employment agreement as
  of the effective time; other named executive officers' existing employment agreements
  (providing severance on a qualifying termination within two years of the
  change-in-control) remained in force, with Capital Bank Financial permitted to pay
  contemplated cash severance amounts to eligible named executives immediately prior to
  the effective time, and CFO Christopher G. Marshall's noncompetition covenant amended
  (per the merger agreement) to not apply after the change in control while his
  nonsolicitation covenant would run two years post-termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A "Existing Employment Agreements between Capital Bank Financial and Certain Named Executive Officers"]

## What did not continue, per closing filings
- Capital Bank Financial's Class A common stock (NASDAQ: CBF) was to cease trading and
  be removed from listing on the NASDAQ Stock Market prior to market open on December 1,
  2017, with a Form 25 notification of removal from listing filed with the SEC, and
  Capital Bank Financial intended to file Form 15 certifications to end its Exchange Act
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Effective upon consummation of the merger (November 30, 2017), Capital Bank
  Financial's directors and executive officers ceased serving in those capacities (other
  than the two individuals who moved to the First Horizon board per the merger
  agreement, and Mr. Taylor's superseding employment agreement with First Horizon). [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- As of the effective time, Merger Sub's certificate of incorporation and bylaws became
  those of Capital Bank Financial; as of the second-step merger's effective time, Capital
  Bank Financial's own certificate of incorporation and bylaws ceased to be in effect by
  operation of law, and First Horizon's organizational documents (as successor to
  Capital Bank Financial) remained the operative charter and bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## Closing confirmation
- The merger, second-step merger, and bank merger (together, the "Transaction") closed
  effective November 30, 2017; the exchange agent was required to effect final
  allocation of merger consideration by December 7, 2017, with the aggregate cash
  consideration set at $410,535,300 and the balance of consideration paid in First
  Horizon common stock at a fixed exchange ratio of 2.1732. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Items 2.01, "Introductory Note"]
- First Horizon filed the closing Form 8-K as "First Horizon National Corporation, as
  successor to Capital Bank Financial Corp.," confirming Capital Bank Financial's
  separate corporate existence ended at the second-step merger's effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K signature block]
- First Horizon issued a press release on December 1, 2017 announcing completion of the
  Transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company, stock-and-cash reverse triangular merger absorbed Capital Bank
  Financial (and its bank subsidiary) entirely into First Horizon and its bank
  subsidiary: the acquired legal entity, its stock-exchange listing, and its
  independent SEC reporting status all terminated at or immediately after closing
  (November 30, 2017), while governance continuity was bridged narrowly — two former
  Capital Bank Financial directors, including its Chairman/CEO under a new employment
  agreement, joined the First Horizon board — and executive severance/employment terms
  were contractually addressed rather than left silent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-002734, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K]
