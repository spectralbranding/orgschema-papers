# Outcome sub-dossier — Skechers U.S.A. / HF Logistics-SKX distribution-facility venture, realized ~3-5-year record

## Public-reporting status (feasibility of an outcome record)
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports on EDGAR; its Form 10-K for the
  fiscal year ended December 31, 2014 was filed on 2015-02-27, providing a public primary
  record within the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- The venture (HF Logistics-SKX, LLC) is a variable interest entity of which the
  registrant is the primary beneficiary and which is consolidated into the registrant's
  consolidated financial statements; the registrant states it has both the power to direct
  the activities that most significantly impact the VIE and the associated economics. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]

## Realized record of the venture (primary-filing sourced)
- The distribution facility contemplated at closing was built and placed in operation: the
  registrant reports leasing the distribution center from the joint venture, HF
  Logistics-SKX, "that we formed with HF Logistics I, LLC in January 2010 for the purpose
  of building and operating the facility," with the lease expiring in 2031. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- The realized base rent on the completed facility is $940,695 per month, or approximately
  $11.3 million per year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- The venture's construction financing grew and was extended over the period: a $55.0
  million construction loan entered April 30, 2010 was modified on November 16, 2012 to
  increase borrowings to $80.0 million and extend maturity to October 31, 2015; proceeds
  repaid the $54.7 million original loan, an $18.3 million loan from HF to the JV, a $2.5
  million loan from Skechers RB, LLC, a $1.9 million deferred management fee to HF, and
  distributions of $0.9 million to each partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- As of December 31, 2014, $77.9 million was outstanding under that loan and its
  modification (included in current installments of long-term borrowings); as of December
  31, 2013 the balance was $78.9 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- The venture's consolidated carrying amounts: HF Logistics-SKX, LLC total assets of
  $125.6 million and total liabilities of $79.9 million at December 31, 2014 (versus total
  assets of $132.9 million and total liabilities of $81.2 million at December 31, 2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- HF Logistics-SKX, LLC made cash capital distributions of $3.7 million during 2014 and
  $3.2 million during 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
- The registrant recorded no impairment charge on its stores during 2014 or 2013, and
  reports no divestiture, dissolution, or write-down of the distribution-facility venture
  within the outcome window; the facility and the venture were retained and consolidated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]

## Items noted but not primary-verifiable
- Reported "non-controlling interest" earnings attributable to the registrant's joint
  venture partners collectively were $13.4 million (2014), $6.1 million (2013) and $1.0
  million (2012); the filing aggregates HF Logistics-SKX with the registrant's several
  international distribution/retail joint ventures, so the portion specific to the
  distribution-facility venture is not separately stated and is not isolated here. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~5-year outcome window the venture achieved its stated construction purpose:
  the ~1,820,000-square-foot distribution facility was completed and in operation, leased
  to the registrant through 2031 at approximately $11.3 million per year; the venture
  remained a consolidated VIE with total assets of $125.6 million at December 31, 2014,
  carried elevated project debt ($77.9 million outstanding on an $80.0 million modified
  construction loan) and made partner distributions, with no divestiture, impairment, or
  dissolution of the venture documented in the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-068511, Form 10-K]
