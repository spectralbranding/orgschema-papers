## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The outcome record is taken from SunPower Corporation's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 28, 2014 (filed February 24, 2015), the registrant's annual report falling roughly 4–4.5 years after the venture's mid-2010 formation and within the fixed 3–5-year window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Realized status of the venture
- The venture continued to operate under the name AUO SunPower Sdn. Bhd. ("AUOSP"), jointly owned and managed by SunPower and AU Optronics Corporation ("AUO"), each holding 50%; it had constructed a manufacturing facility in Malaysia (referred to as Fab 3A) that manufactures solar cells and sells them on a "cost-plus" basis to the Company and AUO. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The venture was retained (no divestiture) through the outcome window and remained an equity-method investment of the Company, which accounts for AUOSP under the equity method as a result of the shared-power arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Best-documented realized scalar (carrying value and equity-method result)
- The Company's investment in AUOSP had a carrying value of $191.7 million as of December 28, 2014 and $112.6 million as of December 29, 2013; the Company's maximum exposure to loss is limited to that carrying value. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The aggregate "Equity in earnings of unconsolidated investees" line (which includes AUOSP together with the Company's other equity-method investees) was $7.241 million for fiscal 2014, $3.872 million for fiscal 2013, and a loss of $0.515 million for fiscal 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Payments made by the Company to investees for products/services were $462.596 million (2014), $480.802 million (2013), and $606.301 million (2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Outstanding advance payments received from AUOSP totaled $167.2 million as of December 28, 2014 and $181.3 million as of December 29, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Documented developments at the product/process interface over time
- In 2012 the Company and AUO decided to postpone construction of a second manufacturing facility ("Fab 3B") contemplated under the venture agreement, and accordingly postponed further equity injections into AUOSP. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Fab 3B "fails to meet certain conditions required to continue to benefit from the tax ruling," and the venture was in discussions with the Malaysian government to extend the period by which buildout had to be completed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- AUOSP holds a $300 million secured loan facility whose covenants require the partners to make scheduled equity injections; in connection with the Fab 3B postponement AUOSP obtained a lender waiver that modified and extended the equity-injection schedule through December 31, 2014, and was in compliance with that covenant as of that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The Company and AUO each agreed to contribute additional amounts to AUOSP through fiscal 2015 amounting to $169.0 million per shareholder; the Company's future financing obligations to the venture through fiscal 2015 totaled $171.9 million as of December 28, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Documented developments at the process/organization interface over time
- The Company reported, among its risks, "problems managing the joint venture with AUO, whom we do not control and whose business objectives may be different from ours and may be inconsistent with our best interests." [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The Company continued to treat AUOSP as a variable interest in which it is not the primary beneficiary, accounting for it under the equity method rather than consolidating it, citing the shared decision-making arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 3–5-year window the venture remained in place and was retained (no divestiture), producing solar cells for both owners on a cost-plus basis, with the Company's equity-method carrying value rising to $191.7 million by December 28, 2014; the record also shows a curtailment of the planned expansion (Fab 3B postponed from 2012, with related loan-covenant waivers and an unresolved tax-ruling condition) and the Company's standing disclosure that it does not control the venture and that the partners' objectives may diverge. [SRC: SEC accession 0000867773-15-000013, Form 10-K FY2014]
