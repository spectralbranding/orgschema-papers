## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- SCANA Corporation (CIK 0000754737) filed its final Form 10-K on February 28, 2019 (for
  fiscal year 2018) and filed a Form 15-12B on March 1, 2019 to deregister its common
  stock under the Exchange Act; SCANA itself has filed no further periodic reports.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059208, Form 15-12B]
- SCANA's wholly owned utility subsidiary (SEC CIK 0000091882), formerly named "South
  Carolina Electric & Gas Co" through March 26, 2019, continued filing separately under
  the same CIK under the new name "Dominion Energy South Carolina, Inc." ("DESC"),
  including annual Forms 10-K for fiscal years 2019 through at least 2025; DESC is a
  wholly owned subsidiary of SCANA, which is in turn a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Dominion Energy, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 1/cover page]
- At February 15, 2023, DESC had 40,296,147 shares of common stock outstanding, all held
  by SCANA Corporation; DESC files under the SEC's reduced disclosure format available
  to certain wholly owned subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory (DESC, FY2021-FY2022)
- DESC's net income was $229 million for the year ended December 31, 2021 and $503
  million for the year ended December 31, 2022, an increase of $274 million, which the
  filing attributes primarily to "the absence of charges associated with the settlement
  of the South Carolina electric base rate case and a decrease in charges associated
  with litigation." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- DESC's operating revenues were $3,146 million (2021) and $3,783 million (2022), an
  increase of $637 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Analysis of Consolidated Operations"]
- "Impairment of assets and other charges" fell from $320 million in 2021 to $6 million
  in 2022. Of the 2021 amount, $249 million ($187 million after-tax) related to DESC's
  South Carolina electric base rate case settlement, including $237 million of
  regulatory assets tied to DESC's 2019 purchases of its own first mortgage bonds that
  became no longer probable of recovery under that settlement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Analysis of Consolidated Operations" / Note 12 "Other Regulatory Matters"]
- At December 31, 2022, DESC had approximately 2,300 employees, of whom approximately
  710 were covered by collective bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 1. Business]

## Documented post-close regulatory implementation
- Amounts previously collected from retail electric customers for the NND Project are
  being credited back to customers over an estimated 11-year period effective February
  2019, and proceeds from monetization of the Toshiba bankruptcy settlement are being
  refunded to electric customers over a 20-year period ending in 2039, both pursuant to
  "the SCANA Merger Approval Order." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 6/regulatory-assets table notes]
- In August 2020 DESC filed a retail electric base rate case; a comprehensive settlement
  agreement (non-fuel base rate increase of $62 million net of certain deferred-tax
  amortization, to $36 million net increase) was approved by the SCPSC in August 2021,
  under which DESC agreed not to file a new base rate case before July 1, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 12 "South Carolina Electric Base Rate Case"]
- The filing discloses that "Dominion Energy has commenced a comprehensive business
  review as announced in November 2022," the outcomes of which "may be subject to
  various risks and uncertainties" that "could have a material impact on DESC's future
  results of operations, cash flows and/or financial condition." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Forward-Looking Statements"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired parent (SCANA) deregistered and ceased public reporting in March 2019,
  but its regulated utility subsidiary continued filing separately under its own CIK
  (renamed Dominion Energy South Carolina, Inc.), providing a traceable 3-4-year
  post-close record: net income rose from $229 million (2021) to $503 million (2022) as
  one-time rate-case-settlement charges ($249 million pretax in 2021) dropped away, on
  operating revenue growth from $3,146 million to $3,783 million, while a 2020-filed
  electric base rate case reached a 2021 SCPSC settlement and the parent, Dominion
  Energy, disclosed in the same filing that it had begun a broader corporate "business
  review" in November 2022 with an acknowledged potential future impact on the
  subsidiary's results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-003304, Form 10-K FY2022]
