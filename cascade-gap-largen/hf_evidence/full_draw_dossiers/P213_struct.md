# Structural sub-dossier — Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc. / HRG Group, Inc. combination (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On February 24, 2018, HRG Group, Inc. ("HRG"), HRG SPV Sub I, Inc. ("Merger Sub 1"),
  HRG SPV Sub II, LLC ("Merger Sub 2"), and Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc. ("Spectrum")
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, as amended June 8, 2018 by Amendment No.
  1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the transaction, HRG was a holding company that conducted its operations
  principally through Spectrum, a majority-owned subsidiary; HRG beneficially owned
  approximately 62% of Spectrum's outstanding common stock as of the record date. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- Spectrum is described as a diversified global branded consumer products company that
  manufactures, markets and/or distributes products in approximately 160 countries
  through retailers, wholesalers, distributors, OEMs, construction companies and hearing
  aid professionals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured so that Merger Sub 1 merged with and into Spectrum,
  with Spectrum surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of HRG (the "First Merger"); a
  contemplated second-step merger of Spectrum into Merger Sub 2 was conditioned on a tax
  opinion ("Second Merger Opt-Out Condition") and did not proceed at closing — Spectrum
  remained the surviving corporation of the First Merger. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-18-217952, Form 8-K]
- Consideration was all-stock: each share of Spectrum common stock outstanding
  immediately before the effective time (other than treasury shares or shares held by
  HRG/its subsidiaries) converted into the right to receive one share of HRG common
  stock (the "Merger Exchange Ratio"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form
  DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-18-217952, Form 8-K]
- Immediately before the merger, HRG effected a reverse stock split at a variable "Share
  Combination Ratio" (based on relative market prices and HRG's net indebtedness, with a
  $200 million upward adjustment described as compensating pre-closing HRG stockholders
  for relinquishing control over Spectrum and for the value of certain tax attributes).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger closed on July 13, 2018 (the "Closing Date"). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-217952, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and controlling-interest changes at closing
- As part of an HRG Charter Amendment, HRG changed its name to "Spectrum Brands
  Holdings, Inc."; in connection with the Merger, the pre-merger Spectrum entity also
  changed its name — SEC filer records show the entity at CIK 1487730 (formerly named
  "Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc." from 2010 to July 10, 2018) renamed to "Spectrum
  Brands Legacy, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A; SEC
  EDGAR company filing history, https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001487730.json]
- The joint proxy statement states that "Spectrum will not have a controlling
  stockholder after the consummation of the Merger." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- Post-closing, pre-closing Spectrum stockholders and pre-closing HRG stockholders were
  expected to hold approximately 39% and 61%, respectively, of outstanding HRG common
  stock, with two HRG stockholders (Leucadia and Fortress) expected to hold
  approximately 14% and 10% respectively after closing; each had separately entered a
  voting agreement to support the transaction, as had HRG (as 62% Spectrum holder).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- HRG common stock continued to be listed on the NYSE and was expected to trade under
  the symbol "SPB" after the Merger, while Spectrum common stock was expected to no
  longer be listed on any exchange and Spectrum was expected to cease being a publicly
  traded company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- At the Closing Date, Spectrum common stock (which had traded under "SPB" on the NYSE)
  ceased trading on, and was removed from listing on, the NYSE. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-217952, Form 8-K]

## Management / organization tier at closing
- At the effective time, the HRG board of directors was to consist of six then-current
  Spectrum directors (Kenneth C. Ambrecht, Norman S. Matthews, David M. Maura, Terry L.
  Polistina, Hugh R. Rovit and Joseph S. Steinberg) plus one independent director
  designated by Leucadia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- The officers of Spectrum immediately before the effective time were to become the
  officers of HRG, with the post-merger executive team led by David M. Maura (Executive
  Chairman and Chief Executive Officer), Douglas L. Martin (Executive Vice President and
  Chief Financial Officer), Nathan E. Fagre (Senior Vice President, General Counsel and
  Secretary) and Stacey L. Neu (Senior Vice President of Human Resources). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]

## Stated rationale (as disclosed, neutral)
- Among the factors the HRG board cited for the transaction: the expectation that
  simplifying the holding-company structure and eliminating the need to comply with two
  separate public reporting companies' legal and administrative obligations would reduce
  overhead and cost; the expectation of eliminating a "sum of the parts" trading
  discount; and the value to HRG stockholders of removing the trading overhang on
  Spectrum common stock associated with HRG's majority ownership. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy separately discussed, as a factor supporting HRG's determination, Spectrum's
  then-pending divestiture of its global batteries business and global appliances
  business (together, the "GBA Businesses") to a third party, which the HRG board
  believed would allow realization of value from certain tax attributes; this divestiture
  was a separate, parallel process from the Merger itself and had not closed as of the
  proxy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction converted an existing majority-ownership holding-company relationship
  (HRG holding ~62% of Spectrum) into a full stock-for-stock combination in which the
  top-level public filer (HRG) took the "Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc." name and NYSE
  ticker "SPB" while the pre-merger operating filer (old Spectrum) survived as a wholly
  owned, no-longer-listed subsidiary under a new name; Spectrum's pre-merger board and
  executive officers were carried forward intact as the post-merger governance and
  management of the renamed public parent, and a separate, already-pending divestiture
  of Spectrum's batteries/appliances businesses proceeded alongside the Merger rather
  than as part of its terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-190106, Form DEFM14A; SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-217952, Form 8-K]
