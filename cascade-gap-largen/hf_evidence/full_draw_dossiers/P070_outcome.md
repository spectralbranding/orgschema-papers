## Outcome window and reporting status
- The fixed outcome window is the registrant's ~2014–2016 record; the primary source is Equity One's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2015, filed February 26, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K cover]
- Equity One remained an SEC-reporting, NYSE-listed company throughout the window, filing consolidated audited financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K cover]

## Realized record of the joint-venture interest
- The minority (noncontrolling) interest arising from the arrangement was carried at $206.1 million, reflected as permanent equity on the consolidated balance sheet as of December 31, 2015; it was the sole component of noncontrolling interests in consolidated entities at that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K note "Noncontrolling Interests"]
- The counterparty's ability to participate in earnings was limited to distributions payable on its Class A joint-venture shares, so earnings attributed to the noncontrolling interest equaled distributions made to it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K note "CapCo"]
- In January 2016, the counterparty (LIH) exercised its redemption right with respect to all of its outstanding Class A Shares in the joint venture, and Equity One elected to satisfy the redemption through the issuance of approximately 11.4 million shares of its common stock to LIH. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K subsequent-events / "CapCo" note]
- As a result, Equity One now owns 100% of CapCo, LIH holds no remaining interests in the Company or its subsidiaries, and David Fischel resigned from Equity One's Board of Directors in connection with the termination of LIH's Board nomination right. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K "CapCo" note]

## Realized operating record (product / process interface)
- As of December 31, 2015, Equity One had invested an aggregate of approximately $103.5 million in active development or redevelopment projects and anticipated an additional $147.6 million to complete them, including $103.0 million to complete the redevelopment of Serramonte Shopping Center (one of the California properties held through the arrangement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K MD&A]
- For the year ended December 31, 2015, six properties — including Serramonte Shopping Center — totaling 1.6 million square feet were moved out of the same-property pool because they were undergoing redevelopment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K MD&A]

## Best-documented outcome scalar
- Equity One reported total net income of $75,467 thousand (approximately $75.5 million) for the year ended December 31, 2015, compared with $61,091 thousand for 2014 and $88,657 thousand for 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K Consolidated Statements of Comprehensive Income]
- No goodwill or asset impairment, write-down, or divestiture of the CapCo portfolio is reported for the arrangement within the window; the noncontrolling interest was resolved through the January 2016 stock-for-shares redemption described above rather than through a loss event. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K notes "Noncontrolling Interests" and "CapCo"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2014–2016 window, the acquired California shopping-center portfolio remained consolidated and under Equity One's management, with the associated $206.1 million noncontrolling interest carried as permanent equity through December 31, 2015; the counterparty's minority stake was then fully unwound in January 2016 when LIH redeemed all its Class A joint-venture shares for approximately 11.4 million Equity One common shares, leaving Equity One as 100% owner of CapCo and prompting the counterparty-designated director's resignation, while the flagship Serramonte asset was in active redevelopment ($103.0 million remaining to complete). Equity One reported total net income of approximately $75.5 million for the year ended December 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001042810-16-000148, Form 10-K MD&A, "CapCo" note, and Consolidated Statements of Comprehensive Income]
