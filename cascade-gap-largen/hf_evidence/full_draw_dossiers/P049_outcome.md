## Outcome window and source
- The fixed outcome window is fiscal years ~2011-2013; the realized record is read from The
  Goodyear Tire & Rubber Company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012
  (filed 2013-02-12), which reports three-year comparatives (2012 / 2011 / 2010). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Continuation of the ventures (feasibility-relevant)
- As of the FY2012 report the global alliance with SRI remained in place and the ventures
  continued to operate: the Company still owned 75% and SRI 25% of Goodyear Dunlop Tires
  Europe B.V. ("GDTE") and Goodyear Dunlop Tires North America, Ltd. ("GDTNA"), with GDTE
  owning and operating substantially all of the Company's tire businesses in Western Europe
  and GDTNA owning the Dunlop brand and operating certain related businesses in North
  America; no dissolution, divestiture or exercise of SRI's exit rights was reported within
  the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- SRI's continuing minority stake was reflected in consolidated results as "Minority
  Shareholders' Net Income" of $25 million in 2012 and $74 million in 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- GDTE continued to operate as a distinct financing entity, with EUR 250 million of its own
  6.75% senior notes due 2019 outstanding at December 31, 2012 and its own French
  accounts-receivable securitization facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Realized financial record (best-documented scalars)
- Company-wide: net sales were $20,992 million (2012) versus $22,767 million (2011); Goodyear
  net income was $212 million (2012) versus $343 million (2011); Goodyear net income
  available to common shareholders was $183 million (2012, $0.74 diluted) versus $321 million
  (2011, $1.26 diluted). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Europe, Middle East and Africa Tire ("EMEA") segment — the region operated principally
  through GDTE — recorded net sales of $6,884 million (2012) versus $8,040 million (2011), and
  segment operating income of $252 million (2012) versus $627 million (2011) and $319 million
  (2010): a decline of roughly 60% year over year in the venture-anchored region. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- 2012 Goodyear net income included net after-tax charges of $325 million ($1.32 per share
  diluted) due to rationalization charges (including accelerated depreciation and asset
  write-offs), debt-redemption and credit-facility charges, and labor claims relating to a
  previously closed facility; company-wide rationalization charges were $175 million in 2012
  versus $103 million in 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Documented friction at the operations / organization interface (over time)
- The Company announced the closure of one of its facilities in Amiens, France (in the EMEA /
  GDTE region), described as reducing high-cost manufacturing capacity; the announced closure
  "remains subject to consultation with the European Central Works Council, our employee
  representatives and other relevant bodies in the EMEA region," and as of the FY2012 report
  the consultation process had "just begun" and the Company was "not able to currently
  estimate the timing" of the outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Company-wide the Company reported approximately 69,000 employees at December 31, 2012,
  including approximately 36,000 covered under collective bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Items noted but not separately disclosed
- The FY2012 report does not disclose a stand-alone net income / loss figure for GDTE or
  GDTNA as separate ventures; the venture-region trajectory is observable only through the
  consolidated EMEA segment results and the minority-interest line, and any venture-specific
  scalar beyond those is recorded here as not separately disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)] [UNVERIFIED: stand-alone GDTE/GDTNA venture net income within the window]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2011-2013 window the joint ventures continued intact (75% Company / 25% SRI,
  no dissolution, divestiture or exit-right exercise), but the venture-anchored EMEA region
  deteriorated sharply — segment operating income fell from $627 million (2011) to $252
  million (2012) on lower sales — while company-wide Goodyear net income fell from $343
  million to $212 million amid $175 million of rationalization charges, and the Company
  announced the closure of an Amiens, France facility that remained subject to European
  works-council consultation with uncertain timing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-13-000902, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
