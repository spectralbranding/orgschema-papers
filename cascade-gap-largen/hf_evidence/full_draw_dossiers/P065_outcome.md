## Realized disposition of the interest
- On September 12, 2014 the registrant completed the sale of its 50% non-operating interest
  in the Decker Mine to an affiliate of Ambre Energy North America, Inc. ("Ambre Energy"),
  now known as Lighthouse Resources Inc.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Under the agreement, Ambre Energy "acquired our 50% interest in the Decker Mine and
  related assets and assumed all reclamation and other liabilities, giving Ambre Energy
  100% ownership of the Decker Mine," and fully replaced the registrant's $66.7 million in
  outstanding reclamation and lease bonds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- As a result of the sale the registrant released the related $72.2 million asset
  retirement obligation and, for the year ended December 31, 2014, recognized a pre-tax
  gain on sale of the Decker Mine interest of $74.3 million ($74,262 thousand).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The disclosed components of that gain include an ARO liability released of $72.175 million,
  a "Millennium Bulk Terminals option" of $5.0 million, and a write-off of $3.183 million of
  prior service costs related to the Decker Mine pension plan; the co-owner (Ambre Energy)
  also assumed the obligations under the Decker Mine pension plan.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Documented effects at the operating/economic interfaces
- The registrant states that revenue decreased for the year ended December 31, 2015 versus
  2014 "primarily due to the sale of the Decker Mine in September 2014," and that cost of
  product sold likewise decreased due to the sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Port access rights acquired in connection with the sale of the Decker Mine interest were
  subsequently impaired: in the fourth quarter of 2015 the registrant recorded non-cash
  impairments of $52.2 million related to port access rights (and $6.0 million related to
  its equity investment in GPT). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Entity-level realized scalars (in-window annual report)
- The registrant reported a consolidated net loss of $18.5 million ($18,508 thousand) for
  fiscal 2014, a net loss of $47.3 million ($47,329 thousand) for fiscal 2015, and a net
  loss of $48.3 million ($48,297 thousand) for fiscal 2016.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Fiscal 2015 additionally included a non-cash goodwill impairment of $33.4 million at the
  8400 Btu Cordero Rojo Mine and a valuation allowance of $111.8 million on deferred tax
  assets; fiscal 2016 included $4.5 million of debt restructuring costs related to Exchange
  Offers completed in the fourth quarter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed outcome window the registrant's 50% joint-venture interest in the Decker
  Mine was fully divested (September 12, 2014) to the co-owner's parent, which took 100%
  ownership and assumed all reclamation, bonding, and pension liabilities; the registrant
  recognized a $74.3 million pre-tax gain but subsequently impaired $52.2 million of the
  port-access rights received as part of the consideration, and reported consecutive
  entity-level net losses of $47.3 million (2015) and $48.3 million (2016).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-009687, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
