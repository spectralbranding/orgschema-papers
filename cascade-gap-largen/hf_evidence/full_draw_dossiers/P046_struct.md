# Structural sub-dossier — Swift Energy Company development joint ventures (closing structure)

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Swift Energy Company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2010 (filed 2011-02-24); it is an annual report that DESCRIBES the
  company's development joint ventures rather than being the venture-formation document.
  The venture terms are therefore sourced additionally from the contemporaneous 2009
  earnings-release Forms 8-K in which the company first announced each agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The 10-K reports two development joint ventures with "large independent oil and gas
  producers" entered into over the prior fifteen months, covering acreage in the
  company's AWP (South Texas) and Burr Ferry (Louisiana) fields, described as allowing
  the company "to both monetize a portion of our significant acreage positions ... and
  share costs of development drilling in these fields in order to accelerate their
  development." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- South Texas venture: on November 2, 2009 the company announced a "joint exploration
  and development agreement with Petrohawk Energy Corporation," under which the company
  retained a 50% interest in an approximately 26,000-acre prospect area in its AWP field
  covering leasehold beneath the Olmos formation (including the Eagle Ford Shale). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-09-024400, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99 press release]
- Louisiana venture: in the second quarter of 2009 (announced in the Q2 2009 earnings
  release) the company "entered into a joint venture agreement with a large independent
  active in the area for development and exploitation in and around the Burr Ferry field
  in Vernon Parish, LA"; the partner is not named in the retrieved filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-09-000027, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release] [UNVERIFIED: Burr Ferry venture partner identity]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- The customer-facing asset moved as a fractional interest, not whole: in the South
  Texas venture the company "leased a 50% working interest in approximately 26,000 gross
  acres to the joint venture partner" and retained a 50% working interest in the same
  acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- In the Louisiana venture the company, "as fee mineral owner, leased a 50% working
  interest in approximately 33,623 gross acres to the joint venture partner," retaining a
  50% working interest plus its fee mineral royalty rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-09-000027, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- Consideration received at signing (South Texas): the company received approximately
  $26 million in cash upon signing, and Petrohawk agreed to fund approximately $13
  million of the company's capital expenditures within the first twelve months (a carried
  amount, with any unused balance to be paid to the company as cash consideration). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-09-024400, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99 press release]
- Consideration received (Louisiana): the 10-K states the company received approximately
  $4.2 million related to the Burr Ferry transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]

## Operations and people at closing (what did NOT move with the asset)
- The producing operation and operatorship were split by phase rather than transferred:
  for the Eagle Ford venture wells "these wells are operated by our partner during the
  drilling and completion phase" and "Swift Energy assumes operations when the wells are
  placed on production." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- In the Louisiana venture the company's initial venture wells were non-operated by the
  company: "During 2010 the Company drilled and completed the first two non-operated
  wells in this joint venture." [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- The retrieved filings describe no transfer of employees, management, or organization to
  a partner or to a separate venture entity; the ventures are structured as contractual
  working-interest/cost-sharing agreements over acreage, and the company continued to run
  its own workforce and (for the Eagle Ford wells) resume operatorship at production. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)] [UNVERIFIED: any separate venture legal entity or staffing]

## Bridging / operating arrangements at closing
- The partner's agreement to carry approximately $13 million of the company's development
  capital within the first twelve months (South Texas) is the closing-time mechanism that
  bridged the split ownership and shared the development cost of the retained-plus-conveyed
  acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-09-024400, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99 press release]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Each transaction conveyed a 50% working interest in a defined acreage block to a partner
  producer for cash plus (in the South Texas case) a carried capital contribution, while
  the company retained the other 50% working interest, its mineral rights, and (for the
  Eagle Ford wells) operatorship at the production phase; no people, management, or
  organization were disclosed as transferring, and the ventures were structured as
  contractual working-interest and cost-sharing agreements over acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-11-000012, Form 10-K (FY2010)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-09-024400, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99 press release] [SRC: SEC accession 0000351817-09-000027, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
