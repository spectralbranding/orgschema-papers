## Whether the acquirer separately discloses the acquired business
- Welltower's Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2021 does not report a distinct "Quality Care Properties" or "QCP" reporting segment; its only reference to Quality Care Properties is the Exhibit Index's incorporation by reference of the April 2018 Agreement and Plan of Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-22-000013, Form 10-K, Exhibit Index]
- Welltower's Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2022 separately reports NOI concentration by "relationship," including a "ProMedica" relationship representing 10% of net operating income in 2022 (12% in 2021, 11% in 2020) and, for the Triple-net segment specifically, approximately 26% of Triple-net segment revenues and 4% of total revenues from ProMedica in 2022 — this is disclosure at the operator-relationship level, not a distinct former-QCP entity or segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-23-000010, Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A]

## Realized record of the ProMedica operator relationship (portfolio/relationship level)
- In December 2022, ProMedica relinquished to Welltower its 15% interest in 147 skilled nursing facilities previously owned by the Welltower/ProMedica joint venture, in exchange for a lease modification that relieved ProMedica of its lease obligation on those 147 properties; the joint venture's remaining 58 assisted living and memory care properties continued to be operated by ProMedica under an amended lease, backed by the existing guaranty. [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-23-000010, Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A]
- Concurrently, Welltower and Integra Healthcare Properties ("Integra") entered into master leases covering the 147-facility skilled nursing portfolio, with approximately 15 regional operators expected to sublease from Integra to operate the properties; in December 2022 and January 2023 Welltower sold Integra a 15% ownership interest in 85 of those facilities, with Integra expected to acquire an interest in the remaining 62 assets during 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-23-000010, Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A]
- Reputable press corroborates the same operator transition, describing it as the formation of a joint venture between Welltower and Integra Health and a plan to transition skilled nursing assets then operated by ProMedica. [SRC: https://welltower.investorroom.com/2022-11-07-Welltower-Announces-the-Formation-of-Joint-Venture-with-Integra-Health-and-Plan-to-Transition-Skilled-Nursing-Assets-Currently-Operated-by-ProMedica]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because QCP was merged whole into a Welltower acquisition subsidiary and deregistered from SEC reporting in August 2018, no separate public 3-5-year financial record exists for QCP as a standalone entity, and Welltower's subsequent Form 10-K filings do not report a distinct former-QCP segment; the entity-level outcome is therefore not publicly observable and is coded as such rather than inferred from Welltower's consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-049804, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-22-000013, Form 10-K]
- At the operator-relationship level, Welltower's own FY2022 Form 10-K separately discloses that the ProMedica-related joint venture holding the post-acute/skilled-nursing real estate underwent a documented operator transition in December 2022/January 2023: 147 skilled nursing facilities moved from ProMedica's operation to Integra Healthcare Properties and roughly 15 regional sub-operators, while 58 assisted living/memory-care properties in the same joint venture remained with ProMedica. [SRC: SEC accession 0000766704-23-000010, Form 10-K, Item 7 MD&A]
