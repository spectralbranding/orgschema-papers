## Later corporate record under the same CIK
- EDGAR's registrant-name history for CIK 0001373561 records the entity's name as
  "NewStar Financial, Inc." until September 11, 2018, and as "First Eagle Private
  Credit, LLC" thereafter. [SRC: SEC EDGAR submissions data, CIK 0001373561, https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001373561.json]
- Under the name "First Eagle Private Credit, LLC," the entity (together with roughly
  thirty affiliated First Eagle investment-management and fund entities, including
  "First Eagle Alternative Capital BDC, Inc." and "First Eagle Private Credit Advisors,
  LLC") was a co-applicant on a Form 40-APP application filed August 13, 2020 (amended
  through 2020) seeking an Investment Company Act exemptive order permitting certain
  joint transactions among the named funds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-218989, Form 40-APP]
- That Form 40-APP is a regulatory application for co-investment exemptive relief across
  a fund complex; it does not report NewStar's/First Eagle Private Credit's own revenue,
  income, assets, or operating results, and is not a periodic financial disclosure of the
  acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-218989, Form 40-APP]
- "First Eagle Alternative Capital BDC, Inc." (CIK 0001464963), which files annual
  reports on Form 10-K, is a separate, independently registered business development
  company (formerly organized under a different name) that predates the NewStar
  transaction and is advised by a First Eagle affiliate; its 10-K reports that BDC's own
  investment portfolio and results, not a segment specific to the entity acquired from
  NewStar's stockholders in the 2017 merger. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0001464963]
