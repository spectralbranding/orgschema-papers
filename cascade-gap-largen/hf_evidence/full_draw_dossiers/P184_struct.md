# Structural sub-dossier — Alliance Holdings GP, L.P. / Alliance Resource Partners, L.P. transaction (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Alliance Holdings GP, L.P. ("AHGP") and Alliance Resource Partners, L.P. ("ARLP")
  entered into a Simplification Agreement dated February 22, 2018, under which, through
  a series of transactions, AHGP became a wholly owned subsidiary of ARLP and each
  outstanding AHGP common unit was canceled and converted into the right to receive ARLP
  common units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- The merger vehicle was Wildcat GP Merger Sub, LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Alliance
  GP, LLC, "AGP," AHGP's general partner), which merged with and into AHGP; AHGP survived
  as a Delaware limited partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- The exchange ratio, based on the disclosed assumptions, was approximately 1.478 ARLP
  common units for each AHGP common unit; following consummation, ARLP was estimated to
  be owned approximately 33.1% by pre-transaction ARLP unitholders and approximately
  66.9% by former AHGP unitholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- The transactions closed May 31, 2018, approved by written consent of holders of
  approximately 68% of AHGP common units outstanding as of the April 25, 2018 record
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038597, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- Controlling intent: Alliance Resource Management GP, LLC ("MGP") remained the general
  partner of ARLP after the transactions, with the proxy stating there would be "no
  change in the management and operation of ARLP's business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- Joseph W. Craft III was, prior to closing, President, Chief Executive Officer and
  Chairman of AGP (AHGP's general partner) and also President, Chief Executive Officer
  and a Director of MGP (ARLP's general partner) — the controlling executive role was
  already common to both entities before the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: AHGP's own cash-generating assets, prior to the transaction, consisted
  only of its ownership interests in ARLP; AHGP held no independent operating product or
  processes of its own to transfer — the transaction consolidated a holding-company
  ownership layer rather than moving an operating business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- The customer-facing product and the operational processes that produce it (coal mining
  and marketing) were, at closing, already held and run at the level of ARLP's operating
  subsidiaries — Alliance Resource Operating Partners, L.P. ("AROP") and Alliance Coal,
  LLC — and the simplification transactions consolidated general-partner and managing
  member interests in those subsidiaries directly into ARLP rather than altering how
  those subsidiaries' operations were conducted. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- People/organization: two of MGP's then-current directors (Craft and Wilson M. Torrence)
  were also directors of AGP, and three of MGP's executive officers were also executive
  officers of AGP, prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]

## Bridging mechanism at closing
- The simplification agreement provided for a defined sequence of distributions and
  contributions occurring immediately before, simultaneous with, and immediately
  following the merger's effective time, by which general partner interests in AROP and
  a managing member interest in Alliance Coal (previously held indirectly by ARLP's
  general partner, MGP, through MGP II, LLC) were routed to AHGP and then contributed by
  Alliance Resource GP, LLC ("SGP") to ARLP in exchange for newly issued ARLP common
  units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]
- A separate Contribution Agreement dated July 28, 2017 governed related consideration to
  SGP; a First Amendment to that Contribution Agreement, executed at closing on May 31,
  2018, corrected the number of ARLP common units issuable to SGP to preserve proportionate
  economics. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038597, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]
- The AGP board's stated reasons for the transactions included eliminating "the
  significant management and operating personnel time and the related duplicative third
  party service costs required to maintain two public companies" and eliminating
  "potential conflicts of interest that may arise as a result of a person being an
  officer of the general partner of AHGP and of the general partner of ARLP." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A]

## Listing and registration status at closing
- AHGP common units were removed from listing on the NASDAQ Global Select Market and
  deregistered under the Exchange Act at closing: NASDAQ filed a Form 25 with the SEC on
  May 31, 2018, and AHGP common units were suspended from trading prior to the opening of
  trading on June 1, 2018. ARLP common units continued to be traded on NASDAQ under the
  symbol "ARLP." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038597, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction consolidated a holding-company ownership layer (AHGP, whose only
  assets were equity interests in ARLP) into the entity it held interests in (ARLP),
  through a merger plus a sequenced set of distribution and contribution steps, while the
  underlying operating subsidiaries (AROP, Alliance Coal) and ARLP's general-partner
  management (MGP, led by the same executive who had also led AHGP's general partner)
  continued unchanged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-003237, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038597, Form 8-K]
