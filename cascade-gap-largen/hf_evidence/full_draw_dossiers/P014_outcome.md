## Public-reporting status in the outcome window

## Realized financial record (audited, FY2022 vs FY2021)
- Revenue was $1,662 million for the year ended December 31, 2022, up four percent from $1,602 million in 2021; renewal revenue rose to $1,203 million (from $1,103 million) while real-estate first-year revenue fell 27 percent to $184 million amid a challenging home-seller market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 Results of Operations]
- Net income was $71 million for 2022, down 45 percent from $128 million in 2021; income before income taxes fell to $93 million from $168 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 Results of Operations]
- Restructuring charges were $20 million in 2022, up from $3 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 Results of Operations]
- Total shareholders' equity was $61 million as of December 31, 2022, compared with $2 million as of December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 Financial Position]

## Documented product/process-interface disruption over time
- The entity had built a technology platform, Streem (augmented reality, computer vision and machine learning), which it was integrating into its core home-services business and also licensed to third-party business customers as software-as-a-service. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 1 Business]
- During the third quarter of 2022 the entity identified indicators of potential impairment for the Streem reporting unit and performed an interim discounted-cash-flow analysis as of September 30, 2022, determining that the reporting unit's carrying amount exceeded its fair value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Critical Accounting Policies — Goodwill and Intangible Assets]
- A $14 million impairment charge was recognized in the third quarter of 2022, comprising the remaining net carrying amount of Streem's goodwill of $9 million and intangibles of $5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 / Notes]

## Documented operating trajectory and organizational change
- The number of home service plans declined to 2.13 million as of December 31, 2022 from 2.21 million a year earlier (a four percent reduction, versus a two percent reduction in 2021), while the customer retention rate rose to 75.7 percent from 74.2 percent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 7 Results of Operations]
- As of December 31, 2022 the entity had 1,712 employees (1,660 in the United States and 52 abroad, primarily in India providing technology services), none represented by a labor union, operating under a virtual-first work environment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Item 1 Human Capital Management]
- A leadership change occurred in the window: William C. Cobb entered an employment agreement with the entity dated May 19, 2022, and signed the FY2022 Form 10-K as Chairman of the Board, Director and Chief Executive Officer (principal executive officer). [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-23-000062, Form 10-K (FY2022), Exhibit Index item 10.21 and signature page]
