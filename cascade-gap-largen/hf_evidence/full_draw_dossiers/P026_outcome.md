## Documented disruption at the product/operations interface
- The company's Vølund & Other Renewable segment was the locus of severe operating losses: adjusted gross profit (loss) in that segment increased $247.2 million year over year to $9.0 million, implying a prior-period loss of roughly $238 million on its European renewable / waste-to-energy engineering-and-supply contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001630805-20-000026, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- In response, the company changed its approach to bidding on and executing renewable energy contracts — refocusing on engineering and supplying its core waste-to-energy technology and electing to limit bidding on additional renewable energy contracts that involved its European resources while it worked through then-existing contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001630805-20-000026, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Documented disruption at the operations/organization interface
- Substantial doubt about the company's ability to continue as a going concern was disclosed, tied to a refinancing that had to be completed on or prior to May 11, 2020; management stated it was taking all prudent actions to address the substantial doubt. [SRC: SEC accession 0001630805-20-000026, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The recapitalization was backstopped by an outside financial party: pursuant to Amendment No. 20 to its Amended Credit Agreement the company entered into a backstop letter agreement with B. Riley Financial, Inc. (and affiliates) to support the refinancing/refinancing raise; the company's continuation was contingent on that backstop and on raising new capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001630805-20-000026, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The capital structure was reset during the window: the company completed a Rights Offering on July 23, 2019 and a one-for-ten reverse stock split on July 24, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001630805-20-000026, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Portfolio actions within the window
