## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant (CIK 0001676479) did not deregister; it continued filing annual
  reports on Form 10-K after the transaction, including a Form 10-K for the fiscal year
  ended December 31, 2022, filed March 3, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- The Form 10-K's consolidated financial statements report the registrant and its bank
  subsidiary on a combined basis; they do not present a separate segment or line-item
  breakout isolating results attributable specifically to any one prior business
  combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]

## Scale at end of the outcome-window fiscal year
- As of December 31, 2022, the registrant had total assets of $3.1 billion, total
  deposits of $2.7 billion, total net loans of $2.3 billion, and shareholders' equity
  of $354.2 million, operating through 23 bank locations with 397 associates. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- Total assets were $3,133.0 million at December 31, 2021 and $3,117.2 million at
  December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]

## Realized earnings record
- Net income was $39.017 million for the year ended December 31, 2022, compared with
  $48.677 million for the year ended December 31, 2021 — a 19.8% decrease. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- Total noninterest income decreased $18.2 million to $24.5 million (17.8% of total
  revenue) for 2022, compared with $42.7 million (30.3% of total revenue) for 2021, a
  decline the filing attributes to lower mortgage-banking and "Tri-Net" fee income. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- The efficiency ratio (noninterest expense to net interest income plus noninterest
  income) was 57.51% for 2022 versus 54.94% for 2021; adjusted for Tri-Net losses,
  operational losses, and an executive-incentive reversal, the 2022 efficiency ratio
  was stated as 56.13%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]

## Documented operating disruption within the window
- In 2022 the Company transferred $105.5 million of Tri-Net (commercial real estate
  loan origination-for-sale) loans from held-for-sale to held-for-investment, recording
  a $1.3 million realized loss and a $2.4 million unrealized loss on the transfer
  (partly offset by a $1.6 million hedge gain); it paused further Tri-Net
  loan-origination commitments in Q3 2022, citing rate volatility. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- 2022 noninterest expense included $1.5 million of operational losses the bank was
  seeking to recover; the filing states executives "voluntarily forego[ed] their annual
  bonuses which totaled $0.9 million in an effort to establish a culture of operational
  excellence following two operational losses and losses in the Company's Tri-Net
  division." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
- Goodwill on the consolidated balance sheet was $41.068 million at both December 31,
  2022 and December 31, 2021; the Company stated that for both fiscal years qualitative
  factors indicated no indicators of goodwill impairment, and no quantitative
  impairment test was performed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year window, the registrant remained an SEC-reporting company
  with growing balance-sheet scale ($3.1 billion in total assets, 23 locations by
  year-end 2022) and no recorded goodwill impairment, but reported a 19.8% year-over-
  year decline in net income (2022 vs. 2021) driven substantially by lower mortgage and
  Tri-Net fee income, alongside a documented pause in Tri-Net loan originations,
  recorded Tri-Net transfer losses, and disclosed operational losses that prompted
  executives to forgo incentive compensation. The Form 10-K's consolidated presentation
  does not separately attribute any of this record to a specific prior business
  combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-006036, Form 10-K]
