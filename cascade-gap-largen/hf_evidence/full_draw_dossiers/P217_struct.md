# Structural sub-dossier — Integrated Device Technology, Inc. merger with Renesas Electronics Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing

- Integrated Device Technology, Inc. ("IDT"), a Delaware corporation developing
  system-level semiconductor solutions — leading products in RF, high-performance
  timing, memory interface, real-time interconnect, optical interconnect, wireless
  charging, and smart sensors for communications, computing, consumer, automotive and
  industrial customers — agreed to a merger with Renesas Electronics Corporation, a
  Japanese corporation (kabushiki kaisha) listed on the Tokyo Stock Exchange (code 6723)
  and described as a leading global supplier of microcontrollers and a leader in analog,
  power, and SoC products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- The Agreement and Plan of Merger was dated September 10, 2018, between Renesas
  ("Parent") and IDT, and was subsequently joined by Chapter Two Company ("Merger Sub")
  pursuant to a Joinder Agreement dated September 17, 2018. Merger Sub was a Delaware
  corporation and a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, formed on September 12,
  2018 solely to engage in the transaction, with no other business activity.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a forward merger: Merger Sub merged with and into
  IDT, with IDT continuing as the surviving corporation and a direct wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]
- Each outstanding share of IDT common stock (other than excluded/dissenting shares) was
  converted into the right to receive $49.00 per share in cash, without interest; the
  aggregate merger consideration was approximately $6.3 billion, funded through a
  combination of third-party debt financing and Parent's cash on hand.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 2.01 and Item 5.01]
- The Merger required expiration of the HSR Act waiting period (expired October 22,
  2018), clearance from the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States
  (CFIUS), and antitrust/competition approvals in the PRC, South Korea and elsewhere;
  CFIUS clearance (no unresolved national security concerns) was announced March 22,
  2019, with closing then expected March 29, 2019, subject to other customary
  conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-083999, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]
- The Merger closed on March 29, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]

## Legal vehicle and controlling-intent tier at closing

- Upon consummation, IDT's certificate of incorporation was amended and restated in its
  entirety, and Parent, as sole stockholder, amended and restated IDT's bylaws by
  written consent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 5.03]
- At the effective time, each of IDT's seven then-serving directors (Ken Kannappan,
  Selena LaCroix, Umesh Padval, Gordon Parnell, Robert Rango, Norman Taffe, and Gregory
  Waters) ceased serving; the board was reduced to two members, Sailesh Chittipeddi and
  Hisanori Kawahara, both designated by Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- IDT requested that Nasdaq suspend trading in its common stock and file a notification
  removing the stock from listing and registration; Nasdaq filed this removal
  notification the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 3.01; SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-19-000166, Form 25-NSE]

## Product, process, and people tiers

- The whole company — including its product lines across RF, timing, memory interface,
  interconnect, wireless charging, and sensor solutions — crossed into Parent's
  ownership as a single going concern; the proxy statement does not describe any
  carve-out of specific product lines or business units from the transaction.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- As of the proxy statement's date, none of IDT's executive officers had entered into
  any agreement with Parent or its affiliates regarding employment with, or equity
  participation in, the surviving corporation or its affiliates; the proxy notes that
  such arrangements might or might not be negotiated before or after closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger Agreement provided that Parent would, or would cause the surviving
  corporation to, provide each continuing employee with certain compensation and
  benefits for a period commencing at the effective time and ending on the first
  anniversary of the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- Existing Executive Agreements (pre-dating the Merger Agreement) provided severance-type
  protections — continued health/life insurance, accelerated equity vesting on a
  qualifying termination, and a golden-parachute excise-tax cutback mechanism — rather
  than newly negotiated forward retention contracts with Parent; the Merger Agreement
  separately gave the Company discretion to accelerate vesting/bonuses or adopt a
  special retention bonus pool ahead of closing for tax-mitigation purposes.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
- IDT's outstanding 0.875% Convertible Senior Notes due 2022 were amended by a
  supplemental indenture, effective at the effective time, to convert solely into cash
  (at $49.00 per implied share) rather than IDT stock; IDT's Credit Agreement dated
  April 4, 2017 was terminated and all outstanding obligations paid off and extinguished
  at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K, Item 1.01 and Item 1.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- The transaction combined a whole-company, all-cash forward merger (IDT surviving as a
  direct wholly-owned subsidiary of a Japanese parent) with an immediate, closing-day
  reconstitution of the legal/governance tier — full board replacement, amended charter
  and bylaws, and removal from Nasdaq listing — while the product and process tiers
  crossed intact as a single going concern and the people tier was bridged only by a
  fixed one-year continuity-of-benefits commitment and pre-existing severance
  arrangements rather than new individually negotiated retention agreements with Parent.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-093186, Form 8-K][SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-341203, Form DEFM14A]
