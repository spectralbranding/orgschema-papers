## Outcome window
- Fixed outcome window: the registrant's realized fiscal 2018–2020 record, as reported in
  its annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020 (filed
  2021-02-26). This sits roughly two to four-and-a-half years after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Total sales from continuing operations were $4,634.4 million in 2020, compared with
  $4,563.9 million in 2019 and $3,800.4 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- Net earnings from continuing operations were $1,452.2 million in 2020, compared with
  $268.1 million in 2019 and $490.2 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- The 2020 continuing-operations result included a non-cash "Unrealized gain on investment
  in Vontier Corporation" of $1,119.2 million (see the divestiture below). [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- Total net earnings, including both continuing and discontinued operations, were
  $1,613.3 million in 2020, compared with $738.9 million in 2019 and $2,913.8 million in
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]

## Realized structural event within the window
- On October 9, 2020 the registrant "completed the separation of our former Industrial
  Technologies segment... by distributing 80.1% of the outstanding shares of Vontier
  Corporation ('Vontier'), the entity we created to hold the corresponding businesses, to
  Fortive stockholders on a pro rata basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- The Vontier businesses were reported as discontinued operations; earnings from
  discontinued operations, net of income taxes, were $161.1 million in 2020, $470.8 million
  in 2019, and $2,423.6 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]

## Documented impairment / interface facts over time
- A goodwill impairment was recorded within the divested (discontinued) operations: a fair
  value assessment "of the Telematics reporting unit using the discounted cash flow method
  ... resulted in the recording of a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $85.3 million,
  which is presented in discontinued operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- For the retained (continuing) business no goodwill impairment was recorded: "No goodwill
  impairment charges were recorded for continuing operations for the years ended
  December 31, 2020, 2019, and 2018." [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the outcome window the registrant remained a going concern with rising continuing-
  operations sales ($3,800.4M in 2018 to $4,634.4M in 2020) and itself executed a further
  pro rata spin-off, distributing 80.1% of Vontier to its stockholders on October 9, 2020;
  the only goodwill impairment in the window ($85.3M, Telematics reporting unit) fell within
  the divested/discontinued business, with none recorded for continuing operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net earnings from continuing operations of $1,452.2
  million in 2020 (which included a one-time $1,119.2 million non-cash unrealized gain on
  the retained Vontier investment), versus $268.1 million (2019) and $490.2 million (2018).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001659166-21-000062, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
