## Public-reporting status after the 2018 transaction, through going-private
- Tallgrass Energy, LP (CIK 0001633651, formerly named Tallgrass Energy GP, LP) filed
  annual reports on Form 10-K through fiscal year 2019, filed 2020-02-12. [SRC: SEC accession 0001633651-20-000013, Form 10-K]
- On December 16, 2019, Tallgrass Energy, LP ("TGE") entered an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger with Prairie Private Acquiror LP ("Buyer"), Prairie Merger Sub LLC, and TGE's
  general partner, providing for Buyer's affiliates to acquire the publicly held Class A
  Shares of TGE. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-110372, Form 8-K]
- The merger completed April 17, 2020 at 7:02 a.m. Central Time: TGE survived as a
  Delaware limited partnership; each outstanding Class A share (other than shares
  already held by the Prairie acquiror entities) was converted into $22.45 cash per
  share, with approximately $3.5 billion paid in aggregate as merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-110372, Form 8-K]
- In connection with that closing, TGE requested the New York Stock Exchange remove its
  Class A Shares from listing (Form 25), and trading in the shares was suspended before
  the opening of trading on April 17, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-110372, Form 8-K]
- TGE filed a Form 15-12B on April 27, 2020 to terminate/suspend its Exchange Act
  reporting duty, certifying approximately 6 holders of record as of that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-120697, Form 15-12B]
- At the April 2020 closing, three independent directors (Roy N. Cook, Thomas A. Gerke,
  Terrance D. Towner) were removed from the board (stated not to result from any
  disagreement over operations/policies), five new directors were appointed, and five
  directors (William R. Moler, Wallace C. Henderson, Matthew J.K. Runkle, Guy G.
  Buckley, and Marcelino Oreja Arburúa) continued to serve; William R. Moler signed the
  closing Form 8-K as Chief Executive Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-110372, Form 8-K]

## Realized record under private ownership (press-sourced, imprecisely windowed)
- Under private ownership, the company (styled "Tallgrass") announced on May 18, 2022
  an agreement with ADM to capture and sequester carbon dioxide emissions from ADM's
  corn-processing complex in Columbus, Nebraska, transporting the CO2 to Tallgrass's
  Eastern Wyoming Sequestration Hub as part of a project to convert its existing
  Trailblazer natural gas pipeline to CO2 service. [SRC: https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20220518005372/en/Tallgrass-to-Capture-and-Sequester-CO2-Emissions-From-ADM-Corn-Processing-Complex-in-Nebraska]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Tallgrass Energy, LP, the surviving public filer of the 2018 transaction, was itself
  acquired and taken private approximately 22 months after that transaction's closing,
  in an approximately $3.5 billion cash merger completed April 17, 2020, followed by
  removal from NYSE listing and deregistration under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-110372, Form 8-K; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-120697, Form 15-12B]
