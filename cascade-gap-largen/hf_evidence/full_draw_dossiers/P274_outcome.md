## Disclosure by the surviving registrant
- Following the mergers, the surviving public registrant is Phillips Edison & Company, Inc. (CIK 1476204), which continued to file periodic reports, including a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 filed February 16, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K]
- That Form 10-K's "Segment Data" / "Segments" disclosure states: "Our principal business is the ownership and operation of community and neighborhood shopping centers. We do not distinguish our principal business, or group our operations, by geography or size for purposes of measuring performance. Accordingly, we have presented our results as a single reportable segment." [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K]
- The same Form 10-K's only references to the 2018 transaction describe a carryover tax basis on certain shopping centers "as a result of our acquisition of PELP, and our merger with Phillips Edison Grocery Center REIT II, Inc." and list a September 2017 credit agreement to which Phillips Edison Grocery Center REIT II, Inc. had been a party, in the exhibit index; no revenue, income, or asset figures are separately attributed to the assets or operations that had been PE REIT II's. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K]
