## Parties and legal vehicle at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is a Maryland corporation electing to be taxed as a REIT; it was incorporated on July 3, 2003 and commenced operations on January 22, 2004, and "engages in the acquisition and ownership of commercial real estate properties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1 Business]
- It conducts business primarily through Wells Operating Partnership II, L.P. ("Wells OP II"), a Delaware limited partnership; the registrant is the sole general partner, "possesses full legal control and authority over the operations of Wells OP II," and owns more than 99.9% of its equity interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1]
- Wells Capital, Inc. ("Wells Capital") is the external advisor to the registrant and is the sole limited partner of Wells OP II. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1]
- The registrant's stock was not listed on a public securities exchange; the charter required that, if the stock were not listed by October 2015, the company must seek stockholder approval to extend that deadline or to begin liquidating investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1]

## The customer-facing asset assembled at the ownership boundary
- As of December 31, 2008 the company owned interests in 63 office properties, one industrial building, one hotel, and one office property under construction — approximately 20.1 million square feet of commercial space in 23 states and the District of Columbia; 62 were wholly owned and four were owned through consolidated joint ventures, and the office and industrial properties were approximately 97.5% leased. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1]
- Acquisitions were financed by equity raised in public offerings plus debt: the initial public offering raised approximately $2.0 billion (approximately 197.1 million shares, through March 2006); the follow-on offering raised approximately $2.6 billion (approximately 257.6 million shares, through November 2008); a third offering commenced November 11, 2008 and had raised approximately $0.1 billion (approximately 11.1 million shares) by December 31, 2008. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Note 8, Commitments and Contingencies / Stockholders' Equity]
- Serial property acquisitions during the year: in the year ended December 31, 2008 the company acquired six properties totaling approximately 3,084,000 square feet for an aggregate purchase price of $813,700 thousand (exclusive of closing costs and acquisition fees) — 13655 Riverport Drive (St. Louis, MO; Feb. 1, 2008; $31,600K), 11200 West Parkland Avenue (Milwaukee, WI; Mar. 3, 2008; $23,600K), Lenox Park Buildings (Atlanta, GA; May 8, 2008; $275,300K), Lindbergh Center Buildings (Atlanta, GA; Jul. 1, 2008; $285,000K), Three Glenlake Building (Sandy Springs, GA; Jul. 31, 2008; $100,600K), and 1580 West Nursery Road Buildings (Linthicum, MD; Sep. 5, 2008; $97,600K). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Note 3, Real Estate Acquisitions]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- Operating processes were performed by the external advisor and its affiliate, not by the registrant's own organization: "The employees of Wells Capital and its affiliate, Wells Management Company, Inc. ('Wells Management') perform substantially all of the services related to our asset management, accounting, investor relations, and other administrative activities." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1, Employees]
- The company reimbursed Wells Capital and Wells Management for its allocable share of personnel and other costs (approximately $13.6 million, $8.8 million, and $6.0 million for 2008, 2007, and 2006, respectively); those costs were allocated among the several WREF-sponsored programs based on personnel time spent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1, Employees]
- Economic dependency: the company "engaged Wells Capital and its affiliates, Wells Management and Wells Investment Securities, Inc. ('WIS')" to provide asset management, supervision of property management and leasing, asset acquisition and disposition services, and the sale of its shares; Wells Capital, Wells Management, and WIS are owned and controlled by Wells Real Estate Funds, Inc. ("WREF"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1, Economic Dependency]
- The advisory/management arrangements were not negotiated at arm's length: acquisition fees paid to Wells Capital and management and leasing fees paid to Wells Management "will be paid irrespective of the quality of their acquisition or property-management services." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1A, Risk Factors]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- A separate contemporaneous primary documents the external advisory relationship: on July 2, 2008 the registrant reported it had renewed its advisory agreement with Wells Capital (the prior agreement effective April 24, 2008 having expired June 30, 2008); the renewed agreement is "substantially the same" and requires a monthly asset management fee based on an annualized fee of 0.75% of the adjusted cost of the company's assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001181431-08-041826, 8-K (Renewal of Advisory Agreement), filed 2008-07-02]
- The renewed advisory agreement had a one-year term and was terminable by either party upon 60 days' written notice without cause or penalty, and it referenced the company's recent acquisition of the AT&T Lindbergh Center. [SRC: SEC accession 0001181431-08-041826, 8-K (Renewal of Advisory Agreement), filed 2008-07-02]
- The re-negotiated advisory agreement allowed the company to continue acquiring properties (until the aggregate purchase price of the portfolio reached $6.5 billion) without an increase in the amount of asset management fees paid to Wells Capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1, Listing Policy]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The registrant assembled a large commercial-property portfolio (~20.1 million square feet across 65 properties as of December 31, 2008, expanded by six acquisitions totaling $813.7 million during 2008), funded by continuous public equity offerings, while the operating processes (asset management, property management, leasing, acquisition and disposition) and the people who run them remained with the external advisor Wells Capital and its affiliate Wells Management under an advisory agreement and related affiliate arrangements rather than being employed or performed by the company itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-068884, 10-K FY2008 Item 1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001181431-08-041826, 8-K (Renewal of Advisory Agreement), filed 2008-07-02]
