# Structural sub-dossier — FNB Bancorp merger into TriCo Bancshares (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On December 11, 2017, TriCo Bancshares ("TriCo") entered into an agreement and plan of
  merger and reorganization with FNB Bancorp ("FNBB"), under which FNBB would merge with
  and into TriCo, with TriCo as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Immediately after the corporate merger, FNBB's wholly-owned bank subsidiary, First
  National Bank of Northern California, would merge with and into Tri Counties Bank, the
  wholly-owned bank subsidiary of TriCo, with Tri Counties Bank as the surviving bank.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A cover letter]
- TriCo, through Tri Counties Bank, operated 57 traditional stand-alone branches, nine
  in-store branches, and two loan production offices across Northern and Central
  California. FNBB, headquartered in South San Francisco, was the bank holding company
  for First National Bank of Northern California, a community bank providing banking
  products and services to small and medium sized businesses and consumers through 12
  retail branches on the San Francisco Peninsula. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Summary"/"Information About FNBB"]

## Merger consideration and structure
- Each outstanding share of FNBB common stock would be cancelled and converted into the
  right to receive 0.98 shares of TriCo common stock (the "exchange ratio"), with cash
  paid in lieu of fractional shares; the exchange ratio was subject to adjustment (up or
  down) only if TriCo's average closing price over a defined pre-closing measurement
  period fell outside a $33.18-$49.78 collar relative to a defined index-linked test.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A cover letter]
- At the December 8, 2017 pre-announcement price, the implied aggregate transaction value
  was approximately $315 million; at the April 16, 2018 price closer to the vote, the
  implied aggregate transaction value was approximately $276 million. TriCo expected to
  issue approximately 7,343,359 shares of its common stock, with FNBB's shareholders
  expected to own approximately 24% of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The merger was structured to be accounted for under the acquisition method under U.S.
  GAAP: TriCo's assets/liabilities would carry forward at recorded amounts while FNBB's
  assets and liabilities would be adjusted to fair value, with any excess of purchase
  price over the fair value of net assets acquired recorded on TriCo's books. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Accounting Treatment of the Merger"]

## What would cross at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: FNBB (the entire holding company, not a carved-out unit) would merge
  into TriCo, with TriCo surviving; First National Bank of Northern California (the
  entire bank subsidiary) would merge into Tri Counties Bank, with Tri Counties Bank
  surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Product/asset and processes: following the bank merger, Tri Counties Bank "intends to
  continue to operate all of the branches acquired from First National Bank." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Information About FNBB"]
- People/organization: FNBB directors and officers would receive cash for cancellation of
  their FNBB stock options with accelerated vesting; TriCo agreed to honor FNBB's
  existing indemnification obligations for six years and to purchase D&O liability
  insurance for FNBB's directors and officers for six years following the merger; certain
  FNBB officers were to receive aggregate cash payments of approximately $2.25 million
  under pre-existing severance/retention agreements; two (not-yet-identified) FNBB
  directors would be appointed to the boards of TriCo and Tri Counties Bank upon
  completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Interests of Certain FNBB Officers and Directors in the Merger"]
- Named individuals James Black (President and a director of FNBB/First National Bank),
  Anthony Clifford (EVP/COO and a director of FNBB/First National Bank), and Randy
  Brugioni (SVP/Chief Lending Officer of First National Bank) had each entered into
  employment agreements with TriCo and Tri Counties Bank, effective as of closing,
  providing for their continued employment following the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Interests of Certain FNBB Officers and Directors in the Merger"]
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus stated that, as of its date, "the plan to
  integrate TriCo's and FNBB's operations is still being developed," with the specific
  details to be determined. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A Note 3 "Merger and Acquisition Integration Costs"]

## Conditions and regulatory process
- Completion required approval by majorities of both TriCo's and FNBB's outstanding
  shares, prior approval (or confirmation none was required) from the Federal Reserve,
  the FDIC, and the California Department of Business Oversight, effectiveness of TriCo's
  registration statement, Nasdaq listing approval for the shares to be issued, and
  opinions that the merger would qualify as a tax-free reorganization under Section
  368(a) of the Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Conditions to the Merger"]
- TriCo's obligation was further conditioned on FNBB's tangible common equity at closing
  being not less than $119 million (as defined, subject to specified adjustments). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Conditions to the Merger"]
- FNBB would owe TriCo a termination fee of $12.0 million if the merger agreement were
  terminated under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A "Termination Fee"]
- On June 6, 2018, TriCo and FNBB jointly announced receipt of Federal Reserve approval,
  with the transaction still subject to statutory waiting periods and customary closing
  conditions and expected to close on July 6, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000799, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Completion
- FNBB's Form 15 certification states that, effective July 6, 2018, FNB Bancorp merged
  with and into TriCo Bancshares, with TriCo Bancshares surviving the merger as the
  surviving corporation, and that the number of holders of record of FNBB common stock as
  of the certification date was zero. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000928, Form 15-12G]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- TriCo Bancshares, an existing Nasdaq-listed California bank holding company, acquired
  the entirety of FNB Bancorp (and its bank subsidiary First National Bank of Northern
  California) in a stock-for-stock merger in which FNBB merged into TriCo and First
  National Bank merged into Tri Counties Bank, with TriCo/Tri Counties Bank as the
  surviving entities, TriCo intending to continue operating all acquired branches, and
  contractual continuity provisions (employment agreements for named FNBB officers,
  multi-year indemnification/insurance) covering the people tier at closing, while the
  parties' own integration plan for operations remained undeveloped as of the proxy
  statement's date; the transaction closed effective July 6, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-125072, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001019056-18-000928, Form 15-12G]
