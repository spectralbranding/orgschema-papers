# Outcome sub-dossier — AOL Inc. web-services / advertising-property program (realized record, ~2013-2015)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction record within a fixed 3-5-year outcome window (~2013-2015). It
> contains no closing-era structural information.

## Outcome window and best-documented financial scalars
- The outcome window is fixed at the registrant's FY2012-FY2014 record as reported in the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2014 (filed February 27, 2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- Total revenues were $2,527.2 million (FY2014), up 9% from $2,319.9 million (FY2013), which in turn was up 6% from $2,191.7 million (FY2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- Net income attributable to AOL Inc. was $125.6 million (FY2014), $92.4 million (FY2013) and $1,048.4 million (FY2012); the FY2012 figure reflects the sale of approximately 800 patents and related applications to Microsoft. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- Advertising and other revenue rose as a share of total revenues over the window (68% FY2012, 72% FY2013, 76% FY2014), while subscription's share declined correspondingly. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]

## Continued platform-assembly activity within the window
- The company continued acquiring advertising-technology properties: it acquired Adap.tv, Inc. (a global programmatic video exchange for advertisers and publishers) in September 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- During 2014 it acquired Project Rover, Inc. ("Gravity," multi-screen content optimization/personalization), Convertro, Inc. (multi-touch attribution modeling, in May 2014) and Vidible, Inc. (a programmatic video-exchange platform, in December 2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- These video and programmatic properties (AOL On, Be On, Adap.tv, AOP, MARKETPLACE, ADTECH, Gravity, Vidible, Convertro) were organized under an "AOL Platforms" grouping. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]

## Documented disruption / write-downs at the product-process interface over time
- A closing-era-acquired local-content property could not be sustained on the platform: in the third quarter of 2013 the company completed a step-two goodwill evaluation and recorded a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $17.5 million "fully impairing the goodwill" related to Patch. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- On January 29, 2014 the company completed the disposition of a majority interest in Patch, retaining a 40% minority interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- During 2014 the company "shuttered or disposed of several brands, including Patch," and recorded long-lived-asset impairment charges (including $10.0 million referenced in costs of revenues) associated with shutdown or disposed brands; cost declines in the period were attributed in part to lower headcount related to Patch. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]

## Terminal outcome — acquisition of the registrant and deregistration
- On May 12, 2015 AOL Inc. entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Verizon Communications Inc. and Hanks Acquisition Sub, Inc. (a wholly owned Verizon subsidiary), under which Acquisition Sub would conduct a tender offer for all AOL common stock at $50.00 per share and then merge with and into AOL. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-184218, 8-K Item 1.01 (May 12, 2015)]
- On May 26, 2015 Acquisition Sub commenced the tender offer; on June 23, 2015 Acquisition Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company surviving and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Verizon, and the Company notified the NYSE that the merger had been consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-231372, 8-K (June 23, 2015)]
- The NYSE filed a Form 25-NSE for AOL on June 23, 2015 (removal from listing/registration). [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-15-000261, Form 25-NSE (June 23, 2015)]
- AOL Inc. filed a Form 15 (Certification and Notice of Termination of Registration under Section 12(g)) on July 6, 2015, deregistering its common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-244944, Form 15-12B (July 6, 2015)]

## Outcome summary
- Within the fixed window the registrant grew revenue (to $2,527.2 million in FY2014) and remained profitable (net income attributable of $92.4M FY2013 and $125.6M FY2014), continuing to acquire and consolidate advertising-technology properties under an "AOL Platforms" grouping. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- Over the same period one prominently invested closing-era local-content property (Patch) was fully goodwill-impaired ($17.5M, Q3 2013), had a majority interest divested (January 2014) and was among brands shuttered/disposed in 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468516-15-000006, 10-K FY2014]
- The registrant's independent public existence ended when it was acquired by Verizon via a $50.00-per-share tender offer and merger consummated June 23, 2015, after which it became a wholly owned Verizon subsidiary, was delisted (Form 25-NSE, June 23, 2015) and deregistered (Form 15, July 6, 2015); no separate 3-5-year post-2015 public record exists for the business as a standalone registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-231372, 8-K (June 23, 2015); SEC accession 0001193125-15-244944, Form 15-12B (July 6, 2015)]
