## Corporate identity change
- On March 8, 2012 the registrant changed its name from Inland Western Retail Real
  Estate Trust, Inc. to Retail Properties of America, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 1 Business/Operating History]
- By the FY2014 10-K, the registrant's SEC commission file number is 001-35481 (a
  national-securities-exchange file number), and its outstanding Class A Common Stock
  and 7.00% Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock are registered on the New
  York Stock Exchange, subject to NYSE listing standards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, cover page and Item 1A Risk Factors]
- The FY2014 10-K states that debt has been funded "through dispositions of assets and
  capital markets transactions, including the completion of a public offering and
  listing of our Class A common stock on the NYSE, [and] the completion of a public
  offering of our Series A preferred stock." No specific listing date is given in the
  text reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 7 MD&A]

## Realized financial trajectory, 2010-2014 (Selected Financial Data table)
- Total assets declined from $6,386,836 thousand at year-end 2010 to $4,803,860 thousand
  at year-end 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders moved from $(95,843) thousand
  (2010) to $(72,609) thousand (2011), $(710) thousand (2012), $4,176 thousand (2013),
  and $33,850 thousand (2014) — a multi-year swing from loss to profit.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Total revenues were $560,150 thousand (2010), $531,077 thousand (2011), $531,171
  thousand (2012), $551,508 thousand (2013), and $600,614 thousand (2014).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Distributions declared per common share rose from $0.49 (2010) to $0.66 (2012 through
  2014); preferred-stock distributions first appear in 2012 ($263 thousand) and reach
  $9,450 thousand in both 2013 and 2014, indicating a preferred-equity component
  introduced during the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]

## Portfolio repositioning program (2012 onward)
- As of December 31, 2014 the company owned 208 retail operating properties.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 1 Business]
- In 2012 management began a "long-term portfolio repositioning effort" targeting 10-15
  markets, aiming to own 3,000,000 to 5,000,000 square feet in each. Since the beginning
  of 2012 the company sold 87 properties, primarily in non-target markets, for aggregate
  consideration of $1,240,149 thousand, with proceeds substantially used for debt
  reduction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 1 Business]
- Beginning in the fourth quarter of 2013, after meeting leverage targets, the company
  resumed acquisitions in its target markets, purchasing 15 properties for a total
  acquisition price of $559,348 thousand through the date of the FY2014 filing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 1 Business]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the 2010-2014 window, the entity (renamed in 2012 and by then NYSE-listed)
  reported a shrinking balance sheet (total assets down roughly 25%), a swing from a
  large net loss attributable to common shareholders in 2010-2012 to net income in 2013
  and 2014, and a disposition program (87 properties sold since 2012, ~$1.24 billion)
  followed by a resumed, more selective acquisition program (15 properties, ~$559
  million) beginning in late 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014, Item 1 Business and Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- No divestiture of the entire company, bankruptcy filing, or dissolution was identified
  in this filing for the 2010-2014 window; the entity continued as a going-concern,
  separately reporting public company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001222840-15-000005, Form 10-K FY2014]
