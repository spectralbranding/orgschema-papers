## The drawn filing (described for what it establishes)

  10-K/A (Amendment No. 2) for the year ended December 31, 2011, filed for the sole
  purpose of revising Item 9A (Controls and Procedures) in response to an SEC Staff
  comment; it does not itself describe any acquisition or disposition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-12-004067, Form 10-K/A (Amendment No. 2) FY2011]
- The registrant is Patriot Coal Corporation, a Delaware corporation headquartered at
  12312 Olive Boulevard, St. Louis, Missouri, whose common stock traded on the New York
  Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-12-004067, Form 10-K/A (Amendment No. 2) FY2011]
- The company is a coal producer; the amended item concerns accounting for its Apogee
  FBR and Hobet ABMet selenium water-treatment facilities, part of a selenium water-

  permits. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-12-004067, Form 10-K/A (Amendment No. 2) FY2011]
- Background identity only (not the analyzed transaction): the exhibit index records the
  entity's origin in a 2007 Separation Agreement, Plan of Reorganization and Distribution

  Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-12-004067, Form 10-K/A (Amendment No. 2) FY2011]

## First court-supervised reorganization (2012–2013) — closing structure
- Patriot and its subsidiaries were debtors-in-possession in a chapter 11 case before the
  U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri, and on October 9, 2013 filed
  a Proposed First Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization and related Disclosure Statement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-13-005969, Form 8-K dated October 9, 2013]
- People/labor substrate at closing: new collective bargaining agreements, a Memorandum of
  Understanding, and settlements of §1113 (active UMWA-represented employees) and §1114
  (UMWA retirees) were approved by the bankruptcy court on August 22, 2013; a UMWA-
  established voluntary employees' beneficiary association (VEBA) assumed the debtors'
  obligations to provide retiree benefits to UMWA retirees effective January 1, 2014.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-13-005104, Form 8-K dated August 22, 2013]
- Consummation of the reorganization: the company emerged as a privately held reorganized
  entity and terminated its SEC registration, filing a Form 15 on December 18, 2013 that
  reported 0 holders of record of its common stock, preferred share purchase rights, and
  8.250% Senior Notes due 2018 (pre-existing public equity extinguished). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-13-007337, Form 15-12B filed December 18, 2013]

## Second court-supervised asset transaction (2015) — closing structure (registrant was private; press-sourced)
- Because the reorganized company deregistered in December 2013, the 2015 asset transaction
  is not on EDGAR; it was documented in a Patriot press release: a second chapter 11 was
  used to sell substantially all assets, the transaction with the Virginia Conservation
  Legacy Fund (VCLF)/ERP Compliant Fuel "would be consummated pursuant to a chapter 11 plan
  and is subject to bankruptcy court approval." [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/patriot-coal-enters-into-agreement-to-sell-certain-assets-to-virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-300129236.html]
- Legal vehicle and parties: the second chapter 11 was filed May 12, 2015 in the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Virginia (Richmond), Case No. 15-32450, and
  resolved through a Section 363 sale to Blackhawk Mining LLC for the principal mining
  operations and to ERP Compliant Fuel, LLC, an affiliate of Virginia Conservation Legacy
  Fund, Inc., for the remaining assets. [SRC: https://elevenflo.com/blog/patriot-coal-second-chapter-11-blackhawk-mining]
- Product/asset split at closing: Blackhawk acquired the Panther, Rocklick, Wells, Kanawha
  Eagle, Paint Creek, and Midland Trail mining operations; ERP/VCLF acquired the remaining
  assets, including the Federal and Corridor G mining complexes, the Hobet 21 mine, other
  reclamation-heavy operations, and mining permits. [SRC: https://elevenflo.com/blog/patriot-coal-second-chapter-11-blackhawk-mining] [SRC: https://www.miningweekly.com/article/virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-to-buy-bankrupt-patriots-remaining-assets-2015-08-17]
- Product and producing processes crossed together (not stranded): "Patriot's mining
  operations and customer shipments will continue in the ordinary course during the sale
  process," i.e., the operations transferred as going concerns rather than the assets
  crossing without their operating processes. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/patriot-coal-enters-into-agreement-to-sell-certain-assets-to-virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-300129236.html]
- Processes moved with the people who run them: a "substantial majority of Patriot
  employees at our mining operations" were expected to be offered employment with Blackhawk
  or VCLF; VCLF stated it expected to maintain West Virginia employment at the then-current
  683-employee level. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/patriot-coal-enters-into-agreement-to-sell-certain-assets-to-virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-300129236.html] [SRC: https://www.miningweekly.com/article/virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-to-buy-bankrupt-patriots-remaining-assets-2015-08-17]
- Liability/obligation substrate allocated at closing: VCLF/ERP assumed liabilities in
  excess of $400 million for Patriot's workers' compensation, state black-lung, and
  environmental obligations, and agreed to assume or replace surety bonds supporting
  reclamation and related liabilities — the legacy reclamation/water-treatment substrate
  concentrated with the reclamation-oriented buyer rather than the going-concern buyer.
  [SRC: https://www.miningweekly.com/article/virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-to-buy-bankrupt-patriots-remaining-assets-2015-08-17]

## Consummation and structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction CONSUMMATED: the plan founded on the two asset sales was confirmed on
  October 9, 2015 and became effective in late October 2015, transferring the operating
  mines to Blackhawk Mining LLC and the reclamation-heavy assets and legacy obligations to
  ERP Compliant Fuel, LLC (VCLF affiliate). [SRC: https://elevenflo.com/blog/patriot-coal-second-chapter-11-blackhawk-mining]
- Net closing structure: a court-supervised sale that split the going-concern mining
  operations (product + producing processes + a substantial majority of the workforce) to
  one buyer while concentrating the reclamation/legacy-liability substrate (permits,
  >$400M of workers'-comp/black-lung/environmental obligations, surety bonds) with a
  reclamation-oriented affiliate of a second buyer; pre-existing Patriot public equity was
  extinguished in the prior 2013 reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-13-007337, Form 15-12B filed December 18, 2013] [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/patriot-coal-enters-into-agreement-to-sell-certain-assets-to-virginia-conservation-legacy-fund-300129236.html]
