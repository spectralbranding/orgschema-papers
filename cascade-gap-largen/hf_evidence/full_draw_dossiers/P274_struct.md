# Structural sub-dossier — Phillips Edison Grocery Center REIT II, Inc. merger into Phillips Edison & Company, Inc. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 17, 2018, Phillips Edison & Company, Inc. ("PECO") and Phillips Edison Grocery Center REIT II, Inc. ("PE REIT II") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger for a 100% stock-for-stock transaction valued at approximately $1.9 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, PE REIT II merged with and into REIT Merger Sub, LLC (a Maryland limited liability company and wholly owned subsidiary of PECO), with REIT Merger Sub surviving as the Surviving Entity; concurrently, OP Merger Sub merged with and into Phillips Edison Grocery Center Operating Partnership II, L.P. ("PE OP II"), with PE OP II surviving as a subsidiary of PECO's operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share of PE REIT II common stock was converted into the right to receive 2.04 shares of PECO common stock (equivalent to $22.54 per share based on PECO's then-most-recent estimated net asset value of $11.05 per share). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, continuing PECO stockholders were estimated to own approximately 71% of the surviving company ("Combined Company") on a fully diluted basis, and former PE REIT II stockholders approximately 29%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- The mergers closed on November 16, 2018; PE REIT II's separate corporate existence ceased at that time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K Item 2.01/5.01]

## Controlling intent and pre-existing relationship
- PECO already served as PE REIT II's external advisor prior to the merger, through an affiliate, Phillips Edison NTR II LLC ("PE-NTR II"); the DEFM14A states the transaction was expected to be a "seamless integration process as PECO's management team has managed PE REIT II since PE REIT II's inception." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- Certain PE REIT II executive officers (Messrs. Edison, Addy, Murphy and Ms. Robison) were also officers of the advisor, PE-NTR II, and were compensated by PE-NTR II rather than by PE REIT II directly. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- As of August 28, 2018, PECO principals Jeffrey Edison and Joseph Addy personally held 51,111.11 and 2,960.61 shares of PE REIT II common stock, respectively, and were to receive PECO shares under the exchange ratio upon closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle, product, and organization
- The Combined Company retained the PECO name and Maryland-corporation form, described in the proxy as a $6.3 billion internally-managed non-traded REIT focused exclusively on grocery-anchored shopping centers, owning and operating 321 such centers (more than 36.6 million square feet) across 33 states after the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- Neither PECO nor PE REIT II common stock had a public trading market before the mergers; both were non-traded REITs at the time of the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]
- PE REIT II itself had no employees; all of its executive officers were also officers of PECO or its affiliates and were compensated by those entities rather than by PE REIT II. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]

## What was terminated at closing (no transition-period bridging agreement)
- In connection with the mergers and effective immediately prior to the REIT Merger Effective Time, the following were terminated: the Amended and Restated Advisory Agreement between PE REIT II/PE OP II and PE-NTR II; certain addenda to the Master Property Management Agreement; and certain addenda to the Master Services Agreement. PECO waived any disposition fee otherwise payable under the advisory agreement, and no termination fees were paid to any party other than fees already accrued in the ordinary course. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- The proxy states this termination reflects removal of "external advisor fees" as part of the Combined Company's move to a fully internal management structure; no transitional-services or shared-services agreement was disclosed to bridge these functions post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]

## Governance changes at closing
- Immediately prior to the REIT Merger Effective Time, all members of the PE REIT II board of directors resigned; the resignations were stated to be in connection with the mergers and not due to any disagreement with the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The Combined Company's board was increased to seven members: the five continuing PECO directors plus two independent PE REIT II directors, David W. Garrison and John A. Strong. The executive officers of PECO immediately prior to closing, including Jeffrey S. Edison as Chief Executive Officer, continued as the executive officers of the Combined Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two REITs that were already linked by a pre-existing external-advisory relationship (PECO's affiliate had managed PE REIT II's day-to-day operations since PE REIT II's formation); at closing that advisory/property-management/services relationship was terminated outright rather than continued under a transition-services agreement, PE REIT II's board resigned, and PE REIT II (which itself had no employees) merged into a wholly owned PECO subsidiary, with the customer-facing grocery-anchored-shopping-center portfolios combined under PECO's existing internally-managed organization and continuing executive team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037155, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-18-000041, Form 8-K]
