# Structural sub-dossier — SunEdison Semiconductor Limited acquisition by GlobalWafers (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The operating company that was the subject of the transaction
- SunEdison Semiconductor Limited, a Singapore-incorporated company, described itself as
  "a global leader in the development, manufacture, and sale of silicon wafers to the
  semiconductor industry," with wafers used "as the base substrate for nearly all
  semiconductor devices." [SRC: SEC accession 0001585854-16-000093, Form 10-K (FY2015) Item 1 Business Overview]
- The business "was established in 1959 and was known during most of our history as MEMC
  Electronic Materials, Inc. ('MEMC')," with a broad product portfolio and an extensive
  global manufacturing footprint. [SRC: SEC accession 0001585854-16-000093, Form 10-K (FY2015) Item 1]
- The company "had approximately 4,400 employees as of December 31, 2015, nearly 1,400 of
  whom were unionized" at its manufacturing facilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001585854-16-000093, Form 10-K (FY2015) Item 1 Employees]

## Transaction and parties as structured
- On August 17, 2016 the Company entered into an Implementation Agreement with GlobalWafers
  Co., Ltd. ("Globe"/"GWC"), a corporation of the Republic of China, and GWafers Singapore
  Pte. Ltd. ("Acquiror"), a Singapore corporation, providing for the Acquiror's acquisition
  of all issued and outstanding ordinary shares of the Company (other than those held by
  Globe/Acquiror and their subsidiaries). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The consideration was US$12.00 per share in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction was structured as a Scheme of Arrangement under Singapore law
  (Section 210 of the Companies Act (Chapter 50) of Singapore), with Globe retaining a
  right, subject to consent and Singapore regulatory clearance, to elect a tender-offer
  structure instead. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Introductory Note]
- Globe already held approximately 4.9% of the Company's outstanding ordinary shares at the
  time of the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing
- On December 2, 2016 (the "Effective Date") the Acquiror acquired all of the outstanding
  ordinary shares pursuant to the Scheme, and "the Company is an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of GWC" — the entire operating company transferred as a going concern rather
  than as separated assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Introductory Note / Item 2.01]
- The company's operating subsidiaries transferred with it, including SunEdison
  Semiconductor B.V. and MEMC Korea Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Items 1.01–1.02]
- Because the whole company (its silicon-wafer product line and the manufacturing
  operations that produce it) transferred intact under single new ownership, the
  customer-facing product was not carved out from its producing processes at closing; no
  transitional-services or shared-services agreement splitting the operating substrate is
  described. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Introductory Note] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 9.01]

## People / management tier at closing
- Employee equity was cashed out at the transaction price: each outstanding option was
  converted into the right to receive the excess of US$12.00 over its exercise price and
  each outstanding RSU into cash at US$12.00 per underlying share (performance RSUs deemed
  vested at 100% of target). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At the Effective Date each member of the Company's board of directors resigned, and
  Doris Hsiu-Lan Hsu, Mark Lynn England, and Woo Heng Thong were appointed directors.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- On December 2, 2016, following closing, the employment of President and Chief Executive
  Officer Shaker Sadasivam was terminated; the closing constituted a "change in control"
  under the Company's Change in Control Severance Plan, and CFO Jeffrey L. Hall entered an
  Additional Compensation Agreement (excise-tax gross-up) on December 1, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Financing and debt substrate at closing
- Globe obtained acquisition financing via debt commitment letters entered before signing;
  a pre-closing Limited Consent to the Company's May 27, 2014 Credit Agreement (Goldman
  Sachs Bank USA as administrative agent) permitted the agreement's execution but did "not
  permit the closing of the Transaction prior to payment of all then outstanding
  indebtedness under the Credit Agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At closing, SunEdison Semiconductor B.V. (as Borrower) entered a new Loan Agreement
  providing up to US$200.0 million of term loans (guaranteed initially by GWC), the
  proceeds "used to repay certain existing secured facilities," and the 2014 Credit
  Agreement plus the MEMC Korea term loans (Shinhan Bank, Hana Bank, Korea Development
  Bank) were repaid in full and terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K Items 1.01–1.02]

## Deal-protection terms
- The Company was subject to customary "no-shop" restrictions (with a fiduciary-duty
  exception) and would owe Globe a termination fee of approximately US$19.2 million in
  specified circumstances; Globe would owe the Company a reverse termination fee of
  US$40 million (for which Globe deposited US$40 million in escrow with Mega International
  Commercial Bank Co., Ltd.) for specified financing/regulatory failures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Completion was conditioned on shareholder approval, Singapore court approval of the
  Scheme, expiration of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, and CFIUS and other
  antitrust/regulatory clearances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash acquisition (US$12.00/share) of a Singapore
  silicon-wafer manufacturer by an ROC acquirer, effected as a Singapore Scheme of
  Arrangement, in which the entire operating company and its subsidiaries transferred
  intact as a going concern while the entire board was replaced, the CEO was terminated at
  closing, and the acquired company's legacy secured debt was refinanced by a new
  acquirer-guaranteed US$200 million facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-784692, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-684473, Form 8-K]
