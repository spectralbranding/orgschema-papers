## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- Under private ownership the company (styled "Verifone") described itself, in a 2023
  release, as employing more than 4,500 "payments architects and commerce experts,"
  operating gateways that process more than 14 billion transactions annually, and
  maintaining an ecosystem of 2,500-plus integrations.
  [SRC: https://www.verifone.com/resources/verifone-announces-new-industry-veteran-ceo]
- The company experienced rapid chief-executive turnover in 2023: Mike Kohlsdorf became
  CEO in August 2023, focused during his tenure on "enhancing employee, customer and
  shareholder satisfaction, as well as broadening current partnerships and starting new
  ones," and was succeeded as CEO by Himanshu Patel effective November 1, 2023 — an
  interval of approximately three months — with Kohlsdorf remaining on the company's
  board of directors. [SRC: https://www.verifone.com/resources/verifone-announces-new-industry-veteran-ceo]
- On April 1, 2025 (beyond the ~3–5-year window), the company announced a $235 million
  preferred-equity investment from Francisco Partners, British Columbia Investment
  Management Corporation (BCI), and other existing shareholders, together with
  extensions of its Revolving Credit, Term Loan, and Accounts Receivable Securitization
  facilities; a Francisco Partners representative stated the company was "well
  positioned for sustained success and growth," and the company's CFO stated the
  financing provided "a strong, stable balance sheet."
  [SRC: https://www.verifone.com/resources/press-room-article-verifone-receives-equity-investment]
