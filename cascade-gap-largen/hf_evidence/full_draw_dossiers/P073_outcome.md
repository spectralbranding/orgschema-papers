## Reporting status and window
- The registrant remained an SEC reporter throughout the window; the realized record is
  drawn from its annual reports, with the fixed outcome window being fiscal years
  2014-2016 (period ends March 31), primarily the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  March 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended March 31, 2016)]

## Subsequent contractual reconfiguration of the vehicle
- In November 2013 a subsidiary of the company and its Angolan partner, Sonangol Holdings
  Lda., executed a new joint venture agreement for Sonatide; it was in effect and set to
  expire, unless extended, two years after a new Angolan entity (intended to be one of the
  Sonatide group of companies) was incorporated — expected to be incorporated in calendar
  2016 after Angolan regulatory approvals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- Effective July 1, 2013 an Angolan "forex law" required offshore oil-and-gas customers to
  pay for goods and services in Angolan kwanzas deposited into Angolan bank accounts,
  which resulted in substantial customer payments being made to Sonatide in kwanzas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- In response, the company and Sonangol signed a "consortium agreement" permitting split
  billing (local services paid in kwanzas; offshore services paid in U.S. dollars);
  Sonatide converted select customer contracts to the split-billing arrangement in the
  quarters ended March 31, 2015 and June 30, 2015; the consortium agreement expired in
  November 2015 and the parties were negotiating a replacement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]

## Documented disruption at the delivery / cash-conversion interface
- The kwanza payment process "deprived the company of significant cash and liquidity,"
  because converting kwanzas into U.S. dollars and expatriating the funds caused payment
  delays, additional operating costs and, through the company's 49% ownership of Sonatide,
  foreign exchange losses, plus exposure to currency-devaluation risk before conversion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- As of March 31, 2016 the company had approximately $339 million due from Sonatide (vs.
  approximately $420 million at March 31, 2015), of which about $97 million reflected
  invoiced but unpaid vessel revenue. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- As of March 31, 2016 Sonatide held the equivalent of approximately $119 million of
  primarily kwanza-denominated deposits in Angolan banks, largely customer receipts not
  yet converted to U.S. dollars and expatriated to the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- The carrying value of the company's investment in the Sonatide joint venture was
  approximately $37 million at March 31, 2016, down from approximately $67 million at
  March 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]

## Documented change at the operations / organization interface
- A 2014 Angolan presidential decree left uncertain whether authorities would interpret it
  to require 100% Angolan ownership of local vessel operators such as Sonatide, which the
  company stated "may result in the need to work with Sonangol to further restructure our
  Sonatide joint venture and our operations in Angola"; the company said it was exploring
  potential alternative structures to comply. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- Angolan operations generated vessel revenues of approximately $213 million (22% of
  consolidated vessel revenue) for the year ended March 31, 2016 from an average of about
  65 company-owned vessels marketed through Sonatide, versus approximately $351 million
  (23%) for the year ended March 31, 2015 from an average of about 80 company-owned
  vessels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- As a mitigating measure the company redeployed vessels away from Angola, resulting in a
  net 23 vessels transferred out of Angola during the year ended March 31, 2016, and noted
  that redeployment would entail costs to redeploy its shore-based employees to other
  areas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- At March 31, 2016 Sonatide owned eight vessels (three then stacked) and earned
  commission income from company-owned vessels marketed through the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]

## Registrant-level realized scalar (context: entity-wide, driven substantially by the offshore E&P downturn)
- The company reported a consolidated net loss of $160.2 million and an operating loss of
  $69.5 million for fiscal 2016, with the consolidated net loss increasing 146% (about
  $95 million) versus fiscal 2015 and revenue decreasing $516.5 million (35%) from fiscal
  2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
- The company recorded asset impairment charges of $117.3 million, $14.5 million and
  $9.3 million in fiscal 2016, 2015 and 2014 respectively, and a $283.7 million non-cash
  goodwill impairment charge in fiscal 2015 (writing off its remaining goodwill). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the fiscal 2014-2016 window the Angolan vessel-services operation held inside the
  Sonatide joint venture was reconfigured (a new joint venture agreement in November 2013,
  a split-billing consortium agreement that later lapsed) yet faced a documented breakdown
  in the cash-conversion pathway — substantial receipts trapped in kwanzas, ~$339 million
  due from the joint venture at March 31, 2016 with ~$97 million invoiced-but-unpaid,
  ~$119 million of kwanza deposits pending conversion, and the investment carrying value
  falling from ~$67 million to ~$37 million — alongside a regulatory prospect of forced
  100% Angolan ownership requiring further restructuring and a net 23 vessels redeployed
  out of Angola; the registrant's fiscal 2016 result was a consolidated net loss of
  $160.2 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-16-019999, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2016)]
