## Note on the drawn accession
- The accession drawn for this case (Form 8-K, filed 2015-05-22) reports the registrant's
  entry on May 20, 2015 into a Forbearance Agreement and Third Amendment to a Credit
  Agreement dated December 14, 2012; it postdates the closing of the combination
  described below and is not the combination-transaction document. The closing structure
  below is therefore sourced from the registrant's December 2014 combination-closing

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction combined the business of Forest Oil Corporation (a New York corporation,
  the registrant, NYSE: FST) with the business of Sabine Oil & Gas LLC, pursuant to an
  Amended and Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger dated May 5, 2014 and amended and
  restated July 9, 2014, as further amended by Amendment No. 1 dated December 16, 2014.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Structurally, Sabine Investor Holdings LLC and FR XI Onshore AIV, LLC ("AIV Holdings")
  contributed all of the equity interests of Sabine Oil & Gas Holdings LLC to Forest, with
  Sabine Holdings becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Forest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-375551, DEFM14A cover letter]
- In exchange for the contribution, Sabine Investor Holdings and AIV Holdings received
  Forest common stock and convertible common-equivalent preferred stock collectively
  representing approximately a 73.5% economic interest and 80% of the total voting power in
  Forest; Forest's pre-transaction shareholders retained approximately a 26.5% economic
  interest and 20% of the total voting power. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-375551, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The combination transaction closed on December 16, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0000038079-14-000101, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Legal vehicle: the public registrant that continued was Forest Oil Corporation; pursuant
  to the merger agreement the registrant filed a Certificate of Amendment on December 19,
  2014 changing the corporation's name from Forest Oil Corporation to Sabine Oil & Gas
  Corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- Customer-facing asset / operating business: the contributed Sabine oil and gas operating
  organization (Sabine Oil & Gas Holdings LLC and its subsidiaries) became a wholly owned
  subsidiary of the public registrant, combining with Forest's own oil and natural gas
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-375551, DEFM14A cover letter]
- People / management: effective at the December 16, 2014 closing, all of the executive
  officers of Forest were removed, and the board appointed David J. Sambrooks (President and
  CEO), R. Todd Levesque (EVP and COO), Cheryl R. Levesque (SVP, Asset Development) and
  Timothy D. Yang (SVP, Land & Legal, General Counsel) as the executive officers.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Board: immediately prior to closing, five Forest directors (James D. Lightner, Loren K.
  Carroll, Richard J. Carty, James H. Lee and Raymond I. Wilcox) resigned, and six new
  directors (Duane C. Radtke, John Yearwood, Thomas N. Chewning, David J. Sambrooks, Alex T.
  Krueger and Brooks M. Shughart) were appointed pursuant to the merger agreement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Auditor / control functions: in connection with the closing the registrant engaged Deloitte
  & Touche LLP as its independent registered public accounting firm effective December 16,
  2014 (Deloitte having served Sabine O&G since fiscal year 2012) and dismissed Ernst & Young
  LLP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 4.01]

## Governance / capital-structure changes at closing
- Pursuant to the merger agreement the registrant filed Certificates of Amendment on
  December 16, 2014 increasing authorized common shares from 200,000,000 to 650,000,000 and
  authorizing a Series A non-voting convertible common-equivalent preferred stock issued to
  the Sabine holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- On the closing the NYSE suspended trading in the registrant's common stock for failure to
  meet initial listing standards; from December 17, 2014 the common stock traded on the OTCQB
  Marketplace under the ticker "FSTO." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction placed a contributed operating oil-and-gas organization (Sabine Holdings)
  inside a continuing public legal vehicle (Forest Oil Corporation, renamed Sabine Oil & Gas
  Corporation), with the contributing parties taking approximately 73.5% economic / 80%
  voting control, the incoming management and the incoming auditor replacing the registrant's
  prior management and auditor at closing, and the prior shareholders retained as an
  approximately 26.5% economic / 20% voting minority. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-375551, DEFM14A cover letter] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-450831, Form 8-K Items 4.01 and 5.02]
