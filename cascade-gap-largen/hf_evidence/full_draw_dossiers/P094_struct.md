## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K reporting an event of July 26, 2017: at a reconvened 2017 Annual Meeting the company's stockholders approved Proposal 3, an amendment and restatement of the charter changing the calculation of the fee cap from a per-share basis to a per-account basis with respect to total upfront selling commissions, dealer manager fees and stockholder servicing fees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-236870, 8-K Item 5.03]
- Under Item 5.07 the stockholder vote on Proposal 3 was 32,662,654.36 for; 1,252,612.96 against; 2,309,764.30 abstained; the Second Articles of Amendment and Restatement (Exhibit 3.1) were filed with the Maryland Secretary of State and went effective July 26, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-236870, 8-K Items 5.03/5.07/9.01]
- The 8-K is a corporate charter/governance event (a fee-cap mechanics amendment), not a property-acquisition or deal-terms document; it was signed by Leon Volchyok, Chief Securities Counsel and Secretary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-236870, 8-K signature block]

## Parties and legal vehicle at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is "a non-exchange traded, perpetual life real estate investment trust" that acquires primarily stabilized income-oriented commercial real estate in the U.S.; it is "externally managed by BX REIT Advisors L.L.C. (the 'Adviser'), a subsidiary of The Blackstone Group L.P." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- The company is the sole general partner of BREIT Operating Partnership L.P. (BREIT OP) and owns all or substantially all of its assets through BREIT OP; it operated in five reportable segments (Multifamily, Industrial, Hotel, Retail Properties, Real Estate-Related Securities). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- The company's structure as a perpetual-life REIT means it has "no pre-determined operational period or the need to provide a 'liquidity' event at the end of that period." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- Capital to fund acquisitions is raised through a continuous best-efforts public offering (registered for up to $5.0 billion); as of March 19, 2018 the company had received net proceeds of $2.3 billion from selling 226,804,071 shares, contributed to BREIT OP for corresponding units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]

## What was assembled at the ownership boundary (the customer-facing assets)
- During the year ended December 31, 2017 the company closed 27 transactions with a total purchase price of $3.6 billion, and invested $3.6 billion in real estate consisting of 92 wholly-owned properties and 23 properties through five joint ventures; as of December 31, 2017 it owned 27 real-estate investments and 37 CMBS positions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- Named portfolio acquisitions in the closing era include: TA Multifamily Portfolio (Apr. 13, 2017; six properties, 2,514 units; $432.6 million); HS Industrial Portfolio (Apr. 18, 2017; 38 industrial properties, ~6M sq ft; $405.9 million); Emory Point (May 2, 2017; 750-unit multifamily leasehold; $201.6 million); and the Aston Multifamily Portfolio (Nov. 15, 2017; twelve properties, 3,301 units; $381.4 million) acquired through a 90% joint venture with the seller. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- The portfolio was assembled by acquisition (not by internal development); the strategy "capitalizes on Blackstone's scale," and "after acquisition, we leverage Blackstone Real Estate's established asset management team, which focuses on value creation through the oversight and improvement of the operating performance" of the holdings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- The company had no in-house operating organization: "We have no employees. Our operations are conducted by the Adviser." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- Under the Advisory Agreement the company "delegated to the Adviser the authority to source, evaluate and monitor our investment opportunities and to make decisions related to the acquisition, management, financing and disposition of our assets," and the Adviser also provides "our executive management team"; the chief executive officer, chief financial officer and other executive officers "are senior Blackstone real estate professionals." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- The Adviser and personnel it supplies "are each not obligated to dedicate any specific amount of time to our business"; the Adviser draws on "the 426 professionals in Blackstone's global real estate group." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- The acquisition, management, financing and disposition functions for the assembled assets are performed by the external Adviser under the Advisory Agreement rather than by the registrant's own staff; the Adviser is entitled to a base management fee (based on NAV) and expense reimbursements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- BREIT Special Limited Partner L.L.C., a wholly owned Blackstone subsidiary, holds a performance participation allocation in BREIT OP; the Adviser and Special Limited Partner "may choose to receive, and have in the past received," common stock or Operating Partnership units in lieu of certain fees or distributions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
- The company has an uncommitted line of credit from an affiliate of Blackstone (Blackstone Holdings Finance Co. L.L.C.), drawn on to help fund several closing-era acquisitions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents a charter/governance amendment (fee-cap mechanics, per-share to per-account), not a property or portfolio purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-236870, 8-K Items 5.03/5.07]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is an externally managed, non-traded perpetual-life REIT that assembles a diversified real-estate portfolio by acquisition (27 transactions / $3.6 billion in 2017), funded by a continuous best-efforts equity offering, while the acquisition, property-management, financing and disposition processes and the people who run them are supplied by the external Blackstone Adviser and asset-management team — the company itself having no employees and conducting its operations through the Adviser. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-006224, 10-K FY2017]
