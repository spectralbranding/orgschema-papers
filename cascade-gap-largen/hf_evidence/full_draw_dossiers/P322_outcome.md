## Realized financial trajectory
- Total net revenues for the year ended December 31, 2021 were $3,167,361 thousand,
  versus $3,705,597 thousand for 2020 and $1,477,404 thousand for 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income for the year ended December 31, 2021 was $1,003,490 thousand (diluted EPS
  $14.87), down from $1,646,884 thousand in 2020 (diluted EPS $20.92) and up from
  $392,965 thousand in 2019 (diluted EPS $4.89); return on average stockholders' equity
  was 28.9% for 2021 versus 61.4% for 2020 and 21.6% for 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Total net revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022 were $1,985,755 thousand,
  versus $3,167,361 thousand for 2021 and $3,705,597 thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-001755, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income for the year ended December 31, 2022 was $475,507 thousand, versus
  $1,003,490 thousand for 2021 and $1,646,884 thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-001755, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Segment income before provision for income taxes for 2021 was reported as $1,044,411
  thousand for the Production segment, $306,678 thousand for the Servicing segment, and
  $8,094 thousand for Investment management (mortgage banking segments totaling
  $1,351,089 thousand), versus $1,964,121 thousand / $262,144 thousand / $14,344 thousand
  respectively for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]

## Documented items over time
- Interest rate lock commitments issued, a mortgage-production activity volume measure,
  were $141,433,359 thousand for 2021 versus $125,614,670 thousand for 2020 and
  $72,698,014 thousand for 2019 — i.e., origination-activity volume continued rising even
  as net income declined in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- The unpaid principal balance of the owned mortgage servicing portfolio (mortgage
  servicing rights and liabilities) was $278,385,373 thousand at December 31, 2021 versus
  $241,268,301 thousand at December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- The entity's common stock closing price ranged from a low of $56.53 to a high of $70.57
  during 2021 (closing 2021 at $70.57), compared with a range of $16.90 to $69.49 during
  2020 (closing 2020 at $65.62). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Dividends declared per share were $0.80 for 2021, $0.54 for 2020, and $0.12 for 2019 —
  a rising per-share distribution across the window notwithstanding the 2021-2022 income
  decline. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The Successor entity (CIK 0001745916) continued to operate and report publicly under
  the name "PennyMac Financial Services, Inc." and the NYSE ticker "PFSI" throughout the
  outcome window, filing uninterrupted annual reports through fiscal year 2022 and beyond.
  Its realized trajectory across the window shows total net revenues declining from
  $3,705,597 thousand (2020) to $3,167,361 thousand (2021, -14.5%) to $1,985,755 thousand
  (2022, -37.3%), and net income declining from $1,646,884 thousand (2020) to $1,003,490

  to grow year over year and per-share dividends continued to rise. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-001740, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-001755, Form 10-K FY2022]
