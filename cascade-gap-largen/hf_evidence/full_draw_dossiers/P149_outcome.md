  (Form 10-Q) and Form 8-K filings dated through October 3, 2012, and no Form 10-K or other

  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]

## Realized business trajectory (from the FY2011 10-K)
- The registrant reported it had "completed divestitures of all of our businesses" —
  including CDMA/LTE to Ericsson, Enterprise Solutions to Avaya, the Packet Core / Next
  Generation Packet Core assets to Hitachi, Optical Networking and Carrier Ethernet to
  Ciena, GSM/GSM-R to Ericsson and Kapsch, Carrier VoIP and Application Solutions to
  GENBAND, and other business and joint-venture interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- It also sold its remaining patents and patent applications (more than 6,000 patents and
  applications) to a consortium of Apple, EMC, Ericsson, Microsoft, Research in Motion and
  Sony for a cash purchase price of US$4,500 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- Approximately US$7,730 million in net proceeds had been generated and received through
  the completed sales of businesses and patents, with substantially all proceeds held in
  escrow pending a final allocation among the various Nortel legal entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]

## Best-documented realized scalars
- Revenues collapsed to US$27 million in 2011 from US$620 million in 2010 (a decrease of
  US$593 million), a decline the registrant attributed to the divestitures of all of its
  businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- The workforce fell from approximately 30,300 employees globally at the January 2009
  petition date to approximately 190 employees as of December 31, 2011 (a figure that
  excludes employees of U.S. and EMEA subsidiaries); the registrant stated approximately
  16,000 jobs were preserved through the sales of its businesses to the purchasers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- Net earnings attributable to NNC were US$3,670 million for 2011, reflecting gains from
  the completed divestitures and patent sale rather than ongoing operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- The registrant disclosed substantial doubt as to its ability to continue as a going
  concern, stating that failure to conclude the creditor-protection proceedings within the
  time granted by the courts would in all likelihood lead to the liquidation of all of its
  assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]

## Realized product/process/organization interface over time
- The registrant described itself as focused on "the sale of our remaining assets, the
  wind-down of our global operations and entities," the creditor-claims process, and
  working toward conclusion of the proceedings, and stated that as a result of the
  divestitures it "no longer invest[s] in R&D" and expected R&D expense to be nil for the
  remainder of the proceedings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]
- The operating processes did not transfer intact with the sold products: a Nortel Business
  Services group established in 2009 provided global transitional services to the purchasers
  (finance, supply chain, IT, R&D, human resources and real estate) for generally up to 24
  months from each closing, and the registrant reported that as of December 31, 2011 it had
  completed substantially all of its obligations under the transition services agreements
  for the business sales. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The realized record within the fixed 2012–2014 window is one of wind-down: all operating
  businesses and the residual patent portfolio were sold, revenues fell to US$27 million,
  the workforce contracted to roughly 190 employees, going-concern doubt was disclosed, and
  the entity operated only to sell remaining assets and administer creditor claims. Separate
  public periodic reporting ceased after 2012 with no annual report filed for FY2012 or
  later, so the deeper 2013–2014 operating tail has no separate public record; there is no
  going-concern operating entity with a full 3–5-year operating trajectory to observe. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-103698, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-414094, Form 8-K]
