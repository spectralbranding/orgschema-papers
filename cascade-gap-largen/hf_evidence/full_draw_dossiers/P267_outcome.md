# Outcome sub-dossier — NorthStar Real Estate Income II realized record after combination, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Because NorthStar II merged into, and its separate
> corporate existence ended within, a newly formed combined company, there is no
> post-closing 10-K filed by NorthStar II itself; this sub-dossier traces what, if
> anything, the surviving public company's own filings disclose about the merged
> business. It contains no information about how the transaction was structured at
> closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- NorthStar Real Estate Income II, Inc.'s last annual report as a standalone registrant
  was its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016, filed March 17, 2017; no Form 10-K covering
  fiscal year 2017 or later was filed under its own CIK. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001564657]
- On February 1, 2018, Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc., acting "as successor by
  merger to NorthStar Real Estate Income II, Inc.," filed a Form 15-12G certifying
  termination of NorthStar II's registration and stating that "NorthStar Real Estate
  Income II, Inc. merged with and into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. on
  January 31, 2018, at which time the separate corporate existence of NorthStar Real
  Estate Income II, Inc. ended." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029431, Form 15-12G]
- No annual report was subsequently filed by NorthStar II; the only entity that continued
  to report to the SEC after the transaction is the surviving combined company, under CIK
  1717547. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001564657] [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001717547]

## What the surviving company's later filings disclose about the merged business
- The surviving company's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 25, 2021, roughly
  three years after the January 2018 closing) reports results only on a fully consolidated
  basis, combining the assets contributed by CLNS with the businesses of NorthStar I and
  NorthStar II "beginning February 1, 2018, following the closing of the Combination," and
  states that "comparisons of the period to period financial information of the Company...
  may not be meaningful" as a result. [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- That filing contains no segment, division, or business-unit breakout attributable
  specifically to the former NorthStar II business or portfolio; its remaining references
  to NorthStar II are limited to (a) historical narrative describing the 2018 combination,
  (b) risk-factor language about NorthStar II's REIT-qualification status for periods
  before the merger, and (c) purchase-accounting notes recording the assets and
  noncontrolling interests assumed from NorthStar I and NorthStar II at the 2018 closing
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- One pre-merger transaction originated by NorthStar II is mentioned as having carried
  forward: a joint venture NorthStar II entered in July 2017 for a $60.0 million
  investment in a $180.0 million mezzanine loan, which "was purchased by the Company in
  connection with the Combination." No post-2018 performance figures specific to that
  investment, or to the former NorthStar II book generally, are disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Items noted but not primary-verifiable as NorthStar-II-specific
- SEC registrant records show the surviving company was named Colony NorthStar Credit
  Real Estate, Inc. at closing, was renamed Colony Credit Real Estate, Inc. in 2018, and
  was renamed again to BrightSpire Capital, Inc. in 2021; because these name changes
  attach to the combined successor entity as a whole (which also carries the CLNS-
  contributed portfolio and the former NorthStar I business), they are not attributable to
  the former NorthStar II business specifically and are recorded here only as public-
  registrant context, not as a NorthStar-II outcome. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001717547.json]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because NorthStar II's separate corporate existence ended at the January 2018 closing
  and its assets were combined with those of NorthStar I and a CLNS-contributed portfolio
  into one surviving public company, no public 3-5-year outcome record exists that is
  specific to the acquired NorthStar II business; the surviving company's later Form 10-K
  filings report only fully consolidated results across all three combined predecessor
  businesses and do not separately disclose the former NorthStar II business's post-close
  trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029431, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
