# Structural sub-dossier — Arch Coal, Inc. Chapter 11 plan-of-reorganization emergence (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Arch Coal, Inc. and certain of its direct and indirect subsidiaries (the "Debtors")
  had filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on January 11, 2016 in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the Eastern District of Missouri. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- On September 13, 2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered an order confirming the Debtors'
  Fourth Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization (the "Plan"), amended September 15, 2016;
  on October 5, 2016 (the "Effective Date") Arch Coal consummated the transactions
  contemplated by the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- The reorganization retained Arch Coal, Inc. as the same continuing legal entity
  (Delaware corporation, IRS EIN 43-0921172); no separate acquirer entity is named as
  taking the business — the ownership boundary crossed was between pre-petition
  claimholders/equityholders and a new post-emergence capital structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the ownership/control boundary at closing
- Controlling equity interest: lenders that had committed to the Debtor-in-Possession
  ("DIP") Credit Agreement received shares of new Common Stock under the Plan and,
  collectively, held 94% of Arch Coal's Common Stock upon emergence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- New capital structure: a New First Lien Debt Facility (senior secured term loan,
  $326.5 million aggregate principal, 5-year maturity, Wilmington Trust as
  administrative/collateral agent) was entered into as a condition of Plan
  effectiveness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- The pre-existing $200 million trade-receivables Securitization Facility (through
  wholly owned SPE Arch Receivable Company, LLC) was extended and amended rather than
  replaced. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- All outstanding obligations under five series of pre-petition senior/second-lien
  notes, the Prepetition Credit Agreement, and the DIP Credit Agreement were cancelled
  or paid in full and terminated by operation of the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- 24,589,834 shares of new Class A Common Stock and 410,166 shares of new Class B
  Common Stock were distributed to secured lenders and certain general-unsecured
  claimholders under a Securities Act Section 1145(a)(1) exemption; warrants for up to
  1,914,856 additional Class A shares were issued to holders of claims under the
  cancelled notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- A new Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation and Amended and Restated
  Bylaws were filed/adopted on the Effective Date, and the Class A Common Stock was
  newly authorized for trading and listed on the NYSE (symbol "ARCH"), ceasing trading
  on the OTC Pink Sheets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]

## What did NOT cross unchanged: governance versus operating management
- Board of directors: ten pre-petition directors ceased to serve on the Effective Date;
  only one existing director, John W. Eaves, continued; six new directors (selected by
  holders of more than 66⅔% of the outstanding principal amount of Prepetition Credit
  Agreement loans, in consultation with Eaves) were added, setting the board at seven
  with a new, previously non-executive Chairman (James N. Chapman). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- Executive officers: by contrast, every existing executive officer continued in the
  same role by operation of the Plan — John W. Eaves as Chief Executive Officer, Paul A.
  Lang as President and Chief Operating Officer, John T. Drexler as Chief Financial
  Officer, Kenneth D. Cochran as Senior Vice President-Operations, Robert G. Jones as
  General Counsel, John A. Ziegler, Jr. as Chief Commercial Officer, Deck S. Slone as
  Senior Vice President-Strategy and Public Policy, and Allen R. Kelley as Vice
  President-Human Resources. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- New indemnification agreements were entered into between Arch Coal and each director
  and executive officer as of the Effective Date, replacing prior agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
- The 8-K describing this closing does not reference any transitional-services,
  operating, or shared-services agreement bridging a carved-out business unit; the
  transaction is structured as a whole-entity balance-sheet reorganization rather than a
  separation of a product line from a parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, controlling equity interest and board composition changed substantially
  (94% new-equity concentration among former secured lenders; nine of ten prior
  directors replaced), while the customer-facing coal-mining business and its full
  executive-officer layer running day-to-day operations carried forward unchanged
  under the same continuing legal entity, bridged by a new first-lien credit facility
  and an extended receivables-securitization facility rather than by any transitional
  services arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-149399, Form 8-K]
