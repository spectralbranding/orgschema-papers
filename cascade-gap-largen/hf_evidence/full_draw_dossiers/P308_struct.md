# Structural sub-dossier — Athens Bancshares Corporation combination with CapStar Financial Holdings, Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 11, 2018, Athens Bancshares Corporation ("Athens") and CapStar Financial
  Holdings, Inc. ("CapStar") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, pursuant to
  which Athens will merge with and into CapStar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- Immediately following completion of the holding-company merger, Athens Federal
  Community Bank, National Association (Athens's wholly owned bank subsidiary) will
  merge with and into CapStar Bank (CapStar's wholly owned bank subsidiary), with
  CapStar Bank continuing as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed effective October 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-288670, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At signing, CapStar (on a consolidated basis, as of March 31, 2018) had total assets
  of $1.4 billion, total deposits of $1.1 billion, total net loans of $1.0 billion, and
  shareholders' equity of $148.7 million. Athens (as of March 31, 2018) had total
  consolidated assets of $482.3 million, gross loans of $334.5 million, deposits of
  $422.2 million, and stockholders' equity of $53.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and legal vehicle
- Each share of Athens common stock issued and outstanding immediately prior to the
  effective time (other than certain excluded shares) was converted into the right to
  receive 2.864 shares of CapStar common stock (a stock-for-stock exchange ratio, the
  "merger consideration"); no cash consideration was paid other than for fractional
  shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- Athens, the acquired legal entity, ceases to exist as a separate corporation upon the
  merger; CapStar continues as the surviving legal vehicle. Athens Federal Community
  Bank's national bank charter is likewise absorbed into CapStar Bank's Tennessee state
  charter, with CapStar Bank as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- CapStar common stock continued trading on the NASDAQ Global Select Market under
  symbol "CSTR" after the merger; upon completion of the merger, Athens common stock
  (previously quoted on the OTCQX Market under symbol "AFCB") was no longer quoted on
  the OTCQX Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]

## Product, process, and people tiers
- CapStar Bank's customer-facing products are commercial and industrial loans (with a
  focus on healthcare-industry businesses), commercial real estate loans, private
  banking/wealth management, and correspondent banking for smaller Tennessee community
  banks, concentrated in the Nashville Metropolitan Statistical Area. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- Athens Federal's customer-facing products are traditional consumer and business
  banking products delivered through ten branch offices and one lending center in
  Athens, Sweetwater, Etowah, Madisonville, Cleveland, and Lenoir City, Tennessee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- As an operational-process detail, Athens "outsources a significant portion of its
  data processing to third-party providers." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, CapStar agreed, prior to the closing date, to appoint two
  members of Athens's then-current board of directors to the boards of directors of
  both CapStar and CapStar Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
- Certain Athens/Athens Community Bank executive officers were to enter new employment
  agreements with CapStar and CapStar Bank effective upon the merger; separately, the
  pre-existing employment agreements and supplemental executive retirement plan
  agreements between Athens (or Athens Community Bank) and certain executives were to
  be terminated as of the effective time, with associated benefits paid by CapStar in a
  single lump sum. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]

## Stated rationale (as disclosed to shareholders)
- Factors the Athens board considered included that its stand-alone financial
  projections would not, even if fully executed, achieve organic shareholder value
  comparable to the merger; the employment prospects for Athens's employees within the
  combined company; and the parties' "shared corporate values." Factors the CapStar
  board considered included creating a combined community bank with nearly $2.0
  billion in assets, the "scale, scope, strength and diversity of operations, product
  lines and delivery systems" achievable together, and "the likelihood of successful
  integration and operation of the combined company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a Nashville-concentrated commercial/private-banking
  franchise (CapStar) with a smaller southeastern-Tennessee retail/community-banking
  franchise (Athens) via a stock-for-stock holding-company merger and a parallel
  bank-level merger into a single surviving bank charter, with two Athens board seats
  and new executive employment agreements as the mechanisms bridging Athens's
  management/governance substrate into the combined entity, and Athens's own IT/data-
  processing function already run through third-party outsourcing at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-227829, DEFM14A]
