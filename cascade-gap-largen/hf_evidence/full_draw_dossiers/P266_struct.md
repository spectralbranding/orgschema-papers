# Structural sub-dossier — Starwood Waypoint Homes combination with Invitation Homes (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Starwood Waypoint Homes ("SFR"), a Maryland real estate investment trust headquartered
  in Scottsdale, Arizona, agreed to combine with Invitation Homes Inc. ("INVH"), a
  Maryland corporation taxed as a REIT and headquartered in Dallas, Texas, through a REIT
  merger in which SFR would merge with and into a wholly-owned INVH merger subsidiary,
  with the merger subsidiary surviving as a subsidiary of INVH, followed by a parallel
  merger of the two companies' operating partnerships. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- Both companies were described in the filing as engaged in the same line of business:
  acquiring single-family rental properties, renovating them, and leasing them to
  residents; the filing states the two companies "have been engaged in the single-family
  rental business in many of the same markets" and "have been familiar with each" other.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- Consideration was fixed stock-for-stock: each SFR common share would convert into
  1.6140 newly issued shares of INVH common stock (the "Exchange Ratio"), with SFR
  operating-partnership units converting at the same 1.6140 ratio into INVH operating-
  partnership units; no cash consideration other than for fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- On a pro forma basis, continuing INVH stockholders were expected to hold approximately
  59.1% of the combined company's fully diluted equity and former SFR shareholders the
  remaining 40.9%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- The combined company was structured to retain the INVH name and Maryland REIT status,
  continue trading on the NYSE under symbol "INVH," and be headquartered at INVH's
  existing Dallas, Texas address; the filing describes the pro forma combined portfolio
  at approximately 82,000 single-family homes and an equity market capitalization of
  approximately $12 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]

## Accounting characterization of the combination
- The transaction was structured to be accounted for as a business combination under the
  acquisition method (ASC 805), with INVH identified as the accounting acquirer. The
  filing bases this on INVH being the entity issuing equity, continuing INVH
  stockholders' expected ~59.1% ownership, INVH's current directors representing the
  majority of the combined board, and INVH's current executive officers representing a
  majority of the combined executive team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing — governance and controlling intent
- Prior to the transaction, affiliates of Blackstone (the "Blackstone Stockholders")
  held approximately 71% of INVH common stock; the filing discusses that the merger
  would dilute the Blackstone Stockholders' position to approximately 41%, and records
  board discussion of adjusting the Blackstone Stockholders' existing stockholders-
  agreement representation rights on the INVH board as part of the negotiation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- The post-closing board of the combined company was structured to consist of eleven
  members, with Bryce Blair (an existing INVH director) appointed Chairman of the board
  and Michael D. Fascitelli appointed Chairman of the Investment Committee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing — people, management, and product/process alignment
- The combined company's executive officers were structured to be a mix drawn from both
  predecessor companies: Frederick C. Tuomi (SFR's President and Chief Executive
  Officer) was designated Chief Executive Officer of the combined company, while Ernest
  M. Freedman (INVH's Chief Financial Officer) and Dallas B. Tanner (INVH's Chief
  Investment Officer) retained their respective roles in the combined company; Charles D.
  Young was designated Chief Operating Officer, Arik Prawer was designated Chief
  Integration Officer, and Mark A. Solls (INVH's Chief Legal Officer) retained that role.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- The combined company's principal executive offices were structured to be located at
  INVH's existing Dallas, Texas address rather than SFR's Scottsdale, Arizona address,
  i.e., the surviving company's physical headquarters location carried over from the
  acquirer side even though the designated Chief Executive Officer carried over from the
  target side. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- INVH separately entered into letter agreements (effective upon closing) with its own
  existing executive officers — including its then-President and CEO and its CFO, CIO,
  Chief Revenue Officer, Chief Operations Officer, and Chief Legal Officer — because the
  merger would not otherwise trigger "change in control" provisions under INVH's existing
  compensatory plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
- The filing's risk factors flag that combining "the operations, network, systems,
  technologies, policies and procedures of the two companies" would involve significant
  fees and expenses and that realizing anticipated cost savings and synergies from
  integrating the two operating platforms was not assured — i.e., the customer-facing
  product (the rental-home portfolios) and the underlying operating platforms/processes
  of the two companies were structured to be combined post-closing rather than kept
  separate, with that combination itself identified as a source of execution risk. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]

## Closing-time listing/registration change
- It was a condition to completion that INVH common stock issuable in the merger be
  authorized for NYSE listing; upon completion, the SFR common shares were structured to
  be removed from NYSE listing and deregistered under the Securities Exchange Act, with
  SFR shareholders instead holding NYSE-listed INVH stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a stock-for-stock combination of two same-industry
  single-family-rental REITs, with INVH as the accounting acquirer, INVH's name/ticker/
  headquarters and a majority of its board and executive team carrying forward, while the
  designated CEO of the combined company and a minority of directors were drawn from SFR;
  the two companies' operating platforms, systems, and processes were structured to be
  integrated post-closing rather than run in parallel, and SFR's separate NYSE listing
  and Exchange Act registration were structured to terminate at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-310310, DEFM14A]
