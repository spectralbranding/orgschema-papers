# Structural sub-dossier — FelCor Lodging Trust Incorporated merger with RLJ Lodging Trust (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- FelCor Lodging Trust Incorporated, a Maryland corporation and REIT traded on the NYSE
  under "FCH," owned a diversified portfolio of primarily upper-upscale full-service
  hotels through FelCor Lodging Limited Partnership ("FelCor LP"), in which FelCor held
  a greater-than-99.5% partnership interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- RLJ Lodging Trust, RLJ Lodging Trust, L.P. ("RLJ LP"), Rangers Sub I, LLC (a Maryland
  LLC and wholly owned subsidiary of RLJ LP, the "REIT Merger Sub"), Rangers Sub II, LP
  (a Delaware LP and indirect wholly owned subsidiary of RLJ LP, the "Partnership Merger
  Sub"), FelCor and FelCor LP entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April
  23, 2017. Both merger-sub entities were formed solely for the purpose of engaging in
  the transactions contemplated by the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as two simultaneous mergers: FelCor merged with and
  into REIT Merger Sub, with REIT Merger Sub surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  RLJ LP (the "REIT Merger"); and Partnership Merger Sub merged with and into FelCor LP,
  with FelCor LP surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of RLJ LP (the
  "Partnership Merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]
- Consideration was stock-for-stock at a fixed exchange ratio: each FelCor common share
  converted into the right to receive 0.362 newly issued RLJ common shares (a taxable
  transaction), and each FelCor Series A Preferred share converted into one newly issued
  RLJ Series A Preferred share with equivalent rights; the exchange ratio was fixed and
  not adjusted for market-value changes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, continuing RLJ shareholders were expected to own approximately 71% of
  the combined company's fully diluted equity and former FelCor stockholders the
  remaining 29%; the combined company retained the name "RLJ Lodging Trust" and
  continued to be listed on the NYSE under symbol "RLJ." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- The Partnership Merger became effective at 11:55 p.m. Eastern time and the REIT Merger
  at 11:56 p.m. Eastern time, both on August 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]

## What FelCor comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Through FelCor LP, FelCor held ownership interests in 39 hotels with 11,500 rooms as
  of March 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately after the Mergers, the combined company's asset base consisted primarily
  of interests in a portfolio of 161 hotels operating in 26 states and the District of
  Columbia, with the combined portfolio diversified across Marriott, Hilton, Hyatt and
  Wyndham-flagged hotels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- FelCor's hotels operated under third-party brand franchise and hotel-management
  arrangements typical of the lodging-REIT structure (a taxable REIT subsidiary may not
  itself operate or manage a lodging facility; an eligible independent contractor
  performs day-to-day management under franchise/management agreements). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- In connection with completion of the Mergers, FelCor notified the NYSE that its common
  and Series A Preferred stock had been cancelled and converted into RLJ shares, and the
  NYSE filed a Form 25 with the SEC to effect removal of FelCor's common and preferred
  stock from listing, terminating their Section 12(b) registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]
- The Commission file number previously used by FelCor Lodging Trust Incorporated was
  carried forward on the closing 8-K under the successor caption "(Rangers Sub I, LLC,
  as successor by merger to FelCor Lodging Trust Incorporated)," reflecting that REIT
  Merger Sub, not FelCor, was the surviving legal vehicle at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]

## Management/organization tier: did not carry over with the acquired entity
- Effective at the REIT Merger Effective Time, all members of FelCor's Board of
  Directors were removed as directors of FelCor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]
- Each of FelCor's named executive officers — Steven R. Goldman (CEO), Troy A. Pentecost
  (President & COO), Thomas C. Hendrick (EVP & CIO), Michael C. Hughes (EVP & CFO),
  Jonathan H. Yellen (EVP, General Counsel & Secretary), Robert P. Carl (SVP, Design &
  Construction) and Jeffrey D. Symes (SVP, CAO & Treasurer) — was removed from the
  listed position effective at the REIT Merger Effective Time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]
- FelCor's Chairman and each executive officer were party to change-in-control and
  severance agreements providing single-trigger accelerated equity vesting on a change
  in control (such as the REIT Merger) and, on a qualifying termination, a lump-sum cash
  severance payment equal to 2.99 times (2.50 times for one named executive) applicable
  compensation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- By contrast, all seven of RLJ's incumbent trustees were expected to continue as
  trustees of the combined company (board expanded to eight with one FelCor-nominated
  director added), and RLJ's Executive Chairman, President/CEO/Chief Investment
  Officer, and Chief Operating Officer/Chief Financial Officer were each expected to
  continue in the same roles for the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services, shared-services, or management-services agreement bridging
  FelCor's corporate functions into the combined company was identified in the reviewed
  merger agreement or closing filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004631, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company, stock-for-stock merger of FelCor's hotel
  portfolio (the acquired asset/product tier) into RLJ's ownership structure, with
  FelCor's separate corporate existence, board, and named executive management (the
  people/organization tier) replaced at the same closing moment by RLJ's continuing
  trustees and executive officers, rather than being retained or bridged by any
  transitional agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055052, Form 8-K]
