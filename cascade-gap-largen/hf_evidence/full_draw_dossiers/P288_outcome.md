# Outcome sub-dossier — Liberty Interactive Corporation / Qurate Retail, Inc. realized record, ~3-year horizon after the 2018 closing

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The registrant continued filing periodic reports
> with the SEC after the transaction closed, so the realized record below is sourced to
> its subsequent Form 10-K. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the
> transaction was structured at closing.

## Registrant identity and reporting status after the transaction
- The registrant (CIK 0001355096) continued as a public SEC filer after the
  transaction; its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 identifies it as "Qurate Retail,
  Inc. ("Qurate Retail", the "Company", "we", "us" and "our"), formerly known as
  Liberty Interactive Corporation prior to the Transactions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- As of the FY2020 10-K, the Company's principal businesses and assets included its
  consolidated subsidiaries QVC, Inc. (which includes HSN, Inc.), Zulily, LLC, and
  Cornerstone Brands, Inc., along with other cost- and equity-method investments.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The Company stated that its 2018 split-off of assets into GCI Liberty (the
  transaction structured at closing) "did not have a major effect on Qurate Retail's
  historical results nor is it expected to have a major effect on Qurate Retail's
  future operations," and that Evite and LendingTree were accordingly not presented
  as discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized financial record (best-documented outcome scalars)
- The Company reported net earnings of $1,262 million for the year ended December 31,
  2020, a net loss of $405 million for the year ended December 31, 2019, and net
  earnings of $964 million for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Total revenue, net, was $14,177 million for 2020, $13,458 million for 2019, and
  $14,070 million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Impairment of intangible assets and long-lived assets was $1,167 million for 2019,
  $33 million for 2018, and nil for 2020; the 2019 impairment related to Zulily
  intangible assets, taken in the third quarter of 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Consolidated operating income increased $1,388 million in 2020 versus 2019 and had
  decreased $1,140 million in 2019 versus 2018; the 2020 increase was attributed
  primarily to the absence of the prior-year Zulily intangible-asset impairment.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- As of December 31, 2020, the Company's wholly owned subsidiary, LI LLC, had $1,986
  million principal amount of publicly traded debt outstanding, and was also a
  co-obligor on $218 million principal amount of 3.5% Senior Exchangeable Debentures
  due 2031. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Other documented developments in the window
- The FY2020 10-K attributed part of a 2018 reduction in Corporate-and-other operating
  losses to "the elimination of corporate costs at the Liberty Ventures Group due to
  the GCI Liberty Split-Off in 2018," confirming the split-off closed in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The 10-K's forward-looking-statement risk factors identify the COVID-19 pandemic as
  a factor that could affect the Company's customers, vendors and businesses, its
  private-label credit-card program collections, and its increasingly remote
  workforce, without providing a single quantified full-year revenue or earnings
  impact figure in the sections reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~2018-2020 window, the registrant (renamed Qurate Retail, Inc.) continued
  to file periodic reports and recorded a swing from net earnings of $964 million
  (2018) to a net loss of $405 million (2019), driven substantially by a $1,167
  million Zulily intangible-asset impairment, then to net earnings of $1,262 million
  (2020) as that impairment did not recur; total revenue held in a comparatively
  narrow $13.5-14.2 billion range across the three years; and the Company's own 10-K
  states that the 2018 split-off transaction did not have a major effect on its
  historical or expected future results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Form 10-K FY2020]
