## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports on Form 10-K throughout the window; the fiscal-year-2014 annual report was filed 2015-02-25 as "The Babcock & Wilcox Company," Commission File Number 001-34658. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2014]

## Realized financial trajectory (fiscal years 2013-2014)
- Consolidated revenues decreased 10.6%, or $346.2 million, to $2.9 billion in the year ended December 31, 2014 compared with $3.3 billion in 2013, driven by declines in the Power Generation, Technical Services and Nuclear Energy segments ($281.6 million, $19.4 million and $129.1 million, respectively), partially offset by a $53.3 million increase in Nuclear Operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Consolidated operating income decreased $539.0 million, to an operating LOSS of $2.6 million in the year ended December 31, 2014, compared with operating income of $536.4 million in 2013; operating income in both years included non-cash pension/postretirement mark-to-market amounts of $(241.2) million in 2014 and $222.7 million in 2013, and special restructuring charges of $41.1 million (2014) and $39.6 million (2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Net income attributable to The Babcock & Wilcox Company was $29.388 million in 2014, down from $346.078 million in 2013 (basic earnings per common share of $0.27 in 2014 versus $3.09 in 2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The registrant completed its annual review of goodwill as of December 31, 2014 and reported no impairment of goodwill, stating the fair value of its reporting units was substantially in excess of carrying value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Documented operational restructuring and program retrenchment
- On April 14, 2014 the registrant announced plans to restructure its mPower small-modular-reactor program to reduce spending and focus on technology development; beginning in the third quarter of 2014 it slowed the pace of development and intended to invest no more than $15 million annually while searching for additional investors in the program. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The registrant continued its Global Competitiveness Initiative (launched in the third quarter of 2012) and additional restructuring activities aimed at improving competitiveness and margins; it incurred $26.8 million of costs on certain of these initiatives for the year ended December 31, 2014 and expected further restructuring charges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Realized structural reorganization within the window
- On November 5, 2014 the registrant announced plans to separate its Power Generation business from its Government & Nuclear Operations business (Nuclear Operations, Technical Services, Nuclear Energy and mPower segments) through a tax-free spin-off, creating a new independent, publicly traded power generation company, Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises, Inc.; concurrently the Company would change its name to BWX Technologies, Inc. ("BWXT"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Management stated it intended to spin off the Power Generation business by mid-summer 2015 and noted the planned separation could result in turnover in its workforce; the ability to complete the spin-off within the expected time frame and without significant disruption was disclosed as a risk. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the ~2013-2015 window the registrant's realized record shows declining consolidated revenues ($3.3 billion to $2.9 billion, 2013 to 2014), a swing from $536.4 million of operating income in 2013 to a $2.6 million operating loss in 2014 (net income attributable falling from $346.1 million to $29.4 million; EPS $3.09 to $0.27), no goodwill impairment at year-end 2014, ongoing multi-year restructuring (Global Competitiveness Initiative; April 2014 retrenchment of the mPower program to no more than $15 million annual spend), and a decision announced November 5, 2014 to reorganize by spinning off the Power Generation business into Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises, Inc. while renaming the registrant BWX Technologies, Inc., targeted for mid-2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-062809, Form 10-K FY2014]
