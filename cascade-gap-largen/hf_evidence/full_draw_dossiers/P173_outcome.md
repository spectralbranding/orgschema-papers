## Continuity and identity of the reporting entity
- The registrant (Commission File Number 001-35512) continued filing periodic reports on EDGAR after the October 2016 emergence; it did not deregister. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K cover page]
- On August 6, 2019, Midstates completed a stock-for-stock business combination (the "Merger") with Amplify Energy Corp. ("Legacy Amplify"), under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated May 5, 2019, effected through merger subsidiaries of Midstates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- On the effective date of the Merger, Midstates changed its corporate name to "Amplify Energy Corp."; each share of Legacy Amplify common stock was converted into 0.933 shares of Midstates (renamed Amplify) common stock, and following closing, Legacy Amplify stockholders and Midstates stockholders each held approximately 50% of the combined company's outstanding stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- For financial-reporting purposes the Merger was accounted for as a "reverse merger," with Legacy Amplify treated as the accounting acquirer; Legacy Amplify's historical results of operations replaced Midstates' historical results of operations for all periods presented prior to the Merger in the Company's post-Merger financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- The registrant's principal executive offices were located at 500 Dallas Street, Suite 1700, Houston, Texas as of the FY2019 Form 10-K, a different city from the Tulsa, Oklahoma address on file at the 2016 emergence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K cover page]
- The FY2019 Form 10-K was signed by Kenneth Mariani as President, Chief Executive Officer and Director (Principal Executive Officer) and by Martyn Willsher as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer; neither individual was among the three named executive officers in place at the October 2016 emergence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K signature page]

## Realized financial trajectory within the outcome window
- Upon closing of the August 2019 Merger, the reserve-based revolving credit facility that Midstates had in place was terminated and all remaining borrowings were repaid. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- The combined company reported net income (loss) of $(35,197) thousand for the year ended December 31, 2019, compared with $54,609 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K, results-of-operations table]
- The company recognized $2.2 million of impairment expense for the year ended December 31, 2019, related to certain unproved leasehold costs in Oklahoma attributed to changes in development plans; no impairment expense was recognized for the year ended December 31, 2018 or for the 2017 predecessor/successor periods. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- General and administrative expense was $55.3 million for 2019 versus $43.1 million for 2018; the 2019 figure includes $17.3 million of Merger-related acquisition expense and $7.3 million of severance payments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- Oil, natural gas and NGL revenues were $274.6 million for 2019 versus $339.8 million for 2018, with average net production volumes of approximately 25.4 MBoe/d in 2019 versus 26.6 MBoe/d in 2018; the filing attributes part of the year-over-year change to the Merger with Midstates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]

## Realized combined operating footprint
- Following the Merger, the combined company evaluated performance as a single reportable business segment, conducted through one wholly owned operating subsidiary, with producing oil and natural gas properties located in Oklahoma, the Rockies, federal waters offshore Southern California, East Texas/North Louisiana, and South Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
- Approximately 37% of the combined company's estimated proved reserves as of December 31, 2019 (the former Midstates asset base) were located in the Oklahoma region, primarily in Alfalfa and Woods counties; the remaining reserves were located in regions (Rockies, offshore Southern California, East Texas/North Louisiana) that had been Legacy Amplify's pre-Merger asset base. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2019-2021 outcome window following the October 2016 plan-effective date, the reorganized entity's separate corporate registration continued, but its name, accounting-acquirer status, headquarters location, and top executive leadership all changed through an August 2019 stock-for-stock combination with Amplify Energy Corp.; none of the three executives who signed employment agreements at the 2016 emergence appear as signing officers on the entity's subsequent annual report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K] The combined entity reported a net loss of $35.2 million and $2.2 million of impairment expense for fiscal year 2019, alongside $17.3 million of Merger-related acquisition costs and $7.3 million of severance payments recorded in the same year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-008959, Form 10-K]
