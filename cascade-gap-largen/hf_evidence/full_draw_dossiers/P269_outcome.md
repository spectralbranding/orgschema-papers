## Continuity of public reporting and identity (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant (CIK 1476204) continued filing annual reports on Form 10-K every year
  after the transaction through the outcome window and beyond; it did not deregister or go
  private. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history, CIK 0001476204]
- By fiscal year 2021, the registrant's exact name as specified in its charter had become
  "PHILLIPS EDISON & COMPANY, INC.," Commission file number 000-54691. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K cover page]

## Realized trajectory within the window: public listing event
- On June 18, 2021, stockholders approved Articles of Amendment that converted each
  outstanding share of the registrant's common stock into one share of a newly created
  class of Class B common stock, as part of the capital-structure recapitalization ahead of
  a public listing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K "Basis of Presentation--Recapitalization"]
- On July 19, 2021, the registrant closed an underwritten initial public offering, issuing
  19.6 million shares of common stock (including the underwriters' overallotment) at an
  initial public price of $28.00 per share, for gross proceeds of $547.4 million; the
  shares were listed on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the ticker symbol "PECO."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K "Underwritten IPO" / cover page Nasdaq listing]
- As of February 1, 2022, the registrant had approximately 113.4 million shares of common
  stock outstanding, held by approximately 17,463 stockholders of record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Item 5 "Market Information"]
- Prior to the underwritten IPO, the registrant disclosed that it "had no operating history
  as a publicly traded company" and flagged as a risk that it could not assure a sustained
  active trading market would develop. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Risk Factors "An active trading market for our common stock may not be maintained"]

## Realized financial record for the year within the window (FY2021 vs. FY2020)
- Total revenues were $532.8 million for fiscal year 2021, up 7.0% from $498.0 million in
  fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Results of Operations table]
- Net income was $17.2 million for fiscal year 2021, compared to net income of $5.5 million
  for fiscal year 2020 and a net loss of $(72.8) million for fiscal year 2019; net income
  attributable to stockholders was $15.1 million in 2021 versus $4.8 million in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Results of Operations table; FFO reconciliation table]
- Total assets were $4.669 billion as of December 31, 2021, compared to $4.679 billion as
  of December 31, 2020; total debt obligations, net, declined to $1.892 billion from $2.293
  billion over the same period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Consolidated Balance Sheet data]
- General and administrative expenses increased $7.4 million (18.0%) year over year; the
  filing attributes part of the increase — $1.2 million — to higher directors' and
  officers' insurance costs "as a result of our underwritten IPO," alongside a larger
  increase from lower 2020 performance-based compensation (itself attributed to the
  COVID-19 pandemic). [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K "General and Administrative Expenses" discussion]
- Fees and management income — the revenue line associated with the third-party
  asset-management business — was $10.3 million in fiscal year 2021, up 5.2% from $9.8
  million in fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Results of Operations table]
- A liability captioned "Earn-out liability" — a fair-valued contingent-consideration
  obligation dating to the 2017 transaction — grew from $22.0 million as of December 31,
  2020 to $52.4 million as of December 31, 2021; the filing attributes the $30.4 million
  fair-value change during 2021 in part to "the commencement of our underwritten IPO as
  well as improved market conditions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K Consolidated Balance Sheet; "Other (Expense) Income, Net" discussion]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year outcome window, the registrant's own periodic report shows a
  business that remained a continuously reporting public company, converted its equity
  structure and completed an underwritten public listing (July 2021, $547.4 million gross
  proceeds, Nasdaq: PECO), grew total revenue 7.0% and swung from prior-year net losses to
  consecutive years of net income, while also carrying a materially larger fair-valued
  contingent-consideration liability tied to the original transaction and an 18.0% increase
  in general and administrative costs partly attributed to newly public-company expenses.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001476204-22-000029, Form 10-K "Underwritten IPO"; Results of Operations table; "Other (Expense) Income, Net" discussion]
