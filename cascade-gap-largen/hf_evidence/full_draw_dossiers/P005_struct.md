# Structural sub-dossier — Gannett SpinCo, Inc. separation from Gannett Co., Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction separates the publishing business from the broadcasting and digital businesses of Gannett Co., Inc. (the "Parent") by means of a spin-off of a newly formed company, Gannett SpinCo, Inc. ("SpinCo"), which will own the publishing business; the Parent will continue to own and operate the broadcasting and digital businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- Upon completion of the spin-off, SpinCo will change its corporate name to "Gannett Co., Inc." and the Parent will change its corporate name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- SpinCo was formed as a Delaware corporation on November 21, 2014 for the purpose of holding the Parent's publishing business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The separation is effected by a pro rata distribution of 98.5% of the outstanding shares of SpinCo common stock to holders of Parent common stock; no vote of Parent stockholders is required and the distribution is expected to be tax-free to Parent stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- SpinCo intends to apply to list its common stock on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "GCI," the same symbol currently used by the Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing product/asset transferred is the publishing business: 100 daily publications in the U.S. and U.K., more than 400 non-daily U.S. publications, Newsquest (a U.K. regional community news provider), and USA TODAY, together with affiliated digital and mobile products and marketing services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Legal vehicle: pursuant to the separation and distribution agreement, the Parent plans to transfer to SpinCo, prior to the distribution, the equity interests of certain entities that operate the publishing business and the assets and liabilities of the publishing business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People/management: SpinCo's management team, led by Robert J. Dickey as President and Chief Executive Officer after the separation, is described as having extensive background in the publishing business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The publishing business's operating results reflect allocations of corporate expenses from the Parent for shared functions, and are stated to be likely less than the expenses SpinCo would have incurred operating as a separate public company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared substrate NOT transferred whole (closing-time)
- The information statement states that SpinCo's business is currently integrated with the Parent's other businesses and that SpinCo has historically shared "economies of scope and scale in costs, employees, vendor relationships and customer relationships," and that transitional arrangements may not fully retain those benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- At separation SpinCo and the Parent enter into a separation and distribution agreement plus certain other agreements — a transition services agreement, a tax matters agreement, and an employee matters agreement — providing for the allocation between SpinCo and Parent of the Parent's assets, employees, liabilities and obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the transition services agreement, the Parent and SpinCo will provide to each other, on an interim transitional basis, specified services including information technology, accounts payable, payroll, legal and other financial and administrative functions; the agreement generally terminates up to 24 months following the distribution date, with per-service early-termination rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the tax matters agreement, SpinCo is subject to restrictions and indemnification obligations intended to preserve the tax-free treatment of the separation and distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction detaches an integrated publishing operation (its titles, digital platforms, its named management team, and the entities that operate it) from a parent whose shared corporate service substrate (IT, payroll, accounts payable, legal, financial/administrative functions, tax and treasury allocations) it had historically relied on, with that substrate bridged at closing by a transition services agreement (up to 24 months) plus tax-matters and employee-matters agreements rather than transferred as an intact standalone function. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-15-002032, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
