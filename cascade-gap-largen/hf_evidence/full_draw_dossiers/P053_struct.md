## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Rex Energy Corporation's Form 8-K dated April 2, 2015 (earliest
  event March 27, 2015). Its Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement) is
  itself the deal-terms disclosure for the venture, so the closing structure is sourced
  directly from it rather than from a separate agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On March 30, 2015, Rex Energy Corporation (the "Company"), through its wholly owned
  subsidiary R. E. Gas Development, LLC ("Rex"), entered into a Participation Agreement
  with AL Marcellus Holdings, LLC ("ArcLight"), an affiliate of ArcLight Capital
  Partners, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company issued a press release on March 31, 2015 furnished as Exhibit 99.1
  "announcing the joint venture agreement with ArcLight," characterizing the transaction
  as a joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Structured as an upstream development participation: ArcLight "agreed ... to participate
  in the development of 32 specifically designated wells located in the Company's Butler
  Operated Area in Butler County, Pennsylvania and operated by Rex." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction is valued at approximately $67 million for the 32 wells that comprise a
  portion of the Company's 2015 drilling program; if ArcLight elects the option wells, the
  Company estimates the value increases by approximately $21.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Customer-facing asset conveyed as a fractional interest, not whole: in return for
  ArcLight's funding of drilling, completing and equipping costs ("well costs"), "Rex will
  assign to ArcLight a working interest of 35% in the funded wells, together with the
  rights to real and personal property, permits, licenses, and other rights that are
  necessary or required to operate and produce oil, gas, and other hydrocarbons ... from
  the wellbores of such wells." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The conveyed interest is temporary and partially reverting: "Upon the achievement of
  certain threshold triggers of ROI ... and IRR ..., ArcLight's working interest will be
  reduced to 17.5%, and the remaining 17.5% working interest will revert back to Rex." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Option to extend the same structure: ArcLight has an option to participate in 17
  additional wells in the Moraine East portion of the Butler Operated Area in 2016 at a
  20% working interest, "in exchange for ArcLight's payment of a portion of well costs and
  on similar terms and conditions," with the option-well interests also subject to a 50%
  reversion upon the ROI/IRR triggers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Operations and people at closing (what did NOT move with the asset)
- Operatorship did not transfer: the 32 designated wells are "operated by Rex"; ArcLight
  receives a working interest and associated operating rights but the Company's subsidiary
  remains operator of the wells. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The retrieved filing discloses no transfer of employees, management, or organization to
  ArcLight or to a separate venture entity; the arrangement is a contractual
  participation/cost-funding agreement over specifically designated wellbores rather than a
  transfer of an operating business or its workforce. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [UNVERIFIED: any separate venture legal entity or staffing]

## Bridging / operating arrangements at closing
- The cost-carry is the closing-time mechanism bridging the split ownership: ArcLight funds
  the drilling/completing/equipping "well costs" in exchange for the assigned 35% working
  interest, with the reversion clause realigning the parties' interests once return
  thresholds are met; "The Participation Agreement includes various provisions for
  adjustment to the parties' respective working interests upon the occurrence of certain
  events to maintain the value (to each party) of the transaction." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Concurrent financing event (same filing, distinct from the venture): on March 27, 2015
  the Company entered a Seventh Amendment to its Amended and Restated Credit Agreement with
  Royal Bank of Canada as Administrative Agent, amending the leverage covenant and setting
  the borrowing base at $350,000,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction conveyed a temporary 35% non-operated working interest (reverting toward
  17.5% on ROI/IRR triggers) in 32 specifically designated Butler County wells to a
  financial capital partner (an ArcLight Capital Partners affiliate) in exchange for that
  partner funding the wells' development costs, valued at approximately $67 million; the
  Company's subsidiary retained operatorship of the wells, no employees/management/
  organization were disclosed as transferring, and the deal was structured as a
  contractual drilling-participation and cost-funding agreement over designated wellbores.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-117866, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
