## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following the merger's close, PAREXEL filed a Form 15-12G on October 10, 2017,
  certifying termination of registration of its common stock under Section 12(g) of the
  Exchange Act and suspension of its duty to file reports under Sections 13 and 15(d),
  with "None" listed for any other class of securities for which a reporting duty
  remained. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-306384, Form 15-12G]
- No Form 10-K or other annual report has been filed under the registrant's CIK
  (0000799729) since the fiscal-year-2017 Form 10-K filed August 29, 2017, before
  closing; a search of the registrant's later filing history shows only sporadic
  Schedule 13G/A and Form 4 filings by third parties into early 2018, with no periodic
  report thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0000799729-17-000044, Form 10-K FY2017 (last filed annual report)]
- Two related entities later filed limited-purpose Form D notices of exempt securities
  offerings — "Parexel International Corp" (CIK 0001892518, Form D filed 2021-11-15) and
  "Parexel Investment Holdings, L.P." (CIK 0001732608, Form D filed 2018-02-26) — but
  neither entity has filed a Form 10-K, 20-F, or other periodic Exchange Act report;
  consequently no audited public financial statements exist for the entity across the

## Leadership transition under private ownership (press-sourced, before the fixed window)
- Effective March 15, 2018, PAREXEL announced that Jamie Macdonald, previously CEO and
  then Vice Chairman of INC Research (later Syneos Health), became Chief Executive
  Officer, while co-founder Josef von Rickenbach, who had served as CEO for 35 years,
  stepped down from day-to-day CEO duties and continued as Chairman of the Board; von
  Rickenbach also subsequently stepped down as Chairman effective December 31, 2018.
  [SRC: https://www.biospace.com/parexel-appoints-jamie-macdonald-as-chief-executive-officer]

## Realized trajectory within the ~3-5-year outcome window (2020-2022)
- On July 2, 2021, PAREXEL announced a definitive agreement under which the EQT IX fund
  (EQT Private Equity) and the Private Equity business within Goldman Sachs Asset
  Management would acquire PAREXEL from Pamplona Capital Management LP for approximately
  $8.5 billion, subject to customary closing conditions and regulatory approvals. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/07/02/2257224/0/en/Parexel-to-be-Acquired-by-EQT-Private-Equity-and-Goldman-Sachs-Asset-Management.html]
- In the announcement, CEO Jamie Macdonald stated that the company had "successfully
  adapted the business to work in the midst of a global pandemic, and supported the
  development of therapies to combat the COVID-19 pandemic itself," and cited an
  anticipated 8-9% CAGR for the outsourced clinical research services market as part of
  the investment rationale. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2021/07/02/2257224/0/en/Parexel-to-be-Acquired-by-EQT-Private-Equity-and-Goldman-Sachs-Asset-Management.html]
- On November 15, 2021, within the fixed outcome window, EQT Private Equity and Goldman
  Sachs Asset Management announced completion of the acquisition of PAREXEL from
  Pamplona Capital Management LP; the release described PAREXEL as having "more than
  18,000 colleagues around the world," with Jamie Macdonald continuing as CEO supported
  by the existing executive team. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/en/news-release/2021/11/15/2334842/0/en/EQT-Private-Equity-and-Goldman-Sachs-Asset-Management-Complete-Acquisition-of-Parexel.html]
- Reputable trade press reported that, at the time Pamplona began exploring a sale in
  2021, PAREXEL's headcount was approximately 17,000 and it conducted clinical trials in
  more than 95 countries; the same coverage characterized the 2021 sale price (~$8.5
  billion) against the approximately $5.0-5.5 billion Pamplona had paid in 2017 as
  giving Pamplona "a healthy return" on its investment. [SRC: https://medcitynews.com/2021/07/cro-parexel-changes-private-equity-hands-again-this-time-for-8-5b/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because PAREXEL was taken private and deregistered in October 2017, there is no
  public audited outcome record (no Form 10-K) for the entity across the ~2020-2022
  outcome window; the traceable realized record shows a pre-window leadership transition
  (the 35-year founder-CEO stepping down in March 2018 in favor of an externally
  recruited CEO), followed within the fixed window by a full change-of-control
  divestiture: on July 2, 2021 Pamplona Capital Management agreed to sell PAREXEL to EQT
  Private Equity and Goldman Sachs Asset Management for approximately $8.5 billion, a
  transaction that completed November 15, 2021 with the company continuing to operate at
  a broadly similar scale (18,000+ employees) under continuing CEO leadership.
