## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG filed a Form 15 on August 7, 2017 to
  terminate its SEC registration under Rule 12h-3(b)(1)(i): all common shares would be
  extinguished in the pending squeeze-out merger, and its remaining outstanding
  securities (5.50% Senior Notes due 2020 and 4.35% Senior Notes due 2025) were held by
  fewer than 300 holders of record, so the registrant would not continue to report under
  Sections 13(a) or 15(d) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-049916, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K was filed by Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG (CIK 1163348)
  after the FY2016 10-K filed February 28, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001163348-17-000008, Form 10-K (FY2016, filed 2017-02-28)]
- Allied World's ultimate parent, Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited (CIK 915191), is a
  separate SEC registrant (Canadian foreign private issuer) that continues to file
  annual reports on Form 40-F; its Form 40-F for fiscal year 2020, filed March 5, 2021,
  attaches an Annual Report (Exhibit 99.2) that separately discloses Allied World as a
  named reportable segment with segment-level financial data. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2 (Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited, 2020 Annual Report)]

## Realized business record via the parent's segment disclosure (fixed window: FY2020 Annual Report)
- Fairfax's 2020 Annual Report describes "Allied World" as "A global property, casualty
  and specialty insurer and reinsurer with a presence at Lloyd's." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- Net premiums earned attributed to the Allied World segment were $2,722.6 million in
  fiscal year 2020, up from $2,335.4 million in fiscal year 2019 (Property $882.6M/
  $832.1M; Casualty $1,755.4M/$1,394.1M; Specialty $84.6M/$109.2M). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- Segment assets attributed to Allied World were $16,975.5 million at December 31, 2020
  versus $15,596.0 million at December 31, 2019; segment liabilities were $12,547.4
  million versus $11,499.3 million over the same dates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- Goodwill allocated to the Allied World cash-generating unit was $940.0 million at
  December 31, 2020 versus $938.8 million at December 31, 2019; allocated intangible
  assets were $611.7 million versus $659.2 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- Net earnings attributable to non-controlling (minority co-investor) interests in
  Allied World were $106.6 million for fiscal year 2020 and $88.1 million for fiscal
  year 2019, against non-controlling voting percentages of 29.1% (2020) and 29.9%
  (2019) and carrying values of $1,329.0 million (2020) and $1,256.3 million (2019).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- At December 31, 2020, Fairfax's ownership interest in Allied World was 70.9%, up from
  70.1% at December 31, 2019, after Allied World received a $100.0 million capital
  contribution from Fairfax on June 30, 2020 "primarily to support its underwriting
  plans." Allied World paid a dividend of $126.4 million to its minority shareholders
  (OMERS, AIMCo and others) on April 30, 2020, matching the $126.4 million dividend paid
  April 29, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- During 2019, Allied World redomesticated from Switzerland to Bermuda. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
- At December 31, 2020, Allied World's subsidiary was reported as in compliance with
  Bermuda's regulatory capital requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Allied World itself stopped SEC periodic reporting within roughly a month of closing
  (Form 15, August 2017), so there is no standalone post-close 10-K record. However,
  because its parent Fairfax separately discloses Allied World as a named reportable
  segment, a realized ~3.5-year record is observable through Fairfax's fiscal-year-2020
  Annual Report: net premiums earned grew from $2,335.4M (2019) to $2,722.6M (2020),
  segment assets grew from $15,596.0M to $16,975.5M, goodwill allocated to the segment
  was essentially stable (~$940M), Fairfax's ownership stake increased from 70.1% to
  70.9% alongside a $100.0M capital contribution to support underwriting plans, and the
  business was redomesticated from Switzerland to Bermuda during 2019.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-21-000519, Form 40-F Exhibit 99.2]
