# Structural sub-dossier — Civeo Corporation acquisition of Noralta Lodge Ltd. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties

- On November 26, 2017, Civeo Corporation ("Civeo") entered into a Share Purchase
  Agreement with Noralta Lodge Ltd. ("Noralta"), the Torgerson Family Trust, five
  affiliated Alberta holding entities, and an individual seller (Lance Torgerson),
  under which Civeo would acquire, directly or indirectly, all of the issued and
  outstanding shares of Noralta (the "Acquisition"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A cover/summary]
- Noralta is described as an Alberta-based provider of remote accommodations to the
  Canadian oil sands region, operating eleven lodges comprising over 5,600 owned rooms
  and 7,700 total rooms, offering full-service open-lodge accommodations, facilities
  management, and turnkey camp/catering solutions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Noralta Lodge Ltd."]
- Civeo, the acquiring/continuing registrant, remained the SEC-reporting public company
  before and after the transaction; the proxy statement (Form DEFM14A) solicited Civeo
  shareholder approval of a "share issuance proposal" to authorize the stock component
  of the consideration, not shareholder approval of Civeo itself being acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A cover letter]

## Consideration structure and what crossed at closing

- Total consideration for the Acquisition was disclosed as approximately US$361
  million, consisting of (i) C$209,500,000 in cash (subject to customary working
  capital/debt/cash adjustments), of which C$28,500,000 was to be held in escrow to
  support the sellers' indemnification obligations; (ii) 32,790,868 Civeo Common
  Shares, of which 13,491,100 were to be held in escrow and released in three equal
  installments tied to a customer contract remaining in place in June 2021, June 2022,
  and June 2023; and (iii) 9,679 Preferred Shares, initially convertible into
  approximately 29,330,303 Common Shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A cover letter / "Questions and Answers"]
- After closing (assuming release of all escrowed shares), current Civeo shareholders
  were projected to own approximately 68% of outstanding Common Shares and former
  Noralta shareholders approximately 32%, on an as-converted, fully diluted basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Questions and Answers"]
- Legal vehicle: Noralta (or its successor following a pre-closing amalgamation
  contemplated by the Purchase Agreement) would become a wholly-owned subsidiary of
  Civeo pursuant to a Share Purchase Agreement under which Civeo acquires all
  outstanding Noralta shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Definitions"]
- Controlling intent: the proxy describes the rationale as "value creation through
  synergies," citing expected annual synergies of approximately C$10 million from
  combining the two previously independently operated businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Reasons for the Acquisition"]

## Bridging arrangements and continuity provisions negotiated at closing

- The Purchase Agreement contemplated Non-Competition Agreements to be entered into
  between Civeo (the "Purchaser") and each of the Vendors, Torgerson, and Corey Smith
  at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Definitions" / Annex B]
- The escrow structure (cash held by Alliance Trust Company as Escrow Agent; a portion
  of the stock consideration held in a separate share escrow releasing over a
  multi-year schedule tied to a specified customer contract) functioned as a
  contractual mechanism bridging seller performance obligations and consideration
  timing across the closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Questions and Answers"]
- The proxy's risk factors note that the Acquisition's success depends on Civeo's
  ability to retain "key" Noralta employees who continue employment with Civeo after
  closing, and flags that these employees "might decide not to remain with Civeo after
  the Acquisition" — i.e., no guaranteed transfer of Noralta's management/operating
  personnel was built into the deal beyond the Non-Competition Agreements with named
  sellers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Risk Factors"]
- The disclosed rationale materials record that Civeo management and its financial
  advisor met with Noralta management to review "Noralta's management team, company
  history, lodge overview, operating model and financial performance" ahead of
  negotiating the Acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "Background of the Acquisition"]

## Regulatory and closing conditions

- Consummation was conditioned on Civeo shareholder approval of the share issuance
  proposal, clearance under the Competition Act (Canada), and a net-benefit-to-Canada
  determination under the Investment Canada Act (given a "cultural business" element
  of Noralta's operations), among other customary closing conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A "The Purchase Agreement — Conditions"]
- A special meeting of Civeo shareholders to vote on the share issuance proposal was
  scheduled for March 28, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-18-005257, Form 8-K (March 22, 2018 supplemental disclosure)]
- On April 2, 2018, Civeo completed its previously announced acquisition of Noralta
  Lodge Ltd., referred to as the "Noralta Acquisition," with consideration described
  as (i) approximately C$207.7 million (approximately US$161.1 million) in cash,
  subject to customary post-closing adjustments, plus the stock and preferred-share
  components described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-19-000055, Form 10-K FY2018 "Overview"/Note 7 Acquisitions]
- Concurrently with closing, an Amended Credit Agreement became effective on April 2,
  2018, which reduced Civeo's aggregate revolving loan commitments by $35.5 million to
  a maximum principal amount of $239.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-19-000055, Form 10-K FY2018 "Liquidity and Capital Resources"]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- Civeo, the continuing public registrant, acquired all outstanding shares of the
  privately held Noralta for a mixed cash/stock/preferred-stock consideration package
  of roughly US$361 million as originally disclosed, with a portion of both the cash
  and stock consideration escrowed and released over a multi-year schedule tied to
  customer-contract retention, and with Non-Competition Agreements — but no assured
  employment-continuity mechanism — governing the transfer of Noralta's operating
  personnel and management relationships alongside the acquired lodging assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029739, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-19-000055, Form 10-K FY2018]
