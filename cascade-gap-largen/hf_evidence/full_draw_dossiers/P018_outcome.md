## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant, Urban Edge Properties ("UE"), continued as an independent SEC-reporting public company and filed an annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 on February 13, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- UE and its operating partnership UELP were created in 2014 and separated from Vornado Realty Trust in January 2015; UE describes itself as owning the majority of Vornado's former shopping center business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Realized financial record (fiscal year 2019)
- For the year ended December 31, 2019, UE reported net income of $116.2 million and net income attributable to common shareholders of $109.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Basic and diluted earnings per common share were $0.91 for fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The company remained profitable across the outcome window: the selected financial data reported net income attributable to common shareholders of $109.5 million (2019), $105.2 million (2018), and $67.1 million (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Realized portfolio and organization

- As of December 31, 2019, UE had 117 employees, with headquarters at 888 Seventh Avenue, New York, NY. [SRC: SEC accession 0001611547-20-000008, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
