## Realized record at/beyond the fixed window (press-sourced)
- On May 19, 2020 — just past the outer edge of the fixed window — Exide Holdings, Inc.
  and four affiliated debtors filed voluntary petitions for Chapter 11 relief in the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware; the filing separated the North American
  business, placed into a court-supervised sale process, from the EMEA/Asia-Pacific
  business, which was offered for sale separately. A company representative was quoted:
  "As it looks today, this is the end of Exide Technologies North America." [SRC: https://www.batteriesinternational.com/2020/05/21/exide-up-for-sale-as-north-american-business-enters-third-chapter-11/]
- The company obtained $40 million in debtor-in-possession financing from a group of
  lenders, including certain existing noteholders, to fund operations during the sale
  process. [SRC: https://www.batteriesinternational.com/2020/05/21/exide-up-for-sale-as-north-american-business-enters-third-chapter-11/]
- The company identified 16 non-performing sites carrying more than $250 million in
  associated costs, with the Vernon, California recycling facility alone accounting for
  $156 million of that figure. [SRC: https://www.batteriesinternational.com/2020/05/21/exide-up-for-sale-as-north-american-business-enters-third-chapter-11/]
- On August 25, 2020, Atlas Holdings completed a court-supervised acquisition, under
  Section 363 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code, of substantially all of the operating assets
  of the Americas business of Exide Technologies, LLC, and established two standalone
  successor companies: Stryten Manufacturing (battery manufacturing, headquartered in
  Milton, Georgia, operating seven U.S. manufacturing plants) and Element Resources
  (battery recycling, operating two U.S. recycling plants). [SRC: https://www.atlasholdingsllc.com/news/atlas-holdings-establishes-stryten-manufacturing-following-acquisition-of-asset/]
- Stryten Manufacturing's announced leadership included Tim Vargo as chief executive
  officer, described in the announcement as having previously served as chairman,
  president, and CEO of Exide Technologies — indicating some continuity of prior senior
  management into the successor entity, alongside Mike Judd as president and chief
  operating officer. [SRC: https://www.atlasholdingsllc.com/news/atlas-holdings-establishes-stryten-manufacturing-following-acquisition-of-asset/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- No audited public financial statement exists for the fixed ~2018-2020 outcome window,
  so the outcome is coded uncertain on any financial scalar; the best-documented realized
  fact is that, just beyond the five-year mark after emergence, the reorganized company's
  North American business entered a second Chapter 11 case (May 2020) separating it from
  its EMEA/Asia-Pacific operations, followed roughly three months later by a Section 363
  sale of its North American operating assets to Atlas Holdings, which re-launched the
  manufacturing and recycling operations under two new corporate names (Stryten
  Manufacturing and Element Resources) rather than continuing Exide Technologies as an
  independent going concern in North America. [SRC: https://www.batteriesinternational.com/2020/05/21/exide-up-for-sale-as-north-american-business-enters-third-chapter-11/] [SRC: https://www.atlasholdingsllc.com/news/atlas-holdings-establishes-stryten-manufacturing-following-acquisition-of-asset/]
