## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K (date of earliest event January 30, 2018) reporting the completion on January 31, 2018 of the transactions contemplated by a Master Combination Agreement dated August 25, 2017 (amended and restated November 20, 2017) among Colony Capital Operating Company, LLC (CLNS OP), NRF RED REIT Corp., Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. (Colony NorthStar Credit), Credit RE Operating Company, LLC, the registrant (NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc., "NorthStar I") and its operating partnership, and NorthStar Real Estate Income II, Inc. and its operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Explanatory Note / Item 2.01]
- Under the Combination, at 11:56 p.m. Eastern Time on January 31, 2018 the registrant merged with and into Colony NorthStar Credit, with Colony NorthStar Credit surviving; each share of the registrant's common stock was cancelled and converted into the right to receive 0.3532 shares of Colony NorthStar Credit Class A common stock plus cash in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Item 2.01]
- Colony NorthStar Credit issued approximately 42.1 million Class A shares to former registrant stockholders; upon consummation, CLNS and its affiliates owned approximately 37%, former NorthStar I stockholders approximately 32%, and former NorthStar II stockholders approximately 31% of Colony NorthStar Credit on a fully diluted basis; the Class A common stock began trading on the NYSE under the symbol "CLNC" effective February 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Items 2.01 and 5.01]
- Under Item 5.03, on January 30, 2018 the registrant filed Second Articles of Amendment to its charter removing certain provisions related to "Roll-Up Transactions" (Exhibit 3.2), and a First Charter Amendment permitting in-kind distributions of beneficial interests in a liquidating trust established to own and liquidate the registrant's remaining assets in connection with the merger (Exhibit 3.1). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Item 5.03 and Item 9.01]
- At closing the registrant's advisory relationships were unwound within the Combination: all 100 outstanding partnership units of NorthStar I OP held by NS Real Estate Income Trust Advisor, LLC were redeemed for an aggregate $910, and 100 special limited partnership units held by NorthStar OP Holdings, LLC (an affiliate of CLNS) were cancelled without consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Item 2.01]
- As a closing mechanism, one asset (the "Excluded Asset," a promissory note of original maximum principal $150,150,000 dated April 11, 2014) was carved out: it was transferred to a wholly owned subsidiary (Holdco), a $65 million senior participation was sold to CFI Holdings (an affiliate of CLNS), and the registrant transferred Holdco to N1 Liquidating Trust, distributing units of beneficial interest in the Trust to its record holders as a special dividend; the Trust's management was placed with CNI NSI Advisors, LLC, the registrant's advisor and an affiliate of CLNS, under a Management Services Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The 8-K was signed on February 1, 2018 by David A. Palam, General Counsel and Secretary, on behalf of "Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc., as successor by merger to NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K signature block]

## Organizational and portfolio-assembly structure at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant was formed in January 2009 as a Maryland corporation and commenced operations in October 2010; it was formed "to originate, acquire and asset manage a diversified portfolio of commercial real estate, or CRE, debt, select equity and securities investments, predominantly in the United States," and elected REIT status commencing with the taxable year ended December 31, 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- The registrant had no in-house workforce: "We are externally managed and have no employees," and "As of December 31, 2016, we had no employees. Our Advisor or its affiliates provide management, acquisition, advisory, marketing, investor relations and certain administrative services for us." [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- Day-to-day operations were run by an external advisor under an advisory agreement: prior to June 30, 2014 the advisor was NS Real Estate Income Trust Advisor, LLC (the "Prior Advisor"); after a series of sponsor-level mergers (NSAM's merger completed January 10-11, 2017 forming Colony NorthStar), the advisor "became a subsidiary of Colony NorthStar" and "manages our day-to-day operations pursuant to an advisory agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- The customer-facing capital (equity) was raised through a continuous best-efforts public offering rather than a single transaction: the registrant registered up to 100,000,000 shares under its Primary Offering plus 10,526,315 distribution-reinvestment-plan (DRP) shares; the Primary Offering was completed July 1, 2013, and the registrant "raised total gross proceeds of $1.1 billion in the Offering" plus an additional $0.2 billion in gross DRP proceeds through March 10, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- The portfolio was assembled by serial origination/acquisition across asset types. As of December 31, 2016 (adjusted through March 10, 2017), it comprised: CRE debt — 18 investments, $656,983 thousand principal/cost (40.9%); operating real estate — 42 properties, $615,450 thousand (38.4%); private-equity investments — 4, $176,263 thousand (11.0%); and CRE securities — 14 CMBS, $155,194 thousand (9.7%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- Reported total assets were $1,768,480 thousand as of December 31, 2016 (versus $1,947,516 thousand as of December 31, 2015; amounts in thousands as reported). [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
- The acquisition and disposition processes were themselves fee-generating advisor functions: originations and acquisitions "entitle our Advisor to acquisition fees and asset management fees," and sales "entitle our Advisor to disposition fees"; the advisor and sponsor could arrange purchases and sales among the sponsor's other "Managed Companies," and the registrant states "We do not have arm's-length agreements" with these affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession reports the January 31, 2018 completion of a multi-party Combination in which the registrant merged into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. (surviving as "CLNC"), its stock converting at 0.3532 CLNC Class A shares each, its charter's "Roll-Up Transactions" provisions removed, its external-advisor partnership units unwound, and one excluded asset carved into a distributed liquidating trust. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029391, 8-K Items 2.01/5.03/8.01]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant was structured as an externally managed, no-employee non-traded REIT that assembled a diversified ~$1.77 billion CRE debt/equity/securities portfolio through serial origination and acquisition funded by a continuous best-efforts public equity offering, with all management, acquisition, asset-management and administrative processes — and the people performing them — resident in an external advisor (an affiliate of the sponsor, ultimately Colony NorthStar) under a fee-bearing advisory agreement rather than employed or performed by the registrant itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-17-000008, 10-K FY2016]
