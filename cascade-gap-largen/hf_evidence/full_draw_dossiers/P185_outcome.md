# Outcome sub-dossier — Pandora business realized record under Sirius XM ownership, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Pandora deregistered from SEC reporting shortly
> after closing, so this record is traced through the acquirer's (Sirius XM Holdings
> Inc.) subsequent annual report, which separately discloses a reportable segment
> covering the Pandora business. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the
> transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following closing, Pandora requested removal of its shares from NYSE listing and
  stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate its own Exchange Act reporting
  obligations; Pandora does not itself file periodic reports after closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]
- Sirius XM Holdings Inc. (CIK 908937) continued as an SEC-reporting company and its
  Form 10-K for fiscal year 2023 (filed 2024-02-01) discloses a distinct reportable
  segment, "Pandora and Off-platform," presenting revenue, cost of services, and gross
  profit separately from the "Sirius XM" segment; this segment is used to source the
  realized record below. [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Realized segment financial trajectory (2021-2023, as reported in the FY2023 10-K)
- Pandora and Off-platform segment total revenue was $2,072 million (FY2021), $2,098
  million (FY2022), and $2,113 million (FY2023) — essentially flat to slightly rising.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Pandora and Off-platform segment gross profit was $743 million (FY2021), $655 million
  (FY2022), and $638 million (FY2023) — a decline of approximately 14% from 2021 to
  2023 even as segment revenue held roughly flat. [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Goodwill allocated to the Pandora and Off-platform reporting unit was $861 million as
  of January 1, 2022, rose to $959 million by December 31, 2022 (a stated
  "Acquisition" addition of $98 million), and remained unchanged at $959 million as of
  December 31, 2023, with no goodwill impairment losses recorded for the reporting unit
  during fiscal years 2021, 2022, or 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Realized user/subscriber trajectory (2022-2023, as reported in the FY2023 10-K)
- The FY2023 10-K's risk-factor section states: "Our Pandora ad-supported service has
  suffered a substantial and consistent loss of monthly active users... The number of
  monthly active users to our ad-supported Pandora service has declined consistently
  for several years, including in 2023, and is likely to further contract in the
  future." [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Pandora monthly active users (all services) declined from approximately 47.6 million
  (December 31, 2022) to approximately 46.0 million (December 31, 2023), about a 3%
  decrease; self-pay subscribers declined from approximately 6.2 million to
  approximately 6.0 million over the same period (also about 3%); and ad-supported
  listener hours declined from 10.88 billion (FY2022) to 10.48 billion (FY2023). The
  10-K attributes the user decline to "an increase in ad-supported listener churn and a
  decline in the number of new users." [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Items noted but outside the fixed outcome window
- A goodwill impairment of $956 million to the Pandora and Off-platform reporting unit
  is disclosed in the FY2023 10-K as having been recognized in fiscal year 2020, prior
  to the fixed ~2022-2024 outcome window; it is not treated as an in-window outcome
  fact here but is the source of the reporting unit's post-2020 goodwill base. [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2022-2024 window, the Pandora and Off-platform segment as separately
  disclosed by Sirius XM Holdings Inc. showed flat-to-slightly-rising segment revenue
  alongside declining segment gross profit, unchanged segment goodwill with no recorded
  impairment in 2021-2023, and a consistent, company-disclosed decline in the
  ad-supported listener base (monthly active users and listener hours both down, with a
  matching decline in self-pay subscribers) between 2022 and 2023.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000908937-24-000008, Form 10-K FY2023]
