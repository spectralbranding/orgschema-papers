# Structural sub-dossier — Pandora Media, Inc. acquisition by Sirius XM Holdings Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On September 23, 2018, Pandora Media, Inc. ("Pandora"), a Delaware corporation whose
  common stock traded on the NYSE under the symbol "P," entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger and Reorganization with Sirius XM Holdings Inc. ("Sirius XM") and a
  wholly-owned Sirius XM merger subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K]
- Sirius XM described itself as the world's largest radio company by revenue (~33.5
  million subscribers), delivering channels via satellite radio and Internet radio on a
  subscription-fee basis. Pandora described itself as a music-discovery platform
  offering ad-supported and on-demand personalized listening, powered by its Music
  Genome Project content-programming algorithms. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as an all-stock combination: Pandora stockholders
  received 1.44 shares of Sirius XM common stock per Pandora share, a fixed exchange
  ratio not adjusted for either company's stock-price changes prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and mechanics at closing
- The transaction was effected through a multi-step merger sequence: a merger sub
  merged into Pandora (Pandora surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of a newly formed
  holding company); Pandora then converted from a Delaware corporation into a Delaware
  LLC, renamed "Pandora Media, LLC"; the holding company then merged into a Sirius XM
  merger subsidiary and ultimately into Sirius XM Radio Inc., with Pandora becoming a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of Sirius XM Radio Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K, Item 5.03]
- All merger-sub entities involved were formed solely to engage in the transaction and
  had not carried on any other business or operations before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]
- On February 1, 2019, the transactions became effective; Pandora notified the NYSE of
  effectiveness, trading in Pandora common stock on the NYSE ceased that morning, and
  Pandora requested removal of its shares from NYSE listing while intending to file a
  Form 15 to terminate its own Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing product/asset that crossed was Pandora's ad-supported and
  subscription music-streaming service; the stated strategic rationale for combining it
  with Sirius XM's business model was to leverage Sirius XM's exclusive content
  alongside Pandora's ad-supported and subscription tiers, in and out of the vehicle.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]
- At closing, Pandora's CEO (Roger Lynch) and CFO (Naveen Chopra) both stepped down
  from their executive positions under Pandora's Executive Severance and Change of
  Control Policy; Mr. Lynch agreed to a three-month paid consulting transition
  ($133,333/month) and Mr. Chopra a two-month paid consulting transition
  ($40,834/month), rather than continuing employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- The proxy statement/prospectus flagged, as a stockholder-disclosed risk, "the failure
  of Sirius XM to retain key employees of either Pandora or Sirius XM" as a potential
  post-closing integration difficulty. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or comparable bridging
  contract for shared operational substrate was identified in the drawn filing or the
  closing 8-K; Pandora and Sirius XM were described as "currently operated
  independently of each other" prior to closing, and the transaction's structure (a
  full merger into a wholly-owned subsidiary, not a carve-out) did not require one. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement/prospectus disclosed that Sirius XM would be required to devote
  significant management attention to integrating Pandora's business, citing potential
  difficulties including "the complexities associated with integrating personnel from
  the two companies and of combining two companies with different histories, cultures
  and customer bases." [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, Pandora's customer-facing streaming product crossed into Sirius XM's
  ownership structure via a multi-step all-stock merger with a fixed 1.44 exchange
  ratio, while Pandora's top two executives (CEO and CFO) departed the company with
  only brief (two- and three-month) paid consulting transition arrangements rather than
  continuing employment; no transition-services or shared-services agreement bridged
  the two companies' operations at closing, and the parties' own disclosures flagged
  integration of differing personnel, histories, and cultures as an anticipated
  post-closing risk. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-18-003625, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-19-000265, Form 8-K]
