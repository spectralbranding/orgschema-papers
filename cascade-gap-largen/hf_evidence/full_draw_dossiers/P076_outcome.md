## Public-reporting status during the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- On June 3, 2019, Cypress Semiconductor Corporation, Infineon Technologies AG, and IFX Merger Sub Inc. (a wholly-owned subsidiary of Infineon) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger; on April 16, 2020, Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Infineon. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-051192, Form 15-12B]
- The Company filed a Form 15-12B on April 27, 2020 certifying termination of the registration of its common stock under the Securities Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-051192, Form 15-12B]
- The Company's last annual report on Form 10-K was filed February 21, 2020 for fiscal year 2019; no Form 10-K or Form 20-F was filed by the Company for any period in the 2021–2023 outcome window, so no audited public financial statement of the Company or its interest in the joint venture exists for that window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000791915-20-000006, Form 10-K (FY2019, last annual report filed)]

## Realized record of the joint venture during the window (press-sourced, non-scalar)
- The joint venture received regulatory antitrust approval and began full operations on April 1, 2019 as SkyHigh Memory Limited, a Hong Kong–based NAND supplier. [SRC: https://www.nasdaq.com/press-release/cypress-announces-closing-joint-venture-transaction-sk-hynix-system-ic-2019-04-21]
- Within the outcome window the joint venture continued to operate as a going concern: on October 11, 2021 SkyHigh Memory announced an expansion of its single-level-cell (SLC) NAND Flash memory family (the ML-3 product line) on 1xnm process technology, with devices described as available for sampling in early 2022. [SRC: https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2021/10/19/skyhigh-memory-slc-nand-flash-memory-family-on-1xnm-process-technology/]
- The joint venture continued to be described in this period as jointly established by the former Cypress (by then part of Infineon Technologies AG) and SK hynix, indicating the Company's minority interest passed to Infineon on the 2020 acquisition rather than being unwound at that time. [SRC: https://www.storagenewsletter.com/2021/10/19/skyhigh-memory-slc-nand-flash-memory-family-on-1xnm-process-technology/]
