# Outcome sub-dossier — ConocoPhillips / Australia Pacific LNG joint venture, realized record ~5 years after closing

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> record of the joint venture in the fixed outcome window (the FY2015 Form 10-K,
> reporting as of December 31, 2015). It contains no information about how the
> transaction was structured at closing.

## Ownership and governance as reported in the outcome window
- As of the FY2015 10-K, the venture, Australia Pacific LNG Pty Ltd (APLNG), was
  reported as a three-party structure: ConocoPhillips holding a 37.5 percent interest,
  Origin Energy Limited holding 37.5 percent, and China Petrochemical Corporation
  (Sinopec) holding 25 percent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The filing states ConocoPhillips is not the primary beneficiary of APLNG (a variable
  interest entity) because it shares with Origin Energy and Sinopec "the power to direct
  the key activities of APLNG," and continues to account for the interest under the
  equity method (not consolidated). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Operating division of labor reported in the outcome window
- The filing describes an operator split: "Origin operates APLNG's upstream production
  and pipeline system, and we [ConocoPhillips] operate the downstream LNG facility,"
  located on Curtis Island near Gladstone, Queensland, as well as the LNG export sales
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Realized production/commercial milestones
- APLNG Train 1 "achieved first LNG in the fourth quarter of 2015 and the first cargo
  sailed in January 2016." Two fully subscribed 4.5-million-tonnes-per-year LNG trains
  had been sanctioned, with Train 2 startup expected in the second half of 2016.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Approximately 3,900 net wells were envisioned to supply both the domestic gas market
  and the LNG sales contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Sales agreements reported: Train 1 LNG sold to Sinopec under a 20-year agreement for
  up to 4.3 million metric tonnes per year (mtpa); Sinopec separately agreed to purchase
  an additional 3.3 mtpa through 2035; Kansai Electric Power Co. (Japan) agreed to
  purchase approximately 1 mtpa for 20 years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Financing and guarantees reported in the outcome window
- APLNG had an $8.5 billion project finance facility, of which $8.4 billion had been
  drawn as of December 31, 2015; ConocoPhillips provided a completion guarantee for its
  pro-rata share of the facility until the project achieves financial completion.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- ConocoPhillips disclosed several outstanding guarantees tied to its 37.5 percent
  interest as of December 31, 2015, including a construction-completion guarantee for
  the project financing (~$3.2 billion maximum), a construction-contract performance
  guarantee (~$110 million maximum), and a guarantee (from the original 2008 purchase of
  the interest from Origin) to reimburse Origin for gas-delivery obligations (~$1
  billion, or ~$1.8 billion in the event of intentional/reckless breach).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Financial-performance record (realized scalar)
- ConocoPhillips recorded a noncash, before- and after-tax $1,502 million impairment of
  its APLNG equity investment in the fourth quarter of 2015, attributed to a sharp
  deterioration in the crude oil and natural gas price outlook that pushed the estimated
  fair value of the investment below its book value; the impairment reduced the
  investment's book value to $10,185 million at December 31, 2015.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The company stated equity earnings from APLNG were lower year-over-year "mostly as a
  result of higher operating expenses and DD&A," and separately reported the impairment
  as a driver of a segment-level (Asia Pacific/Middle East) earnings decrease.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Effective October 1, 2015, in conjunction with Train 1 achieving first LNG,
  ConocoPhillips changed the functional currency of its APLNG investment from Australian
  dollar to U.S. dollar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- By the FY2015 10-K (reporting as of December 31, 2015, roughly five years after the
  closing-era filing), the venture had reached its first major commercial milestone
  (Train 1 first LNG / first cargo sailed) under a reported operating split (Origin
  upstream, ConocoPhillips downstream) and a three-party ownership structure including
  Sinopec, financed through a largely-drawn $8.5 billion project facility backed by
  multiple ConocoPhillips guarantees, while ConocoPhillips simultaneously recorded a
  $1,502 million noncash impairment of its equity investment in the same year, driven by
  a sharp decline in the commodity-price outlook.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-472901, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
