# Structural sub-dossier — Southwest Bancorp, Inc. merger into Simmons First National Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Simmons First National Corporation ("Simmons"), an Arkansas corporation, and Southwest
  Bancorp, Inc. ("OKSB"), an Oklahoma corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger dated December 14, 2016, as amended on July 19, 2017, providing for the merger
  of OKSB with and into Simmons, with Simmons continuing as the surviving corporation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- The same joint proxy statement/prospectus also covered a separately negotiated
  concurrent transaction between Simmons and First Texas BHC, Inc.; the two mergers were
  structured, negotiated, and documented as distinct agreements and are not part of the
  OKSB transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- OKSB was a financial holding company registered under the Bank Holding Company Act; as
  of June 30, 2017 it had, on a consolidated basis, total assets of $2.6 billion, total
  loans of $1.9 billion, total deposits of $2.0 billion, and shareholders' equity of
  $295.5 million, conducted through its wholly owned subsidiary bank, Bank SNB, an
  Oklahoma state-chartered bank ("OKSB Bank"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- The legal vehicle was a direct statutory merger of OKSB with and into Simmons (no
  separate newly formed merger-sub entity was used); Simmons' articles of incorporation
  and bylaws in effect immediately before the effective time became those of the
  surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of OKSB common stock was to be converted into the right to receive 0.3903
  shares of Simmons common stock and $5.11 in cash; in the aggregate Simmons expected to
  issue approximately 7,250,000 shares of common stock and pay approximately $95 million
  in cash, an implied aggregate value of approximately $564.3 million (based on the
  December 13, 2016 closing price) or $466.1 million (based on the September 8, 2017
  closing price). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- OKSB shareholders approved the merger agreement at a special meeting held October 17,
  2017 (14,810,536 for; 11,862 against; 119,779 abstain), and separately approved on an
  advisory basis the merger-related executive compensation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000914374-17-000082, Form 8-K]
- The merger was completed October 19, 2017: OKSB merged with and into Simmons, Simmons
  surviving, and the separate existence of OKSB ceased. [SRC: SEC accession 0000914374-17-000085, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (asset/legal-entity tier)
- OKSB's wholly owned bank subsidiary, Bank SNB, crossed with the parent company as part
  of the whole-company merger; Bank SNB's assets, deposits, and banking operations
  (commercial and personal banking activities) passed into the Simmons corporate family
  as a subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- OKSB's common stock, previously traded on the Nasdaq Global Select Market under the
  symbol "OKSB," ceased trading on Nasdaq with its final trading day occurring October
  19, 2017; OKSB requested that Nasdaq file a notification of removal from listing (Form
  25) with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0000914374-17-000085, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle / process bridging disclosed at closing (bank-charter tier)
- The merger agreement and joint proxy disclosed that, following completion of the OKSB
  merger, OKSB Bank (Bank SNB) would continue to operate as a separate bank subsidiary of
  Simmons until it was merged with and into Simmons Bank, with that bank-level merger
  expected in the second quarter of 2018 — i.e., the bank charter/operations tier was
  disclosed as bridging into the parent on a delayed timetable relative to the
  corporate-level merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent / management-continuity tier
- The directors and officers of Simmons immediately before the effective time were to
  continue as directors and officers of the surviving corporation; if the related
  Simmons director-count proposal was approved, Russell W. Teubner (an OKSB director)
  was to be appointed to the Simmons board following completion of the OKSB merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- The joint proxy disclosed that it was a condition to Simmons' obligation to close that
  Simmons and Mark W. Funke (OKSB's President and CEO) enter into a new, mutually
  satisfactory employment arrangement and that Funke and OKSB terminate his existing
  employment agreement; absent that, most other OKSB officers and employees were
  expected to be retained and to participate in Simmons' benefit plans with credit for
  prior service. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing (October 19, 2017), immediately before the effective time, OKSB and Bank SNB
  entered a Termination Agreement and Mutual Release with Mark Funke terminating his
  employment agreement (contingent on Funke receiving a hiring bonus and equity grant
  from Simmons after the merger) and a like termination agreement with Brent Bates
  terminating his change-of-control agreement on similar terms; separately, OKSB and Bank
  SNB entered Amendments to the Change of Control Agreement with Priscilla Barnes and to
  the Employment Agreement with Joe T. Shockley, Jr. extending a "Transition Period" of
  services to support the integration of Bank SNB with Simmons Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0000914374-17-000085, Form 8-K]
- OKSB had pre-existing change-of-control/employment agreements with its executive
  officers (Funke, Shockley, Barnes, Bates, and Rusty LaForge) providing severance
  payments (multiples of base salary/bonus) triggered by termination without cause or
  resignation for good reason within 24 months following the change of control effected
  by the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, stock-and-cash statutory merger of OKSB directly
  into Simmons (no merger-sub vehicle), with the target's bank-subsidiary charter and
  operations tier explicitly disclosed as bridging into the parent on a delayed,
  separate timetable (a subsequent bank-level merger, not simultaneous with the
  corporate-level merger) and with the target's controlling/executive tier only
  partially continuing intact: the CEO's original employment agreement was terminated at
  closing pending a new arrangement, two other named executives had their agreements
  amended to extend services through a defined transition period, while rank-and-file
  retention was expected as a matter of course rather than under individual agreements.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007845, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0000914374-17-000085, Form 8-K]
