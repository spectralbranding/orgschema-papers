# Structural sub-dossier — combination of Westar Energy, Inc. and Great Plains Energy Incorporated under a newly formed holding company (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Westar Energy, Inc. and Great Plains Energy Incorporated entered into an Amended and
  Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated July 9, 2017, providing for their
  combination under a newly formed holding company, Monarch Energy Holding, Inc.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A cover letter]
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus's own cover letter describes the transaction as
  "the merger of equals of Great Plains Energy and Westar Energy." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A cover letter]
- Under the structure, (1) Great Plains Energy merges with and into Monarch Energy, with
  Monarch Energy surviving, and (2) Westar Energy merges with and into King Energy, Inc.
  (a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of Monarch Energy formed solely for the transaction),
  with Westar Energy surviving as a direct subsidiary of Monarch Energy. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- Consideration was stock-for-stock: Great Plains Energy shareholders received 0.5981
  shares of Monarch Energy common stock per Great Plains Energy share; Westar Energy
  shareholders received one share of Monarch Energy common stock per Westar Energy
  share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- The mergers closed on June 4, 2018; the holding company (by then renamed Evergy, Inc.)
  began trading on the NYSE under ticker "EVRG" on June 5, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-182972, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]

## Governance and management installed at closing
- The Monarch Energy (Evergy) board as of the effective time was to be composed of an
  equal number of directors designated by each of Great Plains Energy and Westar Energy,
  with initial standing committees (compensation, audit, nominating/governance, finance,
  nuclear operations and environmental) chaired by designees split between the two
  companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- The combined company was to maintain corporate headquarters in Kansas City, Missouri,
  and utility operating headquarters in both Topeka, Kansas and Kansas City, Missouri.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- Terry Bassham, Chairman/President/CEO of Great Plains Energy, was to become President
  and CEO of the new holding company; Mark Ruelle, President/CEO of Westar Energy, was to
  become non-executive Chairman of the new holding company's board for a three-year term.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- At the effective time, all existing directors and officers of Westar Energy ceased to
  hold those positions; the Westar Energy board appointed a new slate of directors
  (drawn from both legacy companies) and installed Terry Bassham as President and CEO
  (principal executive officer) of Westar Energy itself, alongside other newly appointed
  Westar Energy officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-182972, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]

## What crossed the boundary at the product/process/people tiers
- The customer-facing product/asset — regulated retail electric utility service — was
  not consolidated into a single combined operating company at closing: Westar Energy
  continued to exist as the surviving Kansas operating utility, a direct subsidiary of
  the new holding company, rather than being merged into Great Plains Energy's utility
  subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A "Summary"]
- As a merger condition, the new holding company's utility subsidiaries were each
  required to maintain separate books and records, and each utility subsidiary was to
  "be held harmless from any business and financial risk exposures associated with
  another utility subsidiary, the new holding company or its other affiliates" — i.e.,
  back-office/financial processes were ring-fenced between the legacy utilities rather
  than pooled at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A, Annex A
  regulatory commitments]
- The parties disclosed an intent to integrate certain processes over time: expected net
  cost savings of approximately $40 million in 2018 growing to approximately $160 million
  in 2022, attributed in part to "standardization of key processes by leveraging existing
  best practices," framed as a post-closing objective rather than something completed at
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A, Annex A regulatory
  commitments / "Summary"]
- On the people tier, the merger agreement's regulatory commitments provided that no
  employee would be involuntarily severed as a result of the merger (including as a
  result of closing several named generating facilities), and that existing collective
  bargaining agreements would be honored. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form
  DEFM14A, Annex A regulatory commitments]

## Transitional/bridging commitments at closing
- The combined company committed to maintain an operating headquarters in Topeka,
  Kansas; to not involuntarily terminate employees as a result of the merger; to
  maintain historic community-involvement and charitable-contribution levels in Kansas
  for at least five years; and, for two years post-closing, to provide continuing
  employees base pay, incentive-compensation opportunities, and benefits "substantially
  comparable" to their pre-merger levels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form
  DEFM14A, Westar Energy Board's reasons for the merger]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On June 4, 2018, in connection with the Westar merger, Westar Energy requested that
  the NYSE withdraw its common stock from listing and file a Form 25 reporting that
  Westar Energy's shares were no longer listed on the NYSE; Westar Energy's shares were
  suspended from trading on the NYSE before the opening of trading that day. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-182972, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]
- Evergy common stock (issued under a Form S-4 registration, File No. 333-220465) was
  approved for listing on the NYSE and began trading under symbol "EVRG" on June 5, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-182972, Form 8-K, Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, Westar Energy and Great Plains Energy were combined stock-for-stock under
  a newly formed holding company (publicly framed by the parties as a "merger of
  equals"), with an equally split board and dual operating headquarters, while the
  legacy operating utilities were preserved as distinct, ring-fenced subsidiaries subject
  to separate-books-and-records and no-cross-liability conditions rather than being
  consolidated into one operating entity; process standardization/cost-saving
  integration was announced as a multi-year post-closing target, and the Westar Energy
  subsidiary's own officer/director tier was replaced at closing (with the former Great
  Plains Energy chief executive installed atop both the new holding company and Westar
  Energy itself), while employee-level continuity was addressed through no-involuntary-
  severance and two-year compensation-parity commitments rather than immediate
  operational integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307179, Form DEFM14A; SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-182972, Form 8-K]
