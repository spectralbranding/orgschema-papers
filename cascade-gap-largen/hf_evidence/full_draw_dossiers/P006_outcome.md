## Realized financial trajectory (fixed outcome window: fiscal year ended December 31, 2020)
- Net income (loss) available to Brighthouse Financial, Inc.'s common shareholders was $(1,105) million for 2020, compared with $(761) million for 2019 and $865 million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY ended 2020-12-31]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: a net loss of $1,105 million available to common shareholders in fiscal 2020 — a second consecutive annual net loss (fiscal 2019: $761 million loss) following positive net income in fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Total revenues were $8,503 million for 2020, compared with $6,554 million for 2019; the 2019 result included net derivative losses of $(1,988) million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented locus in the legacy product book (Run-off segment)
- Adjusted earnings (a non-GAAP measure) by segment show ongoing Annuities of $1,167 million and Life of $148 million for 2020, but the Run-off segment (products no longer actively sold and separately managed) recorded adjusted earnings of $(1,299) million in 2020, deteriorating from $(454) million in 2019 and $(43) million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Guarantees within certain products carried liabilities valued at $2.9 billion as of December 31, 2020, accounted for at estimated fair value with changes reported in net derivative gains (losses), a documented source of profitability volatility over the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented winding-down of the transitional shared substrate
- Among the favorable expense impacts recognized over the period was "the exit of various transition services agreements with MetLife," indicating the transitional shared-services arrangements with the former parent were being wound down. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The company disclosed continuing dependence on third-party providers for administering policies and managing investments, stating that where a provider fails to meet standards "we may be unable to resolve any issues on our own without assistance from the third-party provider, and we could have limited ability to influence the speed and effectiveness of that resolution." [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Roughly three years after the separation, the company recorded consecutive annual net losses (fiscal 2019 and fiscal 2020), with the fiscal 2020 net loss of $1,105 million available to common shareholders, while adjusted earnings in the run-off block of legacy products deteriorated sharply over 2018–2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Over the same period the transitional services arrangements with the former parent were exited, while the company continued to disclose reliance on third-party providers for policy administration and investment management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001685040-21-000008, Form 10-K FY2020]
