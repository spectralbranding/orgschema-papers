# Structural sub-dossier — SCANA Corporation merger with Dominion Energy, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 2, 2018, SCANA Corporation ("SCANA"), a South Carolina corporation, entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Dominion Energy, Inc., a Virginia
  corporation ("Dominion Energy"), and Sedona Corp., a South Carolina corporation and
  wholly owned subsidiary of Dominion Energy ("Merger Sub"), under which Merger Sub
  would merge with and into SCANA, with SCANA surviving the merger as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Dominion Energy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Consideration was stock-for-stock: each outstanding share of SCANA common stock would
  convert into the right to receive 0.6690 of a share of Dominion Energy common stock
  (cash in lieu of fractional shares), structured to be tax-deferred to SCANA
  shareholders; Dominion Energy expected to issue approximately 95,611,418 shares to
  former SCANA shareholders, who would hold approximately 12-13% of Dominion Energy's
  outstanding common stock post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A cover letter/summary]
- The exchange ratio of 0.6690 reflected an implied price of approximately $54.23/share
  based on Dominion Energy's December 29, 2017 closing price, an implied premium of
  approximately 36.3% over SCANA's closing price that day (and approximately 29.7% over
  a 30-day volume-weighted average). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A "Opinion of SCANA's Financial Advisors"]
- SCANA's wholly owned utility subsidiary, South Carolina Electric & Gas Company
  ("SCE&G"), and Dominion Energy jointly petitioned the South Carolina Public Service
  Commission ("SCPSC") to approve the merger together with a cost-recovery/rate-relief
  plan tied to SCE&G's abandoned New Nuclear Development ("NND") Project. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A "The Merger — Regulatory Approvals"]
- Completion was subject to HSR Act clearance (early termination of the waiting period
  was granted February 1, 2018) and to state/federal regulatory approvals including the
  SCPSC, FERC, NRC, NCUC and GPSC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]

## What crossed at closing, by tier
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity — SCANA and its subsidiaries, including
  SCE&G — became a wholly owned subsidiary of Dominion Energy via reverse triangular
  merger; this was a whole-company acquisition, not a carve-out of a business unit.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Product/asset and process/organization together: Dominion Energy's post-signing
  commitments (Merger Agreement Section 5.16) stated its intent to maintain SCE&G's
  corporate headquarters in Cayce, South Carolina, and to give SCANA/subsidiary
  employees "due and fair consideration for other employment and promotion
  opportunities" if positions were realigned or reduced. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex A, "Additional Matters"]
- People/management continuity was contractually built in rather than left silent: for
  non-union employees, Parent committed (Section 5.06, "Continuation Period" through
  December 31, 2019) to base compensation, annual and long-term incentive
  opportunities, and benefits "no less than" pre-closing levels, employment within a
  50-mile radius, and severance protection; union employees' terms continued under
  their existing collective bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex A, "Employee Matters"]
- Governance: the Company's directors were required to submit resignations effective at
  closing, and Dominion Energy committed to appoint one mutually agreeable member of
  SCANA's board or executive management to Dominion Energy's board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex A, "Resignation of Directors" / "Additional Matters"]

## Regulatory/rate settlement bridging the customer-facing asset at closing
- To meet commitments to the SCPSC, SCE&G and Dominion Energy agreed to jointly file a
  cost-recovery petition proposing, among other terms: an immediate rate credit to
  SCE&G's electric customers; an immediate reduction by SCE&G, upon closing, of its
  NND Project construction-work-in-progress investment by approximately $1.4 billion
  (permanently excluded from future retail rates); non-recovery of approximately $320
  million in specified regulatory assets; and an SCPSC finding on recovery of SCE&G's
  approximately $3.3 billion NND Project investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex, "Petition for Approval of Merger and Rate Plan"]
- SCE&G and Dominion Energy committed to jointly "support and advocate for" SCPSC
  approval of these merger terms and to take no inconsistent action, i.e., the rate
  settlement was structured as an integral, contractually bridged part of closing the
  transaction rather than a post-closing contingency left open-ended. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex, "Petition for Approval of Merger and Rate Plan"]

## Deal-protection terms
- The Merger Agreement provided a termination fee of $240,000,000 payable by SCANA to
  Dominion Energy under specified circumstances, and a termination fee of $280,000,000
  payable by Dominion Energy to SCANA under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A Annex A, "Termination Fee"]

## Closing
- On January 1, 2019, pursuant to the Merger Agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into
  SCANA, with SCANA continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Dominion Energy; approximately 95,611,418 shares of Dominion Energy
  common stock were issued to former SCANA shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-000643, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- SCANA notified the NYSE that the merger's effective time was 12:01 a.m. on January 1,
  2019; trading of SCANA common stock on the NYSE was suspended and the NYSE was
  expected to file a Form 25 to remove SCANA common stock from listing and terminate
  its Section 12(b) registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-000643, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company, stock-for-stock merger absorbed SCANA (and its utility subsidiary
  SCE&G) into Dominion Energy as a wholly owned subsidiary, with the legal vehicle,
  product/asset (regulated electric and gas utility operations), and people/management
  substrate all moving together under contractual continuity commitments (employee
  compensation/benefits continuity through 2019, headquarters-location commitment, one
  board seat), and with the customer-rate consequences of the utility's abandoned
  nuclear construction project bridged through a jointly filed, jointly advocated SCPSC
  settlement petition rather than left unresolved at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-193204, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-000643, Form 8-K]
