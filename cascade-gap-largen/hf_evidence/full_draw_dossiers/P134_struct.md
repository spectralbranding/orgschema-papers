# Structural sub-dossier — Smart & Final Stores LLC acquisition of certain Haggen assets under Bankruptcy Code section 363 (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Smart & Final Stores LLC ("Buyer"), a California limited liability company and an
  indirect subsidiary of Smart & Final Stores, Inc. (NYSE: SFS), agreed to acquire
  certain assets from Haggen Operations Holdings, LLC and Haggen Opco South, LLC
  (together, "Seller"/"Haggen") under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated October 2, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K Item 8.01 and EX-99.1 preamble]
- Seller was a debtor and debtor-in-possession in jointly administered chapter 11 cases
  filed September 8, 2015 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware,
  Case No. 15-11874. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 recitals]
- The sale was structured under sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code, free
  and clear of liens (other than permitted liens), subject to a Bid Procedures Order and
  Sale Order; Buyer agreed to serve as the "stalking horse bidder," with the agreement
  subject to higher or better offers at a court-supervised auction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K Item 8.01 and EX-99.1 recitals]
- As structured in the drawn filing, the acquisition covered 28 store leases and related
  assets — 27 closed stores in central and southern California and one closed store in
  Las Vegas, Nevada, most recently operated as "Haggen"-banner stores — for a cash
  purchase price of $56 million, subject to adjustments; Buyer placed a $5,600,000 escrow
  deposit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K Item 8.01, EX-99.2 press release, and EX-99.1 §1.2]
- A break-up fee equal to 3% of the Base Purchase Price plus documented out-of-pocket
  expenses up to $1.5 million was payable to Buyer if Seller accepted an alternative
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Break-Up Fee and Expense Reimbursement)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- The "Assets" comprised the Store Leases and related real-property rights and
  improvements; equipment, fixtures, signage, refrigeration systems, point-of-sale
  hardware and other tangible personal property; assigned contracts and transferred
  vendor agreements; deposits and prepaid expenses; transferable permits and licenses,
  including Type 21 distilled-spirits liquor licenses; and files and records primarily
  related to those assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 §1.1(a)]

## What did NOT cross — excluded assets and retained liabilities
- Excluded assets expressly retained by Seller included all Inventory; all Pharmacy
  Assets; accounts receivable; all Intellectual Property owned or licensed by Seller,
  including trademarks, trade names and all goodwill; Seller's cash, undeposited checks
  and food stamps; software rights; signage and personal property bearing Seller's name
  or logo (including employee uniforms); and books and records relating to Seller's
  employees and to corporate, chain-wide or division-level operations, policies and
  plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 §1.1(b)]
- Buyer assumed only liabilities arising from operation or ownership of the Assets after
  the Effective Time plus post-closing-period taxes; all other liabilities were retained
  by Seller as Excluded Liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 §1.1(c)-(d)]

## People / management (as structured)
- As of the agreement date, Buyer had not agreed to offer employment to any of Seller's
  employees, reserved the right to make independent hiring decisions, and did not assume
  any collective-bargaining, labor-practice or similar agreements covering Seller's
  current or former employees; Seller was obligated to provide all notices required by
  the Worker Adjustment and Retraining Notification (WARN) Act (plant closings and mass
  layoffs). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.1 §4.5]

## Product / operations bridge (as structured)
- The stores were closed at the time of the transaction; Buyer stated it planned to
  convert the acquired store properties to its "Smart & Final Extra!" store format,
  including investment in fixtures and equipment, décor, signage and infrastructure
  upgrades, with conversions expected to begin in the fiscal fourth quarter of 2015 and
  be completed by the fiscal second quarter of 2016, funded from existing cash and
  credit-facility availability. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K EX-99.2 press release]

## Consummation of the sale (closing year)
- In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2015 the Company acquired certain assets — 33 store
  leases and related fixtures, equipment and liquor licenses — of Haggen for a total
  purchase price of $67.9 million inclusive of transaction costs, of which $66.4 million
  was paid in cash; acquired leasehold interests were recorded at fair value as
  finite-lived intangible assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-011125, Form 10-K (FY2015), MD&A and Note 19 "Haggen Transaction"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A customer-facing retail asset base (store leaseholds, fixtures, equipment and
  transferable licenses for closed stores) was purchased out of a chapter 11 estate under
  a section 363 stalking-horse/auction process, while the seller retained inventory,
  pharmacy assets, receivables, intellectual property and goodwill, corporate and
  employee records, and all employees; the buyer assumed no labor agreements and offered
  no employment at signing, with the seller bearing WARN obligations. The stalking-horse
  terms (28 leases, $56 million) were succeeded at closing by an acquisition of 33 store
  leases for $67.9 million total ($66.4 million cash). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-068981, Form 8-K Item 8.01, EX-99.1 §§1.1-1.2, 4.5, EX-99.2] [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-011125, Form 10-K (FY2015), Note 19]
