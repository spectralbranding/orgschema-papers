# Outcome sub-dossier — Keysight Technologies realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after November 2014

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at the separated
> entity's (Keysight Technologies) fiscal years 2017 through 2019 (fiscal year ends
> October 31), i.e. approximately three to five years after the November 1, 2014
> distribution; realized figures are drawn from Keysight's Form 10-K for the fiscal year
> ended October 31, 2019. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the
> transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- Keysight Technologies, Inc. remained an SEC-registered public company trading on the NYSE
  under "KEYS" throughout the window and filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  October 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019 cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- Net revenue rose across the window: $3,189 million (FY2017), $3,878 million (FY2018) and
  $4,303 million (FY2019); the FY2019 selected financial data also list $2,918 million
  (FY2016) and $2,856 million (FY2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019 Selected Financial Data]
- FY2019 net revenue of $4,303 million represented an 11 percent increase over FY2018, and
  FY2018's $3,878 million was a 22 percent increase over FY2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019 MD&A]
- Net income was $621 million in FY2019, compared with $165 million in FY2018 and $102
  million in FY2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019 MD&A]
- The separated electronic measurement business was retained and expanded rather than
  divested; no divestiture of that business is recorded in the FY2019 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Documented acquisition and interface events within the window
- Pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated January 30, 2017, Keysight acquired all
  outstanding common stock of Ixia for $1,622 million net of $72 million of cash acquired,
  completing the acquisition on April 18, 2017; management stated the acquisition
  established Keysight as a market leader in next-generation network test and network
  visibility solutions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]
- In fiscal 2018 Keysight recorded a goodwill impairment charge of $709 million relating to
  the Ixia Solutions Group, based on the results of its annual impairment test. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Ixia Solutions Group revenue was $256 million in FY2017 (from the April 18, 2017
  acquisition date), $451 million in FY2018 and a higher figure in FY2019; effective the
  first quarter of fiscal 2020 Keysight completed an organizational change to manage the
  Ixia Solutions Group within its Communications Solutions Group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The FY2019 10-K notes that income (loss) before taxes was $715 million (FY2019),
  $(411) million (FY2018) and $179 million (FY2017), with the FY2018 pre-tax loss driven in
  part by the goodwill impairment charge; management attributed the FY2019 improvement over
  2018 partly to the non-recurrence of that impairment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019 Selected Financial Data and MD&A]

## Continuing relationship with the former parent
- The FY2019 10-K refers to facilities transferred to Keysight in its separation from
  Agilent on November 1, 2014, and states that in connection with the Separation Agilent
  would indemnify Keysight directly for certain related liabilities; the separation is
  treated as a completed event within the reporting entity's history. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over its fiscal 2017-2019 window the separated entity remained an independent NYSE-listed
  reporting company whose net revenue grew from $3,189 million to $4,303 million and net
  income from $102 million to $621 million; the electronic measurement business was retained
  and expanded, including through the April 2017 Ixia acquisition, while that subsequently
  acquired unit carried a $709 million goodwill impairment in fiscal 2018 and was
  reorganized into the Communications Solutions Group at the start of fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001601046-19-000110, Form 10-K FY2019]
