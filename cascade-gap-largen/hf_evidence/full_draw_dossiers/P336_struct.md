# Structural sub-dossier — Lennar Corporation acquisition of CalAtlantic Group, Inc. (closing structure, Lennar-side filing)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not, as disclosed in the acquirer's own joint proxy statement/prospectus and
> closing-event filing. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On October 29, 2017, CalAtlantic Group, Inc. ("CalAtlantic") entered into an Agreement
  and Plan of Merger with Lennar Corporation ("Lennar") and Cheetah Cub Group Corp., a
  newly formed Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Lennar ("Merger Sub"),
  under which CalAtlantic would merge with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub
  continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Lennar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Information About Merger Sub"]
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus described the transaction as a "strategic merger"
  that would "create the largest homebuilder in the United States and is expected to
  generate significant operating and administrative cost savings for the combined
  entity." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Each outstanding share of CalAtlantic common stock would convert into the right to
  receive 0.885 shares of Lennar Class A common stock and 0.0177 shares of Lennar Class B
  common stock, with an option to elect $48.26 per share in cash instead, subject to an
  aggregate cash cap of $1,162,250,000 (limiting cash elections to 24,083,091 shares);
  the stock-for-stock portion was intended to qualify as a tax-free reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Upon completion, former Lennar Class A and Class B stockholders together would own
  approximately 74% of the combined company, and former CalAtlantic stockholders
  approximately 26%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Lennar accounted for the Merger using the acquisition method of accounting, with Lennar
  designated as the acquiror of CalAtlantic for accounting purposes; the excess purchase
  price over the fair value of CalAtlantic's net tangible and identifiable assets would be
  recorded as goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Accounting Treatment of the Merger"]
- Lennar and CalAtlantic determined the Merger was not subject to the filing requirements
  of the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act, and no other federal or state
  antitrust filings, approvals or consents were required to consummate it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]

## Rationale and expected combination effects (as disclosed)
- The Merger was projected to result in approximately $100 million of cost savings in
  fiscal year 2018 and $365 million per year in fiscal year 2019 and subsequent years,
  attributed to reduced general and administrative costs and reduced homebuilding costs,
  with the filing noting that "Lennar and CalAtlantic have compatible product
  strategies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Lennar Board Recommendation and Its Reasons for the Merger"]
- The filing stated that Lennar's management had previously achieved anticipated cost
  savings following its February 2017 acquisition of WCI Communities, Inc., but noted
  that CalAtlantic was "much larger than WCI Communities, Inc. or any other company
  Lennar has ever acquired." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Risk Factors"]
- Lennar disclosed it would incur integration expenses converting "a large number of
  processes, policies, procedures, operations, technologies and systems," including
  purchasing, accounting and finance, sales, billing, payroll, pricing, revenue
  management, maintenance, marketing and employee benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Risk Factors"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: CalAtlantic's entire corporate structure merged with and into Merger
  Sub, a wholly owned Lennar subsidiary — the whole entity, not a carved-out business
  unit, was the transaction's subject; the filing does not describe a transitional
  services or shared-services agreement bridging a divided operating substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Customer-facing product/asset: CalAtlantic's homebuilding operations (entry-level
  through luxury homes in 43 metropolitan statistical areas across 19 states) together
  with its mortgage, title and escrow services were the subject of the combination in
  full. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Operational processes: the joint proxy framed the expected financial benefit as
  depending on Lennar applying "Lennar's homebuilding techniques" and cost-saving methods
  to CalAtlantic's operations after completion, distinguishing the acquirer's process
  model from that of the acquired company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Lennar Board Recommendation and Its Reasons for the Merger"]
- People/organization: from the effective time until November 30, 2018, Lennar committed
  to provide continuing CalAtlantic employees compensation and benefits "not less
  favorable in aggregate" than pre-closing (or comparable to similarly situated Lennar
  employees), service credit toward benefit eligibility/vesting, carryover of accrued
  vacation, and honoring of existing change-in-control and severance agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Employee Benefits Matters"]
- Senior management/controlling intent: in an early-stage negotiation exchange described
  in the "Background of the Merger" narrative, CalAtlantic's Chief Executive Officer
  understood from Lennar's President that Lennar anticipated "there would be no role for
  CalAtlantic's senior management team with the combined company," and Lennar's President
  separately told CalAtlantic's Executive Chairman that senior CalAtlantic management
  "should expect their employment to terminate as of the closing date." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- Board/governance: the current Lennar directors and executive officers were expected to
  continue after the Merger; the merger agreement additionally provided that Scott D.
  Stowell, CalAtlantic's Executive Chairman, would be added to the Lennar board effective
  at the time of the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Board of Directors and Executive Officers of Lennar After Completion of the Merger"]

## Financing and termination terms
- Completion of the Merger was not conditioned on Lennar obtaining financing; Lennar had
  privately placed $300 million of 2.95% Senior Notes due 2020 and $900 million of 4.75%
  Senior Notes due 2027 in an offering that closed November 29, 2017, intending to use
  proceeds toward the cash consideration, merger-related expenses and general corporate
  purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Financing Related to the Merger"]
- Under specified circumstances (e.g., a change of board recommendation or acceptance of
  a superior proposal), the party in that position would owe the other a termination fee
  of $178.7 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Effect of Termination; Termination Fees; Expense Reimbursement"]
- The merger agreement set an outside date of May 31, 2018 for the closing date to occur,
  subject to specified extensions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A "Termination of the Merger Agreement"]

## Completion
- Lennar and CalAtlantic stockholders both voted on the requisite proposals; the Merger
  closed on February 12, 2018, with CalAtlantic merged into Merger Sub, Merger Sub
  continuing as the surviving corporation and a Lennar subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At closing, CalAtlantic owned or controlled approximately 70,000 homesites, was building
  homes in 566 communities, and had a backlog of home sale contracts for 6,420 homes with
  a total dollar value of $3.2 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Total merger consideration issued was 82,731,943 shares of Lennar Class A common stock,
  1,654,639 shares of Lennar Class B common stock, and $1,162,249,972 in cash (holders of
  only 391,893 shares affirmatively elected cash; CalAtlantic's largest stockholder, MP CA
  Homes LLC, was deemed to have elected cash for the remaining shares up to the cap). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Immediately after the effective time, Scott D. Stowell was elected to Lennar's Board of
  Directors as required by the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Lennar had earlier offered holders of eight issues of CalAtlantic Senior Notes an
  exchange into Lennar Notes with substantially identical financial terms; by close of
  business February 1, 2018, $2.8 billion principal amount of CalAtlantic Notes had been
  tendered for exchange, and Lennar's obligation to issue exchange notes was conditioned
  on consummation of the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 2.03]
- On February 12, 2018, Lennar's stockholders separately approved an amendment to
  Lennar's Restated Certificate of Incorporation increasing authorized Class A common
  stock from 300,000,000 to 400,000,000 shares, to accommodate the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company, primarily stock-for-stock merger (with a capped cash-election option)
  transferred CalAtlantic's entire corporate entity — its homebuilding, mortgage, title
  and escrow business, together with all assets, liabilities and operations — into a
  Lennar-owned surviving subsidiary, with no disclosed carve-out or transitional-services
  agreement dividing the operating substrate. Continuity of rank-and-file employee
  compensation and benefits was contractually guaranteed through a fixed post-closing
  date, but CalAtlantic's own senior management team was understood, during negotiations,
  not to have a role in the combined company; only one CalAtlantic officer/director
  (Scott Stowell) was disclosed as joining Lennar's board, while Lennar's pre-existing
  board and executive officers were expected to continue unchanged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003442, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042884, Form 8-K]
