## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On April 20, 2016 the Company stated: "The Company has suspended the filing of its
  regular periodic reports on Form 10-K and Form 10-Q with the SEC," intending instead to
  furnish its Bankruptcy-Court Monthly Operating Reports under cover of Forms 8-K and to
  continue filing Forms 8-K for material developments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000139, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The registrant filed no Annual Report on Form 10-K (or Form 20-F) after its Form 10-K
  for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2014 (filed March 31, 2015, accession
  0001060990-15-000065); consequently no audited public annual financial statements exist
  for the ~2019-2021 outcome window. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history for CIK 0001060990 (https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001060990.json), form 10-K/20-F listing]
- The registrant filed a Form 15-12B on September 1, 2016 (accession
  0001060990-16-000164), terminating registration of its securities under the Securities
  Exchange Act; after that date it had no ongoing Exchange Act reporting obligation and
  filed no further periodic reports. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history for CIK 0001060990 (https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001060990.json), Form 15-12B accession 0001060990-16-000164]
