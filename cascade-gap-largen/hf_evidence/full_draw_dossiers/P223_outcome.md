## Continuing public-filer identity (feasibility-relevant)
- The legal registrant that had been Knight Transportation, Inc. (CIK 0000929452)
  filed a Form 15-12B on September 21, 2017 terminating its own separate SEC
  registration and filed no further annual report after its FY2016 Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing list, CIK 0000929452, Form 15-12B, filed 2017-09-21]
- The entity that continues to file periodic reports post-transaction is Knight-Swift
  Transportation Holdings Inc., CIK 0001492691 (the former Swift Transportation
  Company, renamed at closing), which filed its FY2020 Form 10-K on February 25, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]

## Retrospective accounting characterization of the 2017 transaction
- In describing the 2017 Merger, the FY2020 Form 10-K states: "Based on the evidence
  available, Knight was designated as the Accounting Acquirer while Swift was the
  acquirer for legal purposes. Therefore, Knight's historical results of operations
  replaced Swift's historical results of operations for all periods prior to the 2017
  Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]

## Realized financial record (fixed window: FY2020, ~3 years post-closing)
- For fiscal year 2020, Knight-Swift reported total revenue of $4,673,863 thousand
  (versus $4,843,950 thousand in fiscal year 2019) and net income attributable to
  Knight-Swift of $410,002 thousand (versus $309,206 thousand in fiscal year 2019),
  with a GAAP operating ratio of 87.9% (versus 91.2% in 2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]
- Goodwill on the consolidated balance sheet was $2,922,964 thousand at the end of
  fiscal year 2020, $2,918,992 thousand at the end of fiscal year 2019, and $2,919,176
  thousand at the end of fiscal year 2018 (including a $3,974 thousand goodwill
  adjustment attributed to the 2017 Merger, allocated to the Trucking segment); the
  10-K states there were no goodwill impairments identified during annual goodwill
  impairment testing in 2020, 2019, or 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]
- As of December 31, 2020, the company reported approximately 22,900 total employees
  (approximately 22,700 full-time), including 17,400 company driving associates and
  driver trainees; during 2020 the Trucking segment operated an average of 18,448
  tractors (16,379 company tractors and 2,069 independent-contractor tractors) and
  57,722 trailers, and the Intermodal segment operated an average of 577 tractors and
  10,604 intermodal containers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]

## Documented process/organization realignment over time
- The FY2020 10-K discloses that "during the first quarter of 2019," the company
  reorganized its operating segments: the Trucking segment "now includes the results
  of the previously-reported Knight Trucking, Swift Truckload, Swift Dedicated, and
  Swift Refrigerated segments"; the Logistics segment "now includes the results of the
  Knight brokerage and Swift logistics businesses which were previously included within
  the Knight Logistics and Swift non-reportable segments, respectively"; and the
  Intermodal segment "now includes the results of the previously-reported Swift
  Intermodal segment and the results of the Knight intermodal business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]
- This disclosure indicates that for approximately the first year and a half after the
  September 2017 closing, the company continued to report Knight's and Swift's
  trucking, logistics, and intermodal operations as separate segments before
  consolidating them into three unified reportable segments in Q1 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]

## Leadership continuity as of the outcome window

  Kevin P. Knight as Executive Chairman, and Gary J. Knight as Executive Vice Chairman

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Approximately three years after the September 2017 closing, the continuing public
  filer (Knight-Swift Transportation Holdings Inc., successor to Swift's SEC
  registration) reported combined revenue of $4.67 billion and net income of $410.0
  million for fiscal year 2020, no goodwill impairment against the approximately $2.92
  billion goodwill balance (part of which traces to the 2017 Merger), and continuity of
  the leadership installed at closing (Jackson as CEO, Kevin Knight as Executive
  Chairman, Miller as CFO). The FY2020 10-K also documents that the company operated
  Knight's and Swift's trucking, logistics, and intermodal businesses as distinct
  reporting segments for roughly the first year and a half after closing before
  combining them into unified segments in the first quarter of 2019, and separately
  discloses that Knight — though not the entity whose corporate shell and NYSE
  registration continued — was designated the Accounting Acquirer for GAAP purposes,
  with its historical results replacing Swift's in the combined company's reported
  financial history. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492691-21-000016, Form 10-K]
