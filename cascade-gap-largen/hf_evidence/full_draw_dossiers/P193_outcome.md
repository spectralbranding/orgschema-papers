## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- ILG, Inc. terminated its own SEC registration shortly after closing: it filed a Form 15-12B on September 14, 2018 to deregister its securities, and a Form 25-NSE was filed September 4, 2018 to remove ILG common stock from NASDAQ listing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-273354, Form 15-12B]
- No further periodic report (10-K or 10-Q) was filed under ILG's own CIK after its final pre-closing Form 10-Q (filed August 3, 2018); consequently there is no standalone ILG annual report covering the outcome window, and the realized record below is drawn from the acquirer's (MVW's) own periodic filings, to the extent MVW separately discloses the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-273354, Form 15-12B]

## Acquirer's segment disclosure isolating the acquired ILG business
- MVW's FY2021 Form 10-K states it operates in two reportable segments, Vacation Ownership and Exchange & Third-Party Management, and explicitly identifies which acquired-ILG brands fall in each: the businesses acquired from ILG operated within the Vacation Ownership segment include Vistana Signature Experiences and Hyatt Vacation Ownership; the businesses acquired from ILG operated within the Exchange & Third-Party Management segment include Aqua-Aston Hospitality, Interval International, Trading Places International, and Vacation Resorts International. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The Vacation Ownership segment blends the acquirer's pre-existing ("Legacy-MVW") business with acquired-ILG and acquired-Welk brands, so it is not a pure isolation of the ILG business; the Exchange & Third-Party Management segment, by contrast, is composed entirely of businesses MVW states it acquired from ILG. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- MVW's FY2021 10-K separately notes it accounts for "Legacy-ILG and Legacy-Welk" vacation-ownership notes receivable using different default/reserve assumptions than "Legacy-MVW" notes receivable, indicating the acquired ILG receivables portfolio continued to be tracked as a distinguishable sub-population within MVW's combined financial statements as of December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Realized financial record of the ILG-isolating segment (Exchange & Third-Party Management)
- For fiscal year 2021, MVW reported total segment revenue of $3,859 million, of which the Exchange & Third-Party Management segment contributed $320 million (8%) and Vacation Ownership contributed $3,539 million (92%); segment Adjusted EBITDA was $144 million for Exchange & Third-Party Management and $699 million for Vacation Ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- For fiscal year 2022, MVW reported total segment revenue of $4,633 million, of which Exchange & Third-Party Management contributed $291 million (6%) and Vacation Ownership contributed $4,342 million (94%); segment Adjusted EBITDA was $148 million for Exchange & Third-Party Management and $1,033 million for Vacation Ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- For comparison, MVW's FY2021 10-K reports fiscal year 2019 (pre-COVID, post-acquisition) Exchange & Third-Party Management segment Adjusted EBITDA of $183 million and fiscal year 2020 Adjusted EBITDA of $119 million for the same segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Goodwill and impairment testing
- MVW's FY2021 10-K states that during the fourth quarter of 2021 it conducted its annual goodwill impairment test and recorded no impairment charges, with the estimated fair values of all reporting units significantly exceeding their carrying values. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- MVW's FY2022 10-K states that during the fourth quarters of both 2022 and 2021 it conducted its annual goodwill impairment test (a qualitative evaluation in each year) and recorded no impairment charges, with reporting-unit fair values exceeding carrying amounts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- ILG deregistered from SEC reporting and NASDAQ listing within two weeks of the September 2018 closing and filed no further periodic reports under its own CIK, so the realized record traces through the acquirer's (MVW's) segment disclosures. MVW's Exchange & Third-Party Management segment — which it states is composed entirely of businesses acquired from ILG — reported segment revenue of $320 million (2021) and $291 million (2022) and segment Adjusted EBITDA of $144 million (2021) and $148 million (2022), against a partially-blended Vacation Ownership segment (containing other acquired-ILG brands alongside Legacy-MVW and Legacy-Welk) that grew revenue from $3,539 million to $4,342 million and Adjusted EBITDA from $699 million to $1,033 million over the same period; no goodwill impairment was recorded against any reporting unit in fiscal years 2021 or 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001524358-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
