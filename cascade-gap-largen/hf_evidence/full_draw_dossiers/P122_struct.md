# Structural sub-dossier — Corporate Property Associates 17 – Global Incorporated combination with W. P. Carey Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On October 31, 2018 the merger of Corporate Property Associates 17 – Global Incorporated ("CPA:17") and CPA17 Merger Sub LLC, an indirect merger subsidiary of W. P. Carey Inc., became effective under the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 17, 2018; CPA:17 merged with and into the Merger Sub, with the Merger Sub surviving as an indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Consideration: each share of CPA:17 common stock issued and outstanding was converted into the right to receive 0.160 shares of W. P. Carey common stock, with cash paid in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The exchange ratio was determined through negotiations between the board of W. P. Carey and a special committee of independent directors of CPA:17, each assisted by separate financial and legal advisors; the parties intended the merger to qualify as a tax-deferred reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- W. P. Carey Inc. is described in the merger 8-K as "the ultimate parent of the external advisor of CPA:17." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## The customer-facing asset that crossed the boundary
- CPA:17 described itself as "a publicly owned, non-traded REIT that invests primarily in commercial properties leased to companies domestically and internationally." [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K (FY2017) Item 1]
- As of December 31, 2017 its net-lease portfolio comprised full or partial ownership interests in 411 properties, substantially all fully occupied and triple-net leased to 116 tenants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K (FY2017) Item 2]

## The operating/management substrate (people, processes, organization)
- The registrant ran no operations of its own with its own staff: "We have no employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K (FY2017) Item 1]
- "We are managed by WPC through certain of its subsidiaries, or collectively, our Advisor." — i.e., the entity being combined was already managed by subsidiaries of the acquiring party before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K (FY2017) Item 1]
- Before the merger, Carey Asset Management Corp. ("CAM"), W. P. Carey & Co. B.V., and W. P. Carey Holdings, LLC (the "Special General Partner"), each an indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey, provided advisory services and served as special general partner to CPA:17 and its operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Advisory services were governed pre-merger by the Amended and Restated Advisory Agreement dated January 1, 2015 and the Amended and Restated Asset Management Agreement dated May 13, 2015, among CPA:17, CPA:17 Limited Partnership and the W. P. Carey advisory entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Contractual treatment of the shared substrate at closing
- The Advisory Agreements were automatically terminated upon the closing of the merger (not carried forward as a bridging arrangement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- On October 31, 2018, subsequent to the merger's effectiveness, W. P. Carey fully repaid CPA:17's obligations under CPA:17's senior unsecured revolving credit facility and delayed-draw term loan facility (the "Senior Credit Facility") and terminated that facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Under the merger agreement W. P. Carey agreed that no subordinated disposition fees would be payable to it and waived certain rights under the operating partnership's limited partnership agreement to change-of-control capital-proceeds distributions and to the Special General Partner interest (collectively the "Advisor Closing Amounts"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Legal vehicle, governance and approvals
- The combination required a registration statement on Form S-4 for the W. P. Carey shares, NYSE listing of those shares, and approval by the stockholders of both W. P. Carey and CPA:17; a definitive joint proxy statement/prospectus was filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005936, DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided a "go-shop" solicitation period ending July 18, 2018 followed by "no-shop" restrictions, and a CPA:17 termination fee of $114.0 million, reduced to $38.0 million for a superior competing transaction entered in compliance with the go-shop provisions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On entry into the merger agreement, CPA:17 suspended distribution reinvestments under its Distribution Reinvestment and Stock Purchase Plan and redemptions under its Redemption Plan (except Special Circumstances Redemptions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Articles of Amendment to CPA:17's charter (filed October 30, 2018; approved October 29, 2018 at a special meeting of stockholders) excluded a transaction involving securities listed on a national securities exchange for at least 12 months — including the Merger — from the charter's procedural and substantive requirements applicable to "Roll-Up Transactions" as that term is defined in the CPA:17 charter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A non-traded net-lease REIT was combined into its own external sponsor: the customer-facing asset — a triple-net-leased commercial-property portfolio (411 properties, 116 tenants at year-end 2017) — crossed into W. P. Carey, while the operating and management substrate that ran that portfolio was already provided before closing by W. P. Carey subsidiaries (the external Advisor), the registrant having no employees of its own; the pre-existing advisory/asset-management agreements were terminated at closing and the registrant's Senior Credit Facility was repaid and terminated, rather than the management substrate being transferred or bridged as a standalone operation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065167, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-040564, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K (FY2017) Item 1]
