# Structural sub-dossier — Wabtec combination with GE Transportation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Westinghouse Air Brake Technologies Corporation ("Wabtec"), a Delaware corporation
  headquartered in Wilmerding, Pennsylvania, entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger dated May 20, 2018 with General Electric Company ("GE"), Transportation
  Systems Holdings Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned GE subsidiary
  ("SpinCo"), and Wabtec US Rail Holdings, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly
  owned Wabtec subsidiary ("Merger Sub"), to combine Wabtec with GE's transportation
  business ("GE Transportation") in a "modified Reverse Morris Trust transaction."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- The structure: GE would transfer the remaining business and operations of GE
  Transportation to SpinCo, distribute a majority of SpinCo's common stock to GE
  stockholders by way of a spin-off (or, at GE's election, a split-off), and
  immediately thereafter Merger Sub would merge with and into SpinCo, with SpinCo
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Wabtec and each share of SpinCo common
  stock converting into the right to receive Wabtec common stock. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger was preceded by a "Direct Sale" of certain GE Transportation assets from
  GE to Wabtec US Rail, Inc. for a cash payment estimated at $2.9 billion in the proxy
  statement, with the Direct Sale Purchaser concurrently assuming certain GE
  Transportation liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger Agreement (as amended January 25, 2019) and the related Separation,
  Distribution and Sale Agreement closed on February 25, 2019: Merger Sub merged with
  and into SpinCo, SpinCo continued as a wholly owned Wabtec subsidiary, and the Direct
  Sale Purchaser paid GE $2.875 billion in cash for the Direct Sale assets. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001140361-19-003790, Form 8-K]
- At closing, Wabtec issued 46,764,016 shares of Wabtec common stock to former SpinCo
  common stockholders (i.e., holders of GE common stock as of February 14, 2019),
  19,018,207 shares of Wabtec common stock directly to GE, 10,000 shares of Wabtec
  Series A non-voting convertible preferred stock to GE, and paid GE $10.0 million in
  cash for all outstanding SpinCo Class B non-voting preferred stock; immediately after
  the Merger, approximately 49.2% of outstanding Wabtec common stock was held
  collectively by GE and former GE stockholders (9.9% held directly by GE, plus 15%
  underlying GE's convertible preferred stock), and approximately 50.8% was held by
  pre-Merger Wabtec stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-003790, Form 8-K]
- GE separately retained 15,000 shares of Class A non-voting preferred stock of SpinCo
  following the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-003790, Form 8-K]
- After the Merger, Wabtec common stock continued to be listed on the New York Stock
  Exchange under its existing symbol "WAB." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543,
  Form DEFM14A]

## What each party comprised at closing (the asset/product tier)
- Wabtec (doing business as Wabtec Corporation) described itself as one of the largest
  providers of value-added, technology-based equipment, systems and services for the
  global passenger transit and freight rail industries, manufacturing products for
  locomotives, freight cars and passenger transit vehicles, with roughly 18,000
  employees; it had acquired Faiveley Transport, S.A. in 2017. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- GE Transportation was described as "a global technology leader and supplier to the
  railroad, mining, marine, stationary power and drilling industries" that "designs,
  engineers and manufactures diesel-electric locomotives, supplies associated
  aftermarket parts and services and provides digital solutions"; as of December 31,
  2017 its global installed base was more than 22,500 diesel-electric locomotives, with
  sales in more than 50 countries, eight primary manufacturing facilities, and
  approximately 8,500 employees, generating 2017 revenue of $3,930 million and net
  earnings of $374 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- Post-closing, GE Transportation's operations were expected to be organized into
  Wabtec's Freight Segment, and the combined business was expected to be one of the
  world's largest providers of technology-enabled equipment, systems and services for
  the locomotive, freight and passenger rail industries, with approximately $8.0
  billion in revenue and 25,000 employees in 52 countries and an installed base of over
  22,500 locomotives. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent / management continuity provisions fixed at closing
- The Merger Agreement provided for the Wabtec Board to be increased by three
  additional independent directors designated as nominees by GE (subject to being
  reasonably acceptable to Wabtec's Nominating and Corporate Governance Committee),
  effective at closing, with each GE designee assigned to a different director class so
  that one comes up for reelection at each of the first three annual meetings following
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- The executive officers of Wabtec immediately prior to the Merger were generally
  expected to continue as the executive officers immediately following the Merger:
  Albert J. Neupaver was expected to remain executive chairman and Raymond T. Betler to
  remain president and CEO of Wabtec; Stéphane Rambaud-Measson was expected to become
  president and CEO of Wabtec's Transit Segment, and Rafael O. Santana — president and
  CEO of GE Transportation — was expected to become president and CEO of Wabtec's
  Freight Segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]

## Employee/organization tier bridging at closing
- Under the form of Employee Matters Agreement, SpinCo (or a subsidiary) and the Direct
  Sale Purchaser were to continue or commence employment of GE Transportation employees
  as "Continuing Employees" as of the closing date; for 12 months following closing,
  Wabtec and its subsidiaries were required to provide compensation, benefits, and
  other material employment terms comparable to those in place immediately prior to
  the Distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- Wabtec (including the Direct Sale Purchaser) would not assume any U.S. collective
  bargaining agreement covering GE Transportation employees, but would be required to
  recognize and bargain with certified labor representatives as a successor employer
  where applicable; non-U.S. collective bargaining agreements would generally be
  assumed by SpinCo or a Direct Sale Transferred Subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- GE and SpinCo agreed to enter a Transition Services Agreement on the Distribution
  Date under which GE would provide SpinCo with information technology, supply chain,
  financial, human resources and other specified services and facility access (and
  SpinCo would provide GE limited reciprocal services), generally planned for an
  initial term of 18 to 24 months and not to exceed 24 months total. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- GE agreed to license certain "GE Names and Marks" to SpinCo under a Trademark License
  Agreement effective as of the Distribution Date, royalty-free during an initial
  five-year term (renewable by SpinCo for one additional five-year period), restricted
  to use as part of SpinCo's corporate name and in connection with existing licensed
  products; GE retained exclusive ownership of the marks and could terminate the
  license on a change of control of SpinCo. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543,
  Form DEFM14A]
- GE and SpinCo agreed to an IP Cross License Agreement granting each other
  non-exclusive, perpetual, royalty-free licenses to specified intellectual property,
  software, technology and data as used by GE Transportation as of the agreement date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
- GE, SpinCo, Wabtec and the Direct Sale Purchaser agreed to a Tax Matters Agreement
  allocating tax liabilities/benefits around the Distribution, Merger and Direct Sale,
  including a requirement that Wabtec pay GE amounts (capped at a specified total) as
  Wabtec realizes cash tax savings from increased tax basis in the acquired assets, and
  restricting SpinCo/Wabtec for two years from actions that could jeopardize the
  transaction's intended tax treatment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form
  DEFM14A]
- Under a Shareholders Agreement between Wabtec and GE, GE's retained ~9.9% direct
  stake (the "Initial Shares") was subject to standstill restrictions and a voting
  agreement, and GE was obligated to sell all Wabtec shares it beneficially owned
  within three years of closing (with a related Tax Matters Agreement obligation to
  sell a portion within two years of the Distribution Date). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined Wabtec with GE Transportation via a modified Reverse Morris
  Trust: a cash Direct Sale of certain assets/liabilities ($2.875 billion as closed),
  a spin-off distribution of SpinCo shares to GE stockholders, and a stock-for-stock
  merger converting SpinCo shares into Wabtec shares, leaving Wabtec as the sole
  surviving NYSE-listed registrant (ticker unchanged) and GE Transportation organized
  into Wabtec's Freight Segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-003790, Form 8-K]
- Controlling-intent and organizational continuity were addressed contractually rather
  than by a clean handover: three GE-designated directors joined the Wabtec board,
  GE Transportation's own CEO was slated to lead the combined Freight Segment while
  incumbent Wabtec executives were slated to remain in the top corporate roles, and the
  employee, tax, trademark, IP and transition-services agreements each separately
  allocated a piece of the pre-closing shared substrate (personnel terms, brand use,
  IP rights, tax attributes, and interim shared services) rather than transferring it
  as a single intact unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040543, Form DEFM14A]
