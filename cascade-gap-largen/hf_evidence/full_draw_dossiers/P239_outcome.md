## Public-reporting status and separate segment disclosure
- Altra's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2022 (filed 2023-03-01) reports two reportable operating segments aligned with key product types and end markets: "Power Transmission Technologies" and "Automation & Specialty" ("A&S") — the latter comprising the Kollmorgen, Portescap and Thomson brands acquired in the 2018 transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The A&S segment's financial results are separately disclosed for fiscal years 2020, 2021 and 2022 in the segment footnote of that 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized segment financial trajectory (A&S segment, per Altra's own disclosure)
- A&S segment net sales: $911.8 million (FY2020); $979.0 million (FY2021); $965.5 million (FY2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- A&S segment earnings (income/(loss) from operations, before certain corporate-level allocations): a loss of approximately $10.4 million (FY2020); a loss of approximately $8.3 million (FY2021); income of approximately $136.0 million (FY2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Consolidated Altra net income/(loss): a net loss of approximately $25.5 million (FY2020); net income of approximately $27.7 million (FY2021); net income of approximately $127.0 million (FY2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Documented impairment and divestiture at the acquired-business tier
- In FY2020, Altra recorded non-cash impairment charges of approximately $8.4 million (indefinite-lived intangible assets) and $139.1 million (goodwill) at the Jacobs Vehicle Systems ("JVS") reporting unit within the A&S segment — JVS being one of the four operating companies (Portescap, Kollmorgen, Thomson, Jacobs Vehicle Systems) comprising the business acquired from Fortive in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- In the fourth quarter of FY2021, Altra committed to a plan to sell the JVS business "in an effort to exit the heavy-duty trucks industry," recording an additional non-cash goodwill impairment of $60.0 million and an asset-held-for-sale impairment of $82.4 million (total $142.4 million) at the JVS reporting unit as a result of that decision. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Altra recorded a further $10.2 million non-cash held-for-sale impairment charge at the JVS reporting unit during FY2022, prior to completing the sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- On February 8, 2022, Altra entered into a purchase and sale agreement with Cummins Inc. to sell the JVS business for $325.0 million in cash (subject to customary adjustments), and completed the sale on April 8, 2022, receiving net cash consideration of approximately $321.7 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The JVS divestiture reduced A&S segment net sales by approximately $141.7 million for FY2022 relative to FY2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Subsequent change of control affecting the continuing public filer (within the outcome window)
- On October 26, 2022, Altra entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Regal Rexnord Corporation and its merger subsidiary, under which Altra would become a wholly-owned subsidiary of Regal Rexnord in exchange for $62.00 in cash per share; Altra's shareholders approved the merger (announced via 8-K filed January 18, 2023). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The merger with Regal Rexnord was completed on March 27, 2023: Altra requested that Nasdaq halt trading of, and withdraw from listing, Altra common stock (previously trading as "AIMC"), and file a Form 25 notification of removal from listing and registration; Altra also stated its intent to file a Form 15 to deregister its common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-23-000298, Form 8-K]
- As a result of the merger's consummation, a change in control of Altra occurred and Altra became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Regal Rexnord Corporation, with its then-sitting directors (including Carl R. Christenson and others) ceasing to serve in that capacity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-23-000298, Form 8-K]
- This change of control, occurring approximately 4.5 years after the October 2018 Fortive A&S transaction, means Altra's FY2022 Form 10-K is the last periodic report in which the A&S segment's results are disclosed as part of Altra's own public filings; no subsequent Altra Form 10-K exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022; SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-23-000298, Form 8-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year outcome window, the acquired A&S segment's results are separately and continuously disclosed in Altra's own Form 10-K, showing operating losses in FY2020 (-$10.4M) and FY2021 (-$8.3M) driven substantially by non-cash goodwill/intangible impairments (totaling approximately $147.5 million across FY2020-FY2021) concentrated at the Jacobs Vehicle Systems reporting unit, followed by the completed divestiture of that business to Cummins Inc. for $325.0 million in April 2022, and a swing to segment operating income of approximately $136.0 million in FY2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-005578, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Separately, within the same outcome window, Altra itself (the continuing public filer) entered into and completed a change-of-control merger with Regal Rexnord Corporation, ceasing to be an independently listed public company as of March 27, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-23-000298, Form 8-K]
