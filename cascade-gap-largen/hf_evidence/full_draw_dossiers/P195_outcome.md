## Financial condition entering the outcome window
- In its annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 (filed February 26,
  2020), the Company reported a net loss of $226.8 million and a loss from operations of
  $188.1 million for 2019, following a net loss of $401.9 million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-002314, Form 10-K FY2019]
- As of December 31, 2019, the Company reported cash and cash equivalents of $144.8
  million, a working capital deficit of $505.5 million, an accumulated deficit of $333.9
  million, and a remaining principal balance of $852.0 million under its Term Loan
  Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-002314, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The Company's independent auditors and management disclosed that recurring losses from
  operations and a net working-capital deficiency, together with dependence on compliance
  with a Second Amended Standstill Agreement and Term Loan Agreement and on successfully
  conducting a "Sale Process," raised substantial doubt about the Company's ability to
  continue as a going concern within one year of the financial statements' issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-002314, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Chapter 11 filing and asset sale
- On May 20, 2020, Akorn, Inc. and its U.S. direct and indirect subsidiaries filed
  voluntary petitions under chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the District of Delaware, continuing to operate as debtors in possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-064893, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Concurrently, the Company entered into a "Stalking Horse" asset purchase agreement
  dated May 20, 2020 with certain of its existing term-loan lenders, under which the
  lenders agreed to purchase substantially all of the Company Parties' assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-064893, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Bankruptcy Court approved the Stalking Horse asset purchase agreement and bidding
  procedures on June 15, 2020; no higher or otherwise better qualified bids were received
  by the applicable deadline, and the planned auction was cancelled. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-111583, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed the Company's Modified Joint Chapter 11 Plan on
  September 4, 2020, and approved the asset sale under Section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code
  on September 2, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-111583, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On October 1, 2020, the Company Parties consummated the Section 363 sale, completing
  the disposition of substantially all of their assets to the lender-buyer group; the
  consideration comprised assumption of certain liabilities, a 100% credit bid of the
  lenders' pre-petition term-loan claims (discharging those claims), and a cash amount set
  by an agreed wind-down budget. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-111583, Form 8-K Items 1.03, 2.01]
- On the October 1, 2020 effective date, all shares of Company common stock outstanding
  immediately before the sale were cancelled and discharged and became of no force and
  effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-111583, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
