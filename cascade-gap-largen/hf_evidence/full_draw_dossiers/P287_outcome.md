## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed under CIK 1060009 (or by its named
  successor "Kindred Healthcare, LLC") after the Form 15-12B deregistration on July 12,
  2018; the last annual report on file is the Form 10-K for fiscal year 2017, filed March
  1, 2018, before the closing. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history, CIK 0001060009]
- The private-equity-controlled parent of the hospital/rehabilitation business
  ("Legacy LifePoint Health, Inc.," CIK 0001301611, itself taken private in 2018) also
  filed no Form 10-K after its own fiscal-year-2017 annual report, so neither successor
  business named at closing has an independent SEC periodic-reporting record in the
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history, CIK 0001301611]

## Realized record: the home-health/hospice/community-care business
- Humana Inc.'s Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 17, 2022) discloses that
  on August 17, 2021 — roughly 3.1 years after the 2018 closing — Humana "acquired the
  remaining 60% interest in Kindred at Home, or KAH, the nation's largest home health and
  hospice provider, from TPG Capital and Welsh, Carson, Anderson & Stowe, two private
  equity funds, for an enterprise value of $8.2 billion," which included Humana's existing
  equity value of $2.4 billion associated with its prior 40% minority ownership interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000049071-22-000017, Form 10-K (Humana Inc., FY2021)]
- Humana's FY2021 10-K reports a $1,129 million gain on its Kindred at Home equity-method
  investment recognized upon completion of the acquisition, together with $128 million of
  transaction and integration costs associated with the Kindred at Home acquisition,
  disclosed as a consolidated income-statement item for 2021 (no comparable amounts in
  2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0000049071-22-000017, Form 10-K (Humana Inc., FY2021)]
- The same filing states the definitive agreement for the transaction was signed April
  27, 2021, and describes KAH as having "locations in 40 states." [SRC: SEC accession 0000049071-22-000017, Form 10-K (Humana Inc., FY2021)]

## Realized record: the hospital/rehabilitation business
- Reputable press reports that in late December 2021 (per LifePoint Health's own
  announcement dated December 23, 2021, and a Modern Healthcare report of the same date),
  Apollo Global Management-affiliated LifePoint Health completed a transaction with the
  hospital/rehabilitation business retained by TPG Capital, WCAS, and Canadian pension
  fund PSP, forming a new company, ScionHealth, from 61 of the former Kindred long-term
  acute care hospitals plus 18 LifePoint community hospitals (79 hospital campuses in 25
  states), while LifePoint retained Kindred's rehabilitation and behavioral-health
  businesses, acute rehabilitation units, outpatient centers, and post-acute facilities. [SRC: https://pestakeholder.org/news/apollo-global-management-completes-merger-of-kindred-healthcare-and-lifepoint-health-shifts-some-hospitals-to-new-company/]
- The same press source reports the transaction was funded by a $350 million preferred-
  equity contribution from LifePoint to ScionHealth plus $550 million in new ScionHealth
  debt, together funding a $900 million payout to TPG Capital, WCAS, and PSP as the prior
  owners of the Kindred hospital/rehabilitation business. [SRC: https://pestakeholder.org/news/apollo-global-management-completes-merger-of-kindred-healthcare-and-lifepoint-health-shifts-some-hospitals-to-new-company/]
- ScionHealth's own announcement (October 26, 2021) of the planned transaction states
  the new company would consist of "79 hospital campuses in 25 states, including
  Kindred's 61 long-term acute care hospitals and 18 of LifePoint's community hospitals,"
  headquartered in Louisville, Kentucky, and that Kindred CEO Benjamin Breier would depart
  upon closing. [SRC: https://www.scionhealth.com/News-Stories/lifepoint-health-and-kindred-healthcare-to-launch-new-company-sc]
- These figures and the December 2021 closing are reported via reputable press because
  neither successor entity (Kindred Healthcare, LLC nor Legacy LifePoint Health, Inc.) has
  a subsequent SEC periodic filing that independently confirms them; no primary-filing
  corroboration was found for this half of the outcome. [UNVERIFIED — no SEC primary filing located for the ScionHealth/LifePoint transaction]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly 3.1 to 3.5 years of the 2018 closing, both businesses separated from the
  original registrant underwent a further change of ownership: the home-health/hospice
  business was fully absorbed into its co-owner Humana's consolidated operations (August
  2021, per Humana's own Form 10-K), and the hospital/rehabilitation business was split
  again and recombined with a different hospital chain's assets under a new entity name,
  ScionHealth (December 2021, per press reporting), with its former private-equity owners
  paid out at that time. [SRC: SEC accession 0000049071-22-000017, Form 10-K (Humana Inc., FY2021)] [SRC: https://pestakeholder.org/news/apollo-global-management-completes-merger-of-kindred-healthcare-and-lifepoint-health-shifts-some-hospitals-to-new-company/]
- No entity carrying the original "Kindred Healthcare" corporate/reporting identity filed
  a 10-K in the outcome window; the realized record above is reconstructed entirely from
  the subsequent owner's own disclosure (for one half) and reputable press (for the
  other), rather than from a continuous public financial record of the acquired business
  itself. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history, CIK 0001060009]
