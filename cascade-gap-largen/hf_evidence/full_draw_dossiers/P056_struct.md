# Structural sub-dossier — Central European Distribution Corporation / Peulla (Whitehall Group) transaction (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is a Form 8-K reporting a Material Definitive Agreement (Item 1.01) and
  Completion of Acquisition (Item 2.01); it IS the deal-terms document, attaching the
  Share Sale and Purchase Agreement (Exhibit 2.1), the Shareholders' Agreement (Exhibit
  10.1), and a form of Registration Rights Agreement (Exhibit 4.1). No separate
  deal-terms primary was required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01/2.01/9.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On May 23, 2008, Central European Distribution Corporation ("the Company") and its
  subsidiary Polmos Bialystok S.A. ("Bialystok") entered into and closed a Share Sale and
  Purchase Agreement and related agreements with Barclays Wealth Trustees (Jersey) Limited
  (as trustee of the First National Trust, "the Trustee") and WHL Holdings Limited
  ("Whitehall"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Bialystok acquired from the Trustee 3,749 Class A shares and 5,625 Class B shares of
  Peulla Enterprises Limited, a Cyprus private limited company and the parent of Whitehall,
  "representing 50% minus one vote of the voting power, and 75% of the economic interests,
  in Peulla." The majority voting power and 25% of economic interests remained held by the
  Trustee for the benefit of the First National Trust. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Consideration: (1) $200.00 million cash paid at closing; (2) 976,946 shares of the
  Company's common stock (par $0.01) to be issued approximately 45 days after closing; and
  (3) EUR 16.05 million payable on the first anniversary of the closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A share-price make-whole applied: if the average closing price of the Company's stock
  over a defined 20-trading-day window fell below a $51.32 Minimum Share Price, the Company
  would pay the Trustee the shortfall on the Share Consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## The customer-facing business (product/asset acquired)
- Whitehall Group is described as "a leading importer of premium spirits and wines in
  Russia." The interest acquired is an equity stake in its Cyprus parent (Peulla), not the
  operating assets or personnel directly. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What did NOT cross to the acquirer at closing (operations / people)
- Management control of the operating business was retained by the founder, not
  transferred: "Mark Kaoufman, Whitehall's founder and chief executive officer, will
  continue to lead Whitehall and its subsidiaries (the 'Whitehall Group')." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- "Mr. Kaoufman, or a management company under his sole effective control, will exercise
  management control over the Whitehall Group as its chief executive officer, subject to
  the Shareholders' Agreement and the overall supervision of the Board." The operating
  organization and its principal thus remained in place under the founder's control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Governance and bridging arrangements at closing
- A Shareholders' Agreement dated May 23, 2008 (Bialystok, the Trustee, and Peulla)
  governs Peulla as jointly owned share capital. Peulla's board has six directors — three
  appointed by Bialystok and three by the Trustee — with an annually rotating chairman and
  a chairman's casting vote on certain deadlocks, except that specified matters (material
  charter amendments, material business-plan changes, mergers, equity/debt issuance,
  certain asset dispositions, and entry into/termination of material contracts) require
  unanimous director approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Shareholders' Agreement grants Bialystok a Call Right to buy, and the Trustee a Put
  Right to sell, all (but not less than all) of the Trustee's Peulla shares; exercise price
  is a function of the Company's financial performance over defined periods. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- These rights are constrained by a pre-existing external arrangement: while "the joint
  venture agreement between Moet Hennessy International and Whitehall remains in effect,"
  the Put/Call rights are subject in certain circumstances to Moet Hennessy International's
  consent, and the Call Right may not be exercised without Mr. Kaoufman's consent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A Registration Rights Agreement was to be executed on delivery of the Share
  Consideration, granting the Trustee registration rights (and transfer restrictions) on
  the shares issued to it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 3.02/Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The Company acquired, through its Bialystok subsidiary, 75% of the economic interest but
  only 50%-minus-one-vote of the voting power in Peulla (parent of the Russian premium
  spirits/wine importer Whitehall Group) for $200.0 million cash, 976,946 of its own
  shares, and EUR 16.05 million deferred; control of the entity was shared through a 3+3
  board with unanimity carve-outs and reciprocal put/call rights, while day-to-day
  management of the operating business remained with founder-CEO Mark Kaoufman, and the
  target's separate Moet Hennessy joint venture continued to constrain the parties' exit
  rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-125713, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
