## Public-reporting status of the acquired entity after the transaction
- Green Bancorp, Inc.'s last annual report as an independent registrant was its Form 10-K
  for fiscal year 2017, filed 2018-03-15; no Form 10-K covering fiscal year 2018 or any
  later period was filed under Green's registrant CIK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-003234, Form 10-K]
- The merger completed January 1, 2019; Green notified the Nasdaq Global Select Market
  that, effective as of that date, Green common stock would be converted into the right to
  receive merger consideration, and Nasdaq filed a Form 25-NSE on 2018-12-31 reporting the
  removal of Green common stock from listing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-19-000005, Form 8-K Item 3.01; SEC accession 0001354457-18-000552, Form 25-NSE]
- Green (by then represented by Veritex "as successor to Green Bancorp, Inc.") filed a
  Form 15-12B on 2019-01-10 certifying termination of registration of its common stock,
  stating that as of that date there were no holders of record of Green common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001606363-19-000009, Form 15-12B]
- Consequently, no separate audited public financial statements exist for Green Bancorp,
  Inc. for the fixed ~3–5-year (approximately 2021–2023) outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001606363-19-000009, Form 15-12B]

## Extent to which the acquirer separately discloses the acquired business
- Veritex Holdings, Inc. (the surviving corporation, CIK 1501570) continued as an SEC
  reporting company after the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-22-000039, Form 10-K]
- In its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed 2022-03-01), Veritex states that "Our
  business is conducted through one reportable segment, community banking," and, in its
  segment-reporting footnote, that "The Company has one reportable segment. All of the
  Company's activities are interrelated, and each activity is dependent and assessed based
  on how each activity of the Company supports the others." [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-22-000039, Form 10-K]
- The same Form 10-K lists "Green Bank ('Green') through Green Bancorp, Inc." among a
  table of prior bank acquisitions (acquired January 2019, 21 branches, Houston and
  Dallas) as historical background; it does not present a separate segment, business
  line, or line-item financial disclosure for the former Green Bank business distinct from
  Veritex's consolidated, single-segment results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001501570-22-000039, Form 10-K]
