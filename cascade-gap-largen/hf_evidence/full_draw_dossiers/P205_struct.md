# Structural sub-dossier — Sun Bancorp, Inc. / OceanFirst Financial Corp. merger (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 30, 2017, OceanFirst Financial Corp. (a Delaware corporation), Sun Bancorp,
  Inc. (a New Jersey corporation), and Mercury Merger Sub Corp. (a New Jersey
  corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of OceanFirst) entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger providing for a two-step combination: (i) Merger Sub merges with and
  into Sun, with Sun surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of OceanFirst (the
  "first-step merger"), then (ii) Sun merges with and into OceanFirst, with OceanFirst
  surviving as the combined company (the "second-step merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- Immediately following completion of the integrated mergers, Sun National Bank (a
  national bank and wholly-owned subsidiary of Sun) was to merge with and into
  OceanFirst Bank (a wholly-owned subsidiary of OceanFirst), with OceanFirst Bank
  surviving the bank merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- At the time of filing, OceanFirst Bank was a community bank with $5.2 billion in
  assets and 51 branches in Central and Southern New Jersey; Sun National Bank had
  $2.2 billion in assets and 35 locations primarily in New Jersey, 30 of which were
  branch offices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and legal mechanics
- Each outstanding share of Sun common stock (except certain shares held by Sun or
  OceanFirst) converts into the right to receive either cash consideration (a formula
  of $3.78 plus 0.7884 multiplied by OceanFirst's trailing volume-weighted average
  share price) or a corresponding number of OceanFirst shares, subject to shareholder
  election and proration provisions in the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- Using the assumed OceanFirst closing price as of September 15, 2017, the aggregate
  value of the merger consideration was approximately $449.5 million, with a maximum
  of 18,646,027 OceanFirst shares estimated issuable at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- Completion of the integrated mergers and the bank merger was conditioned on, among
  other things, approval by OceanFirst stockholders of the share issuance, approval by
  Sun shareholders of the merger, and receipt of required regulatory approvals
  including the Federal Reserve Board and the Office of the Comptroller of the
  Currency. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]

## What crossed at the tier level
- The customer-facing banking product/asset (deposits, loans, the branch network) was
  carried by Sun National Bank's charter, which did not survive as a separate legal
  vehicle: it was merged into and absorbed by OceanFirst Bank's charter, with
  OceanFirst Bank as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- Governance continuity was partial and time-limited: two current members of the Sun
  board were to be appointed to the boards of directors of OceanFirst and OceanFirst
  Bank at the effective time; if such an appointee resigned before the two-year
  anniversary of closing, OceanFirst's Leadership Committee was required to select a
  replacement from the former Sun board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- The employee-benefit-plan substrate was not carried over intact: Sun was to
  terminate the Sun National Bank 401(k) Plan prior to closing, with participants'
  vested balances and outstanding loan balances rolled into an "eligible retirement
  plan" newly designated by OceanFirst or its subsidiaries, rather than the existing
  plan continuing under the acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- Each Sun executive officer was party to a pre-existing change-in-control continuity
  agreement providing a cash severance payment (a multiple of salary plus incentive
  pay), retirement and welfare benefit payments, and accelerated vesting of equity
  awards, triggered by the closing of the integrated mergers or a qualifying
  termination of employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services arrangement, or comparable interim
  operating bridge for the combined banking operations is described in the merger
  agreement or the joint proxy statement/prospectus narrative. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]

## Risk factors disclosed at closing bearing on process/people alignment
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus states that the success of the transactions,
  including anticipated benefits and cost savings, depends in part on OceanFirst's
  ability to combine and integrate the two businesses "in a manner that permits growth
  opportunities and does not materially disrupt existing customer relations nor result
  in decreased revenues due to loss of customers," and that the integration process
  "could" result in such disruption. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
- The same disclosure states that retention of certain employees by Sun or OceanFirst
  "may be challenging while the Transactions are pending," and that key-employee
  departure driven by integration uncertainty could adversely affect the surviving
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company, two-step statutory merger
  (holding-company merger immediately followed by a bank-charter merger) in which the
  target's banking charter, and with it the customer-facing product/branch
  infrastructure, was legally absorbed into the acquirer's surviving bank; governance
  continuity was limited to two time-limited board seats, the target's 401(k) plan was
  terminated rather than continued, and no separate transition-services bridge is
  disclosed in the closing-era filing reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-289525, DEFM14A]
