# Structural sub-dossier — Parkway, Inc. merger with affiliates of Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Parkway, Inc., a Maryland corporation and self-managed REIT owning and operating
  office properties in Houston, Texas, agreed to merge with Real Estate Houston US LLC,
  a subsidiary of Canada Pension Plan Investment Board (CPPIB), with Parkway surviving
  as a subsidiary of CPPIB, pursuant to a definitive Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  June 29, 2017 among Parkway, Parkway Properties LP, Real Estate Houston US Trust, Real
  Estate Houston US LLC and Real Estate Houston US LP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]
- Parent (Real Estate Houston US Trust, a Delaware statutory trust) and its subsidiaries
  Merger Sub (a Delaware LLC) and Merger Partnership (a Delaware LP) were newly formed
  entities created solely to engage in the transaction and had carried on no activities
  before it other than those incidental to formation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties structured the transaction as a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub
  merged with and into Parkway (the "Company Merger"), with Parkway surviving as a
  subsidiary of Parent, and Merger Partnership merged with and into Parkway Properties
  LP (the "Partnership Merger"), with the partnership surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of Parkway common stock were entitled to receive an aggregate of $23.05 per
  share in cash: $19.05 of merger consideration plus a $4.00 special cash dividend paid
  before the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]
- The Mergers were completed on October 12, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]

## What Parkway comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Parkway's portfolio consisted of five Class A office assets comprising 19 buildings
  and approximately 8.7 million rentable square feet in the Greenway, Galleria and
  Westchase submarkets of Houston, plus a fee-based Third-Party Services Business
  operated through a wholly owned subsidiary, Eola Office Partners, LLC, which managed
  approximately 3.8 million square feet for primarily third-party owners as of June 30,
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]

## Separation of the management/process tier from the acquired entity at closing
- Separately on October 12, 2017, in connection with the consummation of the Mergers,
  Parkway and its Operating Partnership entered into a Separation and Purchase Agreement
  and other related agreements with Parkway Property Investments, LLC ("PPI"), a newly
  formed Delaware LLC, pursuant to which Parkway and the Operating Partnership separated
  their management business and operations from the remainder of their business, assets
  and liabilities, and PPI purchased the subsidiaries that held Parkway's management
  business and operations (the "Management Separation"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]
- James Heistand (Chief Executive Officer), Scott Francis (Chief Financial Officer) and
  Jason Bates (Chief Investment Officer) of Parkway each own membership interests in
  PPI. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]
- Also on October 12, 2017, in connection with the Management Separation, Parkway
  entered into an Enterprise Management Agreement with PPI and Parkway Operating
  Partnership LP, under which PPI agreed to provide property management, enterprise
  management, transition and other services with respect to Parkway, its subsidiaries
  and their properties, in exchange for fees and expense reimbursement; the initial term
  of the Enterprise Management Agreement was 14 months. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]
- Parkway's executive officers were expected to continue employment with the Company or
  otherwise provide third-party management services to the Company's properties after
  closing, and had entered into Amendments to their Employment Agreements — effective
  only upon closing — under which they were eligible for a change-in-control cash
  payment at closing; Management was expected to re-invest in the acquisition vehicle an
  amount equal to the lesser of 1% of the equity interests in the acquisition vehicle and
  $8 million after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-256025, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Also on October 12, 2017, following consummation of the Mergers, Parkway's board of
  directors unanimously approved a Plan of complete liquidation and dissolution of the
  Company (effective October 13, 2017), under which Parkway would sell or distribute in
  kind all or substantially all of its assets to its stockholder(s) and thereafter
  dissolve. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]
- On October 12, 2017, in connection with completion of the Company Merger, Parkway
  requested that the New York Stock Exchange suspend trading in its common shares and
  file a Form 25 notification of removal from listing and registration; all common
  shares were removed from trading on the NYSE that day before the opening of trading.
  Following effectiveness of the Form 25, Parkway intended to file a Form 15 to
  terminate registration of the common shares under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company cash merger of the REIT's asset/product tier
  (the Houston office portfolio and the third-party services business) into a newly
  formed CPPIB acquisition vehicle with a same-day, contractually distinct separation of
  the management/process tier: the operating subsidiaries that housed property and
  enterprise management were carved out and sold to a separate, management-team-owned
  entity (PPI), which was then bridged back to the acquired portfolio through a
  fixed-term (14-month) Enterprise Management Agreement rather than being retained
  intact inside the acquired company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311883, Form 8-K]
