# Structural sub-dossier — Education Realty Trust, Inc. acquisition by a Greystar-led investor group (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Education Realty Trust, Inc. ("the Company"), a Maryland corporation electing REIT
  status, agreed to merge with and into GSHGIF REIT ("REIT Merger Sub"), a Maryland
  REIT and wholly owned subsidiary of GSHGIF LTP, LP ("Parent," later renamed Greystar
  Student Housing Growth and Income LTP, LP), an affiliate of Greystar Real Estate
  Partners, LLC, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 25, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- Parent, REIT Merger Sub, OP Merger Sub, and DownREIT Merger Sub were each "formed
  solely for the purpose of facilitating Parent's acquisition of us and our
  subsidiaries and [have] not carried on any activities to date." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share of common stock was to be converted into the right to receive
  $41.50 in cash, without interest, subject to certain dividend-related adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- The mergers (REIT merger, Operating Partnership merger, DownREIT Partnership merger)
  were completed on September 20, 2018; the proposed transaction was described as "the
  acquisition of EdR and its subsidiaries by a private investment group led by Greystar
  through a newly-formed, perpetual-life fund." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Introductory Note] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- Following the mergers the registrant continued in existence as "Greystar Student
  Housing Growth and Income Trust (as successor by merger to Education Realty Trust,
  Inc.)," with the operating partnership renamed "Greystar Student Housing Growth and
  Income OP, LP." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K]

## The business and product at closing
- The Company described itself as "a self-managed and self-advised company ...
  to develop, acquire, own and manage collegiate housing communities located near or
  on university campuses," also providing third-party management and third-party
  development-consulting services on collegiate housing projects. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- As of June 30, 2018 the Company owned 67 collegiate housing communities in 24 states
  (~35,300 beds, 39 university campuses) and provided third-party management services
  for 12 additional communities in 7 states (~7,000 beds, 11 campuses). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]

## Asset-level split of the portfolio at closing
- A separate asset purchase and sale agreement, dated June 25, 2018, between REIT
  Merger Sub and the "Blackstone Asset Purchaser" (defined as a 95%/5% joint venture
  led by an affiliate of the Blackstone Investor with an affiliate of Greystar)
  provided for the Blackstone Asset Purchaser to acquire approximately $1.2 billion of
  the Company's assets from the Operating Partnership immediately prior to, but
  subject to, the closing of the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]
- At closing, "a joint venture between an affiliate of Blackstone Real Estate Income
  Trust, Inc. and an affiliate of Greystar acquired a portfolio of 20 of the Company's
  communities for approximately $1.2 billion," while the balance of the Company's
  assets crossed via the mergers into the wholly Greystar-affiliated Parent structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]
- For U.S. federal income tax purposes the transaction was treated as if the Company
  sold certain assets to the Blackstone Asset Purchaser for approximately $1.2 billion
  and sold the remainder of its assets to REIT Merger Sub for the balance of the
  merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]

## People, management, and organization at closing
- All seven members of the Company's board of directors (Randall L. Churchey, John V.
  Arabia, Kimberly K. Schaefer, Howard A. Silver, John T. Thomas, Thomas Trubiana, and
  Wendell W. Weakley) ceased to be directors at the REIT Merger effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- Each named executive officer (Randall L. Churchey, CEO; Thomas Trubiana, President;
  Edwin B. Brewer, Jr., EVP/CFO; Christine Richards, EVP/COO; Lindsey Mackie Vance,
  SVP/Chief Accounting Officer) ceased to hold those positions effective as of the
  REIT Merger effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- The proxy stated that "although we expect the employment of the NEOs to continue
  following the mergers, each NEO's employment agreement will be terminated at the
  time of closing of the mergers," with each NEO receiving a lump-sum change-in-control
  payment (e.g., cash severance of $4,550,175 for the CEO) plus accelerated bonus and
  equity vesting, and a one-to-three-year post-termination non-compete covering "the
  business of owning and managing off-campus collegiate housing communities" and
  related third-party management/development-consulting services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A]

## Bridging arrangements and process substrate at closing
- In connection with the mergers, Company OP redeemed all outstanding 4.600% Senior
  Notes due 2024, prepaid its Series A and Series B private-placement guaranteed
  senior notes, and repaid and terminated its KeyBank and PNC credit agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- On closing, the Company notified the NYSE that its common stock had converted into
  the right to receive the merger consideration and requested that the NYSE file a
  Form 25 to remove the common stock from listing and deregister it under Section
  12(b) of the Exchange Act; the Company also stated its intent to file a Form 15 to
  suspend its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction structured a whole-company acquisition of a self-managed,
  self-advised collegiate-housing REIT via merger into a newly formed, single-purpose
  acquisition vehicle, with the controlling board and all five named executive
  officers separated from the surviving organization at closing (subject to expected,
  but not contractually assured, continued informal employment) and with the
  underlying real-estate portfolio itself split at closing between the wholly
  Greystar-affiliated acquisition structure and a separate 95%/5% Blackstone/Greystar
  joint-venture purchaser for a $1.2 billion sub-portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-036197, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038813, Form 8-K]
