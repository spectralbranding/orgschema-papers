# Structural sub-dossier — Rice Energy Inc. proposed stalking-horse asset acquisition of Alpha Natural Resources Marcellus/Utica assets (closing structure)

## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K dated (earliest event) April 12, 2016 filed by Rice
  Energy Inc., a Delaware corporation ("the Company"), an independent natural gas and oil
  company operating in the Appalachian Basin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K cover / Item 8.01]
- The Form 8-K reports two distinct matters: (i) under Item 1.01, an Underwriting
  Agreement for an equity offering; and (ii) under Item 8.01, the entry into a
  stalking-horse asset purchase agreement described below (the "Alpha Acquisition").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 8.01]

## Transaction type as structured (the asset acquisition)
- On April 12, 2016 the Company entered into a stalking-horse asset purchase agreement
  ("asset purchase agreement") with a subsidiary of Alpha Natural Resources, Inc.
  ("Alpha") to acquire Marcellus and Utica assets in central Greene County, Pennsylvania
  for $200 million in cash, subject to purchase price adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The seller was in bankruptcy: on August 3, 2015 Alpha and certain wholly-owned
  subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions to reorganize under Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy
  Code, and through the Chapter 11 proceeding Alpha was conducting a sale of these assets
  pursuant to section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]
- The agreement was structured as a "stalking horse bid" in accordance with
  bankruptcy-court-approved bidding procedures; the Company's bid protections (certain
  breakup fees and expense reimbursements) were themselves subject to court approval.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]
- The transaction was expressly conditional and no ownership boundary was crossed at the
  time of the filing: if the stalking-horse bid were approved, Alpha might be required to
  hold an auction before the Company could consummate the acquisition, and consummation
  was subject to the Company's being selected as the successful bidder in any such auction
  and to bankruptcy court approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]

## What was to cross the boundary — the asset/product
- The assets to be acquired were leasehold interest in approximately 27,400 net
  undeveloped Marcellus acres, plus an additional 3,200 gross acres owned in fee that were
  leased to the Company and currently generating royalty cash flow, and included rights to
  the deep Utica on 23,500 net acres. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]
- The net undeveloped acreage was characterized as adding 182 net core Marcellus drilling
  locations and 50 net core Utica drilling locations; approximately 44% of the net
  undeveloped Marcellus acres were held by production or operations or in fee, and the
  acreage was described as directly adjacent to the Company's existing proved developed
  Marcellus position in Greene County. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]
- The subject of the acquisition was thus undeveloped mineral leasehold and fee acreage
  and drilling rights (an asset/resource position), plus producing royalty interests; the
  filings describe no operating organization, workforce, management, or producing/delivery
  processes as transferring with the acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.3]

## Financing structure of the proposed acquisition
- The acquisition was to be funded by the net proceeds of a contemporaneous equity
  offering: on April 12, 2016 the Company and NGP Rice Holdings LLC (the selling
  stockholder) entered into an Underwriting Agreement with Goldman, Sachs & Co. for the
  offer and sale of an aggregate 29,858,891 shares of common stock (20,000,000 issued and
  sold by the Company; 9,858,891 sold by the selling stockholder) at a public price of
  $16.35 per share ($15.60 net of discounts and commissions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The offering was registered under an automatic shelf Form S-3 (File No. 333-202054); the
  Company did not receive any proceeds from the shares sold by the selling stockholder.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Advisors (as structured)
- Vinson & Elkins L.L.P. served as the Company's legal advisor and Lazard served as its
  financial advisor on the proposed acquisition; Alpha's advisors were Jones Day, Jackson
  Kelly PLLC and Hunton & Williams LLP (legal) and Rothschild (financial). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K EX-99.3]

## Structural summary (as structured)
- The transaction was structured as a proposed distressed-asset acquisition: the Company
  would purchase, for $200 million cash (subject to adjustment), undeveloped Marcellus and
  Utica leasehold/fee acreage and deep Utica drilling rights (~27,400 net undeveloped
  Marcellus acres, 3,200 gross fee acres, deep Utica on 23,500 net acres) out of Alpha's
  Chapter 11 estate via a section 363 stalking-horse process, subject to court-approved
  bidding procedures, a possible auction, selection of the Company as successful bidder,
  and bankruptcy court approval; the acquisition was to be funded by the net proceeds of a
  contemporaneous common-stock offering, and no operating organization, workforce, or
  producing processes were described as transferring with the acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543288, Form 8-K Items 1.01/8.01, EX-99.3]
