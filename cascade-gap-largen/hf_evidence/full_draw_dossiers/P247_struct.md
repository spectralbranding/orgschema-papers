# Structural sub-dossier — La Quinta Holdings Inc. spin-off/merger with Wyndham Worldwide (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 17, 2018, La Quinta Holdings Inc. ("La Quinta") entered into a definitive
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with Wyndham Worldwide Corporation ("Wyndham Worldwide")
  and WHG BB Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a wholly owned Delaware subsidiary of Wyndham
  Worldwide formed solely to effect the merger; under the agreement Merger Sub would
  merge with and into La Quinta, with La Quinta surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary
  of Wyndham Worldwide. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- The stated purpose of the structure was that Wyndham Worldwide would acquire only
  La Quinta's management and franchise business; as a condition to and immediately
  prior to the merger, La Quinta separated its owned real estate business and certain
  related assets/liabilities into a newly formed subsidiary, CorePoint Lodging Inc.
  ("CorePoint"), a Maryland corporation incorporated May 8, 2017, followed by a pro
  rata spin-off distribution of 100% of CorePoint's common stock to La Quinta
  stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- At La Quinta's closing-era portfolio scale, the company described itself as owner,
  operator and franchisor of more than 890 select-service hotel properties (~87,500
  rooms) in 48 U.S. states and Canada/Mexico/Honduras/Colombia, operating under the
  La Quinta Inn & Suites, La Quinta Inn and LQ Hotel brands. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- La Quinta stockholders were entitled to receive $8.40 in cash per share (pre-reverse-
  split; $16.80 post-split) as merger consideration, in addition to the CorePoint
  shares received in the spin-off; a 1-for-2 reverse stock split/par-value amendment
  was executed immediately prior to the merger to obtain the intended tax treatment.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- The two steps closed on May 30, 2018: La Quinta first completed the Distribution
  (conveying its owned real estate assets to CorePoint and distributing CorePoint
  shares to La Quinta stockholders) and then, immediately after, Merger Sub merged
  with and into La Quinta, making La Quinta a wholly owned subsidiary of Wyndham
  Worldwide. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]

## What crossed to Wyndham Worldwide vs. what was retained/separated
- The customer-facing product/asset — the owned hotel real estate (the physical
  properties) — did NOT cross to Wyndham Worldwide; it was conveyed instead to the
  newly spun-off CorePoint, which became a separate publicly held company owning and
  operating La Quinta's real estate assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- The business/value-capture model that crossed to Wyndham Worldwide was the
  management-and-franchise business: the brand, franchise agreements, and hotel
  management operations, rather than the underlying real estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- Wyndham Worldwide itself was, at the time of the merger agreement, expected to
  spin off its own hotel business (Wyndham Hotel Group) into an independent publicly
  traded company, and it was anticipated that La Quinta's acquired management and
  franchise business would form part of that independent company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- Concurrently with execution of the merger agreement, La Quinta and CorePoint entered
  into a Separation and Distribution Agreement and an Employee Matters Agreement, and
  agreed to execute a Tax Matters Agreement in connection with the spin-off; the
  Employee Matters Agreement allocates liabilities and responsibilities for employee
  compensation and benefit plans between La Quinta (post-merger, owned by Wyndham
  Worldwide) and CorePoint. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]

## Bridging the process/product interface: management and franchise agreements
- As a condition of closing, La Quinta agreed to cause its applicable subsidiaries to
  enter into (a) management agreements and (b) franchise agreements, in each case
  between CorePoint (or its subsidiaries) on one side and La Quinta/its retained
  subsidiaries (i.e., the Wyndham-owned entity) on the other, together with a Tax
  Matters Agreement and a Transition Services Agreement bridging the two sides.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- On May 30, 2018, in connection with the Distribution, La Quinta and CorePoint (and
  certain subsidiaries) entered into agreements including a Transition Services
  Agreement and a Tax Matters Agreement to govern the parties' relationship following
  the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]
- The defined "Transaction Agreements" in the merger agreement comprised the
  Distribution Agreement, the Transition Services Agreement, the Employee Matters
  Agreement, the Tax Matters Agreement, and the "CPLG Management and Franchise
  Agreements" — i.e., separate contracts were required to keep the spun-off real
  estate (product) operationally connected to the management/franchise business that
  crossed to Wyndham. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]

## People/organization continuity
- Certain La Quinta executive officers (including named executive officers) who began
  employment with CorePoint prior to the effective time of the merger were carved out
  of the "termination without cause" trigger that would otherwise have entitled them
  to merger-related severance, indicating an intended, planned transfer of some
  management personnel to the CorePoint side rather than the Wyndham-retained side.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time of the merger (May 30, 2018), the pre-merger La Quinta board
  members (James Abrahamson, Glenn Alba, Scott Bergren, Alan J. Bowers, Henry G.
  Cisneros, Keith A. Cline, Giovanni Cutaia, Brian Kim, Mitesh B. Shah, and Gary M.
  Sumers) resigned, and Merger Sub's directors (Paul Cash, Robert Loewen, Nicola
  Rossi) became the initial directors of the surviving (Wyndham-owned) La Quinta
  entity; the filing states these were not the result of any disagreement over
  operations, policies, or practices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Immediately prior to the merger, La Quinta amended its certificate of incorporation
  to effect the reverse stock split, and, at the effective time of the merger, La
  Quinta's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated in their
  entirety as those of the surviving (Wyndham-owned) corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]
- In connection with completion of the merger on May 30, 2018, La Quinta notified the
  NYSE and requested that its common stock be removed from listing and registration
  (a Form 25 filing), with trading suspended prior to the opening of trading on
  May 31, 2018; La Quinta also intended to file a Form 15 to deregister the common
  stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]
- CorePoint's common stock, by contrast, was to be listed on the NYSE (ticker CPLG) as
  a condition of the spin-off, i.e., a new public listing was created for the retained
  real estate/product tier at the same time the Wyndham-acquired management/franchise
  tier's listing was removed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction paired a same-day spin-off (La Quinta's owned real estate, spun into
  the newly public CorePoint) with a whole-company cash acquisition (La Quinta's
  management-and-franchise business, into Wyndham Worldwide), so that the
  customer-facing hotel real estate crossed to a separate new public entity while the
  operating/franchise processes and the La Quinta brand crossed to the acquirer.
  The two sides were bridged, not merged, through a package of Transaction Agreements
  (Transition Services, Tax Matters, Employee Matters, Distribution, and
  Management/Franchise Agreements) executed concurrently with closing, with some
  executive personnel specifically retained on the CorePoint side. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-089194, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-179456, 8-K filed 2018-05-31]
