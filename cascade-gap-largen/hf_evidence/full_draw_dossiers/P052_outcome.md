## Reporting status and how the venture's results are realized
- In the outcome window the registrant reports its interest in MillerCoors LLC "under the
  equity method of accounting," describing it as one of its operating businesses
  "operating in the United States." [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The registrant discloses that it does NOT run the venture day to day: "We do not fully
  control the operations and administration of MillerCoors, which represents our interests
  in the U.S. beer business. We jointly control MillerCoors with SABMiller ... MillerCoors
  management is responsible for the day to day operations of the business and therefore, we
  do not have full control over the activities of MillerCoors. Our results of operations
  are dependent upon the efforts of MillerCoors management." [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), Risk Factors]
- Governance in the window: "MillerCoors has a Board of Directors consisting of five
  MCBC-appointed and five SABMiller-appointed directors," and each party's agreement not to
  transfer its economic or voting interests ran "for a period of five years from July 1,
  2008" (i.e., expiring within the outcome window on July 1, 2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), Investment in MillerCoors]

## Realized financial trajectory (equity-method scalars)
- Net income attributable to MillerCoors (the venture's realized result): $1,190.9 million
  in FY2012 (+18.6%), $1,003.8 million in FY2011 (-5.0%), and $1,057.0 million in FY2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), summarized MillerCoors financial information]
- The registrant's proportionate share of MillerCoors net income: $500.2 million (FY2012),
  $421.6 million (FY2011), and $443.9 million (FY2010). [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- "Equity income in MillerCoors" recognized in the registrant's income statement:
  $510.9 million (FY2012), $457.9 million (FY2011), $456.1 million (FY2010); cash
  "Distributions from MillerCoors" matched those amounts each year ($510.9 / $457.9 /
  $456.1 million), i.e., the venture distributed substantially all recognized income to
  the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), Consolidated Statements of Cash Flows]

## Realized operating trajectory of the U.S. product portfolio
- Domestic sales-to-retail (STRs) "declined 1.3% in 2012 versus 2011, driven by declines
  in Miller Lite and the below premium portfolio, partially offset by growth in Coors Light
  and double-digit growth in Tenth and Blake led by the Blue Moon and Leinenkugel's
  brands." [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), U.S. segment results]
- "Total sales volume declined 0.5% in 2012 compared to 2011. Domestic sales-to-wholesalers
  decreased 1.1% versus 2011, driven by the decline in STRs, partially offset by increased
  contract brewing volume. Domestic net sales per hectoliter increased 3.5% in 2012" —
  i.e., realized results reflect volume erosion offset by price/mix and contract brewing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), U.S. segment results]
- The venture remained an integrated producer over the window: MillerCoors continued to
  brew, package and ship products under contract for Pabst Brewing Company (through June
  2020) and to produce beer under contract for the registrant's MCI segment and SABMiller,
  and continued to source aluminum cans via the Rocky Mountain Metal Container (RMMC) joint
  venture near the Golden, Colorado brewery plus a supplemental supply agreement with Ball
  Corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012), U.S. segment]

## Items noted but not primary-verifiable
- No divestiture, dissolution, impairment, or write-down of the registrant's MillerCoors
  investment is disclosed in the FY2012 10-K within the outcome window; the venture is
  reported as an ongoing equity-method investment. No further outcome scalar beyond those

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed outcome window the venture operated as a going concern jointly controlled
  by two parents (5+5 board, equity-method reporting), producing rising net income
  ($1,057.0M -> $1,003.8M -> $1,190.9M FY2010-FY2012) that was distributed substantially
  in full to the registrant, while its U.S. product portfolio showed modest realized volume
  erosion (total sales volume -0.5% in 2012; STRs -1.3%) offset by price/mix and contract
  brewing; the registrant expressly disclosed it did not control day-to-day operations and
  depended on MillerCoors management for results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024545-13-000005, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
