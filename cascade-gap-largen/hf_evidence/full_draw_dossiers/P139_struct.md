## The entity as previously structured — the 2011 leveraged acquisition (background)
- Samson Resources Corporation, a Delaware corporation, was formed in November 2011 in connection with the acquisition of Samson Investment Company from its selling stockholders (the "Acquisition") by affiliates of Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. ("KKR"), ITOCHU Corporation ("ITOCHU") and certain other co-investors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1 Business]
- In December 2011 the Acquisition closed and Samson Investment Company became a direct, wholly-owned subsidiary of Samson Resources Corporation, with KKR and co-investors (including funds affiliated with Crestview Partners II GP, L.P. and Natural Gas Partners IX, L.P.) holding common stock through Samson Aggregator L.P., and ITOCHU holding common stock through JD Rockies Resources Limited. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1]
- The Acquisition was financed with approximately $1.345 billion of borrowings under a reserves-based revolving credit facility ("RBL Revolver"); $2.250 billion under a syndicated senior unsecured bridge facility; $4.145 billion of equity capital from the Principal Stockholders; and $180.0 million aggregate liquidation preference of cumulative redeemable preferred stock issued to the selling stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1]
- In February 2012 the Company issued $2,250,000,000 aggregate principal amount of 9.750% Senior Notes due 2020 and used the proceeds to repay the Bridge Facility in full; a Second Lien Term Loan Credit Agreement dated September 25, 2012 (borrower Samson Investment Company; Bank of America, N.A. as administrative agent) provided an aggregate principal amount of $1.0 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1 and Exhibit index]
- Affiliates of KKR, ITOCHU and the other investors owned substantially all of the Company's capital stock through the Principal Stockholders, whose designees held all of the seats on the board of directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1A Risk Factors]

## The transaction being structured — the August 2015 Restructuring Support Agreement
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K, Date of Report August 14, 2015, reporting under Item 1.01 that the Company reached agreement with certain Lenders and other parties on the terms of a financial restructuring plan expected to reduce long-term debt and annual interest, to be implemented by voluntarily commencing a reorganization under chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code on or before September 16, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company entered into a Restructuring Support Agreement ("RSA"), incorporating a term sheet dated August 14, 2015, with: certain holders of loans under the Second Lien Term Loan (the "Lenders"), who agreed to backstop a rights offering (the "Backstop Parties"); and the Sponsors — Samson Aggregator GP LLC, Samson Aggregator L.P., KKR 2006 Fund, L.P., KKR Fund Holdings L.P., Crestview Partners II, L.P., and certain affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The RSA contemplated restructuring the Company through a debt-for-equity conversion and rights offering effectuated through a chapter 11 plan of reorganization; the RSA is attached as Exhibit 10.1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01 and Item 9.01]

## What was to cross the boundary — the contemplated equity conversion (as structured)
- New Money Investment: a minimum of $325 million to purchase new common equity of the reorganized Company plus a maximum of $125 million of new second lien debt; if projected pro forma liquidity at the effective date fell below $350 million, the New Money Investment would increase by $35 million to a total of $485 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Backstop Terms: the Backstop Parties agreed to backstop the Rights Offering, including $413.25 million of new common equity ("Equity Backstop") and $36.75 million of new second lien debt ("Debt Backstop"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Distributions: the Lenders would receive all new common equity of the reorganized Company, less equity issued to Rights Offering participants, to holders of the Senior Notes due 2020 (Noteholders), and to a board/management incentive plan; the RBL Revolver would be amended and restated with a borrowing base/maximum availability of at least $750 million on the effective date. Noteholders would receive 1.0% of reorganized common equity if they voted for the Plan and 0.5% if they voted against. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Management Incentive Plan: 10.0% of new common equity reserved for a board and management incentive program (5.0% granted on/near the effective date). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Continuity of the operating substrate and governance (as structured)
- The Company stated it expected its oil and gas operations to continue in the ordinary course throughout the restructuring, and that the vast majority of trade creditors and vendors were expected to be paid in full in the ordinary course. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Governance of the reorganized Company: a seven-person Board consisting of the CEO, two directors elected by the second lien lenders, and four directors elected by the Backstop Parties (with CEO consultation and a nationally-recognized executive search firm). All indemnification provisions for current directors, officers and employees were to remain in place after the restructuring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Non-Core Asset Sale: as determined by the reorganized Company's Board, the Company was to sell certain non-core assets and use proceeds to pay down the RBL Revolver, partially prepay new debt, and fund general corporate purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Alternative structure and deal-protection provisions (as structured)
- Under the RSA, if holders of at least 66 2/3% of the outstanding Second Lien Term Loan were not parties to the RSA by October 14, 2015, the Company could pursue a sale of substantially all of its assets under section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code (the "363 Sale"), with the Backstop Parties acting as the stalking-horse bidder; the parties were to negotiate an asset purchase agreement and bid procedures on or before September 16, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Break-up Fee: if the Company exercised its fiduciary out and did not accept the contemplated transaction, it would pay the Backstop Parties a $10 million break-up fee; upon closing, the Backstop Parties would receive a $10 million work fee. The RSA provided upfront mutual releases between each Backstop Party and each Sponsor (subject to a fraud/gross-negligence/willful-misconduct exception). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company intended to forgo the August 17, 2015 interest payment under the Senior Notes Indenture and use the 30-day grace period to build consensus for the restructuring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- An over-leveraged oil-and-gas holding company — itself formed for a December 2011 sponsor-led acquisition financed largely with debt ($2.25 billion 9.750% Senior Notes, a $1.0 billion Second Lien Term Loan, and an RBL Revolver) — agreed in August 2015 to a Restructuring Support Agreement designed to convert second-lien debt to equity via a chapter 11 plan and a rights offering (minimum $325 million to $485 million new money; $413.25 million equity backstop), leaving the operating oil-and-gas assets, trade creditors and existing management/indemnification substantially in place while allocating post-emergence control (seven-member board, majority to second-lien Backstop Parties) and reserving a section 363 whole-asset stalking-horse sale as a fallback path. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-292359, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-113983, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 1]
