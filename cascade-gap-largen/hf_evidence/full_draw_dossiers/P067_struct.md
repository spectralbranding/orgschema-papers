## Transaction and parties as structured
- On February 15, 2011, MEMC Singapore Pte. Ltd., a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of
  MEMC Electronic Materials, Inc., entered into a Joint Venture Agreement with Samsung
  Fine Chemicals Co. Ltd. ("SFC"), a company organized under the laws of Korea, to form
  a new joint venture company ("JVC") in Korea. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction was structured as the formation of a new, jointly owned operating
  company; it was reported under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement)
  and signed for the registrant by Bradley D. Kohn, Senior Vice President, General Counsel
  and Corporate Secretary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K signatures]
- The JVC's stated purpose is to produce high-purity polysilicon at a facility in Ulsan,
  South Korea. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed / did not cross the ownership boundary at closing (by tier)
- Legal vehicle: a newly formed joint venture company is the legal subject; at the Closing
  (subject to customary conditions including governmental approvals) each of MEMC and SFC
  was to hold a 50% ownership interest in the JVC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Customer-facing product / producing processes: the JVC is a greenfield manufacturing
  entity — the polysilicon product and the plant that produces it were to be established
  by the JVC (the JVC bears responsibility for securing debt financing for construction of
  the initial Plant), not transferred as an existing product line or operating plant from
  either parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Capital committed at/after closing: the agreement provides for both equity and debt
  financing; the parties contribute some funds at Closing and additional amounts over
  time so that each party's total cash contributions are approximately $175 million in the
  aggregate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Controlling intent, governance, and people (as structured)
- Board control is shared: the JVC has a six-person board, with each of MEMC and SFC
  entitled to nominate three director representatives, each for a three-year term.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Executive nomination rights are split by function: SFC has the right to nominate the
  Chief Executive Officer and Vice President of Human Resources; MEMC has the right to
  nominate the Chief Financial Officer and Chief Technology Officer of the JVC.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Certain actions require simple-majority approval; certain other fundamental actions
  require consent of at least two-thirds of the JVC shares outstanding; the agreement
  provides a mechanism for resolving any "deadlock" and specified remedies upon events of
  default (including material breaches). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Bridging / restriction terms allocating the shared substrate
- Share-transfer lockup: each of MEMC and SFC is prohibited from transferring any JVC
  shares it holds, except to its respective affiliates, for a period of five years from
  the closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Right of first refusal: during the term of the agreement, MEMC and SFC each grant the
  other a right of first refusal to participate in the establishment of any additional
  polysilicon manufacturing plant planned by either party in Korea. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Term / walk-away: the agreement continues until the JVC is terminated; if the Closing
  does not occur by April 30, 2011, either party may terminate the agreement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction created a new 50/50 jointly owned Korean company to build and operate a
  new high-purity polysilicon plant in Ulsan; rather than transferring an existing product
  or operating base across an ownership boundary, both parties committed roughly $175
  million each of staged equity/debt capital into a greenfield vehicle, split executive
  leadership by function (SFC: CEO/VP-HR; MEMC: CFO/CTO), and bridged shared interests
  through a six-person balanced board, supermajority protections, a deadlock mechanism, a
  five-year share-transfer lockup, and reciprocal rights of first refusal on further Korean
  polysilicon plants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-11-009955, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
