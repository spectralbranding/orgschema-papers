## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On September 13, 2017, Patheon N.V. filed a Form 15-12B certifying termination of
  registration of its ordinary shares under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act and
  suspension of its duty to file reports under Sections 13(a)/15(d); the filing recorded
  approximately 71 holders of record as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056944, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K, 20-F, or other periodic report has been filed under Patheon N.V.'s own
  CIK (1643848) since that deregistration; consequently no audited financial statements
  isolating the acquired business exist for the ~2020-2022 window, and the realized
  record below is limited to what the acquirer, Thermo Fisher Scientific Inc., discloses
  about Patheon in its own Annual Reports on Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056944, Form 15-12B]

## Realized record as disclosed by the acquirer (within the ~2020-2022 window)
- Thermo Fisher Scientific's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31,
  2020 (filed February 25, 2021) lists Patheon among its "industry-leading brands"
  (alongside Thermo Scientific, Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Fisher Scientific, and
  Unity Lab Services) and states: "Patheon is our contract development and manufacturing
  brand, representing the comprehensive offering of services that we provide to
  customers ranging from small biotech to large pharmaceutical companies," including
  support for "development of innovative medicines, including biologics, gene
  therapies." [SRC: SEC accession 0000097745-21-000011, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The same Annual Report reports Thermo Fisher's results in four reportable segments —
  Life Sciences Solutions, Analytical Instruments, Specialty Diagnostics, and Laboratory
  Products and Services — with no segment or note isolating Patheon's own revenue,
  operating income, headcount, or other financial scalar; a 2018 segment-level
  restructuring note (within the Laboratory Products and Services segment) references
  "$16 million of charges to selling, general, and administrative expenses for
  third-party transaction/integration costs related to the acquisition of Patheon," the
  only Patheon-specific dollar figure found, and it predates the ~2020-2022 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000097745-21-000011, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Thermo Fisher's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2021 (filed
  February 24, 2022) again lists Patheon among its named brands (now alongside "PPD"),
  using materially the same description of Patheon as its contract development and
  manufacturing brand. [SRC: SEC accession 0000097745-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Management-continuity fact (acquirer's own disclosure)
- The executive-officer tables in both the FY2020 and FY2021 Annual Reports show that
  Michel Lagarde — Patheon's President and Chief Operating Officer from 2016 to 2017 —
  held the role of Thermo Fisher's Senior Vice President and President, Pharma Services
  from 2017 to 2019, then Executive Vice President from 2019 to 2021, and is listed as
  Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer as of the FY2021 filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000097745-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Patheon N.V. deregistered from SEC reporting on September 13, 2017, there is no
  public audited outcome record (10-K/20-F) under its own CIK for the ~2020-2022 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056944, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer's own Annual Reports for FY2020 and FY2021 confirm the Patheon brand and
  its contract-development-and-manufacturing business continued to operate and be
  marketed under that name through the window, and one former Patheon executive rose to
  an Executive Vice President/Chief Operating Officer role at the acquirer by FY2021, but
  neither filing discloses a segment-isolated financial scalar (revenue, income,
  headcount, impairment, or divestiture) specific to the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000097745-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021]
