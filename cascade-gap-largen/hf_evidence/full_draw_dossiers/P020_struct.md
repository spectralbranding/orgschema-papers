## Transaction and parties at closing
- Air Products and Chemicals, Inc. ("Air Products") furnished this information statement to its stockholders in connection with the distribution to those stockholders of all of the outstanding shares of common stock of Versum Materials, Inc. ("Versum"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Air Products that was formed to hold and operate Air Products' Electronic Materials business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement (dated August 26, 2016)]
- The Electronic Materials business is described as a global business that provides solutions to the semiconductor industry through chemical synthesis, analytical technology, process engineering, and surface science; the distribution is intended to be tax-free for U.S. federal income tax purposes except for cash received in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Distribution mechanics: Air Products would distribute to its stockholders, on a pro rata basis, 100% of the outstanding shares of Versum on the basis of one-half share of Versum common stock for each share of Air Products common stock held on the record date; stockholders were not required to make any payment, or to surrender or exchange their Air Products shares, to receive their Versum shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: Versum described its business as serving the semiconductor industry (over 80% of sales in recent years), in an addressable market it estimated at approximately $5.5 billion, targeting six of the seven critical process steps in semiconductor manufacturing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operating structure: Versum organized itself into two primary operating segments plus a Corporate segment — the Materials segment (leading-edge specialty chemicals and materials, and high-purity specialty gases used in semiconductor manufacturing, for the semiconductor and flat-panel display markets) and the Delivery Systems and Services ("DS&S") segment (design, manufacture, installation, operation, and service of gas and chemical delivery equipment at customers' facilities). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operational processes/facilities crossing with the business: the Materials segment had approximately 980 employees and operated 11 production and 6 research and development sites; the DS&S segment had approximately 650 employees and operated three production facilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / organization: as of September 30, 2015, Versum and its subsidiaries had approximately 1,900 employees, of whom approximately 175 were subject to collective bargaining agreements or similar arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Management continuity/imprint: Guillermo Novo was named to be President and Chief Executive Officer of Versum, described as having 30 years of materials-industry operating and executive experience at Air Products, Rohm and Haas Company, and Dow Chemical Company; of Versum's top 8 most senior leaders, 5 had spent the majority of their careers with these businesses within Air Products while 3 had joined from outside. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Capital structure allocated at separation: Versum would make a cash distribution of approximately $550 million to Air Products prior to the distribution, funded primarily by third-party indebtedness — a senior secured first-lien term loan B facility of $575 million and a senior secured first-lien revolving credit facility of $200 million — and would distribute in-kind to Air Products unsecured senior notes of up to approximately $425 million as partial consideration for the contribution of assets to Versum. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared substrate divided / retained at closing (product crossing without its full producing substrate)
- Because Versum's business had previously operated as part of the wider Air Products organization, Versum stated it would need to provide internally or obtain from third parties services it had received from Air Products — including information technology, accounting, tax, finance, legal, insurance, human resources, compliance, and other administrative activities — and that duplicating Air Products' complex IT networks and systems would be challenging. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Versum disclosed it had begun executing business and organizational changes to become a standalone company, including staffing adjustments, new hires, reassignment of responsibilities, adoption of new processes, systems and controls, and transitioning services previously provided by Air Products to internally provided services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Asset re-allocation at the product/process boundary: pursuant to the Separation Agreement, certain Delivery Systems equipment that had been within the Versum entity and that formed part of Air Products' supply agreements with certain customers was to be transferred to Air Products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- Separation Agreement: to be entered into with Air Products to effect the separation and provide a framework for the post-separation relationship; governs allocation of assets, employees, liabilities, and obligations, along with related matters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Transition Services Agreements (both directions): Air Products would provide certain transition services to Versum and Versum would provide certain transition services to Air Products, generally for no longer than 12 to 24 months following the distribution, for fees at cost for services provided by third parties and at cost plus a stated percentage for services provided by either party. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Intellectual Property Agreements: a two-way cross-license under which Versum exclusively licenses certain patents to Air Products for a defined field of use and Air Products exclusively licenses certain patents to Versum for a defined field of use; Air Products would also grant Versum a license under its Engineering Standards and its Safety, Health and Environmental Standards and Policies for a limited period to allow Versum to continue operations and develop its own standards, and Versum's continued use of the Air Products name in certain jurisdictions until regulatory permits could be changed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Commercial Agreements: leases and utility, supply, and toll-manufacturing contracts with Air Products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Additional agreements: a Tax Matters Agreement and an Employee Matters Agreement governing tax and employee-compensation/benefit allocations, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Shared director: following the distribution, Seifollah (Seifi) Ghasemi, who continued as Chairman, President and Chief Executive Officer of Air Products, would also serve as a director of Versum and as Versum's non-executive Chairman of the board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction carved the Electronic Materials business out of Air Products into a newly formed subsidiary (Versum) and distributed 100% of Versum's shares pro rata to Air Products stockholders (one-half Versum share per Air Products share), tax-free intended; Versum received the two operating segments (Materials and DS&S), approximately 1,900 employees, its production and R&D sites, and its incumbent leadership (CEO Guillermo Novo), while taking on approximately $550 million of new third-party-funded debt paid to Air Products plus up to ~$425 million of in-kind senior notes. Functions historically supplied by the integrated parent (IT, finance, HR, tax, legal, and others) were to be internalized and were bridged at closing by a Separation Agreement, bidirectional Transition Services Agreements (12–24 months; at cost / cost-plus), Tax and Employee Matters Agreements, a two-way IP cross-license plus standards licenses and name-use rights, and commercial (lease, utility, supply, toll-manufacturing) agreements; certain Delivery Systems equipment tied to Air Products' customer supply agreements was transferred back to Air Products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-692200, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
