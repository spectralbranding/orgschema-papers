## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Cannae Holdings, Inc. continued to file periodic reports on EDGAR under its own CIK
  (0001704720) after the Split-Off; the Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 was filed
  2022-02-25. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]

## Realized record of the contributed businesses (separately disclosed by Cannae)
- Ceridian Holding, LLC (Ceridian HCM Holding, Inc. after its 2018 initial public
  offering) was progressively divested by Cannae: as of March 31, 2020, Cannae's voting
  agreement with Ceridian terminated and, combined with a February 2020 share sale,
  Cannae "no longer exercise[d] significant influence over Ceridian," triggering a
  change in accounting from the equity method to fair value (revaluing the stake to
  $993.4 million and recording a $684.9 million gain, net of tax, in FY2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Cannae continued selling down its Ceridian stake through the outcome window: sales of
  2.0 million shares (May 2021, $175.0 million proceeds), 1.0 million shares (September
  2021, $100.0 million proceeds), 1.0 million shares (October 2021, $125.8 million
  proceeds), and 2.0 million shares (January 2022, $173.3 million proceeds), reducing
  Cannae's stake to approximately 5.3% of Ceridian's outstanding common stock as of the
  filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- On December 31, 2019, Cannae completed the contribution of T-System Holdings, Inc.
  to CorroHealth, reclassifying T-System's results to discontinued operations; this
  falls just before the fixed 3-5-year outcome window but documents the trajectory of a
  business contributed at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- American Blue Ribbon Holdings, LLC ("Blue Ribbon"), the restaurant-holding business
  contributed at closing, began a reorganization under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Code on January 27, 2020, and Cannae deconsolidated it; the Chapter 11 plan became
  effective October 2, 2020, and Blue Ribbon emerged from bankruptcy as a set of
  reorganized companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Upon Blue Ribbon's emergence, Cannae acquired the assets and uncompromised
  liabilities of two of its brands, Legendary Baking and VIBSQ, in exchange for $15.5
  million of an outstanding debtor-in-possession loan balance, taking 100% ownership of
  those two entities; Cannae then sold VIBSQ's net assets (closed July 30, 2021),
  certain net assets of "RC" and its subsidiaries (closed September 1, 2021), and
  Legendary Baking (closed September 3, 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- The remaining consolidated Restaurant Group segment as of December 31, 2021 comprised
  O'Charley's (65.4% equity ownership) and 99 Restaurants (88.5% equity ownership). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Beginning in the last two weeks of March 2020, the Restaurant Group brands
  experienced a significant reduction in guest counts due to COVID-19 social
  restrictions; the brands closed dining rooms in substantially all restaurants from
  late March 2020 through early May 2020, operating on a to-go/delivery basis only
  where permitted, and implemented cost-savings measures including temporary restaurant
  closures and modified employee work hours. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]

## Realized financial scalars (Cannae Holdings, Inc., consolidated)
- Net (loss) earnings attributable to Cannae Holdings, Inc. common shareholders was
  $(287.0) million for fiscal year 2021, $1,786.2 million for fiscal year 2020, and
  $77.3 million for fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- The FY2020 net-earnings figure was driven substantially by the $684.9 million
  non-operating gain from the Ceridian fair-value revaluation described above, not by
  Restaurant Group or other operating performance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Total revenue increased $156.5 million in 2021 compared to 2020, "primarily driven by
  an increase in revenue in the Restaurant Group segment," while total revenue had
  decreased $484.3 million in 2020 compared to 2019, "primarily driven by a decline in
  revenue in our Restaurant Group segment" (the COVID-19 disruption described above). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Cannae Holdings, Inc. remained a separate SEC-reporting company through the ~2019-2022
  outcome window and separately discloses the post-close trajectory of the businesses
  contributed to it at the 2017 Split-Off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Within the window, two of the contributed businesses show documented discontinuity
  from their closing-time form: Ceridian was progressively sold down from a
  significant-influence stake to a minority position of roughly 5%, and American Blue
  Ribbon Holdings entered Chapter 11 bankruptcy reorganization (January 2020), emerged
  in reorganized form (October 2020), and had its remaining constituent brands
  (VIBSQ, "RC," Legendary Baking) fully divested by September 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
- Cannae's consolidated results swung from a $1,786.2 million net-earnings year in FY2020
  (substantially non-operating, driven by the Ceridian revaluation gain) to a $287.0
  million net loss in FY2021, against a backdrop of a COVID-19-driven revenue decline
  and partial recovery in the retained Restaurant Group segment (O'Charley's, 99
  Restaurants). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021)]
