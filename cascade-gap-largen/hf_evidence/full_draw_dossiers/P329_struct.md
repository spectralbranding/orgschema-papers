# Structural sub-dossier — The Advisory Board Company: paired sale of its health care business to Optum and its education business to Vista Equity affiliates (closing structure)

## Pre-transaction company and strategic-alternatives process

- Prior to the transactions, The Advisory Board Company ("ABCO" or the "Company")
  operated two businesses referred to throughout its proxy materials as the "health
  care business" and the "education business"; the Company's Board considered multiple
  strategic alternatives for the two businesses together, including a sale of ABCO as a
  whole, a tax-free spin-off of the health care business or the education business into
  separately traded companies, and continuing as a stand-alone public company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger and the Education Transaction"]
- The Board ultimately did not pursue separating the two businesses into two publicly
  traded companies; it instead approved two coordinated, contractually linked sale
  transactions to two different buyers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger and the Education Transaction"]

## Transaction structure — two linked agreements, two different buyers

- On August 28, 2017, ABCO entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with
  OptumInsight, Inc. ("Optum"), an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of UnitedHealth
  Group Incorporated (NYSE: UNH), and Apollo Merger Sub, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary
  of Optum formed solely to effect the merger; under the merger agreement Merger Sub
  would merge with and into ABCO, with ABCO surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Optum. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet"]
- On the same date, ABCO also entered into a separate Stock and Asset Purchase
  Agreement (the "education purchase agreement") with Avatar Holdco, LLC and Avatar
  Purchaser, Inc. (together the "Education Buyer"), entities formed by Vista Equity
  Partners Fund VI, L.P., to sell ABCO's education business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet"]
- The closing of the Optum merger was expressly conditioned on the prior consummation
  of the education transaction; ABCO stockholders were not asked to vote on or approve
  the education purchase agreement or education transaction itself, only on the merger
  agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet"]
- Merger consideration for ABCO common stock was $52.65 in cash plus a variable
  additional cash amount tied to the after-tax per-share value of ABCO's equity
  interests in Evolent Health, Inc.; illustratively $54.35/share as of early October
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet — Merger Consideration"]
- Vista Fund VI's bid for the education business was increased to an enterprise value
  of $1.55 billion and Optum's bid for the health care business was increased to an
  enterprise value of $1.4 billion (inclusive of the value of ABCO's Evolent Health
  interests) during negotiations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger and the Education Transaction"]

## What crossed at closing, by business

- Health care business / people: ABCO employees other than "education employees"
  ("health care employees") had their outstanding equity awards assumed and converted
  into UnitedHealth Group Incorporated equity awards at the merger's effective time.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Treatment of ABCO Equity Awards"]
- Education business / people: employees and independent contractors who transferred to
  Education Buyer ("education employees") had their unvested ABCO equity awards
  addressed separately under the education purchase agreement and related ancillary
  agreements (conversion into cash or restricted cash awards continuing to vest with
  Education Buyer, rather than into acquirer equity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet — Treatment of ABCO Equity Awards"]
- Legal vehicles: the merger used a newly formed, single-purpose merger subsidiary
  (Apollo Merger Sub, Inc.) merging into ABCO (whole-entity acquisition of the surviving
  corporation); the education sale used a stock-and-asset purchase structure into
  newly formed Vista-affiliated entities (Avatar Holdco, LLC / Avatar Purchaser, Inc.).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "Summary Term Sheet"]
- Two named ABCO executive officers (the CEO of the education business and the CFO) were
  disclosed as ceasing employment with the Company as of the closing under the education
  purchase agreement, while other executive officers were disclosed as becoming health
  care employees at the merger's effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Contractual bridges for the shared substrate at closing

- In connection with the education purchase agreement, ABCO and Education Buyer entered
  into ancillary agreements including: a transition services agreement (each party to
  provide certain transition services to the other for specified periods after closing
  of the education transaction), an employee matters agreement (allocating employee
  assets, liabilities, and benefit-plan responsibilities and addressing treatment of
  ABCO equity awards held by education employees), and a cross-license agreement
  (each party granted rights to use certain of the other's intellectual property).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A "The Education Purchase Agreement — Ancillary Agreements"]

## Closing events and regulatory/vote process

- At a Special Meeting held November 15, 2017, ABCO stockholders approved the Merger
  Proposal (33,392,830 votes for versus 7,039 against, 128,706 abstentions) and the
  advisory Compensation Proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- On November 17, 2017 (the "Closing Date"), immediately prior to the merger, ABCO
  completed the previously announced sale of its education business to Avatar Holdco,
  LLC and EAB Global, Inc. (formerly known as Avatar Purchaser, Inc.) pursuant to the
  education purchase agreement; Optum then completed its acquisition of ABCO through the
  merger of Merger Sub with and into ABCO at approximately 12:23 p.m. EST the same day,
  and ABCO became a wholly owned subsidiary of Optum. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K Items 2.01]
- As a result of the merger, ABCO's common stock (NASDAQ: ABCO) was suspended from
  trading effective as of the close of trading on the Closing Date; ABCO requested that
  NASDAQ file a Form 25 to remove the stock from listing and deregister it under
  Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act, and stated its intent to file a Form 15 to
  deregister and terminate its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- ABCO in fact filed Form 25-NSE on November 17, 2017 and Form 15-12G on November 27,
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-17-000234, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-352504, Form 15-12G]
- Effective at the merger, a change of control of ABCO occurred and all of ABCO's
  directors resigned from its Board; ABCO's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were
  amended and restated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K Items 5.01, 5.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- ABCO's two operating businesses were separated from one another and from ABCO's
  public-company legal shell through two contractually linked, simultaneously closing
  transactions on November 17, 2017: the education business was sold via a stock-and-
  asset purchase to Vista Equity Partners Fund VI-affiliated entities (Avatar Holdco,
  LLC and EAB Global, Inc.), and — conditioned on that sale closing first — the
  remaining ABCO entity (retaining the health care business) merged into and became a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Optum, a UnitedHealth Group subsidiary. Employees,
  outstanding equity awards, and certain executive officers were allocated between the
  two buyers along the health-care/education line, with a transition services agreement,
  employee matters agreement, and cross-license agreement bridging shared functions and
  intellectual property between the two resulting ownership structures at closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-307521, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-346675, Form 8-K]
