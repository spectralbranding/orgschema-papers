# Structural sub-dossier — Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG acquisition by Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn document
- The drawn accession (0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A, filed 2017-08-03) is not a
  pre-closing merger-vote proxy; it is a proxy for a follow-on Swiss "squeeze-out"
  merger, mailed to Allied World shareholders who had not tendered into an earlier
  exchange offer that had already closed on July 6, 2017. It recites and incorporates
  the terms of the original Agreement and Plan of Merger dated December 18, 2016 and the
  completed exchange offer, so it is used here as the source for the closing-era deal
  structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]

## Transaction and parties
- On December 18, 2016, Allied World Assurance Company Holdings, AG entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with Fairfax Financial Holdings Limited, pursuant to
  which Allied World would be acquired by Fairfax. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- Fairfax, through its indirect wholly owned subsidiary FFH Switzerland and its direct
  wholly owned subsidiary 1102952 B.C. Unlimited Liability Company ("Canada Sub"), made
  an exchange offer for all outstanding Allied World common shares under a prospectus
  dated May 9, 2017, part of a Form F-4 registration statement Fairfax filed with the
  SEC on February 15, 2017 (as amended). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- The exchange offer expired July 5, 2017; 84,184,397 Allied World common shares (~96.1%
  of shares outstanding) were validly tendered and not withdrawn, all offer conditions
  were satisfied, and Fairfax accepted and paid for the tendered shares. As of the
  DEFM14A's record date, Fairfax indirectly owned or controlled 94.6% of outstanding
  Allied World common shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- Allied World is a Swiss-based holding company whose subsidiaries provide property,
  casualty and specialty insurance and reinsurance solutions worldwide; formed in
  Bermuda in 2001, it redomesticated to Switzerland in 2010. Fairfax is a holding
  company engaged, through its subsidiaries, in property and casualty insurance and
  reinsurance and investment management, with shares traded on the TSX under "FFH."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]

## Consideration
- For each Allied World common share validly tendered, shareholders received $23.00 in
  cash (the "Cash Consideration") plus 0.057937 of a Fairfax subordinate voting share.
  Allied World also paid a $5.00-per-share special cash dividend conditioned on
  completion of the offer, for a combined $28.00 in cash plus 0.057937 of a Fairfax
  share per Allied World common share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- A portion of the cash consideration was funded indirectly through the sale of
  approximately 33% of the Allied World shares to co-investors (OMERS, Alberta
  Investment Management Corporation, CN Canadian Master Trust Fund, and Lake Merritt
  LLC — none affiliated with Fairfax), who received equity interests in FFH Switzerland
  (the entity continuing as Allied World's direct parent) following closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle at closing / mechanics
- Promptly following closing of the exchange offer, Allied World, FFH Switzerland and
  Fairfax (Switzerland) GmbH entered into a Swiss merger agreement dated July 16, 2017,
  under which Allied World would merge with and into Fairfax (Switzerland), with Fairfax
  (Switzerland) as the surviving entity; all of Allied World's assets and liabilities
  transfer to the surviving entity by operation of law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- The DEFM14A's stated purpose for the Swiss squeeze-out merger is "the full legal
  integration of the Surviving Company and the Transferring Company," which it states
  "simplifies the structure and corporate governance." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a divided
  operating substrate is described in this proxy; the transaction as structured brings
  the whole Allied World legal entity (assets, liabilities, and subsidiaries) under
  Fairfax control rather than separating discrete product/process/people tiers.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]

## What the filing says about post-closing operating structure and people
- Under "Plans and Proposals for Allied World," the proxy states that "[f]ollowing the
  Transactions, Allied World will operate within the Fairfax Group on a decentralized
  basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- Among the factors the Fairfax board considered favorably: "Culture and Management.
  There is a strong cultural alignment between Fairfax and Allied World. The executive
  and senior management teams of both Fairfax and Allied World are intended to continue
  in their current roles," and "Integration/Execution Risk. It is expected that the
  continued decentralized operation of Allied World will limit execution and
  integration risk." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
- Directors and executive officers of Allied World, controlling approximately 3.0% of
  outstanding shares, entered into a voting agreement (dated December 18, 2016) to
  tender their shares; the proxy discloses these individuals' interests may differ from
  other shareholders, including "certain directors or executive officers continuing as
  managing officers of Fairfax (Switzerland) following the closing of the Transactions."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction is a whole-company acquisition effected via a
  cash-and-stock exchange offer (96.1% tendered) followed by a Swiss squeeze-out merger
  for the remainder, transferring the entire Allied World legal entity — its insurance
  and reinsurance business, subsidiaries, and management — to Fairfax control, with
  Allied World's board and Fairfax's board both stating an intent for Allied World to
  continue operating on a decentralized basis with its existing management team, rather
  than separating the product/process/people tiers via a transition-services bridge.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004976, DEFM14A]
