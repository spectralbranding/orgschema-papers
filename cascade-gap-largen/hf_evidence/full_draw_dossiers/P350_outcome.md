# Outcome sub-dossier — FB Financial Corporation realized record as a continuing public company, ~3-4-year horizon (FY2020-FY2021)

## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- FB Financial Corporation remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public
  company through fiscal years 2020 and 2021, filing a Form 10-K for the year ended
  December 31, 2020 (filed March 12, 2021) and a Form 10-K for the year ended December
  31, 2021 (filed February 25, 2022), both under the same registrant name and CIK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page]
- As of December 31, 2020 the Company (through FirstBank) had total assets of $11.21
  billion, loans held for investment of $7.08 billion, total deposits of $9.46 billion,
  and total shareholders' equity of $1.29 billion, and was described as the third
  largest Tennessee-headquartered bank by total assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1 "Business"]
- As of December 31, 2021 total assets had grown to $12.60 billion, loans held for
  investment to $7.60 billion, and total deposits to $10.84 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 "Business"]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Net income applicable to FB Financial Corporation was $63,621 thousand for the year
  ended December 31, 2020, down from $83,814 thousand in 2019 (-24.1%) and $80,236
  thousand in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 6 "Selected Financial Data" / consolidated statements of income]
- Net income applicable to FB Financial Corporation rose to $190,285 thousand for the
  year ended December 31, 2021, roughly triple the 2020 figure (+199%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, consolidated statements of income]
- Return on average shareholders' equity was 6.58% for 2020, down from 11.6% in 2019 and
  12.7% in 2018, then rose to 14.0% for 2021; return on average tangible common equity
  followed the same pattern: 15.4% (2019), 8.54% (2020), 17.3% (2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 6 "Selected Financial Data"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, "Overview of recent financial performance"]
- Provision for credit losses rose to $94,606 thousand in 2020, from $7,053 thousand in
  2019 and $5,398 thousand in 2018 (an approximately 13-fold year-over-year increase); an
  additional $13,361 thousand provision for credit losses on unfunded commitments was
  recorded in 2020 with no comparable prior-year amount. In 2021 the provision for credit
  losses reversed to a negative $38,995 thousand (a net release), which the filing
  attributes in part to improved macroeconomic forecasts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, consolidated statements of income] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, allowance for credit losses rollforward note]
- The allowance for credit losses grew from $31,139 thousand at year-end 2019 to $170,389
  thousand at year-end 2020 (reflecting both the 2020 provision and a $25,500 thousand
  initial allowance recorded on loans acquired with credit deterioration during the
  year), then declined to $125,559 thousand at year-end 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, allowance for credit losses rollforward note] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, allowance for credit losses rollforward note]

## Documented events and disclosures over the window
- The Company's annual goodwill impairment evaluation "indicated no impairment of
  goodwill for our reporting segments" as of both the FY2020 and FY2021 assessment
  dates, while noting each year it could not provide assurance against a future
  impairment charge. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 7A / critical accounting policies] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021, critical accounting policies]
- The FY2020 Form 10-K's business-history narrative attributes part of the Company's
  growth and profitability in the Chattanooga and Knoxville metropolitan markets to its
  prior acquisitions, naming "Northwest Georgia Bank, the Clayton Banks, and the branches
  from Atlantic Capital Bank," and separately discloses a much larger 2020 combination
  ("the Franklin merger") reflected in the same period's results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1 "Business" / segment-reporting note]
- The Company's quarterly results for 2020 show the credit-loss provision concentrated
  in the second and third quarters ($24,039 thousand and $45,834 thousand respectively,
  versus $27,964 thousand in Q1 and a $3,231 thousand net benefit in Q4), consistent with
  a mid-year deterioration followed by partial reversal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020, "Selected Quarterly Financial Data"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- FB Financial Corporation continued to operate and report publicly (rather than being
  acquired, delisted, or dissolved) through the FY2020-FY2021 outcome window, growing
  total assets from $11.21 billion (year-end 2020) to $12.60 billion (year-end 2021),
  with no goodwill impairment recorded against its reporting segments in either year.
  Profitability moved in a U-shape across the window: net income fell 24.1%
  year-over-year to $63,621 thousand and return on average equity fell from 11.6% (2019)
  to 6.58% (2020) — driven substantially by a roughly 13-fold increase in the provision
  for credit losses (from $7,053 thousand to $94,606 thousand) plus a new $13,361
  thousand provision on unfunded commitments, alongside an unrelated, larger 2020
  combination ("the Franklin merger") that also affects the consolidated results for that
  period — before net income roughly tripled to $190,285 thousand and return on average
  equity rose to 14.0% in 2021, as the credit-loss provision reversed to a $38,995
  thousand net release. [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001649749-22-000040, Form 10-K FY2021]
