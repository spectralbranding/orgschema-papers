# Structural sub-dossier — Tribune Media Company merger with Sinclair Broadcast Group (closing structure as drawn)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured as of the drawn proxy statement/prospectus — what the
> agreement provided would cross the ownership boundary at closing and what would not.
> It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties as structured
- On May 8, 2017, Tribune Media Company (Delaware corp., NYSE:TRCO Class A stock, Class
  B on OTC Pink), Sinclair Broadcast Group, Inc. (Maryland corp., Nasdaq:SBGI), and
  Samson Merger Sub Inc. (a wholly owned Sinclair subsidiary formed for the deal) entered
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which Merger Sub would merge with and into
  Tribune, with Sinclair acquiring Tribune. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]
- Each Tribune share was structured to convert into $35.00 cash plus 0.2300 of a Sinclair
  Class A share, an implied $43.50 per share; pre-transaction Tribune shareholders were
  structured to hold approximately 16.8% of Sinclair's outstanding common stock but only
  approximately 5.8% of its voting power, because Sinclair's ten-vote-per-share Class B
  stock (held substantially by David, Frederick, J. Duncan, and Robert Smith) was not
  issued to them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]

## What the target comprised (product/asset tier)
- Tribune was structured as the acquired business comprising 42 television stations
  (owned by Tribune or serviced by it for others), the WGN America cable network
  (~80 million households), a radio station, a production studio, real estate assets,
  and minority stakes in Television Food Network and CareerBuilder. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]
- Sinclair's own pre-existing model combined owned stations with others operated under
  local marketing agreements (LMAs) and outsourcing arrangements (joint sales agreements
  and shared services agreements) across 173 additional stations in 81 markets — a
  different value-capture structure than Tribune's own-and-service model. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]

## Divestitures required as a condition of closing (process/asset tier)
- The agreement structured Sinclair's obligation to divest one or more stations
  (Tribune's or Sinclair's own) in ten named markets (Seattle-Tacoma, St. Louis, Salt
  Lake City, Grand Rapids-Kalamazoo-Battle Creek, Oklahoma City, Wilkes Barre-Scranton,
  Richmond-Petersburg, Des Moines-Ames, Harrisburg-Lancaster-Lebanon-York, and
  Greensboro-High Point-Salem) to satisfy the FCC duopoly rule or HSR clearance, plus
  further divestitures as needed for the FCC national ownership cap — meaning a defined
  subset of Tribune's station assets was structured to be carved out to third parties
  rather than cross whole into the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]

## Governance and management at the effective time (people/organization tier)
- The merger agreement provided that Merger Sub's directors immediately prior to the
  effective time would become the surviving corporation's initial directors, replacing
  Tribune's pre-transaction board, while Tribune's own officers immediately prior to the
  effective time were structured to become the surviving corporation's initial officers
  — continuing operational management even as board-level control passed to Sinclair's
  designees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]

## Contractual terms bridging signing and closing
- Tribune was structured to owe Sinclair a termination fee of $135,500,000 plus certain
  costs if shareholders failed to approve the merger, or a fee of the same order if
  Tribune terminated to accept a superior proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]
- The agreement structured Sinclair to seek NASDAQ listing of the new shares issued and,
  after closing, to take the NYSE-rule actions needed to have Tribune's Class A stock
  removed from listing and deregistered under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]
- Completion was structured to require FCC approval and HSR antitrust clearance, with
  closing expected in the fourth quarter of 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (as drawn, closing-time only)
- As structured, the transaction was a whole-company stock-and-cash merger under which
  Tribune's board and voting control were set to pass to Sinclair's merger vehicle (with
  Sinclair's founding family retaining disproportionate voting power through its own
  dual-class stock), Tribune's existing officers were structured to continue running
  operations immediately after closing, and a defined subset of station assets was
  carved out for mandatory divestiture as a condition of regulatory clearance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005567, DEFM14A]
