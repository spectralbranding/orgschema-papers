## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K reporting the results of a special meeting
  of stockholders held March 10, 2014; it is a corporate charter/governance restructuring,
  not an asset or portfolio purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K Item 5.07]
- Under Item 5.03, on March 11, 2014 the company filed its Fifth Articles of Amendment and
  Restatement with the State Department of Assessments and Taxation of Maryland, removing
  the charter provisions that set the voting standard for director elections. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The Board also amended the bylaws to adopt a majority-of-votes-cast standard for
  uncontested director elections and a plurality standard for contested elections; the
  Fifth Articles of Amendment and Restatement (Exhibit 3.1) and Second Amended and Restated
  Bylaws (Exhibit 3.2) are filed with the 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K Item 5.03 and Item 9.01]
- Proposal 1 (modify director voting standards) was approved by stockholders
  (138,264,103 for; 2,570,716 against; 2,386,374 abstentions); Proposal 2 (eliminate
  certain other charter provisions rendered inapplicable by the NYSE listing of the Class A
  common stock) was not approved (53,544,084 for; 87,319,013 against; 2,358,096
  abstentions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K Item 5.07]
- The 8-K was signed by Scott D. Peters, Chief Executive Officer, President and Chairman.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K signature block]

## Organizational and acquisition structure at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is described as "a fully integrated, self-administered and internally
  managed REIT" that conducts substantially all of its operations through its operating
  partnership, Healthcare Trust of America Holdings, LP (HTALP), and is "primarily focused
  on acquiring, owning and operating high-quality medical office buildings." [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
- The legal vehicle is an umbrella-partnership REIT (UPREIT): as of December 31, 2013 the
  parent owned a 98.94% partnership interest in HTALP and, as sole general partner, held
  "the full, exclusive and complete responsibility for HTALP's day-to-day management and
  control." [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
- The acquisition mechanism can transfer the customer-facing asset (a building) into the
  structure without a cash sale: the company "may continue to acquire properties by issuing
  limited partnership units of our operating partnership, HTALP, in exchange for a property
  owner contributing property to us." [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
- Operating processes for the acquired assets are run on an in-house platform: the company
  maintains regional offices (Albany, Atlanta, Boston, Charleston, Dallas, Indianapolis,
  Pittsburgh and Scottsdale) that enable its "in-house property management and leasing
  platform." [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
- Not all acquired assets were yet on that in-house platform at the closing era: the
  internal asset management team "operates approximately 85% of our total portfolio, a
  significant increase from approximately 70% a year ago," implying the balance was managed
  otherwise at that time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
- Consolidation activity in the closing-era year: during the year ended December 31, 2013
  the company acquired $397.8 million of medical office buildings (approximately 1.5 million
  square feet of GLA, an expansion of almost 15% by investment), located in Dallas, Denver,
  Miami, Tampa and West Palm Beach; portfolio occupancy was approximately 91.6% as of
  December 31, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents a corporate charter/governance restructuring (director
  voting standards) following the company's NYSE listing, not a property or portfolio
  acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000020, 8-K Items 5.03/5.07]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is structured as an internally managed UPREIT that
  consolidates medical office buildings — acquiring them directly or in exchange for
  operating-partnership units and bringing the operating processes (property management,
  leasing, asset management) onto its own in-house platform, which at the closing era
  covered approximately 85% of the portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001360604-14-000013, 10-K FY2013]
