# Outcome sub-dossier — Tribune Media Company realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after the 2017 drawn agreement

## The drawn transaction did not close
- On August 9, 2018, Tribune terminated, effective immediately, the May 8, 2017
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with Sinclair Broadcast Group, on the stated basis of
  Sinclair's "willful and material breaches of its covenants and the expiration of the
  second end date" under the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-243421, Form 8-K dated 2018-08-09]
- The same day Tribune terminated a related asset purchase agreement (for the sale of
  seven network affiliates to Fox Television Stations, LLC) and filed suit against
  Sinclair in the Delaware Chancery Court alleging breach of contract, seeking damages
  for Sinclair's alleged failure to use reasonable best efforts to obtain timely
  regulatory approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-243421, Form 8-K dated 2018-08-09]

## Public-reporting status and financial record through fiscal 2018
- Tribune remained a standalone SEC-reporting company after the termination, continuing
  to trade on the NYSE under "TRCO." Net income attributable to Tribune Media Company was
  $194,119 thousand for fiscal 2017 and $412,571 thousand for fiscal 2018 (up from
  $14,246 thousand in fiscal 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0000726513-19-000006, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2018]
- On November 30, 2018, Tribune entered a new merger agreement with Nexstar Media Group,
  Inc. (via subsidiary Titan Merger Sub, Inc.) to be acquired for $46.50 cash per share
  (subject to a per-diem adjustment for closing after August 31, 2019); this agreement
  was still pending as of the fiscal-2018 Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000726513-19-000006, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2018]

## A different transaction closed, and the company deregistered
- On September 19, 2019, Tribune completed its merger with Nexstar: each outstanding
  Tribune share converted into the right to receive $46.687397 cash, and Tribune became a
  wholly owned Nexstar subsidiary. All five pre-merger Tribune directors and all
  pre-merger officers ceased to serve, replaced by Nexstar's merger-sub designees (Perry
  Sook, Thomas Carter, Elizabeth Ryder). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-250119, Form 8-K dated 2019-09-20]
- The same day Tribune requested withdrawal of its Class A stock from NYSE listing; the
  NYSE filed a Form 25 to remove the stock from listing and suspend trading, and Tribune
  stated its intent to file a Form 15 to deregister under the Exchange Act. Concurrently,
  Tribune repaid its existing credit facility and redeemed its outstanding 5.875% Senior
  Notes due 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-250119, Form 8-K dated 2019-09-20]
- On September 30, 2019, Tribune (with listed subsidiary guarantors) filed a Form 15-12B
  certifying termination of its Exchange Act reporting duty for its Class A common stock
  and its 5.875% Senior Notes, recording approximately one holder of record for the Class
  A stock as of that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-258119, Form 15-12B dated 2019-09-30]

## Public-reporting status for the fixed 3-5-year outcome window
- Tribune's last Form 10-K on file covers fiscal year 2018 (filed March 1, 2019); no
  subsequent Form 10-K appears among its EDGAR filings. [SRC: EDGAR filing index for CIK 0000726513, retrieved via `fetch_filing.py list`]
- Because Tribune's stock was removed from listing and its reporting duty terminated
  within roughly two years of the 2017 agreement, there is no Tribune-specific 10-K or
  20-F covering a 3-5-year-later window; any later results for the business would appear,
  if at all, only within Nexstar Media Group's own consolidated filings, which this
  dossier does not draw upon absent a Nexstar disclosure separately breaking out the
  acquired Tribune business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-258119, Form 15-12B dated 2019-09-30]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The transaction agreed with Sinclair in May 2017 was terminated by Tribune in August
  2018 amid litigation over Sinclair's alleged breach; Tribune continued to operate and
  report as a standalone public company through fiscal 2018 (net income of $412,571
  thousand). A separate agreement with Nexstar Media Group, signed November 30, 2018,
  closed on September 19, 2019, at which point Tribune's board and officers were replaced
  by the acquirer's designees, its stock was removed from NYSE listing, and the company
  deregistered with the SEC — leaving no further Tribune-specific periodic filing for the
  remainder of the fixed 3-5-year window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-243421, Form 8-K dated 2018-08-09] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-250119, Form 8-K dated 2019-09-20] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-258119, Form 15-12B dated 2019-09-30]
