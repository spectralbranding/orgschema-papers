## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)

- On March 29, 2019, the same day the merger closed, IDT requested that Nasdaq suspend
  trading in its common stock, and The Nasdaq Stock Market LLC filed a Form 25-NSE
  removing IDT's common stock from listing and registration.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-19-000166, Form 25-NSE]
- On April 8, 2019, IDT filed a Form 15-12G certifying termination of registration of
  its common stock under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act and/or suspension of its
  duty to file reports under Sections 13(a)/15(d); the filing recorded approximately 1
  holder of record as of the certification date and was signed by Sailesh Chittipeddi as
  President and CEO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-100681, Form 15-12G]
- A review of IDT's EDGAR filing history (CIK 0000703361) finds no Form 10-K, Form 10-Q,
  or Form 20-F filed after the April 2019 deregistration; no periodic report covering
  any fiscal year within the ~2022-2024 outcome window was located.
  [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000703361&type=10-K]
- Renesas Electronics Corporation is a Japanese corporation listed on the Tokyo Stock
  Exchange; a search of the SEC EDGAR company database for "Renesas" returns no
  registrant, indicating Renesas does not itself file periodic reports with the SEC and
  has no U.S. filing in which it could separately disclose the acquired IDT business's
  post-close trajectory. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=renesas&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=40]
