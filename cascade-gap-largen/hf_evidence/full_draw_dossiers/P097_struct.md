## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K dated July 29, 2016 (earliest event July 28, 2016) that, under Item 2.02, furnishes a press release announcing the Company's second-quarter 2016 leasing results; it is an earnings/operations furnishing, not a property-acquisition or deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K Item 2.02]
- The furnished press release (Exhibit 99.1) states the Company executed 74 leases totaling 660,373 square feet during the quarter ended June 30, 2016, of which 154,069 square feet were new leases and 506,304 square feet were renewals and other transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- In the press release the term "roll up" / "rollup" is used as a leasing rent metric — the CEO is quoted that "This quarter we produced GAAP roll up in excess of 27 percent and cash roll up of more than 17 percent," and that first-half leasing produced "a total GAAP rollup of 22.2 percent." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The press release notes a closing-era asset purchase referenced in passing: "the July 1 acquisition of 111 River Street in Hoboken, N.J." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The 8-K was signed by Gary T. Wagner, General Counsel and Secretary, for both Mack-Cali Realty Corporation and Mack-Cali Realty, L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K signature block]

## Parties and legal vehicle at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant is "a fully-integrated, self-administered and self-managed real estate investment trust (REIT)" (the General Partner) that controls Mack-Cali Realty, L.P. (the Operating Partnership) as its sole general partner, owning an 89.5 percent common unit interest in the Operating Partnership as of December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- The Operating Partnership "conducts the business of providing leasing, management, acquisition, development, construction and tenant-related services" and is "the entity through which all of the General Partner's operations are conducted"; the Company "performs substantially all real estate leasing, management, acquisition and development on an in-house basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- The Company operates in three reported industry segments: (i) commercial and other real estate, (ii) multi-family real estate, and (iii) multi-family services; all goodwill is attributable to the Multi-family Services segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]

## Portfolio assembled at the ownership boundary (the customer-facing assets)
- As of December 31, 2016 the Company owned or had interests in 248 properties — 119 office and 110 flex properties totaling ~26.6 million square feet leased to ~1,600 commercial tenants, plus 19 multi-family rental properties containing 5,614 residential units — held partly as wholly owned/Company-controlled Consolidated Properties and partly through unconsolidated joint ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- In September 2015 the Company announced a three-year strategic initiative to reposition its portfolio "from office and office/flex to a mix of office, office/flex and multi-family rental properties," redeploying office-sale proceeds into multi-family development and acquisition in core Northeast sub-markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- Closing-era acquisitions: during 2016 the Company acquired five office properties totaling 1,058,462 square feet for ~$326.8 million (funded with available cash and revolving-credit borrowings). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]

## How the multi-family assets were brought across (buy-in-and-consolidate mechanics)
- Multi-family assets entered the consolidated portfolio primarily by the Company buying out its joint-venture partners and then consolidating the property: on January 5, 2016 it acquired the remaining interest in Overlook Ridge Apartment Investors LLC (a 371-unit property in Malden, MA) for $39.8 million cash plus assumption of a $52.7 million mortgage, recording a $10.2 million gain on change of control of interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- In the second quarter 2016 it acquired the remaining interests in Portside Apartment Developers, L.L.C. (a 175-unit property in East Boston, MA) for $39.6 million cash plus assumption of a $42.5 million mortgage, recording a $5.2 million gain on change of control of interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- On April 26, 2016 it acquired the remaining non-controlling interest in a Weehawken, NJ development project (developable land for ~1,100 multi-family units plus office and parking/retail interests) for $36.4 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]

## Where the operating processes / people / principals for the multi-family program sat
- The multi-family operations are conducted through the Company's Roseland subsidiary; that operating platform was itself acquired earlier, via a Membership Interest and Asset Purchase Agreement dated October 8, 2012 among Mack-Cali Realty, L.P., Mack-Cali Realty Corporation, Mack-Cali Realty Acquisition Corp., Roseland Partners, L.L.C., and — "for the limited purposes stated" — the named individuals Marshall B. Tycher, Bradford R. Klatt and Carl Goldberg. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016 (Exhibit index / Purchase Agreement, Exhibit 10.1 to Form 8-K dated Oct 8, 2012)]
- The Company remains dependent on named key personnel, including "chairman of Roseland and president, and chief operating officer of Roseland," and notes it "may become increasingly dependent on non-executive personnel with residential development and leasing expertise" as it repositions toward multi-family. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- Certain executive officers of the Roseland subsidiary and/or family members ("RG") directly or indirectly hold small noncontrolling interests in a consolidated joint venture, and the Company earned $2,464,000 in 2016 from entities in which RG has ownership interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing era)
- The multi-family business is held in a distinct vehicle sub-structure — Roseland Residential Trust (RRT), a wholly owned subsidiary, and Roseland Residential, L.P. (RRLP), the operating partnership through which RRT conducts its operations — governed by a Second Amended and Restated Limited Partnership Agreement of RRLP and a Shareholders Agreement of RRT filed as exhibits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
- A Form of Shared Services Agreement by and between Mack-Cali Realty, L.P. and Roseland Residential, L.P. is filed as an exhibit, allocating shared services between the parent operating partnership and the multi-family operating vehicle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016 (Exhibit index — Shared Services Agreement)]
- On February 27, 2017 the Company, RRT, RRLP and affiliates entered an Investment Agreement with affiliates of Rockpoint Group, L.L.C. providing for equity investments of up to an aggregate $300 million of preferred units of RRLP, with an initial $150 million closing expected by mid-March 2017 (inclusive of a $30 million deposit) — bringing outside preferred capital into the multi-family operating vehicle rather than the parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession is an Item 2.02 quarterly-leasing-results furnishing (in which "roll up" appears only as a rent metric), not a property or portfolio acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-135131, 8-K Item 2.02 / Exhibit 99.1]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is an internally managed UPREIT executing a portfolio repositioning toward multi-family: bringing multi-family assets across primarily by buying out joint-venture partners and consolidating the properties, running the multi-family operations through its acquired Roseland platform (whose founding principals were parties to the 2012 platform-purchase agreement and remain named key personnel), and housing the multi-family business in a separate RRT/RRLP vehicle bridged to the parent by a Shared Services Agreement and, from early 2017, outside Rockpoint preferred capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-012551, 10-K FY2016]
