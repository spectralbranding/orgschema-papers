## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)

  Forms 15-12B to deregister classes of its securities on 2018-07-31, 2018-08-13 and
  2018-08-23, and a Form 25-NSE to remove its common stock from Nasdaq listing on
  2018-08-01; it filed no 10-K for any fiscal year after FY2017. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001520566, Forms 15-12B (accessions 0001193125-18-233454, -247357, -256346) and 25-NSE (accession 0000876661-18-000834)]
- The acquiring public shell, WMIH Corp., changed its registrant name to Mr. Cooper Group
  Inc. on 2018-09-14 and continued as an SEC filer (CIK 0000933136) reporting on a
  business it describes as the largest non-bank servicer of residential mortgage loans in
  the U.S.; because WMIH had no comparable operating business of its own before the
  merger, its post-merger 10-Ks are the only public annual-report record bearing on the
  acquired mortgage-servicing/origination business's realized trajectory. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history, CIK 0000933136 (formerly-names record); SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K "Overview"]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K (filed 2023-02-16) is signed by Jay Bray as Chief Executive
  Officer of Mr. Cooper Group Inc., and lists the registrant's principal executive
  offices at 8950 Cypress Waters Blvd, Coppell, TX 75019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K cover page and signature page]

## Realized business record (FY2020-FY2022, per the FY2022 Form 10-K)
- Mr. Cooper Group Inc. reported net income attributable to Mr. Cooper of $923 million
  for FY2022, $1,454 million for FY2021, and $305 million for FY2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Total assets were $12,776 million at December 31, 2022, down from $14,204 million at
  December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, Consolidated Balance Sheets]
- As of December 31, 2022, the company reported an aggregate servicing/subservicing
  unpaid principal balance of $870 billion across 4.1 million customers ($411 billion
  owned servicing, $459 billion subservicing and other), versus $709.7 billion and a loan
  count of approximately 3.56 million at December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, Item 1 "Servicing" and Table 7 "Key Performance Metrics"]
- The company reported having approximately 6,600 employees across the U.S. and India as
  of December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, "Human Capital Resources"]
- The company's quantitative goodwill assessment for its Servicing, Originations and Xome
  reporting units as of December 31, 2022 and 2021 found no impairment of goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, Goodwill note]
- On March 31, 2022, the company completed the sale of certain assets and liabilities of
  its servicing/subservicing technology platform for performing and non-performing
  mortgage loans (the "Mortgage Servicing Platform") to Sagent, receiving Class A-1
  Common Units equal to a 19.9% ownership interest in Sagent plus $9.9 million in cash,
  for total consideration of $260 million, and recorded a $223 million gain on the
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, Note 3 "Dispositions"]
- As of December 31, 2022, the company reported U.S. federal net operating loss
  carryforwards of approximately $0.5 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K, income-tax note]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the acquired registrant deregistered, the realized record within the fixed
  window is documented only through the surviving public parent's (Mr. Cooper Group
  Inc., f/k/a WMIH Corp.) own 10-K filings, which describe a servicing/origination
  business operating under continuity of brand, headquarters address and chief executive
  with Nationstar's pre-merger business, reaching 4.1 million serviced customers and

  $1,454 million (FY2021) and $923 million (FY2022), no goodwill impairment, and one
  documented partial divestiture (the March 2022 Sagent technology-platform transaction)
  within the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933136-23-000009, Form 10-K]
