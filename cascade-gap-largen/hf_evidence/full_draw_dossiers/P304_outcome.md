## Window and reporting-status note
- The outcome window is fixed to ONEOK, Inc.'s (CIK 1039684) Annual Report on Form 10-K
  for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020, filed February 23, 2021. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- ONEOK, Inc. remained a public reporting company continuously filing Forms 10-K through
  this window; the entity whose formerly separately listed common units ceased trading
  is described in this Form 10-K as "ONEOK Partners, L.P., a wholly owned subsidiary of
  ONEOK, Inc." — i.e., it continued to exist as a wholly owned subsidiary consolidated
  into ONEOK, Inc.'s reported financial results, rather than as a separately reporting
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Realized financial trajectory
- ONEOK, Inc.'s consolidated net income attributable to ONEOK was $612.809 million for
  the year ended December 31, 2020, down from $1,278.577 million for the year ended
  December 31, 2019 and $1,151.703 million for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- ONEOK, Inc. incurred $644.9 million in noncash impairment charges in 2020, which the
  filing states "had an adverse impact on our financial results for the year ended
  December 31, 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Of the $644.9 million, the Natural Gas Gathering and Processing segment recorded
  $382.2 million of impairment charges on certain long-lived asset groups (in the Powder
  River Basin, western Oklahoma and Kansas) described as not recoverable, a $153.4
  million goodwill impairment charge, and a $30.5 million impairment charge related to a
  10.2% equity-method investment in Venice Energy Services Company; the Natural Gas
  Liquids segment recorded $71.6 million of impairment charges on certain inactive assets
  and a $7.2 million impairment charge related to a 50% investment in Chisholm Pipeline
  Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The filing attributes part of a segment-results decline to "a decrease of $607.2
  million due to noncash impairment charges in our Natural Gas Gathering and Processing
  and Natural Gas Liquids segments." [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K
  (FY2020)]
- The filing states that, in the first quarter of 2020, the energy industry experienced
  "historic events that led to a simultaneous demand and supply disruption," referencing
  the World Health Organization's declaration of COVID-19 as a global pandemic, and that
  the goodwill and asset-impairment testing described above was triggered by a decline in
  ONEOK's share price and market capitalization during that period. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Despite the impairment charges, ONEOK, Inc. paid total dividends of $3.74 per common
  share in 2020, an increase of 6% over the $3.53 per share paid in 2019, and in February
  2021 maintained a quarterly dividend of $0.935 per share ($3.74 per share annualized),
  consistent with the immediately preceding quarter. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- As of February 16, 2021, ONEOK, Inc.'s long-term debt carried investment-grade credit
  ratings of Baa3 (Moody's), BBB (S&P), and BBB (Fitch), each with a stable outlook.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Organizational continuity noted in the window
- The Form 10-K's director/officer biographical table shows Terry K. Spencer continuing
  as President and Chief Executive Officer of ONEOK from 2014 to the filing date, having
  also served as President and Chief Executive Officer of ONEOK Partners from 2014 to
  2017 (the year the Partnership's common units ceased separate listing). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Approximately three years after the Partnership's common units ceased separate public
  trading, ONEOK, Inc.'s FY2020 Form 10-K reports a consolidated business that remained
  investment-grade rated and continued to raise its per-share dividend, while also
  recording a substantial year-over-year decline in net income (from $1.279 billion in
  2019 to $612.8 million in 2020) and $644.9 million in noncash impairment charges
  concentrated in the gathering-and-processing and natural-gas-liquids segments,
  attributed by the filing to a 2020 commodity demand-and-supply disruption associated
  with the COVID-19 pandemic. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K
  (FY2020)]
