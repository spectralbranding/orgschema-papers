# Structural sub-dossier — Cloud Peak Energy / Decker Coal Company joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured — the parties, the legal vehicle, and what did and did not
> cross the ownership boundary. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it actually is)
- The drawn Form 8-K reports, under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement),
  that on January 20, 2012 Western Minerals LLC (a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of
  Cloud Peak Energy Inc.) and KCP, Inc. (a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of Ambre
  Energy Limited) entered into Amendment No. 12 to the Decker Coal Company Agreement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The 8-K states the Amendment "clarifies certain matters ... relating to reclamation
  surety bonds required by applicable regulations and mine reclamation expenses"; it is an
  amendment to a pre-existing agreement, not the instrument that formed the venture.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## The legal vehicle and parties (from the amended agreement)
- The exhibit recites that Western Minerals LLC (an Oregon LLC, "Western"), KCP Inc. (a
  Delaware corporation, "KCP") and Montana Royalty Company (a limited partnership) are
  parties to the Decker Coal Company Agreement "dated as of the 1st day of September, 1970,"
  as amended through eleven prior amendments; the agreement as so amended is defined as the
  "Joint Venture Agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K EX-10.1]
- Amendment No. 12 adds a new Section 11(i) providing that 50% of each of the West Decker
  and East Decker reclamation bonds is to be obtained by Western at its sole cost and 50%
  by KCP at its sole cost, with the cost of the bonds and related obligations "several, not
  joint." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K EX-10.1]
- The Amendment states that "Except as specifically hereby amended, all other terms and
  provisions of the Joint Venture Agreement shall remain in full force and effect."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K EX-10.1]

## What crossed / did not cross the ownership boundary (registrant's contemporaneous 10-K)
- Cloud Peak holds a 50% NON-OPERATING interest in the Decker coal mine (Montana) through
  the joint venture agreement; "the other 50% mine owner has responsibility for the
  day-to-day operations" and "markets the thermal coal out of the Decker mine subject to
  the direction of the management committee." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The customer-facing product (sub-bituminous thermal coal) and the interest in the mine
  are held jointly, but the operating PROCESSES remain with the co-owner: "While we
  participate in the management committee of the Decker mine ... we do not control, and our
  employees do not participate in, the day-to-day operations of the Decker mine."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The PEOPLE/organization did not cross to the registrant: the Decker mine "is a
  union-based operation. However, we do not employ any of the Decker mine employees."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Cloud Peak owns the equipment used at its own mines "excluding the Decker mine," i.e. the
  producing assets of Decker sit outside the registrant's operated fleet.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Economics are split equally: the parties "share the profits, losses, operating expenses,
  reclamation obligations and liabilities and assets associated with the Decker mine
  equally with the other 50% owner." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The registrant accounts for the 50% interest using the proportionate consolidation
  method, including its share of Decker's assets, liabilities, revenue and expenses in its
  consolidated statements; Decker Coal Company is excluded from the registrant's restricted
  subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Bridging of the shared substrate at the venture level
- The management committee under the joint venture agreement is the standing governance
  mechanism through which the non-operating owner participates while the operating owner
  runs day-to-day operations and markets the coal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Reclamation obligations are contractually apportioned 50/50 and made several rather than
  joint by Amendment No. 12, with each party free to select its own surety-bond issuer
  (subject to an AM Best A/X rating and regulatory acceptability).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K EX-10.1]

## Structural summary (structure only)
- The transaction is a 50/50 joint venture in which the registrant holds a passive,
  non-operating economic interest in a coal mine and its product, while the co-owner
  (an Ambre Energy subsidiary) holds the other 50% and retains the operating processes,
  the workforce, and day-to-day management; participation is bridged through a management
  committee and a several-liability reclamation-bonding arrangement rather than by
  transferring operations or people across the boundary.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-12-001130, Form 10-K (FY2011); SEC accession 0001104659-12-003562, Form 8-K EX-10.1]
