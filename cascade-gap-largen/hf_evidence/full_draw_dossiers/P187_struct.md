# Structural sub-dossier — Calpine Corporation acquisition by an Energy Capital Partners-led investor consortium (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Calpine Corporation, a Delaware corporation, is described in its own merger proxy as
  "a premier power generation company with 80 power plants primarily in the U.S." that
  sells power and related services to wholesale customers (commercial and industrial
  end-users, state and regional wholesale market operators, and retail affiliates) and
  purchases primarily natural gas (and some fuel oil) as fuel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- On August 17, 2017, Calpine entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Volt
  Parent, LP ("Parent"), a Delaware limited partnership, and Volt Merger Sub, Inc.
  ("Merger Sub"), a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, providing
  for the acquisition of Calpine by Parent; Merger Sub would merge with and into Calpine,
  with Calpine continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a subsidiary of
  Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000916457-17-000121, Form 8-K "8-K MERGER AGREEMENT," Item 1.01]
- Parent and Merger Sub were formed by Energy Capital Partners III, LLC on behalf of
  itself, its affiliated funds, and a consortium of co-investors, including Access
  Industries, Inc. and the Canada Pension Plan Investment Board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, each share of Calpine common stock (other than certain
  excluded shares) would be converted into the right to receive $15.25 in cash, without
  interest, representing a premium of approximately 51% over Calpine's closing share
  price of $10.07 on May 9, 2017, the last trading day before market rumors of a
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing and what did not
- The entire corporate entity and its operating asset base (the 80-power-plant
  generation fleet, wholesale/retail power-marketing operations, and associated fuel
  procurement operations) crossed to the Parent as a whole; Calpine survived the merger
  intact as a subsidiary of Parent rather than being divided, carved out, or
  recombined with another operating business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- The legal vehicle was a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub (a newly formed
  acquisition subsidiary of Parent) merged with and into Calpine, Merger Sub's separate
  corporate existence ceased, and Calpine continued as the surviving corporation and a
  subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- As of the date of the proxy statement, no member of Calpine's management had entered
  into an employment agreement or other agreement or commitment with Parent or its
  affiliates with respect to continuing employment, nor had any member of management
  entered into an equity rollover or co-investment agreement with Parent; the proxy
  noted it was possible such continuing-employment arrangements could be entered into
  before closing, but consummation of the merger was not contingent on any such
  arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- Calpine's Chief Executive Officer (Thad Hill) had an amended and restated employment
  agreement with Calpine itself, entered May 16, 2017 (before the merger agreement was
  signed), under which severance and change-in-control payments would apply in lieu of,
  not in addition to, the company's broader severance plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- Consummation of the merger was subject to customary closing conditions, including
  Calpine stockholder approval and expiration/termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino
  Antitrust Improvements Act waiting period (terminated September 27, 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
- Energy Capital Partners III-A ("the guarantor") entered into a Limited Guarantee,
  dated August 17, 2017, guaranteeing to Calpine certain of Parent's payment
  obligations under the merger agreement, including any termination fee payable by
  Parent under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]

## Consideration mechanics (closing-time)
- If the merger was consummated, Calpine common stock would be removed from listing on
  the New York Stock Exchange and deregistered under the Exchange Act, and Calpine would
  no longer be required to file periodic reports with the SEC on account of its common
  stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company, going-private reverse triangular
  merger: Calpine's entire operating business (generation fleet, wholesale/retail power
  operations, and headquarters organization) crossed to the Parent as a single unit
  rather than being separated into product/process/people components; the legal vehicle
  was a Merger-Sub-into-Calpine merger with Calpine surviving as a subsidiary; and, as
  of the proxy date, no continuing-employment or equity-rollover arrangements had yet
  been documented between incumbent management and the new Parent, though the proxy
  left open the possibility such arrangements could be reached before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-341396, DEFM14A]
