## Public-reporting status in the outcome window (feasibility of a public record)
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports on Form 10-K; the fiscal-year-2021 report (year ended December 31, 2021) is on EDGAR and is the source for the realized-record facts below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized scale and portfolio trajectory
- As of December 31, 2021 the company owned 3,077 properties and 22,575 single family rental homes, an income-producing portfolio "primarily focused in Residential, Industrial, and Net Lease properties, and to a lesser extent Data Centers, Hospitality, Self Storage." [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- Total assets were $91,308,211 thousand (~$91.3 billion) at December 31, 2021, up from $40,684,015 thousand (~$40.7 billion) at December 31, 2020; investments in real estate, net, were $66,941,653 thousand (~$66.9 billion) at December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- During the year ended December 31, 2021 the company acquired interests in 65 real estate investments for $36.9 billion, which comprised 672 residential properties among other assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized income scalar
- Net loss attributable to BREIT stockholders was $(804,783) thousand for the year ended December 31, 2021, compared with $(853,399) thousand for 2020 and $(401,771) thousand for 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- No impairments occurred during the years ended December 31, 2021 and 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized record at the process/organization interface
- The company remained externally managed: "We are externally managed by BX REIT Advisors L.L.C." and "Our operations are conducted by the Adviser." [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- On human capital, the FY2021 report states the company had "no employees other than those employed by the management companies that were acquired in connection with the acquisitions of the Simply Storage Portfolio, Home Partners of America Portfolio, and the ACE Affordable Housing Portfolio, none of whom are executive officers of the Company or are involved in the management of the Company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- These operating teams entered the organization through whole-platform acquisitions: the company describes acquiring "management teams as part of our acquisitions of Simply Self Storage, QTS Realty Trust and Home Partners of America," and states it "may acquire other management teams as part of transactions in the future." [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the outcome window the registrant grew substantially by continued acquisition — total assets rising from ~$40.7 billion (Dec. 31, 2020) to ~$91.3 billion (Dec. 31, 2021) and the portfolio reaching 3,077 properties plus 22,575 single family rental homes — while reporting a net loss attributable to stockholders of $(804,783) thousand for 2021 and recording no impairments in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
- The company remained externally managed with operations conducted by the Blackstone Adviser; the only personnel employed within the consolidated group by the end of the window came in through acquired management companies (Simply Storage, Home Partners of America, ACE Affordable Housing; management teams also from QTS Realty Trust), none of whom were executive officers of or involved in managing the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001662972-22-000027, 10-K FY2021]
