# Structural sub-dossier — MillerCoors LLC joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is the registrant's Form 8-K filed 2007-12-21, whose Exhibit 10.1 is
  the executed "JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT" dated as of December 20, 2007; the drawn filing
  IS the deal-terms document (the venture-formation agreement itself), not a mere
  reference or earnings release. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 Joint Venture Agreement]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The agreement is among Molson Coors Brewing Company (MCBC), Coors Brewing Company (CBC),
  SABMiller plc, Miller Brewing Company (Miller), and MillerCoors LLC (the Company); it is
  structured as a joint venture combining the parties' respective beer operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1]
- The Company is to be formed on the Closing Date "as a Delaware limited liability company
  by filing a certificate of formation with the Secretary of State of the State of
  Delaware," and its name "shall be MillerCoors LLC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.01, §2.03]
- The exclusive purpose of the Company is stated to be "to serve as the vehicle for the
  joint venture between Miller and CBC relating to the combination of their respective
  operations in the Territory." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.06]
- The "Territory" is defined as "the United States and Puerto Rico, including all military
  bases of the United States of America that are located in the United States and Puerto
  Rico." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (Definitions)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary into the joint venture (tier level)
- Each parent contributes its entire U.S. beer business into the Company. The "Coors
  Business" is defined as "the beer, beverage and related operations (including packaging
  and other non-beverage ventures), assets and liabilities" of the Molson Coors Group in
  the Territory; the "Miller Business" is defined in parallel terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (Definitions)]
- MCBC and CBC "shall ... assign, transfer and convey at the Closing to the Company ...
  all right, title and interest of every kind and nature in and to the Molson Coors
  Contributions," intended "to constitute a tax-free contribution to a partnership under
  Section 721 of the Code"; Miller contributes on parallel terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.08(a)]
- The customer-facing PRODUCT crossed with its producing PROCESSES: the Molson Coors
  Contributions include "all current assets of the Coors Business ... including accounts
  receivables, inventory," "all machinery, equipment, vehicles, furniture, fixtures,
  printing plates, spare and replacement parts and other tangible personal property," "the
  CBC Real Properties," equity interests in packaging subsidiaries (Rocky Mountain Metal
  Container LLC, Rocky Mountain Bottle Company LLC, AC Golden Brewing Company LLC), and the
  "Molson Coors Assumed Contracts." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.08(b)(i),(iii),(iv),(v),(vi)]
- The brands and their goodwill crossed: the contributions include the "Molson Coors
  Contributed IP" — Intellectual Property registrations/applications and CBC Owned IP used
  "in connection with any Molson Coors Contributed Brands," plus "all past and present
  goodwill (including the right to represent to third parties that the Company is the
  successor to the Coors Business) and related intangible property ... including
  relationships with suppliers, customers and employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.08(b)(vii)]
- The PEOPLE crossed alongside the processes: the contributions include liabilities "with
  respect to the employment or termination of employment of all Transferring CBC
  Employees," their benefit-plan liabilities and funding assets, and defined-benefit
  pension and retiree-medical obligations ("CBC Retiree Liabilities"); Miller's
  contribution parallels this for "all Transferring Miller Employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.08(b)(ix),(x),(xi); §2.09]
- A cash true-up also crossed: CBC contributes "$51,000,000 plus the Miller Assumed
  Contract Adjustment Payment in cash (the 'CBC Cash Contribution')," and container-deposit
  cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.08(b)(viii)]
- What did NOT cross: each parent retains defined "Excluded Assets," "Excluded
  Liabilities," and "Excluded ... Employees" (e.g., Molson Coors Excluded Assets /
  Excluded Liabilities, Excluded CBC Employees), which remain the sole obligation of the
  contributing group rather than being assumed by the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (Definitions; §2.08)]

## Ownership, control, and governance at closing
- The Company has "authorized capital consisting of 1,000,000 Shares, consisting of
  840,000 Class A Shares and 160,000 Class B Shares"; immediately after Closing "Miller
  will own all 160,000 Class B Shares, while Miller and CBC will each own 420,000 Class A
  Shares." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.04]
- The Class A and Class B Shares "have the same rights and preferences in all respects
  except that the Class B Shares shall not have voting rights or privileges" — i.e., voting
  Class A is split 420,000 / 420,000 (parity) while total economic ownership is Miller
  580,000 / CBC 420,000 (derived from the stated share counts). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.04]
- After Closing the Company "shall be managed by the Board and by officers as provided in
  the Operating Agreement," which the Shareholders execute at Closing to govern "the
  affairs of the Company and the conduct of the Company's business" (Attachment I). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.02, §2.05]

## Bridging / shared-substrate arrangements at closing
- A "Services Agreement" is to be executed and delivered at Closing under which (i) "certain
  transitional services will be provided by the Shareholders ... to the Company and/or by
  the Company to the Shareholders," (ii) "the Company will provide to each of the
  Shareholders ... on an ongoing basis certain of those services which are provided at the
  date hereof using assets which form part of the Miller Contributions and the Molson Coors
  Contributions ... or using Transferring Miller Employees or Transferring CBC Employees,"
  and (iii) the Shareholders "will provide certain services to the Company on an ongoing
  basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (Definitions: Services Agreement)]
- Additional closing-time Transaction Documents bridge the shared IP and physical
  substrate: a "Miller IP License Agreement," a "Molson Coors IP License Agreement," a
  "Brand Cooperation Agreement" governing "the protocols governing the management of
  certain Intellectual Property" (Attachment II), a "Water Supply Lease" under which MCBC,
  CBC and their Affiliates lease and provide water to the Company, plus Intellectual
  Property Assignments and Real Property Transfer Documents. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1 (Definitions: Transaction Documents; Brand Cooperation Agreement; Water Supply Lease)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Two competing parents each folded their entire U.S. beer operating substrate — brands and
  goodwill (product), breweries, machinery, inventory, contracts and packaging subsidiaries
  (processes), and transferring employees with their pension/benefit obligations (people) —
  into a single newly formed Delaware LLC, MillerCoors LLC, as a tax-free partnership
  contribution; voting is held at Class A parity while economic ownership is split by share
  count, with the combined operation governed by an Operating Agreement and its shared
  water, IP, brand-management and cross-provided services bridged by a Water Supply Lease,
  IP License and Brand Cooperation Agreements, and a Services Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-090365, Form 8-K EX-10.1]
