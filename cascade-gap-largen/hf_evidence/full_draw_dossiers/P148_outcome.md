## Public-reporting status and identity in the outcome window
- The combined entity continued as an SEC-reporting, NYSE-listed public company; the
  fiscal-year-2022 Form 10-K was filed under Commission File Number 001-38735. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K cover]
- Effective February 1, 2021, the registrant changed its corporate name from Contura
  Energy, Inc. to Alpha Metallurgical Resources, Inc., stated as being "to more accurately
  reflect our strategic focus on the production of metallurgical coal." [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]

## Realized financial record (best-documented scalar)
- Consolidated net income for the year ended December 31, 2022 was $1,448,545 thousand
  (approximately $1.45 billion); for the year ended December 31, 2021 it was $288,790
  thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The 2022 result was driven by the Met segment (segment net income $1,647,104 thousand),
  partly offset by an "All Other" segment net loss of $(198,559) thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K, segment reconciliation]
- Total revenues were $4,101,592 thousand for 2022 and $2,258,686 thousand for 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- As of December 31, 2022 the company had approximately 3,730 full-time employees and
  described itself as producing, processing and selling met coal and thermal coal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]

## Documented portfolio/operational change over the horizon
- The company entered an agreement with Blackjewel to sell its Western Mines in the Powder
  River Basin (PRB), Wyoming, together with related coal reserves, equipment and
  infrastructure (its former PRB operations); those operations are reported as discontinued
  operations in the consolidated financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2020.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K, Note on Discontinued Operations]
- The corporate rename (Feb 2021) and the exit of the western thermal PRB operations are
  documented as a strategic refocus toward metallurgical coal; approximately 39% of thermal
  coal tons sold were shipped internationally in 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]
- The company disclosed a stock-repurchase program permitting repurchases of up to
  $1.0 billion of its common stock, indicating capital return to shareholders in the
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]

## Interface-disruption evidence
- No bankruptcy, dissolution, or going-concern qualification is stated in the FY2022 10-K;
  the combined entity operated as a continuing, profitable NYSE registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]
- No filing-sourced disclosure of a discrete post-combination integration failure at the
  product/operations or operations/organization interface was identified in the FY2022
  10-K; the documented structural change is the divestiture of the western thermal (PRB)
  operations and the refocus on met coal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Roughly four years after the ~2018 combination, the surviving entity — renamed Alpha
  Metallurgical Resources, Inc. (from Contura Energy, Inc.) effective February 2021 — was a
  continuing, publicly traded coal producer that reported strongly positive results in
  fiscal 2022 (net income ~$1.45 billion on total revenues ~$4.10 billion), up sharply from
  fiscal 2021 (net income ~$288.8 million), driven by its metallurgical-coal segment, while
  having exited its western thermal (Powder River Basin) operations via divestiture to
  Blackjewel and refocused on met coal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-23-000010, 10-K]
