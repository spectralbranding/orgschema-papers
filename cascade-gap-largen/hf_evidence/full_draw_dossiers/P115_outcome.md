## Realized disposition of the acquired brand (best-documented outcome scalar)
- On December 12, 2012, the registrant completed the sale of Simply Wheelz LLC, the wholly owned subsidiary that operated the Advantage Rent A Car business, disposing of the acquired brand within about three and a half years of its 2009 acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Item 1 Business / Note 4 Business Combinations and Divestitures]
- The divestiture was compelled by regulatory conditions on a separate transaction: "On November 19, 2012, we entered into an agreement with the Federal Trade Commission in connection with our acquisition of Dollar Thrifty to divest the Advantage brand and selected Dollar Thrifty airport concessions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Item 1 Business]
- The Simply Wheelz subsidiary, which owned and operated the Advantage brand, was sold to Adreca Holdings Corp., a subsidiary of Macquarie Capital, expected to be operated by Franchise Services of North America Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Item 1 Business]
- The sale price was "approximately $16.0 million, plus the value of current assets as of the closing date, which was approximately $3.6 million," and on the divestiture the registrant "realized a loss (before income taxes) of approximately $31.4 million as of December 31, 2012." [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Note 4 Business Combinations and Divestitures]
- Immediately prior to the divestiture the Advantage brand was operating at 62 U.S. locations, including 35 on-airport locations where it held concessions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Item 1 Business]

## Continuing dependence after the disposition (product moved without a self-sufficient operating substrate)
- The buyer could not stand the business up independently: to assist it in "securing alternative fleet financing arrangements," the registrant entered a senior note credit agreement to loan Simply Wheelz, on a senior unsecured basis, up to $45.0 million over five years at interest rates up to 13%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Note 4 Business Combinations and Divestitures]
- The registrant also "agreed to sublease vehicles to the buyer of Advantage for use in continuing the operations of Advantage, for a period no longer than two years from the closing date," retaining "significant continuing involvement in the operations of the disposed Advantage business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Note 4 Business Combinations and Divestitures]
- Under those arrangements the registrant subleased approximately 20,000 vehicles to Simply Wheelz or its affiliates for use in operating the Advantage brand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]

## Collapse of the divested business within the window
- In October 2013, Simply Wheelz's parent, Franchise Services of North America ("FSNA"), requested that the registrant forbear from collecting amounts owed by Simply Wheelz and renegotiate the commercial arrangements, including the vehicle-sublease terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]
- "On November 2, 2013, we terminated the applicable sublease contracts, and on November 5, 2013, Simply Wheelz filed for bankruptcy protection under Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code." [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]
- In its bankruptcy proceedings Simply Wheelz agreed to sell substantially all of its assets to The Catalyst Capital Group Inc.; on December 16, 2013 the registrant entered a settlement agreement with Simply Wheelz, FSNA, Catalyst and other parties governing continued use and orderly return of the registrant's vehicles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]
- As of February 1, 2014, Simply Wheelz was in possession of approximately 9,000 of the registrant's vehicles, and the registrant disclosed that it "recognized losses as a result of our relationship with" FSNA and Simply Wheelz and "may incur additional losses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly three and a half years of the 2009 asset acquisition, the acquired price-oriented leisure brand was divested (December 12, 2012) at an approximately $31.4 million pre-tax loss under a regulatory condition tied to a different transaction; the buyer required the registrant's own financing and a sublease of about 20,000 vehicles to keep operating, and the divested business (Simply Wheelz) entered Chapter 11 bankruptcy on November 5, 2013 and moved to sell its assets, leaving the registrant with continuing exposure and recognized losses through early 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000446, Form 10-K FY2012 Note 4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001364479-14-000006, Form 10-K FY2013 Item 1A Risk Factors]
