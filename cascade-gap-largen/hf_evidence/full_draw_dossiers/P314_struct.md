# Structural sub-dossier — Energen Corporation acquisition by Diamondback Energy (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Diamondback Energy, Inc. and Energen Corporation entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger dated August 14, 2018, together with Sidewinder Merger Sub Inc., a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Diamondback formed in Alabama solely to effect the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a merger of Merger Sub with and into Energen, with
  Energen continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a direct wholly owned
  subsidiary of Diamondback. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- Consideration was all-stock: each share of Energen common stock was converted into the
  right to receive 0.6442 of a share of Diamondback common stock, with cash in lieu of
  fractional shares; the transaction was initially valued at approximately $9.2 billion
  including Energen's net debt. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- Diamondback is described as an independent oil and natural gas company focused on
  unconventional, onshore oil and natural gas reserves in the Permian Basin in West
  Texas; Energen is described as an oil and natural gas exploration and production
  company with operations in the Midland Basin, the Delaware Basin, and the Central
  Basin Platform areas of the Permian Basin in West Texas and New Mexico — the same
  broad play region as Diamondback's activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed effective November 29, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Item 2.01)]

## Controlling intent, legal vehicle, and organizational tier at closing
- Upon completion, Diamondback's own board of directors and executive management were
  stated to remain unchanged, and Diamondback continued to be headquartered in Midland,
  Texas — Energen's pre-transaction headquarters were in Birmingham, Alabama. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- As a result of the merger, a change in control of Energen (as the surviving,
  now-subsidiary entity) occurred; immediately following the effective time, Energen's
  certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated in their entirety. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Items 5.01, 5.03)]
- All ten then-serving directors of Energen (Jonathan Z. Cohen, Kenneth W. Dewey, M.
  James Gorrie, Jay Grinney, William G. Hargett, Frances Powell Hawes, Vincent J.
  Intrieri, Alan A. Kleier, Lori A. Lancaster, and James T. McManus, II) resigned from
  Energen's board effective at the closing; Diamondback's CEO, Travis D. Stice, became
  the sole director of the surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Item 5.02)]
- Thirteen named Energen corporate officers (including its CEO, James T. McManus, II)
  ceased serving in their respective officer capacities at the effective time; the
  surviving entity's officer roles were filled by Diamondback personnel, with Travis D.
  Stice appointed CEO, Michael L. Hollis appointed President and COO, and four other
  named Diamondback executives appointed to the remaining senior officer posts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Item 5.02)]

## Product / process / people tiers and bridging arrangements
- The merger agreement's employee-matters covenant committed Diamondback (through the
  surviving corporation) to provide "continuing employees" of Energen, for a defined
  continuation period, with base pay, target incentive opportunities, and aggregate
  benefits no less favorable than pre-closing, and severance equal to the greater of
  Energen's pre-existing Change in Control Severance Pay Plan or Diamondback's
  comparable severance terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- Diamondback also agreed to maintain Energen's retiree welfare plans for existing
  beneficiaries for as long as trust assets remained adequate, and to recognize
  continuing employees' prior service with Energen for benefit-plan purposes going
  forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- Completion of the merger was disclosed as capable of triggering change-in-control or
  similar provisions in agreements to which Energen was a party, with counterparties
  potentially able to exercise contractual rights or remedies as a result. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- Energen equity awards (options, time-vesting RSUs, and performance share awards) were
  converted into Diamondback equity awards of approximately equivalent value at the
  effective time rather than being cashed out, per the merger agreement's treatment of
  outstanding awards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]

## Public-market and registration status at closing
- Prior to closing, Diamondback common stock traded on the Nasdaq Global Select Market
  under "FANG" and Energen common stock traded on the New York Stock Exchange under
  "EGN." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A]
- As a result of the merger, Energen common stock ceased to fulfill NYSE listing
  requirements; Energen notified the NYSE that the merger had closed, and the shares
  were removed from listing on the NYSE and deregistered under the Exchange Act, ending
  Energen's obligation to file periodic reports with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Item 3.01)]
- Diamondback subsequently filed a Form 15-12B to formally terminate registration of
  Energen's common stock under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-346260, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two companies operating the same broad business model
  (Permian Basin unconventional oil and gas exploration and production) into a single
  surviving legal entity under Diamondback's control; Energen's controlling
  board/officer tier and its separate public-reporting status did not cross intact —
  they were replaced by Diamondback's board/officer slate and terminated, respectively —
  while operational continuity for rank-and-file "continuing employees" and existing
  benefit obligations was contractually bridged through the merger agreement's
  employee-matters covenant rather than left to lapse. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-306638, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29]
