## Outcome window and primary source
- The fixed outcome window is fiscal years ~2014-2016 (three to five years after the
  February 2011 closing). The realized record is traced primarily through Polaris Industries
  Inc.'s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2015 (filed
  2016-02-19), which reports the venture's results for fiscal 2015 and comparative 2014/2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Realized financial record of the venture (within window)
- The company's reported income from the Polaris Acceptance joint venture was $30.7 million in
  fiscal 2015, $30.5 million in fiscal 2014 (up 1%), and $20.2 million in fiscal 2013 (2014
  was up 51% over 2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The company's total investment in Polaris Acceptance at December 31, 2015 was $99.1 million;
  the investment is accounted for under the equity method and recorded as "investment in
  finance affiliate," with the company's share of income included in income from financial
  services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Polaris Acceptance's wholesale portfolio receivables from U.S. dealers (including securitized
  receivables) were $1,305.1 million at December 31, 2015, a 14 percent increase from
  $1,141.1 million at December 31, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Credit losses in the Polaris Acceptance portfolio are described as modest, averaging less
  than one percent of the portfolio; income and losses continued to be shared 50/50 between
  the two partners' subsidiaries, and the company concluded there were no variable-interest
  entities requiring consolidation in 2015, 2014, or 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Total income from financial services (of which the joint venture was the largest component)
  was $69.3 million in 2015, up 12 percent from $61.7 million in 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Realized organizational / continuity record (within window)
- The venture continued in operation and its term was extended beyond the horizon set at
  closing: the FY2015 10-K states the Polaris Acceptance partnership agreement is "effective
  through February 2022." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The finance-partner side of the venture changed hands during the window: in 2015 GECDF
  announced it agreed to sell a portfolio of assets, including its ownership interests in
  Polaris Acceptance, to Wells Fargo & Company, with closing of that transaction expected in
  the first quarter of 2016; the company disclosed that "the sale is not expected to impact the
  operations of the partnership agreement, which is effective through February 2022." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The company's floor-plan financing continued to be provided through Polaris Acceptance in
  the United States (with GE affiliates providing it in several other countries), and the
  manufacturer's repurchase obligation to the venture remained in force, limited to an annual
  maximum of 15 percent of the prior year's aggregate average month-end balances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the FY2014-2016 window the venture remained a going concern rather than being
  impaired, divested, or dissolved: it grew its financed receivables (to $1,305.1 million at
  year-end 2015, +14%), produced steady equity-method income to the company ($30.5-$30.7
  million in 2014-2015), sustained low credit losses (<1% of portfolio), and had its term
  extended to February 2022; the principal documented change at the organizational interface
  was the finance partner's parent moving from GE (GECDF) to Wells Fargo & Company in a
  transaction expected to close in Q1 2016, which the company disclosed was not expected to
  affect partnership operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-011420, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
