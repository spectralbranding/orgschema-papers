## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- EEP filed a Form 15-12B on 2018-12-31 to terminate the registration of its Class A
  common units and suspend its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 0000880285, Form 15-12B accession 0001193125-18-361861]
- EEP's last annual report on Form 10-K on file with the SEC covers fiscal year 2017
  (filed 2018-02-16); no Form 10-K was filed by EEP for any fiscal year after 2017, so no
  independent, EEP-authored audited financial statements exist for the ~2021-2023 window.
  [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 0000880285]

## EEP's continued existence within the acquirer (documented, not inferred)
- As of December 31, 2021 and again as of December 31, 2022, Enbridge Inc. disclosed
  Enbridge Energy Partners, L.P. as one of two indirect, wholly owned subsidiaries that
  fully and unconditionally guarantee, on an unsecured joint-and-several basis, a list
  of Enbridge's registered debt securities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K Exhibit 22.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K Exhibit 22.1]
- Enbridge Inc.'s FY2022 Form 10-K states that, "[o]n January 22, 2019, Enbridge entered
  into supplemental indentures with its wholly-owned subsidiar[y]... Enbridge Energy
  Partners, L.P. (EEP)," under which Enbridge guaranteed EEP's outstanding notes and EEP
  reciprocally guaranteed a set of Enbridge's senior notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K, "Summarized Financial Information"]

## Documented credit-rating and debt record for EEP specifically (2021-2022, in-window)
- Enbridge Inc.'s FY2021 Form 10-K discloses that "[o]n June 1, 2021, Moody's Investors
  Service (Moody's) upgraded the credit ratings of Enbridge Inc., including our senior
  unsecured and issuer ratings, to Baa1 from Baa2. Moody's also upgraded the credit
  ratings of our subsidiaries: Enbridge Energy Partners, L.P. (EEP), Enbridge Energy
  Limited Partnership (EELP), Spectra Energy Partners, LP (SEP) and Texas Eastern. The
  outlooks of all five entities are stable." [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K, "Credit Rating Action"]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's long-term debt repayments table shows Enbridge Energy Partners,
  L.P. completed a repayment of US$600 million of 4.20% senior notes in June 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K, "Long-Term Debt Repayments"]
- The same 10-K's long-term debt table shows Enbridge Energy Partners, L.P. senior notes
  (6.5% weighted average rate, maturities 2025-2045) outstanding at CAD 3,095 million as
  of December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K, long-term debt table]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K's corresponding long-term debt table shows the same Enbridge
  Energy Partners, L.P. senior notes line item outstanding at CAD 3,320 million as of
  December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K, long-term debt table]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K states that "[a]s at December 31, 2022, the aggregate outstanding
  principal amount of EEP notes was approximately US$2.4 billion," identifying eleven
  specific EEP note series (5.875% Notes due 2025 through 7.375% Notes due 2045) held
  under the guarantee structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K, "Summarized Financial Information"]
- Enbridge Inc.'s FY2021 and FY2022 Form 10-K glossaries continue to define "EEP" as
  "Enbridge Energy Partners, L.P.," and both 10-Ks state that EEP owned pipeline assets
  that "were included in our Liquids Pipelines segment." [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- EEP itself ceased SEC periodic reporting after deregistering in December 2018, so no
  independent EEP-authored financial record exists for the ~2021-2023 window; however,
  the acquirer's own Form 10-K filings separately and repeatedly name EEP by entity, and
  document that EEP persisted through the window as a guarantor subsidiary with its own
  identified notes, received a Moody's ratings upgrade to stable outlook on June 1, 2021,
  completed a US$600 million note repayment in June 2021, and carried approximately
  US$2.4 billion in aggregate outstanding notes as of December 31, 2022 — evidence of
  continuity rather than divestiture or default, without a standalone profit-and-loss
  record. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-22-000011, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000895728-23-000008, Form 10-K]
