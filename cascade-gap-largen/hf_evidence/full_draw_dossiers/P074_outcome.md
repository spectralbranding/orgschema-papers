## Terminal realized event within the observable public record
- On October 15, 2013, MPG Office Trust, Inc. was acquired: pursuant to an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger dated April 24, 2013, the Company merged with and into REIT Merger Sub
  (a subsidiary of Brookfield DTLA Holdings LLC, an indirect subsidiary of Brookfield
  Office Properties Inc.), with the surviving corporation renamed Brookfield DTLA Fund
  Office Trust Inc.; the Company ceased to exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-13-000197, Form 8-K Items 2.01 and 5.03]
- Each share of Company common stock outstanding at the effective time was canceled and
  converted into the right to receive $3.15 per share; aggregate consideration for common
  stock was approximately $181,251,680.40, and Sub REIT/REIT Merger Sub were capitalized
  through a $190 million cash contribution from Brookfield DTLA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-13-000197, Form 8-K Items 2.01 and 5.01]
- The Company's 7.625% Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock was subject to a
  cash tender offer at $25.00 per share (372,901 shares validly tendered and accepted);
  untendered shares were converted into Sub REIT preferred stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-13-000197, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The Company notified the NYSE that its common and preferred stock would be delisted, and
  requested Form 25 notifications of removal from listing effective after the close of
  trading on October 15, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-13-000197, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Management/organization at the terminal event
- Upon closing, all directors of the Company other than Robert M. Deutschman and Edward J.

  surviving corporation — including Mitchell E. Rudin (President and CEO, US Commercial
  Operations), Bryan K. Davis (CFO), G. Mark Brown (Global Chief Investment Officer), and
  Paul L. Schulman (COO, US Commercial Operations). [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-13-000197, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The registrant's prior President and CEO (who signed the March 2012 closing filing) is
