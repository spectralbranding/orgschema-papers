# Structural sub-dossier — Alon USA Energy, Inc. merger with Delek US Holdings (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 2, 2017, Delek US Holdings, Inc. ("Delek"), Alon USA Energy, Inc.
  ("Alon"), Delek Holdco, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of Delek, "HoldCo"), Dione
  Mergeco, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo, "Delek Merger Sub") and Astro
  Mergeco, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo, "Alon Merger Sub") entered into
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger, subsequently amended by a First Amendment (February
  27, 2017) and a Second Amendment (April 21, 2017), providing for a "strategic
  business combination of Delek and Alon." [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- The structure was a double "reverse-triangular" holdco combination: Delek Merger Sub
  would merge with and into Delek, with Delek surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  HoldCo; concurrently Alon Merger Sub would merge with and into Alon, with Alon
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of the same HoldCo. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately prior to the effective time, HoldCo (Delek Holdco, Inc.) would be renamed
  "Delek US Holdings, Inc." and the pre-existing Delek US Holdings, Inc. would be
  renamed "Delek US Energy, Inc."; the newly renamed HoldCo's stock was expected to
  trade on the NYSE under Delek's existing ticker symbol "DK." [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Concurrently with signing the merger agreement, Delek entered into a voting and
  support agreement covering the approximately 47% of Alon common stock it already
  owned (33,691,292 shares as of the record date), agreeing to vote those shares in
  favor of the Alon merger proposal; separate voting agreements with David Wiessman/
  D.B.W. Holdings (2005) Ltd. and with Jeff and Karen Morris brought the total locked-up
  Alon shares to approximately 53.0% of outstanding Alon common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]

## Consideration
- Alon stockholders (other than Delek or its subsidiaries) were entitled to receive a
  fixed exchange ratio of 0.504 shares of New Delek (post-renaming HoldCo) common stock
  for each outstanding share of Alon common stock, with cash paid in lieu of fractional
  shares; Delek stockholders received one share of New Delek common stock for each
  share of Delek common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- Based on the May 25, 2017 exchange ratio calculation, former Delek stockholders were
  expected to hold approximately 76% and former Alon stockholders (other than Delek)
  approximately 24% of outstanding New Delek common stock immediately after closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]

## What Alon comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Alon directly owned a crude oil refinery in Krotz Springs, Louisiana (74,000 bpd
  throughput capacity) and, through its 100% general-partner and 81.6% limited-partner
  interests in the then-publicly traded Alon USA Partners, LP, a crude oil refinery in
  Big Spring, Texas (73,000 bpd) with an integrated wholesale marketing business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- Alon also owned crude oil refineries in California that had not processed crude oil
  since 2012, and operated approximately 300 convenience stores marketing motor fuels
  in Central and West Texas and New Mexico. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- Delek's own refining segment (Tyler, Texas and El Dorado, Arkansas refineries,
  combined 155,000 bpd) and its 60.7% limited-partner interest in the publicly traded
  Delek Logistics Partners, LP (NYSE: DKL) were the reciprocal assets brought to the
  combination; Delek had sold its retail assets (MAPCO Express and affiliates) in
  November 2016, before this transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent / rationale expressed at closing
- The Delek board's stated expectations underlying the transaction included "its belief
  that the businesses and corporate cultures of Delek and Alon are complementary and the
  integration of the two companies can be completed in a timely and efficient manner
  with minimal disruption to employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided that, prior to closing, Delek would elect its own board
  of directors to be the board of directors of HoldCo and appoint its own officers as
  HoldCo's officers; a Special Committee of Alon's board separately negotiated the
  right to designate one individual (David Wiessman) to HoldCo's board and one
  individual (Ron Haddock) to the board of the general partner of Delek Logistics,
  to be added within 30 days after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]

## Governance/management tier as actually implemented at the effective time
- The mergers closed and became effective July 1, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the effective time, all of Alon's then-serving directors — Ilan Cohen, Assaf
  Ginzburg, Ron W. Haddock, William J. Kacal, Zalman Segal, Avigal Soreq, Franklin R.
  Wheeler and David Wiessman — ceased to be directors of Alon (the surviving,
  now wholly owned subsidiary); Kevin Kremke was appointed as a new director, joining
  Ezra Uzi Yemin and Frederec Green, who remained as directors of Alon. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- At the effective time, all of Alon's then-serving officers were removed from their
  positions, and Shai Even was appointed as Senior Vice President; the certificate of
  incorporation of Alon Merger Sub became the certificate of incorporation of the
  surviving Alon entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Items 5.02/5.03]
- Alon's outstanding employment agreements with its executive officers (including its
  then-Interim Chief Executive Officer, Alan Moret) contained change-in-control
  severance and non-compete provisions that were triggered by the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services, shared-services, or supply-bridging agreement between Alon
  and any retained management entity was identified in the drawn joint proxy
  statement/prospectus. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- In connection with the closing, Alon notified the NYSE that trading in Alon common
  stock should be suspended and its listing removed; trading of Alon common stock on
  the NYSE was suspended before the opening of the market on July 3, 2017, and Alon
  requested that the NYSE file a Form 25 application to remove Alon common stock from
  listing and registration under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Alon stated its intent to file a Form 15 to suspend its reporting obligations under
  Sections 13(a) and 15(d) of the Exchange Act as soon as practicable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined Alon's refining asset/product tier (Big Spring and Krotz
  Springs refineries, associated wholesale marketing, non-operating California assets,
  and retail stores) into a renamed holding-company structure alongside Delek's own
  refining and logistics assets, funded entirely in New Delek stock (0.504 exchange
  ratio) with pre-existing ~47% Delek ownership and ~53% total voting lockup ahead of
  the vote; at the same effective time, Alon's own board and executive-officer roster
  were entirely replaced except for two directors who had already been Delek
  appointees on Alon's board, while the Special-Committee-negotiated Alon-designated
  seat on the ultimate holding company's (HoldCo/New Delek) board was a separate,
  parent-level governance arrangement contemplated for the 30 days following closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000049, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000031, Form DEFM14A]
