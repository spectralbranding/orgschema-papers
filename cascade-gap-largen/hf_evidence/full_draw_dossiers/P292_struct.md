# Structural sub-dossier — A. Schulman, Inc. acquisition by LyondellBasell Industries N.V. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- A. Schulman, Inc. (Delaware corporation, Nasdaq: "SHLM") entered an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger dated February 15, 2018 with LyondellBasell Industries N.V. and its
  Merger Sub, LYB Americas Holdco Inc., under which Merger Sub would merge into A.
  Schulman and A. Schulman would become a direct wholly owned subsidiary of
  LyondellBasell. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share converted into the right to receive $42.00 in cash plus one
  contingent value right (CVR). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed August 21, 2018; A. Schulman survived as the surviving corporation
  and became a wholly owned subsidiary of LyondellBasell. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## The customer-facing business/product line at closing
- A. Schulman described itself as an international supplier of high-performance
  plastic compounds, composites, and resins, founded 1928, with about 5,200 employees
  at 54 manufacturing facilities globally, headquartered in Fairlawn, Ohio. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]
- The proxy's account of pre-merger strategic-alternatives discussions notes one
  prospective bidder interested only in the "distribution, engineered plastics and
  engineered composites portion" of the business and another interested only in the
  "engineered plastics and masterbatch portion," each proposing the remaining product
  lines be sold separately — indicating the product lines were organizationally
  separable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and leadership/organization tier at closing
- The legal vehicle — A. Schulman, Inc. — survived the merger as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of LyondellBasell; its certificate of incorporation and bylaws were
  amended and restated at the effective time to match Merger Sub's (retaining only the
  "A. Schulman, Inc." name). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K Item
  5.03]
- At the effective time every pre-merger director ceased to be a director; Merger
  Sub's two directors, Bhavesh V. Patel and Thomas Aebischer, became the surviving
  company's directors, with Patel becoming President and CEO and Aebischer becoming
  EVP and CFO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Concurrently, and "not as a result of any disagreement with the Company," the
  pre-merger CEO/President (Joseph M. Gingo), COO (Gary A. Miller), and CFO (John W.
  Richardson) resigned. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Contractual bridging of the employee and legal-liability substrate
- For a one-year "Continuation Period" from closing, LyondellBasell was required to
  give each "continuing Company employee" salary, target incentive opportunities, and
  benefits no less favorable in the aggregate than pre-merger, plus severance during
  that period and years-of-service credit for most benefit-plan purposes. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]
- Separate pre-existing employment agreements governed severance for Messrs. Gingo and
  Richardson upon qualifying termination following completion of the merger. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A]
- On the Closing Date the company entered a Contingent Value Rights Agreement (named
  committee members including former CEO Gingo, a paying agent, and LyondellBasell)
  governing contingent payments from any net proceeds recovered on pre-existing
  litigation tied to A. Schulman's earlier Citadel/Lucent Polymers acquisitions — a
  mechanism bridging a legacy legal-liability substrate, not an operating or
  transition-services arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K
  Item 1.01]

## Public-listing status at closing
- A. Schulman common stock had been registered under the Exchange Act and listed on
  the Nasdaq Global Select Market as "SHLM." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-160804,
  DEFM14A]
- At closing, A. Schulman notified Nasdaq of its intent to remove the stock from
  listing and have Nasdaq file a Notification of Removal from Listing/Registration;
  trading was halted before the market opened on the Closing Date; the company also
  stated its intent to terminate its Exchange Act registration and suspend its
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- EDGAR shows a Form 25-NSE dated August 21, 2018 and a Form 15-15D dated September
  20, 2018 for A. Schulman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-18-000322, Form 25-NSE]
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-278863, Form 15-15D]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The legal vehicle and its acquired product lines (masterbatch, engineered plastics,
  engineered composites) crossed intact as a wholly owned subsidiary, but the
  pre-merger CEO, COO, CFO and full board were replaced at closing by the acquirer's
  own designated officers/directors, rank-and-file "continuing employees" received
  only a fixed one-year post-closing protection period, and the company's public
  registration and stock listing ended at or immediately after closing. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-160804, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254081,
  Form 8-K]
