## Parties and transaction type at closing
- The registrant is a Maryland corporation formed April 6, 2010 that has elected to be
  taxed as a real estate investment trust; it is the sole general partner of and owns a
  99.99% partnership interest in Cole Corporate Income Operating Partnership, LP, a
  Delaware limited partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]
- The registrant is externally managed: Cole Corporate Income Advisors, LLC ("CCI
  Advisors"), the advisor, is "responsible for managing our affairs on a day-to-day basis
  and for identifying and making acquisitions and investments on our behalf" under an
  advisory agreement of a one-year term reconsidered annually by the board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]
- The sponsor is Cole Real Estate Investments, "a group of affiliated entities"; the
  affiliated property manager is Cole Realty Advisors, Inc.; the dealer manager is Cole
  Capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]
- Capital was raised through a continuous public offering registered on Form S-11 at
  $10.00 per share (with a distribution reinvestment plan at $9.50 per share). [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]

## What crossed the ownership boundary (the property acquisitions)
- During the year ended December 31, 2012 the registrant "acquired 12 commercial
  properties for an aggregate purchase price of $259.9 million (the '2012 Acquisitions')";
  the 2012 Acquisitions were purchased "with net proceeds from the Offering." [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]
- The purchase price of those properties was allocated "to the fair value of the assets
  acquired and liabilities assumed," including acquired in-place leases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]
- As of December 31, 2012 the registrant owned 13 properties comprising approximately 3.2
  million rentable square feet of single-tenant commercial space (office and industrial)
  located in nine states, which were 100% leased. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 2]
- The stated investment approach is to acquire "necessity corporate properties, net leased
  under long-term leases to creditworthy tenants," structured as sale-leaseback
  transactions intended to be characterized as a "true lease" so the registrant is treated
  as owner of the property; under a net lease the tenant remains in occupancy and bears
  operating costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]
- What crossed the boundary was therefore fee title to the real estate together with the
  in-place net leases; the operating tenants and their businesses did not change. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]

## Operating processes and people — NOT owned, provided by affiliates under contract
- Under the note captioned "Economic Dependency," the registrant "has engaged or will
  engage CCI Advisors and its affiliates to provide certain services that are essential to
  the Company, including asset management services, supervision of the management and
  leasing of properties owned by the Company, asset acquisition and disposition decisions,"
  the sale of shares, accounting services, and investor relations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]
- The registrant's properties "are, and we anticipate that substantially all properties we
  acquire in the future will be, managed and leased by our property manager, Cole Realty
  Advisors, Inc. ... an affiliate of our advisor, pursuant to property management and
  leasing agreements." [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]
- The operating personnel reside in the sponsor's organization, not the registrant: the
  sponsor "has built an organization of over 325 employees, who are experienced in the
  various aspects of acquiring, financing and managing" commercial real estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1]

## Contractual bridges over the shared external-management substrate (closing-time)
- The acquired assets are operated through a stack of affiliated-party agreements — the
  advisory agreement (one-year term), the property management and leasing agreements with
  Cole Realty Advisors, and the dealer-manager arrangement with Cole Capital — rather than
  through an operating organization owned by the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Item 1 + notes]
- The drawn 10-K discloses a pending change in the parent that indirectly owns the
  advisor, property manager, and dealer manager: on March 5, 2013 Cole Credit Property
  Trust III, Inc. ("CCPT III"), Cole Holdings Corporation, CREInvestments, LLC, and
  Christopher H. Cole entered into a Holdings Merger Agreement, the consummation of which
  was subject to conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K notes]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Across recurring net-lease acquisitions (12 properties for $259.9 million in 2012; a
  13-property, ~3.2-million-square-foot single-tenant portfolio at year-end 2012), fee
  title to the real estate and its in-place net leases crossed into the registrant while
  the operating tenants stayed in place, and the acquisition, asset-management, and
  property-management/leasing processes and personnel were provided by affiliated Cole
  entities under advisory and property-management agreements rather than owned by the
  registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-13-000027, Form 10-K Items 1-2 + notes]
