## Reporting continuity and segment structure
- The registrant (CIK 1418135) continued filing periodic reports under its post-merger
  name; as of December 31, 2021 its operating structure comprised four reportable
  segments: Coffee Systems, Packaged Beverages, Beverage Concentrates, and Latin America
  Beverages. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- For fiscal year 2021, segment net sales were: Coffee Systems $4,716 million, Packaged
  Beverages $5,882 million, Beverage Concentrates $1,486 million, and Latin America
  Beverages $599 million, for total net sales of $12,683 million (versus $11,618 million
  in fiscal year 2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Best-documented realized financial scalar
- Net income attributable to the Company was $2,146 million for fiscal year 2021, versus
  $1,325 million for fiscal year 2020 and $1,254 million for fiscal year 2019 — an
  increase of $821 million, or approximately 62%, year over year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Total goodwill by reporting unit as of December 31, 2021 was: Coffee Systems $9,800
  million, Packaged Beverages $5,319 million, Beverage Concentrates $4,539 million, and
  Latin America Beverages $524 million, for a total of $20,182 million, essentially flat
  versus $20,184 million at December 31, 2020 (change attributed to foreign-currency
  translation, not impairment). [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- A non-cash impairment charge of $67 million tied to the Bai brand (an indefinite-lived
  intangible asset) was recorded in fiscal year 2020; no comparable brand or goodwill
  impairment was identified for fiscal year 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Documented post-merger integration program (process/organization interface)
- As part of the DPS Merger, the Company developed a restructuring/integration program
  (the "DPS Integration Program") targeting $600 million in synergies over a three-year
  period through supply chain optimization, reduction of indirect spend, elimination of
  duplicative support functions, and advertising/promotion optimization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The program was initially expected to complete in 2021, but the Company disclosed
  that, due to delays attributed to COVID-19, certain initiatives begun during the
  integration period would continue into 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The restructuring/integration program had produced cumulative pre-tax charges of
  approximately $790 million through December 31, 2021, consisting primarily of
  professional fees related to integration/transformation and costs associated with
  severance and employee terminations; the related workforce-reduction liability balance
  was $19 million at December 31, 2021 (versus $14 million at December 31, 2020), after
  $41 million of charges to expense and $36 million of cash payments during fiscal 2021.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Management attributed multiple periods of segment operating-income growth during
  fiscal 2021 in part to "the benefit of productivity and merger synergies" realized
  across the Coffee Systems, Packaged Beverages, Beverage Concentrates, and Latin
  America Beverages segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Items sought but not separately disclosed
- The FY2021 Form 10-K does not separately restate net sales, income, or a stand-alone
  balance sheet for the pre-merger DPSG business in isolation from the combined entity;
  the segment structure mixes DPSG-legacy segments (Packaged Beverages, Beverage
  Concentrates, Latin America Beverages) with the Keurig-legacy Coffee Systems segment,
  so no source isolates a "DPSG-only" post-close trajectory distinct from the combined
  company's results. [UNVERIFIED]
- No divestiture, bankruptcy, or dissolution of any DPSG-legacy segment was identified
  in the reviewed filing for the ~2021 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year window, the surviving registrant (Keurig Dr Pepper Inc.,
  CIK 1418135) reported combined net income of $2,146 million for fiscal year 2021 (up
  approximately 62% year over year), retained all four legacy operating segments intact
  (including the two DPSG-legacy beverage segments and the DPSG-legacy Latin America
  Beverages segment) with essentially unimpaired segment goodwill, and disclosed an
  ongoing, originally three-year post-merger integration program targeting $600 million
  in synergies that had produced cumulative pre-tax integration/restructuring charges of
  approximately $790 million (primarily professional fees and severance/employee
  termination costs) and had run past its original 2021 completion target into 2022.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001418135-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
