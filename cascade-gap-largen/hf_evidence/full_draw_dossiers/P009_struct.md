# Structural sub-dossier — CONSOL Mining Corporation separation from CONSOL Energy Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- In December 2016 the parent, CONSOL Energy Inc. ("ParentCo"), announced its intention
  to separate into two independent, publicly traded companies: a coal company and a
  natural gas exploration and production (E&P) company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The coal company ("CoalCo") was held by CONSOL Mining Corporation, then a wholly owned
  subsidiary of ParentCo; it comprised the Pennsylvania Mining Complex (PAMC), the
  ownership interest in CNX Coal Resources LP (CNXC), the marine terminal at the Port of
  Baltimore, and undeveloped Northern/Central Appalachian and Illinois basin coal reserves
  (collectively, the Coal Business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transaction was structured as a spin-off: ParentCo planned to distribute all
  outstanding shares of CoalCo common stock on a pro rata basis to ParentCo stockholders,
  in a distribution intended to qualify as generally tax-free for U.S. federal income tax
  purposes; no vote of ParentCo stockholders was required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- In connection with the separation, CoalCo was to be renamed CONSOL Energy Inc. and to
  list its common stock on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The separation and distribution was completed November 28, 2017; ParentCo changed its
  name to CNX Resources Corporation (retaining the oil and natural gas E&P business),
  while the distributed coal company became CONSOL Energy Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001710366-18-000006, Form 10-K FY2017]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product / asset: the coal-production operations transferred — the PAMC,
  consisting of three underground mines (Bailey, Enlow Fork, Harvey) with an annual
  production capacity of 28.5 million tons and controlling approximately 767 million tons
  of proven and probable reserves (as of December 31, 2016), plus the CNX Marine Terminal
  at the Port of Baltimore (15 million tons/year throughput). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operational processes: the mining and delivery operations that produce and ship the coal
  (the longwalls and continuous-miner sections of the PAMC, the marine terminal) moved with
  the product as an operating unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / management: all CoalCo executive officers were, at the time, executive officers
  and employees of ParentCo (some also serving as officers of CNXC's general partner);
  after the separation the individuals would no longer be employed by ParentCo, forming the
  standalone company's management team, led by James Brock as Chief Executive Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 "Executive Officers Following the Distribution"]
- Shared corporate substrate that did NOT transfer intact: the business's cash needs had
  historically been satisfied as part of ParentCo's corporate-wide cash management
  policies, and the operations had been included in the Parent's consolidated tax and
  treasury arrangements — functions the standalone company would have to establish or
  procure separately. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- To effect the separation and frame the ongoing relationship, the parties entered into a
  separation and distribution agreement ("SDA") together with a transition services
  agreement ("TSA"), a tax matters agreement, an employee matters agreement, an
  intellectual property matters agreement, and a master cooperation and safety agreement,
  among others. [SRC: SEC accession 0001710366-18-000006, Form 10-K FY2017]
- These agreements provided for the allocation between the coal company and the retained
  gas company of ParentCo's assets, employees, liabilities and obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225493, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The former parent (CNX) had no obligation to provide the standalone company with
  assistance other than the transition services outlined in the transition services
  agreement, along with such other arrangements as were contractually agreed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001710366-18-000006, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction detached a coal-production operation (PAMC mines and the CNX Marine
  Terminal) from ParentCo as a self-contained operating unit — product, producing/delivering
  processes, and the management team crossing together — while the shared corporate
  substrate (cash management, tax, treasury, IT and other functions) was bridged for a
  transitional period by the transition services agreement and related transaction
  agreements rather than transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001710366-18-000006, Form 10-K FY2017]
