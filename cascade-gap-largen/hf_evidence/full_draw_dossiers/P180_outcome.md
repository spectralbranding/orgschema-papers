## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced, non-financial)
- On April 22, 2022, the company (then operating as ClubCorp) announced it was
  rebranding as "Invited," with the rollout beginning in the following months; the
  company described its portfolio at that time as 161 owned and operated golf and
  country clubs, 205 golf courses, 1,000+ tennis and pickleball courts, 32 city and
  sports clubs, seven stadium clubs, and six BigShots Golf locations, operating across
  27 states, with over 430,000 members and approximately 20,000 peak-season employees. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/4/22/2427468/0/en/ClubCorp-Announces-Company-Rebrand-Welcome-To-Invited.html]
- The individual identified as the company's Chief Executive Officer in the April 2022
  rebrand announcement, David Pillsbury, differs from the individual identified as Chief
  Executive Officer (Eric L. Affeldt) in the 2017 closing-era proxy statement; the press
  release does not state the date or circumstances of any CEO transition. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/4/22/2427468/0/en/ClubCorp-Announces-Company-Rebrand-Welcome-To-Invited.html]
- The 2022 announcement does not reference the 2017 Apollo acquisition, does not report
  any divestiture, bankruptcy, or dissolution, and reports no financial figures beyond
  employee counts. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/4/22/2427468/0/en/ClubCorp-Announces-Company-Rebrand-Welcome-To-Invited.html]
