# Structural sub-dossier — AV Homes, Inc. acquisition by Taylor Morrison Home Corporation (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 7, 2018, AV Homes, Inc. ("AV Homes") entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger with Taylor Morrison Home Corporation ("Taylor Morrison"), Taylor Morrison
  Communities, Inc. ("Intermediate Parent"), and Thor Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"),
  under which Merger Sub merged with and into AV Homes, with AV Homes continuing as the
  surviving entity and becoming an indirect subsidiary of Taylor Morrison. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]
- AV Homes, a Delaware corporation, is a homebuilder and land developer of residential
  communities in Florida, the Carolinas, Arizona and Texas, focused on primary
  residential communities for first-time and move-up buyers as well as "active adult"
  communities restricted to residents 55 and older, typically including resort-style
  amenities such as clubhouses, swimming pools and fitness centers; its major active
  adult communities included Solivita (Central Florida), Vitalia at Tradition (Port St.
  Lucie, FL), CantaMia (Goodyear, AZ), Encore at Eastmark (Mesa, AZ) and Creekside at
  Bethpage (Raleigh/Durham). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]
- Taylor Morrison is a large public homebuilder and land developer operating under the
  Taylor Morrison and Darling Homes brand names; the AV Homes board considered that the
  resulting combined company would be the sixth largest homebuilder in the United
  States based on closings and homebuilding revenue. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and legal-vehicle mechanics
- AV Homes stockholders could elect, per share, (i) $21.50 in cash, (ii) 0.9793 shares
  of Taylor Morrison Class A common stock, or (iii) $12.64 cash plus 0.4034 Taylor
  Morrison shares, subject to proration so the aggregate Merger Consideration consisted
  of approximately 58.8% cash and 41.2% Taylor Morrison stock; at closing (October 2,
  2018) Taylor Morrison paid approximately $280.40 million in cash and issued
  approximately 8.95 million Taylor Morrison shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-291002, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.01]
- AV Homes' certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended in their entirety to
  read as the certificate of incorporation and bylaws of Merger Sub (retaining only the
  AV Homes, Inc. name), and Taylor Morrison Communities, Inc. assumed AV Homes'
  obligations under its $400 million 6.625% Senior Notes due 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-291002, Form 8-K Items 1.01/5.03]
- A cash termination fee of $18,472,000 or $10,000,000 (depending on the underlying
  circumstances of termination) was specified under the Merger Agreement; TPG Aviator,
  L.P., holder of approximately 41% of outstanding AV Homes shares, agreed to vote its
  shares in favor of the Merger Agreement's adoption. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]
- Trading of AV Homes common stock on the Nasdaq was suspended on October 2, 2018 and
  Nasdaq filed a Form 25 that day to report removal of AV Homes common stock from
  listing; AV Homes then filed two Form 15-12B certifications terminating its Exchange
  Act registration, one on October 2, 2018 (6.00% Senior Convertible Notes due 2020,
  1 holder of record) and one on October 12, 2018 (common stock, 1 holder of record).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-291002, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-290453, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298493, Form 15-12B]

## What crossed at closing vs. what did not — people/organization tier
- At the Merger's effective time (October 2, 2018), all officers of AV Homes and all
  members of the AV Homes board of directors immediately prior to the Effective Time
  ceased to be officers and directors, respectively, of AV Homes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-291002, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Each AV Homes named executive officer held an employment agreement providing enhanced
  severance upon a qualifying termination (lump-sum cash severance, full equity-award
  vesting, extended health coverage); under a separate letter agreement, Taylor Morrison
  acknowledged each named executive officer would have "good reason" to resign upon
  consummation of the Merger and receive those severance benefits ("double-trigger"),
  while optionally providing up to three months of post-closing integration consulting
  services to Taylor Morrison if requested. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]
- For rank-and-file employees, the Merger Agreement provided that AV Homes employees
  would be immediately eligible to participate in Taylor Morrison benefit plans with
  prior AV Homes service credited for vesting/eligibility purposes, and that AV Homes
  was permitted to implement a retention program for certain employees; the reviewed
  filing does not disclose a transition-services or shared-services agreement bridging
  AV Homes and Taylor Morrison operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]

## Product/process continuity and integration disclosure
- At signing, the specific plan to integrate Taylor Morrison's and AV Homes' operations
  was described as "still being developed," with Taylor Morrison and AV Homes "currently
  in the process of assessing their respective businesses to determine where they may
  eliminate potential redundancies"; Taylor Morrison estimated Merger-related integration
  costs of approximately $70.0 million, expected primarily in the year ending December
  31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]
- The proxy's risk factors identified potential integration difficulties including "the
  integration of management teams, strategies, technologies and operations, products,"
  disruption and distraction of management, and "the integration of corporate cultures
  and maintenance of employee morale." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- AV Homes was acquired whole via reverse triangular merger into an indirect subsidiary
  of Taylor Morrison, with the customer-facing product (active-adult and primary
  residential communities) and the AV Homes legal vehicle (as surviving corporation)
  continuing, while the entire pre-closing officer and director slate departed at the
  effective time and the specific operational-integration plan remained undeveloped at
  signing, bridged only by individual executive consulting options and an employee
  retention-program allowance rather than a disclosed transition-services agreement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-258270, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-291002, Form 8-K]
