## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Westinghouse Air Brake Technologies Corporation ("Wabtec") continued to file annual
  reports on Form 10-K after the transaction closed on February 25, 2019; the report
  cited below is the Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2022, filed
  February 15, 2023 — approximately four years after closing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022, cover page]
- Wabtec's common stock remained registered and traded on the New York Stock Exchange
  under the symbol "WAB" as of this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675,
  Form 10-K FY2022, cover page]
- The FY2022 10-K lists Wabtec's principal executive offices at 30 Isabella Street,
  Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized segment-level financial record
- The FY2022 10-K states that Wabtec's 2019 acquisition of GE Transportation "brought a
  global technology leader and supplier of locomotives, equipment, services and digital
  solutions to the rail, mining, marine, stationary power and drilling industries into
  Wabtec," and that, as a result of this and other acquisitions plus organic growth,
  Wabtec is "now one of the world's largest providers of value-added, technology-based
  locomotives, equipment, systems and services for the global freight rail and
  passenger transit industries." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K
  FY2022]
- Wabtec reported approximately 27,000 employees (excluding contingent workers) and
  operations in over 50 countries as of the FY2022 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Freight Segment net sales were $5,239 million in 2021 and $6,012 million in 2022;
  Transit Segment net sales were $2,583 million in 2021 and $2,350 million in 2022;
  total company net sales were $7,822 million in 2021 and $8,362 million in 2022. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Consolidated net income attributable to Wabtec shareholders was $558 million in 2021
  and $633 million in 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The FY2022 10-K does not separately disaggregate GE Transportation's own post-close
  net sales or net income from the rest of the Freight Segment (which also includes
  Wabtec's pre-existing freight business and subsequent bolt-on acquisitions, e.g. the
  March 2021 acquisition of Nordco); an outcome scalar isolating GE Transportation alone
  is not available in this source and is therefore recorded as [UNVERIFIED].

## Goodwill and intangible-asset record (no impairment through the window)
- Goodwill by segment was: Freight Segment $6,872 million / Transit Segment $1,613
  million (total $8,485 million) at December 31, 2020; Freight Segment $7,073 million /
  Transit Segment $1,514 million (total $8,587 million) at December 31, 2021; and
  Freight Segment $7,085 million / Transit Segment $1,423 million (total $8,508
  million) at December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K
  FY2022]
- Wabtec has identified three goodwill reporting units — two within the Freight Segment
  and the Transit Segment itself — and its fiscal-2022 quantitative impairment testing
  concluded that the estimated fair value of all three reporting units, and of all
  material indefinite-lived trade names, exceeded their respective carrying values,
  resulting in a conclusion of no impairment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675,
  Form 10-K FY2022]
- Wabtec's trade names had a net carrying amount of $602 million at December 31, 2022
  (down from $635 million at December 31, 2021); the 10-K notes that these intangibles
  are treated as indefinite-lived "with the exception of the right to use the GE
  Transportation trade name," which is amortized over an original useful life of 5
  years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Continuity of the acquired legal vehicle and the GE relationship
- The FY2022 10-K identifies "GE Transportation, a Wabtec Company" as a distinct
  subsidiary guarantor of certain Wabtec notes, noting that it "has 15,000 shares
  outstanding of Class A Non-Voting Preferred Stock held by General Electric Company."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The 10-K reports a remaining "contingent consideration" payable to GE "resulting from
  the 2019 acquisition of GE Transportation," with the timing of cash payments tied to
  Wabtec's future realized tax benefits and described as subject to change. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- On January 1, 2022 — roughly three years after closing — Wabtec completed an internal
  legal-entity reorganization that changed which subsidiaries and operating divisions
  serve as guarantors under its U.S. notes, requiring reclassification of certain prior
  year amounts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Leadership succession at the controlling-intent tier
- Rafael Santana — president and CEO of GE Transportation since November 2017 — is
  reported in the FY2022 10-K as having served as Wabtec's Executive Vice President
  from February 2019 to July 2019 and as having been named President and Chief
  Executive Officer of Wabtec effective July 1, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Post-close restructuring initiative (process/organization interface)
- In the first quarter of 2022, Wabtec announced "Integration 2.0," described as a
  three-year strategic initiative targeting incremental run-rate synergies of $75-90
  million by 2025 through consolidating the operating footprint, reducing headcount,
  streamlining manufacturing, restructuring North America distribution channels,
  expanding operations in low-cost countries, and systems enablement; Wabtec estimated
  one-time charges of approximately $135-165 million for the initiative and reported
  incurring approximately $46 million of such charges during 2022, primarily for
  employee-related costs from European site consolidations and North America
  distribution-channel restructuring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form
  10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Wabtec remained the continuing NYSE-listed public filer through the ~2021-2023
  outcome window, with the former GE Transportation business organized into and
  reported within its Freight Segment; segment net sales and consolidated net income
  both grew from 2021 to 2022, and goodwill associated with the acquisitions (including
  GE Transportation) was tested and found not impaired as of the FY2022 filing. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Documented developments within the window include a mid-2019 change in Wabtec's
  overall chief executive (the former GE Transportation CEO becoming Wabtec's CEO), a
  January 2022 internal legal-entity reorganization affecting note-guarantor
  subsidiaries, and a Q1 2022 multi-year restructuring initiative targeting headcount,
  manufacturing footprint, and distribution-channel changes with material one-time
  charges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-003675, Form 10-K FY2022]
- A GE Transportation-specific financial outcome scalar, isolated from the rest of the
  Freight Segment, was not found in this source and is coded [UNVERIFIED]; the
  Freight-Segment-level scalars above are the best-documented realized record
  available.
