## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- On June 25, 2019, the company (still controlled by affiliates of funds managed by
  Apollo Global Management, LLC) announced a rebrand from "West Corporation" to
  "Intrado," reorganizing its previously reported segment structure into four newly
  named units: Life & Safety, Enterprise Collaboration, Digital Media, and Health &
  Wellness. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2019/06/25/1874053/0/en/West-Corporation-Announces-Rebrand-to-Intrado.html]
- On September 16, 2022 (within the ~3-5-year window), Intrado Corporation — described
  in the release as "controlled by affiliates of certain funds managed by Apollo Global
  Management, Inc. (NYSE: APO)" — announced a definitive agreement for Stonepeak-advised
  investment funds to acquire Intrado's Safety business (its public emergency
  telecommunications/911-infrastructure operations) for $2.4 billion, with Intrado
  retaining its separate Digital Workflows segment; closing was expected in the first
  quarter of 2023. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/09/16/2517685/25418/en/Stonepeak-to-Acquire-Safety-Business-from-Intrado.html]
- On November 15, 2022 (at the outer edge of / just beyond the ~5-year window), Intrado
  Corporation announced that its holding company was being rebranded again, to "West
  Technology Group, LLC," with the Intrado brand name itself transitioning to the Safety
  business ahead of its divestiture; the announcement listed the group's remaining
  operating businesses as Notified, Mosaicx, TeleVox, SchoolMessenger, West Utilities,
  and West Telecom, plus the Safety business pending sale to Stonepeak, and again
  described the holding company as controlled by affiliates of Apollo Global Management
  funds. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2022/11/15/2556638/25418/en/Intrado-Corporation-Announces-Holding-Company-Rebrand-to-West-Technology-Group-LLC.html]
