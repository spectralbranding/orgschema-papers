## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced, within window)
- On February 3, 2023, the company (still operating and communicating publicly as
  "Calpine") announced it was commencing development efforts for a new approximately
  425 MW peaking facility adjacent to its existing Freestone Energy Center in Texas,
  citing a state regulatory framework (a Performance Credit Mechanism) adopted to
  incent new generation. [SRC: https://www.calpine.com/calpine-announces-initial-development-for-new-facility-at-freestone-energy-center/]
- On December 14, 2023, Texas Governor Greg Abbott and ERCOT's CEO visited Calpine's
  Houston headquarters as Calpine CEO Thad Hill announced the company's decision to
  proceed with advanced development and construction of that new 425-megawatt
  simple-cycle gas-fired plant adjacent to the existing 1,063-megawatt Freestone
  combined-cycle facility, targeted to be operational before summer 2026. [SRC: https://www.calpine.com/governor-abbott-joins-calpine-for-announcement-on-texas-power-investment/]
- The December 2023 announcement identifies Thad Hill as Calpine's CEO at that date —
  the same individual whose employment agreement with Calpine (entered May 2017) is
  discussed in the closing-era merger proxy — indicating continuity of that executive
  role through at least the end of the outcome window. [SRC: https://www.calpine.com/governor-abbott-joins-calpine-for-announcement-on-texas-power-investment/]
