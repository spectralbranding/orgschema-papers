## Acquirer's disclosure naming the acquired business (Simmons First National Corporation)
- Simmons' Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 25, 2022) states: "Also in
  October 2017, we completed the acquisition of Southwest Bancorp, Inc. ('OKSB'),
  including its wholly-owned bank subsidiary, Bank SNB. Headquartered in Stillwater,
  Oklahoma, OKSB provided us with $2.7 billion in assets, allowed us additional entry
  into the Oklahoma, Texas and Colorado banking markets, and strengthened our Kansas
  franchise and our product offerings in the healthcare and real estate industries. The
  systems conversion was completed in May 2018, at which time Bank SNB was merged into
  Simmons Bank." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003933, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The identical passage (same wording, same May 2018 systems-conversion/charter-merger
  date) appears in Simmons' Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 25, 2021),
  indicating no further business-specific update to this disclosure was made in the
  10-Ks bracketing the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003318, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The disclosed systems-conversion/bank-charter-merger completion date (May 2018)
  precedes the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window; it is the most recent acquirer-disclosed
  milestone specifically naming the acquired business located in the periodic filings
  covering the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003933, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Segment/financial-scalar disclosure (feasibility-relevant)
- Simmons describes itself in its FY2021 Form 10-K as operating banking as a single
  reportable operating segment (with trust, investment, and insurance services
  considered separate operating segments that do not meet quantitative thresholds for
  separate reporting); no OKSB- or Bank SNB-specific line item, reporting unit, or
  segment financial disclosure is broken out separately from consolidated results.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003933, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Company-wide (not business-specific) goodwill totaled $1.15 billion at December 31,
  2021 and $1.08 billion at December 31, 2020; Simmons states that goodwill impairment
  "was neither indicated nor recorded in 2021, 2020 or 2019," including in interim
  goodwill-impairment assessments it performed each quarter of 2020 in response to a
  share-price decline associated with the COVID-19 pandemic. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003933, Form 10-K FY2021]
- This goodwill/no-impairment disclosure is enterprise-wide across all of Simmons'
  acquisitions (including OKSB, First Texas, and later acquisitions such as Reliance
  Bancshares) and is not isolated to, or separately attributed to, the former OKSB/Bank
  SNB business specifically. [UNVERIFIED as an OKSB-specific scalar]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Southwest Bancorp, Inc. deregistered from SEC reporting via Form 15-12B on October 30,
  2017, and no subsequent target-specific periodic report exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-324968, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer, Simmons First National Corporation, continues as an SEC reporting
  company and does name the acquired business in its subsequent 10-Ks, disclosing that
  Bank SNB's systems conversion and bank-charter merger into Simmons Bank were completed
  in May 2018 — a milestone that predates the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window — but
  provides no segment- or entity-level financial trajectory specific to the former OKSB
  business within the window itself, consistent with Simmons' single-reportable-segment
  structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003933, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003318, Form 10-K FY2020]
