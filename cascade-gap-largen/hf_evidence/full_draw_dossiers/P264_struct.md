# Structural sub-dossier — Monogram Residential Trust, Inc. acquisition (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Monogram Residential Trust, Inc., a Maryland corporation, is described as "a fully
  integrated self-managed real estate investment trust" ("REIT") that invests in,
  develops and operates multifamily communities; as of June 30, 2017 its portfolio
  comprised investments in 48 multifamily communities in ten states comprising 13,438
  residential units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]
- Under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 4, 2017, Monogram merged with and
  into Acquisition Sub (GS Monarch Acquisition, LLC), a Delaware limited liability
  company formed solely for the transaction, with Acquisition Sub surviving as a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Parent (GS Monarch Parent, LLC). Parent and Acquisition Sub were
  formed by a private investment group led by Greystar Real Estate Partners and
  including affiliates of APG, GIC, and Ivanhoe Cambridge via a newly formed investment
  fund, Greystar Growth and Income Fund, LP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share of Monogram common stock (other than shares held by Parent,
  Acquisition Sub, or their affiliates) was converted into the right to receive $12.00
  in cash, without interest. The merger became effective September 19, 2017; the total
  portfolio value of the merger and related transactions was approximately $4.4 billion,
  funded through equity contributions from the Greystar-led investment group and debt
  financing, and included the value of the institutional joint venture with Stichting
  Depositary PGGM Private Real Estate Fund ("PGGM"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-057909, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing asset/product — the 48-community, 13,438-unit multifamily
  portfolio and the operating REIT vehicle itself — crossed whole into a wholly owned
  subsidiary of the Greystar-led Parent via statutory merger; the separate corporate
  existence of Monogram ceased and Acquisition Sub (as successor by merger) continued
  as the surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]
- The controlling intent/imprint of the pre-transaction organization did NOT continue
  unchanged: at the effective time, all seven of Monogram's directors resigned from the
  board and all board committees, and all of Monogram's named officers (Chief Executive
  Officer/President/COO and other executive officers) resigned from their respective
  offices; the directors and officers of Acquisition Sub became the directors and
  officers of the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-057909, Form 8-K]
- The merger agreement separately provided that Parent would cause the surviving entity
  to assume and honor all deferred compensation, severance, retention, incentive, and
  change-in-control agreements applicable to employees of the Company or its
  subsidiaries in effect immediately before the effective time, and that Greystar or an
  affiliate would pay 2017 annual bonuses at 100% of target and earned-but-unpaid 2017
  commissions for such employees; separately, for health and welfare benefit plans
  maintained by Greystar or its affiliates for "continuing employees," Parent would
  waive eligibility waiting periods and pre-existing-condition limitations — indicating a
  subset of Monogram's operating workforce, distinct from the departing named officers
  and directors, was contemplated to continue as employees under the acquirer's benefit
  structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]
- Monogram's executive officers were parties to pre-existing severance agreements
  providing benefits upon certain qualifying terminations in connection with the merger;
  golden-parachute compensation payable to the named executive officers (Chief Executive
  Officer/President/COO, EVP & CFO, SVP-Planning/CAO/Treasurer, EVP-Property Management,
  and SVP & Chief Development Officer) was separately disclosed and was contractually
  payable upon completion of the merger regardless of the outcome of the advisory
  stockholder vote on that compensation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- In connection with the merger, Parent and its affiliates entered separate agreements
  with Monogram's two institutional co-investment joint-venture partners: the PGGM
  joint venture was to be restructured concurrently with closing, and the joint-venture
  interests held by Milky Way Partners, L.P. (the NPS-affiliated partner) were to be
  purchased by Parent under a separate assignable purchase and sale agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]
- A private investment group led by Greystar and including affiliates of APG, GIC, and
  Ivanhoe Cambridge (the "Investors/Guarantors") executed an equity commitment letter
  dated July 4, 2017 to capitalize Parent, and executed a limited guarantee in favor of
  the Company guaranteeing, on a several basis, Parent's payment obligations under the
  merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A]

## Public-market status change contracted at closing
- The merger agreement obligated the Company, prior to the effective time, to use
  commercially reasonable efforts to cause Monogram common stock to be removed from
  listing on the New York Stock Exchange and to cause the shares to be deregistered
  under the Exchange Act as promptly as practicable after the effective time; on
  September 19, 2017 Monogram notified the NYSE that the merger was completed and its
  common stock ceased trading on the NYSE effective September 20, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-057909, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, cash-out statutory merger in which the entire
  operating REIT (portfolio, legal vehicle, and joint-venture interests) crossed into a
  wholly owned subsidiary of a newly formed, private-investment-group-controlled Parent;
  the pre-transaction board and named executive officers departed at the effective time
  and were replaced by Acquisition Sub's own directors/officers, while a defined
  "continuing employee" population was contractually bridged into the acquirer's benefit
  and compensation arrangements, and outstanding institutional joint-venture interests
  were separately restructured or purchased concurrent with closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005183, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-057909, Form 8-K]
