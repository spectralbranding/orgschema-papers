# Outcome sub-dossier — CA, Inc. business under Broadcom Inc. ownership, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. CA, Inc. terminated its SEC registration shortly
> after the transaction closed; there is no subsequent CA, Inc. 10-K. The realized record
> below is traced through the acquirer's (Broadcom Inc.) own SEC filings, which are the
> only public source that continues to reference the acquired business over time. This

> closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- CA, Inc. filed a Form 15-12B on November 15, 2018, certifying and giving notice of
  termination of registration of its common stock and several series of notes under the
  Securities Exchange Act of 1934; the filing lists the approximate number of holders of
  record of CA common stock as 1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-327495, Form 15-12B]
- No further annual report (Form 10-K) or other periodic report was filed by CA, Inc.
  after this deregistration; no CA-specific audited public financial statements exist for
  a ~2021-2023 outcome window under the CA, Inc. registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-327495, Form 15-12B]

## Realized record as disclosed by the acquirer (Broadcom Inc.)
- Broadcom completed its acquisition of CA on November 5, 2018 (the "CA Acquisition
  Date"), for total purchase consideration of $18,844 million (comprising $18,402 million
  cash for CA common stock, $274 million to retire CA's term loan, $101 million for
  vested CA equity awards, and $67 million fair value of assumed partially vested equity
  awards), or $16,094 million net of cash acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
- Broadcom's purchase-price allocation assigned $9,796 million to goodwill (described as
  "primarily attributable to the assembled workforce and anticipated synergies and
  economies of scale expected from the integration of the CA business") and $12,045
  million to identified intangible assets, including developed technology ($4,957
  million, 6-year weighted-average amortization), customer contracts and related
  relationships ($4,190 million, 6 years), order backlog ($2,569 million, 3 years), and
  trade name and other ($137 million, 5 years). [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
- On December 31, 2018, Broadcom sold Veracode, Inc. — a CA subsidiary providing
  application security testing solutions that had been classified as held-for-sale and
  which Broadcom states "was not aligned with our strategic objectives" — to Thoma Bravo,
  LLC for cash consideration of $950 million, before working capital adjustments; Broadcom
  states it has no material continuing involvement with the business and has presented
  its results in discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
- Broadcom states that its results of continuing operations for fiscal year 2019 included
  $3,377 million of net revenue attributable to CA, but that "it was impracticable to
  determine the effect on net income attributable to CA as we had integrated a
  substantial portion of CA into our ongoing operations during the year." [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
- Broadcom states that the results of operations of CA were included in its
  "Infrastructure Software" reportable segment, the same segment into which Broadcom
  subsequently folded the Symantec Enterprise Security business acquired in November
  2019; Broadcom's segment and goodwill disclosures do not break out CA-specific figures
  separately from this combined segment in the fiscal-year-2021 filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
- Broadcom recorded $73 million of CA-Merger-related transaction costs within selling,
  general and administrative expense for fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because CA, Inc. deregistered from SEC reporting within weeks of closing, there is no
  CA-specific public 10-K record for the ~2021-2023 window; the traceable realized record
  is limited to what the acquirer, Broadcom, chose to disclose in its own periodic
  filings: a one-time fiscal-year-2019 net-revenue figure attributable to CA accompanied
  by Broadcom's own statement that determining CA's net-income effect had become
  impracticable due to integration, a near-immediate post-closing divestiture of CA's
  Veracode subsidiary as strategically non-aligned, and the absorption of CA's remaining
  operations into a combined multi-acquisition "Infrastructure Software" segment without
  further separate CA breakout in the fiscal-year-2021 filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001730168-21-000153, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2021)]
