# Outcome sub-dossier — realized record of the separated La Quinta franchise business (Wyndham Hotels & Resorts) and spun-off real estate (CorePoint Lodging), ~3-5-year horizon (~2021-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The transaction produced two separate public
> filers after closing (Wyndham Hotels & Resorts, Inc., which received the La Quinta
> management/franchise business, and CorePoint Lodging Inc., which received the real
> estate); this dossier draws on each entity's own later disclosure of the relevant
> business. It contains no information about how the transaction was structured at
> closing.

## Public-reporting status of each successor entity
- The La Quinta management/franchise business became part of Wyndham Hotels &
  Resorts, Inc. (CIK 0001722684), a separate public filer that continued to file
  annual reports on Form 10-K through the outcome window, including a Form 10-K for
  fiscal year 2021 filed 2022-02-16. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722684-22-000005, Form 10-K]
- CorePoint Lodging Inc. (CIK 0001707178), which received La Quinta's owned real
  estate, filed annual reports on Form 10-K through fiscal year 2020 (filed
  2021-03-12); this was its last annual report before it was itself taken private.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- On March 3, 2022, CorePoint completed a going-private merger (pursuant to a Merger
  Agreement dated November 6, 2021) with an affiliate of Cavalier Acquisition (an
  entity associated with Highgate), converting each share of CorePoint common stock
  into the right to receive $15.99 in cash; CorePoint requested removal of its common
  stock from listing on the NYSE and intended to deregister via Form 15.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-063995, Form 8-K filed 2022-03-03]

## Realized record of the retained management/franchise business (Wyndham Hotels & Resorts)
- As of the FY2021 10-K, the La Quinta brand (within Wyndham Hotels & Resorts'
  Midscale segment) comprised 922 properties (906 in the U.S.) and 90,098 rooms, with
  a reported average daily rate of $56.37 — modestly larger than the ~890
  properties/~87,500 rooms La Quinta reported at the 2018 closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722684-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- In November 2021, Wyndham Hotels & Resorts agreed to terminate all remaining
  hotel-management agreements with CorePoint effective at the closing of CorePoint's
  sale to Highgate (expected Q1 2022), in exchange for a termination fee to Wyndham
  of approximately $84 million; the corresponding hotel franchise agreements with the
  CorePoint hotels were stated to remain in place and be assumed by a Highgate
  subsidiary on their existing terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722684-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Realized record of the spun-off real estate business (CorePoint Lodging)
- For fiscal year 2020, CorePoint reported a net loss of $178 million ($3.14 per
  fully diluted share), including a non-cash impairment charge of $54 million,
  compared with a net loss of $212 million ($3.71 per fully diluted share, including
  a $141 million impairment charge) for fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- CorePoint's FY2020 10-K attributes disruption to the COVID-19 pandemic: proactive
  cost-containment measures (reduced staffing, eliminated non-essential amenities,
  deferred capital investment, suspended dividends, and a $110 million precautionary
  draw on its Revolving Credit Facility), and states that by December 2020 the
  portfolio had reached over 75% of pre-pandemic December-2019 occupancy levels.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- During 2020, CorePoint sold 61 hotels for gross proceeds of $274 million (a gain on
  sale of $71 million) as part of a stated non-core hotel disposition strategy, and
  reduced its CMBS Facility debt by $196 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Throughout FY2020, LQ Management L.L.C. ("LQM"), a subsidiary of Wyndham, remained
  the manager of all of CorePoint's hotel properties under the May 30, 2018 hotel
  management agreements, charging a management fee of 5% of total gross revenues
  ($20 million for FY2020); the agreements' stated term ran through May 30, 2038
  (subject to two five-year renewal options at LQM's discretion). [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Termination of the management-tier bridge at the close of the outcome window
- On March 3, 2022 — coincident with CorePoint's going-private sale — the hotel
  management agreements dated May 30, 2018 between CorePoint TRS L.L.C. and LQ
  Management L.L.C. were terminated, and CorePoint paid LQ Management approximately
  $85 million in termination fees in connection with that termination.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-063995, Form 8-K filed 2022-03-03]
- The same March 3, 2022 filing also records that CorePoint's Credit Agreement and
  CMBS Loan Agreement (both dated May 30, 2018) were repaid in full and terminated,
  and that the Stockholders' Agreement and Registration Rights Agreement with
  Blackstone affiliates (also dated May 30, 2018) were terminated, without material
  early-termination penalties on the debt instruments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-063995, Form 8-K filed 2022-03-03]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2021-2022 outcome window, the two successor businesses show
  different trajectories: the retained management/franchise business (Wyndham Hotels
  & Resorts) reported the La Quinta brand at modestly larger scale than at spin-off
  (922 vs. ~890 properties) [SRC: SEC accession 0001722684-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]; the spun-off real estate
  business (CorePoint) recorded multi-year net losses and impairment charges
  (FY2020: $178 million net loss with $54 million impairment; FY2019: $212 million
  net loss with $141 million impairment), executed a non-core disposition program
  (61 hotels sold in 2020), and was itself sold in a going-private transaction on
  March 3, 2022 — approximately 3.75 years after the original 2018 closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001707178-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-063995, Form 8-K filed 2022-03-03]
- The contractual bridge connecting the two separated tiers — the hotel-management
  agreements between the Wyndham-owned manager (LQM) and the CorePoint-owned real
  estate — persisted for the bulk of the outcome window before being terminated
  concurrently with CorePoint's 2022 sale, for a termination fee of approximately
  $84-85 million (the two filings report closely matching but not identical
  figures); the corresponding hotel-franchise agreements (governing the La Quinta
  brand standards) were, per Wyndham's own disclosure, retained and assumed by the
  new owner rather than terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722684-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-063995, Form 8-K filed 2022-03-03]
