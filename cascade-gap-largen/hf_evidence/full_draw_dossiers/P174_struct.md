## Transaction and parties at closing
- On May 15, 2016, Breitburn Energy Partners LP (the Partnership) and certain of its
  affiliates filed voluntary petitions for relief under chapter 11 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York,
  jointly administered as In re Breitburn Energy Partners LP, et al., Case No. 16-11390;
  no trustee was appointed and the Partnership continued to manage itself and operate as
  debtor in possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-16-000334, Form 8-K Item 1.03, filed 2016-05-16]
- The Chapter 11 filing was an event of default accelerating obligations under the
  Partnership's pre-petition debt: approximately $1.197 billion outstanding plus $45.3
  million of undrawn letters of credit under its Third Amended and Restated Credit
  Agreement; $650 million of 9.25% Senior Secured Second Lien Notes due 2020; $305
  million of 8.625% Senior Notes due 2020; and $850 million of 7.875% Senior Notes due
  2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-16-000334, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On May 19, 2016 the Partnership's wholly owned subsidiary Breitburn Operating LP
  entered a Debtor-in-Possession Credit Facility with Wells Fargo Bank as administrative
  agent, providing a revolving facility of up to $75 million (with an offer to arrange
  an additional $75 million), maturing on the earlier of a plan of reorganization's
  effective date or January 15, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-16-000348, Form 8-K Item 1.01, filed 2016-05-26]
- On March 13, 2018 the Debtors filed their Third Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan (With
  Technical Modifications), which superseded an Initial Plan filed October 11, 2017 and
  amended November 28 and December 1, 2017; on March 26, 2018 the Bankruptcy Court
  entered an order confirming the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03, filed 2018-03-28]
- Press reporting states the Plan became effective April 6, 2018, at which point the
  reorganized business began operating as a newly formed entity, Maverick Natural
  Resources LLC, described as majority owned and controlled by funds and accounts
  managed by EIG Global Energy Partners. [SRC: https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/companies/article/17297280/breitburn-emerges-from-chapter-11-as-maverick-natural-resources]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- Legal vehicle: the pre-petition legal vehicle was a Delaware limited partnership
  (Breitburn Energy Partners LP) managed by its general partner, Breitburn GP LLC. The
  Plan replaced this vehicle with two newly formed entities effective on the Plan
  Effective Date: a limited liability company ("LegacyCo") that would own all of the
  Debtors' assets other than assets located in the Permian Basin, and a newly formed
  corporation ("New Permian Corp.") that would acquire all equity of a newly formed LLC
  owning the Permian assets and would also hold 7.5% of LegacyCo's equity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Controlling intent / equity: under the Plan, holders of the Second Lien Notes (allowed
  claims of approximately $793 million including accrued interest) received a pro rata
  share of 92.5% of LegacyCo's equity; holders of Senior Unsecured Notes that were
  "eligible offerees" received rights to purchase shares of New Permian Corp. through a
  $465 million rights offering, backstopped by an ad hoc group of noteholders under a
  Backstop Commitment Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Partnership's common and preferred unitholders received no distribution or
  consideration on account of their equity interests, and all such units were cancelled
  on the Plan Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On March 29, 2018 the Partnership filed a Form 15 to terminate registration of its
  Common Units (approximately 152 holders of record) and to suspend its duty to file
  reports under the Exchange Act, stating it did not expect to make further SEC filings.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000015, Form 15-12B, filed 2018-03-29]
- Customer-facing product/asset (the underlying crude oil and natural gas production and
  reserves): the Plan allocated these assets between the two successor entities along a
  Permian / non-Permian geographic line rather than transferring them to a single
  successor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Press reporting on the reorganized business as it began operating states production
  averaging 39,742 boe/d, estimated proved reserves of 152.2 million boe (97% proved
  developed), and post-emergence debt of approximately $105 million with $295 million of
  additional borrowing capacity under a new bank credit facility — substantially reduced
  from the pre-petition debt levels described above. [SRC: https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/companies/article/17297280/breitburn-emerges-from-chapter-11-as-maverick-natural-resources]
- People / management / organization: no trustee was appointed during the case; the
  Partnership stated it would "continue to manage itself and its affiliates and operate
  their businesses as debtors in possession." [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-16-000334, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Whether the pre-petition management team continued in the same roles at the
  reorganized entity(ies) after the Plan Effective Date is [UNVERIFIED] in the sources
  reviewed.

## Contractual arrangements bridging shared substrate
- A separate, unrelated Omnibus Agreement (dated August 26, 2008) between Pacific Coast
  Energy Company LP and its affiliates and the "Breitburn Parties," which had allocated
  certain business-opportunity rights, was terminated effective June 7, 2017 — before
  the Plan's confirmation — following the June 30, 2016 termination of the parties'
  Administrative Services Agreement; no early termination penalties were incurred. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-17-000020, Form 8-K Item 1.02, filed 2017-06-08]
- The confirmed Plan and Confirmation Order did not, in the sources reviewed, describe a
  transition-services or shared-services agreement bridging operations between the
  Partnership and the two successor entities (LegacyCo / New Permian Corp.); the summary
  of Plan terms in the 8-K addresses claims treatment and equity allocation but no such
  bridging agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a chapter 11 restructuring under which the Debtors' existing legal
  vehicle (the publicly traded limited partnership) was replaced at the Plan Effective
  Date by two newly formed, non-publicly-registered entities dividing the underlying oil
  and gas assets along a Permian / non-Permian line; pre-petition secured and unsecured
  creditors received newly issued equity in the successor entities in varying
  proportions, while existing common and preferred unitholders received no distribution
  and had their units cancelled; the Partnership deregistered its units with the SEC
  within days of Plan confirmation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.03] [SRC: SEC accession 0001357371-18-000015, Form 15-12B]
