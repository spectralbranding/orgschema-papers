## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant remained an SEC-reporting public company throughout the window and filed an annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended October 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended October 31, 2020)]

## Realized financial trajectory (fiscal 2018–2020)
- Total net revenue declined across the window: $30,852 million in fiscal 2018, $29,135 million in fiscal 2019 and $26,982 million in fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Earnings]
- The registrant recorded a net loss of $(322) million in fiscal 2020, versus net earnings of $1,049 million in fiscal 2019 and $1,908 million in fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Earnings]
- Earnings (loss) from continuing operations were $(322) million in fiscal 2020, $1,049 million in fiscal 2019 and $2,012 million in fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020, Consolidated Statements of Earnings]
- The fiscal 2020 result included a goodwill impairment charge of $865 million recorded in the second quarter of fiscal 2020, associated with the HPC & MCS reporting unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Workforce and operating realignment in the window
- The registrant reported approximately 59,400 employees as of October 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Under the HPE Next initiative the registrant reduced the number of countries in which it had a direct sales presence, migrating to a channel-only model in the remainder. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]
- During the third quarter of fiscal 2020 the registrant launched a Cost Optimization and Prioritization Plan, running through fiscal 2023, to realign its cost structure and workforce toward areas of growth. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020, Note 4 "Transformation Programs"]
- The registrant identified the COVID-19 pandemic as affecting its business, operations, liquidity, employees, customers, partners and supply chain during fiscal 2020, and cited manufacturing/supply-capacity constraints in North America in the first quarter of fiscal 2020 among the drivers of lower revenue. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Prior portfolio changes referenced in the outcome filing (pre-window context, not the outcome scalar)
- The registrant's FY2020 10-K states that on April 1, 2017 it completed the separation and merger of its Enterprise Services business with DXC Technology Company (the "Everett Transaction"), and on September 1, 2017 it completed the separation and merger of its Software business segment with Micro Focus International plc (the "Seattle Transaction"). Both transactions were completed in fiscal 2017, before the fixed outcome window, and are recorded here only as context for the composition of the entity whose in-window financials are reported above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the fixed ~2018–2020 window the registrant's total net revenue declined from $30,852 million (fiscal 2018) to $26,982 million (fiscal 2020) and its bottom line moved from net earnings of $1,908 million (fiscal 2018) to a net loss of $(322) million (fiscal 2020), the latter including an $865 million goodwill impairment charge; over the same period the workforce stood at approximately 59,400 as of October 31, 2020, and the registrant pursued multiple transformation programs (HPE Next; the Cost Optimization and Prioritization Plan launched in fiscal 2020) to realign its cost structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001645590-20-000056, Form 10-K FY2020]
