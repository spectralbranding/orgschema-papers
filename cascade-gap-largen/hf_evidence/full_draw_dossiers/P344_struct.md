# Structural sub-dossier — Envision Healthcare Corporation acquisition by an affiliate of Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties

- On June 10, 2018, Envision Healthcare Corporation ("Envision" or the "Company"), a
  Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Enterprise
  Parent Holdings Inc. ("Parent") and Enterprise Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub"), an
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, providing for Merger Sub to merge with and
  into Envision, with Envision surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Parent and Merger Sub were both newly formed Delaware corporations, affiliates of
  investment funds affiliated with Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. ("KKR"), each
  "formed solely for the purpose of entering into the merger agreement and consummating
  the transactions contemplated by the merger agreement," with no prior operating
  activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "The Companies"]
- At signing, Envision described itself as "a leading provider of physician-led
  services, post-acute care, and ambulatory surgery services," delivering physician
  services (emergency department, hospitalist, anesthesiology, radiology/tele-radiology,
  children's services) to more than 1,800 clinical departments in 45 states and the
  District of Columbia, and owning/operating 261 surgery centers and one surgical
  hospital in 35 states and the District of Columbia. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "The Companies — Envision Healthcare Corporation"]
- Each outstanding share of Envision common stock (other than certain excluded shares)
  would be converted into the right to receive $46.00 in cash, without interest —
  representing approximately a 32% premium over the volume-weighted average share price
  from November 1, 2017 (the day after Envision first announced it was reviewing
  strategic alternatives) to the date of the merger agreement — a whole-company, all-cash
  going-private acquisition structured as a reverse triangular merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A cover letter]
- If completed, Envision common stock would be removed from listing on the NYSE Global
  Select Market and its registration under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 would be
  terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Proposal 1: The Merger — effect on public trading status"]

## Sale process and prior divestiture context

- The Envision board conducted a strategic-alternatives review lasting approximately
  seven months, including 17 board meetings; 22 parties (including KKR) entered
  non-disclosure agreements and received due-diligence materials, and multiple bidders
  submitted indications of interest to acquire the whole company for cash, including KKR
  at $42.00-$46.00 per share. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- Prior to this transaction, Envision had already sold its American Medical Response,
  Inc. ("AMR") ambulance-transport unit to Air Medical Group Holdings, Inc. for
  approximately $2.4 billion, closing in August 2017 — a separate, earlier divestiture
  outside the scope of the KKR merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier

- Legal vehicle: Merger Sub merged into Envision, with Envision surviving as a wholly
  owned (indirect) subsidiary of Parent — the entire corporate entity and its
  subsidiaries were the transaction's subject, not a carved-out business unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Customer-facing product/asset and legal-vehicle continuity for the physician-services
  operating model: the merger agreement's definitions preserve the "Affiliated Medical
  Group" structure — professional corporations/associations owned by physicians for
  which an Envision subsidiary provides management services under an "Affiliated Medical
  Group Physician Services Agreement" or "Affiliated Medical Group Management Services
  Agreement" — as an ongoing feature of the surviving company rather than something
  altered by the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A Annex A, Merger Agreement Section 8.15 "Definitions"]
- People/organization: for one year following the effective time, Parent was required to
  provide each continuing Company employee base compensation and target annual cash
  incentive opportunity "no less favorable" than immediately before the effective time,
  and other compensation/benefits "substantially comparable" in the aggregate, plus
  severance benefits matching pre-existing Company arrangements for terminations within
  that year — i.e., contractual workforce and compensation continuity was built into the
  deal. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A Annex A, Merger Agreement Section 5.6 "Employee Matters"]
- Directors/officers: Envision's directors and executive officers were disclosed as
  having interests in the merger "different from, or in addition to" stockholders
  generally (equity-award treatment, continued indemnification and insurance coverage,
  a prorated 2018 bonus on a severance-qualifying termination). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Interests of the Company's Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger"]

## Financing structure (what funded the closing)

- KKR Americas Fund XII L.P. (the "Sponsor") committed, under an equity commitment
  letter dated June 10, 2018, to contribute or cause to be contributed to Parent an
  aggregate amount in cash equal to $3.5 billion at closing; certain KKR-affiliated
  investment vehicles that already held Envision common stock were expected to be given
  the opportunity to roll a new investment into Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Financing"]
- Parent separately entered into a debt commitment letter, dated June 10, 2018, with a
  syndicate of lenders (Credit Suisse, Citigroup, Morgan Stanley, Barclays, Goldman
  Sachs, Jefferies, UBS, Royal Bank of Canada, HSBC, Mizuho, and affiliates), committing
  up to $8.05 billion in aggregate debt financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Financing"]
- KKR Americas Fund XII L.P. also entered a limited guarantee with Envision, guaranteeing
  Parent's payment obligations for the parent termination fee and certain other
  specified payments, capped at $280 million in the aggregate. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Limited Guarantee"]
- On the October 11, 2018 closing date, Merger Sub (as Borrower/Issuer, succeeded by the
  surviving Company) entered first-lien credit facilities including a five-year
  asset-based revolving facility, issued $1,225,000,000 aggregate principal amount of
  8.75% senior notes due 2026, and issued $525.0 million aggregate principal amount of
  privately placed senior unsecured notes due 2026, with proceeds used in part to repay
  in full amounts outstanding under Envision's pre-existing credit agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-297466, Form 8-K Item 1.01/2.03]

## Closing conditions, vote, and regulatory clearance

- Completion was conditioned on, among other things, expiration or termination of the
  Hart-Scott-Rodino Act waiting period; the Company and Parent filed their HSR
  notification and report forms on June 29, 2018 and received early termination of the
  waiting period on July 19, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals"]
- The merger agreement provided for a company termination fee of $167 million payable by
  Envision to Parent, and a parent termination fee of $275 million payable by Parent to
  Envision, each under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A "Company Termination Fee" / "Parent Termination Fee"]
- On October 11, 2018 (the "Closing Date"), Envision Healthcare Corporation completed
  the transactions contemplated by the Merger Agreement: Merger Sub merged with and into
  the Company, with the Company surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  Parent, which is controlled by investment funds affiliated with KKR; at the effective
  time each share of Company common stock was converted into the right to receive $46.00
  in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-297466, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- A financial sponsor (KKR, via newly formed Parent and Merger Sub entities) acquired
  the entire Company for $46.00/share cash in a whole-company going-private merger,
  backed by $3.5 billion of committed equity and up to $8.05 billion of committed debt
  financing, with contractual one-year employee-continuity terms and the pre-existing
  physician "Affiliated Medical Group" management-services structure preserved intact;
  the deal closed October 11, 2018, funded in part by newly issued senior notes and
  first-lien credit facilities entered on the closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-18-008376, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-297466, Form 8-K]
