## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The registrant remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company
  throughout the outcome window, filing a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November
  30, 2021 (filed January 28, 2022) and a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November
  30, 2022 (filed January 26, 2023), both under the registrant name "Lennar
  Corporation," CIK 0000920760. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022 cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory (consolidated)
- Total consolidated revenues for fiscal year 2021 (ended November 30, 2021) were
  $27,130,676 thousand, up from $22,488,854 thousand in fiscal 2020 and $22,259,561
  thousand in fiscal 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Net earnings attributable to Lennar for fiscal year 2021 were $4,430,111 thousand,
  compared with $2,465,036 thousand in fiscal 2020 and $1,849,052 thousand in fiscal
  2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Total consolidated revenues for fiscal year 2022 (ended November 30, 2022) were
  $33,671,010 thousand, up from $27,130,676 thousand in fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Net earnings attributable to Lennar for fiscal year 2022 were $4,614,125 thousand,
  compared with $4,430,111 thousand in fiscal 2021; basic earnings per share were $15.74
  in fiscal 2022 versus $14.28 in fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- New home deliveries (including deliveries from unconsolidated entities) were 59,825 in
  fiscal 2021, compared to 52,925 in fiscal 2020 and 51,491 in fiscal 2019; the average
  sales price of a Lennar home (excluding unconsolidated-entity deliveries) was $424,000
  in fiscal 2021, up from $395,000 in fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, "Business"]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's annual goodwill impairment analysis and its FY2022 counterpart
  (performed as of September 30, 2022) each resulted in no goodwill impairment recorded,
  a determination made on a consolidated, reporting-unit basis rather than broken out for
  any single acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, "Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates"]

## Self-description and disclosed history of the CalAtlantic acquisition
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's Item 1 Business overview states: "We are the largest homebuilder
  in the United States by net earnings, an originator of residential and commercial
  mortgage loans, a provider of title insurance and closing services and a developer of
  multifamily rental properties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, "Business"]
- The same filing's history-of-acquisitions narrative states: "In 2018, we acquired
  CalAtlantic Group, Inc. ('CalAtlantic'), a major homebuilder which was building homes
  across the homebuilding spectrum, from entry level to luxury, in 43 metropolitan
  statistical areas spanning 19 states, and providing mortgage, title and escrow
  services." No CalAtlantic-specific revenue, earnings, unit count, or other financial or
  operating scalar accompanies that sentence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, "Business"]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K contains the same single-sentence historical mention, plus two
  further references: one noting goodwill "recorded in connection with the 2018
  acquisition of CalAtlantic" without a CalAtlantic-specific carrying value, and one
  noting that certain outstanding senior notes "were obligations of CalAtlantic when it
  was acquired, and were subsequently exchanged in part for notes of the Company."
  Neither reference attaches a CalAtlantic-specific revenue, earnings, or unit figure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022, "Business" / "Critical Accounting Policies and Estimates" / Notes to Financial Statements]
- Both the FY2021 and FY2022 Forms 10-K organize Lennar's homebuilding operations into
  geographic segments (Homebuilding East, Central, West, Houston and Other) rather than
  by acquired-company origin; no segment, product line, or geography in either filing is
  labeled as attributable specifically to the former CalAtlantic business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Documented personnel continuity
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's executive-officer disclosure states that Jeff J. McCall, listed
  as Lennar's Executive Vice President, "served as Executive Vice President and Chief
  Financial Officer of CalAtlantic Group, Inc., or a predecessor" from June 2011 to
  February 2018 (the closing month), then served as Lennar's Senior Vice President from
  February 2018 to January 2020, and as Lennar's Executive Vice President from January
  2020 onward — i.e., one of CalAtlantic's former named executive officers continued in
  progressively senior roles at Lennar through the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021, "Information about our Executive Officers"]
- No director-continuity disclosure for Scott D. Stowell (elected to Lennar's board at
  closing) was located in the body text of either the FY2021 or FY2022 Form 10-K; Item 10
  (Directors, Executive Officers and Corporate Governance) in both filings is incorporated
  by reference to Lennar's proxy statement rather than restated in the 10-K itself, so
  continuing board membership could not be confirmed from these two filings. [UNVERIFIED]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant continued to operate and report publicly under an unchanged name
  throughout the outcome window rather than being acquired, delisted, or dissolved, and
  its own Item 1 Business disclosure describes it as "the largest homebuilder in the
  United States by net earnings." Its realized consolidated trajectory shows total
  revenues rising from $22,488,854 thousand (fiscal 2020) to $27,130,676 thousand (fiscal
  2021, +20.6%) to $33,671,010 thousand (fiscal 2022, +24.1%), and net earnings
  attributable to the company rising from $2,465,036 thousand (fiscal 2020) to
  $4,430,111 thousand (fiscal 2021) to $4,614,125 thousand (fiscal 2022), with no goodwill
  impairment recorded in either FY2021 or FY2022. The FY2021 and FY2022 Forms 10-K
  reference the 2018 CalAtlantic acquisition only in brief historical mentions, without a
  separate segment, revenue, earnings, or unit-level breakout for the acquired business,
  and organize homebuilding operations geographically rather than by acquired-company
  origin; one former CalAtlantic named executive officer is documented as having
  continued into progressively senior roles at the acquirer through the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-001450, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-001606, Form 10-K FY2022]
