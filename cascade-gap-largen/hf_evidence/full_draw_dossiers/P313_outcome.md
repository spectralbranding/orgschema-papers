## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- Rowan ordinary shares ceased trading on the NYSE before the market opened on the transaction's closing date (April 11, 2019), and Rowan requested that the NYSE file a Form 25 notification to remove its shares from listing and deregister them under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-019311, Form 8-K]
- Rowan filed a Form 15-12B on April 22, 2019 to deregister its ordinary shares under Exchange Act Section 12(g) and suspend its reporting obligations under Sections 13(a) and 15(d). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-022840, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K, 10-Q, or 8-K has been filed under Rowan's own SEC registrant (CIK 0000085408) since its April 11, 2019 closing-related Form 8-K; Rowan's last periodic report filed as an independent registrant was its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2018, filed February 27, 2019. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing history for CIK 0000085408, accessions 0000085408-19-000010, 0001144204-19-019311, and 0001104659-19-022840]

## Whether the surviving combined registrant separately discloses the acquired business
- As a result of the transaction, Rowan became an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of the combined company, the surviving public registrant (CIK 0000314808, formerly Ensco plc). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-019311, Form 8-K]
- That registrant's later Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 22, 2022) reports its offshore-drilling rig fleet in two operating categories, "Floaters" and "Jackups," organized by rig type; it does not present a separate segment, financial, or operating disclosure attributable specifically to the legacy Rowan business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]
- The only "legacy Rowan" references identified in that filing concern the consolidation of legacy Rowan employee retirement and savings plans into combined-entity benefit plans, not a separately disclosed business or financial trajectory for the acquired operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]

## Outcome summary and no-record note (§4)
- Because Rowan's shares stopped trading and the company deregistered from SEC reporting within about two weeks of the transaction's closing, and because the surviving combined registrant's subsequent public filings report on a consolidated basis with no segment or comparable disclosure specific to the legacy Rowan business, there is no public 3–5-year outcome record specific to the acquired business as a distinct entity. Consistent with the no-record rule, the post-transaction trajectory of the acquired business is coded uncertain rather than inferred from the combined registrant's consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-022840, Form 15-12B; SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021]
