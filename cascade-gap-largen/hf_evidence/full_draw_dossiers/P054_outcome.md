## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The outcome window is Micron Technology, Inc.'s fiscal years 2011–2013 (fiscal year ended
  August 29, 2013), the 3–5-year record following the ~2008 closing. The source is Micron's
  Form 10-K for fiscal 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K (FY ended Aug 29, 2013)]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Micron accounted for Inotera under the equity method throughout the window and did not
  consolidate it. Micron's equity in the net loss of Inotera was $(112) million in 2011,
  $(189) million in 2012, and $(79) million in 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Equity Method Investments note]
- Inotera incurred net losses of $541 million for its fiscal year ended December 31, 2012;
  for the six-month period ended June 30, 2013 it generated net income of $91 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013]
- As of June 30, 2013, Inotera's current liabilities exceeded its current assets by $1.0
  billion, exposing it to liquidity risk; Inotera was not in compliance with certain loan
  covenants and had not been in compliance for the past several years, which could result in
  lenders requiring repayment during the next year, and Inotera had applied for a waiver
  from the June 30, 2013 financial covenants. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Micron's recorded investment balance in Inotera was $370 million (40% ownership) at
  fiscal 2012 and $344 million (35% ownership) at fiscal 2013; the market value of Micron's
  equity interest was $854 million as of August 29, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Equity Method Investments note]
- An Inotera share issuance in 2013 reduced Micron's ownership interest from 39.7% to 35.5%,
  producing a $48 million gain; as of August 29, 2013 Micron held ~35%, Nanya and certain
  affiliates ~36%, and the remainder was publicly held. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Product / process interface over time
- The volume of DRAM Micron drew from Inotera rose across the window: DRAM acquired from
  Inotera was 33% of Micron's DRAM gigabit production in 2011, 46% in 2012, and 54% in 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, MD&A]
- Micron's cost of product purchased from Inotera under the supply agreements was $641
  million (2011), $646 million (2012), and $1,260 million (2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Related Party Transactions / MD&A]
- Micron recognized losses on its purchase commitment under the Inotera supply arrangement:
  in 2011, quarterly losses of $28 million, $3 million, $12 million, and $11 million; in
  2012, losses of $40 million, $19 million, and $17 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Micron disclosed that its Inotera supply arrangement "involves numerous risks," including
  higher costs under market-based provisions, "difficulties and delays in ramping production
  at Inotera," and "difficulties in transferring technology to Inotera." [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Item 1A Risk Factors]

## Process / organization interface over time
- Micron determined it "do[es] not have the power to direct the activities of Inotera that
  most significantly impact its economic performance," primarily due to (1) limitations on
  its governance rights that require the consent of other parties for key operating
  decisions and (2) Inotera's dependence on Nanya for financing and Inotera's ability to
  operate in Taiwan; accordingly Micron did not consolidate Inotera. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Variable Interest Entities / Equity Method Investments note]
- The joint venture relationship was renegotiated within the window: on January 17, 2013
  Micron entered agreements with Nanya (a new Joint Venture Agreement, Supply Agreement, and
  Facilitation Agreement) amending the relationship. The new Inotera Supply Agreement,
  retroactively effective January 1, 2013, obligates Micron to purchase for an initial
  three-year term (through January 2016) substantially all of Inotera's output at a discount
  from market prices, replacing the prior right to 50% of wafer capacity on a margin-sharing
  formula. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, MD&A / Exhibits 10.122–10.124]
- Under applicable accounting guidance, the new Inotera Supply Agreement is treated as
  containing an embedded operating lease with respect to Inotera's production assets during
  the initial three-year term. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Related-party payables due to Inotera, primarily for DRAM purchases, were $130 million at
  fiscal 2012 and $345 million at fiscal 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Related Party Transactions note]

## Legal challenge to the acquisition
- On January 20, 2011, Dr. Michael Jaffé, administrator for Qimonda AG's insolvency
  proceedings, filed suit against Micron and Micron Semiconductor B.V. in the District Court
  of Munich seeking to void, under Section 133 of the German Insolvency Act, the fall-2008
  share purchase agreement under which Micron purchased Qimonda's Inotera shares, and an
  order requiring Micron to retransfer those shares to the Qimonda estate; the complaint also
  sought to terminate a related patent cross-license. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K FY2013, Legal Proceedings / Commitments and Contingencies]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across fiscal 2011–2013 Inotera remained a separately-run, publicly-traded equity-method
  investee that Micron did not gain the power to control; it ran deep operating losses ($541
  million for calendar 2012) and covenant non-compliance while Micron's share of its losses,
  its cost of Inotera-sourced DRAM, and its dependence on Inotera output all rose; the
  parties renegotiated the venture in January 2013 into a new supply/JV structure, and the
  underlying 2008 share purchase was under legal challenge by Qimonda's insolvency
  administrator. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-13-000228, Form 10-K (FY ended Aug 29, 2013)]
