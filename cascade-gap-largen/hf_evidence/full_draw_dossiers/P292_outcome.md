# Outcome sub-dossier — the business acquired from A. Schulman, Inc., as disclosed within LyondellBasell Industries N.V. (~3-4 years after closing)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. A. Schulman, Inc. terminated its own Exchange
> Act registration shortly after closing and has filed no periodic report since, so
> there is no entity-specific 10-K for it in the outcome window. This sub-dossier
> instead draws only on the acquirer's (LyondellBasell Industries N.V., CIK 1489393)
> own fiscal-year-2021 Form 10-K (filed February 2022, roughly 3-4 years after the
> ~2018 closing), insofar as it separately names and discloses figures tied to the
> acquired business. It contains no closing-structure information.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- A. Schulman's last periodic filing on its own CIK was a Form 10-Q filed June 28,
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000087565-18-000031, Form 10-Q]
- A Form 25-NSE (notification of removal from listing/registration) dated August 21,
  2018 and a Form 15-15D (suspension of Exchange Act reporting duty) dated September
  20, 2018 appear in A. Schulman's EDGAR history, and no Form 10-K or 20-F appears
  after fiscal year 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-18-000322, Form 25-NSE] [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-18-278863, Form 15-15D]
- Consequently there is no A. Schulman-specific audited annual report covering the
  ~2021-2023 window; the facts below are limited to what LyondellBasell's own
  consolidated fiscal-year-2021 Form 10-K discloses by explicitly naming "A.
  Schulman." [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]

## Acquirer's segment- and item-level disclosure naming the acquired business
- LyondellBasell manages its continuing operations through six reportable segments,
  one being "Advanced Polymer Solutions" (APS), which "produces and markets
  compounding and solutions, such as polypropylene compounds, engineered plastics,
  masterbatches, colors and powders, engineered composites and advanced polymers."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
- Of the company's $1,875 million total goodwill as of December 31, 2021, $1,357
  million "is related to the acquisition of A. Schulman in 2018, which is included in
  our APS segment, and is mainly attributed to acquired workforce and expected
  synergies"; no impairment of this goodwill is reported as of that date. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
- APS segment operating results "include integration costs of $37 million and $116
  million in 2020 and 2019, respectively, associated with our acquisition of A.
  Schulman in August 2018," and "Integration activities related to our acquisition of
  A. Schulman were substantially completed by the third quarter of 2020." [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
- Discussing the 2021-versus-2020 change in APS segment EBITDA ($409 million in 2021
  versus $378 million in 2020, up $31 million or 8%), the filing attributes part of
  the increase to the "absence of integration costs incurred in 2020 associated with
  the acquisition of A. Schulman Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form
  10-K]
- A consolidated subsidiary "formerly known as A. Schulman, Inc." continues to have
  outstanding cumulative perpetual special stock, reported as a redeemable
  non-controlling interest on LyondellBasell's balance sheet, entitled to a 6%
  cumulative dividend on a $1,000-per-share liquidation preference. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
- The filing separately reports a United Kingdom "A. Schulman" defined-benefit pension
  plan, distinct from LyondellBasell's other UK plans, with its own disclosed
  asset-allocation mix for 2021 and 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009,
  Form 10-K]

## Segment financial detail (APS, the segment housing the acquired business)
- APS segment sales and other operating revenues were $5,145 million in 2021 versus
  $3,913 million in 2020; APS segment operating income was $286 million in 2021 versus
  $226 million in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
- The filing attributes part of the 2021 revenue increase to higher average sales
  prices and volumes and favorable foreign exchange, and states "compounding and
  solutions results remained relatively unchanged during 2021." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]

## Caveat on segment attribution
- The APS segment also includes advanced-polymer product lines (Catalloy,
  polybutene-1) the filing does not attribute to the A. Schulman acquisition, so the
  segment-level revenue/operating-income/EBITDA figures above describe a broader
  segment that houses, but is not limited to, the acquired business; only the
  goodwill, integration-cost, redeemable-non-controlling-interest, and UK-pension
  items above are explicitly tied by the filing to "A. Schulman" by name. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because A. Schulman terminated its own SEC registration and reporting shortly after
  closing, there is no stand-alone A. Schulman annual report for the ~2021-2023
  window; the traceable realized record instead comes from the acquirer naming the
  acquired business within its own consolidated filing — acquisition-related goodwill
  carried without a disclosed impairment, acquisition-integration costs running
  through the third quarter of 2020, a surviving "A. Schulman" legal subsidiary with
  outstanding special stock, a named UK "A. Schulman" pension plan, and segment-level
  revenue and EBITDA growth in the segment that houses the acquired product lines.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001489393-22-000009, Form 10-K]
