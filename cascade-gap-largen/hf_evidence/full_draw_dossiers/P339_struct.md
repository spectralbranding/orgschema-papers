# Structural sub-dossier — Bill Barrett Corporation / Fifth Creek Energy combination under a newly formed parent (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On December 4, 2017, Bill Barrett Corporation ("BBG"), a Delaware corporation, and
  Fifth Creek Energy Operating Company, LLC ("Fifth Creek"), a Delaware limited
  liability company, entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger together with Red Rider
  Holdco, Inc. ("Holdco"), a wholly owned subsidiary of BBG formed solely for the
  transaction, and two merger-sub entities (Rio Merger Sub, LLC and Rider Merger Sub,
  Inc.), and, for limited purposes, Fifth Creek Energy Company, LLC ("FCEC") and NGP
  Natural Resources XI, L.P. ("NGP" or the "Fund"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A pp.1-2]
- The agreement provided that Rider Merger Sub would merge with and into BBG (BBG
  surviving), and Rio Merger Sub would merge with and into Fifth Creek (Fifth Creek
  surviving), so that BBG and Fifth Creek would each become direct wholly owned
  subsidiaries of Holdco, which would be renamed upon closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A p.1]
- Each BBG common share would convert into one share of Holdco common stock, and all
  outstanding Fifth Creek equity interests, in the aggregate, would convert into
  100,000,000 shares of Holdco common stock; based on then-current estimates BBG
  stockholders would hold approximately 52% and Fifth Creek's sole member approximately
  48% of the combined company's outstanding common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A p.1/p.915]
- Fifth Creek is described as an exploration and production company based in Greenwood
  Village, Colorado, developing oil and gas reserves in the Hereford Field area of the DJ
  Basin in rural northern Weld County, Colorado; BBG's own operations were also in the DJ
  Basin, on a "contiguous and complementary" acreage position conducive to the same
  extended-reach-lateral development methods BBG employed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "Information About Fifth Creek" / "Background of the Mergers"]
- Fifth Creek's sole member, FCEC, was controlled by NGP; NGP was disclosed as the fund
  that would receive Holdco shares on Fifth Creek's behalf. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "The Parties"]

## What was to cross at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: both BBG and Fifth Creek would become direct wholly owned subsidiaries
  of a newly formed parent (Holdco); BBG would continue to file periodic reports with
  the SEC on account of its common stock pending the mergers, with Holdco as the new
  public registrant afterward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A p.16]
- Controlling intent / board: upon completion, Holdco's board was structured to consist
  of eleven directors — six "Non-Investor Directors" continuing from BBG's then-current
  board and five "Investor Directors" designated by FCEC (including Scott Gieselman,
  Craig Glick and Michael Starzer), with board-designation rights tied to FCEC's
  percentage ownership interest remaining above specified thresholds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "The Stockholders Agreement — Governance Matters"]
- Management: upon completion of the mergers, R. Scot Woodall — BBG's Chief Executive
  Officer and President at signing — was designated to become the Chief Executive
  Officer of Holdco. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "Summary"]
- Employees: the merger agreement provided that, for one year following the closing
  date, Holdco would provide Fifth Creek employees who continued in Holdco's employ
  with compensation and benefits "commensurate with the compensation and benefits
  provided to similarly situated employees of BBG," and full credit under employee
  benefit plans for prior service — i.e., contractual alignment of the acquired
  workforce toward the surviving company's existing compensation structure rather than
  either an assumed or a silent term. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Employee Matters"]

## Financing / credit substrate bridged at closing
- Effective March 19, 2018 (the closing date), BBG, a wholly owned BBG subsidiary, and
  HighPoint Resources Corporation (f/k/a Red Rider Holdco, Inc.) entered a Fifth
  Amendment, Waiver and Consent to BBG's existing Third Amended and Restated Credit
  Agreement, under which HighPoint became a guarantor and pledged its equity interest in
  BBG, and under which financial reporting under the Credit Agreement following the
  mergers would thereafter be by HighPoint. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- BBG's pre-existing "at-the-market" Equity Distribution Agreement with Goldman Sachs
  (up to $100,000,000 in common stock) was terminated by BBG on March 16, 2018, ahead of
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Item 1.02]

## Termination-fee and process terms
- BBG would be obligated to pay Fifth Creek a termination fee of $22.5 million under
  specified circumstances (e.g., termination for a superior proposal or a change in
  board recommendation). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A "Expenses and Termination Fees Relating to the Termination of the Merger Agreement"]
- BBG and Fifth Creek expected to complete the mergers in the first half of fiscal year
  2018, subject to stockholder approval and other customary closing conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A p.978]

## Consummation
- The mergers were completed March 19, 2018: Rider Merger Sub merged with and into BBG
  (BBG surviving) and Rio Merger Sub merged with and into Fifth Creek (Fifth Creek
  surviving), so that BBG and Fifth Creek each became direct wholly owned subsidiaries
  of HighPoint Resources Corporation (f/k/a Red Rider Holdco, Inc.); former BBG holders
  received an aggregate 110,933,552 shares of HighPoint common stock and former Fifth
  Creek equity holders received 100,000,000 shares of HighPoint common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Immediately after closing, pursuant to a post-closing Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated March 19, 2018, Fifth Creek (then a direct wholly owned subsidiary of HighPoint)
  was merged with and into BBG, with BBG as the surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- BBG requested that the NYSE file a Form 25 to withdraw BBG common stock from listing;
  BBG shares were suspended from trading on the NYSE prior to the market open on March
  20, 2018, and HighPoint common stock began trading on the NYSE under the ticker "HPR"
  that same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Items 2.01/3.01]
- Effective with the mergers, all directors of BBG except R. Scot Woodall were replaced
  by Kenneth A. Wonstolen, while BBG's pre-merger executive officers continued as BBG's
  executive officers; BBG amended and restated its Certificate of Incorporation and
  Bylaws pursuant to the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K Items 5.02/5.03]
- BBG and Fifth Creek both filed Form 15-12B notices terminating registration of their
  own securities on March 30, 2018, following the mergers, as HighPoint became the sole
  SEC-reporting parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103398, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103380, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- BBG and Fifth Creek combined via a double merger under a newly formed parent
  (renamed HighPoint Resources Corporation at closing), with BBG's corporate vehicle,
  senior management (CEO) and credit facility continuing forward as the surviving
  operating subsidiary and guarantor structure, while board composition and roughly
  half of pro forma equity ownership were allocated to the counterparty's investor
  (Fifth Creek/NGP) side under a stockholders agreement, and Fifth Creek's incoming
  employees were bridged to BBG's existing compensation and benefit structure for a
  contractual one-year period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042797, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-087985, Form 8-K]
