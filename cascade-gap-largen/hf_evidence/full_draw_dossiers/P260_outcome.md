## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Two separately SEC-reporting registrants resulted from the transaction and continue to
  file their own periodic reports: The Walt Disney Company (CIK 0001744489, the renamed
  merger holding company that acquired 21CF's studio, international-television, Hulu and
  Sky interests) and Fox Corporation (CIK 0001754301, the former "New Fox," which holds
  the retained news/sports/broadcast businesses). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]
- Neither registrant's Form 10-K breaks out the specific asset perimeter that crossed
  from 21CF to Disney as its own reported segment matching the pre-merger 21CF
  boundary; Disney discloses the acquired assets only within its combined Entertainment
  segment / reporting-unit structure, so the scalars below are at the consolidated or
  reporting-unit level, not isolated to the acquired perimeter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-22-000213, Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001744489-23-000216, Form 10-K]

## Disney's disclosed record touching the acquired assets (fiscal 2022-2023)
- Disney's fiscal 2022 Form 10-K states that "the Company's restructuring plan
  implemented in connection with the 2019 acquisition of TFCF to realize cost synergies
  was completed in fiscal 2021," recording $1.8 billion of related restructuring
  charges, including $1.4 billion of severance (including employee contract
  terminations) and $0.3 billion of equity-based compensation costs "primarily for TFCF
  awards that were accelerated to vest upon the closing of the acquisition"; the
  associated reserve stood at $676 million as of September 28, 2019 and had run down to
  $50 million by October 2, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-22-000213, Form 10-K, Note 18]
- Disney continues to separately disclose "TFCF and Hulu acquisition amortization" as a
  distinct line item years after closing: $2,418 million in fiscal 2021, $2,353 million
  in fiscal 2022, and $1,998 million in fiscal 2023, described as amortization of
  intangible assets "recognized in connection with the TFCF acquisition in fiscal 2019." [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-23-000216, Form 10-K]
- Disney's fiscal 2023 Form 10-K reports fiscal 2023 restructuring and impairment
  charges of $3,892 million, comprising a $2,577 million content impairment charge, $721
  million of goodwill impairments, $357 million of severance, a $141 million equity
  investment impairment, and $96 million from exiting businesses in Russia; the $721
  million goodwill impairment is attributed to the "entertainment" and "international
  sports linear networks" reporting units, with the international sports linear
  networks unit's goodwill fully impaired. The filing does not state what portion, if
  any, of these charges is specifically attributable to assets acquired from 21CF as
  opposed to Disney's pre-existing businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-23-000216, Form 10-K, Note 18]
- In November 2023, NBCUniversal exercised a contractual put right requiring Disney to
  purchase NBCUniversal's remaining 33% equity interest in Hulu, LLC — the streaming
  service into which 21CF's pre-merger 30% Hulu interest had been folded at closing —
  with the purchase price to be based on Hulu's fair value as of September 30, 2023 or a
  guaranteed floor, and Disney agreeing to share 50% of its resulting tax benefit with
  NBCUniversal over approximately 15 years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-23-000216, Form 10-K]

## Fox Corporation's own disclosed record (fiscal year ended June 30, 2022)
- For fiscal 2022, Fox Corporation reported total revenues of $13,974 million (up 8%
  from fiscal 2021's $12,909 million) and net income attributable to Fox Corporation
  stockholders of $1,205 million, down 44% from fiscal 2021's $2,150 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-022584, Form 10-K]
- The filing attributes the fiscal 2022 net income decline primarily to "the change in
  fair value of the Company's investment in Flutter Entertainment plc and the absence of
  the reimbursement from Disney" of a divestiture-tax amount received in fiscal 2021
  under the tax matters agreement executed at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-022584, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Both entities resulting from the transaction remain independent SEC filers with
  ongoing periodic disclosure more than four years after the March 2019 closing.
  Disney's own filings document a restructuring program explicitly tied to the "TFCF"
  acquisition running from closing through fiscal 2021 ($1.8 billion, mostly severance
  and accelerated equity compensation), continuing acquisition-related intangible
  amortization through fiscal 2023, a fiscal 2023 reporting-unit goodwill impairment in
  linear-network businesses not attributed specifically to the acquired perimeter, and a
  November 2023 forced buyout of NBCUniversal's Hulu stake. Fox Corporation's own fiscal
  2022 net income fell 44% year-over-year for reasons its filing attributes to an
  investment fair-value swing and the absence of a prior-year one-time Disney
  reimbursement, not to any acquisition-integration item. Because neither filer
  separately breaks out the acquired perimeter's own results, the realized record for
  that specific asset base beyond the disclosed items above is coded uncertain. [SRC: SEC accession 0001744489-22-000213, Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001744489-23-000216, Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001628280-22-022584, Form 10-K]
