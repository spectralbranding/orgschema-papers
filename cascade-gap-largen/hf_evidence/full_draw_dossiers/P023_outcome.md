## Outcome window and registrant
- Outcome window fixed as the registrant's fiscal year 2018 annual report — its own in-window 3–5-year realized record. The registrant filing is Washington Prime Group Inc. (and its operating partnership Washington Prime Group, L.P.), an Indiana entity, principal executive offices at 180 East Broad Street, Columbus, Ohio, for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Realized portfolio trajectory
- As of December 31, 2018 the company owned interests in 108 shopping centers in the United States, comprising approximately 58 million square feet of managed gross leasable area, managed and reported as a single reportable segment (enclosed retail properties and open-air properties). [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The company's portfolio at the separation consisted of interests in 98 shopping centers (the "WPG Legacy Properties"); on January 15, 2015 the company acquired Glimcher Realty Trust ("GRT") in a stock-and-cash transaction valued at approximately $4.2 billion, acquiring interests in 23 shopping centers and assuming mortgages on 14 properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Best-documented outcome scalars (FY2018 vs prior in-window years)
- Net income for FY2018 was $108.655 million, versus $231.593 million in 2017 and $77.416 million in 2016; net income attributable to the company was $93.604 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Funds from operations (FFO) of the Operating Partnership was $386.819 million in 2018, versus $452.128 million in 2017 and $398.091 million in 2016; FFO per share/unit was $1.73 in 2018, $2.04 in 2017 and $1.80 in 2016 — a declining trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Impairment losses recognized over the window: $147.979 million (2015), $21.879 million (2016) and $66.925 million (2017), with no impairment loss (--) recognized in 2018 or 2014 per the selected financial data. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The company maintained common dividends of $1.00 per common share/unit for 2018 (and $1.00 for 2017); on February 12, 2019 the Board declared a first-quarter common dividend of $0.25 per common share/unit, payable March 15, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Documented operating disruption over the window
- Ending occupancy rate was 93.7% at December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Comparable NOI decreased 3.0% for the core portfolio comparing calendar year 2018 to 2017; the enclosed retail properties had a comparable-NOI decrease of 3.5%, which was driven primarily by the impact of 2018 department store bankruptcies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Tenant disruption: certain department stores affecting the portfolio included The Bon-Ton Stores, Inc., which liquidated in 2018, and Sears Holdings Corporation; the company disclosed exposure to tenant bankruptcy, liquidation and store closures as a risk to results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Outcome summary
- Within its fixed 3–5-year window (FY2018), the registrant remained a going concern and continued to pay a common dividend of $1.00 per share/unit while reporting positive but declining profitability — FFO per share/unit fell from $2.04 (2017) to $1.73 (2018) and net income fell from $231.6 million (2017) to $108.7 million (2018) — with comparable core NOI down 3.0% (enclosed retail down 3.5%) amid 2018 department-store bankruptcies (Bon-Ton liquidation, Sears), ending occupancy of 93.7%, and impairment losses recognized in 2015 ($148.0M), 2016 ($21.9M) and 2017 ($66.9M); the portfolio had grown from 98 to 108 shopping centers (~58 million sq ft) following the January 2015 acquisition of Glimcher Realty Trust. [SRC: SEC accession 0001594686-19-000007, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
