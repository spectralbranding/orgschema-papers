## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is the registrant's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012, filed under 1933 Act Commission file number 333-169533; it describes an ongoing serial-acquisition program building a portfolio, not a single deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012 cover]
- The registrant is "a Maryland corporation that was formed on July 27, 2010 (Date of Inception)" and believes it qualifies and intends to be taxed as a REIT beginning with the taxable year ended December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- It commenced principal operations on April 13, 2012 upon satisfying its escrow conditions and issuing approximately 308,000 shares of common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]

## Parties, legal vehicle, and controlling structure (closing era)
- The company is the sole general partner of, and owns directly or indirectly 100% of the partnership interest in, Cole Operating Partnership IV, LP (a Delaware limited partnership, "CCPT IV OP"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- The company "is externally managed by Cole REIT Advisors IV, LLC ('CR IV Advisors'), an affiliate of [its] sponsor," Cole Real Estate Investments, a group of affiliated entities that has sponsored various real estate investment programs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- "We have no paid employees and are externally advised and managed by CR IV Advisors." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]

## The value-capture model and the customer-facing asset (closing era)
- The company intends to use substantially all net offering proceeds to "acquire and operate a diversified portfolio of commercial real estate investments primarily consisting of retail properties located throughout the United States," which "primarily have been and will be single-tenant properties and multi-tenant 'power centers'." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- Acquisition capital is raised through a continuous best-efforts initial public offering on Form S-11 (Registration No. 333-169533) of up to a maximum of 250.0 million shares at $10.00 per share, plus up to 50.0 million additional shares under a distribution reinvestment plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- As of December 31, 2012 the company had issued 29.9 million shares for gross offering proceeds of $298.4 million, before offering costs and selling commissions of $32.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- Closing-era acquisition activity: using net offering proceeds and indebtedness, the company "acquired $514.7 million in real estate and related assets and incurred acquisition costs of $14.4 million, including costs of $10.3 million in acquisition fees and expense reimbursements to CR IV Advisors." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- As of December 31, 2012 the company owned 89 properties comprising 2.5 million rentable square feet of single- and multi-tenant retail space located in 27 states (including buildings on land subject to ground leases); 81 of the properties were freestanding single-tenant retail properties and eight were multi-tenant retail properties, and the rentable space was 99.1% leased. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012 Item 2]
- Fixed-rate debt as of December 31, 2012 consisted of a $75.0 million interest-only note payable at 3.69% maturing January 1, 2023; the ratio of debt to total gross real estate and related assets (net of gross intangible lease liabilities) was 53.3%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- Acquisition/identification and portfolio-building processes sit with the external advisor: to succeed "we and our advisor must, among other things: identify and acquire investments that further our investment objectives; increase awareness of the Cole Credit Property Trust IV, Inc. name...; expand and maintain our network of licensed broker-dealers...". [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- Property-level operating processes are run by an advisor affiliate, not by the company's own organization: properties are managed by Cole Realty Advisors, Inc. ("Cole Realty Advisors"), "an affiliate of our advisor, pursuant to property management and leasing agreements with our subsidiaries that hold title to our properties," and Cole Realty Advisors is expected to also serve as property manager for properties owned by other Cole-sponsored programs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- The company is dependent on the advisor group for essentially all functions: "We are dependent on CR IV Advisors and its affiliates for services that are essential to us, including the sale of shares of our common stock, asset acquisition decisions, property management and other general administrative responsibilities." [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
- The advisor's directors/officers also serve other REITs, general partners, and advisors of other Cole Real Estate Investments-sponsored programs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- Acquisition fees are paid to the advisor group based on the cost of each investment "regardless of the quality of the properties acquired," an arrangement the filing identifies as a conflict. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- At the closing era the registrant is an externally managed, employee-less Maryland REIT that raises equity through a continuous best-efforts public offering ($10.00/share) and deploys it to assemble a single-tenant / power-center retail portfolio (89 properties, 2.5M sq ft, 27 states as of December 31, 2012), while the processes that identify, acquire, and manage those properties — and the people performing them — reside with affiliates of its external sponsor (CR IV Advisors and Cole Realty Advisors) under advisory and property-management/leasing agreements rather than being employed or performed by the company itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001498547-13-000024, 10-K FY2012]
