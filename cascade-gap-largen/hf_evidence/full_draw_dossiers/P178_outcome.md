## Realized financial trajectory (2020-2022)
- Consolidated net revenue: $3,045,578 thousand (FY2020); $3,820,806 thousand (FY2021);
  $4,029,262 thousand (FY2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Net income: $164.3 million, 5.4% of net revenue (FY2020); $363.6 million, 9.5% of net
  revenue (FY2021); $310.7 million, 7.7% of net revenue (FY2022) — net income declined
  year-over-year in FY2022 even as net revenue continued to grow. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A results table]
- Restructuring and other charges, net: $33.094 million expense (FY2020); $14.942 million
  expense (FY2021); a net gain of $66.700 million (FY2022), the FY2022 figure driven
  substantially by the divestiture of the company's semiconductor test and thermal
  business ("the Qinex Business"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022, Note on Restructuring and Other Charges]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant continued uninterrupted public reporting under the same CIK after the
  2018 closing. Over fiscal years 2020-2022 it reported continued net-revenue growth
  ($3.05B to $4.03B) alongside a net-income trajectory that rose from FY2020 to FY2021 and
  then declined in FY2022 despite further revenue growth, a swing in restructuring/other
  charges from a net expense in 2020-2021 to a net gain in 2022 (substantially reflecting
  a business divestiture unrelated to the 2018 transaction), and a reported year-end 2022
  workforce of approximately 20,800 employees. The filing does not attribute any of these
  developments to the 2018 reincorporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022]
