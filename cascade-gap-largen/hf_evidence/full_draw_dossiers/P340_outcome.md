## Public-reporting status and entity identity through the outcome window
- Following the December 20, 2018 closing, the newly formed holding company (formerly
  "Halfmoon Parent, Inc.," renamed "Cigna Corporation") is the entity that continued as
  the publicly traded, Exchange Act-reporting registrant; the pre-merger operating
  company (renamed "Cigna Holding Company" at closing) became its subsidiary and did not
  itself continue to file annual reports as an independent public registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, "Business" p.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-045478, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The registrant filed a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 24, 2022) and a
  Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed February 23, 2023), both under the registrant
  name "Cigna Corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022 cover page]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K discloses that "Cigna Corporation was renamed The Cigna Group in
  February 2023," a name change that occurred after the close of the fiscal year covered
  by that filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, "Business" p.1]
- As of January 31, 2023 the registrant had 297,059,973 shares of common stock
  outstanding, and the aggregate market value of voting stock held by non-affiliates as
  of June 30, 2022 was approximately $82.8 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022 cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Consolidated total revenues were $174,078 million for the year ended December 31,
  2021, versus $160,401 million in 2020 and $153,566 million in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"]
- Consolidated shareholders' net income was $5,365 million for 2021, down 37% from
  $8,458 million in 2020 (2020 included a $4,203 million gain on sale of business); 2019
  shareholders' net income was $5,104 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"]
- Consolidated total revenues were $180,516 million for the year ended December 31, 2022,
  a 4% increase over 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"]
- Consolidated shareholders' net income was $6,668 million for 2022, up 24% from $5,365
  million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"]
- As of December 31, 2021, goodwill and other intangible assets carried a combined value
  of approximately $80 billion, representing 52% of total consolidated assets, with
  goodwill of $35.1 billion reported in the Evernorth segment (which includes the former
  Express Scripts pharmacy and benefits-management businesses) and $10.7 billion in the
  Cigna Healthcare segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, "Risk Factors" p.33] [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 18 "Goodwill, Other Intangibles and Property and Equipment"]
- The goodwill roll-forward in Note 18 for 2020-2021 (beginning balance $44,602 million;
  goodwill acquired, net $1,428 million in 2021; foreign-currency translation impact; a
  reclassification to assets held for sale; ending balance $45,811 million at December
  31, 2021) does not list an impairment charge among the reported changes to the goodwill
  balance in either year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 18 "Goodwill, Other Intangibles and Property and Equipment"]

## Documented events over time
- The Evernorth segment (described in both Forms 10-K as encompassing pharmacy
  solutions, benefits-management, care-delivery, and intelligence solutions, and
  continuing to market products under the "Express Scripts Pharmacy," "Express Scripts
  PBM," "Express Scripts SafeGuardRx," and related brand names) remained one of the
  registrant's two principal reportable segments through FY2021 and FY2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, "Business" p.1-4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, "Business" p.1-4]
- The registrant sold its U.S. Group Disability and Life insurance business effective
  December 31, 2020, recording the $4,203 million gain on sale reflected in the 2020
  results above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"]
- In FY2022 the registrant recorded a $1,662 million gain on sale of businesses,
  including the sale of certain international life, accident and supplemental benefits
  operations (Australia, New Zealand, South Korea, Taiwan and Thailand) to Chubb INA
  Holdings, Inc. for approximately $5.4 billion in cash (the "Chubb transaction"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Consolidated Results of Operations"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022, "Segment Information"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The publicly traded successor holding company continued to operate and report under
  Exchange Act obligations throughout the outcome window (renamed The Cigna Group in
  February 2023, just after the window). Consolidated revenue grew from $174,078 million
  (2021) to $180,516 million (2022, +4%), while shareholders' net income fell 37% in 2021
  versus a 2020 base that included a large one-time divestiture gain, then rose 24% to
  $6,668 million in 2022. The Evernorth segment built substantially around the former
  Express Scripts pharmacy and pharmacy-benefits businesses remained a core reportable
  segment and continued operating under its legacy brand names through both fiscal years,
  and no goodwill impairment charge appears in the reported FY2020-FY2021 goodwill
  roll-forward. The registrant also completed unrelated portfolio divestitures during the
  window, including the 2022 sale of certain international insurance operations to Chubb
  for approximately $5.4 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-22-000007, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001739940-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
