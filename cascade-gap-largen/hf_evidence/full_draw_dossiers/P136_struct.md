# Structural sub-dossier — Walter Energy, Inc. Chapter 11 §363 sale of Alabama coal assets (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it establishes) and the seller's bankruptcy posture
- The drawn 8-K reports that on July 15, 2015 Walter Energy, Inc. ("the Company") and
  certain wholly owned domestic subsidiaries filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the
  U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Northern District of Alabama, jointly administered as In
  re Walter Energy, Inc., et al., Case No. 15-02741-TOM11; the Canadian and U.K.
  operations were not included. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-051290, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The same 8-K reports a Restructuring Support Agreement dated July 15, 2015 among the
  Company, the Filing Subsidiaries, certain first-lien lenders and certain holders of the
  9.500% Senior Secured Notes due 2019, providing for either a consensual debt-to-equity
  conversion under a Chapter 11 plan or, on a "Triggering Event," a sale of substantially
  all assets under Bankruptcy Code Sections 105, 363 and 365. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-051290, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-10.1]
- Filing the petitions accelerated the Company's debt, including approximately $978.2
  million outstanding under an April 1, 2011 Credit Agreement plus $70.2 million of
  letters of credit, and four indentures (9.875% Notes due 2020 ~$388.0M; 8.500% Notes due
  2021 ~$383.3M; 9.500% Senior Secured Notes due 2019 ~$970.0M; 11.0%/12.0% PIK Toggle
  Notes due 2020 ~$360.5M). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-051290, Form 8-K Item 2.04]

## Transaction and parties as structured (the §363 asset sale)
- On November 5, 2015 the Company and certain subsidiaries entered into a stalking-horse
  Asset Purchase Agreement with Coal Acquisition LLC, a Delaware LLC formed by members of
  the Company's senior lender group, to acquire substantially all of the Company's Alabama
  assets (the "Subject Assets"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The sale was structured under Sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code, subject
  to Court-approved bidding procedures and higher-or-better bids at auction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-2.1 Recitals]
- The Company determined it received no higher or otherwise better bids; on January 8,
  2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered a Sale Order approving the sale of the Subject Assets
  to Coal Acquisition free and clear of all liens, claims, interests and encumbrances, and
  approving assumption and assignment of certain executory contracts and unexpired leases.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-16-002829, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The sale closed on March 31, 2016; immediately prior to closing the parties entered an
  amended and restated Asset Purchase Agreement, and the buyer had been renamed Warrior
  Met Coal, LLC (f/k/a Coal Acquisition LLC), an entity owned by the Company's first-lien
  creditors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Consideration (as structured)
- As set out in the November 2015 agreement, consideration was approximately $5.4 million
  in cash (subject to adjustment), a credit bid of first-lien obligations of approximately
  $1.25 billion, and the assumption of certain liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At closing the amended-and-restated agreement stated aggregate consideration of
  approximately $5.4 million in cash (subject to adjustment), a credit bid of first-lien
  obligations of approximately $1.15 billion (as adjusted), and assumption of certain
  liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- The Buyer purchased the Acquired Assets and assumed the Assumed Liabilities pursuant to
  the Sale Order, free and clear of all Encumbrances (other than Permitted Encumbrances),
  under Sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §2 / Recitals]
- The Subject Assets comprised substantially all of the Company's Alabama assets — its
  core coal-mining operating assets, including real property, permits and mining assets as
  enumerated in the agreement's asset schedules. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K EX-2.1 (definitions of Acquired Assets, Real Property, Permits, Mining)]
- The closing press release characterized the Alabama coal assets as "substantially all of
  [the Company's] core assets" and stated that with the close the Company had "transferred
  ownership of virtually all of its U.S. operating assets to third parties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## People / management (as structured)
- Employees did not transfer automatically: prior to closing the Buyer set the initial
  terms and conditions of employment and determined, "in its sole discretion," which
  employees to offer employment to; only employees who accepted and actually commenced
  employment became "Buyer Employees," with no obligation on the Buyer to retain them.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.5(a)]
- Severance, separation pay and key-employee retention payments for employees terminated
  on or before closing remained obligations of the Sellers, not the Buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.5(a)]
- The Buyer assumed only those Benefit Plans it designated on a schedule (the "Buyer
  Benefit Plans") as Assumed Liabilities; other seller benefit plans were not assumed.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.5(c)]

## Labor / collective bargaining bridging the workforce substrate (closing-time)
- A closing condition was relief from the successor clauses in the collective bargaining
  agreements between the Debtors and the United Mine Workers of America (UMWA) and the
  United Steel Workers (USW), and execution of a new collective bargaining agreement
  between the Buyer and the USW, if applicable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On December 28, 2015 the Bankruptcy Court entered an order authorizing the Debtors to
  reject their collective bargaining agreements with the UMWA, implement final labor
  proposals, and terminate retiree benefits (the "UMWA Order"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-16-002829, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Under a court-supervised Section 363 process, a customer-facing coal-mining asset base
  (substantially all of the Company's Alabama operating assets, real property, permits and
  mining assets) was sold out of a Chapter 11 estate, free and clear of liens and claims,
  to Coal Acquisition LLC / Warrior Met Coal, LLC (formed by the Company's first-lien
  creditors) for approximately $5.4 million cash plus a credit bid of approximately $1.15
  billion and assumption of certain liabilities, while existing collective bargaining
  agreements were rejected, retiree benefits terminated, and the workforce transferred
  only at the Buyer's sole discretion under Buyer-set terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-076243, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-2.1 §§8.5(a)(c)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-16-002829, Form 8-K Item 7.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.01]
