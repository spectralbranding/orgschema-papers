# Outcome sub-dossier — AOL Inc. realized record, ~3-5 years after separation (through FY2013)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction trajectory over the years following the transaction. It contains no
> information about how the transaction was structured at closing. The outcome window is
> fixed at the ~3-5-year horizon after the 2009 separation and is traced through AOL
> Inc.'s Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2013.

## Realized financial trajectory (post-separation, through FY2013)
- Net income (loss) attributable to AOL Inc. by fiscal year: $(782.5) million (2010),
  $13.1 million (2011), $1,048.4 million (2012), and $92.4 million (2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY ended 2013-12-31, Selected Financial Data]
- The 2010 fiscal year included a $1,414.4 million non-cash impairment charge to reduce
  the carrying value of goodwill (and a $106.0 million gain on the sale of the ICQ
  operations). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013, Selected Financial Data footnote]
- The 2012 net income was driven substantially by a one-time asset disposition: AOL sold
  approximately 800 of its patents and related applications to Microsoft, recording a gain
  of $946.5 million (net of transaction costs) on the sale plus $96.0 million of income
  from licensing its retained patent portfolio to Microsoft. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013, Selected Financial Data footnote]
- Total revenues were $2,202.1 million (2011), $2,191.7 million (2012), and $2,319.9
  million (2013). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013, Selected Financial Data]

## Documented disruption at the product/operations interface over time
- The subscription access product continued to erode: domestic AOL subscribers numbered
  3.3 million, 2.8 million, and 2.5 million at December 31 of 2011, 2012, and 2013
  respectively, and subscription revenues were $803.2 million, $705.3 million, and $650.1
  million for those years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The Patch hyperlocal-content product could not be sustained within the operation: AOL
  performed a goodwill impairment analysis after a third-quarter-2013 triggering event in
  the Patch reporting unit and recorded a $17.5 million charge fully impairing Patch's
  goodwill; 2013 restructuring "primarily related to our efforts to align our
  organizational structure with our strategy, including the restructuring of our Patch
  operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]
- On January 29, 2014, AOL contributed Patch into a new limited liability company entered
  into as a joint venture with DMEP Corp. dba Hale Global, which would operate and majority
  own the entity; AOL retained a minority interest accounted for as an equity-method
  investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Documented realized dependence on the search-partner substrate
- The advertising product remained dependent on the external Google search relationship:
  advertising revenues associated with the Google relationship were $373.3 million for the
  year ended December 31, 2013, and search advertising comprised approximately 24.1% of
  total advertising revenues; AOL disclosed that a decline or unfavorable change in the
  Google relationship could materially affect its advertising revenues. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~3-5 years after separation AOL swung from a large net loss in the first full
  standalone year (FY2010 net loss of $782.5 million, driven by a $1,414.4 million non-cash
  goodwill impairment) to modest profitability, with the single largest positive result
  (FY2012 net income of $1,048.4 million) attributable chiefly to a one-time $946.5 million
  patent-sale gain rather than operating growth. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net income (loss) attributable to AOL Inc. of
  $(782.5)M (2010) → $13.1M (2011) → $1,048.4M (2012) → $92.4M (2013), with continuing
  erosion of the subscription-access product and a full write-off and divestiture of the
  Patch product operation into a Hale Global-controlled joint venture by early 2014.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058842, Form 10-K FY2013]
