# Structural sub-dossier — SunEdison Semiconductor Limited separation from SunEdison, Inc. and SMP polysilicon joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is a Form 8-K (date of earliest event May 27, 2014; filed June 2, 2014)
  filed under Item 1.01 (Entry Into a Material Definitive Agreement); it enumerates the
  agreements the registrant entered into "in connection with the completion of the
  Company's underwritten initial public offering," which was completed on May 28, 2014.
  It is itself a deal-terms document and reports both the parent separation and the joint
  venture accession described below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- SunEdison Semiconductor Limited (a Singapore company; the "Company") was separated from
  its former parent SunEdison, Inc. by means of an initial public offering; a separation
  agreement dated May 27, 2014 "governs certain pre-IPO transactions between SunEdison and
  the Company, as well as aspects of the relationship between SunEdison and the Company
  following the IPO." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The business separated is the semiconductor-wafer business; the arrangements repeatedly
  distinguish "the Company's semiconductor wafer business" from "SunEdison's solar energy
  business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Customer-facing product: the semiconductor-wafer business was separated into the
  standalone Company at the IPO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Core producing IP did NOT cross as ownership: the Czochralski ("CCZ") silicon crystal
  growth and diamond-coated-wire technology was LICENSED to the Company, not transferred —
  a "non-exclusive, perpetual, non-transferable ... royalty-free" license limited to "the
  semiconductor industry and the production of semiconductor wafers," and "SunEdison
  exclusively own[s] all improvements and other intellectual property developed by the
  Company with respect to the intellectual property licensed under the agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A patent and technology cross-license agreement grants each party exclusive rights in its
  own field of use (Company field limited to semiconductor wafers), and "excludes all
  intellectual property related to CCZ and diamond coated wire" and "FBR and high-pressure
  FBR." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- People/management continuity: on May 28, 2014 the Company entered a four-year employment
  agreement with Dr. Shaker Sadasivam as director, President and Chief Executive Officer,
  who also received IPO option and RSU grants; i.e., existing leadership continued with the
  separated business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Bridging / transitional arrangements at closing (shared substrate)
- A transition services agreement (dated May 27, 2014) has the Company and SunEdison
  "mutually provide each other certain corporate, general and administrative services,
  including services related to information technology and telecommunications, payroll,
  benefits and human resources administration, taxes, real estate and facilities
  management, office management, intellectual property management and research and
  development"; the parties are to transition each service to their own organization "within
  24 months after the completion of the IPO." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company estimated it would pay SunEdison approximately $1,000,000 per year and receive
  approximately $3,500,000 per year under the transition services agreement initially. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A tax matters agreement and a technology joint development and rights agreement further
  bridge the separated substrate; under the latter, "SunEdison is the sole owner of any such
  intellectual property or technology that SunEdison reasonably determines is necessary or
  useful to the development, application or use of CCZ or diamond coated wire," and it
  provides for employee secondment and shared workspace/laboratory space. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## The joint venture at closing (SMP)
- On May 27, 2014, SunEdison contributed to the Company 2,877,700 shares in SMP Ltd (which
  SunEdison had acquired from Samsung Fine Chemicals Co., Ltd.); "upon the contribution, SMP
  is owned approximately 35% by the Company, approximately 50% by a subsidiary of SunEdison
  and approximately 15% by Samsung Fine Chemicals Co., Ltd." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company acceded to the venture via a Joinder and Amendment Agreement to the SMP Joint
  Venture Agreement (dated May 27, 2014, among Samsung Fine Chemicals Co., Ltd., SunEdison
  Products Singapore Pte. Ltd. and SunEdison Semiconductor B.V.), "whereby the Company became
  a party to and undertook its pro rata share of the obligations of the SMP joint venture
  agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 10.11]
- Purpose and operations: "the joint venture parties formed SMP to construct, own and operate
  a facility in South Korea to produce high purity polysilicon, including electronic grade
  polysilicon," with expected initial annual capacity of approximately 10,000 metric tons;
  the facility was "currently under construction and is expected to be completed in the second
  half of 2014." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Offtake and transfer terms: once operational, "SMP is required to sell to the joint venture
  partners their pro rata share ... of SMP's polysilicon production at prices negotiated and
  mutually agreed upon ... based on a standard cost plus a markup," and until February 15,
  2019 the partners "have agreed not to transfer interests in SMP to any party other than
  respective affiliates," with a right of first refusal thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Financing at closing
- Concurrent with the IPO, the Company and its subsidiary SunEdison Semiconductor B.V. entered
  a senior secured credit facility (May 27, 2014) providing a term loan up to $210,000,000
  (five-year term ending May 27, 2019) and a revolving facility up to $50,000,000; term-loan
  proceeds were used in part "to repay amounts outstanding under intercompany loans owed by
  the Company and its ... subsidiaries to SunEdison International, Inc., its controlling
  shareholder, and SunEdison." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing the semiconductor-wafer PRODUCT and its leadership separated into a standalone
  IPO'd company, while the producing CCZ/diamond-coated-wire technology remained owned by the
  former parent and was licensed back under field-of-use restrictions; a 24-month transition
  services agreement and related tax, cross-license and joint-development agreements bridged
  the shared corporate and IP substrate rather than transferring it whole; and the Company
  simultaneously acceded (via a joinder) to a three-party polysilicon manufacturing joint
  venture (SMP: ~35% Company / ~50% SunEdison subsidiary / ~15% Samsung Fine Chemicals) whose
  Korean facility was still under construction and governed by cost-plus offtake and transfer
  restrictions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-222549, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
