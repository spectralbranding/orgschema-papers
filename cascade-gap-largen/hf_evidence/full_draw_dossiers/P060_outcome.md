## Documented pre-window realized trajectory (before the outcome window; primary filings)
- For fiscal 2013 the registrant reported a net loss of $400.2 million, compared with a loss of
  $139.4 million in 2012 — the best-documented realized scalar. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-14-000005, Form 10-K MD&A]
- Cash and cash equivalents fell to $179.8 million at December 31, 2013 from $535.7 million at
  December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-14-000005, Form 10-K MD&A Liquidity Outlook]
- On March 4, 2014 the registrant announced a program to close up to 1,100 underperforming stores,
  which produced a non-cash fixed-asset impairment charge of $11.2 million and an inventory write-down
  of $10.1 million reflected in the 2013 financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-14-000005, Form 10-K MD&A Store Closure Program]
- On February 2, 2015 the NYSE determined the registrant's common stock should be delisted, citing
  market capitalization and stockholders' equity below the $50 million continued-listing minimums;
  trading was suspended after the close that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-15-000007, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- On February 5, 2015 RadioShack Corporation and its direct and indirect domestic subsidiaries filed
  voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, entering a
  Debtor-in-Possession credit facility of approximately $285 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- In subsequent 2015 filings the registrant reported under the changed name "RS Legacy Corporation."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-15-000015, Form 8-K registrant name]
