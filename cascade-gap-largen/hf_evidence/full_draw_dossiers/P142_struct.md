## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement): on October 10, 2012 Quad/Graphics, Inc. ("Quad/Graphics," a Wisconsin corporation, NYSE: QUAD) and Vertis Holdings, Inc. ("Vertis," Baltimore, MD) announced the execution of an Asset Purchase Agreement under which Quad/Graphics Marketing, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, will acquire substantially all of the assets comprising Vertis' businesses for $258.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The $258.5 million included the payment of approximately $88.5 million for current assets in excess of normalized working capital requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction type as structured
- The transaction was structured as an asset acquisition (not a stock/whole-company acquisition): the Company would acquire "substantially all of the assets comprising Vertis' businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- To facilitate the intended sale, Vertis and its subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code and simultaneously filed documents seeking Bankruptcy Court approval of the proposed Asset Agreement; the sale was to proceed as a Court-supervised auction process under Section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code, with the Asset Agreement comprising the initial stalking-horse bid subject to competing higher/better offers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Vertis obtained $150 million in debtor-in-possession financing from a group of lenders led by GE Capital, Restructuring Finance, to operate its business as usual until the sale closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K EX-99 (press release)]
- Vertis' board and senior management accepted the offer following a several-month review of strategic opportunities; Vertis had the support of its lenders with respect to the sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K EX-99]

## What crossed the boundary — the acquired asset/product
- The customer-facing asset is Vertis' businesses in retail advertising inserts, direct marketing and in-store marketing solutions; Vertis is described as a marketing-communications company delivering advertising, direct marketing and interactive solutions, with offerings including integrated data solutions, digital program management systems, creative services, print and mail production, logistics, out-of-home, and business-process outsourcing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K EX-99 ("About Vertis")]
- Vertis was represented to generate approximately $1.1 billion in revenues and approximately $60 million in EBITDA (adjusted for restructuring, impairment and other transaction-related expenses) during fiscal year 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K EX-99]

## What did NOT cross — retained liabilities
- As an asset acquisition, the Company would not assume certain liabilities of Vertis and its subsidiaries, including their underfunded pension and retirement obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## People / processes provisions (as structured)
- The press release framed the transaction as providing "continuity, financial stability and continued business investment for Vertis' clients and employees," and stated the Company "look[s] forward to welcoming Vertis' clients and employees"; Vertis filed first-day motions seeking authority to continue paying employee wages and benefits during the Chapter 11 case. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K EX-99]
- Committed contractual terms for transfer of the workforce/operating organization (e.g. an APA employee-transfer section) were not set out in the drawn 8-K or its press-release exhibit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-99]
- Integration of operating processes was expressly deferred to after closing: "The Company intends to announce its plans for integration following the completion of the sale process, including any changes to the combined companies' manufacturing and service platform." [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Financing, legal vehicle and regulatory conditions
- The acquiring vehicle was Quad/Graphics Marketing, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company; Quad/Graphics intended to use cash on hand and draw on its revolving credit facility to finance the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Completion was subject to Bankruptcy Court approval plus customary conditions and regulatory approvals, including expiration or termination of the applicable waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Consummation of the sale (closing)
- On January 16, 2013 Quad/Graphics completed its acquisition of substantially all of the assets of Vertis; the total purchase price paid at closing was $267 million (including $97 million for current assets in excess of normalized working capital requirements), for a net purchase price of $170 million, financed with cash on hand and a draw on the revolving credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-13-000002, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At completion, the combined company was described as employing approximately 25,000 employees from more than 70 print-production facilities in North America, Latin America and Europe. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-13-000002, Form 8-K EX-99 (completion press release)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured and consummated as a distressed-asset acquisition: Quad/Graphics Marketing, LLC (a wholly owned Quad/Graphics subsidiary) purchased substantially all of the assets comprising Vertis' retail-insert, direct-marketing and in-store-marketing businesses out of Vertis' Chapter 11 estate via a Section 363 stalking-horse/auction process, for a total closing price of $267 million ($170 million net), financed with cash and a revolver draw, while certain Vertis liabilities — including underfunded pension and retirement obligations — were not assumed; committed workforce-transfer terms and the manufacturing/service-platform integration plan were not set at signing and were expressly deferred to after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-12-000026, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-99] [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-13-000002, Form 8-K Item 2.01 / EX-99]
