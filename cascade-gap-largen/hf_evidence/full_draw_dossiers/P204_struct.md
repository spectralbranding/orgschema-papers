# Structural sub-dossier — Orbital ATK, Inc. acquisition by Northrop Grumman Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On September 17, 2017, Orbital ATK, Inc. ("Orbital ATK") and Northrop Grumman
  Corporation ("Northrop Grumman") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, under
  which Neptune Merger, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Northrop Grumman ("Sub"),
  would merge with and into Orbital ATK, with Orbital ATK continuing as the surviving
  corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Northrop Grumman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A p.1 & "The Merger" p.67]
- Orbital ATK, headquartered in Dulles, Virginia, conducted business in three segments
  at the time of the merger agreement: Flight Systems Group (rockets/launch vehicles and
  aircraft/launch-vehicle structures), Defense Systems Group (military ammunition and
  tactical-missile propulsion systems), and Space Systems Group (small/medium-class
  satellites and human-rated space systems). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A p.1]
- Consideration: each outstanding share of Orbital ATK common stock would be converted
  into the right to receive $134.50 in cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A "Merger Consideration" p.67]
- The merger was subject to Orbital ATK stockholder approval and to receipt of
  governmental/regulatory approvals, including expiration or termination of the waiting
  period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act (a joint HSR filing was
  required with the DOJ Antitrust Division and the FTC). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals" pp.65,321]
- The closing occurred June 6, 2018; Sub merged with and into Orbital ATK at
  approximately 4:05 p.m. Eastern time, with Orbital ATK surviving as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Northrop Grumman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K "Introductory Note"]
- At closing, Orbital ATK's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and
  restated in their entirety and the company's name was changed from Orbital ATK, Inc.
  to Northrop Grumman Innovation Systems, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- The legal vehicle: the whole legal entity (all outstanding shares) was acquired via a
  reverse triangular merger; Orbital ATK survived as a corporate shell that became a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Northrop Grumman, rather than a divisional/asset purchase.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A "The Merger" p.67]
- Controlling intent / governance: at the effective time all of Orbital ATK's directors
  voluntarily resigned and the directors of Sub became the directors of the surviving
  corporation; all of Orbital ATK's officers ceased to be officers and the officers of
  Sub became the officers of the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The customer-facing product/asset (the acquired businesses' launch vehicles, tactical
  missile/ammunition products, and satellite systems) transferred with the acquired
  legal entity as a going concern, not as a stripped asset sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A p.1]
- People/processes bridge: the merger agreement obligated Orbital ATK, pending closing,
  to conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and to
  "use reasonable best efforts to keep available the services of its current officers
  and other employees, to preserve its assets and preserve its relationships with
  material customers, suppliers, distributors." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A "Conduct of Business Pending the Merger" p.~112]
- A post-closing employee-matters covenant required Northrop Grumman, for the 12-month
  period following the closing date, to provide continuing employees with base
  compensation, target incentive opportunities, and severance benefits no less favorable
  in the aggregate than immediately before the merger, and to recognize prior service
  for eligibility, vesting, and benefit accrual under Parent benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Sec. 5.05(a)-(b), Annex A]
- A separate covenant required the parties, following execution of the agreement, to
  "cooperate to ensure an orderly transition and integration process ... in order to
  minimize the disruption to, and preserve the value of, the business of the Surviving
  Corporation and its Subsidiaries." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Sec. 5.02(b), Annex A]
- Financing: the aggregate merger consideration payable by Northrop Grumman was
  approximately $7.7 billion, funded from proceeds of debt financing completed in
  October 2017 and cash on hand; this is disclosed as a closing-financing fact, not a
  target-company operating baseline. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- On the closing date, in connection with consummation of the merger, Orbital ATK
  repaid and terminated all amounts outstanding under its pre-existing 2015 senior
  secured credit facilities with Wells Fargo Bank as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- Listing status: on the closing date the company notified the NYSE that its common
  stock had been cancelled/converted and requested the NYSE file a notification of
  removal from listing on Form 25; the company stated its intent to file a Form 15 to
  deregister its common stock under Section 12(g). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-entity, all-cash reverse triangular merger (not a
  spin-off, carve-out, or asset purchase): Orbital ATK's legal entity, product lines,
  and workforce transferred together into the acquirer's ownership structure, with
  governance (board/officers) replaced immediately at closing and the entity renamed,
  while a contractual bridge (12-month employee-matters continuity covenant plus a
  general transition/integration-cooperation covenant) governed the shared substrate
  through the transition period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006525, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Secs. 5.02(b), 5.05, Annex A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-038649, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 5.02, 5.03]
