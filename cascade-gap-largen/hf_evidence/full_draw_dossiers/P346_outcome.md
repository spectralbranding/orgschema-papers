## Realized financial trajectory (FY2018-FY2019, consolidated results including the
former CB&I business from the closing date forward)

- Per the FY2019 Form 10-K's five-year selected financial data, the registrant's net
  result attributable to it was a loss of $2,687 million for fiscal year 2018 (the
  closing year) and a loss of $2,909 million for fiscal year 2019, compared with net
  income of $179 million in fiscal year 2017 (the year before the transaction). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Consolidated revenues rose from $2,985 million (2017) to $6,705 million (2018) to
  $8,431 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Fiscal year 2018 results included a goodwill write-down of $2,168 million, recorded
  following the registrant's "first annual quantitative impairment analysis during the
  fourth quarter of 2018" — i.e., in the first full quarter after the transaction closed.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019, MD&A]
- Fiscal year 2019 results included further asset write-downs of approximately $1,430
  million (described as $1.1 billion in the "NCSA" segment and $319 million in the "EARC"
  segment). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019, MD&A]
- The FY2019 Form 10-K's consolidated financial statements disclosed that the
  registrant's Chapter 11 filing (below) and weak industry conditions had "negatively
  impacted" its results of operations and cash flows and that "these factors raise
  substantial doubt about our ability to continue as a going concern." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019, MD&A / Note 2]

## Bankruptcy filing and reorganization

- On January 21, 2020 — about 20 months after the transaction closed — McDermott
  International, Inc. and certain subsidiaries entered a Restructuring Support Agreement
  and commenced a Joint Prepackaged Chapter 11 case in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the
  Southern District of Texas, jointly administered as *In re McDermott International,
  Inc., et al.*, No. 20-30336. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-010618, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Under the Restructuring Support Agreement and Plan, subsidiaries agreed to sell the
  "Lummus Technology" division — described in the filing as the same "Technology
  Business" that McDermott subsidiaries had separately paid cash to acquire from CB&I at
  the 2018 closing — to a buyer, Illuminate Buyer, LLC, for a purchase price of
  approximately $2.725 billion, subject to Bankruptcy Court approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-077117, Form 8-K, "Lummus Technology Sale"]
- On March 12, 2020, the Bankruptcy Court entered an order confirming the Second Amended
  Joint Prepackaged Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization and approving the Lummus Technology
  sale in its entirety; under the Plan, all existing equity interests in the registrant
  (approximately 193,081,224 shares outstanding as of February 26, 2020) were to be
  cancelled, released and extinguished without distribution, with certain creditor
  classes instead receiving new common stock and warrants of "Reorganized McDermott."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-077117, Form 8-K, "Capital Structure"]
- The Plan became effective June 30, 2020; the registrant's own Form 15 states that on
  that date "the Debtors emerged from bankruptcy" and that all previously issued and
  outstanding equity interests, including the pre-emergence common stock, "were
  automatically cancelled and extinguished." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-184065, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- The party that ceased to exist in the transaction deregistered from SEC reporting
  within about two weeks of closing. The continuing/surviving registrant absorbed the
  acquired business into its consolidated results and reported large net losses in both
  full fiscal years following closing ($2,687 million in 2018; $2,909 million in 2019),
  alongside roughly $3.6 billion in combined goodwill write-downs recorded across those
  same two years, disclosed substantial doubt about its ability to continue as a going
  concern, and — approximately 20 months after closing — filed for Chapter 11
  protection. Its confirmed reorganization plan, effective June 30, 2020, cancelled all
  pre-emergence equity (including that issued to the acquired company's former
  shareholders) and required the sale of the technology business that had been carved
  out and separately cash-purchased at closing, for a price higher than the cash paid to
  acquire it. The registrant then terminated its own SEC registration, and no further
  periodic report exists in the public record beyond that point; the full 3-5-year
  (2021-2023) outcome window is therefore UNCERTAIN for lack of a public record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-007841, Form 10-K FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-077117, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-184065, Form 15-12B]
