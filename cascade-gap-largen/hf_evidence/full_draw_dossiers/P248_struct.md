# Structural sub-dossier — Gener8 Maritime, Inc. merger with Euronav NV (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Gener8 Maritime, Inc., a Marshall Islands corporation, entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger dated December 20, 2017 with Euronav NV, a corporation organized under
  the laws of the Kingdom of Belgium, and Euronav MI Inc., a Marshall Islands corporation
  and wholly-owned subsidiary of Euronav ("Merger Sub"); Merger Sub was to merge with and
  into Gener8, with Gener8 continuing its corporate existence as the surviving
  corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Euronav. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, each outstanding Gener8 common share was to be canceled and
  automatically converted into the right to receive 0.7272 of an Euronav ordinary share
  (the "Exchange Ratio"); Euronav agreed to apply to list the shares issued in the merger
  on the NYSE and on Euronext Brussels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- Approval required a majority vote of Gener8's outstanding common shares; under a Voting
  Agreement, certain significant holders — including all of Gener8's then-current
  directors and executive officers other than Nicolas Busch, representing approximately
  42% of outstanding shares — agreed to vote in favor of the Merger Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger completed on June 12, 2018; immediately following completion, pre-merger
  Euronav shareholders held approximately 72% of the combined company and former Gener8
  shareholders held approximately 28%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What each party comprised at closing (product/asset tier)
- As of May 2, 2018, Gener8 owned a fleet of 29 tankers on the water (21 VLCC, six
  Suezmax, two Panamax vessels; approximately 7.4 million dwt), the result of a prior
  combination of General Maritime Corporation and Navig8 Crude Tankers Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- As of May 2, 2018, Euronav owned or operated a fleet of 53 vessels (approximately 13.4
  million dwt: 28 VLCCs, 1 V-Plus, 22 Suezmax vessels, and two FSOs in which Euronav held
  a 50% interest). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the Merger, and assuming a related sale of six Gener8 VLCCs to International
  Seaways and a purchase of one vessel from International Seaways, the resulting
  "Combined Fleet" was expected to comprise two V-Plus crude carriers, 43 VLCCs
  (including four chartered-in vessels), 28 Suezmax vessels, two Panamax vessels, and two
  FSOs, with an aggregate carrying capacity of approximately 19.4 million dwt. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- Concurrent with the Merger Agreement, Euronav and Merger Sub entered a stock purchase
  and sale agreement with Seaways Holding Corporation (a subsidiary of International
  Seaways, an unaffiliated third party) for the sale of six Gener8 VLCCs by acquisition of
  their owning entities for an aggregate purchase price of up to $434 million, subject to
  certain lenders' consent; a related memorandum of agreement provided for Euronav's
  purchase of one vessel from International Seaways. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]

## Operational/process tier — commercial vs. technical management
- Substantially all vessels in Gener8's fleet were contracted to operate in tanker pools
  managed by entities affiliated with the Navig8 Group, an independent vessel pool
  manager; Gener8's own vessel management was conducted through its subsidiary Gener8
  Maritime Management LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- Euronav disclosed its intention to employ, as soon as commercially practicable, the
  Gener8 vessels on the spot market, including within the Tankers International Pool, and
  stated that upon completion of the Merger the combined company might seek to withdraw
  vessels from the existing Navig8-affiliated pools or terminate those pool agreements.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- Gener8 director Nicolas Busch, who abstained from board votes approving the Merger due
  to a conflict of interest, was associated with the Navig8 Group, "a company which
  commercially managed substantially all of Gener8's vessels." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]

## People/management tier
- At the Effective Time, all members of Gener8's board of directors and all board
  committees resigned; Paddy Rodgers and Brian Gallagher (Euronav nominees) became the
  sole members of the board, with Rodgers becoming President and Gallagher becoming Vice
  President and Secretary of the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- At Euronav's request, Gener8's senior officers relinquished their positions as of the
  Effective Time: Peter Georgiopoulos (Chief Executive Officer), Leonard Vrondissis
  (EVP, CFO and Secretary), John Tavlarios (EVP and COO), and George Fikaris (VP); Dean
  Scaglione (Controller and Treasurer) continued working for the company in a transitional
  capacity, currently expected to last through August 2018, under a working notice
  separation agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Milton Gonzales, previously Manager and Technical Director of Gener8 Maritime
  Management LLC, was appointed Chief Operating Officer of the surviving company for a
  transitional period expected to last through October 2018; Sean Bradley, Manager and
  Commercial Director of Gener8 Maritime Management LLC, also relinquished his position at
  the Effective Time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- In connection with the Merger, Georgiopoulos, Vrondissis, Gonzales, and Tavlarios were
  each permitted to receive a transaction bonus and/or severance payment upon closing, and
  Gener8 was permitted to waive applicable non-competition covenants for each of them as
  of closing; Georgiopoulos was additionally permitted to enter a post-termination
  consulting services agreement with the company terminating on the first anniversary of
  his termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle / governance / listing at closing
- At the Effective Time, Gener8's articles of incorporation and bylaws were amended and
  restated "to be in a form appropriate for a wholly-owned subsidiary," and the surviving
  corporation was renamed Euronav MI II Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- Trading in Gener8 common stock on the NYSE (symbol "GNRT") ceased at the close of the
  market on June 11, 2018 in connection with the Merger; on June 12, 2018 the company
  notified the NYSE of the closing and expected the NYSE to file a notification with the
  SEC to remove the common stock from trading and registration under Section 12(b) of
  the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Euronav agreed, per the Merger Agreement, to take actions necessary so that Steven
  Smith (a then-current Gener8 director) would be proposed for election to Euronav's own
  board of directors at Euronav's next annual shareholder meeting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]
- As of December 31, 2017, Euronav intended to assume $1,358.0 million of Gener8's
  existing indebtedness in connection with completion of the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a 100%-stock-for-stock whole-company merger — folding
  Gener8's on-the-water fleet and existing indebtedness into Euronav's Combined Fleet and
  balance sheet at closing — with a same-transaction sale of six Gener8 VLCCs to an
  unaffiliated third party (International Seaways); at closing, Gener8's entire board and
  nearly all its senior executive officers were replaced by Euronav nominees (one
  technical-operations executive retained only transitionally through 2018), while the
  vessels' pre-existing commercial-pool management arrangements with the Navig8 Group
  were flagged by the acquirer as arrangements it might unwind after closing; Gener8 was
  renamed, re-registered as a wholly-owned subsidiary, and its shares ceased NYSE trading
  the day before the merger closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-027198, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039613, Form 8-K]
