## Public-reporting status
- The entity remained an independent SEC registrant after the separation and filed its own
  annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2019, from which the
  realized-record facts below are drawn. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY ended 2019-09-30]

## Realized financial trajectory (~3-year horizon)
- Net income (loss) attributable to Adient, per the five-year selected financial data:
  fiscal 2019 $(491) million, fiscal 2018 $(1,685) million, fiscal 2017 $877 million,
  fiscal 2016 $(1,546) million, fiscal 2015 $460 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Net sales declined to $16,526 million in fiscal 2019 from $17,439 million in fiscal 2018,
  and gross profit fell to $801 million from $904 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Diluted loss per share was $(5.25) in fiscal 2019 and $(18.06) in fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The narrower fiscal 2019 net loss versus fiscal 2018 was "primarily attributable to
  $881 million of prior year net-of-tax impairment charges related to the seat structure
  and mechanism operations ($602 million related to long-lived assets and $279 million
  related to goodwill), and $330 million of prior year net-of-tax charges related to YFAI."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Documented locus of the disruption (product/operations and joint-venture interfaces)
- Fiscal 2018 restructuring costs and impairment charges included "a $1,086 million of
  non-cash pre-tax charges related to seat structure and mechanism operations ($787 million
  relates to long-lived assets and $299 million relates to goodwill), a $358 million
  non-cash pre-tax impairment charge of the YFAI investment, a $49 million non-cash pre-tax
  impairment charge related to assets held for sale and a $46 million restructuring charge."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- The fiscal 2018 goodwill impairment of $299 million was "associated with the seat
  structure and mechanism operations" — the operations that produce and deliver the seating
  product. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Fiscal 2019 charges "primarily relate to a $66 million long-lived asset impairment charge
  associated with the seat structure and mechanism operations, $18 million of impairment
  charges on assets held for sale and $92 million of net restructuring charges." [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Comprehensive loss attributable to Adient was $529 million for fiscal 2019 compared to a
  comprehensive loss of $1,819 million for fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~3 years after separation the entity recorded consecutive large net losses in
  fiscal 2018 ($1,685 million) and fiscal 2019 ($491 million), on declining net sales and
  gross profit, with the largest charges concentrated in the seat structure and mechanism
  operations that produce the seating product and in the YFAI joint-venture investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net loss attributable to Adient of $491 million (fiscal
  2019), following a $1,685 million net loss (fiscal 2018) that included ~$1,086 million of
  non-cash pre-tax charges tied to the seat-structure-and-mechanism operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001670541-19-000057, Form 10-K FY2019]
