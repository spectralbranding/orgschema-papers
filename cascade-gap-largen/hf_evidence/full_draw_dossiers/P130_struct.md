# Structural sub-dossier — Jarden Corporation combination with Newell Rubbermaid (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn accession, Jarden Corporation's Form 8-K dated December 14, 2015, is
  primarily a governance filing: Item 5.03 reports that Jarden's Board adopted a
  Delaware forum-selection amendment to its bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- The same filing's Item 8.01 discloses, and its Exhibit 99.1 furnishes as a joint press
  release, that Jarden and Newell Rubbermaid Inc. had that day entered into an Agreement
  and Plan of Merger; the deal-terms detail below is drawn from that press release and
  from Jarden's own follow-on Item 1.01 filing of the Merger Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K Item 8.01 / EX-99.1]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On December 13, 2015, Jarden, Newell Rubbermaid Inc., and two Newell merger
  subsidiaries (NCPF Acquisition Corp. I and II) entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger providing for a "Combination": Newell Merger Sub would merge into Jarden (the
  "Jarden Merger"), after which Jarden would merge into Successor Merger Sub, leaving
  Successor Merger Sub — holding Jarden's legacy business — as a direct wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Newell; Newell would then rename itself Newell Brands Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402840, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Jarden shareholders were to receive, per share, $21.00 cash plus 0.862 of a Newell
  common share; based on Newell's December 11, 2015 closing price this implied $60 per
  Jarden share, a 24 percent premium to Jarden's 30-day volume-weighted average price;
  Newell shareholders would hold approximately 55 percent of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The Combination was structured to qualify as a tax-free reorganization under Internal
  Revenue Code Section 368(a), was not subject to a financing contingency, and carried
  termination fees of $385 million (superior-proposal termination) and $900 million
  (Newell financing failure). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402840, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The merger closed April 15, 2016; Jarden's stockholders' shares were converted into
  the merger consideration and ceased to confer any other stockholder rights, and Jarden
  requested NYSE suspend trading and deregister the stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Items 2.01/3.01/3.03]

## Controlling intent, governance, and business model at closing
- Jarden's pre-closing business model, per its FY2015 Form 10-K, was that of a
  diversified consumer-products company built substantially through a series of
  acquisitions of branded businesses (e.g., Jostens for ~$1.5B in Nov. 2015; Waddington
  for ~$1.35B in Jul. 2015; Rexair for ~$349M in 2014; Yankee Candle for ~$1.8B in
  2013), organized around a stated culture the filing calls "Jarden's DNA." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-482215, Form 10-K "Business"/"Acquisitions"]
- The Merger Agreement itself constrained Jarden's continuation of that acquisition
  strategy pending closing: the FY2015 10-K states the Agreement "restricts the
  Company's ability to enter into acquisition agreements or consummate acquisitions
  without the prior consent of Newell Rubbermaid." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-482215, Form 10-K "Acquisitions"]
- Post-closing governance was to be led by the acquirer: Newell Rubbermaid's CEO
  Michael B. Polk was to become CEO of Newell Brands, and Newell's non-executive
  Chairman Michael Cowhig was to remain chairman of an expanded 13-member board that
  added only three Jarden directors (including founder/Executive Chairman Martin E.
  Franklin and Co-Founder Ian G. H. Ashken). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- At the effective time of the closing merger, all nine incumbent Jarden directors
  (including Franklin, Ashken, and CEO James E. Lillie) ceased serving on Jarden's
  board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Legal vehicle and financing at closing
- Jarden became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Newell upon the First Merger Effective
  Time; Newell financed the cash portion of the consideration and refinanced/assumed
  outstanding Jarden debt (including terminating Jarden's own ~$3.06 billion credit
  facility) with approximately $9.5 billion of new financing ($8.0 billion of newly
  issued Newell debt securities, a Term Loan Facility, and available cash). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Items 1.02/5.01]

## Product, process, and people crossing the boundary
- The customer-facing asset that crossed was Jarden's brand portfolio itself — described
  at signing as "over 120 trusted, quality brands" across three segments (Branded
  Consumables, Consumer Solutions, Outdoor Solutions) — transferred via the share
  conversion described above, with no carve-out of individual brands at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-482215, Form 10-K "Business"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- At signing (December 2015), the companies announced that Bill Burke, then Newell's
  Chief Operating Officer, would lead the legacy Jarden business post-closing, "working
  closely with Richard Sansone, Jarden's Executive Vice President of Operations," to
  deliver existing business plans — i.e., an acquirer executive was named to head the
  target's operations, paired with a retained Jarden operations officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- At the closing itself, Jarden's top executive officers — CEO James E. Lillie,
  EVP-Administration/General Counsel John E. Capps, and CFO Alan W. LeFevre — ceased to
  be executive officers of Jarden; Capps and LeFevre separately had their employment
  terminated under separation agreements characterized as "Termination Without Cause" in
  connection with a "Change in Control." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Richard T. Sansone's Jarden employment agreement was terminated in connection with the
  mergers, and the filing states he became "an employee of Newell as of the date
  hereof" — i.e., the operations officer named at signing to bridge the businesses
  crossed into the acquirer under a new employment relationship rather than a continued
  Jarden contract. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Transitional arrangements
- The closing-era filings reviewed (the drawn 8-K, the Item 1.01 Merger Agreement 8-K,
  the closing 8-K, and the FY2015 10-K) do not describe a transition-services agreement,
  shared-services agreement, or supply arrangement bridging Jarden and Newell
  operations; the only closing-time bridging instruments identified are the debt
  refinancing/guarantee arrangements and the named individual separation and restricted-
  stock agreements described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Items 1.01/1.02/5.01/5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company combination converted Jarden's outstanding shares into cash and Newell
  stock via a double-merger structure, leaving Jarden's brand portfolio and operating
  entity a wholly-owned Newell subsidiary; Newell's own CEO and board chair led the
  combined company; Jarden's founder-level directors and its CEO/GC/CFO departed at
  closing (two with formal severance), while one named operations executive was
  specifically identified, at signing, to bridge Jarden's day-to-day operations under
  the acquirer's designated operating leader, and separately crossed into Newell
  employment at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-402097, Form 8-K EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-543023, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
