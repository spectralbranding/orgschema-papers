## Realized portfolio scale
- As of December 31, 2016 the registrant's undepreciated gross investment in real estate
  and loans totaled approximately $8.2 billion, representing 2,615 properties (2,541 owned
  outright); owned properties were approximately 98.2% occupied with a weighted average
  remaining lease term of approximately 10.7 years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- During 2016 the registrant purchased 269 properties for an aggregate gross investment of
  $704.9 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Documented organizational disruption over the window
- During the quarter ended December 31, 2015 the registrant "made the strategic decision to
  relocate the Company's headquarters from Scottsdale, Arizona to Dallas, Texas," incurring
  $7.1 million of restructuring charges in 2015, the majority of which were estimated
  employee separation costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Compensation expense in 2015 reflected acceleration of approximately $2.2 million of cash
  and non-cash stock compensation "related to the departure of certain executive officers
  during the year ended December 31, 2015." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Documented internal-control / accounting disruption over the window
- In October 2016 the registrant identified a deficiency in its internal control over
  financial reporting considered a material weakness, "which... led to the restatement of
  our audited consolidated financial statements for the year ended December 31, 2015 and
  our interim unaudited consolidated financial statements for the quarters ended March 31,
  2015, June 30, 2015, September 30, 2015, March 31, 2016 and June 30, 2016." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The material weakness related to "our failure to maintain effective procedures and
  controls over the evaluation of and accounting for goodwill related to the disposal of
  assets and the allocation of goodwill to held for sale assets in determining impairment
  charges"; the registrant stated it had remediated the material weakness as of December
  31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-17-000012, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Tenant concentration and market data
