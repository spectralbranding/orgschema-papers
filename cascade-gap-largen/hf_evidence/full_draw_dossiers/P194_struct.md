# Structural sub-dossier — Rockwell Collins, Inc. acquisition by United Technologies Corporation (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written

> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On September 4, 2017, Rockwell Collins, Inc., United Technologies Corporation ("UTC"),
  and Riveter Merger Sub Corp. (a wholly owned subsidiary of UTC, "Merger Sub") entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which Merger Sub would merge with and into
  Rockwell Collins, with Rockwell Collins surviving the merger as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of UTC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.1]
- Merger Sub was a Delaware corporation incorporated on August 30, 2017 solely to effect
  the merger and had not conducted any activities other than those incidental to its
  formation and the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.13]
- Merger consideration per Rockwell Collins share: $93.33 in cash, without interest, plus
  a fraction of a share of UTC common stock valued (subject to a two-way collar) around
  $46.67, with the exchange ratio fixed at 0.37525 if the UTC stock price was at or above
  $124.37 and at 0.43613 if at or below $107.01. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.1]
- If completed, Rockwell Collins common stock (NYSE: COL) will cease to be listed on the
  NYSE and will be deregistered under the Exchange Act; UTC agreed to use reasonable best
  efforts to have the UTC shares issued in the merger approved for NYSE listing, which is
  itself a condition to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.99]

## Business as structured at closing
- Rockwell Collins is described as supplying cabin interior products and services to
  aircraft manufacturers and airlines, designing, producing, and supporting communications
  and aviation systems for commercial and military customers, and providing information
  management services, organized into four operating segments: Interior Systems,
  Commercial Systems, Government Systems, and Information Management Services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.13]
- Rockwell Collins had completed its own merger with B/E Aerospace on April 13, 2017, and
  the integration of the Rockwell Collins/B/E Aerospace businesses was still in process at
  the time this proxy statement was filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.44]

## What was, and was not, aligned at closing (product/process/people)
- The proxy statement discloses that, due to legal restrictions (antitrust/pre-closing
  limitations), UTC and Rockwell Collins were permitted to conduct only limited planning
  for integration of the two companies and had not yet determined the exact nature of how
  the businesses and operations of the two companies would be combined after the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.44]
- The document states the two companies "currently operate as independent public
  companies" and that success depends on UTC's ability to "successfully combine and
  integrate" them post-closing, including managing risks that integration may not
  materially disrupt customer, supplier, and employee relations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.44]
- Listed integration risk factors include maintaining employee morale, retaining key
  management and other employees, retaining existing business/operational relationships
  (customers, suppliers, employees, other counterparties), and integrating a large number
  of processes, policies, procedures, operations, technologies, and systems. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.44]

## Employee/management continuity provisions at closing
- The merger agreement provides that, for one year after completion, covered employees
  (those actively employed by Rockwell Collins or subsidiaries immediately before closing)
  will receive compensation and benefits from UTC that are substantially comparable in
  value, in the aggregate, to what they had immediately before closing (certain
  retention/change-of-control/transaction bonuses and defined-benefit pension plans
  excepted), with UTC permitted to modify legacy B/E Aerospace employee terms beginning
  January 1, 2019; UTC also agreed to recognize covered employees' prior service with
  Rockwell Collins for vesting, eligibility, vacation-accrual, and severance purposes,
  subject to stated exceptions (401(k) non-elective contributions, retiree medical,
  frozen/grandfathered plans). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.117]
- The merger agreement permits Rockwell Collins to establish a cash-based retention bonus
  program totaling $16 million, allocated among 162 previously identified employees (which
  may include certain executive officers) plus up to 30 additional employees (excluding
  those already covered by a change-of-control agreement or the Rockwell Collins Change in
  Control Severance Plan); certain Rockwell Collins executive officers may become officers
  or employees of UTC or the surviving corporation, or may otherwise be retained to
  provide services to it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.91, p.118]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction is a cash-and-stock merger in which Rockwell Collins
  (a four-segment aerospace/avionics supplier) becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of UTC,
  ceases to be separately listed, and is folded into UTC's existing aerospace business;
  the merger agreement contractually bridges employee compensation/benefits and service
  credit for one year post-closing and authorizes a defined retention-bonus pool, while
  the proxy statement itself discloses that legal restrictions limited the parties to only
  limited pre-closing integration planning and that the precise combination of the two
  companies' businesses and operations had not yet been determined as of filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-365512, DEFM14A p.44, p.99, p.117-118]
