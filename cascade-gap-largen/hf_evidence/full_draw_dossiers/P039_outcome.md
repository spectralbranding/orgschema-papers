## Public-reporting status in the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The company remained an independent SEC-reporting registrant throughout the window,
  filing annual reports on Form 10-K for fiscal 2018 (2019-02-27), fiscal 2019 (2020-02-28)
  and fiscal 2020 (2021-03-02), so a public audited outcome record exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019; SEC accession 0001635881-19-000006, Form 10-K FY2018; SEC accession 0001635881-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized financial trajectory (primary scalar)
- The company reported a net loss of $102.4 million for the year ended December 31, 2019,
  compared with net income of $24.9 million for the year ended December 31, 2018 (and net
  income of $33.9 million for 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Total revenues were $1,317.4 million for 2019, down from $1,360.9 million for 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Impairments rose to $74.4 million in 2019 from $3.9 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
- As of December 31, 2019 the company had approximately 3,600 employees and operated in
  approximately 25 countries, with manufacturing facilities in the U.S., Singapore and the
  United Arab Emirates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Documented disruption at the asset/operations interface over time
- The largest 2019 impairment, $52.6 million, was recorded against idle compressor units in
  the contract operations segment that were determined not to be of a type, configuration,
  condition, make or model cost-efficient to maintain and operate, and were retired from the
  active fleet; a further $0.7 million was recorded on other assets and $3.0 million on
  additional assets reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Product sales gross margin decreased in 2019 compared with 2018 due to lower revenue and
  "higher expenses on a specific project in the North America region," with the margin
  percentage further pressured by a shift in product mix in that region. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Approximately 99% of 2019 product sales revenue was recognized over time, a method that
  requires management to estimate total labor hours and expected profit per project, so
  project-execution estimates directly drove reported margins. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Documented process/controls history disclosed in the in-window report
- The in-window 10-K discloses that senior management had identified errors in the
  application of percentage-of-completion accounting (first identified in the first quarter
  of 2016), and the company continued to carry a "restatement related charges (recoveries),
  net" line in its results, with such charges of $48 thousand in 2019 and negative amounts
  (recoveries) in prior periods. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Customer-concentration record in the window
- For 2019, XTO Energy Inc. and Basrah Gas Company accounted for approximately 21% and 12%
  of total revenue, respectively; no other customer exceeded 10% in 2019, 2018 or 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The company remained an independent public filer across the fixed 2018-2020 window
  (assets retained, no divestiture of the whole company recorded in-window) but its realized
  record deteriorated within it: from net income of $24.9 million in 2018 to a net loss of
  $102.4 million in 2019, driven substantially by a jump in impairments to $74.4 million —
  chiefly a $52.6 million write-down of idle contract-operations compression assets retired
  from service — alongside product-sales margin compression tied to higher expenses on a
  specific North America project, against the backdrop of a disclosed history of
  percentage-of-completion accounting errors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001635881-20-000012, Form 10-K FY2019]
