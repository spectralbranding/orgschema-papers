## Realized fate of the transaction
- During 2012 Nationstar pursued a potential acquisition of the mortgage servicing rights and other assets of a financial services company in bankruptcy and received the right to a break-up fee in the event that it did not win the bankruptcy auction for those assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001520566-13-000021, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2012]
- Nationstar "were not the highest bidder in the auction" and did not win the bankruptcy auction for those assets; it subsequently received the break-up fee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001520566-13-000021, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2012]
- The transaction therefore did not consummate: no ownership boundary was crossed and Nationstar did not acquire the servicing rights, platforms or assets it had sought. [SRC: SEC accession 0001520566-13-000021, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2012]

## Realized outcome scalar over the fixed record
- The single best-documented realized scalar attributable to the transaction is a break-up fee recorded as $15.6 million of contract termination fees for the year ended December 31, 2012, recorded net of the portion payable to Nationstar's co-investment partner in the potential acquisition; Nationstar recognized no contract termination fees in 2011 or 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001520566-13-000021, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2012]
- Because the acquisition did not close, there is no acquired-business outcome scalar (net income/loss, goodwill or asset write-down, impairment, or divestiture) attributable to this transaction; the $15.6 million contract termination fee is the realized item, reported as other income. [SRC: SEC accession 0001520566-13-000021, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2012]
