# Structural sub-dossier — Accenture Holdings plc merger into Accenture plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction merged Accenture Holdings plc (a direct subsidiary of Accenture plc)
  into Accenture plc, with Accenture plc as the surviving entity and Accenture Holdings
  plc dissolved without going into liquidation, under a Common Draft Terms of Merger
  dated December 15, 2017, structured as a "merger by acquisition" under the Irish
  Companies Act 2014, and completed on March 13, 2018 (the "Merger Date"), after the
  Irish High Court confirmed the fairness of the terms to Accenture Holdings
  shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Effect of the Merger"/"Transaction Mechanics"; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080691, Form 8-K Item 3.02]
- At the time of the proposal, Accenture plc described itself as "an Irish public limited
  company with no material assets other than Accenture Holdings ordinary shares," already
  the controlling shareholder of Accenture Holdings and already consolidating Accenture
  Holdings' results in its own financial statements before the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Parties"]

## Consideration and what crossed the boundary
- By operation of Irish law, all assets and liabilities of Accenture Holdings were
  acquired by Accenture plc on the Merger Date; each Accenture Holdings shareholder
  (other than Accenture entities) received one Class A ordinary share of Accenture plc
  per Accenture Holdings ordinary share held, with no cash consideration — Accenture plc
  issued a total of 25,554,372 Class A ordinary shares in the exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Exchange Ratio"; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080691, Form 8-K Item 3.02/8.01]
- Accenture Holdings shareholders "were primarily our former partners and current
  members of our leadership" — the personnel-side holders of the pre-merger
  holding-company shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Exchange of Accenture Holdings Shares"]
- Contracts, agreements and legal proceedings involving Accenture Holdings continued
  with Accenture plc substituted as the party in its place. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080691, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Controlling intent, business model, and operating substrate
- The Board's stated purpose was to eliminate a holding-company tier determined "no
  longer necessary," for cost savings and administrative efficiencies; the company
  described the transaction as "internal and administrative in nature" with "no effect
  on ... how Accenture goes to market, how it manages its business or how it serves its
  clients." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Purpose of this Proposal"/"Effects of Transaction"]
- The proxy's merger-mechanics and effects disclosure covers only the share exchange,
  asset/liability transfer, and contract substitution described above; no
  transition-services, shared-services, tax-matters, or employee-matters agreement is
  disclosed, and Accenture Holdings is not presented as an independently run operating
  business with its own product, process, or personnel functions apart from Accenture
  plc's existing consolidated operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Transaction Mechanics"]
- Accenture plc owned 96% of the outstanding Accenture Holdings voting shares and stated
  it would vote all of its shares in favor, making shareholder approval assured. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Accenture Holdings Shareholder Approval"]

## Listing status change at closing
- Accenture Holdings plc was itself a separate SEC registrant (CIK 0001647339), having
  filed its own periodic reports including a Form 10-K for fiscal year ended August 31,
  2017 (filed October 26, 2017) and its own definitive information statement (Form
  DEFM14C, filed December 15, 2017) on the same merger. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company filing history for CIK 0001647339 (Accenture Holdings plc)]
- On March 13, 2018, Accenture Holdings plc filed its own Form 15-12G terminating
  registration of its ordinary shares (approximately zero holders of record as of the
  certification date), signed by "Accenture plc, as successor by merger to Accenture
  Holdings plc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080792, Form 15-12G (CIK 0001647339)]
- Separately, on the Merger Date Accenture plc redeemed the outstanding Class X ordinary
  shares of Accenture plc held by former Accenture Holdings shareholders and filed its
  own Form 15-12G to terminate registration of that Class X share class (16 holders of
  record as of the certification date); this did not affect Accenture plc's reporting
  obligations for its Class A ordinary shares, which continued to trade and report as
  before. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080691, Form 8-K Item 8.01; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080787, Form 15-12G]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction consolidated a two-tier public holding structure (Accenture plc over
  Accenture Holdings plc, itself over the operating subsidiaries) into a single tier by
  merging the intermediate holding company into the top-level public parent; the
  operating subsidiaries, and Accenture plc's own continuing Class A public listing,
  were stated to be unaffected in how the business goes to market, is managed, or serves
  clients, while both Accenture Holdings plc's own registration and Accenture plc's
  Class X share registration were separately terminated at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-370759, DEFM14A "Effects of Transaction"; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080792, Form 15-12G; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-080787, Form 15-12G]
