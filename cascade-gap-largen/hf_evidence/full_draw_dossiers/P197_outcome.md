## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On November 8, 2017 (about five weeks after the merger closed), the acquired company
  filed a Form 15-12B and a Form 15-12G to deregister its common stock and preferred
  stock, respectively, under the Exchange Act, and its EDGAR filing history shows no Form
  10-K or Form 20-F filed for fiscal year 2018 or any later year; consequently there is no
  standalone audited financial record for the acquired entity covering the ~2020-2022
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-066845, Form 15-12B; SEC accession 0001104659-17-066844, Form 15-12G]

## Acquirer's separately-named integration/restructuring record (Alere-attributed)
- Abbott's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states that "[f]rom 2017 to 2021, Abbott
  management approved restructuring plans as part of the integration of the acquisitions
  of St. Jude Medical, Inc. ... into the Medical Devices segment, and Alere Inc. (Alere)
  into the Diagnostic Products segment, in order to leverage economies of scale and reduce
  costs." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 7 - Restructuring Plans]
- The same note discloses that the accrued liability balance associated with the combined
  St. Jude Medical/Alere integration actions was $41 million as of December 31, 2018, and
  that from 2019 to 2021 Abbott recorded employee-related severance and other charges
  under these actions totaling approximately $95 million ($10 million in 2021, $13 million
  in 2020, $72 million in 2019), leaving an accrued balance of $9 million as of December
  31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 7 - Restructuring Plans]
- The same 10-K's segment-earnings reconciliation (Note 15) states that the "Other, net"
  adjustment to segment operating earnings "includes integration costs associated with the
  acquisition of St. Jude Medical and Alere and restructuring charges in 2021, 2020 and
  2019," indicating the Alere-attributed integration-cost line item was still active
  through the full outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 15 - Segment and Geographic Area Information]

## Product-line continuity context (not itself labeled "Alere" in the acquirer's filing)
- Abbott's FY2021 10-K states that for segment reporting purposes, the Core Laboratory
  Diagnostics, Rapid Diagnostics, Molecular Diagnostics and Point of Care divisions are
  aggregated and reported together as the single Diagnostic Products reportable segment;
  Rapid Diagnostics is not separately reported as its own GAAP segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 15 - Segment and Geographic Area Information]
- Within its narrative (non-segment) disclosure, Abbott states that in Rapid Diagnostics,
  "COVID-19 testing-related sales were $6.6 billion and $2.6 billion in 2021 and 2020,
  respectively," driven by demand for its ID NOW, BinaxNOW and Panbio rapid-testing
  platforms; this text does not itself use the name "Alere." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 7 MD&A]
- The Diagnostic Products reportable segment as a whole (Core Laboratory + Rapid +
  Molecular Diagnostics + Point of Care combined) reported net sales of $15,644 million
  (2021), $10,805 million (2020) and $7,713 million (2019), and operating earnings of
  $6,256 million (2021), $3,725 million (2020) and $1,912 million (2019); these totals are
  not broken out by division and are not specific to any acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 15 - Segment and Geographic Area Information]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired entity deregistered from SEC reporting within weeks of closing and filed no
  further periodic reports, so no standalone financial record for it exists across the
  ~2020-2022 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-066845, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer's own subsequent 10-K filings do separately name the acquisition and track
  an integration/restructuring cost line item attributed to it by name (Alere) through
  fiscal year 2021, declining from a $41 million accrued balance at year-end 2018 to $9
  million at year-end 2021 as roughly $95 million of severance and related charges were
  recognized from 2019-2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 7 - Restructuring Plans]
- A related product platform within the acquirer's Diagnostic Products segment ("Rapid
  Diagnostics," encompassing the ID NOW, BinaxNOW and Panbio brands) reported large
  COVID-19-testing-related revenue growth ($2.6 billion in 2020 to $6.6 billion in 2021),
  but the acquirer's filing does not itself equate this division with the acquired entity
  by name, and the broader Diagnostic Products segment aggregates four internal divisions
  without a standalone financial breakout for any one of them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-025141, Abbott Laboratories Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 7 MD&A and Note 15]
