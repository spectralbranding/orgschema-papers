# Structural sub-dossier — Reynolds American Inc. merger into British American Tobacco (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- British American Tobacco p.l.c. (BAT) already beneficially owned approximately 42% of
  Reynolds American Inc.'s (RAI) outstanding common stock — 601,368,171 shares as of
  June 12, 2017 — held through its wholly owned subsidiaries Brown & Williamson
  Holdings, Inc. (B&W) and Louisville Securities Limited. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Under a merger agreement (entered into and later amended June 8, 2017), an indirect
  wholly owned subsidiary of BAT ("Merger Sub") will merge with and into RAI, with RAI
  continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming an indirect, wholly owned
  subsidiary of BAT. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- RAI shareholders (other than BAT and its subsidiaries and certain excluded holders)
  will receive, for each RAI share, 0.5260 of a BAT American Depositary Share (ADS) plus
  $29.44 in cash; based on pricing at signing this represented approximately $59.64 per
  RAI share, and approximately $65.82 per RAI share based on pricing shortly before the
  shareholder vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Following completion, BAT will beneficially own 100% of RAI's outstanding capital
  stock, and former RAI shareholders (other than BAT) will own approximately 19% of
  BAT's enlarged share capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]

## Pre-existing governance relationship between the parties
- Prior to the merger, RAI, BAT, and B&W were already parties to a governance agreement
  under which entry into a transaction such as the merger required approval of a
  majority of RAI's independent directors other than those designated for nomination by
  B&W; RAI's board had appointed a Transaction Committee of such independent directors
  to evaluate the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- If the merger were not completed, RAI would have remained an independent public
  company with its common stock listed on the NYSE, and BAT would have remained a
  42% beneficial owner of RAI subject to the terms of that governance agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, and what did not
- Legal vehicle: RAI's corporate existence continues in the surviving corporation; its
  articles of incorporation remain in effect (as amended to allow the RAI board size to
  be set by resolution) and Merger Sub's bylaws become the surviving corporation's
  bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Governance/control tier: the individuals serving as directors of Merger Sub
  immediately before completion become the directors of the surviving corporation —
  i.e., BAT's designees replace RAI's pre-merger board — while the individuals serving
  as officers of RAI immediately before completion continue as the officers of the
  surviving corporation, until their resignation, removal, or replacement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Product/operating continuity: in reaching their valuation conclusions, the BAT parties
  treated RAI as "a viable, going concern" and noted that "the combined company will
  continue to operate the businesses of RAI following the merger" — no divestiture of
  RAI's product lines is contemplated at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Corporate headquarters: RAI's pre-merger corporate offices are at 401 North Main
  Street, Winston-Salem, North Carolina; the combined company's headquarters following
  the merger will instead be located in London, United Kingdom, at BAT's then-current
  headquarters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Listing/reporting tier: upon completion there will no longer be any publicly held
  shares of RAI common stock (then listed on the NYSE under "RAI"); RAI common stock
  will be removed from listing on the NYSE and deregistered under the Exchange Act as
  soon as practicable, and RAI will no longer be required to file periodic reports with
  the SEC in respect of that stock, while BAT's ADSs (previously trading with unlisted
  trading privileges on the NYSE MKT) will instead be listed on the NYSE itself under
  "BTI." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Reporting regime going forward: for so long as BAT qualifies as a foreign private
  issuer, BAT will be required to file an annual report on Form 20-F with the SEC,
  rather than the Form 10-K RAI itself had filed as a domestic registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]

## Bridging arrangements and closing conditions
- The drawn filing does not describe a transition-services, shared-services, or
  operating agreement bridging a divided customer-facing product/process/workforce
  substrate; rather, it treats RAI's legal entity, product lines, operating processes,
  and workforce as continuing intact within the surviving corporation, with only the
  governance/control layer (the board of directors) changing hands at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
- Completion was conditioned on, among other things, antitrust clearance: BAT and RAI
  filed Hart-Scott-Rodino Act notifications with the FTC and DOJ on February 6, 2017
  (requesting early termination of the waiting period, which expired March 8, 2017), and
  filed notifications with the Japan Fair Trade Commission on March 23, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, the transaction converts an existing ~42%-owned, separately governed
  public affiliate into a 100%-owned subsidiary: the RAI legal entity, its product
  portfolio, and its operating processes and officers carry forward intact into the
  surviving corporation, while the pre-merger board of directors is replaced by BAT's
  designees, RAI's separate NYSE listing and periodic SEC reporting end, and the
  combined company's corporate headquarters sits at BAT's London address rather than
  RAI's Winston-Salem, North Carolina offices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-203053, DEFM14A]
