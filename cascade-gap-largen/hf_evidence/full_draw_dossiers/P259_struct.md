# Structural sub-dossier — RSP Permian, Inc. merger with Concho Resources Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- RSP Permian, Inc., a Delaware corporation based in Dallas, Texas, and an independent
  oil and natural gas company engaged in the acquisition, exploration, exploitation,
  development and production of unconventional oil and associated liquids-rich natural
  gas reserves in the Permian Basin, agreed to combine with Concho Resources Inc., a
  Delaware corporation based in Midland, Texas, also an independent oil and natural gas
  company operating in the Permian Basin, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated March 27, 2018 among Concho, Green Merger Sub Inc. (a newly formed, wholly owned
  subsidiary of Concho incorporated March 23, 2018 solely to effect the merger, referred
  to as "Merger Sub"), and RSP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties structured the transaction as a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub
  merged with and into RSP, with RSP surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary
  of Concho. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration was all-stock and fixed: each eligible share of RSP common stock was
  converted into the right to receive 0.320 of a share of Concho common stock, with no
  adjustment for changes in either company's stock price; based on shares outstanding as
  of May 31, 2018 and the 0.320 exchange ratio, former RSP stockholders were expected to
  own approximately 25.5% of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- The issuance of Concho shares was registered on Form S-4 (File No. 333-224354),
  declared effective June 6, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed on July 19, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]

## What RSP comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- RSP's acreage was located on large, contiguous acreage blocks in the core of the
  Midland Basin (primarily Midland, Martin, Andrews, Ector and Glasscock counties) and
  the Delaware Basin (Loving and Winkler counties), both sub-basins of the Permian
  Basin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- Concho's own core operations were focused across four operating areas — the Northern
  Delaware Basin, the Southern Delaware Basin, the Midland Basin and the New Mexico
  Shelf — into which RSP's acreage was combined at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- Total consideration for the acquisition, reported by Concho, was approximately 51
  million shares of Concho common stock issued at $148.27 per share, or approximately
  $7.5 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001358071-20-000011, Form 10-K (FY2019), Note 4 "RSP Acquisition"]

## Management/organization tier at closing
- Under the merger agreement, Concho's board was increased by one member and RSP's
  Chief Executive Officer, Steven Gray, was appointed to fill that seat as a Class III
  director; the RSP board had initially sought two continuing board seats but accepted
  one. Concho's own directors and executive officers were expected to continue in their
  current positions after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, all of RSP's own pre-merger directors ceased to be directors of
  RSP; Timothy A. Leach (Concho's CEO) became RSP's sole director and was appointed CEO
  of RSP-as-subsidiary, with Jack F. Harper as President/CFO, E. Joseph Wright as
  EVP/COO, and Brenda R. Schroer as SVP/Chief Accounting Officer/Treasurer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]
- RSP maintained an executive-officer severance plan providing "double trigger"
  payments and benefits to executive officers upon a qualifying termination (without
  cause, or for good reason) occurring within two years of a change of control, meaning
  continued employment of RSP's own executive officers after closing was not assured by
  the merger agreement itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- Concho's subsequently filed annual report states that severance payments to certain
  RSP employees totaling $56 million were recorded in connection with the acquisition
  for the year ended December 31, 2018 (the closing year). [SRC: SEC accession 0001358071-20-000011, Form 10-K (FY2019), Note 4 "RSP Acquisition"]
- RSP and Concho, and separately RSP and former RSP director Steven Gray, entered a
  Non-Competition, Non-Solicitation and Confidentiality Agreement effective upon closing
  under which Mr. Gray was restricted for 12 months from acquiring interests or
  soliciting employees within a defined market area, in exchange for a $425,000 payment
  from Concho. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]

## Bridging arrangements negotiated for the transition
- During negotiation of the merger agreement, the RSP board identified as a priority
  "implementing retention and severance arrangements that would promote employee
  retention during the pendency of the merger," and, in exchange for accepting a single
  continuing board seat rather than two, obtained "a commitment from Concho to keep
  RSP's corporate headquarters in Dallas open for some period of time following the
  merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, RSP and its subsidiary RSP Permian, L.L.C. terminated all outstanding
  commitments under RSP's own credit agreement and redeemed all of its outstanding 6.625%
  and 5.25% senior notes, discharging RSP's pre-merger financing vehicle rather than
  carrying it forward intact into the combined company's capital structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- At the effective time, RSP's Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation was
  amended and restated in accordance with the merger agreement, and the bylaws of
  Merger Sub became the bylaws of RSP (as surviving subsidiary). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]
- In connection with closing, RSP notified the NYSE that its common stock had converted
  into the right to receive Concho shares and requested that NYSE withdraw the listing
  of RSP common stock; NYSE filed a notification of removal from listing on Form 25, and
  RSP's common stock ceased being traded before the market opened on July 19, 2018. RSP
  stated its intention to subsequently file a Form 15 to suspend its Exchange Act
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined RSP's Permian Basin product/asset base (Midland and
  Delaware Basin acreage) into Concho's operations through an all-stock reverse
  triangular merger, while the pre-merger RSP organization — its board, its named
  executive officers (subject to severance-plan payouts on qualifying termination), and
  its stand-alone credit/notes financing — did not carry forward intact: Concho's own
  leadership retained control of the surviving entity, with only RSP's CEO gaining a
  single Concho board seat, and the shared Dallas-office substrate was bridged only by a
  negotiated, time-limited commitment rather than being formally documented in a
  separate, disclosed transitional-services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-186002, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-221512, Form 8-K]
