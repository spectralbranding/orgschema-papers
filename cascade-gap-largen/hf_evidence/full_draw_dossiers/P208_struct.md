# Structural sub-dossier — Patheon N.V. acquisition by Thermo Fisher Scientific (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Patheon N.V., a Dutch public company with limited liability (*naamloze vennootschap*)
  and a leading global provider of outsourced pharmaceutical development and
  manufacturing services, was the subject of a cash tender offer by Thermo Fisher (CN)
  Luxembourg S.à r.l. ("Purchaser"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Thermo Fisher
  Scientific Inc. (a Delaware corporation), at $35.00 per ordinary share, pursuant to a
  Purchase Agreement dated May 15, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001306, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a tender offer followed by a Dutch-law Post-Offer
  Reorganization rather than a US-style merger vote: Patheon's shareholders were asked at
  an Extraordinary General Meeting (held August 2, 2017) to approve, under article
  2:107a of the Dutch Civil Code, an Asset Sale (the sale, transfer and assumption of
  substantially all of Patheon's business, assets and liabilities to Purchaser) together
  with Patheon's own dissolution and liquidation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001306, Form DEFM14A]
- Majority Shareholders holding approximately 75% of outstanding shares (JLL Patheon
  Co-Investment Fund L.P. and other JLL Partners affiliates, and Koninklijke DSM N.V.)
  signed Tender and Support Agreements committing to tender their shares and vote for the
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001306, Form DEFM14A]
- The Offer expired August 28, 2017; 138,406,058 shares (approximately 95.3% of shares
  outstanding) were validly tendered and not withdrawn; Purchaser accepted them for
  payment on August 29, 2017 (the "Offer Closing"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
- Aggregate consideration was approximately $7.2 billion, assuming acquisition of all
  remaining outstanding shares and including assumption of debt, funded from Thermo
  Fisher's balance sheet and prior equity/debt offering proceeds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the boundary — business/product tier and financing entanglements
- Patheon operated as a contract development and manufacturing organization for
  pharmaceutical customers, reported through three segments: Drug Product Services,
  Pharmaceutical Development Services, and Drug Substance Services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001306, Form DEFM14A]
- At the Offer Closing, Patheon's pre-existing financing arrangements were unwound rather
  than assumed intact: Patheon Holdings I B.V. (the "Parent Borrower") repaid in full and
  terminated its Credit Agreement, and redeemed in full its $450 million 7.50% Senior
  Notes due 2022 at 105.625% of principal, releasing all related guarantees and liens. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
- The Shareholders' Agreement among Patheon, JLL Patheon Co-Investment Fund and DSM
  terminated automatically upon the Offer Closing, and a separate Biologics Agreement
  between Patheon affiliates and DSM was terminated in exchange for a $37,900,000 payment
  by Patheon to DSM (an earnout-related accrued liability). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
- Outstanding Patheon equity awards (options, RSUs, PSUs) that were vested were canceled
  and cashed out at the Offer Consideration; unvested awards were converted into Thermo
  Fisher equivalent awards using an exchange ratio of 0.1996919, preserving substantially
  the same vesting terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]

## People/organization tier at closing
- At the Offer Closing, ten named Patheon directors — including Chief Executive Officer
  James C. Mullen — resigned and ceased to be directors or board-committee members; the
  proxy statement states these resignations were not the result of any disagreement over
  Patheon's operations, policies or practices. All former officers of Patheon ceased to
  be officers at the Offer Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
- Anthony H. Smith became Patheon's executive director at the Offer Closing; four other
  individuals became non-executive directors, and two pre-existing independent directors
  (Jeffrey P. McMullen and William B. Hayes) remained on the board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
- For the broader employee base (below the officer tier), the Purchase Agreement bridged
  the transition contractually: for one year after the Offer Closing, each "Continuing
  Employee" was to receive at least the same base salary and target bonus, benefits
  substantially as favorable as similarly situated Thermo Fisher/Purchaser employees, and
  the same severance entitlement as under pre-existing Patheon severance policy; years of
  service were credited toward Thermo Fisher/Purchaser benefit plans; existing labor
  agreements were to be honored. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001306, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On August 22, 2017, Patheon gave notice to the New York Stock Exchange of its intention
  to voluntarily remove its ordinary shares from listing, conditioned on completion of
  the tender offer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001749, Form 8-K]
- Following the Offer Closing, Patheon requested that the NYSE suspend trading in its
  shares after the close of business on September 1, 2017, and a Form 25 notification of
  removal from listing and registration was filed with the SEC that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055224, Form 25]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a Dutch-law tender-offer-plus-asset-sale/liquidation
  mechanism (rather than a triangular merger) with a same-day, wholesale replacement of
  Patheon's board and executive-officer tier at the moment of closing, while the
  underlying contract-development-and-manufacturing business (its three service
  segments) and its broader (non-officer) workforce were bridged forward via one-year
  compensation/benefits-parity and severance commitments in the Purchase Agreement, and
  Patheon's pre-existing acquisition-era debt and shareholder/earnout agreements were
  extinguished at closing rather than carried into the acquirer's capital structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-055475, Form 8-K]
