# Structural sub-dossier — KCG Holdings, Inc. acquisition by Virtu Financial, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On April 20, 2017, KCG Holdings, Inc. (the "Company") and Virtu Financial, Inc.
  ("Virtu") entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger providing for Virtu to acquire
  the Company in an all-cash transaction for $20.00 per share of outstanding Company
  common stock, with Orchestra Merger Sub, Inc., an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  Virtu, merging with and into the Company so that the Company would survive as an
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Virtu. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- The Company is described as "a leading independent securities firm" combining
  technology with client service across market making, agency execution and trading
  venues, and engaging in principal trading via exchange-based electronic market
  making; its Class A common stock traded on the NYSE under "KCG." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- Virtu is described as "a leading technology-enabled market maker and liquidity
  provider," making markets in more than 12,000 securities on more than 235 exchanges,
  markets and liquidity pools in 36 countries, and also generating technology-services
  revenue from proprietary infrastructure and agency execution services for third
  parties; its Class A common stock traded on NASDAQ under "VIRT." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- The Company currently expected to complete the merger in the third quarter of 2017,
  subject to stockholder approval, regulatory approvals, and other closing conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing
- Consideration was 100% cash; Company stockholders would not receive equity in Virtu
  and would not participate in any future earnings growth of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- Company stock options, SARs and RSUs held by directors, executive officers and other
  employees would be cancelled for a cash payment, and Company warrants converted into a
  right to a cash payment upon exercise. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- The initial written proposal presented by Virtu "did not provide for any continuing
  role at the combined company for the Company's directors or executive officers," and
  no such roles had been discussed subsequently between the Company and Virtu as of the
  proxy statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- If the merger completed, Company common stock would be removed from listing on the
  NYSE and deregistered under the Exchange Act, and the Company would no longer file
  periodic reports with the SEC on account of its common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement required, concurrently with closing, the payoff of the Company's
  existing Credit Agreement (dated June 5, 2015, among KCG Americas LLC, the Company,
  Bank of America, N.A., BMO Harris Bank N.A., and other lenders). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]

## People, culture, and organizational continuity
- For continuing employees (Company employees who remained employed by the surviving
  corporation after the effective time), Virtu agreed, for the one-year period following
  closing, to provide base salary/wage and target annual cash bonus opportunities either
  generally comparable to similarly situated Virtu employees or substantially comparable
  to what the Company provided immediately before closing, plus benefits on the same
  basis, and severance under the Company's existing broad-based severance plan for those
  terminated during that year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- Prior to closing, the Company was required to terminate its voluntary deferred
  compensation, incentive unit and deferred cash plans, with Virtu or the surviving
  corporation distributing all balances to participants after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- A disclosed risk factor identified "potential difficulties in the Company employee
  retention as a result of the announcement of the merger" and "the inability of the
  Company to maintain relationships with customers, counterparties and key employees" as
  risks associated with the pendency of the transaction; disclosed pre-signing
  correspondence shows the Company's then-CEO asking Virtu's CEO to "outline Virtu's
  plans with respect to retention of Company employees," with the document not
  disclosing the substance of any such plan beyond the one-year commitments above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the transition (closing-time)
- The merger agreement contained additional covenants on the removal of Company common
  stock from listing, cooperation with stockholder litigation, "integration," and
  employee matters, alongside conventional closing covenants (consents, access to
  information, public announcements). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement required the surviving corporation to maintain the Company's
  existing directors'-and-officers' liability insurance (or a comparable substitute) for
  a six-year tail period following the effective time and to continue existing indemnification rights for current
  and former directors, officers and employees for pre-closing acts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction was a negotiated, all-cash, whole-company merger in
  which the Company's customer-facing securities-trading business (market making,
  agency execution, trading venues) was to be absorbed as an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of Virtu, with cash consideration substituting for any continuing equity
  interest for Company stockholders; the closing documents disclosed no continuing role
  for incumbent Company directors or executive officers at the combined company, while
  providing one-year compensation/benefits continuity commitments for other continuing
  employees and flagging employee-retention risk during the pendency of the deal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-003764, DEFM14A]
