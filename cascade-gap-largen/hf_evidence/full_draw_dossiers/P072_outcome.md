## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- Hilton Grand Vacations Inc. remained an SEC-registered filer and filed a Form 10-K for
  the year ended December 31, 2021 on 2022-03-01, so an audited public record of the
  realized position exists for the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- BRE Ace LLC continued to be disclosed as an ongoing interest of the registrant; the
  registrant held a 25 percent ownership interest in BRE Ace LLC, treated as a variable
  interest entity that the registrant does not consolidate because it is not the primary
  beneficiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 11]

## Realized record of the retained interest (audited)
- The interest was retained, not divested, through the window: as of December 31, 2021 the
  registrant continued to report a 25 percent equity-method interest in BRE Ace LLC, which
  owns the timeshare resort property and related operations known as "Elara, by Hilton
  Grand Vacations." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 21]
- During the year ended December 31, 2021 the registrant received a cash distribution of
  approximately $2 million from its investment in BRE Ace LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 11]
- The carrying amount of the registrant's investments in its two unconsolidated affiliates
  (BRE Ace LLC and 1776 Holdings, LLC, reported together) totaled $59 million at December
  31, 2021 and $51 million at December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 11]
- Those two unconsolidated affiliates had aggregated debt balances of $463 million and
  $454 million at December 31, 2021 and 2020, respectively; the debt is secured by their
  assets and is without recourse to the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 11]
- Equity in earnings from the registrant's unconsolidated affiliates (BRE Ace LLC and 1776
  Holdings, LLC combined) was $10 million (2021), $5 million (2020) and $4 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 21]

## Realized record at the operating / delivery interface (audited)
- The producing/delivering operation stayed under the registrant through the window: the
  registrant continued to earn commissions and other fees under fee-for-service agreements
  with the investees to sell timeshare interests at "Elara, by Hilton Grand Vacations" (and
  at the second affiliate). Commissions and other fees from the two related-party investees
  were $105 million (2021), $55 million (2020) and $136 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 21]
- The registrant reported $20 million and $7 million of outstanding receivables related to
  those fee-for-service agreements in Accounts receivable, net at December 31, 2021 and
  December 31, 2020, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Note 21]
- No impairment, write-down, dissolution, buy-out, or divestiture of the BRE Ace LLC
  interest is disclosed in the FY2021 Annual Report; the relationship is described as a
  continuing equity interest plus ongoing fee-for-service arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Notes 11 and 21]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 3-5-year window the acquired interest was retained and remained
  productive: the registrant held its 25 percent equity-method interest in BRE Ace LLC
  throughout, recorded a ~$2 million distribution from it in 2021, and carried its two
  unconsolidated-affiliate investments at $59 million; the operation that markets, sells and
  manages the underlying resort stayed with the registrant under fee-for-service agreements
  that generated $105 million of commissions and other fees in 2021, with no disclosed
  impairment, restructuring, or divestiture of the interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002564, Form 10-K (FY2021) Notes 11 and 21]
