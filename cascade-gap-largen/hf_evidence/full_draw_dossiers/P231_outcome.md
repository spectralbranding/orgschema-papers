# Outcome sub-dossier — SUPERVALU/UNFI acquired wholesale-and-retail business, realized record ~3-5 years after closing (~FY2021-FY2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. SUPERVALU itself deregistered shortly after
> closing and filed no further periodic reports, so the realized record here is drawn
> from the acquirer's (United Natural Foods, Inc., "UNFI," CIK 1020859) own 10-Ks,
> which separately and repeatedly name and disclose facts specific to the acquired
> business. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- SUPERVALU filed a Form 15-12B on November 6, 2018 to deregister its common stock
  and terminate its reporting obligations under the Exchange Act; no Form 10-K was
  filed by SUPERVALU after that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-066343, Form 15-12B]
- Because SUPERVALU itself filed no post-close periodic reports, this dossier draws
  the realized record from UNFI's fiscal-year 2021 (year ended July 31, 2021) and
  fiscal-year 2022 (year ended July 30, 2022) Form 10-Ks, both of which fall within a
  ~3-5-year window after the October 22, 2018 closing and each of which names the
  acquired business specifically rather than only reporting UNFI's undifferentiated
  consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized record of the acquired business (acquirer's separate disclosure)
- UNFI's FY2021 10-K states it "acquired Supervalu for an aggregate purchase price of
  approximately $2.3 billion, which included the assumption of outstanding debt and
  liabilities," on October 22, 2018 (the "Supervalu acquisition date"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- UNFI reorganized the acquired Wholesale operations into its Wholesale reportable
  segment, run through four U.S. geographic regions (Atlantic, South, Central,
  Pacific). [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Of the Retail banners acquired via Supervalu, the Hornbacher's, Shop 'n Save and
  Shop 'n Save East banners, plus certain Shoppers stores, were disposed of and are
  presented as discontinued operations; Cub Foods and the remaining Shoppers stores
  were retained within continuing Retail operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The FY2021 10-K states: "We had previously announced our intention to thoughtfully
  and economically divest our retail businesses acquired as part of the Supervalu
  acquisition to focus on our core Wholesale distribution business. At this time, we
  do not have any current plans to divest our Retail business" — a reversal, by the
  ~3-year mark, of UNFI's own earlier stated plan for the acquired Retail tier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Fiscal-2020 restructuring, acquisition and integration-related expenses totaled $87
  million, including $42 million of integration-related costs and $40 million of
  closed-property reserve charges "related to the divestiture of retail banners." [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- As of July 30, 2022, the retained Retail segment (the Cub Foods and Shoppers
  banners originally part of the acquired business) comprised 73 stores (54 Cub Foods,
  19 Shoppers). [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]
- UNFI's FY2022 10-K states the company "has prioritized the integration of Supervalu
  and debt reduction and has not undertaken any additional material acquisitions"
  since closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Goodwill / impairment (best-documented outcome scalar)
- UNFI recorded a goodwill impairment charge of $422 million in the first quarter of
  fiscal 2020, reflecting "the impairment of all of the U.S. Wholesale reporting
  unit's goodwill," attributed to a sustained decrease in market capitalization and
  enterprise value together with "a goodwill finalization charge attributable to the
  Supervalu acquisition"; fiscal 2019 separately carried a goodwill impairment charge
  the filing attributes directly "to the Supervalu acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Wholesale-segment goodwill fell from $432 million (as of August 3, 2019) to $10
  million (as of August 1, 2020) and remained at $10 million through July 31, 2021
  and July 30, 2022. Cumulative goodwill-impairment charges against the Wholesale
  reporting unit were $293 million (fiscal 2019), $717 million (fiscal 2020), $717
  million (fiscal 2021), and $717 million (fiscal 2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Workforce / organization continuity over time
- As of July 31, 2021, approximately 11,000 of UNFI's 28,300 employees (about 39%)
  were covered by 48 collective bargaining agreements, "including agreements under
  negotiation," which expire through April 2027; the risk-factor discussion
  specifically states that "the terms of some legacy Supervalu collective bargaining
  agreements may limit our ability to increase efficiencies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021]
- As of July 30, 2022, approximately 10,900 of 30,300 employees (about 36%) were
  covered by 48 collective bargaining agreements, expiring through May 7, 2027; the
  equivalent FY2022 risk-factor language refers only generically to "our collective
  bargaining agreements" and no longer singles out "legacy Supervalu" agreements by
  name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Although SUPERVALU itself deregistered and ceased periodic reporting within weeks
  of closing, the acquirer's own subsequent 10-Ks separately and repeatedly name and
  quantify facts tied specifically to the acquired business, so a sourced record of
  the acquired business's realized trajectory is available for the ~3-5-year window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The realized record over that window shows: (1) an acquisition-attributed goodwill
  impairment that eliminated essentially all of the Wholesale reporting unit's
  goodwill within about 21 months of closing, holding at $717 million cumulative
  through fiscal 2022; (2) disposal, as discontinued operations, of several of the
  acquired Retail banners, alongside an announced-then-reversed intention to divest
  the remaining acquired Retail business; and (3) a workforce-continuity issue
  specifically attributed to "legacy Supervalu" collective-bargaining terms as of the
  FY2021 filing, with that specific attribution dropped from the equivalent FY2022
  language. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-21-000083, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001020859-22-000054, Form 10-K FY2022]
