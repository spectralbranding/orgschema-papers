## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- AHGP (CIK 0001344980) filed a Form 15-12G on 2018-06-11 to deregister its securities
  under the Exchange Act; AHGP does not itself file periodic reports (10-K) after this
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-039401, Form 15-12G, filed 2018-06-11]
- ARLP (CIK 0001086600) is the surviving publicly traded registrant and continues to file
  annual reports on Form 10-K covering the combined business. The trajectory below is
  drawn from ARLP's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022, filed 2023-02-24. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized financial trajectory (within window)
- Total revenues increased 53.3% to a record $2.41 billion in 2022, compared to $1.57
  billion in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A]
- Net income attributable to ARLP rose 224.0% to a record $577.2 million ($4.39 per basic
  and diluted limited partner unit) in 2022, compared to $178.2 million ($1.36 per unit)
  in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A]
- Coal sales price realizations increased 37.4% to a record $59.07 per ton sold in 2022,
  compared to $42.98 per ton sold in 2021, on higher sales volumes (35.6 million tons sold
  in 2022 versus 32.3 million tons in 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A]
- On January 27, 2023, ARLP declared a quarterly per-unit distribution of $0.70, totaling
  approximately $89.0 million, paid February 14, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 12 Partners' Capital]

## Documented disruption and recovery over time
- The FY2022 Form 10-K's quarterly-distribution table (reporting 2020 through 2022)
  shows the per-unit distribution paid was $0.400 in Q1 2020 and $0.00 in Q2, Q3, and Q4
  of 2020, followed by partial resumption in 2021 ($0.100, $0.100, $0.200 in Q2-Q4;
  $0.00 in Q1) and further increases through 2022 ($0.250 to $0.500 across the four
  quarters). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 12 Partners' Capital]
- During the first quarter of 2020, the company recognized a $132.0 million non-cash
  goodwill impairment charge tied to its Hamilton mine, citing "adverse industry and
  market developments, including the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic," and a related
  $23.5 million long-lived asset impairment charge in its Illinois Basin Coal Operations
  segment tied in part to "weakened coal market conditions including the impact of the
  COVID-19 pandemic." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Notes 4-5]
- The same Form 10-K states there were no goodwill impairments during 2022 or 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 4]
- The Form 10-K's risk-factor discussion states the company "experienced this during
  2020 as lower sales price realizations, caused by lower global energy demand during the
  COVID-19 pandemic and actions of major oil producing countries, had a significant
  impact on our royalty revenues." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 7A]

## Governance/management continuity within the window
- Joseph W. Craft III is reported as having been President and CEO and a member of the
  Board of Directors of ARLP's general partner "since ARLP's inception," and assumed the
  Chairman role of that board effective January 1, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 10]
- Two directors on ARLP's general-partner board as of the FY2022 10-K (Robert J. Druten
  and Wilson M. Torrence) are disclosed as having previously served as directors of
  Alliance GP, LLC, described in the filing as "the former general partner of Alliance
  Holdings GP, L.P. ('AHGP')," through 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 10]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2021-2023 window, the traceable public record (via the continuing
  registrant ARLP, since AHGP itself deregistered) shows a business that recorded record
  revenue and record net income in 2022 following a materially reduced or suspended
  unit distribution and non-cash impairment charges recorded in 2020, with the FY2022
  Form 10-K expressly stating no goodwill impairments occurred in 2021 or 2022, and with
  the pre-transaction controlling executive continuing to lead the general partner
  throughout the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-23-002034, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A, Notes 4-5 and 12, Item 10]
