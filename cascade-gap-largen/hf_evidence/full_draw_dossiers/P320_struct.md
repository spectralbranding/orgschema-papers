# Structural sub-dossier — INC Research Holdings, Inc. / Double Eagle Parent, Inc. (inVentiv) combination (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On May 10, 2017, INC Research Holdings, Inc. ("INC Research"), a Delaware corporation,
  and Double Eagle Parent, Inc. ("inVentiv"), a Delaware corporation, entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger providing for the business combination of the two
  companies through the merger of inVentiv with and into INC Research, with INC Research
  continuing as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A cover/Notice of Special Meeting]
- inVentiv is described as "a leading global provider of outsourced clinical development
  and commercialization services to biopharmaceutical companies," serving more than 550
  client organizations in 2016 including all 20 of the largest global biopharmaceutical
  companies, with approximately 15,000 employees globally. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Business of inVentiv"]
- inVentiv was itself the product of a 2010 acquisition and subsequent roll-up by
  affiliates of Thomas H. Lee Partners, L.P. ("THL") and, following a November 9, 2016
  transaction ("the Double Eagle Acquisition"), was jointly controlled by funds
  affiliated with Advent International Corporation ("Advent") and THL, with governance
  rights shared evenly between the two sponsors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Business of inVentiv"]
- The transaction was structured as a stock-for-stock combination: the merger
  consideration payable to inVentiv's stockholders, RSU holders, and option holders
  consisted of 49,989,839 fully diluted shares of INC Research common stock (an exchange
  ratio of approximately 3.4900 INC Research shares per inVentiv share as of the facts
  used in the proxy). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "Merger Consideration"]
- The stock issuance to inVentiv's stockholders represented approximately 47% of INC
  Research's outstanding fully diluted shares on a pro forma basis, requiring a separate
  stockholder vote to satisfy Nasdaq's 20%-issuance listing rule. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A Notice of Special Meeting]
- Completion of the merger was conditioned on, among other things, INC Research
  stockholder approval, expiration of the HSR Act waiting period plus antitrust consents
  in Germany and Russia, Nasdaq listing authorization for the newly issued shares, and
  qualification of the transaction as a tax-free reorganization under Section 368 of the
  Internal Revenue Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "Conditions to Completion of the Merger"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: inVentiv (the Delaware holding company) merged with and into INC
  Research, with INC Research as the surviving corporation and inVentiv's separate
  corporate existence ceasing; INC Research's certificate of incorporation and bylaws
  were amended and restated pursuant to the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Certificate of Incorporation of the Surviving Corporation"]
- Controlling intent / governance: post-closing the board of directors was set at 10
  members by contract — INC Research's then-CEO, inVentiv's then-CEO, four incumbent INC
  Research directors, two THL-designated directors, and two Advent-designated directors —
  giving the two private-equity sponsors contractually guaranteed board seats and
  "significant influence over future decisions of the combined company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Merger — INC Research Board of Directors Following the Merger"]
- Management: the parties' pre-signing management discussions produced a proposal under
  which Alistair Macdonald (INC Research's CEO) would serve as CEO of the combined
  company, with Michael Bell (inVentiv's CEO) serving as Executive Chairman of the
  combined company and President of the Commercial Division. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- Customer-facing product/business model: the combination joined INC Research's clinical
  research organization (CRO) services with inVentiv's integrated clinical-development
  and commercialization services, with the proxy noting that "because INC Research has
  not historically had a commercial business segment," integration risk existed that the
  combined company might not achieve the anticipated benefits of adding a commercial
  services line it had not previously operated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "Risk Factors — Risks Relating to the Businesses of the Combined Company"]
- People/organization — employee continuity: the merger agreement's Employee Benefits
  provision (Section 5.11) required that, for continuing employees, target annual cash
  bonus opportunity and employee benefits be "no less favorable" than those provided to
  similarly situated employees of the surviving corporation, with service-crediting and
  unused-vacation carryover provisions — i.e., workforce continuity terms were built into
  the deal contractually rather than left silent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A Annex A (Merger Agreement) Section 5.11]
- Headquarters: the merger agreement (Section 5.20) contractually required the parties to
  "cooperate to cause the global headquarters of the Company and the Surviving
  Corporation to be located in Raleigh, North Carolina from and after the Closing." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A Annex A (Merger Agreement) Section 5.20]
- Corporate identity: the merger agreement (Section 5.21, "Name and Trading Symbol")
  provided that, prior to the effective time, the parties would "cooperate in good faith
  to select a name for the Surviving Corporation and a ticker symbol," with the fallback
  that Parent's (inVentiv's) name and ticker would be used absent agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A Annex A (Merger Agreement) Section 5.21]
- Pre-closing internal restructuring: inVentiv agreed to cause two intermediate holding
  subsidiaries (Double Eagle Intermediate I and Intermediate II) to merge upward into
  inVentiv before the closing, structured as complete liquidations under Sections 332 and
  337 of the Internal Revenue Code, so that inVentiv's capital structure was simplified
  ahead of the combination with INC Research. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Restructuring"]

## Financing bridging the combined capital structure
- INC Research and inVentiv agreed to jointly pursue new senior secured term loan and
  revolver debt financing, arranged via an engagement letter with Credit Suisse
  Securities (USA) LLC, with a fallback debt commitment letter (also involving Credit
  Suisse, Goldman Sachs, Bank of America Merrill Lynch, and Morgan Stanley) if the
  best-efforts financing was not obtained. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]
- Concurrently with closing, INC Research agreed to enter a contribution agreement
  conveying its 100% membership interest in INC Research LLC to inVentiv Group Holdings,
  in exchange for one share of inVentiv Group Holdings common stock — a step taken to
  preserve covenant capacity under inVentiv Group Holdings' existing senior notes
  indenture for the combined entity's new debt guarantees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Restructuring"]

## Termination-fee and deal-protection structure (deal-window)
- The merger agreement provided for an INC Research termination fee of $70.7 million (or
  $82.1 million in certain superior-proposal circumstances) payable to inVentiv, and an
  inVentiv termination fee of $82.1 million payable to INC Research, each under specified
  triggering conditions; expense reimbursement was separately capped at $10,000,000 in
  the aggregate for either party. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — Termination Fees" / "Expense Reimbursement"]

## Closing
- The merger closed on August 1, 2017: inVentiv merged with and into INC Research, with
  INC Research as the surviving corporation; upon completion, former INC Research
  stockholders held approximately 53% of the surviving corporation on a fully diluted
  basis and former inVentiv stockholders held approximately 47%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-243354, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Concurrently with closing, the surviving corporation's certificate of incorporation and
  bylaws were amended and restated as previously described in the June 30, 2017 proxy
  statement, and the contractually-specified 10-member board (including THL- and
  Advent-designated directors) took office as of the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-243354, Form 8-K Item 5.02 / Item 5.03]
- On August 7, 2017, the surviving corporation completed a further internal
  reorganization in which inVentiv Group Holdings, Inc. (by then a wholly owned
  subsidiary) was merged with and into the surviving corporation, and the surviving
  corporation assumed inVentiv Group Holdings' obligations, and obtained new guarantees
  from additional subsidiaries, under the pre-existing $675 million 7.500% Senior Notes
  due 2024 indenture (then $405 million outstanding). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-252750, Form 8-K Items 2.03 / 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The combination was structured as a stock-for-stock merger of two clinical-research/
  commercialization services companies (a public company, INC Research, and a
  privately-sponsor-controlled company, inVentiv), with inVentiv merging into INC
  Research as the surviving legal entity; former inVentiv holders received approximately
  47% of the combined company's stock. Governance, management-role allocation (CEO /
  Executive Chairman split), headquarters location, employee-benefit continuity, and even
  the surviving corporation's future name/ticker were each fixed by explicit merger
  agreement provisions rather than left to be worked out post-closing, while integration
  of inVentiv's commercial-services business line into INC Research's historically
  CRO-only operations was flagged in the proxy's own risk factors as an unresolved
  execution question at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220226, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-243354, Form 8-K]
