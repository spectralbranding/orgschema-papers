## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The acquired parent filed a Form 15-12B on February 11, 2019 to deregister its common
  stock and terminate its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-034086, Form 15-12B]
- The acquired parent's intermediate utility holding company and two of its regulated
  utility subsidiaries jointly filed a Form 15-12B on February 21, 2019 to deregister.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-047165, Form 15-12B]
- None of these registrants filed a subsequent Form 10-K reporting on the acquired
  businesses' own stand-alone operating or financial results; their realized record is
  traceable only through the disclosures of the acquiring parent, CenterPoint Energy,
  Inc., which continued (and continues) to file Forms 10-K as a public company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-034086, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-047165, Form 15-12B]
- One of the formerly separate registrant CIKs later resumed periodic SEC filings in
  2023-2024 as sponsor/depositor for a distinct asset-backed-securities issuing entity
  formed to securitize utility bonds; that filer's own annual report identifies itself
  as an issuing entity under a securitization program, not as a report on the acquired
  utility's general operating business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-24-040588, Form 10-K "SIGECO Securitization I, LLC" cover page]

## Documented events over time
- The acquiring parent's merger with the acquired parent closed on February 1, 2019; the
  acquired parent and its subsidiaries became wholly owned subsidiaries of the acquirer.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 3 "Merger with Vectren"]
- For the year ended December 31, 2019, the acquirer recognized integration costs of $83
  million in connection with the merger, included in operation and maintenance expenses.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 3 "Merger with Vectren"]
- Subsequent to closing, the acquirer recognized severance totaling $61 million for
  employees terminated immediately after the merger closed (inclusive of change-of-control
  severance to former executives of the acquired company), plus $37 million of
  incremental cost for accelerated vesting of pre-existing equity awards, for total
  severance cost of $102 million in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 3 "Merger with Vectren"]
- On February 3, 2020, the acquirer's subsidiary entered a Securities Purchase Agreement
  to sell the Infrastructure Services Disposal Group — the underground pipeline
  construction and repair business (Miller Pipeline, LLC and Minnesota Limited, LLC,
  operated through VISCO, formerly a wholly owned Vectren subsidiary) — to PowerTeam
  Services for approximately $850 million; the transaction closed April 9, 2020,
  roughly fourteen months after the merger closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 4 "Divestiture of Infrastructure Services"]
- Upon classifying the Infrastructure Services Disposal Group as held for sale in
  connection with financial statements as of March 31, 2020, the acquirer recorded a
  goodwill charge of approximately $82 million on that disposal group, and recognized a
  net pre-tax loss of $6 million in connection with closing the disposition during the
  year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 4 "Divestiture of Infrastructure Services"]
- In connection with financial statements for the three months ended March 31, 2020, the
  acquirer identified triggering events (macroeconomic conditions related in part to the
  COVID-19 pandemic and a resulting decline in the acquirer's own common-stock price and
  enterprise market capitalization below book value) to perform interim goodwill testing
  across its reporting units; the test produced a non-cash goodwill charge of $185
  million specific to the Indiana Electric reporting unit (the former SIGECO electric
  business), reducing that unit's carrying value to an implied fair value of $936
  million as of March 31, 2020, recorded in the year ended December 31, 2020. All other
  reporting units' fair values exceeded carrying value in that test. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 6 "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]
- No further reductions in the Indiana Electric reporting unit's goodwill carrying value
  were recorded in the years ended December 31, 2021 or December 31, 2022 (annual
  goodwill tests performed each year concluded no charge was required for any reporting
  unit). [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 6] [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-23-000013, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 6 "Goodwill and Other Intangible Assets"]

## Continuing operating presence
- As of the FY2021 Form 10-K, the acquirer continued to report Indiana Gas, SIGECO, and
  VEDO as operating regulated-utility businesses (grouped as "Indiana Utilities":
  Indiana Electric, Indiana North, and Indiana South) within its Electric and Natural
  Gas reportable segments, and continued to hold VUHI as the intermediate holding
  company for those utilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, "Glossary of Key Terms and Abbreviations"]
- Credit-rating disclosure in the FY2021 Form 10-K showed a separate issuer rating for
  the acquired parent entity ("Vectren Corp.") from at least one rating agency alongside
  senior-unsecured ratings for VUHI and Indiana Gas, indicating those legal entities
  continued to exist within the combined organization's capital structure as of that
  filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021, "Credit Ratings"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired regulated-utility businesses did not continue as a separately reporting
  public company; all associated registrants deregistered within roughly three weeks of
  the February 1, 2019 closing, and the traceable 3-5-year realized record comes from
  the acquiring parent's own subsequent Forms 10-K rather than from any stand-alone
  filing. Within that record: the acquirer incurred $83 million of merger integration
  costs and $102 million of merger-related severance in the first partial year (2019);
  divested the acquired pipeline-construction subsidiary (Miller Pipeline/Minnesota
  Limited, operated through VISCO) for approximately $850 million roughly fourteen
  months after closing, recognizing an approximately $82 million goodwill charge and a
  $6 million loss on that disposal; and recorded a $185 million non-cash goodwill charge
  specific to the acquired electric utility (Indiana Electric/former SIGECO) in 2020,
  attributed to pandemic-related market-capitalization decline, with no further goodwill
  charges recorded against that unit in 2021 or 2022 and the Indiana Gas/SIGECO/VEDO
  utilities continuing to be reported as operating segments through the FY2022 filing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-22-000023, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001130310-23-000013, Form 10-K FY2022]
