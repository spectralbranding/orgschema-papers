# Outcome sub-dossier — Rice Energy Inc. realized record after merger with EQT Corporation, ~3-5-year horizon (~2020-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Rice Energy Inc. was deregistered shortly after
> closing and has no separate post-close 10-K; the traceable realized record here is
> drawn from the acquirer's (EQT Corporation, CIK 33213) own periodic filings, where it
> separately discloses specific facts tied to the acquired business. This sub-dossier
> contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On November 24, 2017, a Form 15-12B was filed (by EQT RE, LLC as successor in
  interest to Rice Energy Inc.) certifying termination of registration/suspension of
  reporting obligations under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-070327, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K, 10-Q, or 8-K was filed under Rice Energy Inc.'s CIK (1588238) in 2018
  or 2019, and none has been filed since; the last periodic report on that CIK is the
  FY2016 10-K filed March 1, 2017, before closing. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001588238&type=10-K]
- No stand-alone or separately reported segment financial statements for the acquired
  Rice business (as a discrete unit) were located in EQT's later 10-Ks; EQT
  consolidates the acquired exploration-and-production and midstream assets into its
  combined operations without a "Rice segment" break-out, so a comprehensive aggregate
  financial outcome (stand-alone revenue, net income, or asset value of the acquired
  business specifically) is not separately disclosed. [UNVERIFIED]

## Realized record documented in the acquirer's (EQT) own disclosures
- Toby Z. Rice — Rice Energy's former President and Chief Operating Officer — was
  appointed President and Chief Executive Officer of EQT Corporation in July 2019 and
  elected to EQT's board of directors at that time; he continued to sign as EQT's
  President and CEO on EQT's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 17, 2021)
  and fiscal year 2021 (filed February 10, 2022), spanning the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-22-000007, Form 10-K]
- Daniel J. Rice IV, a former Rice Energy director, became a member of EQT
  Corporation's board of directors in November 2017 and continued serving as an EQT
  director through the FY2021 10-K (signed February 10, 2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-22-000007, Form 10-K]
- EQT's officer biographies in the FY2020 10-K report that several other former senior
  Rice Energy executives did not continue in their roles at EQT for an extended
  period: the former Rice Chief Information Officer left around the time of closing
  (November 2017) to found a separate technology incubator; the former Rice Vice
  President, Human Resources, left EQT-related employment by early 2018; and the
  former Rice Senior Director, Technical Accounting and Financial Reporting, remained
  only "through its acquisition by EQT Corporation in November 2017 and until February
  2018" before departing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K]
- Rice Energy's former Senior Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary
  left at the November 2017 closing, worked as an outside advisor from May 2018, and
  was appointed EQT's General Counsel in July 2019 (the same month Toby Z. Rice became
  CEO). [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K]

## Documented financial trace of the acquired business's assets
- Intangible assets recorded at the Rice Energy acquisition date — composed solely of
  non-compete agreements with former Rice Energy executives, with an original gross
  value of $108.689 million and a 3-year useful life — were fully amortized as of
  December 31, 2020 (net book value $0, down from $26.006 million at December 31,
  2019); EQT recognized a $15.4 million impairment of these intangible assets in the
  third quarter of 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K]
- EQT reports holding federal net operating loss carryforwards attributable to the
  2017 Rice Energy acquisition (the "Rice Merger"), commingled with NOLs generated in
  2017 in excess of amounts carried back to prior years. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-22-000007, Form 10-K]
- Outside the fixed ~2020-2022 window but disclosed as background in the FY2020 10-K:
  following EQT's separate November 2018 spin-off of its midstream business
  (Equitrans Midstream Corporation), two reporting units traced to Rice-acquired
  midstream assets — "Rice Retained Midstream" and "RMP PA Gas Gathering" — were
  allocated to discontinued operations, and a $267.9 million goodwill impairment was
  recorded against the RMP PA Gas Gathering unit in 2018 after volumetric-fee revenue
  indicators were identified following announced production curtailments. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K]
- A Rice-origin upstream subsidiary, Rice Drilling B LLC, appears as a continuing
  named party (with EQT Corporation, EQT Production Company, EQT Energy LLC, and EQM
  Gathering OpCo LLC) to a Gas Gathering and Compression Agreement dated February 26,
  2020 that was amended repeatedly through at least December 2021/January 2022,
  indicating the acquired upstream acreage continued operating under formal gathering
  arrangements throughout the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-22-000007, Form 10-K Exhibit Index]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Rice Energy Inc. was absorbed and deregistered in November 2017, there is no
  stand-alone public 10-K record for the acquired business, and EQT does not
  separately report an aggregate financial outcome (revenue, net income, or valuation)
  for the acquired assets as a discrete unit within the ~2020-2022 window — that
  aggregate scalar is not available. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-070327, Form 15-12B]
- Within that limit, EQT's own filings do separately and specifically tie several
  facts to the Rice acquisition across the window: two former Rice principals reached
  EQT's top leadership (CEO; director) by mid-2019 and remained there through early
  2022, several other former Rice senior executives departed EQT within roughly three
  months to two years of closing, the acquisition-related non-compete intangible asset
  was fully written down/amortized by year-end 2020, and a Rice-origin operating
  subsidiary remained a continuing, actively-amended contracting party through the
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-21-000006, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000033213-22-000007, Form 10-K]
