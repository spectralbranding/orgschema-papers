# Outcome sub-dossier — Verso Corporation realized record after the NewPage acquisition, ~2018-2020 horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed on Verso Corporation
> (the acquiring registrant, which remained public), read from its annual reports covering
> fiscal years 2018 and 2019 (~3-5 years after the January 2015 transaction). This
> sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status across the window
- Verso Corporation continued to file annual reports on Form 10-K through the outcome
  window; the in-window report used here covers the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 and
  reports figures for 2019 and 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K cover]
- As established by that in-window report, Verso and substantially all of its subsidiaries
  had filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on January 26, 2016; the plan of reorganization
  became effective and the debtors emerged from Chapter 11 on July 15, 2016 — i.e., the
  registrant entered the outcome window as a reorganized going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K]

## Best-documented realized scalar
- Verso reported net income of $96 million for 2019 and net income of $171 million for 2018
  (per the liquidity/cash-flow discussion in the fiscal-year-2019 annual report). [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K Liquidity and Capital Resources]

## Documented disruption at the product/process interface (over time)
- Verso permanently closed the Luke Mill, on the border of West Virginia and Maryland, in
  June 2019, recording approximately $20 million of noncash restructuring charges related
  to the closure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K]
- Verso completed the sale of its Wickliffe Mill in 2018, recording a $9 million gain on
  the sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K]
- On November 11, 2019 Verso entered a membership-interest purchase agreement to sell its
  Androscoggin Mill and Stevens Point Mill to Pixelle Specialty Solutions LLC, and
  completed that sale on February 10, 2020; those two mills collectively represented
  approximately 22% of revenues over the twelve months ended December 31, 2019, and the
  company stated its business would be "reduced and less diversified" following the sale.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K]

## Documented change at the process/organization interface (integration of the acquired substrate, over time)
- As of December 31, 2018, the Verso Paper Corp. Pension Plan for Hourly Employees
  (Androscoggin) and the NewPage Cash Balance Plan for Non-Bargained Employees were merged
  into the NewPage Retirement Plan for Bargained Hourly Employees to form a combined plan
  renamed the Verso Corporation Employee Pension Plan — a consolidation of the acquired
  benefit/organization substrate into the parent several years after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K Note 12 Retirement Benefits]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2018-2020 window the reorganized registrant returned to reported net income
  ($171 million in 2018; $96 million in 2019) while progressively contracting its physical
  footprint — closing the Luke Mill (June 2019) and divesting the Wickliffe Mill (2018) and
  the Androscoggin and Stevens Point Mills (agreed November 2019, closed February 2020,
  together ~22% of trailing revenue) — and consolidating the acquired benefit/organization
  substrate (pension-plan merger effective December 31, 2018), leaving a smaller, less
  diversified coated- and specialty-paper business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001421182-20-000006, Form 10-K]
