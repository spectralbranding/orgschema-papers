## Realized business record noted in reputable secondary press (window-dated)
- Reporting dated November 1, 2021 (Bloomberg News, republished by Al Jazeera and other
  outlets) states that "[s]ince the transaction closed in December 2017, Fortress has
  been run independently," and that, "[t]o win approval from the Committee on Foreign
  Investment in the U.S., SoftBank agreed to cede any control of day-to-day operations
  of Fortress." [SRC: https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2021/11/1/softbank-mulls-sale-of-fortress-investment-group]
- The same report states that SoftBank Group Corp. was, as of that date, "considering a
  sale of Fortress Investment Group as the Japanese conglomerate reviews options for the
  asset manager," attributing the review in part to the acquirer's having been "unable
  to mesh Fortress's operations with its own," with discussions described as at an
  early stage. [SRC: https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2021/11/1/softbank-mulls-sale-of-fortress-investment-group]
- The report states the three founding Principals (Briger, Edens, Nardone) continued to
  be identified as the entity's founders, and cites the entity's own website as
  reporting $53.9 billion in assets under management as of June 30, 2021 across credit,
  private equity, and permanent-capital vehicles. [SRC: https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2021/11/1/softbank-mulls-sale-of-fortress-investment-group]
