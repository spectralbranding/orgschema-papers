## Acquirer's disclosure of the acquired business over the window
- Taylor Morrison Home Corporation's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed February 22,
  2023) organizes its business into four reportable segments defined by geography
  (East, Central, West) plus a Financial Services segment; no segment or disclosure
  category is defined by, or separately attributed to, the AV Homes business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-004524, Form 10-K FY2022]
- That FY2022 filing's only textual reference to AV Homes is in a tax-footnote
  discussion of Section 382 net-operating-loss limitations, identifying the "2018
  acquisition of AV Homes" as one of three historical ownership-change events
  constraining use of net operating losses; no operating or financial metric specific
  to the acquired business is disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-004524, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Taylor Morrison's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 23, 2022) references
  the "acquisition of AV Homes, Inc." only in connection with 2019 transaction-expense
  recognition, reports the same geography-based reportable-segment structure, and lists
  its consumer-facing brand names as "Taylor Morrison, Darling Homes Collection by
  Taylor Morrison, and Esplanade" with no reference to AV Homes or its community brands
  (e.g., Solivita); the filing also describes its 55-plus buyer product line as
  "55-plus active lifestyle (formerly referred to as active adult)" without attributing
  that product line to a specific predecessor entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-003466, Form 10-K FY2021]
