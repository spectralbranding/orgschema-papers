# Outcome sub-dossier — Sprague Resources LP realized record, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-closing record within a fixed outcome window; the window is fixed on the
> registrant's last in-window audited annual report (Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021,
> filed 2022-03-04) plus the terminal public event that ended its reporting. It contains
> no information about how the closing-era transactions were structured.

## Public-reporting status through the window
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports on Form 10-K, most recently for
  fiscal year 2021 (filed 2022-03-04), so an in-window audited outcome record is publicly
  available. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The public record was terminated when the registrant was taken private: it filed a Form
  15-12G on 2022-11-14 to deregister its common units and suspend its Exchange Act
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-117756, Form 15-12G]

## Best-documented realized scalar (FY2021)
- For the year ended December 31, 2021, operating income "decreased $111.8 million, or
  140%, to an operating loss of $(32.2) million, as compared to operating income of $79.6
  million for the year ended December 31, 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The registrant recorded a net loss of $68.9 million for fiscal year 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Adjusted EBITDA for the year ended December 31, 2021 was $56.8 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Continuing business at the window
- As of December 31, 2021 the registrant "owned, operated, and/or controlled a network of
  refined products and material handling terminals and storage facilities predominantly
  located in the Northeast United States from New York to Maine and in Quebec, Canada." [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- It reported approximately 654 full-time employees supporting its operations (75 covered
  by five collective bargaining agreements), and its Canadian subsidiary Kildair Service
  ULC reported 103 employees as of December 31, 2021 (40 covered by one collective
  bargaining agreement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Acquired-asset / integration trajectory over time
- The deferred-consideration obligation carried from the 2017 Carbo terminal acquisition
  remained on the balance sheet through the window, with a $2.7 million short-term portion
  included in the current portion of other obligations at December 31, 2021 — i.e., the
  acquired-terminal payment obligation was still being serviced years after the asset
  transfer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Contingent consideration related to the 2017 Coen Energy acquisition "ended in 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- A named officer served as Vice President, Corporate Development with oversight
  responsibility for Kildair and Coen Energy "as well as leading Sprague's acquisition
  sourcing and integration efforts," indicating a continuing acquisition-and-integration
  function during the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Terminal public event (end of the window)
- On November 1, 2022, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 2, 2022 (as
  amended August 31, 2022), Sparrow HP Merger Sub, LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Sparrow
  HP Holdings, LLC, itself a wholly owned subsidiary of Hartree Partners, LP) merged with
  and into the Partnership, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of the parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-113199, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Each outstanding common unit not owned by the parent was converted into the right to
  receive $20.00 per unit in cash; approximately $133.8 million was paid as merger
  consideration; the parent held approximately 74.5% of the then-outstanding common units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-113199, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Trading of the common units was suspended before the opening of trading on November 1,
  2022; the registrant requested NYSE delisting via Form 25 and stated its intent to file a
  Form 15; three independent directors (Beth A. Bowman, C. Gregory Harper and John A.
  Shapiro) resigned, in each case not resulting from any disagreement with the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-113199, Form 8-K Items 3.01 and 5.02]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed outcome window the registrant remained a going concern operating its
  Northeast-U.S. and Quebec terminal network with ~654 employees, but its fiscal-year-2021
  audited results swung to an operating loss of $(32.2) million (from $79.6 million operating
  income the prior year) and a net loss of $68.9 million; the ten-year Carbo deferred-payment
  obligation persisted while the Coen Energy contingent consideration ended in 2020; and the
  public record closed when the entity was taken private by an affiliate of Hartree Partners
  at $20.00 per unit (~$133.8 million) on November 1, 2022 and deregistered via Form 15-12G
  on November 14, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001525287-22-000013, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-113199, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-22-117756, Form 15-12G]
