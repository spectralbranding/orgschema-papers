# Outcome sub-dossier — DuPont Fabros Technology business after combination with Digital Realty Trust, ~3-5-year horizon

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Because the acquired entity deregistered from SEC

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- DuPont Fabros Technology, Inc. and DuPont Fabros Technology, L.P. filed a Form 15-12B
  on September 28, 2017 to terminate/suspend SEC reporting, identifying Digital Realty
  Trust, Inc. as "successor by merger to DuPont Fabros Technology, Inc." and reporting no
  remaining holders of record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-297819, Form 15-12B]
- Digital Realty's FY2020 Form 10-K (filed 2021-03-01, roughly 3-4 years into the fixed
  outcome window) states "the Company has one operating segment and therefore one
  reporting segment," with operating revenue split only by U.S. / outside-U.S.
  geography, not by acquired-company origin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-002191,
  Form 10-K FY2020, Segment Information note]
- No standalone revenue, net operating income, occupancy, or other performance figures
  for the former DFT data-center portfolio, isolated from Digital Realty's consolidated
  results, were located in that filing. [UNVERIFIED]

## What Digital Realty's later 10-K confirms about the completed transaction
- "On September 14, 2017, we completed the acquisition of DuPont Fabros Technology,
  Inc., or DFT, in an all-stock merger... for equity consideration of approximately $6.2
  billion," which the filing states "expanded our reach with a complementary portfolio in
  top U.S. metropolitan areas." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-002191, Form 10-K
  FY2020]
- Intangible assets recognized in the DFT Merger's purchase-price allocation were "fully
  amortized" during the year ended December 31, 2019 (about two years post-closing).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-002191, Form 10-K FY2020]
- A tax protection agreement entered at closing with certain former DFT Operating
  Partnership unitholders continued to obligate the surviving Operating Partnership to
  maintain certain debt levels and offer guarantee opportunities through at least March
  1, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-002191, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The filing lists the "DFT Merger" alongside Digital Realty's other acquisitions (Telx,
  European Portfolio, Interxion) in a general risk disclosure about realizing
  "anticipated benefits," without giving an acquisition-specific realized outcome for the
  DFT Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-002191, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired entity terminated separate SEC reporting shortly after closing (Form
  15-12B, September 28, 2017). By the FY2020 10-K, the acquirer reported as a single
  operating and reporting segment with no standalone disclosure isolating the former DFT
  portfolio's financial or operating performance; the only DFT-specific facts traceable
  in that later filing are the closing date and aggregate consideration ($6.2 billion,
  September 14, 2017), the amortization timeline of merger-related intangibles, and a
  continuing tax protection agreement with certain former DFT unitholders. No public
  3-5-year realized performance record isolates the former DFT business from Digital
  Realty's consolidated results, so this case is coded uncertain on the outcome per the
  no-record rule. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-297819, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001558370-21-002191, Form 10-K FY2020]
