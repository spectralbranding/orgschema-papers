# Outcome sub-dossier — OneBeacon / Intact U.S. (OneBeacon) realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after the September 2017 merger

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. OneBeacon Insurance Group, Ltd. deregistered
> shortly after the merger closed and filed no further SEC periodic reports; the
> acquirer, Intact Financial Corporation, is a Canadian company that is not itself an
> SEC Form 10-K/20-F/40-F filer. The realized-record facts below are therefore drawn from
> Intact's own separately disclosed reporting on the acquired business (its published
> consolidated Annual Report / MD&A, which names and reports the acquired entity as a
> distinct reporting subsidiary) and reputable press, within a fixed ~2020 (3-year)
> outcome window. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing.

- OneBeacon's last annual report on Form 10-K on file with the SEC covers fiscal year
  2016 and was filed February 27, 2017, before the merger closed; no subsequent Form 10-K

- A full-text search of SEC EDGAR for Intact Financial Corporation as a filer of Form

## Realized record of the acquired business, as separately disclosed by the acquirer
- Effective February 18, 2020, OneBeacon Insurance Group Holdings, Ltd. was renamed
  Intact Insurance Group USA Holdings Inc., referred to in Intact's financial statements
  as "Intact U.S. (OneBeacon)." [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, Consolidated Financial Statements Note 1 - Status of the Company, https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- In Intact's segment reporting for "P&C U.S.," direct premiums written were CAD $1,823
  million in 2020 versus CAD $1,650 million in 2019; net earned premiums were CAD $1,582
  million versus CAD $1,431 million; underwriting income was CAD $81 million in 2020
  versus CAD $97 million in 2019; the combined ratio was 94.9% in 2020 versus 93.2% in
  2019. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, MD&A Section 7.1 "P&C U.S.," https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- At the time of the acquisition, Intact had set two objectives targeting completion by
  the end of 2020: reaching $3 billion in direct premiums written across its North
  American Specialty platform (reported as achieved in 2020) and achieving a sustainable
  low-90s combined ratio in the U.S.; Intact reported taking actions including profit
  improvement plans, targeted exits from underperforming business units, and roughly $30
  million of annual expense synergies, while stating the 2020 combined ratio of 94.9% was
  "higher than expected" and that it remained confident in "our ability to deliver a low
  90's combined ratio on a sustainable basis." [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, MD&A Section 7.2 "Performance vs objectives," https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- Intact U.S. (OneBeacon) exited its U.S. Healthcare business effective July 1, 2019, and
  as of December 31, 2019 entered into a loss portfolio transfer and prospective quota
  share reinsurance contract ceding Healthcare claims liabilities of $158 million and
  unearned premiums of $27 million, at a net reinsurance cost of $13 million. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- Underwriting results of "exited lines" (predominantly the exited Healthcare business)
  reported a loss of $57 million in 2020 and $66 million in 2019, net of reinsurance,
  mainly driven by adverse prior-year development in that business. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, MD&A Section 7.3 "Other performance matters," https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- An adverse development cover of US$200 million purchased in connection with the
  September 2017 acquisition, covering Intact U.S.'s claims liabilities for accident
  years 2016 and prior, had its full US$200 million maximum recoverable amount recorded
  as of December 31, 2020, with approximately two-thirds of that amount related to lines
  the company had by then exited. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
- As of the 2020 Annual Report, third-party rating agencies assigned Intact U.S.
  (OneBeacon)'s U.S. regulated entities financial strength ratings of A+ (A.M. Best),
  AA(low) (DBRS), A2 (Moody's), and AA- (Fitch), and senior unsecured debt ratings of a-,
  A, Baa2, and A- respectively. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, MD&A Table 31 "Ratings," https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]

## Subsequent brand integration (press-sourced, within window)
- On October 14, 2020, Intact announced it was bringing together its Canadian and U.S.
  specialty insurance operations — including the U.S. business acquired via OneBeacon in
  2017 and The Guarantee Company of North America, acquired in 2019 — under a single
  unified brand, "Intact Insurance Specialty Solutions," described as spanning more than
  20 specialty focus areas; CEO Charles Brindamour stated the move brought "the Intact
  brand to the U.S." as part of "building a world-class specialty insurance provider,"
  and Mike Miller was named president of the combined entity's U.S. operations. [SRC: https://www.insurancejournal.com/news/national/2020/10/14/586496.htm]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because OneBeacon deregistered from SEC reporting within a month of the September 2017
  closing and its acquirer, Intact, does not itself file U.S. annual reports, there is no
  SEC periodic-filing outcome record; the realized record is instead drawn from Intact's
  own separate disclosure of the acquired business (as "Intact U.S. (OneBeacon)," later
  folded into "Intact Insurance Specialty Solutions"). Within the ~2020 window, that
  business grew premium volume and met its acquisition-era premium-scale target, but ran
  a combined ratio above its own stated target range, fully exhausted a US$200 million
  adverse-development reinsurance cover purchased at acquisition (two-thirds tied to
  lines since exited), and exited its Healthcare product line in 2019 with continuing
  losses on that exited business through 2020. [SRC: Intact Financial Corporation, 2020 Annual Report, MD&A Section 7.1-7.3 and Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, https://s1.q4cdn.com/321139868/files/doc_financials/2020/ar/Intact-Financial-Corporation-2020-Annual-Report.pdf]
