## Outcome window and source
- The fixed outcome window is approximately 2021-2023 (three to five years after the ~2018
  closing). The registrant remained a public filer through this window: rather than a Form
  10-K, it files a certified annual report on Form N-CSR as a registered closed-end
  investment company. The primary outcome source is its fiscal-year-2021 Form N-CSR (period
  ended December 31, 2021; filed February 11, 2022). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]

## Realized corporate trajectory (dissolution and wind-down)
- The Board unanimously approved and adopted a Plan of Complete Liquidation and Dissolution
  on April 2, 2019; stockholders approved it at a special meeting held on June 27, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]
- On October 4, 2019 the registrant filed a certificate of dissolution with the Delaware
  Secretary of State (effective 4:00 p.m. Eastern Time that day); it closed its stock
  transfer books and ceased to be traded on The NASDAQ Global Select Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]
- On May 28, 2020 the registrant filed a verified petition in the Delaware Court of
  Chancery for determinations under Section 280 of the Delaware General Corporation Law
  (In re Altaba, Case No. 2020-0413-JTL); on August 18, 2020 it agreed to retain an
  aggregate $1.76 billion as security for claims of the IRS, subject to agreed release
  mechanisms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]
- By this window the registrant had "sold, distributed or otherwise disposed of
  substantially all of its remaining non-cash assets," and its activities were limited to
  winding up its business affairs; it stated it currently expects to deregister as an
  investment company under the 1940 Act after distributing substantially all of its assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]

## Disposition of the former portfolio holdings
- The former minority holdings were monetized: the FY2021 report references the gain on the
  sale of Alibaba Shares in connection with the registrant's 2018 and 2019 tax years, and
  closing agreements with New York State/City tax authorities regarding that gain. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]
- By December 31, 2021 the schedule of investments held no Alibaba or Yahoo Japan equity;
  total investments of $2,696,856 (thousands, 77.7% of net assets) consisted of short-term
  fixed-income / U.S. Government debt securities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]

- An Interim Liquidating Distribution of $8.33 per share ($4,327,529,678.78 in the
  aggregate) was paid on November 2, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]

- A distribution of $0.54 per share ($280,536,137.64 in the aggregate) was paid on

- For the year ended December 31, 2021, total liquidating distributions to common

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2021-2023 window the registrant executed a court-supervised complete
  liquidation and dissolution rather than continuing as an operating or holding entity:
  it had disposed of substantially all non-cash assets (the former Alibaba and Yahoo Japan
  minority stakes were monetized), held its remaining assets in short-term U.S. Government
  and fixed-income securities, and returned capital through successive liquidating
  distributions ($4,327.5M in 2020 and $4,514.6M in 2021), while retaining reserves
  (including $1.76 billion for IRS claims) under a Delaware Section 280 proceeding, with
  net assets reduced to $3,470,092 (thousands) by December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-036939, Form N-CSR (FY2021)]
