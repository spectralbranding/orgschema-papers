## Public-reporting status of the acquired business (feasibility-relevant)

## Realized record — second Chapter 11 filing
- On March 8, 2017, General Wireless Operations Inc. dba RadioShack and affiliated debtors
  filed a voluntary petition for relief under chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for
  the District of Delaware, assigned Case No. 17-10506 — the second Chapter 11 filing for
  the RadioShack brand within roughly two years. [SRC: https://www.abi.org/feed-item/new-delaware-chapter-11-filing-%E2%80%93-general-wireless-operations-inc-dba-radioshack-%E2%80%93-case-no]
- Reputable press reported the filing on March 9, 2017: "General Wireless Operations Inc.,
  doing business as electronics convenience store RadioShack," announced it had filed for
  Chapter 11 protection. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/radioshack-files-for-second-bankruptcy-in-24-months-closing-200-stores/437752/]

## Realized record — documented disruption over time
- The court filing indicated that while the core RadioShack retail business had returned
  to profitability, the partnership with Sprint Solutions Inc. did not succeed and left
  the debtors with inadequate capital to continue as a going concern; the cases were
  filed to close additional underperforming stores and to pursue a sale or restructuring.
  [SRC: https://www.abi.org/feed-item/new-delaware-chapter-11-filing-%E2%80%93-general-wireless-operations-inc-dba-radioshack-%E2%80%93-case-no]
- Approximately 200 RadioShack stores were to close, with options being evaluated on the
  remaining approximately 1,300 locations; press reported Sprint "helps run about a third
  of its brick-and-mortar operations in co-branded stores." [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/radioshack-files-for-second-bankruptcy-in-24-months-closing-200-stores/437752/]

## Realized record — disposition of the brand
- On November 19, 2020, Retail Ecommerce Ventures (REV) acquired the RadioShack brands and
  related assets, including RadioShack.com and related websites across the U.S., Canada,
  India, Australia, Europe and China; the prior owner, General Wireless Operations,
  retained a minority stake, and the deal terms were not disclosed. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/radioshack-brand-acquired-by-new-owner-of-pier-1-modells-ip/589377/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired RadioShack retail business, held by a privately owned buyer that files no
  SEC reports, re-entered Chapter 11 on March 8, 2017 (Case No. 17-10506) — approximately
  two years after the 2015 sale — with roughly 200 stores set to close and about 1,300
  evaluated, the filing attributing the shortfall to a co-branding partnership with Sprint
  that did not succeed and left inadequate capital; the RadioShack brand and related web
  assets were subsequently sold to Retail Ecommerce Ventures on November 19, 2020, with
  the prior owner retaining a minority stake.
