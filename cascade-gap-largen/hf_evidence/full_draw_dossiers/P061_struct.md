# Structural sub-dossier — Weight Watchers International acquisition of Danone's 49% interest in the China joint-venture entity (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K, Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement),
  with the share purchase agreement attached as Exhibit 10.1; the 8-K itself narrates the
  transaction and the exhibit sets out its terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)]
- The 8-K reports that on April 27, 2011, Weight Watchers Asia Holdings Ltd. acquired
  Danone Dairy Asia's 49% minority equity interest in Weight Watchers Danone China Limited,
  and that as a result Weight Watchers "now indirectly owns 100% of Weight Watchers China." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Transferor: Danone Dairy Asia, organized under the laws of France, an indirect wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Groupe DANONE S.A. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 Share Purchase Agreement, Preamble/Recitals]
- Transferee: Weight Watchers Asia Holdings Ltd., organized under the laws of the Cayman
  Islands, a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of Weight Watchers International, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §Preamble]
- The entity whose shares changed hands is Weight Watchers Danone China Limited, a company
  incorporated under the laws of Hong Kong, established and invested in by the two parties
  under a Joint Venture Agreement dated February 5, 2008; it in turn had established Weight
  Watchers Danone (China) Weight Loss Consultation Co., Ltd., a wholly foreign-owned
  enterprise (WFOE) in the PRC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §Recitals (A)]
- Danone Dairy Asia owned 5,390 shares of the entity, constituting 49% of its total issued
  share capital; those Transfer Shares are what the Transferee purchased. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §Recitals (B)]
- Consideration for the 49% Transfer Shares was a purchase price of US$1,000, payable at
  Closing by wire transfer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.3, §2.4]
- Closing was to take place within three business days of the date of the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary, and what changed at the tier level
- Customer-facing operation / value-capture model: the underlying business is a weight
  management business established to operate in the People's Republic of China, run through
  the WFOE ("Weight Loss Consultation Co., Ltd."); the transaction moved the remaining 49%
  equity of that operation to the majority holder so that it became wholly owned. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §Recitals (A)]
- Legal vehicle: only the equity interest transferred — the Hong Kong entity and its PRC
  WFOE remained in place; the transaction changed ownership rather than moving the operating
  assets out of their vehicles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §2.1, §Recitals (A)]
- People / management: at Closing the Transferor delivered written resignation letters
  (Schedule 1 form) from each of the directors, supervisors, and management personnel of the
  entity and the WFOE that the Transferor had appointed, and board resolutions appointed the
  Transferee's nominated replacements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.2(a)(iii), (iv), (v)]
- Governing agreements / processes: at Closing the parties confirmed termination of the
  February 5, 2008 Joint Venture Agreement (except expressly surviving provisions — e.g.
  non-competition, non-solicitation, confidential information, governing law), termination of
  the services agreement dated February 5, 2008 (as amended December 5, 2008) between Danone
  Asia Pacific Management Co. Ltd. (DAPM) and the WFOE, and termination of a related side
  letter agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.3(b), (c), (d)]
- The 8-K likewise records that, in connection with the acquisition, the February 5, 2008
  joint venture agreement between Weight Watchers Asia and Danone Asia "terminated in
  accordance with its terms." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)]
- Confidential information held by the Transferor and its representatives was to be returned
  or destroyed at Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.3(a)]
- Name change: the entity and the WFOE were required to remove "Danone" (and the
  corresponding Chinese characters) from their names and to discontinue use of Danone-branded
  documents and materials within six months of Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.4]

## Shared-substrate / transitional arrangements at closing
- Rather than a new transition-services arrangement to bridge shared functions, the agreement
  provided for TERMINATION of the pre-existing Danone-side services agreement (DAPM to the
  WFOE); the record of this filing contains no transitional-services, operating, or
  shared-services agreement carrying Danone-provided support forward past Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.3(c)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A majority holder acquired the remaining 49% equity of a Hong Kong entity (and its PRC
  operating WFOE) from its counterparty for US$1,000, taking it to full ownership; at Closing
  the counterparty's appointed directors/supervisors/management resigned, the founding joint
  venture agreement and the counterparty's services agreement terminated, and the
  counterparty's name was to be removed — the equity crossed while the counterparty's people
  and its service-support arrangement were exited rather than transferred forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K Item 1.01 (d8k.htm)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-115257, Form 8-K EX-10.1 §3.2, §3.3, §3.4]
