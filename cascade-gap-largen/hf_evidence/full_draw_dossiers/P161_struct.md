# Structural sub-dossier — Peabody Energy Corporation reorganization (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K dated August 10, 2016 by Peabody Energy Corporation, a
  Delaware corporation (Commission File No. 1-16463), filed under Item 7.01 (Regulation
  FD). It is not a transaction-terms document; it attaches, as Exhibit 99.1, a
  "2017–2021 Business Plan" and, as Exhibit 99.2, a "Non-GAAP Financial Measures
  Discussion." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-677563, Form 8-K Items 7.01 and 9.01]
- The 8-K states the business plan was furnished to certain lenders under the company's
  Superpriority Secured Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement (approved by the Bankruptcy
  Court on May 18, 2016), which required the debtors to provide five-year business plans
  for their United States and Australian operations and a consolidated enterprise plan.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-677563, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The attached plan describes a target operating model of two business units (Americas
  and Australia), a marketing/trading services function, and a "lean and scalable
  corporate structure offering strategy, compliance, shared services." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-677563, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## The transaction as structured (contemporaneous primary filings)
- On April 13, 2016, Peabody Energy Corporation and a majority of its wholly owned
  domestic subsidiaries, plus one international subsidiary in Gibraltar (the "Debtors"),
  filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri, jointly
  administered as Case No. 16-42529; each Debtor continued to operate its business as a
  debtor in possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-539191, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The company's Australian operating subsidiaries were not among the Debtors; the company
  made available to its Australian platform a committed US$250 million revolving
  intercompany loan facility to support ongoing Australian operations during the
  reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-539191, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Debtor-in-possession financing consisted of a $500 million DIP term loan facility, a
  $100 million letter-of-credit facility, and a $200 million bonding accommodation
  facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-539191, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## What crossed / did not cross the ownership boundary at the Effective Date
- On March 17, 2017, the Bankruptcy Court entered a Confirmation Order confirming the
  Debtors' Second Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization (as revised March 15, 2017); on
  April 3, 2017 (the "Effective Date") the plan became effective and the Debtors emerged
  from Chapter 11. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The operating business — the coal-mining assets and workforce — was retained by the
  same corporate registrant (Peabody Energy Corporation, Delaware, Commission File No.
  1-16463, CIK 1064728); no third-party acquirer took the enterprise. What changed was
  the capital structure and equity ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K]
- Prepetition debt obligations of the Debtors were cancelled, and each share of the
  company's pre-Effective-Date common stock, including all related options and warrants,
  was extinguished, cancelled, and discharged with no further force or effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K Items 1.02 and 3.03]
- New equity was issued to holders of prepetition indebtedness and other parties: shares
  of new Common Stock and new Series A Convertible Preferred Stock, including 51,225,813
  shares of Common Stock plus warrants issued through a completed Rights Offering for
  total consideration of approximately $704 million, and 29,999,999 shares of Preferred
  Stock issued under a Private Placement Agreement dated December 22, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K Item 3.02]
- The board of directors was reconstituted on the Effective Date: eight named prior
  directors ceased to serve and a new slate was seated (among them Nicholas J. Chirekos,
  Stephen E. Gorman, and Glenn L. Kellow, the latter executing the standard officer
  participation agreement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- On the Effective Date the receivables purchase (securitization) facility was amended
  and restated to, among other things, add certain Australian subsidiaries as
  originators, continuing the arrangement bridging the U.S. and Australian platforms.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A subsequent Form 8-K clarified that, on a fully-converted basis, the reorganized
  company would have approximately 137.3 million shares of common stock issued (assuming
  conversion of the Series A preferred and exercise of emergence warrants). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-125199, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a Chapter 11 balance-sheet reorganization in which the coal-mining
  product, operating processes (mines), and workforce remained with the same corporate
  registrant while prepetition debt was cancelled and prior equity was extinguished and
  replaced by new common and preferred equity issued to creditors and investors; the
  Australian operating platform, excluded from the debtor group, was bridged during the
  case by a US$250 million intercompany loan facility and, at emergence, by an amended
  receivables facility adding Australian originators. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-108650, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-539191, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
