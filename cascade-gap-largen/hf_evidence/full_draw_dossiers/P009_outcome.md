## Outcome window
- The realized record is read at the ~3-year horizon after the November 28, 2017
  separation, using the annual report for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized financial trajectory
- The standalone company reported a net loss attributable to CONSOL Energy Inc.
  shareholders of $10 million for the year ended December 31, 2020, compared with net
  income attributable to shareholders of $76 million for the year ended December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Total revenue and other income was $895 million for 2020, down from $1,332 million the
  prior year, a decline of $437 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Total cost of coal sold operating costs fell to $543 million from $846 million, tracking
  the lower volumes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented operational disruption over time (product / operations)
- In response to a decline in demand for its coal driven by the COVID-19 pandemic, the
  company idled four of its five longwalls for periods of time beginning in the second
  quarter of 2020; as demand improved it restarted longwalls and ultimately ran four of the
  five. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The company cited an extended decline in the prices it receives for its coal, the effects
  of the COVID-19 pandemic on its business and on the global economy, and the possibility
  that it may need to curtail coal production, among its risk factors for the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Continuity of the operating footprint
- As of the FY2020 report the company continued to consist of the Pennsylvania Mining
  Complex and the CNX Marine Terminal — the coal-production operations and export terminal
  it began with — with no divestiture of that core operating footprint reported for the
  period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~3 years after separation the standalone coal company remained an operating
  going concern retaining its core mining and terminal assets, but its result swung from a
  $76 million net income in 2019 to a $10 million net loss in 2020 as pandemic-driven demand
  weakness cut revenue by roughly one third and forced temporary idling of most of its
  longwall production capacity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net loss attributable to shareholders of $10 million
  (FY2020), versus net income of $76 million (FY2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-21-002802, Form 10-K FY2020]
