# Structural sub-dossier — Akorn, Inc. proposed acquisition by Fresenius Kabi AG (2017 deal terms and 2018 termination)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the transaction as
> structured/proposed and the contemporaneous 2017-2018 record of its termination. It
> contains no post-2018 outcome information.

## Transaction and parties as proposed
- On April 24, 2017, Akorn, Inc. ("the Company"), a Louisiana corporation, entered into
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Fresenius Kabi AG, a German stock corporation
  ("Fresenius Kabi"), Quercus Acquisition, Inc., a Louisiana corporation and wholly owned
  subsidiary of Fresenius Kabi ("Merger Sub"), and, solely for purposes of one article,
  Fresenius SE & Co. KGaA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, Notice of Special Meeting / Summary Term Sheet]
- The Company is a specialty generic pharmaceutical company founded in 1971 that
  develops, manufactures and markets generic and branded prescription pharmaceuticals,
  over-the-counter consumer health products, and animal health pharmaceuticals; Fresenius
  Kabi is a global healthcare company specializing in infusion, transfusion, and clinical
  nutrition products and technologies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "The Parties" section]
- Merger Sub, formed by Fresenius Kabi solely to complete the transaction, would merge
  with and into the Company, with the Company continuing to exist after the merger as the
  surviving corporation and a subsidiary of Fresenius Kabi; Merger Sub would cease to
  exist upon consummation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "The Merger" section]
- Each outstanding share of Company common stock (other than specified excluded shares)
  would be converted into the right to receive $34.00 in cash, without interest and less
  applicable withholding taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, Summary Term Sheet / "The Merger" section]
- The merger agreement contained no financing-related closing condition; Fresenius Kabi
  represented it would have sufficient funds at closing, expected to be derived from a mix
  of Euro- and U.S.-dollar-denominated debt instruments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, Summary Term Sheet, "Financing of the Merger"]
- Akorn shareholders voted to approve the transaction on July 19, 2017. [SRC: https://www.akorn.com press release furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to SEC accession 0000950157-18-000456]

## What would have crossed the ownership boundary at closing, as structured
- Legal vehicle: a reverse triangular merger in which Merger Sub would merge into the
  Company and disappear, leaving the Company as the surviving corporation and a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Fresenius Kabi — i.e., the entire corporate entity, not a
  carved-out product line or asset set, was the object of the transaction.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "The Merger" section]
- Customer-facing product/business: as structured, the whole of the Company's specialty
  generic and branded pharmaceutical, OTC, and animal-health product business would
  become part of the surviving corporation under Fresenius Kabi's ownership; the proxy
  does not describe a partial-asset carve-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "The Parties" / "The Merger" sections]
- People/organization: the merger agreement provided that for not less than one year
  following the effective time, Fresenius Kabi would, and would cause the surviving
  corporation to, provide each continuing employee with base salary no less favorable,
  severance benefits no less favorable, and employee benefit plans substantially
  comparable in the aggregate to pre-closing arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "Continuing Employee Benefits" section]
- The merger agreement further provided that the surviving corporation would pay each
  continuing employee's prorated annual bonus/commission/incentive award for the plan
  year in which closing occurred. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "Continuing Employee Benefits" section]
- The proxy's reviewed sections describe the post-closing employee-benefits continuity
  commitment above as the mechanism bridging the organizational tier; no separate
  transition-services or shared-corporate-substrate agreement (of the kind used in
  spin-off/carve-out structures) was identified in the reviewed sections.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "Continuing Employee Benefits" section]
- If the Company terminated the agreement to accept a superior proposal, it would owe
  Fresenius Kabi a $129 million termination fee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, "Background of the Merger" section]

## Termination of the proposed transaction (2017-2018 deal window)
- On April 22, 2018, Fresenius Kabi announced it was seeking to terminate the April 24,
  2017 merger agreement, citing an ongoing investigation into alleged breaches of FDA
  data-integrity requirements relating to product development at the Company; the Company
  stated it "categorically disagree[d]" and that the investigation was not a closing
  condition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-000454, Form 8-K Item 8.01 and Exhibit 99.1]
- On April 23, 2018, the Company filed a complaint in the Delaware Court of Chancery
  asking that Fresenius Kabi be required to fulfill its obligations under the merger
  agreement, characterizing Fresenius's stated basis for termination as "without merit."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-000456, Form 8-K Item 8.01 and Exhibit 99.1]
- On October 1, 2018, the Company issued a statement responding to a ruling by the
  Delaware Court of Chancery on its complaint against Fresenius Kabi. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-001017, Form 8-K Item 8.01 and Exhibit 99.1]
- On October 18, 2018, the Company announced it had filed a notice of appeal to the
  Supreme Court of the State of Delaware from the Court of Chancery's decision rejecting
  the Company's claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-18-007068, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- On December 7, 2018, the Company announced that the Supreme Court of the State of
  Delaware had ruled against it, upholding the lower court's decision to allow Fresenius
  Kabi to terminate the April 2017 merger agreement, and that it would move forward as an
  independent company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-063636, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Following the litigation's conclusion, the Company's Board announced a formal search
  for a new chief executive officer, with the then-current CEO remaining until a successor
  was hired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-063636, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Structural summary
- The transaction as proposed and agreed on April 24, 2017 was a whole-company,
  reverse-triangular cash merger under which the entire Company (legal entity, product
  business, and, per a one-year continuing-employee-benefits commitment, its workforce)
  would have become a subsidiary of Fresenius Kabi for $34.00/share; no partial-asset
  carve-out or shared-substrate bridging agreement distinct from that employee-benefits
  commitment was identified in the reviewed proxy sections.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001308179-17-000193, DEFM14A, Summary Term Sheet / "The Merger" / "Continuing Employee Benefits" sections]
- This structure was never consummated: Fresenius Kabi announced its intent to terminate
  on April 22, 2018, the Company's suit to compel performance was rejected by the Delaware
  Court of Chancery, and the Delaware Supreme Court upheld that ruling on December 7,
  2018, ending the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-000454] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-063636, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
