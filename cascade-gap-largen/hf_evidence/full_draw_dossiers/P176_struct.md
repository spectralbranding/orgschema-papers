# Structural sub-dossier — C. R. Bard, Inc. acquisition by Becton, Dickinson and Company (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- C. R. Bard, Inc. (Bard), a New Jersey corporation, and Becton, Dickinson and Company
  (BD), a New Jersey corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  April 23, 2017 providing for the acquisition of Bard by BD. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a reverse-triangular merger: Lambda Corp. (referred
  to as Merger Corp), a New Jersey corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of BD, would
  merge with and into Bard, with Bard surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  BD. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- As a result of the merger, Bard would continue as the surviving company and would no
  longer be a publicly held company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary (the acquired business)
- The entire Bard company was acquired: BD obtained 100% ownership of Bard, whose business
  is described as "the manufacture, packaging, distribution and sale of medical, surgical,
  diagnostic and patient care devices," selling "a broad range of products to hospitals,
  individual healthcare professionals, extended care facilities and alternate site
  facilities on a global basis," and participating "in the markets for vascular, urology,
  oncology and surgical specialty products." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- Because the vehicle was a whole-company merger with Bard surviving intact as a
  subsidiary, the customer-facing product lines, the operations that produce and deliver
  them, and the people/management that run them were transferred together as a going
  concern rather than separated; the proxy describes the post-closing task as "the
  integration of Bard's operations, products and employees into BD." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- Bard equity awards would, at the effective time, be converted into the right to receive
  the per-share merger consideration or become equity awards of BD, i.e., Bard personnel's
  equity incentives carried across into the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]

## Consideration and post-closing ownership split
- Each issued and outstanding share of Bard common stock would be converted into the right
  to receive $222.93 in cash, without interest, and 0.5077 of a share of BD common stock
  (the exchange ratio). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- Based on the BD share price at announcement, the implied value of the per-share merger
  consideration was $317.00 per Bard share; based on BD's NYSE closing price of $193.23 on
  June 29, 2017 (the latest practicable date before mailing), the implied value was $321.03
  per Bard share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- The issuance of BD common stock was subject to a cap of 19.9% of BD common stock
  outstanding as of April 23, 2017; if the merger's issuable BD shares would exceed that
  cap, the mechanism would adjust per the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately after completion of the merger, former Bard shareholders were expected to own
  in the aggregate approximately 15% of the then-outstanding shares of BD common stock.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]

## Financing structure disclosed at closing
- BD funded the cash portion in part through capital-markets issuance: on June 6, 2017, BD
  issued $9.675 billion aggregate in an underwritten public offering in connection with the
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]

## Transitional / bridging arrangements
- The proxy statement/prospectus does not describe a transition-services, shared-services,
  or supply agreement bridging a substrate retained by a former parent, consistent with a
  whole-company acquisition in which the acquired entity survives intact as a subsidiary
  rather than being carved out of a larger seller. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- BD acquired 100% of C. R. Bard, Inc. by merging a wholly owned BD subsidiary into Bard,
  with Bard surviving as a wholly owned BD subsidiary; the whole company — its vascular,
  urology, oncology and surgical-specialty product lines together with the operations and
  people that run them — transferred as a going concern for mixed cash-and-stock
  consideration of $222.93 cash plus 0.5077 BD share per Bard share (implied ~$317–$321
  per share), leaving former Bard holders with approximately 15% of BD. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220263, Form DEFM14A]
