# Structural sub-dossier — MainSource Financial Group combination with First Financial Bancorp (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 25, 2017, First Financial Bancorp. and MainSource Financial Group, Inc.
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which MainSource would merge with
  and into First Financial, with First Financial as the surviving corporation; following
  that merger, MainSource Bank (a wholly-owned bank subsidiary of MainSource) would merge
  with and into First Financial Bank, with First Financial Bank as the surviving bank.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus]
- Each outstanding share of MainSource common stock was to be converted into the right to
  receive 1.3875 shares of First Financial common stock (the exchange ratio), a
  stock-for-stock structure, with cash paid only in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement was conditioned on receipt of opinions that the transaction would
  qualify as a tax-free "reorganization" under Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue
  Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- The merger was anticipated to close in early 2018; the surviving corporation's later
  Form 10-K confirms the transaction completed in April 2018 (executives who transitioned
  from MainSource were appointed to First Financial roles effective April 1, 2018). [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0000708955-21-000014,
  Form 10-K]
- At the effective time, MainSource had approximately $4.5 billion in assets and operated
  88 full-service offices in Indiana, Ohio, Illinois, and Kentucky; total purchase
  consideration was approximately $1.1 billion, recognized as approximately $675.6 million
  of goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0000708955-21-000014, Form 10-K]

## What crossed the boundary at the tier level
- Legal vehicle: MainSource Financial Group (Indiana corporation) ceased to exist as a
  separate corporate entity upon merging into First Financial (an Ohio corporation); at
  the bank level, MainSource Bank's separate charter did not survive — it merged into
  First Financial Bank, which continued as the surviving chartered bank. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product/asset: the community-banking franchise (deposit accounts,
  consumer/commercial/agribusiness/real-estate loans, corporate trust services) that
  MainSource Bank delivered from its Indiana/Ohio/Illinois/Kentucky branch network was the
  asset absorbed into First Financial Bank's operations. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent/board: First Financial's board was expanded to 15 seats effective at
  the merger, comprising nine continuing First Financial directors and six continuing
  MainSource directors, to be mutually agreed by the respective board chairs/CEOs. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- People/management: MainSource's President and CEO (Archie M. Brown, Jr.) and First
  Financial's CEO (Claude E. Davis) each entered employment agreements effective at the
  merger's effective time; upon completion, Davis became Executive Chairman and Brown
  became President and CEO of the combined company. Three additional MainSource executive
  officers (Chris M. Harrison, James M. Anderson, Karen B. Woods) were named to continue
  as officers of First Financial after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575,
  DEFM14A]
- The joint proxy statement's "Background of the Merger" records that the MainSource
  board's evaluation weighed "the complementary and compatible aspects of the business of
  MainSource and First Financial, including similar cultures... and similar management
  philosophies with respect to credit and operations." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]

## Community commitments and listing status at closing
- The merger agreement required the surviving corporation to contribute at least $1
  million over the five years following the merger to charitable/community groups in the
  greater Greensburg, Indiana area (MainSource's headquarters community), and the
  MainSource board's stated rationale for the transaction cited a First Financial
  commitment to retain at least 100 jobs in Greensburg. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
- MainSource common stock traded on NASDAQ under "MSFG"; upon completion of the merger,
  MainSource common stock would no longer be quoted on NASDAQ, while First Financial
  common stock (NASDAQ: "FFBC") continued to trade. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two community banks via a stock-for-stock merger in which the
  acquired entity's legal vehicle (both the holding company and its bank subsidiary's
  charter) did not survive, while its customer-facing banking product/branch network was
  absorbed into the surviving bank and a substantial share of its controlling
  people/management (six board seats, the CEO becoming the combined-company CEO, and
  three other named executives) crossed into the combined organization alongside
  contractual community/employment commitments tied to the acquired company's home
  market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001626129-17-000575, DEFM14A]
