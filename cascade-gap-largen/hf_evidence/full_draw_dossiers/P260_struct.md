# Structural sub-dossier — Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. combination with The Walt Disney Company and related spin-off (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Twenty-First Century Fox, Inc. ("21CF") and The Walt Disney Company ("Disney")
  structured the transaction as a combination: prior to the merger, 21CF engaged in an
  internal restructuring ("separation") transferring a portfolio of news, sports and
  broadcast businesses — Fox News Channel, Fox Business Network, Fox Broadcasting
  Company, Fox Sports, Fox Television Stations Group, FS1, FS2, Fox Deportes and Big Ten
  Network, plus certain other assets/liabilities — to a newly formed, wholly owned
  subsidiary, New Fox, Inc.; 21CF then distributed all outstanding New Fox common stock
  pro rata to 21CF stockholders (the "distribution"), and immediately thereafter Disney
  merger subsidiaries merged with and into 21CF and into Disney itself, with the
  remaining 21CF businesses (RemainCo) becoming part of Disney. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger vehicles were WDC Merger Enterprises I, Inc. and WDC Merger Enterprises II,
  Inc. (Disney-side merger subs) and 21CF Distribution Merger Sub, Inc. (the entity that
  effected the distribution merger with 21CF surviving); New Fox, Inc. was, prior to the
  distribution, a wholly owned Delaware subsidiary of 21CF. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration as structured: 21CF stockholders could elect $38.00 per share in cash or
  Disney stock at an exchange ratio collared between 0.3324 and 0.4063 depending on
  Disney's average stock price; total transaction equity value was estimated at $71.3
  billion, consisting of $35.7 billion in cash and roughly 343 million New Disney shares
  valued at approximately $35.6 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the distribution, New Fox was to pay 21CF (RemainCo) an $8.5 billion dividend,
  funded by New Fox incurring new indebtedness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- At actual closing, the transaction was completed via the "Disney Merger" (a Disney
  merger sub into Old Disney) and the "21CF Merger" (a Disney merger sub into 21CF),
  effective March 19-20, 2019; the distribution ratio was set so that 0.263183 of each
  21CF share was exchanged for 1/3 of one Fox Corporation ("FOX") share, and the final
  21CF merger per-share value was $51.572626 in cash or 0.4517 shares of Disney stock,
  with holders of approximately 51.37% of shares electing cash, 36.44% electing stock,
  and 12.19% making no election. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]

## Asset/product tier: what separated and what was acquired
- The retained/spun-off business (New Fox, later renamed Fox Corporation) comprised the
  news, sports and broadcast product lines listed above; 21CF (RemainCo, folded into
  Disney) retained the Twentieth Century Fox film and television studios and certain
  cable and international television businesses, along with 21CF's 30% equity interest
  in Hulu, LLC and 39% interest in Sky. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent, legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- The Murdoch Family Trust and its corporate trustee, Cruden Financial Services LLC,
  beneficially owned approximately 38.4-38.9% of 21CF's super-voting class B common
  stock and entered Voting Agreements with Disney supporting the combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- At the merger effective time, the pre-existing directors of 21CF ceased to be
  directors, and the directors/officers of the Disney merger sub (WDC Merger Enterprises
  II, Inc.) — including Kevin Mayer, Christine McCarthy and Alan Braverman — became the
  directors/officers of 21CF as a Disney subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]
- Rupert Murdoch became Co-Chairman and Lachlan Murdoch became Chairman and Chief
  Executive Officer of New Fox upon completion of the transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the 21CF effective time, 21CF common stock (previously trading under "FOXA"
  and "FOX" on Nasdaq) was suspended from trading and, following the merger, removed
  from listing and deregistered under the Exchange Act; New Disney common stock was to
  be listed on the NYSE under "DIS." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]

## Employee and process continuity across the boundary
- Outstanding equity awards were split by post-closing employer: awards converted into
  New Fox equity for employees employed by New Fox and into Disney restricted stock
  units for employees employed by Disney, each vesting based on continued service with
  the respective post-closing employer; an Employee Matters Agreement was contemplated
  to govern these and related employment matters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]
- In February 2018, 21CF granted Retention RSUs (approximately $325 million of
  compensation expense) to certain senior executives and established a cash-based
  retention program for other employees, with vesting/payment split across dates
  before and after the closing, contingent on continued employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]

## Transitional arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- A Separation Agreement divided or reassigned "shared contracts" relating to both the
  New Fox business and the retained business, provided for New Fox's indemnification of
  21CF's pre-closing guarantees, and required New Fox to post a rolling 12-month letter
  of credit for certain guaranteed obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]
- A Tax Matters Agreement allocated responsibility for taxes arising from the separation
  and distribution between Disney/RemainCo and New Fox. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- A Transition Services Agreement provided that RemainCo/Disney and New Fox would supply
  each other transition services (including technology services) at cost for up to two
  years, and on arm's-length terms thereafter if needed; separate Commercial Agreements
  covered content-sharing, co-production and marketing arrangements between the two
  companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]
- New Fox retained ownership of the "Fox" trademarks and the Fox studio lot; it licensed
  limited, time-bound trademark use back to RemainCo (an 18-month U.S. regional-sports
  license and a 5-year international license) and leased office space on the studio lot
  back to RemainCo for an initial seven-year term with two five-year renewal options. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a spin-off of the retained news/sports/broadcast product
  line and its controlling family's continued leadership into a newly independent
  company (New Fox/Fox Corporation) with a whole-entity cash-and-stock merger of the
  studio, international-television, Hulu and Sky interests into Disney, in which the
  acquired legal entity's pre-existing board and officers were replaced by Disney's own
  designees while a bundle of transitional agreements (separation, tax matters,
  transition services, commercial, IP licenses, studio lease) contractually bridged
  shared contracts, functions, trademarks and facilities between the two resulting
  companies rather than assigning them wholly to one side. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-207641, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0000950157-19-000308, Form 8-K]
