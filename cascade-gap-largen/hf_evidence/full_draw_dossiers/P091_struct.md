## The drawn closing-era filing (what the accession actually reports)
- The drawn closing-era filing is a Form 8-K, dated June 19, 2007 (earliest event June 14, 2007), reporting under Item 8.01 that effective June 14, 2007 the company amended its articles of incorporation to add a new Article XVI providing that, where the board believes the NASAA Statement of Policy Regarding Real Estate Investment Trusts (adopted Sept. 29, 1993) conflicts with the Maryland General Corporation Law, the NASAA REIT Guidelines will control except where the MGCL provisions are mandatory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-048468, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The amendment had been approved by stockholders at the June 4, 2007 annual meeting; the Fifth Articles of Amendment and Restatement are filed as Exhibit 3.1 under Item 9.01. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-048468, 8-K Items 8.01/9.01]
- The 8-K is a corporate charter/governance event, not a property-acquisition or deal-terms document; it was signed by Lori J. Foust, Treasurer and Principal Accounting Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-048468, 8-K signature block]
- Exhibit 3.1 restates the full articles of incorporation (name, purposes/powers as a Maryland corporation qualifying as a REIT, principal office and resident agent, etc.); it is a charter instrument, not a transaction agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-048468, 8-K Exhibit 3.1 (Fifth Articles of Amendment and Restatement)]

## Parties and vehicle at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant was incorporated in October 2004 as a Maryland corporation "to acquire and develop a diversified portfolio of commercial real estate, primarily multi-family, office and industrial distribution buildings, lodging facilities and retail properties, as well as triple-net, single use properties," located in the United States and Canada. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- The company is externally organized around affiliates of its sponsor, Inland Real Estate Investment Corporation, a subsidiary of The Inland Group, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Capital to fund acquisitions was raised through continuous best-efforts public offerings: as of December 31, 2007 the company had raised approximately $5.5 billion of gross offering proceeds and issued 533,404,201 shares; a second public offering commenced August 1, 2007. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]

## What was assembled at the ownership boundary (the customer-facing asset)
- As of December 31, 2007 the company owned interests in 546 retail properties (~11.8M sq ft), 26 office properties (~7.0M sq ft), 61 industrial properties (~14.1M sq ft), 76 hotel properties (10,411 rooms), eight multi-family properties (2,235 units) and ten development properties, all located within the United States. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Reported total assets grew from $3,040,037 thousand (Dec. 31, 2006) to $8,211,758 thousand (Dec. 31, 2007); mortgages, notes and margins payable grew from $1,107,113 thousand to $3,028,647 thousand over the same period (amounts in thousands as reported). [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- A specific whole-company acquisition within the program: on July 1, 2007 the company completed a merger with Winston Hotels, Inc., a publicly traded REIT (Raleigh, NC) owning 44 hotels (5,993 rooms across thirteen states); the company purchased 100% of Winston's common and Series B preferred stock; the transaction valued Winston at ~$822.0 million plus $19.8 million paid to the Business Manager (capitalized), for a total cost of $841.8 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Lodging assets are held through wholly owned subsidiaries — Inland American Winston Hotels, Inc., Inland American Orchard Hotels, Inc. and Inland American Lodging Corporation — which owned 76 lodging properties in 22 states. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- Property-level operating processes were not run by the company's own organization: it entered property management agreements with four affiliated managers (Inland American Retail Management LLC, Inland American Office Management LLC, Inland American Industrial Management LLC, Inland American Apartment Management LLC), all affiliates of The Inland Group, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Day-to-day business operations were run under a business management agreement with Inland American Business Manager & Advisor, Inc., an affiliate of the sponsor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Acquisition/identification processes likewise sat with sponsor affiliates: "Except for the persons employed by our lodging and student housing subsidiaries, we do not employ directly any person(s) responsible for identifying and acquiring properties or other real estate assets," relying instead on IREA (Inland Real Estate Acquisitions, Inc., a wholly owned indirect subsidiary of The Inland Group) and other affiliates under a property acquisition agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- The company employed 104 full-time individuals, primarily at its lodging and student-housing subsidiaries; its executive officers received no compensation from the company and were officers of one or more of The Inland Group's affiliated entities, compensated by those entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- The agreements with the business manager and property managers were not negotiated at arm's length: "We do not have arm's-length agreements with our business manager, property managers or any other affiliates of our sponsor." [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- The property managers (owned principally by related parties of the Business Manager) were entitled to property management fees up to 4.5% of gross operating income plus a 1% oversight fee on operating companies purchased; the company incurred and paid property management fees of $14,328 thousand in 2007. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Under the property acquisition agreement with IREA, the company held rights to acquire properties/REITs/operating companies IREA identifies, subject to superior prior rights IREA had granted to other REITs sponsored by the same sponsor — so acquisition flow was allocated contractually across affiliated programs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
- Growth was also pursued through non-consolidated joint ventures: as of December 31, 2007 the company had joint-venture agreements with 12 entities and had invested approximately $500 million in cash in them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents a charter/governance amendment (subordinating the charter to the NASAA REIT Guidelines where they conflict with the MGCL), not a property or portfolio purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-07-048468, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant assembled a large, diversified commercial-property portfolio (total assets rising from ~$3.0B to ~$8.2B during 2007, including the $841.8M whole-company acquisition of Winston Hotels), funded by continuous best-efforts equity offerings, while the operating processes (property management, leasing, acquisition identification) and the people who run them remained with affiliates of the external sponsor under business-management, property-management and property-acquisition agreements rather than being employed or performed by the company itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-08-000020, 10-K FY2007]
