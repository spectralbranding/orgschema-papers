# Outcome sub-dossier — DISH Network / L-Band Acquisition bid for LightSquared LP assets, realized record

> the transaction and its record over the fixed outcome window. It contains no information
> about how the transaction was structured. Outcome window fixed at construction: the
> documented non-consummation (bid withdrawn effective January 10, 2014), re-confirmed in
> DISH Network Corporation's in-window annual report for fiscal year 2016 (Form 10-K filed

## Realized fate of the transaction
- The bid was terminated by the DISH subsidiary: "On January 7, 2014, LBAC delivered written
  notice of termination of the PSA to the LightSquared LP Lenders. As a result, the PSA
  terminated effective on January 10, 2014, and the LBAC Bid was withdrawn." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-14-012023, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]
- The withdrawal is re-stated in the in-window annual report: the FY2016 Form 10-K again
  reports that the PSA "terminated effective on January 10, 2014, and the LBAC Bid was
  withdrawn." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]
- The target proceeded through its own reorganization rather than the DISH-subsidiary
  purchase: "On March 27, 2015, the Bankruptcy Court entered an order confirming the Modified
  Second Amended Joint Plan pursuant to Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code and, on December 7,
  2015, the Plan became effective." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]

## Realized record over the fixed outcome window
- No assets of the LightSquared LP Entities crossed to DISH or its L-Band subsidiary under
  this transaction: the acquisition was not consummated, the bid having been withdrawn
  effective January 10, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-14-012023, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]
- Related litigation resolved without liability to DISH: on August 6, 2013 Harbinger Capital
  Partners LLC and affiliates filed an adversary proceeding against DISH, LBAC, EchoStar,
  Charles W. Ergen and SP Special Opportunities, LLC ("SPSO"); in "Post-Trial Findings of
  Fact and Conclusions of Law entered on June 10, 2014, the Bankruptcy Court rejected all
  claims against us and EchoStar, and it rejected some but not all claims against the other
  defendants." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]
- The in-window FY2016 Form 10-K contains no acquired LightSquared business line, no related
  goodwill, no impairment, and no divestiture attributable to this transaction — consistent
  with a bid that was withdrawn and never consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The proposed acquisition of the LightSquared LP Entities' assets by DISH's L-Band
  Acquisition, LLC subsidiary did not close: LBAC delivered notice of termination of the
  Plan Support Agreement on January 7, 2014, the PSA terminated effective January 10, 2014,
  and the bid was withdrawn; the target instead completed its own Chapter 11 plan (confirmed
  March 27, 2015; effective December 7, 2015), and DISH's in-window FY2016 annual report
  shows no LightSquared business within its consolidated operations, consistent with a
  transaction that was never consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-14-012023, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-000748, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2016]
