# Structural sub-dossier — Convergys Corporation merger into SYNNEX Corporation (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- SYNNEX Corporation, Delta Merger Sub I, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of SYNNEX),
  Concentrix CVG Corporation (a wholly owned subsidiary of SYNNEX) and Convergys
  Corporation, an Ohio corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  June 28, 2018, as amended August 22, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as two sequential mergers: in the initial merger,
  Merger Sub I merged with and into Convergys, with Convergys surviving as a wholly
  owned subsidiary of SYNNEX and ceasing to be publicly held; immediately thereafter,
  in the subsequent merger, Convergys merged with and into Concentrix CVG Corporation,
  with Concentrix CVG Corporation surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of SYNNEX. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- Convergys shareholders were entitled to receive $13.25 in cash plus 0.1193 shares of
  SYNNEX common stock (subject to a collar) for each Convergys common share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- The mergers closed on October 5, 2018; former Convergys shareholders received an
  aggregate of $1.2 billion in cash and approximately 11.5 million shares of SYNNEX
  common stock, the share consideration valued at approximately $1.0 billion at the
  pre-closing SYNNEX share price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]
- Immediately prior to completion, SYNNEX stockholders were expected to hold
  approximately 78% and former Convergys shareholders approximately 22% of SYNNEX
  common stock outstanding (excluding shares issuable on conversion of Convergys'
  convertible debentures). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]

## What Convergys comprised at closing (business-model / product tier)
- Convergys described itself as a global leader in customer-experience outsourcing
  with approximately 110,000 employees in 33 countries, interacting with clients'
  customers in 58 languages, and identified itself as the second-largest global
  provider in its industry. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- SYNNEX described itself as a business-process-services company providing IT
  distribution, logistics and integration services, and stated that its existing
  wholly owned subsidiary Concentrix Corporation already offered "a portfolio of
  strategic solutions and end-to-end business services around customer engagement
  strategy, process optimization, technology innovation, front and back-office
  automation and business transformation" across ten identified industry verticals —
  i.e., a business the parties described as being in the same customer-engagement
  services line as Convergys. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- Convergys' board identified as a rationale for the mergers the belief that the
  combined company could generate $150 million in annual synergies by fiscal year
  2021 by "reducing costs, expanding its geographic reach and leveraging best
  practices from each company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging Convergys'
  operations to a separate entity was found in the reviewed portions of the DEFM14A;
  the structure folds Convergys directly into an existing operating subsidiary
  (Concentrix CVG Corporation) rather than carving it out. [UNVERIFIED — absence based on the sections of the filing reviewed, not an exhaustive text search of every exhibit]

## Leadership and governance changes at closing (people tier)
- Convergys publicly announced on January 25, 2018 — before the June 28, 2018 merger
  agreement was signed — that its chief executive officer, Andrea J. Ayers, would
  transition from her role once a successor had been selected, and that Convergys had
  initiated a search for her successor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- Convergys and Ms. Ayers entered into a separation and consulting agreement dated
  February 20, 2018, under which the cash award amount for her outstanding Convergys
  RSU and PSU awards would vest upon a change of control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- Each Convergys executive officer was entitled to severance benefits under the
  Convergys Corporation Senior Executive Severance Pay Plan upon a qualifying
  termination in connection with a change of control, except chief commercial officer
  Cormac Twomey, a non-U.S. employee, who was entitled to equivalent severance under
  his individual employment agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- Each director and certain executive officers of Convergys, in their individual
  capacities as shareholders, entered into a voting agreement with SYNNEX to vote
  their Convergys shares in favor of adopting the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time of the mergers, all directors of Convergys resigned from the
  board, and the directors of Concentrix CVG Corporation became the directors of the
  surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On the closing date, all Convergys common shares were cancelled and converted into
  the right to receive the merger consideration and were removed from trading on the
  New York Stock Exchange before the market opened; the NYSE filed a Form 25
  notification of removal from listing with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]
- Concentrix CVG Corporation — the Delaware corporation that survived the subsequent
  merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of SYNNEX — stated its intent to file a Form 15
  to deregister the former Convergys common shares under Section 12(g) of the Exchange
  Act, which would suspend its reporting obligations with respect to those shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]
- Convergys' outstanding convertible debentures ($125.0 million aggregate principal as
  of June 30, 2018) became obligations of Concentrix CVG Corporation, the surviving
  company in the subsequent merger, and ceased to be convertible on the basis of
  Convergys common shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a two-step forward merger — Convergys first survived a
  reverse triangular merger, then was immediately merged into an existing wholly owned
  SYNNEX operating subsidiary (Concentrix CVG Corporation), which survived as the
  legal vehicle going forward — with cash-plus-stock consideration to Convergys
  shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]
- At the governance level, Convergys' entire board resigned at closing and was
  replaced by the directors of the acquiring subsidiary, while Convergys' own chief
  executive had already announced her departure and separation terms months before
  the merger agreement was even signed; the customer-facing business itself (customer
  experience/BPO services, ~110,000 employees) was described by both parties as
  operating in the same line of business as SYNNEX's existing Concentrix subsidiary,
  with no transitional bridging agreement identified in the reviewed filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-037148, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-040187, Form 8-K]
