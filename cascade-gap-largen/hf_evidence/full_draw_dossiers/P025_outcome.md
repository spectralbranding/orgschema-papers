## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented outcome scalars)
- For fiscal 2018 the company reported net sales of $1,610.6 million and a net loss of $(31.7) million (basic and diluted loss per share $(0.71)). [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Selected Financial Data]
- The Selected Financial Data table records a net loss in each of the four full fiscal years following the separation and net income in the separation year: net (loss) income of $46.1 million (2014), $(45.0) million (2015), $(105.5) million (2016), $(43.8) million (2017), and $(31.7) million (2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Selected Financial Data]
- Net sales over the same period were $1,674.2 million (2014), $1,106.2 million (2015), $869.5 million (2016), $1,329.2 million (2017), and $1,610.6 million (2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Selected Financial Data]
- Fiscal 2018 net sales rose $281.4 million (21.2%) over 2017; excluding surcharges the increase was $167.4 million (16.1%), attributed to favorable price/mix (approximately $107 million) and higher volumes (approximately $61 million), with ship tons up 49 thousand tons (4.3%) driven by higher demand in industrial and energy end markets, partially offset by a reduction in billet shipments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A]
- The fiscal 2018 net loss included a fourth-quarter loss from remeasurement of benefit plans of $43.5 million and a third-quarter executive severance cost of $1.7 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Selected Quarterly Financial Data notes]
- The company remained in a valuation-allowance position in 2018 and did not experience a significant reduction in its effective income tax rate or net deferred federal income tax assets from the U.S. tax law changes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A]

## Product / process / organization continuity over time
- The company's product line and operations continued along the same lines established at separation: as of fiscal 2018 it manufactured more than 500 grades of high-performance carbon, micro-alloy and alloy steel sold as ingots, bars, tubes and billets, custom-made for industrial, energy and mobile customers, describing this on the basis of its metallurgical expertise and operational capabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Business]
- Workforce: at December 31, 2018 the company had approximately 3,000 employees, with about 61% covered under one of two collective bargaining agreements (expiring December 2019 and September 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018, Employment]
- No divestiture, sale, or dissolution of the business was reported in the fiscal 2018 annual report; the company continued to operate the steel business as a going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0001598428-19-000018, Form 10-K FY2018]
