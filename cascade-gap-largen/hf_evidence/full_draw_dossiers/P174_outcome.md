## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- On December 2, 2020 (within the outcome window), a press release described an
  all-equity transaction in which FourPoint Energy, LLC — a separate Anadarko Basin
  producer that had, per the release, spent "several months" in negotiations with its
  key stakeholders to reduce its debt and restructure its balance sheet — was renamed
  Unbridled Resources, LLC and merged into a subsidiary of Maverick Natural Resources.
  [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/12/02/2138279/0/en/Western-Anadarko-Basin-Assets-Formerly-Known-and-Managed-as-FourPoint-Energy-are-Renamed-and-Merged-into-new-Maverick-Natural-Resources-Subsidiary-Unbridled-Resources.html]
- The release states that, through this restructuring, funds and accounts managed by EIG
  Global Energy Partners — also described as Maverick's controlling equity owner —
  became the controlling equity owner of FourPoint by exchanging Senior Secured Notes
  for equity, and that "all past and future liabilities and commitments associated with
  the Company will reside with Maverick." [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/12/02/2138279/0/en/Western-Anadarko-Basin-Assets-Formerly-Known-and-Managed-as-FourPoint-Energy-are-Renamed-and-Merged-into-new-Maverick-Natural-Resources-Subsidiary-Unbridled-Resources.html]
- The acquired position was press-described as spanning approximately 700,000 acres
  across the Granite Wash and Cleveland Sand plays in western Oklahoma and northern
  Texas, and as including midstream entities MidPoint Midstream, LLC and Wheeler
  Midstream, LLC. [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2020/12/02/2138279/0/en/Western-Anadarko-Basin-Assets-Formerly-Known-and-Managed-as-FourPoint-Energy-are-Renamed-and-Merged-into-new-Maverick-Natural-Resources-Subsidiary-Unbridled-Resources.html]

## Items beyond the fixed ~3-5-year window (noted for context only)
- On August 16, 2024 (beyond the ~2019-2021 window), press reporting stated that EIG was
  exploring a potential sale of Maverick Natural Resources valued at approximately $3
  billion including roughly $800 million of debt, working with Jefferies as advisor on a
  confidential process that reportedly included an asset-backed securitization tied to
  some of Maverick's assets; production was reported at approximately 60,000 boe/d.
  Neither EIG nor Maverick had publicly commented as of that report. [SRC: https://www.offshore-technology.com/news/maverick-owner-eig-explores-sale/]
