# Structural sub-dossier — Fortress Investment Group LLC acquisition by SB Foundation Holdings LP / SoftBank Group Corp. (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

## Transaction and parties
- On February 14, 2017, Fortress Investment Group LLC (the "Company") entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with SB Foundation Holdings LP ("Parent"), a Cayman
  Islands exempted limited partnership formed February 6, 2017 solely for the
  transaction, and Foundation Acquisition LLC ("Merger Sub"), a Delaware LLC and wholly
  owned subsidiary of Parent, also formed February 6, 2017 solely for the transaction;
  Merger Sub would merge with and into the Company, with the Company surviving as a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A cover letter / "Parties Involved in the Merger"]
- Parent's sole limited partner and the managing member of its general partner is
  Softbank Group Corp. ("Sponsor"), a Japanese kabushiki kaisha established in 1981, a
  holding company for mobile/broadband/fixed-line telecom and internet businesses, whose
  ordinary shares trade on the Tokyo Stock Exchange (code "9984") and which had over
  63,500 employees as of the end of its fiscal year ended March 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Softbank Group Corp."]
- The Company is described as "a leading, highly diversified global investment
  management firm" with approximately $69.6 billion in AUM as of December 31, 2016,
  founded in 1998, with 1,078 asset-management employees (including ~271 investment
  professionals) plus 1,765 employees at senior-living properties it managed for third
  parties; its Class A shares traded on the NYSE under "FIG." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Fortress Investment Group LLC"]
- Under the merger agreement, each outstanding Class A share (with specified exclusions)
  would convert into the right to receive $8.08 in cash without interest — a premium of
  approximately 38.6% over the last trading day before signing — for aggregate Class A
  consideration of approximately $1.91 billion; each Class B share would be cancelled and
  retired for no direct consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A cover letter]

## Founders Agreement (concurrent transaction covering operating-partnership units)
- Concurrently, FIG Corp. and FIG Asset Co. LLC (subsidiaries of the Company, the
  "Buyers") agreed, under a separately executed Founders Agreement, to purchase from the
  three Principals (Peter Briger, Wesley Edens, Randal Nardone) and related holders 100%
  of the "FOG units" — interests in the limited partnerships through which the Company
  conducts its business — not already owned by the Company, at $8.08 per unit (subject to
  reduction for interim tax distributions), for aggregate consideration of approximately
  $1.37 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A cover letter / "Founders Agreement"]
- Each Founders Agreement seller was required to place 50% of after-tax FOG-unit sale
  proceeds into escrow, released 80% on the fourth anniversary and the remainder on the
  fifth anniversary of the Founders Closing (subject to earlier release on specified
  termination events), and to retain investments equal to 90% of existing holdings in
  Company vehicles or SoftBank-related investments until that release. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Founders Agreement"]

## People/organization: retained management authority and employment terms
- In connection with the Founders Agreement, each Principal entered an Amended and
  Restated Employment, Non-Competition, and Non-Solicitation Agreement with FIG LLC, an
  operating subsidiary, effective on the Founders Closing, with an initial five-year term
  auto-renewing annually absent 90-day nonrenewal notice. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Amended and Restated Employment Agreements with the Principals"]
- The Founders Agreement's defined term "Founder Matters" reserves to each Principal
  responsible for an "Applicable Segment" the supervision and authority over the
  day-to-day management, operations, and affairs of that segment — including investment
  of Foundation Fund capital, hiring/termination and compensation allocation for segment
  employees, and new-product creation — "in a manner consistent with the practice of the
  Acquired Companies prior to the Founders Closing," subject to the overall Governance
  Framework. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A Annex B "Founders Agreement" definitions]
- The Principal Compensation Plan was amended and restated effective on the Founders
  Closing, and a related TRA waiver agreement amended tax-related payment assumptions
  affecting future payments to the Principals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Founders Agreement"]
- A "Good Reason" trigger for the Principals under the employment/founders documents
  included failure of the Company Board to be majority-comprised of the three Principals
  together with SoftBank designees, and any acquisition of 65%-or-more beneficial
  ownership by a party other than Softbank Group Corp. and its controlled affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A Annex B "Founders Agreement" — "Good Reason" definition]

## Financing structure
- Sponsor committed, via an equity commitment letter, to fund approximately $3.4 billion
  of equity in Parent; debt commitment letters provided for $1,075 million in aggregate
  debt financing (two senior secured term loans of $800 million and $275 million); the
  DEFM14A states completion was "not subject to any financing condition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]
- No reverse termination fee was payable by Parent; instead, the Company's remedy for a
  Parent breach was specific performance and, under a limited guarantee, an action to
  compel Sponsor to fund its equity commitment. The Company could owe Parent a
  termination fee of $98,350,000 or $131,140,000 depending on the circumstances of
  termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Termination Fees" / "Specific Performance"]

## Closing conditions and regulatory process
- Consummation required expiration/termination of the HSR Act waiting period (filed
  April 21, 2017; expired May 22, 2017), Canadian antitrust approval (granted May 2,
  2017), CFIUS clearance under the Defense Production Act, and satisfaction of an ITAR
  pre-notification requirement and bank-regulatory-matter conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "HSR Act and U.S. Antitrust Matters" / "CFIUS"]
- Supporting Members (the Principals and related holders) entered voting agreements
  covering approximately 34.99% of total voting power, agreeing to vote in favor of the
  merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A "Voting Agreements"]

## Consummation
- On December 27, 2017, the merger closed: the Company became a wholly owned indirect
  subsidiary of Parent, each Class A share converted to $8.08 cash, and, substantially
  concurrently, the Founders Closing occurred with the Buyers purchasing 100% of the
  remaining FOG units at $7.79 per unit (the $8.08 price reduced $0.29 for interim excess
  tax distributions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380394, Form 8-K "Introductory Note"]
- Following the Effective Time, Briger, Edens, and Nardone remained Company directors;
  Michael G. Rantz, George G. Wellde Jr., Yoshimitsu Goto, and Rajeev Misra became
  additional directors; the sole member approved a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited
  Liability Company Agreement for the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380394, Form 8-K Item 5.02 / 5.03]
- In connection with closing, FIG LLC repaid in full and terminated all commitments
  under its January 2016 Credit Agreement, and the Founders' February 2007 Shareholders
  Agreement terminated by its terms at the Founders Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380394, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- The Company notified the NYSE of consummation and requested that the Class A shares no
  longer be listed, and stated its intent to file a certification with the SEC to
  deregister the Class A shares and suspend Exchange Act reporting obligations; a
  Form 15-12B was filed January 8, 2018, showing approximately one (1) holder of record.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380394, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-004978, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A Japan-based strategic holding company (SoftBank Group Corp.), acting through
  newly formed shell entities, acquired 100% of a NYSE-listed global alternative
  investment manager for cash (~$1.91 billion to Class A holders, ~$1.37 billion to
  FOG-unit holders), with financing fully committed and no financing closing condition.
  The founding Principals' day-to-day operating authority over their respective business
  segments, employment terms, and compensation plan were contractually carried forward
  via a concurrent Founders Agreement, employment agreements, and an escrow/investment-
  retention structure, rather than the operating business being restructured under new
  management at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-197197, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380394, Form 8-K]
