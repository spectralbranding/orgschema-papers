## Public-reporting status of the direct acquirer (Concho Resources Inc.)
- Concho Resources Inc. (CIK 0001358071) continued as an SEC-reporting filer after the
  RSP merger, but its last annual report as an independent registrant was its Form 10-K
  for fiscal year 2019, filed February 19, 2020 — approximately 1.5 years after the RSP
  merger closed and before the start of the fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001358071-20-000011, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- On January 15, 2021, ConocoPhillips completed its previously announced acquisition of
  Concho: Falcon Merger Sub Corp. (a wholly owned ConocoPhillips subsidiary) merged with
  and into Concho pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated October 18, 2020,
  with Concho becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of ConocoPhillips; each Concho share was
  converted into the right to receive 1.46 shares of ConocoPhillips common stock. In
  connection with the merger, Concho requested that NYSE withdraw the listing of its
  common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-004795, Form 8-K]
- Concho subsequently filed a Form 15-12B on January 29, 2021 to terminate its own
  registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-009083, Form 15-12B]
- Because Concho itself deregistered on January 29, 2021 — before the fixed ~2021-2023
  outcome window is reached in any of Concho's own periodic filings — no Concho-level
  10-K exists within the window that could separately disclose the trajectory of the
  RSP-derived asset base. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-009083, Form 15-12B]
