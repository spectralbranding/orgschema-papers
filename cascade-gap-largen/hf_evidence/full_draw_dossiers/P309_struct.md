# Structural sub-dossier — Whole Foods Market, Inc. acquisition by Amazon.com, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction is structured as a merger under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  June 15, 2017 among Amazon.com, Inc. (a Delaware corporation), Walnut Merger Sub, Inc.
  (a Texas corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Amazon.com, the "Merger Sub"), and
  Whole Foods Market, Inc. (a Texas corporation, the "Company"), pursuant to which Merger
  Sub merges with and into the Company, with the Company surviving the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, the Company becomes a wholly owned subsidiary of Amazon.com; each
  outstanding share of Company common stock converts into the right to receive $42.00 in
  cash, without interest (an all-cash consideration, no stock component). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time the surviving corporation retains the name "Whole Foods Market,
  Inc.," continues its corporate existence as a Texas corporation, and adopts an amended
  and restated articles of incorporation (per the merger agreement's Exhibit A) together
  with bylaws carried over from Merger Sub. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- As a result of the merger, the Company's common stock will cease trading on the NASDAQ
  Global Select Market and will be deregistered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934,
  ending the Company's status as a separately publicly traded and separately SEC-reporting
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was consummated on August 28, 2017; upon consummation the Company became an
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Amazon.com, Inc., its common stock ceased trading on
  the NASDAQ, and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Amazon.com became the sole holder
  of record of the Company's equity, with no established trading market thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865436-17-000238, Form 10-K (FY ended 2017-09-24), Item 5]

## The business, product, and organization being acquired
- At the time of the proxy statement, the Company described itself as having one
  operating segment — natural and organic foods supermarkets — the leading natural and
  organic foods supermarket and the first national "Certified Organic" grocer, employing
  approximately 87,000 team members as of September 25, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company was incorporated in Texas in 1978, completed its initial public offering in
  January 1992, and is headquartered at 550 Bowie Street, Austin, Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- The origination of the transaction followed a period of shareholder activism: JANA
  Partners representatives met with Company leadership in April 2017 and pressed for
  board and strategic changes; separately, during the week of April 17, 2017 the Company's
  CEO and an Executive Vice President of Operations, together with an outside consultant,
  discussed a media report on Amazon's possible interest, and the consultant was
  authorized to and did contact Amazon's Senior Vice President of Corporate Affairs to
  inquire about an exploratory meeting, which led to the negotiations culminating in the
  merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company represents in the merger agreement that, as of the agreement date, neither
  it nor its subsidiaries is a party to any collective bargaining agreement or other
  agreement with a labor union or works council, and that no work stoppage or labor
  strike was pending or threatened. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]

## Management and workforce continuity provisions at closing
- The joint press release announcing the transaction on June 16, 2017 stated that the
  Company's Chief Executive Officer would remain CEO of Whole Foods Market; the proxy
  statement notes Amazon.com had preliminary discussions with certain Company executive
  officers about retaining them following closing, though no new post-closing employment
  agreements had been finalized as of the proxy statement's filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- Most of the Company's other executive officers participate in a pre-existing "Whole
  Foods Market Executive Retention Plan and Non-Compete Arrangement," which the merger
  triggers as a change-of-control event: it provides two years of employment protections
  and severance following the merger, plus non-compete payments payable in semiannual
  installments over the five years following a qualifying termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- Between signing and closing the Company is contractually restricted (subject to
  specified exceptions) from materially changing employee compensation or benefit plans,
  hiring senior personnel above stated thresholds, entering collective-bargaining
  agreements, or terminating executive officers without cause, operating the workforce in
  the ordinary course pending completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- Amazon.com is required to cause the surviving corporation to maintain, for six years
  after closing, "tail" directors'-and-officers' and fiduciary liability insurance on
  terms at least as favorable as the Company's existing coverage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]

## Regulatory and deal-protection terms
- Completion is conditioned on expiration/termination of the waiting period under the
  Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act (and the Canadian Competition Act); the
  parties filed HSR notifications on June 23, 2017, and Amazon voluntarily withdrew and
  planned to re-file its HSR filing to extend the government's review period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement sets an outside date of February 15, 2018 (extendable to May 16,
  2018 in specified circumstances), a Company termination fee of $400 million payable to
  Amazon.com in specified circumstances, and a capped expense-reimbursement obligation of
  up to $25 million if shareholders do not approve the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction is a whole-company, all-cash reverse merger: the Company survives as an
  intact, wholly owned subsidiary of the acquirer under its pre-existing name, with its
  customer-facing retail business, operating segment, and workforce (absent a collective-
  bargaining structure) crossing together as a going concern rather than being carved into
  separate product/process/people components; the principal negotiated bridges at closing
  are executive-retention/non-compete arrangements for named officers and a public
  statement of CEO continuity, rather than a transition-services or shared-services
  agreement (none was identified in the reviewed filing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006849, Form DEFM14A]
