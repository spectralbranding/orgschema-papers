## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)

- Following the October 2017 merger, the surviving company (Caesars Entertainment
  Corporation, "CEC") continued to file its own periodic reports, including annual
  reports on Form 10-K for fiscal years 2017 through 2019 (the FY2019 Form 10-K was
  filed February 25, 2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0000858339-20-000023, Form 10-K (FY2019), filed 2020-02-25]
- On July 20, 2020, CEC ("Former Caesars") became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Eldorado
  Resorts, Inc., which changed its NASDAQ ticker symbol from "ERI" to "CZR" and
  continued under the name Caesars Entertainment, Inc. ("the Company"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1 Business Overview]
- CEC itself filed a Form 15-12B on July 30, 2020 to deregister its common stock,
  ending its own separate periodic SEC reporting; its realized trajectory after that
  date is disclosed through the successor's (Caesars Entertainment, Inc.) consolidated
  filings, which separately break out Former Caesars' post-acquisition results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-204319, Form 15-12B]

## Realized financial record within the outcome window

- The total purchase consideration for Former Caesars was $10,875 million, comprising
  $6,090 million cash consideration, $2,381 million of shares issued to Former Caesars
  shareholders, $2,356 million cash paid to retire Former Caesars debt, and $48 million
  of other consideration; Former Caesars common stock was converted into the right to
  receive approximately 0.3085 shares of the Company's common stock plus approximately
  $12.41 in cash per share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- For the period July 20, 2020 through December 31, 2020, the properties of Former
  Caesars generated net revenues of $2.1 billion, excluding discontinued operations,
  and a net loss of $1.2 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- On a consolidated basis, the Company (Caesars Entertainment, Inc., reflecting Former
  Caesars' operations from the July 20, 2020 acquisition date forward) reported net
  income (loss) attributable to Caesars of $(1,757) million for fiscal year 2020 and
  $(1,019) million for fiscal year 2021, compared with $81 million for fiscal year
  2019 (the acquirer's last full year before the acquisition). [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Impairment charges recognized in the Company's Consolidated Statements of Operations
  were $215 million for fiscal year 2020 and $102 million for fiscal year 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- At its annual goodwill-impairment assessment as of October 1, 2021, the Company
  determined that the fair value of each reporting unit exceeded its carrying value;
  its goodwill balance was $11,076 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]

## Further corporate activity within the window

- On April 22, 2021, the Company completed a further acquisition, William Hill PLC;
  for the period April 22, 2021 through December 31, 2021, William Hill's operations
  generated net revenues of $183 million, excluding discontinued operations, and a net
  loss of $415 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021), Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Within the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window, the entity that had resulted from the
  2017 all-stock combination (Caesars Entertainment Corporation) was itself absorbed,
  on July 20, 2020, into Eldorado Resorts, Inc. (renamed Caesars Entertainment, Inc.)
  for total consideration of $10,875 million, and deregistered as a separate SEC filer;
  the acquired business's stand-alone post-acquisition results in 2020 (July 20-Dec 31)
  showed net revenues of $2.1 billion against a net loss of $1.2 billion, and the
  combined successor company reported consolidated net losses attributable to Caesars
  of $1,757 million (FY2020) and $1,019 million (FY2021), alongside impairment charges
  of $215 million (FY2020) and $102 million (FY2021), before a subsequent goodwill test
  in October 2021 showed no impairment indicated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590895-22-000061, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-204319, Form 15-12B]
