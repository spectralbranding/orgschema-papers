## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material
  Definitive Agreement); the date of earliest event reported is April 13, 2010 and the
  report was signed April 14, 2010 by Dennis M. Seremet, Senior Vice President and Chief
  Financial Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / signature block]
- On April 13, 2010, NVR, Inc. (a Virginia corporation, the "Company") entered into a
  "stalking horse" Asset Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement") with Orleans Homebuilders,
  Inc. and certain of its affiliates (collectively, the "Seller"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Seller is a debtor in a Chapter 11 case before the United States Bankruptcy Court
  for the District of Delaware (the "Bankruptcy Court"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction type as structured
- Under the Agreement the Company agreed to purchase "substantially all of the land under
  development, homebuilding work in process and other related assets of the Seller"
  (together, the "Assets"), subject to the terms and conditions contained therein. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The effectiveness of the Agreement was subject to the approval of the Bankruptcy Court;
  if approved, the Company would be designated as the "stalking horse" bidder in an auction
  of the Assets under Section 363 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code, and its offer would be the
  standard by which other bids would be evaluated. Other interested bidders who submitted
  qualifying offers would be permitted to participate in the auction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What was proposed to cross the boundary — the asset/product
- The customer-facing asset base is that of a homebuilding operation: the Assets to be
  acquired are the Seller's land under development, homebuilding work in process, and other
  related assets. The reported terms describe a purchase of these assets and the assumption
  of specified liabilities related to the Assets; the Form 8-K does not describe a transfer
  of the Seller's workforce, management, or operating organization as part of the reported
  terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The full terms are governed by the Asset Purchase Agreement filed as Exhibit 10.1
  ("Asset Purchase Agreement Among Orleans Homebuilders, Inc. and the Seller Affiliates
  Named Herein, as Sellers, and NVR, Inc., as Purchaser, Dated as of April 13, 2010");
  the exhibit itself was not quoted in this sub-dossier and any term not appearing in the
  Item 1.01 narrative is [UNVERIFIED] here. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 9.01 / Index to Exhibits, Exhibit 10.1]

## Proposed consideration and legal mechanics
- Absent any higher or otherwise better bid, the Company agreed to acquire the Assets for
  $170 million in cash plus the assumption of specified liabilities related to the Assets,
  subject to adjustment based upon changes in the balance sheet subsequent to February 28,
  2010 and other adjustments customary for real estate transactions of this nature. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company was to deposit in the aggregate $17 million into escrow, to be credited to
  the purchase price on completion of the acquisition; if the Agreement were terminated the
  deposit would be returned to the Company unless the Company defaulted, in which event the
  Seller would retain the deposit. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- If the Bankruptcy Court approved the Agreement and it were later terminated for certain
  reasons — including because the Seller entered into a competing transaction — the Seller
  could be required to pay the Company a termination fee equal to $3.4 million plus the
  Company's reasonable transaction expenses up to $1 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Conditions to completion (as structured)
- Completion of the acquisition was subject to a number of customary conditions, including,
  among others, entry of the Bidding Procedures Order and the Sale Order by the Bankruptcy
  Court, performance by each party of its obligations under the Agreement, and the material
  accuracy of each party's representations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (as structured)
- The transaction was structured as a proposed distressed-asset acquisition: NVR, Inc., as
  stalking-horse bidder, agreed to purchase substantially all of the land under development,
  homebuilding work in process, and other related assets of a Chapter 11 debtor (Orleans
  Homebuilders, Inc. and affiliates) for $170 million cash plus assumption of specified
  liabilities, via a court-approved Section 363 auction subject to higher or better bids,
  backed by a $17 million escrow deposit and a $3.4 million (plus up-to-$1 million expenses)
  reverse termination fee payable to NVR under specified conditions; the reported terms
  concern an asset base (land/work-in-process inventory) and do not describe transfer of the
  Seller's workforce or operating organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-034817, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Item 9.01]
