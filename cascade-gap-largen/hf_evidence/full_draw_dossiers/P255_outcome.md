## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On July 3, 2017, Alon USA Energy, Inc. filed a Form 15 certifying termination of
  registration of its common stock under Section 12(g)/Rule 12h-3(b) of the Exchange
  Act, recording approximately 2 holders of record as of the certification date; no
  subsequent Form 10-K was filed by Alon USA Energy, Inc. itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000051, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer's post-merger holding company, renamed Delek US Holdings, Inc. (CIK
  0001694426, ticker DK; formerly "Delek Holdco, Inc." through July 10, 2017), is a
  continuing SEC filer and separately discloses the former-Alon refining assets by
  name in its own annual reports; this dossier uses its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020
  (filed 2021-03-01) as the in-window outcome record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized operating record of the acquired refining assets (~2020)
- As of fiscal year-end 2020, Delek US Holdings reported that it "owns and operates
  four independent refineries located in Tyler, Texas, El Dorado, Arkansas, Big Spring,
  Texas and Krotz Springs, Louisiana," a combined 302,000 bpd of crude throughput
  capacity, naming the two Alon-acquired refineries (Big Spring and Krotz Springs) as
  continuing, identifiable operating units within its integrated refining segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Delek's FY2020 10-K states that effective July 1, 2017 it "acquired the outstanding
  common stock of Alon USA Energy, Inc. ("Alon")," and that by virtue of the merger it
  also acquired an 81.6% limited-partner interest and a non-economic general-partner
  interest in the then-publicly traded Alon USA Partners, LP, subsequently acquiring
  the remaining outstanding units of that partnership on February 7, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented disruption at the acquired-asset level (~2020)
- Delek's FY2020 10-K discloses that its "quantitative assessment of goodwill performed
  on the reporting units in our refining and retail segments during the fourth quarter
  of 2020, resulted in an impairment of $126.0 million during the year ended December
  31, 2020, related to our Big Spring refinery and Krotz Springs refinery reporting
  units" — i.e., specifically the two refineries that had been Alon's directly held
  operating assets at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The same disclosure states the impairment "was predominantly the result of the
  continued uncertainty regarding the impact of the COVID-19 Pandemic," which affected
  valuation inputs including the weighted-average cost of capital used in the
  discounted-cash-flow fair-value analysis of those reporting units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]
- In the same fiscal year, Delek disclosed that "in 2020 we focused on divesting our
  remaining non-operating refinery located in Bakersfield, California" — part of the
  non-operating California refining assets Alon had held at closing — and on May 7,
  2020 sold its equity interest in that non-operating Bakersfield refinery to a
  subsidiary of Global Clean Energy Holdings, Inc. for $40.0 million in cash,
  recording a realized gain on the sale of $56.8 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Alon USA Energy, Inc. itself deregistered from SEC reporting on July 3, 2017 and has
  no post-close public periodic-reporting record; the acquirer, Delek US Holdings, Inc.
  (the renamed HoldCo entity), continues as a public filer and separately discloses the
  acquired refining assets by name, satisfying the acquirer-discloses-the-business
  branch of the no-record convention. [SRC: SEC accession 0001325955-17-000051, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Within the fixed ~2020 outcome window, the documented realized record for the
  acquired assets shows continued physical operation of both acquired refineries
  (Big Spring, Krotz Springs) as named units inside a larger four-refinery system,
  alongside a $126.0 million goodwill impairment charge specifically attributed to
  those two reporting units and a completed divestiture (at a realized gain) of the
  non-operating California refining asset also acquired in the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001694426-21-000034, Form 10-K FY2020]
