## Realized annual financial record (last full public year)
- For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2015 the company reported net income of $41,623 thousand, following net losses in each of the prior reported years: $(51,685) thousand (2014), $(54,745) thousand (2013), $(68,659) thousand (2012) and $(26,976) thousand (2011). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-012359, Form 10-K FY2015]
- Net income (loss) allocable to common shareholders was $40,746 thousand for 2015, versus $(51,756) thousand for 2014; basic earnings per share allocable to common shareholders was $0.70 for 2015 versus $(0.90) for 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-012359, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The FY2015 net income included a $26,558 thousand gain on extinguishment of debt; the company stated it had "a history of prior net losses" and could not assure "sustained profitability going forward." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-16-012359, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Realized ownership outcome (whole-company acquisition and deregistration)
- On July 6, 2016 (the "Effective Time") the company completed a merger with BSREP II Retail Holdings Corp., a wholly owned subsidiary of BSREP II Retail Pooling LLC and an affiliate of Brookfield Asset Management Inc. ("Brookfield"), whereby the company became a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; the merger was effected pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated February 25, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-131198, Form 8-K (July 6, 2016)]
- At the Effective Time each outstanding share of company common stock was converted into the right to receive $18.25 in cash (other than shares held by Brookfield-affiliated Exchange Parties, Parent, Acquisition Sub, the Guarantors, and dissenting shares perfecting appraisal rights). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-131198, Form 8-K (July 6, 2016)]
- On July 6, 2016 the company requested that the NYSE suspend trading and withdraw the common stock from listing; the common stock was to be delisted on July 18, 2016, and the company stated it intended to file a Form 15 to deregister under the Exchange Act and suspend its reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-131198, Form 8-K (July 6, 2016)]
- The company filed a Form 15-12B on July 18, 2016 to certify termination of registration / suspend reporting duties for the common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-132940, Form 15-12B]

## Realized management / organizational record at the interface
- In connection with the merger and at the Effective Time, President and Chief Executive Officer Andrew Silberfein resigned as CEO and as a director, and Chief Financial Officer John Wain resigned; the termination of both was without cause. Brian Kingston (previously sole director of Acquisition Sub) continued as the company's sole director. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-131198, Form 8-K (July 6, 2016)]
- On July 8, 2016 Rouse Properties, Inc. was converted into a limited liability company named "Rouse Properties, LLC" (as successor in interest to Rouse Properties, Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-132441, Form 8-K (July 8, 2016)]
