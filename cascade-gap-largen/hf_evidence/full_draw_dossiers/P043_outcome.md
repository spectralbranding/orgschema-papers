## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant remained an independent SEC-reporting public company and filed an audited
  Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016 (period ended December 31, 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Best-documented realized outcome scalar
- Net income attributable to Phillips 66 for 2016 was $1,555 million, compared with $4,227
  million in 2015 and $4,762 million in 2014 (selected financial data). [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016 (Selected Financial Data)]
- The company reported that earnings from continuing operations decreased $2,672 million, or
  63 percent, in 2016, mainly reflecting lower realized refining margins and lower olefins
  and polyolefins margins, and the 2015 recognition of $242 million of deferred gain related
  to the 2013 sale of the Immingham Combined Heat and Power Plant, partially offset by lower
  equity losses from DCP Midstream. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016 (Management's Discussion and Analysis)]
- Total assets were $51,653 million and long-term debt was $9,588 million at year-end 2016
  (selected financial data). [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016 (Selected Financial Data)]
- The company had approximately 14,800 employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Documented reconfiguration at the process/organization interface over time
- By fiscal 2016 the company reported its operations in four operating segments — Midstream,
  Chemicals, Refining, and Marketing and Specialties (M&S) — rather than the three-segment
  structure in place at the 2012 separation, with the former combined refining/marketing
  activity now reported as two separate segments (Refining, and Marketing and Specialties).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The Midstream segment by 2016 included the company's own master limited partnership,
  Phillips 66 Partners LP, alongside the 50 percent equity investment in DCP Midstream, LLC
  — a midstream vehicle established under independent ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The Refining segment operated 13 refineries in 2016, mainly in the United States and
  Europe. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]
- The Chemicals segment continued to consist of the 50 percent equity investment in Chevron
  Phillips Chemical Company LLC (CPChem). [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]

## Documented disruption at the product/process interface over time
- The company attributed the 2016 earnings decline chiefly to lower realized refining margins
  and lower olefins/polyolefins margins — margin compression in the core refining and
  chemicals operations rather than an inability to operate the assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016 (Management's Discussion and Analysis)]
- No goodwill impairment or asset write-down of the registrant's own operations is recorded
  as a driver of the 2016 result in the reviewed disclosure; the reference to goodwill and
  other asset impairments recorded in 2015 was attributed to the DCP Midstream equity
  investee, not to the registrant's consolidated operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016 (Management's Discussion and Analysis)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~4-5-year outcome window the registrant continued as an independent,
  going-concern public company: it remained operationally intact (13 refineries; four
  operating segments; ~14,800 employees) and expanded its own corporate substrate (forming
  the Phillips 66 Partners LP master limited partnership), while realized net income
  attributable to Phillips 66 fell to $1,555 million in 2016 from $4,227 million in 2015 —
  a 63 percent decrease in continuing-operations earnings that management attributed to lower
  refining and petrochemical margins rather than to any documented failure to align the
  acquired assets with operations or operations with the organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001534701-17-000051, Form 10-K FY2016]
