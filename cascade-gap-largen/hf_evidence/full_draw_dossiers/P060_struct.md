## The drawn filing (described truthfully)
- The drawn document is RadioShack Corporation's Annual Report on Form 10-K for the fiscal
  year ended December 31, 2012, filed February 26, 2013; it is an annual report, not the
  transaction-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000010, Form 10-K cover]
- That 10-K's exhibit index lists the venture agreements only by incorporation-by-reference:
  a Joint Venture Agreement dated June 6, 2012 (filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Form 10-Q filed
  July 25, 2012) and an Amended and Restated Joint Venture Agreement dated January 31, 2013
  (filed as Exhibit 99.1 to the Form 8-K filed February 1, 2013), among RadioShack Global
  Sourcing (Hong Kong) Ltd., First Honest Enterprises Limited, and Perfect Legend Development
  Limited; the 10-K body does not set out the venture's terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000010, Form 10-K Index to Exhibits]

## The registrant's own operation at closing (what remained on RadioShack's side)
- RadioShack Corporation, incorporated in Delaware in 1967, primarily engaged in the retail
  sale of consumer electronics goods and services through its RadioShack store chain, operating
  4,395 U.S. company-operated stores under the RadioShack brand at December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000010, Form 10-K Item 1 Business]
- At December 31, 2012 RadioShack employed approximately 34,500 people. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000010, Form 10-K Item 1 Employees]
- Its private brands included RadioShack, AUVIO, Enercell, and Gigaware; its store operations
  were supported by internal functions — three U.S. distribution centers, RadioShack Technology
  Services (point-of-sale/IT), and RadioShack Global Sourcing (import/export, sourcing, logistics,
  quality control). [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000010, Form 10-K Item 1 Patents and Trademarks / Support Operations]

## The transaction as structured (from the deal-terms filing)
- A subsidiary of RadioShack Corporation and a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of SMS Marketing
  Service (Asia) Co., Ltd. ("Cybermart"), an affiliate of Hon Hai Precision Industry Co. Ltd.,
  formed a jointly owned entity through which the parties would operate small-format retail stores
  in China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau offering consumer electronic products, accessories, and
  related services, owned, leased, or franchised by the entity or its subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Amended and Restated Joint Venture Agreement was entered on January 31, 2013, completing the
  formation of the entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Equity was split RadioShack 49% / Cybermart 51%; the board initially comprised five directors,
  two nominated by RadioShack and three by Cybermart — i.e., the counterparty held majority equity
  and majority board representation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Initial cash contributions were US$2.94 million (RadioShack) and US$3.06 million (Cybermart), with
  up to an additional US$34 million contributable in proportion over the next three years. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Share transfers were restricted; on missed financial thresholds or a deadlock, a buy-sell mechanism
  at fair market value applied, failing which the entity could be liquidated; the parties granted the
  entity a right of first refusal on certain retail business opportunities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the boundary, and how the shared substrate was bridged
- The customer-facing brand/product was extended into the new entity by contract: RadioShack entered
  a License Agreement granting the entity the exclusive right to operate under the "RadioShack" brand
  and trademarks in China, Taiwan, Hong Kong, and Macau, and a separate License Agreement permitting
  the entity to manufacture (or cause to be manufactured) "RadioShack"-branded televisions for sale
  there. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Product supply was bridged by a Master Sourcing Agreement designating RadioShack as the preferred
  supplier of certain accessory products, including RadioShack private brands, to the entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The in-market retail operations were to be run by the majority-controlled new entity; RadioShack's
  own U.S. store-operating processes and personnel (Item 1 above) were not transferred into it — the
  brand, product-supply, and capital crossed the boundary via license/supply contracts and a
  contribution schedule rather than by moving RadioShack's producing/delivering store operations or
  its store organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A U.S. consumer-electronics retailer contributed its brand (trademark license), product supply
  (preferred-supplier sourcing agreement), and minority capital (49% equity, 2 of 5 board seats) into
  a newly formed, counterparty-majority-controlled entity that would itself run small-format retail
  stores in four Asian markets — the brand and product-supply crossing by contract while the
  operating processes and management of the in-market stores sat with the majority holder, bridged at
  closing by the license, sourcing, and capital-contribution agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000096289-13-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
