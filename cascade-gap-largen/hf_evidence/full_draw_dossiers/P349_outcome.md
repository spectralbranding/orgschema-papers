## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)

- Following the May 23, 2019 closing, the acquired entity filed a Form 15-12G to terminate its own registration and reporting obligations under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act (filed June 3, 2019); no Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by the acquired entity itself since deregistration, so no independent, audited standalone financial statements exist for the acquired business for the ~2021-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-163317, Form 15-12G]
- The acquirer's own consolidated Forms 10-K for fiscal years 2021 and 2022 do separately disclose a number of facts specific to the acquired business (reserve development under a dedicated reinsurance treaty tied to the acquisition, a divestiture of part of the acquired international operations, and financial-strength ratings of the acquired insurance subsidiaries), even though the acquired business is not reported as its own segment — its results are folded into the acquirer's "Commercial Lines" reporting segment / "global specialty" business unit rather than disclosed as a standalone income statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-23-000023, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Realized record: acquired-business-specific disclosures (acquirer's FY2021-FY2022 Forms 10-K)

- The acquired insurance subsidiaries continue to be marketed under the "Navigators" brand, described in the acquirer's FY2021 Form 10-K as "a brand of The Hartford," with "the vast majority of the business written by our Navigators Group insurance subsidiaries" reported within the acquirer's global specialty business unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 / Note 11]
- Immediately after closing (effective May 23, 2019), the acquirer purchased a dedicated excess-of-loss reinsurance agreement (the "Navigators ADC") from National Indemnity Company covering up to $300 million of adverse net loss reserve development on the acquired insurers' pre-acquisition (2018 and prior accident year) reserves, paying a $91 million reinsurance premium at inception; the acquirer's FY2021 Form 10-K separately reports $102 million (before tax, in 2020) and $91 million (before tax, in 2021) of additional adverse reserve development on those 2018-and-prior Navigators reserves, ceded under this treaty. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 2 "Business Acquisitions" / MD&A]
- The acquirer's FY2022 Form 10-K discloses that, "As of December 31, 2022, the Company has recorded a reinsurance recoverable under the Navigators ADC of $300 [million] as estimated cumulative loss development on the 2018 and prior accident year reserves has exhausted the treaty limit," and that there is "no remaining limit available" under that treaty as of that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-23-000023, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Reinsurance for A&E and Navigators Group Reserve Development"]
- On December 29, 2021, the acquirer completed the sale of all of the issued and outstanding equity of Navigators Holdings (Europe) N.V., a Belgium holding company acquired as part of the transaction, and its subsidiaries Bracht, Deckers & Mackelbert N.V. and Assurances Continentales Continentale Verzekeringen, receiving approximately $20 million before approximately $9 million of transaction costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 22 "Business Dispositions"]
- On August 15, 2022, S&P upgraded the financial-strength rating of The Navigators Group, Inc. and its core operating subsidiaries, including Navigators Insurance Company ("NIC"), to A+ from A with a stable outlook; as of the FY2022 Form 10-K's ratings table (dated February 23, 2023), NIC was rated A+ by A.M. Best, A+ by S&P, and "Not Rated" by Moody's. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-23-000023, Form 10-K FY2022, "Ratings"]
- The acquirer states it "has been successful in cross-selling global specialty product lines acquired through the Navigators Insurance Group acquisition to customers of small commercial and of middle & large commercial and seeks to expand cross-sell opportunities in the future." [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1 "Marketing and Distribution"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- The acquired entity deregistered under the Exchange Act shortly after closing and has filed no periodic reports since, so no independent public financial-statement record exists for it in the ~2021-2022 outcome window. The acquirer's own Forms 10-K for that window separately disclose (but do not consolidate into a standalone segment income statement) several business-specific facts: the acquired insurers' brand and underwriting operations continued under the "Navigators" name within the acquirer's global specialty business unit; a dedicated $300 million adverse-development reinsurance treaty tied to the acquired business's pre-2019 reserves was fully exhausted by year-end 2022 after cumulative reserve strengthening; part of the acquired international operations (a Belgian holding company and its subsidiaries) was divested in December 2021; and the acquired insurance subsidiaries' financial-strength ratings were upgraded by S&P in August 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-22-000019, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000874766-23-000023, Form 10-K FY2022]
