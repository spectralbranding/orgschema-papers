# Structural sub-dossier — ConocoPhillips / Origin Energy Australia Pacific LNG joint venture (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- In October 2008 ConocoPhillips closed a transaction with Origin Energy, described as
  "an integrated Australian energy company," forming a 50/50 joint venture, Australia
  Pacific LNG (APLNG), focused on coalbed methane (CBM) production from the Bowen and
  Surat Basins in Queensland, Australia, and on LNG processing and export sales.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- The stated purpose was to give ConocoPhillips access to Australian coalbed methane
  resources and to "enhance our LNG position" through an additional LNG hub targeting
  Asia Pacific markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- As of this filing, gas from the venture was being sold to domestic customers, while
  the LNG processing and export business remained in development; a final investment
  decision on the initial phase of the LNG project was planned for mid-2011, after this
  10-K's filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- Two initial 4.2-million-tons-per-year LNG trains were anticipated, envisioning over
  10,000 gross wells and supporting gas gathering, processing/compression, water
  treatment, and a new export pipeline — all described as future development, not yet
  built. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- The project received environmental approval from the Queensland state government in
  November 2010 and from the Australian federal government in late February 2011.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]

## Consideration and accounting form
- Under the agreements with Origin Energy, ConocoPhillips could make up to four
  additional payments of $500 million each to Origin, conditional on up to four LNG
  trains being approved and developed by the joint venture and on other financial and
  operating milestones being achieved — i.e., a contingent, milestone-triggered
  consideration structure rather than a fixed lump-sum purchase price.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- At December 31, 2010, the book value of ConocoPhillips's equity-method investment in
  APLNG was $9,159 million, including $3,244 million of cumulative translation effects
  from a strengthening Australian dollar; its 50 percent share of APLNG's historical
  cost-basis net assets under U.S. GAAP was $1,187 million, producing a basis difference
  of $7,948 million allocated to APLNG's exploration and production license areas.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- ConocoPhillips accounted for its APLNG interest under the equity method (i.e., APLNG
  was not consolidated into ConocoPhillips's financial statements); the filing lists
  APLNG among several "affiliated companies in which we had a significant equity
  investment," alongside other 50/50 or minority-interest joint ventures.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not, at this filing date
- What crossed: a 50 percent equity interest in a jointly controlled venture holding
  CBM exploration/production licenses and rights to develop an LNG export business, plus
  a contractual obligation for further milestone-conditioned payments to the JV partner.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- What had NOT yet crossed into operation at this filing date: the customer-facing LNG
  export product itself — no LNG trains, processing facilities, or export pipeline had
  been built; the business was pre-final-investment-decision, and only domestic gas
  sales (not LNG) were occurring. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- The filing does not name a specific legal-entity vehicle for APLNG beyond describing
  it as a "50/50 joint venture," does not describe an operator/management division of
  labor between ConocoPhillips and Origin Energy for upstream versus downstream
  activities, and discloses no dedicated APLNG personnel counts, management team, or
  employee transfer from either partner into the venture.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
- As a general risk factor applicable to its joint-venture portfolio (including APLNG),
  ConocoPhillips disclosed that such investments "decrease our ability to manage risk"
  because it "may share control with our joint venture participants," who may have
  interests or objectives inconsistent with its own.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]

## Structural summary (closing/filing-date only)
- At this filing date the transaction had transferred a 50 percent equity-method
  ownership interest and milestone-conditioned future payment obligations into a
  jointly controlled CBM/LNG development venture with Origin Energy; the customer-facing
  LNG export product, its producing/delivering infrastructure (trains, pipeline,
  processing), and any operator/management division of labor had not yet been built out
  or disclosed, with the venture still pending a final investment decision and pending
  federal environmental approval as of the filing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-016957, Form 10-K (FY2010)]
