## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is a Form 8-K filed 2008-11-10 that reports two distinct matters: (i)
  Item 2.01, completion on November 6, 2008 of a separate asset purchase from Independence
  Lead Mines Company (not the transaction dossiered here), and (ii) Item 3.02, the
  November 4, 2008 issuance of unregistered shares under a joint-venture operating
  agreement. The joint venture — the transaction in scope — is described in the drawn
  filing only by reference; its terms are sourced below from the contemporaneous
  formation and amendment Forms 8-K it incorporates. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-002254, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.02]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On February 21, 2008, Rio Grande Silver, Inc. ("RGS"/"Rio"), a wholly-owned subsidiary
  of Hecla Mining Company, entered into an Exploration, Development and Mine Operating
  Agreement with Emerald Mining & Leasing, LLC ("EML") and Golden 8 Mining, LLC ("G8") to
  explore, evaluate and possibly develop and mine mineral resources on certain properties
  in Mineral County, Colorado; the parties "manage and operate under the name of 'San Juan
  Silver Mining Joint Venture'," with RGS as the initial operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Structure is a contractual unincorporated joint venture governed by the operating
  agreement (Exhibit 2.1), not a new legal entity acquisition; RGS is appointed Manager
  "with overall management responsibility for Operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- The customer-facing asset (the mineral properties) did NOT change hands at closing: EML
  and G8 contribute the Assets (valued at $9,428,571; $4,714,286 each), and "Title to the
  Assets shall be held by EML and G8 as equal cotenants" until RGS earns a 70%
  Participating Interest or withdraws, at which point EML and G8 assign RGS a co-tenancy
  interest equal to its Participating Interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §3.4, §5.1]
- Initial Participating Interests at the Effective Date: RGS 0%, EML 50%, G8 50%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §4.1 (Initial Participating Interests)]
- RGS's stake is a phased earn-in funded by Hecla stock and future spending, not an
  outright purchase: an Initial Contribution of $8,000,000 in Hecla common stock (paid as
  927,716 unregistered shares valued at $8,900,000, EML and G8 each 463,858) earning a 30%
  inchoate interest; then $6,000,000 in Qualifying Expenses plus $2,000,000 in Hecla shares
  by the first anniversary → 60%; $9,000,000 in Qualifying Expenses within 24 months → 65%;
  $12,000,000 in Qualifying Expenses within 36 months → 70%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §5.1, §5.2; SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Operations and people at closing (what moved, what did not)
- Operatorship (the producing/exploring PROCESS) crossed to the Hecla side while asset
  title stayed with EML and G8: RGS "shall be the initial operator" and, as Manager,
  "shall manage, direct and control Operations" and prepare Programs and Budgets. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K Item 1.01; SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.1, §8.2]
- Governance is shared through a Management Committee, with certain extension votes taken
  "on a per capita vote (not a vote based on Participating Interest)." [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §5.2, Art. VIII]
- The retrieved filings disclose no transfer of employees, management, or an organization
  from EML/G8 to the Hecla side; the venture is structured as a contractual
  interest-plus-operatorship arrangement over acreage. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [UNVERIFIED: any transfer of EML/G8 personnel or a separate venture staffing]

## Amendment at closing era and bridging / guaranty arrangements
- On October 24, 2008, Rio and the counterparties amended the operating agreement: the
  Qualifying-Expense schedule was delayed (Rio must now incur $9,000,000 by the fourth
  anniversary and $12,000,000 by the fifth anniversary, and is no longer required to incur
  the initial $6,000,000 by the first anniversary), and Hecla was required to issue EML and
  G8 $2,000,000 in unregistered shares ($1,000,000 each) by no later than November 1, 2008. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-220316, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The amendment provided that upon that share issuance, "Rio will cause Hecla to guarantee
  certain indemnification obligations of EML and G8 up to a maximum liability of $2,500,000
  pursuant to a conditional limited guaranty" — the closing-era mechanism bridging the
  parties' shared substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-220316, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On November 4, 2008, Hecla issued a total of 633,360 unregistered shares (316,680 each to
  EML and G8), valued at approximately $2,000,000, under the amended agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-002254, Form 8-K Item 3.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing the transaction was a contractual mining joint venture in which EML and G8
  contributed and retained title to the Colorado mineral properties (initial interests EML
  50% / G8 50% / RGS 0%), while Hecla's subsidiary RGS took the operator/Manager role and
  a phased earn-in toward a 70% Participating Interest funded by Hecla stock and staged
  exploration spending, bridged by a Hecla conditional limited guaranty capped at
  $2,500,000; no personnel or organization were disclosed as transferring, and the asset
  itself did not change ownership at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-000403, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-2.1 §3.4, §4.1, §5.1, §5.2, §8.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-08-220316, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000897101-08-002254, Form 8-K Item 3.02]
