## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The combined holding company, Evergy, Inc. (CIK 0001711269), continued as an SEC
  registrant after closing and filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31,
  2021, filed February 25, 2022, which falls within a ~3-5-year window after the ~2018
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2021)]
- As of that filing, Evergy, Inc. common stock remained registered and listed on the
  New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "EVRG." [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]

## Organizational and naming record within the window
- The FY2021 Form 10-K describes Evergy as "a public utility holding company
  incorporated in 2017 and headquartered in Kansas City, Missouri," operating through
  wholly-owned direct subsidiaries including Evergy Kansas Central, Inc., Evergy Metro,
  Inc., Evergy Missouri West, Inc., and Evergy Transmission Company, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- The filing's exhibit index identifies Evergy Kansas Central, Inc. as "formerly Westar
  Energy, Inc." and Evergy Metro, Inc. as "formerly Kansas City Power & Light Company" —
  i.e., the legacy operating subsidiaries continued to exist within the group but under
  new subsidiary names adopted after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- At December 31, 2021, the Evergy companies reported 4,930 employees, including 2,632
  represented by five local unions of the International Brotherhood of Electrical
  Workers and one local union of the United Government Security Officers of America.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]

## Realized financial record (best-documented outcome scalars)
- Net income attributable to Evergy, Inc. was $879.7 million in 2021 (diluted EPS
  $3.83), compared with $261.4 million in 2020 (diluted EPS $1.11); the filing
  attributes the 2021 increase primarily to non-regulated energy-marketing margins tied
  to the February 2021 winter weather event, higher retail sales, and lower operating
  and maintenance expenses, partially offset by higher property taxes, depreciation and
  income tax expense. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- Adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings were $812.6 million ($3.54 per share) in 2021 and $705.5
  million ($3.10 per share) in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- In computing adjusted (non-GAAP) earnings, the company excluded $10.5 million (2021)
  and $7.6 million (2020) of "costs associated with executive transition ... including
  inducement bonuses, severance agreements and other transition expenses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- The annual goodwill impairment test for "$2,336.6 million of goodwill from the Great
  Plains Energy and Evergy Kansas Central merger" was conducted as of May 1, 2021 on a
  single consolidated reporting unit; the fair value of the reporting unit "substantially
  exceeded" its carrying amount including goodwill, and no goodwill impairment was
  recorded. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- In February 2022, Evergy's board declared a quarterly common dividend of $0.5725 per
  share, payable March 21, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~3-5-year post-closing window, the surviving combined public filer (Evergy,
  Inc.) continued to report as a single consolidated NYSE-listed utility holding company,
  with the two legacy operating utilities continuing to exist under new subsidiary names
  rather than as separately disclosed segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- The single best-documented outcome scalar tied specifically to the transaction is the
  goodwill from the merger ($2,336.6 million), which was tested for impairment as of May
  1, 2021 and found not impaired (fair value substantially exceeded carrying value).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- The only documented friction at the management/organization tier within the window is
  a recurring "executive transition" cost line (inducement bonuses and severance
  agreements: $10.5 million in 2021, $7.6 million in 2020) that the company excludes from
  its non-GAAP adjusted earnings; the filing does not attribute these costs explicitly to
  the 2018 merger integration, and no further detail on which executives or roles were
  involved was located in the reviewed sections. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
- Net income and adjusted earnings both rose substantially from 2020 to 2021, though the
  filing attributes most of the increase to a February 2021 winter weather event and
  operating factors rather than to merger-specific integration effects. [SRC: SEC accession 0001711269-22-000008, Form 10-K]
