## Public-reporting status within the outcome window
- The registrant's final standalone annual report was the Form 10-K for fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2013 (accession 0001490626-14-000006, filed 2014-03-31); no Form 10-K was
  filed for any fiscal year after 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001490626-14-000006, Form 10-K] [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001490626&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40]

  closing-era 8-K, its attached press release, and its Form 15 deregistration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-005758, Form 15-12G]

## Realized liquidity event (merger into Select Income REIT)
- The registrant entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated August 30, 2014 with
  Select Income REIT ("SIR") and SC Merger Sub, LLC ("SIR Merger Sub"), a wholly owned
  subsidiary of SIR, providing for the registrant to merge with and into SIR Merger Sub,
  with SIR Merger Sub surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of SIR. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-021749, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- At a special meeting held January 27, 2015 (record date December 12, 2014, with
  197,605,729 shares outstanding), stockholders approved the merger by 120,005,549.10 for,
  5,659,664.95 against, and 3,635,589.03 abstaining; the press release stated approximately
  92.8% of shares voted were in favor, representing approximately 60.7% of all eligible
  shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-021749, Form 8-K Item 5.07 + EX-99.1]
- Merger consideration: each registrant share entitled the holder to elect either $10.50 in
  cash or 0.36 SIR common shares, subject to a limitation that neither the cash nor the
  share consideration exceed 60.0% of the total; registrant stockholders were expected to
  own approximately 32% of SIR's outstanding shares based on a 60% cash election. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-021749, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- By this date the registrant was described as "a public, non-listed REIT managed by Cole
  Capital," Cole Capital being "the private capital management business of American Realty
  Capital Properties, Inc. (NASDAQ: ARCP)" — i.e. the external manager's ultimate parent was
  then ARCP; the registrant had completed a $2.0 billion capital raise in September 2013.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-021749, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Consummation and deregistration (end of standalone public existence)
- On January 30, 2015 a Form 15 (Rule 12g-4(a)(1) / 12h-3(b)(1)(i)) was filed by "SC Merger
  Sub LLC (as successor to Cole Corporate Income Trust, Inc.)," c/o Select Income REIT, Two
  Newton Place, Newton, Massachusetts, certifying termination of registration of the common
  stock under Section 12(g); the approximate number of holders of record as of the notice
  date was 1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-005758, Form 15-12G]
- The successor designation and single holder of record establish that the registrant was
  absorbed into SIR and ceased to file periodic reports thereafter; no subsequent periodic
  or annual report appears on its EDGAR record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-15-005758, Form 15-12G] [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001490626&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40]

## Disclosed integration risk factors (forward-looking, not realized outcomes)
- The January 27, 2015 communication cautioned that "costs or difficulties related to the
  integration of SIR's and CCIT's operations will be greater than expected," that "operating
  costs and disruption of management's attention from ongoing business operations may be
  greater than expected," and noted risks to relationships with customers, tenants, and
  lenders; these are stated risk factors, not documented realized results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-021749, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
