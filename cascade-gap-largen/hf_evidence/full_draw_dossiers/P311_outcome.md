## Documented post-closing operational changes (press-sourced)
- In a statement reported in early March 2019, the acquirer announced it would close a
  distribution center in Sidney, Nebraska (the former headquarters community) and a
  merchandise return center in Oshkosh, Nebraska, affecting a combined 162 positions
  (121 at the Sidney facility, 41 at Oshkosh), as part of "an extensive review of all
  Bass Pro Shops and Cabela's operations." [SRC: https://sgbonline.com/bass-pro-shuttering-facilities-in-sidney-oshkosh-in-nebraska/]
- That same report stated the Sidney distribution facility, originally built during
  World War II, had been in operation 77 years and was found to handle the least volume
  of the combined company's distribution centers, and that the company said it "remains
  committed to maintaining a presence in Sidney." [SRC: https://sgbonline.com/bass-pro-shuttering-facilities-in-sidney-oshkosh-in-nebraska/]
- On January 13, 2020, the company announced the closure of the Cabela's retail
  locations in Fort Oglethorpe, Georgia and Bristol, Virginia, consolidating each into a
  nearby Bass Pro Shops location, stating "we can better serve our customers with
  greater product assortment, value and local expertise in a single location." [SRC: https://sgbonline.com/cabelas-closing-north-georgia-store/]
- A local news report on the same Fort Oglethorpe/East Ridge consolidation quoted the
  company's statement that "Two large format stores located just 2.4 miles apart are not
  necessary to serve our customers in the Greater Chattanooga area," and reported that
  "virtually all Cabela's team members are being offered the opportunity to transfer to
  the new location." [SRC: https://wdef.com/2020/01/13/catoosa-cabelas-closing-merge-bass-pro-shops-location]
- A Site Selection Magazine feature (undated in the retrieved copy, but referencing
  events in the months following the September 2017 closing) reported that the
  acquirer intended to keep "up to 125 IT jobs and some accounting personnel" at the
  former Cabela's Sidney campus, that roughly 800 employees remained at that campus at
  the time of reporting (down from an employment peak of about 2,000), and that "most of
  the remaining positions would either transfer to Missouri or be phased out during the
  consolidation." [SRC: https://siteselection.com/sidneys-next-saga-finding-jobs-to-replace-those-lost-to-cabelas-bass-pro-merger/]
- The same feature reported that the acquirer's CEO pledged to donate up to $10 million
  of his own money to a severance fund for laid-off Sidney employees, and asked former
  Cabela's executives who received "golden parachutes" in the sale to contribute to that
  fund. [SRC: https://siteselection.com/sidneys-next-saga-finding-jobs-to-replace-those-lost-to-cabelas-bass-pro-merger/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Cabela's Incorporated deregistered its securities on October 10, 2017 and its
  acquirer is privately held with no identified SEC periodic-report filing, there is no
  public 3-5-year audited outcome record for this business; the outcome is coded
  **uncertain** on that basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-306982, Form 15-12B]
- The traceable press record for the roughly two-to-five-year period following the
  September 2017 closing documents facility and headcount contraction at the former
  target's home base (a 77-year-old Sidney, NE distribution center and the Oshkosh, NE
  return center closed in 2019, affecting 162 jobs; corporate-campus headcount reported
  down from about 2,000 at peak to about 800, with most remaining roles slated to
  transfer to the acquirer's Missouri headquarters or be phased out) and store-network
  consolidation (Fort Oglethorpe, GA and Bristol, VA Cabela's locations closed and
  merged into nearby Bass Pro Shops locations in January 2020), alongside stated
  intentions by the acquirer to retain a reduced footprint in Sidney and to fund a
  severance program for displaced employees.
