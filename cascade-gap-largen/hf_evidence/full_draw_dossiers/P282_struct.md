## Transaction and parties at closing
- The boards of Prologis, Inc. (a Maryland corporation) and DCT Industrial Trust Inc. (a
  Maryland corporation) unanimously approved an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April
  29, 2018, under which DCT merged with and into Prologis (Prologis surviving, "the
  company merger") and DCT Industrial Operating Partnership LP merged with and into
  Prologis, L.P. (Prologis, L.P. surviving, "the partnership merger"); the combined entity
  retained the name "Prologis, Inc." Each DCT common share converted into 1.02 Prologis
  common shares (fractional shares cash-settled); each DCT OP unit converted into 1.02
  Prologis OP units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
- The mergers were consummated on August 22, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-254519, Form 8-K filed 2018-08-22]
- As of March 31, 2018, DCT was described as a leading industrial real estate company
  specializing in ownership, acquisition, development, leasing and management of bulk
  distribution and light industrial properties in high-demand U.S. distribution markets,
  owning interests in 425 properties (~73.7 million square feet leased to ~840 customers).
  Prologis was described as the global leader in logistics real estate, owning, managing
  and developing high-quality logistics facilities in high-barrier, high-growth markets.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary, and what did not, by tier
- Legal vehicle: DCT ceased to exist as a separate corporate entity, absorbed into
  Prologis's surviving corporate and partnership entities. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product/asset: DCT's bulk-distribution/light-industrial property
  portfolio was combined into the Combined Company's logistics real estate portfolio,
  expected to have a pro forma enterprise value of ~$52.6 billion and to remain the
  largest industrial REIT by enterprise value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973,
  DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent/imprint: the Combined Company retained the Prologis name, Prologis's
  existing San Francisco principal executive offices, and the NYSE ticker "PLD." [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
- People/organization: Prologis's incumbent executive officers continued as the Combined
  Company's executive officers (Hamid R. Moghadam continuing as CEO); the board grew to 12
  members — the 11 incumbent Prologis directors plus a single addition, Philip L. Hawkins
  (DCT's President/CEO and a DCT director), who joined only as a director. No DCT executive
  officer other than Mr. Hawkins was identified as continuing in an executive-officer role
  at the Combined Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
- DCT directors/executive officers held interests in the mergers "different from, or in
  addition to," other DCT stockholders, including "continued service as a director or
  officer of the Combined Company or, in the alternative, a severance payment if
  terminated upon, or following, consummation of the mergers" — via a preexisting
  employment agreement with DCT's CEO and DCT's Executive Change in Control and Severance
  Plan covering its other officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
- Listing: the proxy disclosed that after completion, DCT common stock would cease to be
  listed on the NYSE and be removed from listing and deregistered under the Exchange Act;
  on August 22, 2018 DCT notified the NYSE accordingly. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A; SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-254519, Form 8-K filed
  2018-08-22]

## Transitional arrangements and anticipated integration
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or comparable transitional
  operating arrangement bridging DCT's operations into the Combined Company was identified
  in the DEFM14A; the transaction was a direct statutory merger in which DCT ceased to
  exist as a separate registrant, rather than a carve-out/spin-off requiring an interim
  services bridge. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A] [UNVERIFIED — this
  absence is based only on the drawn filing, not independently confirmed elsewhere.]
- The proxy's risk factors disclosed that the Combined Company expected substantial
  expenses integrating DCT's and Prologis's operations and systems, and identified as a
  risk the possible "inability to successfully combine the operations of Prologis and DCT"
  in a manner realizing anticipated cost savings and synergies within the anticipated
  timeframe. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two independent, similarly structured logistics/industrial
  REITs through a direct statutory merger in which DCT's corporate/partnership entities
  were absorbed into Prologis's surviving entities, DCT's property portfolio was combined
  into the Combined Company under the Prologis name, Prologis's incumbent executive team
  and the great majority of its board continued unchanged (one DCT board addition), and
  DCT's own executive officers held contractual severance rights tied to the change in
  control rather than a documented guarantee of continued executive roles. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-18-004973, DEFM14A]
