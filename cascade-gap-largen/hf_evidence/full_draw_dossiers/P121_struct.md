## Transaction and parties at closing
- The registrant (NorthStar Real Estate Income II, Inc., a Maryland corporation, Commission File No. 000-55189) reported completion on January 31, 2018 of the transactions contemplated by a Master Combination Agreement dated August 25, 2017 and amended and restated November 20, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Explanatory Note]
- The agreement had eight parties: Colony Capital Operating Company, LLC ("CLNS OP," the operating company of Colony NorthStar, Inc. ("CLNS")); NRF RED REIT Corp. ("RED REIT," an indirect subsidiary of CLNS OP); Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc.; Credit RE Operating Company, LLC ("Credit OP," a wholly owned subsidiary of Colony NorthStar Credit); NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc. and its operating partnership; and the registrant and its operating partnership (NorthStar Real Estate Income Operating Partnership II, LP). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Explanatory Note]
- The structure combined multiple entities into one surviving corporation: two registrant REITs each merged with and into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. (which survived each merger), while CLNS OP and RED REIT separately contributed selected portfolios of assets and liabilities into the combined structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Explanatory Note / Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset: the registrant's real estate portfolio was absorbed into the surviving corporation by statutory merger; at the merger effective time the registrant merged with and into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc., with that entity surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01 / Item 5.01]
- Legal vehicle: the registrant's separate corporate existence did not survive; each share of the registrant's common stock ($0.01 par value) issued and outstanding was cancelled and converted into the right to receive 0.3511 shares of the surviving entity's Class A common stock, plus cash in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01 / Item 3.03]
- The parallel registrant REIT (NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc.) merged on the same terms of mechanism, its shares converting into 0.3532 shares of the surviving entity's Class A common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Controlling ownership: as of consummation, CLNS and its affiliates owned approximately 37%, former stockholders of the registrant approximately 31%, and former NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust stockholders approximately 32% of the combined entity, on a fully diluted basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- The surviving combined entity issued approximately 40.4 million shares of Class A common stock to former stockholders of the registrant, approximately 42.1 million shares to former NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust stockholders, and approximately 44.4 million shares of Class B-3 common stock to CLNS OP; Credit OP issued approximately 3.1 million units to RED REIT and approximately 126.9 million units to the surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## People / management-tier facts at closing
- The registrant's operating partnership had been held in part by an external advisory affiliate: at closing, all 100 outstanding partnership units of the registrant's operating partnership held by NS Real Estate Income Advisor II, LLC (the "Limited Partner") were redeemed for an aggregate payment of $904, and all 100 special limited partnership units held by NorthStar OP Holdings II, LLC (an affiliate of CLNS) were cancelled and terminated without payment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Restricted stock outstanding under the registrant's Long Term Incentive Plan automatically became fully vested and entitled to the merger consideration at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The drawn filing does not describe the management or servicing arrangement of the surviving combined entity, nor whether the personnel or advisory processes running the registrant's portfolio continued under a new arrangement after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K]

## Registration mechanism and listing
- The Class A common stock issued in the combination was registered under the surviving entity's Form S-4 (File No. 333-221685), filed November 21, 2017 and declared effective December 6, 2017; the Class B-3 common stock and Credit OP units were issued in reliance on the Section 4(a)(2) exemption. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The surviving entity's Class A common stock was approved for listing on the New York Stock Exchange and began trading under the symbol "CLNC" effective as of the opening of trading on February 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Bridging arrangements and controlling-principal fusion
- The drawn filing describes a full statutory merger of the registrant into a single surviving corporation; it does not describe any transitional-services, operating, or shared-services agreement bridging a retained shared substrate between separated tiers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K]
- No fusion of a controlling natural person with any organizational tier is described; the filing was executed by the surviving entity's General Counsel and Secretary as successor by merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Signatures]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Multiple separate real estate entities and contributed asset/liability portfolios were merged and contributed into a single surviving, newly exchange-listed corporation; the registrant's shares and portfolio crossed by cancellation-and-conversion into the surviving entity's stock while the registrant's own corporate vehicle and its external-advisory operating-partnership interests were unwound at closing, with no transitional-services agreement described. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K Item 2.01 / Item 5.01]
