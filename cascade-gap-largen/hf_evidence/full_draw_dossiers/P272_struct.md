# Structural sub-dossier — Hines Global REIT, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction as structured
- Hines Global REIT, Inc. ("the Company"), a Maryland corporation structured as an
  umbrella-partnership REIT conducting substantially all of its business through Hines
  Global REIT Properties LP (the "Operating Partnership"), filed a definitive proxy
  statement calling an annual meeting for July 17, 2018 at which stockholders would be
  asked to approve a Plan of Liquidation and Dissolution adopted by the board on April
  23, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- Under the Plan, the Company would undertake an orderly sale of all or substantially
  all of its properties and other assets, pay or provide for its liabilities, make
  liquidating distributions to stockholders, and then wind up and dissolve by filing
  Articles of Dissolution with the Maryland State Department of Assessments and
  Taxation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- The board explicitly considered "a sale or merger of the Company or similar business
  combination transaction" as an alternative and determined that such a transaction was
  not reasonably likely to enhance stockholder value to a greater extent than the
  planned liquidation; no single acquirer of the whole company was identified or
  proposed in this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- As of April 23, 2018, the Company owned interests in 33 real estate investments
  totaling approximately 13.9 million square feet of leasable space, diversified by
  asset type (office, industrial, retail, multi-family) and geography (United States,
  Europe, Australia, Russia); the Company had raised approximately $3.1 billion through
  public offerings since August 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- As of March 31, 2018, the Company had outstanding liabilities of approximately $2.1
  billion, of which approximately $1.9 billion was loans secured by properties owned;
  these loan obligations were expected to be paid off in connection with the property
  sales. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- The board estimated aggregate distributions to stockholders of approximately $10.00
  to $11.00 per share, comprised of a previously paid Special Distribution, Return of
  Invested Capital Distributions, and an estimated $8.83-$9.83 per share of liquidating
  distributions, as of April 23, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]

## Asset-level disposition already underway (independent of the vote)
- Separately from the Plan and not conditioned on stockholder approval, the board had
  on February 26, 2018 determined to sell the "Logistics Portfolio" — five industrial
  parks in Poland (Warsaw, Wroclaw, Upper Silesia), five logistics buildings in Germany
  (Erfurt, Forchheim, Nuremberg, Karlsdorf, Duisburg), and a single property in Moscow,
  Russia (FM Logistic) — and intended to complete that sale regardless of the vote
  outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement does not identify a specific named buyer or purchase-and-sale
  agreement for the Logistics Portfolio or for any other individual property; it
  describes the disposition process as marketing properties to find buyers on the most
  favorable terms reasonably available, with prices and timing subject to market
  conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent, management, and people
- The Company has no employees; its day-to-day operations, including property
  acquisitions and dispositions, are managed by Hines Global REIT Advisors LP (the
  "Advisor"), an affiliate of the Company's sponsor, Hines Interests Limited
  Partnership ("Hines"), under an Advisory Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- Pursuant to the Advisory Agreement, the Advisor or its affiliates would be paid a
  disposition fee of 1.0% of the sales price of each real estate investment sold (or
  1.0% of the Company's pro rata share for indirect investments); the Company estimated
  aggregate disposition fees payable to the Advisor of between $47.0 million and $49.5
  million in connection with the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- Named officers and directors who could receive compensation tied to disposition fees
  at the Advisor's discretion included Jeffrey C. Hines, Colin P. Shepard, Charles M.
  Baughn, Sherri W. Schugart (President and CEO), Ryan T. Sims (CFO and Secretary),
  Kevin L. McMeans, J. Shea Morgenroth, and David L. Steinbach (Chief Investment
  Officer). [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement anticipated that current directors and officers would continue to
  serve in their capacities after approval of the Plan, subject to the separate
  Election of Directors proposal at the same meeting; no change in the identity of the
  managing organization (the Advisor/Hines) was contemplated as part of the Plan.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services agreement bridging to an outside operator was structured,
  because operations, leasing, and disposition of the properties were to continue being
  performed by the same Advisor/Hines organization throughout the liquidation process,
  independent of which buyers ultimately acquired individual properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured at the time of this filing, the transaction was a stockholder-approved,
  asset-by-asset sale of a diversified real estate portfolio (not a single-buyer
  whole-company merger), to be followed by dissolution of the legal entity; the
  customer-facing assets (the properties) were to be sold individually to unnamed
  buyers determined through an ongoing marketing process, while the operating and
  disposition processes and the personnel/management (the Advisor, an affiliate of
  Hines) remained constant and un-transferred throughout. [SRC: SEC accession 0001453818-18-000020, DEFM14A]
