## Public-reporting status after the transaction

  filed 2017-03-14, covering fiscal year 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-001763, Form 10-K]
- State National filed a Form 15-12B (Certification and Notice of Termination of
  Registration) on 2017-12-01, terminating its duty to file reports under the Securities

  of the certification date was 1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001610793-17-000007, Form 15-12B]
- No annual report (Form 10-K) was filed by State National as an independent registrant

## Acquirer's separate disclosure of the acquired business (qualitative)
- In its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-02-19), Markel Corporation describes
  a "State National division" within its Insurance segment, stating that the Insurance
  segment "is written through our Markel Specialty, Markel International and State
  National divisions, with the exception of our State National division's program
  services business, which is not included in a reportable segment." [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-21-000032, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The same filing states that "the State National division writes collateral protection
  insurance, which insures personal automobiles and other vehicles held as collateral
  for loans made by credit unions, banks and specialty finance companies through its
  lender services product line," and that its program services business "generates fee
  income, in the form of ceding (program service) fees, by offering issuing carrier
  capacity" to third parties, and is "separately managed from our underwriting
  divisions...in order to protect our program services customers and eliminate internal
  competition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-21-000032, Form 10-K FY2020]
- In its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed 2023-02-17), Markel again describes "the
  State National division writes collateral protection insurance in the U.S." and
  states "the results of our program services operations are not included in a
  reportable segment," using materially the same description as the FY2020 filing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-23-000033, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Both the FY2020 and FY2022 Markel Form 10-K filings list the same set of underwriting
  subsidiaries (City National Insurance Company, Independent Specialty Insurance
  Company, National Specialty Insurance Company, State National Insurance Company, Inc.,
  and United Specialty Insurance Company) as the entities through which the program
  services business continues to be written. [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-21-000032, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-23-000033, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- State National Companies, Inc. operated as an independent SEC registrant through
  fiscal year 2016, was acquired by Markel Corporation, and deregistered its common
  stock via a Form 15-12B filed 2017-12-01 reporting a single holder of record;
  consequently there is no separate audited public 3-5-year financial record for State
  National as a standalone entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-17-001763, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001610793-17-000007, Form 15-12B]
- Markel's own Form 10-K filings for fiscal years 2020 and 2022 separately describe a
  continuing "State National division" performing the same collateral-protection and
  program-services (fronting) business lines through the same named underwriting
  subsidiaries, but disclose no segment- or entity-level quantified financial trajectory
  for that division specifically; the outcome over the fixed window is therefore coded
  uncertain on any quantified scalar, documented only by this qualitative operational
  continuity rather than fabricated or inferred from Markel's consolidated results.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-21-000032, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001096343-23-000033, Form 10-K FY2022]
