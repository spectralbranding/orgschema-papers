# Structural sub-dossier — Parker Aerospace / GE Aviation joint venture (Advanced Atomization Technologies, LLC) (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what each parent contributed to the joint
> venture and what did not cross into it. It contains no post-transaction outcome
> information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Parker-Hannifin Corporation (NYSE: PH), through its Parker Aerospace operating
  segment, and GE Aviation formed a 50-50 joint venture named Advanced Atomization
  Technologies, LLC. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]
- The transaction was structured as a joint venture in which Parker sold a 50 percent
  equity interest in one of its wholly-owned subsidiaries to GE Aviation, described as "a
  non-related party." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000114, Form 10-K Note 2 (Deconsolidation of Subsidiary)]
- The joint venture was finalized during the second quarter of fiscal 2014 (the quarter
  ended December 31, 2013); the drawn Form 8-K reporting that quarter's results describes
  it as the "previously announced joint venture agreement between Parker Aerospace and GE
  Aviation." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000005, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99.1 press release]

## What each parent contributed to the joint venture at closing (tier level)
- The customer-facing product placed into the joint venture is fuel nozzles: the venture
  was created "to produce fuel nozzles for current and future GE Aviation commercial
  engine platforms (including aerospace and aero-derivative engines)." [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]
- The producing operation was contributed together with its product: the joint venture is
  "located in the Parker Gas Turbine Fuel Systems Division facility in Clyde, New York,"
  the existing Parker facility that houses the operation. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]
- The people who run the operation were contributed alongside the facility and product:
  "The joint venture will employ approximately 300 skilled assemblers, technicians,
  engineers, and other professionals," described as "Continuing its long history in the
  Clyde community." [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]
- Because Parker contributed a whole wholly-owned subsidiary rather than a product line
  alone, the deconsolidated subsidiary's sales are, after closing, reported by the joint
  venture rather than by Parker: Parker's fiscal 2014 second-quarter Aerospace Systems
  segment sales "decreased 4.7 percent to $503.8 million, reflecting the impact of the
  previously announced joint venture between Parker Aerospace and GE Aviation." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000005, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99.1 press release]

## Ownership, control, and vehicle allocated at closing
- Ownership was split 50-50 between the two parents. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]
- The sale of the 50 percent equity interest "resulted in a loss of control of the
  subsidiary, and therefore it was deconsolidated from the Company's financial statements
  during the second quarter of fiscal 2014." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000114, Form 10-K Note 2]
- Parker's retained 50 percent interest in the joint venture is held as an equity-method
  investment, carried at $263,246 (thousand) at June 30, 2014; "A significant portion of
  the underlying net assets of the joint venture are related to goodwill." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000114, Form 10-K Note 2]
- The legal vehicle is a limited liability company (Advanced Atomization Technologies,
  LLC). [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html]

## Financial consideration recognized at closing
- Parker recognized a pre-tax gain of $413 million on the deconsolidation, "measured as
  the fair value of the consideration received for the 50 percent equity interest in the
  former subsidiary and the fair value of the retained investment less the carrying amount
  of the former subsidiary's net assets"; approximately $186 million of the pre-tax gain
  "is attributable to the remeasurement of the retained investment in the former
  subsidiary to its current fair value." [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000114, Form 10-K Note 2]
- The after-tax gain related to the joint venture agreement was $255,652 (thousand), which
  increased second-quarter fiscal 2014 earnings by $1.68 per diluted share. [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000005, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99.1 press release]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction contributed an entire wholly-owned Parker operating subsidiary — a
  fuel-nozzle product line, its Clyde, New York producing facility, and its approximately
  300-person workforce — into a newly formed 50-50 limited-liability joint venture with GE
  Aviation (the product's customer), with Parker selling half the equity, losing control,
  deconsolidating the subsidiary, and retaining a 50 percent equity-method interest. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/parker-aerospace-and-ge-aviation-launch-joint-venture-179292031.html] [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000114, Form 10-K Note 2] [SRC: SEC accession 0000076334-14-000005, Form 8-K Item 2.02 EX-99.1 press release]
