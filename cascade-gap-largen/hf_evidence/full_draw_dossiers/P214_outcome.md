## Segment / business-line disclosure in the outcome window
- For fiscal year 2021 the Company reported "three reportable segments": Information &
  Transaction Processing Solutions ("ITPS"), Healthcare Solutions ("HS"), and Legal &
  Loss Prevention Services ("LLPS") — the same three segment names used for SourceHOV's
  pre-combination business lines. ITPS was "our largest segment, with $874.1 million of
  revenues for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2021, representing 74.9% of our
  revenues." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The FY2021 10-K uses "we," "us," or "our" to refer to "Exela Technologies, Inc. and
  its consolidated subsidiaries, and where applicable, our predecessors SourceHOV and
  Novitex prior to the closing of the Novitex Business Combination," but does not
  present Novitex's pre-combination Digital Services / Document Logistics lines as a
  separate reportable segment or subsidiary note distinct from the three
  SourceHOV-derived segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K
  FY2021]
- No discontinued-operations disclosure or divestiture of the SourceHOV or Novitex
  predecessor businesses was found in this filing; the only reference to divestiture is
  a generic debt-covenant risk-factor statement that indebtedness "may... require
  non-strategic divestitures," not a description of an occurred event. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Realized financial trajectory within the window
- Consolidated revenue for the year ended December 31, 2021 was $1,166.6 million,
  described as a decrease of "$126.0 million, or 9.7%," from $1,292.6 million for the
  year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K
  FY2021]
- Net loss was $142.4 million for fiscal year 2021, compared to a net loss of $178.5
  million for fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K
  FY2021]
- Goodwill of $358.3 million represented 34.6% of total consolidated assets as of
  December 31, 2021; the Company's annual goodwill-impairment tests as of October 1,
  2021 and October 1, 2020 both concluded "there was no impairment." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The filing discloses that "substantial doubt regarding the Company's ability to meet
  its obligation as they become due within one year... which was raised by management
  prior to the second quarter of 2021, is not applicable" as of the filing date, after
  the Company "raised $406.8 million in gross proceeds from equity financings" and
  "reduced indebtedness by $338.5 million" during 2021. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Corporate/capital-structure events within the window
- Stockholders authorized a reverse stock split at the Company's 2020 Annual Meeting;
  the Board of Directors set the ratio at one-for-three effective January 25, 2021, and
  Common Stock began trading on Nasdaq on a split-adjusted basis January 26, 2021, with
  no change to the ticker symbol. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K
  FY2021]
- The FY2021 10-K's risk factors state that "during 2022, we failed to meet the
  requirement that our minimum bid price exceed $1.00 for at least 30 consecutive
  business days" under Nasdaq Listing Rule 5550(a)(2) and "received a letter from
  Nasdaq regarding the infraction," with no assurance the Company would remedy the
  deficiency and remain listed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K
  FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The renamed successor entity (Exela Technologies, Inc., same CIK) remained a public
  reporting company through the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window, disclosing consolidated
  segment and financial results but no filing-level separation of the two 2017 predecessor
  businesses' individual post-close trajectories. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The best-documented realized scalar is a continued consolidated net loss narrowing from
  $178.5 million (FY2020) to $142.4 million (FY2021) on declining revenue ($1,292.6
  million to $1,166.6 million), alongside a temporarily disclosed going-concern doubt
  (raised before Q2 2021, stated resolved after a $406.8 million equity raise and $338.5

  notice. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003779, Form 10-K FY2021]
