## Corporate identity change within the outcome window
- SEC's EDGAR company database records CIK 0001299939's registrant name as
  "BancorpSouth Bank" from 2004-08-11 until 2021-11-10, and as "Cadence Bank"
  beginning 2021-11-10. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001299939.json]
- On October 29, 2021 (within the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window), Cadence
  Bancorporation — a separate, NYSE-listed bank holding company headquartered in
  Houston, Texas, unrelated to the 2017 reorganization — completed a merger under
  which Cadence Bancorporation merged with and into BancorpSouth, with BancorpSouth
  surviving; in connection with the closing, BancorpSouth Bank changed its name to
  "Cadence Bank" and changed its NYSE ticker symbol from "BXS" to "CADE." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-21-035867, Form 8-K, filed by Cadence Bancorporation (CIK 0001614184)]
- Cadence Bancorporation's then Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, Paul B. Murphy,
  Jr., ceased serving as an executive officer of that company and was appointed
  Executive Vice Chairman of the surviving entity; BancorpSouth's then Chairman and
  Chief Executive Officer, James D. Rollins III, was named Chairman and Chief
  Executive Officer of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-21-035867, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001140361-21-023608, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement (as described in the Definitive Merger Proxy Statement
  Cadence Bancorporation filed July 7, 2021), for the 36 months following the
  merger's effective time the combined company's main office and bank headquarters
  would remain located in Tupelo, Mississippi, while its corporate headquarters would
  be located in Houston, Texas — the surviving entity's main/bank office and its
  corporate headquarters were split between the two pre-merger companies' home
  cities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-21-023608, Form DEFM14A]
- The combined company's board was set at 20 members for the period until its first
  annual shareholder meeting — 11 who had been BancorpSouth directors and 9 who had
  been Cadence Bancorporation directors — after which it was to be reduced to 18. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-21-023608, Form DEFM14A]
- Cadence Bancorporation separately filed a Form 15-12B in November 2021 to terminate
  its own SEC registration in connection with the merger's completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-21-135505, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the transaction's own terms moved the surviving entity's periodic reporting
  from the SEC to the FDIC, no SEC-filed 10-K exists to document the surviving bank's
  financial trajectory over the ~2020-2022 window, and no such record was located
  elsewhere within the scope of this dossier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]
- The single best-documented event within the fixed outcome window is a further
  business combination: on October 29, 2021, the entity that had survived the 2017
  reorganization (by then still doing business as BancorpSouth Bank) absorbed Cadence
  Bancorporation in a merger, retained its status as the surviving legal entity, but
  adopted its merger partner's name ("Cadence Bank") and ticker symbol ("CADE"), split
  its main/bank office (Tupelo) from its corporate headquarters (Houston), and
  installed BancorpSouth's own then-CEO as the combined company's Chairman and CEO
  while the merger partner's CEO moved to a vice-chairman role. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-21-035867, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001140361-21-023608, Form DEFM14A]
