# Outcome sub-dossier — Entercom Communications Corp. / Audacy, Inc. realized record, ~2020-2022 window

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at fiscal years
> 2020-2021 (~3-5 years after the November 2017 closing), traced through the
> registrant's own subsequent Forms 10-K. This sub-dossier contains no information about
> how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Registrant continuity and identity change
- The combining entity continued as the same SEC registrant (CIK 0001067837) after
  closing; no deregistration occurred. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003544, Form
  10-K FY2020 cover]
- In 2021 the registrant changed its corporate name from Entercom Communications Corp.
  to Audacy, Inc.; its common stock, previously traded under symbol "ETM" from January
  28, 1999, began trading under symbol "AUD" on the New York Stock Exchange effective
  April 9, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 5]

## Realized financial trajectory
- For fiscal year 2020, the Company (then Entercom) reported net revenues of $1,060,898
  thousand, down from $1,489,929 thousand for fiscal year 2019, and a net loss of
  $242,224 thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003544, Form 10-K FY2020,
  Selected Financial Data / Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- For fiscal year 2021, the Company (renamed Audacy, Inc.) reported net revenues of
  $1,219,404 thousand and a net loss of $3,572 thousand, an improvement management
  attributed to "economic recovery and improvements across all segments of our business
  from the depressed levels of the prior year." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551,
  Form 10-K FY2021, Selected Financial Data / MD&A]
- During the fourth quarter of 2020 the Company recorded a $246.0 million impairment
  charge ($180.4 million, net of tax) on its broadcasting licenses. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-21-003544, Form 10-K FY2020, Notes to Financial Statements]
- A five-year cumulative total shareholder return table in the FY2021 Form 10-K shows a
  $100 investment made December 31, 2016 in the Company's stock was worth $19.37 by
  December 31, 2021, versus $233.41 for the S&P 500 index and $90.51 for the disclosed
  peer group over the same period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551, Form 10-K
  FY2021, Item 5 performance graph]

## Documented merger-related and pandemic-related disruption over time
- The Company disclosed that, "in connection with the Merger," it incurred integration
  costs (transition services, consulting services, and professional fees) of $0.5 million
  for the year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551, Form
  10-K FY2021, MD&A]
- The Company disclosed that, "in connection with the COVID-19 pandemic and the Merger,"
  it incurred restructuring charges including workforce reductions and other
  restructuring costs of $12.0 million for the year ended December 31, 2020 and $5.7
  million for the year ended December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551,
  Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A]
- During fiscal 2020 the Company disclosed pandemic-response measures including temporary
  salary reductions across senior management and the broader organization, freezing of
  contractual salary increases, furlough and termination of select employees, suspension
  of new-employee hiring, and suspension of its quarterly dividend program. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001628280-21-003544, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A]
- Corporate general and administrative expenses in fiscal 2021 increased in part due to
  "an increase in corporate rebranding costs in connection with our corporate name
  change." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-22-004551, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Under the same continuing public registrant (renamed Audacy, Inc. in 2021), the
  combined business's net revenues fell from $1,489,929 thousand (FY2019) to $1,060,898
  thousand (FY2020), accompanied by a $242,224 thousand net loss and a $246.0 million
  broadcasting-license impairment charge in FY2020, before revenues recovered to
  $1,219,404 thousand and the net loss narrowed to $3,572 thousand in FY2021; across the
  window the Company separately itemized merger-related integration and workforce-
  reduction costs alongside COVID-19-pandemic cost measures, and its cumulative
  shareholder return over the five years to year-end 2021 (an index value of $19.37 per
  $100 invested at year-end 2016) trailed both the S&P 500 and its disclosed peer group.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003544, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession
  0001628280-22-004551, Form 10-K FY2021]
