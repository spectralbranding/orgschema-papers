## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On December 10, 2018, a Form 15 was filed on behalf of "LaSalle Hotel Properties
  (Pebblebrook Hotel Trust as assignee of Ping Merger Sub, LLC, successor by merger to
  LaSalle Hotel Properties)" certifying termination/suspension of the duty to file
  reports under Sections 12(g), 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act for LaSalle's common
  shares, 6.375% Series I preferred shares, and 6.3% Series J preferred shares, each
  showing zero holders of record as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001053532-18-000104, Form 15-12B]
- LaSalle Hotel Properties' EDGAR filing history under CIK 1053532 shows no Form 10-K
  filed after the FY2017 10-K (filed 2018-02-20, accession 0001053532-18-000006); no
  annual report covering any part of the ~2021-2023 outcome window was filed under this
  CIK. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 0001053532]
- The surviving public registrant, Pebblebrook Hotel Trust (CIK 1474098), continued
  filing annual reports on Form 10-K after the merger; its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021
  (accession 0001474098-22-000035) and its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (accession
  0001474098-23-000038) contain no narrative discussion identifying "LaSalle" or the
  acquired business as a distinct, separately reported segment or operating unit — the
  hotel portfolio is reported on a combined basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001474098-22-000035, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001474098-23-000038, Form 10-K FY2022]
