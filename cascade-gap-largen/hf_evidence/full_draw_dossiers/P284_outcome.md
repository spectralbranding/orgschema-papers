## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The merger closed on July 18, 2017; at the effective time the Company became a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Parent and an indirect subsidiary of JAB, all pre-closing
  directors resigned (replaced by two JAB-affiliated nominees), trading in the common
  stock on NASDAQ was suspended that morning, and the Company requested that NASDAQ file
  a Form 25 to remove the stock from listing and registration; NASDAQ filed that Form
  25-NSE the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-229854, 8-K Items 2.01/3.01/5.01/5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-17-000134, Form 25-NSE]
- On July 31, 2017 the Company filed a Form 15-12G certifying termination of
  registration under Section 12(g) and suspension of its reporting duty, stating "one"
  approximate holder of record as of that date (the Company had become a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Rye Parent Corp. on July 18, 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-241443, Form 15-12G]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed under CIK 0000724606 since the
  fiscal-year-2016 Form 10-K (filed 2017-02-22, before the closing); consequently there
  is no audited public financial-statement record for Panera Bread Company itself for
  the ~2020-2022 window, and the entity-level realized record below relies on a related
  public company's SEC filings and reputable press rather than the entity's own primary
  filings. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 0000724606 (https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000724606&type=10-K)]

## Organizational developments in the outcome window (secondary/related-entity sourced)
- Trade press reports that in 2021 the Panera Bread, Einstein Bros. Bagels, and Caribou
  Coffee brands (the latter two previously acquired separately by JAB) were brought
  together under one ownership umbrella, a move described as "seen at the time as
  preparation for a spinoff," with the three brands together totaling about 3,800 units. [SRC: https://www.restaurantbusinessonline.com/operations/panera-breads-turbulent-transformation]
- On November 8, 2021, a related public filer, USHG Acquisition Corp. (NYSE: HUGS, a
  special purpose acquisition company, unaffiliated CIK 0001836894), entered into an
  Investment Agreement and Plan of Merger with "Panera Brands, Inc." and a merger
  subsidiary, under which a subsidiary of Panera Brands, Inc. would merge into USHG
  Acquisition Corp. and Panera Brands, Inc. would become the resulting public company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-323804, 8-K Item 1.01 (CIK 1836894)]
- Beyond the ~3-5-year window, on November 23, 2022, USHG Acquisition Corp. disclosed
  that it "believes that it is unlikely to consummate a suitable... Business
  Combination" and moved toward redemption of its public shares and winding up (the New
  York Stock Exchange separately moved to remove its warrants from listing); in December
  2022 it held a special meeting to accelerate its termination date and redeem its
  public shares, and subsequently filed to deregister — no completed combination between
  USHG Acquisition Corp. and Panera Brands, Inc. is reflected in USHG Acquisition Corp.'s
  SEC filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-292011, 8-K Item 3.01 (CIK 1836894)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-302821, 8-K (CIK 1836894)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-23-004740, Form 15-12G (CIK 1836894)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Panera Bread Company (CIK 0000724606) was acquired whole and deregistered
  within two weeks of closing (Form 15-12G, July 31, 2017), there is no public audited
  outcome record for the entity itself for the ~2020-2022 window. The only
  entity-adjacent public record traced within or just beyond that window is a related
  SPAC's (USHG Acquisition Corp.) own SEC filings describing an announced November 2021
  agreement to bring a combined "Panera Brands, Inc." to public markets through that
  SPAC, followed by the SPAC's November-December 2022 disclosures that it was unlikely
  to complete a Business Combination and was winding up — with no completed combination
  appearing in the record. Absent a primary filing separately disclosing Panera Bread's
  own post-close financial or operating performance, the realized outcome for the
  matched entity is not publicly observable within the fixed window on the present
  record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-241443, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-323804, 8-K (CIK 1836894)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-292011, 8-K (CIK 1836894)]
