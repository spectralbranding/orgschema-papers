## Transaction and parties at closing
- West Corporation, a Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger under which Olympus Merger Sub, Inc. ("Sub"), a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Mount Olympus Holdings, Inc. ("Parent"), would merge with and into West, with West
  continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- Parent is an affiliate of funds managed by affiliates of Apollo Management VIII, L.P.
  (itself affiliated with Apollo Global Management, LLC); Parent and Sub were both
  formed on May 5, 2017 solely to engage in this transaction and had no other business
  activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- Merger consideration was $23.50 per share in cash; following completion, all of
  West's equity would be beneficially owned by Parent, and none of West's then-current
  stockholders would retain any ownership interest in West, the surviving corporation,
  or Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- West described itself as a global provider of communication and network
  infrastructure services, operating five segments — Unified Communications Services,
  Telecom Services, Safety Services, Interactive Services, and Specialized Agent
  Services — aggregated into four reportable segments. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Legal vehicle: the entire corporate entity crossed intact — Sub merged into West,
  West's separate corporate existence continued, and West succeeded to and assumed all
  of Sub's rights and obligations as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- Product/business model: no divestiture, carve-out, or spin-off of any segment or
  asset to a third party at closing is described; the whole going-concern business (all
  four reportable segments) transferred to Parent's ownership as a single unit. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- Processes/people: Parent agreed the surviving corporation would assume, honor and
  continue West's existing employment, severance, retention and termination plans as in
  effect immediately before the merger, with base salary, bonus opportunity, severance,
  and other benefits guaranteed no-less-favorable for a specified post-closing period
  and pre-merger service credited as service with the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- West's individual executive employment agreements and Change in Control Agreements
  remained the governing instruments for executive severance/consulting terms, and
  Apollo separately committed, after signing, to an employee retention plan of up to $3
  million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]

## Financing and controlling-stockholder arrangements (closing-time)
- An affiliate of Sub obtained a bank-syndicate debt commitment letter for up to $4.4
  billion (senior secured term facility, $350 million revolving facility, senior
  unsecured bridge facility), and Parent received equity commitments from the Apollo
  Funds of up to $1.3 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- Existing significant stockholders THL, Quadrangle, and Gary and Mary West (the
  Company's founders), collectively holding approximately 45% of West's voting power,
  each executed voting agreements supporting the merger; none is described as retaining
  an equity interest post-merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a divided
  operating substrate is described in the reviewed sections — consistent with a
  whole-company acquisition rather than a carve-out or separation. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-215084, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company, all-cash going-private
  acquisition: a newly formed vehicle merged into the target, the target survived as an
  intact wholly owned subsidiary of the acquirer, all four of the target's reportable
  segments transferred together as a single going concern, and the target's employment,
  severance, retention and executive change-in-control arrangements were contractually
  continued rather than restructured at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-215084,
  DEFM14A]
