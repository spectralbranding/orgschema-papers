## What the drawn filing is (described truthfully)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K/A (Amendment No. 1), filed 2016-12-07, that amends the
  registrant's Form 8-K filed 2016-09-26 "solely to add the information required under
  Items 9.01(a) and 9.01(b)" — i.e. it supplies the acquired-business audited financial
  statements and pro forma financial information, not the deal terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-787484, Form 8-K/A explanatory note]
- Per the 8-K/A, the acquired-business financial statements are for Abengoa Bioenergy

- The SEC Division of Corporation Finance, by letter dated September 30, 2016, stated it

  statements for the York plant in lieu of full statements of operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-787484, Form 8-K/A Item 9.01(a)]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction is an asset acquisition: Green Plains Inc. (Omaha, Nebraska; Iowa
  incorporation) acquired three ethanol plants, combined production capacity 236 million
  gallons per year, located in Madison, Illinois, Mount Vernon, Indiana and York, Nebraska,
  from subsidiaries of Abengoa S.A. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-787484, Form 8-K/A explanatory note]
- The acquisition was effected through two asset purchase agreements between Green Plains
  Inc. and the Abengoa sellers (Abengoa BioEnergy Company, LLC, Abengoa BioEnergy of
  Illinois, LLC and Abengoa BioEnergy of Indiana, LLC). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The initiating June 12, 2016 asset purchase agreement covered AB Illinois's business
  related to its Madison, IL plant and AB Indiana's business related to its Mount Vernon,
  IN plant (combined ~180 million gallons/year) for ~$200 million in cash plus certain
  inventory and assumption of certain liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-620265, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Sale mechanism at the seller side (legal vehicle)
- The Abengoa sellers and certain affiliates had pending cases under Chapter 11 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Missouri, and the
  sale was to be conducted under Sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-620265, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Green Plains's June 2016 agreement constituted a "stalking horse bid" subject to bidding
  procedures and to higher or otherwise better bids at a proposed auction; if Green Plains
  were not the successful bidder, the sellers owed a break-up fee of $2.5 million per plant
  plus expense reimbursement up to $500,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-620265, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Closing was conditioned on Bankruptcy Court approval, accuracy of representations,
  material compliance, and customary regulatory approvals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-620265, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- The customer-facing productive assets — three operating ethanol plants and their ethanol
  production capacity (236 million gallons/year) — crossed to Green Plains; the June
  agreement acquired the sellers' "business related to" each plant, i.e. operating
  facilities rather than bare equipment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-620265, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Green Plains took the plants into newly designated subsidiaries — Green Plains Madison
  LLC, Green Plains Mount Vernon LLC and Green Plains York LLC — that held the acquired
  assets at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Whether any of the sellers' plant workforce, plant management or operating personnel
  transferred with the facilities is not stated in the reviewed closing-era filings. [UNVERIFIED]

## Same-day internal drop-down and bridging agreements (closing-time)
- Effective September 23, 2016, immediately following the Abengoa purchase, the ethanol
  STORAGE assets embedded in the acquired plants were sold by the Green Plains buyer
  entities to subsidiaries of Green Plains Partners LP (a related master limited partnership
  in which Green Plains Inc. held a 62.5% limited-partner interest, a 2% general-partner
  interest, and all incentive distribution rights) for $90 million cash — the "Drop Down
  Transaction." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The storage-asset consideration was financed through borrowings under the partnership
  Operating Company's 5-year, $155 million revolving credit facility with Bank of America,
  N.A. as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- In connection with the drop-down the parties amended a set of standing shared-substrate
  agreements: a Second Amendment to the Omnibus Agreement (partnership reimburses Green
  Plains Inc. for direct/allocated general and administrative services on assets it
  acquires); Amendment No. 2 to the Operational Services and Secondment Agreement (Green
  Plains Inc. seconds certain employees to the general partner to provide management,
  maintenance and operational functions for the storage assets); and Amendment No. 2 to the
  Ethanol Storage and Throughput Agreement (raising the minimum throughput commitment to
  296.6 million gallons per calendar quarter). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Consummation status
- The transaction CONSUMMATED: effective September 23, 2016, Green Plains Inc. acquired the
  three ethanol plants for approximately $237 million in cash plus certain working-capital
  adjustments, reported under Item 2.01 (Completion of Acquisition). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-787484, Form 8-K/A explanatory note]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-plant productive asset base (three operating ethanol facilities, 236 mgy) moved
  across the boundary through a bankruptcy-court Section 363 sale into newly designated
  buyer subsidiaries; the storage layer of the same assets was simultaneously routed into a
  related partnership, with the operating and general/administrative substrate for the
  storage layer bridged by secondment and omnibus service agreements rather than transferred
  as an intact standalone organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-719192, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 2.01]
