# Structural sub-dossier — Alere Inc. acquisition by Abbott Laboratories (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Alere Inc. ("the Company"), a Delaware corporation, and Abbott Laboratories ("Abbott"),
  an Illinois corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated January 30,
  2016, under which Abbott agreed to acquire the Company at $56.00 per share in cash;
  Angel Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Abbott ("Merger
  Sub"), became a party to the agreement on February 2, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A p.1]
- On April 13, 2017 the Company, Abbott and Merger Sub entered into an Amendment to the
  Agreement and Plan of Merger, reducing the per-share cash consideration from $56.00 to
  $51.00 and extending the termination date to September 30, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A p.1, p.109]
- Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub was to merge with and into the Company, with the
  Company surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Abbott (the "surviving
  corporation"); Merger Sub itself would cease to exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A "The Merger" p.28]
- At the effective time each outstanding share of Company common stock (other than
  excluded/dissenting shares) converted into the right to receive $51.00 in cash, without
  interest; the $51.00 price represented a premium of approximately 37.1% over a
  reference measure disclosed in the proxy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A p.1775]
- A press release attached to the proxy statement describes the Company at signing as
  "the global leader in point of care diagnostics," headquartered in Waltham,
  Massachusetts, focused on rapid diagnostics for cardiometabolic disease, infectious
  disease and toxicology. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A annex press release p.8064-8068]
- Prior to the merger agreement, the Company had already divested its health management
  business to OptumHealth Care Solutions, Inc. (completed January 9, 2015) and its BBI
  business to Exponent Private Equity LLP (completed November 17, 2015) as part of a
  publicly announced strategic refocus on its core diagnostics business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger" p.29]

## Regulatory-conditioned divestiture bridging the closing
- Consummation of the merger was conditioned on antitrust clearance; the parties
  submitted a proposal to the European Commission conditioning merger approval on the
  divestiture of certain of the Company's products to a buyer approved by the European
  Commission, and the merger agreement separately obligated the parties to take
  divestiture actions as needed to resolve FTC/DOJ objections, subject to a materiality
  cap on what could be required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A p.3970; DEFM14A Merger Agreement §5.02-5.03]
- Pursuant to that regulatory requirement, the Company agreed to sell its Triage(R)
  MeterPro business ("Triage Business") to Quidel Cardiovascular Inc. (f/k/a QTB
  Acquisition Corp.), a wholly owned subsidiary of Quidel Corporation, under a Purchase
  Agreement dated July 15, 2017 and amended and restated September 15, 2017; aggregate
  consideration was $400.0 million in cash plus assumption of certain post-closing
  liabilities, and closing of this sale was conditioned on consummation of the Abbott
  merger and expected within 30 days after it. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-17-001699, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-19]
- The Company separately agreed to sell assets and liabilities relating to its contractual
  arrangement with Beckman Coulter, Inc. for supply and distribution of the Triage(R) BNP
  Test ("BNP Business") to the same Quidel purchaser, for aggregate consideration of up to
  $40.0 million (EEA installment payments) plus $240.0 million (six annual installments of
  $40.0 million) and assumption of certain post-closing liabilities; this sale was also
  conditioned on and timed to follow consummation of the Abbott merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-17-001699, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-19]
- The Triage Business sale contemplated ancillary agreements at its own closing: a mutual
  transition services agreement, a manufacturing and supply agreement (Purchaser to supply
  certain components to the Company), and lease agreements for San Diego, California real
  property. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-17-001699, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-19]
- The BNP Business sale contemplated a transition services agreement under which the
  Company would provide certain transitional services to the Purchaser. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-17-001699, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-19]

## Employee matters at closing
- Under merger agreement Section 5.08, Abbott/the surviving corporation agreed that until
  December 31, 2017 (the "Continuation Period"), each employee of the Company or its
  subsidiaries immediately prior to the effective time would receive base salary/bonus
  opportunities, severance benefits, and employee benefit plans no less favorable than
  those in effect immediately before the merger, plus a 2017 long-term incentive award. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A Merger Agreement §5.08(a)]
- The same section expressly states it does not obligate Abbott or the surviving
  corporation to retain the employment of any particular employee following the effective
  time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A Merger Agreement §5.08(e)]

## Consummation
- The merger closed on October 3, 2017; Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with
  the Company surviving as a subsidiary of Abbott, and each outstanding share converted
  into the right to receive $51.00 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-060404, Form 8-K filed 2017-10-03]
- On the closing date the Company requested that the New York Stock Exchange remove its
  common stock and Series B Convertible Perpetual Preferred Stock from listing prior to
  market open, and filed a Form 25 to report the removal from listing and deregistration
  under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-060404, Form 8-K filed 2017-10-03; SEC accession 0001104659-17-060405, Form 25]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash-out merger in which the Company became a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Abbott, with the customer-facing diagnostics product line
  transferring intact except for two named product lines (Triage MeterPro and the Triage
  BNP Test business) that were carved out and sold to a third-party purchaser as an
  antitrust condition, with the process substrate for those carved-out products bridged by
  transition-services and manufacturing/supply agreements between the Company/Abbott and
  the purchaser; continuity of employee compensation/benefits (but not of any specific
  position) was contractually fixed only through the end of calendar 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-195381, DEFM14A; SEC accession 0000950142-17-001699, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-19]
