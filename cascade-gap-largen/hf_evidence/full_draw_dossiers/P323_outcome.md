## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced, within the ~2021-2023 window)
- In December 2021, Bloomberg News reported (subsequently covered by Reuters) that
  Veritas Capital was exploring a sale of the health-IT company Cotiviti that could
  value it at more than $15 billion; the report stated Veritas had earlier been
  considering an initial public offering of Cotiviti but was weighing a sale instead
  amid stock-market volatility, with an official sale process potentially starting as
  early as January 2022. [SRC: https://www.medicalfinancenews.com/2021/12/10/pe-firm-veritas-weighs-sale-of-cotiviti/]
- No subsequent press report identified in this search documents a completed sale or
  IPO of Cotiviti within the 2021-2023 window; the December 2021 report describes an
  exploration of strategic alternatives, not a consummated transaction. [UNVERIFIED: whether the explored 2021-2022 sale process was completed, abandoned, or continued]

## Item noted but outside the fixed ~3-5-year window
- On May 2, 2024 (beyond the ~3-5-year window), Cotiviti announced completion of a
  recapitalization transaction in which investment funds managed by KKR and Cotiviti's
  long-standing owner Veritas Capital became co-sponsors with equal ownership stakes;
  the company's CEO, Dr. Emad Rizk, and Veritas Capital's CEO, Ramzi Musallam, issued
  statements describing "sustained, transformative growth" over the ownership period,
  and the announcement stated Cotiviti's solutions were used by more than 200 healthcare
  payers. No revenue, profit, or employee-count figures were disclosed in the release. [SRC: https://www.veritascapital.com/cotiviti-completes-recapitalization-with-kkr-and-long-standing-owner-veritas/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Cotiviti was taken private and deregistered its common stock in September
  2018, and because no successor entity in the transaction (Verscend Holding Corp. and
  its affiliated holding entities) has ever filed periodic financial reports with the
  SEC, there is no publicly available, audited outcome record for Cotiviti's business
  for the fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window. The only in-window public information located
  is a December 2021 press report of an explored (not completed) sale process valuing
  the company at more than $15 billion, roughly three times the 2018 take-private
  transaction value; whether that process concluded, and the company's financial
  performance across the window, is not publicly documented. Per the no-record
  provision for whole-company acquisitions, this case's outcome is coded **uncertain**:
  it is not fabricated or inferred from an acquirer's consolidated results, because no
