# Structural sub-dossier — KapStone Paper and Packaging Corporation merger with WestRock Company (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- WestRock Company, KapStone Paper and Packaging Corporation, Whiskey Holdco, Inc.
  ("Holdco"), Whiskey Merger Sub, Inc. and Kola Merger Sub, Inc. — all Delaware
  corporations, the latter three newly formed solely to effect the transaction — entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated January 28, 2018, structured as two
  concurrent mergers: Whiskey Merger Sub merged with and into WestRock (surviving as a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Holdco), and Kola Merger Sub merged with and into KapStone
  (surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Holdco); as a result Holdco became the
  ultimate parent of both. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- Each KapStone share was to be converted, at the holder's election, into $35.00 cash or
  0.4981 Holdco shares, with stock elections capped so that no more than 25% of KapStone
  shares issued and outstanding could convert into Holdco stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- Holdco was to be renamed "WestRock Company" and the surviving WestRock entity renamed;
  Holdco shares were expected to be listed on the NYSE under WestRock's existing symbol
  "WRK." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- The mergers were completed on November 2, 2018; that day KapStone also repaid all
  amounts (including principal) outstanding under its then-existing credit facility.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-18-007571, Form 8-K]
- WestRock's FY2021 Form 10-K records the KapStone acquisition consideration as $4.9
  billion including assumed debt, an assumed long-term financing obligation and assumed
  equity awards, comprising approximately $3.3 billion cash and 1.6 million WestRock
  shares valued at $70.1 million (approximately 0.6% of shares outstanding
  post-closing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-057778, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 3]

## What KapStone comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- KapStone, founded in 2005 and headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois, was described as
  the fifth-largest North American containerboard producer and the largest North
  American kraft paper producer by capacity, operating in two segments: Paper and
  Packaging (containerboard, corrugated products, specialty paper) and Distribution
  (Victory Packaging, L.P., a distribution company with U.S./Canada/Mexico facilities).
  It operated four containerboard mills, 23 corrugated products manufacturing plants and
  approximately 60 distribution centers, employing approximately 6,400 people. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- KapStone's own Form 10-K for fiscal 2017 identifies the four containerboard mills as
  located in North Charleston, South Carolina; Roanoke Rapids, North Carolina; Cowpens,
  South Carolina; and Longview, Washington, with the North Charleston mill (acquired
  from MeadWestvaco Corporation in 2008) staffed by approximately 560 employees under a
  collective bargaining agreement running through June 2025. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001015, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Governance and management tier at closing
- Pursuant to the merger agreement and Section 251(g) of the Delaware General
  Corporation Law, the directors and officers of Holdco following the mergers were to be
  the directors and officers of WestRock (not of KapStone) as of the effective time.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- Concurrently with signing, WestRock entered into voting agreements with KapStone's
  Executive Chairman Roger Stone, President/CEO Matthew Kaplan, and affiliated
  Stone-family holders (together owning approximately 9.5% of KapStone shares) committing
  them to vote in favor of the mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- KapStone entered into change-in-control severance agreements with named executive
  officers (including Andrea K. Tarbox, Randy J. Nebel, Wilbur G. Kessinger and five
  others) providing cash severance and health benefits upon a qualifying termination
  within one year of closing; these payments were contingent on the mergers closing and
  were not conditioned on continued employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]

## Integration and business-model bridging at closing
- The proxy stated that WestRock expected to generate cost synergies and performance
  improvements "through the integration of KapStone's operations into WestRock's
  corrugated packaging system," and disclosed a KapStone-management-prepared illustrative
  synergy estimate of $145-235 million (explicitly not for public-disclosure purposes).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy's risk factors flagged that realizing anticipated synergies would depend on
  successfully combining the businesses, including "differences in the business cultures
  of the two companies and retaining key personnel," and that integration "may also be
  complex and time consuming, and require substantial resources and effort." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement obligated KapStone to "reasonably cooperate" with WestRock "to
  facilitate the planning of the integration of the parties and their respective
  businesses after the closing date," with no separate transitional-services or
  shared-services agreement disclosed in this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing status at closing
- The merger agreement provided that, following completion of the mergers, Holdco would
  cause KapStone shares to be removed from listing on the NYSE and deregistered under the
  Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined KapStone whole (both its containerboard/corrugated product
  tier and its Victory Packaging distribution tier) into a newly formed Holdco alongside
  WestRock, with cash-or-stock consideration to KapStone holders; but the post-closing
  governance tier (Holdco's directors and officers) carried over from WestRock only, not
  KapStone, and KapStone's named executives held change-in-control severance rights
  rather than assured continuity, while the parties' own disclosure flagged unresolved
  integration risk around combining business cultures and retaining personnel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-041383, Form DEFM14A]
