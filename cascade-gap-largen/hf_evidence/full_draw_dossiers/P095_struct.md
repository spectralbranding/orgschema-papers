## Platform acquisition — Foam Fabricators (deal structure at closing)
- On February 15, 2018, FFI Compass, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of the Company ("Acquisition Sub"), acquired all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Foam Fabricators, Inc. pursuant to a Stock Purchase Agreement dated January 18, 2018 between Acquisition Sub and Warren F. Florkiewicz; Foam Fabricators became a wholly owned subsidiary of Acquisition Sub and an indirect subsidiary of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000009, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The Company paid a purchase price of approximately $247.5 million, before working-capital and other adjustments, funded through a draw on its revolving credit facility; the Company's initial equity ownership in Acquisition Sub was approximately 100% on a primary and 92% on a fully diluted basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000009, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Concurrent with closing, the Company (as lender) provided an intercompany credit facility to Foam Fabricators and its wholly owned subsidiary Foam Fab, Inc. (the "Foam Fab Credit Agreement"), with approximately $115.0 million outstanding on the closing date, secured by substantially all assets of the guarantors and by pledges of the equity interests in Foam Fabricators and Foam Fab; the Company noted the terms were "similar to the terms and conditions of the credit facilities in place with its existing subsidiary businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000009, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The customer-facing business acquired: Foam Fabricators is "a leading designer and manufacturer of custom molded protective foam solutions and OEM components made from expanded polymers such as expanded polystyrene (EPS) and expanded polypropylene (EPP)," headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona, operating 13 molding and fabricating facilities across North America, serving appliances/electronics, pharmaceuticals, health-and-wellness, automotive and building-products end-markets, with trailing-twelve-months (ended Nov 30, 2017) net revenue of approximately $126 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017 Subsequent Events]

## Add-on acquisition — Rimports (deal structure at closing)
- On February 26, 2018, CODI's existing portfolio company Sterno Products, LLC acquired all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of Rimports, Inc. (a Utah corporation) under a Stock Purchase Agreement dated January 23, 2018 with Jeffery W. Palmer and affiliated family trusts; Rimports became a wholly owned subsidiary of Sterno and an indirect subsidiary of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000011, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Sterno paid a purchase price of approximately $145 million (excluding working-capital adjustments and a potential earn-out of up to $25 million based on future financial performance), funded through a draw on the Company's revolving credit facility; the acquisition was folded into Sterno's existing October 10, 2014 intercompany credit and guarantee/collateral arrangements by amendment (additional term loans of $136.0 million and revolving loans of $10.0 million), with Rimports joined as a guarantor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000011, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The filing frames this explicitly as consolidation under an existing platform: "The following description relates to the recent add-on acquisition of a business by one of CODI's existing portfolio companies, Sterno." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000011, 8-K Item 8.01]

## Holding-company / roll-up structure at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant and the Trust (collectively "CODI") "were formed to acquire and manage a group of small and middle-market businesses headquartered in North America"; the Company states "We acquire controlling interests in and actively manage businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017]
- The vehicle is externally managed: day-to-day operations and affairs of the Company are managed by Compass Group Management LLC ("CGM" or "our Manager") under a Management Services Agreement; the Company "pay[s] our Manager a quarterly management fee," which "covers all expenses related to the services performed by our Manager, including the compensation of our Chief Executive Officer and other personnel." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017]
- Acquired businesses are run on a decentralized basis with their own management retained: the Company states "we operate our businesses on a stand-alone basis, primarily relying on existing management teams for management of their day-to-day operations," while noting that "opportunities exist to augment existing management at such businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017]
- As of December 31, 2017 the Company held controlling interests in a portfolio of separately operated businesses (e.g., it "made loans to and purchased a controlling interest in" Crosman on June 2, 2017 for approximately $150 million), each described as maintaining its own management team and market niche. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession is an Item 2.02 earnings release reporting Q1-2018 results; the transactions it records are the platform acquisition of Foam Fabricators (a controlling stock purchase into a newly formed Acquisition Sub, ~$247.5 million, closed Feb 15, 2018) and the add-on acquisition of Rimports by existing subsidiary Sterno (~$145 million + up to $25 million earn-out, closed Feb 26, 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000037, 8-K Exhibit 99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000009, 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000011, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Both are structured as whole-stock purchases that bring the customer-facing product operations (and, per the annual report, their existing management teams and stand-alone operating processes) under CODI's controlling ownership, financed through the parent's revolving facility and intercompany credit agreements, within an externally managed holding structure that leaves acquired businesses to run on a stand-alone basis under CGM oversight. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000014, 10-K FY2017] [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-18-000009, 8-K Item 8.01]
