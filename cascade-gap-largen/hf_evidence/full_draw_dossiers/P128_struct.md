# Structural sub-dossier — US Foods Holding Corp. corporate structure and history of formation (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the entity's
> corporate structure was assembled and organized as disclosed at the closing-era

> contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Parties, legal vehicle, and transaction type
- US Foods Holding Corp. is a Delaware holding company with no direct operations; it
  conducts all operations through its wholly owned operating subsidiary, US Foods, Inc.
  ("USF"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1 filed 2016-02-09]
- The company describes itself as "[b]uilt through organic growth and acquisitions,"
  tracing its roots "back over 150 years" to heritage companies Monarch Foods (1853),
  Sexton (1883), PYA (1903), Rykoff (1911), and Kraft Foodservice (1976). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
- In 2007, investment funds associated with Clayton, Dubilier & Rice, LLC ("CD&R") and
  Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. ("KKR") (together, the "Sponsors") acquired
  US Foodservice from Royal Ahold N.V.; in November 2011 the company rebranded from
  "US Foodservice" to "US Foods." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
- In December 2013 the company agreed to merge with Sysco Corporation; the agreement was
  terminated June 26, 2015 after failing to obtain regulatory approvals. From fiscal 2010
  through 2013 it separately completed twelve acquisitions of broadline/specialty
  distributors, plus a further Milwaukee-market distributor in December 2015. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]

## Controlling intent and governance vehicle
- Following the offering, CD&R and KKR affiliates remain controlling shareholders under a
  stockholders agreement: each Sponsor currently designates three directors (one CD&R
  designee to chair), the CEO is jointly designated by the Sponsors and sits on the board,
  and the Sponsors hold approval rights over certain corporate transactions. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
- The company is separately party to consulting agreements with each Sponsor for "ongoing
  consulting and management advisory services," compensated by a monthly management fee
  ($0.8 million/month; $10 million recorded in each of fiscal 2012-2014) paid to
  Sponsor-affiliated funds, distinct from the board-governance relationship. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]

## Customer-facing product and operational substrate
- The customer-facing product is foodservice distribution: over 400,000 fresh, frozen,
  and dry food/non-food SKUs from 5,000+ suppliers to 250,000+ customer locations, via a
  private-brand portfolio and e-commerce platform, distributed from 61 facilities and
  ~6,000 trucks plus a shared-services center in Tempe, Arizona. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
- The S-1 discloses that "[s]ince our 2007 acquisition by CD&R and KKR" the company made
  "far-reaching structural changes to our operating model," including "standardizing and
  centralizing certain business processes and moving to a common technology
  infrastructure" across the businesses assembled through its acquisition history, with a
  stated further intent to move toward a hub-served multi-site distribution-center model.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]

## People / management continuity
- Pietro Satriano has served as President, CEO, and a director since July 2015; from
  February 2011 until that appointment he served as Chief Merchandising Officer, having
  joined from LoyaltyOne (2009-2011) after prior leadership roles at Loblaw Companies.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
- John Lederer stepped down from the President and CEO roles in July 2015, and
  Mr. Satriano was promoted from within into the position. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As registered in this filing, the entity is a holding-company vehicle assembled through
  a 2007 sponsor-led acquisition of a pre-existing distribution business (itself a
  multi-decade roll-up of heritage foodservice distributors) plus a further twelve
  acquisitions from 2010-2015, with a single integrated customer-facing distribution
  product/operating network, internally promoted management continuity at the CEO level,
  and Sponsor control exercised through a stockholders agreement and separate paid
  consulting agreements rather than direct operating management. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-16-455364, Form S-1]
