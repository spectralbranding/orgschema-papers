## Public-reporting entity and window (feasibility-relevant)
- The surviving public filer of the 2018 combination — Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. — is
  registered under CIK 0001723128 (a CIK distinct from Impax Laboratories, Inc.'s
  CIK 0001003642); it has filed a Form 10-K annually since fiscal year 2017/2018,
  including a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 filed March 1, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The fixed outcome window used here is fiscal years 2019-2021 (approximately 1-3.5
  years after the ~May 2018 closing), drawn from the comparative-period figures reported
  in the FY2021 Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented outcome scalars)
- Consolidated net income (loss): $(603,573) thousand for fiscal year 2019, $68,578
  thousand for fiscal year 2020, and $20,170 thousand for fiscal year 2021; net income
  (loss) attributable to Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc. common stockholders was
  $(361,917) thousand (2019), $91,059 thousand (2020), and $10,624 thousand (2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Net revenue for fiscal year 2021 was $2,093,669 thousand (approximately $2.1 billion),
  an increase of approximately 5% ($101 million) from $1,992,523 thousand (approximately
  $2.0 billion) in fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The Company performed its annual quantitative goodwill impairment test as of October
  1, 2021 and determined that the estimated fair values of the Generics, Specialty, and
  AvKARE reporting units each exceeded their respective carrying amounts; no goodwill
  impairment charge was recorded for fiscal year 2021 and no impairment indicators arose
  in the subsequent period through the filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Reported business segments and product-line continuity
- As of the FY2021 Form 10-K, the Company reports three reportable segments: Generics,
  Specialty, and AvKARE. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The Specialty segment — engaged in development, promotion, sale and distribution of
  proprietary branded CNS-disorder products including Rytary and a Zomig nasal spray
  product — reported net sales of $378 million (2021), $356 million (2020), and $318
  million (2019), with operating income of $56 million (2021), $57 million (2020), and
  $43 million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The Generics segment reported net sales of $1.4 billion, $1.3 billion, and $1.3
  billion, and operating income (loss) of $281 million, $189 million, and $(133)
  million, for 2021, 2020, and 2019 respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The AvKARE segment originates from a separate, later transaction (the "Rondo
  Acquisitions," completed January 31, 2020, of AvKARE, Inc. and Dixon Shane, LLC d/b/a
  R&S Northeast LLC), not from the 2018 Impax-Amneal combination; the Company also
  completed further acquisitions during 2021 (Puniska Healthcare Pvt. Ltd. on November 2,
  2021; a "KSP Acquisition" on April 2, 2021), each carrying its own
  acquisition-and-integration expense line. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Acquisition, transaction-related and integration expenses (covering the Puniska, KSP,
  and Rondo integrations) were reported at $8,055 thousand for fiscal year 2021 and
  $8,988 thousand for fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Ongoing legal-contingency disclosures
- The FY2021 Form 10-K discloses that the Company and certain affiliates are named
  defendants in multiple state and federal court actions relating to the sale of
  prescription opioid pain relievers, brought by state Attorneys General, county and
  municipal governments, hospitals, tribes, pension funds, third-party payors, and
  individuals seeking unspecified monetary damages ("Note 21. Commitments and
  Contingencies — Prescription Opioid Litigation"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The same filing discloses ongoing private antitrust litigation and FTC scrutiny
  concerning generic-drug "reverse payment" patent settlement agreements to which the
  Company is a party, including bellwether-case designations by the court as of May 2021
  and a separate Canadian price-fixing action. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- "Charges related to legal matters, net" were reported at $25,000 thousand for fiscal
  year 2021, up from $5,860 thousand in fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The combined public filer (Amneal Pharmaceuticals, Inc., CIK 0001723128) continued to
  report and grow both legacy product lines through the outcome window: consolidated net
  revenue rose from $2.0 billion (FY2020) to $2.1 billion (FY2021), the Specialty segment
  (tracing to former Impax CNS products) and the Generics segment (tracing to the
  Amneal/Impax generics businesses) both posted net sales growth and positive operating
  income in FY2021, and the annual goodwill impairment test found no impairment for any
  reporting unit as of October 1, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- A large consolidated net loss of $(603,573) thousand was recorded in fiscal year 2019
  (attributable-to-Company loss of $(361,917) thousand), followed by consolidated net
  income of $68,578 thousand (FY2020) and $20,170 thousand (FY2021) — a trajectory from
  a large loss in the year following closing to modest sustained profitability by the
  fixed outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- By the FY2021 filing, the Company's Co-Chief Executive Officers were Chirag Patel and
  Chintu Patel and its Chairman was Paul M. Meister, with no mention found of the two
  named individuals associated with Chief Executive Officer / Executive Chairman roles
  at the time of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The Company disclosed ongoing opioid-related and generic-drug antitrust litigation and
  a year-over-year increase in "charges related to legal matters, net" (from $5.86
  million in FY2020 to $25.0 million in FY2021) within the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001723128-22-000006, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
