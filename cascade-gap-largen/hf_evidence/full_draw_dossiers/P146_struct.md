# Structural sub-dossier — Alpha Natural Resources, Inc. Chapter 11 plan / §363 credit-bid sale to Contura Energy (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On August 3, 2015 (the "Petition Date"), Alpha Natural Resources, Inc. (the "Company") and certain of its direct and indirect subsidiaries (the "Debtors") filed voluntary petitions under chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Eastern District of Virginia; the cases are captioned In re Alpha Natural Resources, Inc., et al., Case No. 15-33896 (Bankr. E.D. Va.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On March 7, 2016 the Debtors filed a proposed plan of reorganization (the "Plan") and related disclosure statement; the Bankruptcy Court approved the Disclosure Statement on May 26, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Plan contemplated two major components: (1) the Debtors would sell certain self-sustaining assets (the "Reserve Price Assets") to a newly formed entity, Contura Energy, Inc. (the "Purchaser"), pursuant to the stalking-horse credit bid (the "Stalking Horse Bid") of the Debtors' senior secured lenders (the "First Lien Lenders"); and (2) the Debtors would reorganize as new entities (the "Reorganized Debtors") that operate eighteen mines and eight preparation plants and fulfill certain reclamation, mitigation and water-treatment obligations relating to their retained assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 1.03] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Transaction type and legal vehicle (as structured)
- The sale was effectuated under sections 105, 363, 365 and 1123(a)(5)(D) of the Bankruptcy Code, via an Asset Purchase Agreement dated as of July 26, 2016 among Contura Energy, Inc., Alpha Natural Resources, Inc., certain subsidiaries of Alpha Natural Resources, Inc. (as sellers' representative), and ANR, Inc. (the "Stalking Horse APA"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 (recitals) / Exhibit Index EX-2.5]
- On March 11, 2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered an order establishing bid and auction procedures and approving the Stalking Horse Bid as the lead bid; the Stalking Horse Bid included no bid protections (no break-up fee or expense reimbursement) and merely set a floor at no cost to the estates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The Board of Directors did not qualify any competing bids for the Reserve Price Assets (other than the PLR Assets, below), and on May 13, 2016 the Debtors designated the Stalking Horse Bid as the successful bid. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed the Plan and approved the sale of the Reserve Price Assets (the "NewCo Asset Sale") on July 7, 2016, and entered the order confirming the Plan (the "Confirmation Order") on July 12, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Consideration mechanics (as structured)
- The Plan contemplated the release by the lenders under the Credit Agreement of $325 million of indebtedness (the "Credit Release") in exchange for consummation of the transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 (recitals)]
- As consideration for the Purchased Assets — in addition to assumption of the Assumed Liabilities and the Credit Release — the Buyer was to issue and deliver to Alpha Natural Resources: (i) 10,000,000 shares of Buyer common stock, representing 100% of the issued and outstanding Buyer common stock; (ii) warrants to acquire 810,811 shares of Buyer common stock; (iii) a promissory note ("Buyer Takeback Paper"); and (iv) a promissory note (the "GUC Distribution Note"). Under no circumstances was the Buyer obligated to pay cash in satisfaction of the Purchase Price except as required by a purchase-price adjustment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 §2.06]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- Under the Stalking Horse APA the Debtors were to transfer or assign to the Purchaser: (1) all assets (including all mineral rights, fixed and mobile equipment and logistics assets) used or held for use primarily in connection with (a) the Alpha Coal West mine complexes in Wyoming, (b) the McClure and Toms Creek mine complexes in Virginia and (c) the Nicholas mine complex in West Virginia; (2) all coal operations and substantially all reserves located in Pennsylvania, including the Cumberland mine complex, the Emerald mine complex, the Freeport reserves and the Sewickley reserves, and all logistics-related assets used therewith; (3) the Debtors' 41% interest in Dominion Terminal Associates, a coal export terminal in Newport News, Virginia; (4) certain other designated assets, including certain working capital; and (5) certain unexpired leases and executory contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What did NOT cross — retained assets and reorganized entity
- Prior to the Petition Date the Company caused Alpha Natural Resources Holdings, Inc. ("ANR Holdings"), an entity not affiliated with the Debtors, to be incorporated; ANR Holdings formed ANR, Inc. Pursuant to the Plan, on the Effective Date the Company was to transfer all equity interests in its subsidiary Debtors, all assets other than the Reserve Price Assets, and its equity to ANR, Inc., and subsequently dissolve; ANR Holdings would become the ultimate parent of the Reorganized Debtors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 1.03 (Restructuring Transactions)]
- Certain natural-gas assets (the "PLR Assets") in southwestern Pennsylvania owned by Debtor Pennsylvania Land Resources, LLC were initially included in the Reserve Price Assets but were removed after a separate $200 million stalking-horse bid; the Purchaser's bid for the remaining Reserve Price Assets was reduced by $175 million. The PLR Assets were sold at a May 16, 2016 auction to Vantage Energy Appalachia II, LLC for $339.5 million in cash; that sale closed June 2, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- All equity interests in the Company (preferred stock, common stock, options, warrants and other rights) were to be cancelled on the Effective Date and were not entitled to any distributions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Item 3.03]

## People / management (as structured)
- Employees did not transfer automatically: no later than one business day prior to Closing the Buyer was to provide the Sellers a list of the Business Employees it desired to offer employment ("Offered Employees"); the Sellers would terminate the employment of each Offered Employee at Closing; Offered Employees who accepted the Buyer's offer and commenced work became "Transferred Employees." Any Business Employee who did not become a Transferred Employee, and the associated severance/termination costs, were retained on the seller side (NonCoreCo and its affiliates). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 §9.02(a)]
- Neither the Buyer nor any Designated Buyer assumed any employee benefit plan (ERISA plans or other compensatory/health/welfare plans) sponsored or maintained by any Seller; such plans were to be transferred to ReorgCo or its subsidiaries prior to Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 §9.01(f) / §9.02(b)]

## Arrangement bridging a shared substrate (closing-time)
- The transaction documents included a Transition Services Agreement, dated as of the Closing Date, between the Buyer, Alpha Natural Resources and ReorgCo, in a form to be mutually agreed under §7.11, delivered at Closing by both sides. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 (definition of "Transition Services Agreement"; §2.09 deliverables)]
- The Agreement provided for "Pre-Closing Restructuring Steps" (steps 1–16 of Schedule B), including "Subsidiary Transfers" whereby the ANR Subsidiaries transferred all Purchased Assets and Assumed Liabilities they held to Alpha Natural Resources immediately before Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K EX-2.5 (recitals: Pre-Closing Restructuring Steps / Subsidiary Transfers)]

## Consummation (closing)
- As of July 26, 2016 all conditions to the effective date were satisfied or waived and the Effective Date of the Plan occurred; the Company filed a Notice of Effectiveness with the Bankruptcy Court that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 1.03, EX-99.2]
- The Bankruptcy Court approved the NewCo Asset Sale on July 12, 2016, and the NewCo Asset Sale closed on the Effective Date (July 26, 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Under a court-approved section 363 / chapter 11 plan process, a self-sustaining core of coal-mining assets (mine complexes in Wyoming, Virginia, West Virginia and Pennsylvania with their mineral rights, fixed and mobile equipment and logistics, a 41% coal-export-terminal interest, certain working capital and assigned leases/contracts) was transferred to a lender-formed new entity, Contura Energy, Inc., in exchange for a $325 million credit release, assumption of Assumed Liabilities, 100% of the Buyer's common stock, warrants and two promissory notes (no cash), while the remaining assets and subsidiary equity, all employee benefit plans, and any employees the Buyer did not elect to hire were retained by reorganized entities under ANR Holdings; a Transition Services Agreement bridged shared services at Closing, and the sale closed on the Effective Date of July 26, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-650114, Form 8-K Items 1.03/3.03] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660260, Form 8-K Items 1.03/2.01, EX-2.5, EX-99.2]
