## Proceeding and parties at closing
- Midstates Petroleum Company, Inc. (Delaware) and its debtor affiliate Midstates Petroleum Company LLC filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions on April 30, 2016 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, jointly administered as *In re Midstates Petroleum Company, Inc., et al.*, Case No. 16-32237. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K Item 8.01]
- Before filing, the Debtors entered a Plan Support Agreement with certain secured creditors (April 30, 2016), amended a second time on August 31, 2016, obligating the Consenting First Lien Lenders and Consenting Noteholders to support an Amended Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Debtors filed a Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization on May 14, 2016 and a First Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization on August 24, 2016, attached as Exhibit 99.1 to the September 7, 2016 Form 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K Item 8.01 and EX-99.1]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered an order confirming the First Amended Plan on September 28, 2016, and the Plan became effective, with the Debtors emerging from Chapter 11, on October 21, 2016 (the "Effective Date"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 1.01]

## Treatment of claims and capital structure at closing
- Allowed First Lien Claims (approximately $252,024,575 in principal) received a Pro Rata share of approximately $82 million in cash plus either New Credit Facility Revolving Loans or New Credit Facility Term Loans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- Allowed Second Lien Notes Claims (aggregate principal $625,000,000) received a Pro Rata share of 97.5% of the New Common Stock (subject to dilution) plus Excess Cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- Allowed Third Lien Notes Claims (aggregate principal $529,653,388) received a Pro Rata share of 2.5% of the New Common Stock plus Third Lien Notes Warrants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- Existing Interests in Parent (pre-petition common stock) were cancelled and extinguished on the Effective Date, with no distribution to their holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1; SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 1.02]
- On the Effective Date the Company issued 24,062,500 shares of new Common Stock to holders of Second Lien Notes Claims, 625,000 shares plus 4,411,765 warrants to holders of Third Lien Notes Claims, and 312,500 shares plus 2,213,789 warrants to holders of Unsecured Notes/General Unsecured Claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 3.02]
- Ten percent of the New Common Stock (fully diluted) was reserved under a post-Effective-Date Management Incentive Plan for officers and other management of the Reorganized Debtors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- A Registration Rights Agreement, entered into on the Effective Date with creditors receiving the new Common Stock, governs resale registration of the newly issued shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 1.01]

## New financing entered at closing
- On the Effective Date, Midstates Petroleum Company LLC (as borrower) entered a new Senior Secured Credit Agreement (the "Exit Facility") with SunTrust Bank as administrative agent, with an initial borrowing base of $170 million, secured by first-priority mortgages on at least 95% of the borrower's oil and gas properties and substantially all other assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Exit Facility matures on the earlier of September 30, 2020 or four years from the Effective Date and carries minimum EBITDA-to-interest and leverage-ratio covenants plus capped capital expenditures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 1.01]

## Legal vehicle, governance, and organization at closing
- Each Debtor was provided to continue to exist after the Effective Date as a separate corporate entity under its pre-existing certificate of incorporation/bylaws, except as amended pursuant to the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- On the Effective Date the Company filed a Second Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation with the Delaware Secretary of State and adopted Amended and Restated Bylaws. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The Plan provided that the term of the pre-Effective-Date board would expire and a New Board be appointed by parties to the Plan Support Agreement holding at least 50.1% of the Second Lien Notes Claims held by such parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- On the Effective Date the Board expanded to seven members: two continuing directors — Frederic F. Brace, the Company's chief executive officer, and Alan J. Carr, who became non-executive Chairman — plus five new directors designated under the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 5.02]
- All three named executive officers in place before the Effective Date — Frederic F. Brace (President & CEO), Nelson M. Haight (EVP & CFO), and Mitchell G. Elkins (EVP of Operations) — continued in the same roles after the Effective Date, each entering a new executive employment agreement effective October 21, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Item 5.02]
- Existing management employment agreements were assumed and/or amended and assumed under the Plan, and existing employee benefit, retirement, and severance-related agreements in place as of the Effective Date were assumed by the Reorganized Debtors and continued after the Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- Existing director and officer liability insurance policies were assumed by the Reorganized Debtors with no reduction in coverage after the Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]
- All property of the Debtors' bankruptcy estates vested in the Reorganized Debtors on the Effective Date, and each Reorganized Debtor was authorized to operate its business and use, acquire, or dispose of property without further Bankruptcy Court supervision. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction restructured Midstates' balance sheet through a confirmed Chapter 11 plan effective October 21, 2016 that cancelled approximately $1.15 billion in prepetition secured and unsecured notes plus the pre-petition common stock, converted the bulk of that claims value into new equity concentrated in the Second Lien Notes Claims (97.5% of New Common Stock) and Third Lien Notes Claims (2.5%), and replaced the pre-petition credit facility with a new $170 million secured Exit Facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-143491, 8-K EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Items 1.01, 3.02]
- The legal vehicle (the same Delaware corporation, re-chartered), the operating business (upstream oil and gas production, unchanged), the full pre-existing senior management team, and a minority (two of seven) of the pre-existing board carried forward across the Effective Date without interruption, while ownership of the entity's equity was substantially reallocated to the former secured creditors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-152625, 8-K Items 3.02, 5.02]
