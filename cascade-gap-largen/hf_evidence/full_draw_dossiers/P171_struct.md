# Structural sub-dossier — SandRidge Energy, Inc. Chapter 11 plan-of-reorganization closing structure (effective October 4, 2016)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- SandRidge Energy, Inc. and certain of its direct and indirect subsidiaries had filed
  voluntary petitions on May 16, 2016 seeking relief under Chapter 11 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K introductory paragraph]
- On September 20, 2016 the Bankruptcy Court entered an amended order confirming the
  Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization of SandRidge Energy, Inc. and its
  Debtor Affiliates, dated September 19, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K introductory paragraph]
- On October 4, 2016 (the "Effective Date") the Plan became effective and the Debtors
  emerged from the Chapter 11 Cases; the reporting entity (SandRidge Energy, Inc., a
  Delaware corporation) is the same legal registrant before and after the Effective
  Date — no new acquiring company or successor entity is identified in the Report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K introductory paragraph and cover page]
- The New Common Stock was approved for listing on the NYSE on October 4, 2016 and
  trading commenced that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## What crossed the ownership/control boundary at closing, tier by tier
- Controlling equity interest: all previously outstanding shares of the Company's
  common stock and preferred stock (the "Pre-Effective Date" equity) were cancelled,
  discharged, and released by operation of the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.02, Item 3.03]
- New controlling equity was issued to holders of Allowed Claims: 19,371,229 shares of
  New Common Stock, $281,780,873 of 0.00% convertible senior subordinated notes due
  2020 (convertible into approximately 43.68% of New Common Stock on an as-converted
  basis) issued to holders of Second Lien Note Claims, and 4,913,251 Series A Warrants
  plus 2,068,690 Series B Warrants issued to holders of General Unsecured Claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01, Item 3.02]
- Prior secured and unsecured debt instruments — the Fourth Amended and Restated
  Credit Agreement (First Lien Credit Agreement), six series of unsecured/second-lien
  notes, and their related indentures and collateral agreements — were cancelled by
  operation of the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- New financing was put in place on the Effective Date: a $425 million New First Lien
  Exit Facility with Royal Bank of Canada as administrative agent, a $35 million New
  Building Note secured by the Company's headquarters facility, and the New Convertible
  Notes described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Legal vehicle: the Company filed an Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation
  and adopted Amended and Restated Bylaws on the Effective Date; this amended the
  existing charter rather than forming a new corporate entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- Customer-facing asset (oil, natural gas and NGL reserves/production): the New First
  Lien Exit Facility is secured by first-priority mortgages on at least 95% of the PV-9
  pricing of proved developed producing reserves and 95% of PV-9 pricing of all proved
  reserves in the Company's most recent reserve report, indicating the reserve base
  remained with the Company and its subsidiaries at closing; no sale or divestiture of
  the oil-and-gas asset base is described in the Report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Operating subsidiaries: SandRidge Holdings, Inc., SandRidge Exploration and
  Production, LLC, SandRidge Midstream Inc., SandRidge Operating Company, Lariat
  Services, Inc., and Integra Energy, L.L.C. are named as Guarantors of the New First
  Lien Exit Facility on the Effective Date — the same operating-subsidiary set
  continuing to guarantee the Company's new obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- People/management: the Company's executive-officer group as of the Effective Date
  "consisted of the following existing executive officers": James D. Bennett
  (President and CEO), Julian Bott (EVP and CFO), Steven D. Turk (EVP and COO), Duane
  M. Grubert (EVP — Investor Relations and Strategy), R. Scott Griffin (SVP — People
  and Culture), and Philip T. Warman (SVP, General Counsel and Corporate Secretary) —
  i.e., the incumbent management team continued unchanged through the Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Board/governance: seven directors ceased to serve as of the Effective Date (Jeffrey
  S. Serota, Jim J. Brewer, Everett R. Dobson, Edward R. Moneypenny, J. Michael Stice,
  Alan J. Weber, Dan A. Westbrook); a new five-member board was appointed (James
  Bennett, Michael Lynn Bennett, John V. Genova as chairman, William M. Griffin, Jr.,
  David J. Kornder). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Contractual/bridging arrangements at closing
- The Report describes no transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement
  bridging a divested or carved-out business unit; the pre-petition operating
  subsidiaries listed above continued in place as the post-emergence Guarantors, and
  no separation of a customer-facing business line from the reporting entity is
  described. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A Registration Rights Agreement was entered into on the Effective Date among the
  Company and holders of at least 10% of New Common Stock (on a fully diluted basis),
  giving those Required Holders the right to demand registration of their shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Indemnification agreements were entered into between the Company and each of its
  directors and executive officers as of the Effective Date, replacing prior
  indemnification agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a Chapter 11 debt-to-equity recapitalization of a single
  continuing legal entity rather than an acquisition by, or combination with, a
  separate acquiring company: the same registrant, its charter (as amended), its
  operating subsidiaries, its reserve base, and its incumbent executive-officer team
  all continued unchanged through the Effective Date, while pre-Effective-Date equity
  and most pre-Effective-Date debt were cancelled and replaced with new equity,
  convertible notes, and warrants issued to former creditors, and a majority of the
  board of directors was replaced. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-733811, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 1.02, 3.02, 3.03, 5.01, 5.02, 5.03]
