## Documented facility-level event tied to a specific legacy-KapStone asset
- KapStone's own Form 10-K for fiscal 2017 identifies a containerboard mill in North
  Charleston, South Carolina as one of its four containerboard mills (acquired from
  MeadWestvaco Corporation in 2008), staffed by approximately 560 employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001015, Form 10-K FY2017]
- WestRock's Form 10-K for fiscal 2019 (the first full fiscal year after the merger)
  disclosed plans to "reconfigure" its North Charleston, SC mill beginning in fiscal
  2020, reducing linerboard capacity by approximately 288,000 tons and annual costs by
  approximately $40 million, including a multi-month workforce reduction. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-19-011534, Form 10-K FY2019]
- WestRock's Form 10-K for fiscal 2023 states that during fiscal 2023 the company
  "recorded various impairments and other charges associated with our decision to
  permanently cease operations at our Tacoma, WA and North Charleston, SC containerboard
  mills," which "ceased production in September 2023 and June 2023, respectively." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-064855, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Segment-level financial record (not KapStone-specific)

  reportable segment (the segment that includes former KapStone operations alongside
  pre-existing WestRock assets); the filing states this segment-level impairment
  "materially adversely affected" fiscal 2023 operating results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-064855, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- KapStone deregistered as a public reporting company on November 15, 2018 and filed no
  further standalone periodic reports; its financial results were folded into WestRock's
  combined Corrugated Packaging segment with no separate segment-level disclosure, so no
  standalone financial-performance scalar for the acquired business is publicly available
  for the ~2021-2023 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-18-007975, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-057778, Form 10-K FY2021]
- One specific, dated, documented event ties to a named legacy-KapStone asset within the
  fixed window: the North Charleston, SC containerboard mill — one of KapStone's four
  mills at the time of the 2018 transaction, already flagged for capacity reconfiguration
  in WestRock's first post-merger annual report — permanently ceased production in June
  2023, with associated impairment and restructuring charges recorded at the WestRock
  segment level. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-19-011534, Form 10-K FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-23-064855, Form 10-K FY2023]
