# Outcome sub-dossier — NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust realized record after combination, ~3-5-year horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Because NorthStar I merged into, and its separate
> corporate existence ended within, a newly formed combined company, there is no
> post-closing 10-K filed by NorthStar I itself reporting on its acquired investment
> business; this sub-dossier traces (a) what the surviving public company's own filings

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On February 1, 2018, Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc., acting "as successor by
  merger to NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust, Inc.," filed a Form 15-12G certifying
  termination of NorthStar I's registration and stating that "NorthStar Real Estate
  Income Trust, Inc. merged with and into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. on
  January 31, 2018, at which time the separate corporate existence of NorthStar Real
  Estate Income Trust, Inc. ended." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029425, Form 15-12G]
- No annual report was subsequently filed by NorthStar I reporting on its own acquired
  investment business; the entity that continued to report to the SEC on that business is
  the surviving combined company, under CIK 1717547. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001455650] [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0001717547]

## What the surviving company's later filings disclose about the merged business
- The surviving company's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 25, 2021, roughly
  three years after the January 2018 closing) reports results only on a fully consolidated
  basis, combining the assets contributed by Colony Capital with the businesses of
  NorthStar I and NorthStar II "beginning February 1, 2018, following the closing of the
  Combination," and states that "comparisons of the period to period financial
  information of the Company... may not be meaningful" as a result. [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- That filing contains no segment, division, or business-unit breakout attributable
  specifically to the former NorthStar I business or portfolio; its remaining references
  to NorthStar I are limited to (a) historical narrative describing the 2018 combination,
  (b) risk-factor language about NorthStar I's REIT-qualification status for periods
  before the merger, and (c) purchase-accounting notes recording the assets, liabilities
  and noncontrolling interests assumed from NorthStar I and NorthStar II at the 2018
  closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- SEC registrant records show the surviving company was named Colony NorthStar Credit
  Real Estate, Inc. at closing, was renamed Colony Credit Real Estate, Inc. in June 2018,
  and was renamed again to BrightSpire Capital, Inc. in June 2021; because these name
  changes attach to the combined successor entity as a whole (which also carries the
  Colony-contributed portfolio and the former NorthStar II business), they are not
  attributable to the former NorthStar I business specifically and are recorded here only
  as public-registrant context. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001717547.json]

## Realized record of the asset excluded from the merger (retained by legacy stockholders)
- A single loan excluded from the merger — held through N1 Liquidating Trust, formed
  under Maryland law and reporting under NorthStar I's former SEC file number — carried a
  junior participation interest with a carrying value of approximately $28.1 million as
  of December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000007, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-01]
- On September 25, 2020, citing the sustained COVID-19 pandemic impact on the shopping
  mall and retail industry collateralizing the loan, the Trust's board approved a
  discounted payoff of the $125.8 million principal loan for $33.0 million, and the
  Trust's management concluded it would recognize a non-cash impairment charge of
  approximately $23.5 million on the junior participation interest for the year ending
  December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000007, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-01]
- The discounted payoff closed October 13, 2020; the Trust's $85.15 million junior
  participation interest (its sole remaining asset) received $3.3 million from the payoff
  proceeds plus accrued interest, and the Trust estimated a final distribution of
  approximately $0.07 net per unit of beneficial interest to unitholders on or around
  October 31, 2020, representing "the final disbursement of the unitholders' interest in
  the Trust," with the Trust's existence expected to terminate shortly after. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000010, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-19]
- This retained-asset record pertains only to the single loan carved out of the merger
  for the benefit of legacy NorthStar I stockholders; it is a distinct economic unit from
  the CRE investment portfolio that merged into the surviving public company, which (per
  the section above) discloses no separate post-close business record of its own. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000004, Form 10-K FY2019 (N1 Liquidating Trust)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000010, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-19]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because NorthStar I's separate corporate existence ended at the January 2018 closing
  and its investment portfolio was combined with NorthStar II's and a Colony-contributed
  portfolio into one surviving public company, no public 3-5-year outcome record exists
  that is specific to the acquired NorthStar I investment business; the surviving
  company's later Form 10-K filings report only fully consolidated results across all
  three combined predecessor businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029425, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001717547-21-000010, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The one quantified, primary-sourced post-close record traceable to NorthStar I concerns
  not the acquired business but a single asset excluded from the merger and placed in a
  liquidating trust for legacy stockholders: that asset's carrying value fell from
  approximately $28.1 million (year-end 2019) to a discounted payoff of $33.0 million on
  a $125.8 million loan (with the Trust's junior share of proceeds only $3.3 million plus
  accrued interest), producing a roughly $23.5 million impairment and a final unitholder
  distribution of about $0.07 per unit before the Trust's October 2020 termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000007, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001455650-20-000010, Form 8-K dated 2020-10-19]
