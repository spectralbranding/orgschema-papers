# Structural sub-dossier — Paragon Offshore plc separation from Noble Corporation plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 11, 2014, the board of Noble Corporation plc approved the spin-off of Paragon
  Offshore plc — an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of Noble — by means of a pro rata
  distribution of 100% of Noble's interest in Paragon Offshore to Noble shareholders.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution ratio was one Paragon Offshore ordinary share for every three Noble
  ordinary shares held as of the July 23, 2014 record date; the distribution date was
  expected to be August 1, 2014, requiring no payment or share surrender by holders.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The registration of the securities to be distributed was made on a Form 10 (Form
  10-12B) under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Controlling intent / archetype at closing
- The stated rationale was to give each company a more focused strategy: Noble to move
  forward as a high specification and deepwater/ultra-deepwater drilling company, and
  Paragon Offshore to operate as a "pure-play global provider of standard specification
  offshore drilling rigs." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset/product: Noble's "standard specification drilling business" — a
  fleet consisting solely of standard specification rigs, "34 jackups and eight floaters
  (five drillships and three semisubmersibles)"; the business would include all of Noble's
  standard specification drilling business other than three rigs retained by Noble.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Value-capture model: Paragon contracts its rigs, related equipment and work crews to
  conduct oil-and-gas drilling and workover operations for exploration-and-production
  customers on a dayrate basis worldwide. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operating people/crews: as of March 31, 2014, approximately 2,700 employees of Noble
  and 1,400 contractors were dedicated to the operations; certain employees in the U.K.,
  Nigeria, Canada and Brazil are parties to collective bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Financing that accompanied the asset transfer: Paragon expected to incur a new Term
  Loan Facility and to issue senior debt securities as partial consideration for the
  standard specification assets acquired from Noble. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared corporate substrate that did NOT transfer intact
- The business historically received service and support functions from Noble, whose
  costs were allocated (by head count, services rendered, assets assigned) for the
  functional groups: information technology, legal services, accounting, tax
  administration, and treasury and corporate finance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The combined financial statements were "prepared on a stand-alone basis" derived from
  Noble's consolidated statements, and were stated to be not necessarily indicative of
  results had the Company been a separate stand-alone entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Risk disclosure: if Noble did not perform the transition services, Paragon "may not be
  able to operate our business effectively"; after the transition services agreement
  expired Paragon "will no longer have access to Noble's infrastructure, and we will need
  to establish our own," at substantial cost. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- The parties would enter into a master separation agreement identifying the assets
  transferred, liabilities assumed and contracts assigned, and providing indemnification
  obligations and ongoing commitments between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Additional agreements: an employee matters agreement (allocating employment,
  compensation, benefits liabilities) and a tax sharing agreement (allocating taxes and
  tax benefits, with Paragon generally indemnifying Noble for taxes attributable to its
  business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Two transition services agreements: one under which Noble would continue to provide
  interim corporate support services to Paragon (and Paragon to Noble); and one under
  which Paragon would provide rig-based and shore-based support services for Noble's
  continuing offshore Brazil operations through the term of the existing rig contracts.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A newly separated standard-specification drilling fleet (34 jackups, eight floaters)
  plus its dedicated operating crews crossed to a formerly wholly-owned subsidiary, while
  the shared corporate functions (IT, legal, accounting, tax, treasury/corporate finance)
  historically supplied by the parent did not transfer as an intact substrate and were
  bridged at closing by a master separation agreement, employee matters agreement, tax
  sharing agreement, and two transition services agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-266775, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
