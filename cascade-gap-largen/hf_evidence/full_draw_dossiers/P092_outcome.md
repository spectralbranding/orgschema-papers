## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant (Schweitzer-Mauduit International, Inc., ticker SWM) remained a public
  reporting company throughout the window and filed audited annual reports on Form 10-K for
  FY2016 (filed 2017-02-24), FY2017 (filed 2018-03-01), and FY2018 (filed 2019-03-01), so a
  public outcome record exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Realized segment record (best-documented outcome scalar)
- By FY2017 the registrant reported two segments — Advanced Materials & Structures ("AMS")
  and Engineered Papers ("EP") — with the AMS segment manufacturing resin-based products for
  specialty applications in filtration, infrastructure and construction, transportation and
  other end markets; the DelStar-branded business is reported within AMS rather than as a
  separate segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
- AMS segment net sales were $433.2 million for 2017 compared to $280.6 million for 2016;
  AMS segment operating profit was $48.5 million for the year ended December 31, 2017.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
- The registrant states the AMS increase was driven substantially by acquisitions rather
  than by the originally acquired business alone: AMS gross profit increased by $46.8 million
  primarily due to incremental gross profit from newly acquired operations, and in 2016 AMS
  benefited from $31.2 million in incremental gross profit from newly acquired operations.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
- Annual goodwill impairment tests performed on October 1, 2017 and 2016 did not indicate
  any impairment of goodwill; the acquired businesses were retained with no goodwill
  write-down and no divestiture disclosed in the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Continued consolidation and integration over the window
- The registrant continued a series of acquisitions building out AMS: it identifies DelStar,
  Argotec, and Conwed, plus two "bolt-on" acquisitions, as having been integrated into the
  segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
- In 2017 the registrant acquired Conwed Plastics LLC, a producer of specialty resin-based
  netting; it states Conwed's similarities to its existing netting production assets
  presented an opportunity to drive synergies through footprint rationalization, procurement,
  and organization, and that the acquired companies were "integrated into a more holistically
  aligned operating segment." [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Documented activity at the process / organization interface
- As part of its enterprise transformation and integration efforts related to its recently
  acquired companies, the registrant "re-branded the acquired companies and transitioned our
  AMS sales operations toward a more unified organization." [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
- The registrant reported impairment charges for certain equipment together with employee
  severance expenses associated with downsizing or restructuring activities during the
  period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Items noted as uncertain (not separately disclosed)
- The specific realized trajectory of the originally acquired DelStar business is not
  disclosed on a stand-alone basis: its results are consolidated within the AMS segment
  alongside subsequently acquired companies (Argotec, Conwed, and bolt-ons), so a
  DelStar-specific outcome scalar is not publicly observable and that aspect is coded
  uncertain. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant remained a public reporting company through the window; the acquired
  resin-based business was retained (no divestiture, no goodwill impairment on the FY2016 and
  FY2017 tests) and was folded into an expanding AMS segment (net sales $280.6M in 2016 to
  $433.2M in 2017; 2017 operating profit $48.5M) whose growth the registrant attributes
  substantially to further acquisitions, accompanied by re-branding, a transition toward a
  "more unified" AMS sales organization, and equipment impairment plus severance from
  restructuring activity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001000623-18-000044, Form 10-K FY2017]
