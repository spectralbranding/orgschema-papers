## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The entity remained an SEC-registered foreign private issuer throughout the outcome window, filing Form 20-F annual reports under CIK 1698514; the report covering fiscal year 2023 was filed April 30, 2024. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023 cover page]
- The Form 20-F covering fiscal year 2022 was not filed until December 29, 2023 — roughly eight months after its normal due date and with no separate Form 20-F filed for fiscal year 2021 in the interim — and its late filing generated additional SG&A costs in the following fiscal year "associated with the preparation and audit of our Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended December 31, 2022." [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, MD&A "Results of Operations"]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Total revenue was $876.7 million for the year ended December 31, 2021, $909.5 million for the year ended December 31, 2022 (+3.9%), and $1,145.9 million for the year ended December 31, 2023 (+26.0% year-over-year), driven by increased hydraulic fracturing and coiled tubing activity across MENA operating locations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, MD&A "Revenue" / segment tables]
- Net loss was $(64.6) million for the year ended December 31, 2021 and $(36.4) million for the year ended December 31, 2022; the Company returned to net income of $12.6 million for the year ended December 31, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Gross profit as a percentage of revenue was 0.3% (FY2021), 7.2% (FY2022), and 13.0% (FY2023). [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- During the window NESR's NPS subsidiary further acquired Sahara Petroleum Services Company S.A.E. ("SAPESCO," June 1, 2020) and specific oilfield service lines of Action Energy Company W.L.L. (May 5, 2021), and NESR separately acquired a minority stake in W. D. Von Gonten Engineering LLC (July 1, 2022) — additions to, not divestitures from, the combined business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, "Basis of this Annual Report"]

## Documented internal-control and disclosure events
- Management concluded that internal control over financial reporting and disclosure controls and procedures were not effective as of December 31, 2021, December 31, 2022, and December 31, 2023, due to material weaknesses, including that "Senior Management failed to set an appropriate tone at the top sufficient to ensure a culture of compliance with the Company's accounting, finance and internal control policies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, Item 15 "Controls and Procedures"]
- The Company's Annual Report on Form 20-F for the year ended December 31, 2022 disclosed a restatement of its previously issued audited financial statements as of and for the year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, "Risk Factors"]
- As of the FY2023 Form 20-F, the Company disclosed it was the subject of an ongoing SEC inquiry, stated it was fully cooperating, and stated it was "unable to predict when the SEC's inquiry will conclude or what the consequences may be." [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, "Risk Factors"]

## Trading/listing status
- NESR's ordinary shares and warrants were removed from listing on the Nasdaq Capital Market effective April 28, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, cover page footnote / "Nasdaq Delisting"]
- As of the filing date of the FY2023 Form 20-F, the Company's securities traded on the OTC Pink Market under the symbols "NESR" and "NESRW" on an "unsolicited only" basis; the Company stated it planned to seek relisting on Nasdaq "as soon as practicable" but could not assure that relisting would succeed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023, "Nasdaq Delisting"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The entity continued to operate and report publicly under the same registrant name and CIK throughout the window rather than being acquired or dissolved. Revenue grew across the window ($876.7M to $1,145.9M, FY2021-FY2023) and the Company moved from net losses in FY2021-FY2022 to modest net income in FY2023, while over the same period it disclosed persistent material weaknesses in internal control over financial reporting for all three years, a restatement of its FY2020 financial statements, an unresolved SEC inquiry, a roughly eight-month-late annual report for FY2022, and removal of its ordinary shares and warrants from Nasdaq listing in April 2023 (trading thereafter on the OTC Pink Market pending a stated intent to relist). [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-24-017128, Form 20-F FY2023]
