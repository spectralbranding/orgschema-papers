# Structural sub-dossier — Fidelity National Financial, Inc. Split-Off of Cannae Holdings, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Fidelity National Financial, Inc. ("FNF") maintained two tracking stocks, FNF Group
  common stock and FNFV Group common stock, since a June 30, 2014 recapitalization; at a
  special meeting of FNFV Group common stockholders held November 17, 2017, holders
  approved a redemption proposal under which FNF would redeem all outstanding shares of
  FNFV common stock for shares of a newly formed, wholly owned subsidiary, Cannae
  Holdings, Inc. ("Splitco"/"Cannae"), on a one-for-one basis (the "Split-Off"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the redemption, FNF contributed to Splitco all of the businesses, assets and
  liabilities attributed to the FNFV Group common stock (the "Splitco Assets and
  Liabilities"); all of FNF's core title insurance, real estate, technology and mortgage
  related businesses attributed to the FNF Group common stock that were not held by
  Splitco remained with FNF. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- The Split-Off was completed at 5:00 p.m. Eastern time on November 17, 2017; the
  Redemption Proposal passed with 51,926,906 votes for versus 65,324 against (59,009
  abstentions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-069547, Form 8-K]
- Cannae common stock began "regular-way" trading on the New York Stock Exchange under
  the symbol "CNNE" on November 20, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704720-22-000020, Form 10-K (Cannae Holdings, Inc., FY2021), "Company Background"]

## What crossed to Splitco at closing (the asset/investment tier)
- The businesses and investments contributed to Splitco primarily consisted of American
  Blue Ribbon Holdings, LLC ("ABRH"), Ceridian Holding, LLC ("Ceridian"), Triple Tree
  Holdings LLC, Wine Direct, Inc., Fidelity National Timber Resources, Inc., Fidelity
  National Environmental Solutions, LLC, Northern California Mortgage Fund, and the debt
  of Colt Defense, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- The closing 8-K confirms Cannae's businesses, assets and liabilities "primarily
  consist of those formerly attributed to the FNFV common stock, including Ceridian
  Holding, LLC, American Blue Ribbon Holdings, LLC and T-System Holding LLC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-069547, Form 8-K]
- Splitco was formed solely for the purpose of effecting the Split-Off and for
  controlling, operating or holding the FNFV Group's business and investments,
  including the portfolio companies; it intended to engage primarily in the business of
  managing and operating its controlled subsidiaries rather than in any single
  customer-facing product line of its own. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- FNF's core, customer-facing title insurance product (via subsidiaries including
  Fidelity National Title Insurance Company, Chicago Title Insurance Company,
  Commonwealth Land Title Insurance Company, Alamo Title Company and National Title
  Insurance of New York) was NOT part of the businesses contributed to Splitco and
  remained with FNF. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]

## Financial and ownership bridges established at closing
- In exchange for a $100.0 million cash contribution to Splitco, FNF or its subsidiaries
  received Splitco common stock representing approximately 8% of Splitco's outstanding
  shares immediately after the Split-Off, which FNF agreed to dispose of "as soon as a
  disposition is warranted" but in no event later than five years after the Split-Off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- In connection with the Split-Off, FNF extended a $100.0 million revolving line of
  credit to Splitco (the "revolver note"), replacing a prior $100.0 million line of
  credit FNF had extended to the FNFV Group upon formation of the tracking stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- At closing FNF and Cannae entered into a Reorganization Agreement (governing the
  principal corporate transactions effecting the Split-Off and the parties' ongoing
  relationship), a Tax Matters Agreement (governing respective rights and obligations
  regarding taxes, tax benefits, and tax return filings/audits), and a Voting Agreement
  (under which FNF agreed to vote any retained Cannae shares proportionally with other
  Cannae stockholders), each dated November 17, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-069547, Form 8-K]
- The deal-terms document also describes a corporate services agreement to be entered
  into between Splitco and FNF, under which FNF would provide Splitco certain "back
  office" services, including investor-relations assistance, for up to three years
  following the Split-Off (renewing automatically thereafter absent mutual termination). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]

## Management/personnel overlap at closing (controlling-principal continuity)
- William P. Foley, II, who had served as Chairman of FNF's board of directors since
  1984, was disclosed as a Splitco director who "also will serve on the boards of
  directors of FNF or its subsidiaries" after the Split-Off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
- Four of Splitco's executive officers — Brent B. Bickett, Richard L. Cox, Michael L.
  Gravelle and David Ducommun — were disclosed as also being employed by FNF or FNF's
  subsidiaries after the Split-Off, creating overlapping director and officer
  relationships between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle rationale disclosed at closing
- FNF stated the Split-Off was intended to enhance FNF's eligibility for inclusion in
  certain stock indices (tracking-stock structures generally being excluded from
  Standard & Poor's indices), reverting FNF to a single class of common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction separated FNF's non-insurance investment portfolio (restaurant,
  human-capital-management-software, and other minority/majority equity holdings) from
  FNF's core title insurance, real estate, technology and mortgage businesses by
  redeeming a tracking-stock class into a newly formed, separately listed holding
  company (Cannae Holdings, Inc.), with the two companies' corporate substrate (tax
  matters, back-office/investor-relations services, a revolving credit line, and a
  residual ~8% cross-ownership stake) bridged by a package of agreements rather than
  cleanly severed, and with the controlling individual (William P. Foley, II) and
  several senior officers retaining roles at both entities after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-006453, Form DEFM14A]
