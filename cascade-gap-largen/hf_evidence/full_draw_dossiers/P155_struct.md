# Structural sub-dossier — ATP Oil & Gas Corporation §363 asset sale to Bennu Oil & Gas, LLC (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing, described for what it establishes
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K dated December 13, 2012 (earliest event December 9, 2012)
  reporting Amendment No. 2 to ATP's Senior Secured Super Priority Priming Debtor in
  Possession Credit Agreement; it is a debtor-in-possession financing amendment, not the
  asset-sale terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-501946, 8-K Item 1.01]
- That amendment put in place "[n]umerous milestones ... for the sale process of the
  Company's shelf properties and deepwater properties and related assets, including the
  dissemination of marketing materials, approved bidding procedures, commencement of an
  auction process, and consummation of the sale process." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-501946, 8-K Item 1.01]

## Legal vehicle and parties at closing
- ATP filed a voluntary Chapter 11 petition on August 17, 2012 in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the Southern District of Texas, Case No. 12-36187; the asset sale was effected
  through that bankruptcy case under a court Sale Order. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-501946, 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01]
- On October 17, 2013 ATP obtained a Final Order approving the sale of certain assets free
  and clear of claims and liens and approving assumption and assignment of contracts and
  leases (Docket #2706). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The Sale Order approved an Asset Purchase Agreement dated June 20, 2013 by and among ATP
  and Credit Suisse AG (exclusively as administrative agent and collateral agent for the
  DIP Lenders) and Bennu Oil & Gas, LLC, which was designated as Buyer pursuant to a
  Joinder Agreement dated October 29, 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01 / Item 9.01 Exhibits 10.1, 10.2]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing
- ATP sold "substantially all of its operating shelf and deepwater properties and related
  assets," specifically the leases, oil and gas wells, production facilities, structures,
  equipment, permits, servitudes, hydrocarbons and accounts receivable, contracts, records,
  maps, data, intellectual properties, overriding royalty interests, and credits and
  payments attributable to the assets sold. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The buyer also assumed certain liabilities: the amount due by ATP's wholly owned
  subsidiary ATP Titan Holdco to Beal Bank; the net profits interests and overriding
  royalty interests associated with the acquired oil and gas properties; and selected
  payables and abandonment obligations of ATP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01]
- The exhibits accompanying the sale were the Asset Purchase Agreement, the Joinder
  Agreement, and the Sale Order; no transition-services, shared-services, or operating
  agreement bridging a shared substrate is listed among the filed sale exhibits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 9.01]

## People/management relative to the transferred assets
- On October 7, 2013 — days before the sale hearing — the Chief Operating Officer
  (George R. Morris) and the Chief Accounting Officer and Corporate Secretary
  (Keith R. Godwin) resigned to "accept positions with other exploration and production
  companies," effective October 18, 2013, "the day after the anticipated sale-hearing."
  The filings do not describe any transfer of ATP's management or workforce to the buyer
  alongside the assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-394254, 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a court-approved §363 sale in which a defined pool of oil-and-gas
  producing assets, facilities, data, and intellectual property was transferred free and
  clear to a buyer designated by the DIP lender group (Bennu Oil & Gas, LLC, joined
  through Credit Suisse AG as lender agent), with specified liabilities assumed, and with
  no filed transitional-services agreement and no described transfer of the seller's
  operating management to the buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-433589, 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-394254, 8-K Item 5.02]
