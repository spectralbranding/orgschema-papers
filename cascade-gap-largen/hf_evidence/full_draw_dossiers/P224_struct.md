# Structural sub-dossier — ONEOK, Inc. acquisition of the outstanding common units of ONEOK Partners, L.P. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 31, 2017, ONEOK, Inc., an Oklahoma corporation ("ONEOK"), New Holdings
  Subsidiary, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company ("Merger Sub"), ONEOK Partners,
  L.P., a Delaware limited partnership formed in 1993 ("ONEOK Partners"), and ONEOK
  Partners GP, L.L.C., the general partner of ONEOK Partners, entered into an Agreement
  and Plan of Merger under which ONEOK would acquire all outstanding common units of
  ONEOK Partners that it and its subsidiaries did not already own. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger Sub, a wholly owned subsidiary of ONEOK, was formed on January 31, 2017 solely
  to effect the merger and had not conducted any activities other than those incidental
  to its formation and the matters contemplated by the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger Sub was to merge with and into ONEOK Partners, with ONEOK Partners continuing
  as the surviving entity and a wholly owned subsidiary of ONEOK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding ONEOK Partners common unit not owned by ONEOK or its subsidiaries was
  to be converted into the right to receive 0.985 of a share of ONEOK common stock (the
  "merger consideration"), a fixed exchange ratio not adjusted for subsequent price
  changes; the aggregate value of the merger consideration was approximately $9.3
  billion based on the closing price of ONEOK common stock on January 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Units held by ONEOK Partners itself were to be automatically cancelled; units held by
  ONEOK Partners GP, ONEOK, or ONEOK subsidiaries (other than ONEOK Partners) were to
  remain outstanding, unaffected by the merger, as were the General Partner Percentage
  Interests and the Class B units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Following completion, former ONEOK Partners common unitholders were expected to own
  approximately 44.5% and existing ONEOK shareholders approximately 55.5% of the
  combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed and became effective on June 30, 2017; ONEOK acquired all 171.5
  million outstanding ONEOK Partners common units it did not already own. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220993, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]
- Approximately 98% of ONEOK Partners units voted (representing approximately 75% of
  outstanding units) approved the merger proposal; approximately 98% of ONEOK shares
  voted approved the related stock-issuance proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220993, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]

## Pre-existing control relationship and controlling intent
- Before the merger, ONEOK's interests in ONEOK Partners consisted of a 2% general
  partner interest (held through ONEOK's 100% ownership of ONEOK Partners GP) plus
  common and Class B unit holdings representing, in the aggregate, a 41.2% direct and
  indirect ownership interest in ONEOK Partners. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- ONEOK did not directly own any midstream operating assets; its revenue derived from
  its general and limited partner interests in ONEOK Partners, and substantially all of
  ONEOK's cash flows were generated from distributions ONEOK received from ONEOK
  Partners. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Because ONEOK controlled ONEOK Partners both before and after the merger, the change
  in ONEOK's ownership interest was accounted for as an equity transaction under ASC
  810, with no gain or loss recognized in ONEOK's consolidated income statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- ONEOK's stated goal in the relationship was "to provide management and resources to
  ONEOK Partners, enabling it to execute its growth strategies and allowing ONEOK to
  grow its dividend." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, ONEOK's president and chief executive officer stated the transaction
  reflected "our decision to create a larger, stand-alone operating company, resulting
  in solid dividend coverage and a lower cost of funding, enhancing our ability to
  execute on our long-term growth strategy as one of the country's leading midstream
  energy companies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220993, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]

## The customer-facing product/asset tier
- ONEOK Partners was described as one of the largest publicly traded master limited
  partnerships and a leader in the gathering, processing, storage and transportation of
  natural gas in the United States, and as owner of one of the nation's premier natural
  gas liquids ("NGL") systems, connecting NGL supply in the Mid-Continent, Permian and
  Rocky Mountain regions with key market centers, applying its capabilities across the
  value chain through vertical integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]

## Management/process tier: continuity across the ownership change
- Several named executive officers held identical executive titles at both ONEOK and
  ONEOK Partners GP before the merger (e.g., Walter S. Hulse III as Executive Vice
  President; Stephen W. Lake as Senior Vice President, General Counsel; Derek S.
  Reiners as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer; Sheppard F. Miers III
  as Vice President and Chief Accounting Officer; Wesley J. Christensen as Senior Vice
  President-Operations; Kevin L. Burdick as Executive Vice President-Chief Commercial
  Officer), and the filing states "[e]ach of these individuals will retain their
  position with ONEOK following the merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Several directors of ONEOK Partners GP (Julie H. Edwards, John W. Gibson, Steven J.
  Malcolm, Jim W. Mogg, Terry K. Spencer) were also directors of ONEOK before the
  merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- Proxy solicitation costs for the transaction were to be borne in part by "directors
  and officers of ONEOK and employees of ONEOK and its affiliates who provide services
  to ONEOK" and by "directors and officers of ONEOK Partners GP and employees of
  affiliates of ONEOK Partners who provide services to ONEOK Partners," language
  reflecting that operating personnel were provided to the partnership through
  affiliated ONEOK entities rather than employed directly at the ONEOK Partners level. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- ONEOK Partners common units, listed on the NYSE under the symbol "OKS," were, upon
  completion of the merger, to cease being listed on the NYSE and to be subsequently
  deregistered under the Exchange Act; ONEOK common stock (already listed on the NYSE
  under "OKE") was to be the surviving public equity, with approval to list the newly
  issued shares a condition to completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
- The closing press release confirmed that, as a result of the transaction, ONEOK
  Partners common units would no longer be publicly traded on the New York Stock
  Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-220993, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction converted a partial (41.2%) controlling ownership interest that ONEOK
  already held in ONEOK Partners — including its 2% general partner interest and a
  workforce and executive team already substantially shared between the two entities —
  into full ownership, exchanging all outstanding public common units for ONEOK common
  stock at a fixed 0.985 ratio and folding ONEOK Partners in as a wholly owned
  subsidiary; the named executive officers and several directors who already served
  both entities were retained in their same roles, and no separate transitional-services
  or shared-services agreement was documented in the merger proxy filing because the
  management/process substrate had not been organizationally separate from ONEOK's
  before the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176922, Form DEFM14A]
