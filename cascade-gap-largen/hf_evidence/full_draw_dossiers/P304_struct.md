# Structural sub-dossier — ONEOK, Inc. acquisition of the publicly held common units of ONEOK Partners, L.P. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- ONEOK, Inc. ("ONEOK"), a wholly owned merger subsidiary, ONEOK Partners, L.P. (the
  "Partnership"), and ONEOK Partners GP, L.L.C. (the Partnership's general partner)
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which the merger subsidiary would
  merge with and into the Partnership, with the Partnership surviving as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of ONEOK, and the Partnership's common unitholders (other than ONEOK and its
  subsidiaries) would receive 0.985 of a share of ONEOK common stock for each common
  unit held. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was an acquisition by ONEOK of the outstanding publicly held common
  units of the Partnership that ONEOK and its subsidiaries did not already own; before
  the merger ONEOK already beneficially owned approximately 19% of the Partnership's
  outstanding common units and 100% of its Class B units (41.2% of the Partnership in
  total as of a May 2017 record date), and ONEOK Partners GP — a wholly owned subsidiary
  of ONEOK — was already the Partnership's sole general partner. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- Because ONEOK already controlled the Partnership both before and after the merger, the
  transaction was accounted for as an equity transaction rather than a business
  combination, and ONEOK's historical consolidated financial statements already included
  the Partnership prior to the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- The aggregate value of the merger consideration, based on ONEOK's closing stock price
  on the last trading day before public announcement, was approximately $9.3 billion.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- Stated reasons for the transaction included simplifying ONEOK's corporate structure,
  eliminating potential conflicts of interest between ONEOK and the Partnership, and
  eliminating the Partnership's incentive distribution rights (IDRs) held by ONEOK,
  which management expected would lower the combined company's cost of funding relative
  to the Partnership continuing as a standalone growth-investment vehicle. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- At the time of the filing, the merger was expected to close in the second quarter or
  early in the third quarter of 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle: what continued unchanged
- Under the merger agreement, the merger subsidiary would merge with and into the
  Partnership; the merger subsidiary's separate existence would cease, and the
  Partnership would survive and continue to exist as a Delaware limited partnership (the
  "Surviving Entity"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, the Partnership's certificate of limited partnership and its
  partnership agreement, as in effect immediately before the merger, were to remain
  unchanged and continue as the organizational documents of the Surviving Entity, and the
  Partnership was to continue without dissolution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926,
  DEFM14A]

## Product/asset and business model
- The Partnership's common units were listed on the NYSE under the symbol "OKS"; the
  Partnership was described as one of the largest publicly traded master limited
  partnerships and a leader in the gathering, processing, storage and transportation of
  natural gas, and it also owned a natural gas liquids (NGL) system connecting NGL supply
  in the Mid-Continent, Permian and Rocky Mountain regions with market centers. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- ONEOK itself did not directly own any midstream operating assets; its main source of
  cash flow was described as the distributions it received from its general- and
  limited-partner interests in the Partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926,
  DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent / people and organization
- ONEOK Partners GP, the Partnership's sole general partner, was already a wholly owned
  subsidiary of ONEOK before the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926,
  DEFM14A]
- Several individuals held director and executive positions concurrently at both
  entities: Julie H. Edwards, John W. Gibson, Steven J. Malcolm, Jim W. Mogg and Terry K.
  Spencer, directors of ONEOK Partners GP, were also directors of ONEOK; Terry K. Spencer
  was President and Chief Executive Officer of both ONEOK and the Partnership, and Robert
  F. Martinovich was Executive Vice President and Chief Administrative Officer of both
  ONEOK and the Partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]

## Public-listing change at closing
- Upon completion of the merger, the Partnership's common units, then listed on the NYSE,
  were to cease to be listed on the NYSE and would subsequently be deregistered under the
  Securities Exchange Act of 1934. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- Approval of the merger required separate affirmative votes: a majority of ONEOK common
  stock voted at ONEOK's special meeting (plus a majority of outstanding ONEOK common
  stock for a related charter amendment), and a majority of the Partnership's outstanding
  common units (excluding units held by ONEOK and its affiliates for that vote, per the
  Partnership's disclosed voting mechanics) at the Partnership's special meeting. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]
- A conflicts committee of independent directors of ONEOK Partners GP's board reviewed
  the proposed exchange ratio and, together with the ONEOK Partners GP board, determined
  the merger to be fair and reasonable to, and in the best interests of, the Partnership
  and its common unitholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction converted ONEOK's pre-existing controlling and consolidating interest
  in the Partnership (already exercised through its wholly owned general partner and a
  41.2% economic interest) into full ownership, by merging a wholly owned merger
  subsidiary into the Partnership for stock consideration; the Partnership's legal
  vehicle (its Delaware limited-partnership certificate and partnership agreement) and
  its general-partner/executive personnel overlap with ONEOK were carried forward
  unchanged, while the Partnership's common units ceased to be separately listed and
  were deregistered from public trading. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-176926,
  DEFM14A]
