# Structural sub-dossier — TPG Pace Energy Holdings Corp. business combination with EnerVest-affiliated oil and gas entities (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- TPG Pace Energy Holdings Corp. ("TPGE"), through its direct wholly owned subsidiary
  TPG Pace Energy Parent LLC ("Pace LLC"), agreed to acquire oil and natural gas assets
  and a midstream interest from EnerVest-affiliated entities under three agreements: a
  Contribution and Merger Agreement for the Karnes County Assets (Eagle Ford Shale,
  South Texas), a Purchase and Sale Agreement for the Giddings Assets (Austin Chalk),
  and a Membership Interest Purchase Agreement for an approximate 35% interest in
  Ironwood Eagle Ford Midstream, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration: Giddings Sellers received approximately $308 million cash plus up to
  $47 million contingent cash through 2021; Karnes County Contributors received
  approximately $2.09 billion (minimum $610 million cash, remainder in stock) plus up to
  13,000,000 additional earnout shares over five years tied to EBITDA/free-cash-flow or
  stock-price thresholds; Ironwood Sellers received $25 million cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was proposed and negotiated as an "Up-C" structure in which the Karnes
  County Contributors could elect Class B Common Stock plus corresponding Pace LLC
  Units, redeemable for Class A Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the acquisition, TPGE (a blank-check company) "has no operations, has not
  generated any revenues and has no liabilities other than those incurred in connection
  with" its formation and the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Closing occurred July 31, 2018; the Company changed its name from TPG Pace Energy
  Holdings Corp. to Magnolia Oil & Gas Corporation on the Closing Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-239706, Form 8-K]
- At closing, TPGE's previously combined trading units ceased trading on the NYSE; the
  Class A Common Stock and warrants continued to be listed on the NYSE under new
  symbols "MGY" and "MGY WS." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-239706, Form 8-K]

## Asset/product tier — what crossed
- The Karnes County Assets, Giddings Assets, and the 35% Ironwood interest (collectively
  the "Target Assets") crossed into Pace LLC (renamed Magnolia LLC) as the acquired
  customer-facing production asset base. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]

## Process tier — bridged by a fixed-term services agreement, not carried by new hires
- Concurrently with Closing, the Company and EnerVest Operating, L.L.C. ("EVOC," an
  EnerVest affiliate) entered a Services Agreement under which EVOC, "under the
  direction of the Company's management," would provide services "identical to the
  services historically provided by EVOC in operating the Target Assets," including
  administrative, back-office, and day-to-day field-level services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the Services Agreement, EVOC was to provide approximately 100 dedicated
  operating, technical, and field-level employees and maintain shared services for
  certain corporate functions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration for the Services Agreement was a fixed annual services fee of
  approximately $23.5 million plus per-well overhead payments of approximately $7
  million initially; the term was five years, terminable by either party after two years,
  with a $17.5 million termination fee payable by the Company if it terminated early for
  unsatisfactory performance, and a nine-month EVOC transition-services period following
  any termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy's risk factors state plainly that "the Target Assets will be operated on a
  day-to-day basis by EVOC and its employees pursuant to the Services Agreement, and
  TPGE will not be involved in the day-to-day operations of the Target Assets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy also discloses that EV Energy Partners, L.P. — a separate entity for which
  EnerVest previously provided operating services — entered bankruptcy in April 2018,
  as context for the risk of relying on EVOC as contract operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]

## People/management tier — a newly constituted team, not the operating staff
- The post-combination company was to be led by Stephen Chazen (Chairman, President and
  CEO), who spent 22 years at Occidental Petroleum Corporation including as CEO from
  2011 to 2016, joined by Christopher Stavros as CFO, most recently Occidental's CFO from
  2014 to 2017 — neither previously affiliated with EnerVest or EVOC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy states explicitly that persons employed by EVOC "may provide services to the
  post-combination company through the Services Agreement," but "[n]one of the persons
  employed by EVOC will be executive officers of the post-combination company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- Under a Stockholder Agreement entered at Closing, the Karnes County Contributors were
  entitled to nominate two directors and the Sponsor (TPG Pace Energy Sponsor, LLC) was
  entitled to nominate two directors to the board, subject to ownership thresholds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]

## Additional contractual bridge and retained-seller economics
- A Non-Competition Agreement between the Company and EnerVest, Ltd. ("EV Ltd.")
  restricted EV Ltd. and affiliates from competing in the defined Market Area until the
  later of the four-year anniversary of Closing or termination of the Services Agreement,
  while granting EV Ltd. co-investment, right-of-first-offer, tag-along, and drag-along
  rights, plus up to 4,000,000 contingent Class A shares tied to stock-price thresholds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company's Second Amended and Restated Charter, adopted at Closing, increased
  authorized Class A shares from 200,000,000 to 1,300,000,000, created a new Class B
  Common Stock, shortened director terms, adopted Delaware as exclusive litigation forum,
  and eliminated blank-check-specific provisions, alongside the name change to Magnolia
  Oil & Gas Corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction moved the customer-facing oil and gas production asset base into the
  acquirer's legal vehicle (an Up-C structure via Pace LLC) at closing, but the
  operational processes that produce and deliver that asset's output were not carried
  over by new hires: they were bridged through a fixed-term, fee-based Services
  Agreement under which the seller's affiliated operator (EVOC) continued to supply the
  field-level workforce (~100 employees) "under the direction of" a newly constituted
  management team (Chazen/Stavros, both ex-Occidental, with no prior EnerVest/EVOC
  affiliation) that itself was not drawn from the Sellers' operating organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211420, Form DEFM14A]
