# Structural sub-dossier — NII Holdings, Inc. Chapter 11 §363 sale of Nextel Mexico to AT&T (closing structure)

## Nature of the drawn filing and the seller's bankruptcy (closing-time context)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material
  Definitive Agreement) and Item 7.01 (Regulation FD): on March 5, 2015 NII Holdings, Inc.
  ("NII"), together with certain U.S. and Luxembourg-domiciled subsidiaries (the "NII
  Debtors") that were debtors and debtors-in-possession in Chapter 11 cases pending in the
  U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York, together with holders of ~70%
  of the senior notes issued by NII Capital Corp. and ~72% of the senior notes issued by NII
  International Telecom S.C.A. (the "Consenting Noteholders") and the official committee of
  unsecured creditors, entered into a Plan Support Agreement ("PSA") governing the terms of
  a revised plan of reorganization (the "Plan"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Plan was to implement a consensual reorganization of the NII Debtors "following the
  completion of the proposed sale of NII's operations in Mexico pursuant to the Purchase and
  Sale Agreement dated January 26, 2015 among NII and certain of its subsidiaries and New
  Cingular Wireless Services, Inc., an indirect subsidiary of AT&T, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Plan provided for conversion of the senior notes into a combination of cash (a portion
  of the net proceeds from the sale of NII's Mexican operations) and equity interests in the
  reorganized NII, plus a settlement of certain estate claims and guarantee-release claims.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Creditors party to the Revised Plan Support Agreement agreed to provide $350 million in
  post-petition financing to fund the Company until it completed the sale of its operations
  in Mexico. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (news release, March 6, 2015)]
- The Company's operations in Mexico, Brazil and Argentina were not included in the pending
  bankruptcy proceedings and continued to operate in the ordinary course outside Chapter 11.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (news release, March 6, 2015)]

## The transaction in scope and its structure (deal-terms document)
- The transaction the Plan is anchored on is documented in a separate contemporaneous
  Form 8-K (Item 1.01): on January 26, 2015 NIU Holdings LLC ("Seller"), a subsidiary of
  NII, entered into a Purchase and Sale Agreement ("Purchase Agreement") with New Cingular
  Wireless Services, Inc. (an indirect subsidiary of AT&T, Inc.), NIHD Telecom Holdings B.V.,
  Nextel International (Uruguay) LLC ("Nextel Mexico Parent") and certain Seller guarantors,
  under which AT&T agreed to purchase all of the outstanding stock of Nextel Mexico Parent,
  the parent of Comunicaciones Nextel de México, S.A. de C.V. ("Nextel Mexico"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Transaction type as structured: a purchase of the STOCK of the parent holding company of
  the operating subsidiary (a going-concern equity sale), required to be conducted as an
  auction under section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code and subject to higher or better offers,
  with the auction conducted under bidding procedures to be approved by the Bankruptcy Court.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Consideration: a purchase price of approximately $1.875 billion in cash, subject to
  adjustment for agreed net indebtedness of Nextel Mexico at closing and for specified
  spending between signing and closing; the agreement was not subject to a financing
  condition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Escrow / risk-allocation mechanics: ~$32 million of the purchase price to be held in
  escrow to secure specified AT&T obligations; a further $187.5 million of the purchase price
  to be held in escrow for two years to secure Nextel Mexico's breaches of representations,
  warranties and covenants; a termination fee of ~$32 million plus expense reimbursement (up
  to $10 million) payable by Nextel Mexico Parent if AT&T was not the successful bidder.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — product, processes and organization
- Because the transaction was structured as a sale of all outstanding stock of the parent of
  the operating company, the operating subsidiary Nextel Mexico transferred as a going
  concern rather than as a set of carved-out assets. Nextel Mexico "provides wireless
  services in major Mexican business centers... as well as in small markets and along related
  transportation corridors throughout Mexico. Nextel Mexico is headquartered in Mexico City
  and has many regional offices throughout Mexico." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Bridging / preservation covenant at signing: pending consummation, Nextel Mexico agreed to
  (i) conduct its business in the ordinary course consistent with past practice and (ii) use
  commercially reasonable efforts to maintain and preserve intact its business organization
  and preserve intact certain business relationships and relationships with applicable
  regulatory authorities — i.e., the customer-facing product, the operating processes and the
  operating organization were to be held together and preserved within the transferred
  entity through closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Completion conditions included Bankruptcy Court orders, all required governmental
  approvals, and the absence of a material adverse effect on Nextel Mexico. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The full text of the Purchase and Sale Agreement was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the
  January 26, 2015 Form 8-K; the exhibit itself was not retrieved for this dossier, so any
  specific transitional-services / employee-transfer provisions within it are [UNVERIFIED].

## Consummation of the sale (closing)
- On April 30, 2015, NII, together with its wholly-owned subsidiary NIU Holdings LLC,
  completed the sale of the Company's Mexican operations to New Cingular Wireless, an indirect
  subsidiary of AT&T; "the transaction was structured as a sale of all the outstanding stock
  of the parent company of Comunicaciones Nextel de México, S.A. de C.V.... for the purchase
  price of approximately $1.875 billion, including $187.5 million deposited in escrow to
  satisfy potential indemnification claims." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-15-000011, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The net proceeds of the sale were $1.448 billion, after deducting Nextel Mexico's
  outstanding indebtedness net of cash and applying estimates of other specified purchase
  price adjustments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-15-000011, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Within a Chapter 11 proceeding, the divesting parent sold its Mexican wireless operating
  company as a going concern — structured as a §363 auction sale of all outstanding stock of
  the parent holding company of Comunicaciones Nextel de México — to an indirect AT&T
  subsidiary for ~$1.875 billion cash (net proceeds $1.448 billion), with $187.5 million held
  in a two-year indemnification escrow and a pre-closing covenant to preserve intact the
  operating organization and business relationships; the sale proceeds funded a
  creditor-supported plan of reorganization (conversion of senior notes into cash plus equity
  in the reorganized company) backed by $350 million of post-petition financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-000405, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-15-000011, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-15-001716, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / EX-99.1]
