# Structural sub-dossier — TerraForm Power, Inc. sponsor-change merger with a Brookfield affiliate (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction is a merger in which a merger subsidiary formed by affiliates of
  Brookfield Asset Management Inc. ("Merger Sub," a wholly-owned subsidiary of Orion US
  Holdings 1 L.P., "Brookfield Holdco") merges with and into TerraForm Power, Inc. (the
  "Company"), with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Before the transaction, SunEdison, Inc. (then in bankruptcy proceedings) indirectly
  held all of the Company's outstanding Class B common stock, giving SunEdison
  approximately 83.9% of the combined voting power of the Company's Class A common
  stock; a related settlement/exclusion agreement with SunEdison and a stand-alone
  conversion option are described as the mechanism for resolving SunEdison's equity
  position in connection with the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction agreement includes the exchange of Class B units of TerraForm Power,
  LLC and the redemption and retirement of all shares of the Company's Class B common
  stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Following closing, Brookfield Holdco is to hold a 51% interest in the Company, subject
  to a potential adjustment tied to resolution of certain pending litigation involving
  the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Public stockholders are given an election for each share of Class A common stock: a
  per-share cash consideration, or retention of one share of Class A common stock of the
  surviving corporation (the per-share stock consideration). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The Company's shares are stated to remain listed on Nasdaq under the symbol "TERP"
  and are stated as not being deregistered under the Exchange Act as a result of the
  transaction — i.e., the public reporting entity and its Nasdaq listing continue rather
  than being absorbed into a private acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Board composition changes at closing: the post-closing board of directors is to
  consist of seven members, of whom four are appointed by Brookfield Holdco and three
  are drawn from the Company's current independent directors as agreed between
  Brookfield Holdco and the Company's existing conflicts committee; a new conflicts
  committee and a new nominating and corporate governance committee are established.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the transaction, the Company's chief executive officer, former chief
  operating officer, and former interim chief accounting officer were employees of
  SunEdison, with their services provided to the Company under an existing management
  services agreement and project-level asset management and operations-and-maintenance
  agreements with SunEdison and its affiliates (disclosed as a related-party
  arrangement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- At and after closing, the people/organization function providing top management is
  restructured under a new master services agreement (part of a set of "Sponsorship
  Agreements"): Brookfield affiliates providing services will arrange for qualified
  personnel, including providing the Company's chief executive officer, chief financial
  officer, and general counsel, each of whom will be dedicated on a full-time basis with
  their primary responsibility being service to the Company and its subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- As consideration under the master services agreement, the Company will pay a base
  management fee (a fixed component of $2.5 million per quarter for each of the first
  four quarters following the closing date); the agreement has no fixed term and either
  side may terminate it without a termination fee upon specified events. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]

## Bridging / transitional arrangements at closing
- Brookfield is to provide the Company with a $500 million revolving senior credit
  facility secured by a pledge of all of the Company's assets (the "sponsor line
  agreement"), intended to give the Company a lower medium/long-term cost of capital and
  a revolving line of credit for acquisitions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
- Brookfield is to give the Company a right of first offer on wind and solar asset
  acquisition opportunities in North America and Western Europe from Brookfield's
  development/acquisition pipeline, along with a quarterly report on operating solar and
  wind assets acquired by Brookfield in the territory and why the Company was not
  offered such opportunities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, the customer-facing renewable-energy-generation business and the
  Company's public legal vehicle and Nasdaq listing continued intact and were not
  absorbed into the acquiring group; what changed was the controlling equity position
  (from SunEdison's indirect majority-voting Class B position, redeemed and retired, to
  a 51% Brookfield Holdco interest with board control of four of seven seats) and the
  provider of top management and certain operating/financial support (from SunEdison,
  under a prior management services and O&M arrangement, to Brookfield affiliates, under
  a new master services agreement, a sponsor credit line, and a pipeline right of first
  offer). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001844, Form DEFM14A]
