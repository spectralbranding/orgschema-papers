# Structural sub-dossier — Hilton Grand Vacations / BRE Ace LLC transaction (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 18, 2017, HRC Islander LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an
  indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Hilton Grand Vacations Inc. ("HGV"), entered into
  an Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "Joint Venture
  Agreement") of BRE Ace LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, with BRE Ace Holdings
  LLC, a Delaware limited liability company and an affiliate of the real estate
  investment fund commonly known as Blackstone Real Estate Partners VIII. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- HRC Islander contributed approximately $40.0 million in cash for a 25% interest in the
  new company; BRE Ace Holdings held the remaining interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The full Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company Agreement of BRE Ace LLC, dated
  July 18, 2017, was filed as Exhibit 10.1 to the Current Report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 9.01]

## The asset that came under the new entity's ownership
- The new company owns, through one or more subsidiaries, the remaining unsold timeshare
  inventory in a 1,201-key timeshare resort property and related operations, commonly
  known as "Elara, by Hilton Grand Vacations," together with remaining commercial and
  penthouse space, an undeveloped land parcel at 80 East Harmon Avenue, Las Vegas,
  Nevada, and certain promissory notes/receivables related to timeshare-interest sale
  financings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- In May 2017 the new company acquired 100% of the direct and indirect interests in the
  entities that own the Property from its prior owner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Governance / managing authority as structured at closing
- BRE Ace Holdings, as the sole managing member, has exclusive and sole power and
  authority over the management of the new company, including day-to-day business and
  affairs, subject to HGV's approval on certain major actions (e.g., certain affiliate
  transactions or acquiring unrelated real property). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- BRE Ace Holdings, as sole managing member, has sole authority to require HGV to make
  additional capital contributions; failure to fund may result in dilution of HGV's
  interest or a loan bearing interest at the lesser of 20% or the maximum lawful rate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- HGV may not transfer its interest without BRE Ace Holdings' prior approval (except to
  wholly-owned HGV affiliates); the agreement includes rights of first offer and
  drag-along and tag-along rights on transfers by BRE Ace Holdings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Operating / delivery processes and the contractual substrate at closing
- HGV, through its subsidiaries, had managed the Property on a fee-for-service basis since
  2012, i.e., before the entity was restructured. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At closing HGV will continue to market, sell inventory and manage the Property under the
  existing fee-for-service agreements it had entered into with the prior owner of the
  Property; the marketing/sales/management operation therefore stays with HGV by contract
  rather than moving into the ownership entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Property is subject to a secured financing of approximately $211 million, none of
  which HGV agreed to guarantee at closing; future financings may, at BRE Ace Holdings'
  option, obligate HGV to provide a guaranty/indemnity limited to its capital-sharing
  percentage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A license agreement between HGV and Hilton Worldwide Holdings Inc. governs the branded
  "Elara, by Hilton Grand Vacations" identity; the Joint Venture Agreement restricts
  transfers by BRE Ace Holdings that would make HGV an affiliate of a Hilton competitor
  and thereby breach that license. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Affiliates of The Blackstone Group L.P. then owned approximately 5% of HGV's outstanding
  common stock, and one HGV director, Kenneth A. Caplan, was an employee of The Blackstone
  Group L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Ownership of the customer-facing product — the Elara timeshare resort's remaining
  inventory, related real estate and receivables — was consolidated into a Delaware LLC
  controlled by the Blackstone-affiliated party as sole managing member, with HGV taking a
  25% minority interest for ~$40 million and holding consent rights on major actions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The operation that produces and delivers that product (marketing, inventory sales and
  property management) did not move into the ownership entity; it remained with HGV under
  pre-existing fee-for-service agreements, bridging the shared operating substrate by
  contract at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-232892, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
