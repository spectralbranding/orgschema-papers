## Public-reporting status in the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The entity continued to exist and to file annual reports as an independent public company; it filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended October 2, 2020, describing itself as "a leading innovator, designer and manufacturer of X-ray tubes, digital detectors" and related X-ray imaging components. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020 (fiscal year ended 2020-10-02)]
- As of the FY2020 annual report the company reported approximately 2,000 full-time employees, located at manufacturing and service-center sites in North America, Europe, and Asia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The company reported two operating and reportable segments, Medical and Industrial, with Medical representing approximately 79% and Industrial approximately 21% of total revenues in FY2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Realized financial record (primary-sourced)
- For fiscal year 2020 the company reported a net loss attributable to Varex of $(57.9) million and basic loss per share of $(1.49), compared with net earnings attributable to Varex of $15.5 million in fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Revenues were $738.3 million in FY2020, down from $780.6 million in FY2019 and $773.4 million in FY2018; gross profit fell to $190.2 million in FY2020 from $256.7 million in FY2019, and the loss before taxes was $(72.6) million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- In the third quarter of fiscal year 2020 the company took an approximately $15.8 million pre-tax charge for the write-down of associated inventory and restructuring activity, impaired $2.8 million of intangible assets, and wrote off a $2.7 million cost investment in a privately held company; these actions followed reduced demand and a decision "to discontinue certain low margin, low demand products." [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The company attributed a significant part of the FY2020 deterioration to the COVID-19 pandemic, which it stated "has had a significant effect on hospitals, clinics and outpatient imaging centers as they have encountered declines in surgeries and other non-emergency" procedures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented facts bearing on product/process and process/organization coherence over time
- On the annual goodwill impairment test performed for FY2020, FY2019 and FY2018 the company found no impairment for either of its two reporting units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Medical-segment revenues decreased approximately $12.3 million, which the company attributed primarily to decreased sales of X-ray tubes and digital detectors for oncology, dental, mammography and fluoroscopic applications, partly offset by increased OEM X-ray tube sales for CT applications and a full-year contribution from an April 2019 acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 losses and charges were tied to demand and product-portfolio decisions (inventory write-down, restructuring, discontinuation of low-margin/low-demand products) rather than to any disclosed goodwill impairment or divestiture of the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed outcome window (FY2020 annual report), the entity remained an operating, independent public company (approximately 2,000 full-time employees; Medical and Industrial segments), but recorded its first reported annual net loss of the period examined — a net loss attributable to Varex of $(57.9) million on revenues of $738.3 million — driven by pandemic-related demand declines and a Q3 FY2020 inventory write-down, restructuring charge, and product-line discontinuation, while its annual goodwill impairment test found no impairment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681622-20-000116, Form 10-K FY2020]
