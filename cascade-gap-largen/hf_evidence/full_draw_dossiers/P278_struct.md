# Structural sub-dossier — DST Systems, Inc. acquisition by SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing

- DST Systems, Inc. ("DST"), a Delaware corporation, entered an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger dated January 11, 2018 with SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. ("SS&C") and
  Diamond Merger Sub, Inc., an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of SS&C ("Merger Sub").
  Merger Sub was to merge with and into DST, with DST surviving as a wholly owned
  indirect subsidiary of SS&C. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]
- Consideration: each outstanding share of DST common stock (other than excluded
  shares) converts into the right to receive $84.00 per share in cash, an approximate
  39-40% premium over DST's closing stock price in the days immediately preceding the
  final offer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]
- SS&C's later Form 10-K lists the DST acquisition, closed April 16, 2018, at a total
  transaction value of $5,400.0 million, described as providing "additional scale and
  breadth across institutional and retail asset management, alternatives, wealth
  management, and healthcare sectors." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002107, SS&C
  Technologies Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021), Item 1 acquisition-history table]
- The merger was not conditioned on SS&C obtaining financing; SS&C had a debt
  commitment letter for $7.123 billion of senior secured loans plus intended proceeds
  of $1.250 billion from notes/equity issuance or a bridge facility. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]

## Business models of the two parties as described in the proxy

- DST is described as "a leading provider of specialized technology, strategic
  advisory, and business operations outsourcing to the financial and healthcare
  industries," with segments at signing comprising Domestic Financial Services,
  International Financial Services, and Healthcare Services. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]
- SS&C is described as "a leading provider of mission-critical, sophisticated software
  products and software-enabled services" automating front-, middle- and back-office
  functions for financial services providers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059,
  DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing and what did not

- The whole legal entity (DST and its subsidiaries) crossed as a going concern via the
  reverse-triangular merger; no operating business, product line, or segment of DST was
  carved out or excluded from the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059,
  DEFM14A]
- The document contains no transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or
  supply agreement bridging DST's operations to a retained parent, consistent with a
  whole-company cash acquisition rather than a carve-out or spin-off; pre-closing
  covenants instead required DST to operate "in the ordinary course of business
  consistent with past practice" and to "preserve intact its present business
  organization" through closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A,
  merger agreement Section 5.01]
- A retention plan for management and certain DST employees was contemplated to "help
  assure the continuity of management" through closing. DST's then-CEO and another
  senior executive each entered separation agreements terminating their employment for
  "good reason" at the merger's effective time, with cash severance and cash-out of
  equity awards at closing; other executive officers' equity awards instead converted
  into equivalent SS&C awards continuing to vest post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]

## Regulatory and process conditions to closing

- Completion required clearance under the U.S. Hart-Scott-Rodino Act (notifications
  filed February 1, 2018), Ireland's competition law, and approvals from FINRA, the UK
  Financial Conduct Authority, the Central Bank of Ireland, and Luxembourg's Commission
  de Surveillance du Secteur Financier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059,
  DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement set a termination fee of 3.5% of enterprise value (~3.9% of
  equity value) and required DST to cooperate with SS&C to have DST common stock
  removed from NYSE listing and deregistered under the Exchange Act within 10 days of
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A, merger agreement Section
  6.15]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- The transaction was structured as a whole-company cash-out merger in which DST's
  entire legal entity, all reported segments, and its executive/management structure
  (subject to negotiated severance/retention/equity-conversion terms) crossed into
  SS&C's ownership together, with no carve-out and no transition-services agreement;
  the deal required antitrust and financial-regulatory clearances across the U.S.,
  Ireland, the UK, and Luxembourg before it could close. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001144204-18-011059, DEFM14A]
