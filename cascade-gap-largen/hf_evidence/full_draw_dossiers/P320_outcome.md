## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The entity remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company throughout
  the outcome window, filing under the same SEC CIK; it filed a Form 10-K for fiscal year
  2020 (filed February 18, 2021) and a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 17,
  2022), both under the registrant name "Syneos Health, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page]
- The FY2020 Form 10-K states: "We were established as INC Research in 1998... We
  changed our name to Syneos Health, Inc. after our 2017 merger (the 'Merger') with
  Double Eagle Parent, Inc. ('inVentiv'), the parent company of inVentiv Health, Inc." —
  i.e., the registrant's corporate identity, not merely its ticker, changed following the
  transaction, with continuing SEC registration and CIK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1 "Business"]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Total revenue was $4,415,777 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2020, a decrease
  of $260,038 thousand (5.6%) from $4,675,815 thousand in 2019 (which had itself been up
  6.5% from $4,390,116 thousand in 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income was $192,787 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2020, versus
  $131,258 thousand in 2019 and $24,284 thousand in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Total revenue was $5,212,970 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income was $234,831 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2021, versus
  $192,787 thousand in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- "Transaction and integration-related expenses" — a separately reported income-statement
  line tied to the 2017 combination and subsequent acquisitions — were $30,242 thousand
  in 2020, $61,275 thousand in 2019, and $64,841 thousand in 2018; the FY2021 10-K states
  that beginning in 2021 this expense category was "no longer reported separately and
  are included in selling, general, and administrative expenses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, MD&A "Results of Operations"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- As of the annual goodwill impairment test performed October 1, 2020 (goodwill assigned
  to five reporting units, "principally related to the acquisition of inVentiv completed
  in August 2017"), the company concluded there were no impairments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 7A / Note "Goodwill"]

## Documented events over time
- The company disclosed that the COVID-19 pandemic "adversely impacted our business and
  results of operations" and that it experienced "pronounced disruptions in our
  operations during the year ended December 31, 2020, the largest of which occurred
  during the second and third quarters of 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1A / MD&A]
- Two putative securities class actions were filed against the company and certain
  officers (including the former INC Research CEO and former inVentiv CEO) alleging that
  the merger proxy statement contained inaccurate or incomplete information regarding
  inVentiv's financial performance and the combined company's business outlook; one
  action (Bermudez v. INC Research, Inc.) was voluntarily dismissed in March 2018, while
  the other (the consolidated Vaitkuviene action, E.D.N.C.) remained pending as of the
  FY2020 10-K, with a magistrate judge recommending in August 2020 that the defendants'
  motion to dismiss be denied and the defendants filing objections to that recommendation
  in September 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, "Legal Proceedings"]
- Revenue attribution by customer segment shifted over the window: large biopharmaceutical
  companies represented approximately 55% of total revenue in 2020 versus approximately
  49% in 2021 (small/mid-sized biopharmaceutical customers rising from approximately 45%
  to approximately 51% of revenue over the same comparison). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 1]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The entity continued to operate and report publicly under its post-merger name
  (Syneos Health, Inc.) throughout the outcome window rather than being acquired,
  removed from listing, or dissolved. Its realized financial trajectory showed revenue
  declining 5.6% in 2020 versus 2019 before rising to $5,212,970 thousand in 2021 (a
  year-over-year increase), while net income rose in both years reviewed ($131,258
  thousand in 2019 to $192,787 thousand in 2020 to $234,831 thousand in 2021); no
  goodwill impairment was recorded against the goodwill principally attributable to the
  2017 combination. Alongside that financial trajectory, a securities class action
  alleging merger-proxy misstatements about the combined company's outlook remained
  unresolved through the FY2020 filing, and a separately tracked "transaction and
  integration-related expenses" line item persisted at material levels ($30-65 million
  per year) through 2020 before being folded into general SG&A reporting starting in
  2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-006471, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-22-005428, Form 10-K FY2021]
