## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following the merger's effective time, Energen's common stock ceased to fulfill NYSE
  listing requirements and was removed from listing; Energen's obligation to file
  periodic reports with the SEC under the Exchange Act ended at that point. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29 (Item 3.01)]
- The acquirer subsequently filed a Form 15-12B to formally terminate registration of
  Energen's common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-346260, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by Energen Corporation (CIK
  0000277595) since its final annual report covering fiscal year 2017; a search of the
  registrant's SEC filing history shows no subsequent 10-K, 20-F, or comparable annual
  report. [SRC: SEC accession 0000277595-18-000014, Form 10-K (FY2017, filed 2018-02-28) — last annual report on file]

## Acquirer's subsequent disclosure of the acquired business (feasibility-relevant)
- The acquirer, Diamondback Energy, Inc., does not report segment-level or
  business-unit-level financial results for the acquired Energen operations in its own
  subsequent annual reports; a full-text check of Diamondback's Form 10-K for fiscal
  year 2021 (filed within the outcome window) found no discussion of "Energen" as a
  distinguishable ongoing business unit, and the sole reference to "Energen" in that
  filing concerns Diamondback's 2021 redemption of senior notes that had originally been
  issued by Energen and assumed in the merger — a financing-history note, not a
  business-performance disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001539838-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- No separate divestiture, impairment, or discontinued-operations disclosure specific to
  the acquired Energen assets was located in Diamondback's fiscal-year-2021 Form 10-K;
  the acquired Permian Basin assets appear to have been fully consolidated into
  Diamondback's single reporting entity without business-unit-level public disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001539838-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Energen was absorbed whole into its acquirer and deregistered under the
  Exchange Act shortly after closing, there is no public, business-unit-level 3-5-year
  outcome record for the acquired operations: Energen itself filed no further periodic
  reports, and the acquirer's own subsequent Form 10-K filings within the outcome window
  do not separately disclose the acquired business's financial or operating trajectory,
  reporting it as part of a single consolidated entity. The realized outcome for this
  case is therefore not publicly observable at the business-unit level and is coded
  uncertain rather than inferred from the acquirer's consolidated results. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339277, 8-K dated 2018-11-29] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-346260, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0001539838-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
