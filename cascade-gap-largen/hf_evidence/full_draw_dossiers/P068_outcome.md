## Public-reporting status (feasibility of an outcome record)
- The registrant (Sotheby's, CIK 823094) continued filing annual reports on EDGAR through
  the outcome window; its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2016 was filed
  on 2017-02-27, so a public realized record exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]

## Realized ownership and consolidation status
- As of the FY2016 report, Sotheby's (Beijing) Auction Co., Ltd. ("Sotheby's Beijing") —
  the joint venture formed in September 2012 — remained a consolidated entity in which the
  registrant held a controlling 80% ownership interest; the 20% non-controlling interest
  continued to be reported within equity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]
- No divestiture, put-option exercise, dissolution, or wind-up of Sotheby's Beijing is
  disclosed in the FY2016 report; the venture was retained on the same 80/20 basis through
  the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]

## Best-documented realized scalar (venture-specific)
- Sotheby's Beijing is the registrant's only consolidated entity with a non-controlling
  interest, so the reported "Net (Loss) Income Attributable to Noncontrolling Interest"
  isolates 20% of the venture's result. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]
- Net (loss) income attributable to the non-controlling interest was $(73) thousand in
  2016, $(246) thousand in 2015, and $197 thousand in 2014 — a swing from a small share of
  profit in 2014 to small shares of loss in 2015 and 2016 (i.e., the venture at 100%
  implies roughly $985K income in 2014, $1.23M loss in 2015, and $365K loss in 2016).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]

## Realized scalar (registrant-wide, for context)
- Net income attributable to Sotheby's was $74.112 million (2016), $43.727 million (2015),
  and $117.795 million (2014); diluted EPS was $1.27, $0.63, and $1.68 respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]
- Total revenues were $805.377 million (2016), $961.494 million (2015), and $938.053
  million (2014); the registrant stated that after the expansion of the global art market,
  fourth-quarter 2015 and full-year 2016 auction results indicated the market had "entered
  a period of lower sales." [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]
- Revenue attributable to "Hong Kong and China" was $145.885 million (2016), $146.262
  million (2015), and $165.066 million (2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]

- No impairment, write-down, or disruption specifically attributed to Sotheby's Beijing is
  disclosed in the FY2016 report. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K for FY2016]
