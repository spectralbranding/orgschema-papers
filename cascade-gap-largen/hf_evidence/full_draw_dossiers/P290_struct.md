# Structural sub-dossier — CA, Inc. acquisition by Broadcom Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties as structured
- On July 11, 2018, CA, Inc. ("CA"), a Delaware corporation headquartered in New York
  City, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Broadcom Inc. ("Broadcom"), a
  Delaware corporation headquartered in San Jose, California, and Collie Acquisition
  Corp. ("Merger Sub"), a Delaware corporation formed by Broadcom solely for the purpose
  of entering into the merger agreement and completing the transactions it contemplates.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub will merge with and into CA, with CA continuing
  as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Broadcom; upon
  completion, Merger Sub will cease to exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- Each issued and outstanding share of CA common stock (subject to customary exclusions)
  will be converted into the right to receive $44.50 in cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- CA is described as "a global leader of software solutions, including enterprise
  solutions and mainframe solutions," whose solutions "enable customers to plan, develop,
  automate, manage and secure applications across mobile, cloud, distributed and
  mainframe platforms." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- Broadcom is described as "a leading designer, developer and global supplier of a broad
  range of digital and analog semiconductor connectivity solutions," serving wired
  infrastructure, wireless communications, enterprise storage and industrial end markets.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provides for a termination fee of $566 million, payable by CA
  under specified circumstances, representing approximately 3% of CA's equity value as
  agreed in the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger is not conditioned on any financing arrangement or contingency; Broadcom
  stated the funds needed to pay the merger consideration and related indebtedness/fees
  would be funded through cash on hand and new third-party debt financing, backed by a
  debt commitment letter for senior unsecured term facilities of approximately $18
  billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing and what did not
- CA's common stock will cease to represent equity in CA; upon completion of the merger,
  CA common stock will be removed from listing on the NASDAQ Global Select Market and its
  registration under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934 will be terminated, ending CA's
  status as an independent publicly traded company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties agreed to cooperate to take all actions necessary to remove CA common stock
  from NASDAQ listing and terminate its Exchange Act registration, provided that such
  removal and termination would not be effective until at or after the effective time of
  the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to closing, CA agreed to use reasonable best efforts to obtain resignations from
  each of its then-sitting directors, effective upon the effective time, and to cooperate
  with Broadcom in preparing for the replacement of CA subsidiaries' directors and
  officers with persons designated by Broadcom. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- Broadcom agreed to assume, honor and fulfill all CA benefit plans in accordance with
  their terms, and, for a period of twelve months after the effective time, to provide
  each continuing employee at least the same base wage/salary, incentive-compensation
  opportunities and health/welfare benefits (excluding severance), and severance benefits
  no less favorable than those in effect immediately before the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- For benefit-plan purposes (vesting, eligibility, level of benefits) after the effective
  time, continuing employees are to be credited with their years of prior service with CA
  and its subsidiaries, and are to be immediately eligible to participate in Broadcom's
  post-closing benefit plans without a waiting period, subject to specified conditions.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
- CA separately maintains a pre-existing "CIC severance policy" (initially approved by
  the CA board in October 2004, most recently amended August 8, 2018) under which named
  executive officers are eligible for cash severance (calculated as a multiple of base
  salary plus bonus amount, 2.99x for three named executives and 2.00x for others named)
  upon a qualifying termination within two years following the change in control that the
  merger constitutes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction is a cash-for-stock reverse triangular merger in which a
  Broadcom-formed acquisition vehicle merges into CA, making the entire CA corporate
  entity (including its NASDAQ-listed public status, board, and equity capitalization) a
  wholly owned Broadcom subsidiary at closing; governance control (board composition,
  officer designations at CA's subsidiaries) is contractually reserved to Broadcom
  effective at the merger's effective time, while operational continuity for CA's
  rank-and-file workforce (compensation, benefits, service credit) is contractually
  bridged for a fixed twelve-month post-closing period rather than left unaddressed.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-246044, Form DEFM14A]
