# Structural sub-dossier — Atwood Oceanics, Inc. merger with Ensco plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Ensco plc, Echo Merger Sub LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Ensco, "Merger Sub") and
  Atwood Oceanics, Inc. entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated May 29, 2017,
  under which Merger Sub would merge with and into Atwood, with Atwood continuing as the
  surviving company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Ensco. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration was all-stock and fixed: each outstanding share of Atwood common stock
  (par value $1.00) was to be converted into the right to receive 1.60 Ensco Class A
  ordinary shares (nominal value $0.10); the exchange ratio does not adjust for
  fluctuations in either company's stock price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- The Ensco shares issuable in the transaction were registered on a Form S-4 (File No.
  333-218808) declared effective August 18, 2017, of which the joint proxy statement/
  prospectus (the drawn DEFM14A) forms a part. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]
- Immediately after closing, former Ensco and Atwood shareholders were expected to hold
  approximately 69% and 31%, respectively, of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- Atwood would owe Ensco a $30 million termination fee, and Ensco would owe Atwood a $50
  million termination fee, if the merger agreement were terminated under specified
  circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger Sub merged with and into Atwood at 7:00 a.m. Houston time on October 6, 2017
  (the Effective Time), with Atwood continuing as the surviving corporation and an
  indirect, wholly owned subsidiary of Ensco. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]

## What Atwood comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Atwood was described as a global offshore drilling contractor engaged in drilling and
  completion of exploratory and developmental oil and gas wells, owning a fleet of 10
  mobile offshore drilling units located in the Mediterranean Sea, offshore West Africa,
  offshore Southeast Asia and offshore Australia, and employing 938 personnel (as of the
  year ended September 30, 2016); Atwood had recently executed a sale-and-recycling
  agreement for one of its drilling units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- Ensco separately owned and operated an offshore drilling rig fleet of 53 rigs, some of
  which were under construction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties described the strategic rationale as creating "a leading global offshore
  drilling company given the complementary fleet composition, geographic scope and
  customer bases of the two companies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- The pro forma financial presentation included an adjustment for "additional Ensco
  interest capitalized from acquiring rigs under construction," indicating the acquired
  asset base included Atwood newbuild rigs not yet delivered at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a carved-out
  operating substrate was identified in the reviewed filings; the transaction combined
  two complete, already-operating drilling businesses rather than separating a business
  unit from a parent. [UNVERIFIED]

## Continuity of the controlling/management tier at closing
- At the Effective Time, Atwood's directors and executive officers ceased serving in
  those capacities, and the officers of Merger Sub immediately prior to the Effective
  Time became the directors and officers of Atwood (by then a wholly owned subsidiary).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- Two individuals who had been members of Atwood's board before the transaction, Jack E.
  Golden and Phil D. Wedemeyer, were appointed as directors of Ensco (the parent) on
  October 6, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- Each of Atwood's executive officers was subject to a change-of-control agreement
  providing cash severance and other benefits upon a qualifying termination within 24
  months of a change of control (a "double-trigger" arrangement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]
- Ensco agreed to honor each change-of-control or severance agreement between Atwood and
  its subsidiaries and their employees following the Effective Time, on terms comparable
  to those provided to Ensco's other similarly situated employees; estimated aggregate
  change-in-control severance for certain executive and other key Atwood employees was
  $8 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-005317, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle, financing, and listing changes at closing
- On October 6, 2017, Atwood and its subsidiary Atwood Offshore Worldwide Limited (AOWL)
  terminated all outstanding commitments under their April 10, 2014 Amended and Restated
  Credit Agreement and paid off all outstanding principal, interest and fees, releasing
  the associated liens. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- AOWL separately terminated its uncommitted letter-of-credit facility with BNP Paribas
  on October 5, 2017 (no obligations were outstanding at termination). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- Atwood redeemed all of its outstanding 6.50% Senior Notes due 2020 on October 6, 2017,
  satisfying and discharging the related indenture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- In connection with closing, Atwood requested that the NYSE suspend trading in Atwood
  common stock and remove it from listing, and file a Form 25 application to deregister
  the stock under Exchange Act Section 12(b); trading was suspended before the opening
  of business on October 6, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, all-stock reverse-triangular merger in which
  Atwood's fleet/asset tier (10 rigs plus newbuild units under construction) crossed
  intact into Ensco as a wholly owned subsidiary, while Atwood's own controlling and
  management tier did not continue in place at Atwood — its directors and executive
  officers were replaced at the Effective Time by Merger Sub's officers, with only two
  former Atwood directors continuing in any governance role (as new appointees to
  Ensco's own board, not as Atwood-subsidiary officers) — and Atwood's own credit
  facilities and public debt were terminated or redeemed concurrently with closing
  rather than carried forward inside the surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-061339, Form 8-K]
