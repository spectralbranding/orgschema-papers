## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Blue Hills Bancorp, Inc. (CIK 0001601545) filed a Form 15-12B on April 11, 2019,
  certifying termination of registration of its common stock under Section 12(g) of the
  Exchange Act, with zero holders of record reported; the form was signed on behalf of
  "Independent Bank Corp., a Massachusetts corporation, (as successor by merger to Blue
  Hills Bancorp, Inc., a Maryland corporation)." [SRC: SEC accession 0001214659-19-002684, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed under CIK 0001601545 since that
  Form 15-12B; EDGAR's filing history for this CIK shows its last 10-K was filed March 8,
  2019 (for fiscal year 2018), before the merger's effective date, with no subsequent
  annual report. Consequently there is no separate, audited 3-5-year public financial
  record for the acquired business filed under its own registrant; the realized-record
  facts below are drawn instead from the surviving registrant's (Independent Bank Corp.,
  CIK 0000776901) consolidated disclosures. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001601545&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40]

## What the surviving registrant's later disclosures show (consolidated, not segment-level)
- Independent Bank Corp.'s Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states that "the community
  banking business is the Company's only reportable operating segment," managed "as a
  single strategic unit" — the surviving registrant does not separately disclose the
  acquired business's post-close financial trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0000776901-22-000048, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The same filing's tax-rate reconciliation states the effective tax rate for the year
  ended December 31, 2020 included "a benefit of $4.8 million associated with the net
  operating loss (NOL) carryback provision of the CARES Act," generated "in relation to
  the Blue Hills Bancorp ('BHB acquisition')"; and states that, "in conjunction with the
  acquisition of Blue Hills Bancorp, Inc., parent of Blue Hills Bank (collectively
  'BHB')," the Company acquired and continued to account for BHB's defined benefit
  pension plan through fiscal year-end 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000776901-22-000048, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The filing's goodwill-impairment disclosure states the Company "last performed its
  annual goodwill impairment testing during the third quarter of 2021 and determined that
  the Company's goodwill was not impaired as of September 30, 2021"; this test is
  consolidated and not broken out by acquisition. It identifies "Rockland Trust – Blue
  Hills Charitable Foundation" as one of three affiliated charitable foundations that
  together donated over $2.4 million to 390 nonprofit organizations in 2021 — the only
  place the "Blue Hills" name persists in this filing, naming a charitable vehicle rather
  than a banking or operating unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0000776901-22-000048, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The filing's merger-and-acquisition-expense discussion attributes 2021 merger and
  acquisition expenses (change-in-control contracts, severance, branch closure/conversion
  costs) to a different, later acquisition (Meridian), and states there were no
  merger-and-acquisition costs incurred during 2020; it does not identify any 2020 or
  2021 merger-and-acquisition-expense line item as attributable to the BHB combination
  specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0000776901-22-000048, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Blue Hills Bancorp, Inc. deregistered from SEC reporting within about ten days
  of the merger's effective date and Independent Bank Corp. reports community banking as
  its sole operating segment, there is no separately disclosed 3-5-year public financial
  record for the acquired business as such. The only realized, dated facts traceable to
  the acquisition within Independent's later filings are a one-time $4.8 million tax
  benefit (FY2020) tied by name to the BHB acquisition, continued accounting for the
  acquired BHB pension plan through fiscal year 2021, a consolidated (not BHB-specific)
  finding that goodwill was not impaired as of September 30, 2021, and the persistence of
  the "Blue Hills" name solely in one of three affiliated charitable foundations. No
  segment-level profit/loss, asset-quality, or divestiture record for the acquired
  business was identified in the public record within the fixed outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001214659-19-002684, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0000776901-22-000048, Form 10-K FY2021]
