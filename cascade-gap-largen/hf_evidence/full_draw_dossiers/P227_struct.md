# Structural sub-dossier — Dr Pepper Snapple Group, Inc. / Keurig Dr Pepper Inc. merger (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Dr Pepper Snapple Group, Inc. ("DPSG"), Maple Parent Holdings Corp. ("Maple," the
  indirect parent of Keurig Green Mountain, Inc.), and Salt Merger Sub, Inc. (a wholly
  owned DPSG subsidiary, "Merger Sub") entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated
  January 29, 2018, under which Merger Sub would merge with and into Maple, with Maple
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of DPSG. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, each Maple share converted into a right to receive DPSG common stock
  at an exchange ratio, and DPSG separately declared a special cash dividend of $103.75
  per DPSG share, payable to DPSG stockholders of record immediately before closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the merger, holders of DPSG common stock as of immediately before the
  effective time collectively retained approximately 13% of the combined company's
  outstanding shares (fully diluted), while the equity holders of Maple collectively
  received approximately 87%; JAB Holding Company and its affiliates alone were
  expected to hold approximately 73%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed on July 9, 2018; at that date the exchange ratio of
  approximately 96.40 resulted in the issuance of 1,206,245,016 shares of the Company's
  common stock to Maple's former equity holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-044357, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.01]

## Legal vehicle, name, and listing at closing
- DPSG (a Delaware corporation) was the surviving public registrant; concurrently with
  closing its certificate of incorporation was amended to increase authorized shares and
  to change the Company's name to "Keurig Dr Pepper Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was accounted for as a reverse merger with Maple as the accounting
  acquirer; as a result the Company's fiscal year end automatically changed to Maple's,
  and the Board then acted to align it back to the pre-merger December 31 fiscal
  year-end. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-044357, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- DPSG common stock traded on the NYSE under the symbol "DPS" before the merger; the
  combined company's common stock was expected to be listed on the NYSE under the
  symbol "KDP" following consummation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]

## Governance / management tier (what did and did not cross)
- The combined company's board was structured to comprise twelve members: eight
  appointed by Maple (including Bart Becht of JAB as chairman, Bob Gamgort of Keurig,
  four further JAB appointees, and two Mondelez-designated directors), two continuing
  directors appointed by DPSG (including Larry Young, DPSG's then-CEO), and two directors
  mutually agreed as independent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- Bob Gamgort, Keurig's then-CEO, was designated to become Chief Executive Officer of
  the combined company, and Ozan Dokmecioglu, Keurig's then-CFO, was designated to become
  the combined company's Chief Financial Officer; other than Larry Young's continuing
  board seat, Maple made no arrangements with DPSG's management team regarding continued
  employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, DPSG's other named executive officers — Larry D. Young, Martin
  M. Ellen, and James J. Johnston — resigned from employment (Young's board seat
  continued separately), and seven then-sitting DPSG directors resigned from the Board.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-044357, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The combined company was structured to qualify as a "controlled company" under NYSE
  Section 303A rules (more than 50% of voting power held by a group), exempting it from
  certain independent-board-majority and committee-composition requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]

## Financing bridging the transaction
- Maple entered a $2.7 billion term loan agreement and a $2.4 billion revolving credit
  agreement (dated February 28, 2018) to fund the merger, with the Company assuming
  those obligations via borrower joinders executed July 9, 2018; the Company also
  assumed $8 billion aggregate principal amount of senior notes previously issued by an
  escrow subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-044358, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.03]
- A separate bridge financing commitment (up to $9.0 billion, later reduced to $8.0
  billion and then terminated as permanent financing was secured) had backstopped the
  transaction pending the term loan, credit agreement, and notes issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined DPSG's beverage business with Keurig via a reverse triangular
  merger in which Maple's equity holders received approximately 87% of the renamed,
  continuing public registrant's shares (DPSG survived nominally as "Keurig Dr Pepper
  Inc.") and DPSG shareholders received approximately 13% plus a large special cash
  dividend; the legal vehicle, listing, and product portfolios of both companies
  combined into the single continuing registrant, while the incoming CEO, CFO, and
  board chair were drawn from Keurig/JAB and DPSG's own CEO and other named executives
  exited employment (one retaining only a continuing board seat). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-004118, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-044357, Form 8-K]
