## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant (Seventy Seven Energy Inc., a Delaware corporation) continued to file periodic reports with the SEC; its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2016 was filed on February 13, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]

## Realized record — reorganization
- On June 7, 2016, the registrant and its subsidiaries (the "Debtors") filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, case number 16-11409. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- On July 14, 2016, the Bankruptcy Court issued a Confirmation Order confirming the Debtors' Joint Pre-packaged Plan of Reorganization; the Plan became effective on August 1, 2016 (the "Effective Date"), when the Debtors emerged from their Chapter 11 cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- The Plan effectuated a substantial reduction in debt, including $1.1 billion in the aggregate of the face amount of the 2019 Notes and 2022 Notes; on the Effective Date the company issued an aggregate of 22,000,000 shares of New Common Stock to the holders of those notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- The company applied fresh-start accounting, so financial statements on or after August 1, 2016 (the "Successor" period) are not comparable with financial statements for periods prior to the Effective Date (the "Predecessor" period). [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]

## Realized financial record
- Consolidated net loss was $63.6 million for the 2016 Successor Period (five months ended December 31, 2016) and $155.7 million for the 2016 Predecessor Period (seven months ended July 31, 2016); consolidated net loss was $221.4 million for the year ended December 31, 2015 and $8.0 million for the year ended December 31, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- Total revenues for the 2016 Successor Period (five months ended December 31, 2016) were $222.4 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]

## Customer/process interface over time
- CHK and its working interest partners accounted for approximately 51%, 65%, 70%, and 81% of the company's total revenues for the 2016 Successor Period, the 2016 Predecessor Period, and the years ended December 31, 2015 and 2014, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- Excluding certain payments, non-CHK revenue as a percentage of total revenue was 58% for the 2016 Successor Period and 42% for the 2016 Predecessor Period, compared with 32% for the year ended December 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]

## Realized record — change of control
- On December 12, 2016, the registrant entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Patterson-UTI Energy, Inc. and Pyramid Merger Sub, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of Patterson-UTI). [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K]
- On April 20, 2017, Patterson-UTI completed the merger: Merger Sub merged with and into the registrant, with the registrant surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Patterson-UTI; each issued and outstanding share of registrant common stock was converted into the right to receive Patterson-UTI common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-130325, Form 8-K]
- On April 20, 2017, the registrant filed a Form 15-12G to terminate the registration of its securities under the Securities Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-130424, Form 15-12G]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within approximately three years of the separation, the standalone company filed a pre-packaged Chapter 11 (June 7, 2016) and emerged on August 1, 2016 after eliminating $1.1 billion in aggregate face amount of notes through a debt-for-equity exchange (22,000,000 new shares to noteholders); it reported consolidated net losses of $63.6 million and $155.7 million across the two 2016 periods and $221.4 million in 2015; its former parent remained its single largest customer through the period (declining from approximately 81% of total revenues in 2014 to approximately 51% in the 2016 Successor Period); and on April 20, 2017 it was acquired by Patterson-UTI Energy, becoming a wholly-owned subsidiary and terminating its SEC registration the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001532930-17-000006, Form 10-K; SEC accession 0001193125-17-130325, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001193125-17-130424, Form 15-12G]
