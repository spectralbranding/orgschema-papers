# Structural sub-dossier — Lamb Weston Holdings separation from ConAgra Foods (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On November 17, 2015, ConAgra Foods, Inc.'s board of directors approved a plan to
  separate the company's frozen potato products business from ConAgra through a spin-off,
  distributing 100% of ConAgra's interest in the business to ConAgra stockholders as a
  dividend. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The frozen potato products business was transferred to Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc., a
  newly created Delaware corporation incorporated on July 5, 2016; after the distribution
  Lamb Weston would be an independent, publicly traded company (NYSE: "LW"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Following the spin-off, ConAgra Foods, Inc. would be renamed Conagra Brands, Inc.,
  comprising primarily the operations then reported as its Consumer Foods segment (NYSE:
  "CAG"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: value-added frozen potato products (plus frozen sweet
  potatoes and frozen appetizers), sold under the Lamb Weston brand and customer labels,
  reported in four segments (Global, Foodservice, Retail, Other); the segment's largest
  customer was McDonald's Corporation and its affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The separation and distribution agreement provided that ConAgra would transfer to Lamb
  Weston all the assets and generally all the liabilities relating to the frozen potato
  products business, on an "as is," "where is" basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Producing/delivering processes crossed WITH the product: the potato-processing
  (manufacturing) facilities across Idaho, Oregon, Washington, Louisiana, Minnesota,
  Alberta (Canada), the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, Austria, and China; associated
  warehouses/distribution facilities; a research and development facility in Richland,
  Washington; over 20,000 acres of company farmland in eastern Washington; and the
  strategic/long-term grower relationships and potato supply agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People/organization crossed WITH the processes: at May 29, 2016 the business had
  approximately 6,400 employees (approximately 5,700 in the United States), approximately
  34% party to collective bargaining agreements; under the employee matters agreement
  Lamb Weston became responsible for the employment, compensation, and benefit liabilities
  of the Lamb Weston business's employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Operating-management continuity: designated CEO Thomas P. Werner had led ConAgra's Lamb
  Weston and Foodservice businesses as President, Commercial Foods; Timothy R. McLevish was
  designated Executive Chairman. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared corporate substrate that did NOT transfer as an intact operating layer
- The business had historically received from ConAgra a set of centralized corporate
  services — treasury and cash management, procurement, information technology, general
  accounting and finance, payroll and human resources, legal and communications, and real
  estate and facilities — with a portion of ConAgra overhead cost-allocated into the
  business's combined financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Post-separation, Lamb Weston was expected to establish its own group health/welfare and
  retirement plans (except as bridged by the transition services agreement), indicating the
  corporate administrative layer would be stood up separately rather than transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- The parties would enter a separation and distribution agreement, a tax matters agreement,
  an employee matters agreement, a transition services agreement, and a trademark license
  agreement, plus other agreements governing ongoing commercial relationships such as
  intellectual property and supply arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Transition services agreement: ConAgra would provide/make available various administrative
  services for a period of no more than 18 months from the distribution date; personnel
  performing the services would remain employees/contractors of ConAgra and would NOT be
  under Lamb Weston's direction or control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Trademark license agreement: ConAgra would license certain trademarks (including the
  Alexia trademark) to Lamb Weston on a perpetual, irrevocable, fully paid-up, royalty-free,
  worldwide basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction detached an integrated operating business — its frozen-potato product
  lines together with the manufacturing plants, sourcing/supply chain, R&D, employees, and
  operating management that produce and run it — from ConAgra, with the customer-facing
  product moving alongside its producing/delivering processes and the people who run them;
  the layer NOT transferred intact was ConAgra's centralized corporate/administrative
  services substrate, bridged at closing by a ≤18-month transition services agreement plus
  the tax, employee, trademark, and commercial agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-693702, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
