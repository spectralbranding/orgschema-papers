## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Columbia Sportswear Company's Form 8-K dated August 6, 2012 (filed
  2012-08-08); its Item 1.01 reports entry into the material definitive agreement that
  forms the joint venture, so the drawn filing IS the deal-terms document (no separate
  formation primary was required). Its Exhibit 99.1 is the corresponding press release.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On August 6, 2012, Columbia Sportswear Company (the "Company") entered into a
  Subscription and Shareholders' Agreement (the "Joint Venture Agreement") with CSMM
  Hong Kong Limited ("Columbia Hong Kong," a wholly owned Company subsidiary), Swire
  Resources Limited, and SCCH Limited ("Swire," a wholly owned subsidiary of Swire
  Resources), to form a joint venture ("Joint Venture Company") between Columbia Hong
  Kong and Swire. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Swire Resources (Shanghai) Trading Company Limited ("Swire Shanghai") and affiliates
  had served as the exclusive independent distributor of the Company's Columbia and
  Mountain Hardwear branded products in the People's Republic of China (excluding Hong
  Kong and Macau) since 2004, and in Hong Kong and Macau since 2002. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Joint Venture Agreement provides for a term of up to 20 years and replaces the
  distribution agreements then in place between the Company and Swire Shanghai; operations
  by the Joint Venture Company are to begin January 1, 2014 with headquarters in Shanghai,
  subject to specified conditions including required regulatory approvals and licenses in
  China. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Ownership boundary and control (tier: controlling intent / vehicle / value model)
- Through their respective wholly owned subsidiaries, the Company and Swire Resources
  agreed to contribute and own 60% and 40%, respectively, of the capital of the Joint
  Venture Company, funded with a combination of cash and shareholder loans; profits and
  losses are shared in proportion to ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Joint Venture Company's board was initially set at five directors, with Columbia
  Hong Kong entitled to appoint three and Swire two; Tim Boyle, President and CEO of the
  Company, would serve as Chairman; the venture is to be managed per a mutually agreed
  business plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Beginning January 1, 2019, Columbia Hong Kong holds a call option to purchase, and
  Swire a put option to require Columbia Hong Kong to purchase, all of Swire's interest;
  these options may be accelerated upon specified events, including a material breach.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crosses the ownership boundary at closing (product / process / people)
- Customer-facing product: the Joint Venture Company shall initially distribute only the
  Columbia and Mountain Hardwear brands, with a right of first refusal over wholesale,
  distribution and retail of the Sorel and Montrail brands. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Asset transfer: on or prior to January 1, 2014, Swire will transfer to the Joint
  Venture Company certain assets related to the import, distribution and retail sale of
  Columbia branded products in China by Swire Shanghai, and will assist in establishing or
  continuing certain contractual relationships necessary to operate the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- People: the Company anticipated that approximately 650-700 employees then working with
  or for Swire Shanghai would become employees of, or provide services to, the joint
  venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Existing operating footprint at signing (as of June 30, 2012): Swire Shanghai sold to a
  wholesale-dealer network operating approximately 530 Columbia and 45 Mountain Hardwear
  retail locations across 135 cities, and Swire directly operated more than 70 Columbia
  branded retail locations in 7 cities; Swire's 2011 Columbia sales in China totaled
  approximately $123 million, generating low double-digit EBITDA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]

## Bridging / transitional arrangements at closing
- Certain support services will be provided to the Joint Venture Company by Swire Shanghai
  and Swire Resources on a transitional basis, i.e., the closing-time mechanism bridging
  the operating substrate the venture does not yet independently run. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Swire Resources will continue to distribute the Columbia and Mountain Hardwear brands
  exclusively in Hong Kong and Macau (outside the joint venture's China scope). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction forms a 60/40 (Columbia/Swire) capital joint-venture company in which
  the distributor's China import/distribution/retail assets and approximately 650-700 of
  its people are to move into a jointly owned vehicle carrying the Columbia and Mountain
  Hardwear product lines, with Swire Shanghai and Swire Resources contracted to provide
  support services on a transitional basis pending the venture's independent operation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-343820, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
