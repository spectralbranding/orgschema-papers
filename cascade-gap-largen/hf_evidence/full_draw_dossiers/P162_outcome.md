## Realized disposition and winding down
- Going-out-of-business sales at the Company's stores commenced on April 20, 2018,
  executing the court-approved disposition rather than a continuation of the operating
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The registrant did not emerge from Chapter 11 as a reporting going concern: it filed a
  Form 15-12G on April 25, 2018, certifying termination of the registration of its common
  stock (relying on Rule 15d-6) and suspending its duty to file reports; it reported
  approximately 178 holders of record as of the notice date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001065, Form 15-12G]
