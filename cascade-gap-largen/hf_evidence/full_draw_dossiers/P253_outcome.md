## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- FelCor Lodging Trust Incorporated filed a Form 15 certifying termination of Exchange
  Act registration on September 8, 2017, following the merger of its separate corporate
  existence into Rangers Sub I, LLC on August 31, 2017; FelCor itself files no periodic
  reports after that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056310, Form 15-12B]
- RLJ Lodging Trust (CIK 0001511337), the surviving/acquiring public filer, remains a
  continuing SEC registrant and has filed subsequent annual reports on Form 10-K,
  including one covering fiscal year 2021, filed February 24, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001511337-22-000009, Form 10-K, FY2021]

## RLJ's consolidated portfolio record within the outcome window (combined-company scale)
- As of December 31, 2021 and December 31, 2020, RLJ owned 98 and 103 hotel properties,
  respectively; RLJ's Form 10-K notes that operating results are not comparable across
  periods because certain properties were acquired, sold, or otherwise disposed, and
  that the non-comparable set includes nine hotels sold or otherwise disposed and three
  hotels acquired between January 1, 2020 and December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001511337-22-000009, Form 10-K, FY2021]
- During the year ended December 31, 2021, RLJ recorded impairment losses of $144.8
  million in total, of which $5.9 million related to two hotel properties sold in May
  2021 and $138.9 million related to the DoubleTree Metropolitan New York City, which
  was sold in December 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001511337-22-000009, Form 10-K, FY2021]
- These portfolio-level and impairment figures describe RLJ's combined-company results
  as a whole; the FY2021 Form 10-K does not identify any specific hotel, disposition, or
  impairment as originating from the hotels acquired in the FelCor merger, nor does it
  present a segregated financial or operating disclosure for that acquired group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001511337-22-000009, Form 10-K, FY2021]
