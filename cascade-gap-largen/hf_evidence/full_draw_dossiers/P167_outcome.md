## Realized financial trajectory
- For fiscal year 2021, the registrant reported net income of $17.3 million, versus a
  net loss of $59.7 million for fiscal year 2020; Adjusted EBITDA was $134.9 million for
  2021 versus $81.3 million for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-22-000028, Form 10-K FY2021 MD&A]
- For fiscal year 2022, the registrant reported net income of $16.2 million (a $1.0
  million decrease from 2021); net revenue increased $37.0 million (4.0%) year over
  year, driven by higher spot broadcast, political, and digital advertising revenue; and
  Adjusted EBITDA rose to $166.0 million (up 23.1% from 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022 MD&A]
- During fiscal year 2020 the registrant recorded a $4.5 million impairment charge on
  its FCC broadcast licenses; during fiscal year 2022 it recorded a further $15.5 million
  impairment charge on FCC broadcast licenses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-22-000028, Form 10-K FY2021 Risk Factors] [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-23-000010, Form 10-K FY2022 Risk Factors]
