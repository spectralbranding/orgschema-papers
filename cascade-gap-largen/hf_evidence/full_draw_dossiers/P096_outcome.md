## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The realized-record window is fixed as the registrant's fiscal years ~2017-2019 (~3-5 years after the ~2014 closing era). No annual report for the operating registrant exists within that window, for the reason documented below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 2.01]

## Terminal event: cash acquisition and deregistration (the realized record)
- On November 4, 2015 the company merged with and into a merger sub (Western Logistics II LLC) under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 28, 2015 among the company, Western Logistics LLC ("Parent") and the merger sub, with the merger sub continuing as the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the merger effective time each issued and outstanding share of common stock (including restricted stock) was converted into the right to receive $10.30 in cash, without interest and subject to applicable withholding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 2.01]
- The merger was valued at approximately $4.55 billion, subject to certain transaction costs, and was funded through equity financing provided to Parent by Global Logistic Properties Limited (GLP) and debt financing from Column Financial, Inc. and Morgan Stanley Bank, N.A. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 5.01]
- At the merger effective time each of the company's stockholders ceased to have any rights as a stockholder other than the right to receive the cash merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 3.03]
- In connection with the merger, the entire board (Stanley A. Moore, Marshall M. Burton, Dwight L. Merriman III, Charles B. Duke and Evan H. Zucker) resigned and the company's officers were removed, with the merger sub's own manager and officers continuing as the surviving entity's management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365695, 8-K Item 5.02]
- The company terminated its Exchange Act registration: a Form 15-12G to deregister/suspend the reporting obligation was filed on November 4, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365711, Form 15-12G]

## No independent public trajectory for the acquired business (feasibility of an in-window outcome)
- Because the operating business was acquired for cash by a GLP-funded private acquirer and the registrant deregistered its common stock, no audited public annual report of the acquired operating industrial/logistics portfolio was filed for the fixed ~2017-2019 window; GLP's acquiring entities are not U.S. public reporting companies that separately disclose this business's trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-365711, Form 15-12G]

## Same-CIK later filings are a distinct residual successor entity (verification, per no-record rule)
- Later Form 10-K filings under the same CIK (1464720) and commission file number (000-54372) are filed by a DIFFERENT registrant, "DC Industrial Liquidating Trust" — "the terms 'DC Industrial Liquidating Trust,' the 'Trust,' 'we,' 'our,' or 'us' refer to DC Industrial Liquidating Trust and its consolidated subsidiaries" — a Maryland liquidating trust, not the acquired operating REIT. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1464720/000119312517025118/d282824d10k.htm (DC Industrial Liquidating Trust, 10-K FY2016)]
- This liquidating trust holds only residual, non-transferred assets: as of December 31, 2016 it "owned 11 properties, of which four were fully constructed and leased, five were fully constructed and in active lease-up stage, one was under development and one was a land parcel" — a small residual, distinct from the ~296-building operating portfolio the acquirer absorbed. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1464720/000119312517025118/d282824d10k.htm]
- The operating portfolio did not remain with this reporting shell: the properties were contributed to DC Liquidating Assets Holdco LLC (the "Liquidating Company") in exchange for the common equity interests in it, and the residual entity itself has no employees. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1464720/000119312517025118/d282824d10k.htm]
- The last annual report under this CIK is the DC Industrial Liquidating Trust 10-K for FY2016, filed 2017-01-31; no Form 10-K, 10-Q, or 20-F was filed under CIK 1464720 for FY2017 or FY2018 (no in-window operating-business report exists). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-025118, 10-K FY2016 (DC Industrial Liquidating Trust)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The realized record of the acquired transaction is terminal: on November 4, 2015 the registrant was acquired for cash at $10.30/share (~$4.55 billion) by GLP-funded Western Logistics entities, its board and officers were replaced, and it deregistered its common stock (Form 15-12G, 2015-11-04). Consequently there is NO independent audited public 3-5-year (~2017-2019) trajectory for the acquired operating business, and the only later filings under the same CIK are those of a distinct residual successor (DC Industrial Liquidating Trust, holding 11 residual properties at Dec 31, 2016) rather than the acquired portfolio. The outcome for the acquired operating business is therefore coded **uncertain** (no public in-window record).
