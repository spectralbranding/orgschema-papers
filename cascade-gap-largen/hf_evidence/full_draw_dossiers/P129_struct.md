## Corporate structure and acquisition pattern
- Platform Specialty Products Corporation is a Delaware holding company that states it
  "continually seek[s] opportunities to act as an acquirer and consolidator of specialty
  chemical businesses on a global basis," prioritizing targets meeting an "Asset-Lite,
  High-Touch" profile (concentration of employees in R&D and sales/service, low fixed-
  asset intensity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- As of the FY2014 10-K, Platform had completed four acquisitions: the MacDermid
  Acquisition (October 31, 2013), the Agriphar Acquisition (October 1, 2014), the CAS
  (Chemtura AgroSolutions) Acquisition (November 3, 2014), and the Arysta Acquisition
  (February 13, 2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The MacDermid Acquisition (Oct 31, 2013) — acquisition of substantially all equity of
  MacDermid Holdings, which owned 97% of MacDermid, Incorporated — was structured as the
  registrant becoming a holding company for the MacDermid business; total consideration
  was approximately $1.80 billion, including assumption of $754 million of indebtedness,
  plus up to $100 million of contingent consideration tied to EBITDA and stock-price
  performance metrics over a seven-year period. The registrant changed its name to
  "Platform Specialty Products Corporation" concurrently with this closing. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The Agriphar Acquisition (Oct 1, 2014) was completed for approximately €300 million
  ($370 million): $350 million cash plus 711,551 restricted shares of common stock. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The CAS Acquisition (Nov 3, 2014) was completed for $1.04 billion: $983 million cash
  plus 2,000,000 shares of common stock and assumption of certain liabilities. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- The Arysta Acquisition (Feb 13, 2015) — completion of the previously announced
  acquisition of Arysta LifeScience Limited, an Irish private limited company, pursuant
  to a Share Purchase Agreement initially entered into October 20, 2014 with Nalozo
  S.à.r.l. (as amended) — was completed for approximately $3.51 billion: $2.91 billion in
  cash (subject to working capital and other adjustments) plus $600 million of newly
  issued Series B Convertible Preferred Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-000857,
  Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The Arysta Acquisition was financed from cash on hand, a note offering ($1.10 billion
  aggregate principal of 6.500% USD senior notes due 2022 and €350 million of 6.000% EUR
  senior notes due 2023, issued via an escrow issuer merged into the registrant at
  closing), and an amendment to the existing credit agreement providing $500 million of
  new term loans, a €83 million increase to the euro term-loan tranche, and $75 million
  increases each to the USD and multicurrency revolving facilities. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001171843-15-000857, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.03]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Legal vehicle: Arysta LifeScience Limited and its subsidiaries were acquired as a
  going, wholly owned corporate group via a stock/share purchase (not a merger of
  equals, spin-off, or carve-out); the Share Purchase Agreement and its amendments are
  the operative legal instruments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-000857, Form 8-K
  Item 2.01]
- Customer-facing product: Arysta's crop-protection and biosolutions product portfolio
  (agrochemical active ingredients, biostimulants, biocontrol) transferred as the
  acquired asset. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Process/brand convergence: in Q4 2014, Platform created a new AgroSolutions operating
  segment to manage the newly acquired agrochemical businesses (Agriphar, CAS, Arysta),
  "which now includes Agriphar's, CAS' and Arysta's complementary businesses and which is
  expected to uniformly operate under the Arysta LifeScience brand." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- People/management: effective at the Arysta Acquisition's closing, Wayne M. Hewett —
  who had served as President and CEO of Arysta since January 2010 — was appointed
  President of Platform, "leading the agrochemical businesses and overseeing Platform's
  ongoing operations." The FY2014 10-K states Hewett "leads the AgroSolutions business
  segment, as well as the integration of CAS, Agriphar and Arysta," and that CAS's
  management team, including its president, also joined the AgroSolutions segment upon
  the CAS Acquisition's completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-000857, Form 8-K
  Item 5.02; SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Controlling intent: the FY2014 10-K identifies an "Office of the Chairman" comprising
  Martin E. Franklin (Founder and Chairman), Daniel H. Leever (CEO), and Wayne M. Hewett
  (President), whose "experience in the specialty chemical industry and/or our business
  has largely contributed to our recent strategic acquisitions." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001171843-15-001732, Form 10-K FY2014]
- Bridging/transitional arrangements: the Arysta closing 8-K's exhibit index lists the
  Share Purchase Agreement and its amendments, a Supplemental Indenture, a Credit
  Agreement amendment, and a Registration Rights Agreement as the closing-date
  instruments; no transition-services, shared-services, or supply-bridging agreement is
  listed among the filed exhibits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-000857, Form 8-K
  Item 9.01(d)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Platform Specialty Products Corporation's Delaware holding-company structure grew
  through a sequence of whole-business share/asset purchases (MacDermid 2013, Agriphar
  and CAS in 2014, Arysta in February 2015) rather than a spin-off or carve-out; each
  acquired business's product line, and in the agrochemical cluster its incoming
  management (Hewett, CAS's president), were folded into a single new AgroSolutions
  segment operating under one brand, with no transition-services agreement identified
  bridging a shared substrate at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001171843-15-000857] [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001171843-15-001732]
