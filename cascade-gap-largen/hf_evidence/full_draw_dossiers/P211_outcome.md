## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On February 11, 2019, Brazos Valley Longhorn, L.L.C. (identified on the filing as
  "successor in interest to WildHorse Resource Development Corporation") filed a Form
  15-12B certifying termination of registration of the former WildHorse common stock
  under Section 12(g), recording approximately one holder of record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681714-19-000019, Form 15-12B]
- The last annual report filed under WildHorse's own SEC filer identity (CIK 1681714)
  was a Form 10-K covering fiscal year 2018, filed March 4, 2019 (after the merger
  closed); no further 10-K, 10-Q, or 8-K was filed under that filer identity in 2020 or
  2021 (search of the registrant's EDGAR filing history for those years and form types
  returned no results). [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001681714&type=10-K]

## Acquirer's separate disclosure of the acquired business by name (feasibility-relevant)
- Chesapeake's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2019 described six geographic operating areas,
  including "Brazos Valley - Southeast Texas assets acquired in our WildHorse
  acquisition on February 1, 2019," listed as distinct from a pre-existing "Eagle Ford -
  South Texas" operating area. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-20-000061, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Chesapeake's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 still listed "Brazos Valley - Southeast
  Texas" as one of five distinct operating areas, separate from "Eagle Ford - South
  Texas." [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-21-000078, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Chesapeake's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 2022) listed only four
  operating areas — Marcellus, Haynesville, "Eagle Ford - Eagle Ford Shale in South
  Texas," and Powder River Basin — with no separate "Brazos Valley" line; the filing
  does not state explicitly why the line was combined. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-22-000029, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Realized record within the fixed outcome window (~2022-2024)
- Chesapeake's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2023 discloses that Chesapeake divested its
  Eagle Ford asset position entirely during 2023, in three transactions: (1) an
  agreement dated January 17, 2023 to sell a portion of its Eagle Ford assets to
  WildFire Energy I LLC for approximately $1.425 billion, closed March 20, 2023,
  recognizing a gain of approximately $337 million; (2) an agreement dated February 17,
  2023 to sell a portion of its remaining Eagle Ford assets to INEOS Upstream Holdings
  Limited ("INEOS Energy") for approximately $1.4 billion, closed April 28, 2023,
  recognizing a gain of approximately $470 million; and (3) an agreement entered in
  August 2023 to sell "the final portion of our remaining Eagle Ford assets" to SilverBow
  Resources, Inc. for approximately $700 million (plus up to $50 million in contingent
  consideration), closed November 30, 2023, recognizing a gain of approximately $140
  million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-24-000013, Form 10-K FY2023]
- The FY2023 10-K does not state what fraction of the divested Eagle Ford asset base
  traces specifically to the WildHorse-acquired (Brazos Valley) acreage versus the
  legacy pre-2019 Eagle Ford position that had already been combined with it in
  reporting; the filing describes the divestitures only at the combined "Eagle Ford"
  area level. [UNVERIFIED]

## Context outside the fixed window (reported for completeness, not the outcome scalar)
- Before the fixed outcome window, on June 28, 2020 (approximately 16 months after
  closing), Chesapeake and certain of its subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for
  reorganization under Chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-20-000131, Form 8-K]
- Brazos Valley Longhorn, L.L.C. and Brazos Valley Longhorn Finance Corp. (the surviving
  entities from the WildHorse merger) are named among the "Company Parties" in Exhibit A
  of the debtor-in-possession credit agreement filed with Chesapeake's Chapter 11 8-K,
  indicating they were among the filing Debtor subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-077745, Form 8-K Exhibit 10.1]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered the Confirmation Order for the Fifth Amended Joint Chapter
  11 Plan of Reorganization of Chesapeake Energy Corporation and its Debtor Affiliates on
  January 16, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-22-000029, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired WildHorse business ceased separate SEC reporting within two weeks of
  closing (Form 15-12B, February 11, 2019) and no periodic report was filed under its
  own filer identity thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001681714-19-000019, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer separately disclosed the acquired business by name as a distinct
  operating area ("Brazos Valley") through its FY2019 and FY2020 annual reports; by the
  FY2021 annual report the area had been folded into the acquirer's combined "Eagle
  Ford" reporting line. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-20-000061, Form 10-K FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-22-000029, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Within the fixed ~2022-2024 outcome window, the acquirer fully divested the combined
  Eagle Ford asset position across three 2023 transactions (WildFire Energy I LLC, INEOS
  Energy, and SilverBow Resources), for aggregate disclosed consideration on the order of
  $3.5 billion and aggregate disclosed gains on the order of $947 million relative to
  carrying value; the filings do not isolate what portion of these figures is
  attributable specifically to the originally WildHorse-acquired acreage as distinct from
  the acquirer's legacy Eagle Ford position. [SRC: SEC accession 0000895126-24-000013, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Before the fixed window, the surviving legal vehicles from the merger (Brazos Valley
  Longhorn, L.L.C. and its finance subsidiary) were named Debtor parties in the
  acquirer's June 2020 Chapter 11 filing, which was resolved via a confirmed plan of
  reorganization in January 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-20-077745, Form 8-K Exhibit 10.1]
