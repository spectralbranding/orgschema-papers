## Transaction and parties at closing
- VWR Corporation is described as "a global independent provider of product and service solutions to laboratory and production customers," with sales in excess of $4.5 billion in 2016 and a team of more than 10,200 associates serving pharmaceutical, biotechnology, industrial, education, government and healthcare customers; its common stock traded on NASDAQ under the symbol "VWR." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Avantor, Inc. is described as "a global supplier of ultra-high-purity materials for the life sciences and advanced technology markets," providing products to approximately 7,900 customers with a portfolio of more than 30,000 products marketed under brands including J.T.Baker, Macron Fine Chemicals, Rankem, BeneSphera, Puritan Products, POCH, NuSil, and CareSil. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Vail Acquisition Corp ("Merger Sub") was a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of Avantor, formed on May 3, 2017 solely to effect the transaction and having no other business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Avantor and Merger Sub are described as affiliates of the private equity investment firm New Mountain Capital L.L.C. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated May 4, 2017, Merger Sub was merged with and into VWR, with VWR continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly-owned subsidiary of Avantor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Each issued and outstanding share of VWR common stock (other than excluded/dissenting shares) was converted into the right to receive $33.25 in cash, without interest; outstanding VWR stock options became fully vested and were cancelled and converted into cash based on the spread to $33.25, and outstanding restricted stock units were converted into a vested right to cash equal to the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was financed in part through a debt commitment letter and a preferred equity commitment letter; under the latter, BSPI committed to purchase $2 billion of senior preferred stock and $650 million of junior convertible preferred stock (plus warrants) of the Avantor-affiliated issuer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided for a termination fee payable by VWR of $170 million (reduced to $85 million under specified go-shop-period circumstances) and a reverse termination fee payable by Avantor of $300 million under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on November 21, 2017 (the "Closing Date"); on the Closing Date each share of VWR common stock (other than excluded shares) was converted into the right to receive the merger consideration, and outstanding options and RSUs were cancelled/converted on the terms described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form 8-K dated 2017-11-21]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Product/business: VWR's laboratory- and production-customer product and service distribution business (2016 sales in excess of $4.5 billion) became wholly owned by Avantor as a result of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Pre-existing product/process relationship: the proxy statement's background narrative states that "[f]or several years, VWR and Avantor have shared a distribution and supply relationship," predating any discussion of a combination between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: VWR Corporation continued to exist post-closing as the surviving corporation of the merger rather than being dissolved, now held as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Avantor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent / management participation: the VWR Board identified, as a factor in its fairness determination, "[t]he fact that New Mountain and Avantor did not require management participation in the merger (either via continued employment with the combined company or the rollover of management's equity ownership in the Company) as a condition to executing the merger agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- People/management: as of the proxy statement's date, a "New Management Arrangements" section disclosed that it was expected VWR's then-CFO, Mr. Cowan, would become Chief Financial Officer "of the combined company" effective at closing, with specified continuing compensation terms; no comparable continued-employment or equity-rollover arrangement was disclosed as of that date for VWR's other named executive officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Officers and directors: pursuant to the merger agreement, on the Closing Date certain officer/director changes occurred, including resignations of directors effective the Closing Date, stated not to result from any disagreement over Company operations or policies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form 8-K dated 2017-11-21]
- Indemnification: existing director/officer indemnification and exculpation rights were, by the merger agreement's terms, assumed by the surviving corporation without further action and continued in effect for a tail period following the date of the merger agreement (rather than being extinguished at closing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]
- Employee benefits bridging: the merger agreement required that, for a specified period following the closing date, continuing employees be provided base compensation, target incentive opportunities, and employee benefits (excluding defined-benefit pension plan benefits) no less favorable in the aggregate than those in place immediately before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]

## Ownership concentration and voting support
- Varietal Distribution Holdings, LLC, an entity in which several of VWR's named executive officers (including Mr. Cowan) were investors, held approximately 34.7% of VWR common stock at the time the merger agreement was executed and was a party to a Voting and Support Agreement; Varietal was expected to liquidate following completion of the merger, with its members receiving their pro rata share of the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash acquisition in which Avantor (a manufacturer/marketer of performance materials, backed by New Mountain Capital, with a pre-existing distribution and supply relationship with VWR) acquired VWR (an independent laboratory- and production-products distributor) via a reverse triangular merger for $33.25 per share in cash; VWR survived as a wholly-owned legal subsidiary; continued employment was arranged for only one named VWR executive (the CFO) as of the signing date, and management participation was expressly not a condition of the deal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-202703, Form 8-K dated 2017-11-21]
