# Outcome sub-dossier — Corning Incorporated / Dow Corning Corporation equity joint venture, realized record ~2015-2017

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at 3-5 years after the
> ~2012 closing-era snapshot (i.e., roughly 2015-2017). This sub-dossier describes ONLY
> the realized post-close record and contains no closing-structure information.

## Realized trajectory: the venture was unwound within the outcome window

- On December 11, 2015, Corning announced a Transaction Agreement (dated December 10,
  2015) among The Dow Chemical Company, Corning, Dow Corning Corporation, and a newly
  formed entity, HS Upstate Inc., under which Corning would exchange its 50% stock
  interest in Dow Corning for 100% of the stock of the new entity plus approximately $4.8
  billion in cash; upon completion, Dow Chemical would hold 100% of Dow Corning. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-15-000063, Form 8-K, Exhibit 1.1 Transaction Agreement and Exhibit 99.1 press release dated 2015-12-11]
- The December 2015 announcement press release quoted Corning's chairman/CEO stating that
  "Dow Corning's silicones business lies outside the technologies and manufacturing
  platforms core to our strategic framework." [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-15-000063, Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1 press release dated 2015-12-11]
- The transaction closed on May 31, 2016: Corning completed the exchange of its 50%
  interest in Dow Corning for 100% of the new entity, which held approximately a 40%
  interest in Hemlock Semiconductor Group and approximately $4.8 billion in cash;
  Corning's chairman/CEO stated the company would "no longer receive equity earnings or
  dividends from Dow Corning's silicones business." [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-16-000090, Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1 pro forma financial information and Exhibit 99.2 press release dated 2016-06-01]

## Realized financial record (audited, FY2016 Form 10-K)

- Corning recognized a $2.7 billion non-taxable gain in the year ended December 31, 2016
  on the strategic realignment of its Dow Corning ownership interest, alongside cash
  proceeds of $4,818 million received upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-17-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016, MD&A and Cash Flow discussion]
- Equity earnings attributable to the Dow Corning relationship declined across the
  outcome window before the exchange: $252 million (2014), $281 million (2015), and $82
  million for the partial period through the May 31, 2016 closing date, after which
  earnings were reported separately from the retained Hemlock Semiconductor Group
  interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-17-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016, Note 7 Investments]
- Corning's retained equity investment in Hemlock Semiconductor Group carried a negative
  carrying value of $383 million at the transaction date, which the 10-K attributes to "a
  one-time charge to this entity in 2014 for the permanent abandonment of certain
  assets," noting that excluding this charge the entity was profitable. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-17-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016, Note 7 Investments]

## Items noted but outside strict primary verification within this window

- The December 2015 announcement described Hemlock Semiconductor as expected to generate
  approximately $1 billion in sales and $160 million in earnings for 2015 and to
  contribute approximately $65 million in gross equity earnings to Corning; these are
  forward-looking company estimates as stated in the press release rather than audited
  figures and are recorded here as company-sourced projections, not confirmed outcomes.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-15-000063, Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.1 press release dated 2015-12-11]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Within the 3-5-year outcome window, the equity joint venture was not sustained as a
  continuing 50/50 structure: Corning exited its ownership interest entirely (May 31,
  2016), converting the relationship into a $4.8 billion cash payment plus a direct
  interest in one formerly-shared subsidiary (Hemlock Semiconductor Group), while Dow
  Chemical assumed full ownership of the remainder of the venture; the exiting party's
  own public statements characterized the divested business as strategically
  non-core, and Corning's audited results show declining venture equity earnings in the
  two years preceding the exit alongside a negative carrying value in the retained
  minority holding traced to a 2014 asset-abandonment charge. [SRC: SEC accession 0000024741-16-000090, Form 8-K, Exhibit 99.2 press release dated 2016-06-01; SEC accession 0000024741-17-000011, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016, Note 7 Investments]
