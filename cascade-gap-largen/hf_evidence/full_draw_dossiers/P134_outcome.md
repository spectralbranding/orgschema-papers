## Public-reporting status within and at the end of the window
- The registrant's last full public Annual Report on Form 10-K covers the fiscal year
  ended December 30, 2018 and was filed March 15, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- On June 20, 2019 the registrant completed a merger under which First Street Merger Sub,
  Inc. merged into the Company, which became a wholly owned subsidiary of First Street
  Parent, Inc., an entity controlled by equity funds managed by Apollo Management IX, L.P.;
  the merger followed a cash tender offer at $6.50 per share into which approximately 87%
  of outstanding shares were tendered, with aggregate consideration for all equity
  securities of approximately $545 million, funded by Apollo-affiliated equity and debt
  financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-036602, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.01]
- In connection with the merger the Company notified the New York Stock Exchange, requested
  filing of a Form 25 to delist and deregister its shares under Section 12(b), and stated
  it intended to file a Form 15 to suspend its reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-036602, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- The Company filed a Form 15-12B on July 1, 2019 to terminate the registration of its
  common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-038378, Form 15-12B]
- Consequently no audited public annual report exists for fiscal years after fiscal 2018,
  and the realized record for the latter part of the ~2018-2020 window is not publicly
  observable through primary SEC filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-038378, Form 15-12B]

## Realized financial record (best-documented outcome scalars, FY2018 annual report)
- For the fiscal year ended December 30, 2018 the Company reported a net loss of
  approximately $112.2 million, compared with a net loss of approximately $138.9 million in
  the prior fiscal year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018), consolidated statements of operations]
- In the fourth quarter of fiscal 2018 the Company recorded a goodwill impairment charge of
  $94.0 million related to the Smart & Final stores reporting unit, compared with a goodwill
  impairment charge of $180.0 million in fiscal 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018), Note 3 "Goodwill Impairment" and MD&A]
- As of December 30, 2018 the Company reported goodwill of approximately $291.9 million,
  representing approximately 17% of total assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018), Risk Factors]

## Store portfolio and format execution over time
- As of December 30, 2018 the Company operated 201 Extra!-format stores, of which 105
  represented conversions or relocations of legacy Smart & Final stores and 96 represented
  new store openings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018), Business]
- The fiscal 2018 annual report does not separately disclose the financial results or a
  distinct trajectory of the store properties acquired in the fourth-quarter-2015 asset
  acquisition; those properties are not identified as a separately reported line. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-19-001282, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2018-2020 window, the publicly observable record shows the registrant
  reporting successive annual net losses (approximately $138.9 million in fiscal 2017 and
  $112.2 million in fiscal 2018) and successive goodwill impairment charges ($180.0 million
