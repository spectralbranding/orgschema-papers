# Structural sub-dossier — Kindred Healthcare, Inc. going-private merger and internal business separation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Kindred Healthcare, Inc. entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated December 19,
  2017, with Kentucky Hospital Holdings, LLC ("HospitalCo Parent"), Kentucky Homecare
  Holdings, Inc. ("Parent"), and Kentucky Homecare Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"); Merger
  Sub merged with and into Kindred on July 2, 2018, with Kindred continuing as the
  surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the effective time, each share of Kindred common stock was cancelled and converted
  into the right to receive $9.00 in cash, representing a premium of approximately 27% to
  the 90-day volume-weighted average price ended December 15, 2017; aggregate merger
  consideration paid to holders of common stock, options, and stock awards was
  approximately $737.2 million in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Item 2.01]
- Parent and HospitalCo Parent were newly formed entities with no prior business
  operations, created solely to enter the merger and separation agreements; Parent was to
  be controlled, following the transactions, by affiliates of TPG Global LLC ("TPG"),
  Welsh, Carson, Anderson & Stowe ("WCAS"), and Humana Inc. ("Humana") (together the
  "Consortium"), while HospitalCo Parent was to be controlled by affiliates of TPG and
  WCAS only. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.2-3]
- Equity financing commitments totaling $1.940 billion were provided severally by TPG
  Partners VII, L.P., TPG Kentucky Co-Invest, L.P., WCAS affiliated funds, Humana, and
  Port-aux-Choix Private Investments Inc.; debt financing commitments of up to $2.115
  billion (for Parent) and up to $860 million (for HospitalCo Parent) were separately
  arranged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.10]

## Business separated at closing: two customer-facing product lines
- Prior to closing, Kindred operated three lines: a home health, hospice, and community
  care business ("Kindred at Home," 609 sites of service in 40 states); a hospital
  division of 77 transitional-care/long-term acute care hospitals in 18 states; and a
  Kindred Rehabilitation Services division of 19 inpatient rehabilitation facilities plus
  101 hospital-based acute rehabilitation units across 45 states. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.30]
- Simultaneously with the merger agreement, Kindred entered a Separation Agreement (also
  dated December 19, 2017) among Kindred, Parent, HospitalCo Parent, and Kentucky
  Hospital Merger Sub, Inc. ("Hospital Merger Sub"), under which — promptly following the
  merger's effective time — the surviving entity (converted to a Delaware LLC and renamed
  Kindred Healthcare, LLC) separated its home health/hospice/community-care "Homecare
  Business" from its other ("Retained"/"Hospital") businesses and distributed the Homecare
  Business to Parent, while Hospital Merger Sub merged into Kindred Healthcare, LLC,
  leaving it an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of HospitalCo Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.1, definitions §] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Introductory Note & Items 2.01/5.03]
- Net effect: the customer-facing home-health/hospice/community-care product line
  (Kindred at Home) and the hospital/rehabilitation product line crossed the ownership
  boundary into two differently controlled successor structures — Parent (Consortium
  including Humana) for Homecare, HospitalCo Parent (TPG/WCAS only) for the hospital and
  rehabilitation business — rather than as one integrated unit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Item 2.01]

## Contractual bridge for the shared operating substrate
- The merger agreement's Transition Plan provisions required a Transition Services
  Agreement addressing transitional services, separation or replacement of shared
  systems, and a proposed timeline for separating the Homecare Business from the Retained
  (hospital) Business, including allocation of assets, liabilities, and identified
  "Shared Services Employees" between the two. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A merger agreement text]
- The agreement defines distinct "Homecare Employees" (exclusively serving the Homecare
  Business) versus employees retained with the Hospital Entities, and provides for
  severance costs for Shared Services Employees whose roles are eliminated "in connection
  with the consummation of the Separation Transactions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A merger agreement text]
- Kindred's then-CEO Benjamin A. Breier and EVP/Kindred at Home President David A. Causby
  each entered non-binding term sheets — Breier with HospitalCo Parent, Causby with
  Parent (which would operate the Homecare Business) — to negotiate post-closing
  employment terms, indicating the two most senior operating executives were being
  routed to the two different post-separation entities rather than staying together.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-051132, DEFM14A p.9-10]

## Ownership and reporting status change at closing
- Kindred common stock had traded on the NYSE under ticker "KND"; on July 2, 2018, in
  connection with the merger's closing, Kindred requested the NYSE file a Form 25 to
  remove the common stock from listing and registration, and stated its intent to
  subsequently file a Form 15 to suspend its Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Item 3.01]
- A Form 15-12B was filed July 12, 2018 by "Kindred Healthcare, LLC (successor in interest
  to Kindred Healthcare, Inc.)," certifying approximately one (1) holder of record of the
  common stock class. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-216790, Form 15-12B]
- At the effective time, Kindred's pre-merger directors ceased serving and were replaced;
  following the entity's conversion to an LLC, Benjamin A. Breier was appointed President
  and CEO of Kindred Healthcare, LLC (the hospital/rehabilitation successor entity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a going-private whole-company merger (all Kindred common
  stock converted to cash, NYSE listing removed, Exchange Act reporting suspended) with a
  concurrent internal separation that routed the home-health/hospice/community-care
  product line to one Consortium-controlled parent (including Humana) and the hospital/
  rehabilitation product line to a second, TPG/WCAS-controlled parent, with a Transition
  Services Agreement and identified Shared Services Employees bridging the shared
  substrate between the two resulting businesses at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-211443, 8-K Introductory Note & Items 2.01/3.01/5.03]
