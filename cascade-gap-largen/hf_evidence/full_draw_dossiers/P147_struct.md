## Transaction and parties as structured
- On May 13, 2012 Nationstar Mortgage LLC entered into an asset purchase agreement (subsequently amended; amended and restated on June 28, 2012, the "ResCap Purchase Agreement") with Residential Capital, LLC and its related entities (the "ResCap Sellers"), each an indirect subsidiary of Ally Financial Inc., in connection with the ResCap Sellers' proceedings before the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York (the "Bankruptcy Case"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- The transaction was structured as a purchase of assets out of ResCap's proposed Chapter 11 asset sale, to be consummated pursuant to the provisions of the Bankruptcy Code; consummation was subject to (i) competitive bidding pursuant to sale procedures approved for the Bankruptcy Case on June 28, 2012, (ii) certain licensing and regulatory approvals including expiration or termination of the Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, and (iii) customary closing conditions and termination rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- The seller was a Chapter 11 debtor at signing: ResCap filed petitions for relief under the Bankruptcy Code in the Southern District of New York, and the transaction would be consummated pursuant to the Bankruptcy Code. This debtor status of the seller is a structural feature of the transaction at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- Nationstar had been approved as the stalking-horse bidder by the Bankruptcy Court; other bidders could submit bids, and the auction for the assets was scheduled to commence on October 23, 2012, with no assurance Nationstar would be the winning bidder. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4]

## What was to cross the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- Under the ResCap Purchase Agreement, Nationstar agreed to purchase the rights to service residential mortgage loans with an aggregate unpaid principal balance ("UPB") of approximately $361 billion, comprising mortgage servicing rights ("MSR") related to approximately $194 billion UPB of loans and subservicing contracts related to approximately $167 billion UPB of loans, together with approximately $1.7 billion of related servicing advance receivables, each measured as of June 30, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- Approximately 69% of loans in the total portfolio (by UPB) were owned, insured or guaranteed by Fannie Mae, Freddie Mac or Ginnie Mae. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4]
- Nationstar also agreed to acquire the ResCap Sellers' mortgage origination platform and servicing platforms and certain other assets, and to assume certain liabilities — i.e., the customer-facing servicing rights/asset were to cross together with the operating platform that produces and delivers the servicing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- The transaction was to be accounted for as a business combination using the acquisition method of accounting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]

## What was NOT to cross — excluded assets
- The pro forma adjustments exclude the historical assets and liabilities of ResCap not acquired or assumed as part of the transaction, which primarily include ResCap's legacy business in run-off or in the process of strategic dispositions and the deconsolidation of variable interest entities ("VIEs"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- As part of the transaction, Nationstar was required to purchase any ResCap mortgage loans intended to be included in a pool to be sold to or guaranteed by Ginnie Mae, subject to the total UPB not exceeding $75.0 million; as of June 30, 2012 ResCap held no such loans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]

## Consideration and financing mechanics (as structured)
- The aggregate cash purchase price for the purchased assets was approximately $2.3 billion (pro forma as of June 30, 2012), expected to be funded through a combination of cash on hand, the proceeds of a $450 million coinvestment by Newcastle Investment Corp. ("Newcastle") (and possibly certain Newcastle affiliates), the proceeds of advance financing facilities (approximately $1.5 billion expected, to fund the servicing advance receivables), and further issuances of equity or debt including the notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- Nationstar made an earnest money cash deposit of $72 million, to be applied towards the purchase price upon closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- Stalking-horse protection: if the ResCap Purchase Agreement were terminated as a result of the Bankruptcy Court's approval of a competing bid, the ResCap Sellers would be required to pay Nationstar a $24 million break-up fee in cash, a portion of which would be payable to Newcastle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]

## Bridging arrangements over a shared substrate (as structured)
- Coinvestment / financing bridge: Nationstar entered into an agreement with Newcastle to sell the right to receive up to approximately 65% of the excess servicing fees from the acquired mortgage servicing rights after receipt of a fixed basic servicing fee (the "ResCap Excess MSR Agreement"), for $450.0 million; this arrangement was to be treated as a financing arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]
- Continuing servicing relationship with the seller's affiliate: under the ResCap Purchase Agreement, Nationstar would continue to subservice for Ally Bank unless the related MSRs at Ally Bank were transferred to another party. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3]

## Timing as structured
- Subject to the auction process and satisfaction of the other conditions, the transaction was expected to close in late 2012 or early 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.4]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, Nationstar Mortgage LLC (approved stalking-horse bidder) agreed under the amended and restated ResCap Purchase Agreement to buy, out of the ResCap Sellers' Chapter 11 estate via a Bankruptcy-Code competitive-bidding sale, the rights to service approximately $361 billion UPB of residential mortgages (MSRs on ~$194 billion + subservicing on ~$167 billion) plus ~$1.7 billion of servicing advance receivables and the ResCap mortgage origination and servicing platforms, for an aggregate cash price of approximately $2.3 billion, funded with cash on hand, a $450 million Newcastle coinvestment, advance financing facilities and debt/equity; a $72 million earnest deposit was posted, a $24 million break-up fee (portion to Newcastle) was payable if a competing bid prevailed, and the customer-facing servicing rights were to cross together with the producing/delivering platform, with an October 23, 2012 auction and a late-2012/early-2013 expected close. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-396051, Form 8-K EX-99.3, EX-99.4]
