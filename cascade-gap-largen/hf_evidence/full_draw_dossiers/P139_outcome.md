## Realized trajectory — the Chapter 11 case
- In September 2015 Samson Resources Company and eight affiliates filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware. [SRC: https://www.coleschotz.com/new-delaware-chapter-11-filing-samson-resources-company-et-al/]
- The reorganization did not proceed uncontested: on approximately September 27, 2016 the bankruptcy judge refused the debtors' request to extend their exclusivity period, allowing the official unsecured creditors committee to file a competing reorganization plan; Judge Sontchi stated "I think this is a situation where the debtor is using exclusivity as a sword" and found "the debtors have not engaged constructively or even in good faith with the official committee." [SRC: https://www.forbes.com/sites/debtwire/2016/09/27/samson-resources-bankruptcy-judge-wont-extend-exclusivity-opening-door-for-competing-creditor-plan/]
- The unsecured creditors committee argued the debtor refused to negotiate a plan and sought to pursue fraudulent-transfer and lien-challenge claims; the debtor's own proposal at that stage would have paid first lien lenders (owed approximately $950 million) in full, with partial reduction from asset sales and refinancing of the remainder into a revolving loan facility. [SRC: https://www.forbes.com/sites/debtwire/2016/09/27/samson-resources-bankruptcy-judge-wont-extend-exclusivity-opening-door-for-competing-creditor-plan/]

## Realized trajectory — confirmation and emergence
- The U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware confirmed the plan of reorganization on February 13, 2017, and the company emerged from Chapter 11 on March 1, 2017. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/samson-resources-emerges-from-bankruptcy-300416368.html]
- Under the plan, substantially all of Samson Resources Corp.'s remaining assets, including its subsidiaries, were transferred to a newly formed entity, Samson Resources II, LLC; the majority of the equity in Samson II was distributed to Samson Resources Corp.'s second lien lenders, both on account of their bankruptcy claims and in connection with a $60 million rights offering. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/samson-resources-emerges-from-bankruptcy-300416368.html] [SRC: https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/article/17288064/samson-resources-emerges-from-bankruptcy-sheds-4-billion-in-debt]
- The reorganization discharged approximately $4 billion of debt and nearly $300 million of annual interest expense; post-emergence debt financing consisted of a first lien revolving credit facility with an initial borrowing base of $280 million, of which $245 million was outstanding on the effective date, leaving greater than $60 million in total liquidity. [SRC: https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/samson-resources-emerges-from-bankruptcy-300416368.html] [SRC: https://www.ogj.com/general-interest/article/17288064/samson-resources-emerges-from-bankruptcy-sheds-4-billion-in-debt]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- After agreeing to the restructuring in August 2015 and immediately terminating its voluntary SEC reporting, the registrant carried out the contemplated chapter 11 restructuring: it filed for Chapter 11 in Delaware in September 2015, passed through a contested plan process (loss of exclusivity in 2016 and a competing creditor plan), and had a plan confirmed on February 13, 2017 with emergence on March 1, 2017 — the going-concern oil-and-gas business surviving intact but with its equity transferred to a new holding entity (Samson Resources II, LLC) owned chiefly by the former second lien lenders after roughly $4 billion of debt was discharged.
