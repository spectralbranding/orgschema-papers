## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant remained an SEC-reporting public company throughout the window and filed an annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended December 31, 2023)]
- The filing is a combined annual report for Dow Inc. and its subsidiaries and The Dow Chemical Company and its subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Realized financial trajectory (fiscal 2021-2023)
- Net sales were $44,622 million in 2023, $56,902 million in 2022, and $54,968 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023, Consolidated Statements of Income]
- Net income available for Dow Inc. common stockholders was $589 million in 2023, $4,582 million in 2022, and $6,311 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023, Consolidated Statements of Income]
- Diluted earnings per common share were $0.82 in 2023, $6.28 in 2022, and $8.38 in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023, Consolidated Statements of Income]
- Income before income taxes was $656 million in 2023, $6,090 million in 2022, and $8,145 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023, Consolidated Statements of Income]
- Restructuring and asset related charges - net were $528 million in 2023, $118 million in 2022, and $6 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023, Consolidated Statements of Income]

## Operating realignment in the window
- In January 2023 the Board approved restructuring actions (the "2023 Restructuring Program") to achieve structural cost improvement initiatives in response to the continued economic impact from the global recessionary environment and to enhance the Company's agility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Under the 2023 Restructuring Program the Company recorded pretax restructuring charges of $535 million, consisting of severance and related benefit costs of $344 million and asset write-downs and write-offs of $191 million; the severance related to a global workforce reduction of approximately 2,000 employees, with the majority of separations occurring by the end of the second quarter of 2023 and the remainder primarily through the end of 2024. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Equity in earnings (losses) of nonconsolidated affiliates moved to a loss of $119 million in 2023 from earnings of $268 million in 2022, described as primarily due to declines at Sadara and the Kuwait joint ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Workforce and operations in the window
- At December 31, 2023, the Company permanently employed approximately 35,900 people on a full-time basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]
- The Company states that Dow operates manufacturing sites in 31 countries and delivered sales of approximately $45 billion in 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Reference to the separation agreements (context, not the outcome scalar)
- The FY2023 filing references agreements entered into with DuPont and Corteva as part of the separation and distribution (related to Corporate); these are noted only as context and are not the outcome scalar reported above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001751788-24-000010, Form 10-K FY2023]
