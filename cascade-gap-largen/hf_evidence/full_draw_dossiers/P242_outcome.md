## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Microsemi Corporation filed a Form 15-12G on June 8, 2018, certifying termination of
  registration of its common stock and reporting approximately one holder of record; no
  Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed by Microsemi Corporation itself after that
  date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-188087, Form 15-12G]
- The acquired business's post-close trajectory is instead traceable through Microchip
  Technology Incorporated's own annual reports on Form 10-K, which separately discuss the
  Microsemi acquisition, its purchase accounting, and its ongoing integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-21-000106, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2021)]

## Realized record of the acquired business within Microchip's disclosures (~2021-2023 window)
- As of March 31, 2021, Microchip reported goodwill of $6.67 billion and net intangible
  assets of $4.79 billion, which it stated increased significantly in connection with the
  completion of the Microsemi acquisition; amortization of acquired intangible assets in
  fiscal 2021 was $932.3 million, down from $993.9 million in fiscal 2020, a decrease
  Microchip attributed primarily to lower amortization from the Microsemi acquisition and
  prior acquisitions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-21-000106, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2021)]
- As of March 31, 2021, Microchip stated that for foreign subsidiaries acquired as part of
  the Microsemi acquisition, the U.S. dollar would become the functional currency for such
  entities "once integrated into the Company's legal structure and intercompany
  agreements are executed" — indicating that, nearly three years after closing, that
  integration step had not yet been completed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-21-000106, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2021)]
- Microchip stated that, as a result of the Microsemi acquisition, it acquired several
  smaller wafer fabrication facilities using older technologies, which it planned to
  continue operating with modest investment, with the exception of its Santa Clara,
  California facility, which it planned to close by December 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-21-000106, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2021)]
- As of March 31, 2023, Microchip reported goodwill of $6.67 billion and net intangible
  assets of $3.37 billion (down from $4.79 billion at March 31, 2021), continuing to
  attribute the increase in goodwill and intangible assets to the Microsemi acquisition.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]
- As of March 31, 2023, Microchip described the Santa Clara facility acquired via
  Microsemi as still planned for closure (the facility is again listed as the exception to
  continued modest-investment operation of the acquired older-technology fabrication
  sites), and continued to identify the Microsemi transaction as a source of ongoing
  restructuring/synergy-related costs it was "not able to estimate" in amount going
  forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]
- Microchip disclosed that it "became involved with third-party claims, litigation,
  governmental investigations and disputes related to" the Microsemi and Atmel
  transactions, and as of March 31, 2023 continued to disclose a pending shareholder
  derivative lawsuit (Reid v. Sanghi, et al., Superior Court of Arizona, Maricopa County,
  Case No. CV2019-002389) alleging that defendants breached fiduciary duties by, among
  other things, "purportedly failing to conduct adequate due diligence regarding
  Microsemi prior to its acquisition" and misrepresenting the combined company's business
  prospects. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]
- As of March 31, 2023, Microchip disclosed that, in connection with its acquisitions of
  Microsemi and Atmel, it had assumed unfunded foreign pension plans covering certain
  French and German employees, with a projected benefit obligation of $53.9 million.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]
- Microchip disclosed that it financed the Microsemi acquisition with approximately $8.10
  billion of additional borrowings and stated it "has substantially more debt than we had
  prior to May 2018" as a result; at March 31, 2023, principal outstanding indebtedness
  was $6.47 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Microsemi Corporation was taken private and deregistered in June 2018, there is
  no periodic report in its own name for the ~2021-2023 window; Microchip, the acquirer,
  separately discloses the acquired business across this window through purchase-
  accounting goodwill/intangible-asset figures (declining from $4.79 billion to $3.37

  functional-currency integration step as of FY2021, assumed pension obligations, and a

  that remained unresolved through FY2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-21-000106, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000827054-23-000080, Form 10-K (FY ended March 31, 2023)]
