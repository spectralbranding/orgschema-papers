# Structural sub-dossier — Pinnacle Entertainment, Inc. merger with Penn National Gaming, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Pinnacle Entertainment, Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger dated December 17, 2017 with Penn National Gaming, Inc., a
  Pennsylvania corporation, and Franchise Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and
  wholly owned subsidiary of Penn. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- At the effective time on October 15, 2018, Merger Sub merged with and into Pinnacle,
  with Pinnacle surviving the merger and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Penn.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- Each outstanding share of Pinnacle common stock was converted into the right to
  receive $20.00 in cash plus 0.42 of a share of Penn common stock. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- Outstanding Pinnacle stock options and long-term incentive awards vested and were
  cancelled and converted into the right to receive the merger consideration (or, for a
  portion of awards, assumed by Penn as time-based awards). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]

## What Pinnacle comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- At the time of the merger agreement, Pinnacle owned and operated 16 gaming,
  hospitality and entertainment businesses, of which 15 operated in leased facilities.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001164, Form DEFM14A]
- A majority of Pinnacle's leased real estate was subject to a triple-net master lease
  with Gaming and Leisure Properties, Inc. ("GLPI"), a real estate investment trust,
  originally effective April 28, 2016 — i.e., at the time of this transaction Pinnacle
  already operated an asset-light structure separated from ownership of much of its own
  real estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001164, Form DEFM14A]

## What did NOT cross to Penn: contemporaneous third-party divestitures
- Immediately prior to the effective time, Pinnacle and Penn became parties to a
  Membership Interest Purchase Agreement (the "Boyd Divestiture Agreement") under
  which Boyd Gaming Corporation's subsidiary acquired the gaming operations at
  Ameristar Casino Kansas City and Ameristar Casino St. Charles (Missouri), Belterra
  Casino Resort (Indiana), and Belterra Park (Ohio) — four of Pinnacle's businesses
  that were carved out and sold to Boyd rather than crossing into Penn. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- Concurrently, the real estate associated with the Belterra Park casino was sold under
  a separate purchase agreement to a Penn subsidiary and then leased back to Boyd's
  purchasing entity as part of a new Boyd/GLPI master lease, rather than being retained
  by the surviving Pinnacle entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]

## Employee matters (people/process tier bridging)
- Under the merger agreement, for a period commencing at the effective time and
  ending on the later of December 31, 2019 or the first anniversary of the effective
  time, Penn agreed to provide each continuing Pinnacle employee at least the same base
  salary, substantially similar short- and long-term incentive opportunities, and
  substantially similar other compensation and benefits as immediately before the
  merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001164, Form DEFM14A]
- Penn agreed to waive pre-existing-condition/waiting-period limitations under its
  welfare plans for continuing Pinnacle employees, to credit prior copayments and
  deductibles, to credit accrued vacation, and to give continuing employees full
  service credit under Penn benefit plans for their prior service with Pinnacle
  (excluding certain frozen/grandfathered plans). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-001164, Form DEFM14A]

## Board and management changes at closing
- Upon completion of the merger, the four individuals who had constituted the board of
  Merger Sub (Timothy J. Wilmott, Jay Snowden, William J. Fair and Carl Sottosanti)
  became the directors of the surviving Pinnacle entity, and all eight incumbent
  Pinnacle directors ceased to be directors "not because of any disagreement with the
  Company or its operations, policies or practices." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- Six named Pinnacle executive officers (Anthony M. Sanfilippo, Carlos A. Ruisanchez,
  Donna S. Negrotto, Virginia E. Shanks, Neil E. Walkoff, and Troy A. Stremming) were
  reported to resign effective October 16, 2018, the day after closing. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- At the effective time, Pinnacle's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were
  amended to be in the same form as those of Merger Sub. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On the closing date, Pinnacle notified the Nasdaq Global Select Market that the
  merger had been consummated and requested that trading of its common stock be
  suspended and its listing withdrawn, with Nasdaq to file a Form 25 to remove the
  shares from listing and deregister them under Exchange Act Section 12(b); Pinnacle
  stated its intent to separately file a Form 15 to terminate registration under
  Section 12(g). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904, Form 8-K]
- As a result of the merger, a change of control of Pinnacle occurred and Pinnacle
  became a wholly owned subsidiary of Penn. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904,
  Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company stock-and-cash merger of Pinnacle's gaming
  asset/product tier (12 of its 16 businesses, already operated under a triple-net
  lease/asset-light model) into Penn, with a contemporaneous carve-out of four
  Pinnacle businesses sold to a third party (Boyd) rather than crossing into the
  surviving entity, a full turnover of the board and several named executive officers
  at closing, and a contractual one-year compensation/benefits-parity bridge for
  continuing rank-and-file Pinnacle employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298904,
  Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-001164, Form DEFM14A]
