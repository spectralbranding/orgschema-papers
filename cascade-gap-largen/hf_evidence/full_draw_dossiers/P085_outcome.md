# Outcome sub-dossier — Verizon Communications Inc. / Verizon Wireless realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after closing era

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-closing-era record; it contains no information about how the joint venture was
> structured at closing.

## The realized structural event within the outcome window
- On September 2, 2013, Verizon entered a stock purchase agreement with Vodafone Group
  Plc and Vodafone 4 Limited under which Verizon agreed to acquire Vodafone's indirect
  45% interest in Cellco Partnership d/b/a Verizon Wireless for aggregate consideration
  of approximately $130 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Note 2]
- On February 21, 2014, the transaction closed: Verizon acquired all of the issued and
  outstanding capital stock of Vodafone Americas Finance 1 Inc. and its subsidiaries,
  which indirectly held the Vodafone interest, paying approximately $58.89 billion in
  cash, issuing approximately $60.15 billion of Verizon common stock (approximately
  1.27 billion shares), issuing $5.0 billion of senior unsecured Verizon notes, selling
  Verizon's 23.1% interest in Vodafone Omnitel N.V. valued at $3.5 billion, and
  providing approximately $2.5 billion of other consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Note 2]
- As a result, Verizon acquired 100% ownership of Verizon Wireless; Vodafone's
  noncontrolling interest in the partnership was eliminated and cash distributions from
  Verizon Wireless to Vodafone were discontinued going forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Item 7; SEC accession 0001193125-15-057710, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 7]
- The cash portion and related transaction costs were financed through the incurrence
  of third-party indebtedness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Note 2]

## Realized financial record
- Net income attributable to Verizon was $9,625 million for fiscal year 2014 (the first
  full year following the ownership change), compared with $11,497 million for fiscal
  year 2013 and $875 million for fiscal year 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-057710, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 8]
- The company's effective income tax rate rose to 21.7% in 2014 from 19.6% in 2013,
  which the company attributes primarily to additional income taxes on the incremental
  income from the Wireless Transaction completed February 21, 2014, partially offset by
  tax credits associated with the related Omnitel transaction and by the effect of
  severance/pension charges recorded in 2014 versus severance/pension credits recorded
  in 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-057710, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 7]
- The company separately disclosed that, after the closing, its provision for income
  taxes and cash taxes paid were expected to increase due to 100% ownership of Verizon
  Wireless, and its interest expense was expected to increase due to debt issued to
  finance the transaction — partially offset by the discontinuation of cash
  distributions to Vodafone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Item 7]

## Product/process/organization interface over time
- Verizon Wireless continued to be operated and reported as the same consolidated
  "Verizon Wireless" reportable segment both before and after the February 2014
  ownership change; no reorganization of the customer-facing product, its operating
  network, or its management is documented as accompanying the change in ownership
  percentage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-057710, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 7]
- The transaction is treated in the company's accounting as a change in ownership
  interest while Verizon retained a controlling financial interest throughout,
  recorded as an equity transaction (an adjustment to the carrying amount of the
  noncontrolling interest) rather than as a new acquisition of previously
  unconsolidated assets or operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Note 2]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the outcome window, the joint-venture ownership structure documented at
  closing (Verizon 55% / Vodafone 45%, consolidated with a noncontrolling interest) was
  converted into full 100% Verizon ownership via a $130 billion stock purchase
  agreement signed September 2, 2013 and closed February 21, 2014, financed with cash,
  stock, notes, an asset sale, and other consideration, and funded in part by new
  third-party debt. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-073266, Form 10-K (FY2013), Note 2]
- The realized financial record shows net income attributable to Verizon falling from
  $11,497 million (2013) to $9,625 million (2014) alongside a rise in the effective tax
  rate to 21.7%, developments the company attributes to the tax and financing effects of
  the ownership change combined with severance/pension-charge timing; no disruption at
  the product/process or process/organization interfaces is documented, as the segment's
  operations and management continued unchanged in the company's own reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-057710, Form 10-K (FY2014), Item 7; Item 8]
