# Structural sub-dossier — Urban Edge Properties separation from Vornado Realty Trust (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Vornado Realty L.P. ("VRLP"), the operating partnership of Vornado Realty Trust ("Vornado"), declared the distribution of all of the outstanding common shares of Urban Edge Properties ("UE"), then a wholly-owned subsidiary of VRLP, to Vornado and the other holders of common limited partnership units of VRLP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- UE was described as a newly formed indirect subsidiary of Vornado formed to hold, directly or indirectly, Vornado's shopping center business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transaction was structured as a spin-off effected through a pro rata special distribution of UE common shares: each Vornado common shareholder was entitled to receive one UE common share for every two Vornado common shares held as of the record date, and each VRLP common limited partner one UE share for every two common units held. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- No vote of Vornado shareholders or VRLP limited partners was required and holders were not required to take any action or pay consideration to receive UE shares; the separation of the shopping center business and the distribution were expected to qualify as tax-free for U.S. federal income tax purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- UE applied to list its common shares on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "UE"; Vornado common shares were to continue trading on the NYSE under "VNO". [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset (property portfolio): UE would hold the shopping center business consisting of 79 strip shopping centers located primarily in the Northeast and three malls — one in New Jersey and two in San Juan, Puerto Rico — plus a warehouse park adjacent to the East Hanover strip shopping center property. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Legal vehicle / value-capture model: pursuant to the Separation and Distribution Agreement, the interests in certain properties (including interests in entities holding properties) held directly or indirectly by VRLP were to be contributed or transferred to UE in exchange for 100% of UE's outstanding common shares; immediately following the distribution UE would contribute its interest in the received properties to its operating partnership, UELP, intending to own its properties and conduct substantially all of its business through UELP and its subsidiaries (an UPREIT structure). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / organization: VRLP's Retail employees were to become employees of UE, and UE expected to have approximately 100 employees following the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Management: Jeffrey S. Olson, an industry veteran who joined Vornado on September 1, 2014 to work on the separation, would become UE's Chairman of the Board of Trustees and Chief Executive Officer upon completion; Robert Minutoli, Vornado's then-Executive Vice President-Retail, would be UE's Chief Operating Officer; and they would be joined by the team that managed the strip center and mall portfolio at Vornado, with key department heads averaging ten years' tenure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Continuing overlap of controlling principal: Steven Roth, Vornado's Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, would serve as a trustee of UE after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared substrate retained at Vornado and bridged at closing
- Corporate support functions were not transferred whole with the portfolio: Vornado and its subsidiaries would provide various corporate support services to UE, initially including treasury management, human resources, information technology, tax, financial reporting, SEC compliance and insurance, and possibly other matters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- UE stated it was dependent on Vornado to provide these services under the Transition Services Agreement and that it might be difficult to replace them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- Separation and Distribution Agreement: governed the contribution/transfer of property interests, the pro rata distribution of 100% of UE's shares, and provided the framework for the post-separation relationship with Vornado, including allocation of assets and liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Transition Services Agreement: Vornado would provide certain interim transitional support to UE for approximately two years; the costs of the services were estimated at approximately $3.4 million annually and expected to diminish over time as UE filled vacant positions and built its own infrastructure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Additional agreements entered in connection with the separation included a Tax Matters Agreement and an Employee Matters Agreement, providing for the allocation between UE and Vornado of Vornado's assets, liabilities and obligations attributable to the relevant periods. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction carved Vornado's shopping center business out of an integrated REIT into a newly formed, separately listed public company (UE) via a pro rata special distribution (one UE share per two Vornado shares); UE received the strip-center and mall portfolio (79 strip shopping centers, three malls, and a warehouse park), the Retail employees (~100), and the incumbent portfolio-management team under a newly recruited Chairman/CEO, held through an UPREIT operating partnership (UELP), while corporate support functions (treasury, HR, IT, tax, financial reporting, SEC compliance, insurance) remained at Vornado and were bridged at closing by a Separation and Distribution Agreement, a two-year Transition Services Agreement (~$3.4 million/yr), a Tax Matters Agreement, and an Employee Matters Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-009844, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
