 Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 
 
 On June 3, 2016, Baxalta Incorporated (the &#147; Company &#148;), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire plc, a Jersey public company (&#147; Shire &#148;), pursuant to the closing on June 3, 2016 of the merger of BearTracks, Inc., an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire (the &#147; Merger Sub &#148;), with and into the Company (the &#147; Merger &#148;), with the Company surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire, entered into a Second Supplemental Indenture (the &#147; Second Supplemental Indenture &#148;) to the Indenture, dated June 23, 2015, with Shire and The Bank of New York Mellon Trust Company, N.A. as trustee (the &#147; Trustee &#148;) (as supplemented by the First Supplemental Indenture, dated June 23, 2015 (the &#147; First Supplemental Indenture &#148;), the &#147; Indenture &#148;), relating to the guarantee by Shire of the outstanding notes issued by the Company under the Indenture (the &#147; Shire Guarantee &#148;), consisting of $375,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s floating rate senior notes due 2018 (the &#147; Floating Rate Notes &#148;), $375,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s 2.000% senior notes due 2018 (the &#147; 2018 Notes &#148;), $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s 2.875% senior notes due 2020 (the &#147; 2020 Notes &#148;), $500,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s 3.600% senior notes due 2022 (the &#147; 2022 Notes &#148;), $1,750,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s 4.000% senior notes due 2025 (the &#147; 2025 Notes &#148;) and $1,000,000,000 aggregate principal amount of the Company&#146;s 5.250% senior notes due 2045 (the &#147; 2045 Notes &#148; and together with the 2018 Notes, the 2020 Notes, the 2022 Notes and the 2025 Notes, the &#147; Fixed Rate Notes &#148;). The Fixed Rate Notes and the Floating Rate Notes are collectively hereinafter referred to as the &#147; Notes ,&#148; and each of the Floating Rate Notes, the 2018 Notes, the 2020 Notes, the 2022 Notes, the 2025 Notes and the 2045 Notes are hereinafter referred to as a &#147; series &#148; of Notes. 
 
 Under the terms of the Second Supplemental Indenture, Shire fully and unconditionally guarantees to the holders of each series of the Notes and to the Trustee the full and punctual payment when due, whether at stated maturity, by acceleration or otherwise, of the principal of, premium, if any, on and interest on each series of Securities and any other amounts due and payable under the Indenture, subject to any applicable grace period or notice requirement or both. 
 
 The foregoing description of the Second Supplemental Indenture does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Second Supplemental Indenture, which is filed as Exhibit 4.1 hereto and is incorporated by reference into this Item 1.01. 
 
 As a result of the Shire Guarantee, the Company, as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire, will be exempt from any reporting requirements of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the &#147; Exchange Act &#148;) that may apply to the Company as a result of the Notes. In connection therewith, Shire will include the information required by Section 3-10 of Regulation S-X in its financial statements. 
 
 Item 1.02. Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement. 
 
 On June 3, 2016, the Company terminated the Five-Year Credit Agreement, dated as of July 1, 2015, as amended, among Baxalta Incorporated as Borrower, JPMorgan Chase Bank, National Association, as Administrative Agent and certain other financial institutions named therein. 
 
 Item 2.01. Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets. 
 
 As previously disclosed, on January 11, 2016, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the &#147; Merger Agreement &#148;) with Shire and Merger Sub , pursuant to which, upon the terms and subject to the conditions thereof, Merger Sub merged with and into the Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire. 
 
 At the effective time (the &#147; Effective Time &#148;) of the Merger on June 3, 2016, each outstanding share of common stock of the Company, par value $0.01 per share (each a &#147; Share &#148;) (other than: (i) Shares held by the Company as treasury stock, (ii) any Shares owned by Shire, Merger Sub or any other direct or indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire, and (iii) Shares held by the Company&#146;s stockholders who have properly exercised appraisal rights with respect to such Shares in accordance with Section 262 of the General Corporation Law of the State of Delaware) was canceled and converted into the right to receive, in each case without interest and subject to any applicable withholding taxes: (i) $18.00 in cash (the &#147; Per Share Cash Consideration &#148;), and (ii)(a) 0.1482 of an American Depositary Share of Shire (a &#147; Shire ADS &#148;) or (b) if a stockholder of the Company validly elected, 0.4446 of an ordinary share of Shire (a &#147; Shire Ordinary Share &#148;) in lieu of such fraction of a Shire ADS described in (ii)(a) above (the consideration described in (ii)(a) and (b) above, the &#147; Per Share Stock Consideration &#148; and, collectively with the per share cash consideration, the &#147; Per Share Merger Consideration &#148;). 
 
 Dyax Corp. (&#147; Dyax &#148;), an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of Shire, paid approximately $12.37 billion in cash and Shire issued approximately 305.2 million Shire ordinary shares in the aggregate in consideration in the Merger. 
 
 The foregoing description of the Merger and the Merger Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Merger Agreement, which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to the Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the &#147; SEC &#148;) by the Company on January 11, 2016, and which is incorporated herein by reference. 
 
 Item 3.01. Notice of Delisting or Failure to Satisfy a Continued Listing Rule or Standard; Transfer of Listing. 
 
 In connection with the consummation of the Merger, the Company requested that the New York Stock Exchange (the &#147; NYSE &#148;) suspend trading of the Shares effective as of prior to the opening of business on June 3, 2016, remove the Shares from listing on the NYSE, and file a notification of removal from listing on Form 25 with the SEC with respect to the delisting of the Shares and the deregistration of the Shares under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. The Company intends to file with the SEC a certification on Form 15 under the Exchange Act, requesting the deregistration of the Shares and suspending the Company&#146;s reporting obligations under Section 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange Act. 
 
 The information set forth in Item 2.01 of this Current Report on Form 8-K is incorporated by reference in this Item 3.01.
