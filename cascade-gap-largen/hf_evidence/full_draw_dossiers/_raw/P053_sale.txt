 Item 2.01 
 Completion of Acquisition or Disposition of Assets 
 As previously disclosed, on May 18, 2018, Rex Energy Corporation (&#147;Rex Energy&#148; or the &#147;Company&#148;) and certain of its wholly owned
subsidiaries (together with the Company, the &#147;Debtors&#148;) filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under chapter 11 of title 11 of the United States Code (the &#147;Bankruptcy Code&#148;) in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the
Western District of Pennsylvania (the &#147;Bankruptcy Court&#148;). The Debtors&#146; chapter 11 cases (the &#147;Chapter 11 Cases&#148;) are being administered under the caption In re R.E. Gas Development, LLC , Case No. 18-22032 (JAD). On September 28, 2018, the Company and certain of its wholly owned subsidiaries (the
&#147;Sellers&#148;) completed the disposition of substantially all of their assets to, and assumption of certain associated liabilities by, PennEnergy Resources, LLC (the &#147;Buyer&#148;) (the &#147;Sale&#148;) pursuant to sections 105, 363 and
365 of the Bankruptcy Code in accordance with the terms of the Asset Purchase Agreement (the &#147;Purchase Agreement&#148;) by and between the Sellers and the Buyer, dated August 24, 2018, as amended by that certain Amendment to the Purchase
Agreement, dated September 28, 2018, by and between the Sellers and the Buyer. The aggregate purchase price for the Sale was $600.5 million, subject to certain adjustments. The Sale was previously approved by the Bankruptcy Court on
August 31, 2018. The material terms of the Purchase Agreement were reported under the heading &#147;Asset Purchase Agreement&#148; in Item 1.01 of
the Company&#146;s Current Report on Form 8-K filed with the Securities Exchange Commission on August 27, 2018 and are incorporated herein by reference.
