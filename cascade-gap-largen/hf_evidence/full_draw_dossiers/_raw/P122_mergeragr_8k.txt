 Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 
 
 Merger Agreement 
 
 On June 17, 2018, Corporate Property Associates 17 &#150; Global Incorporated (&#147;CPA &#174; :17&#148;) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the &#147;Merger Agreement&#148;) with W. P. Carey Inc., a Maryland corporation and the ultimate parent of the external advisor of CPA &#174; :17 (&#147;W. P. Carey&#148;), CPA17 Merger Sub LLC, a Maryland limited liability company and indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey (&#147;Merger Sub&#148;) and, for the limited purposes set forth therein, Carey Asset Management Corp. (&#147;CAM&#148;), W. P. Carey & Co. B.V. (&#147;Foreign Subsidiary&#148;) and W. P. Carey Holdings, LLC (&#147;Special General Partner&#148;), each an indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey, and CPA &#174; : 17 Limited Partnership, a Delaware limited partnership (&#147;CPA17 LP&#148;). Upon the terms and subject to the conditions set forth in the Merger Agreement, CPA &#174; :17 will merge with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub surviving the merger as an indirect subsidiary of W. P. Carey (the &#147;Merger&#148;). 
 
 Subject to the terms and conditions of the Merger Agreement, each CPA &#174; :17 stockholder (the &#147;CPA17 Stockholders&#148;) of record as of the Effective Time (as defined in the Merger Agreement) will receive, for each share of CPA &#174; :17 common stock, $0.001 par value per share, 0.160 shares (the &#147;Exchange Ratio&#148;) of W. P. Carey common stock, $0.001 par value per share. All CPA17 Stockholders entitled to receive fractional shares will receive cash in lieu of fractional shares. 
 
 The Exchange Ratio was determined as a result of negotiations between the board of directors of W. P. Carey and a special committee of independent directors of the board of directors of CPA &#174; :17 (the &#147;CPA17 Special Committee&#148;), with the assistance of separate financial and legal advisors. 
 
 The Merger Agreement contains customary representations, warranties and covenants of CPA &#174; :17 and W. P. Carey, including, among others, covenants (i) to conduct their respective businesses in the ordinary course during the period between the execution of the Merger Agreement and consummation of the Merger and (ii) not to engage in certain kinds of transactions during such period. 
 
 During the period beginning on June 18, 2018 and continuing until 11:59 p.m. (New York City time) on July 18, 2018 (the &#147;Solicitation Period End Date&#148;), CPA &#174; :17 may initiate, solicit and encourage competing transactions from third parties, including by furnishing non-public information, and participate in discussions with third parties with respect to competing transactions. From and after the Solicitation Period End Date, CPA &#174; :17 will be subject to customary &#147;no-shop&#148; restrictions on its ability to solicit competing transactions from third parties and to provide non-public information to and engage in discussions with third parties regarding competing transactions, except that in certain circumstances CPA &#174; :17 may continue to engage in the aforementioned activities with certain third parties that made a proposal for a competing transaction before the Solicitation Period End Date or with third parties that made an unsolicited proposal thereafter if the CPA17 Special Committee determines in good faith that such proposal would result in, or is reasonably likely to result in, a Superior Competing Transaction (as defined in the Merger Agreement). 
 
 The consummation of the Merger is subject to various conditions, including (a) approval of the Merger by the stockholders of W. P. Carey and the CPA17 Stockholders, (b) absence of any law or order prohibiting the consummation of the Merger, (c) effectiveness of a registration statement on Form S-4 (the &#147;Form S-4&#148;) relating to the shares of W. P. Carey common stock to be issued to the CPA17 Stockholders in connection with the Merger, (d) approval for the listing on the NYSE of the shares of W. P. Carey common stock to be issued to the CPA17 Stockholders in connection with the Merger, (e) obtaining of all consents, approvals, permits and authorizations and (f) other customary closing conditions. In addition, W. P. Carey&#146;s and CPA &#174; :17&#146;s respective obligations to consummate the Merger are subject to certain other conditions, including, among others, (1) subject to the standards set forth in the Merger Agreement, accuracy of the other party&#146;s representations and warranties, (2) performance of the other party&#146;s covenants in the Merger Agreement in all material respects, (3) delivery of opinions from counsel relating to the U.S. federal income tax code treatment of the Merger and the tax status of certain of the parties to the Merger Agreement and (4) no occurrence of a change, event or circumstance individually or in the aggregate constituting a material adverse effect on the other party and its subsidiaries. 

 The Merger Agreement contains certain termination rights for both CPA &#174; :17 and W. P. Carey. Each of CPA &#174; :17 and W. P. Carey has agreed that if the Merger Agreement is terminated because it breaches any of its representations, warranties, covenants or agreements, it will pay the other party&#146;s out-of-pocket expenses. CPA &#174; :17 has agreed that if its board of directors withdraws or adversely modifies its recommendation of the Merger or the Merger Agreement, or approves or recommends a Superior Competing Transaction, or if CPA &#174; :17 enters into an agreement for a Superior Competing Transaction, CPA &#174; :17 will pay W. P. Carey a termination fee of $114.0 million (the &#147;CPA17 Termination Fee&#148;), which will be reduced to $38.0 million in the event CPA &#174; :17 enters into a Superior Competing Transaction in connection and in compliance with the &#147;go-shop&#148; provisions of the Merger Agreement. 
 
 CAM, Foreign Subsidiary, Special General Partner and certain of their affiliates provide advisory services and serve as special general partner to CPA &#174; :17 and its operating partnership subsidiary (the &#147;OP&#148;) pursuant to certain advisory agreements and special general partner arrangements. If the Merger is consummated, W. P. Carey has agreed that no subordinated disposition fees shall be payable to it under the advisory agreements, and W. P. Carey has further agreed to waive all rights under the OP&#146;s limited partnership agreement to receive (i) distributions of capital proceeds upon a change of control event and related allocation of profits and losses and (ii) rights to amounts in respect of the Special General Partner&#146;s interest in the OP (the &#147;Special GP Amount&#148;), that are payable to W. P. Carey and its affiliates in respect of the Merger or any competing transaction ((i) and (ii) collectively, the &#147;Advisor Closing Amounts&#148;). W. P. Carey retains the rights to such fees and Advisor Closing Amounts if the Merger is not consummated; provided, however, that if (x) the Merger Agreement is terminated by CPA &#174; :17 or W. P. Carey in certain circumstances described in the Merger Agreement relating to a withdrawal or change in CPA &#174; :17&#146;s recommendation of the Merger or CPA &#174; :17&#146;s approval of a Superior Competing Transaction, (y) the required CPA17 Termination Fee is paid by CPA &#174; :17 and (z) the Advisor Closing Amounts become payable as a result thereof, an amount equal to the lesser of the CPA17 Termination Fee and the Special GP Amount will be credited against the Advisor Closing Amounts and no subordinated disposition fees shall be payable to W. P. Carey. 
 
 The parties to the Merger Agreement intend that the Merger satisfy applicable requirements to qualify as a tax-deferred reorganization. 
 
 The foregoing descriptions of the Merger Agreement and the transactions contemplated thereby are not complete and are subject to and qualified in their entirety by reference to the Merger Agreement, a copy of which is filed as Exhibit 2.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and is incorporated herein by reference.
