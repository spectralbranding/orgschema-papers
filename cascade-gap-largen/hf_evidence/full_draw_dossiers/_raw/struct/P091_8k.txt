 Item 8.01. Other
Events. 

 Effective
June 14, 2007, Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) amended
its articles of incorporation to add a new Article XVI providing that, if in
the opinion of the Company&#146;s board of directors the provisions of the Statement
of Policy Regarding Real Estate Investment Trusts adopted by the North American
Securities Administrators Association, Inc. on September 29, 1993 (the &#147;NASAA
REIT Guidelines&#148;) conflict with the provisions of the Maryland General
Corporation Law, as amended (the &#147;MGCL&#148;), the NASAA REIT Guidelines will
control, except where the MGCL provisions are mandatory. The amendment was previously approved and
adopted by the Company&#146;s stockholders at the Company&#146;s annual meeting held June
4, 2007. A copy of the Fifth Articles of
Amendment and Restatement is attached hereto as Exhibit 3.1 and is
incorporated into this Item 8.01 disclosure by reference.
