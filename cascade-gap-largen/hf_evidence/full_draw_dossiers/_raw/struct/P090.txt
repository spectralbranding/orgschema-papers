 Item 1.01 
 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement SunEdison, Inc., a debtor and
debtor-in-possession (the &#147;Company&#148;) in a pending case in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York (the &#147;Bankruptcy Court&#148;) under Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code, obtained certain
amendments to that certain Senior Secured Superpriority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement, dated as of April 26, 2016 (the &#147;DIP Credit Agreement&#148;), entered into by and among the Company, the lenders from time to time party thereto
(the &#147;DIP Lenders&#148;), Deutsche Bank AG New York Branch, as administrative agent (in such capacity, the &#147;DIP Agent&#148;), and the letter of credit issuers and other financial institutions and entities party thereto from time to time.
Such amendments are reflected in that certain Amendment No. 1 to Senior Secured Superpriority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement, dated as of May 18, 2016 (the &#147;Amendment&#148;), among the Company, the DIP Lenders party thereto and
the DIP Agent. The Amendment modifies certain provisions in the DIP Credit Agreement relating to, among other things: (a) the
Tranche B roll-up loans and the syndication of Tranche B loans; (b) post-closing guaranty and collateral matters; (c) certain milestones relating to the Company&#146;s restructuring efforts and (d) designated assets that constitute
permitted dispositions under the DIP Credit Agreement. In connection with the Amendment, the requisite DIP Lenders also approved an updated 13-week budget. 
 The foregoing description of the Amendment does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the text of such
document, which is filed as Exhibit 10.1 hereto and is incorporated by reference herein.
