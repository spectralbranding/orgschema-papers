 Item 7.01 
 Regulation FD Disclosure. 
 As previously reported, on October 15, 2018 (the &#147;Petition Date&#148;), Sears Holdings Corporation (the &#147;Company&#148;) and certain of its
subsidiaries (collectively, the &#147;Debtors&#148;) filed voluntary petitions (the &#147;Chapter 11 Cases&#148;) in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York (the &#147;Bankruptcy Court&#148;) seeking relief under
chapter 11 of title 11 of the United States Code. On December 28, 2018, the Debtors filed with the Bankruptcy Court their monthly operating report
for the period beginning October 15, 2018 and ending November 3, 2018 (the &#147;October Monthly Operating Report&#148;), and their monthly operating report for the period beginning November 4, 2018 and ending December 1, 2018
(the &#147;November Monthly Operating Report&#148;). On January 25, 2019, the Debtors filed with the Bankruptcy Court their monthly operating report for the period beginning December 2, 2018 and ending January 5, 2019 (the
&#147;December Monthly Operating Report&#148;, and together with the October Monthly Operating Report and the November Monthly Operating Report, the &#147;Monthly Operating Reports&#148;). The October Monthly Operating Report, the November Monthly
Operating Report and the December Monthly Operating Report are attached hereto, respectively, as Exhibits 99.1, 99.2 and 99.3, and are incorporated herein by reference. This Current Report on Form 8-K 
(including the exhibits hereto) will not be deemed an admission as to the materiality of any information required to be disclosed solely by Regulation FD. The Monthly Operating Reports and other filings with the Bankruptcy Court related to the
Chapter 11 Cases may be available electronically at http://restructuring.primeclerk.com/sears . The information contained in this Item 7.01 shall
not be deemed to be &#147;filed&#148; for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the &#147;Exchange Act&#148;), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, and shall not be deemed to be
incorporated by reference into any of the Company&#146;s filings under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, whether made before or after the date hereof and regardless of any general incorporation language in such filings,
except to the extent expressly set forth by specific reference in such a filing. Cautionary Statement Regarding the Monthly Operating Reports
