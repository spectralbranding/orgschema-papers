 Item 8.01. Other Events. 
 
 On October 2, 2015, Smart & Final Stores, Inc. (the &#147;Company&#148;) announced that Smart & Final Stores LLC (&#147;Smart & Final&#148;), an indirect subsidiary of the Company, has entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (&#147;Purchase Agreement&#148;) whereby Smart & Final has agreed to become a &#147;stalking horse bidder&#148; to acquire certain assets, including 28 store leases and related assets, of Haggen Operations Holdings, LLC and Haggen Opco South, LLC (together, &#147;Haggen&#148;), for a purchase price of $56 million, subject to certain adjustments (the &#147;Acquisition&#148;). 
 
 Haggen has filed the Purchase Agreement with the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware (the &#147;Court&#148;) along with Haggen&#146;s motion seeking the establishment of bid procedures for an auction that allows other qualified bidders to submit higher or otherwise better offers, as required under Section 363 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code. The closing of the Acquisition, which remains subject to higher or otherwise better offers, approval by the Court and customary closing conditions, would be expected to close in the Company&#146;s fourth quarter of 2015. If Haggen accepts an alternative offer other than pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, Smart & Final will be entitled to be paid a break-up fee and a reimbursement of its transaction expenses. 
 
 Pursuant to the Purchase Agreement, Smart & Final will acquire certain leasehold interests in California and Nevada, as well as associated improvements, fixtures, equipment, permits and licenses, and will assume certain specified liabilities of Haggen. Haggen will retain inventory, pharmacy assets, prescription files and certain other assets with respect to each of the stores. 
 
 The foregoing description of the terms of the Purchase Agreement is qualified in its entirety by reference to the Purchase Agreement, a copy of which is included as Exhibit 99.1 hereto and incorporated herein by reference in its entirety. 
 
 On October 5, 2015, the Company issued a press release in connection with the foregoing. The text of the press release is included as Exhibit 99.2 to this Form 8-K.
