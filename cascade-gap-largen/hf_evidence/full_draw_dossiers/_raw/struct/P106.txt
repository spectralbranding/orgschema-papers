 Item 8.01 Other Events. 
 
 On June 9, 2016, WL Ross Holding Corp. (now known as Nexeo Solutions, Inc., the &#147;Company&#148;) issued a press release announcing the completion of its business combination with Nexeo Solutions Holdings, LLC. The full text of the press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated in its entirety herein by reference. 
 
 In connection with the completion of the business combination, the Company&#146;s stockholders approved an amendment to the Company&#146;s certificate of incorporation to allow it to continue as a corporate entity with perpetual existence. As such, the special meeting of stockholders called for the purpose of extending the expiration of the Company&#146;s existence, which was initially convened on June 8, 2016 and adjourned to June 10, 2016, has been cancelled.
