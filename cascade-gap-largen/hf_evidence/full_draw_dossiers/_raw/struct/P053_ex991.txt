 Rex Energy Announces Joint Venture Agreement in Butler Operated Area, 
 Re-determined Borrowing Base and Amendment to Credit Facility 

 &#149; 
 
 Joint venture agreement in Moraine East Area and Legacy Butler Operated Area 

 &#149; 
 
 Operating capital expenditures reduced by ~30% 

 &#149; 
 
 New senior secured leverage covenant of 3.0x with total leverage covenant removed STATE COLLEGE, Pa.,
March 31, 2015 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) &#150; Rex Energy Corporation (Nasdaq: REXX) (&#147;the company&#148;) announced a new joint venture agreement in the Butler Operated Area, re-determined borrowing base and amended financial covenants to the
senior secured credit facility. Joint Venture Agreement 
 Rex Energy has entered into a joint venture agreement with an affiliate of ArcLight Capital Partners, LLC (&#147;ArcLight&#148;) to jointly develop 32
specifically designated wells in the company&#146;s Butler Operated Area. ArcLight will participate and fund 35% of the well costs in the designated wells. The joint venture agreement is expected to reduce the company&#146;s 2015 capital expenditure
program by $60 million to $135 - $145 million, or a decrease of 30% at the midpoint of guidance. Total consideration for the transaction will equal $67 million, with $16.6 million received at closing. Upon achieving certain threshold triggers of IRR
and ROI on specific well groups, ArcLight&#146;s working interest will revert to 17.5%. In addition, ArcLight will have the option to participate as a 20% working interest partner in 17 additional Moraine East wells in 2016. 
 &#147;This joint venture is consistent with our 2015 strategy to further HBP our liquids-rich prospects in the Butler Operated Area, specifically in the
Moraine East Area&#148;, said Tom Stabley, Rex Energy&#146;s Chief Executive Officer. &#147;This new relationship provides greater flexibility for Rex to manage its capital program, while continuing to increase production and reserves. We continue
to evaluate solutions that will further enhance our ability to develop the Butler Operated Area.&#148; Borrowing Base 
 Rex Energy&#146;s bank group has unanimously approved an amendment to the company&#146;s senior secured credit facility. The approved amendment includes a new
senior secured debt to EBITDAX covenant of 3.0x and permanently removes the total debt to trailing twelve months EBITDAX covenant. In addition, as part of the semi-annual borrowing base redetermination, the banks have unanimously approved a $350
million borrowing base. &#147;I would like to thank the bank group for our strong relationship, and for their continued support of Rex Energy&#146;s
strategy&#148;, said Tom Stabley, Chief Executive Officer of Rex Energy. Production and Operating Capital Expenditure Guidance 
 Rex Energy is providing an update for its first quarter 2015 production, full year 2015 production and operating capital expenditure guidance, which takes into
account the new joint venture agreement in the Butler Operated Area. The company expects first quarter 2015 production volumes to be at the midpoint of its previously announced guidance of 190 &#150; 196 MMcfe/d. Full year 2015 production volumes
are

 Rex Energy is headquartered in State College, Pennsylvania and is an independent oil and gas exploration and production company operating in the Appalachian
