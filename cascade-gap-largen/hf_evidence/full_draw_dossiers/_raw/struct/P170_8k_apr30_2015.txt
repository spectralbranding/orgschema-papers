 Item 8.01. Other Events. 
 On April 30, 2015, Exide Technologies, a Delaware corporation (the &#147;Company&#148;), announced that it had emerged from bankruptcy as a
newly reorganized company. A copy of the Company&#146;s press release is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1 and is incorporated herein by reference. 
 In connection with its emergence from bankruptcy, the Company will file a Form 15 with the Securities and Exchange Commission soon after the
filing of this Current Report, pursuant to which the Company&#146;s reporting obligations under Sections 13 and 15(d) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended, will be suspended immediately. Following the filing of the Form 15,
information to be delivered to holders of the Company&#146;s securities will be provided through a private website or third party service provider.
