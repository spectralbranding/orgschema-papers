# Structural sub-dossier — Quinpario Acquisition Corp. 2 business combination with SourceHOV and Novitex (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Quinpario Acquisition Corp. 2 ("Quinpario") was a Delaware special purpose acquisition
  company formed in July 2014 to effect a merger or similar business combination; its
  stockholders were asked to approve a "Business Combination Agreement" (as amended) to
  acquire Novitex Holdings, Inc. ("Novitex") and SourceHOV Holdings, Inc. ("SourceHOV").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- The Business Combination was structured as two parallel wholly-owned-subsidiary
  mergers: SourceHOV Merger Sub merged with and into SourceHOV (SourceHOV surviving as
  an indirect subsidiary), and Novitex Merger Sub merged with and into Novitex (Novitex
  surviving as an indirect subsidiary); Novitex's equity holders were entitled to
  30,600,000 shares of Quinpario Common Stock at closing, and a newly formed entity owned
  by SourceHOV's former equity holders was entitled to 80,600,000 shares. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- Closing was conditioned on the sum of the Trust Account balance and PIPE Investment
  proceeds being at least $275.0 million; as of March 31, 2017 the Trust Account held
  approximately $201.1 million, with $275.5 million in PIPE Investment funds committed.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- The Business Combination closed on July 12, 2017; concurrently the Company entered a
  First Lien Credit Agreement ($350 million term loan, $100 million revolver, via
  wholly owned subsidiary Exela Intermediate LLC) and issued $1 billion of 10% First
  Priority Senior Secured Notes due 2023 (via Exela Intermediate LLC and Exela Finance
  Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-045583, Form 8-K]
- Also at closing, the Company's certificate of incorporation was amended to change its
  name from "Quinpario Acquisition Corp. 2" to "Exela Technologies, Inc."; the common
  stock, previously listed on Nasdaq as "QPAC," was continued for listing under the
  symbol "XELA." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession
  0001104659-17-045583, Form 8-K]
- At closing the Company's status changed "from a 'shell' company to an operating
  company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-045584, Form 8-K, Item 5.06]

## What crossed at closing — the product/business-model tier
- SourceHOV was described as "a leading provider of platform-based enterprise
  information management ('EIM') and transaction processing solutions ('TPS')" across
  three segments (Information and Transaction Processing Solutions; Healthcare
  Solutions; Legal & Loss Prevention Services), serving clients from approximately 120
  delivery centers with approximately 16,000 employees as of March 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- Novitex was described as "a North American provider of document management and
  digital business process services," organized around Digital Services (customer
  communications management, recurring document processing, business process
  automation, managed print services) and Document Logistics (delivery logistics,
  mailroom management, reprographics), branded as the "Integrated Document Life
  Cycle" (IDLC), with approximately 400 clients and approximately 7,000 employees
  across approximately 1,200 client sites as of March 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- Both target businesses (their customer-facing product lines/platforms) crossed into
  the renamed combined company as indirect subsidiaries; the proxy identified expected
  cost synergies of "over $70.3 million in total run-rate cost savings" concentrated in
  Information Technology, Operations, Facilities, and "Corporate and Shared Services"
  (finance, accounting, legal, human resources) to be consolidated post-closing, rather
  than a pre-closing transitional-services or shared-services agreement bridging the
  two operating companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed at closing — the management/people tier
- The proxy stated that "the management of the newly formed entity is expected to be
  primarily composed of the management of SourceHOV," naming Par Chadha (Executive
  Chairman), Ronald Cogburn (Chief Executive Officer), Jim Reynolds (Chief Financial
  Officer), Shrikant Sortur, Suresh Yannamani, and Mark Fairchild. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- The July 18, 2017 Form 8-K's post-closing executive-officer/director table lists Par
  Chadha, Ronald Cogburn, Jim Reynolds, Suresh Yannamani, Mark Fairchild, and Shrikant
  Sortur alongside new outside directors; Novitex's pre-closing Executive Chairman and
  CEO, Giovanni Visentin, does not appear on this post-closing roster. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001104659-17-045584, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]
- Novitex's named executives (Visentin, Joseph Trost, Irina Novoselsky) held pre-existing
  employment agreements, 2016 retention-bonus agreements, and proprietary-interest
  protection agreements with Novitex Acquisition, LLC, disclosed in the proxy as
  compensation arrangements of the Novitex entity rather than of the post-closing
  combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]
- All of Quinpario's own pre-closing directors and officers (Paul J. Berra III, D. John
  Srivisal, A. Craig Ivey, and five other directors) resigned effective upon closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-045584, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]

## Sponsor / controlling-interest economics at closing
- The closing resulted in a change of control: an entity group affiliated with HOVS
  LLC, HandsOn Fund 4 I, LLC and Par Chadha (the "HGM Group") received 80,600,000 shares
  of Common Stock plus PIPE-Investment shares, giving it beneficial ownership of
  approximately 59.5% of Common Stock; the Company determined it was a "controlled
  company" under Nasdaq listing standards as a result. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001104659-17-045583, Form 8-K]
- Quinpario's Sponsor (Quinpario Partners 2, LLC), which had paid an aggregate of
  $25,000 for its Founder Shares, agreed under a Forfeiture Agreement to cancel
  18,000,000 Private Placement Warrants and 716,429 Founder Shares at closing; the
  Sponsor was permitted to retain 8,033,571 Founder Shares, of which three parties
  received an interest in 6,133,571 shares as consideration for facilitating the
  Business Combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two whole, already-operating businesses (SourceHOV and
  Novitex) into a renamed public shell via parallel reverse-triangular mergers, with
  their customer-facing product platforms and headcount crossing intact as indirect
  subsidiaries and no transitional-services agreement identified; leadership of the
  combined company was drawn primarily from SourceHOV's management, and Novitex's own
  pre-closing chief executive did not appear among the combined company's post-closing
  named executive officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004226, Form DEFM14A; SEC
  accession 0001104659-17-045584, Form 8-K]
