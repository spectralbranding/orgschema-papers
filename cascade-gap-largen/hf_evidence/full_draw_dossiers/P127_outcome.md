# Outcome sub-dossier — Sila Realty Trust, Inc. (formerly Carter Validus Mission Critical REIT II, Inc.), realized record ~2019-2021

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The registrant remained a reporting company
> throughout the fixed 3-5-year outcome window and continues to file periodic reports;
> the realized record below is drawn from its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
> December 31, 2021. This sub-dossier contains no information about how the entity or
> its portfolio was structured at 2017 closing.

## Entity continuity and identity change
- The registrant remained SEC-reporting throughout the outcome window (it has continued
  to file annual reports through at least fiscal year 2025) and was not deregistered or
  absorbed into an unrelated acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-26-000008, Form 10-K FY2025 (EDGAR filing list)]
- By the fiscal year 2021 Form 10-K, the registrant's name had changed to "Sila Realty
  Trust, Inc."; corresponding subsidiary entities were also renamed (e.g., "Sila Realty
  Operating Partnership, LP," "Sila Realty Management Company, LLC"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Prior sister-REIT merger (2019, near start of window)
- On October 4, 2019, a merger closed with Carter Validus Mission Critical REIT, Inc.
  ("the REIT Merger"), a separate, affiliated non-traded REIT sponsored by the same
  Advisor family; a $500,000,000 term loan was funded upon consummation of that merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Internalization of management (September 30, 2020)
- On July 28, 2020, the registrant and its Operating Partnership entered into a
  Membership Interest Purchase Agreement to internalize the external management
  functions previously performed by its Advisor (the "Internalization Transaction"),
  which closed September 30, 2020; the Former Advisor ceased to be affiliated with the
  registrant, and individuals previously employed by an affiliate of the Former Advisor
  became direct employees of the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- As part of the Internalization Transaction the registrant recognized goodwill across
  three reporting units; during the year ended December 31, 2021 it wrote off
  approximately $671,000 of that goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- As of December 31, 2021 (post-internalization, post-divestiture), the registrant
  reported 63 employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Divestiture of the data center segment (2021)
- The Board determined to focus on the registrant's healthcare properties and sell its
  data center properties; on May 19, 2021 the registrant and subsidiaries entered into a
  purchase and sale agreement for the entirety of the data center segment (29
  properties). The filing itself characterizes this as "a strategic shift that had a
  major effect on our results and operations and assets and liabilities." [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- On July 22, 2021 the registrant completed the sale of all 29 data center properties
  (~3,298,000 rentable square feet) for an aggregate sale price of $1,320,000,000,
  generating net proceeds of approximately $1,295,367,000 and a gain of approximately
  $395,801,000. Proceeds funded a special cash distribution of $1.75/share
  (approximately $392,685,000 in aggregate, paid July 30, 2021) and repayment of all
  data-center-related notes payable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- As of December 31, 2021, following the divestiture, the registrant owned 125 real
  estate healthcare properties (~5,245,000 rentable square feet) across 54 metropolitan
  and two micropolitan statistical areas, 99.5% leased — i.e., its two-segment
  (data-center + healthcare) portfolio at 2017 had become a single-segment,
  healthcare-only portfolio by year-end 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Best-documented financial outcome scalar
- Net income attributable to common stockholders was $402,660,000 for the year ended
  December 31, 2021, versus $36,776,000 for the year ended December 31, 2020 — an
  increase driven substantially by the one-time $395,801,000 gain on the Data Center
  Sale rather than by recurring operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The registrant recorded an impairment loss on real estate of $27,166,000 for the year
  ended December 31, 2021 (versus none reported for 2020 in the same reconciliation
  table). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only, ~2019-2021 window)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year outcome window, the entity did not fail or deregister;
  instead it underwent three sequential, sourced structural changes to its post-2017
  substrate: a merger with its affiliated sister REIT (2019), a transition from
  externally-managed to internally-managed operations that moved the Advisor's
  personnel onto the registrant's own payroll (2020), and a complete divestiture of one
  of its two founding asset segments — the data center portfolio — realized at a large
  one-time gain that dominates the period's reported net income (2021), leaving a
  single-segment healthcare REIT renamed Sila Realty Trust, Inc. by year-end 2021.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567925-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
