## Realized financial trajectory through the first full reporting year
- For fiscal year 2018 (the registrant's first full year following the February 9, 2018 closing), the Form 10-K — filed August 27, 2019, several months after its normal due date — reported a net loss for the "Successor" period (February 9-December 31, 2018) of $3,249,347 thousand on a total basis, of which $1,524,699 thousand was attributable to Alta Mesa Resources, Inc. stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-19-000059, Form 10-K FY2018, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The FY2018 10-K attributes the loss "mainly to impairment of our proved and unproved oil and gas properties and the long-lived assets, intangible assets and goodwill of our Midstream segment," reporting total impairment of assets of $3,205.1 million, comprising $2,033.7 million in the Upstream segment (unproved properties $742.1 million; proved properties $1,291.6 million) and $1,171.3 million in the Midstream segment (property/equipment $68.4 million; intangible assets $395.0 million; goodwill $692.0 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-19-000059, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A / Notes to Financial Statements]
- The same Form 10-K disclosed that current liabilities exceeded current assets by approximately $813.5 million at December 31, 2018, and that management "concluded that these circumstances create substantial doubt regarding our ability to continue as a going concern," citing indebtedness covenants and the prevailing commodity-price environment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-19-000059, Form 10-K FY2018, "Going Concern"]

## Documented events over time
- On September 11, 2019, Alta Mesa Resources, Inc. and certain subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, jointly administered under Case No. 19-35133. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-19-000068, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On September 13, 2019, Nasdaq notified the company that, as a result of the Chapter 11 filing, its common stock would be removed from listing and registration on Nasdaq; trading was suspended at the opening of business on September 24, 2019, with the company expecting to trade over-the-counter under "AMRQ" and "AMRWWQ." [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-19-000068, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- On January 12, 2020, additional subsidiaries — including SRII Opco GP, LLC, SRII Opco, LP, Kingfisher Midstream, LLC, and related Kingfisher entities — also filed Chapter 11 petitions, jointly administered with the September 2019 cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-20-000006, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- On December 31, 2019, subsidiaries entered two Purchase and Sale Agreements with a single buyer, BCE-Mach III LLC: the Kingfisher assets (gathering systems, pipelines, compressor stations and related facilities) for total cash consideration of $85,250,000, and substantially all of the Alta Mesa upstream assets (wells, leases and related equipment) for total cash consideration of $224,750,000 — combined cash consideration of $310,000,000, implemented through the Chapter 11 proceedings and contingent on Bankruptcy Court approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-20-000003, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On January 24, 2020, the Bankruptcy Court approved execution of both purchase and sale agreements ("the Bankruptcy Sales"), with closing expected in February 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-20-000027, Form 15-12B]
- On February 13 and February 19, 2020, the registrant filed Forms 15-12G and 15-12B terminating/suspending its duty to file reports under the Securities Exchange Act, reporting approximately 66 holders of record of its warrants at that time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001690769-20-000027, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant's first full post-closing fiscal year (2018) produced a reported net loss of approximately $3.2 billion, driven primarily by $3,205.1 million of combined upstream and midstream asset/intangible/goodwill impairments, alongside a disclosed going-concern qualification. Roughly nineteen months after closing the registrant and its subsidiaries entered Chapter 11 proceedings and were removed from Nasdaq listing; substantially all operating assets (both the upstream and midstream businesses) were subsequently sold in bankruptcy to a single private buyer for combined cash consideration of $310 million, and the registrant terminated its SEC registration in February 2020. No public periodic-report record of the underlying businesses exists for the remainder of the originally fixed 3-5-year outcome window.
