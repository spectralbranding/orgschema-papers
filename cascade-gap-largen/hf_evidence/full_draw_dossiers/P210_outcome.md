## Acquirer's disclosure naming the acquired properties (Penn National Gaming / PENN Entertainment, CIK 0000921738)
- Penn National Gaming's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed 2023-02-23) states that
  in October 2018 the company completed the acquisition of Pinnacle Entertainment,
  Inc. ("the Pinnacle Acquisition"), adding 12 gaming properties to its portfolio and
  assuming a triple-net master lease with GLPI originally effective April 28, 2016
  (the "Pinnacle Master Lease"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000921738-23-000010, Form 10-K
  (FY2022)]
- The same 10-K's property table lists the 12 former-Pinnacle properties by name —
  Ameristar East Chicago, Plainridge Park Casino, Ameristar Vicksburg, Boomtown Bossier
  City, Boomtown New Orleans, L'Auberge Baton Rouge, L'Auberge Lake Charles,
  Margaritaville Resort Casino, Ameristar Black Hawk, Cactus Petes and Horseshu,
  Ameristar Council Bluffs, and River City Casino — as still operating as of December
  31, 2022, each tagged to the Pinnacle Master Lease (or the Margaritaville Lease) but
  organized within the company's own "Northeast," "South," "West," and "Midwest"
  reporting segments alongside properties the acquirer owned before the acquisition,
  rather than as a separately identified Pinnacle business unit or segment. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0000921738-23-000010, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The same property listing and segment structure (former-Pinnacle properties folded
  into the acquirer's Northeast/South/West/Midwest segments) also appears in the
  acquirer's FY2020 Form 10-K, indicating the integrated, commingled reporting
  treatment was already in place roughly two years after closing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000921738-21-000009, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
