## Public-reporting status through the outcome window
- The entity remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company throughout
  the outcome window: it filed a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 28,
  2022) under the registrant name "Rent-A-Center, Inc." and a Form 10-K for fiscal year
  2022 (filed February 24, 2023) under the registrant name "Upbound Group, Inc." — i.e.,
  the registrant's corporate name changed between the two filings while its SEC CIK and
  common-stock listing continued uninterrupted. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-23-000050, Form 10-K FY2022 cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2021 were $4,583,451 thousand, an
  increase of approximately $1,769.3 million (62.9%) versus $2,814,191 thousand in
  2020, "primarily due to the acquisition of Acima Holdings" together with same-store
  sales growth in the Rent-A-Center Business segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net earnings for the year ended December 31, 2021 were $134,940 thousand, down 35.2%
  from $208,115 thousand in 2020, notwithstanding the large revenue increase — driven in
  part by lower gross margin (48.8% of revenue in 2021 versus 59.4% in 2020, attributed
  to Acima Holdings' different lease-merchandise depreciation policy) and higher income
  tax expense. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022 were $4,245,392 thousand, a
  decrease of $338,059 thousand (7.4%) versus 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-23-000050, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net earnings for the year ended December 31, 2022 were $12,357 thousand, down 90.8%
  from $134,940 thousand in 2021; earnings before income taxes fell 68.4% ($61,471
  thousand versus $194,304 thousand) and operating profit fell 47.1% ($148,538 thousand
  versus $280,539 thousand) over the same comparison. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-23-000050, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- "Other charges" for fiscal year 2022 totaled $235.3 million (down from $289.9 million
  in 2021) and were stated to include, among other components, $6.8 million of asset
  impairments, $80.8 million of depreciation and amortization of acquired software and
  intangible assets, and $143.2 million of stock compensation expense tied to vesting of
  equity consideration issued in the Acima Holdings acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-23-000050, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]

## Documented events over time
- On February 17, 2021 the company completed the acquisition of Acima Holdings, LLC, a
  virtual lease-to-own platform, issuing the former owners of Acima approximately
  10,779,923 shares of common stock (valued at $51.14/share at closing) plus cash
  consideration, for total estimated consideration of $1.4 billion; a Term Loan Facility
  and $450.0 million of senior unsecured notes due February 15, 2029, bearing 6.375%
  interest, were issued to help fund the cash portion. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, "Recent Developments" / Note B / "Senior Notes"]
- Following the Acima acquisition, the company's pre-existing operations (renamed the
  "Rent-A-Center Business" segment) declined to approximately 44% of consolidated net
  revenues for the year ended December 31, 2021, with the newly acquired Acima segment
  accounting for the balance. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, "Our Operating Segments"]
- Acima Holdings had received, prior to the acquisition's execution, a Civil
  Investigative Demand dated October 1, 2020 from the Consumer Financial Protection
  Bureau; as of the FY2021 Form 10-K the company stated it was "fully cooperating with
  the CFPB investigation" and was "currently unable to predict the eventual scope,
  ultimate timing or outcome" of that investigation. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, "Legal Proceedings" / risk factors]
- Management's assessment of internal control over financial reporting as of December
  31, 2021 excluded Acima Holdings, which the filing states "constituted 33% of the
  Company's total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2021." [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 9A]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The entity continued to operate and report publicly (renamed Upbound Group, Inc.
  during the window) rather than being acquired, delisted, or dissolved. Its realized
  trajectory across the outcome window shows revenue roughly doubling year-over-year in
  2021 on the back of the debt- and equity-funded Acima Holdings acquisition (closed
  February 2021) but net earnings falling sharply in both subsequent years — from
  $208,115 thousand (2020) to $134,940 thousand (2021, -35.2%) to $12,357 thousand
  (2022, -90.8% year-over-year) — alongside a shift in the acquired Acima segment to a
  majority of consolidated revenue, an unresolved CFPB investigation into the acquired
  business's practices, and disclosed asset impairments and elevated integration-related
  "other charges" in the second full year post-acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-22-000046, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0000933036-23-000050, Form 10-K FY2022]
