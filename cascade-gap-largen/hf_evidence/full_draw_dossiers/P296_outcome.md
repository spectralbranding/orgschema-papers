## Realized record documented in the company's own pre-deregistration filings
- On June 18, 2019, Legacy Reserves Inc. and certain of its subsidiaries (collectively,
  the "Debtors") commenced voluntary cases under chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code
  in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- On June 19, 2019, the Bankruptcy Court granted joint administration of the Chapter 11
  Cases under the caption In re Legacy Reserves Inc., et al. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- On August 2, 2019, the Debtors filed a Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization for
  Legacy Reserves Inc. and its Debtor Affiliates with the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- On November 15, 2019, the Bankruptcy Court entered a Confirmation Order confirming the
  Plan, and on December 11, 2019 (the "Effective Date") the Plan became effective and the
  Debtors emerged from the Chapter 11 Cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- Upon emergence, shares of the company's existing common stock outstanding immediately
  prior to the Effective Date — approximately 114,810,671 shares — were cancelled, and
  Reorganized Legacy issued New Common Stock to certain holders of claims against and
  interests in the Debtors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- The Plan's exit financing included up to $500.0 million in aggregate principal amount
  under a new senior secured first-lien reserve-based revolving credit facility, plus
  $189.8 million in proceeds from the purchase of approximately 30.88% of the New Common
  Stock, backstopped pursuant to a Sponsor Backstop Commitment Agreement dated June 13,
  2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- Upon emergence, the company adopted fresh-start accounting under ASC 852, under which
  financial statements on or after the Effective Date are not comparable to those before
  it; the company's results are reported in "Successor" (December 11-31, 2019) and
  "Predecessor" (January 1-December 10, 2019, and prior periods) periods. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- The company recognized reorganization expense of $447.9 million for the Predecessor
  period January 1-December 10, 2019, associated with the Chapter 11 Cases (gains from
  liabilities settled and fresh-start accounting adjustments), versus zero in the
  Successor period and zero for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- Net income/(loss) was reported as $3.1 million for the Successor period (December
  11-31, 2019), $173.9 million for the Predecessor period (January 1-December 10, 2019),
  and $43.8 million for the year ended December 31, 2018 (Predecessor). [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- As a result of the market value of equity issued upon emergence, the company realized
  cancellation-of-debt income of approximately $640 million, which reduced the
  company's U.S. net-operating-loss carryovers of $195 million to zero and further
  reduced its tax basis in producing properties by $445 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
- In connection with the closing of the Chapter 11 Cases, all outstanding equity awards
  were terminated, and named executive officers entered into amended and restated
  employment agreements effective December 11, 2019, in connection with the emergence.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly nine months of the September 2018 corporate reorganization closing, the
  reorganized entity filed for Chapter 11 protection (June 18, 2019); it emerged under a
  confirmed Plan of Reorganization on December 11, 2019 with its pre-petition common
  stock (~114.8 million shares) cancelled, new equity issued to claimholders, $447.9
  million in reorganization expense recognized, and approximately $640 million of
  cancellation-of-debt income realized. [SRC: SEC accession 0001735828-20-000006, 10-K for FY2019]
  The company's SEC reporting obligations were then formally terminated via Form 15-12B
  on 2020-01-02, and no periodic public filing exists beyond that point; consequently
  there is no public record for the fixed 3-5-year-after-closing window, and any
  characterization of the business's condition in that later period is not
  publicly documented. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-20-000026, Form 15-12B]
