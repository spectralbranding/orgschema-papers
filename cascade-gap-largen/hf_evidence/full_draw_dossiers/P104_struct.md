## The drawn filing and the registrant
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K furnished under Item 2.02 (Regulation FD): on June 14, 2010 the registrant issued information for use at the Macquarie Small & Mid Cap Conference (June 16, 2010), attached as Exhibit 99.1; it is not a merger, purchase, or other deal-terms agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K Item 2.02 cover (form8k.htm)]
- The registrant is CNO Financial Group, Inc. (Carmel, Indiana), a Delaware corporation, formerly named Conseco, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K Item 2.02 cover (form8k.htm)]

## Transaction type and what was assembled
- The predecessor, Conseco, pursued a roll-up / consolidation strategy of acquiring insurance companies: 19 acquisitions for combined consideration of approximately $6 billion through 1997. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1 (Macquarie conference presentation)]
- In 1998 it made a "diversifying" acquisition of manufactured-housing lender Green Tree for approximately $6 billion -- a lending business whose model was distinct from the assembled insurance operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The public holding company was placed into bankruptcy in 2002 (the insurance companies continued operating) and the enterprise emerged from bankruptcy in 2003, which reconstituted the legal vehicle. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Tier composition at the closing-era snapshot
- Value-capture model: a focused provider of supplemental health, protection, and accumulation products to the senior/middle-income market; 3.9 million policies in force; 94% controlled distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Customer-facing products carried by the assembled companies: Medicare supplement, life, annuity, long-term care, Medicare Part D and Medicare Advantage, final expense, and specified-disease products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Operational processes / distribution ran through three channel-defined segments: Bankers Life (a career-agent "kitchen table" model with 5,600+ career agents and 150+ branches nationwide); Colonial Penn (a direct-response model using media, mail lead generation, and telemarketing); Washington National (independent producers plus PMA, a wholly owned marketing company). [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- People / organization: the field force comprised 5,600+ career agents and sales managers (Bankers Life), and Washington National distributed through independent producers together with the wholly owned PMA marketing company (over 800 producers). [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## How the shared substrate was arranged (closing-era re-engineering)
- Back-office processes were consolidated across the assembled companies: 250 back-office jobs were moved from Chicago to Carmel to create a single back office. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The legacy long-term-care business was separated from the operating company: it was spun off to a Pennsylvania trust to reduce risk and earnings volatility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- A $3 billion block of annuities was reinsured (a risk/cash transfer of an in-force block), with additional reinsurance deals completed in 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Product-with/without-process and process-with/without-people observations
- In the insurance roll-up, whole going-concern insurance companies were acquired and continued operating as subsidiaries, so the customer-facing products crossed together with the processes and people that produced and delivered them; by contrast the 1998 Green Tree acquisition added a manufactured-housing lending operation whose business model differed from the insurance core. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Operational processes were re-engineered without a stated relocation of the acquired companies' distribution field forces: back-office functions were centralized into a single Carmel back office while the career-agent, direct, and independent channels continued to operate as separate segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Structural summary
- The transaction is a multi-company insurance roll-up (19 acquisitions through 1997, ~$6 billion) subsequently diversified by a ~$6 billion manufactured-housing lending acquisition (Green Tree, 1998), assembled under a public holding company that entered bankruptcy in 2002 and re-emerged in 2003; at the ~2010 closing-era snapshot the assembled operations ran as three distribution-channel segments (Bankers Life career agents; Colonial Penn direct; Washington National independent producers plus wholly owned PMA) over a centralized single back office, with the legacy long-term-care block separated to a Pennsylvania trust. [SRC: SEC accession 0001224608-10-000028, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
