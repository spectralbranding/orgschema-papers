# Structural sub-dossier — Corteva, Inc. separation from DowDuPont (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Corteva, Inc. is a Delaware holding company organized on March 16, 2018, formed to hold
  DowDuPont Inc.'s agriculture business; DowDuPont's board approved distributing all
  issued and outstanding Corteva common stock to DowDuPont stockholders as a pro rata
  dividend (a spin-off). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution was structured to be tax-free for U.S. federal income tax purposes
  (except cash paid in lieu of fractional shares); stockholder approval was not required
  and stockholders surrendered no shares to receive Corteva stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Distribution ratio as finalized: one share of Corteva common stock for every three
  shares of DowDuPont common stock held at the close of business on May 24, 2019 (record
  date); distribution date June 1, 2019; approximately 750 million Corteva shares
  estimated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-138437, Form 10-12B/A (Amendment No. 4) EX-99.1 information statement]
- The Corteva separation was one of two distributions effecting DowDuPont's plan to
  separate into three independent public companies (agriculture = Corteva; materials
  science = Dow; specialty products = New DuPont). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product/asset: the agriculture business combining DuPont Pioneer, Dow
  AgroSciences and DuPont Crop Protection — a seed platform (germplasm and traits) and a
  crop protection platform (formulations against weeds, insects, disease), operating in
  two reportable segments (seed and crop protection), serving farmers in more than 130
  countries with pro forma annual sales of $14 billion for the year ended December 31,
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Legal vehicle: Corteva Parent holds 100% of the common stock of EID (E. I. du Pont de
  Nemours and Company); EID continues as a Corteva subsidiary and remains a separate SEC
  reporting issuer, and EID preferred stock is unaffected by the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Producing/delivering processes: the agriculture assets, liabilities and legal entities
  of Historical DuPont and Historical Dow were aligned into Corteva subsidiaries via an
  "Internal Reorganization" and "Business Realignment" — i.e., the product line and its
  producing operations (R&D pipeline, routes to market) were conveyed together into the
  new company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Shared corporate substrate that did NOT transfer intact: financing and capital
  expenditure needs had historically been met through "Historical DuPont's company-wide
  cash management practices, with certain portions of our business having satisfied such
  requirements through the practices of Historical Dow" — a corporate treasury/cash
  substrate to be re-established standalone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- As a standalone company Corteva estimated "approximately $175 million to $225 million of
  annual leveraged functional and corporate expenses" it expected to incur that had been
  provided at the DowDuPont level. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Substantially simultaneously with the distributions, Corteva was to enter into a
  separation agreement with DowDuPont (to become New DuPont) and Dow to effect the
  separation and provide the framework for the ongoing relationship among the three
  companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Various other agreements were to be entered into: a tax matters agreement, an employee
  matters agreement, intellectual property cross-license agreements, trademark license
  agreements, and "certain other intellectual property, services, supply and real
  estate-related agreements," collectively allocating the assets, liabilities and
  obligations of DowDuPont among Corteva, New DuPont and Dow. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The information statement refers to "transition services and the other services...
  called for under the services and other related agreements," indicating the corporate
  service substrate was to be provided across companies for a period rather than
  transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the separation and tax matters agreements, Corteva agreed to assume and indemnify
  New DuPont and Dow for certain liabilities, including environmental and litigation
  liabilities relating to its business and to the discontinued and divested businesses of
  Historical DuPont; most of these indemnification obligations are uncapped. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A single agriculture operating business (seed and crop protection product platforms,
  held through EID and its subsidiaries and its aligned entities) was separated whole from
  a three-way parent by spin-off, while the parent's shared corporate substrate (cash
  management/treasury, tax, IP, trademarks, services, supply, real estate) was not
  transferred intact but allocated and bridged at closing through a separation agreement
  and a suite of related tax, employee, IP cross-license, trademark, services and supply
  agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-352727, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
