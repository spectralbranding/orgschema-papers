## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- ONEOK, Inc. (CIK 0001039684) remains a continuing SEC registrant and periodic filer;
  its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020 was filed February 23, 2021,
  and its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2021 was filed March 1, 2022,
  both within the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Realized business record within the fixed window (primary filings)
- The FY2020 Form 10-K's five-year selected financial data shows net income of $612.8
  million for 2020, down from $1,278.6 million for 2019; diluted EPS fell from $3.07
  (2019) to $1.42 (2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 Form 10-K states: "In 2020, due to the commodity price and market
  environment, we experienced a significant decline in our share price and market
  capitalization, and performed a Step 1 analysis to test our goodwill for impairment
  and evaluated certain long-lived asset groups and equity investments for impairment.
  As a result, we incurred $644.9 million in noncash impairment charges, which had an
  adverse impact on our financial results for the year ended December 31, 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 Form 10-K states the company expected "to maintain sufficient liquidity and
  financial stability into 2021 due to cash on hand from our June 2020 equity issuance,
  cash flows from operations and access to our undrawn $2.5 Billion Credit Agreement." [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K reports net income of $1,499.7 million for 2021, up from $612.8
  million for 2020 (an increase of $886.9 million), with diluted EPS of $3.35 versus
  $1.42, and states operating income increased $1.2 billion in 2021 versus 2020
  primarily due to, among other items, "an increase of $607.2 million due to noncash
  impairment charges in our Natural Gas Gathering and Processing and Natural Gas
  Liquids segments in 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Management/organization continuity and change within the window
- Walter S. Hulse III, an executive officer named at the time of the 2017 merger, signed
  both the FY2020 and FY2021 Forms 10-K as Chief Financial Officer, Treasurer and
  Executive Vice President, Strategy and Corporate Affairs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Directors Julie H. Edwards, John W. Gibson (Chairman), Steven J. Malcolm, and Jim W.
  Mogg — all named as common ONEOK/ONEOK Partners GP directors at the time of the 2017
  merger — signed both the FY2020 and FY2021 Forms 10-K as continuing directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The chief executive officer changed within the window: Terry K. Spencer signed the
  FY2020 Form 10-K (filed February 23, 2021) as President, Chief Executive Officer and
  Director, while Pierce H. Norton signed the FY2021 Form 10-K (filed March 1, 2022) as
  President, Chief Executive Officer and Director. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Neither Form 10-K narrative section examined mentions the 2017 merger or the
  historical ONEOK Partners transaction by name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Items sought but not found
- No divestiture, bankruptcy, or dissolution of the acquired midstream business was
  located in the reviewed filings within the window; the business continued to be
  reported as ONEOK's core consolidated operations (Natural Gas Gathering and
  Processing, Natural Gas Liquids, and Natural Gas Pipelines segments). [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K attributes $607.2 million of the 2020 noncash impairment charges
  to the Natural Gas Gathering and Processing and Natural Gas Liquids segments
  specifically (out of the $644.9 million total reported for 2020); no further
  per-segment split of the remaining balance was located in the reviewed excerpts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2020-2022 window, ONEOK, Inc.'s own periodic filings show a
  documented single-year net-income decline in 2020 (from $1,278.6 million in 2019 to
  $612.8 million), attributed by the company to $644.9 million in noncash goodwill and
  long-lived-asset impairment charges taken amid a commodity-price and market-
  capitalization decline, followed by a recovery to $1,499.7 million net income in 2021 —
  above the pre-decline 2019 level. [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-21-000015, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001039684-22-000015, Form 10-K FY2021]

  through both fiscal years in the window, while the chief executive officer title
  passed from Terry K. Spencer to Pierce H. Norton between the FY2020 and FY2021
