# Outcome sub-dossier — Apollo Global, Inc. realized record, ~2012-2014 horizon

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized record
> in the fixed 3-5-year outcome window after ~2009 (fiscal years 2011-2013 as reported in
> the FY2013 annual report). It contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at formation.

## Outcome window and source
- The outcome window is fixed at fiscal years 2011-2013; the realized record is drawn from
  Apollo Group, Inc.'s Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended August 31, 2013, which reports
  those years. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Best-documented realized scalar — buyout of the minority partner
- During the first quarter of fiscal year 2013, Apollo Group purchased the 14.4%
  noncontrolling ownership interest in Apollo Global from The Carlyle Group for $42.5 million
  cash, plus a contingent payment based on a portion of Apollo Global's operating results
  through the fiscal years ending August 31, 2017; as a result Apollo Group owns 100% of
  Apollo Global. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- On the purchase date the contingent payment's fair value was estimated at $6.0 million; the
  purchase was accounted for as an equity transaction, removing Carlyle's noncontrolling
  interest and recording a $48.5 million adjustment to additional paid-in capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The buyout was effected under a Stock Purchase Agreement among Apollo Group, Inc., Apollo
  Global, Inc., and Carlyle U.S. Growth Fund III, L.P. (f/k/a Carlyle Venture Partners III,
  L.P.) and CVP III Coinvestments, L.P., dated October 12, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Realized record at the product / process interface (acquired institutions)
- BPP recorded a $219.9 million goodwill and other intangibles impairment in fiscal year 2011.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- UNIACC recorded a $16.8 million goodwill and other intangibles impairment charge in fiscal
  year 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Reported goodwill and other intangibles impairment totaled $16.8 million, $219.9 million and
  $184.6 million in fiscal years 2012, 2011 and 2010, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The Apollo Global reportable segment operated at a loss across the window; its fiscal-2013
  operating loss decreased by $7.0 million versus fiscal 2012, attributed primarily to the
  absence of UNIACC's fiscal-2012 impairment and to reduced operating expenses from
  restructuring activity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Realized record at the process / organization interface (divestiture, restructuring)
- BPP's subsidiary Mander Portman Woodward (MPW) was sold in the fourth quarter of fiscal year
  2012, generating a $26.7 million gain reported in discontinued operations (no tax expense
  recorded, as the gain was not taxable under United Kingdom tax law). [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The registrant implemented workforce reductions to reengineer business processes and refine
  delivery systems, and continued restructuring after August 31, 2013 with workforce
  reductions of approximately 500 non-faculty personnel. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Enterprise-level context for the window
- Consolidated net income attributable to Apollo was $248.5 million in fiscal 2013, down from
  $422.7 million in fiscal 2012 and $572.4 million in fiscal 2011; consolidated net revenue
  was $3,681.3 million (2013), $4,253.3 million (2012) and $4,711.0 million (2011). [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the window the minority partner (Carlyle) fully exited (bought out Q1 FY2013 for
  $42.5 million cash plus a contingent payment, leaving Apollo Group as sole owner); the
  acquired international institutions carried by the entity recorded successive goodwill/
  intangibles impairments (BPP $219.9 million in FY2011; UNIACC $16.8 million in FY2012), one
  acquired sub-institution (MPW) was divested for a $26.7 million gain in FY2012, and the
  segment operated at a loss amid workforce-reduction restructuring — while the enterprise's
  consolidated net income declined across FY2011-FY2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000929887-13-000150, Form 10-K FY2013]
