## Parties, vehicle, and value-capture model at the closing era (contemporaneous annual report)
- The registrant describes itself as focused on acquiring income-producing real estate assets consisting primarily of high-quality distribution warehouses and other industrial properties leased to creditworthy corporate customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- The legal vehicle is an umbrella-partnership REIT (UPREIT): the company holds all or substantially all of its assets through its operating partnership, Industrial Income Operating Partnership LP, a Delaware limited partnership of which the company is sole general partner and a limited partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- The company is externally managed: it relies on the Advisor to manage its day-to-day operating and acquisition activities and to implement its investment strategy under a fourth amended and restated Advisory Agreement dated February 21, 2013 among the company, the Operating Partnership, and Industrial Income Advisors LLC (the "Advisor"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- The sponsor is Industrial Income Advisors Group LLC; the Advisor and its affiliates also sponsor or advise other investment programs, including Industrial Property Trust Inc., with which the company may compete for assets and personnel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- Capital to fund the program was raised through continuous best-efforts public offerings (an initial offering of up to $2.0 billion at $10.00/share plus a follow-on of up to $2.4 billion at $10.40/share, plus distribution-reinvestment shares). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]

## What was assembled at the ownership boundary (the customer-facing asset)
- As of December 31, 2013 the consolidated portfolio comprised 296 industrial buildings totaling approximately 57.2 million square feet with 553 customers throughout the U.S., at a weighted-average remaining lease term of 5.3 years; 284 buildings (~54.8M sq ft) were the operating portfolio and 12 buildings (~2.4M sq ft) the development portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- The portfolio was assembled by serial acquisition: during 2013 the company acquired 75 buildings (~13.1M sq ft) for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $917.3 million, funded with public-offering proceeds and debt financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- Also during 2013 the company acquired its partner's equity interest in the IIT North American Industrial Fund I Partnership joint venture (which owned 31 buildings, ~7.2M sq ft) for approximately $129.6 million and assumed approximately $241.3 million of the Fund I Partnership's debt, resulting in consolidation of that partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]

## Where the operating processes and people sat relative to the assembled assets (closing era)
- The company had no in-house organization to run the assembled assets: "Employees We have no employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- Property-level operating processes were run by a related party, not the company: management and leasing were performed by Dividend Capital Property Management LLC (the "Property Manager"), which earned management and leasing fees irrespective of underlying real-estate or service quality during the agreement term. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- Day-to-day operating and acquisition activities, and the key personnel who perform them, sat with the Advisor: "We rely on key personnel of the Advisor to manage our day-to-day operating and acquisition activities," and all of the company's current executives and other key personnel of the Advisor also provide services to one or more other investment programs sponsored or advised by affiliates of the Sponsor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared operating substrate (closing era)
- The Advisor assumes principal responsibility for managing the company's affairs and is compensated for those services; the fees, compensation, expense reimbursements and other payments to the Advisor and its affiliates may be increased or decreased by the board and reduce cash available for operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
- The management structure could later be internalized: "At some point in the future, we may internalize the functions performed for us by the Advisor," potentially by hiring the company's own executives/employees or acquiring the Advisor or its assets (including its existing workforce), with any internalization requiring significant payments to the Advisor's owners — indicating the operating workforce and functions did not reside inside the company at the closing era. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]

## Structural summary (closing era only)
- The drawn accession documents a charter/bylaw correction that merely restates the defined term "Roll-Up Transaction," not a property or portfolio purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-110223, 8-K Item 5.03]
- Contemporaneously, the registrant is an externally managed, non-traded UPREIT that assembled a large national industrial/logistics portfolio (296 buildings / ~57.2M sq ft by Dec 31, 2013, including ~$917.3M of buildings and a ~$129.6M JV-interest buyout acquired during 2013) funded by continuous best-efforts equity offerings, while holding no employees and running the acquisition and property-management processes — and the people who perform them — through the external Advisor (Industrial Income Advisors LLC) and the related-party Property Manager (Dividend Capital Property Management LLC) rather than inside the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-058795, 10-K FY2013]
