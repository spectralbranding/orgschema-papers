# Structural sub-dossier — Pattern Energy Group Inc. / PSP Investments co-investment (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 16, 2017, Pattern Energy Group Inc. (the "Company," an owner/operator of
  wind and solar power projects) entered into a Joint Venture Agreement with the Public
  Sector Pension Investment Board ("PSP Investments"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Under the Joint Venture Agreement, PSP Investments received the right to co-invest up
  to an aggregate of approximately $500,000,000 alongside the Company in energy projects
  the Company may acquire under its purchase-rights agreements, acquiring on a
  project-by-project basis either 30% or a greater elected percentage of the Company's
  combined ownership interest in each project. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The counterparties to the broader arrangement include the Company's project sponsors,
  Pattern Energy Group LP ("Pattern Development 1.0") and Pattern Energy Group 2 LP
  ("Pattern Development 2.0"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, by tier
- The customer-facing asset is electricity generation from operating and near-operating
  wind farms sold under long-term power purchase agreements (PPAs). Three specific
  project companies were placed into the co-investment at closing via Purchase and Sale
  Agreements dated June 16, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Meikle PSA: the Company (or a subsidiary) would acquire a 50.99% limited-partner
  interest in Meikle Wind Energy LP plus 70% of Meikle Wind Energy Corp for CAD
  $85,425,000, while Vertuous Energy Canada Inc. (a wholly owned PSP subsidiary) would
  acquire a 48.99% LP interest plus 30% of Meikle Corp for CAD $82,075,000; the Meikle
  project is an approximately 179 MW wind farm in British Columbia (commercial operations
  Q1 2017), with the Company's owned capacity after closing approximately 91 MW. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- MSM PSA: parallel structure — the Company acquiring a 50.99% LP interest (plus 70% of
  the general partner and 70% of MSM ULC) for CAD $53,040,000 and Vertuous Canada
  acquiring 48.99%/30%/30% for CAD $50,960,000, in the approximately 143 MW Mont
  Sainte-Marguerite wind farm in Québec (commercial operation expected late 2017);
  Company owned capacity after closing approximately 73 MW. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- PH2 PSA: the Company would sell to Vertuous Energy LLC (a PSP subsidiary) a 98%
  membership interest in a newly formed LLC holding 50% of the Class B interests in
  Panhandle Wind Holdings 2 LLC (the approximately 182 MW Panhandle wind farm in Carson
  County, Texas, in commercial operation since Q4 2014) for $58,800,000; the Company's
  owned capacity after closing approximately 75 MW. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Equity-level stake: on June 16, 2017, PSP Investments separately acquired 8.7 million
  shares (approximately 9.9%) of the Company's outstanding Class A common stock from a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Pattern Development 1.0, and agreed to a customary 12-month
  standstill; the Company may add a PSP-designated person to its Board of Directors after
  a compliance date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01/Item 8.01]

## Whether the operating processes and people moved with the asset
- The operating and administrative PROCESSES for the jointly owned project companies were
  retained by the Company rather than transferred to the co-investor: under a Sponsor
  Services Agreement dated June 16, 2017, the Company will provide agreed services to PSP
  Investments and its affiliates with respect to the administration of the joint ownership
  of the project companies PSP invests in, in exchange for fees. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Management and personnel arrangements were governed within the existing Pattern
  organization, not moved to PSP: the Amended and Restated Multilateral Management
  Services Agreement (June 16, 2017) among the Company, Pattern Development 1.0 and
  Pattern Development 2.0 sets the terms under which management services are provided and
  under which employees of the Pattern Development companies may be reintegrated as the
  Company's employees ("PEG 1 / PEG 2 Employee Reintegration"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Amended and Restated Purchase Rights Agreements (1.0 and 2.0) and a Second Amended and
  Restated Non-Competition Agreement (all June 16, 2017) allocate first-offer rights on
  the sponsors' development pipeline (expanded to a 10 GW pipeline; 910 MW on the
  identified right-of-first-offer list) and reserve development activity to Pattern
  Development 2.0. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01/Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction is a co-investment / joint venture in which a financial partner (PSP
  Investments) acquired minority economic interests in specific operating and
  near-operating wind-farm project companies (the customer-facing generation asset) while
  the Company retained majority ownership and continued to run the operating and
  administrative processes; the shared operating substrate was bridged contractually by
  the Sponsor Services Agreement (administration of joint ownership for fees) and the
  A&R Multilateral Management Services Agreement (management services and employee
  arrangements), rather than by transferring operations or personnel to the co-investor. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-17-005834, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
