## Fixed outcome window
- The outcome window is the acquiring registrant's fiscal-year-2022 record, reported in the Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2022 (filed 2023-02-23) — approximately four years after the March 2019 closing and within the 3–5-year horizon. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Retention of the acquired asset
- The acquired product remained within the acquirer through the outcome window: Trulance (plecanatide) is listed among the acquirer's marketed products in the FY2022 Form 10-K, and is described as a product available from a single source — indicating it was retained, not divested. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The FY2022 Form 10-K reaffirms that the acquirer's completed acquisition of certain assets of Synergy Pharmaceuticals Inc. "included the Trulance product." [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Best-documented realized scalar (segment the acquired product joined)
- The Salix segment — the operating segment into which the acquired product was placed — reported revenue of $2,090 million in 2022 versus $2,074 million in 2021, an increase of $16 million (1%). [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022) Salix segment results]
- Salix segment profit was $1,489 million in 2022 versus $1,493 million in 2021, a decrease of $4 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022) Salix segment results]
- Within that segment the acquired product remained a minor contributor: the Xifaxan product line (a pre-existing Salix product, not part of the Synergy assets) accounted for approximately 81% of Salix segment revenues in 2022 and 79% in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022) Salix segment discussion]

## Documented events at the acquired product over time
- Higher gross product sales of the acquired product (Trulance) were cited by the acquirer as a positive contributor to Salix product-sales movement across the period. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022) revenue discussion]
- The acquired product drew generic competition and litigation: in April 2021 the acquirer commenced Paragraph IV patent-infringement litigation against MSN Laboratories Private Ltd. and Aurobindo Pharma Limited over their ANDA filings for generic plecanatide (Trulance) 3 mg tablets; the defendants alleged the Trulance patents were invalid, unenforceable and/or not infringed, and the acquirer stated it intended to defend its intellectual property. [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022) legal proceedings]

## Items noted but not primary-verifiable in the window

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2022 outcome window the acquired product (Trulance) was retained (not divested) inside the acquirer's Salix segment, which posted broadly flat results — revenue up 1% to $2,090 million and profit down $4 million to $1,489 million year over year — while the acquired product remained a small share of a segment dominated by the pre-existing Xifaxan line (~81% of segment revenue), and the acquired product faced generic-entry litigation (Paragraph IV suits commenced April 2021 against MSN Laboratories and Aurobindo Pharma). [SRC: SEC accession 0000885590-23-000009, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
