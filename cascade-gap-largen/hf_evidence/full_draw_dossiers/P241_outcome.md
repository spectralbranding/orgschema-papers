## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- On August 28, 2017 (11 days after the merger closed), a Form 15-12B was filed
  certifying termination of registration of the common stock under Section 12(g) of
  the Exchange Act and suspension of the duty to file reports under Sections 13(a)/
  15(d); the filing reported approximately zero (stated "None") holders of record as
  of the certification date, and identified the filer as "Sabra Health Care REIT,
  Inc., as successor by merger to Care Capital Properties, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-054020, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has since been filed under the acquired
  company's own CIK (0001639947); its business is observable only through Sabra Health
  Care REIT, Inc.'s (CIK 0001492298) continuing consolidated public filings as the
  surviving registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-054020, Form 15-12B]

## Acquirer's continuing, partial disclosure of the acquired business
- Sabra's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 22, 2021) separately itemized
  a "CCP transition expenses" line within non-recurring items, declining from $1.5
  million (fiscal 2018) to $0.2 million (fiscal 2019) to $0.1 million (fiscal 2020). [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The same 10-K reported $2.2 million (fiscal 2020) and $2.1 million (fiscal 2019) of
  other income "related to settlement payments received related to legacy CCP
  investments." [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Sabra's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 22, 2022) reported this same
  settlement-income line item at $0.4 million for fiscal 2021, down from $2.2 million
  in fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-22-000014, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Beyond these itemized lines, neither 10-K presents a standalone segment income
  statement, balance sheet, or property-level disclosure isolating the acquired
  portfolio's financial results from Sabra's consolidated results for the ~2020-2022
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020; SEC accession 0001492298-22-000014, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Documented disruption traceable to a pre-merger counterparty
- The merger proxy's own disclosure of the acquired company's recent transactions
  history lists a July 2015 transaction between the acquired company and Senior Care
  Centers, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- Sabra's fiscal-2020 10-K states that during the year ended December 31, 2019
  (approximately 16 months after closing) it recognized $121.8 million of impairment
  of real estate, of which $95.2 million related to "30 Senior Care Centers facilities
  that we sold and one additional Senior Care Centers facility that we transitioned to
  another operator," including $10.2 million of estimated contractual indemnification
  obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
- This $121.8 million impairment was recognized in fiscal 2019, which precedes the
  fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window; it appears in the record only as a comparative
  figure disclosed within the in-window fiscal-2020 10-K. Sabra's own in-window
  impairment-of-real-estate figures were materially smaller: $4.0 million for fiscal
  2020 (one skilled nursing/transitional care facility sold during the year and three
  senior housing communities) and $0.5 million for fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020; SEC accession 0001492298-22-000014, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired company ceased to exist as a separate SEC registrant 11 days after
  closing; the acquirer, Sabra, is the sole source of any post-close record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-054020, Form 15-12B]
- Sabra's fiscal-2020 and fiscal-2021 10-Ks — both within the fixed ~2020-2022 window —
  separately itemize two small, declining legacy-specific lines (transition expenses
  falling to $0.1 million; legacy-investment settlement income falling from $2.2
  million to $0.4 million), alongside disclosure of a large ($121.8 million,
  fiscal-2019, just outside the window) impairment tied to the exit of a pre-merger
  counterparty (Senior Care Centers, LLC), followed by materially smaller in-window
  impairments ($4.0 million fiscal 2020; $0.5 million fiscal 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020; SEC accession 0001492298-22-000014, Form 10-K FY2021]
- No source separately isolates the acquired portfolio's full financial trajectory
  beyond these itemized lines; the realized record for the ~2020-2022 window is
  therefore partial rather than complete. [SRC: SEC accession 0001492298-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020; SEC accession 0001492298-22-000014, Form 10-K FY2021]
