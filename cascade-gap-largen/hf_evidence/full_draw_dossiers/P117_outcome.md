# Outcome sub-dossier — Chemtura Corporation realized record, fixed ~2012-2014 window

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at the registrant's
> realized 3-5-year record after the closing-era anchor and is traced through its
> Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2013. This sub-dossier contains no

## Public-reporting status and realized trajectory
- The registrant emerged from Chapter 11: the Bankruptcy Court confirmed the plan of
  reorganization on November 3, 2010, and the plan became effective ("the Effective
  Date") on November 10, 2010, when the Debtors "substantially consummated their
  reorganization through a series of transactions contemplated by the Plan." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Emergence from Chapter 11]
- As of December 31, 2013 the Bankruptcy Court had "entered orders granting final decree
  closing all of the Debtors' Chapter 11 cases except the Chapter 11 case of Chemtura
  Corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Emergence from Chapter 11]
- The registrant continued to file audited annual reports as a public company; for the
  year ended December 31, 2013 it reported global net sales of $2.2 billion and, as of
  December 31, 2013, total assets of $2.7 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Business]

## Best-documented outcome scalar
- Net loss attributable to Chemtura for 2013 was $(177) million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Within that result, loss from continuing operations attributable to Chemtura for 2013
  was $(22) million, or $(0.23) per share, "as compared with earnings from continuing
  operations attributable to Chemtura of $103 million, or $1.04 per share, for 2012."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Results of Operations]
- Loss on sale of discontinued operations, net of tax, attributable to Chemtura for 2013
  was $(180) million, or $(1.84) per share, comprising a net loss of $155 million and
  $25 million "associated with the Antioxidant and Consumer Products businesses,
  respectively." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Results of Operations]
- Impairment charges were recorded against the divested lines: $47 million in 2012
  (property, plant and equipment of $35 million and intangible assets of $11 million,
  related to the Antioxidant business) and $7 million in 2013 (property, plant and
  equipment, related to the Consumer Products business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Results of Operations]

## Documented divestitures over the window
- The registrant "completed the sale of our Antioxidants business in April 2013 and
  Consumer Products business in December 2013"; recent divestitures were characterized as
  businesses that "did not fit with the strategic criteria for our portfolio." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Business and Portfolio Management]
- The Antioxidant sale was agreed in November 2012 with SK Blue Holdings, Ltd. ("SK"), an
  affiliate of SK Capital Partners III, L.P., and amended in January 2013 with SK and
  Addivant USA Holdings Corp. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Acquisitions and Divestitures]

## Documented interface disruption over time
- Product/process interface: the Antioxidant sale transferred "certain dedicated plants
  in the U.S., France and Germany, and certain dedicated assets in shared facilities,"
  but the registrant "were to retain assets that are shared with our other business
  components ... and utilize those assets under supply agreements with SK" — a mixed
  transfer in which dedicated production assets moved while shared production assets were
  retained and bridged by supply agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Acquisitions and Divestitures]
- Process/organization interface: through 2013 the registrant "eliminated stranded costs
  associated with the sale of the Antioxidant business" and "developed and began the
  implementation of plans to eliminate stranded costs associated with the Consumer
  Products business," and in the fourth quarter of 2013 "initiated restructuring actions
  to consolidate our Industrials business organizational structure to streamline their
  activities and gain efficiencies and cost savings." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Results of Operations]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the fixed ~2012-2014 window the registrant operated as a reorganized going concern
  (plan effective November 10, 2010; all Debtors' Chapter 11 cases closed by December 31,
  2013 except the registrant's own), reported a 2013 net loss of $(177) million on
  net sales of $2.2 billion, and executed two 2013 divestitures (Antioxidants in April,
  Consumer Products in December) that produced a $(180) million loss on sale, retained
  shared production assets bridged by supply agreements, left "stranded costs," and
  prompted a fourth-quarter 2013 organizational restructuring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-001239, Form 10-K (FY2013), Business, Results of Operations, and Acquisitions and Divestitures]
