## Realized financial trajectory (outcome window)
- Net sales were $4,881 million in fiscal 2015, $6,489 million in fiscal 2016, and
  $7,095 million in fiscal 2017; net income attributable to the Company was $86 million
  (fiscal 2015), $236 million (fiscal 2016), and $340 million (fiscal 2017), per the
  Company's own five-year Selected Financial Data tables. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Operating income was $408 million (fiscal 2015), $581 million (fiscal 2016), and $732
  million (fiscal 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- The Company recorded property, plant and equipment impairment charges of $2 million and
  $3 million, tied to facility shutdowns, in fiscal years 2015 and 2016 respectively; it
  recorded no impairment on goodwill or on indefinite-lived intangible assets in fiscal
  2015 or fiscal 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Further acquisitions within the outcome window
- In October 2015 the Company acquired 100% of the capital stock of AVINTIV Inc. for a
  purchase price of $2.26 billion net of cash acquired — a nonwoven specialty-materials
  producer with 23 locations in 14 countries and an employee base of over 4,500 as of the
  acquisition date — financed with $400 million of new second-priority senior secured
  notes and a $2.1 billion increase to the Company's existing term loan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- In January 2017 the Company acquired AEP Industries Inc. for a purchase price of $791
  million net of cash acquired, funded partly by issuing 6.4 million Berry common shares
  (valued at $324 million at closing) and partly by a $500 million incremental term-loan
  facility; the Company expected annual cost synergies of approximately $80 million from
  the AEP transaction with full realization expected in fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Effective April 2017 the Company changed its name from Berry Plastics Group, Inc. to
  Berry Global Group, Inc. (its wholly owned operating subsidiary similarly renamed from
  Berry Plastics Corporation to Berry Global, Inc.), continuing to trade on the NYSE
  under the symbol BERY. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Documented process/organization integration choices and disclosed integration friction
- In November 2015 the Company reorganized from four operating divisions into three
  segments (Health, Hygiene & Specialties; Consumer Packaging; Engineered Materials) and
  states it "included 100% of the acquired Avintiv business in the Health, Hygiene &
  Specialties segment in order to maintain management continuity and limit business
  integration risk" — i.e., the acquired business's existing management and reporting
  structure were preserved as a distinct segment rather than merged into the pre-existing
  organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The fiscal-2016 operating-income increase is attributed in part to "$36 million
  increase in depreciation and amortization expense primarily related to purchase
  accounting adjustments associated with the Avintiv acquisition" and "an $10 million
  increase in business integration costs," which the filing attributes to "restructuring
  costs associated with the Avintiv acquisition and a $2 million impairment charge
  related to plant shutdowns." [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- For goodwill-impairment testing purposes the Company organized itself into seven
  reporting units (four Health, Hygiene & Specialties regional units plus Consumer
  Packaging, Engineered Materials, and Tapes), stating that within the Consumer
  Packaging, Engineered Materials, and Tapes units it aggregated components because they
  share "similar products, management oversight, production processes, markets served,
  and/or common geographic region," while the HHS segment (built substantially from the
  acquired Avintiv business) was assessed as separate regional reporting units with their
  own regional management teams overseeing operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The Company's audit opinion on internal control over financial reporting as of
  September 30, 2017 explicitly excluded AEP Industries, Inc. and Adchem Corp. (AEP's
  subsidiary), which together constituted 8% of total assets and 11% of net sales for
  fiscal 2017 — the acquired entities' internal controls had not yet been evaluated or
  integrated into the Company's control framework as of that reporting date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed fiscal-2015-through-fiscal-2017 outcome window, the Company's net
  sales and net income both grew substantially (net sales roughly $4.9-7.1 billion;
  net income attributable to the Company $86-340 million), driven materially by two
  large further acquisitions (Avintiv, $2.26 billion, October 2015; AEP Industries, $791
  million, January 2017) rather than organic growth alone, and by April 2017 the parent
  entity had renamed itself Berry Global Group, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- The Company's own disclosures document that it managed at least one large acquisition
  (Avintiv) as a segment-level carve-out preserving pre-existing management rather than
  full integration, that it incurred identified "business integration costs" and
  restructuring/impairment charges tied to plant shutdowns in the window, and that as of
  the fiscal-2017 year-end its internal-control evaluation had not yet been extended to
  its most recently acquired business (AEP/Adchem). [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-16-000097, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001378992-17-000028, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
