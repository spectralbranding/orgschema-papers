## Public-reporting status (feasibility)
- The registrant remained a public reporting company throughout the outcome window and filed a Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2022 on March 1, 2023, so an audited public 3-5-year record exists. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022]

## Realized consolidated scalar (best-documented outcome)
- Consolidated net income was $51,438 thousand for the year ended December 31, 2022, $126,809 thousand for 2021, and $27,197 thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 consolidated statements of comprehensive income]

## Realized record of the acquired businesses (transaction units)
- The custom-molded-protective-foam business acquired in 2018 was retained through the outcome window and rebranded: the report identifies "Altor Solutions ('Altor Solutions' or 'Altor') (formerly 'Foam Fabricators'), headquartered in Scottsdale, Arizona," still "a designer and manufacturer of custom molded protective foam solutions and OEM components made from expanded polystyrene (EPS) and other expanded polymers." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022]
- That business grew its operating footprint over the window: Altor Solutions "maintains a national footprint of 18 manufacturing locations across North America," and itself completed an add-on acquisition in July 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022]
- Altor Solutions remained a continuing reportable segment in FY2022 (it appears in the FY2022 goodwill schedule with net carrying amounts of $117,012 thousand (2022) and $160,418 thousand (2021)); it was neither divested nor impaired in the window per the goodwill roll-forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 goodwill note]
- The add-on business acquired by Sterno in 2018 was integrated into Sterno's consumer operations and generated documented product/operational friction: the report attributes reserves "recorded in 2021 due to product rationalization as Sterno Home integrated with Rimports, and increases in distribution costs, wages and raw materials." [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 MD&A]
- Sterno remained a continuing reportable segment in FY2022 (goodwill net carrying amounts of $168,951 thousand (2022) and $238,620 thousand (2021)); it was not divested within the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 goodwill note]

## Realized record of the surrounding portfolio (holding-company trajectory)
- The holding company continued to reshape the portfolio across the window: it acquired Lugano in September 2021 and PrimaLoft on July 12, 2022, plus several add-on acquisitions in the fourth quarter of 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 MD&A]
- It also executed a divestiture: on July 16, 2021 the LLC, as majority stockholder of Liberty Safe Holding Corporation, sold that business, which is reported in discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 Note D — Discontinued Operations]
- A potential divestiture of another subsidiary, Advanced Circuits, "was terminated during the third quarter," with associated transaction costs incurred. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 MD&A]

## Documented impairment in the window (a different subsidiary, noted for completeness)
- The company recorded a goodwill impairment expense of $20.6 million on its Ergobaby reporting unit as of December 31, 2022, after "the fair value of the Ergobaby reporting unit did not exceed the carrying value"; Ergobaby (a subsidiary distinct from the 2018 transaction units above) had operating income of $9.1 million in the 2021 comparative period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022 goodwill note and MD&A]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~2021-2023 window the holding company remained a public reporting entity with consolidated net income of $51.4 million in FY2022 (down from $126.8 million in FY2021), while continuing to acquire (Lugano 2021, PrimaLoft 2022, Q4-2021 add-ons) and divest (Liberty Safe sold July 2021; a terminated Advanced Circuits divestiture). [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022]
- The two businesses acquired in early 2018 were both retained through the window: the platform business (Foam Fabricators, rebranded Altor Solutions) expanded to 18 North American locations with goodwill carried at $117.0 million and no impairment, and the Sterno add-on (Rimports) was folded into Sterno Home with documented product rationalization and rising distribution/wage/raw-material costs recorded in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001345126-23-000020, 10-K FY2022]
