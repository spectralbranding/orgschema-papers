# Structural sub-dossier — FB Financial Corporation acquisition of the Clayton Banks (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- FB Financial Corporation ("the Company"), through its wholly-owned banking
  subsidiary FirstBank, entered into a Stock Purchase Agreement dated February 8, 2017
  (as amended May 26, 2017) with Clayton HC, Inc., a Tennessee corporation ("Seller"),
  Clayton Bank and Trust ("CBT"), American City Bank ("ACB," and together with CBT, the
  "Clayton Banks"), and James L. Clayton, the approximately 98%-owner of Seller.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A p.1/summary]
- Under the agreement FirstBank committed to purchase from Seller all of the issued and
  outstanding shares of the Clayton Banks; following consummation, the Clayton Banks
  would merge with and into FirstBank, with FirstBank continuing as the surviving
  banking corporation — i.e., a whole-entity stock purchase followed by a bank-level
  merger, not a partial asset/branch carve-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A p.1/summary]
- CBT was headquartered in Knoxville, Tennessee with approximately $885 million in
  assets and 13 branches; ACB was headquartered in Tullahoma, Tennessee with
  approximately $314 million in assets and five branches; the Clayton Banks together
  operated 18 branches. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "Clayton HC and the Clayton Banks"]

## Consideration and financing structure at signing
- As consideration, Clayton HC was to receive at closing $124,200,000 in cash, 1,521,200
  shares of FB Financial common stock, and a $60 million Subordinated Note issued by
  FirstBank (10-year maturity, 5.5% fixed rate for the first five years); the Clayton
  Banks were also to distribute $79,500,000 of excess capital and approximately $4.8
  million book value of specified assets to Clayton HC before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "Questions and Answers"]
- To fund the cash consideration, FB Financial agreed to sell 4,806,710 shares of common
  stock at $33.00/share in a private placement, including 180,303 shares to Gordon
  Inman, an emeritus director and employee of the Company, whose participation required
  separate shareholder ratification. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A p.1/summary]
- Completion of the acquisition was conditioned on FB Financial shareholder approval of
  the stock issuance to Clayton HC (and ratification of the Inman private-placement
  shares), and on the receipt of required regulatory approvals from the Federal Reserve
  Board, FDIC, and Tennessee Department of Financial Institutions; the May 26, 2017
  amendment addressed competitive-effects concerns the Federal Reserve raised regarding
  Clayton HC's contemplated FB Financial ownership stake alongside its continued 50%
  ownership of a separate bank holding company (Apex Bancorp) with overlapping markets.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A p.1/summary; "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Acquisition"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle / product: 100% of the outstanding shares of the two operating banks
  (the customer-facing deposit- and lending-services businesses) transferred to
  FirstBank, which then merged the Clayton Banks into itself as surviving entity — the
  product and legal vehicle moved together, into the acquirer's own charter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "The Stock Purchase Agreement"]
- People/management: four named Clayton Banks executives (CBT's CEO and president, ACB's
  president/CEO, and CBT's chief lending officer) entered customary employment letters
  and restrictive-covenant agreements with FirstBank effective at closing, each for a
  two-year term, taking defined post-closing roles (e.g., Knoxville Market President,
  area president, President of a new Specialty Finance division); none would become
  executive officers of FB Financial or FirstBank. Mr. Clayton separately agreed to
  remain employed for three years post-closing to support integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "Employment Arrangements"]
- Governance: neither Clayton HC nor Mr. Clayton was granted any right to designate or
  appoint a director to the FB Financial or FirstBank boards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "Director Designation Rights"]
- Processes/systems: the merger agreement contained a dedicated "Transition;
  Informational Systems Conversion" provision governing the post-closing conversion of
  the Clayton Banks' operating systems into FirstBank's, indicating the operational/IT
  substrate was scheduled to be re-platformed onto the acquirer's systems rather than
  retained as a separate operating stack. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A Annex A §5.14]

## Regulatory approval, vote, and closing
- All required approvals from the Federal Reserve Board, FDIC, and TDFI necessary to
  consummate the stock purchase and bank merger were obtained the last week of June 2017.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A "Expected Timing of the Acquisition"]
- At a Special Meeting held July 28, 2017 in Nashville, Tennessee, FB Financial
  shareholders approved the share issuance to Clayton HC (22,975,336 for; 284 against;
  28,160 abstain) and ratified the Inman private-placement shares (22,996,570 for; 1,817
  against; 5,393 abstain). [SRC: SEC accession 0001157523-17-002166, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- On July 31, 2017, FB Financial completed the acquisition; pursuant to the first
  amendment, FirstBank elected to eliminate the $60 million Subordinated Note and instead
  paid Clayton HC total cash consideration of $184,200,000 (increased from the original
  $124,200,000), funded from the June 2017 private-placement proceeds plus cash on hand,
  alongside the 1,521,200 shares of Stock Consideration; the Clayton Banks were then
  merged with and into FirstBank, with FirstBank continuing as the surviving banking
  corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-245368, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-entity stock purchase of two privately-held Tennessee state banks (18 branches,
  combined ~$1.2 billion in assets) by a publicly traded bank holding company's banking
  subsidiary, funded by a mix of cash (raised via a dedicated equity private placement),
  newly issued acquirer stock, and (before an eleventh-hour amendment substituted
  additional cash) a subordinated note; the acquired banks' legal charters were
  extinguished into the acquirer via post-closing merger, key acquired-bank executives
  were retained under fixed-term employment agreements, and the operating/IT systems
  were scheduled for conversion onto the acquirer's platform under a dedicated
  transition provision. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-225253, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-245368, Form 8-K]
