  its common units under the Exchange Act, and its final Form 10-K under CIK 1179060 (for
  fiscal year 2018) was filed the following day; no further Form 10-K was filed under
  that CIK afterward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-009148, Form 15-12B]
- EnLink Midstream, LLC (CIK 1592000) continued to be an Exchange Act-reporting public
  company throughout the outcome window, with its common units continuing to trade on
  the New York Stock Exchange under the ticker "ENLC"; it filed a Form 10-K for fiscal
  year 2021 on February 16, 2022 and a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 on February 15,

## Realized financial trajectory (consolidated, EnLink Midstream, LLC)
- Total revenues were $6,052.9 million for the year ended December 31, 2019, $3,893.8
  million for 2020, $6,685.9 million for 2021, and $9,542.1 million for 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Operations] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-23-000003, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Net income (loss) attributable to ENLC was $(1,119.3) million for 2019, $(421.5)
  million for 2020, $22.4 million for 2021, and $361.3 million for 2022 — a swing from
  large multi-year losses to positive net income by the second and third years of the
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Operations] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-23-000003, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The 2019 and 2020 net losses included asset impairment charges of $1,133.5 million
  (2019) and $362.8 million (2020); the reported 2021 impairment charge was $0.8 million
  and no impairment charge was reported for 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, Consolidated Statements of Operations] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-23-000003, Form 10-K FY2022, Consolidated Statements of Operations]

## Documented operational facts over time
- As of December 31, 2021, the company (through its subsidiaries) employed 1,073
  full-time employees, of whom 249 were general and administrative, engineering,
  accounting, or commercial personnel and the remainder operational. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, "Human Capital"]
- As of December 31, 2022, the company (through its subsidiaries) employed 1,132
  full-time employees, of whom 268 were general and administrative, engineering,
  accounting, or commercial personnel and the remainder operational. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-23-000003, Form 10-K FY2022, "Human Capital"]
- The FY2021 Form 10-K's glossary confirms, as background, that on January 25, 2019 the
  merger subsidiary "merged with and into ENLK with ENLK continuing as the surviving
  entity and a subsidiary of ENLC," and that the underlying operating assets continued to
  be held through EnLink Midstream Operating, LP, described as "a Delaware limited
  partnership and wholly-owned subsidiary of ENLK." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, Glossary of Terms]
- Customer concentration in the outcome window included Devon Energy (6.7% of
  consolidated revenues in 2021), Dow Hydrocarbons and Resources LLC (14.5% in 2021), and
  Marathon Petroleum Corporation (13.4% in 2021), each individually exceeding 10% of
  consolidated revenues in at least one of the three years shown. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021, "Customers"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The operations represented by the drawn registrant continued to be publicly reported,
  not through that registrant's own CIK (which stopped filing after fiscal year 2018 and
  deregistered its units in February 2019) but through the surviving public filer's
  consolidated Forms 10-K. Across the outcome window, consolidated revenues rose from
  $3,893.8 million (2020) to $6,685.9 million (2021) to $9,542.1 million (2022), while net
  income attributable to the filer moved from large losses in 2019-2020 (driven
  substantially by asset impairment charges of $1,133.5 million and $362.8 million
  respectively) to positive net income of $22.4 million (2021) and $361.3 million (2022),
  alongside modest full-time headcount growth (1,073 to 1,132) and no reported impairment
  charge in fiscal year 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-22-000006, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592000-23-000003, Form 10-K FY2022]
