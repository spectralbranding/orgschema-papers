## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following completion of the merger, trading in the Company's common stock was
  suspended prior to the open of trading on September 13, 2017, and the Company
  requested that the Nasdaq Stock Market file a Form 25 with the SEC to remove the
  common stock from listing and deregister it under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056725, Form 8-K item 3.01]
- On September 22, 2017, the Company filed a Form 15-12G certifying termination of the
  registration of its common stock under Exchange Act Section 12(g) and suspension of
  its duty to file periodic reports, listing an approximate number of holders of record
  of "one." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-058398, Form 15-12G]
- SEC EDGAR's electronic filing history for the registrant (CIK 0000791519) shows no
  Form 10-K filed after the 10-K covering the fiscal year ended January 28, 2017 (filed
  March 9, 2017) and no Form 8-K filed after September 2017. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000791519.json]
- Consequently no audited public financial statements exist for the registrant covering
  a ~2020-2022 outcome window; the realized-record facts below rely on reputable press
  and third-party regulatory-tracking sources concerning the business as operated under
  private ownership, not on primary SEC filings of the acquired entity itself.

## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced, within or near the fixed window)
- In early 2021 the company (still owned by an affiliate of Sycamore Partners) made an
  approximately $1 billion offer to acquire the consumer retail unit of ODP Corp.
  (Office Depot); in November 2021 it reaffirmed its interest, stating it was confident
  the parties could obtain the necessary antitrust approvals. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/office-depot-owner-delays-spinoff-as-it-mulls-sale-of-retail-business/617202/]
- On June 21, 2022, ODP Corp.'s board announced it would not proceed with a sale or
  spin-off of its consumer businesses, rejecting the acquisition bids it had received
  (including the offer from Staples) and citing "current market and macroeconomic
  conditions" and the benefits of maintaining purchasing and supply-chain synergies;
  the board stated it would remain "whole and independent." [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/after-much-mulling-office-depot-owner-rejects-sale-and-split/625844/]
- A labor-and-financial watchdog organization's regulatory-violation tracking
  attributed to the Company a Massachusetts wage-and-hour penalty of $165,000 (2019),
  multiple U.S. OSHA workplace-safety penalties ranging from approximately $6,270 to
  $16,102 (dated across 2019-2021), and a California South Coast Air Quality Management
  District environmental penalty of $15,000 (2022). [SRC: https://pestakeholder.org/news/sycamore-partners-strategy-has-hurt-workers-generated-poor-returns-for-investors/]

## Items noted but outside the fixed window or not independently confirmed
- Trade press reported in March 2019 (before the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window) that
  the sponsor was said to be preparing a recapitalization plan that would add roughly
  $1 billion of debt to the Company, raising total debt to approximately $5.325
  billion; the report describes a plan under consideration at that date, and this
  dossier does not independently confirm the plan's completion. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/bloomberg-sycamore-seeks-1b-in-staples-recapitalization/551325]
- Trade press dated July 14, 2023 (beyond the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window)
  reported total debt maturities exceeding $5 billion through 2027, a fiscal-2023
  first-quarter revenue of approximately $2.676 billion and EBITDA of approximately
  $209 million, and market sources expressing concern about a possible "aggressive
  liability management exercise" by the sponsor, referencing the sponsor's history
  with other portfolio-company restructurings. [SRC: https://ionanalytics.com/insights/debtwire/staples-in-focus-as-return-to-office-stagnates-market-mulls-threat-of-financial-maneuvers/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because the Company's stock ceased trading and its Exchange Act registration was
  terminated within about ten days of closing, and no periodic report was filed
  thereafter, there is no primary-source audited outcome record for the ~2020-2022
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-058398, Form 15-12G] [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000791519.json]

  third-party regulatory tracker, shows a business that remained strategically active
  (an approximately $1 billion bid to acquire a competitor's retail unit, ultimately

  penalties and, in reporting from just after the window, market commentary about the
  sponsor's approach to the balance sheet — a record insufficient to support a
  definitive coding of the realized trajectory. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/after-much-mulling-office-depot-owner-rejects-sale-and-split/625844/] [SRC: https://pestakeholder.org/news/sycamore-partners-strategy-has-hurt-workers-generated-poor-returns-for-investors/]
