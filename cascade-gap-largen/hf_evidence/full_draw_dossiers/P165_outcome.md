## Outcome window and reporting status
- The reorganized registrant continued as an SEC-reporting company after the reorganization became effective, and the fixed outcome window is its fiscal-year-2022 annual report (period ended December 31, 2022), filed February 28, 2023 — approximately 4 years after the ~2018 closing. All scalars below are from that primary filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The final monthly operating report was filed for the period March 1–31, 2019, and "on May 1, 2019, the conditions to the effectiveness of the Modified Fifth Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization … were satisfied and the Debtors emerged from Chapter 11." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-140648, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Realized financial record (FY2022, with prior-year comparatives)
- Revenue was $3,912.3 million in 2022 versus $3,558.3 million in 2021, an increase of 9.9%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Operating income was $56.9 million in 2022 versus $154.9 million in 2021, a decrease of 63.3%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Net loss attributable to the Company "increased to $264.7 million during the year ended December 31, 2022," compared with a net loss of $159.2 million in 2021 and $1,914.7 million in 2020; basic net loss per common share was $(1.79) in 2022 versus $(1.09) in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Adjusted EBITDA was $950.3 million in 2022 versus $811.1 million in 2021 (a non-GAAP measure the Company reconciles to net loss). [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Disruption at the product / asset interface over time
- In the fourth quarter of 2022 the Company recognized a non-cash impairment charge of $302.1 million on its FCC licenses (the broadcast-radio operating asset), following a reevaluation of the fair values of its reporting units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The Company describes having "transitioned our business from a single platform radio broadcast operator to a company with multiple platforms including digital, podcasting, networks and events, as well as ad technology capabilities," and reports on three reportable segments — the Multiplatform Group (broadcast radio, networks, sponsorships and events), the Digital Audio Group, and the Audio & Media Services Group (Katz Media). [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Revenue mix shifted toward the newer platform: Digital Audio Group revenue increased $187.3 million versus the prior year, while Multiplatform Group revenue increased $108.2 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Disruption at the process / organization interface over time
- The Company continued to incur restructuring expenses (primarily severance) of $75.8 million in 2022 versus $73.3 million in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Under "operating-expense-savings initiatives" the Company "taken proactive steps to streamline our real estate footprint and reduce related lease and operating" expenses, actions it states "typically result in impairment charges due to the write-down of the affected right-of-use assets and related fixed assets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Status of the separated businesses
- The separation of Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. remained effected and continued to be governed post-emergence by inter-company arrangements: in connection with "the separation (the 'Separation') of Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. as part of our plan of reorganization, we entered into the Tax Matters Agreement" with iHeartCommunications, Clear Channel Holdings, Inc., and Clear Channel Outdoor Holdings, Inc. (the "Outdoor Group"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Through the fixed FY2022 window the reorganized registrant remained a going concern operating the retained audio business at scale (revenue $3,912.3 million, +9.9% year over year; Adjusted EBITDA $950.3 million) while continuing to report a net loss (attributable net loss increasing to $264.7 million), recognizing a $302.1 million non-cash impairment on its FCC broadcast-license asset in Q4 2022, and incurring continued restructuring/severance expense ($75.8 million) and right-of-use-asset write-downs as it moved from a single-platform radio operation to a multi-platform audio and ad-technology model; the separated outdoor business (New CCOH) remained an independent company governed by the inter-company Tax Matters Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001400891-23-000008, Form 10-K FY2022]
