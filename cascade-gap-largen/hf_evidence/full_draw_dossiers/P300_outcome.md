## Public-reporting status at and after completion (feasibility-relevant)

- On November 8, 2017, Baton Merger Corp. merged with and into Bankrate, Inc., with
  Bankrate continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned indirect
  subsidiary of Red Ventures Holdco, LP; the surviving entity's SEC filings are made under
  the name Baton Holding, LLC (as successor to Bankrate, Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-337270, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The aggregate cash paid in the merger (including certain debt repaid in connection with
  it) was approximately $1.4 billion, funded by Red Ventures with proceeds of term loans
  under its credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-337270, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At the effective time, the company's pre-merger directors and executive officers ceased
  serving as directors and executive officers of the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-337270, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- On the closing date the company notified the NYSE that the merger had completed and
  requested that the NYSE suspend trading, remove the common stock from listing prior to
  the open of trading on November 9, 2017, and file a Form 25 to remove the stock from
  listing and deregister it under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-337270, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- In connection with the merger the company repaid in full and terminated its $70,000,000
  Revolving Credit Agreement and redeemed all outstanding 6.125% Senior Notes due 2018,
  after which the trustee discharged the related indenture, extinguishing the registrant's
  outstanding public debt instruments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-337270, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- On November 9, 2017, Baton Holding, LLC (as successor to Bankrate, Inc.) filed a Form
  15-12B certifying termination of registration of the common stock under Exchange Act
  Section 12(g) / suspension of reporting duties under Sections 13 and 15(d), stating the
  approximate number of holders of record as "None." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-338563, Form 15-12B]
- SEC EDGAR's filing history for CIK 0001518222 shows no Form 10-K, 20-F, or other periodic
  report filed after the Form 10-K for fiscal year 2016 (filed March 22, 2017, i.e. before
  the closing); no periodic filings appear under this CIK across the fixed ~2020-2022
  outcome window. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001518222.json]
- SEC EDGAR's company search returns no registrant matching "Red Ventures" (no CIK, no
  periodic-filing history), consistent with the acquiring parent not being an SEC
  reporting company. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&company=red+ventures&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100&output=atom]
