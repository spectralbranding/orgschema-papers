## Public-reporting status through the window
- Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. remained an SEC-reporting registrant throughout the window and
  filed annual reports on Form 10-K for each fiscal year, including the fiscal year ended
  May 30, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- Net income attributable to Lamb Weston Holdings, Inc. was $478.6 million in fiscal 2019,
  $365.9 million in fiscal 2020, and $317.8 million in fiscal 2021 — a decline of
  approximately 13% from fiscal 2020 to fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]
- Consolidated net sales declined $121.5 million, or 3%, to $3,670.9 million in fiscal 2021,
  compared with $3,792.4 million in fiscal 2020; volume declined 6% while price/mix
  increased 3% (net sales and volume declined 2% and 6% respectively excluding the benefit
  of a 53rd week in the prior year). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]

## Documented disruption at the product/demand interface
- The fiscal-2021 decline in sales volume reflected soft demand for much of the first three
  fiscal quarters following government-imposed pandemic-related social restrictions,
  including on restaurants and other foodservice operations; sales volumes increased in the
  fiscal fourth quarter on a recovery in demand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]
- The company identified the COVID-19 pandemic and related governmental actions —
  operative in calendar 2020 and continuing into calendar 2021 — as materially affecting
  demand for its products and its results, given its concentration in restaurant and
  foodservice channels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]

## Documented condition at the process/operations interface
- During the pandemic the company's manufacturing facilities were deemed essential under
  government guidelines and remained open and operational, though production was subject to
  pandemic-related absenteeism and the shutdown of lines for site cleaning and similar
  items. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]
- The company performed its annual goodwill impairment assessment in the fourth quarter of
  fiscal 2021 on a qualitative basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fiscal-2019-2021 window the company operated continuously as a standalone
  SEC-reporting registrant with net sales near $3.7 billion, while net income attributable
  to the company declined over the period (to $317.8 million in fiscal 2021 from
  $478.6 million in fiscal 2019); the fiscal-2021 softness was attributed to
  pandemic-driven demand declines in its restaurant/foodservice channels, with
  manufacturing operations kept running as essential but subject to pandemic-related
  absenteeism and line stoppages, followed by a fourth-quarter demand recovery. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-009330, Form 10-K (fiscal year ended May 30, 2021)]
