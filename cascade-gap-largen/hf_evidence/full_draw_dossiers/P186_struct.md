# Structural sub-dossier — Praxair, Inc. / Linde AG business combination (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Praxair, Inc. and Linde Aktiengesellschaft ("Linde AG") entered into a business
  combination agreement, dated June 1, 2017, as amended, providing for a combination
  of their businesses under a newly formed Irish holding company, Linde plc (formerly
  known as Zamalight plc). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- Praxair's business was to be brought under the new holding company through a merger,
  in which each Praxair share would be converted into the right to receive one Linde
  plc ordinary share; Linde AG's business was to be brought under the new holding
  company through an exchange offer, in which Linde AG shareholders would be offered
  1.540 Linde plc shares for each Linde AG share; assuming full participation, former
  Praxair shareholders and former Linde AG shareholders were each expected to own
  approximately 50% of Linde plc upon completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- Linde plc is a newly incorporated public limited company formed under the laws of
  Ireland on April 18, 2017, that would become the parent company of both Praxair,
  Inc. and Linde AG upon completion; it formed Zamalight Holdco LLC (Delaware) and
  Linde Holding GmbH (Germany) as transaction vehicles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]

## Listing and legal-entity effects at closing
- Linde plc applied to list its shares on the New York Stock Exchange (trading in
  U.S. dollars) and the Frankfurt Stock Exchange (trading in euros); Praxair shares,
  previously listed on the NYSE under symbol "PX," were to be removed from NYSE
  listing upon or as soon as practicable after completion, as permitted by applicable
  law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- Linde plc's principal executive offices were set in Guildford, Surrey, United
  Kingdom, with a registered office in Dublin, Ireland; Linde plc was expected to be
  tax resident solely in the United Kingdom. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- Because Linde plc would hold at least 75% but not 100% of Linde AG's outstanding
  shares under the minimum-acceptance condition, Linde AG would become an indirectly
  majority-owned, legally "dependent company" of Linde plc under Section 17 of the
  German Stock Corporation Act, with the framework for that dependency governed by
  Sections 311 et seq. of that Act absent a separate domination agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]

## Governance and management structure agreed at closing
- The Linde plc board of directors was to consist, at the effective time of the
  merger, of twelve members: Stephen F. Angel (Praxair's then-Chairman and CEO) as
  CEO and a board member; Prof. Dr. Wolfgang Reitzle (then-Chairman of Linde AG's
  supervisory board) as Chairman of the board; five additional non-executive
  directors designated by Praxair; and five additional non-executive directors
  designated by Linde AG. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- The stated governance design reinforced the target 50:50 ownership with "an equal
  representation of six board members from each of Linde and Praxair on the board of
  directors of Linde plc and an equal representation of three key executives each on
  the Management Committee which comprises the key executives from both Praxair and
  Linde who directly report to the CEO of Linde plc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- The operations of the combined group were stated to be "appropriately divided
  between Munich and Danbury," i.e., two operating locations corresponding to Linde
  AG's and Praxair's respective pre-combination headquarters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- As part of the negotiated terms, the agreement was to provide for "adequate
  protection of employment in Germany, including no further headcount reductions
  beyond those announced by Linde in connection with its LIFT program for a period
  of three years." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]

## Regulatory and integration terms stated at closing
- Completion was conditioned on antitrust and other regulatory approvals in multiple
  jurisdictions, including U.S. Hart-Scott-Rodino clearance from the FTC/DOJ and
  clearance under the EU Merger Regulation from the European Commission; the filing
  disclosed that such agencies "may impose requirements, limitations or costs or
  require divestitures" as a condition of approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
- The business combination was projected, at signing, to create approximately $1.2
  billion (€1.1 billion) in annual synergies and cost reductions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a stock-for-stock combination of two
  going-concern industrial-gas businesses under a newly formed common parent, with
  no single acquirer/target legal form: both operating companies (Praxair, Inc. and
  Linde AG) became subsidiaries of the new holding company, Linde plc, with
  contractually specified equal board and Management Committee representation, a
  split of operating locations between the two legacy headquarters (Danbury and
  Munich), and a three-year negotiated employment-protection commitment in Germany;
  completion remained conditioned on multi-jurisdiction antitrust clearance that
  could require asset divestitures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-259883, Form DEFM14A/prospectus]
