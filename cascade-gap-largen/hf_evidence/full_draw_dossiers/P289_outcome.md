## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- HSN, Inc. (CIK 0001434729) filed a Form 15-12B to deregister its common stock, dated
  2018-01-11, and filed no Form 10-K under its own CIK afterward; there is no separate
  entity-level annual report for HSN, Inc. covering the 3-5-year post-closing period.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001845, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer, Qurate Retail, Inc. (CIK 0001355096), continued filing consolidated
  Form 10-Ks; its FY2020 10-K (filed 2021-02-26, ~3 years after the December 2017
  closing) is used here as the fixed outcome-window source, to the extent it separately
  discloses HSN-specific facts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail
  Form 10-K FY2020]

## Post-closing internal restructuring and reporting changes
- On December 31, 2018, Qurate Retail transferred its 100% ownership interest in HSN to
  QVC, Inc. (an operating subsidiary) "through a transaction among entities under common
  control." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]
- On October 17, 2018, Qurate Retail announced initiatives "designed to better position
  its HSN and QVC U.S. businesses" (the "QRG Initiatives"); as a result, during Q1 2019
  it changed its reportable segments to combine HSN and QVC U.S. into a single segment
  called "QxH," ending HSN's standalone segment-level financial reporting. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented HSN-specific financial trajectory
- The company recorded impairment losses to the HSN indefinite-lived tradename
  intangible asset of $30 million (year ended December 31, 2018) and $147 million (year
  ended December 31, 2019), based on a quantitative fair-value assessment against HSN's
  own projected future operating performance; no HSN goodwill impairment was necessary
  in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]
- No impairment loss was recorded against HSN's tradename or goodwill for the year ended
  December 31, 2020 — the fiscal year within the fixed ~3-year outcome window covered by
  this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]

## Documented disruption at the process/organization interface
- In October 2018 the company announced HSN and QVC U.S. would open a new distribution
  facility in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania in 2019, with anticipated closure of existing
  facilities in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, Roanoke, Virginia, and Greeneville, Tennessee in
  2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]
- The company disclosed that, in late 2019, it began shipping from Bethlehem "but it is
  not operating at full capacity," citing package-handling and warehouse-management
  systems "not performing as anticipated," which "has caused delays" and could in turn
  delay closing the legacy facilities including Lancaster. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]
- As of the FY2020 10-K, the combined "QxH" segment is described as still owning the
  Lancaster distribution center (among others) and only leasing Bethlehem, indicating
  the facility-consolidation process begun in 2018 had not fully concluded as planned by
  fiscal year-end 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form
  10-K FY2020]
- The HSN brand and "HSN" channel/website continued to operate as a named component of
  the combined QxH segment as of the FY2020 10-K (e.g., "the main QVC and HSN channels,"
  HSN.com), indicating the brand persisted as an identifiable operating unit within the
  combined segment rather than being discontinued. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- HSN, Inc. deregistered and files no separate periodic reports, so there is no
  standalone public annual report for the business; the acquirer's Form 10-K does,
  however, separately disclose HSN-specific facts: its ownership was reorganized within
  the corporate group about a year after closing; its segment reporting was merged with
  QVC U.S. approximately fifteen months after closing; its tradename was impaired by $30
  million (2018) and $147 million (2019), with no further tradename or goodwill
  impairment recorded for 2020; and a distribution-facility consolidation announced
  alongside the segment change showed documented operational delays extending into and
  past fiscal year 2020, while the HSN brand itself continued operating as a named unit
  within the combined segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail
  Form 10-K FY2020]
