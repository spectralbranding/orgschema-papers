# Structural sub-dossier — XL Group Ltd acquisition by AXA SA (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- XL Group Ltd, a Bermuda exempted company, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated March 5, 2018 with AXA SA and Camelot Holdings Ltd, a Bermuda exempted company
  and wholly owned subsidiary of AXA formed solely to effect the transaction ("Merger
  Sub"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- XL Group Ltd, through its subsidiaries, is described as a global insurance and
  reinsurance company providing property, casualty and special products to industrial,
  commercial and professional firms, insurance companies and other enterprises on a
  worldwide basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and consideration
- The transaction was structured as a statutory merger under Section 105 of the Bermuda
  Companies Act 1981: Merger Sub would merge with and into XL, with XL surviving the
  merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of AXA; under Bermuda law XL's and Merger Sub's
  respective undertakings, property and liabilities become vested in XL as the surviving
  company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- At the effective time each issued and outstanding XL common share ($0.01 par value),
  other than shares held by XL as treasury, by XL's wholly owned subsidiaries, or by
  AXA / Merger Sub / AXA's wholly owned subsidiaries (with certain exceptions), would be
  canceled and converted into the right to receive $57.60 in cash, without interest and
  less any required withholding taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- The $57.60 per-share price compared to a $37.34 NYSE closing price on February 6, 2018
  (the last full trading day before reports that XL was a potential acquisition target)
  and $43.30 on March 2, 2018 (the last full trading day before the announcement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the boundary at closing (tier evidence)
- Controlling intent: 100% of XL's equity was acquired by AXA; XL continued in existence
  as a Bermuda exempted company but as a wholly owned subsidiary of AXA, i.e. control of
  the whole going concern passed to AXA at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- Business model / product: the insurance and reinsurance underwriting operations
  (property, casualty and special products) crossed intact as XL's subsidiaries continued
  under the surviving company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- Stated integration intent: AXA's initial offer set out that XL "would retain
  significant autonomy within AXA, while being combined with certain components of AXA's
  existing businesses"; discussions contemplated that members of XL management (with
  Mr. Hendrick possibly serving as CEO of the combined business unit) would have a role
  running the combined business unit, though no specific management-retention terms were
  set at the offer stage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
- People / equity substrate: at closing XL equity awards would vest and be cashed out at
  the $57.60 price — including an estimated 16,000 restricted shares ($921,600), 853,861
  performance unit awards ($49,182,422), 216,465 restricted share unit awards
  ($12,468,450), 7,856,873 stock option awards ($202,526,066) and 22,265 restricted cash
  unit awards ($1,282,464). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]

## Listing status change effected at closing
- The merger agreement provided that, upon completion, XL's common shares would be
  removed from listing on the NYSE (symbol "XL") and the Bermuda Stock Exchange (symbol
  "XL.BH") and deregistered under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, after which there
  would be no public market for the common shares and XL would no longer file periodic
  reports with the SEC on account of its common shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction was a negotiated all-cash whole-company statutory
  merger transferring 100% control of a global insurance/reinsurance underwriting group
  to a foreign acquirer (AXA SA), with the operating subsidiaries crossing intact as the
  surviving company and a stated intent that the business retain "significant autonomy"
  while being combined with certain AXA businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000279, DEFM14A]
