> information about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The outcome entity is Iron Mountain Incorporated, and the outcome window is its fiscal
  year ended December 31, 2022 — approximately four years after the January 10, 2018
  closing — as reported in its Annual Report on Form 10-K for 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Realized financial scalars (FY2022)
- Total revenues were $5,103,574 thousand for 2022, compared with $4,491,531 thousand for
  2021 and $4,147,270 thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Net income (loss) was $562,149 thousand for 2022, compared with $452,725 thousand for
  2021 and $343,096 thousand for 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The Global Data Center Business segment reported segment revenue of $426,074 thousand in
  the 2022 segment reconciliation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Realized record of the acquired data-center operations
- As of December 31, 2022, the company reported 747 megawatts in land and buildings owned
  or operated and described itself as among the largest global data center operators. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The Global Data Center Business comprised 21 operating data centers across 19 global
  markets, held directly or through unconsolidated joint ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- As of December 31, 2022 the company reported 192 MW of leasable capacity with an
  additional 556 MW under construction or held for development. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The company disclosed that it had built the Global Data Center Business through
  acquisitions and organic growth, and in September 2022 announced "Project Matterhorn," a
  global program to accelerate business growth. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Property acquired in the IO Data Centers transaction remained on the company's books: a
  properties footnote identifies property "acquired in connection with our acquisition of
  IO Data Centers, LLC" — i.e., the acquired facilities were retained, with no divestiture
  of them disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Documented disruption at the tier interfaces (searched)
- No goodwill impairment was recorded against the Global Data Center reporting unit: the
  company's accumulated goodwill impairment balance was unchanged at $132,409 thousand from
  December 31, 2021 to December 31, 2022, and at the October 1, 2022 annual impairment test
  all reporting units had estimated fair values exceeding their carrying values by greater
  than 20%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The retrieved filing documents no impairment, divestiture, or operational fracture of the
  acquired data-center operations within the outcome window; the data center business is
  described as continuing to grow. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The company employed approximately 26,000 people as of December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Approximately four years after the January 2018 acquisition, Iron Mountain reported total
  revenues of $5.10 billion and net income of $562.1 million for 2022; the acquired data
  center facilities were retained (no divestiture disclosed), the Global Data Center segment
  was one of 21 operating centers in a 747-MW owned-or-operated portfolio, and no
  data-center impairment or operational disruption was recorded in the filing for the
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001020569-23-000043, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
