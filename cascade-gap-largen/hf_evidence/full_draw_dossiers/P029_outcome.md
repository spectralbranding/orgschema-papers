## Public-reporting status across the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The separated company became an independent publicly-traded company as Delphi
  Technologies PLC on December 4, 2017; its last standalone annual report on Form 10-K
  covers the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019 (filed 2020-02-13). [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- On October 1, 2020, BorgWarner Inc. completed its acquisition of Delphi Technologies PLC
  by means of a court-sanctioned scheme of arrangement, after which Delphi Technologies
  became a wholly-owned subsidiary of BorgWarner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-261833, Form 8-K (event date October 1, 2020)]
- Prior to the effective time the shares had traded on the NYSE under the symbol DLPH; a
  Form 25-NSE (notification of delisting) was filed 2020-10-02 and a Form 15-12B
  (certification and notice of termination of registration under Section 12(b)) was filed
  2020-10-13, ending the company's public reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-20-000869, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0001206774-20-002875, Form 15-12B]
- Consequently no standalone public annual report exists for fiscal years 2020, 2021, or
  2022; the best-documented realized scalars below come from the last standalone Form 10-K
  (FY2019) and the primary filings recording the October 2020 acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Realized financial record — last standalone audited year (FY2019 vs FY2018)
- Net sales were $4,361 million for the year ended December 31, 2019, down from $4,858
  million for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019), selected financial data]
- Operating income was $141 million in 2019, down from $434 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019), selected financial data]
- Net income was $33 million in 2019, down from $380 million in 2018; net income
  attributable to Delphi Technologies was $17 million in 2019, down from $358 million in
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019), selected financial data]

## Documented operational strain and reorganization over the window
- Cost of sales in 2019 was affected by a shift in customer demand from higher-margin
  passenger-car diesel fuel injection systems toward lower-margin GDi fuel injection
  systems, an increase in impairment charges of $22 million related to declines in the fair
  values of certain fixed assets, and $14 million of additional depreciation expense. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Selling, general and administrative expense in 2019 included an increase in impairment
  charges of $9 million related to certain capitalized information technology assets and a
  real estate operating lease asset. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- As of December 31, 2019 the company reorganized the management reporting structure,
  splitting the former Powertrain Systems segment into three distinct segments (with the
  Aftermarket segment unchanged), stated to "better align our product portfolios and process
  technology expertise." [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Terminal transaction within the window
- On January 28, 2020 the company announced a definitive transaction agreement under which
  BorgWarner would acquire it in an all-stock transaction, with each Delphi Technologies
  ordinary share to be exchanged for 0.4534 shares of BorgWarner common stock plus cash in
  lieu of fractional shares; this transaction closed October 1, 2020 as described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001707092-20-000004, Form 10-K (FY2019)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-261833, Form 8-K (event date October 1, 2020)]

  October 1, 2020, delisted, and deregistered in October 2020, so no standalone public
