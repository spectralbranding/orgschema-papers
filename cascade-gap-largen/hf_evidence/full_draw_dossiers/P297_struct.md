# Structural sub-dossier — Blue Hills Bancorp, Inc. combination with Independent Bank Corp. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Blue Hills Bancorp, Inc. ("BHB") and Independent Bank Corp. ("Independent") entered
  into an Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated September 20, 2018, by and among
  Independent, Rockland Trust Company, BHB, and Blue Hills Bank; under the agreement BHB
  merges with and into Independent, with Independent as the surviving company, and
  immediately thereafter Blue Hills Bank (BHB's wholly owned bank subsidiary) merges with
  and into Rockland Trust Company (Independent's wholly owned bank subsidiary), with
  Rockland Trust as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration: for each share of BHB common stock, stockholders receive $5.25 in cash
  plus 0.2308 of a share of Independent common stock, leaving BHB stockholders holding
  approximately 18-20% of the combined company. The merger became effective April 1,
  2019, after the parties announced on March 26, 2019 that all regulatory approvals had
  been received. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0000943374-19-000172, Form 8-K dated 2019-03-26]

## Controlling intent and business rationale (as stated by each board)
- Independent's board characterized the transaction as "a natural expansion and
  strengthening" of its Norfolk, Suffolk, and Nantucket County, Massachusetts franchise,
  citing access to new deposit markets (including Nantucket, where Rockland Trust had no
  prior physical presence) and the opportunity "to maintain and deepen relationships with
  customers of Blue Hills by offering Independent's deeper set of products." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- BHB's board cited, among other factors, that stockholders would receive consideration
  largely in Independent stock and participate in the combined company's future
  earnings, and Independent's "successful track record in executing mergers." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- As a structural consequence, the combined institution was expected to exceed $10
  billion in total consolidated assets (each of Independent and Rockland Trust reaching
  approximately $11.5 billion), triggering additional regulatory regimes (Durbin
  Amendment interchange restrictions, CFPB supervision) that did not apply to either
  standalone below that threshold. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed at the tier level, and what was bridged rather than transferred whole
- The customer-facing product/asset — BHB's and Blue Hills Bank's loan and deposit base
  and banking relationships — was folded into Independent/Rockland Trust's existing
  operation and broader product set at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- The operational/processing substrate did NOT transfer intact: merger-agreement Section
  5.15 ("Transition; Informational Systems Conversion") provides that the parties "shall
  meet on a regular basis to discuss and plan for the conversion of the data processing
  and related electronic informational systems of Company... to those used by Buyer,"
  covering third-party vendor arrangements, non-renewal of BHB's leases/software
  licenses, retention of outside consultants and additional employees to assist with
  conversion, and outsourcing of proprietary system services as appropriate after the
  effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- Section 5.16 ("Access to Customers and Suppliers") separately required the parties to
  "work together to promote good relations" between Blue Hills Bank and its customers,
  with any customer meetings to discuss the combination requiring "the express, prior
  permission of" BHB — customer-facing access was contractually gated pending the
  operational conversion above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- Governance continuity: Independent agreed to elect three individuals from BHB's board
  to the boards of Independent and Rockland Trust as a condition of BHB's obligation to
  close. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]

## People / management substrate at closing
- The Blue Hills Bank Employee Stock Ownership Plan was terminated as of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
- BHB executive officers held pre-existing change-in-control agreements paying, upon a
  qualifying termination within two years of closing, two times base salary plus average
  short-term incentive compensation; one named executive's agreement was instead
  terminated immediately pre-closing for a $10,000 lump-sum release payment. BHB's/Blue
  Hills's chief executive, William M. Parent, held a separate employment agreement
  (lump-sum severance valued at approximately $2,118,366 if terminated without cause/for
  good reason within 24 months) and a Non-Competition Agreement under which Rockland
  Trust pays him $1 million, offsetting that severance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing status at closing
- The merger agreement's mutual covenants include removal of BHB common stock from
  listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market in connection with the transaction, while
  Independent's common stock ("INDB") continues to be listed on Nasdaq after the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined BHB/Blue Hills Bank's customer-facing banking business and
  loan/deposit base into Independent/Rockland Trust's existing operation at the holding-
  company and bank levels simultaneously, while the underlying data-processing/systems
  substrate and customer-contact access were explicitly NOT treated as transferring
  automatically — both were bridged by dedicated merger-agreement provisions (Sections
  5.15 and 5.16) governing a post-closing conversion and access process, alongside
  board-seat continuity for three former BHB directors and individually negotiated
  executive retention/severance/non-competition terms for BHB's senior officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-347556, Form DEFM14A]
