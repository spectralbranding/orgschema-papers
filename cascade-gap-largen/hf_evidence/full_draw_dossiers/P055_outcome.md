# Outcome sub-dossier — Owens Corning glass-reinforcements business (realized 3–5-year record)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction record within the fixed outcome window. It contains no closing-time
> structural information.

## Outcome window and source
- Outcome window fixed at construction: the fiscal 2010–2012 record of Owens Corning (the
  consolidating registrant), i.e., 3–5 years after the ~mid-2007 closing. The primary
  source is Owens Corning's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2011, filed February 15, 2012
  (accession 0001193125-12-062185); scalars are cited within this in-window filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Retention and integration of the acquired business
- The business was retained and integrated rather than divested: the FY2011 10-K reports
  that "integration work associated with the 2007 Acquisition was completed in 2010,"
  referring to Owens Corning's acquisition of "Saint-Gobain's reinforcement and composite
  fabrics business in 2007." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- By FY2011 the reinforcements business is reported as part of the Composites segment,
  "which includes our Reinforcements and Downstream businesses"; within Reinforcements the
  Company "manufactures, fabricates and sells glass reinforcements in the form of fiber."
  Composites accounted for approximately 36% of total reportable-segment net sales in 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Realized financial scalars (in-window)
- Composites-segment EBIT "improved by 15 percent from $175 million in 2010 to $201 million
  in 2011." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- Company-wide net earnings attributable to Owens Corning were $276 million in 2011,
  compared with $933 million in 2010 (the 2010 figure was driven by the reversal of a
  valuation allowance against certain U.S. deferred tax assets, not by operations). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Documented rationalization at the product/process interface
- On May 18, 2011, Owens Corning "sold its Composites glass reinforcements facility in
  Capivari, Brazil to Chongqing Polycomp," for proceeds of $9 million; the FY2011 10-K
  states these facilities "were sold as a result of the integration of the Saint-Gobain's
  reinforcement and composite fabrics business in 2007 ('2007 Acquisition')." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- The Capivari divestiture reduced 2011 Composites net sales by approximately $30 million
  versus 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Documented facts at the process/organization (people) interface
- A former Saint-Gobain executive assumed senior line responsibility for the combined
  business: the FY2011 10-K lists Owens Corning's "Group President, Composite Solutions
  since December 2010" as "formerly ... President of Saint-Gobain Reinforcement and
  Composites Business and Textile Solutions Business, Paris." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Residual contingency from the transaction
- The FY2011 10-K discloses a tax claim of approximately 123 million euros against a
  subsidiary the Company acquired as part of the 2007 Acquisition, relating to transactions
  that occurred prior to closing; under the purchase agreement governing the 2007
  Acquisition, "Saint-Gobain is required to indemnify Owens Corning and its subsidiaries for
  pre-closing tax claims and related damages, attorney fees and expenses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed FY2010–2012 window, the acquired glass-reinforcements business was fully
  integrated (integration reported complete in 2010) and retained inside Owens Corning's
  Composites segment rather than divested wholesale; the segment posted rising EBIT ($175M →
  $201M, 2010→2011); management of the combined business passed in part to a former
  Saint-Gobain executive (Group President, Composite Solutions from December 2010); and the
  Company rationalized one overlapping plant (Capivari, Brazil, sold May 2011 for $9M),
  while a ~€123M pre-closing tax contingency at an acquired subsidiary remained subject to a
  Saint-Gobain indemnity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-062185, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
