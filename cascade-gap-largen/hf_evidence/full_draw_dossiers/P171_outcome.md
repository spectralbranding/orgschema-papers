## Realized financial trajectory
- Net income attributable to the Company for fiscal year 2017 was $47.062 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Selected Financial Data]
- The Company reported a net loss attributable to the Company of $9.075 million for
  fiscal year 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Selected Financial Data]
- The Company reported a net loss of $449.305 million for fiscal year 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Consolidated Statements of Operations; confirmed in SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The Company reported a net loss of $277.353 million for fiscal year 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- Full cost ceiling charges against the Company's oil-and-gas asset base were $409.6
  million for fiscal year 2019 and $218.4 million for fiscal year 2020 (none recorded
  for fiscal years 2017 or 2018); the cumulative full cost ceiling charge from the
  Effective Date through December 31, 2020 totaled $947.1 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Oil and Natural Gas Properties note]

## Executive leadership turnover during the window
- On February 8, 2018 the Company's then-CEO, James Bennett, separated employment from
  the Company, and on February 22, 2018 the then-CFO, Julian Bott, also separated
  employment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Employee Termination Benefits note]
- In the second quarter of 2018 the Company engaged in a proxy contest with its largest
  shareholder, Carl C. Icahn and certain affiliated entities, which resulted in the
  election of a majority of non-incumbent directors to the board; a Settlement
  Agreement among the Company and the Icahn parties was dated June 19, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Proxy Contest note and exhibit index]
- On December 12, 2019 the board appointed John P. Suter (then COO) as Interim
  President and CEO, succeeding Paul D. McKinney, who resigned as President, CEO, and
  as a director. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Recent Events]
- On July 1, 2020 the then-CFO, Michael A. Johnson, and the then-COO, John Suter, both
  separated employment from the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Employee Termination Benefits note]
- The Company entered a Letter Agreement with Carl F. Giesler, Jr. in April 2020 (filed
  as an exhibit to an 8-K dated April 7, 2020); Mr. Giesler signed the FY2020 Form 10-K
  as President and Chief Executive Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) exhibit index and signature page]
- On March 3, 2021 the Company named Grayson Pranin (formerly VP for Reserves and
  Engineering) as SVP and COO, and named Salah Gamoudi to the CFO/Chief Accounting
  Officer role. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Subsequent Events note]

## Capital-structure and portfolio events during the window
- On November 30, 2020 the Company entered into a new $30 million credit facility
  (a $10 million revolving loan facility and a $20 million term loan facility) with
  Icahn Agency Services LLC, an affiliate of Icahn Enterprises and a related party to
  the Company, as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Indebtedness/Credit Facility note]
- The Company adopted a Tax Benefits Preservation Plan dated July 1, 2020, filed as an
  exhibit to the FY2020 Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) exhibit index]
- In February 2021 the Company divested all of its North Park Basin assets, after which
  approximately 90.5% of proved reserves and approximately 89.2% of annual production
  were concentrated in the Mid-Continent area. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Item 2 Properties]
- Workforce reductions occurred in the first quarter of 2018 and the second quarter of
  2019, each associated with cash and share-based severance costs recorded as employee
  termination benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Employee Termination Benefits note]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across fiscal years 2017-2020 the Company moved from a modest net income ($47.062
  million in FY2017) to progressively larger net losses (FY2018: $9.075 million;
  FY2019: $449.305 million; FY2020: $277.353 million), driven substantially by full
  cost ceiling impairment charges that cumulatively reached $947.1 million from the
  Effective Date through December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Selected Financial Data] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Consolidated Statements of Operations and Oil and Natural Gas Properties note]
- Over the same window the Company experienced repeated senior-leadership turnover
  (two full CEO transitions plus a CFO/COO transition), a 2018 proxy contest that
  replaced a majority of the board, a November 2020 financing arrangement with a party
  affiliated with the activist investor from that 2018 contest, and a February 2021
  divestiture that concentrated the asset base in a single operating area. [SRC: SEC accession 0001349436-20-000012, Form 10-K (FY2019) Proxy Contest note and Recent Events] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-21-003933, Form 10-K (FY2020) Indebtedness note, Subsequent Events note, and Item 2 Properties]
