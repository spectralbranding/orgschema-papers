# Structural sub-dossier — Vantiv, Inc. acquisition of Worldpay Group plc (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn accession (0001193125-17-281198, Form 8-K filed 2017-09-11) is not the
  deal-terms document. It reports a third amendment and restatement of the existing
  senior secured credit facilities of Vantiv, LLC (a subsidiary of Vantiv, Inc.), with
  certain of the amendments expressly conditioned on Vantiv "acquiring the shares of
  Worldpay Group plc ... in a cash and stock transaction" (the filing's term for the
  transaction, "the Business Combination"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-281198, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The amended facilities comprised term A loan tranches of $4,012,640,625, term B loan
  tranches of $3,160,711,500, and a $1,250,000,000 revolving credit facility, with
  several tranches' maturities and fundings keyed to "the funding of the Business
  Combination." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-281198, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The actual closing structure is sourced below from the parties' announcement 8-K,
  Worldpay's/FIS's later completion 8-K, and the co-operation agreement, per the
  brief's "unit of analysis is the transaction" instruction.

## Transaction and parties
- On July 5, 2017, Vantiv, Inc. (a Delaware corporation listed on the NYSE) and
  Worldpay Group plc (a UK public limited company) announced agreement in principle on
  a "potential merger of Worldpay and Vantiv" to form a "Combined Group." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000116, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 press release]
- Under the agreed terms, each Worldpay ordinary share would receive 55 pence in cash
  and 0.0672 new Vantiv shares, plus a 5-pence dividend; following completion, Worldpay
  shareholders would own approximately 41% of the Combined Group's share capital on a
  fully diluted basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000116, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Vantiv's board described the businesses as "complementary," combining Vantiv's US
  distribution and Worldpay's "unrivalled" position in global eCommerce to serve four
  core regions (US, Europe, Asia-Pacific, South America). [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000116, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The transaction was effected on January 16, 2018 as a court-sanctioned scheme of
  arrangement between Worldpay and its shareholders under Part 26 of the UK Companies
  Act 2006, following an August 9, 2017 Rule 2.7 recommended-offer announcement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Final consideration paid to Legacy Worldpay shareholders was approximately 134.4
  million shares of the Company's Class A common stock and approximately $1.7 billion
  in cash (including interim and special dividends of 0.8 pence and 4.2 pence per
  share). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Legal vehicle and ownership structure at closing
- Vantiv, Inc. (the pre-existing US registrant, Commission File No. 001-35462) remained
  the surviving public company and, effective January 16, 2018, changed its name to
  "Worldpay, Inc."; the legacy Worldpay Group plc shares ceased trading on the London
  Stock Exchange the same day, while the Company's Class A stock continued on the NYSE (new symbol "WP")
  and began a secondary standard listing on the LSE ("WPY"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 5.03]
- The 134.4 million newly issued Class A shares were contributed to Vantiv Holding, LLC
  in exchange for Class A Units, after which the Company held approximately 95.1% of
  the equity interests in Vantiv Holding, LLC, with Fifth Third Bank retaining the
  remaining interest — a minority structure carried forward rather than newly created
  by this transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Item 3.02]
- Vantiv had itself grown in part by acquisition before this transaction; for example,
  it acquired Moneris Solutions, Inc. ("Moneris USA") for $406.8 million in cash on
  December 21, 2016, folding its operations into Vantiv's existing Merchant Services
  segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000015, Form 10-K (FY2016) Item 7]

## Controlling intent and governance
- The Combined Group was to be led jointly: Charles Drucker (Vantiv CEO) as Executive
  Chairman and Co-CEO, and Philip Jansen (Worldpay CEO) as Co-CEO, with Stephanie
  Ferris (Worldpay) as CFO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000116, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Vantiv's bylaws were amended to require a 75%-of-the-board "Supermajority Vote" to
  remove either Co-CEO (other than for cause) for two years following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-255103, Form 8-K Exhibit 2.1 (A&R Co-operation Agreement) §11]
- The Board was expanded to thirteen members on closing, with five named former
  Worldpay directors (including Sir Michael Rake and Philip Jansen) joining alongside
  the existing Vantiv directors; two prior Vantiv directors resigned. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Product, process, and people at the tier level
- Pre-transaction, Vantiv described itself as the second-largest US merchant acquirer
  and largest US PIN debit acquirer by transaction count, operating an "integrated
  technology platform" serving merchants and financial institutions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000015, Form 10-K (FY2016) Item 7]
- The customer-facing product/asset combined at closing was payment-processing and
  acquiring capability spanning both parties' markets; the announcement described the
  Combined Group as gaining "deep payments capabilities, product and vertical
  expertise" across both companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-17-000116, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The parties contractually deferred operational integration of the underlying
  processes and people: Vantiv agreed to "invite Worldpay to establish a joint
  integration team to plan for the post-closing integration of the two businesses,"
  with competitively sensitive information exchanged only under "clean team"
  arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-255103, Form 8-K Exhibit 2.1 (A&R Co-operation Agreement) §10.1]
- Vantiv further agreed that, prior to the Effective Date, it would "not contact or
  issue any communication to any employee of Worldpay" without Worldpay's prior written
  consent — i.e., the two organizations' people/management substrate was kept separate
  by contract until legal closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-255103, Form 8-K Exhibit 2.1 (A&R Co-operation Agreement) §10.2]
- Legacy Worldpay's existing employee share-based award arrangements (performance
  share plans, deferred bonus and co-investment plans) were rolled over into awards
  over the Company's stock on substantially their original vesting terms rather than
  immediately conformed to Vantiv's own incentive plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-255103, Form 8-K Exhibit 2.1 (A&R Co-operation Agreement) Schedule 1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two operating companies — a US-listed payments company with
  its own acquisition history and a UK-listed payments company with global eCommerce
  strength — into a single surviving Delaware/NYSE registrant via a scheme of
  arrangement, with cash-and-stock consideration, a negotiated dual-CEO governance
  structure, an expanded board drawn from both sides, and an explicit contractual
  deferral of employee contact and operational integration planning to a post-closing
  "joint integration team," while a pre-existing minority equity interest (Fifth Third
  Bank) in the operating subsidiary was carried forward unchanged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-011164, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.02, 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-255103, Form 8-K Exhibit 2.1 (A&R Co-operation Agreement) §§10-11]
