# Structural sub-dossier — Energy XXI Gulf Coast, Inc. acquisition by an affiliate of Cox Oil (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- Energy XXI Gulf Coast, Inc. ("EGC") was formed in December 2016 as the restructured
  successor of EXXI Ltd upon its emergence from a voluntary chapter 11 reorganization;
  EGC is headquartered in Houston, Texas, and develops, exploits, and operates oil and
  natural gas properties primarily offshore on the Gulf of Mexico Shelf and onshore in
  Louisiana and Texas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- On June 18, 2018, EGC entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with MLCJR LLC, a
  Texas limited liability company ("Cox"), and YHIMONE, Inc., a Delaware corporation and
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Cox ("Merger Sub"), under which Merger Sub would
  merge with and into EGC, with EGC surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of
  Cox. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Cox is an affiliate of Cox Oil Offshore, L.L.C. and Cox Operating, L.L.C. (together,
  "Cox Oil"), privately held entities that own and operate Gulf of Mexico assets and were
  founded by Brad E. Cox; Cox Oil is based in Dallas, Texas with operating staff in New
  Orleans, Louisiana and, at signing, operated more than 200 producing wells over 25
  fields. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "Information About the Parties"]
- Each outstanding share of EGC common stock (excluding excluded/dissenting shares) would
  be converted into the right to receive $9.10 in cash without interest, a whole-company
  cash acquisition; the parties' negotiation history in the proxy shows the price moving
  from an initial non-binding $6.55/share indicative proposal to the $9.10/share signed
  price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"; "Background of the Merger"]
- Completion of the merger was not conditioned on Cox obtaining financing: "In no event
  shall the receipt by, or the availability of any funds or financing to, Parent or any
  of its Affiliates or any other financing be a condition to Parent's obligation to
  consummate the transactions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A Merger Agreement 6.1(h)]

## What would cross at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: Merger Sub (newly formed solely to effect the merger) would merge into
  EGC, with EGC continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming an indirect wholly
  owned subsidiary of Cox — the entire corporate entity, not a carved-out asset, was the
  transaction's subject. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Product/asset: at signing EGC's disclosed reserve base (as of December 31, 2017) was
  88.2 MMBOE (84% oil), with interests in 577 gross producing wells across 421,974 net
  developed acres and 55 producing fields, including nine of the largest Gulf of Mexico
  Shelf oil fields by cumulative production; these assets transferred intact as part of
  the acquired corporate entity rather than as a piecemeal asset sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "Information About the Parties"]
- Employee benefits: the merger agreement's Employee Benefits Matters provision required
  Cox to "take commercially reasonable efforts" to make Cox-group benefit plans available
  to continuing EGC employees and to waive certain pre-existing-condition/eligibility
  requirements, but expressly stated it created no right to continued employment for any
  specific period and did not obligate Cox to continue any particular EGC benefit plan.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "The Merger — Employee Benefits Matters"]
- People/organization at the effective time: EGC's President/CEO, CFO, COO, General
  Counsel, and each other director voluntarily resigned "in connection with the Merger
  and not because of any disagreement with EGC," and the directors and officers of Merger
  Sub became the directors and officers of the surviving EGC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054328, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Consideration, conditions, and process
- Completion was conditioned on, among other things, EGC stockholder approval (two-thirds
  of outstanding shares) and expiration/satisfaction of Hart-Scott-Rodino requirements;
  Cox determined the fair market value of EGC's assets fell below the HSR filing
  threshold, and Cox and EGC agreed the HSR-related closing condition was satisfied
  without a filing, based on informal FTC staff interpretive advice dated June 29, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "Regulatory Clearances Required for the Merger"]
- The agreement set a "termination date" of November 15, 2018, and provided for an EGC
  termination fee of $8,000,000 (plus up to $2,000,000 of Cox expense reimbursement) in
  specified circumstances, and a damages cap of $35,000,000 for a willful and material
  breach. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A "Termination Fees and Expenses"]
- On September 6, 2018, at a special meeting, EGC stockholders approved adoption of the
  merger agreement, 23,085,021 shares for versus 1,336,636 against (253,914 abstaining);
  the related non-binding advisory compensation proposal failed to receive majority
  support. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-048234, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- On September 9, 2018, EGC, Cox, and Merger Sub entered a First Amendment to the merger
  agreement extending the contemplated closing date to on or about October 10, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-048715, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The merger closed on October 18, 2018: EGC became an indirect wholly owned subsidiary
  of Cox (through intermediate holding company CEXXI, Inc.), each outstanding share
  converted into the right to receive the $9.10 merger consideration, and — as a change
  of control — EGC notified NASDAQ the same day that trading in its common stock should
  be suspended and its listing removed; EGC subsequently filed to deregister its common
  stock under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054328, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A privately held Gulf of Mexico operator (Cox, via newly formed holding and merger
  subsidiaries) acquired the entire public company EGC for $9.10/share in cash in a
  whole-company merger not conditioned on financing; EGC's full asset base (reserves,
  wells, acreage) and legal entity crossed intact into the Cox structure, while EGC's
  pre-merger executive officers and board resigned at closing and were replaced by
  Merger Sub's officers and directors, with only a "commercially reasonable efforts"
  (not guaranteed-continuity) undertaking on employee benefits. The transaction closed
  October 18, 2018 after a two-thirds stockholder vote and an HSR threshold
  determination, and EGC ceased to be a NASDAQ-listed, Exchange Act-reporting company.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-18-000420, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054328, Form 8-K]
