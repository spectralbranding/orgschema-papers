## Portfolio trajectory within the window
- The Company's owned multifamily portfolio contracted from sixty-three properties at
  December 31, 2013 to thirty-five properties (9,442 apartment homes plus 21,130 square
  feet of commercial space at two properties, and a 10% interest in one additional
  property) at December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 2]
- This contraction occurred through individual property dispositions at gains,
  including Willow Crossing Apartments sold for $79,000,000 (gain $24,136,113), Heights
  at 2121 sold for $47,000,000 (gain $14,394,129), and Mapleshade Park sold for
  $30,750,000 (gain $9,628,549), among other disposed properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 7]

## Realized financial record within the window
- The Company reported a net loss of $25,579,651 for the year ended December 31, 2016,
  followed by net income of $72,473,867 for the year ended December 31, 2017 — an
  increase attributed primarily to a $96,573,171 increase in gain on sales of real
  estate, partially offset by lower total revenues from the smaller portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 7]
- Net income increased further to $89,083,552 for the year ended December 31, 2018,
  an increase attributed primarily to a further $21,642,447 increase in net gain on
  sales of real estate together with lower operating/maintenance/management expense,
  lower real estate taxes and insurance, and lower fees paid to affiliates — all
  consistent with a shrinking managed portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 7]
- The board's periodically determined estimated value per share moved from $10.35
  (December 31, 2014) to a peak of $11.65 (December 31, 2016), then declined to $10.84
  (December 31, 2017) and, after a $1.00-per-share special distribution paid April 20,
  2018, to $9.84, and finally to $9.40 (December 31, 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001468010-19-000013, Form 10-K (FY2018) Item 5]

## Terminal event within/at the edge of the window: merger into an affiliated REIT
- On August 5, 2019, the Company, Steadfast Apartment REIT, Inc. ("STAR"), the
  respective operating partnerships, and a wholly owned STAR merger subsidiary entered
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which the Company would merge with and into the
  merger subsidiary, which would survive as a wholly owned subsidiary of STAR and the
  Company's separate corporate existence would cease; on the same date STAR separately
  (non-contingently) agreed to merge with a third affiliated entity, Steadfast Apartment
  REIT III, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-213846, Form 8-K Items 7.01/8.01]
- On March 2, 2020 (just beyond the ~3-5-year window measured from the ~2014 closing
  era), at a special meeting, holders of approximately 58.06% of the Company's
  73,910,716 outstanding shares voted, and stockholders approved both the merger and a
  charter amendment removing provisions related to "Roll-Up Transactions" in connection
  with it; each share of the Company's common stock was to convert into the right to
  receive 0.5934 shares of STAR common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-060357, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
- The merger closed on March 6, 2020: the Company merged with and into the STAR
  subsidiary, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of STAR, and the Company's
  separate corporate existence ended; the Company (by then STAR, as successor by
  merger) filed a Form 15-12G on March 10, 2020 terminating the Company's Exchange Act
  registration, certifying "None" holders of record remaining under the Company's own
  name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-068610, Form 15-12G]
