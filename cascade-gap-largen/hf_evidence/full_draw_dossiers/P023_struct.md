# Structural sub-dossier — SPG SpinCo Subsidiary Inc. separation from Simon Property Group, Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The board of directors of Simon Property Group, Inc. ("SPG") declared the distribution of common shares of SPG SpinCo Subsidiary Inc. ("SpinCo"), a wholly owned subsidiary of SPG, to SPG shareholders; SpinCo holds, directly or indirectly, SPG's interests in 44 smaller enclosed malls (each generating annual net operating income of approximately $10 million or less) and 54 strip centers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution was structured as a pro rata special dividend to SPG shareholders of all of the outstanding SpinCo common shares (100% of SpinCo), issued in book-entry form; the separation and distribution were expected to be tax-free to SPG shareholders, shareholder approval was not required, and holders were not required to take any action or surrender their SPG shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Following the distribution, SPG shareholders would own shares in both SPG and SpinCo; SPG common stock would continue to trade on the NYSE under "SPG," and the transaction was described as allowing SPG to focus on its global portfolio of larger malls, Mills and Premium Outlets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Legal vehicle
- SPG SpinCo Subsidiary Inc. was formed as an Indiana corporation on December 13, 2013; its operating partnership, SpinCo L.P., was formed as an Indiana limited partnership on January 17, 2014, and the registrant intended to own its properties and conduct substantially all of its business through the operating partnership and its subsidiaries. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- SpinCo intended to elect and qualify to be taxed as a real estate investment trust ("REIT") for U.S. federal income tax purposes from and after the taxable year that includes the distribution; its amended and restated articles of incorporation would contain ownership- and transfer-restriction provisions to assist in qualifying as a REIT. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset: upon completion, SpinCo would operate a portfolio anticipated to consist of interests in 54 strip centers and 44 smaller enclosed mall properties — 98 properties in 23 states totaling approximately 53 million square feet. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The interests in the properties (including interests in entities holding properties) currently held by Simon Property Group, L.P. would be contributed or transferred to SpinCo L.P.; SPG would then contribute to SpinCo all of its common units in SpinCo L.P. plus interests in the remaining properties, in exchange for SpinCo common shares, and distribute 100% of the shares pro rata. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- People / organization: SPG's strip center team would become employees of SpinCo — described as a well-seasoned team with extensive experience working together — and the company's executive management team would consist of a combination of individuals previously employed by SPG plus externally recruited executives, entirely independent of SPG following the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Management/board continuity with the parent: David Simon, Chairman and CEO of SPG, would also serve as a director of SpinCo, and Richard Sokolov, SPG's President and Chief Operating Officer and a board member, would also become Chairman of SpinCo's board of directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Capital structure allocated at separation: SpinCo L.P. was expected to assume at least $965 million of existing secured property-level indebtedness, and SpinCo expected approximately $2 billion of total debt outstanding upon completion (approximately $1.5 billion secured and $500 million unsecured). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Product crossed WITHOUT its producing/delivering processes — bridged contractually at closing
- SpinCo's malls would continue to receive property management services from SPG under property management agreements for an initial term of two years with automatic one-year renewals (terminable by either party on 180 days' notice); i.e., the mall properties transferred to SpinCo while the management services that operate them were to be supplied by SPG. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the property management agreements, SPG employees with expertise and in-depth knowledge of the mall properties would continue to provide management services under the direction of SpinCo's executive team; SpinCo would pay SPG annual fixed-rate property management fees of 2.5% of base minimum and percentage rents (plus leasing/development fees and reimbursement of on-site employee costs) for its wholly owned malls. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- SpinCo's strip center support functions would be provided by SPG on a transitional basis for up to two years, with an option for SpinCo to extend any one or more of the transition services for a one-year term; management stated the properties "will be seamlessly integrated into our company due to the continuity of on-site operations, the institutional knowledge of our property management teams and our deep familiarity with the markets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- SpinCo entered into a separation agreement with SPG, and would enter into property management agreements, a transition services agreement, a tax matters agreement, and an employee matters agreement, providing for the allocation between SpinCo and SPG of SPG's assets, liabilities and obligations (investments, property, employee and tax-related) attributable to periods before, at and after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Transition services agreement: SPG and its subsidiaries would provide various corporate support services to SpinCo on an interim, transitional basis — treasury management, human resources, information systems, tax, financial reporting audit services, legal services and financial planning — with charges intended to recover out-of-pocket costs plus a predetermined profit mark-up; the cost of these services was estimated at $2.5 million annually. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The registrant disclosed dependence on SPG to provide services under the property management agreements and transition services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction carved a strip-center and smaller-mall portfolio (98 properties, 23 states, ~53 million sq ft) out of a larger integrated REIT and distributed it as a new, separately listed Indiana-incorporated REIT (SpinCo, operating through SpinCo L.P.) via a pro rata, tax-free special dividend of 100% of the shares; the strip-center operating team moved to SpinCo, but the properties' management processes remained with the parent — mall property management was to be provided by SPG under two-year property management agreements (2.5% fee) and strip-center support functions on a transitional basis up to two years, with corporate support (treasury, HR, IS, tax, audit, legal, planning) supplied by SPG under a cost-plus transition services agreement (~$2.5M/yr), alongside separation, tax matters and employee matters agreements, while board/management ties to the parent persisted through shared directors (David Simon; Richard Sokolov as SpinCo board chairman). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-14-000794, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
