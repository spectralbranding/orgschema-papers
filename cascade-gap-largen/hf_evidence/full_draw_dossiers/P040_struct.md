# Structural sub-dossier — Time Inc. separation from Time Warner Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Source note
- The structural facts below are drawn from the preliminary information statement (Exhibit
  99.1 to the Form 10-12B registration statement), dated as subject to completion March 7,
  2014; certain terms (e.g. distribution ratio, Special Dividend amount) appear as blanks
  in this preliminary version. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Time Inc. was a wholly owned subsidiary of Time Warner Inc. ("Time Warner"); the
  transaction was structured as a spin-off in which Time Warner would distribute all of
  the shares of Time Inc. common stock on a pro rata basis to Time Warner stockholders,
  after which Time Inc. would be an independent publicly-traded company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The distribution was intended to be tax-free to Time Warner stockholders for U.S. federal
  income tax purposes (except cash in lieu of fractional shares); Time Warner stockholders
  paid no consideration and surrendered no Time Warner shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Time Warner's board retained sole and absolute discretion over the terms of, and whether
  to proceed with, the distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What the separated entity comprised (the customer-facing operation)
- Time Inc. held Time Warner's "Time Inc." reporting segment, consisting principally of its
  magazine publishing business and related websites and operations; it was the largest
  magazine publisher in the United States (by readership and print advertising revenues)
  and largest in the U.K. (by print newsstand revenues), with a portfolio of more than 90
  magazines. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- As of December 31, 2013 it published 23 magazines in the U.S. (including People, Sports
  Illustrated, InStyle, Time, Real Simple, Southern Living, Entertainment Weekly, Fortune)
  and over 70 outside the U.S., primarily through IPC in the U.K. and Grupo Editorial
  Expansión (GEX) in Mexico, plus over 45 websites. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- As of December 31, 2013 the business employed approximately 7,900 people worldwide
  (approximately 5,100 in the United States and 2,800 outside it). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## The Internal Reorganization — assembling and detaching the business
- A portion of the Publishing Business had historically been held by Time Warner
  subsidiaries other than Time Inc.; Time Warner undertook an "Internal Reorganization" so
  that Time Inc. would hold the entire Publishing Business before distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Reorganization steps included Historic TW Inc. contributing to Time Inc. certain
  trademarks, tradenames and service marks and the equity of subsidiaries comprising the
  Publishing Business; a series of transactions to transfer the U.K. publishing business to
  Time Inc. (acquired by Time Atlantic from Time Warner Limited in exchange for the "IPC
  Note"); and Time Inc.'s issuance of shares to Time Warner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- To capitalize the standalone entity, Time Inc. was to issue debt securities to
  third-party investors and establish a term loan and revolving commitments with a
  syndicate of third-party lenders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Time Inc. was to declare and distribute a special cash dividend to Time Warner (the
  "Special Dividend"); the dollar amount appears as a blank in this preliminary information
  statement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared corporate substrate not transferred whole; bridged contractually
- The business had historically operated as part of Time Warner's corporate organization,
  which provided various corporate functions, including cash management, and included the
  business in the Parent's consolidated tax and treasury arrangements; these functions the
  standalone company would have to establish or procure separately. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The financial statements were prepared on a historical combined basis as an indirect
  wholly owned subsidiary; allocated expenses for services historically provided by Time
  Warner (including cash management) may not reflect standalone costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Closing-era agreements to govern and allocate the relationship: a Separation and
  Distribution Agreement, a Transition Services Agreement, a Tax Matters Agreement, and an
  Employee Matters Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the Transition Services Agreement, Time Warner was to provide Time Inc. certain
  administrative services, information technology systems and infrastructure support
  (including support for benefit plans, human resources, tax and treasury) for a
  transitional period, with Time Inc. providing certain limited services (payroll taxes,
  data center hosting) to Time Warner subsidiaries; the arrangement was short-term, and
  Time Inc. stated it was increasing internal capabilities to eliminate reliance on Time
  Warner as quickly as possible after the spin-off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The Employee Matters Agreement was to address employment, compensation and benefits
  matters, including allocation and treatment of assets and liabilities relating to
  employees and benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Intercompany agreements between Time Inc. and Time Warner would terminate at the
  distribution except specified surviving agreements; assets were to be transferred on an
  "as is, where is" basis with generally no representations or warranties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A customer-facing magazine-publishing operation (more than 90 titles and ~45 websites,
  ~7,900 employees) — assembled into a single subsidiary via an Internal Reorganization and
  newly capitalized with third-party debt and a term loan — was detached from the Parent's
  shared corporate service substrate (cash management, tax, treasury, IT, HR), with that
  substrate bridged at closing only by a short-term Transition Services Agreement plus Tax
  Matters and Employee Matters agreements rather than transferred as an intact operating
  whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
