# Structural sub-dossier — Cumulus Media Inc. financial restructuring (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Drawn filing vs. the actual transaction
- The originally drawn filing (accession 0001058623-17-000028, dated 2017-05-15) is a
  Form 8-K furnished under Item 2.02 attaching a press release of the registrant's
  operating results for the three months ended March 31, 2017; it is an earnings
  release, not a deal-terms document, and does not itself describe any acquisition,
  divestiture, or restructuring transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-17-000028, Form 8-K Item 2.02]
- The actual closing-era transaction is the registrant's chapter 11 financial
  restructuring, documented in the registrant's later 2017 and 2018 Forms 8-K cited
  below; the unit of analysis here is that transaction, not the drawn earnings release.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On November 20, 2017, the registrant was notified that Nasdaq had determined to
  remove its Class A common stock from The Nasdaq Global Select Market effective
  November 22, 2017 for noncompliance with Nasdaq Listing Rules 5550(a)(2) and
  5550(b)(1), moving the stock to the OTC Markets' OTCQX tier under the same symbol.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001058623-17-000089, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- On November 29, 2017, the registrant and certain subsidiaries entered a Restructuring
  Support Agreement with creditors holding more than two-thirds of the term-loan
  principal under the Amended and Restated Credit Agreement, and with equityholder
  Crestview Radio Investors, LLC and its affiliates, contemplating conversion of more
  than $1.0 billion of funded debt into equity via a chapter 11 joint plan of
  reorganization, and on the same date filed voluntary chapter 11 petitions in the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York (In re: Cumulus Media Inc., et
  al., Case No. 17-13381), continuing to operate as debtor in possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-356291, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Item 1.03]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered an order on May 10, 2018 confirming the First Amended
  Joint Plan of Reorganization, effective June 4, 2018 (the "Effective Date"), on which
  date the debtors emerged from chapter 11. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Explanatory Note]

## What crossed the ownership/legal boundary at closing and what did not
- At the Effective Date, the pre-petition parent (renamed CM Wind Down Topco Inc., f/k/a
  Cumulus Media Inc.) transferred its remaining assets to an indirectly wholly owned
  subsidiary of a new Delaware corporation (f/k/a CM Emergence Newco Inc.), which took
  the name Cumulus Media Inc. and became the successor issuer under Exchange Act Rule
  12g-3; the ultimate legal parent entity therefore changed at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Explanatory Note]
- All outstanding shares, options and warrants of the pre-petition Class A, Class B and
  Class C common stock (including Crestview's equity interest) were extinguished with no
  recovery, and the pre-petition credit agreement, senior notes indenture, and a 2017
  rights agreement were discharged; in exchange, the reorganized company issued
  11,052,211 Class A shares, 5,218,209 Class B shares, and two series of warrants to
  former term-loan, senior-note, and general unsecured claimholders (term-loan
  claimholders receiving 83.5% of reorganized common equity, other claimholders 16.5%,
  subject to dilution) under a Securities Act Section 1145 exemption. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Cancellation of Prior Equity Securities / Item 1.02 / Item 3.02]
- A new $1.3 billion senior secured term loan credit facility (maturing May 15, 2022)
  was entered into by an indirect subsidiary on the Effective Date, replacing the
  canceled pre-petition credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Item 1.01 "Credit Agreement"]
- The debtors' broadcast businesses and properties continued to be managed and operated
  throughout the chapter 11 cases under debtor-in-possession status; no filing reviewed
  describes a sale, transfer, or interruption of the customer-facing radio-station /
  advertising operations to a third party. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-356291, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- No transitional-services, operating, or shared-services agreement bridging a shared
  corporate substrate was identified in the reviewed closing-era filings; the closing
  instruments entered on the Effective Date were financial/governance in nature (new
  credit agreement, warrant agreement, indemnification agreements). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Governance and management at closing
- On the Effective Date, six of the seven pre-petition directors (Jeffrey Marcus, Jan
  Baker, Jill Bright, John W. Dickey, Ralph B. Everett and Ross Oliver) ceased to be
  directors; the new seven-member board comprised Mary G. Berner (President and Chief
  Executive Officer) plus six independent directors selected by the term lender group.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Item 5.02 "Board of Directors"]
- Mary G. Berner, the pre-petition President and Chief Executive Officer, continued as
  President and Chief Executive Officer of the reorganized company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Item 5.02 "Board of Directors"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction restructured the capital structure and ultimate legal parent of an
  operating radio-broadcasting business through a chapter 11 plan of reorganization:
  pre-petition equity (including a named private-equity-affiliated holder) was
  eliminated and replaced with equity issued to former creditors, the top corporate
  legal entity changed via a successor-issuer mechanism, and a new credit facility
  replaced the prior one — while the day-to-day broadcast operations continued
  uninterrupted as a debtor in possession and the pre-petition CEO continued in place
  under the new board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-183509, Form 8-K Explanatory Note / Item 5.02]
