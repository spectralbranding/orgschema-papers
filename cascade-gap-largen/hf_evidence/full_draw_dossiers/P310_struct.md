# Structural sub-dossier — Staples, Inc. going-private acquisition (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 28, 2017, Staples, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger with Arch Parent Inc. ("Parent") and Arch Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger
  Sub," a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent), providing for Merger Sub to merge with and
  into Staples, with Staples surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent. Parent
  and Merger Sub are beneficially owned by funds managed by Sycamore Partners
  Management, L.P., a private equity firm specializing in consumer and retail
  investments; Parent and Merger Sub had not engaged in any business except activities
  incidental to formation and the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- Each share of Company common stock was converted into the right to receive $10.25 in
  cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- The transaction was not subject to a financing condition, although funding of the debt
  and equity financing was subject to conditions in the respective commitment letters.
  On July 26, 2017, the U.S. Federal Trade Commission granted early termination of the
  Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period with respect to the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- A special meeting of stockholders to adopt the merger agreement was scheduled for
  September 6, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- On September 12, 2017, the merger was completed: Merger Sub merged with and into the
  Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056727, Form 8-K item 2.01]

## Business/legal vehicle and product that transferred
- The Company described itself as bringing "technology and people together in
  innovative ways to consistently deliver products, services and expertise" to
  business and consumer customers, having pioneered the office-products superstore
  concept (first store opened in Brighton, Massachusetts in 1986) and, at the time of
  the filing, serving "businesses of all sizes and consumers primarily in North
  America," with additional offices in South America and Asia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- The surviving corporation retained the Company's Delaware corporate charter and the
  Staples legal entity; Parent and Merger Sub were newly formed acquisition vehicles
  with no independent operating business of their own before or after the merger,
  i.e., the legal/operating vehicle that transferred to the new ownership was the
  pre-existing Staples corporate entity itself, not a newly assembled structure.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]

## Financing of the acquisition (capital structure that replaced the prior one)
- Parent estimated total funds required to complete the merger and related
  transactions at approximately $6.9 billion, to be funded through (a) cash equity
  contributions from investment funds managed by Sycamore and Neuberger Berman Private
  Equity and funds affiliated with HarbourVest Partners of up to $1,920 million, and
  (b) debt-financing commitments comprising a senior secured first-lien term loan
  facility ($2,400 million), an asset-based revolving credit facility ($1,200 million),
  a senior unsecured bridge facility or notes ($1,600 million), and two further
  asset-based revolving facilities ($200 million and $600 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- Investment funds managed by Sycamore and Neuberger Berman Private Equity and funds
  affiliated with HarbourVest Partners delivered a limited guarantee of Parent's
  termination-fee and certain indemnification obligations to the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]
- At closing (September 12, 2017) the Company entered a new Term Loan Credit Agreement
  (an approximately $2,900.0 million secured term loan facility) and a new ABL Credit
  Agreement (an approximately $1,200.0 million secured asset-based facility); proceeds
  of a previously announced $1.0 billion offering of 8.500% Senior Notes due 2025 were
  released from escrow the same day; and the Company repaid and terminated its prior,
  pre-merger credit agreement (originally dated November 22, 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056727, Form 8-K items 1.01/2.03/1.02]

## Controlling-intent / governance tier versus operating-management tier at the effective time
- At the effective time of the merger, all ten then-serving outside directors resigned
  from the Company's board and its committees; by operation of the merger agreement,
  the two individuals who had served as directors of Merger Sub, Stefan Kaluzny and
  Peter Morrow, became the directors of the Company as the surviving corporation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056725, Form 8-K item 5.02]
- The officers of the Company immediately prior to the effective time continued to
  serve as the officers of the Company as the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056725, Form 8-K item 5.02]

## Interim conduct-of-business covenant bridging signing to closing
- Under the merger agreement, pending closing the Company and its subsidiaries were
  required to use commercially reasonable efforts to carry on business in the ordinary
  course, to preserve intact the business organization, and to preserve satisfactory
  business relationships with significant suppliers and material customers, licensors,
  licensees, lessors, governmental entities, creditors and others having material
  business dealings with the Company, subject to negative covenants restricting
  extraordinary corporate actions absent Parent's written consent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, cash-out merger in which the pre-existing legal
  entity, its office-products/business-supply operating business, and its
  customer-facing brand passed intact to the new private-equity ownership as a single
  surviving corporation, funded by a combination of sponsor-fund equity and newly
  arranged term-loan, asset-based, and notes financing that replaced the Company's
  prior credit facility; at the effective time the controlling/governance tier was
  replaced in full (the entire outside board resigned in favor of two nominees of the
  acquiring funds) while the officer/management tier that runs day-to-day operations
  continued unchanged, bridged pending closing by an interim ordinary-course-of-business
  covenant rather than a separate transitional-services arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004941, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056725, Form 8-K item 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-056727, Form 8-K item 2.01]
