## Transaction and parties at closing
- Biglari Holdings Inc. (the "Company"), an Indiana corporation whose common stock traded on the NYSE under the symbol "BH," entered into an Amended and Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 5, 2018 (the "Reorganization Agreement") with NBHSA Inc. ("New BH"), a newly formed, direct, wholly owned Indiana subsidiary of the Company, and BH Merger Company ("Merger Sub"), a direct, wholly owned Indiana subsidiary of New BH. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- Under the Reorganization Agreement, Merger Sub was to merge with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of New BH; upon completion, New BH would be renamed "Biglari Holdings Inc." and would replace the Company as the publicly held parent corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- The merger was structured to become effective upon the filing of Articles of Merger with the Indiana Secretary of State, under Section 23-1-40 of the Indiana Business Corporation Law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement/prospectus (Form DEFM14A) is dated April 2, 2018 and was first mailed to shareholders on or about that date, ahead of a Special Meeting held immediately following the 2018 Annual Meeting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- Shareholders approved the Amended and Restated Agreement and Plan of Merger at the Special Meeting held April 26, 2018, by a preliminary vote of 1,221,366 shares for to 668,485 against, with 5,356 abstentions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000251, Form 8-K]
- Under the exchange mechanics, holders of the Company's common stock were to receive, for every ten shares held, ten shares of New BH Class B common stock (no voting rights) and one share of New BH Class A common stock (full voting rights); New BH's Class A shares were expected to trade on the NYSE as "BHA" and its Class B shares under "BH," the Company's pre-existing ticker. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]

## Business held by the entity at closing
- At the time of the transaction the Company was a holding company owning subsidiaries engaged in diverse business activities including media, property and casualty insurance, and restaurants, with its largest operating subsidiaries involved in the franchising and operating of restaurants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- The proxy states that New BH would be "a holding company owning subsidiaries engaged in a number of diverse business activities, including restaurant, insurance and media," and that New BH and its subsidiaries "will own all of the businesses we currently own." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not, by tier
- Controlling intent: Sardar Biglari, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, beneficially owned approximately 54.8% of the votes entitled to be cast, and a stated purpose of the recapitalization was to preserve "the founder's controlling interest" within the new two-tier structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- People / governance: the proxy states the New BH board of directors "presently consists of the same persons comprising the [Company] Board" and would "continue to be comprised of the same individuals" immediately following completion, and that "the executive officers of New BH ... will be the same as those of the Company immediately prior to" the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: the top-level publicly traded legal vehicle changed from the pre-transaction Company to New BH (formerly NBHSA Inc.), renamed "Biglari Holdings Inc."; the pre-transaction Company itself continued to exist, but as a non-public, wholly owned subsidiary of New BH. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing businesses / operating processes: the proxy describes New BH's subsidiaries as coming to "own all of the businesses" the pre-transaction Company owned, without describing any change to how those operating businesses produce or deliver their products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, employee-matters agreement, or similar instrument bridging a shared corporate substrate between a divesting parent and a departing business is described anywhere in the proxy; the document does not describe any operating function being carved out or left behind. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
- A disclosed rationale for the restructuring was administrative/branding: the pre-transaction holding company (previously named "The Steak n Shake Company") sought to "eliminate confusion" among third parties with contractual relationships tied to that legacy name, since after the transaction such parties would deal with an operating subsidiary of the new holding company rather than with the top-level public filer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction converted a single existing public holding company into a two-tier structure: a newly formed entity assumed the public-parent role (and took the original company's name), while the pre-existing legal entity continued its operating businesses unchanged as a wholly owned, non-public subsidiary. The proxy discloses continuity of board composition, executive officers, and the controlling shareholder across the restructuring, with the disclosed change concentrated in the creation of two share classes (voting Class A / non-voting Class B) and in the identity of the top-level publicly filing legal vehicle, bridged by no separately disclosed transitional-services arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-103534, DEFM14A]
