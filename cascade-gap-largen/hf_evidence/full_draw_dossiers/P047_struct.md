## The drawn filing, described for what it establishes
- The drawn source is a Form 8-K, Item 8.01 (Other Events), dated May 29, 2009 (earliest
  event reported May 22, 2009); it is a current report by which the registrant disclosed
  that it had, through a wholly owned subsidiary, filed an action in the Delaware Court of
  Chancery concerning its capital-call obligations under the joint venture agreement — it
  is not itself the venture-formation agreement, but it recites the venture's structure.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The wholly owned subsidiary that holds the interest and that brought the action is
  identified as Inland American Concord (Sub), LLC ("IA Sub"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) Note 16 / legal proceedings]

## Transaction and parties at inception
- The transaction is a joint venture: Inland American Real Estate Trust, Inc. holds a
  minority interest in Concord Debt Holdings LLC ("Concord"), alongside Lex-Win Concord
  LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- On August 2, 2008, Inland American entered into the Concord Debt Holdings, LLC joint
  venture with Lex-Win Concord LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]
- The venture's business is investing in commercial real-estate-related debt instruments
  and common shares of publicly traded REITs, which it primarily finances through its own
  borrowings; the FY2009 annual report describes the venture as one that "originates and
  acquires real estate securities and real estate related loans." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]

## What crossed the ownership boundary into the venture at inception (tier level)
- What Inland American placed into the venture was CAPITAL — a minority, preferred equity
  interest held through a subsidiary created in 2008 for that purpose; no operating
  product line, producing facility, or workforce was contributed by Inland American.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Inland American obtained approximately 23.5% ownership of Concord on or about August 2,
  2008 in exchange for an initial capital contribution of $20 million and — under
  specified conditions — a commitment to increase its investment to $100 million; as of
  the date of the complaint it had contributed a total of $76 million in cash.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Lex-Win Concord LLC holds approximately 76.5% ownership of Concord and "has at all
  relevant times acted as its managing member" — i.e., the operating control and
  management of the venture rested with the majority holder, not with Inland American.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Inland American's interest is accounted for under the equity method (the venture is not
  consolidated by Inland American), consistent with a non-controlling financial interest.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]

## Legal vehicle and governing instruments at inception
- The legal vehicle is a limited liability company, Concord Debt Holdings LLC.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The venture is governed by the Second Amended and Restated Limited Liability Company
  Joint Venture Agreement (the "Joint Venture Agreement") together with a Preferred
  Interest Purchase Agreement under which Inland American's interest was acquired.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The Joint Venture Agreement sets forth the venture's investment parameters and includes
  common-equity thresholds binding on the managing member, including a provision the
  registrant reads as requiring Lex-Win to maintain a capital contribution in Concord at
  least twice the amount invested by Inland American, and a distinction between ordinary
  capital calls / "Permitted Investments" and calls for an "Advance Rate Paydown" ("ARP")
  used to satisfy the venture's obligations to its lenders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (inception only)
- The transaction is a passive, minority preferred-equity participation: Inland American
  contributed capital (an initial $20 million, ~23.5% interest, with a conditional
  commitment toward $100 million) into an existing debt-investing limited liability
  company controlled and managed by the majority member, Lex-Win Concord LLC (~76.5%,
  managing member), with the parties' respective funding obligations, investment
  parameters, and equity thresholds fixed by a Preferred Interest Purchase Agreement and
  the Second Amended and Restated Joint Venture Agreement — capital crossing the ownership
  boundary without any operating product, process, or workforce crossing with it.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-09-035874, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]
