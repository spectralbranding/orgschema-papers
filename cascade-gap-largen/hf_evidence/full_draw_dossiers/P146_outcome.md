## Public-reporting status of the case entity (feasibility-relevant)
- The debtor, Alpha Natural Resources, Inc. (CIK 1301063), filed a Form 15-12B to terminate registration of its common stock under the Securities Exchange Act on July 27, 2016, contemporaneous with emergence; it filed no further annual reports, so it has no separate public 3–5-year outcome record of its own. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-660264, Form 15-12B]
- The core coal assets were acquired by Contura Energy, Inc., which became a public registrant (NYSE; Commission File No. 001-38735), so the realized record of the acquired business over the outcome window is traced through Contura's SEC periodic reports. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (cover page)]

## Realized record — subsequent business combination
- On November 9, 2018, Contura Energy, Inc. merged with Alpha Natural Resources Holdings, Inc. and ANR, Inc. (the "Alpha Merger"), combining the buyer of the 2016 core assets with the reorganized entities that had retained the remaining assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Business / Note 4)]
- In connection with the Alpha Merger, Contura entered into an Amended and Restated Asset-Based Revolving Credit Agreement (borrowing capacity $225 million; facility maturing April 3, 2022) and assumed additional letter-of-credit and credit-and-security agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Liquidity and Capital Resources)]

## Realized record — outcome scalars (fiscal year 2019)
- Contura reported a net loss of $316.3 million for the year ended December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Consolidated cash-flow discussion, year ended December 31, 2019)]
- The FY2019 results included a goodwill impairment of $124.4 million; goodwill-impairment testing as of December 31, 2019 wrote down the full carrying value of goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Note 2; MD&A goodwill impairment)]
- The FY2019 results also included an asset impairment of $83.5 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Consolidated cash-flow discussion, year ended December 31, 2019)]
- For comparison within the filing, Contura had reported net income of $299.2 million for the year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Consolidated cash-flow discussion, year ended December 31, 2018)]

## Realized record — operations over time
- As of the FY2019 report, the combined company operated in three reportable segments — CAPP - Met (Central Appalachia metallurgical coal), CAPP - Thermal, and NAPP (Northern Appalachia) — producing low-ash metallurgical coal for coke and steel producers and thermal coal for power generation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K (Business / segments)]
- Contura Energy, Inc. subsequently changed its name; its fiscal-year-2020 annual report was filed under the name Alpha Metallurgical Resources, Inc. (same CIK 0001704715). [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-21-000026, Form 10-K (filer/cover, Alpha Metallurgical Resources, Inc.)]

## Outcome coding note
- The 2016 transaction was consummated (the NewCo Asset Sale closed on the July 26, 2016 Effective Date), so a realized post-close record exists. Because the debtor entity deregistered at emergence, the record is observed through the buyer/successor registrant (Contura Energy, Inc., later Alpha Metallurgical Resources, Inc.), whose FY2019 annual report — within the fixed outcome window — shows a $316.3 million net loss, a full ($124.4 million) goodwill write-down and an $83.5 million asset impairment, following a November 2018 combination with the reorganized Alpha entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001704715-20-000010, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The core coal assets transferred in 2016 were held by Contura Energy, Inc., which became a public NYSE registrant, combined with the reorganized Alpha entities on November 9, 2018, and — within the fixed FY2019 window — reported a net loss of $316.3 million, a $124.4 million goodwill impairment writing down the full carrying value of goodwill, and an $83.5 million asset impairment (versus $299.2 million net income for FY2018); the registrant was later renamed Alpha Metallurgical Resources, Inc. The original debtor, Alpha Natural Resources, Inc. (CIK 1301063), deregistered via Form 15-12B at emergence and has no separate public 3–5-year record.
