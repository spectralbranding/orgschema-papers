# Structural sub-dossier — combination of Contura Energy, Inc. with ANR, Inc. and Alpha Natural Resources Holdings, Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The registrant, Contura Energy, Inc. (a Delaware corporation, SIC 1221), registered
  common stock to be issued in a two-step merger with ANR, Inc. ("ANR") and Alpha Natural
  Resources Holdings, Inc. ("Holdings"), under a joint proxy statement / prospectus on
  Amendment No. 1 to Form S-4. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A cover]
- Structure: Prime Acquisition I, Inc. ("Merger Sub 1"), a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Contura, merges with and into Holdings, with Holdings surviving as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Contura (the "First Merger"); Prime Acquisition II, Inc. ("Merger Sub 2"),
  a wholly owned subsidiary of Merger Sub 1, merges with and into ANR, with ANR surviving
  (the "Second Merger"); together the "Transaction." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- The Transaction is governed by an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 29, 2018,
  among Contura, ANR, Holdings, Merger Sub 1 and Merger Sub 2. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]

## Consideration and control apportioned at closing
- Consideration is Contura common stock issued to Holdings and ANR stockholders; each
  eligible Alpha share converts at an exchange ratio of 0.4071 fully paid and nonassessable
  shares of Contura common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- Upon consummation, existing Contura stockholders were expected to own approximately 53.5%,
  and Alpha stockholders approximately 46.5%, of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- The combination was framed by the parties as intended to be "as close as possible to a
  merger of equals," with proportionate representation of Alpha on the board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]

## What each party brought across the boundary (tier level)
- Legal vehicle: Contura Energy, Inc. (Delaware) is the surviving parent/registrant; the
  combined company's common stock was to be listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the
  symbol "CTRA." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- Both parties trace to the 2016 restructuring of Alpha Natural Resources ("Old Alpha"):
  Contura was incorporated in Delaware on June 10, 2016 to acquire and operate certain of
  Old Alpha's core coal operations; ANR (a Delaware corporation incorporated under the Plan
  of Reorganization) became the parent of the reorganized private Alpha, applying fresh-start
  accounting on the July 26, 2016 Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- Customer-facing product (both sides): each entity produces, processes and sells
  metallurgical ("met") coal and thermal/steam coal to steel producers and utilities; the
  combination would create a company described as the largest U.S. supplier of metallurgical
  coal and a premier supplier of thermal coal, with approximately 30 mines and combined
  reserves over a billion tons. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- Operational processes crossing at closing: mining, processing and coal-blending
  operations of both companies combine into one operating footprint, with stated
  "improved metallurgical coal blending capabilities and significantly enhanced scale."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- People / workforce: as of approximately June 30, 2018, Contura had approximately 1,750
  employees (with UMWA representation noted) and Alpha had approximately 2,700 employees
  (UMWA representing 2%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
- Governance / control at closing: the combined company's board is expanded to nine
  directors — five seats filled by current Contura directors and four by Alpha designees
  (John E. Lushefski, Daniel J. Geiger, David J. Stetson and Harvey L. Tepner). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]

## Accounting characterization of the combination
- The combination is accounted for under the acquisition method, with Contura treated as
  the accounting acquirer of Alpha; excess consideration over identifiable net assets is
  recorded as goodwill. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]

## Pre-existing shared-substrate arrangement bridging the two entities
- Contura, Predecessor Alpha and ANR had a pre-existing Transition Services Agreement
  ("TSA") dated July 26, 2016 (amended by a First Amendment and a Second Amendment dated
  October 20, 2016) under which Contura provided certain back-office services — i.e., a
  shared back-office substrate connecting the two entities since the 2016 restructuring,
  in place at the time of the combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The Transaction re-combines, by stock-for-stock merger under a two-tier merger-sub
  structure, two coal producers (Contura and the ANR/Holdings reorganized-Alpha entities)
  that had been split apart in Old Alpha's 2016 bankruptcy restructuring — bringing both
  companies' met- and thermal-coal products, mining/processing/blending operations, and
  workforces into a single Contura-parent vehicle (NYSE: CTRA), framed as a near "merger of
  equals" (~53.5% / ~46.5%), accounted for as a Contura acquisition of Alpha, with a
  pre-existing 2016 back-office Transition Services Agreement already bridging the two.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-011255, Form S-4/A]
