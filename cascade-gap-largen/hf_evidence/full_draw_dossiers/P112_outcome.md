## Realized business record under private ownership (press-sourced)
- The business continued as a going concern under private ownership and was retained intact
  (no divestiture, dissolution or bankruptcy is documented): on 22 June 2021 — within the
  ~3–5-year window — CVC Capital Partners VIII signed a definitive agreement to acquire a
  majority interest in ExamWorks, with Leonard Green & Partners, L.P. and GIC retaining
  significant equity stakes alongside CVC and management. [SRC:
  https://www.cvc.com/media/news/2021/2021-06-22-cvc-capital-partners-viii-to-invest-in-examworks/]
- Management continuity persisted across the horizon: at the 2021 transaction the company's
  senior leadership — co-founders Richard Perlman and Jim Price and co-CEOs Wes Campbell and
  Miguel Fernandez de Castro — retained equity in the company and continued to lead it.
  [SRC: https://www.cvc.com/media/news/2021/2021-06-22-cvc-capital-partners-viii-to-invest-in-examworks/]
- The business description remained the same line of work over the horizon: independent
  medical examinations, peer reviews, bill reviews, Medicare compliance, record retrieval
  and document management services, serving property and casualty insurers, law firms,
  third-party claim administrators and government agencies across the United States, United
  Kingdom, Australia and Canada. [SRC:
  https://www.cvc.com/media/news/2021/2021-06-22-cvc-capital-partners-viii-to-invest-in-examworks/]
