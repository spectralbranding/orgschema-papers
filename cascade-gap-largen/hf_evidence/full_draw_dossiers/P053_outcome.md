## Outcome window
- The fixed outcome window is the ~3-5-year record after the ~2015 closing (approximately
  2018-2020). The registrant's public reporting terminated inside this window (see
  deregistration below), so the realized record is traced through its final annual report
  and its bankruptcy/asset-sale current reports. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Realized financial trajectory (final annual report, FY2017)
- Total revenue from continuing operations for the year ended December 31, 2017 was
  $205.3 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Net loss for 2017 was approximately $64.2 million, following a net loss of approximately
  $176.7 million in 2016 (a $112.5 million, 63.6% reduction in the loss year over year). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- The Company recorded impairment expense of approximately $21.4 million during 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Going-concern doubt: "We have concluded that we need to restructure our balance sheet to
  continue as a going concern over the long term," and the Company's independent registered
  public accounting firm "concurred with our determination that there is substantial doubt
  about our ability to continue as a going concern," including an explanatory paragraph in
  its audit report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Restructuring path: beginning September 2017 the Company entered confidentiality
  agreements and commenced discussions with advisors for a committee of its senior
  noteholders to explore restructuring/refinancing/reorganization/asset-sale transactions,
  and by the FY2017 filing had "not yet reached agreement on mutually acceptable terms." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-18-008166, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Realized corporate outcome (bankruptcy and disposition)
- On May 18, 2018, Rex Energy Corporation and certain wholly owned subsidiaries (the
  "Debtors") filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under chapter 11 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the Western District of Pennsylvania,
  jointly administered as In re R.E. Gas Development, LLC, Case No. 18-22032. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-167436, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Concurrent with the filing the Company executed a Restructuring Support Agreement (May 18,
  2018) with its First Lien Lenders (AGES/Angelo, Gordon Energy Servicer, LLC as agent) and
  71.8% of its Prepetition Second Lien Noteholders, providing for a court-supervised sale
  process for "substantially all of the Debtors' assets" backed by debtor-in-possession
  financing, targeting emergence within 170 days. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-167436, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On September 28, 2018 the Company and certain subsidiaries "completed the disposition of
  substantially all of their assets to, and assumption of certain associated liabilities
  by, PennEnergy Resources, LLC" under sections 105, 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy Code;
  the aggregate purchase price for the sale was $600.5 million, subject to adjustments; the
  Bankruptcy Court had approved the sale on August 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-289546, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The Company terminated its SEC registration: it filed a Form 15-12B on November 14, 2018
  to deregister its securities under the Exchange Act; consequently no annual report exists
  for the later part of the ~2018-2020 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-326782, Form 15-12B]

## Interface-relevant realized facts
- The realized outcome was a whole-entity insolvency and §363 sale of substantially all
  assets to a third-party operator (PennEnergy Resources, LLC) rather than a continuation of
  the standalone enterprise; equity holders were disclosed as facing "little or no recovery"
  under a plan of reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-289546, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-167436, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Because the assets moved to a new operator through the court-supervised sale and the
  registrant deregistered, there is no continuing public 10-K disclosure of the acquired
  business's separate trajectory after September 2018; any post-sale operating record under
  PennEnergy Resources, LLC (a private entity) is not publicly reported on EDGAR. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-326782, Form 15-12B] [UNVERIFIED: post-2018 operating record of the transferred assets under PennEnergy Resources, LLC]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2018-2020 window the registrant reported a going-concern qualification
  and a $64.2 million net loss and $21.4 million impairment for FY2017, then filed Chapter 11
  on May 18, 2018 and, on September 28, 2018, sold substantially all of its assets to
  PennEnergy Resources, LLC for $600.5 million through a bankruptcy §363 process, and
  deregistered its securities in November 2018 — a realized trajectory of insolvency and
  disposition rather than continuation as an independent public company.
