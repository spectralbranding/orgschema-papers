## Public-reporting status in the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant remained an independent SEC-reporting public company and filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalar)
- Net loss attributable to Alcoa Corporation for 2020 was $170 million, compared with a net loss of $1,125 million in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- As of December 31, 2020, the Company had approximately 12,900 employees in 16 countries; approximately 9,200 of its global employees were covered by collective bargaining agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Reorganization of the operating structure over time (process/organization interface)
- The Company adopted a new operating model, effective November 1, 2019, that "resulted in a leaner, more integrated, operator-centric organization," with a streamlined executive team that "eliminated the business unit structure," consolidated sales, procurement and other commercial capabilities at an enterprise level, and reduced overhead; the Company realized $60 million in annual savings beginning in the second quarter of 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The new operating model was implemented in 2020; the Company recorded a charge of $37 million related to employee termination and severance costs for approximately 260 employees company-wide, and a further 60 positions were eliminated as open roles or retirements were not replaced. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- By December 31, 2020, the Company's operations consisted of three worldwide reportable segments — Bauxite, Alumina, and Aluminum — a reduction from the segment structure in place at inception. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Multi-year portfolio review, closures, and impairments
- The Company announced a multi-year portfolio review of Company assets to assess each facility's strategic benefits, competitiveness, and viability; in December 2019 it announced the permanent closure of its alumina refinery in Point Comfort, Texas as the first action of the review. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The smelters at Avilés and La Coruña (Spain) were curtailed in February 2019; charges recorded prior to completion of the divestiture included asset impairments of $80 million, severance and employee-related costs of $20 million, and contract termination costs of $8 million, plus inventory write-downs of $16 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Restructuring charges recorded in 2019 related to a facility closure included asset impairments of $129 million, asset retirement obligations of $72 million, environmental remediation costs of $69 million, and severance costs of $4 million (layoff of approximately 40 employees). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Divestiture at the product/process interface (rolled-products / packaging)
- The Company met its non-core asset-sale target range through the sale of its hazardous waste treatment business in Gum Springs, Arkansas (closed January 2020) and its agreement to sell its Warrick rolling mill business, expected to close by the end of the first quarter of 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- On November 30, 2020, the Company entered into an agreement to sell the Warrick Rolling Mill — an integrated aluminum manufacturing site near Evansville, Indiana that "produces aluminum sheet primarily for the production of aluminum cans" — to Kaiser Aluminum Corporation for total consideration of approximately $670 million, comprising $587 million in cash and the assumption of $83 million in other postretirement benefit liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Approximately 1,170 employees at the Warrick Rolling Mill (including the casthouse, hot mill, cold mills, and coating and slitting lines) would become employees of Kaiser once the transaction was complete; Alcoa would also enter into a ground lease agreement with Kaiser for property Alcoa would continue to own at Warrick Operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~3-5-year window the registrant remained an independent public company but its realized record shows a business under active restructuring: net losses in both 2019 ($1,125 million) and 2020 ($170 million); a headcount reduction to approximately 12,900 employees; a November 2019 "new operating model" that eliminated the business unit structure and cut roles (~260 terminations, $37 million charge, $60 million annual savings); a multi-year portfolio review producing the permanent closure of the Point Comfort alumina refinery and curtailment/divestiture of the Spanish smelters (with associated impairments); a consolidation of the reportable-segment structure down to three (Bauxite, Alumina, Aluminum); and the November 2020 agreement to sell the Warrick can-sheet rolling mill business to Kaiser Aluminum for approximately $670 million with roughly 1,170 employees transferring to the buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-21-008854, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
