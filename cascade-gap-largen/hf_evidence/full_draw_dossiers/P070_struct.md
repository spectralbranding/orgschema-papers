# Structural sub-dossier — Equity One, Inc. / EQY-CSC LLC joint venture (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 4, 2011 (the "Closing Date"), pursuant to a Contribution Agreement dated May 23, 2010 (amended November 8, 2010) among Liberty International Holdings Limited ("LIH", organized in England and Wales), Equity One, Inc. (a Maryland corporation), and Capital Shopping Centres plc ("CSC"), Equity One acquired a majority ownership interest in C&C (US) No. 1, Inc. ("CapCo", a Delaware corporation) through a joint venture with LIH (the "CapCo Transaction"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- LIH and CSC are subsidiaries of Capital Shopping Centres Group PLC ("CSC Group"), a public limited company organized under the laws of England and Wales. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The customer-facing asset was CapCo's portfolio of 13 properties in California totaling approximately 2.5 million square feet, including Serramonte Shopping Center (Daly City), Plaza Escuela (Walnut Creek), The Willows Shopping Center (Concord), 222 Sutter Street (San Francisco), and The Marketplace Shopping Center (Davis). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Legal vehicle: Equity One and LIH formed EQY-CSC LLC and entered into its Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "Joint Venture Agreement"), dated as of the Closing Date; CapCo was held through this joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Consideration in: LIH contributed all outstanding shares of CapCo's common stock to the joint venture in exchange for 11,357,837 Class A LLC Shares; Equity One contributed a $600 million shared appreciation promissory note in exchange for 25,543,212 Class A LLC Shares and 15,023,893.20 Class B LLC Shares (all outstanding Class B Shares). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- LIH also transferred and assigned to Equity One an outstanding $67 million CapCo promissory note in exchange for approximately 4.1 million shares of Equity One common stock and one share of newly designated Class A Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Control of operations (process tier): the joint venture is managed by a board of managers elected by Equity One as holder of a majority of the Class A LLC Shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Retained minority governance (people/organization tier): LIH Shareholders' consent is required for a limited set of specified actions (e.g., bankruptcy or liquidation of the joint venture, incurrence of debt, modification/prepayment of the Note, and — prior to February 3, 2016 — transfers by Equity One of its LLC Shares outside specified exceptions). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Economic bridge instead of full acquisition: LIH's Class A LLC Shares are redeemable at the LIH Shareholder's request until the tenth anniversary of closing, for cash or — at Equity One's option — shares of Equity One common stock on a one-for-one basis (subject to adjustments). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Distribution waterfall: each Class A LLC Share receives distributions equivalent to dividends on Equity One common stock; thereafter, at the board's discretion, remaining available cash goes first to Equity One (as Class B holder) up to a specified return, then 16.67% to Class A holders and 83.33% to Class B holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Equity One and LIH entered into a Registration and Liquidity Rights Agreement (dated the Closing Date) covering resale of Equity One common stock issued to LIH. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- An Equityholders Agreement (dated May 23, 2010, among Equity One, CSC Group, LIH and others) was effective as of the Closing Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The $600 million shared appreciation Note bears a 7% fixed interest rate plus contingent interest equal to 30% of appreciation in certain real properties owned by Equity One or its subsidiaries, and matures on the tenth anniversary of issuance. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 2.03]

## People / board arrangements at closing
- Equity One agreed to increase its Board of Directors by one seat effective at the Closing Date and appoint a CSC Group designee; immediately following closing, David Fischel (Chief Executive of CSC Group) was appointed a director, and the Board was increased to 10 directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- One share of newly established Class A Common Stock was issued to CSC Ventures Limited (a subsidiary of LIH), convertible into 10,000 shares of common stock in certain circumstances and carrying voting rights tied to the number of Class A LLC Shares held by LIH/CSC Group affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## Debt allocated at closing
- At the closing, CapCo had approximately $243 million of mortgage debt (including its proportionate share of debt held by CapCo's joint ventures), following the simultaneous repayment of an $84 million mortgage secured by Serramonte Center, funded by Equity One and cash held by CapCo. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Equity One took managing control of a 13-property California shopping-center portfolio by acquiring a majority interest in the property-owning corporation (CapCo) through a jointly owned LLC (EQY-CSC LLC), while the seller (LIH/CSC) retained a redeemable minority economic interest (Class A LLC Shares), specified consent rights, one board seat, and a special-voting Class A common share — a joint-venture structure in which the asset and its management crossed to Equity One's control while the counterparty's stake was bridged contractually rather than bought out at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-11-001490, Form 8-K Items 1.01, 5.02, 5.03]
