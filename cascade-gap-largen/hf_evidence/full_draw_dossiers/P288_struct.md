# Structural sub-dossier — Liberty Interactive Corporation / GCI reorganization transactions (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The transaction was effected under an Agreement and Plan of Reorganization dated
  April 4, 2017, among Liberty Interactive Corporation (a Delaware corporation),
  Liberty Interactive LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Liberty Interactive), and
  General Communication, Inc. ("GCI," an Alaska corporation), as amended November 8,
  2017, and comprised several sequential steps collectively termed "the Transactions":
  a reclassification of GCI's common stock, an automatic conversion, a reattribution of
  certain assets between Liberty Interactive's two internal tracking-stock groups, a
  contribution of assets to GCI (renamed "GCI Liberty, Inc." at closing), and a
  split-off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- Liberty Interactive's corporate structure at the time comprised two tracking stocks —
  QVC Group common stock and Liberty Ventures Common Stock — each tracking a separate,
  non-legal-entity collection of attributed businesses and assets; the Ventures Group
  had attributed to it, among other things, Liberty Interactive's equity interests in
  FTD, LendingTree, Charter and Liberty Broadband, plus cash and certain liabilities.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- Liberty Interactive and Liberty LLC contributed certain Ventures Group assets and
  subsidiaries to GCI Liberty (the "contribution") in exchange for (i) a controlling
  equity interest in GCI Liberty, (ii) cash, and (iii) GCI Liberty's assumption of
  certain liabilities attributed to the Ventures Group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the contribution, holders of Liberty Ventures Common Stock received one
  share of the corresponding class of GCI Liberty Common Stock in redemption for each
  share held (the "split-off"). Upon completion, former GCI shareholders would hold
  approximately 23% of the undiluted equity and approximately 16% of the undiluted
  voting power of GCI Liberty; former Ventures Group stockholders would hold the
  remaining equity and voting interests. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- The contribution was to be accounted for as a reverse acquisition under the
  acquisition method of accounting under GAAP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing operating asset that remained in place at GCI Liberty was GCI's
  own Alaska communications business — wireless, data, video, voice and managed
  services delivered under the GCI brand through GCI's existing facilities, including
  its undersea fiber-optic cable systems and statewide wireless network; GCI's founding
  and operating history predate the Transactions (GCI was founded in 1979). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- What Liberty Interactive contributed into GCI Liberty from the Ventures Group was
  principally a portfolio of equity interests and cash — stakes in Charter, Liberty
  Broadband, and (subject to certain exceptions) Giggle, LendingTree, Evite and FTD,
  along with holdings such as Sound Ventures, Quid, and Liberty Israel Venture Fund II —
  rather than an integrated operating business with its own customer-facing product
  and delivery processes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- GCI's executive officers other than Mr. Duncan (CEO) and Mr. Hughes were anticipated
  to enter new employment agreements with an operating subsidiary of GCI Liberty,
  effective January 1, 2018, with an expected term of five years (shorter for one
  officer), continuing GCI's existing management team in place to run the operating
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- At the top of the combined structure, following completion of the contribution, most
  of GCI Liberty's executive officers would also serve as executive officers of Liberty
  Interactive, Liberty Media, Liberty TripAdvisor, Liberty Broadband and Liberty
  Expedia, with overlapping directors across these companies; Gregory B. Maffei was to
  be Chief Executive Officer, President and a director of GCI Liberty as well as of
  Liberty Interactive, Liberty Media, Liberty TripAdvisor and Liberty Broadband, and
  John Malone was to be Chairman of the Board of GCI Liberty and of the other named
  Liberty companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- GCI (as GCI Liberty) was to continue in existence initially as an Alaska corporation,
  with a subsequent "reincorporation merger" of GCI Liberty into a Delaware subsidiary
  (also to be named "GCI Liberty, Inc."), the sole stated purpose of which was to
  change GCI Liberty's state of incorporation from Alaska to Delaware. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- At the split-off, GCI Liberty and Liberty Interactive/Liberty Media entered into a
  reorganization agreement, an indemnification agreement covering certain losses from
  the split-off, a services agreement under which, for three years following the
  split-off, Liberty Media would provide GCI Liberty with insurance administration and
  risk-management services and other services typically performed by Liberty Media's
  legal, investor-relations, tax, accounting and internal-audit departments, a
  facilities-sharing agreement under which GCI Liberty would share office facilities
  with Liberty Interactive and Liberty Media for three years, and aircraft
  time-sharing agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing changes at closing
- Following the auto conversion, the reclassified GCI Class A-1 and Class B-1 common
  stock ceased to be listed or traded on the Nasdaq Global Select Market and
  over-the-counter market, respectively; following the split-off redemption, the
  Liberty Ventures Common Stock (LVNTA/LVNTB) likewise ceased to be listed or traded on
  the Nasdaq Global Select Market. GCI Liberty's newly issued Class A common stock,
  Class B common stock and Preferred Stock were expected to be listed on the Nasdaq
  Global Select Market under the symbols "GLIBA," "GLIBB" and "GLIBP." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
- Following the split-off, the QVC Group common stock became the only outstanding
  common stock of Liberty Interactive, ceasing to function as a tracking stock (pending
  a later charter amendment to remove the tracking-stock-specific provisions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, GCI's own Alaska operating business, facilities and (via anticipated new
  employment agreements) its management team below the top executive layer remained in
  place inside the renamed entity, while Liberty Interactive contributed a portfolio of
  equity stakes and cash (not an integrated operating business) in exchange for a
  controlling interest, with top governance and executive leadership (Maffei as CEO,
  Malone as Chairman) drawn from the Liberty side; the two sides' pre-existing shared
  corporate functions (legal, tax, accounting, investor relations, insurance, office
  facilities) were bridged for a fixed three-year period by a services agreement and a
  facilities-sharing agreement rather than transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007840, Form DEFM14A]
