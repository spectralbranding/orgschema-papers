# Structural sub-dossier — Comcast/NBCUniversal transaction with General Electric (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 28, 2011, Comcast Corporation closed a transaction with General Electric
  Company ("GE") to form a new company, NBCUniversal, LLC ("NBCUniversal Holdings");
  Comcast controls and owns 51% of NBCUniversal Holdings and GE owns the remaining 49%.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "NBCUniversal Transaction"]
- As part of the transaction, GE contributed the businesses of NBC Universal, Inc. (now
  named NBCUniversal Media, LLC), which became a wholly owned subsidiary of NBCUniversal
  Holdings; Comcast contributed its national cable programming networks, regional sports
  and news networks, certain internet businesses (including DailyCandy and Fandango) and
  other related assets (the "Comcast Content Business"), and in addition made a cash
  payment of $6.2 billion at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "NBCUniversal Transaction"]
- The contributed NBCUniversal businesses included national cable programming networks,
  the NBC network and owned NBC-affiliated local television stations, the Telemundo
  network and owned Telemundo-affiliated stations, Universal Pictures filmed
  entertainment, and the Universal Studios Hollywood theme park, among other assets.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "NBCUniversal Transaction"]
- The transaction was subject to a Federal Communications Commission order (the
  "NBCUniversal Order") and a Department of Justice consent decree, each imposing
  numerous commitments and conditions on the combined company's conduct.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "Legislation and Regulation / NBCUniversal Order and NBCUniversal Consent Decree"]

## Governance and ownership mechanics at closing
- Under the terms of NBCUniversal Holdings' operating agreement, GE was granted rights,
  exercisable in stated future windows beginning July 28, 2014 and January 28, 2018, to
  cause NBCUniversal Holdings to redeem portions of GE's 49% interest, with Comcast
  correspondingly granted rights to purchase portions of that interest in windows
  beginning January 28, 2016 and January 28, 2019; the redemption/purchase price formula
  was tied to a percentage of NBCUniversal Holdings' fully distributed public market
  trading value (or an appraisal if not publicly traded). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K MD&A]
- Until July 28, 2014, GE was restricted from transferring its interest in NBCUniversal
  Holdings; Comcast held a right of first offer over any subsequent GE transfer.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K MD&A]
- Immediately prior to closing, NBCUniversal distributed approximately $7.4 billion to
  GE, and in connection with the transaction NBCUniversal issued $9.1 billion of senior
  debt securities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K MD&A]
- Following the close, Comcast reorganized its reporting structure to present five
  segments (Cable Distribution, Cable Networks, Broadcast Networks, Filmed Entertainment,
  Theme Parks), combining its own contributed Programming and content businesses with
  NBCUniversal's businesses into the new segment structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "NBCUniversal Transaction"]

## What crossed and what was flagged as a closing-time integration risk
- The company's own closing-year 10-K risk factors state that the transaction "involves
  the integration of the Comcast Content Business with NBCUniversal's businesses, as well
  as the integration of NBCUniversal's businesses with our cable distribution and other
  businesses," that "challenges involved in the integration include successfully
  integrating each company's operations, technologies and content, and combining
  corporate cultures, maintaining employee morale and retaining key employees," and that
  there is no assurance the two companies "can successfully integrate these businesses."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "Risk Factors"]
- The same risk factors separately flag reliance on "on-air and creative talent" whose
  continued efforts, retention costs, or departure could affect the business.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "Risk Factors"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, Comcast obtained a 51% controlling equity and consolidation interest in a
  newly formed joint-venture holding company (NBCUniversal Holdings) that combined GE's
  contributed NBC Universal operating businesses with Comcast's contributed content
  assets, with GE retaining a 49% interest subject to a multi-year, contractually
  scheduled redemption/purchase mechanism rather than an outright sale, and with the
  filing's own risk-factor language identifying corporate-culture and personnel
  integration, rather than product or process transfer, as the open question at closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "NBCUniversal Transaction"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-047243, Form 10-K "Risk Factors"]
