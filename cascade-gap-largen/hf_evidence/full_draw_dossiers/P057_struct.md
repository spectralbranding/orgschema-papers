# Structural sub-dossier — Altaba Inc. holding structure and Yahoo Japan arrangement (closing structure)

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Altaba Inc.'s Form 8-K dated July 9, 2018 (July 10, 2018 Japan
  Standard Time), a current report under Item 7.01 (Regulation FD) that reports two
  concurrently executed definitive agreements with SoftBank concerning the registrant's
  holding of Yahoo Japan Corporation stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Because the 8-K describes the unwinding of a shareholding/governance arrangement, the
  contemporaneous holding structure (the registrant's status and its minority equity
  positions) is sourced additionally from the registrant's fiscal-year-2017 certified
  annual report on Form N-CSR (period ended December 31, 2017; filed February 26, 2018). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]

## The registrant and how the holding entity was formed
- On June 13, 2017, Yahoo! Inc. completed the sale of its internet operating business
  ("Yahoo Operating Business") to Verizon Communications Inc.; on June 16, 2017 Yahoo
  changed its name to "Altaba Inc." and filed a Notification of Registration on Form N-8A
  and a Registration Statement on Form N-2 to register as a publicly traded,
  non-diversified, closed-end management investment company under the Investment Company
  Act of 1940. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]
- After the sale, the remaining assets of Altaba consisted of an approximately 15% equity
  stake in Alibaba Group Holding Limited; an approximately 36% equity stake in Yahoo Japan
  Corporation; cash, cash equivalents and marketable debt securities; certain minority
  investments; and Excalibur IP, LLC, which owns certain patent assets not core to Yahoo's
  operating business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]

## What the holding entity owned at closing (tier level; $ in thousands)
- Alibaba Group Holding Limited: 383,565,416 common shares held, carried at a fair value
  of $66,138,185 (thousands). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]
- Yahoo Japan Corporation: 2,025,923,000 common shares held, carried at a fair value of
  $9,294,545 (thousands). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]
- Total common shares held were carried at a fair value of $75,587,070 (thousands); total
  liabilities were $18,717,167 (thousands); net assets applicable to common shares at
  December 31, 2017 were $62,485,919 (thousands). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]
- The registrant held only equity positions and governance rights in Alibaba and Yahoo
  Japan; the operating businesses, workforces, and processes of Alibaba and Yahoo Japan
  were run by those companies, not by the registrant, whose activities were those of a
  closed-end investment company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]

## The Yahoo Japan arrangement being restructured (from the drawn 8-K)
- The Yahoo Japan shareholding and governance arrangement was governed by a Joint Venture
  Agreement dated April 1, 1996 between the registrant (the "Fund") and SoftBank, as
  amended by an Amendment Agreement dated September 17, 1997 and Amendment Agreement No. 2
  dated June 17, 2015, and supplemented by Joinder Agreements dated March 27, 2008 and
  May 23, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Tender Offer Agreement: on July 9, 2018 (July 10, 2018 JST) the Fund entered a definitive
  agreement with SoftBank Corp. under which SoftBank Corp. agreed to commence a tender
  offer for 613,888,888 shares of Yahoo Japan common stock at JPY 360 (approximately
  US$3.26) per share, and the Fund agreed to tender at least 613,888,888 Yahoo Japan shares
  it owned into that offer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Termination Agreement: concurrently, the Fund, SoftBank Group Corp. and (for limited
  purposes) SBBM Corporation and SoftBank Group Japan Corporation agreed that the Joint
  Venture Agreement, which governed the parties' shareholding and other governance
  arrangements relating to Yahoo Japan, was terminated with immediate effect. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## What crossed the ownership/governance boundary at closing (from the drawn 8-K)
- Governance representation was withdrawn: in connection with the termination, the Fund
  caused Arthur Chong and Alexi A. Wellman to resign from the board of directors of Yahoo
  Japan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- Residual contractual rights were retained transitionally: for a period of 12 months, or
  until the Fund's ownership interest in Yahoo Japan fell below five percent of Yahoo
  Japan's issued and outstanding shares, the Fund would continue to have consent rights
  with respect to certain extraordinary transactions involving Yahoo Japan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The Fund was to be provided certain access rights to Yahoo Japan for purposes of due
  diligence by potential purchasers of Yahoo Japan shares and compliance with tax and
  other legal and regulatory requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- A press release announcing the two agreements was furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to the 8-K;
  the report was signed by Arthur Chong, General Counsel and Secretary, dated July 10, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 9.01 / signature]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing the registrant was a closed-end management investment company whose principal
  assets were minority equity positions (approximately 15% of Alibaba and approximately 36%
  of Yahoo Japan, carried at roughly $66.1 billion and $9.3 billion respectively), with no
  operating business, workforce, or operational processes of the two portfolio companies
  belonging to it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-058471, Form N-CSR (FY2017)]
- The July 2018 agreements restructured the Yahoo Japan position by terminating the 1996
  Joint Venture Agreement, removing the registrant's two Yahoo Japan board designees, and
  providing for a SoftBank Corp. tender offer for 613,888,888 Yahoo Japan shares at JPY 360
  per share, while the registrant retained only transitional consent and access rights for
  up to 12 months or until its stake fell below five percent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-214960, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
