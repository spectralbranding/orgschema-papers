# Structural sub-dossier — Cotiviti Holdings, Inc. / Verscend Technologies, Inc. merger (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 19, 2018, Cotiviti Holdings, Inc. ("Cotiviti") entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger with Verscend Technologies, Inc. ("Verscend") and its wholly owned
  merger subsidiary, Rey Merger Sub, Inc.; Merger Sub was to merge with and into
  Cotiviti, with Cotiviti continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Verscend. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]
- Cotiviti described itself as "a leading provider of payment accuracy and
  analytics-driven solutions" reviewing healthcare claims for billing accuracy, contract
  compliance, payment responsibility and clinical appropriateness before and after
  claims are paid; Verscend was described as driving "better healthcare outcomes through
  data analytics" via Payment Accuracy, Risk Adjustment, and Quality and Performance
  solutions — a business description covering the same payment-accuracy/analytics
  category as Cotiviti's. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]
- Cotiviti common stock traded on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "COTV." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and financing structure
- Each outstanding share of Cotiviti common stock was to convert into the right to
  receive $44.75 in cash, with outstanding options and restricted stock/RSU awards
  cashed out on the same basis; the filing fee calculation stated a "proposed maximum
  aggregate value of transaction" of $4,352,965,378.42. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]
- Financing combined private-equity sponsor equity, third-party debt, and preferred
  equity: The Veritas Capital Fund VI, L.P. committed an equity contribution of up to
  $790 million; lenders led by JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. committed debt financing of up
  to $4,615 million; and a preferred equity commitment of up to $575 million was also
  obtained; the Merger Agreement was not conditioned on receipt of this financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]

## Management, employment, and stockholder-approval provisions at closing
- Named Cotiviti executives each held pre-existing Employment Agreements providing
  severance and full accelerated equity vesting upon a qualifying termination within one
  year following a change of control, and certain executives were entitled to
  transaction/retention cash bonuses in connection with the Merger; the Compensation
  Committee had approved a separate employee retention program during the strategic
  review leading to the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A]
- Certain Cotiviti stockholders entered into a Voting and Support Agreement with
  Verscend, dated June 19, 2018, agreeing (subject to its terms) to vote their shares in
  favor of the Merger Proposal; at the Special Meeting held August 24, 2018, the
  proposal to adopt the Merger Agreement was approved by the requisite stockholder vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007223, Form 8-K Item 5.07]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the Effective Time (August 27, 2018)
- At the Effective Time, each share of Cotiviti common stock was cancelled and converted
  into the right to receive the $44.75 cash Merger Consideration; Cotiviti became a
  wholly owned subsidiary of Verscend, which funded the acquisition through the debt,
  equity, and preferred equity financing described above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007234, Form 8-K Items 2.01 and 5.01]
- Every member of Cotiviti's pre-merger board of directors resigned immediately prior to
  the Effective Time; the four directors of Merger Sub (Emad Rizk, James Dimitri, Ramzi
  Musallam, and Joseph Longosz) became Cotiviti's directors effective as of the Effective
  Time — a full replacement of the controlling governance body — and Cotiviti's
  certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated to be identical to
  Merger Sub's (retaining the "Cotiviti" name). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007234, Form 8-K Items 5.02 and 5.03]
- Cotiviti's Employee Stock Purchase Plans and its 2012 and 2016 Equity Incentive Plans
  were terminated immediately prior to the Effective Time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007234, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Cotiviti requested that the NYSE file a Notification of Removal from Listing and/or
  Registration under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act on Form 25; trading in Cotiviti
  common stock under "COTV" was suspended prior to the opening of trading on the NYSE on
  August 27, 2018, and Cotiviti subsequently filed a Form 15-12B, dated September 6,
  2018, to terminate its Exchange Act registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007234, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001341004-18-000534, Form 15-12B]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a negotiated, all-cash going-private merger between
  two companies operating in the same payment-accuracy/healthcare-analytics business
  category, funded through a combination of private-equity sponsor equity, third-party
  debt, and preferred equity, with the target's full board and equity incentive plans
  replaced/terminated and its public listing and Exchange Act registration removed at
  the Effective Time; pre-existing management retention and change-in-control
  arrangements were the closing-time mechanism bridging continuity of the target's
  senior operating personnel into the new ownership structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005113, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-18-007234, Form 8-K]
