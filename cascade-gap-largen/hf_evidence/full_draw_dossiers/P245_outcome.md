## Public-reporting continuity (feasibility-relevant)
- The successor registrant (renamed Alexander & Baldwin, Inc. at the November 8, 2017
  merger effective time) continued to file periodic reports under CIK 0001545654,
  including a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2017 filed March 1, 2018, and unbroken annual
  Form 10-K filings in every year thereafter through the outcome window and beyond.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-18-000014, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- Alexander & Baldwin, Inc. described itself, as of fiscal year 2020, as "a fully
  integrated real estate investment trust ('REIT')" whose commercial real estate
  portfolio resided entirely in Hawai'i, and continued to operate three reportable
  segments: Commercial Real Estate, Land Operations, and Materials & Construction.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Realized financial trajectory within the outcome window
- For fiscal year 2020, the Company reported Net Income (Loss) Attributable to A&B
  Shareholders of $5.6 million, on operating revenue of $305.3 million (down 29.8%,
  or $129.9 million, from 2019), with an asset-impairment charge of $5.6 million for
  the year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- For fiscal year 2021, the Company reported Net Income (Loss) Attributable to A&B
  Shareholders of $35.4 million, compared with $5.6 million in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- For fiscal year 2022, the Company reported a Net Income (Loss) Attributable to A&B
  Shareholders of $(50.6) million, compared with net income of $35.4 million in 2021,
  driven substantially by a loss from discontinued operations, net of income taxes, of
  $(86.6) million in 2022 (versus a discontinued-operations loss of $(1.1) million in
  2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

## Documented segment divestiture within the outcome window
- In December 2022, "in connection with the evaluation of strategic alternatives to
  monetize and dispose of Grace Pacific, the Company's Board of Directors authorized
  Management to complete a sale of Grace Pacific and the Company-owned quarry land on
  Maui" (the "Grace Disposal Group"); the Company concluded the Grace Disposal Group
  met the criteria for classification as held for sale and discontinued operations as
  of December 31, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The Company's independent auditor identified the held-for-sale/discontinued-operations
  classification of Grace Pacific and the Maui quarries as a critical audit matter,
  citing "significant judgments" in determining whether the planned sale represented
  "a strategic shift that has a major effect on the Company's operations and financial
  results." [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The Company characterized this step as part of a longer-run strategy, in place since
  shortly after the 2017 REIT conversion, "to simplify its business and allocate its
  capital resources to commercial real estate." [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- As of the FY2021 Form 10-K (filed February 25, 2022), the Company still reported
  Materials & Construction as an active, ongoing reportable segment, with no sale or
  held-for-sale classification of Grace Pacific yet disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The publicly traded successor to the 2017 merger continued filing periodic reports
  under the same CIK without interruption through the ~2020-2022 outcome window,
  reporting a Net Income (Loss) Attributable to A&B Shareholders that moved from $5.6
  million (FY2020) to $35.4 million (FY2021) to a $(50.6) million net loss (FY2022).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The FY2022 net loss was driven primarily by the Board's December 2022 authorization
  to sell the Materials & Construction segment (Grace Pacific and the Maui quarry
  land) and its reclassification as held for sale/discontinued operations, described by
  the Company as advancing a strategy to concentrate capital in its commercial real
  estate business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- No divestiture, impairment classification, or discontinuation was disclosed for the
  Commercial Real Estate segment (the core Hawai'i commercial property business) within
  the outcome window; the documented divestiture activity is confined to the Materials
  & Construction segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-21-000006, Form 10-K (FY2020)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-22-000008, Form 10-K (FY2021)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001545654-23-000008, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
