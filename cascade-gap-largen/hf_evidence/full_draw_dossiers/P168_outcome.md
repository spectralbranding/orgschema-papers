## Reporting basis for the outcome window
- The Company applied fresh-start accounting as of the Plan Effective Date (October 1,
  2016 convenience date), such that financial statements after that date are presented
  as those of a new reporting entity ("Successor") and are not comparable to
  pre-emergence ("Predecessor") financial statements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The source for this outcome window is the annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal
  year ended December 31, 2019, filed February 11, 2020 — approximately 3.3 years after
  the October 5, 2016 emergence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Net income was $238,450 thousand for fiscal 2017, $312,577 thousand for fiscal 2018,
  and $233,799 thousand for fiscal 2019 — positive in each of the three fiscal years
  reported in this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Total revenues were $2,324,623 thousand (2017), $2,451,787 thousand (2018), and
  $2,294,352 thousand (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The Company divested its Lone Mountain operation in 2017; Lone Mountain had provided
  approximately $74.9 million in coal sales in 2017 prior to the divestiture, and the
  2019-vs-2017 sales-volume decline discussed in the filing was described as "effectively
  all related to the divestiture of Lone Mountain." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The Company also recorded proceeds from other, smaller disposals and divestitures of
  approximately $12 million in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Capital-return activity in the window
- The Company paid dividends on its common stock totaling $30.2 million in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Under its share repurchase program, the Company repurchased 2,872,548 shares for
  approximately $243.5 million during the year ended December 31, 2019, bringing total
  repurchase-program authorization to $1.05 billion, with $223 million remaining
  authorized as of the filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Management continuity through the outcome window
- John W. Eaves, who has served as Chief Executive Officer since 2012, signed the
  FY2019 Form 10-K as Chief Executive Officer and Director on February 11, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Paul A. Lang is reported in the same filing as having served as President and Chief
  Operating Officer since April 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- No filing reviewed in this window documents a change in Chief Executive Officer or
  President/Chief Operating Officer, nor any second Chapter 11 filing or other
  insolvency proceeding by Arch Coal, Inc. during fiscal years 2017-2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the fiscal years 2017 through 2019 (roughly the first one to three years after
  the October 2016 emergence), Arch Coal, Inc. reported positive net income each year
  ($238.5M, $312.6M, $233.8M respectively), divested one operation (Lone Mountain, 2017)
  while continuing revenues near $2.3-2.5 billion annually, resumed shareholder capital
  returns (a $30.2 million dividend and a $243.5 million share repurchase in 2019 under
  a program authorized up to $1.05 billion), and retained the same Chief Executive
  Officer and President/Chief Operating Officer who held those roles at emergence,
  with no documented second insolvency proceeding through the FY2019 filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-20-001344, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
