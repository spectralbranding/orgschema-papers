## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The Company (DigitalGlobe) filed a Form 15-12B on October 16, 2017 to deregister its common stock under the Exchange Act; it filed no further Form 10-K or other periodic report of its own after the merger closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-062366, Form 15-12B]
- Because there is no independent post-close public financial record for DigitalGlobe as a standalone registrant, the realized-record facts below are drawn from the acquirer's own subsequent SEC filings, in which the acquired business is reported as a separate segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-062366, Form 15-12B]

## Acquirer identity and corporate-structure changes after closing
- The acquirer's SEC registrant record (CIK 0001121142) shows the entity was named "MACDONALD, DETTWILER & ASSOCIATES LTD." through October 5, 2017, then "Maxar Technologies Ltd." from October 6, 2017 (the day after the merger closed) through December 21, 2018, and "Maxar Technologies Inc." thereafter — consistent with completion of the previously disclosed plan to reincorporate from British Columbia to the United States. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001121142.json]
- On April 8, 2020, Maxar completed the sale of the "MDA Business" — the original, pre-merger Canadian space-robotics and communications-satellite operations that MDA itself brought into the combination — for an aggregate purchase price of $729 million (C$1.0 billion); following that sale, the MDA segment was no longer a reportable segment and its results were reclassified to discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Item 1 and Note 3]
- After the MDA Business sale, the combined company's two remaining reportable segments were "Earth Intelligence" (the former DigitalGlobe business) and "Space Infrastructure" (the former Space Systems/Loral business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Item 1]

## Realized segment record — Earth Intelligence (former DigitalGlobe business), as reported in the fiscal-year-2020 Form 10-K (filed February 2021, roughly 3-4 years after closing)
- Earth Intelligence segment revenue was $1,081 million for fiscal year 2020, $1,085 million for fiscal year 2019, and $1,059 million for fiscal year 2018 — essentially flat across the three years reported in that filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Segment Results]
- Earth Intelligence segment Adjusted EBITDA was $513 million for fiscal year 2020, $548 million for fiscal year 2019, and $516 million for fiscal year 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Segment Results]
- As of the October 1, 2020 annual goodwill-impairment testing date, management determined it was more likely than not that the fair value of the Earth Intelligence reporting unit exceeded its carrying value, including goodwill; Earth Intelligence's goodwill balance, net of a $172 million addition from the 2019 Vricon acquisition, stood at $1,627 million as of December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Note 9 Goodwill]
- The same filing's goodwill note shows accumulated impairment specific to the Earth Intelligence reporting unit of $142 million (part of a combined $159 million goodwill write-down across Earth Intelligence and Space Infrastructure recorded in fiscal year 2018, attributed in the filing's MD&A to a sustained decline in the combined company's stock price); this predates the 3-5-year outcome window but is disclosed as comparative trend data within the in-window filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Note 9 Goodwill and MD&A]
- The same filing's comparative disclosure also states that in December 2018 the combined company lost the imaging capability of its WorldView-4 satellite, recording a $162 million loss on the satellite, related assets and future premium payments; this event predates the 3-5-year outcome window but is disclosed as comparative context within the in-window filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), MD&A]

## Company-wide (consolidated) trend disclosed in the same filing
- Consolidated net income (loss) for the combined company (Earth Intelligence plus Space Infrastructure plus corporate items; not Earth Intelligence alone) was $303 million for fiscal year 2020, $109 million for fiscal year 2019, and a loss of $1,250 million for fiscal year 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), Selected Financial Data]
- The filing's MD&A states that in 2018 and 2019 the combined company undertook restructuring plans intended to reduce headcount and other cost-saving initiatives, and separately notes a year-over-year decrease in "transaction and integration related expenses" between fiscal 2019 and fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020), MD&A and Risk Factors]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- DigitalGlobe itself deregistered and filed no further periodic reports after the October 2017 closing, so no independent post-close public record exists for it as a standalone entity; the acquirer, however, separately discloses the acquired business as its "Earth Intelligence" reportable segment. Within that segment disclosure, as reported roughly three to four years after closing, revenue and Adjusted EBITDA were essentially flat year-over-year (2018-2020) and the segment's goodwill was determined to exceed carrying value at the most recent test date, with no new goodwill impairment recorded on Earth Intelligence during 2019-2020. The combined enterprise's earlier comparative disclosures (2018) show a period of much larger consolidated losses, a goodwill write-down, and a satellite-capability loss, followed by a 2020 divestiture of the acquirer's own original, pre-merger space-robotics business while the acquired imagery business was retained as one of two remaining segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001635, Form 10-K (fiscal year 2020)]
