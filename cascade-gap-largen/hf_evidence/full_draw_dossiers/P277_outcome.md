## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- The entity's most recent Annual Report on Form 10-K, covering fiscal year 2017, was
  filed March 15, 2018; no subsequent Form 10-K was filed under its name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001390213-18-000007, Form 10-K FY2017]
- On November 1, 2018, the entity filed a Form 15-12G terminating the registration of
  its common stock under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act. The notice certifies that,
  pursuant to the June 17, 2018 Agreement and Plan of Merger, the entity "merged with
  and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub surviving the merger as an indirect wholly-owned
  subsidiary of W. P. Carey," effective October 31, 2018, and states the approximate
  number of holders of record at the certification date as "One." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065168, Form 15-12G]
- Consequently, no audited public financial statements exist under the entity's own name
  for the ~2021-2023 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065168, Form 15-12G]

## Acquirer's subsequent accounting treatment
- W. P. Carey's Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal year 2018 describes the
  transaction (the "CPA:17 Merger") as accounted for "as a business combination under
  the acquisition method of accounting," with the purchase price allocated to assets
  acquired and liabilities assumed at their preliminary fair values as of October 31,
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]
- W. P. Carey's Annual Report on Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 reports two operating
  segments, "Real Estate" and "Investment Management"; the properties obtained in the
  CPA:17 Merger are consolidated within the Real Estate segment together with the rest
  of W. P. Carey's owned portfolio, with no separate segment or sub-segment financial
  disclosure specific to the former CPA:17 – Global business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.), Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- Notes to that same filing record a $8.4 million loss on change in control of interests
  during the third quarter of 2019 tied to a real estate investment obtained in the
  CPA:17 Merger, and state that federal and state net operating loss and interest
  carryforwards "were subject to limitations as a result of the CPA:17 Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]

## Traceable subset: operating (self-storage / hotel) properties attributed to the merger
- W. P. Carey's FY2018 Form 10-K states that the company's portfolio "includes 48
  operating properties, comprised of 46 self-storage properties and two hotels,
  substantially all of which we acquired in connection with the CPA:17 Merger." [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]
- W. P. Carey's FY2021 Form 10-K reports that at December 31, 2021 the operating-property
  count in this same category had declined to 20 properties (19 self-storage properties
  and one hotel); it further discloses that of the two hotel operating properties
  obtained in the CPA:17 Merger, "one of which was sold in January 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]
- For the remaining hotel operating property, the FY2021 filing reports average
  occupancy of 45.2% for the year ended December 31, 2021, which it attributes to "the
  adverse effect of the COVID-19 pandemic." [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The entity's separate SEC registration and reporting ended within days of closing, and
  no periodic report has been filed under its name since; the acquirer accounted for the
  transaction as a business combination and folded the acquired real estate into its
  existing Real Estate segment without a separate segment or sub-segment financial
  breakout, so no standalone financial trajectory for the former business is publicly
  observable for the ~2021-2023 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065168, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]
- The one subset with a traceable multi-year record is the self-storage and hotel
  operating properties "substantially all of which" the acquirer attributes to this
  merger: their count declined from 48 (at fiscal year-end 2018) to 20 (at fiscal
  year-end 2021), including the documented sale of one of the two acquired hotel
  properties in January 2020 and reduced occupancy at the remaining hotel property
  during 2021, which the acquirer's filing attributes to pandemic conditions rather than
  to the transaction itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 (W. P. Carey Inc.)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001025378-22-000041, Form 10-K FY2021 (W. P. Carey Inc.)]
