## Public-reporting status across the outcome window
- The registrant's last annual report on Form 10-K covered fiscal year 2014 and was filed
  March 2, 2015; no Form 10-K covering FY2015 or any later year appears on EDGAR, so no
  audited public annual financial statements exist within the 2016-2019 outcome window and
  the realized-record facts below rely on the registrant's current reports. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, 10-K FY2014]
- In March 2016 the Company disclosed it would not meet the deadline to file its FY2015
  Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-16-000340, 8-K (10-K deadline)]

## Realized outcome scalar — bankruptcy and default
- On April 21, 2016 SunEdison, Inc. and certain of its domestic and international
  subsidiaries (collectively, the Debtors) filed voluntary petitions for relief under
  Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the
  Southern District of New York. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Item 1.03]
- The bankruptcy petition triggered events of default under debt instruments representing
  in the aggregate approximately $3.82 billion of outstanding indebtedness of the Company;
  as a result the principal and interest thereunder became immediately due and payable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Item 1.03]
- In connection with the filing the Debtors sought approval of senior secured superpriority
  debtor-in-possession financing, including a $300.0 million revolving facility and a $350.0
  million term loan facility, with $90.0 million of the DIP Term Loan Facility funded
  concurrently with execution of the DIP credit agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Item 1.03]
- The drawn May 2016 filing reflects continued restructuring: Amendment No. 1 (dated May 18,
  2016) to that Senior Secured Superpriority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement modified,
  among other things, Tranche B roll-up loans and syndication, post-closing guaranty and
  collateral matters, restructuring milestones, and permitted dispositions; it was signed by
  John S. Dubel, Chief Restructuring Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-600567, 8-K Item 1.01]

## Interface disruption tied to the acquired assets over time
- Obligations from the 2014 First Wind acquisition came due at the petition: under the
  November 17, 2014 agreement pursuant to which the Company and its affiliates acquired First
  Wind Holdings, LLC, payments totaling $231 million in the aggregate had become due and
  payable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Item 1.03]

## Delisting from public markets
- Following the April 21, 2016 bankruptcy announcement, NYSE Regulation issued a delisting
  notice; the Company's common stock was suspended immediately from trading at the market
  opening on the NYSE on April 21, 2016, the Staff citing the uncertainty as to the timing
  and outcome of the bankruptcy proceedings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Item 3.01]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly a year of the 2015 acquisition era the registrant ceased filing audited
  annual reports (last 10-K covered FY2014), missed its FY2015 10-K deadline, and on April
  21, 2016 filed a Chapter 11 petition covering the parent and numerous subsidiaries;
  approximately $3.82 billion of indebtedness was accelerated, acquisition-related payments
  of $231 million became due, the company operated under debtor-in-possession financing, and
  its common stock was suspended from NYSE trading the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, 8-K Items 1.03/3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-600567, 8-K Item 1.01]
