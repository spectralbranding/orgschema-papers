# Structural sub-dossier — Eastern Virginia Bankshares, Inc. merger into Southern National Bancorp of Virginia, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Southern National Bancorp of Virginia, Inc. ("SONA"), the bank holding company for
  Sonabank, and Eastern Virginia Bankshares, Inc. ("EVBS"), the bank holding company for
  EVB, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated December 13, 2016, as amended,
  under which EVBS merged with and into SONA as a direct statutory merger (no separate
  merger subsidiary), with SONA surviving as the "Continuing Corporation" and retaining
  the SONA name; each EVBS common and Series B Preferred share converted into 0.6313
  shares of SONA common stock, and the merger was accounted for under the acquisition
  method with SONA as accounting acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus]
- At March 31, 2017, SONA reported total assets of $1.2 billion versus EVBS's total
  assets of approximately $1.4 billion — EVBS was the larger balance sheet despite SONA
  being the surviving/continuing legal entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed effective June 23, 2017; SONA issued approximately 11.6 million
  shares as merger consideration, valued at approximately $198.8 million at SONA's
  closing price that day; immediately following the holding-company merger, EVB merged
  with and into Sonabank the same day, with Sonabank surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-033996, Form 8-K dated 2017-06-26 (event date 2017-06-23)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing product: the combined bank operated 47 branch locations across
  Virginia and Maryland, combining SONA's 24 Sonabank branches with EVBS's 24 full-
  service EVB branches, two drive-in facilities and one loan production office. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent / legal vehicle: SONA was the surviving entity, the accounting
  acquirer, and the party whose already-listed common stock continued as the security of
  the Continuing Corporation; EVBS's separate corporate existence ceased at the
  effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- Ownership split: SONA shareholders were expected to hold approximately 51.4% and
  former EVBS shareholders approximately 48.6% of the Continuing Corporation's stock
  after the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- Governance/management: the post-merger board comprised 11 directors — 6 from SONA and
  5 from EVBS plus one EVBS-designated observer; Georgia S. Derrico (SONA) continued as
  executive chairman and R. Roderick Porter (SONA) as executive vice chairman, while Joe
  A. Shearin (EVBS's former president/CEO) was named president and CEO of Sonabank (the
  bank subsidiary, not the holding company), with other named EVBS executives taking
  co-chief credit officer and similar subordinate operating roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- People: five former EVBS directors and one observer were appointed to the SONA board
  at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-033996, Form 8-K]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- Employee benefits: EVBS employees continuing with the Continuing Corporation were to
  receive benefits substantially comparable to their pre-merger EVBS benefits, with
  EVBS's plans maintained in the interim until integration; the EVBS and SONA 401(k)
  plans were to be merged, and the EVBS Cash Balance Pension Plan was to be fully funded
  and terminated with 100% vesting before the effective date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- Workforce transition: a contractual severance formula (two weeks of base pay per year
  of service, maximum 26 weeks) applied to EVBS or SONA employees involuntarily
  terminated within six months after the effective date, and a retention bonus pool was
  established for certain non-executive employees of both companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A]
- Listing mechanics: EVBS notified NASDAQ on June 23, 2017 that its common stock was
  cancelled and converted into the merger consideration and requested that NASDAQ
  suspend trading and remove EVBS common stock from listing and registration; trading
  in EVBS common stock on NASDAQ was suspended before the market opened on June 26,
  2017, while SONA's own shares (including those newly issued in the merger) continued
  to be listed on the NASDAQ Global Market. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-033996, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- This was a whole-company, going-concern combination in which the legally surviving
  party (SONA) absorbed the larger-by-assets party (EVBS) through a direct statutory
  merger immediately followed by a same-day merger of the two parties' bank
  subsidiaries; the product (branch network), the legal vehicle, and a majority of
  governance control (6 of 11 board seats, both top holding-company executive titles)
  sat with the acquirer side, while EVBS's operating personnel and branches were
  absorbed under explicit benefit-harmonization, severance and retention provisions
  rather than through an ongoing transitional-services arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-004905, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-033996, Form 8-K]
