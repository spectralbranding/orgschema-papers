# Structural sub-dossier — AMR Corporation / US Airways Group business combination (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## The drawn filing (described truthfully)
- The drawn accession (0000006201-12-000001, Form 8-K, filed 2012-01-05) is a notice
  filing reporting that the registrant's common stock and two series of debt securities
  had fallen below the New York Stock Exchange's minimum-price continued-listing
  standard, that trading in those securities would be suspended on the NYSE ahead of the
  2012-01-05 market open, and that the common stock would begin trading over the counter
  under the symbol "AAMRQ." [SRC: SEC accession 0000006201-12-000001, Form 8-K Items 3.01/8.01]
- The same filing states this action followed the registrant's voluntary Chapter 11
  petitions (filed November 29, 2011) and that the registrant did not oppose the
  exchange-listing action because of the pending reorganization case. [SRC: SEC accession 0000006201-12-000001, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- This filing is a listing/trading-status notice, not the document that sets the terms
  of the transaction that closed roughly two years later; the transaction's terms are
  sourced below from the contemporaneous closing-era filings that actually state them.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The registrant (AMR Corporation, together with certain of its domestic subsidiaries,
  the "Debtors") and US Airways Group, Inc. entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated February 13, 2013, with a wholly owned merger subsidiary of AMR ("Merger Sub")
  as the third party; Merger Sub was to merge with and into US Airways Group, with US
  Airways Group surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of AMR. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The combination was structured to be effected through, and was conditioned on
  Bankruptcy Court confirmation of, a plan of reorganization of the Debtors in their
  then-pending Chapter 11 case. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered an order on October 21, 2013 confirming the Debtors'
  fourth amended joint plan of reorganization, and on December 9, 2013 (the "Effective
  Date") the Debtors consummated the reorganization principally through the merger
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Introductory Note]
- On the Effective Date, US Airways Group survived the merger as a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of the (renamed) parent company rather than being dissolved into it; each
  share of US Airways Group common stock converted into the right to receive one share
  of the parent's common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Immediately after the merger's effective time the parent changed its name from AMR
  Corporation to American Airlines Group Inc., and its common stock began trading on
  Nasdaq under the symbol AAL, with US Airways Group's own common stock separately
  removed from exchange trading. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Items 3.01/5.03]

## Ownership / economic-interest split agreed at signing
- Under the Merger Agreement, US Airways equityholders (stockholders, convertible-note
  holders, optionees and restricted-stock-unit holders) were to receive shares
  representing 28% of the post-plan diluted capitalization of the combined company; the
  remaining 72% was to be distributed, under the plan of reorganization, to the Debtors'
  own stakeholders, labor unions and certain employees, with all pre-existing AMR common
  stock cancelled and replaced by a claims-based distribution of the new stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01; SEC accession 0001193125-13-466914, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Controlling intent / governance tier
- The Merger Agreement fixed the initial 12-member board of the combined company:
  AMR's then chairman/CEO as chairman for up to one year, US Airways' then CEO as CEO of
  the combined company (and as chairman thereafter), two AMR-designated independent
  directors, three US Airways-designated independent directors, and five directors
  designated by a search committee representing the Debtors' unsecured-creditors
  committee and certain other creditors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On the Effective Date the board seated matched that structure (12 members), and the
  combined company's headquarters were fixed at the registrant's pre-existing Fort Worth,
  Texas offices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Item 2.01; SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## People / management tier — who ran the combined company at closing
- On the Effective Date the registrant's own pre-merger president and CEO ceased to hold
  an executive role and moved solely into the non-executive chairman position; four other
  named pre-merger executive officers (an EVP, the CFO, the general counsel/chief
  compliance officer, and the SVP of operations) ceased to be executive officers of the
  company at the same time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-466973, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The executive-officer slate elected on the Effective Date across the parent and its
  operating subsidiaries was led by the CEO of US Airways Group immediately before the
  merger (now CEO of the combined company); the other newly elected named executive
  officers (president; EVP/CFO; COO, who was also named CEO of the US Airways operating
  subsidiary; EVP-people and communications) likewise came from the pre-merger US Airways
  side, alongside one pre-merger AMR-side executive retained as EVP-corporate affairs.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-466973, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The company separately entered into a transition agreement, effective the Effective
  Date, with its former president/CEO covering his move to a non-executive role, and
  separate severance arrangements (entered before closing) applied to the other departing
  pre-merger executive officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-466973, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Legal vehicle and organizational documents
- The parent's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated and a
  certificate of amendment changing its name to American Airlines Group Inc. was filed
  with the Delaware Secretary of State, effective at or immediately before the merger's
  Effective Time; US Airways Group's pre-existing equity-incentive plans and outstanding
  award agreements were assumed by the parent and continued on their existing terms
  rather than being terminated at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Item 5.03; SEC accession 0001193125-13-466973, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Bridging arrangement at closing
- The closing-era filings reviewed describe the transaction as a full legal combination
  (the smaller company becoming a wholly-owned operating subsidiary of the renamed
  parent, with the parent's board, name and executive slate reconstituted at signing/
  closing) rather than a carve-out; none of the reviewed closing filings describe a
  transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging a separated
  substrate, consistent with the transaction consolidating rather than separating
  operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01; SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, control of the combined company's executive management passed
  predominantly to the smaller pre-merger party's (US Airways') leadership team, while
  the larger pre-merger party (AMR) retained the surviving legal registrant, its name
  (renamed), its headquarters location, and a non-executive board chairmanship for its
  outgoing CEO; the smaller party's own operating subsidiary was preserved intact as a
  wholly-owned subsidiary rather than dissolved. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-057477, Form 8-K Item 1.01; SEC accession 0001193125-13-465950, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.02/5.03; SEC accession 0001193125-13-466973, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
