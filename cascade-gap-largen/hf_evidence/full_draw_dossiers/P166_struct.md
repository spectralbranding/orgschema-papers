## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn current report furnishes, under Regulation FD (Item 7.01), a Monthly Operating
  Report covering October 6, 2014 to November 1, 2014, filed with the Bankruptcy Court;
  it is expressly "furnished," is unaudited, was not prepared in accordance with GAAP or
  SEC financial-statement regulations, and is not a deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-005438, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The drawn filing records that on October 6, 2014 the registrant and certain subsidiaries
  (the "Debtors") had filed voluntary petitions for reorganization relief under Chapter 11
  in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of New Hampshire, jointly administered as
  In re GT Advanced Technologies Inc., et al., Case No. 14-11916 (HJB). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-005438, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Parties and transaction type at the closing-era event
- The transaction is a court-supervised Chapter 11 reorganization begun October 6, 2014;
  the Debtors continued to operate their businesses and manage their properties as
  debtors-in-possession under the jurisdiction of the Bankruptcy Court. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004279, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The counterparties to the closing-era settlement resolving claims within the case are
  Apple Inc. and Platypus Development LLC (together the "Apple Parties"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Obligations triggered at the boundary event (October 6, 2014)
- The Chapter 11 filing constituted an event of default accelerating: $220 million principal
  of 3.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2017; $214 million principal of 3.00% Convertible
  Senior Notes due 2020; and the Prepayment Agreement between Apple Inc. and GTAT Corporation
  dated October 31, 2013, under which GTAT Corp. had received three installments totaling
  approximately $440 million; all such obligations became automatically due and payable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004279, Form 8-K Item 2.04]

## What crossed the ownership boundary in the settlement (dated October 21, 2014)
- Under the Adequate Protection and Settlement Agreement the Debtors agreed to wind down
  their sapphire materials business, while remaining in the business of selling (a) specialty
  sapphire materials (including titanium-doped sapphire material) and (b) advanced sapphire
  growth furnaces ("ASFs"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Debtors agreed to sell ASFs, a portion of the sale proceeds of which would satisfy
  Apple's claim of $439 million (the "Claim"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Apple was deemed to hold a perfected security interest in the ASFs located at the
  company's Mesa, Arizona facility, and its Claim was to be solely recoverable therefrom. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The customer-facing furnace assets (ASFs) thus moved toward disposition to satisfy the
  Claim, while the company retained ownership of all intellectual property rights related
  to its sapphire growth technology. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Apple agreed to release the Debtors from all exclusivity restrictions relating to the
  sale of ASFs, and the parties agreed to mutual releases of their claims against one
  another. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the substrate at the closing-era event
- The Debtors were permitted to secure debtor-in-possession financing using ASFs at the
  Mesa facility as collateral (up to $150 million), with Apple's security interest in that
  collateral subordinated to the DIP financing lender. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Effectiveness of the Settlement Agreement was conditioned on Bankruptcy Court approval
  and the approval order becoming final and non-appealable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On October 30, 2014 the Bankruptcy Court concluded the settlement terms should be
  publicly available, and the company filed a complete unredacted copy of the Settlement
  Agreement; on November 3, 2014 the Debtors and the Apple Parties entered a Waiver and
  Amendment Agreement waiving certain conditions of effectiveness. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004770, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing-era event only)
- The closing-era transaction is a court-supervised reorganization in which a supply
  relationship was unwound by settlement: the furnace assets (ASFs) at a single facility
  were directed toward sale to satisfy a $439 million secured claim, the sapphire materials
  operation was set to wind down, exclusivity restrictions were released, and the sapphire
  growth intellectual property was retained by the registrant — with a subordinated
  debtor-in-possession facility bridging the estate's operations pending court approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-004468, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
