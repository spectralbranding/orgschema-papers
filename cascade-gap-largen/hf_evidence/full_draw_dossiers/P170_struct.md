# Structural sub-dossier — Exide Technologies Chapter 11 reorganization (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is Exide Technologies' Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended March 31,
  2013, filed June 14, 2013 — four days after the company's bankruptcy petition — and it
  reports the petition as a recent event within the same document rather than as a
  transaction-terms filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]

## The Chapter 11 filing and case structure (petition-era, June 2013)
- On June 10, 2013 (the "Petition Date"), Exide Technologies, a Delaware corporation
  (the "Debtor"), filed a voluntary petition for relief under Chapter 11 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, administered
  as In re Exide Technologies, case number 13-11482, stated as intended "to deleverage
  its balance sheet and implement operational restructuring." [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]
- None of the Debtor's direct or indirect subsidiaries filed a voluntary petition for
  relief under Chapter 11 or were involved in any other restructuring proceeding — only
  the parent Delaware corporation was the Debtor. [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The company sought Bankruptcy Court approval of debtor-in-possession financing under a
  Superpriority Debtor-in-Possession Credit Agreement dated June 9, 2013, among Exide
  Technologies as U.S. Borrower, Exide Global Holding Netherlands C.V. as Foreign
  Borrower, various lenders, and JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Agent, providing up to
  $500.0 million (a $225.0 million ABL revolving facility and a $275.0 million "last out"
  term loan facility). [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]

## The pre-petition operating business (product / process / people, FY2013)
- The company reported through four segments — Transportation Americas, Transportation
  Europe and ROW, Industrial Energy Americas, and Industrial Energy Europe and ROW —
  selling branded transportation and industrial batteries (Exide, Centra, DETA, Orbital,
  Fulmen, Tudor, and private labels); transportation represented approximately 61% of net
  sales in fiscal 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The company operated three lead-acid battery recycling facilities in North America,
  supplying approximately 75-80% of the recycled lead used in its North American
  Transportation and Industrial Energy products. [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The company employed approximately 9,628 persons at March 31, 2013 (1,233 salaried and
  2,691 hourly in the Americas; 2,237 salaried and 3,467 hourly in Europe and ROW), with
  a portion of hourly employees in both regions represented by unions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]

## What crossed / did not cross the ownership boundary at the Effective Date (April 30, 2015)
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed the company's Plan of Reorganization on March 27, 2015;
  the Plan became effective April 30, 2015, at which point the company stated it had
  "emerged from Chapter 11 as a privately held Company, substantially in its current form
  — operating across all Industrial Energy and Transportation business segments
  globally." The same registrant retained the operating business and product lines; no
  third-party acquirer took the enterprise. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-161529, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- Under the Plan the company reduced debt by approximately $600 million, received
  approximately $165 million through a rights offering, and closed on $200 million of
  exit financing arranged by Bank of America, N.A., PNC Capital Markets, LLC, and BMO
  Capital Markets Corp. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-161529, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Pursuant to the Plan, the company's pre-existing common stock (formerly traded OTCQB:
  XIDEQ) was cancelled; common stockholders received no distribution and retained no
  property under the Plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-161529, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- Robert M. Caruso, described in the release as the company's President and Chief
  Executive Officer, continued in that role on an interim basis after emergence, pending
  appointment of a new CEO — indicating continuity of at least part of the pre-petition
  senior management team into the reorganized entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-161529, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- An earlier-stage Plan proposal described in the FY2014 Form 10-K identified the source
  of contemplated new capital as the "Unofficial Committee of Senior Secured Noteholders"
  ("UNC") — holders of a substantial majority of the DIP term loan and pre-petition
  Senior Secured Notes — proposing approximately $485.0 million in new capital comprised
  of a preferred convertible equity commitment (partly a UNC-backstopped rights offering,
  partly a direct UNC investment) and a UNC-backstopped bond issuance; this indicates the
  new post-emergence equity was contemplated to pass to former creditors rather than to
  an outside acquirer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-14-003297, Form 10-K FY2014]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was an in-court Chapter 11 balance-sheet reorganization of a single
  Delaware parent corporation (its subsidiaries never filed); the customer-facing product
  lines, manufacturing/recycling processes, global segment structure, and at least part
  of senior management (the CEO) continued under the same registrant with no acquiring
  third party, while the ownership layer changed — pre-petition common equity was
  cancelled and new capital/equity was contemplated to flow to the holders of pre-petition
  secured debt — and the non-debtor international operations were bridged into the case
  through the DIP facility's Foreign Borrower provision rather than a separate
  transitional-services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-161529, Form 8-K EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0000813781-13-000016, Form 10-K FY2013]
