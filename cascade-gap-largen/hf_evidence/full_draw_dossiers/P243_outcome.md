## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On October 19, 2018, Concentrix CVG Corporation — "successor in interest to
  Convergys Corporation" — filed a Form 15-12B certifying termination of Exchange Act
  registration of the former Convergys common shares, recording one (1) holder of
  record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-303023, Form 15-12B]
- EDGAR's record for the Convergys Corporation registrant (CIK 0001062047) shows no
  filing after November 2018; no Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed by this
  registrant for any fiscal year within the ~2021-2023 outcome window. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001062047&type=10-K]
- The acquiring parent, SYNNEX Corporation, itself changed its name to TD SYNNEX
  Corporation effective November 2, 2021 (CIK 0001177394), reflecting a subsequent
  corporate combination separate from the Convergys transaction. [SRC: SEC EDGAR company search, CIK 0001177394 (formerly "SYNNEX CORP" through 2021-11-02)]
- On December 1, 2020, the technology-infused customer-experience business that had
  absorbed Convergys — Concentrix Corporation (CIK 0001803599) — completed a separation
  and tax-free spin-off from SYNNEX, distributing one share of Concentrix common stock
  for each SYNNEX share held, and began trading independently on Nasdaq under the
  symbol "CNXC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-21-000045, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Whether the acquired business is separately disclosed within the window
- Concentrix Corporation's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended November 30, 2022
  (filed within the ~2021-2023 window) states that "the Company operates in a single
  segment, based on how the chief operating decision maker ... views and evaluates the
  Company's operations," with its President and CEO identified as the CODM. [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-23-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- The same Form 10-K names Convergys only in a list of past strategic acquisitions —
  "We have made targeted acquisitions to increase our technology expertise, enter new
  verticals and geographies, and increase our scale, including the IBM Customer Care
  Business, Convergys Corporation, PK and ServiceSource" — with no separate revenue,
  income, or segment disclosure isolating the former Convergys business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-23-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- No goodwill-impairment charge, divestiture, or other outcome scalar attributed
  specifically to the former Convergys business was found in the FY2022 Form 10-K's
  goodwill-testing discussion, which addresses goodwill at the reporting-unit level
  without a Convergys-specific breakout. [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-23-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022)]

  sites in the year following closing: approximately 258 positions in Erlanger,
  Kentucky (October 2018), approximately 200 in Southfield, Michigan (November 2018),
  approximately 117 in Watertown, New York (February 2019), and up to 785 at a Tucson,
  Arizona center originally run by Convergys (April 2019, tied to expiring client
  program contracts). [SRC: https://nearshoreamericas.com/concentrix-lays-employees-merges-convergys/] [SRC: https://nearshoreamericas.com/concentrix-lay-785-employees-tucson-arizona/]

  October 2018 closing and thus **predate** the case's fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window;
  they are noted here for completeness but are not treated as within-window outcome
  facts. [SRC: https://nearshoreamericas.com/concentrix-lay-785-employees-tucson-arizona/]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired registrant (Convergys Corporation, CIK 0001062047) deregistered from
  SEC reporting within two weeks of closing and has filed nothing since; no periodic
  report covering any fiscal year in the ~2021-2023 window exists for it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-303023, Form 15-12B]
- The surviving legal vehicle and its later corporate parent (Concentrix CVG
  Corporation, then Concentrix Corporation post-spin-off) continued to operate at
  scale and remained a public filer, but its FY2022 Form 10-K — the periodic report
  falling within the fixed window — discloses the company in a single reportable
  segment and names Convergys only as a historical acquisition, without isolating any
  financial or operating scalar for the former Convergys business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-23-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- Per the no-record convention (Amendment 4), because no acquirer/successor filing
  separately discloses the former Convergys business's post-close trajectory within
  the fixed ~2021-2023 window, this case is coded **uncertain** on the outcome for the
  specific acquired business; documented integration-related workforce reductions
  exist in the press record but fall before the fixed window and are not substituted
  as the outcome scalar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001803599-23-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
