## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Upon completion of the merger, trading in VWR common stock on NASDAQ was suspended effective the closing date, and NASDAQ filed a Form 25 with the SEC to remove the common stock from listing and registration; the Company also stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate its registration and reporting obligations under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-349353, Form 8-K dated 2017-11-21]
- A search of EDGAR filings by VWR Corporation (CIK 0001412232) shows no periodic report (10-K, 10-Q, or 8-K) filed after November 2017; the registrant's own public filing history ends with the closing-era filings. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001412232&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100]
- Consequently, no standalone 10-K or other periodic report exists for VWR Corporation as a discrete registrant for any post-closing period, including the ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001412232&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100]
- However, Avantor, Inc. (incorporated in Delaware in May 2017 "in anticipation of the VWR acquisition") itself became an SEC-reporting company through an initial public offering of its common stock on the New York Stock Exchange in May 2019, and its subsequent Form 10-K annual reports separately identify facts specific to the VWR business acquired in 2017 rather than reporting only fully consolidated figures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Avantor, Inc. Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020]

## Realized record documented in the acquirer's subsequent 10-K filings
- Avantor's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed 2021-02-16) describes VWR as "a global manufacturer and distributor of laboratory and production products and services founded in 1852 that now represents the primary ordering platform for our customers." [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The same filing reports "VWR integration and planning expenses," described as "expenses incurred related to the planning and integration of VWR," as a distinct adjustment line in Avantor's Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation for fiscal years 2018 ($36.2 million), 2019 ($22.5 million), and 2020 ($9.9 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 10-K states that Avantor generated "over $220 million of annualized cost synergies" from a program that "was originally envisioned to last for three years following the VWR acquisition and has concluded," expected to favorably affect results "in 2021 and beyond." [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Avantor's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed 2022-02-11) states that "the vast majority of these expenses were incurred through 2020 with an immaterial amount incurred in 2021," and no separate VWR-integration line item appears in that year's Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation; the filing again describes the synergy program as concluded, now stating the benefit is expected "in 2022 and beyond." [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-22-000030, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The FY2020 10-K reports that of two U.S. defined-benefit pension plans, one was originally acquired from VWR in 2017 and a second plan acquired from VWR "was merged with" Avantor's own plan in 2018; the filing separately states that eight non-U.S. pension plans "for our employees around the world that we acquired from VWR in 2017" continued to accrue future benefits for most participants as of the filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 10-K reports the VWR trade name as a finite-lived intangible asset amortized over a life of 6.4 years, with a carrying value of $91.4 million as of December 31, 2020, down from $141.0 million as of December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 10-K's executive-officer biographies show at least one former VWR senior executive continuing in an Avantor role: an individual who served as VWR's "Senior Vice President, Strategic Partners and Global Export" from March 2016 to November 2017 is reported as Avantor's "Executive Vice President, Strategic Partners," a position held "since November 2017" through the filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The FY2020 10-K states that the VWR acquisition was partially funded by debt issued by a wholly-owned Avantor subsidiary (Avantor Funding, Inc.), and that certain of those debt agreements restrict dividend payments up the ownership chain, subject to limited exceptions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because VWR Corporation deregistered its securities following the November 2017 closing, no separate 3-5-year public record exists for VWR Corporation as a standalone registrant. The best-documented realized record instead comes from the acquirer's own SEC filings: Avantor continued to report VWR-specific integration and planning expenses through fiscal year 2020 (an immaterial amount in 2021), described VWR as remaining the "primary ordering platform" for its customers, amortized a separately tracked VWR trade name, reported the partial merger and continuation of VWR-origin pension plans, retained at least one named former VWR executive in a combined-company role, and characterized its VWR-tied, three-year, $220-million-plus cost-synergy program as concluded as of the FY2021 filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-21-000040, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001722482-22-000030, Form 10-K FY2021]
