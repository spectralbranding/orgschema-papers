# Outcome sub-dossier — PharMerica Corporation realized record, ~3-5-year horizon (~2020-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier contains ONLY the realized
> record after the transaction closed; it contains no information about how the

## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The last annual report (Form 10-K) filed by PharMerica Corporation under its own CIK
  covered fiscal year 2016 (filed February 24, 2017, before closing); PharMerica filed a
  Form 15-12B on December 18, 2017 terminating its Exchange Act registration, and no
  subsequent Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed under that CIK — there is no
  audited standalone public record for PharMerica Corporation itself for the ~2020-2022
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001388195-17-000004, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-17-046543, Form 15-12B]
- The corporate entity that had been PharMerica Corporation continued to exist and, on
  March 5, 2019, expanded through the acquisition of BrightSpring Health Holdings Corp
  ("BHS Acquisition"); the surviving entity was renamed BrightSpring Health Services,
  Inc. (CIK 0001865782). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1, Notes to
  Consolidated Financial Statements, "Description of Business"]
- BrightSpring Health Services, Inc. filed a Form S-1 registration statement (initial
  filing October 18, 2021, amended through January 14, 2022) proposing an IPO; this
  registration was withdrawn via a Form RW filed November 23, 2022, so no public listing
  was completed within the ~2020-2022 window. Because that S-1 contains SEC-reviewed
  audited consolidated financial statements covering fiscal years including 2020 and
  interim 2021, it is the best available public primary-source record touching the
  (by-then renamed) surviving entity within the outcome window, though it reports the
  combined post-2019 business rather than a standalone PharMerica segment. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-292092,
  Form RW]

## Realized business record within the window (S-1-sourced, FY2020 and H1 2021)
- The combined entity organized its business into two reportable segments, Provider
  Services and Pharmacy Solutions (the segment into which the former PharMerica
  institutional-pharmacy business was organized, alongside home infusion, specialty
  pharmacy, and other pharmacy lines). 2020 Pharmacy Solutions segment revenue was
  $3,635.9 million (65.2% of total revenue) and segment EBITDA was $275.5 million
  (52.3% of total Segment EBITDA). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1,
  "Business"; "Prospectus Summary"]
- Total revenue grew from $2,536.1 million (2018) to $5,580.4 million (2020), attributed
  largely to acquisitions (the BHS Acquisition alone contributed $1,915.1 million); net
  income (loss) moved from $(7.6) million (2018) to $21.2 million (2020), and Adjusted
  EBITDA grew from $163.8 million to $412.0 million over the same period. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1, "Prospectus Summary"]
- The company reported a script-volume reduction of approximately 2.7 million scripts in
  2020 versus pre-pandemic early-2020 levels, with an estimated $134.3 million reduction
  in Pharmacy Solutions segment revenue attributed to COVID-19, and a continued
  reduction of approximately 1.9 million scripts (an estimated $94.4 million revenue
  impact) for the six months ended June 30, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1, "Risk Factors"]
- The annual goodwill impairment test as of October 1, 2020 concluded the fair value of
  each reporting unit exceeded its carrying amount, and no intangible impairment was
  recorded during 2020, 2019, or 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form
  S-1, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements, "Goodwill and Other Intangible
  Assets"]
- In May 2015 (pre-transaction), PharMerica had entered into a five-year Corporate
  Integrity Agreement with the HHS Office of Inspector General and a Memorandum of
  Agreement with the DEA settling alleged Controlled Substances Act and False Claims Act
  matters; on December 18, 2020, PharMerica received a letter from the OIG confirming
  completion of the five-year CIA term without a stated adverse finding. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements,
  "Commitments and Contingencies--Regulatory Matters"]
- Bob Dries, who had served as PharMerica's Executive Vice President and Chief Financial
  Officer since January 2017, was serving as President of PharMerica and Executive Vice
  President of BrightSpring Health Services as of the S-1 filing, a role he had held
  since March 2019; in December 2020, the Board approved additional cash bonuses tied to
  the successful completion of the integration of BrightSpring and PharMerica following
  the BHS Acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1, "Management";
  "Executive Compensation"]

## Beyond the ~2020-2022 window (noted for completeness, not part of the outcome record)
- BrightSpring Health Services, Inc. subsequently completed a public listing: its common
  stock ("BTSG") and 6.75% Tangible Equity Units ("BTSGU") are registered and trading on
  the Nasdaq Stock Market as reported in its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2023, filed March
  6, 2024 — outside the fixed ~2020-2022 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000950170-24-027316, Form 10-K, cover page]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- PharMerica Corporation itself deregistered from SEC reporting at closing and never
  again filed as a standalone public reporting entity; within the ~2020-2022 window the
  only public primary-source financial record touching the business is a withdrawn IPO
  registration statement (S-1, October 2021-January 2022; withdrawn November 2022) filed
  by BrightSpring Health Services, Inc., the renamed successor to the PharMerica legal
  entity following a March 2019 acquisition that combined it with BrightSpring Health
  Holdings Corp. That filing shows a combined business (PharMerica's operations folded
  into a "Pharmacy Solutions" segment) that grew revenue and Adjusted EBITDA from 2018
  to 2020, recorded no goodwill or intangible impairment through 2020, completed a
  pre-existing five-year regulatory integrity agreement without a stated adverse finding
  in December 2020, retained PharMerica's former CFO as president of the PharMerica
  business line, and was still incentivizing completion of the BrightSpring/PharMerica
  integration as of December 2020 — but it does not provide a standalone, audited record
  isolated to the pre-2019 PharMerica business, and no independent 3-5-year public
  outcome record exists for PharMerica Corporation as it was structured at the 2017
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-21-301155, Form S-1] [SRC: SEC accession
  0001140361-17-046543, Form 15-12B]
