# Structural sub-dossier — Cole Credit Property Trust II combination with Spirit Realty Capital (closing structure)

## The registrant and its operating structure entering the transaction
- The registrant is Cole Credit Property Trust II, Inc. ("the Company"), a Maryland
  corporation that elected to be taxed as a real estate investment trust; it commenced
  principal operations in 2005 and its common stock was not listed on a national exchange.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Company had "no paid employees and are externally advised and managed by Cole
  Advisors II, our advisor"; pursuant to an advisory agreement, Cole REIT Advisors II, LLC
  (an affiliate of the sponsor, Cole Real Estate Investments) was "responsible for
  managing our affairs on a day-to-day basis and for identifying and making acquisitions
  and investments on our behalf." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Company held its assets through an operating partnership, Cole OP II, of which the
  Company was sole general partner owning a 99.99% interest, with Cole Advisors II as sole
  limited partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Securities were sold through an affiliated dealer manager, Cole Capital Corporation, an
  affiliate of the advisor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## The asset that would combine at closing
- As of December 31, 2012 the Company owned 752 properties comprising 21.2 million
  rentable square feet in 45 states and the U.S. Virgin Islands, which were 96% leased with
  a weighted average remaining lease term of 9.8 years; the portfolio consisted primarily
  of freestanding, single-tenant retail and commercial properties net leased to investment
  grade and other creditworthy tenants, plus 69 mortgage notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Many properties were leased to single tenants of large national retail chains or
  franchises; the largest tenant exposures included Walgreens (70 leases, 9% of rental
  revenue), Church's Chicken, Academy Sports, Circle K (83 leases), CVS, and Home Depot.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## The transaction as structured
- On January 22, 2013 the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Spirit
  Realty Capital, Inc. ("Spirit"), a publicly-listed REIT; the agreement provides for "the
  merger of Spirit with and into the Company, with the Company as the surviving
  corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Upon consummation, Spirit stockholders would receive 1.9048 Company shares for each
  Spirit share (an inverse exchange ratio of 0.525 Spirit shares per Company share), and
  each Company share would remain outstanding in the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Upon closing the combined company would list its shares on the New York Stock Exchange
  and trade under Spirit's existing ticker "SRC." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## What would run the combined entity — control and management at closing
- The board of the combined company "will consist of all seven of Spirit's board members
  combined with two board members designated by the Company," and "Spirit's management will
  oversee the ongoing operations of the combined Company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Company's own management function had been supplied entirely by the affiliated
  external advisor (Cole Advisors II); that advisor was not designated to run the combined
  company. Instead, "after the Merger is consummated, Cole Advisors II and Spirit may enter
  into a transition services agreement whereby Cole Advisors II would provide property and
  asset management services on Spirit's behalf or for Spirit for amounts agreed upon by the
  parties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Deal-protection terms provided that, under certain circumstances, the Company would pay
  Spirit a termination fee of $55.0 million and/or up to $10.0 million in expense
  reimbursement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined the Company's single-tenant net-lease property portfolio and
  operating partnership into a surviving corporate vehicle, but the operating/management
  function that had historically produced and delivered that portfolio (day-to-day
  management, acquisitions, and property/asset management performed by the affiliated
  external advisor) did NOT carry over as the combined company's management: the
  counterparty's directors formed the majority of the combined board and the counterparty's
  management was designated to run operations, with the prior external advisor's role only
  optionally bridged by a possible post-closing transition services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001308606-13-000004, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
