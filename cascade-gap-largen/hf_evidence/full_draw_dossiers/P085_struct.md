## Note on the drawn filing
- The accession drawn for this case, a Form 8-K dated July 22, 2011, is a routine
  quarterly disclosure: Item 2.02 furnishes Verizon's second-quarter 2011 earnings
  release and financial tables, and Item 5.02 reports the Board's acceptance of Ivan G.
  Seidenberg's resignation as CEO and the appointment of Lowell C. McAdam as President
  and CEO effective August 1, 2011; it contains no deal-terms disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-194187, Form 8-K Items 2.02/5.02/9.01]
- The earnings-release exhibit's standard company description states, in boilerplate
  form, that "Verizon Wireless is a joint venture of Verizon Communications ... and
  Vodafone." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-194187, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Per the sourcing protocol for 8-K-drawn joint-venture cases, the closing structure
  below is drawn instead from the registrant's contemporaneous closing-era annual
  report (Form 10-K for fiscal year 2011).

## Parties, legal vehicle, and ownership split
- The registrant's Verizon Wireless segment is primarily comprised of Cellco
  Partnership, doing business as Verizon Wireless, a joint venture formed in April 2000
  by the combination of the U.S. wireless operations and interests of Verizon and
  Vodafone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011) Exhibit 13]
- Verizon owns a controlling 55% interest in Verizon Wireless and Vodafone owns the
  remaining 45%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011) Exhibit 13]
- Because Verizon holds the controlling interest, all financial results of Verizon
  Wireless are consolidated into Verizon's financial statements, with Vodafone's 45%
  interest carried as a noncontrolling interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011) Exhibit 13]
- Governance of the partnership is exercised through a Board of Representatives, which
  in July 2011 declared a distribution to the partnership's owners in proportion to
  their partnership interests, payable January 31, 2012 in the aggregate amount of $10
  billion; on payment, Vodafone Group Plc received $4.5 billion (its 45% share) and the
  remainder was received by Verizon. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011) Exhibit 13]

## Tier-level: product, operations, and organization
- Verizon Wireless is operated and reported as one of Verizon's two reportable
  segments (Verizon Wireless and Wireline), organized by products and services rather
  than as a separately managed external entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011), Item 7]
- The customer-facing product — voice, data, and equipment sales delivered over the
  wireless network under the Verizon Wireless brand — and the operating/management
  substrate that produces and delivers it are both folded into Verizon's own segment
  structure rather than run as an arm's-length affiliate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011), Item 7]

## Contemporaneous joint-venture-related transactions (same closing era)
- In December 2011, Verizon entered agreements to acquire Advanced Wireless Services
  (AWS) spectrum licenses held by SpectrumCo, LLC (itself "a joint venture between
  Comcast Corporation, Time Warner Cable, and Bright House Networks") and Cox TMI
  Wireless, for an aggregate value of approximately $3.9 billion, subject to FCC and
  DOJ approval and expected to close in 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011), Item 7; SEC accession 0001193125-11-328252, Form 8-K Exhibit 99 press release dated December 2, 2011]
- Concurrently, Verizon Wireless and the cable companies (Comcast, Time Warner Cable,
  Bright House Networks, and Cox Communications) entered commercial agreements making
  each other's products sales agents, with an option over time for the cable companies
  to resell Verizon Wireless service wholesale; separately, the cable companies (other
  than Cox) and Verizon Wireless formed a technology-innovation joint venture "for the
  development of technology and intellectual property to better integrate wireline and
  wireless products and services," which as of the FY2011 10-K filing remained under
  DOJ review. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011), Item 7]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The subject entity, Cellco Partnership d/b/a Verizon Wireless, is a joint venture in
  which Verizon holds a controlling 55% interest and consolidates the entity, with
  Vodafone's 45% carried as a noncontrolling interest and governed through a Board of
  Representatives that allocates distributions pro rata to ownership share; the
  customer-facing product and its operating/management substrate sit inside Verizon's
  own segment structure rather than at arm's length. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011) Exhibit 13]
- In the same closing-era window, a separate spectrum-and-commercial-agreement
  transaction with the cable-company-owned SpectrumCo joint venture, plus formation of a
  distinct technology-innovation joint venture between Verizon Wireless and most of
  those cable companies, was pending regulatory review. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-328252, Form 8-K Exhibit 99; SEC accession 0001193125-12-077846, Form 10-K (FY2011), Item 7]
