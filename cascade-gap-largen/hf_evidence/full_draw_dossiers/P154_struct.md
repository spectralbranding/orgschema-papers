# Structural sub-dossier — Molycorp, Inc. Chapter 11 plan of reorganization and Mountain Pass asset sale (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Molycorp, Inc. and certain of its domestic and foreign subsidiaries (collectively the "Debtors") filed voluntary Chapter 11 petitions in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on June 25, 2015, styled In re Molycorp, Inc., et al., Case No. 15-11357. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Debtors' proposed plan contemplated two alternative structures: (1) a sale of substantially all assets including the downstream business units — Chemicals & Oxides, Magnetic Materials & Alloys, and Rare Metals (the "Downstream Businesses") — with net proceeds distributed to creditors; or (2) if the sale attracted insufficient bids, a stand-alone reorganization built around the Downstream Businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The plan also provided for the sale of assets of the "Molycorp Minerals Debtors" (Molycorp Minerals, LLC; PP IV Mountain Pass, Inc.; PP IV Mountain Pass II, Inc.; RCF IV Speedwagon Inc.; Industrial Minerals, LLC; Molycorp Advanced Water Technologies, LLC) related to the Mountain Pass rare-earth mining and processing facility in San Bernardino County, California. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Which alternative closed, and how the assets were split at the boundary
- After the marketing process did not produce more than one qualified bid for any asset group by the February 26, 2016 bid deadline, the whole-company Sale did not proceed; the remaining "Plan Debtors" instead pursued the stand-alone reorganization plus a separate sale of certain Molycorp Minerals assets under a credit-bid asset purchase agreement submitted by an ad hoc group of holders of the 10% Senior Secured Notes ("Ad Hoc 10% Noteholders"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Under the stand-alone reorganization, on the plan's effective date Molycorp, Inc. was to be dissolved and a newly created entity would serve as the new parent for the Downstream Businesses, issuing new equity distributed 92.5% to OCM MLYCo CTB Ltd. ("Oaktree", the prepetition senior secured and postpetition lender) and 7.5% to holders of general unsecured claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- All existing equity interests in Molycorp, Inc. (common and preferred stock, options, warrants) were to be cancelled on the effective date with no distribution to equity holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 3.03]

## What crossed the boundary versus what did not (tier detail)
- The Downstream Businesses (Chemicals & Oxides, Magnetic Materials & Alloys, Rare Metals) crossed into the new reorganized parent as a continuing going concern; throughout the cases the Company and 14 other Plan Debtors "continued in possession of their properties and managed their businesses, as debtors-in-possession," indicating the operating processes and management of the downstream units moved with them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000120, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Mountain Pass credit-bid sale transferred to Secure Natural Resources LLC (an entity formed by the Ad Hoc 10% Noteholders) the subsurface mineral rights ("Specified Mineral Properties") at the Mountain Pass facility, the water-treatment intellectual property and associated goodwill (including the SorbX and PhosFIX trademarks and specified process patents), related contracts, and certain cash collateral. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The physical Mountain Pass mining and processing facility itself was held by the Molycorp Minerals Debtors (principally Molycorp Minerals, LLC), whose cases were withdrawn from the plan and severed; the mineral-rights and process-IP thus crossed the ownership boundary to the buyer without the producing/processing plant and its associated operating entity moving alongside them. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- On April 8, 2016 the Molycorp Minerals Debtors moved to convert their cases to Chapter 7; on April 13, 2016 the court directed appointment of a Chapter 11 trustee (Paul E. Harner, approved May 2, 2016), and their cases were severed and separately administered as In re Molycorp Minerals, LLC, et al., Case No. 15-11371 — separating the mine-holding entities and their personnel from the reorganized downstream group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Intercompany transfers and continuing operating substrate at closing
- Pursuant to the 10% Noteholder Group Settlement, certain "Downstream Transferred Assets" — the equity interests in non-debtor Molycorp Silmet AS, an intercompany promissory note and loan agreement, intellectual property other than the purchased IP, and scheduled executory contracts and leases — were transferred from the Molycorp Minerals Debtors to the Plan Debtors; these transfers were completed on April 27, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Numerous operating subsidiaries outside the U.S. (in Hong Kong, China, Thailand, Japan, Korea, Germany, the United Kingdom, Estonia, and Singapore) and a majority-owned joint venture in Quapaw, Oklahoma were not debtors in the cases and continued operating outside the bankruptcy estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000120, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Consummation
- The credit-bid Mountain Pass asset sale to Secure Natural Resources LLC was approved by the Sale Order on April 5, 2016 and closed on April 15, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered the Confirmation Order approving the Fourth Amended Joint Plan of Reorganization on April 8, 2016 (with technical modifications approved May 2, 2016), and the Plan Debtors emerged from Chapter 11 with an effective date of August 31, 2016 — the stand-alone reorganization and the asset sale both consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000120, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A court-supervised Chapter 11 reorganization split a rare-earth enterprise at the ownership boundary: the downstream processing businesses were reorganized as a going concern under a new lender-owned parent (Oaktree 92.5%) with management and operations continuing, while the upstream Mountain Pass mineral rights and water-treatment process IP were credit-bid to a separate newly formed buyer, leaving the physical mine facility and its holding entity behind under a Chapter 11 trustee; existing Molycorp, Inc. equity was cancelled and the company dissolved on the August 31, 2016 effective date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-120339, Form 8-K Item 1.03] [SRC: SEC accession 0001489137-16-000120, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
