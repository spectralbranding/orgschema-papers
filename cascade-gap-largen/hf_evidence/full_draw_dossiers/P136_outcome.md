# Outcome sub-dossier — Walter Energy, Inc. realized record after the asset sale (~3-5-year horizon)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at construction as the
> ~3-5 years after the March 31, 2016 closing (roughly 2019-2021). This sub-dossier
> describes ONLY the realized post-closing record; it contains no information about how the
> transaction was structured at closing.

## Realized terminal state of the registrant immediately after closing
- Following consummation of the asset sale on March 31, 2016, the registrant reported that
  "the Company and the other Debtors have no further material business operations" and that
  "the Debtors are evaluating their options with respect to wind down of their remaining
  assets." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The closing press release stated that with the close the Company had "transferred
  ownership of virtually all of its U.S. operating assets to third parties," having earlier
  in 2016 also sold its non-core U.S. assets to affiliates of ERP Compliant Fuels, LLC and
  Virginia Conservation Legacy Fund, Inc. (Seminole Coal Resources, LLC; ERP Compliant
  Coke, LLC; ERP Environmental Fund, Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The registrant's only remaining asset described as of the closing was Walter Energy
  Canada Holdings, Inc., a subsidiary that on December 7, 2015 obtained creditor protection
  under the Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act via an Initial Order of the Supreme Court
  of British Columbia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Realized organizational dissolution (post-close)
- Effective upon the March 31, 2016 closing, every director other than William G. Harvey
  resigned from the board (Michael T. Tokarz, Jerry W. Kolb, Patrick A. Kriegshauser,
  Joseph B. Leonard, Bernard G. Rethore, Walter J. Scheller III, A.J. Wagner, and Mary R.
  Henderson). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Effective upon closing, every officer other than Mr. Harvey resigned from all positions,
  including Chief Executive Officer Walter J. Scheller III, EVP/General Counsel Earl H.
  Doppelt, SVP/Chief Commercial Officer Michael T. Madden, Chief Accounting Officer Brian
  M. Chopin, and Jim Walter Resources President Richard A. Donnelly; the by-laws were
  amended to require a minimum of one director rather than five. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Items 5.02 and 5.03]

## Realized public-reporting status over the outcome window
- The registrant's last filing on EDGAR is the April 1, 2016 Form 8-K reporting the sale's
  completion; no annual report (Form 10-K), quarterly report, or Form 15 deregistration was
  filed thereafter, and the registrant's last-filed Form 10-K covered fiscal year 2014.
  [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index, CIK 0000837173, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000837173&type=&dateb=&owner=include&count=100]
- The common stock traded on OTC Pink under the symbol WLTG at the time of the closing
  (having ceased trading on the NYSE), and holders of the Company's common stock were
  "contemplated not to receive any distribution or other favorable treatment within the
  Chapter 11 Cases." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-16-002829, Form 8-K forward-looking-statements section]

## No separate 3-5-year record for the fixed outcome window
- Because the registrant ceased all SEC reporting after the April 1, 2016 completion filing
  and had no further material business operations, there is NO public SEC periodic record
  covering the fixed ~2019-2021 outcome window; the realized record is limited to the
  immediate post-close terminal state (no operations; wind-down of remaining assets). Any
  operating trajectory of the acquired Alabama assets thereafter would be that of the buyer
  (Warrior Met Coal, LLC), a separate entity, and is not the seller registrant's record and
  is not inferred here. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant's realized post-closing record is terminal: immediately after the March
  31, 2016 sale it had no further material business operations, was winding down its
  remaining assets, saw its entire board and officer corps depart save one, and ceased all
  SEC reporting; common equity was contemplated to receive no distribution. No separate
  audited or periodic public record exists for the fixed ~2019-2021 outcome window, so the
  far-window record is uncertain (no public record). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-109130, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.02/5.03, EX-99.1]
