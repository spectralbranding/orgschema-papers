# Structural sub-dossier — EnLink Midstream Partners, LP merger into EnLink Midstream, LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Pre-transaction relationship of the parties
- Before the transaction, EnLink Midstream, LLC ("ENLC") already held an indirect
  controlling position in EnLink Midstream Partners, LP ("ENLK"): as of December 4, 2018
  ENLC held 88,528,451 ENLK common units (a 21.5% limited partner interest) and, through
  EnLink Midstream GP, LLC ("EGP", an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary of ENLC), a 0.4%
  general partner interest in ENLK plus all of ENLK's outstanding Incentive Distribution
  Rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- On July 18, 2018, subsidiaries of Devon Energy Corporation sold all of their equity
  interests in ENLK, ENLC, and EnLink Midstream Manager, LLC ("EMM", the managing member
  of ENLC) to affiliates of Global Infrastructure Partners ("GIP Stetson"); through that
  prior transaction GIP Stetson acquired control of EMM, ENLC, and (via ENLC's indirect
  ownership of EGP) ENLK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]

## Transaction as structured
- On October 21, 2018, ENLC, EMM, NOLA Merger Sub, LLC (a wholly-owned ENLC subsidiary
  formed solely to effect the transaction), ENLK, and EGP entered an Agreement and Plan
  of Merger under which Merger Sub would merge with and into ENLK, with ENLK surviving as
  a subsidiary of ENLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Each outstanding ENLK common unit not already held by ENLC or its subsidiaries (the
  "ENLK Public Units") would convert into the right to receive 1.15 ENLC common units, an
  all-unit (non-cash) exchange; the transaction is described in the filing's negotiation
  history as an "ENLC Simplification" pursued to "streamline and simplify" the corporate
  structure and to eliminate one of the two public companies EnLink maintained. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- Outstanding Series B preferred units (held by Enfield Holdings, L.P.) remained
  outstanding as ENLK limited-partner interests with modified, exchangeable terms rather
  than being converted in the Merger itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Effects of the Merger"]
- In connection with closing, the IDRs held by EGP were cancelled for no consideration.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Effects of the Merger"]
- The filing advised that ENLK common units would no longer be publicly traded following
  the Merger and would be removed from listing from the NYSE, while ENLC common units
  (including the newly issued ones) would continue trading on the NYSE under "ENLC". [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Questions and Answers"]

## What crossed at each tier
- Legal vehicle: ENLK, a Delaware limited partnership, continued to exist post-closing as
  a subsidiary of ENLC rather than being dissolved or merged out of existence; the merger
  subsidiary (not ENLK) was the entity that ceased to exist. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Controlling intent / management: because EGP (ENLK's general partner) and EMM (ENLC's
  managing member) were already common, affiliated entities before the Merger, the
  transaction did not introduce a new controlling party — separate ENLC and ENLK
  Conflicts Committees (advised respectively by Barclays and Evercore, each rendering a
  fairness opinion on the exchange ratio) were convened specifically because of this
  pre-existing affiliation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Recommendation of the ENLC Conflicts Committee..." / "Opinion of Barclays" / "Opinion of Evercore"]
- People / organization: effective at closing, all ENLK Board members other than Barry E.
  Davis (Executive Chairman of EGP) and Michael J. Garberding (President and CEO of EGP)
  were to resign; the executive officers of EGP and of EMM prior to the Merger were
  expected to continue in their capacities afterward, i.e. management continuity was
  built around the entities' shared pre-existing officer corps rather than a newly
  combined team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Effects of the Merger"]
- Equity awards: ENLK equity awards held by directors and officers of EGP/EMM would
  convert automatically into awards over ENLC common units on substantially the same
  terms, without new agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "Certain Relationships; Interests of Certain Persons in the Merger"]
- No transition-services or shared-services agreement was newly created to bridge
  corporate functions between the two entities; the filing's own disclosures do not
  identify one, consistent with ENLK's operations already being conducted by ENLK's
  Operating Partnership subsidiaries under EGP/EMM's pre-existing management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]

## Approval, closing, and post-closing governance mechanics
- Consummation required approval of the ENLK Merger Proposal by holders of a majority of
  ENLK Voting Units (common and Series B units voting together), and separately required
  ENLC unitholder approval of the related ENLC common unit issuance; Supporting Voting
  Unitholders holding approximately 58.8% of ENLK Voting Units had separately agreed by
  Support Agreements to vote in favor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The Merger became effective at 9:30 a.m. Central Time on January 25, 2019, when NOLA
  Merger Sub, LLC merged with and into ENLK, with ENLK surviving as a subsidiary of ENLC.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-003438, Form 8-K "Explanatory Note"]
- Effective with closing, seven members of the General Partner's board tendered
  resignations, the GP board was reduced from nine to three directors, and one new
  director (Alaina K. Brooks, an existing EGP executive officer) was appointed alongside
  continuing directors Davis and Garberding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-003438, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- ENLK, already indirectly controlled by ENLC through a common general partner/manager
  and a minority unit stake, was merged into an ENLC subsidiary in a unit-for-unit
  exchange that eliminated ENLK as a separately traded public entity while preserving it
  as an operating legal subsidiary; the pre-existing shared management and general
  partner structure carried forward without a new transition-services bridge, and the
  transaction was explicitly framed in negotiation as consolidating two affiliated public
  companies into one rather than combining two previously independent organizations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-007553, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-003438, Form 8-K]
