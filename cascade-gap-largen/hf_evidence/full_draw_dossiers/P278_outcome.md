## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)

- The last annual report (Form 10-K) filed under DST Systems, Inc.'s own CIK was for
  fiscal year 2017, filed February 28, 2018 — before closing. A Form 25-NSE
  (notification of removal from listing) was filed April 17, 2018, and a Form 15-12B
  (certification of termination of registration) was filed April 23, 2018; no further
  periodic report was filed under DST's CIK thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000714603-18-000013, DST Systems, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2017)] [SRC: SEC accession
  0000876661-18-000381, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-18-005048, Form
  15-12B]
- Consequently, no audited public financial statements exist for DST as a standalone
  reporting entity for the ~2021-2023 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000950103-18-005048, Form 15-12B]

## Acquirer's own segment/financial disclosure (feasibility-relevant)

- SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc.'s Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states that SS&C
  "operate[s] in one operating segment," with results reported by geography rather
  than by acquired business unit — no DST-specific revenue, income, or loss figure is
  disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002107, SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc.
  Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 19 — Segment and Geographic Information]
- The same Form 10-K states DST was tracked as a distinct goodwill "reporting unit" as
  of December 31, 2019 and through June 30, 2020; effective July 1, 2020, following "a
  change in our management structure," the reporting units became "our health
  business" and "the rest of our operations" — DST no longer appears as a distinct
  reporting unit from mid-2020 onward. No goodwill impairment was recorded as of
  December 31, 2021 or 2020 for SS&C's (consolidated) reporting units. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0000950170-22-002107, SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021),
  "Goodwill and Intangible Assets"]

## Documented legal/organizational continuity of the DST entity post-closing

- SS&C's FY2021 Form 10-K discloses that DST and named DST committees/former
  officers/directors (the "DST Defendants") remained active named parties in multi-year
  ERISA litigation and mass arbitration proceedings arising from DST's own 401(k) Plan,
  including a putative class action ("Ferguson"), related individual actions, and a
  U.S. Department of Labor action, all still pending as of the filing date; roughly 579
  individual arbitration demands had been filed by former/current DST employees through
  January 2021, and between October and December 2021 courts confirmed 177 arbitration
  awards and entered judgments against DST, which DST appealed. In Q3 2021 SS&C
  recorded a $43.4 million accrued liability tied to this litigation; a separate $27
  million proposed class settlement (agreed in principle mid-2020) remained unapproved
  as of the filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002107, SS&C Technologies
  Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021), Note 18 — Commitments and Contingencies]

## Financing and brand continuity

- The credit facilities SS&C entered on April 16, 2018 to fund the DST acquisition — a
  $5.046 billion tranche and a $1.8 billion tranche, both maturing April 16, 2025 —
  remained outstanding as of December 31, 2021 (a smaller, separate tranche had been
  fully repaid in 2019). "DST" and "DST SYSTEMS" continued to be listed among SS&C's
  registered trademarks as of the same filing, indicating continued commercial use of
  the name more than three years after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002107,
  SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Because DST terminated its SEC registration within days of the April 2018 closing,
  there is no separate public financial-performance record for DST for the
  ~2021-2023 window, and the acquirer reports as a single operating segment with no
  DST-specific figure disclosed — the financial outcome for the acquired business is
  not publicly ascertainable and is treated here as uncertain rather than inferred from
  SS&C's consolidated results. The traceable non-financial realized record shows the
  DST entity persisting as a legally distinct subsidiary that continued to be sued, to
  litigate, and to be separately named in the acquirer's contingencies disclosures more
  than three years after closing, its internal goodwill reporting-unit identity being
  folded into broader units roughly 27 months after closing, its brand name remaining
  in commercial/trademark use, and the acquisition-linked debt financing remaining
  largely outstanding through the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-002107,
  SS&C Technologies Holdings, Inc. Form 10-K (FY2021)]
