## Outcome window and public-reporting status (feasibility-relevant)
- Fixed outcome window: the realized ~2020–2022 record for Pattern Energy Group Inc. The
  registrant's last public annual report is the Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December
  31, 2019, filed March 2, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- On November 3, 2019, the Company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with
  Pacific US Inc. (an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Canada Pension Plan Investment
  Board / "CPP Investments") under which Parent agreed to acquire the Company for $26.75
  per share in an all-cash transaction; each share outstanding at the effective time
  would be converted into the right to receive $26.75 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- A special meeting of stockholders was scheduled for March 10, 2020 to consider the
  merger; a shareholder advisory firm recommended shareholders vote against the merger and
  certain stockholders publicly opposed it, while the board reiterated its recommendation
  to vote for it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The Company's common stock was subsequently delisted (Form 25-NSE filed March 16, 2020)
  and its Securities Exchange Act registration was terminated (Form 15-12B filed April 27,
  2020); no Form 10-K was filed thereafter, so there is no public audited annual record
  for the ~2020–2022 in-window period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-20-000122, Form 25-NSE] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-20-008115, Form 15-12B]

## Best-documented realized scalars (last public annual report, FY2019)
- Total revenue was $541 million for 2019, compared with $483 million (2018) and $411
  million (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The Company reported a net loss of $107 million for 2019; net loss attributable to
  Pattern Energy was $31 million for 2019 (compared with net income attributable of $142
  million in 2018 and a net loss of $18 million in 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Realized state of the co-investment and sponsor relationships
- The relationship with PSP Investments was described in the FY2019 report as an active
  "strategic joint venture" and strategic partnership that was intended to expand the
  Company's capital access and improve flexibility in managing capital requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The Company's proportionate share of total revenue increased $33 million (85%) in 2019
  versus the prior year, attributed to the sale of the Grady development project to a
  third party; several projects (including Meikle and MSM, the wind farms placed into the
  co-investment at inception) continued to be accounted for as unconsolidated investments.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Sponsor-relationship disruption: Pattern Energy Group LP (the "Pattern Development 1.0"
  sponsor) notified the Company of its intention to wind down operations in an orderly
  manner, indicating a change in the development-sponsor organization that had supplied
  the project pipeline. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The FY2019 report noted that uncertainty about the pending merger's effect on the
  Company's employees, suppliers, off-takers, and business partners could have an adverse
  effect on the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The best-documented realized record ends with the FY2019 Form 10-K: total revenue of
  $541 million and a net loss attributable to Pattern Energy of $31 million for 2019, an
  ongoing co-investment relationship with PSP Investments, and a development sponsor
  (Pattern Development 1.0) winding down. The Company was then acquired by CPP Investments
  at $26.75 per share in an all-cash go-private, delisted (March 2020) and deregistered
  (April 2020). Because no public annual report was filed for the fixed ~2020–2022 window,
  the in-window realized record is not publicly observable and the outcome is coded
  UNCERTAIN (no public in-window record). [SRC: SEC accession 0001561660-20-000027, Form 10-K (FY2019)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950103-20-008115, Form 15-12B]
