# Structural sub-dossier — Knight Transportation, Inc. combination with Swift Transportation Company (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Knight Transportation, Inc. (an Arizona corporation) and Swift Transportation
  Company (a Delaware corporation) agreed to combine pursuant to an Agreement and Plan
  of Merger dated April 9, 2017, among Swift, Bishop Merger Sub, Inc. (a direct wholly
  owned subsidiary of Swift), and Knight. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- The joint proxy statement/prospectus describes the transaction as one under which
  Knight and Swift "agreed to a combination transaction after which their respective
  businesses would be operated separately under a single combined company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was structured so that Merger Sub would merge with and into Knight, with
  Knight becoming a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Swift; immediately before the
  merger, Swift amended its certificate of incorporation to change its name to
  "Knight-Swift Transportation Holdings Inc.," the name of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- Each Knight share was converted into the right to receive one Class A share of the
  renamed Swift entity (the "combined company share"); separately, all outstanding
  Swift Class B shares converted into Class A shares and every Class A share of Swift
  was then combined via a reverse stock split into 0.720 of a share (the "share
  consolidation ratio"), producing a single class of combined-company stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- Based on outstanding share counts as of August 4, 2017, former Knight stockholders
  and former Swift stockholders were expected to hold approximately 46% and 54%,
  respectively, of the combined company's approximately 177,583,033 outstanding shares
  immediately after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on September 8, 2017 (the "Closing Date"); Merger Sub merged with
  and into Knight, with Knight continuing as the surviving corporation and a direct
  wholly owned subsidiary of Knight-Swift (f/k/a Swift). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]

## Legal vehicle and listing changes at closing
- The combined company's shares (the renamed Swift entity's continuing NYSE
  registration) continued to be listed on the NYSE and were expected to, and did, trade
  under the symbol "KNX," the ticker Knight shares had previously used. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]
- Upon closing, shares of Knight Common Stock ceased trading on the NYSE and Knight
  notified the NYSE to file a Form 25 notification of removal from listing; Knight
  stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate or suspend its own reporting
  obligations under the Exchange Act. Knight subsequently filed a Form 15-12B on
  September 21, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]; [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing list, CIK 0000929452, Form 15-12B, filed 2017-09-21]
- Knight's own board of directors was reconstituted at the effective time: six prior
  Knight directors (Michael Garnreiter, Kathryn L. Munro, Richard J. Lehmann, Richard C.
  Kraemer, Roberta Roberts Shank and Robert Synowicki Jr.) resigned, and Kevin P.
  Knight, Gary J. Knight and David A. Jackson became the board of the now-subsidiary
  Knight entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]
- Knight's articles of incorporation and bylaws were amended and restated in their
  entirety at the effective time pursuant to the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]

## Governance and management continuity at the combined-company level
- The merger agreement provided that, as of the effective time, Knight's then-current
  Executive Chairman and President/CEO would become the Executive Chairman and
  President/CEO, respectively, of the combined company (Swift/Knight-Swift). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, Kevin Knight was appointed Executive Chairman of the combined company's
  board and Gary Knight was appointed Vice Chairman; the executive team was led by
  Kevin Knight as Executive Chairman, David Jackson as President and Chief Executive
  Officer, and Adam Miller as Chief Financial Officer — the individuals who had held
  the equivalent roles at Knight. It was anticipated that Knight's senior leadership
  team would serve as the combined company's senior leadership team following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided that members of the Knight board of directors would
  constitute a substantial majority of the combined company's board of directors, and
  the combined company's charter and bylaws were structured to reflect predominantly
  the terms of Knight's own charter and bylaws (including a classified board). [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- Jerry Moyes, Swift's founder and former CEO, did not take an executive or board role
  in the combined company; under an amended and restated letter agreement effective at
  closing, he was to serve, from the effective time through at least December 31, 2019,
  as a non-executive consultant styled Senior Advisor to the Executive Chairman and
  Vice Chairman, continuing to receive $200,000 per month in that capacity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- Jerry Moyes, Vickie Moyes, the Jerry and Vickie Moyes Family Trust, and two of the
  Moyeses' adult children (collectively holding approximately 55% of Swift's voting
  power) entered a Swift Support Agreement obligating them to vote in favor of the
  transaction; at closing the Swift Supporting Stockholders were expected to hold
  approximately 24% of combined company shares, and a separate Swift Stockholders
  Agreement gave Jerry Moyes limited rights to designate up to two board nominees
  (reduced to one, and eventually to none, as the Supporting Stockholders' voting power
  declined below stated thresholds) plus standstill obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]

## Employee-benefit and indemnification continuity mechanism
- The merger agreement required the combined company to honor all contractual
  obligations under both the pre-existing Swift benefit plans and Knight benefit plans,
  and to credit each continuing employee's years of service with either predecessor
  company for eligibility and vesting purposes under the combined company's plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement required indemnification and exculpation rights of Knight's and
  Swift's pre-merger directors and officers to be assumed by the surviving corporation
  and preserved in the combined company's organizational documents for six years after
  the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined the two truckload/logistics businesses into a single
  NYSE-listed holding company (the renamed Swift entity, "Knight-Swift Transportation
  Holdings Inc.") via a stock-for-stock reverse triangular merger, with Knight
  continuing as a wholly owned operating subsidiary. Governance and senior management
  of the resulting combined company were staffed predominantly from Knight (Executive
  Chairman, CEO, CFO, and board majority all drawn from Knight), while the legal/public
  filer identity, NYSE listing continuity, and corporate name carried forward from
  Swift (subsequently renamed); Knight's own shares were removed from the NYSE and its
  separate SEC registration was terminated. Swift's founder was retained only in a
  time-limited, non-executive advisory role rather than an operating position, and
  employee-benefit/indemnification continuity for both predecessor workforces was
  bridged contractually at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007299, Form DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-17-047373, Form 8-K]
