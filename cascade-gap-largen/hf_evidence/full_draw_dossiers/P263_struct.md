# Structural sub-dossier — Delek US Holdings, Inc. combination with Alon USA Energy, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On January 2, 2017, Delek US Holdings, Inc. ("Delek"), Alon USA Energy, Inc.
  ("Alon"), Delek Holdco, Inc. ("HoldCo," a wholly owned subsidiary of Delek), Dione
  Mergeco, Inc. (a wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo), and Astro Mergeco, Inc. (a
  wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  providing for a combination of Delek and Alon. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A joint proxy statement/prospectus]
- The transaction was structured as two simultaneous mergers under a newly formed top
  holding company rather than a direct one-company acquisition: one merger sub was to
  merge into Delek, with Delek surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo, and
  the other merger sub was to merge into Alon, with Alon surviving as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of HoldCo. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "The Mergers"]
- Delek stockholders were to receive one share of HoldCo common stock ("New Delek
  common stock") for each Delek share held (a 1-for-1 exchange); Alon stockholders
  (other than Delek and its subsidiaries) were to receive a fixed 0.504 shares of New
  Delek common stock per Alon share, with cash paid in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A]
- Deal counsel discussed a reverse triangular tax structure intended in part to
  preserve Delek's existing U.S. federal income tax basis in Alon common stock that
  Delek had already acquired prior to the Mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Background of the Mergers"]

## Business lines of the parties at closing
- Delek described itself as an integrated downstream energy business focused on
  petroleum refining (refineries in Tyler, Texas and El Dorado, Arkansas) and the
  wholesale distribution of refined products; in November 2016 (before this
  transaction) Delek had sold its retail convenience-store assets, including MAPCO
  Express, Inc., to a subsidiary of Compania de Petroleos de Chile COPEC S.A. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Information About the Parties"]
- Alon described itself as an independent refiner and marketer of petroleum products
  operating primarily in the South Central, Southwestern, and Western United States;
  it held general- and majority limited-partner interests in a separately NYSE-listed
  partnership operating a Big Spring, Texas refinery, marketed asphalt through
  terminals, and operated roughly 300 convenience stores (as the largest 7-Eleven
  licensee in the country) marketing motor fuels in Central/West Texas and New Mexico. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Information About the Parties"]

## Legal vehicle and controlling-entity naming
- Upon completion, the Delek surviving entity was to be renamed "Delek US Energy,
  Inc." and continue as a wholly owned subsidiary of HoldCo; HoldCo was simultaneously
  to amend its certificate of incorporation to change its own name to "Delek US
  Holdings, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Deal-background disclosure records that Delek officers and representatives of
  Alon's Special Committee met at Delek's Brentwood, Tennessee headquarters during
  negotiations, and that the parties discussed that the headquarters of the combined
  enterprise would remain in Brentwood. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Background of the Mergers"]

## Listing status at closing
- The merger became effective on July 1, 2017; each share of "Delek US Holdings, Inc.
  (Old)" common stock was converted by operation of law into one share of "Delek US
  Holdings, Inc. (New)" common stock, and the New York Stock Exchange filed notice
  removing the old share class from listing and registration (trading in the old
  class suspended July 3, 2017; removal effective July 14, 2017), while the new
  common stock continued trading on the NYSE. [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-17-000379, Form 25-NSE notice filed by NYSE Regulation]
- The merger agreement separately obligated the parties to take actions necessary to
  cause Alon's own common stock to be removed from NYSE listing and to terminate
  Alon's registration and reporting obligations under the Exchange Act following
  completion; the combined enterprise's shares were expected to continue trading on
  the NYSE under Delek's existing ticker symbol "DK." [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Section 7.19 / "De-Listing and Deregistration of Alon Common Stock"]

## People / organization continuity provisions
- The merger agreement's Employee Matters covenant obligated HoldCo and the surviving
  entities, from and after the Alon merger's effective time, to recognize the
  applicable labor unions as exclusive bargaining representatives, continue honoring
  existing benefit plans and collective bargaining agreements, continue retiree
  medical benefits, and fully vest certain pension-plan participants upon an
  involuntary termination within a specified post-closing window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A Merger Agreement Section 7.14 "Employee Matters"]
- Deal-background disclosure identifies specific named Alon executives (its Chief
  Financial Officer, General Counsel, and a Senior Vice President of Supply and
  Logistics) as the designated points of contact for communications with Alon
  employees and for due-diligence coordination during the negotiation period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Background of the Mergers"]
- Outstanding unvested Alon restricted stock awards were to be automatically assumed
  by HoldCo and converted into New Delek restricted stock awards on substantially the
  same terms and vesting conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A]

## Disclosed integration risk (closing-time risk factor)
- The filing's risk factors disclosed that Delek was expected to incur substantial
  expense integrating "the business, policies, procedures, operations, technologies
  and systems" of Alon with those of Delek across a large number of systems
  (management information, purchasing, administrative, accounting and finance, sales,
  marketing, billing, payroll and benefits, installation, engineering, infrastructure,
  and regulatory compliance), and that the timing and total amount of integration
  expense was difficult to estimate precisely at the time of filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A "Risk Factors"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, both parties' operating businesses were placed under a newly formed
  top holding company via simultaneous mergers rather than one company's stock or
  assets moving directly to the other; Delek's stockholders retained their position
  at a 1-for-1 exchange while Alon's stockholders received a fixed, non-1:1 exchange
  ratio, Alon's own listed securities were slated for removal from NYSE listing and
  Exchange Act deregistration, and continuity of labor/benefit arrangements was
  addressed by an Employee Matters covenant rather than a separate transition-services
  agreement disclosed in this filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001351541-17-000155, DEFM14A]
