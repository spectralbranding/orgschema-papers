# Structural sub-dossier — Delphi Jersey Holdings plc separation of the Powertrain Systems business from Delphi Automotive PLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On May 3, 2017, Delphi Automotive PLC (the "Parent") announced its intention to separate
  its Powertrain Systems segment by means of a spin-off of a newly formed company expected
  to trade on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- Delphi Jersey Holdings plc ("DPS") is a Jersey corporation that, prior to the separation,
  was a wholly-owned subsidiary of the Parent; after the separation it will be an
  independent publicly traded company. The registration was made on Form 10. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transaction is structured as a pro-rata distribution: the Parent will distribute all
  of the ordinary shares of DPS to holders of the Parent's ordinary shares according to a
  stated distribution ratio (the ratio and record/distribution dates are left as blanks in
  this preliminary information statement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- DPS was organized for the purpose of holding the Parent's Powertrain Systems segment,
  "including the subsidiary entities, employees, operations, assets and liabilities
  associated with the Powertrain Systems segment"; internal reorganization transactions
  facilitate the transfers of those entities and related assets and liabilities from the
  Parent to DPS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The customer-facing product line crossing into DPS is a portfolio of powertrain
  technologies — advanced fuel injection systems, actuators, valvetrain products, sensors,
  electronic control modules and power electronics — sold to original equipment
  manufacturers, plus an Aftermarket line (engine control modules, pumps, injectors, fuel
  modules, ignition coils, and related parts) sold through its Products & Service Solutions
  segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The producing/delivering operations crossed with the product: at separation the business
  comprised 20 major manufacturing facilities, 12 major technical centers, and approximately
  5,000 scientists, engineers and technicians worldwide, operating in 24 countries on a
  regional service model. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Dedicated management was in place for the standalone company at separation: Liam
  Butterworth signs the information statement as President and Chief Executive Officer of
  DPS, distinct from Kevin P. Clark, President and Chief Executive Officer of the Parent.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Shared corporate substrate that did NOT transfer intact
- Functions historically performed by the Parent do not all move with the business:
  following the separation the Parent will provide some of these functions to DPS under a
  transition services agreement, and DPS "will need to make investments to replicate or
  outsource from other providers certain facilities, systems, infrastructure, and personnel"
  it previously drew from the Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Certain components will continue to be produced by the Parent and supplied to DPS rather
  than being produced inside the separated business at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- The parties will enter into a Separation and Distribution Agreement (effecting the
  separation and the principal corporate transactions), a Transition Services Agreement, a
  Tax Matters Agreement, an Employee Matters Agreement, and certain other agreements that
  govern the DPS-Parent relationship after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under contract manufacturing services agreements, the Parent will provide components to
  DPS for a period of up to 48 months; under the Transition Services Agreement the Parent
  will provide certain information technology, supply chain management, and other services
  after the separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction detaches an operating business — its product portfolio, its manufacturing
  and technical-center operations, and its employees and dedicated management — from the
  Parent as a standalone company, while the Parent's shared corporate substrate (IT, supply
  chain management, and certain manufactured components) is retained and bridged at closing
  by a transition services agreement, contract manufacturing services agreements (up to 48
  months), and related separation agreements rather than transferred whole. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-279255, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
