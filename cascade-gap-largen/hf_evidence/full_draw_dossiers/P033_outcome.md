# Outcome sub-dossier — AbbVie Inc. realized record, ~3-5-year horizon after the 2013 separation

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at construction on
> AbbVie Inc.'s own realized record, read through its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2017
> (filed February 16, 2018 — the fourth full year after the January 1, 2013 separation).
> This sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was structured at
> closing.

## Public-reporting status (feasibility)
- AbbVie Inc. remained an SEC-registrant standalone reporting company and filed an annual
  report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 cover page]
- AbbVie described itself as "a global, research-based biopharmaceutical company formed in
  2013 following separation from Abbott Laboratories," operating in one business
  segment — pharmaceutical products. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Item 1 Business]

## Best-documented realized scalar (five-year trajectory)
- FY2017 worldwide net revenues were $28,216 million, with net earnings of $5,309 million
  and diluted earnings per share of $3.30; operating earnings were $9.6 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Selected Financial Data / MD&A]
- The five-year statement-of-earnings series shows net revenues rising from $18,790
  million (2013) to $19,960 million (2014), $22,859 million (2015), $25,638 million
  (2016), and $28,216 million (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Selected Financial Data]
- Net earnings over the same series were $4,128 million (2013), $1,774 million (2014),
  $5,144 million (2015), $5,953 million (2016), and $5,309 million (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Selected Financial Data]

## Realized record bearing on the product / operations relationship
- The realized portfolio remained concentrated in a single product: HUMIRA, AbbVie's
  largest product, had worldwide net revenues of approximately $18.4 billion in 2017 and
  accounted for approximately 65% of AbbVie's total net revenues. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Item 1 / Risk Factors]
- The U.S. composition-of-matter patent for HUMIRA expired in December 2016, and the
  equivalent European Union patent was disclosed as nearing expiry, identifying a
  documented exposure of the lead product to loss of exclusivity within the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Risk Factors]
- AbbVie expanded operations by acquiring Pharmacyclics in May 2015 for approximately
  $20.8 billion (including $12.4 billion cash and equity consideration), adding to the
  standalone product and operating base. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 MD&A / Notes]

## Realized record bearing on the operations / organization relationship
- The organization remained at comparable scale to closing: AbbVie employed approximately
  29,000 persons as of January 31, 2018, versus the approximately 30,000 expected at the
  distribution date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 Item 1 Employees]
- Separation-from-Abbott costs continued to be recognized after closing: SG&A expenses in
  2015 included $265 million of costs associated with the separation from Abbott,
  indicating the standalone build-out extended beyond the closing year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017 MD&A]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2016-2018 window, AbbVie operated as an independent SEC-reporting
  company that grew net revenues from $18.8 billion (2013) to $28.2 billion (2017) while
  its realized product base stayed heavily concentrated in a single medicine (HUMIRA,
  ~65% of 2017 revenue) whose principal U.S. patent had already expired, and it carried
  post-closing separation costs and a bolt-on acquisition as it built out standalone
  operations at roughly its closing headcount. [SRC: SEC accession 0001551152-18-000014, Form 10-K FY2017]
