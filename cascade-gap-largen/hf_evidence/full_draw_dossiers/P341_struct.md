## Transaction and parties at closing
- National Energy Services Reunited Corp. ("NESR") was a blank check company incorporated in the British Virgin Islands on January 23, 2017 for the purpose of effecting a merger, capital stock exchange, asset acquisition, stock purchase, reorganization or similar business combination with one or more businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A "Information About NESR"]
- On November 12, 2017, NESR entered into two stock purchase agreements: a Stock Purchase Agreement for 100% of the equity interests of NPS Holdings Limited ("NPS," a company delivering drilling, customized well services and support to customers in the Middle East, North Africa and Far East Asia) and an Agreement for the Sale and Purchase of Shares in Gulf Energy S.A.O.C. ("GES," a provider of integrated oilfield service solutions in the Middle East and North Africa). [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter]
- GES equity was acquired in three tranches: 61% directly from GES Selling Stockholders under the GES Stock Purchase Agreement, 11.7% via a Shares Exchange Agreement with NESR's own sponsor (NESR Holdings Ltd.), and 27.3% via a Contribution Agreement with SV3 Holdings Pte Ltd. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Consideration for NPS included an aggregate of up to approximately 28.4 million NESR ordinary shares (valued at $10.00-$11.244/share to different sellers), total cash of $292.8 million, and contingent earn-out shares/cash; consideration for GES was an aggregate of approximately 28.2 million NESR ordinary shares at $10.00/share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The transactions (together, the "Business Combination") closed on June 6, 2018 ("Closing Date"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Explanatory Note"]

## Legal vehicle and accounting treatment
- NESR was the surviving/continuing SEC registrant and legal vehicle: as a result of the Business Combination the Company continued the operating businesses of NPS and GES as a publicly traded company under the existing name National Energy Services Reunited Corp., rather than through a newly formed merger subsidiary or name change. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Explanatory Note"]
- For accounting purposes, NESR was determined to be the accounting acquirer, but NPS was designated the accounting predecessor; the filing states that in future SEC filings the historical financial statements prior to the Business Combination would be replaced with the historical financial statements of NPS, with the combined company's results presented on a consolidated basis going forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Explanatory Note"]
- Prior to the Business Combination NESR was a "shell company" as defined under Exchange Act Rule 12b-2; as a result of the Business Combination it ceased to be a shell company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Item 5.06 Change in Shell Company Status"]

## What crossed at closing, by tier
- Product/asset: the customer-facing oilfield-services product lines and operations of NPS (well services, electric line logging, well testing, integrated drilling and work-over operations) and GES (well intervention, drilling technology solutions, fishing and remedial services, performance drilling and evaluation, work-over/hoist services) were the businesses acquired; NESR itself had "no current active business" prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A "Information About NPS" / "Information About GES"; DEFM14A p.1]
- Legal/organizational structure: the acquisitions were structured as direct stock/share purchases of the target companies' equity (not a merger-sub reverse-triangular merger into either target and not a corporate carve-out); the proxy's "Background of the Business Combination" section notes NESR had specifically avoided pursuing certain other prospective targets partly because "the complexity and uncertainty involved (such as a corporate carve-out) caused the opportunity to be too unlikely to warrant" pursuit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A "Background of the Business Combination"]
- Governance: the board was to expand from four to nine directors; all four incumbent NESR directors (Sherif Foda, Thomas Wood, Antonio J. Campo Mejia, Hala Zeibak) remained, and five new directors were added — two nominated by NPS Selling Stockholders, two by GES Selling Stockholders, and one by SV3. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A "Director Election Proposal" / Business Combination Proposal summary]
- Executive leadership: NESR's pre-existing CEO, Sherif Foda, continued as CEO and became Chairman of the board of the combined company; at closing NESR separately appointed a new Chief Financial Officer, Melissa Cougle (from Ensco plc), rather than retaining a CFO from either target company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter/"Management After the Business Combination"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Item 5.02"]
- People/organization at the target level: pre-existing executive employment agreements at the target companies (e.g., NPS's CEO Adnan Ghabris, employed since 2008 with a profit-sharing bonus arrangement) were disclosed as continuing arrangements rather than being renegotiated as part of the stock purchase. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A "Executive Employment Agreements"]

## Financing and post-close ownership
- NESR entered a Forward Purchase Agreement (April 27, 2018) with a Backstop Investor for up to $150 million of NESR ordinary shares, structured as a mandatory $70 million primary placement at closing plus an optional $80 million secondary placement, to offset shareholder redemptions and help fund the cash consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Assuming no additional redemptions, existing NESR shareholders (including the Sponsor and public shareholders) were projected to retain approximately 30% ownership of the combined company, the Selling Stockholders approximately 55%, and the Backstop Investor approximately 15% (assuming full draw of the Backstop Commitment). [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Separately, on June 5, 2018 NESR borrowed $50,000,000 from Hana Investments Co. WLL (an NPS selling-side party) under an unsecured Loan Agreement maturing December 17, 2018, prepayable in NESR shares or cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K "Item 1.01"]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A British Virgin Islands blank-check company with no operating business acquired 100% of the equity of two established, going-concern Middle East/North Africa oilfield-services companies (NPS and GES) via direct stock purchase agreements, funded with a mix of newly issued ordinary shares, cash, a backstop equity commitment, and a short-term unsecured loan from a selling-side investor; the SEC-registered legal shell continued under its existing name and CEO, while accounting predecessor status passed to one of the acquired operating companies (NPS) and a new CFO was brought in from outside either target at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-006365, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001493152-18-008542, Form 8-K]
