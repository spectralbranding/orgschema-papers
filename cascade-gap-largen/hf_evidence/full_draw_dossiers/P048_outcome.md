# Outcome sub-dossier — Columbia Sportswear Company / Swire China joint venture, realized record (fixed window 2015-2017)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized record
> after the venture commenced; it contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing. The outcome window is fixed at 2015-2017 (the joint venture
> commenced operations January 1, 2014). The venture is consolidated by the registrant,
> so its realized results are traced through the registrant's Forms 10-K.

## Realized existence and consolidation status
- The joint venture with Swire Resources Limited commenced operations effective January 1,
  2014; the Company owns a 60% controlling interest, and the venture's accounts and
  operations are included in the Company's consolidated financial statements for the years
  ended December 31, 2016, 2015 and 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016) Note 4]
- As of the FY2016 report the venture operated 92 retail locations plus brand-specific
  e-commerce websites, with distribution relationships with approximately 60 wholesale
  dealers operating approximately 750 retail locations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- As of the FY2017 report the venture sold through brand-specific e-commerce sites in
  China and had distribution relationships with approximately 50 wholesale dealers
  operating approximately 750 retail locations; it remained a 60% majority-owned,
  consolidated entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Best-documented realized scalar (venture profitability via non-controlling interest)
- Net income attributable to non-controlling interest (Swire's 40% share of the joint
  venture's net income) rose across the window: $4,686,000 (2014), $5,514,000 (2015),
  $6,541,000 (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016) Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- It continued rising to $7,192,000 for the year ended December 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017) Consolidated Statements of Operations]
- The non-controlling equity interest on the consolidated balance sheet grew from
  $16,013,000 (December 31, 2015) to $20,691,000 (December 31, 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- No goodwill impairment, asset write-down, divestiture, or dissolution of the venture is
  disclosed within the window; the put/call options for Swire's interest were not
  exercisable until January 1, 2019 (beyond this window) and no exercise is reported.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Realized record at the operating-substrate interface (transition wind-down)
- Upon commencement, the venture entered Transition Services Agreements ("TSAs") with
  Swire, under which Swire rendered administrative and information-technology services to
  the venture; fees valued at Swire's cost declined year over year: $8,638,000 (2014),
  $5,974,000 (2015), $3,294,000 (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016) Note 22]
- The TSA fees fell further to $1,006,000 for 2017, and the Company stated it "continues
  to reduce its costs under the TSAs as it internalizes the back-office functions and
  related personnel, including the transition of the joint venture's systems to the
  Company's platform in the second quarter of 2017." [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017) Note 21]
- Related-party funding: in 2014 both parties funded long-term loans to the venture; the
  Swire note (97,600,000 RMB, US $14,053,000 at December 31, 2016) matures December 31,
  2018 at 7% fixed interest, with interest expense of $1,053,000 (2014), $1,099,000
  (2015), and $1,041,000 (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016) Note 22]
- Interest expense on the Swire note fell to $429,000 for 2017; payables to Swire for
  service fees and interest declined from $1,472,000 (2015) to $707,000 (2016) to $89,000
  (2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017) Note 21]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed 2015-2017 window the joint venture remained in operation as a
  consolidated 60%-owned entity that grew in profitability (non-controlling-interest share
  of venture net income rising $5,514,000 → $6,541,000 → $7,192,000) and retained its
  China retail and e-commerce footprint, with no impairment, write-down, divestiture, or
  dissolution disclosed; over the same period the operating-substrate transition wound
  down as the Company internalized the venture's back-office functions, personnel, and IT
  systems (TSA fees falling from $5,974,000 in 2015 to $1,006,000 in 2017, with the
  systems migration to the Company's platform completed in Q2 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-17-000013, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001050797-18-000004, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
