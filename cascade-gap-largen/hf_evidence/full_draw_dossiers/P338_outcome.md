## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Capital Bank Financial ceased to exist as a separate legal entity effective November
  30, 2017 upon completion of the second-step merger into First Horizon, and requested
  removal of its Class A common stock from NASDAQ listing and deregistration of that
  stock under the Exchange Act (Form 15) as promptly as practicable; no subsequent
  Form 10-K was filed by Capital Bank Financial as an independent registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.01]
- First Horizon (CIK 36966) continued as the surviving, Exchange Act-reporting public
  company; its Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 (filed February 25, 2021, accession
  0000036966-21-000012) reports consolidated results only — it does not present a
  separate segment, business unit, or financial-statement line specific to the former
  Capital Bank Financial business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020]

## What First Horizon separately disclosed about the absorbed business
- First Horizon's FY2020 10-K states that "we completed systems integration for the
  Capital Bank transaction in 2018," identifying integration completion as a discrete,
  dated event but without a standalone post-integration performance figure for the
  acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 7 "Overview"]
- The same filing states that "[m]any year-end balance sheet figures (loans, deposits,
  etc.) increased substantially in 2017 due to the Capital Bank transaction" and that
  "[f]ull-year operating results were less noticeably impacted until 2018, because the
  Capital Bank transaction closed late in 2017," and separately that "net loan growth in
  2019 (vs. 2018) was purely organic," implying the acquired business's growth
  contribution was not separately tracked into 2019-2020 results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 7]
- First Horizon performed its required annual goodwill impairment test as of October 1,
  2020 and reported that the test "did not indicate impairment in any of FHN's reporting
  units as of the testing date," with no interim impairment test deemed necessary
  thereafter through the filing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, "Critical Accounting Estimates" / Note re: goodwill]
- During the fourth quarter of 2020, First Horizon reorganized its internal management
  and segment-reporting structure (reallocating goodwill to new reporting units using a
  relative fair value approach); the filing attributes this reorganization to First
  Horizon's broader management restructuring rather than to the Capital Bank Financial
  transaction specifically, and First Horizon's reportable segments as disclosed (e.g.,
  "Regional Banking") are not organized around the former Capital Bank Financial entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, "Business Segment Results" / Note 20]
- In 2020, First Horizon stated it "finalized IRS examinations for the FHN federal
  consolidated tax returns for 2013 through 2015 and for the Capital Bank Financial
  Corporation federal consolidated tax returns for 2010 through 2012," with no
  additional tax liability amount disclosed in this passage. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, "Income Taxes"]
- R. Eugene Taylor, Capital Bank Financial's former Chairman and CEO who joined First
  Horizon's board at closing under a new employment agreement, is identified in the
  FY2020 10-K's director listing (Item 11, by reference to the 2021 proxy statement) with
  the title "Retired Chairman of the Board and Chief Executive Officer, Capital Bank
  Financial Corp.," indicating continued board service into the outcome window under a
  "retired" designation for his prior operating role. [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020, Item 11 director listing]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Capital Bank Financial was absorbed whole into First Horizon and deregistered
  at closing, there is no independent public 3-5-year financial record for it. First
  Horizon's own FY2020 Form 10-K — the surviving registrant's filing within the outcome
  window — discloses that systems integration for the transaction was completed in
  2018, that the transaction's balance-sheet and operating impact was folded into
  consolidated results without a separately tracked post-2018 performance line, that
  First Horizon's October 2020 goodwill impairment test found no impairment in any
  reporting unit, and that a 2020 segment reorganization was tied to First Horizon's
  broader restructuring rather than to the former Capital Bank Financial business
  specifically. No separately disclosed financial-performance scalar for the acquired
  business exists in the reviewed filing; per the no-record convention, the outcome for
  this case is coded uncertain (no independent public outcome record). [SRC: SEC accession 0000930413-17-003919, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000036966-21-000012, Form 10-K FY2020]
