# Structural sub-dossier — CU Bancorp merger with and into PacWest Bancorp (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties

- On April 5, 2017, CU Bancorp ("CUB"), a California corporation, and PacWest Bancorp
  ("PacWest"), a Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under
  which CUB would merge with and into PacWest, with PacWest continuing as the surviving
  corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Immediately following that merger, California United Bank ("CU Bank"), CUB's wholly
  owned bank subsidiary, would merge with and into Pacific Western Bank, PacWest's wholly
  owned bank subsidiary, with Pacific Western Bank as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]
- The structure was a direct statutory merger of the target corporation into the
  acquirer (no separate merger-subsidiary vehicle was used at the holding-company level).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]

## Merger consideration and tax structure

- Each CUB common share (other than excluded and dissenting shares) would convert into
  the right to receive $12.00 in cash plus 0.5308 of a share of PacWest common stock (the
  "exchange ratio"), with cash paid for fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Outstanding CUB stock options would be cancelled for a cash amount tied to the same
  cash-plus-exchange-ratio formula; outstanding CUB restricted shares and RSUs would
  automatically accelerate and be converted into the merger consideration. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Treatment of CUB Stock Options" / "Treatment
  of CUB Restricted Shares and CUB RSUs"]
- The merger was structured, and completion was conditioned on legal opinions, to
  qualify as a reorganization within the meaning of Section 368(a) of the Internal
  Revenue Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]

## Regulatory and shareholder approval process

- Completion required regulatory approvals from the Federal Reserve Board, the Federal
  Deposit Insurance Corporation, and the California Department of Business Oversight
  (CDBO); CDBO approval was received June 27, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]
- Each CUB director, as a shareholder, entered into a voting agreement with PacWest
  agreeing to vote his or her CUB shares in favor of the merger proposal and against
  competing proposals. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Interests of
  CUB Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger"]
- The merger agreement provided for a termination fee of $26.5 million payable by CUB to
  PacWest under specified circumstances (e.g., a competing acquisition proposal). [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Termination Fee"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier

- Legal vehicle: CUB's separate corporate existence ended — it merged into and was
  absorbed by PacWest; California United Bank's separate bank-charter existence
  similarly ended, absorbed into Pacific Western Bank. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Directors/officers: the directors and officers of PacWest in place immediately before
  the effective time were to continue as the directors and officers of the surviving
  corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Board of Directors and
  Officers of PacWest After the Merger"]
- Consistent with that, upon closing CUB's own directors and executive officers ceased
  serving in those capacities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-063201, Form 8-K Item
  5.02]
- In lieu of continuing employment or board seats, PacWest separately entered into
  post-closing consulting agreements — effective upon and conditioned on closing — with
  three CUB executives (Chairman/CEO David I. Rainer, President K. Brian Horton, and CFO
  Karen A. Schoenbaum), each containing non-competition and non-solicitation
  restrictions and a fixed term (65 months for Mr. Rainer; two years for Mr. Horton and
  Ms. Schoenbaum). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "New Agreements with
  PacWest — Consulting Agreements"]
- CUB directors and officers retained continued indemnification and insurance coverage
  after closing; the merger agreement provided for maintenance of directors'-and-
  officers' liability insurance covering pre-closing acts (a "tail" policy) for six
  years, capped at 250% of CUB's then-current annual D&O premium. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A "Interests of CUB Directors and Executive Officers in
  the Merger" / merger agreement Section 5.11(c) text]
- Product/process (branch network): the proxy statement/prospectus disclosed that it was
  "currently contemplated" that certain CU Bank branches would be closed or consolidated
  into nearby Pacific Western Bank branches, and certain Pacific Western Bank branches
  would be closed or consolidated into nearby CU Bank branches — i.e., the combined
  branch/delivery network was to be reorganized rather than the acquired branch network
  simply continuing intact. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A risk
  factor "CUB will be subject to business uncertainties and contractual restrictions
  while the merger is pending"]

## Consummation

- The merger closed on October 20, 2017. Immediately thereafter, California United Bank
  merged into Pacific Western Bank as described above. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001104659-17-063201, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At closing, PacWest delivered approximately $224 million in cash and approximately 9.3
  million shares of PacWest common stock to former CUB shareholders, who as a group held
  approximately 7% of PacWest's outstanding common stock immediately after the merger
  (pre-merger PacWest holders retained approximately 93%). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001104659-17-063201, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- CUB notified NASDAQ that trading in its common shares should be halted before market
  open on October 23, 2017 and that the listing should be removed from NASDAQ, and stated
  its intent to file a Form 15 to deregister the shares and suspend its Exchange Act
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-063201, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- CU Bancorp was absorbed whole into PacWest Bancorp via a direct statutory merger (with
  a parallel bank-level merger of California United Bank into Pacific Western Bank),
  for cash-plus-stock consideration qualified as a tax-free reorganization; CUB's own
  board and executive-officer roles terminated at closing rather than continuing into
  the surviving corporation, with continuity of three senior CUB executives bridged only
  through fixed-term, non-employment consulting agreements, and continuity of the
  combined branch network handled through a disclosed plan to close/consolidate branches
  on both sides rather than retain both networks intact. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-004755, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-063201, Form 8-K]
