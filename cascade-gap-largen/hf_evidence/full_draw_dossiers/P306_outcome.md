## Public-reporting status over the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- SEC submissions records for CIK 1533932 show the registrant's name was "Vantiv, Inc."
  from November 9, 2011 until January 8, 2018, and "Worldpay, Inc." thereafter — i.e. the
  entity continued as the same public filer under a new name rather than deregistering at
  closing. [SRC: SEC EDGAR submissions API, CIK0001533932.json, formerNames field]
- The registrant filed exactly one Form 10-K covering a full fiscal year after the
  transaction closed: the fiscal-year-2018 Form 10-K, filed February 26, 2019. No
  fiscal-year-2019 or later Form 10-K was filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- On July 31, 2019, the registrant's Class A common stock was removed from listing and
  registration on the New York Stock Exchange via a Form 25-NSE filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-19-000798, Form 25-NSE]
- On August 12, 2019, the registrant filed a Form 15-12B certifying termination of its
  Exchange Act registration. That filing states that, effective July 31, 2019, pursuant to
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 17, 2019 among Fidelity National
  Information Services, Inc. ("FIS"), a merger subsidiary of FIS, and Worldpay, Inc.,
  "Worldpay merged with and into Merger Sub, with Worldpay continuing as the surviving
  corporation and a wholly-owned subsidiary of FIS," and that as of the filing date there
  were no remaining holders of record of Worldpay common stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-218602, Form 15-12B]
- Consequently, the registrant's independent public annual-report record after the
  transaction spans only fiscal year 2018 (roughly one year post-closing); it ceased
  independent public reporting in mid-to-late 2019, before reaching the 3-5-year mark
  after the ~2017 closing. No independent public 10-K/20-F exists for the entity for the
  later portion of the fixed 3-5-year outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-218602, Form 15-12B]

## Realized record for the one reported post-closing fiscal year (FY2018 Form 10-K)
- Net revenue "increased 85% to $3,925.4 million for the year ended December 31, 2018
  from $2,123.3 million for the year ended December 31, 2017. The prior year period
  excludes $1,498.1 million of Legacy Worldpay generated net revenue prior to our
  acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Management's Discussion and Analysis]
- Net income attributable to Worldpay, Inc. was $12.8 million for the year ended December
  31, 2018, compared with $130.1 million for the year ended December 31, 2017 and $213.2
  million for the year ended December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Selected Financial Data / Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- Goodwill on the consolidated balance sheet was $14,137.9 million at December 31, 2018,
  compared with $4,173.0 million at December 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Consolidated Balance Sheets]
- As of December 31, 2018, the company reported 8,186 employees globally, of whom 4,331
  were located in the U.S., comprising 754 in Technology Solutions, an unspecified count
  in Merchant Solutions, 85 in Issuer Solutions, 4,953 in IT and Operations, and 1,337 in
  general and administrative roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Business/Employees]

## Documented interface disruption over the reported period
- The FY2018 Form 10-K states: "The integration of the departments, systems, business
  units, operating procedures and information technologies of the two businesses continue
  to present certain challenges to management. There can be no assurance that [the
  company] will be able to integrate the operations of Worldpay Group plc successfully."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Risk Factors]
- The same filing discloses that the company "incurred integration planning costs during
  2017 and 2018" and "expect[s] to continue to incur additional operating expenses while
  [it] continue[s] to integrate the combined company," attributed to funding "significant
  capital and operating expenses to support the integration of the combined operations,"
  including "significant transaction, consulting and third party service fees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Risk Factors]
- The filing separately discloses that the company "may fail to realize the anticipated
  benefits and operating synergies expected from our acquisition of Worldpay Group plc,
  which could adversely affect our business, financial condition and operating results,"
  and that success "depends, in significant part, on our ability to successfully
  integrate the acquired business, grow our revenue and realize the anticipated strategic
  benefits and synergies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018), Risk Factors]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The registrant continued public reporting for one fiscal year after the transaction
  closed (FY2018), during which it reported net revenue growth to $3,925.4 million (up
  85% on a comparable basis) alongside a decline in net income attributable to the
  company to $12.8 million (from $130.1 million a year earlier) and a goodwill balance
  that grew to $14,137.9 million; the same filing disclosed ongoing, unresolved
  challenges in integrating the two businesses' departments, systems, operating
  procedures, and technologies, and warned it might not realize the acquisition's
  anticipated synergies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001533932-19-000044, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The registrant ceased independent public reporting in mid-2019 (NYSE listing removed
  July 31, 2019; Exchange Act registration terminated August 12, 2019, upon becoming a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of another public company), roughly a year and a half after the
  ~2017 closing — well short of the fixed 3-5-year outcome window — so no independent
  public annual-report record exists for the entity for the remainder of that window.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-218602, Form 15-12B]
