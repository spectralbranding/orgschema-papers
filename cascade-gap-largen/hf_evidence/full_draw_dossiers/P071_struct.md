# Structural sub-dossier — Piedmont Natural Gas entry into the Atlantic Coast Pipeline, LLC joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On September 2, 2014, Piedmont Natural Gas Company, Inc. (the "Company"), acting through
  Piedmont ACP Company, LLC ("Piedmont ACP"), an indirect wholly-owned subsidiary, entered
  into a Limited Liability Company Agreement of Atlantic Coast Pipeline, LLC (the "Joint
  Venture Agreement") with subsidiaries of Dominion Resources, Inc., Duke Energy Corporation
  and AGL Resources, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction was structured as the acquisition by Piedmont ACP of a 10% membership
  interest in Atlantic Coast Pipeline, LLC ("ACP"), a newly formed joint-venture entity.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- ACP's stated purpose is to construct, operate and maintain a 550-mile natural gas pipeline
  and related facilities from West Virginia, through Virginia and into eastern North Carolina
  (the "Pipeline"), with a target in-service date of late 2018, subject to receipt of
  regulatory approvals including approval of customer agreements.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, by tier
- Legal vehicle: a newly formed limited liability company (ACP); Piedmont's participation is
  held indirectly through a purpose-formed subsidiary, Piedmont ACP Company, LLC — i.e., a
  minority equity membership interest was created, not a whole business acquired.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Controlling intent / governance: Piedmont ACP's rights to distributions and to vote are
  generally proportionate to its 10% membership interest, subject to certain tiered voting
  thresholds keyed to the nature of the action being considered; the agreement contains
  representations, warranties and covenants customary for such transactions, including
  provisions preserving each member's and affiliate's rights to pursue certain
  Pipeline-related projects in their respective service territories.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Value-capture model / obligation: as a member, Piedmont ACP is obligated to contribute its
  proportional share of capital contributions to fund the estimated cost of constructing the
  Pipeline, then estimated at between $4.5 billion and $5 billion.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Customer-facing asset (the Pipeline): at closing the Pipeline did not yet exist; it was a
  to-be-constructed asset, and the interest acquired was a capital-contribution stake in the
  vehicle that would build and own it, not a delivered operating asset.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Allocation of the operating substrate (processes / organization)
- The operational processes — construction, operation and maintenance of the Pipeline — were
  not brought into Piedmont's own organization: ACP contracted with a subsidiary of Dominion
  Resources, Inc. to perform the construction, operation and maintenance. Piedmont's
  participation is thus a funding/ownership interest in an asset whose producing and
  delivering processes were assigned by contract to one member's affiliate rather than
  performed by Piedmont ACP itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Pre-existing commercial relationships among the members at closing: the Company provides
  natural gas transportation and redelivery services to affiliates of Duke Energy Corporation;
  the Company and an affiliate of AGL Resources, Inc. are members of SouthStar Energy Services
  LLC; and the Company purchases transportation and storage services from affiliates of
  Dominion Resources, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Form 8-K states that the summary does not purport to be a complete description of the
  Joint Venture Agreement and that a copy would be filed as an exhibit to the Company's annual
  report on Form 10-K for the year ending October 31, 2014.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing the Company acquired, through a purpose-formed indirect subsidiary, a 10% capital
  membership interest in a newly formed LLC whose to-be-built pipeline asset would be
  constructed, operated and maintained under contract by a different member's affiliate — so
  the funding/ownership stake was separated at closing from the processes and organization
  that would produce and run the asset, with governance and funding allocated proportionally
  by the LLC agreement and tiered voting thresholds.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001299933-14-001348, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
