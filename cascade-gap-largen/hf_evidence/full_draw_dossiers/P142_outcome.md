## Best-documented realized scalars (consolidated registrant record)
- For the year ended December 31, 2015, Quad/Graphics recorded pre-tax non-cash goodwill impairment charges of $808.3 million ($542.4 million net of tax). [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016 (Selected Financial Data / MD&A)]
- Net loss attributable to Quad/Graphics common shareholders was $(830.0) million for the year ended December 31, 2015; net earnings (loss) attributable to common shareholders from continuing operations was $(641.9) million in 2015 and $44.9 million in 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016 (Selected Financial Data)]
- Restructuring, impairment and transaction-related charges were $80.6 million in 2016 ($48.4 million net of tax) and $164.9 million in 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016]

## Documented restructuring, facility and workforce disruption over time
- The 2016 restructuring, impairment and transaction-related charges of $80.6 million included $12.9 million of employee-termination charges related to workforce reductions through facility consolidations and involuntary separation programs; $26.8 million of impairment charges (including $14.7 million for machinery and equipment and $12.1 million of land- and building-related impairment for the Atglen, Pennsylvania plant closure); and $2.2 million of transaction-related charges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016 (MD&A restructuring detail)]
- Restructuring actions initiated by the Company beginning in 2010 had resulted in the announcement of 37 plant closures and reduced headcount by approximately 11,100 employees through December 31, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016]
- The Company announced four additional plant closures in December 2016 to occur in the first quarter of 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016]
- As of December 31, 2016, within its United States Print and Related Services segment the Company operated 63 owned or leased manufacturing facilities (approximately 23,204,000 square feet), and within its International segment eight owned or leased manufacturing facilities (approximately 1,820,000 square feet); it had approximately 22,600 full-time-equivalent employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016 (Properties / Employees)]

## Realized-context note
- The registrant continued to describe the commercial-printing industry as characterized by significant overcapacity, downward pricing pressure and fluctuating demand, and its stated strategy included reducing excess manufacturing capacity through plant closures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016 (Business / risk factors)]
- The restructuring charges, plant closures and impairments above are reported on a Company-wide consolidated basis; the FY2016 Form 10-K does not attribute any specific portion of them to the acquired Vertis business, so no such attribution is made here. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 2015–2017 outcome window, the registrant Quad/Graphics posted a large loss year in 2015 — a $(830.0) million net loss attributable to common shareholders driven substantially by $808.3 million of pre-tax non-cash goodwill impairment — followed by a return to positive net earnings from continuing operations of $44.9 million in 2016, alongside continued restructuring ($80.6 million in 2016), further plant closures (37 announced since 2010 plus four more announced in December 2016 for Q1 2017) and cumulative headcount reduction of approximately 11,100 since 2010; the distinct trajectory of the acquired business is not separately disclosed and is coded uncertain. [SRC: SEC accession 0001481792-17-000006, Form 10-K for year ended December 31, 2016]
