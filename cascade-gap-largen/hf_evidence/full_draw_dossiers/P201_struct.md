# Structural sub-dossier — NCI Building Systems / Ply Gem Parent combination (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On the execution date, NCI Building Systems, Inc. ("NCI") entered into an Agreement
  and Plan of Merger with Ply Gem Parent, LLC ("Ply Gem"), and, for certain limited
  purposes, Clayton, Dubilier & Rice, LLC ("Sponsor"/"CD&R"), pursuant to which Ply Gem
  would merge with and into NCI. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- NCI is described as one of North America's largest integrated manufacturers and
  marketers of metal products for the nonresidential construction industry, with
  approximately 5,300 employees across 38 manufacturing locations in North America;
  NCI common stock traded on the NYSE under the symbol "NCS." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- Ply Gem is described as a privately held Delaware limited liability company in which
  the Sponsor Fund X Investor (CD&R) holds a controlling interest, and the parent
  company of a leading manufacturer of exterior building products (siding, fencing,
  stone, windows and doors) for U.S./Canada residential new-construction and
  repair-and-remodeling markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- Upon satisfaction or waiver of closing conditions, Ply Gem was to merge with and into
  NCI, with NCI continuing its existence as the surviving Delaware corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- The Special Shareholder Meeting approving the merger agreement and related share
  issuance was held November 15, 2018, and NCI reported that Ply Gem was merged with
  and into the Company effective November 16, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20 (event date 2018-11-15/16)]

## Consideration, ownership and governance
- The merger consideration to Ply Gem's equity holders was 58,709,067 newly issued
  shares of NCI common stock; outstanding NCI shares held before the merger remained
  outstanding after closing. Following consummation, NCI's pre-merger stockholders
  were estimated to hold approximately 53% of the surviving corporation's common
  stock and former Ply Gem equity holders approximately 47%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- NCI's certificate of incorporation was amended (authorized shares raised to
  200,000,000) alongside a new stockholders agreement and registration rights
  agreement among NCI, CD&R affiliates, and Golden Gate Private Equity affiliates (a
  minority Ply Gem holder). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- At the effective time the surviving corporation's board was to consist of 12
  directors — seven nominated by NCI and five nominated by the Sponsor Investors
  (CD&R) — and the Sponsor Investors retained consent rights over specified matters
  under the new stockholders agreement notwithstanding their minority equity position. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- On November 16, 2018 the Board approved the Sixth Amended and Restated By-Laws,
  which provide the CD&R Investor Group with certain governance rights under the new
  stockholders agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20]

## People / management at closing
- Effective upon consummation, the Company appointed James S. Metcalf — previously an
  NCI director — as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, under a new employment
  agreement with a three-year initial term. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20]
- Effective upon consummation, Donald R. Riley resigned as President and CEO of the
  Company and was appointed CEO, NCI Division and Head of Supply Chain & Technology,
  stepping down from the Board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20]
- The post-merger organization retained an "NCI Division" as a distinct reporting line
  under its own division CEO immediately upon closing, rather than an immediate single
  unified operating structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20]

## Stated rationale / expected integration benefits (as disclosed pre-closing)
- The NCI board and special committee cited a "Transformational Combination Creating a
  Large-Scale Public Company," expecting combined Adjusted EBITDA growth of greater
  than 20% between 2018 and 2019, high-teens annual growth beyond 2019, near-term G&A/
  procurement/manufacturing cost savings, and longer-term cross-selling opportunities
  across the combined product portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- Risk factors in the same filing separately flagged that realizing anticipated
  synergies and integrating the businesses (including retaining employees through the
  integration) was not assured. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- This was a whole-company stock-for-stock combination in which Ply Gem (a
  CD&R-controlled private company) merged into NCI (the NYSE-listed public company),
  which survived as the registrant; consideration was entirely newly issued NCI shares
  (no cash component to equity holders was identified in the merger terms), with the
  Sponsor retaining board seats and consent rights despite becoming a minority
  shareholder group. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
- At closing, management/organization was not fully unified: the former NCI CEO moved
  to head a retained "NCI Division" under the newly installed group Chairman/CEO,
  indicating the two businesses' operating structures were initially preserved as
  divisions rather than immediately merged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-061046, Form 8-K dated 2018-11-20]
- No transitional-services agreement or shared-services bridging arrangement between
  NCI and Ply Gem was identified in the reviewed closing-era filings; as a
  whole-company stock merger (not a carve-out), the full corporate substrate of each
  party combined at closing under the single surviving legal entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-054087, DEFM14A]
