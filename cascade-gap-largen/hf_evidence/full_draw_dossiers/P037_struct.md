# Structural sub-dossier — The Chemours Company separation from E. I. du Pont de Nemours and Company (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- The Chemours Company (Chemours) is a wholly owned subsidiary of E. I. du Pont de Nemours
  and Company (DuPont) that operates DuPont's Performance Chemicals segment, comprising its
  titanium technologies, fluoroproducts and chemical solutions businesses; DuPont was to
  distribute all outstanding Chemours common stock on a pro rata basis to DuPont's common
  stockholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The distribution ratio was one Chemours share for every five DuPont shares held on the
  record date, with the distribution expected to occur on July 1, 2015, after which Chemours
  would be an independent, publicly traded company intending to list on the New York Stock
  Exchange under the symbol "CC". [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The distribution was intended to be tax-free to DuPont shareholders for U.S. federal
  income tax purposes, subject to a private letter ruling from the IRS and opinions of tax
  counsel confirming tax-free treatment under Section 355 and related Code provisions.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Chemours was organized as a limited liability company under Delaware law and was converted
  to a Delaware corporation on April 30, 2015; the separation was registered on Form 10-12B.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing product operation crossed into the new company: three operating
  segments (Titanium Technologies, Fluoroproducts, Chemical Solutions) with a trademark
  portfolio including Ti-Pure and Vantage (titanium dioxide) and Suva, ISCEON, Freon, Opteon,
  Teflon, Tefzel, Viton, Krytox, Nafion and Capstone (fluoroproducts), plus Virkon and Oxone.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The operational process base crossed with the business: 37 production facilities in 12
  countries serving several thousand customers in more than 130 countries, together with
  proprietary process technologies (e.g. the chloride process for TiO2 production).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The people ran with the business: under the Employee Matters Agreement, as of the
  distribution Chemours' employees ceased active participation in DuPont's benefit plans and
  became eligible for Chemours plans, with pre-distribution service credited; Chemours would
  generally retain or assume wage, salary, welfare and incentive-compensation obligations for
  its current employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What did NOT transfer intact at closing
- Shared corporate functions historically provided by DuPont did not cross as an intact
  operating substrate: the information statement states the business "historically operated
  as part of DuPont's corporate organization" and that DuPont "performed significant
  corporate functions for us, including tax and treasury administration and certain
  governance functions, including internal audit and external reporting." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Following the separation the company would "need to provide internally or obtain from
  unaffiliated third parties the services we currently receive from DuPont," specifically
  "information technology, research and development, finance, legal, insurance, compliance
  and human resources activities," and noted that DuPont's IT networks and systems were
  "complex, and duplicating these networks and systems will be challenging." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Internal controls and procedures for standalone operation "have not yet been fully
  implemented," and the historical/pro forma financials reflected allocations of corporate
  expenses from DuPont rather than the company's own standalone cost base. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the separated shared substrate (closing-time)
- The parties intended to enter a Separation Agreement plus a Transition Services Agreement,
  an IT Transition Services Agreement, a Tax Matters Agreement, an Employee Matters
  Agreement, an Intellectual Property Cross-License Agreement, and certain manufacturing and
  supply arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Under the Transition Services Agreement and IT Transition Services Agreement, DuPont would
  provide functional and information-technology services to Chemours "for a limited time,
  generally for no longer than 24 months following the date of the distribution," for
  specified fees — at cost for third-party services and at cost plus five percent for
  services provided by Chemours or DuPont. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The Separation Agreement was to identify assets transferred, liabilities assumed and
  contracts assigned so that each party "retain[ed] the assets necessary to operate our
  respective businesses," while the Tax Matters Agreement and Employee Matters Agreement
  allocated tax and employee/benefit responsibilities between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction detached DuPont's Performance Chemicals operation — its products,
  production facilities/process technologies, and the employees who run them — into a newly
  formed standalone company by pro rata distribution, while the shared corporate/IT/finance/
  HR service substrate was not transferred whole and was instead bridged for up to 24 months
  by Transition Services and IT Transition Services Agreements plus related separation,
  tax, employee-matters, IP cross-license and supply arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-186063, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
