## Public-reporting status of the acquired entity after the transaction
- C. R. Bard, Inc. (CIK 9892) filed its final annual report on Form 10-K on 2017-02-13
  (for fiscal year 2016) and filed no subsequent Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-040742, Form 10-K]
- Bard terminated its SEC registration: it filed a Form 15-12B on 2018-01-08 to deregister
  its securities under the Exchange Act; consequently no separate audited public financial
  statements exist for the acquired entity in the fiscal 2019–2021 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-004979, Form 15-12B]

## Extent to which the acquirer separately discloses the acquired business
- BD reports results under three worldwide business segments — BD Medical, BD Life Sciences,
  and BD Interventional — rather than reporting a standalone "C. R. Bard" line; the acquired
  entity is not presented as a distinct reportable going concern in BD's annual reports.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- The BD Interventional segment is described as providing "vascular, urology, oncology and
  surgical specialty products" — the same product scope attributed to the acquired business
  — and BD explicitly attributes segment items to the acquisition (for example, "the
  expiration in 2019 of a royalty income stream acquired in the Bard transaction"),
  indicating the acquired product lines are housed principally within this segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]

## Realized trajectory observable through the acquirer's segment disclosure (fiscal 2019–2021)
- BD Interventional segment revenues were $3,926 million (fiscal 2019), $3,762 million
  (fiscal 2020), and $4,239 million (fiscal 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- BD Interventional segment operating income was $903 million (fiscal 2019), $724 million
  (fiscal 2020), and $933 million (fiscal 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- BD attributed the fiscal 2020 decline to pandemic-related declines in its Surgery and
  Peripheral Intervention units and to the expiration in 2019 of a royalty income stream
  acquired in the Bard transaction; it attributed the fiscal 2021 recovery to increased
  hospital utilization across all three units and new product offerings. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]

## Integration status and assumed obligations recorded in the outcome window
- BD recorded integration costs in fiscal 2020 and fiscal 2021 arising from the acquisition
  of Bard (completed in the first quarter of fiscal year 2018), and reported achievement of
  cost synergies resulting from the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- The legacy Bard U.S. defined-benefit pension plan was merged with the BD defined-benefit
  cash-balance pension plan in the first quarter of fiscal year 2021, producing a $24 million
  net remeasurement gain — an organizational-consolidation event within the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- BD carried restricted cash largely representing funds restricted for certain product-
  liability matters assumed in the acquisition of C. R. Bard, Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
- Product-liability matters assumed in the acquisition include claims relating to hernia
  repair implant products, surgical continence and pelvic-organ-prolapse products, and vena
  cava filter products; a multidistrict-litigation trial concerning the Hernia Product Claims
  began in August 2021 and resulted in a complete defense verdict. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

  periodic reports thereafter, so no standalone audited trajectory exists for it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-004979, Form 15-12B] The acquirer does separately
  disclose the acquired product lines within its BD Interventional segment, whose revenues
  moved $3,926M → $3,762M → $4,239M and operating income $903M → $724M → $933M across
  fiscal 2019–2021 (a pandemic-year dip and 2021 recovery), with integration costs and
  realized cost synergies recorded and assumed product-liability matters (hernia, surgical
  continence/pelvic-organ-prolapse, vena cava filters) carried forward; the legacy Bard
  pension plan was merged into BD's plan in fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K] Because the segment is BD's own
  reportable unit and is not represented as coextensive with the former entity, the acquired
  business's standalone trajectory is only partially observable; no independent going-concern
  outcome record for the former entity itself is public. [SRC: SEC accession 0000010795-21-000091, Form 10-K]
