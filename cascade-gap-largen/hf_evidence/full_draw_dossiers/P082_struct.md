## Parties and ownership structure (closing era, FY2014 10-K)
The facts below are drawn from the registrant's contemporaneous 2014 filings that
document its Indonesia ownership/joint-venture arrangement.
- PT Freeport Indonesia ("PT-FI") is an Indonesian limited liability company; FCX directly
  owns 81.28 percent of its outstanding common stock and indirectly owns a further 9.36
  percent through wholly owned subsidiary PT Indocopper Investama, with the Indonesian
  government owning the remaining 9.36 percent. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-15-000016, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- PT-FI has an unincorporated joint venture with Rio Tinto plc ("Rio Tinto"), under which
  Rio Tinto holds a 40 percent interest in certain assets and in future production above
  specified annual amounts of copper, gold and silver through 2021 in Block A of PT-FI's
  Contract of Work (COW), and 40 percent of all Block A production after 2021; FCX and its
  predecessors have been the only operator of Block A since 1967, and PT-FI's share of
  copper production/sales was 98 percent in 2014 — Rio Tinto's interest is a cash-flow
  entitlement above thresholds, not an operating role. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-15-000016, Form 10-K (FY2014)]

## Contemporaneous regulatory/structural event — the July 2014 MOU
- On July 25, 2014, PT-FI entered a Memorandum of Understanding ("MOU") with Indonesia's
  Directorate General of Mineral and Coal and Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources to
  negotiate, over the following six months, an amended COW addressing concession size,
  royalties/taxes, domestic processing, divestment, local content, and post-2021
  continuation of operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-14-000039, Form 8-K (event date 2014-07-25)]
- Effective with signing, PT-FI agreed to pay new export duties, provide a $115 million
  assurance bond for smelter development, and raise royalties to 4.0 percent copper / 3.75
  percent gold (from 3.5 / 1.0 percent), enabling resumption of concentrate exports from
  August 2014; items reserved for the COW negotiation included cost-shared smelter/
  refining development and provisions for FCX to divest up to an additional 20.64
  percentage points (30 percent total) to the Indonesian government/nationals at fair
  value, with operations continuing 2022-2041. Under the MOU, no other COW terms were to
  change until the amended COW was completed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-14-000039, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the boundary / what stayed at closing
- Operating control of Grasberg remained with FCX/PT-FI as sole historical operator; the
  MOU restructured fiscal terms and opened a path toward a larger government ownership
  share but did not itself transfer operating control, management, or the workforce
  running PT-FI's mining operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-14-000039, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0000831259-15-000016, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- The customer-facing product (copper/gold/silver production and concentrate) continued
  to be produced and sold by PT-FI under the existing COW; the Rio Tinto joint venture
  affected only sharing of production/cash flows above thresholds, not which entity
  produced. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-15-000016, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- The legal vehicle (PT-FI operating under its COW) was retained at closing; the MOU
  contemplated only a future COW amendment, not its replacement or a new incorporated
  joint-venture entity at this stage. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-14-000039, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At the 2014 closing point the Grasberg-area structure comprised FCX's 90.64 percent
  combined direct-and-indirect equity in PT-FI, the Indonesian government's 9.36 percent,
  and a separate unincorporated Rio Tinto production/cash-flow-sharing joint venture over
  Block A, with the July 2014 MOU opening a fiscal and prospective-divestment
  renegotiation without altering, at that time, who operated the mine, produced the
  product, or managed the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-15-000016, Form 10-K (FY2014); SEC accession 0000831259-14-000039, Form 8-K]
