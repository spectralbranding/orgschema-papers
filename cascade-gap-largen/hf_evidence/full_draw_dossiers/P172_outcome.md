## Reporting-entity continuity (feasibility-relevant)

- The legal entity that carried the Chapter 11 case merged into its parent at the
  reorganization's effective date and terminated its own separate Exchange Act
  registration effective October 1, 2012, reporting approximately zero holders of
  record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-12-066793, Form 15-12G] Consequently, the
  post-2012 realized record is traced through the surviving parent's own subsequent
  Form 10-K filings rather than through a filing under the original debtor's own CIK.

## Realized financial trajectory, fiscal years 2013-2017

- Net income (loss) attributable to Dynegy Inc.: $(356) million for FY2013; $(273)
  million for FY2014; $50 million for FY2015 ($28 million after preferred-stock
  dividends, attributable to common stockholders). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001379895-16-000022, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Net loss attributable to Dynegy Inc. of $(1,240) million for FY2016. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001379895-17-000004, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Net income attributable to Dynegy Inc. of $76 million for FY2017. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001379895-18-000003, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Documented asset impairments over the window

- For the year ended December 31, 2015, the company recorded impairment charges of $99
  million in total, comprising $74 million for its Wood River facility and $25 million
  for its Brayton Point facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-16-000022, Form 10-K
  (FY2015)]
- The company performed a goodwill assessment as of October 1, 2015 and, separately, an
  annual goodwill assessment as of October 1, 2016; in both years it concluded no
  goodwill impairment was required. [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-16-000022, Form 10-K
  (FY2015)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-17-000004, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- For the year ended December 31, 2016, the company recorded an aggregate asset
  impairment charge of $849 million across three generation facilities — $56 million
  (Stuart), $148 million (Newton), and $645 million (Baldwin) — following asset
  impairment analyses performed that year. [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-17-000004,
  Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Subsequent combination, announced within the window

- On October 29, 2017, the company entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with
  Vistra Energy Corp., a Delaware corporation, under which the company would merge with
  and into Vistra Energy in an all-stock, tax-free transaction, with Vistra Energy as
  the surviving corporation; under the agreement's terms, the company's stockholders
  were to receive 0.652 shares of Vistra Energy common stock per share held. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001379895-18-000003, Form 10-K (FY2017)] This combination was announced
  within the ~3-5-year outcome window but, per the company's FY2017 Form 10-K, remained
  subject to stockholder and regulatory approvals as of that filing and had not yet
  closed; its consummation (reported to have occurred in 2018) falls outside the
  primary sources retrieved for this window and is noted here only as a documented,
  not-yet-closed event as of the FY2017 filing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001379895-18-000003, Form 10-K (FY2017)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Over fiscal years 2013-2017, the surviving reporting entity's attributable net result
  moved from losses of $(356) million and $(273) million (2013-2014), to a small profit
  of $50 million (2015), to a much larger loss of $(1,240) million (2016) driven by an
  $849 million aggregate impairment across three named generation facilities, to a
  return to profit of $76 million (2017) — the year in which the company also entered
  into a merger agreement to combine with another company, with the counterparty as
  surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-16-000022, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001379895-17-000004, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession
