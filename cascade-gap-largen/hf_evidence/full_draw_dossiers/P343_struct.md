# Structural sub-dossier — Beneficial Bancorp, Inc. merger with WSFS Financial Corporation (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On August 7, 2018, WSFS Financial Corporation ("WSFS"), a Delaware corporation and
  parent holding company of Wilmington Savings Fund Society, FSB ("WSFS Bank"), and
  Beneficial Bancorp, Inc. ("Beneficial"), a Maryland corporation and parent holding
  company of Beneficial Bank, a Pennsylvania-chartered savings bank, entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Reorganization, as amended on November 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, Beneficial merged with and into WSFS, with WSFS
  continuing as the surviving corporation, and simultaneously Beneficial Bank merged
  with and into WSFS Bank, with WSFS Bank continuing as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- Each share of Beneficial common stock was converted into the right to receive 0.3013
  shares of WSFS common stock (with cash for fractional shares) plus $2.93 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- At June 30, 2018, WSFS was a $7.11 billion-asset institution with 77 offices in
  Delaware, Pennsylvania, Virginia and Nevada; Beneficial was a $5.77 billion-asset
  institution operating 61 full-service banking offices in the greater Philadelphia and
  South New Jersey regions, plus two lending offices and two leasing offices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- As of the effective time, the Articles of Incorporation and Bylaws of Beneficial
  ceased to be in effect by operation of law, and the organizational documents of WSFS
  became those of the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-011959, 8-K Item 5.03]
- The mergers were structured to qualify as a "reorganization" within the meaning of
  Section 368(a) of the Internal Revenue Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]

## Governance and controlling structure at closing
- The WSFS board would expand from 10 to 13 members, adding Gerard P. Cuddy (Beneficial's
  President and CEO) and two other current Beneficial directors mutually agreed upon by
  the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- Following completion, Mark A. Turner was anticipated to be Executive Chairman of the
  combined company, Rodger Levenson President and Chief Executive Officer, and Mr. Cuddy
  Vice Chairman of WSFS Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- WSFS agreed to establish an advisory board inviting Mr. Levenson, Mr. Cuddy, and
  Beneficial directors not appointed to the combined board, to advise WSFS with respect
  to Beneficial. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, Beneficial's own directors and executive officers ceased serving in
  those capacities for the registrant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-011959, 8-K Item 5.02]

## What crossed the boundary and what was bridged contractually
- The transaction captured the customer-facing deposit-taking and lending franchise
  (branch network, loans, deposits) of Beneficial Bank into the surviving WSFS Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- For one year after the effective time, WSFS agreed to provide employees actively
  employed by Beneficial or its subsidiaries on the closing date ("covered employees")
  base salary/wage rates and target cash bonus opportunities no less favorable than
  those in effect immediately before closing, and benefits substantially comparable to
  similarly situated WSFS employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- WSFS agreed to assume the existing Severance Pay Plan for Eligible Employees of
  Beneficial Bank and to provide severance, job counseling and outplacement assistance
  to covered employees involuntarily terminated without cause before December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- WSFS agreed to maintain and administer a retention pool for specified employees of
  Beneficial and Beneficial Bank, subject to certain conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- Beneficial's executive change-in-control severance agreements and employment
  agreements with certain named executives were terminated as of closing and replaced by
  separate letter agreements with WSFS and WSFS Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- In connection with the planned integration of operations, WSFS anticipated
  nonrecurring charges for items including systems implementation, severance and
  accelerated vesting of equity awards, though the timing, nature and amount of these
  charges were not determinable as of the proxy statement date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- During the quarter ended September 30, 2018, Beneficial entered into an agreement to
  sell the net assets of Beneficial Insurance Services, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Beneficial Bank, receiving net proceeds of $14.1 million upon consummation effective
  September 30, 2018 — a carve-out of a specific subsidiary ahead of the merger's
  completion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]

## Regulatory and listing mechanics at closing
- The Beneficial board could terminate the merger agreement if WSFS's average closing
  stock price fell below thresholds specified in the agreement (absolute and relative to
  the Nasdaq Bank Index), subject to WSFS's right to adjust the exchange ratio to
  prevent termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- The parties agreed to use reasonable best efforts to obtain required regulatory
  approvals, subject to a negotiated "burdensome condition" ceiling on what WSFS would
  be required to accept. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
- In connection with the closing, Beneficial requested that Nasdaq withdraw its common
  stock from quotation on the Nasdaq Global Select Market and file a Form 25 removal
  notification, and Beneficial stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate its
  Exchange Act registration and reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-19-011959, 8-K Items 3.01, 3.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was structured as a whole-company stock-and-cash merger of Beneficial
  into WSFS (with a simultaneous bank-level merger of the subsidiary banks), under which
  the customer-facing banking franchise, governance seats for select Beneficial leaders,
  and the employee base transferred, while continuity of employee compensation/benefits
  and severance terms was contractually bridged for a defined one-year post-closing
  period rather than left to arise ad hoc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001552781-18-000472, DEFM14A]
