## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalar)
- For the fiscal year ended December 31, 2020 the registrant reported a net loss available to common shareholders of $(51,387) thousand (approximately a $51.4 million net loss) and a net loss available to common unitholders of $(56,786) thousand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- This was a swing from net income in the immediately preceding year: the same selected financial data reports net income available to common shareholders of $111,861 thousand for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]

## Impairments and held-for-sale write-downs during the window
- During 2020 the Company recorded a $36.6 million impairment charge on its two adjacent Weehawken, New Jersey hotel assets, after determining a shortened expected period of ownership and "the adverse effect the COVID-19 pandemic has had, and continues to have, on its hotel operations"; one of the two hotels had closed its rooms since March 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- The Company identified 16 office properties, a retail pad and several developable land parcels as held for sale as of December 31, 2020, and recognized an unrealized loss allowance of $15.7 million on six of these properties and certain land parcels ($14 million of which was from discontinued operations). [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- The Company also recorded additional land and other impairment charges on held-and-used land parcels during 2020; the specific dollar amount was not cleanly legible in the retrieved stripped text and is not restated here. [UNVERIFIED]

## Realized portfolio and organization at the window's end
- As of December 31, 2020 the registrant owned or had interests in 52 properties, consisting of 25 office properties (~8.1 million square feet leased to ~225 commercial tenants), 19 multi-family rental properties containing 5,825 residential units, four parking/retail properties, three hotels (723 rooms) and a parcel of land leased to a third party, plus developable land. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- By this point the Company described its portfolio as "comprised predominantly of Class A office and multi-family rental properties located primarily in the Northeast." [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- The Company had approximately 256 full-time employees as of December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]
- The General Partner owned a 90.4 percent common unit interest in the Operating Partnership as of December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001562762-21-000063, 10-K FY2020]

## Corporate name change within the window
- The registrant's SEC-registered corporate name changed from "MACK CALI REALTY CORP" to "Veris Residential, Inc." on EDGAR effective December 7, 2021 (CIK 0000924901 unchanged); the former name was of record from 1997-12-31 to 2021-12-07. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000924901.json]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2019-2021 window the registrant swung to a net loss of approximately $51.4 million for fiscal 2020 (from net income the prior year), took a $36.6 million impairment on its Weehawken hotels amid COVID-19 disruption plus a $15.7 million held-for-sale loss allowance, continued marketing office assets for sale (16 office properties held for sale at year-end 2020), operated a substantially reduced footprint (52 properties, ~256 employees at December 31, 2020) weighted toward Class A office and multi-family, and changed its corporate name to Veris Residential, Inc. effective December 7, 2021.
