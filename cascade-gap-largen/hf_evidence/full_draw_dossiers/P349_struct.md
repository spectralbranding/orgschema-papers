# Structural sub-dossier — The Navigators Group, Inc. acquisition by The Hartford Financial Services Group, Inc. (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties

- On August 22, 2018, The Navigators Group, Inc. (the "Company"), a Delaware corporation and global specialty insurance holding company, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with The Hartford Financial Services Group, Inc. (the "Parent"), a Delaware corporation and "a leader in property and casualty insurance, group benefits and mutual funds," and Renato Acquisition Co., a Delaware corporation and direct wholly owned subsidiary of Parent formed solely for purposes of the transaction ("Merger Sub"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A cover letter / "The Companies"]
- Under the merger agreement, Merger Sub would merge with and into the Company, with the Company surviving the merger as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, and each issued and outstanding share of Company common stock (other than excluded and appraisal shares) would be cancelled and converted into the right to receive $70.00 per share in cash, without interest — a whole-company, all-cash going-private acquisition structured as a reverse triangular merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "The Merger (Proposal 1)"]
- The $70.00 per-share price represented approximately 1.8x the Company's tangible book value as of June 30, 2018 and an approximately 21.8% premium over the Company's closing stock price on July 12, 2018 of $57.45; on August 21, 2018, the last full trading day prior to public announcement, Company common stock closed at $64.25. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger" / cover letter]
- Upon completion, the Company would cease to be a publicly traded company, its Exchange Act reporting obligations would terminate, and it would become a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Certain Effects of the Merger"]
- The merger agreement contained a 45-day "go-shop" provision under which the Company, assisted by financial advisors Goldman Sachs and Moelis, solicited 44 potential acquirers during the go-shop period; the Company did not receive any alternative acquisition proposal during that period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Go-Shop and Restrictions on Solicitation of Acquisition Proposals"]

## Financing structure (what funded the closing)

- Completion of the merger was not conditioned on Parent's receipt of financing; the proxy statement states Parent had "sufficient cash and short-term investments on hand to fund the aggregate merger consideration," while noting Parent was expected to consider alternative sources of capital, including dividends from its insurance-company subsidiaries subject to state regulatory approval, and was not expected to issue common equity in connection with the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]

## What would cross at closing, per tier

- Legal vehicle: a newly formed merger subsidiary (Renato Acquisition Co.) would merge into the Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent — the entire corporate entity, not a carved-out business unit, was the transaction's subject. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement"]
- Product/brand: the Company was described as providing "customized insurance solutions" across lines including maritime, construction, energy, environmental and professional services, underwritten through its Navigators Insurance Company and Navigators Specialty Insurance Company subsidiaries and a Lloyd's of London syndicate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Information About the Companies"]
- People/organization: prior to closing, the Company expected to adopt a new severance plan generally covering full-time U.S. employees not already party to an individual agreement providing severance, with a severance period for executive officers of two weeks per year of service (26-week minimum, 52-week maximum); separately, in negotiations, the Board's position was that (i) equity awards would be cashed out and (ii) Company employee benefit plans would remain in place for at least one year following closing, before employees moved to Parent's benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "New Severance Plan" / "Background of the Merger"]
- Directors/officers: at signing, voting agreements were entered into with Terence N. Deeks, Monica J. Deeks, the Deeks Family Foundation and related trusts, and separately with Company CEO Stanley A. Galanski; together the voting-agreement stockholders beneficially owned approximately 22.5% of outstanding Company common stock as of August 22, 2018 and agreed to vote their shares in favor of adopting the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "The Voting Agreements"]

## Closing conditions and regulatory process

- Consummation was conditioned on, among other things, expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, the parties having filed their HSR notifications as of September 18, 2018; a Statement Regarding the Acquisition of Control of or Merger with a Domestic Insurer filing under Section 1506 of the New York Insurance Law and approval from the New York Department of Financial Services (with respect to Navigators Insurance Company and Navigators Specialty Insurance Company); and Form E pre-acquisition notification filings with insurance regulators in other jurisdictions where the Company's insurance subsidiaries were licensed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Regulatory Approvals Required for the Merger"]
- The merger agreement provided for a Company termination fee of $68.25 million (or a reduced $42 million fee in the case of a termination connected to a superior proposal arising from the go-shop process), plus reimbursement of Parent's documented expenses up to $7 million under specified circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Expenses; Termination Fees"]
- At a Special Meeting, Company stockholders were asked to adopt the merger agreement; stockholders of record as of the October 10, 2018 record date (29,777,883 shares outstanding) were entitled to vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A "Record Date and Quorum"]

## Consummation

- As of May 1, 2019, completion of the merger remained subject to receipt of approval from the New York State Department of Financial Services (NYDFS), the sole remaining unsatisfied closing condition disclosed at that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-130756, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- On May 23, 2019, The Hartford Financial Services Group, Inc. completed the acquisition, with Merger Sub merging with and into the Company and the Company continuing as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; each outstanding share of Company common stock (other than excluded/appraisal shares) was converted into the right to receive $70.00 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-154705, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- In connection with closing, the Company notified Nasdaq that trading would be suspended and requested withdrawal of its listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market, with a Form 25 filed to remove the shares from listing and a Form 15 filed to terminate the Company's Exchange Act registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-154705, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- At the effective time, directors Saul L. Basch and Terence N. Deeks (among others named in the filing) ceased to serve and were replaced by Merger Sub's designated directors, Doug Elliot and Sabra Purtill; immediately following the effective time, Company CEO Stanley A. Galanski and Company CFO Ciro M. DeFalco ceased to serve in those officer roles, with Mr. DeFalco assuming an executive advisory role with Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-154705, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- The Hartford acquired the entire Company for $70.00/share in an all-cash, whole-company merger funded from Parent's own cash and short-term investments (not third-party debt/equity financing), with the Company's negotiated position preserving employee benefit-plan continuity for at least one year post-closing before integration into Parent's plans; the transaction closed May 23, 2019 following satisfaction of HSR and state insurance-regulatory (including NYDFS) approvals, after which the Company deregistered from Nasdaq and the Exchange Act and its pre-closing CEO and CFO ceased to hold those offices. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-298223, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-154705, Form 8-K]
