## Outcome window
- The fixed outcome window is the registrant's own realized record 3–5 years after the ~2016 closing era (approximately 2019–2021). The public reporting entity in that period is Nexeo Solutions, Inc. (SEC CIK 1604416, formerly WL Ross Holding Corp.), through which the business became publicly reporting after mid-2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018 (fiscal year ended September 30, 2018)]
- The standalone public record terminates at the start of the window: the entity was acquired and deregistered in early 2019, so no separate 3–5-year (2019–2021) public record exists for the business as an independent registrant. The outcome is therefore bounded/uncertain (see the terminal-event section below). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-071988, Form 15-12B filed March 12, 2019]

## Last realized standalone annual record (fiscal year ended September 30, 2018)
- Sales and operating revenues were $4,034.2 million for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2018, up $397.3 million (10.9%) from $3,636.9 million for fiscal 2017; by line of business, Chemicals were $1,904.5 million and Plastics were $1,980.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]
- Net income was $29.4 million for fiscal 2018, compared with $14.4 million for fiscal 2017 (a 104.2% increase); gross profit was $460.1 million (versus $398.4 million in fiscal 2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]
- Net cash provided by operating activities was $88.7 million for fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]
- At September 30, 2018 the company had approximately 2,760 employees worldwide (approximately 2,190 in the U.S.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]

## Realized platform activity over the period
- The distribution platform continued to add businesses during the public period: the Ultra Chem Acquisition closed April 3, 2017 (the equity interests of the Mexico City, Mexico–based chemicals distribution business of the Ultra Chem Group). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]

## Terminal event and end of the standalone public record
- On September 17, 2018, Nexeo entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Univar Inc. (with Merger Sub I and Merger Sub II) providing for the acquisition of Nexeo by Univar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, 10-K FY2018]
- On February 28, 2019, Univar Inc. completed the acquisition via a two-step merger: Merger Sub I merged with and into Nexeo (with Nexeo surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Univar), and immediately thereafter Nexeo merged with and into Merger Sub II, which survived as the surviving company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Univar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, 8-K dated March 1, 2019]
- In connection with the closing, Nexeo Solutions, LLC repaid all amounts outstanding under its $575 million multicurrency ABL credit facility (dated June 9, 2016) effective February 28, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, 8-K Item 1.02]
- The Nexeo common shares, warrants and units (Nasdaq symbol "NXEOU") ceased trading and were delisted from Nasdaq as of the market open on March 1, 2019; Forms 25 to delist and deregister the securities under Section 12(b) were filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, 8-K; SEC accession 0001354457-19-000102, Form 25-NSE filed March 1, 2019]
- The entity filed a Form 15-12B to deregister/suspend its reporting obligations on March 12, 2019, ending its separate public reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-071988, Form 15-12B]
