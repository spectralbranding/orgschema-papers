# Outcome sub-dossier — Verra Mobility Corporation (formerly Gores Holdings II, Inc.), realized record ~3 years after closing (fiscal year 2021)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The registrant continued filing under the same
> SEC CIK after closing (renamed), so the realized record below is drawn from its
> Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2021 — approximately 3 years after
> the closing of the underlying transaction. This sub-dossier contains no information
> about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status after the transaction
- The registrant (SEC CIK 0001682745) continued filing periodic reports under the
  same CIK after closing, under the name "Verra Mobility Corporation," common stock
  trading on Nasdaq under symbol "VRRM." Its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2021 was filed 2022-04-22. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Realized financial trajectory (fiscal year 2021, ~3 years after closing)
- Total revenue increased $157.0 million (40%) from $393.6 million in fiscal 2020 to
  $550.6 million in fiscal 2021; revenue had previously declined from $448.7 million
  in fiscal 2019 to $393.6 million in fiscal 2020 "due to the significant negative
  impact from COVID-19." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A]
- Net income was $41.4 million for fiscal 2021 versus a net loss of $4.6 million for
  fiscal 2020 — a $46.0 million swing attributed primarily to recovery in the travel
  industry benefiting rental-car customers. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A]
- As of December 31, 2021 the Company reported 1,433 employees (1,306 full-time, 127
  part-time; 933 full-time in the United States, 373 international). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The Tax Receivable Agreement liability (entered into at closing) stood at
  approximately $61.7 million at December 31, 2021 ($5.1 million current, $56.6
  million non-current), with $4.8 million and $5.1 million paid during 2021 for tax
  years 2019 and 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Business-model and organizational developments within the window
- The Company acquired Redflex Holdings Limited on June 17, 2021, expanding its
  Government Solutions segment into international markets, and acquired T2 Systems
  Parent Corporation on December 7, 2021, forming a newly created "Parking Solutions"
  operating segment — a new line of business diversifying beyond the pre-existing
  toll/violations and government-safety segments; Parking Solutions generated $6.5
  million in revenue for the post-acquisition period of December 7-31, 2021. The T2
  Systems acquisition was financed partly with additional debt. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Documented internal-control and organizational disruption within the window
- During fiscal 2021 the Company identified material weaknesses in internal control
  over financial reporting related to (i) "the monitoring and control activities over
  the acquisition of Redflex Holdings Limited due to the lack of sufficient qualified
  accounting resources to timely identify and assess accounting implications of
  revenue arrangements assumed as part of the acquisition and to provide adequate
  controls over the completeness and accuracy of inputs used in accounting for the
  business combination"; and (ii) design/maintenance of certain revenue and reporting
  controls tied to a third-party application. Management concluded internal control
  over financial reporting "was not effective as of December 31, 2021." [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 9A]
- Separately, a material weakness in review controls over accounting for Private
  Placement Warrants (tied to an April 2021 SEC Staff Statement on SPAC warrant
  accounting) produced a material misstatement of warrant liability, additional
  paid-in capital, and accumulated deficit for periods including 2018-2020; the
  Company restated its consolidated financial statements for those periods and
  completed remediation during fiscal 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, Item 9A]
- The Company disclosed "significant expenses related to legal, accounting, and other
  professional services in connection with the internal investigation regarding
  potential revenue recognition matters at the Company's subsidiary, Redflex," along
  with "the delay in timely filing this Annual Report on Form 10-K, and the diversion
  of the attention of the management team," stated to have adversely affected, and
  expected to continue adversely affecting, its business, financial condition, and
  cash flows. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, Risk Factors]
- On March 31, 2022, the Company notified lenders it could not file the FY2021
  Form 10-K within the 90-day period required under its Term Loan, Senior Notes, and
  Revolver agreements, creating "a technical default"; the filing was completed
  within the 30-day cure period so no Event of Default resulted, and the Company
  stated it was compliant with all debt covenants at December 31, 2021. Loss of
  "well-known seasoned issuer" status (from the late filing) was disclosed as
  restricting use of certain short-form registration statements for at least 12
  months even after becoming current with delinquent filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021, Note 20 / Risk Factors]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Approximately 3 years after closing, the realized record combines substantial
  top-line recovery and growth (revenue up 40% year-over-year to $550.6 million; a
  swing from a $4.6 million net loss to $41.4 million net income) and two 2021
  acquisitions (Redflex; T2 Systems, creating a new operating segment) with
  documented internal-control weaknesses specifically tied to the accounting
  integration of the Redflex acquisition and to a third-party reporting application,
  a related internal investigation into Redflex revenue-recognition matters, a
  multi-year restatement of prior consolidated financial statements, and a late
  10-K filing that triggered (and cured within its grace period) a technical default
  under the Company's debt agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950170-22-005974, Form 10-K FY2021]
