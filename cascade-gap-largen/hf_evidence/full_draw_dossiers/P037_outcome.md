# Outcome sub-dossier — The Chemours Company realized record, fixed ~2018–2020 window (FY2019)

## Realized financial record (fixed FY2019 window)
- For the year ended December 31, 2019 the company reported a net loss attributable to
  Chemours of $52 million, versus net income of $995 million in 2018 and $746 million in
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) consolidated statements of operations]
- Basic loss per share of common stock was $(0.32) for 2019, compared with basic earnings
  per share of $5.62 in 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- Net sales decreased by $1.1 billion (17%) to $5.5 billion for 2019, down from $6.6 billion
  in 2018, with declines in price and volume across all three segments — including a 24%
  volume decline in the Titanium Technologies segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) MD&A]
- No impairment charges were recognized on long-lived assets during the years ended
  December 31, 2019 and 2018, and the annual goodwill impairment tests as of October 1, 2019
  required no adjustment to the carrying value of goodwill (assets retained). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) notes]

## Documented operational disruption (product/process interface)
- Cost of goods sold in 2019 included $150 million incurred for environmental remediation
  activities related to Fayetteville. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) MD&A]
- The Fluoroproducts segment's Adjusted EBITDA fell 26% to $578 million, attributed in part
  to start-up costs for a new Opteon refrigerants facility in Corpus Christi, Texas, and
  "unplanned outages at certain facilities," alongside illegal imports of legacy HFC
  refrigerants into the EU. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) MD&A]
- The company had approximately 7,000 employees at the FY2019 reporting date; a 2017
  voluntary separation program had resulted in approximately 300 employees separating from
  the company through the end of 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019)]

## Documented fracture with the former parent (process/organization interface)
- The company disclosed that it "filed a lawsuit against DuPont regarding indemnification
  matters," and that "multiple lawsuits have been filed by third parties containing
  allegations that DuPont's separation of Chemours was fraudulent." [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) risk factors]
- On August 24, 2017 the company and DuPont amended the separation agreement concerning
  future PFOA litigation and costs not covered by the MDL Settlement; the company remained a
  named defendant and/or indemnitor of DuPont in litigation over PFOA, GenX/HFPO Dimer Acid,
  AFFF and other PFAS compounds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
- The company noted that some of its manufacturing operations "have significant shared
  services and lease agreements with DuPont" and that such agreements "may adversely impact
  our ability to make … strategic decisions regarding our manufacturing operations," with
  potential future charges if the agreements were terminated or revised. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019) risk factors]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Roughly four to five years after the separation, the standalone company remained a public
  filer that retained its asset base without goodwill or long-lived-asset impairment, but its
  FY2019 record showed a swing to a $52 million net loss on a 17% decline in net sales,
  segment volume weakness (notably a 24% Titanium Technologies volume drop), a $150 million
  Fayetteville environmental-remediation charge, and continuing indemnification litigation
  with the former parent — including the company's own suit against DuPont and third-party
  allegations that the separation was fraudulent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-004829, Form 10-K (FY2019)]
