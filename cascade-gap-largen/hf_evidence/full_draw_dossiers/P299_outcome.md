## Realized financial record (fiscal years 2017-2019, per the FY2019 Form 10-K)
- Operating revenues, net: $610.471 million (2017), $766.570 million (2018), $941.240
  million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Net loss: $236.303 million (2017), $153.327 million (2018), $206.585 million (2019).
  Net (loss) income attributable to Class A common stockholders: $(160.154) million
  (2017), $12.380 million (2018), $(148.684) million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K, Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- A qualitative goodwill impairment test performed as of December 1, 2019 for the
  goodwill balance associated with the 2018 Saeta acquisition ($128.0 million) concluded
  that further evaluation was not necessary and that goodwill was not impaired at
  December 31, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]

## Documented business activity in the window
- In the second quarter of 2018 the Company completed the acquisition of Saeta Yield,
  S.A., adding Portugal Wind, Uruguay Wind, and Spanish Regulated Solar and Wind
  reportable segments representing its entire operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- On September 26, 2019 the Company completed the "WGL Acquisition," an approximately
  320 MW distributed generation portfolio in the United States acquired from
  subsidiaries of AltaGas Ltd. for a purchase price of $720.0 million plus $15.1 million
  for working capital, and on December 18, 2019 completed a further approximately 45 MW
  portfolio acquisition (the "X-Elio Acquisition"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- The FY2019 10-K's Adjusted EBITDA reconciliation continued to list "management fees to
  Brookfield" as a line item added back to net income (loss), indicating the sponsor
  management-fee arrangement remained in place through the period covered. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]

## Subsequent change in ownership and reporting status (disclosed in the FY2019 10-K)
- On January 11, 2020 the Company received an unsolicited, non-binding proposal from
  Brookfield Renewable Partners L.P. ("Brookfield Renewable," an affiliate of
  Brookfield) to acquire all outstanding shares of Common Stock other than the
  approximately 62% then held by Brookfield and its affiliates; the proposal was
  conditioned on approval by a committee of independent directors and by a majority of
  the shares not affiliated with Brookfield Renewable. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- The Company's board formed a special committee of disinterested, independent directors
  to review, evaluate, and negotiate or explore alternatives to the proposal, and
  resolved not to approve the proposal or an alternative without that committee's prior
  favorable recommendation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- The 10-K discloses that Brookfield and certain of its affiliates subsequently entered
  into a definitive "Reorganization Agreement" for Brookfield Renewable to acquire all of
  the Company's outstanding Common Stock not already owned by Brookfield, in exchange for
  Brookfield Renewable limited partnership units or Class A exchangeable subordinate
  voting shares of Brookfield Renewable Corporation, with consummation subject to
  regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions and stated as not assured.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- That transaction is reflected in the later corporate record by the Form 15-12B filed
  on 2020-08-11 in the name of "TerraForm Power NY Holdings, Inc. (as successor by merger
  to TerraForm Power, Inc.)," terminating the registration of the Class A Common Stock.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-20-018036, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Through fiscal year 2019 the entity continued to file annual reports, reported growing
  revenues ($610.5 million to $941.2 million, 2017-2019) alongside continuing net losses
  each year ($236.3 million, $153.3 million, and $206.6 million respectively), and
  completed several renewable-asset acquisitions (Saeta Yield in 2018; the WGL and
  X-Elio acquisitions in 2019), with the Brookfield sponsor management-fee arrangement
  still reflected in its 2019 financial disclosures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K]
- Before reaching the later years of a fixed 3-5-year post-closing window, a January
  2020 unsolicited proposal from a Brookfield-affiliated entity to acquire the remaining
  publicly held shares led to a Reorganization Agreement and, per the entity's own
  subsequent-events disclosure and the later Form 15-12B, to termination of the Class A
  Common Stock's registration in August 2020; no periodic report has been located for
  the entity covering the later part of the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001599947-20-000012, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-20-018036, Form 15-12B]
