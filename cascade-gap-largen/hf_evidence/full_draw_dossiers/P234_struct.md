# Structural sub-dossier — BancorpSouth, Inc. reorganization into BancorpSouth Bank (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On July 26, 2017, BancorpSouth, Inc. (the "Company"), a Mississippi corporation and
  registered bank holding company, and its wholly owned bank subsidiary, BancorpSouth
  Bank (the "Bank"), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Reorganization, amended and
  restated on August 15, 2017, under which the Company would merge with and into the
  Bank, the Company's separate corporate existence ceasing and the Bank continuing as
  the surviving corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- Each outstanding share of Company common stock, par value $2.50, was to be converted
  into the right to receive one share of Bank common stock, also par value $2.50, on a
  one-for-one basis; the Bank would assume the Company's restricted stock, performance
  share, stock option, and non-equity performance incentive plans and all outstanding
  awards under them, unchanged in terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- Company shareholders approved the plan of reorganization at a special meeting held
  September 27, 2017; completion also required approval from the FDIC and the
  Mississippi Department of Banking and Consumer Finance (MDBCF). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- The reorganization was completed on October 31, 2017 (effective 11:59 p.m. Central
  Time) by the filing of Articles of Merger with the MDBCF and the Mississippi
  Secretary of State; the Bank was thereby established as successor issuer to the
  Company under Rule 12g-3(a) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]
- The proxy statement/offering circular states the reorganization's purpose as
  improving efficiency by eliminating redundant corporate infrastructure and the
  Federal Reserve Board (FRB) oversight applicable to registered bank holding
  companies; it separately identifies, as an example of the Bank's growth strategy,
  the then-pending Central Community Corporation and Ouachita Bancshares Corp.
  acquisitions described in a Form 8-K the Company filed on August 16, 2017 — those
  are distinct, separately structured transactions, not part of this reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and regulatory tier
- Before the reorganization, the Company was a registered bank holding company subject
  to FRB regulation and supervision; the Bank was, and remained, a Mississippi
  state-chartered, FDIC-insured bank that is not a member of the Federal Reserve
  System. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction collapsed the two-tier holding-company structure into a single bank
  charter as the surviving legal vehicle: following the reorganization the Bank (as
  surviving entity) is directly subject to FDIC and MDBCF regulation and is no longer
  subject to FRB bank-holding-company regulation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]
- Concurrent with the reorganization, the Exchange Act reporting agency for the
  surviving entity's periodic and current reports changed from the SEC to the FDIC:
  both the proxy statement and the closing 8-K state that, after the reorganization,
  the Bank would not file periodic or current reports with the SEC but would instead
  file annual reports (Form 10-K), quarterly reports (Form 10-Q), and current reports
  (Form 8-K) with the FDIC and the NYSE. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]

## What crossed at closing (tier-level continuity)
- Board of directors: the Bank had the same directors, with the same terms of service,
  as the Company immediately prior to the reorganization, and the same standing
  committees — Nominating and Corporate Governance, Audit, Executive Compensation and
  Stock Incentive, and Risk Management — with the same committee charters and
  chairmen. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- Officers: officers of the Company immediately prior to the reorganization held the
  same positions and titles with the Bank after closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]
- Operations: the proxy statement states that "the business of the Bank will remain
  unchanged" after completion, that the Bank's offices and other business premises
  would continue to be occupied by the Bank, and that the Bank's articles of
  incorporation and bylaws were "substantially similar" to the Company's as in effect
  immediately before the reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- People / benefit plans: the Bank assumed all of the Company's employee benefit plans
  (retirement, profit sharing, health and welfare, and fringe-benefit arrangements)
  and the Company's change-in-control agreements with its key officers; the
  reorganization was expressly stated not to constitute a "change in control" under
  those agreements, and no related payments were triggered by the reorganization
  itself. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services, shared-substrate, or operating agreement is described in
  the closing filings, because the reorganization did not separate a business unit
  from a parent — it merged a holding company into its own wholly owned operating
  subsidiary. Board, officers, committees, premises, benefit plans, and equity/
  incentive awards all continued intact through the same legal transition in which
  only the top corporate shell and the primary banking regulator changed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing / trading
- In connection with the reorganization, Company common stock was removed from
  listing on the NYSE effective October 31, 2017; Surviving Entity (Bank) common stock
  began trading on the NYSE under the unchanged ticker symbol "BXS" effective November
  1, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction restructured BancorpSouth's corporate form rather than combining it
  with an unaffiliated business: the parent bank holding company (BancorpSouth, Inc.)
  merged with and into its own wholly owned bank subsidiary (BancorpSouth Bank), which
  survived as a single-tier, FDIC/MDBCF-regulated Mississippi state bank, while board,
  officers, committees, premises, and employee/incentive arrangements all continued
  unchanged; the principal structural discontinuity was regulatory and reporting-venue
  (FRB bank-holding-company oversight and SEC periodic reporting were both replaced by
  FDIC oversight and FDIC-filed periodic reporting for the surviving entity), not any
  separation of product, process, or people. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-271952, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-328144, Form 8-K]
