## Realized record under private ownership within the fixed window (secondary/press-sourced)
- On November 9, 2021, RingCentral, Inc. and Mitel announced a strategic partnership under which RingCentral paid Mitel $650 million to acquire intellectual property rights and patents covering network and call management, security, and infrastructure, including Mitel's CloudLink technology, and became Mitel's exclusive UCaaS (cloud) partner; as part of the same transaction, Mitel's existing investor group led by affiliates of Searchlight Capital Partners invested $200 million in newly issued RingCentral shares. [SRC: https://ir.ringcentral.com/news/press-release-details/2021/RingCentral-and-Mitel-Announce-Strategic-Partnership-to-Enable-Customers-to-Transition-Seamlessly-to-Cloud-Based-Unified-Business-Communications/default.aspx]
- On October 18, 2022, Mitel entered into an agreement with a majority of its first-lien and second-lien lenders to amend its credit agreements: $156 million of new-money superpriority "first out" term debt (plus a new superpriority revolving facility) was issued to consenting lenders; $603 million of existing First Lien Debt held by consenting lenders was exchanged for $576 million of new "Second Out" debt; and $152 million of existing Second Lien Debt was exchanged for $125 million of new "Third Out" debt; a new Omnibus Intercreditor Agreement established a payment waterfall that contractually subordinated the liens and loans of non-consenting lenders to the new financing structure. [SRC: https://www.hugheshubbard.com/news/mitel-networks-another-development-in-liability-management-transactions]
- Non-consenting lenders subsequently filed suit alleging that the October 2022 amendment violated protections in the pre-existing credit agreements. [SRC: https://www.hugheshubbard.com/news/mitel-networks-another-development-in-liability-management-transactions]

## Beyond the fixed outcome window (noted for context, not counted as the in-window record)
- On March 9, 2025 (approximately six years and three months after the November 2018 closing, outside the ~2021-2023 window), Mitel announced a Chapter 11 restructuring agreement; the restructuring was completed on June 20, 2025, reducing debt by approximately $1.15 billion and annual cash interest expense by approximately $135 million, with approximately $125 million of new funding raised, and with ownership transitioning from Searchlight Capital Partners to a group of the company's former lenders. [SRC: https://www.mitel.com/about/financial-restructuring]
