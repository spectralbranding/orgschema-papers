# Structural sub-dossier — ExamWorks Group, Inc. acquisition by Gold Parent, L.P. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn filing is a Current Report on Form 8-K (Item 8.01) dated July 18, 2016 that
  supplements the company's definitive proxy statement; it is not itself the deal-terms
  document. It confirms that on April 26, 2016 ExamWorks Group, Inc. (a Delaware
  corporation) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Gold Parent, L.P.
  ("Parent") and Gold Merger Co, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), under which Merger Sub would merge
  with and into ExamWorks, with ExamWorks surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-133094, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Its sole exhibit (EX-99.1) is a putative shareholder class-action complaint, not a
  deal-terms exhibit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-133094, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- It records that Leonard Green's representatives indicated certain company directors and
  officers would be appointed to the board of Parent after closing, and that the three
  directors chosen and appointed by Leonard Green would hold a majority of votes cast so
  long as Leonard Green affiliates continue to own a majority of Parent's equity. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001104659-16-133094, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing (deal-terms sources)
- Parties: ExamWorks Group, Inc.; Gold Parent, L.P. (a Delaware limited partnership,
  "Parent"); and Gold Merger Co, Inc. (a Delaware corporation and an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent, "Merger Sub"). Parent and Merger Sub are affiliates of Leonard
  Green & Partners, L.P., a private equity firm based in Los Angeles. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]
- Structure: Merger Sub merged with and into ExamWorks, with ExamWorks continuing as the
  surviving corporation and as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (a
  reverse-triangular take-private of an NYSE-listed company, symbol "EXAM"). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]
- Consideration: each outstanding share of common stock (other than excluded/rollover and
  properly dissenting shares) was converted into the right to receive $35.05 in cash,
  without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed on July 27, 2016 (the "Closing Date"); the same corporate
  entity continued as the surviving company under an amended and restated certificate of
  incorporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001437749-16-035715, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 5.03]

## Business / asset that changed ownership
- The acquired business is a provider of independent medical examinations, peer reviews,
  bill reviews, Medicare compliance services, case management, record retrieval and
  document management services ("IME services"), delivered principally through a panel of
  credentialed physicians and other medical providers; its clients are property and
  casualty insurance carriers, law firms, third-party claim administrators and government
  agencies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]
- This was a whole-company acquisition: the customer-facing product (IME services), the
  operational processes that deliver it (the medical panel and service operations), and the
  operating legal entity transferred together as a single going concern under new
  ownership; no business unit was carved out or divested at closing. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001437749-16-035715, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Controlling intent and people/management at closing
- Rollover investors — Richard E. Perlman (Executive Chairman), James K. Price (Chief
  Executive Officer), Wesley J. Campbell (President), J. Miguel Fernandez de Castro (Chief
  Financial Officer), and William A. Shutzer (director) — agreed under a rollover and
  subscription agreement that each "will continue to serve in their respective current
  positions of the surviving company following the closing," contributing up to $45 million
  of ExamWorks stock (valued at $35.05/share) in exchange for units of Parent and retaining
  an indirect interest of approximately 3.3%–3.7%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-013975,
  DEFM14A]
- All rollover investors except Shutzer were to participate in a new equity incentive plan
  of Parent (investment in Class A Units and grants of Class B Units) adopted after closing.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]
- At the Effective Time, Perlman, Price and Shutzer remained directors of the surviving
  company, while J. Thomas Presby, Peter M. Graham, Peter B. Bach and David B. Zenoff
  resigned as directors (no resignation for disagreement). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001437749-16-035715, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Financing of the transaction (closing structure)
- The transaction was funded through a combination of up to approximately $1.172 billion in
  equity financing (Leonard Green funds plus Hux Investment Pte. Ltd.) and up to
  approximately $1.12 billion in debt financing under commitment letters from Bank of
  America, N.A., Barclays Bank PLC and Deutsche Bank, among others. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]

## Bridging of shared substrate (closing-time)
- Because the entire operating company was acquired intact as the surviving corporation,
  product, processes and people remained within one legal entity; the merger documents
  describe no transition-services, carve-out or shared-services agreement bridging a
  separated substrate (the only "divestiture" references are hypothetical antitrust-remedy
  contingencies, not an actual separation at closing). The rollover-and-subscription
  agreement and the continuing-position arrangements are the instruments that retained
  management with the business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A public company was taken private in an all-cash reverse-triangular merger ($35.05/share;
  ~$1.172B equity + ~$1.12B debt) by affiliates of a private equity firm; the operating
  IME-services business, its delivery processes and its senior management all crossed the
  ownership boundary together within the surviving legal entity, with management retained
  via equity rollover and continuing positions rather than replaced or separated. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-16-013975, DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001437749-16-035715, Form 8-K]
