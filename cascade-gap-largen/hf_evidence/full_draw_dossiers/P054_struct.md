# Structural sub-dossier — Micron / Nanya joint venture in Inotera Memories (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source is Micron Technology, Inc.'s Form 8-K dated November 26, 2008 (event
  "Second Closing Under Inotera Share Purchase Agreement"); it is itself the deal-terms
  document, reporting the second closing of the share purchase and summarizing the
  joint-venture, supply, technology-transfer, and loan agreements entered at that closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Items 1.01/1.02/2.03/8.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On October 11, 2008 Micron and Micron Semiconductor B.V. ("MNL", a wholly owned Micron
  subsidiary) entered a Share Purchase Agreement with Qimonda AG and Qimonda Holding B.V.
  ("Sellers") to purchase 1,184,088,059 common shares of Inotera Memories, Inc.
  ("Inotera") in two closings, plus a Qimonda sale of 4,483,800 secondary shares on the
  Taiwan Stock Exchange with proceeds remitted to MNL. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K introductory text]
- First Closing (October 20, 2008): MNL purchased 592,044,028 shares for $200.0 million.
  Second Closing (November 26, 2008): MNL purchased the remaining 592,044,031 shares for
  $200.0 million using proceeds of the NPC Loan (below). [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K introductory text]
- Following the purchases, Micron, through its subsidiaries, owned approximately 35.61% of
  the outstanding common stock of Inotera, a publicly-traded Taiwan-based company that
  manufactures DRAM products. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K introductory text / Item 1.01]
- The transaction was structured as a minority equity stake plus a contractual
  joint-venture governance and supply arrangement between MNL and Nanya Technology
  Corporation ("NTC") over the jointly owned, separately incorporated Inotera; a prior
  50/50 MNL–NTC venture entity, MeiYa Technology Corporation, was concurrently restructured
  and its formation agreements set for termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 1.02]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- Customer-facing product: DRAM products remained produced by Inotera; Micron did not take
  the manufacturing operation onto its own books. Instead, under the Joint Venture
  Agreement, Micron and NTC agreed to purchase all of Inotera's output, generally on a
  50-50 basis (other than trench products sold to Qimonda during a ramp-down period). [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Joint Venture Agreement]
- Under a separate Supply Agreement (Micron, NTC, Inotera), Inotera would sell trench and
  stack DRAM products to Micron and NTC; initially each would purchase products from 50% of
  Inotera's aggregate manufacturing capacity (Micron's trench share net of the Qimonda
  ramp-down), with pricing based on manufacturing costs and margins. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Supply Agreement]
- Producing processes / technology: Micron agreed to transfer to Inotera certain
  intellectual property for the manufacture of stack DRAM products, receiving transfer fees
  and royalties; a Technology Transfer Agreement (Micron, NTC, Inotera) covered stack-DRAM
  technology on process nodes below 50nm developed under the joint development program. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Technology Transfer Agreement]
- Joint development: an Amended and Restated Joint Development Program Agreement (Micron,
  NTC) provided that the parties jointly develop stack-DRAM process technology and designs,
  remaining in effect at least 10 years from the original April 21, 2008 agreement, with
  R&D conducted jointly and costs shared approximately equally after a threshold. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 JDP Agreement]
- People / management / governance: Inotera continued as a separate publicly-traded
  operating company. MNL and NTC each could initially designate five of Inotera's twelve
  directors (adjusting with ownership), each could nominate one independent director and
  select two supervisors, and the parties would use best efforts to require a supermajority
  vote for Board actions; each party could nominate an executive officer serving at the
  pleasure of Inotera's Board. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Joint Venture Agreement]
- The retrieved filing describes no transfer of Inotera's employees, workforce, or
  operating organization to Micron; Inotera runs its own manufacturing, and neither MNL nor
  NTC is required to contribute capital to Inotera. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Joint Venture Agreement]

## Bridging / transitional and financing arrangements at closing
- Financing at closing: Nan Ya Plastics Corporation ("NPC", an NTC affiliate) loaned MNL
  $200.0 million (the "NPC Loan", 3-month LIBOR + 2%, Micron-guaranteed, secured by pledged
  Inotera shares) used to pay the Second Closing purchase price; Inotera loaned Micron $85.0
  million (3-month LIBOR + 2%) for general corporate purposes; and MeiYa loaned Micron $50
  million at 4.25%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Loan Agreements and introductory text]
- Governance/support agreements bridged the split control: a Facilitation Agreement
  (Micron, NTC, Inotera) obligated Inotera to take actions effectuating the JV Agreement's
  intent on officer nomination and output rights; a Micron Guaranty Agreement guaranteed
  MNL's performance under the JV Agreement for NTC's benefit. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.01 Facilitation Agreement / Micron Guaranty]
- Transition from the seller: Micron and/or MNL entered a back-end services agreement and a
  transition services agreement with Qimonda, and Inotera's Qimonda Supply Agreement
  obligated Qimonda to purchase trench DRAM products during an eight-month ramp-down after
  the Second Closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K introductory text / Item 1.01 Supply Agreement]
- Concurrent restructuring: a Master Agreement (Micron, MNL, NTC, MeiYa, Inotera) and the
  Item 1.02 terminating agreements unwound the prior MeiYa 50/50 venture (each party had
  been to contribute NT$1.2 billion, ~US$40 million, at MeiYa's closing plus a further
  ~US$510 million by December 31, 2009), while certain provisions relating to transferred
  employees under the terminating joint venture agreement were preserved. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Item 1.02 Terminating Agreements]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing Micron acquired an ~35.61% minority equity interest in the standalone,
  publicly-traded manufacturer Inotera and, jointly with NTC, took contractual rights to
  Inotera's DRAM output (initially ~50/50) and shared board governance requiring
  supermajority consent, while agreeing to transfer stack-DRAM manufacturing technology to
  Inotera; the producing operation, workforce, and management remained inside Inotera and
  did not move to Micron, and the split was bridged by supply, facilitation, guaranty,
  transition-services, and multiple loan agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0000723125-08-000116, Form 8-K Items 1.01/1.02/2.03/8.01]
