## Entity and business model (Form 10-K for fiscal year 2015)
- The registrant is an externally managed, non-listed, daily-valued, perpetual-life real
  estate investment trust ("REIT") that owns and manages a diversified portfolio of
  apartment, industrial, office, retail, and other properties primarily in the United
  States; it was incorporated in Maryland on May 28, 2004 and has elected REIT status
  since the taxable year ended December 31, 2004. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The registrant is managed by an external Advisor, LaSalle Investment Management, Inc.,
  a subsidiary of the registrant's Sponsor, Jones Lang LaSalle Incorporated (NYSE: JLL).
  Under the Advisory Agreement (first amended and restated effective October 1, 2012, and
  renewed May 5, 2015 for a term expiring June 5, 2016), the registrant pays a fixed
  advisory fee of 1.25% of NAV calculated daily plus a performance fee equal to 10% of the
  total return in excess of 7% per annum for each share class. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Capital is raised through a continuous public offering of Class A, Class M, Class A-I,
  Class M-I, and Class D common stock registered on Form S-11; LaSalle Investment
  Management Distributors, LLC, an affiliate of the Advisor, serves as dealer manager.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Day-to-day, on-site property management and leasing is not performed by the registrant
  or by an acquired seller's retained staff; the registrant states it "hire[s] property
  management and leasing companies" per property, using "a mix of property management and
  leasing service providers that include large national real estate service firms,
  including an affiliate of our Advisor and smaller local firms." [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- As of December 31, 2015, the registrant owned interests in 54 properties, 53 located in
  14 U.S. states and one in Canada. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Growth mechanism: a series of separately closed property acquisitions, not one discrete combination
- EDGAR filing history for 2015 shows a sequence of distinct, individually captioned 8-K
  acquisition announcements (e.g., "skokiecommonsacq," "coppellacq," "aqrittenhouseacquisition,"
  "whitestonemarketacqusition," "ohareindustrialportfol," "nyretaildeal," "mauiacquisition"),
  each filed on its own accession on a different date across 2015 — there is no single
  merger agreement, Form S-4, or DEFM14A covering these as one combined transaction.
  [SRC: SEC EDGAR submissions listing for CIK 1314152, filings dated 2015-01-21 through 2015-12-28]
- Per the FY2015 Form 10-K's "2015 Acquisitions" discussion, each property was acquired on
  a separate date with its own separately negotiated financing: DFW Distribution Center
  (Apr 15, 2015, ~$44,200, ten-year mortgage at 3.23%); Skokie Commons (May 15, 2015,
  ~$43,800, ten-year mortgage at 3.31%); a 90% interest in Townlake of Coppell (May 22,
  2015, ~$43,200, five-year mortgage at 3.25%); AQ Rittenhouse (Jul 30, 2015, ~$51,000,
  ten-year mortgage at 3.65%); Whitestone Market (Sep 30, 2015, ~$51,500, cash on hand);
  O'Hare Industrial Portfolio (Sep 30, 2015, ~$71,000, cash on hand); an approximate 28%
  interest in a newly formed fund, Madison NYC Core Retail Partners, L.P., which itself
  acquired an approximate 49% interest in entities owning 15 New York City-area retail
  properties (Dec 8, 2015, ~$85,600 for the registrant's portion); 140 Park Avenue (Dec
  21, 2015, ~$45,600, cash on hand); and Maui Mall (Dec 22, 2015, ~$91,100, funded partly
  by a $37,000 line-of-credit draw). [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The registrant states these 2015 acquisitions "are in keeping with the investment
  strategy we began over three years ago," describing continuation of an established
  ongoing acquisition program rather than a one-time combination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## What crossed at each acquisition and what did not
- Controlling intent: acquisition decisions are directed by the external Advisor under
  the standing Advisory Agreement rather than by a controlling parent that itself
  operates the acquired properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Legal vehicle: the registrant remained the same Maryland corporation across all 2015
  acquisitions; properties were acquired directly or through fractional/joint interests
  (e.g., the 90% Townlake of Coppell interest; the ~28% Retail Fund interest, where
  "LaSalle advises clients representing an approximate 90% ownership in the Retail Fund").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Customer-facing product: each acquired property came with its own pre-existing tenant
  roster that continued after acquisition — e.g., DFW Distribution Center "100% leased to
  nine tenants"; O'Hare Industrial Portfolio "92% occupied"; Maui Mall "91% leased" and
  anchored by Whole Foods, Regal Cinemas, CVS, and TJ Maxx; 140 Park Avenue "100% leased
  for 15 years to Summit Medical Group." [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Operational processes: on-site management/leasing for newly acquired properties is
  assigned to third-party service providers selected under the registrant's standing
  provider-selection practice, not retained from any acquired seller's own operating
  staff. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- People/organization: no transfer of an acquired business's employees or management team
  is described for any of the 2015 property acquisitions; the disclosed operating model is
  external management (Advisor) plus hired third-party property managers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Financing arrangements bridging the acquisitions (closing-time only)
- Acquisitions were funded through a mix of new asset-level mortgage loans, draws on a
  revolving line of credit, and cash on hand; the line of credit was expanded twice during
  2015, increasing the available balance from $40,000 to $100,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or similar substrate-bridging
  contract is described for any 2015 property acquisition; each acquisition's financing and
  operating handoff (to a selected property manager) is documented as a standalone,
  property-level arrangement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Structural summary (closing-era only)
- The drawn 8-K records only annual-meeting voting results and a minor charter amendment.
  The registrant's actual 2015 growth activity was a series of separately negotiated,
  separately financed direct real-estate acquisitions by an externally advised,
  continuously offered non-traded REIT, with day-to-day property operations assigned to
  hired third-party managers under a standing per-property selection practice rather than
  through any single business combination that would carry over a target company's own
  workforce or management team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001314152-15-000067, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001314152-16-000156, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
