# Structural sub-dossier — Quicksilver Resources Inc. Chapter 11 §363 U.S. oil and gas asset sale to BlueStone Natural Resources II, LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Nature of the drawn filing
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 1.01 that on September 15, 2015
  Quicksilver Resources Canada Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Quicksilver Resources
  Inc. (the "Company"), entered into a Third Forbearance Agreement with JPMorgan Chase
  Bank, N.A. (as global and Canadian administrative agent) and the lenders party to the
  Canadian Credit Agreement, extending forbearance from remedies for defaults related to
  the Company's chapter 11 filings until the earlier of December 15, 2015 and specified
  triggering events. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000090, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Under Item 7.01 the same 8-K reports that on September 17, 2015 the Company issued a
  press release announcing commencement of a marketing and sale process to sell
  substantially all or a portion of its U.S. and Canadian assets; the U.S. process was
  initiated by filing a motion in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware
  seeking approval of bidding procedures to commence a sale process under section 363 of
  the Bankruptcy Code, expected to be heard on October 6, 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000090, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The drawn 8-K thus announces the initiation of the sale process; it does not set out the
  deal terms. The structure of the consummated transaction is sourced from the Company's
  contemporaneous closing-era primary filings below.

## Seller's bankruptcy (a closing-time structural feature of the §363 sale)
- On March 17, 2015 the Company and its subsidiaries Barnett Shale Operating LLC, Cowtown
  Drilling, Inc., Cowtown Gas Processing L.P., Cowtown Pipeline Funding, Inc., Cowtown
  Pipeline L.P., Cowtown Pipeline Management, Inc., Makarios Resources International
  Holdings LLC, Makarios Resources International Inc., QPP Holdings LLC, QPP Parent LLC,
  Quicksilver Production Partners GP LLC, Quicksilver Production Partners LP, and Silver
  Stream Pipeline Company LLC each filed a voluntary petition for relief under chapter 11
  in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, jointly administered as
  In re: Quicksilver Resources Inc., et al., Case No. 15-10585, with the debtors operating
  as debtors-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000056, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Company's wholly owned subsidiary Quicksilver Resources Canada Inc. was not among
  the chapter 11 debtors (a "Non-Filer"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000056, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The chapter 11 filing constituted an event of default accelerating the Company's debt,
  including a U.S. Credit Agreement ($137 million principal), a Canadian Credit Agreement
  ($98 million), a senior secured second lien term loan ($625 million), second lien notes
  due 2019 ($200 million), 9.125% senior notes due 2019 ($298 million), 11.00% senior
  notes due 2021 ($325 million), and 7.125% senior subordinated notes due 2016 ($350
  million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000056, Form 8-K Item 2.04]

## The transaction as structured
- On January 22, 2016 Quicksilver Resources Inc. and its U.S. subsidiaries (together the
  "Sellers") entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement with BlueStone Natural Resources II,
  LLC (the "Buyer") under which the Buyer agreed to purchase substantially all of the
  Sellers' U.S. oil and gas assets for a cash purchase price of $245.0 million, subject to
  adjustment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The signed exhibit identifies the Sellers party to the Asset Purchase Agreement dated
  January 22, 2016 as Quicksilver Resources Inc., Cowtown Gas Processing L.P. and Cowtown
  Pipeline L.P., with BlueStone Natural Resources II, LLC as Buyer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 9.01 / EX-2.1]
- The sale was structured under section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code following a
  Bankruptcy-Court-approved auction: on January 23, 2016 the Company announced the
  Sellers' completion of a successful auction at which the Buyer was declared to have the
  highest or best bid; Barnett Shale Gas LLC was approved as the backup bidder. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 8.01]
- The Sellers obtained final approval of the transactions via a Sale Order entered by the
  U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware on January 27, 2016; consummation was
  subject to customary closing conditions and expected to close on or before March 31,
  2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company stated it expected the shares of its existing common stock to be cancelled in
  the chapter 11 proceedings with no recovery to holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Consideration mechanics (as structured)
- The $245.0 million cash price was subject to adjustments for unobtained consents, casualty
  loss/condemnation between signing and closing, exercised preferential purchase rights, and
  a "true up" allocating revenues and expenses between Sellers (pre-effective time) and Buyer
  (at/after effective time, defined as the first day of the month of Closing). [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Buyer was required to post a deposit equal to twenty percent of the purchase price,
  payable to Sellers as liquidated damages on a Buyer breach; the Sellers were required to
  fund a $15,000,000 escrow as the Buyer's sole and exclusive recovery for a Seller breach,
  supplemented on a fiduciary-out termination by a Superpriority Administrative Expense Claim
  not to exceed $24,500,000. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- On the Closing Date the Sellers were to sell to the Buyer, free and clear of all
  Encumbrances (other than Permitted Encumbrances), the "Oil and Gas Assets," defined to
  include all of the Sellers' right, title and interest in all Leases (other than Excluded
  Leases) and Mineral Interests (other than Excluded Mineral Interests) and related assets.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §2.1 (Purchase and Sale)]
- The Buyer was to purchase the Oil and Gas Assets and assume the Assumed Liabilities upon
  the terms set forth in the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §2.1]

## What did NOT cross — excluded assets, contracts and liabilities
- The agreement carved out "Excluded Assets" and "Excluded Liabilities" retained by the
  Sellers, and defined "Excluded Contracts" as all contracts to which any Seller is a party
  other than the "Assigned Contracts," and "Excluded Leases" as those leases described on a
  schedule (Schedule 2.2(h)). [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §§2.2, 2.4, 2.5 and definitions]
- The exhibits and schedules to the Asset Purchase Agreement (including the schedules
  identifying Excluded Leases/Mineral Interests, Excluded Surface Interests, Assumed
  Accounts Payables and Cure Costs) were omitted from the filing pursuant to Item 601(b)(2)
  of Regulation S-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Item 9.01]

## People / management (as structured)
- Employees did not transfer automatically: within two business days of signing the Sellers
  were to send WARN notices to all of their U.S. employees, and the Buyer "shall have the
  right, but not the obligation, to offer employment to such U.S. employees of Sellers and
  their Subsidiaries as determined by Buyer on terms and conditions determined by Buyer";
  employees accepting such an offer became "Buyer Employees." [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.6(a) (Employee Matters)]
- The Sellers agreed to continue to employ each of their U.S. employees who did not become
  Buyer Employees (excluding any who voluntarily separated) for sixty (60) days following
  the date the WARN notices were sent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §8.6(a)]

## Consummation of the sale (closing)
- The disposition of substantially all of the Company's U.S. oil and gas assets closed in
  April 2016: in connection with the closing, on April 13, 2016 Stan Page was removed from
  the office of Senior Vice President - U.S. Operations of the Company, effective April 15,
  2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000137, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Effective April 16, 2016 the Company placed its remaining executive officers (Glenn
  Darden, President and CEO; Vanessa LaGatta, SVP-CFO and Treasurer; Romy Massey, VP-Chief
  Accounting Officer; Anne Self, VP-Human Resources) on hourly compensation arrangements.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000137, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Out of a District of Delaware chapter 11 estate (Case No. 15-10585), the Company sold
  substantially all of its U.S. oil and gas assets (the "Oil and Gas Assets" — leases,
  mineral interests and related assets) to BlueStone Natural Resources II, LLC via a section
  363 court-approved auction for $245.0 million cash under an Asset Purchase Agreement dated
  January 22, 2016 (Sale Order January 27, 2016), free and clear of encumbrances, while
  contracts other than assigned contracts, excluded leases, retained cash/records, and
  liabilities other than the defined assumed liabilities stayed with the estate; employees
  did not transfer automatically (WARN notices to all U.S. staff; Buyer had a discretionary
  right to offer employment), and the disposition closed in April 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000108, Form 8-K Items 1.01/8.01, EX-2.1 §§2.1-2.5, 8.6] [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-16-000137, Form 8-K Item 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001060990-15-000056, Form 8-K Items 1.03/2.04]
