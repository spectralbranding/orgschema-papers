## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Select Income REIT (CIK 0001537667) filed a Form 15-12B on 2018-12-31 to deregister
  its common shares under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and filed no further
  periodic reports (no 10-K, 10-Q, or 8-K) under that CIK after that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-075364, Form 15-12B]
- No annual report (Form 10-K) or other periodic report has been filed by Select
  Income REIT as a standalone registrant since deregistration; consequently there is
  no audited public financial record specific to Select Income REIT as a standalone
  entity for the 2021-2023 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-075364, Form 15-12B]
- The surviving public company that absorbed Select Income REIT (formerly Government
  Properties Income Trust, CIK 0001456772) is recorded in SEC EDGAR's company records
  as having changed its name to "Office Properties Income Trust" effective 2019-01-02,
  and it continues to file periodic reports under that CIK. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0001456772&type=10-K&dateb=&owner=include&count=40&output=atom]
- Office Properties Income Trust's exhibit index in its fiscal-year 2022 Form 10-K
  identifies certain outstanding senior notes as obligations of "the Company (as
  successor to Select Income REIT)," confirming Select Income REIT's debt obligations
  were assumed by the surviving company rather than separately retired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022, Exhibit Index]

## Reporting structure of the surviving company (no separate disclosure of the acquired business)
- In its fiscal-year 2022 Form 10-K, Office Properties Income Trust discloses: "We
  operate in one business segment: ownership of real estate properties." [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 ("We operate in one business segment: ownership of real estate properties.")]
- The same Form 10-K's accounting-policy notes repeat that the company "operate[s] in
  one business segment: direct ownership of real estate properties," with no
  disaggregation of results by the pre-merger constituent entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements ("Segment Reporting. We operate in one business segment: direct ownership of real estate properties.")]
- Because the surviving company does not separately disclose a segment, subsidiary
  financial statements, or other trajectory specific to the properties and operations
  it obtained from Select Income REIT, no realized-outcome scalar can be isolated to
  the acquired business from this or any subsequent Office Properties Income Trust
  filing reviewed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022]

## Combined-enterprise record (not attributable to the acquired business specifically)
- For informational context only (this reflects the entire combined enterprise,
  including the pre-existing business of the surviving company and post-merger
  acquisition/disposition activity, and is not isolable to the business obtained from
  Select Income REIT): the surviving company's consolidated Form 10-K for fiscal year
  2022 reports a net loss of $6,109 thousand (fiscal year 2021 comparative: net loss
  of $8,180 thousand) and a loss on impairment of real estate of $21,820 thousand for
  fiscal year 2022 (fiscal year 2021 comparative: $62,420 thousand). [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022, Management's Discussion and Analysis]
- The same filing reports that during fiscal year 2022 the combined enterprise sold 18
  properties (including one leasable land parcel) totaling approximately 2,326,000
  rentable square feet for an aggregate sales price of $211,020 thousand, and states
  that "the pace of our dispositions has moderated" due to commercial real estate
  market conditions including rising interest rates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 ("During the year ended December 31, 2022, we sold 18 properties... the pace of our dispositions has moderated...")]
- No "going concern" qualification or statement of "substantial doubt" about the
  surviving company's ability to continue as a going concern appears in this fiscal
  year 2022 Form 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022]

  disclosed breakout of the business obtained from Select Income REIT, there is no
  public 3-5-year outcome record that can be attributed specifically to the acquired
  business; the outcome for the acquired business is coded **uncertain** on that
  basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001456772-23-000012, Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired registrant ceased independent public reporting at closing (Form
  15-12B, filed 2018-12-31), and its debt and operations were absorbed into a
  surviving company that reports as one undifferentiated real-estate segment; the
  surviving combined enterprise's own consolidated results for the ~2021-2023 window
