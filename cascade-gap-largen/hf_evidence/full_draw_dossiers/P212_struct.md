## Transaction and parties
- On February 21, 2017, Tronox Limited entered into a transaction agreement with The National Titanium Dioxide Company Limited ("Cristal") and Cristal Inorganic Chemicals Netherlands Cooperatief W.A. ("Cristal Netherlands"), under which Tronox agreed to acquire Cristal's TiO2 business, primarily through acquisition of the stock of Cristal Inorganic Chemicals Netherlands BV ("Cristal BV"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- Tronox is a public limited company registered in Western Australia and headquartered in Stamford, Connecticut, operating two vertically integrated mining and inorganic chemical businesses (TiO2 pigment and Alkali/soda ash); Cristal is a privately-held company registered in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, headquartered in Jeddah, with TiO2 manufacturing, mining, and research facilities in seven countries across five continents. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- Tronox shareholders were asked to approve, at a special meeting held October 2, 2017, the issuance of 37,580,000 Class A ordinary shares to Cristal Netherlands as required under NYSE Listed Company Manual Section 312.03 and Australian Corporations Act Section 611 Item 7. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and consideration
- Pursuant to the transaction agreement, Cristal and Cristal Netherlands undertook a restructuring reorganizing the TiO2 business assets/operations under Cristal BV (and Cristal Australia Pty Ltd, "CAP"), while Cristal separately established a new entity in Saudi Arabia to hold certain KSA-specific assets and operations not transferred; following the restructuring, the Seller (Cristal Netherlands) would own the BV Shares (and CAP shares), which it transferred to Tronox at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, Tronox agreed to (i) make an aggregate cash payment of $1,673 million (subject to working-capital/cash/non-current-liability adjustments) to Cristal and Cristal Netherlands, and (ii) issue 37,580,000 Class A Shares to Cristal Netherlands; the share portion represented approximately $542 million based on the pre-announcement closing price. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon completion, pre-transaction Tronox shareholders were expected to own approximately 76% and Cristal Netherlands approximately 24% of Tronox's outstanding Class A and Class B Shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- Tronox announced it would fund the majority of the cash consideration through proceeds of a separate, concurrently announced sale of its own Alkali business to Genesis Energy, L.P. for $1.325 billion, with the remainder from additional indebtedness and/or cash on hand; closing of the Cristal transaction was not conditioned on completion of the Alkali sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle change concurrent with closing (re-domicile)
- On March 27, 2019, immediately before completion of the Cristal acquisition, Tronox Limited completed a "Re-Domicile Transaction" that changed the jurisdiction of incorporation of the group's publicly traded parent from Australia to the United Kingdom, with Tronox Limited continuing as a wholly-owned subsidiary of the newly formed English public limited company, Tronox Holdings plc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006327, Form 8-K, Item 1.01, Amendment No. 2 to Transaction Agreement]
- To account for completion of the Re-Domicile Transaction, the parties amended the transaction agreement so that the 37,580,000 "New Consideration Shares" would be issued by Tronox Holdings plc (the new UK parent) rather than the original Australian entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006327, Form 8-K]

## Governance and management continuity provisions (agreed pre-closing)
- Upon consummation, Tronox agreed to appoint two Class A Directors designated by Cristal's shareholder parties (or one, if their holding fell in a lower threshold band); the Tronox board would remain the same overall size (nine directors), so two incumbent Class A Directors were designated to resign before closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- "The current executive officers of Tronox are expected to remain unchanged" following the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- Tronox and Cristal agreed there would be "appropriate integration committees, including to consider certain employee related matters," with committee representatives designated by Cristal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- The Tronox board's Human Resources and Compensation Committee created an Integration Incentive Award program covering approximately thirty employees with specific integration tasks, and granted performance-based restricted share units to six executive employees, vesting on achievement of 80-100% of publicly announced synergies within two years of closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction agreement did not obligate Tronox to replicate, replace, or cash out equity awards of Cristal employees, nor did it prevent amendment/termination of Cristal benefit plans for continuing employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, Tronox entered a shareholders agreement with Cristal, Cristal Netherlands, and Cristal's underlying shareholders, granting board-nomination rights tied to a beneficial-ownership threshold, preemptive rights, a two-year transfer restriction (subject to a carve-out of up to ~4% resaleable shares), and non-competition obligations on certain Cristal shareholders for two years post-closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006913, Form 8-K, Item 1.01]

## Closing-time divestitures required as a condition of completing the acquisition
- Completion (April 10, 2019) coincided with an FTC Order and Decision permitting the transaction to proceed conditioned on divestiture of Cristal's North American TiO2 business to INEOS Enterprises, with that divestiture scheduled to close May 1, 2019; a separately announced divestiture of an 8120 paper-laminate grade product line to Venator Materials PLC, approved by the European Commission in August 2018, was also pending closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006913, Form 8-K, EX-99.1 press release]
- Cristal was described at closing as operating eight manufacturing plants in seven countries on five continents and employing approximately 4,100 people worldwide. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006913, Form 8-K, EX-99.1 press release]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a stock acquisition of Cristal's global TiO2 operating entities (Cristal BV and, contingently, Cristal Australia Pty Ltd) for mixed cash-and-stock consideration ($1,673 million cash plus 37,580,000 Class A Shares, ~24% of the combined company) with (a) a contemporaneous acquirer-side legal-vehicle change (Tronox's re-domicile from Australia to a new UK parent, folded into the share-issuance mechanics), (b) a pre-closing carve-out that routed certain Cristal KSA-specific assets/operations to a separately established Saudi entity rather than to the acquired stock, and (c) FTC/EU-mandated divestiture of the acquired North American TiO2 operations and an 8120 paper-laminate product line to third parties (INEOS and Venator, respectively) as conditions of closing — meaning part of the nominally "acquired" product/asset base was structured to pass through Tronox and out again at or near closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-001809, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-19-006913, Form 8-K]
