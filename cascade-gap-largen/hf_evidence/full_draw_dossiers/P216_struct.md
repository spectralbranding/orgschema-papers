# Structural sub-dossier — TerraForm Global, Inc. merger with affiliates of Brookfield Asset Management Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On March 6, 2017, TerraForm Global, Inc. (a Delaware corporation) entered into a
  merger agreement providing for the merger of BRE GLBL Holdings Inc. ("Merger Sub"),
  a wholly owned subsidiary of Orion US Holdings 1 L.P. ("Parent"), with and into the
  Company, with the Company surviving as a subsidiary of Parent; Merger Sub and Parent
  are entities formed by affiliates of Brookfield Asset Management Inc. ("Brookfield").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of Class A common stock were entitled to receive $5.10 in cash per share,
  without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- Prior to the merger, SunEdison, Inc. indirectly held 100% of the Company's Class B
  common stock and approximately 98.2% of combined voting power. Per a settlement
  agreement among SunEdison, the Company, and affiliates, immediately before the
  merger's effective time SunEdison exchanged its Class B units in subsidiary TerraForm
  Global, LLC for Class A shares representing 25% of Class A shares (fully diluted);
  all Class B shares were then automatically cancelled and outstanding incentive
  distribution rights were transferred to Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on December 28, 2017 (after the parties extended the merger
  agreement's termination date from December 6, 2017 to March 6, 2018 on December 5,
  2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-17-045069, 8-K; SEC accession 0000950157-17-001639, 8-K]

## What the acquired entity comprised at closing (product/asset tier)
- The Company described itself as a globally diversified renewable energy company
  owning long-term contracted solar and wind power plants in Brazil, China, India,
  Malaysia, South Africa, Thailand, and Uruguay, with aggregate net capacity of 919.2 MW
  as of August 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- Brookfield described itself as a global alternative asset manager with approximately
  $250 billion in assets under management and, within renewable power specifically, as
  owning and operating over 10,000 megawatts of assets ($30 billion in power assets
  across eight countries) with over 2,000 operating employees holding expertise in
  asset-level operations and maintenance, power marketing, and regulatory oversight.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]

## Process/people tier at closing — pre-existing dependence on a third party
- Historically, the personnel who managed the Company's operations (other than the
  Chairman/Interim CEO and two former officers) had been employees of SunEdison, with
  their services provided to the Company under a management services agreement and
  project-level asset management and operations-and-maintenance agreements with
  SunEdison and its affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- On September 7, 2017, the Company's operating subsidiary entered a Transition
  Services Agreement (TSA) with SunEdison covering information technology, tax, human
  resources, treasury, finance, and controllership services, applied retroactively from
  February 1, 2017 and terminating no later than October 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- Separately, the settlement agreement referenced an "agreed-upon services" workstream
  for the "transfer of GAM [asset management] operations" from SunEdison to the
  Company's operating subsidiary, with costs to be negotiated and paid to SunEdison —
  i.e., the insourcing of the SunEdison-run asset-management function was a distinct,
  still-executing workstream at the time of the proxy. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the merger agreement, Parent committed that for one year after closing,
  continuing employees would receive base pay/bonus opportunity, pension/welfare
  benefits, and severance no less favorable than immediately before the merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent background carried into the transaction
- Until November 2015 a majority of the Company's board were SunEdison employees; the
  board was then reconstituted with independent directors. SunEdison (and certain
  affiliates, not the Company) filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on April 21,
  2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- At the merger's effective time, all ten pre-merger directors resigned and were
  replaced by the three directors of Merger Sub (Jean Burgess, Julian Deschatelets,
  Ningkun Wang); none of the resignations was for cause or the result of disagreement.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-17-001639, 8-K]
- On December 28, 2017, in connection with consummation of the merger, the Company
  requested that Nasdaq file a Form 25 Notification of Removal from Listing and/or
  Registration to remove the Class A shares from listing and deregister them under
  Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-17-001639, 8-K]
- The merger constituted a "Change of Control" under the indenture governing the
  operating subsidiary's 9.75% Senior Notes due 2022, triggering a required offer to
  repurchase the notes at 101.0% of principal plus accrued interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-17-001639, 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash-out merger (Merger Sub into the Company,
  Company surviving as a Brookfield-affiliate subsidiary) of a multi-country renewable
  power portfolio, layered onto a pre-existing structural condition in which the
  customer-facing product (the power plants) had historically been operated by
  personnel and service agreements belonging to a different corporate parent
  (SunEdison) rather than the Company's own organization; that dependency was bridged
  at closing by a time-limited Transition Services Agreement and a separately tracked,
  not-yet-complete transfer of the asset-management ("GAM") function from SunEdison to
  the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001567619-17-002164, Form DEFM14A]
