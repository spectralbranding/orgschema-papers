# Structural sub-dossier — Xenith Bankshares, Inc. merger with Union Bankshares Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Union Bankshares Corporation ("Union") and Xenith Bankshares, Inc. ("Xenith"), both
  Virginia bank holding companies headquartered in Richmond, Virginia, entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Reorganization dated May 19, 2017, with a related Plan of
  Merger, under which Xenith would merge with and into Union, with Union surviving.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- No separate merger-sub entity was interposed: the joint proxy statement/prospectus
  describes a direct statutory merger of Xenith into Union rather than a reverse
  triangular structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration was a fixed exchange ratio: each share of Xenith common stock was
  converted into the right to receive 0.9354 shares of Union common stock (cash paid
  only in lieu of fractional shares), implying an aggregate value of approximately
  $687.2 million based on the last trading day before announcement and approximately
  $678.1 million based on the closing price the day before the proxy statement date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was completed on January 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-000171, Form 8-K]
- Following completion, Union was expected to have approximately $11.9 billion in
  assets, $9.2 billion in deposits, and $8.9 billion in gross loans on a combined
  basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]

## What Xenith comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Xenith owned all of the stock of its subsidiary bank, Xenith Bank, a Virginia
  chartered commercial bank offering banking products and services to individuals and
  businesses in Virginia, Maryland, North Carolina, and the greater Washington, D.C.
  area through 40 full-service branches and two loan production offices.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle tier: subsidiary bank merger
- Shortly after the parent-level merger's effective time, Xenith Bank, the Virginia
  chartered bank subsidiary of Xenith, merged with and into Union Bank & Trust, the
  Virginia chartered bank subsidiary of Union, for no additional consideration and
  pursuant to a bank merger agreement attached as an exhibit to the merger agreement,
  with Union Bank & Trust surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001144204-18-000171, Form 8-K]
- No transitional-services, operating, or shared-services agreement bridging a
  partially-retained substrate is described in the joint proxy statement/prospectus;
  the transaction was structured as a full parent-level merger followed promptly by
  full subsidiary-bank consolidation rather than a phased carve-out.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent, management, and process tier
- Union's executive officers were expected to continue serving in their current
  positions after the merger; separately, T. Gaylon Layfield, III, Xenith's Chief
  Executive Officer, was to serve as Executive Vice Chairman of Union Bank for a
  transition period following the merger and subsidiary bank merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- Union agreed to cause two directors of Xenith to be appointed to the Union board of
  directors (and, following the subsidiary bank merger, to the Union Bank board) as of
  the effective time; as of the date of the joint proxy statement/prospectus the
  specific directors had not yet been selected. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- Notwithstanding the above continuity provisions, per the closing Form 8-K: all of
  Xenith's directors ceased serving on the Xenith board as of the effective time, and
  each of Xenith's four named executive officers (Layfield; Donna W. Richards,
  President and COO; Thomas W. Osgood, EVP and CFO; and Thomas B. Dix III, EVP
  Commercial Lending) was terminated effective immediately prior to the effective
  time, with the combined company assuming the severance obligations under their
  existing employment agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-000171, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement stated that the two companies and their subsidiaries would
  consult on strategic and operational matters after signing "to facilitate the
  integration of the operations of UBSH and XBKS... and to permit the coordination of
  their related operations on a timely basis." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]
- A risk factor in the joint proxy statement/prospectus stated an intent to "retain
  and integrate the appropriate personnel of Xenith Bank into the operations of Union
  Bank, as well as reducing overlapping bank personnel," and separately warned that
  integration "could result in the loss of key employees" and "disruption of each
  party's ongoing business." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing status at closing
- Xenith requested that NASDAQ suspend trading in Xenith common stock at the close of
  business on December 29, 2017 and file a notification removing Xenith common stock
  from listing and registration under Section 12(b); as a result, Xenith common stock
  ceased trading on NASDAQ. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-000171, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company, fixed-exchange-ratio stock merger of the
  parent holding company (Xenith into Union) with an immediate, no-additional-
  consideration merger of the operating bank subsidiary (Xenith Bank into Union Bank &
  Trust) rather than a phased carve-out bridged by a transitional-services agreement;
  contractual continuity provisions placed two Xenith directors on the Union board and
  the Xenith CEO in a transition role at Union Bank, while the same closing Form 8-K
  documents that all Xenith directors ceased serving and all four Xenith named
  executive officers were formally terminated at the effective time.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-007906, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001144204-18-000171, Form 8-K]
