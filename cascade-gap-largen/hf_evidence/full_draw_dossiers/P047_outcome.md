## Realized trajectory of the venture shortly after inception (pre-window, cited transparently)
- The venture deteriorated rapidly: during 2009 Concord received margin calls on certain
  of its loan facilities requiring it to identify loans for disposal to meet pay-down
  requirements, and recorded impairment losses and loan-loss reserves of $225.1 million
  for the year ended December 31, 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]
- Inland American's share of the venture's net loss reduced the carrying value of its
  investment to $(9.9) million as of December 31, 2009, recorded "up to the amount at
  which the Company approximately believes it is obligated to fund." [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]
- On December 22, 2009, Lexington Realty Trust, Winthrop Realty Trust, Inland American,
  and their respective subsidiaries entered into a settlement agreement resolving the IA
  Sub v. Concord action; it provides, among other things, for termination of any party's
  obligation to contribute capital to Concord, equal allocation of distributions among
  Inland, Lexington and Winthrop, and formation of a new entity to be owned by their
  subsidiaries — conditioned on cancellation of certain CDO bonds held by Concord Debt
  Funding Trust by August 14, 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes]

## Status entering the fixed window

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The venture's realized record concentrated shortly after inception: heavy 2009
  impairments and loan-loss reserves at the venture level ($225.1 million), a reduction of
  the registrant's carrying value to $(9.9) million by December 31, 2009, and a December
  2009 settlement terminating the parties' capital-contribution obligations and forming a
  successor entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-10-000005, Form 10-K (FY2009) financial-statement notes] By the fixed 2012-2014 window the venture is no longer
  separately disclosed (referenced only historically in fiscal 2010, absent from fiscal
  2012), so the venture-specific outcome within the window is uncertain; the registrant
  itself continued to report entity-level net losses (~$69.3 million for fiscal 2012) and
  retained its Inland American name through fiscal 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-11-064003, Form 10-K (FY2010) MD&A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-13-000007, Form 10-K (FY2012)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001307748-15-000008, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
