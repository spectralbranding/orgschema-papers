## Realized fate before the outcome window opened
- The entity was acquired whole: on December 18, 2014, The Pantry, Inc. entered an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with Couche-Tard U.S. Inc. and its wholly owned subsidiary
  CT-US Acquisition Corp., under which the subsidiary merged into the Company, "with the
  Company continuing as the surviving company and a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent";
  Parent is a subsidiary of Alimentation Couche-Tard Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Introduction]
- "The Merger was completed pursuant to the Merger Agreement on March 16, 2015," and each
  outstanding share of common stock "was converted into the right to receive ... $36.75 in
  cash, without interest"; the funds "were from the cash on hand and credit facilities of
  Couche-Tard and its affiliates." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Documented change at the process/organization interface
- At the effective time the pre-merger management and board were removed: "each member of
  the Company's board of directors prior to the Merger voluntarily resigned," replaced by
  the Merger Sub's directors (Kathy Cunnington, Darrell Davis, Geoffrey Haxel), and "each
  of the Company's named executive officers were removed from their offices." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The surviving company's finance/accounting function was folded into the acquirer's
  shared organization: the newly appointed officers were Darrell Davis as President
  (principal executive and operating officer) and Kathy Cunnington as "Vice-President,
  Shared Services North America and Treasurer (principal financial officer and principal
  accounting officer)." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The Company's charter and bylaws were replaced with those of the Merger Sub (retaining
  only the name "The Pantry, Inc."), confirming the entity was absorbed into the acquirer's
  legal form. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 5.03]

## Capital-structure consequences of the change of control
- The merger triggered a "Change of Control" under the indenture governing the Company's
  8.375% Senior Notes due 2020, requiring a change-of-control offer to repurchase the
  "approximately $250 million of the outstanding 2020 Notes at a purchase price equal to
  101% of the principal amount," which the Company stated it intended instead to redeem
  in full. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 2.04]
- Three previously disclosed material credit-facility agreements (the Fourth Amended and
  Restated Credit Agreement, Pledge Agreement and Security Agreement, each dated August 3,
  2012, with Wells Fargo Bank as administrative agent) were terminated in connection with
  the merger's consummation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 1.02]

## Termination of public reporting (why the outcome window has no separate record)
- On March 16, 2015 the Company notified NASDAQ of its intent to delist and requested a
  Form 25 filing to delist and deregister the common stock, and stated it would file a
  Form 15 to suspend its reporting obligations under Sections 13 and 15(d) of the Exchange
  Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- The Company filed a Form 15 (Certification and Notice of Termination of Registration)
  dated March 26, 2015, reporting a single holder of record of its common stock; this
  ended its public periodic reporting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-105949, Form 15-12G]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed by the entity after fiscal 2014
  (last annual report accession 0000915862-14-000072, filed December 9, 2014); consequently
  no audited public financial statements exist for the fixed ~2016-2018 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000915862-14-000072, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-105949, Form 15-12G]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The realized record is that the entity was acquired whole for $36.75 per share in cash,
  the merger closing on March 16, 2015; the entire pre-merger board and named executive
  officers were replaced and finance/accounting was moved into the acquirer's "Shared
  Services North America"; the change of control forced repurchase/redemption of the ~$250
  million 2020 Notes and terminated the entity's credit facilities; and the entity delisted
  and deregistered (Form 15 dated March 26, 2015). Because that deregistration preceded the
  fixed ~2016-2018 outcome window, there is no separate public audited record within the
  window, and the case is coded **uncertain** on the outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-093270, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-105949, Form 15-12G]
