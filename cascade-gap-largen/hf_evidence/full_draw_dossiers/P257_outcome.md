## A subsequent, separately reported combination within the window
- On June 7, 2020, South State (the "Company") acquired all outstanding common stock of
  CenterState Bank Corporation ("CSFL") in a stock-for-stock transaction: CSFL merged
  with and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation,
  and immediately thereafter South State Bank ("SSB") merged with and into CenterState
  Bank ("CSB"), with CSB continuing as the surviving bank and changing its name to South
  State Bank, National Association; CSFL shareholders received 0.3001 shares of the
  Company's common stock per CSFL share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001976, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Following this second combination, the Company's total assets increased 137.4% and
  shareholders' equity increased 95.9% during 2020, and full-time-equivalent employees
  grew to 5,184 as of December 31, 2020 (compared with 2,547 a year earlier); the
  Company reported 285 full-service branches as of December 31, 2020, concentrated in
  Florida (36%), South Carolina (34%), Georgia (17%), North Carolina (6%), Alabama (6%)
  and Virginia (1%). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001976, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Because this later combination occurred within the outcome window and was reported
  on a fully consolidated basis together with all prior acquisitions (including Park
  Sterling), the reviewed filing does not permit isolation of a Park-Sterling-specific
  post-close trajectory distinct from the Company's overall consolidated results.

## Realized financial scalar (consolidated, FY2020)
- Net income available to common shareholders decreased 35.3% to $120.6 million in
  2020, compared with $186.5 million in 2019 (itself down from $178.9 million in 2018);
  diluted/basic earnings per common share fell to $2.20 in 2020 from $5.40 in 2019.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001976, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The filing attributes the 2020 net-income decline mainly to an increase in the
  provision for credit losses (including day-one CECL-related provisions recorded upon
  the 2020 combination) and to expenses related to that combination, alongside effects
  of the COVID-19 pandemic on credit conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001976, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Goodwill grew from $1,002.9 million at the start of 2020 to $1,563.9 million at
  year-end 2020, an addition of $561.0 million attributed to the 2020 combination; the
  Company's own goodwill-impairment testing (as of its April 30, 2020 annual test date
  and again as of December 31, 2020) concluded no impairment charge was necessary,
  notwithstanding that the Company's stock price traded below book value for much of
  2020 amid the pandemic. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001976, Form 10-K FY2020]
