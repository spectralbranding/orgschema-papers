## Reporting entity and public status in the outcome window
- The spun-off registrant reported as "EchoStar Corporation" and remained an SEC reporting company, filing a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- By December 31, 2012, EchoStar operated in three business segments: EchoStar Technologies (digital set-top boxes plus digital broadcast operations), Hughes (satellite broadband and enterprise network services), and EchoStar Satellite Services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Best-documented realized financial scalars (year ended December 31, 2012; dollars in thousands)
- Revenue: $3,121,704 for 2012, up from $2,761,431 (2011) and $2,350,369 (2010). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012), Item 6 Selected Financial Data]
- Operating income: $99,886 for 2012 (2011: $80,838; 2010: $142,325). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012), Item 6]
- Net income attributable to EchoStar: $211,048 for 2012 (2011: $3,639; 2010: $204,358); the company remained profitable in the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012), Item 6]

## Realized events within the outcome window
- On June 8, 2011, EchoStar completed the acquisition of Hughes Communications, Inc. and its subsidiaries, which became the new Hughes segment; financial statements on and after June 9, 2011 give effect to the acquisition, and the company stated its FY2012 results were "not comparable" to prior years for this reason. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- In 2011 the company determined the carrying amount of its suspended CMBStar satellite was not recoverable and recognized a $33 million impairment, reducing its carrying amount to an estimated fair value of $19 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- A second-quarter 2012 step-one goodwill impairment test found the estimated fair values of the Hughes reporting units exceeded their carrying amounts; the company concluded Hughes goodwill was not impaired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Product/customer interface over time
- The former parent (now DISH Network Corporation) remained the dominant customer: DISH Network accounted for 49.5% of total EchoStar revenue in 2012, down from 59.9% (2011) and 82.5% (2010). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Within the EchoStar Technologies segment specifically, DISH Network accounted for 76.9% of segment revenue in 2012 (2011: 79.4%; 2010: 82.8%); Bell TV in Canada accounted for 13.4%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Effective January 1, 2012, EchoStar entered a new receiver agreement with DISH Network, expiring December 31, 2014, under which DISH Network had the right but not the obligation to purchase set-top boxes at cost-plus pricing; the company disclosed DISH Network "has no obligations to continue to purchase our products" and the relationship "could be terminated or substantially curtailed with little or no advance notice." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The EchoStar Technologies segment continued to provide digital broadcast operations (uplinking/downlinking, transmission, signal processing, conditional access) "primarily to DISH Network." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Process/organization interface over time
- Employee headcount grew to approximately 4,000 as of December 31, 2012 (from approximately 1,500 disclosed at separation). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- DISH Network continued to provide EchoStar with certain management and administrative services, "which include the services of certain employees of DISH Network," under a Management Services Agreement and a Professional Services Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Mark W. Jackson continued as President of EchoStar Technologies L.L.C. (a role he had held since 2004), overseeing day-to-day operations of that segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Regarding the Hughes acquisition, the company disclosed that integration was "complex, time-consuming, and expensive," could "disrupt our business or result in the loss of ... customers or key employees," and that "issues arising from the integration ..., including, among others, differences in corporate culture, may affect our ability to retain technically skilled employees of either company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2011-2013 outcome window the spun-off entity (EchoStar Corporation) remained a going, profitable, publicly-reporting concern: FY2012 revenue of $3.12 billion and net income of $211.0 million, no goodwill impairment, and headcount roughly tripled to ~4,000. Its realized trajectory included a June 2011 acquisition (Hughes Communications) that added a distinct broadband/enterprise business and segment, a 2011 $33 million satellite impairment, a continued (though declining, 82.5% → 49.5% of total revenue) dependence on the former parent DISH Network as its largest customer under a terminable cost-plus receiver agreement, and continued reliance on DISH Network personnel for certain management and administrative services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-13-011985, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
