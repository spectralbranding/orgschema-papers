# Structural sub-dossier — Ford Motor Company China assembly joint-venture restructuring (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn source, a Form 8-K filed 2012-03-15, reports Ford Motor Company's entry
  into a Seventh Amendment to its 2006 revolving Credit Agreement — extending revolving
  commitments from $8.9 billion maturing November 30, 2013 to $9.0 billion maturing
  November 30, 2015, and amending several financial covenants (restricted-payments
  baskets, incremental-facilities cap, asset-sale and lien covenants). It is a
  corporate financing event and does not describe any joint venture, acquisition, or
  divestiture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-12-000015, Form 8-K Items 1.01/2.03]
- The transaction analyzed below — a restructuring of Ford's China assembly joint
  venture — is sourced instead from Ford's contemporaneous FY2011 and FY2012 Forms
  10-K, the annual reports bracketing the closing event. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-12-000007, Form 10-K (FY2011)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Prior to closing, Changan Ford Mazda Automobile Corporation, Ltd. ("CFMA") was a
  three-party joint venture producing and distributing Ford, Mazda, and Volvo
  passenger-vehicle models in China, held 35% by Ford, 15% by Mazda, and 50% by
  Chongqing Changan Automobile Co., Ltd. ("Changan"), operating from facilities in
  Chongqing and Nanjing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-12-000007, Form 10-K (FY2011)]
- On November 30, 2012, CFMA was restructured into two independent 50/50 joint
  ventures: Changan Ford Automobile Corporation, Ltd. ("CAF"), between Ford and
  Changan, and Changan Mazda Automobile Corporation, Ltd. ("CAM"/"CMA"), between Mazda
  and Changan; Changan remained a 50% partner in each resulting venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Immediately after the split, Ford and Mazda fully exchanged their respective
  interests in the two joint ventures, so that Ford's ownership in the combined
  Chongqing operation rose from 35% to 50% (as CAF) while Mazda's interest moved
  entirely to the separate Nanjing-based venture (CMA). [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (tier level)
- The customer-facing product/asset split by location: CFMA's Nanjing operations
  transferred to CMA, while CAF retained the Chongqing facilities and continued to
  produce and distribute Ford passenger vehicle models (plus Volvo models). [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The legal vehicle changed form rather than merely changing hands: CFMA was renamed
  CAF (Ford/Changan only) and a new, separately named entity CMA (Mazda/Changan only)
  was constituted to hold the Nanjing assets and interests previously shared three ways. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Controlling intent at CAF did not change hands to a new outside party: Changan
  remained the 50% co-controlling partner throughout, and Ford — already a JV partner
  pre-closing — simply absorbed Mazda's exiting share of the Chongqing entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The retrieved filings do not disclose any transfer of CFMA/CAF employees, management,
  or organizational personnel as part of the split; no headcount or staffing detail
  attaching to the restructuring is given. [UNVERIFIED: employee/management transfer detail for the CAF/CMA split]
- A related three-party engine venture, Changan Ford Mazda Engine Company, Ltd.
  ("CFME") — Ford 25%, Mazda 25%, Changan 50%, located in Nanjing — was NOT part of the
  restructuring and continued unchanged as a three-way venture producing engines for
  both Ford- and Mazda-brand vehicles. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Bridging arrangement at closing (operational process substrate)
- CMA agreed to continue assembling vehicles for CAF as a contract manufacturer until
  2014, bridging the physical separation of the Nanjing production processes from
  CAF's now-separate Chongqing-based legal entity and ownership structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- At the time of the FY2012 report, CAF had two additional vehicle assembly plants, an
  engine plant, and a transmission plant under construction to support further regional
  growth. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Consideration exchanged at closing
- Upon the interest exchange, Ford de-recognized the historical carrying value of its
  equity investment in CMA ($115 million), increased its equity investment in CAF by
  the fair value of the interest received ($740 million), and recognized a fourth-
  quarter-2012 pre-tax gain of $625 million in Automotive interest income and other
  income/(expense), net. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The drawn 8-K was an unrelated corporate credit-facility amendment; the actual
  closing event was a same-day-partner reorganization of an existing three-way China
  assembly joint venture into two 50/50 ventures, in which Ford's legal vehicle and
  ownership percentage changed (35% to 50% of the Chongqing-based CAF) while its
  co-venture partner Changan's stake was unchanged, the Nanjing production process
  moved to a newly separated Mazda/Changan entity, and that entity was bound by a
  contract-manufacturing bridge to continue supplying CAF through 2014; no employee or
  management transfer was disclosed. [SRC: SEC accession 0000037996-13-000014, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
