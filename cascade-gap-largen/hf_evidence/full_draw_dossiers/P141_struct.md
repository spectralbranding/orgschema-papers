# Structural sub-dossier — James River Coal Company Chapter 11 §363 asset sale to Blackhawk Mining (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On April 7, 2014, James River Coal Company ("the Company") and its wholly-owned
  subsidiaries (together, the "Debtors") filed voluntary petitions for reorganization
  under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the
  Eastern District of Virginia (Richmond Division); the cases are jointly administered
  under the caption "In re: James River Coal Company, et al. (Case No. 14-31848)."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- On May 9, 2014 the Bankruptcy Court entered an Order approving the Strategic Transaction
  Bidding Procedures, the Bid Deadlines and the Auction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- On August 15, 2014 the Debtors entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement (the "Agreement")
  with JR Acquisition, LLC, a wholly owned subsidiary of Blackhawk Mining LLC (together,
  "Blackhawk"), and selected the Agreement to serve as the Stalking Horse Bid for the
  purchase of the Debtors' mining complexes referred to as the Hampden Complex (including
  the assets of Debtor Logan & Kanawha Coal Company, LLC), the Hazard Complex (other than
  the assets of Debtor Laurel Mountain Resources LLC) and the Triad Complex (the "Purchased
  Assets"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction type and pricing mechanics (as structured)
- The sale was structured as a sale of assets under sections 363 and 365 of the Bankruptcy
  Code, free and clear of encumbrances (other than permitted encumbrances), through a
  court-approved bidding-procedures and public-auction process with Blackhawk as stalking-
  horse bidder. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-2.1 (recitals; definition of "Sale Order")]
- The initial Agreement contemplated Blackhawk paying $20.0 million cash plus a $25.0 million
  third-lien secured promissory note, and delivering a $5.0 million second-lien secured
  promissory note to a Debtors' lessor in lieu of cash cure costs — approximately $50 million
  plus assumption of certain liabilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A public auction was held August 18-21, 2014; Blackhawk increased its consideration to
  $52 million plus assumption of certain liabilities and, on August 21, 2014, its revised
  bid ($20.0 million cash, a $27.0 million third-lien note, and a $5.0 million second-lien
  note for cure costs) was selected as the winning bid. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Agreement was amended by a First Amendment dated August 21, 2014 and a Second
  Amendment dated August 28, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003378, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- The "Purchased Assets" comprised the owned real property; the Assumed Leases and Purchased
  Leased Real Property; all equipment, fixed and tangible assets (including all mobile mining
  equipment, the Sellers' helicopter, and parts/supplies located at loadouts, preparation
  plants, active mining areas, reclamation areas and coal storage areas); all coal inventory
  (including Saleable Coal Inventory); the Assumed Contracts; deposits and prepaid charges;
  the Transferred Permits; and personnel files to the extent relating to Transferred
  Employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §2.01]

## What did NOT cross — excluded assets and retained items
- The "Excluded Assets" expressly excluded: all cash and cash equivalents (other than
  certain deposits included in the Purchased Assets); all Permits other than the Transferred
  Permits; the Insurance Policies; books, records, minute books and corporate records; the
  Sellers' rights under the Agreement; tax refunds and tax returns; all tax assets and net
  operating losses; avoidance actions (except specified ones); all equity interests in James
  River's subsidiaries; the Specifically Excluded Assets; the Excluded Leases; and the
  Excluded Contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §2.02]

## People / management (as structured)
- Employees did not transfer automatically. No later than five business days before closing,
  the Buyer was to give the Sellers a list of Business Employees to whom it desired to offer
  employment ("Offered Employees"), and was to make bona fide offers to a sufficient number
  to avoid triggering a "plant closing" or "mass layoff" under the WARN Act; as of the
  Closing Date the Sellers would terminate the Offered Employees' employment, and those who
  accepted and commenced work for the Buyer became "Transferred Employees." The Sellers
  retained all severance and termination liabilities for Business Employees who did not
  become Transferred Employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K EX-2.1 §9.02(a)]
- Effective with the closing on August 29, 2014, Peter T. Socha resigned as Chairman and
  Chief Executive Officer, and directors Alan F. Crown, Ronald J. FlorJancic, Leonard J.
  Kujawa and Joseph H. Vipperman each resigned from the Board; the filing states these
  resignations were not the result of any disagreement with the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- William B. Murphy was retained as Chief Restructuring Officer under a July 31, 2014
  Consulting Services Agreement with Byron Advisors, LLC (Court-approved August 28, 2014),
  and was elected to the Board on August 28, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 5.02, EX-10.1]

## Agreement bridging a shared substrate (closing-time)
- At closing James River and the Buyer executed a Transition Services Agreement dated as of
  the Closing Date, delivered by both the applicable Sellers and the Buyer among the closing
  deliverables. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K EX-2.1 (definition of "Transition Services Agreement"; §§ closing deliverables)]

## Consummation of the sale (closing)
- On August 28, 2014 the Bankruptcy Court entered a Sale Order approving the sale of the
  Sellers' assets under the Amended Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 7.01, EX-2.2]
- On August 29, 2014 the Sellers completed the sale of their Hazard (excluding Laurel
  Mountain), West Virginia and Indiana assets to Blackhawk for $52.0 million, consisting of
  $20.0 million cash, a $27.0 million third-lien secured promissory note, and a $5.0 million
  second-lien secured promissory note to a Sellers' lessor for cure costs. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Under a section 363/365 process, a customer-facing productive asset base (owned/leased coal
  mining real property, mobile mining equipment and fixed assets, coal inventory, assumed
  contracts and transferred permits, plus goodwill) was sold out of a Chapter 11 estate to
  JR Acquisition, LLC / Blackhawk Mining LLC for $52.0 million (cash plus secured notes),
  while the Debtors retained corporate cash, insurance, corporate records, tax attributes/
  NOLs, subsidiary equity interests, non-assumed contracts and leases, and all severance
  liabilities; employees did not transfer automatically (only buyer-selected Offered
  Employees who accepted became Transferred Employees); the top management (CEO and four
  directors) departed at closing; and a Transition Services Agreement bridged shared
  functions between seller and buyer at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003342, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-2.1 §§2.01-2.02, §9.02(a)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001019687-14-003434, Form 8-K Items 2.01/5.02/7.01]
