## Realized fate of the transaction
- The proposed acquisition did not close. The Company's Form 10-Q for the quarterly period
  ended June 30, 2016 states: "The previously contemplated acquisition of the Marcellus
  and Utica assets in central Greene County, Pennsylvania from a subsidiary of Alpha
  Natural Resources, Inc. for $200.0 million, which was to be funded by the net proceeds
  [of the April 2016 Equity Offering], was not consummated, and, as a result, the Company
  intends to use the net proceeds of the April 2016 Equity Offering for general corporate
  purposes, which may include funding a portion of its 2017 capital budget." [SRC: SEC accession 0001588238-16-000044, Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended June 30, 2016]
- No assets of Alpha Natural Resources crossed to the Company under this transaction: the
  acquisition was not consummated, so no ownership boundary was crossed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001588238-16-000044, Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended June 30, 2016]

## Realized record over the fixed outcome window
- Because the transaction was never consummated, there is no acquired business, and no
  post-close acquired-business outcome scalar (net income/loss, goodwill/asset write-down,
  impairment, or divestiture) attributable to this transaction to report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001588238-16-000044, Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended June 30, 2016]
- The registrant ceased to exist as a separate public filer before the ~2019-2021 window:
  on November 13, 2017 the Company became a wholly owned subsidiary of EQT Corporation
  ("EQT") pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 19, 2017 (as amended
  October 26, 2017), with a merger subsidiary merging into the Company and the Company then
  merged into EQT RE, LLC (an EQT subsidiary and successor in interest to the Company).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K Items 2.01/3.01]
- In connection with the merger the Company notified the New York Stock Exchange that
  trading in its common stock should be suspended and its listing removed; a Form 25-NSE
  (notification of removal from listing) was filed on November 13, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-068865, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000876661-17-000676, Form 25-NSE]
- EQT RE, LLC, as successor in interest to the Company, filed a Form 15-12B on November 24,
  2017 to suspend the Company's Exchange Act reporting obligations; consequently no separate
  annual report (Form 10-K) or other periodic report was filed by the Company for any
  period after fiscal year 2016, and no separate public 3-5-year record exists for the
  ~2019-2021 window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-070327, Form 15-12B]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The proposed $200 million stalking-horse asset acquisition of Alpha Natural Resources'
  Marcellus/Utica Greene County acreage by the Company was not consummated, as the Company
  disclosed in its Form 10-Q for the quarter ended June 30, 2016; no assets crossed the
  ownership boundary and no acquired-business outcome scalar exists. The Company itself was
  acquired by EQT Corporation on November 13, 2017, was delisted from the NYSE, and had its
  Exchange Act reporting obligations suspended via a Form 15-12B filed November 24, 2017,
  so no separate public record exists for the fixed ~2019-2021 outcome window.
