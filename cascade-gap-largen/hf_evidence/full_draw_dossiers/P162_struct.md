## Registrant and the filing that was drawn
- The registrant is The Bon-Ton Stores, Inc., a Pennsylvania corporation (Commission File
  No. 0-19517) with principal offices in York, Pennsylvania; it operated department
  stores. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-000532, Form 8-K cover]
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting a management change, not deal terms: on
  December 29, 2017 the Company accepted the resignation of its Executive Vice
  President — Chief Financial Officer, Nancy A. Walsh, effective January 22, 2018, and
  announced that Michael G. Culhane joined the Company on January 2, 2018 as Executive
  Vice President — Chief Financial Officer (Item 5.02). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-000532, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Under his offer letter Mr. Culhane received an initial base salary of $600,000 per year
  and a signing bonus of $600,000, with responsibility for Accounting, Treasury, Tax,
  Credit, Investor Relations, Legal and Internal Audit. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-000532, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- The customer-facing operation comprised department stores under multiple nameplates:
  Bon-Ton, Bergner's, Boston Store, Carson's, Elder-Beerman, Herberger's and Younkers;
  at the Chapter 11 filing the chain comprised 227 stores, of which the Company owned the
  real estate on 22. [SRC: https://www.retaildive.com/news/bon-ton-files-for-chapter-11/516299]

## The transaction as structured (court-supervised sale under the Bankruptcy Code)
- On February 4, 2018 the Company and certain direct and indirect subsidiaries (the
  "Debtors") filed voluntary petitions under chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code in the
  United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, jointly administered under
  "In re: The Bon-Ton Stores, Inc., et al." Case No. 18-10248, operating as
  debtor-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000301, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The Chapter 11 filing was an event of default that accelerated the Debtors' funded debt,
  including a May 28, 2013 indenture with an outstanding principal amount of $350 million
  and the March 21, 2011 Second Amended and Restated Loan and Security Agreement
  (Prepetition ABL), with an outstanding amount (incl. accrued interest and fees) of
  approximately $493 million as of February 3, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000301, Form 8-K Item 2.04]
- On February 7, 2018 the Debtors entered into a Senior Secured, Super-Priority
  Debtor-in-Possession Loan and Security Agreement (up to $725 million; Bank of America,
  N.A. as administrative agent, Wells Fargo Bank, N.A. as co-collateral agent), into which
  approximately $525 million of prepetition ABL obligations (including letters of credit)
  were rolled; this DIP facility financed continued operations and the administration of
  the cases through the sale process. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000350, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A going-concern acquisition track was pursued but did not prevail: on March 30, 2018 the
  Company announced active discussions on a going-concern bid and extended the qualified-bid
  deadline, and on April 9, 2018 announced a signed letter of intent from an investor group
  composed of DW Partners, Namdar Realty Group (with its partner Mason Asset Management) and
  Washington Prime Group proposing to acquire the Company "as a going concern" in the
  court-supervised sale, with an auction scheduled for April 16, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000702, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000865, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- At the Section 363 auction held April 16–17, 2018, the successful bid was NOT the
  going-concern investor group but a contractual joint venture composed of GA Retail, Inc.
  and Tiger Capital Group, LLC (the "Agent"), together with Wilmington Savings Fund Society,
  FSB, as indenture agent and collateral trustee for the 8.00% second-lien senior secured
  notes due 2021 (the "Second-Lien Notes Trustee"; together the "Successful Bidder"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## What crossed the boundary at closing (April 18–19, 2018)
- The transaction took the form of an Agency Agreement, not a whole-company going-concern
  transfer: the Company (i) engaged the Agent as its exclusive agent to conduct
  going-out-of-business sales of Merchandise and Owned FF&E at the Company's stores and
  other locations, and (ii) granted the Successful Bidder exclusive designation rights over
  the disposition of substantially all of the Company's other assets, including Owned Real
  Property, unexpired Leases and Intellectual Property. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The Bankruptcy Court entered the Approval Order authorizing the Agency Agreement on
  April 18, 2018, and the Company and the Successful Bidder executed the Agency Agreement
  and consummated its closing on April 19, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 1.03 / Item 8.01]
- Thus at closing the customer-facing merchandise inventory and owned store fixtures/
  equipment passed to a liquidation agent for disposition, and rights over the real estate,
  leases and intellectual property passed as designation rights to the Successful Bidder;
  the operating business was NOT transferred to a continuing operator as a going concern,
  and no controlling intent, business model, management, workforce or organization was
  conveyed to a successor operator. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000865, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- Documented fusion of a controlling principal with a tier: none identified in the closing
  documents. [UNVERIFIED]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The closing structure is a court-approved Section 363 disposition of a department-store
  operator's assets: merchandise and owned fixtures/equipment engaged to a
  liquidation-agent joint venture for going-out-of-business sales, with designation rights
  over real property, leases and intellectual property granted to the Successful Bidder,
  after a competing going-concern acquisition proposal did not prevail at auction; the
  Debtors' operations were bridged through the process by up to $725 million of
  debtor-in-possession financing rather than transferred as an intact going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-001037, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950142-18-000350, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
