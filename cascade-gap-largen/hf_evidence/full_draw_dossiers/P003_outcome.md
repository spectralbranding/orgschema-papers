## Outcome window
- The registrant completed its separation on August 31, 2009; the outcome observed here is
  fiscal year 2013 (ended June 30, 2013), approximately four years later, per the
  registrant's annual report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY ended 2013-06-30]

## Realized financial trajectory
- For fiscal 2013 the company generated revenue of $3.55 billion and income from continuing
  operations of $389 million; for fiscal 2012 revenue was $3.60 billion and income from
  continuing operations was $361 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- In the five-year selected financial data, net income was $385 million (FY2013),
  $293 million (FY2012) and $249 million (FY2011); income from continuing operations was
  $389, $361 and $299 million for FY2013/2012/2011, respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Based on its annual impairment test as of the fourth quarter of fiscal 2013, the company
  did not record any goodwill or other indefinite-lived intangible asset impairments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Standing up standalone operations (process/organization interface over time)
- One-time expenditures in connection with the separation (employee-related costs, costs to
  start up certain stand-alone functions and information-technology systems, and other
  one-time transaction costs) were approximately $80 million in fiscal 2011 and $3 million
  in fiscal 2012; no such expense was incurred in fiscal 2013, and the company believed all
  substantive expenditures to stand up operations from the spinoff were complete. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The company reported incurring increased costs as an independent, publicly traded company,
  primarily from higher charges than in the past for transition services and from
  establishing or expanding its own corporate support (information technology, human
  resources, treasury, tax, risk management, accounting and financial reporting, investor
  relations, legal, procurement). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- The company realigned its operations: it moved from two reportable segments (Critical Care
  Technologies; Medical Technologies and Services) to two new segments (Medical Systems;
  Procedural Solutions) in the quarter ended September 30, 2011, stating this was done to
  reduce complexity and provide clearer governance for investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Portfolio reshaping (product/operations over time)
- After the spinoff the company both acquired and divested businesses. Divestitures
  included Audiology (October 2009), Research Services (May 2010), OnSite Services (March
  2011), International Surgical Products (April 2011) and Nicolet neurodiagnostic monitoring
  equipment (July 2012); the Audiology, International Surgical Products and Nicolet results
  were reflected in discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Loss from discontinued operations, net of tax, was $(68) million in fiscal 2012 and
  $(50) million in fiscal 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Acquisitions over the period included Medegen (May 2010), Vestara (April 2011), Rowa
  (August 2011), PHACTS (April 2012), UK Medical Holdings (June 2012) and Intermed
  (November 2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Documented product/quality-process friction over time
- The company continued operating under an amended consent decree with the FDA related to
  its U.S. infusion-pump (Alaris) business (entered February 2007, amended February 2009 to
  include all infusion pumps), under which it implemented a corrective action plan, had its
  infusion-pump facilities and recall procedures inspected by independent experts, and in
  July 2010 was notified by the FDA that it could proceed to the audit-inspection phase;
  associated audit and remediation costs could be significant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Approximately four years after separation the standalone company was profitable and
  growing modestly on income from continuing operations (FY2013 $389M vs FY2012 $361M) with
  no goodwill impairment, while it actively reshaped its portfolio through post-spin
  divestitures and acquisitions, realigned its reporting segments, absorbed the cost of
  standing up its own corporate functions, and continued to operate its infusion-pump
  business under an amended FDA consent decree. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: income from continuing operations of $389 million in
  fiscal 2013 (net income $385 million), on revenue of $3.55 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-329216, Form 10-K FY2013]
