# Structural sub-dossier — Linn Energy, LLC Chapter 11 reorganization (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

## The drawn filing (what it establishes, described truthfully)
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K furnished under Item 7.01 (Regulation FD): a press
  release announcing that LinnCo, LLC extended the subsequent offering period of its
  offer to exchange each outstanding unit of Linn Energy, LLC for one LinnCo share,
  with the period now expiring at midnight on June 30, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-599897, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- As of that report, approximately 12,066,714 Linn Energy units had been tendered during
  the subsequent offering period and LinnCo had issued new shares for them, leaving LinnCo
  the owner of approximately 68.9% of the Company's outstanding units. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-599897, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The stated purpose of the exchange offer was to let holders maintain their economic
  interest through LinnCo, an entity taxed as a corporation rather than a partnership.
  This filing is not the deal-terms document; it references the reorganization terms set
  out in the primaries below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-599897, Form 8-K Item 7.01]

## Parties and transaction type as structured
- On May 11, 2016 Linn Energy, LLC (the Company), its affiliate LinnCo, LLC, certain
  direct and indirect subsidiaries (the "LINN Debtors"), and Berry Petroleum Company, LLC
  filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Court for the Southern District of Texas (In re Linn Energy, LLC, et al., Case No.
  16-60040). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Items 1.01/1.03]
- The transaction was structured as a comprehensive restructuring of the debtors' long-term
  debt, effectuated through one or more plans of reorganization, under a Restructuring
  Support Agreement dated May 10, 2016 with creditors holding at least 66.67% of the
  principal under the LINN and Berry credit facilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, by tier
- Controlling intent / equity: all existing equity interests of the Company, LinnCo and
  Berry were to be extinguished without recovery; unsecured claims (including second-lien
  and unsecured notes) were to convert to equity in the reorganized Company or reorganized
  LinnCo — i.e., control was to pass from existing unitholders to former creditors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- At confirmation, each of the Company's units outstanding immediately before the effective
  date (355,032,380 units as of October 31, 2016) was to be cancelled, and new equity of a
  reorganized Company — "a new entity, the form of which shall be determined on or before
  the effective date" — issued to holders of second-lien and unsecured notes claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-023984, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- Product / operating assets and processes: the oil and natural gas exploration-and-production
  operations were retained by the reorganized entities rather than sold; the Company stated it
  expected ordinary-course operations to continue substantially uninterrupted during and after
  the cases, with the debtors operating as debtors-in-possession. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Items 1.01/1.03]
- People / management: the plan provided for a management incentive plan reserving no less
  than 10% of the new common stock of the reorganized Company (and of reorganized Berry) for
  grants to officers and other key employees of the respective reorganized entities — i.e.,
  the operating workforce and management were retained alongside the operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Legal-vehicle split: the Restructuring Support Agreement contemplated that Berry Petroleum
  would separate from the LINN Debtors under the Plan; confirmation proceeded via two plans —
  one for Linn Energy and its debtor affiliates other than Linn Acquisition Company, LLC and
  Berry, and a separate plan for Linn Acquisition Company, LLC and Berry. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-023984, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Financing / bridging arrangements at closing
- As confirmed on January 27, 2017, the plan provided for rights offerings of $530 million
  backstopped by certain creditors, a $500 million cash payment and a $1.7 billion exit
  facility for the LINN lenders (or non-conforming term notes for lenders electing out), and
  issuance of reorganized-Company common stock to second-lien and unsecured noteholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-023984, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The plan incorporated an integrated settlement with releases and exculpations for the
  debtors, certain claimholders and related parties; all existing funded debt and equity was
  to be discharged/extinguished on the effective date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-023984, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a creditor-led recapitalization: ownership and control crossed from the
  Company's existing unitholders to its former second-lien and unsecured noteholders (existing
  equity cancelled, claims converted to new equity), while the underlying oil-and-gas operating
  assets, processes and key employees were retained intact by the reorganized entities (operations
  continued substantially uninterrupted; management incentive equity reserved for retained
  officers/employees), and Berry Petroleum was separated under its own plan. The shared substrate
  was bridged by a Restructuring Support Agreement, rights offerings, and a new exit facility rather
  than by an asset sale to a third party. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-587315, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-023984, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
