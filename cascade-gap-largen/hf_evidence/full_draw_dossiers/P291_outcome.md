## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following the merger, the registrant (renamed "Virtu KCG Holdings LLC") filed a Form
  15-12G on 2017-07-31 to terminate the registration of its Class A Common Stock under
  Section 12(g) of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-048052, Form 15-12G]
- No Form 10-K or other annual/periodic report has been filed under CIK 1569391 (KCG
  Holdings, Inc.) since the company's FY2016 10-K (filed 2017-02-24); there is no
  independent post-closing periodic-report record for the entity. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001569391.json]
- The acquirer, Virtu Financial, Inc. (CIK 1592386), reports two operating segments —
  Market Making and Execution Services — and one non-operating segment, Corporate; it
  does not report a standalone financial segment for the former KCG business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Note "Goodwill and Intangible Assets" / segment disclosure]
- Consequently, no isolated post-closing profit/loss, revenue, or balance-sheet record
  exists in the public record for the acquired KCG business as a standalone unit; the
  facts below are limited to those instances where Virtu's own filings name "KCG" or
  the "Acquisition of KCG" specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Realized record specifically attributed to the acquired business (acquirer's FY2020 Form 10-K, filed 2021-02-25)
- As a result of the Acquisition of KCG, the Company (Virtu) carried non-U.S. net
  operating losses of $239.0 million at both December 31, 2020 and December 31, 2019,
  with an associated deferred tax asset of $44.9 million at each date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Note 15/Income Taxes]
- A full valuation allowance was recorded against that deferred tax asset at both
  December 31, 2020 and December 31, 2019 "as it is more likely than not that this
  deferred tax asset will not be realized." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Note 15/Income Taxes]
- Legacy KCG (and ITG) entities were disclosed as "currently the subject of various
  regulatory reviews and investigations by federal, state and foreign regulators and
  SROs, including the SEC and FINRA," with the filing noting such matters "may
  ultimately result in a disciplinary action and/or a civil or administrative action,
  penalties, fines, judgments, censures and settlements." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Risk Factors]
- The Company disclosed that it was "in the process of terminating or subleasing office
  space that we no longer need, subsequent to the consolidations in staff and technology
  following the Acquisition of KCG and ITG Acquisition." [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Item 2 Properties]
- A recurring adjusted-earnings reconciliation line item, "Termination of office
  leases," is described as the write-off expense for office space vacated "as part of
  the effort to integrate and consolidate office space in connection with the
  Acquisition of KCG and the ITG Acquisition," combining the impairment of operating
  lease right-of-use assets, leasehold improvements, fixed assets, and dilapidation
  charges; the filing does not isolate a KCG-only dollar amount from the combined
  KCG/ITG figure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Non-GAAP reconciliation discussion]
- In its annual goodwill impairment assessment (performed as of July 1, 2020, on a
  reporting-unit basis defined as an operating segment or one level below), the Company
  performed a qualitative assessment for each reporting unit and identified no
  impairment of goodwill; this assessment is not broken out by the former KCG
  acquisition specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020), Note "Goodwill and Intangible Assets"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired entity deregistered its common stock within roughly two weeks of closing
  and has filed no periodic report since, so no independent 3-5-year public financial
  record exists for the business as a standalone unit; the acquirer does not carry a
  separate reporting segment for it either. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-048052, Form 15-12G] [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Within that constraint, the acquirer's FY2020 Form 10-K names the acquired business
  specifically in three respects: a fully valued-away deferred tax asset tied to
  KCG-attributed non-U.S. net operating losses; ongoing regulatory reviews/investigations
  of "legacy KCG" entities; and continued office-space/technology consolidation
  activity tied explicitly to "the Acquisition of KCG," alongside a qualitative
  goodwill assessment that identified no impairment across reporting units generally. [SRC: SEC accession 0001592386-21-000005, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
