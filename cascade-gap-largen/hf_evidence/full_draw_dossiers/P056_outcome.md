# Outcome sub-dossier — Central European Distribution Corporation (realized 3–5-year record)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction record within the fixed outcome window. It contains no closing-time
> structural information.

## Outcome window
- Fixed window: fiscal years 2011–2013 (approximately three to five years after the May
  2008 closing). The best-available in-window primary is the registrant's Form 10-K for
  the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012, filed 2013-06-18, which reports through the 2013
  reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Change in the acquired business's status within the window
- "In February 2011, the Company finalized acquisition of Whitehall. Before the transaction
  the Company's interest was accounted for under equity method. As a result of this
  transaction, the Company acquired full voting and economic control over Whitehall Group
  and changed the accounting treatment for its interest in Whitehall from the equity method
  of accounting to consolidation beginning on February 7, 2011." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- "On March 30, 2011, the Company through its ownership of Whitehall sold its stake in MH
  to Moet Hennessy. As of the completion of this transaction, Whitehall no longer has any
  direct ownership stake in MH." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The consolidation of Whitehall added approximately $154.6 million to net sales in 2011
  (total net sales rose from $702.1 million in 2010 to $829.6 million in 2011). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- Net loss attributable to the Company: $(150.9) million for FY2010, $(1,325.6) million for
  FY2011, and $(363.2) million for FY2012 (amounts in thousands: 150,929 / 1,325,620 /
  363,238). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Company "recognized a significant impairment of goodwill and of other intangible
  assets for the fourth quarter of 2012," having "incurred impairment charges in 2011 and
  each of the preceding two years." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- FY2012 goodwill impairment charge of $327.8 million for the core business in Poland and
  Russia (the corresponding FY2011 goodwill impairment charge amounted to $930.1 million).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- At December 31, 2012 the Company held goodwill and other intangible assets of $842.9
  million, constituting 48% of total assets; total consolidated debt was approximately
  $1.4 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Documented product/asset-value deterioration
- The FY2012 trademark impairment (a $39.2 million fourth-quarter charge, plus a $5.8
  million customer-relationships charge) is attributed to "the related underperformance of
  certain brands in Russia, primarily Kaufman, Zhuravli and Parliament" (elsewhere listed
  as "Kaufman, Zhuravli and Urozhay"), "due to the continued lower performance as compared
  to our expectations in 2012." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Reorganization, control change, and delisting
- "On April 7, 2013, CEDC and its subsidiaries CEDC Finance Corporation International, Inc.
  and CEDC Finance Corporation, LLC ... filed voluntary petitions for reorganization ...
  under Chapter 11 ... in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware
  ..." (Case No. 13-10738), to effectuate a prepackaged Plan of Reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The restructuring was pursued with Roust Trading Ltd. (RTL), controlled by Mr. Roustam
  Tariko / the Russian Standard Corporation, "a significant equity and debt holder in the
  Company"; RTL made a $172.0 million cash investment as part of the transactions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The Plan of Reorganization "was confirmed by the Bankruptcy Court on May 13, 2013" and
  "eliminated approximately $665.2 million in debt." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- "All of the Company's shares of common stock outstanding prior to the Effective Date of
  the Plan were cancelled"; as of June 5, 2013 "Roust Trading Limited and its affiliates
  acquired control of 100% of the Company's new common stock." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- NASDAQ delisting: by letter dated April 10, 2013 the NASDAQ Hearings Panel affirmed the
  decision to delist the Company's shares, which "were suspended from trading on NASDAQ,
  effective at the open of business on Friday, April 12, 2013." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Outcome summary
- Within the window the acquired Russian importer was brought under full control and
  consolidated (February 2011) and exited its own Moet Hennessy joint venture (March 2011);
  the consolidated group then recorded net losses each year (peaking at $1.33 billion in
  FY2011), recognized large goodwill and trademark impairments (including on the
  Kaufman/Zhuravli Russian brands), and in April 2013 entered a prepackaged Chapter 11
  under which the pre-existing common stock was cancelled and 100% control passed to Roust
  Trading Ltd., with the shares delisted from NASDAQ. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-13-261578, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
