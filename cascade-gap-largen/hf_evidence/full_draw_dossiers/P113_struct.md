## Transaction and parties at closing
- In April 2009 the registrant completed the acquisition of certain assets of Advantage
  Rent A Car ("Advantage"), described as a brand focused on price-oriented customers at
  key U.S. leisure travel destinations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- The transaction was structured as an acquisition of selected assets rather than of a
  whole going-concern company: the purchase agreement provided the rights to purchase
  certain rights, trademarks and copyrights to use the Advantage brand name, website and
  phone numbers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- The agreement additionally provided an option to have assigned certain leases, fixed
  assets, airport concession agreements and other agreements associated with approximately
  20 locations that Advantage is or previously was operating. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]

## What crossed the boundary at the tier level, and what did not
- Customer-facing product / asset that crossed: the Advantage brand identity — brand name,
  website, phone numbers, trademarks and copyrights — i.e., the marketable brand rather
  than an intact operating company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- Selected operational assets that could cross by option: leases, fixed assets and airport
  concession agreements tied to roughly 20 Advantage locations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- The consideration for intangible assets acquired in 2009 was allocated primarily to the
  Advantage tradename and concession agreements (reported together with technology assets
  from a separate acquisition), and each transaction was accounted for using the
  acquisition method of accounting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- People / management / organization: the primary describes the acquisition of brand
  assets and an option over selected location assets; it does not describe any transfer of
  Advantage's workforce, management, or an intact operating organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
- Delivery through the acquirer's own organization: as of December 31, 2009 the registrant
  operated 24 corporate Advantage-brand rental locations in the United States — i.e., the
  brand was run through the acquirer's own corporate locations and operating processes.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]

## Transitional / shared-substrate arrangements at closing
- The FY2009 primary describes no transition-services agreement, operating agreement, or
  shared-services arrangement bridging Advantage's former operating substrate; the brand
  assets were folded directly into the registrant's existing car-rental operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction transferred a customer-facing brand and its associated marks (name,
  website, phone numbers), plus an option over selected leases, fixed assets and airport
  concessions for about 20 locations, into an existing large car-rental operator; it did
  not transfer an intact producing/delivering organization, and the acquirer ran the brand
  through its own corporate locations, with no transitional bridging arrangement described.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-10-001717, Form 10-K (FY2009)]
