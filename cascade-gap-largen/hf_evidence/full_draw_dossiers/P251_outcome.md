## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed by Cavium, Inc. under its own CIK
  after the pre-merger period; the acquired entity's own public reporting ended at the
  July 6, 2018 closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-213483, Form 8-K]
- The acquirer, Marvell Technology, Inc. (formerly Marvell Technology Group Ltd.; CIK
  0001835632 / 0001058057), continued as a public filer and reports its business as a
  single operating segment with a single reporting unit for goodwill-impairment
  testing purposes, meaning no standalone segment-level revenue or income statement is
  separately published for the acquired Cavium business as such. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K (FY2022, filed 2022-03-10)]
- Within that consolidated reporting structure, Marvell's FY2022 Form 10-K (covering
  the fiscal year ended January 29, 2022, filed March 10, 2022 — within the fixed
  ~2021-2023 outcome window) separately discloses several facts specifically tied to
  assets and product lines acquired from Cavium, cited below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]

## Documented restructuring/impairment tied to the acquired business
- Marvell's restructuring-liability rollforward in the FY2022 10-K tracks a
  separately-named "July 2018 Restructuring" program (dated to the month Cavium's
  acquisition closed) alongside distinct "November 2019," "July 2020," and "Fiscal 2022"
  restructuring programs; the July 2018 program still carried an open liability balance
  of $388 thousand (employee severance) and $1,204 thousand (other) as of January 29,
  2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K, Note 10 - Restructuring]
- During the second quarter of fiscal 2021 (quarter ended August 1, 2020), Marvell
  recognized $119.0 million of restructuring-related charges associated with its
  "server processor product line," describing a change in strategy "transitioning our
  product offering from standard server processors to the broad server market to focus
  only on customized server processors for a few targeted customers"; of that $119.0
  million, $50.3 million was impairment of "certain intangible assets acquired from
  Cavium," with the remainder in impairment of purchased IP licenses and equipment/
  inventory impairment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K, Notes to Consolidated Financial Statements]
- Total restructuring and other related charges recognized during fiscal 2021 were
  $180.4 million, comprising the $119.0 million tied to the server processor product
  line and $61.4 million "recorded in connection with prior acquisitions" (severance and
  lease-related costs). [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]

## Continuity of specific former-Cavium product lines
- Marvell's FY2022 Form 10-K business description continues to list product families
  originating from Cavium, including the "OCTEON multi-core infrastructure processor
  families" (integrated Layer 4-7 data and security processing) and the "LiquidIO Server
  Adapter family" (a programmable adapter platform for cloud data centers and
  enterprises to offload server processors), indicating these specific acquired product
  lines continued to be marketed within the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]
- The same filing shows the "server processor" product line (the standard/broad-market
  ARM server processor business) was narrowed in scope during fiscal 2021, rather than
  continued unchanged, per the restructuring disclosure above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired Cavium business does not have a standalone public periodic-reporting
  record after the July 2018 closing; Marvell reports as a single operating segment and
  does not publish separate segment financials for the acquired business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]
- Within that limit, Marvell's FY2022 Form 10-K (filed within the fixed ~2021-2023
  outcome window) separately documents: an ongoing "July 2018 Restructuring" liability
  still being drawn down as of January 29, 2022; a $119.0 million restructuring charge
  in fiscal 2021 that included a $50.3 million impairment specifically of intangible
  assets acquired from Cavium, tied to a narrowing of the standard server-processor
  product line to a smaller set of customized-customer offerings; and continued
  marketing, within the outcome window, of other former-Cavium product families (OCTEON,
  LiquidIO). [SRC: SEC accession 0001835632-22-000016, Form 10-K]
- Because Marvell separately discloses these Cavium-specific facts (rather than only
  consolidated results), this case is coded on a **documented, partial** outcome basis
  per the no-record convention: the product-line-level restructuring/impairment record
  is sourced and real, while a standalone revenue or net-income trajectory for the
  acquired business specifically is **not** publicly available and remains uncertain.
