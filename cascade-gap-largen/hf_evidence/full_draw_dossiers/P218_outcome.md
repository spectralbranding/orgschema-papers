## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Andeavor filed a Form 15-12B on October 11, 2018 to terminate registration of its
  common stock and suspend its periodic-reporting obligations under the Exchange Act.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000050104-18-000242, Form 15-12B]
- A search of Andeavor's (CIK 0000050104) EDGAR filing history finds no Form 10-K filed
  after the merger closed; no periodic report exists for the ~2021-2023 outcome window
  under Andeavor's own registrant identity. [SRC: https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=0000050104&type=10-K]
- The acquirer, Marathon Petroleum Corporation, does not report an "Andeavor" segment;
  its reportable segments are Refining & Marketing, Midstream and (through 2021)
  Retail. Acquired Andeavor operations were consolidated into MPC's existing segments
  rather than disclosed as a discrete post-merger business line. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001510295-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Business-combination facts disclosed by the acquirer (feasibility context)
- MPC's FY2021 Form 10-K states: "On October 1, 2018, we acquired Andeavor. Andeavor
  shareholders received in the aggregate approximately 239.8 million shares of MPC
  common stock valued at $19.8 billion and $3.5 billion in cash." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001510295-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The same filing states the Andeavor acquisition "substantially increased our
  geographic diversification and the scale of our assets." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001510295-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- MPC discontinued separately reporting Andeavor transaction-related costs effective
  October 1, 2019, "as one year has passed since" the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001510295-21-000027, Form 10-K (FY2020)]

## Documented trajectory of specific Andeavor-heritage refining assets, within/near the fixed window
- On September 21, 2022, MPC closed the formation of the "Martinez Renewable Fuels"
  joint venture, a 50/50 partnership with Neste Corporation, covering the former
  Martinez, California refinery (an Andeavor-heritage asset); MPC states it is
  "converting the Martinez facility from refining petroleum to manufacturing renewable
  fuels," with a planned full capacity of approximately 730 million gallons per year
  of renewable diesel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001510295-23-000012, Form 10-K (FY2022)]
- This joint-venture formation followed an earlier, February 24, 2021 announcement (at
  the edge of, or just before, the fixed outcome window) that MPC would "strategically
  reposition the Martinez refinery to a renewable diesel facility." [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001510295-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- Preceding the fixed outcome window (2020, roughly two years post-closing): MPC
  "indefinitely idled" the Gallup, New Mexico refinery (another Andeavor-heritage
  asset) and recorded a first-quarter-2020 long-lived-asset impairment charge of $142
  million specific to the Gallup refinery's property, plant and equipment; a
  fourth-quarter-2020 impairment charge of $67 million tied to abandoned MPLX terminal
  assets near the Gallup refinery; and an additional $44 million impairment from a
  revised salvage-value estimate for the Gallup refinery asset group. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001510295-21-000027, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- The same 2020 restructuring (idling the Gallup refinery and repositioning the
  Martinez refinery, together with a workforce-reduction plan) generated $367 million
  of restructuring expense company-wide for the year, of which $195 million was
  specifically attributed to exit costs at the Martinez and Gallup refineries, and
  affected approximately 2,050 employees combined across the refinery actions and the
  broader involuntary workforce-reduction plan. [SRC: SEC accession 0001510295-21-000027,
  Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Separately, MPC recorded a $1.20-1.24 billion impairment expense in 2019 related to
  MPLX goodwill associated with the ANDX (Andeavor Logistics) gathering and processing
  businesses acquired as part of the Andeavor acquisition — also preceding the fixed
  outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001510295-21-000027, Form 10-K (FY2020); SEC
  accession 0001510295-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Andeavor was merged out of existence and deregistered in October 2018, there
  is no public periodic-reporting record under its own name for the ~2021-2023 window.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000050104-18-000242, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer does not separately report an Andeavor segment or an Andeavor-specific
  financial outcome; the only traceable within/near-window realized facts concern
  specific Andeavor-heritage refining assets: the Martinez, California refinery was
  converted from petroleum refining toward renewable-diesel production via a 50/50
  joint venture with Neste that closed September 21, 2022 (within the fixed window),
  preceded by a 2020-2021 sequence of idling (the Gallup, New Mexico refinery),
  asset-specific impairment charges, restructuring costs and workforce reductions
  affecting the Andeavor-heritage refining base (occurring shortly before the fixed
  window). [SRC: SEC accession 0001510295-23-000012, Form 10-K (FY2022); SEC accession
  0001510295-21-000027, Form 10-K (FY2020)]
- Per the no-record/no-inference convention (Amendment 4), this case is coded
  **uncertain** on any aggregate financial outcome for the acquired business as a
  whole: MPC's consolidated results are not attributed by MPC to the Andeavor business
  specifically, and no source isolates a comprehensive post-close trajectory for the
  acquired company; only the discrete, asset-level facts above (Martinez JV formation;
  Gallup refinery idling and impairments) are affirmatively documented.
