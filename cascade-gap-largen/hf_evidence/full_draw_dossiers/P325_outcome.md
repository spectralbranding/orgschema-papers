## Public-reporting status through the outcome window

- The entity remained an independent, Exchange Act-reporting public company throughout
  the outcome window: it filed a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 (filed February 28,
  2022) and a Form 10-K for fiscal year 2022 (filed March 1, 2023), both under the
  registrant name "Civeo Corporation," with Common Shares continuing to be registered
  under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021 cover page] [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022 cover page]

## Realized financial trajectory

- Consolidated total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2021 were $594,463
  thousand, an increase of $64,734 thousand (12%) versus $529,729 thousand in 2020,
  attributed primarily to higher billed rooms at Canadian oil sands lodges related to
  customer turnaround activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net loss attributable to Civeo for 2021 was $0.6 million ($0.04 per diluted share),
  which included a $7.9 million pre-tax loss from impairment of fixed assets, partially
  offset by $4.7 million of pre-tax income associated with the settlement of a
  representations-and-warranties claim related to the Noralta Acquisition, recorded in
  the Canada segment's Other income. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- By comparison, the FY2021 filing reports Civeo's net loss attributable to Civeo for
  2020 was $136.1 million ($9.64 per diluted share), which included a $93.6 million
  pre-tax goodwill-impairment loss in the Canada segment (Noralta's reporting segment)
  and a $38.1 million pre-tax long-lived-asset impairment loss in the same segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- As of December 31, 2021, goodwill at Civeo's Australian reporting unit represented 1%
  of total assets ($8.2 million); the Canadian reporting unit's goodwill had been
  reduced in a prior period (see below), so no Canada-segment goodwill remained by this
  point in the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021, "Risk Factors" / Note 4]
- Consolidated total revenues for the year ended December 31, 2022 were $697,052
  thousand, versus $594,463 thousand in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Net income attributable to Civeo for 2022 was $2.2 million (a $0.21 loss per diluted
  share once preferred-dividend allocation under the two-class method is applied),
  which included a $5.7 million pre-tax loss from impairment of fixed assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A "Results of Operations"]
- Long-lived-asset impairments across the three most recently reported years were
  disclosed as $5.7 million (2022), $7.9 million (2021), and $50.5 million (2020); a
  goodwill impairment of $93.6 million was recorded in 2020 only, and no further
  Canada-segment goodwill impairment is reported in 2021 or 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, "Risk Factors"]

## Documented events over time relating to the earlier consideration structure

- During the fourth quarter of 2018, 637 Series A preferred shares that had been held
  in escrow to support certain obligations of the Noralta Acquisition were released. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 16 "Preferred Shares"]
- On October 30, 2022, 3,617 Series A preferred shares were repurchased from the
  holders for approximately $30.6 million (including under $0.1 million of accrued
  dividends). [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 16 "Preferred Shares"]
- On December 13, 2022, the holders of the remaining 5,425 outstanding Series A
  preferred shares elected to convert them into approximately 1,504,539 common shares;
  as of December 31, 2022, Civeo had no Series A preferred shares outstanding, and no
  further preferred dividends were required to be paid thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 16 "Preferred Shares"]
- The Canada segment (comprising the acquired Noralta lodges together with Civeo's
  pre-existing Canadian operations) reported revenues of $321,378 thousand (2021) and
  $395,997 thousand (2022), versus $269,649 thousand in 2020 — i.e., the segment that
  absorbed the acquired business grew across the outcome window rather than being
  divested or wound down. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021, "Results of Operations — Canada"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022, "Results of Operations — Canada"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Civeo continued to operate and report publicly across the outcome window rather than
  being acquired, delisted, or dissolved, with the acquired Noralta lodges continuing
  to be reported within its Canada segment. The realized trajectory shows a $93.6
  million Canada-segment goodwill impairment and a $136.1 million net loss in 2020
  (outside but immediately preceding the fixed window), followed by a near-breakeven
  $0.6 million net loss in 2021 (on 12% revenue growth) and a $2.2 million net income
  in 2022 (on further revenue growth to $697.1 million), alongside continued but
  smaller annual long-lived-asset impairments ($7.9 million in 2021, $5.7 million in
  2022) and no further Canada-segment goodwill impairment after 2020. The preferred
  shares issued as part of the original consideration were fully repurchased or
  converted to common shares by the end of 2022, closing out that component of the
  original consideration structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-22-000011, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001590584-23-000043, Form 10-K FY2022]
