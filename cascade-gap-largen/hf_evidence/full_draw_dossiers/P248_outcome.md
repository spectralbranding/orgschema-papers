## Realized record of the acquired entities (Euronav NV's separate disclosure)
- Euronav NV's Form 20-F for fiscal year 2022 (filed April 12, 2023) states: "On June 12,
  2018 we completed the merger with Gener8 Maritime Inc. (Gener8) ... whereby Gener8
  became our wholly-owned subsidiary," and describes Gener8 at the merger date as owning
  a fleet of 29 tankers (21 VLCC, six Suezmax, two LR1 vessels; approximately 7.4 million
  dwt). [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- The same Form 20-F states, regarding the vessel-owning entities acquired in the merger:
  "All of the vessels have been transferred to Euronav NV in 2018. The Group liquidated a
  majority of those subsidiaries in 2019 and 2020." [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- As of December 31, 2022, 2021, and 2020, Euronav's "Group entities" note lists Euronav
  MI II Inc. (formerly Gener8 Maritime, Inc.), Gener8 Maritime Subsidiary II Inc., Gener8
  Maritime Subsidiary New IV Inc., and Gener8 Maritime Management LLC — each a Marshall
  Islands entity — as 100.00%-owned Group entities in each of those three years. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- The Form 20-F discloses that the Group subleases office space to third parties in
  certain leased offices of "Euronav UK and Euronav MI II Inc. (formerly Gener8 Maritime
  Inc.)," with non-current sublease receivables of $1,591 thousand and $2,294 thousand as
  of December 31, 2022 and December 31, 2021 respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- The Form 20-F discloses pending litigation as of the report date: RMK Maritime
  commenced proceedings in the London High Court against Euronav on September 30, 2022,
  seeking US$12,993,720 in damages for allegedly unpaid advisory services "concerning its
  merger with Gener8 in 2016 and 2017"; Euronav's defense was served December 29, 2022,
  and a case management conference hearing was scheduled for May 5, 2023; Euronav's
  management stated it believed it had strong arguments against the claim based on the
  written Advisory Agreement's terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Gener8 was absorbed through a 100% stock-for-stock merger and deregistered
  in mid-2018, there is no periodic report filed by the acquired entity alone; unlike a
  case with no public record at all, however, the acquirer (Euronav NV) separately names
  and discusses the acquired entities in its own Form 20-F: the acquired vessels were
  transferred into Euronav's ownership in 2018, a majority of the acquired vessel-owning
  subsidiaries were liquidated in 2019-2020, and four Gener8-associated corporate
  entities (including the renamed former parent and its former management subsidiary)
  remained 100%-owned Group entities through year-end 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- Euronav's FY2022 Form 20-F also discloses a continuing third-party legal dispute
  specifically tied to the Gener8 merger (the RMK Maritime advisory-fee claim) and a
  continuing sublease arrangement involving former Gener8 office space. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
- No segment-specific financial-performance scalar attributable solely to the former
  Gener8 fleet is separately disclosed in the reviewed filing; the realized record for
  this case is therefore documented on structural-continuity and dispute facts rather
  than on an income-statement or balance-sheet scalar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001604481-23-000004, Form 20-F (FY2022)]
