# Outcome sub-dossier — Northrop Grumman's realized record for the Orbital ATK acquisition, ~2021-2023 window

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The acquired entity deregistered from separate
> SEC reporting shortly after closing, so this sub-dossier traces the realized record
> through the acquirer's (Northrop Grumman Corporation, CIK 1133421) subsequent public
> filings within a fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window. It contains no information about how
> the transaction was structured at closing.

## Public-reporting status of the acquired entity after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The acquired entity (by then renamed Northrop Grumman Innovation Systems, Inc., CIK
  866121) filed Form 15-12B notices to terminate registration of its securities on
  2018-07-19. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-000840, Form 15-12B] [SRC: SEC accession 0000950157-18-000839, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report was filed under CIK 866121 after the FY2017
  10-K (filed 2018-02-22, pre-closing); consequently no standalone audited financial
  statements exist for the entity in the ~2021-2023 outcome window, and any trajectory
  must be read through the acquirer's consolidated/segment disclosures. [SRC: SEC accession 0000866121-18-000014, 10-K (FY2017, filed 2018-02-22)]

## Acquirer segment disclosure of the acquired business (Amendment 4 assessment)
- At closing (June 6, 2018) Northrop Grumman established "Innovation Systems" as a new,
  fourth business sector, and the operating results of legacy Innovation Systems
  (formerly Orbital ATK) were included in Northrop Grumman's consolidated results as a
  separately identified reportable segment for the remainder of FY2018 and FY2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) Note 2 "Acquisitions and Dispositions"]
- Effective January 1, 2020, Northrop Grumman reorganized its sectors "to better align
  the company's broad portfolio to serve its customers' needs"; from that date the
  company was aligned in four operating/reportable segments — Aeronautics Systems,
  Defense Systems, Mission Systems, and Space Systems — and the operating results of
  legacy Innovation Systems subsequent to the June 6, 2018 merger date are reflected
  across the Space Systems, Defense Systems, and Aeronautics Systems segments rather
  than as a standalone segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) "Segment Operating Results — Basis of Presentation" and Note 2]
- This four-segment structure (Aeronautics Systems, Defense Systems, Mission Systems,
  Space Systems) was still in place as reported in Northrop Grumman's FY2022 Form 10-K
  (filed 2023-01-26), i.e., through the end of the fixed outcome window; the acquired
  business's results are therefore observable only as embedded, undifferentiated
  components of these three multi-legacy segments for the entire ~2021-2023 window, not
  as a separately reported Orbital-ATK/Innovation-Systems line. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-23-000006, 10-K (FY2022)]

## Realized record within the window (segment-level, not Orbital-ATK-specific)
- Purchase-accounting goodwill of $6,259 million was recognized on the June 6, 2018
  acquisition and, following the January 2020 reorganization, is carried within the
  Aeronautics Systems, Defense Systems, and Space Systems segments; Northrop Grumman
  attributed the goodwill to "expected revenue synergies generated by the integration of
  Northrop Grumman products and technologies with those of legacy Orbital ATK," cost
  synergies, and intangible assets including "the assembled workforce of Orbital ATK." [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) Note 2]
- As of December 31, 2020, accumulated goodwill impairment losses (since the relevant
  acquisitions, not isolated to Orbital ATK) totaled $417 million at the Aeronautics
  Systems segment and $153 million at the Space Systems segment; these are segment-level
  figures that include legacy businesses beyond the acquired Orbital ATK operations and
  cannot be isolated to the acquired entity alone. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) Note 8 "Goodwill"]
- Northrop Grumman's FY2020 and FY2022 Forms 10-K each continued to disclose, among
  litigation/regulatory matters that can divert financial and management resources,
  "matters relating to the Orbital ATK Federal Trade Commission decision and order,"
  indicating this closing-era regulatory condition remained an active, disclosed matter
  through the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) Item 3/risk disclosure] [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-23-000006, 10-K (FY2022) Item 3/risk disclosure]
- Executive-biography disclosures in the FY2020 and FY2022 Forms 10-K show individuals
  who held roles at Orbital ATK before the merger (e.g., Chief Operating Officer,
  2015-2018) continuing into leadership of the "Former Innovation Systems Sector"
  (2018-2020) and then the Space Systems Sector (2020 onward); a second individual is
  similarly traced from "Space Systems Group, Orbital ATK, Inc. (2015-2018)" through the
  Innovation Systems Sector (2018-2020) into the Space Systems Sector (2020-2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020) "Information about our Executive Officers"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-23-000006, 10-K (FY2022) "Information about our Executive Officers"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired entity deregistered from separate SEC reporting within weeks of closing
  (Form 15-12B, 2018-07-19), so no standalone audited outcome record exists for it. The
  acquirer disclosed the acquired business as its own reportable segment ("Innovation
  Systems") only through FY2019; effective January 1, 2020 that segment was dissolved
  and its results folded into three other, multi-legacy segments (Aeronautics Systems,
  Defense Systems, Space Systems), a structure that persisted through FY2022. Within the
  fixed ~2021-2023 window the observable record is therefore segment-level rather than
  entity-specific: a purchase-goodwill balance explicitly tied in part to Orbital
  ATK-related integration synergies and workforce, segment-level (not Orbital-ATK-only)
  goodwill impairment balances, continued disclosure of a closing-era FTC regulatory
  order as an active matter, and biographical continuity of several pre-merger Orbital
  ATK executives into post-reorganization Space Systems Sector leadership roles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-21-000006, 10-K (FY2020)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001133421-23-000006, 10-K (FY2022)]
