## Pre-petition business and organizational structure
- At December 31, 2011 the Company owned or operated a fleet of 111 double-hulled
  vessels (10.9 million deadweight tons), organized into strategic business units
  focused on three market segments: International Flag Crude Tankers, International
  Flag Product Carriers, and U.S. Flag vessels; the Company also held joint-venture
  interests operating four LNG carriers and two Floating Storage and Offloading
  vessels. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-12-011875, Form 10-K Item 1 Business]
- Dedicated chartering and commercial personnel managed specific fleets while
  technical ship-management operations and corporate departments supported global
  operations; as of December 31, 2011 the Company had approximately 3,600 employees,
  including seagoing personnel covered by U.S. and international maritime union
  agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-12-011875, Form 10-K Item 1 Business]
- All of the Company's International Flag vessels were owned or operated by foreign
  corporations that were subsidiaries of OSG International, Inc. ("OIN"), a wholly
  owned subsidiary of the Company incorporated in the Marshall Islands, treated as
  branches of OIN for U.S. federal tax purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-12-011875, Form 10-K, Taxation of the Company]
- The Company's certificate of incorporation and by-laws required, in order to
  maintain the vessels' U.S. coastwise-trade (Jones Act) rights, that at least 77% of
  the Company's common stock be held by U.S. citizens, with any transfer in violation
  of that requirement ineffective to convey the shares or associated rights. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-12-011875, Form 10-K, Risk Factors]

## Filing of voluntary petitions (November 14, 2012)
- On November 14, 2012, Overseas Shipholding Group, Inc. and 180 of its subsidiaries
  filed voluntary petitions for reorganization under Chapter 11 of the U.S. Bankruptcy
  Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District of Delaware, with a motion pending
  to consolidate the subsidiaries' proceedings under OSG's docket number. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-12-000032, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
- The petitions constituted an event of default or termination event under the
  Company's material direct financial obligations, including $300 million of 8.125%
  Senior Notes due 2018, $146 million of 7.500% Senior Notes due 2024, $63.603 million
  of 8.750% Debentures due 2013, $1.489 billion outstanding under a $1.5 billion
  revolving credit facility (borrowers OSG, OSG Bulk Ships, Inc. and OSG International,
  Inc.), and secured term-loan facilities with the Export-Import Bank of China, Danish
  Ship Finance A/S, and ING Bank, N.V. as agents. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-12-000032, Form 8-K Item 2.04]
- NYSE Regulation announced the immediate suspension of trading in the Company's
  common stock effective November 14, 2012, determining the stock no longer suitable
  for continued listing in light of the petitions, with the Company not intending to
  appeal that determination. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-12-000032, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- Effective November 14, 2012, the Board appointed John J. Ray, III as Chief
  Reorganization Officer ("CRO") to lead the reorganization process, reporting to the
  Board Chairman; Mr. Ray's services were provided as an independent contractor through
  an engagement with Greylock Partners LLP (of which he is a Senior Managing Director
  and founder), and he was not an employee of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-12-000032, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Plan of reorganization and the effective date (August 5, 2014)
- On March 7, 2014 the Debtors filed a plan of reorganization supported by lenders
  under the $1.5 billion credit agreement (the "Lender Plan"); on April 18, 2014 the
  Debtors received an alternative proposal from holders of existing equity interests,
  which the Debtors determined more favorable to creditors and interest holders, and
  filed a competing plan effectuating that proposal (the "Equity Plan"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed the Equity Plan by order entered July 18, 2014; the
  Equity Plan became effective on August 5, 2014 (the "Effective Date"), on which OSG
  emerged from the Chapter 11 proceeding. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- On the Effective Date all previously outstanding shares of common stock were
  cancelled and retired; the Company issued new Class A common stock and Class B
  common stock together with Class A and Class B penny warrants for an aggregate
  offering amount of $1.510 billion (306,857,778 Class A shares and 213,715,419 Class
  A warrants under Rule 506(b); 5,457,591 Class B shares and 2,469,013 Class B warrants
  under Bankruptcy Code Section 1145), with proceeds used to satisfy Equity Plan cash
  obligations and fund post-emergence working capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- To preserve Jones Act citizenship compliance, ownership of each new stock class by
  non-U.S. citizens continued to be capped under Company policy; on the Effective Date
  the cap on Class A common stock had already been reached. On the Effective Date the
  Company also adopted an Amended and Restated Certificate of Incorporation and
  Amended and Restated By-Laws reflecting the new capital structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 8.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 5.03]
- On the Effective Date the Debtors entered new secured Exit Financing Facilities: an
  approximately $75 million asset-based revolving facility and an approximately $603
  million term loan (the "Domestic Term Loan"), each secured primarily by U.S. Flag
  assets, and an approximately $628.4 million term loan and $50 million revolver, each
  secured by International Flag assets — separately collateralized by the two asset
  pools within the single post-emergence parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149, Form 8-K Item 8.01/2.03]

## Governance and management at emergence
- Pursuant to the Plan, on the Effective Date all former directors ceased to serve on
  the Board and nine New Directors — nominated by the CRO and approved by the prior
  Board — began service, with three new standing committees established (audit; human
  resources and compensation; corporate governance and risk assessment). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048556, Form 8-K Item 5.02]
- Mr. Ray, who had served as CRO through an independent-contractor engagement with
  Greylock Partners (never an employee of the Company), ceased to be CRO on the
  Effective Date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048556, Form 8-K Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (petition through emergence only)
- The reorganization proceeded from a November 2012 Chapter 11 filing covering the
  parent and 180 subsidiaries to an August 2014 effective plan that cancelled all
  prior equity, issued new securities to creditors/interest holders, replaced the
  entire board, and separately collateralized the surviving parent's U.S. Flag and
  International Flag asset pools under distinct exit-financing facilities, while
  retaining the pre-petition three-segment operating structure (Crude Oil, Products,
  U.S. Flag) and the Marshall Islands subsidiary vehicle (OIN) holding the
  International Flag operating entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000075208-12-000032] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048149] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-14-048556]
