# Structural sub-dossier — Entercom Communications Corp. / CBS Radio Inc. combination (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Entercom Communications Corp. ("Entercom"), a Pennsylvania corporation, proposed to
  combine with CBS Radio Inc. ("CBS Radio"), a Delaware corporation and, prior to
  closing, a wholly owned subsidiary of CBS Corporation ("CBS"). [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A explanatory note]
- The transaction combined (i) an internal CBS reorganization consolidating CBS's radio
  business under CBS Radio; (ii) a split-off/spin-off distributing CBS Radio shares to
  CBS shareholders (the "Final Distribution"); and (iii) a merger in which Constitution
  Merger Sub Corp., a wholly owned Entercom subsidiary, merged into CBS Radio, with CBS
  Radio surviving as a wholly owned Entercom subsidiary and its shares converting into
  Entercom Class A Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A
  explanatory note]
- The transaction was designed to be effected as a Reverse Morris Trust transaction,
  intended to be tax-free to CBS and its stockholders under Section 355 of the Internal
  Revenue Code, per the merger agreement dated February 2, 2017 among CBS, CBS Radio,
  Entercom, and Constitution Merger Sub Corp. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047,
  DEFM14A]
- Entercom filed a registration statement on Form S-4 (Reg. No. 333-217273) for the
  shares issued in the merger; CBS Radio filed Form S-4/Form S-1 (Reg. No. 333-217279)
  for the shares distributed in the split-off/spin-off. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A explanatory note]

## Ownership and control at closing
- Immediately after the merger, approximately 72% of outstanding Entercom Common Stock
  was expected to be held by pre-merger CBS Common Stock holders, and approximately 28%
  by pre-merger Entercom holders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A
  summary]
- Entercom's existing dual-class share structure (Class A one vote, Class B ten votes)
  was retained; Joseph M. Field and David J. Field (Entercom's controlling pre-merger
  holders) were expected to retain a large minority voting interest — approximately 25%
  of total voting power of the combined entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047,
  DEFM14A summary]
- CBS held no formal or informal appointment/consent/approval rights over post-merger CBS
  Radio (subsidiary) directors; post-merger CBS Radio officers were the same individuals
  continuing as Entercom's President & CEO (David J. Field), EVP & CFO, and SVP/General
  Counsel. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A management section]
- The post-closing Entercom board was to be classified and consist of ten members: all
  six pre-merger Entercom directors (or successors) plus four directors selected by CBS.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A background section]

## What crossed the boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing asset that crossed was CBS Radio's station portfolio and
  broadcasting business, converted into a wholly owned Entercom subsidiary. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A explanatory note]
- Corporate governance and executive leadership (the surviving registrant, board
  majority process, and management) were retained by Entercom's pre-merger team,
  notwithstanding CBS Common Stock holders receiving the majority of outstanding shares;
  the combined-company management team was represented as officers and employees "of
  both Entercom and CBS Radio led by David Field as President & Chief Executive Officer."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A background/reasons section]
- Operational/back-office functions supporting CBS Radio's business were, prior to
  closing, "generally being performed under CBS's centralized systems," and the proxy
  disclosed integration of the CBS Radio business as "dependent on ongoing transition
  services from CBS." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A risk factors]

## Contractual bridge for the shared substrate
- Entercom and CBS agreed to enter a Transition Services Agreement and a Joint Digital
  Services Agreement, under which CBS would provide transition services to Entercom for
  defined periods following closing; a recipient could terminate a given service on sixty
  days' written notice. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A "Other
  Agreements" section]
- CBS and Entercom also entered a Tax Matters Agreement and Trademark License Agreements,
  collectively (with the above) the "Ancillary Agreements." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A "Other Agreements" section]

## Regulatory condition affecting the asset boundary
- Post-merger Entercom would own radio stations in seven markets in excess of FCC
  local-ownership limits; Entercom proposed to divest 19 stations (eight Entercom, eleven
  CBS Radio), and as of October 10, 2017 had a binding agreement with Educational Media
  Foundation for stations in three markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047,
  DEFM14A "Divestitures" section]
- Stations not divested to third parties by closing were to be transferred to a
  divestiture trust and held separately pending sale. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A "Divestitures" section]
- The combined company was projected (a forward estimate, not a realized figure) to
  achieve annual cost synergies of $25 million within 12-18 months of consummation. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A "Reasons for the Merger" section]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined Entercom's public-company legal vehicle, governance
  structure, and pre-merger management/board control with CBS Radio's station-broadcast
  asset base and a non-controlling majority shareholder base, bridging CBS Radio's
  centralized-systems dependence on CBS through Transition/Joint Digital Services
  Agreements, and bridging the regulatory station-ownership excess through negotiated
  third-party divestitures and a divestiture trust rather than immediate full transfer.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-311047, DEFM14A explanatory note, summary, "Other
  Agreements" and "Divestitures" sections]
