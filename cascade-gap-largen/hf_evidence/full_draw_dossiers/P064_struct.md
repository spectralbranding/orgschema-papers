# Structural sub-dossier — Sauer-Danfoss Inc. / Daikin Industries manufacturing joint-venture restructuring (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On October 30, 2012, Sauer-Danfoss Inc. (a Delaware corporation headquartered at 2800
  East 13th Street, Ames, Iowa; the "Company") entered into a Second Amended and Restated
  Manufacturing Joint Venture Agreement (the "Agreement") with Daikin Industries, Ltd.
  ("Daikin"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01, filed 2012-10-31]
- The transaction restructures a pre-existing joint venture: in 2001 the Company and Daikin
  had formed two joint-venture entities — Sauer-Danfoss-Daikin Ltd. (the "Sales JV") and
  Daikin-Sauer-Danfoss Ltd. (the "Manufacturing JV") — through which they manufactured and
  sold hydrostatic transmissions and other hydraulic and mechanical products. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company and Daikin stated they entered into the Agreement "for the purpose of
  restructuring the joint venture." [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Ownership boundary — what crossed and what did not
- Legal vehicles (the tier that changed hands): the two Japanese-style "Ltd." joint-venture
  entities. Prior to the Agreement the Company owned 65% of the Sales JV and 45% of the
  Manufacturing JV, with the remaining interest in each owned by Daikin. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Equity that crossed: the Sales JV will redeem Daikin's entire ownership interest in the
  Sales JV for a price between $18 million and $24 million; as a result the Company will own
  all of the outstanding equity interests in the Sales JV. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Equity that did NOT cross: the Company will continue to own 45% of the equity interests in
  the Manufacturing JV (Daikin retaining the majority of that entity). [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Assets that crossed between the two JV entities: the Manufacturing JV will purchase certain
  assets from the Sales JV for a purchase price between $18 million and $24 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Product tier vs. producing-process tier
- Customer-facing product: the line of hydrostatic transmissions and other hydraulic and
  mechanical products currently manufactured and sold ("Existing Products"), plus a right for
  the Manufacturing JV to develop, manufacture, and sell pumps and motors in the 0–32 cc
  displacement range for the mobile-hydraulics market ("New Products"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The sales/distribution entity (Sales JV) consolidates fully under the Company, but the
  producing operations remain with the Manufacturing JV, which the Company only 45%-owns:
  the Manufacturing JV "will continue to manufacture and sell" the Existing Products it
  currently makes — i.e. the manufacturing process for the product line does NOT move under
  the Company's full control. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Rather than transferring the producing process, the Company and the Sales JV will license
  certain technology to the Manufacturing JV to enable that continued manufacture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate (closing-time)
- Reciprocal, geography-split distribution bridges the two entities: the Company will
  distribute the Manufacturing JV's products in markets outside Japan on a non-exclusive
  basis, and the Manufacturing JV will distribute the Company's products in the Japan market
  on a non-exclusive basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Product off-take rights: the Manufacturing JV may sell Existing Products to the Company, to
  original-equipment manufacturers in Japan, and (for certain open-circuit piston pumps and
  components) to Daikin for resale to industrial-hydraulic markets; New Products may be sold
  worldwide directly or, at the Manufacturing JV's option, through a distributorship
  agreement with the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Superseded instruments: the Amended and Restated Sales Joint Venture Agreement dated
  September 27, 2001 will be terminated, and the Amended and Restated Manufacturing Joint
  Venture Agreement dated September 27, 2001 will be superseded by the Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Tiers not addressed by the filing
- The 8-K does not describe any transfer, retention, or realignment of people, management, or
  organizational/culture at either JV entity in connection with the restructuring. [UNVERIFIED]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction gives the Company sole ownership of the joint sales/distribution entity
  (redeeming Daikin's interest for $18–24M) while leaving the producing operations in the
  Daikin-majority Manufacturing JV, which retains and continues the product-line manufacture;
  the resulting product/process split is bridged contractually by a technology license and a
  reciprocal non-exclusive distribution arrangement rather than by moving the producing
  process under common ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0000865754-12-000051, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
