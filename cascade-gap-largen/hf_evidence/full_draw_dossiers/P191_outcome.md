## Realized record traceable through press disclosures (non-financial; no primary financial scalar available)
- On December 21, 2021, Prysmian Group announced the legal-entity merger of General Cable
  Industries, Inc. and Prysmian Cables and Systems USA, LLC, with the resulting U.S.
  operations adopting the name "Prysmian Group US" effective January 1, 2022, describing
  this as concluding the integration process begun with the 2018 acquisition. [SRC: https://na.prysmian.com/resources/press-releases/prysmian-group-announces-legal-entity-integration-and-finalizes-rebranding-with-general-cable-in-the-united-states]
- At that announcement, the combined North American operation was described as comprising
  27 manufacturing facilities, 8 distribution centers, 6 R&D centers, and over 5,800
  employees, with stated planned investments of $100 million (U.S. Power Distribution
  plants) and $85 million (Telecom plant equipment/technology). [SRC: https://na.prysmian.com/resources/press-releases/prysmian-group-announces-legal-entity-integration-and-finalizes-rebranding-with-general-cable-in-the-united-states]
- Prysmian Group's CEO was quoted: "Three years after joining forces, we are stronger
  than ever ... we offer the most complete portfolio of wire and cable solutions crucial
  to supporting the energy transition and digital transformation in the U.S." These press
  disclosures describe organizational/branding integration milestones and do not report
  a segment- or entity-level financial scalar attributable specifically to the formerly
  separate General Cable business. [SRC: https://na.prysmian.com/resources/press-releases/prysmian-group-announces-legal-entity-integration-and-finalizes-rebranding-with-general-cable-in-the-united-states]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- General Cable deregistered its securities within two weeks of closing (Form 15-12B,
  June 18, 2018), and its acquirer, Prysmian S.p.A., does not file U.S. annual or
  periodic operating reports (its SEC CIK covers only an ADR facility); no primary-filing
  financial record therefore exists for the acquired business at the fixed ~2021-2023
  outcome window. The only located public record within the window is a December 2021
  Prysmian press release describing a legal-entity merger and rebranding of the former
  General Cable U.S. operations to "Prysmian Group US," effective January 1, 2022,
  alongside stated facility counts, headcount, and planned capital investment. This case
  is accordingly coded uncertain on the outcome for lack of a public financial record,
  per the no-record convention. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-194946, Form 15-12B] [SRC: https://na.prysmian.com/resources/press-releases/prysmian-group-announces-legal-entity-integration-and-finalizes-rebranding-with-general-cable-in-the-united-states]
