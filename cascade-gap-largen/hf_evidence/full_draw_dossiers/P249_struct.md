# Structural sub-dossier — First Potomac Realty Trust merger with affiliates of Government Properties Income Trust (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- First Potomac Realty Trust ("the Company"), a Maryland real estate investment trust
  formed in 2003, was a self-administered, self-managed REIT focused on owning,
  operating, developing and redeveloping office and business park properties in the
  greater Washington, D.C. region, with Common Shares publicly traded on the NYSE
  (NYSE: FPO). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]
- Under an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated June 27, 2017, the Company agreed to
  combine with Government Properties Income Trust ("GOV"), GOV NEW OPPTY REIT
  ("REIT Merger Sub", a newly formed, wholly-owned GOV subsidiary) and GOV NEW OPPTY LP
  ("Partnership Merger Sub", majority-owned by REIT Merger Sub and wholly owned by GOV).
  REIT Merger Sub and Partnership Merger Sub were created solely to engage in the
  transaction and had carried on no prior activities other than those incidental to
  formation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as two sequential mergers: the Partnership Merger
  (Partnership Merger Sub merged with and into the Company's operating partnership,
  which survived as a majority-owned subsidiary of GOV), immediately followed by the
  REIT Merger (the Company merged with and into REIT Merger Sub, with REIT Merger Sub
  surviving and the Company's separate corporate existence ceasing). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-301026,
  Form 8-K]
- Each Common Share was converted into the right to receive $11.15 in cash, without
  interest. The Mergers closed on October 2, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-301026, Form 8-K]
- Total consideration for the Mergers was approximately $1.4 billion, comprising
  approximately $652 million in cash, repayment of approximately $493 million of the
  Company's debt, assumption of approximately $168 million of the Company's mortgage
  debt, and transaction fees/expenses net of cash on hand; funding included
  approximately $560 million drawn under GOV's existing $750 million unsecured
  revolving credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-301026, Form 8-K]

## What the Company comprised at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- At June 30, 2017 the Company wholly owned properties totaling 6.0 million square
  feet and held a noncontrolling ownership interest in an additional 0.4 million
  square feet through two unconsolidated joint ventures; consolidated properties were
  92.6% occupied by 378 tenants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]
- By contrast, at the same date GOV owned 74 properties (96 buildings) across 31
  states and the District of Columbia totaling approximately 11.5 million rentable
  square feet, of which 57.8% was leased to the U.S. government and 21.8% to 13 state
  governments — a government-tenant-concentrated portfolio distinct from the Company's
  Washington-D.C.-region office/business-park tenant base. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle / management-model tier
- The Company was explicitly self-administered and self-managed. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]
- GOV was managed by an external manager, The RMR Group LLC ("RMR LLC"), described in
  the proxy as "GOV's manager." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]

## People/organization tier: what did not cross intact
- The employment of the Company's named executive officers (Robert Milkovich,
  President/CEO/COO; Andrew P. Blocher, EVP/CFO/Treasurer; Samantha S. Gallagher,
  EVP/General Counsel/Secretary) was terminated upon consummation of the mergers,
  triggering severance benefits under their existing Employment Agreements. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001193125-17-301026,
  Form 8-K]
- The Company and its subsidiaries agreed to terminate the employment of their other
  respective employees as well; RMR LLC (GOV's external manager) "may offer comparable
  employment to certain of such persons," with GOV required to cause RMR LLC to credit
  hired employees for prior years of service for eligibility/vesting purposes under
  RMR LLC's benefit plans. Any employee not offered comparable employment by RMR LLC
  was instead entitled to severance under the Company's existing severance guidelines,
  and GOV was required to cause RMR LLC to provide continuing COBRA coverage to
  employees who did not accept an RMR LLC offer. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A]
- Effective at closing on October 2, 2017, all members of the Company's board of
  trustees and all of its officers ceased to hold those positions; the Company ceased
  to exist as a corporate entity, with REIT Merger Sub continuing as the surviving
  corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-301026, Form 8-K]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On October 2, 2017, in connection with completion of the REIT Merger, the Company
  requested that the NYSE suspend trading in its Common Shares and file a Form 25
  notification of removal from listing and registration; all Common Shares were
  removed from trading on the NYSE that day before the opening of trading, with the
  Company intending to thereafter file a Form 15 to terminate registration under
  Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-301026, Form
  8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction moved the Company's customer-facing asset base (the Washington-D.C.-
  region office/business-park portfolio) whole into a newly formed GOV acquisition
  vehicle via a cash merger, while the Company's self-managed operating organization —
  its board, its named executive officers, and its broader employee base — was
  terminated rather than retained intact; the surviving entity's operating processes
  were instead bridged, at GOV's option, through discretionary offers of employment
  from GOV's own external manager, RMR LLC, to some (not all) of the Company's former
  employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-265999, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession
  0001193125-17-301026, Form 8-K]
