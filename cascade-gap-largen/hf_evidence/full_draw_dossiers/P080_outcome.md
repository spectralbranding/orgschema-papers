## Realized cost and schedule trajectory within the fixed window

- Per the FY2012 Form 10-K (filed 2013-02-22), total estimated first-phase project
  costs were reported at $52 billion, with start-up of the first LNG train "expected
  in late 2014, leading to the first LNG cargo in first quarter 2015"; as of year-end
  2012, more than 55 percent of project activities had been completed. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form 10-K]
- Per the FY2013 Form 10-K (filed 2014-02-21), total estimated first-phase project
  costs were reported at $54 billion, with plant start-up and first cargo "planned for
  mid-2015"; as of year-end 2013, approximately 75 percent of project activities had
  been completed, and through early 2014, 20 of 21 Train 1 LNG plant modules had been
  delivered and installed at Barrow Island, with the final module still pending. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]
- Between the FY2012 and FY2013 filings, the disclosed total project cost estimate
  rose by $2 billion ($52B to $54B) and the disclosed target start-up slipped from
  "late 2014 / Q1 2015" to "mid-2015." [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form
  10-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]

## Ownership, contracting, and operatorship continuity

- Both the FY2012 and FY2013 Form 10-Ks report Chevron continuing to hold a 47.3
  percent-owned and operated interest in the Gorgon Project, unchanged across the
  window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession
  0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]
- In both the FY2012 and FY2013 filings, Chevron reports binding long-term LNG Sales
  and Purchase Agreements with six Asian customers for about 4.8 million metric tons
  per year, representing about 65 percent of Chevron's share of project LNG in each
  year; discussions to raise this share (to 85 percent in the FY2012 filing, to around
  80 percent in the FY2013 filing) are described as continuing rather than concluded.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession
  0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]

## Capital allocation and reserves within the window

- Australia capital expenditures (Gorgon and the adjacent Wheatstone LNG project
  combined, not separately broken out) were reported at approximately $7.7 billion for
  2012 and approximately $9.6 billion for 2013; both filings also attribute Australia
  reserve additions (a 358 BCF revision increase and a 747 BCF extensions-and-
  discoveries increase, in the respective reporting years) primarily to Gorgon Project
  drilling results. [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC
  accession 0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)

- Within the fixed ~2012-2014 outcome window, Chevron's disclosed total cost estimate
  for the Gorgon Project's first phase rose from $52 billion to $54 billion and its
  disclosed target start-up slipped from "late 2014 / Q1 2015" to "mid-2015," while
  Chevron's 47.3 percent operated ownership interest and its ~65-percent-of-share
  binding LNG offtake commitments remained unchanged across the same two annual
  reports. [SRC: SEC accession 0000093410-13-000003, Form 10-K] [SRC: SEC accession
  0000093410-14-000011, Form 10-K]
