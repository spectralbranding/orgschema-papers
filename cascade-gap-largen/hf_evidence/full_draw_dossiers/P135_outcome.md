## Realized fate of the transaction
- The proposal was resubmitted in modified form: R.R. Donnelley originally made its
  proposal on May 12, 2009 and "the proposal was subsequently modified to respond to
  feedback from certain Quebecor World creditors and resubmitted on June 2 and June 8" of
  2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- On June 10, 2009 R.R. Donnelley announced that "its proposal to acquire substantially all
  of the assets and assume certain liabilities of Quebecor World has expired." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.1]
- The press release attributes the expiration to Quebecor World's rejection: "We are
  disappointed by the decision of Quebecor World to reject our June 8 proposal," and states
  that "a transaction ascribing a higher value to Quebecor World than we offered in our last
  proposal is simply not in the interests of RR Donnelley" (Thomas J. Quinlan III). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The same release references Quebecor World's "proposed stand-alone plan of
  reorganization," indicating Quebecor World proceeded with its own reorganization rather
  than the R.R. Donnelley asset sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Realized record over the fixed outcome window
- No assets of Quebecor World crossed to R.R. Donnelley under this transaction: the
  acquisition was not consummated, the proposal having expired on June 10, 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.1]
- R.R. Donnelley's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2013 (within the fixed
  window) contains no reference to "Quebecor World" (or a "World Color" successor) in its
  business description, segment disclosures, or financial statements — i.e., the proposed
  acquisition produced no acquired-business line, goodwill, impairment, or divestiture in
  R.R. Donnelley's consolidated results for the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-14-000376, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]

## Outcome coding note
- Because the transaction was never consummated (no ownership boundary was crossed), there
  is no post-close acquired-business outcome scalar (net income/loss, goodwill/asset
  write-down, impairment, or divestiture) attributable to this transaction to report. This
  is a documented non-consummation, primary-sourced to the June 10, 2009 expiration filing,
  not a data gap; the acquired-business trajectory for the fixed window is not applicable
  (no acquired business). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-14-000376, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The proposed acquisition of Quebecor World's assets by R.R. Donnelley did not close: after
  a May 12, 2009 proposal modified and resubmitted on June 2 and June 8, 2009, Quebecor
  World rejected the June 8 proposal and R.R. Donnelley announced on June 10, 2009 that its
  proposal had expired; R.R. Donnelley's in-window FY2013 annual report shows no Quebecor
  World business within its consolidated operations, consistent with a transaction that was
  never consummated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-128758, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-14-000376, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]
