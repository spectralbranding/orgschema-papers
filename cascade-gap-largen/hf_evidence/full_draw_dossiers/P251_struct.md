# Structural sub-dossier — Cavium, Inc. merger with Marvell Technology Group Ltd. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Cavium, Inc., a Delaware corporation, entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated November 19, 2017 with Marvell Technology Group Ltd., a Bermuda exempted
  company ("Marvell"/"Parent"), and Kauai Acquisition Corp., a Delaware corporation and
  indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent formed on November 17, 2017 solely to
  effect the merger ("Merger Sub"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Merger Sub is a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Marvell Technology, Inc. ("MTI"),
  itself a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Marvell; under the Merger Agreement Merger
  Sub merged with and into Cavium, with Cavium continuing as the surviving corporation
  and an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Marvell (a reverse triangular merger).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration was fixed (no shareholder election mechanism found in the drawn filing):
  each issued and outstanding Cavium share converted into the right to receive 2.1757
  Marvell common shares plus $40.00 in cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately following the Merger, Marvell's pre-existing shareholders were expected to
  own approximately 76.7% of Marvell's issued common shares, and Cavium's former
  shareholders approximately 23.3%, based on shares outstanding as of January 22, 2018.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Cavium's stockholders approved adoption of the Merger Agreement on March 16, 2018; the
  Merger was completed on July 6, 2018, governed by Section 251 of the Delaware General
  Corporation Law. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-213483, Form 8-K, Items 2.01/Introductory Note]

## What crossed the boundary at closing (the acquired asset/product tier)
- Cavium was described as a provider of highly integrated semiconductor processors
  enabling intelligent processing for wired and wireless infrastructure and cloud
  networking, communications, storage, and security applications; its products
  comprised multi-core processors for embedded and data center applications, network
  connectivity for servers and switches, storage connectivity, and security processors
  for offload and appliance use, sold to networking OEMs serving enterprise, data
  center, service-provider, and other end markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- The whole company (product/asset base, corporate entity, and outstanding equity) was
  acquired via the merger; no carve-out or partial-business exclusion was identified in
  the reviewed sections of the drawn filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling intent and process leading to the transaction
- Discussions between Syed Ali (Cavium's Co-Founder, President, CEO and Chairman) and
  Matt Murphy (Marvell's President and CEO) about a potential strategic transaction
  began informally by March 2017, with the possibility of a transaction first raised by
  Mr. Murphy at an in-person meeting; a formal Merger Agreement followed in November
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy's forward-looking-statement risk factors named "risks related to the
  retention of employees and protection of intellectual property of Marvell and Cavium"
  and the transaction and retention costs to be incurred regardless of completion,
  indicating an anticipated management/employee-retention risk at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]

## People / management tier: what was retained, what was contractually bridged
- Cavium entered into retention bonus agreements with each of its executive officers,
  under which each would receive a lump-sum payment equal to six months of base salary
  following the closing date, contingent on remaining employed through the payment date
  (or, if terminated without cause pre-closing, paid 60 days after termination). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the Merger Agreement, Marvell agreed that the Chairman and two other
  Cavium-designated board members would become members of the Marvell board of
  directors at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]
- Two of Cavium's named executive officers, including Mr. Hussain, entered into offer
  letter agreements with Marvell providing for continued service with Marvell's U.S.
  operating subsidiary (Marvell Semiconductor, Inc.) after the effective time,
  contingent on completion of the Merger — i.e., select management continuity was
  contractually arranged rather than automatic for all officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and financing/agreement changes at closing
- On July 6, 2018, in connection with consummation of the Merger, Cavium terminated its
  existing credit agreement (dated August 16, 2016, as amended March 20, 2017) with
  JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-213483, Form 8-K, Item 1.02]
- On July 6, 2018, Cavium requested that NASDAQ suspend trading of its shares prior to
  market open; NASDAQ filed a Form 25 with the SEC that day to remove the shares from
  listing and registration under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-213483, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or supply/operating
  agreement bridging a shared substrate between Cavium and Marvell was located in the
  reviewed portions of the drawn DEFM14A; the reviewed text did not surface such an
  arrangement. [UNVERIFIED]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a fixed cash-plus-stock reverse triangular merger folding the
  whole of Cavium's corporate entity and semiconductor-processor product line into
  Marvell as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary, with Cavium's listing removed the same
  day; management continuity was handled selectively through named-executive retention
  bonuses, two contingent offer-letter agreements, and three Cavium board designees
  joining the Marvell board, rather than wholesale retention of Cavium's management
  layer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-032604, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-213483, Form 8-K]
