# Structural sub-dossier — R.R. Donnelley proposed asset acquisition of Quebecor World Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured — what was proposed to cross the ownership boundary and what
> was not — as documented in the contemporaneous filings. It contains no post-transaction
> outcome information.

## Nature of the drawn filing and the parties
- The drawn filing is a Form 8-K reporting under Item 7.01 (Regulation FD Disclosure): on
  May 12, 2009 R.R. Donnelley & Sons Company ("R.R. Donnelley," a Delaware corporation)
  issued a press release announcing its proposal to acquire all or substantially all of
  the assets of Quebecor World Inc. ("Quebecor World," a Canadian corporation); the press
  release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 and the underlying letter as Exhibit 99.2. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The proposal was communicated in a letter from Thomas J. Quinlan III, R.R. Donnelley's
  President and CEO, to Jacques Mallette (President and CEO, Quebecor World Inc.), David
  McCarthy (President, Quebecor World (USA) Inc.) and Steven Strom (Managing Director,
  Jefferies & Company, Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.2]
- The document is expressly non-binding: "This letter is not a legally-binding offer or
  agreement. Any legally-binding offer or agreement will be set forth only in definitive
  documentation approved by you and us." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2]

## Transaction type as structured
- The proposed transaction is a purchase of "all or substantially all of the assets and
  properties of the Quebecor Debtors (including shares of their Latin American
  subsidiaries), free and clear of all claims and interests," to be implemented in a
  court-approved sale under Section 363(b) of the U.S. Bankruptcy Code with accompanying
  proceedings under the Canadian Companies' Creditors Arrangement Act ("CCAA"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2 (Proposal)]
- Quebecor World was in insolvency proceedings at the time: the letter references the draft
  First Amended Plan of Reorganization in the U.S. bankruptcy proceeding and the draft Plan
  of Reorganization and Compromise in the Canadian reorganization of Quebecor World Inc.,
  and states R.R. Donnelley believed its proposal offered creditors superior terms to those
  draft plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.1 / EX-99.2]

## What was proposed to cross the boundary — the asset/product
- The customer-facing asset is Quebecor World's printing operations; R.R. Donnelley stated
  the acquisition would "enhance our ability to provide customers more comprehensive
  end-to-end printing solutions [and] expand our geographic reach into the important
  Canadian market." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
- The assets and properties were to be transferred "free and clear of all claims and
  liabilities (other than ordinary course trade payables, specific contracts that we ask
  you to assume and assign to us, and other liabilities to be agreed)." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2 (Valuation Assumptions (v))]

## Proposed consideration and legal vehicle
- Proposed purchase consideration: cash equal to the amount contemplated for distribution
  under the draft Plans (believed ~US$700,000,000); plus cash on balance sheet (estimated
  at $257,000,000 as of June 30, 2009 per the Plans); plus 30 million shares of R.R.
  Donnelley common stock (~15% of outstanding shares, valued at US$394,200,000 based on the
  May 11, 2009 closing price). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.1 / EX-99.2 (Purchase Consideration)]
- The purchaser would be "one or more wholly-owned subsidiaries of RRD [with] no other
  investors or sources of capital or financing"; the transaction "is not subject to any
  financing condition and no shareholder approval is required," the only further internal
  approval being that of R.R. Donnelley's Board of Directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.1 / EX-99.2 (Structure and Documentation; Financing; Internal Approvals)]

## People / processes provisions (as proposed)
- The proposal contemplated a due-diligence phase including "meetings with QWI's management,
  and the reasonable opportunity to inspect QWI's facilities," but set out no committed
  terms for transfer of the workforce, management, or operating organization at the proposal
  stage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2 (Due Diligence)]
- Stalking-horse mechanics: no exclusivity, no-shop, or expense reimbursement required
  before a definitive asset purchase agreement; upon execution of a definitive agreement,
  R.R. Donnelley would require customary stalking-horse protections (overbid protections,
  milestones to closing, expense reimbursement, and a break-up fee "in an amount to be
  agreed"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2 (Stalking Horse Protections)]
- Regulatory conditions identified: U.S. Hart-Scott-Rodino waiting period, the Competition
  Act (Canada), and Investment Canada Act approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.2 (Regulatory Matters)]

## Later revision of the proposed terms
- On June 8, 2009 R.R. Donnelley made a revised non-binding proposal to Quebecor World Inc.
  for the acquisition of all or substantially all of the assets and properties of Quebecor
  World and certain debtor affiliates, communicated in a letter from Thomas J. Quinlan III
  to Alain Rhéaume (Lead Director) and Jacques Mallette (President and CEO). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-126600, Form 8-K Item 7.01 / EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (as structured)
- The transaction was structured as a proposed distressed-asset acquisition: one or more
  wholly-owned R.R. Donnelley subsidiaries would purchase all or substantially all of the
  assets and properties of the bankrupt Quebecor World (and Latin American subsidiary
  shares) free and clear of claims via a Section 363(b) / CCAA court-approved sale, for cash
  (~US$700M plus ~US$257M balance-sheet cash) plus 30 million R.R. Donnelley shares
  (~US$394.2M), with no financing condition and only board (not shareholder) approval
  required; committed transfer terms for the workforce/operating organization were not
  set at the proposal stage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-109081, Form 8-K EX-99.1 / EX-99.2] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-09-126600, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
