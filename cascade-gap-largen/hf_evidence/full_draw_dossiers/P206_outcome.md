## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- On October 17, 2017, Atwood Oceanics, Inc. filed a Form 15 certifying termination of
  registration of its common stock under Section 12(g) and suspension of its duty to
  file reports under Sections 13/15(d) of the Exchange Act; the filing recorded
  approximately one holder of record as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-062499, Form 15-12B]
- No Form 10-K, 20-F, or other periodic report was filed by Atwood Oceanics, Inc. (CIK
  0000008411) after this deregistration; no audited public financial statements exist
  for Atwood as a standalone reporting entity within the ~2020-2022 outcome window.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-062499, Form 15-12B]
- The acquirer, Ensco plc (CIK 0000314808), does not report Atwood as a separate segment
  or business line in its own periodic filings; a search of its fiscal-2021 Form 10-K
  finds no consolidated financial disclosure isolating the former Atwood business, only
  a residual reference to an "Atwood LTIP" (a legacy equity incentive plan assumed in
  the merger). [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Realized record of specific acquired assets identified by the acquirer
- The acquirer's own fiscal-2017 Form 10-K identifies four specific units acquired in
  the merger by their pre-merger Atwood names and post-merger Ensco fleet designations:
  ENSCO DS-12 (formerly Atwood Achiever), ENSCO DS-13 (formerly Atwood Admiral), ENSCO
  DS-14 (formerly Atwood Archer), and ENSCO 115 (formerly Atwood Orca); DS-13 and DS-14
  are there described as ultra-deepwater drillships still under construction at a South
  Korean shipyard. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-18-000019, Form 10-K (FY2017)]
- By the acquirer's fiscal-2021 Form 10-K (filed under the registrant's current name,
  Valaris plc, following Ensco's April 11, 2019 combination with Rowan Companies Inc.),
  the same four identified units had the following status: VALARIS DS-12, located
  offshore Angola, "Under contract"; VALARIS 115 (a jackup), located offshore Thailand,
  "Under contract"; VALARIS DS-13 and VALARIS DS-14, both still listed as "Under
  construction" in South Korea, with contractual status changed to an "Option" rather
  than a firm delivery commitment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- The same filing states that, in connection with the registrant's Chapter 11
  reorganization, on February 26, 2021 the Company entered into amended agreements with
  the shipyard for DS-13 and DS-14 providing an option, but not an obligation, to take
  delivery, with delivery obtainable any time up to a December 31, 2023 date, and
  estimated remaining milestone payments of approximately $119.1 million for DS-13 and
  $218.3 million for DS-14. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]

## Acquirer-level restructuring context (not attributed to Atwood specifically)
- The registrant (by then named Valaris plc) and certain subsidiaries filed voluntary
  Chapter 11 bankruptcy petitions on August 19, 2020 (Case No. 20-34114); the
  Bankruptcy Court confirmed a plan of reorganization on March 3, 2021, and the company
  emerged from Chapter 11 on April 30, 2021, eliminating $7.1 billion of debt and
  obtaining a $520 million new capital commitment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- This bankruptcy and reorganization were events of the consolidated acquirer entity
  (which by 2020 also included the businesses combined from Rowan Companies in 2019);
  no source was found in which the acquirer separately attributes the bankruptcy, or a
  specific financial-performance scalar, to the former Atwood business or fleet as such.

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Atwood Oceanics, Inc. was merged into Ensco (later Valaris plc) and
  deregistered in October 2017, there is no public audited outcome record (10-K/20-F)
  for Atwood as a standalone business for the ~2020-2022 window, and the acquirer does
  not report Atwood as a segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-062499, Form 15-12B]
- At the level of specific assets that the acquirer's own filings identify as
  originating from Atwood, the realized record by fiscal-2021 shows a mixed status: two
  of the four traceable units (the former Atwood Achiever and Atwood Orca) were under
  contract and operating, while the other two (the former Atwood Admiral and Atwood
  Archer, both newbuild drillships already under construction at closing) remained
  undelivered more than three years after the merger, with their construction
  commitments converted from a firm obligation to a non-binding option following the
  acquirer's 2020-2021 Chapter 11 reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-22-000011, Form 10-K (FY2021)]
- No source was found isolating a consolidated financial-performance figure (revenue,
  net income, impairment, or comparable scalar) for the former Atwood business as a
  whole within the fixed outcome window; per the no-record convention, the case is coded
  **uncertain** on any enterprise-level outcome for Atwood, while the asset-level record
  above is the best-documented, specifically-attributed realized fact available.
  [UNVERIFIED]
