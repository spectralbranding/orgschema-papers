## Public-reporting status (feasibility-relevant)

## Realized record of the Mexico operation
- Retention and expansion, not divestiture: at August 28, 2016 the company operated 715
  warehouses worldwide, of which 36 were Mexico locations — an increase from the 32 Mexico
  warehouses reported at the 2011 reference date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000909832-16-000032, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Mexico is listed among the countries where the company is "principally engaged in the
  operation of membership warehouses" (U.S. and Puerto Rico, Canada, U.K., Mexico, Japan,
  Australia, Spain, plus majority-owned Taiwan and Korea), indicating the operation was
  retained as an ongoing consolidated business. [SRC: SEC accession 0000909832-16-000032, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The company operated 214 warehouses in eight countries outside the U.S. during 2016 and
  stated it planned "to continue expanding our international operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0000909832-16-000032, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Management continuity across the window: Dennis R. Zook is listed as Executive Vice
  President, Chief Operating Officer, Southwest Division and Mexico, an officer with the
  company since 1993, indicating the Mexico operation continued under the same divisional
  management structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0000909832-16-000032, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
