## Entity continuity (feasibility of the outcome record)
- The registrant's later annual report is filed under the same CIK (1313918), same
  Commission file number (1-32551), and same I.R.S. Employer Identification Number
  (20-2008622) as the 2008-era registrant, now named CIFC Corp. (Delaware), confirming the
  continuous entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012) cover]
- The outcome record below is taken from CIFC Corp.'s Annual Report on Form 10-K for the
  fiscal year ended December 31, 2012 (filed April 1, 2013), which reports FY2012 with
  FY2011 comparatives. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Realized corporate trajectory within the window
- On April 13, 2011 (the "Merger Closing Date"), the registrant completed a merger with
  Commercial Industrial Finance Corp. ("Legacy CIFC"), an asset manager focused primarily
  on management of corporate credit investments; Legacy CIFC became CIFCAM, a wholly-owned
  subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Following the merger the registrant "re-focused on our core business as a fee-based
  corporate credit asset manager for third party investors" and was renamed CIFC Corp.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- During the year ended December 31, 2011 the Company recorded impairment charges on
  intangible assets of $1.8 million, "primarily related to the write-off of the 'Deerfield
  Capital Management' trade name as the Company determined it would no longer utilize the
  trade name to launch new investment products." [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Fate of the CDO management-contract business
- CDO assets under management declined during the year ended December 31, 2012, "primarily
  due to the exit of their scheduled 'reinvestment periods'"; the Company stated "We do not
  expect to issue new CDOs so we expect CDO AUM to continue to decline going forward as
  these funds run-off per their contractual terms." [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Total assets under management grew over the window on the loan-based (CLO) side: total
  AUM increased by $0.5 billion during FY2012, reflecting loan-based AUM up $1.2 billion
  offset by CDO AUM down $0.7 billion. Fee Earning AUM was reported at 49 accounts /
  approximately $14.3 billion at December 31, 2012 versus 44 accounts / approximately $13.8
  billion a year earlier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Best-documented outcome scalars
- Net loss attributable to CIFC Corp. was $(9.6) million for FY2012 and $(32.6) million for
  FY2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- On a total basis (including consolidated variable interest entities), net income (loss)
  was $(240.3) million for FY2012 and $(343.8) million for FY2011, with a net loss
  attributable to noncontrolling interests and Consolidated VIEs of $230.7 million in
  FY2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Operating expenses in the window included impairment of intangible assets of $1.8 million
  (FY2012) and $1.8 million (FY2011), and restructuring charges of $5.9 million (FY2012)
  and $3.7 million (FY2011). [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Documented interface / integration facts
- The management-contract roll-up continued through and after the merger: the Company noted
  that in acquiring Legacy CIFC it "also acquired CypressTree, a credit asset manager that
  Legacy CIFC acquired in December 2010, and CypressTree's CLO management contracts," and
  the FY2011 intangible impairment discussion also references the "Primus I management
  contract." [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The realized record shows the acquired-era asset-management identity being wound down
  (write-off and discontinuation of the "Deerfield Capital Management" trade name for new
  products) and the CDO portfolio on the drawn-transaction side placed into contractual
  run-off, while the surviving platform's growth shifted to the loan-based (CLO) business
  under the CIFC name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2011-2012 window the registrant survived as a going concern but merged
  into and rebranded as CIFC Corp. (April 13, 2011), reported net losses attributable to
  the company of $(32.6) million (FY2011) and $(9.6) million (FY2012), wrote off the
  "Deerfield Capital Management" trade name, and placed its CDO management-contract business
  into declared contractual run-off while growth migrated to the loan-based CLO platform.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001313918-13-000003, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
