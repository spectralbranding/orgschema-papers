## Registrant status and remaining operations in the outcome window
- NII Holdings continued as a reporting company (common stock on the Nasdaq Global Select
  Market) and filed a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2018 (cover)]
- By the outcome window the registrant's operations had contracted to a single operating
  company: "We conduct substantially all of our business through our Brazilian operating
  company, Nextel Telecomunicações Ltda., which we refer to as Nextel Brazil." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- The former operating companies had been divested and reported as discontinued operations:
  "In connection with the sale of Nextel Argentina, Nextel Mexico, Nextel Chile and Nextel
  Peru, we have included the results of these former operating companies for all periods
  presented as discontinued operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018, Note 6 Discontinued Operations]

## Best-documented realized financial scalars
- Net loss attributable to NII Holdings for the year ended December 31, 2018 was
  $143.1 million (reported $(143,080) thousand), compared with $293.1 million for 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A]
- Consolidated operating revenues declined by $250.0 million, or 29%, from 2017 to 2018,
  "partially as a result of a $135.0 million decrease in iDEN-based operating revenues
  resulting from the wind down of Nextel Brazil's iDEN network in mid-2018" and a 15%
  depreciation of the Brazilian real. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A]

## Realized disruption at the product / process and process / organization interfaces
- Product/network retirement: in September 2017 Nextel Brazil decided to wind down its iDEN
  operations; after migrating some subscribers to its WCDMA network, it disconnected all of
  its remaining iDEN subscribers at the end of the second quarter of 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Restructuring / asset actions in the record include charges related to the early
  termination of leases for approximately 600 transmitter and receiver sites in connection
  with a RAN-sharing agreement Nextel Brazil entered into with Telefónica Brazil, S.A. in May
  2016, plus non-cash asset impairment charges related to transmitter and receiver sites in
  Brazil. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018, MD&A]
- Organization/people strain surrounding the pending divestiture: "Retention of certain
  employees by us may be challenging while the sale is pending, as certain employees may
  experience uncertainty about their future roles with América Móvil." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 (risk factors)]

## Terminal disposition within the window
- Sale of the last operating unit: "On March 18, 2019, NII Holdings, Inc. and NII
  International Holdings S.à r.l., or NIIH... entered into a purchase agreement with América
  Móvil, S.A.B. de C.V., or AMX, and AI Brazil Holdings B.V.... pursuant to which NII and AI
  Brazil Holdings will sell their jointly-owned wireless operations in Brazil for an aggregate
  purchase price of $905.0 million on a debt free and cash free basis, subject to certain
  adjustments at closing." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- AMX agreed to place $30.0 million of the purchase price into an 18-month escrow. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Planned dissolution: "In connection with the proposed sale of Nextel Brazil, NII's Board of
  Directors has approved a plan to dissolve and wind up its headquarters upon the completion
  of this transaction, which is also subject to approval by NII's stockholders." "Following
  approval of our stockholders and the consummation of the sale of Nextel Brazil, we plan to
  begin the process of liquidation and dissolution of NII Holdings and its remaining
  subsidiaries." [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- The FY2018 10-K (filed March 18, 2019) was the registrant's last annual report; no Form
  10-K for fiscal year 2019 or 2020 was filed by CIK 1037016. [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 1037016, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1037016&type=10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- After divesting its Mexican operations in 2015, the registrant's remaining business
  narrowed to a single Brazilian operating company that continued to report net losses
  ($143.1 million in 2018), saw a 29% decline in consolidated revenues into 2018, retired its
  legacy iDEN network in mid-2018, and in March 2019 agreed to sell that last operating unit
  to América Móvil for an aggregate $905.0 million, with the board approving a plan to
  dissolve and wind up the company on completion; the FY2018 Form 10-K was the registrant's
  final annual report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001037016-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018] [SRC: SEC EDGAR filing index for CIK 1037016, https://www.sec.gov/cgi-bin/browse-edgar?action=getcompany&CIK=1037016&type=10-K]
