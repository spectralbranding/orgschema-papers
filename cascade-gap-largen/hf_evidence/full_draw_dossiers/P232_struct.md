# Structural sub-dossier — GGP Inc. merger with affiliates of Brookfield Property Partners (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- GGP Inc., a Delaware corporation described in its own joint proxy statement/prospectus
  as "a self-administered and self-managed REIT" whose primary business is "owning and
  operating best-in-class retail properties," entered into an Agreement and Plan of
  Merger dated March 26, 2018 (as amended June 25, 2018) with Brookfield Property
  Partners L.P. (BPY) and Goldfinch Merger Sub Corp., an indirect, wholly owned
  subsidiary of BPY, which together with certain BPY affiliates already held
  approximately 34% of outstanding GGP common stock at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger was structured so that Goldfinch (the merger sub) merged with and into GGP,
  with GGP surviving as an indirect subsidiary of BPY and immediately renamed Brookfield
  Property REIT Inc. ("BPR"), intended to be a publicly traded U.S. REIT issuing a new
  class A stock structured to provide economic equivalence to an investment in BPY. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed and became effective at 8:20 a.m. Eastern time on August 28, 2018;
  related charter amendments, the pre-closing dividend, and the Class B Stock Exchange
  had effective/closing dates of August 27-28, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]

## Consideration structure (whole-company cash/stock election)
- Unaffiliated GGP common stockholders were entitled to a combination of (i) a
  pre-closing special dividend and (ii) a per-share merger consideration, together
  structured around an aggregate value of up to $23.50 per share, satisfied through a
  cash-or-stock election (class A stock or, via an exchange option, BPY units), subject
  to proration if either the cash or stock election was oversubscribed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A]
- The aggregate per-share merger consideration pool was defined relative to a
  $9,250,000,000 base figure, adjusted for partnership-unit and restricted-stock cash
  amounts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A]
- Based on closing prices on the NASDAQ on August 28, 2018, the aggregate implied value
  of the consideration paid by BPY in the transaction was approximately $14.4 billion,
  comprising approximately $9.3 billion in cash and approximately $5.2 billion in BPY
  units and class A stock; funding sources included approximately $4.1 billion of cash
  proceeds from sales of certain joint-venture interests/assets by five investor groups
  and syndicated debt financing (revolving credit and term loan facilities). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
- At the Merger Effective Time BPY and its affiliates came to own, directly or
  indirectly, 87% of the voting securities of BPR. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]

- Shares of GGP common stock and the pre-existing Series A preferred stock were
  suspended from trading on the New York Stock Exchange effective as of the opening of
  trading on August 28, 2018; at BPR's request the NYSE filed a Form 25 with the SEC to
  remove the shares from listing and terminate their registration under Section 12(b) of
  the Exchange Act, and BPR stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate
  registration; BPR's new class A stock and Series A preferred stock were separately
  approved for listing on the NASDAQ and began trading there under the ticker symbols
  "BPR" and "BPRAP" effective the same day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]

## Management/process tier: transition from self-managed to externally managed
- Prior to the transaction, GGP described itself as "a self-administered and
  self-managed REIT," in contrast to BPY, which the joint proxy statement/prospectus
  itself described elsewhere as structured as "an externally managed non-U.S. publicly
  traded partnership rather than an internally managed U.S. REIT like GGP." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A]
- At the closing of the transaction, BPR and GGP Operating Partnership, LP (GGPOP)
  entered into a Master Services Agreement, dated August 27, 2018, with affiliates of
  Brookfield Asset Management Inc. (BAM), under which the BAM affiliates (the "service
  providers") would provide management and administration services to BPR/GGPOP (the
  "service recipients") for a base management fee, on terms consistent with the
  amended and restated master services agreement already in place between BPY and its
  own service providers; a separate management fee letter agreement provided for BAM to
  waive certain of these management fees for the first twelve months following closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A][SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
- Following the Merger Effective Time, BPR "has no employees and relies on the Service
  Providers to conduct its operations"; two BAM/service-provider personnel (Brian W.
  Kingston and Bryan K. Davis) were expected to perform functions similar to a principal
  executive officer and principal financial officer, respectively, for BPR, rather than
  BPR employing its own executive officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]

## People/organization tier: officer and director turnover at closing
- Effective as of the Merger Effective Time, "all of the former officers of GGP ceased
  to act as officers of BPR." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
- Effective as of the Merger Effective Time, GGP's chief executive officer Sandeep
  Mathrani and directors Richard B. Clark, Mary Lou Fiala, J. Bruce Flatt, Janice R.
  Fukakusa, John K. Haley, Daniel B. Hurwitz, Brian W. Kingston, and Christina M.
  Lofgren resigned from the GGP/BPR board; the filing states none of these resignations
  resulted from any disagreement with GGP, its management, or its board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
- The incoming BPR board was instead drawn from the directors of the BPY general
  partner: Jeffrey M. Blidner, Dr. Soon Young Chang, Richard B. Clark, Omar Carneiro da
  Cunha, Stephen DeNardo, Louis Joseph Maroun, and Lars Rodert became BPR directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
- GGP employees other than departing officers were noted as potentially continuing:
  the joint proxy statement/prospectus stated "certain employees of GGP may be employed
  by BAM in connection with the management of BPR." Separately, BPR, BPY, the BPY
  general partner, and BP US REIT LLC (formerly Brookfield Properties, Inc.) entered
  into a joint governance agreement, dated August 28, 2018, intended to facilitate the
  governance of BPR and BPY together given the shared management team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-204333, Form DEFM14A][SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction converted a self-administered, self-managed U.S. REIT (GGP) into an
  indirect, majority-owned subsidiary of BPY (87% of voting securities) via a
  whole-company merger with cash/stock-election consideration (~$14.4 billion implied
  aggregate value); the surviving entity's customer-facing retail real estate portfolio
  and legal corporate shell (renamed Brookfield Property REIT Inc.) crossed into the
  BPY structure, but its self-managed operating/process substrate did not carry over
  intact — it was replaced at closing by a Master Services Agreement under which BPR
  "has no employees" and relies on BAM-affiliated service providers, while GGP's entire
  prior slate of officers and most named directors departed simultaneously, bridged
  only by a joint governance agreement and a temporary (12-month) management-fee
  waiver. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-260735, Form 8-K]
