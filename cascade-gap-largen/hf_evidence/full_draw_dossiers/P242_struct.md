# Structural sub-dossier — Microsemi Corporation merger with Microchip Technology Incorporated (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Microsemi Corporation, a Delaware corporation headquartered at One Enterprise, Aliso
  Viejo, California, agreed to merge with Maple Acquisition Corporation, a Delaware
  corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Microchip Technology Incorporated, pursuant
  to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 1, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties structured the transaction as a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub was
  to merge with and into Microsemi, with Microsemi surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary
  of Microchip; Merger Sub was newly incorporated solely to consummate the transaction and
  had engaged in no other business activity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of Microsemi common stock were entitled to receive $68.78 per share in cash,
  representing an approximately 7% premium to the closing price on the last trading day
  before the public announcement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- The Microsemi Board unanimously determined the merger fair to and in the best interests
  of Microsemi and its stockholders, and Qatalyst Partners rendered a fairness opinion on
  the $68.78 per share consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided for a $290,000,000 termination fee payable by Microsemi
  under specified circumstances, including termination to accept a superior proposal.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- Microchip obtained binding financing commitments of $8.747 billion, not conditioned on
  Microchip's or Merger Sub's obligations under the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on May 29, 2018: Merger Sub merged with and into Microsemi, with
  Microsemi surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Microchip; the aggregate cash
  merger consideration paid to former equityholders was approximately $8.2 billion.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K item 2.01]

## What crossed at the asset/product tier
- Microsemi's business as a whole — described as a comprehensive portfolio of
  semiconductor and system solutions for aerospace and defense, communications, data
  center and industrial markets (analog mixed-signal ICs, FPGAs/SoCs/ASICs, power
  management, timing and synchronization, RF, and related custom design services) —
  crossed intact into the surviving corporation as a wholly owned subsidiary; the merger
  agreement contemplated no carve-out or partial-asset separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
- Microsemi's outstanding secured credit agreement was repaid in full and terminated in
  connection with the closing, and its 9.125% senior notes due 2023 were redeemed and the
  related indenture satisfied and discharged, replacing Microsemi's existing debt
  structure at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K items 1.02, 8.01]

## Legal vehicle, control, and listing/registration changes at closing
- At the effective time, Microsemi's certificate of incorporation and bylaws were amended
  and restated to those of Merger Sub, and a change in control of the company occurred as
  it became a wholly owned subsidiary of Microchip (Parent). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K items 5.01, 5.03]
- On closing, Microsemi requested that Nasdaq suspend trading of its common stock and file
  a notification to remove the shares from listing and registration under the Exchange
  Act, and stated its intent to subsequently file a Form 15 to terminate its Exchange Act
  reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K item 3.01]
- Microsemi filed a Form 15-12G on June 8, 2018, certifying termination of registration of
  its common stock and reporting approximately one holder of record as of that date.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-188087, Form 15-12G]

## What did not carry over at the people/management tier
- At the effective time, the members of Merger Sub's board of directors immediately prior
  to closing — Steve Sanghi, Ganesh Moorthy and J. Eric Bjornholt — became the directors
  of the surviving corporation, and Merger Sub's executive officers (Sanghi as Chief
  Executive Officer, Moorthy as President, Bjornholt as Chief Financial Officer, plus a
  Secretary and Assistant Secretary) became the surviving corporation's executive
  officers; these were Microchip-side officers installed via the Merger Sub shell, not
  Microsemi's pre-merger executive team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K item 5.02]
- Ahead of closing, Microsemi's own executive officers (including its CEO, CFO, and other
  named officers) were each party to a retention agreement providing severance benefits
  if their employment was terminated without cause, or by the officer for good reason,
  following the change in control constituted by the merger (a "qualifying termination");
  the Board also approved additional cash and equity retention arrangements, including
  double-trigger severance, for other key employees in view of retention risk the
  business combination was expected to create. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]

## Contractual bridge and financing
- No transition-services, shared-services, or supply agreement bridging Microsemi's
  operations to Microchip at closing was identified in the reviewed filings; the
  documented closing-time arrangements were the merger consideration, the retention/
  severance agreements for Microsemi personnel described above, and financing/debt
  refinancing mechanics. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-company cash merger — Microsemi's product/asset base
  and legal entity crossing intact into a wholly owned Microchip subsidiary via a reverse
  triangular merger — with an immediate replacement, at the surviving-corporation board
  and executive-officer level, of Microsemi's pre-merger leadership by Microchip's own
  officers (Sanghi/Moorthy/Bjornholt), while Microsemi's own named executive officers and
  other key employees were covered instead by pre-negotiated retention/severance
  agreements rather than continuing in their surviving-corporation roles as installed
  officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-176068, Form 8-K item 5.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-021362, Form DEFM14A]
