## Outcome window
- Fixed outcome window: fiscal years ended December 31, 2022 and December 31, 2023, as reported in the annual reports on Form 10-K filed February 21, 2023 and February 22, 2024, respectively (approximately 3-5 years after the combination). Historical background items (the 2019 renaming and the 2020-2021 Chapter 11 restructuring) are drawn from the same in-window 10-K's disclosures and from the registrant's contemporaneous 8-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY ended 2022-12-31] [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY ended 2023-12-31]

## Early post-closing corporate identity change
- As announced July 2, 2019, the registrant (then named Ensco Rowan plc) changed its name to Valaris plc; effective July 31, 2019, its NYSE ticker for Class A ordinary shares changed to "VAL" from "ESV." [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-19-000134, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Chapter 11 proceedings and emergence
- On August 19, 2020 (the "Petition Date"), Valaris plc ("Legacy Valaris") and certain direct and indirect subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for relief under Chapter 11 in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court, captioned In re Valaris plc, et al., Case No. 20-34114 (MI). [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 2 - Chapter 11 Proceedings]
- As a result of the Chapter 11 filing, the Class A ordinary shares of Legacy Valaris were delisted from the NYSE effective September 14, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, market information section]
- The Bankruptcy Court confirmed the Debtors' Chapter 11 plan of reorganization on March 3, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 2 - Chapter 11 Proceedings]
- On the Effective Date, April 30, 2021, the company completed its financial restructuring and emerged from the Chapter 11 Cases: it eliminated $7.1 billion of debt and obtained a $520 million capital injection by issuing First Lien Notes; Legacy Valaris' equity was cancelled and approximately 75.0 million new Common Shares and 5.6 million Warrants were issued, which began trading on the NYSE under "VAL" and "VAL WS." [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, Note 2 - Chapter 11 Proceedings]
- As part of the restructuring, the registrant's disclosures as of the FY2023 10-K describe it as "a Bermuda exempted company," subject to Bermuda law rather than the England-and-Wales incorporation used at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY2023, description of capital stock / risk factors]

## Realized financial trajectory in the fixed window
- The FY2022 10-K reports net income (loss) attributable to Valaris of $176.5 million for the year ended December 31, 2022, compared with a net loss of $(27.4) million for the eight months ended December 31, 2021 (Successor period) and net losses of $(4,467.0) million and $(4,494.4) million for the four months ended April 30, 2021 and the year ended December 31, 2020 (Predecessor periods), respectively. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, selected financial data]
- The FY2023 10-K reports net income (loss) of $866.8 million and net income attributable to Valaris of $865.4 million for the year ended December 31, 2023, an increase of $688.9 million, or approximately 390%, over the $176.5 million attributable to Valaris for 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY2023, results of operations]
- The company has not paid or declared any dividends on its Common Shares since emergence, and its Indenture and Credit Agreement include provisions limiting its ability to pay dividends. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY2023, dividends section]

## Realized operating configuration
- As of the FY2022 10-K, Valaris Limited described itself as owning 52 rigs (11 drillships, four dynamically positioned semisubmersible rigs, one moored semisubmersible rig, and 36 jackup rigs) plus a 50% equity interest in Saudi Aramco Rowan Offshore Drilling Company ("ARO"), its 50/50 unconsolidated joint venture with Saudi Aramco, which itself owned an additional seven rigs. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 1 Business]
- As of the FY2023 10-K, the company reported owning 53 rigs (13 drillships, four dynamically positioned semisubmersible rigs, one moored semisubmersible rig, and 35 jackup rigs) plus the 50% ARO interest, with ARO then owning an additional eight rigs. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY2023, Item 1 Business]
- The ARO joint venture with Saudi Aramco continued to be reported as an unconsolidated 50/50 interest through both fiscal years in the window, with newbuild jackup rigs on order for ARO delayed from planned 2022 delivery into 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022, Item 1 Business]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Following its 2018-2019 combination and 2019 renaming to Valaris plc, the registrant filed for Chapter 11 protection in August 2020, was delisted from the NYSE in September 2020, and emerged in April 2021 as a Bermuda-domiciled entity after eliminating $7.1 billion of debt and relisting on the NYSE; within the fixed FY2022-FY2023 outcome window it recorded a return to profitability, with net income attributable to the company rising from $176.5 million (FY2022) to $865.4 million (FY2023, a 390% increase), while operating a broadly similar-sized rig fleet (52 owned rigs in FY2022, 53 in FY2023) plus its continuing 50% interest in the pre-existing ARO joint venture with Saudi Aramco, and while paying no dividends due to debt-covenant restrictions. [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-23-000018, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0000314808-24-000014, Form 10-K FY2023]
