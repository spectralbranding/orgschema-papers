# Structural sub-dossier — Care Capital Properties, Inc. merger with Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc. (a Maryland corporation) and Care Capital Properties,
  Inc. ("CCP", a Delaware corporation) entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger
  dated May 7, 2017, together with PR Sub, LLC (a Delaware LLC and wholly owned
  subsidiary of Sabra, the "Merger Sub"), Sabra Health Care Limited Partnership, and
  Care Capital Properties, LP. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured so that CCP merged with and into Merger Sub (the
  "Merger"), and immediately thereafter Merger Sub merged with and into Sabra (the
  "Subsequent Merger"), with Sabra continuing as the surviving corporation; Care
  Capital Properties, LP simultaneously merged with and into Sabra Health Care Limited
  Partnership (the "Partnership Merger"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of CCP common stock outstanding immediately prior to the effective time
  was converted into the right to receive 1.123 newly issued shares of Sabra common
  stock (a fixed exchange ratio, not adjusted for stock-price changes, with cash paid
  in lieu of fractional shares); no cash-election alternative was offered. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- Sabra and CCP each held a special stockholder meeting on August 15, 2017 to approve
  the transaction, which the parties' negotiation history describes as having been
  structured and negotiated as an all-stock "merger of equals." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger, Subsequent Merger, and Partnership Merger were completed on August 17,
  2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]

## What crossed at closing (asset/product tier)
- As of March 31, 2017, CCP's portfolio comprised 337 properties operated by 39
  private regional and local care providers, spread across 36 states, containing
  approximately 37,000 beds/units — skilled nursing facilities and other healthcare
  assets leased to unaffiliated tenants under long-term triple-net leases, plus a
  smaller portfolio of secured and unsecured loans made primarily to skilled nursing
  facility operators and other post-acute care providers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- In connection with consummation of the Merger, effective August 17, 2017, Care
  Capital Properties, LP repaid all outstanding obligations and terminated the
  commitments under its Credit and Guaranty Agreement (dated August 17, 2015) and its
  Term Loan and Guaranty Agreement (dated January 29, 2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]

## Management/process tier: did not cross intact
- Effective upon consummation of the Merger, and as contemplated by the merger
  agreement rather than because of any disagreement, CCP's directors ceased serving
  in that capacity, and CCP's four named executive officers — Raymond J. Lewis (Chief
  Executive Officer), Lori B. Wittman (Executive Vice President and Chief Financial
  Officer), Timothy A. Doman (Executive Vice President and Chief Operating Officer),
  and Kristen M. Benson (Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Corporate
  Secretary) — resigned as officers of the Company at the effective time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]
- Three former CCP directors — Raymond J. Lewis, Ronald G. Geary, and Jeffrey A.
  Malehorn — were appointed to the Sabra board of directors at closing, which was
  expanded from five to eight directors to accommodate them; this was a governance
  (board-seat) role, not a continuing executive or operating role. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K; SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- CCP's outgoing executive officers held employment or noncompetition agreements
  (an employment agreement for Mr. Lewis; employee protection and noncompetition
  agreements for Ms. Wittman, Mr. Doman, and Ms. Benson) providing severance and other
  separation payments and benefits upon certain terminations of employment following
  a change in control of the kind the merger constituted. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- The joint proxy statement states that "the current senior leadership team of Sabra
  is not expected to change as a result of the merger and will be led by Mr. Matros,
  the chairman and chief executive officer of Sabra." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle and listing at closing
- The merger agreement obligated the parties to cooperate to remove CCP common stock
  from listing on the NYSE and terminate its registration under the Exchange Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]
- Effective at approximately 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time on August 17, 2017, CCP common
  stock ceased trading on the NYSE, and CCP requested that the NYSE file a
  notification of removal from listing and registration on Form 25 with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]
- Following the Merger, Subsequent Merger, and Partnership Merger, the surviving
  registrant identifies itself as "Sabra Health Care REIT, Inc., as successor by
  merger to PR Sub, LLC, as successor by merger to Care Capital Properties, Inc."; CCP
  no longer exists as a separate corporate entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]

## Transitional arrangements
- No transition-services, shared-services, or external-management agreement specific
  to this transaction is described in the drawn filing. The only transition-services
  agreement referenced in the document is a prior, unrelated arrangement between CCP
  and Ventas, Inc. dating from CCP's own 2015 separation from Ventas, which had already
  concluded by August 31, 2016, before this transaction was negotiated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined CCP's customer-facing asset/product tier — a 337-property,
  39-operator, ~37,000-bed/unit skilled-nursing and healthcare real estate portfolio —
  into Sabra through a stock-for-stock reverse triangular merger plus parallel
  partnership merger, with Sabra as the sole surviving public corporation. The
  process/management tier did not cross intact: all of CCP's executive officers
  resigned at the effective time under severance/change-in-control provisions, and
  continuity from CCP into the combined company's governance was limited to three
  non-executive board seats (of an eight-member board), while Sabra's own pre-existing
  senior leadership team was expected to, and did, continue running the combined
  company. CCP's separate legal existence and NYSE listing ended at closing, with no
  transition-services or shared-services agreement identified bridging the two
  organizations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-224333, Form DEFM14A; SEC accession 0001104659-17-052481, Form 8-K]
