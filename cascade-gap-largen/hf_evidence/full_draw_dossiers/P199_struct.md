# Structural sub-dossier — KLX Inc. / The Boeing Company merger, concurrent with the KLX Energy Services spin-off (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Parties and pre-closing structure

- Before closing, KLX Inc. operated through two segments: an Aerospace Solutions Group
  ("a leading distributor and value added service provider of aerospace fasteners and
  consumables") and an Energy Services Group ("completion, intervention and production
  services" to onshore U.S. oil and gas producing regions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Parties"]
- The Boeing Company is described in the proxy as "the world's largest aerospace
  company," with approximately 140,000 employees; Boeing common stock traded on the
  NYSE under "BA." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Parties"]
- Kelly Merger Sub, Inc. was a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of
  Boeing, formed for the sole purpose of effecting the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Parties"]

## Transaction structure as agreed (Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated 2018-04-30, amended 2018-06-01)

- Merger Sub was to merge with and into KLX, with Merger Sub ceasing to exist and KLX
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Boeing (a whole-entity merger of the KLX
  corporate shell, not an asset carve-out). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Merger"]
- KLX stockholders were to receive $63.00 in cash per share for their KLX common stock
  as merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "Merger Consideration"]
- Prior to or simultaneously with the merger, KLX was to transfer its Energy Services
  Group business to a newly formed subsidiary, KLX Energy Services Holdings, Inc., and
  then distribute 100% of that subsidiary's shares pro rata to KLX stockholders (the
  "spin-off"); the merger consideration was structured as separate from and additional
  to the spin-off distribution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Merger"]
- KLX stockholder approval was required for the merger but not for the spin-off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "The Merger"]

## What separated (Energy Services Group tier) and how the shared substrate was bridged

- On 2018-07-13, KLX and KLX Energy Services entered a distribution agreement, an
  employee matters agreement, a transition services agreement, and an IP matters
  agreement (together, the "spin-off transaction agreements") governing the parties'
  relationship after the spin-off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "Spin-Off Transaction Agreements"]
- The employee matters agreement allocated employees whose duties related to the Energy
  Services business to KLX Energy Services; a subset would remain formally employed by
  KLX after the distribution date while providing shared services to both companies,
  transferring to KLX Energy Services on or before completion of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "Employee Matters Agreement"]
- The transition services agreement had KLX (or its subsidiaries/third-party providers)
  continue to supply KLX Energy Services with treasury (including payroll), internal
  audit, tax, accounting, human resources/benefits, legal, IT, and other administrative
  services, for a term of not more than six months from the merger's closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "Transition Services Agreement"]
- The IP matters agreement governed transfer of certain KLX trademarks to KLX Energy
  Services upon merger consummation, and the distribution agreement included a
  five-year worldwide non-compete on KLX Energy Services against engaging in the sale
  of aerospace fasteners and consumables to the same aerospace/defense customer set
  KLX served. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A, "IP Matters Agreement" / "Non-Compete"]
- During the interval between the spin-off and the merger's consummation, KLX Energy
  Services' directors were also to serve as KLX's directors, and KLX Energy Services'
  Chairman/CEO/President and CFO were also to serve as KLX's Chairman/CEO and
  President/COO respectively — a period of overlapping leadership across the two
  now-separate corporate entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-057602, Form 8-K dated 2018-09-19]
- The spin-off closed on 2018-09-14: each KLX stockholder as of 2018-09-03 received 0.4
  shares of KLX Energy Services common stock per KLX share held; KLX Energy Services
  began trading on Nasdaq under "KLXE" on 2018-09-17. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-057602, Form 8-K dated 2018-09-19]

## What crossed to Boeing at closing (Aerospace Solutions Group tier) and governance change

- The merger closed on 2018-10-09; Merger Sub merged with and into KLX, and KLX (by
  then comprising the Aerospace Solutions Group business, the Energy Services Group
  having already been spun off) became a wholly owned subsidiary of Boeing, retaining
  its corporate existence rather than being dissolved into Boeing's own corporate shell. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
- The total aggregate consideration paid was approximately $3.2 billion in cash,
  excluding assumed net debt; Boeing financed the merger with available cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
- All eight incumbent KLX directors, including Chairman/CEO Amin J. Khoury, resigned at
  the effective time and were replaced by a single director, Verett A. Mims (previously
  the director of Merger Sub). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
- Seven incumbent KLX officers (including Khoury) resigned as officers at the effective
  time; five new officers (Christopher J. Wolter, Denise McKinney, Verett A. Mims,
  Gregory C. Vogelsperger, and Stephanie L. Hernandez) took office in their place. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
- In connection with the merger's consummation, KLX notified Nasdaq and requested that
  trading of its common stock be suspended prior to market open on 2018-10-09, and
  requested that Nasdaq file a Form 25 removing the common stock from listing and
  deregistering it under Exchange Act Section 12(b). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
- KLX subsequently filed a Form 15 on 2018-10-19 certifying termination of its
  Exchange Act reporting obligations, listing one (1) holder of record as of that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-062897, Form 15-12B dated 2018-10-19]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- The transaction combined a business-model separation (Energy Services Group carved
  out into an independent, separately traded company, with the shared corporate
  substrate bridged by distribution/employee-matters/transition-services/IP agreements
  and a period of overlapping leadership) with a whole-entity acquisition of the
  remaining KLX corporate shell (holding the Aerospace Solutions Group business) by
  Boeing, in which the surviving KLX entity's board and executive officers were replaced
  in full at the effective time while the entity itself continued to exist as a wholly
  owned Boeing subsidiary rather than being merged into Boeing's own operations at
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-005152, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-061104, Form 8-K dated 2018-10-09]
