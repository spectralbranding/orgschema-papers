# Structural sub-dossier — PAREXEL International Corporation acquisition by West Street Parent, LLC / Pamplona Capital Management LLP affiliate (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On June 19, 2017, PAREXEL International Corporation (the "Company") entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with West Street Parent, LLC ("Parent") and West Street
  Merger Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), providing for the acquisition of the Company by an
  affiliate of the private equity investment firm Pamplona Capital Management LLP
  ("Pamplona"); Merger Sub, a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, would merge with and
  into the Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent
  under the Massachusetts Business Corporation Act. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Parent, a Delaware limited liability company, was formed on June 19, 2017 solely to
  enter into the Merger Agreement and related agreements and complete the merger; Parent
  had not engaged in any business activities prior to the proxy statement other than in
  connection with the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- PAREXEL was described as a leading global biopharmaceutical services company providing
  clinical research, consulting, medical communications, and technology solutions,
  headquartered near Boston, Massachusetts, with offices in 86 locations in 51 countries
  and approximately 19,600 employees; its common stock traded on NASDAQ under "PRXL."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- Pamplona was described as managing over $10 billion in assets across a platform
  spanning private equity, fund of hedge funds, and single-manager hedge fund
  investments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "The Parties to the Merger"]
- At the special shareholder meeting held September 15, 2017, shareholders were asked to
  vote on adopting the Merger Agreement, plus a non-binding advisory compensation vote
  and an adjournment proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A notice of special meeting]

## Merger consideration and what crossed at closing
- Each outstanding share of PAREXEL common stock (other than excluded and dissenting
  shares) would be automatically canceled and converted into the right to receive $88.10
  in cash, without interest — a whole-company, all-cash going-private acquisition
  structured as a reverse triangular merger; the same $88.10/share cash conversion
  applied to outstanding stock options, RSU awards, and PRSU awards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "The Merger" / "Treatment of PAREXEL Equity Awards"]
- The Company's Employee Stock Purchase Plan was terminated on July 31, 2017 pursuant to
  board resolutions adopted under the Merger Agreement, with accumulated participant
  contributions used to purchase shares that were then converted into the $88.10/share
  merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Treatment of PAREXEL's ESPP"]
- If completed, the Company would cease to be an independent public company, its common
  stock would be removed from listing on NASDAQ, and it would be deregistered under the
  Exchange Act with periodic SEC reporting ceasing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A section on removal from listing and deregistration of PAREXEL common stock]
- Legal vehicle: Merger Sub (a newly formed subsidiary of Parent) would merge into the
  Company, with the Company surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent — the
  entire corporate entity, not a carved-out business unit, was the transaction's
  subject. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "The Merger Agreement — The Merger"]

## People / organization provisions bridging the transition
- Pursuant to the Merger Agreement, for a period of not less than 18 months after
  closing, Parent would provide continuing employees base salaries/hourly rates and
  annual target cash incentive opportunities "at least equal, in the aggregate" to those
  provided immediately before closing, and employee benefits (excluding equity,
  nonqualified deferred compensation, defined benefit, and retiree welfare benefits)
  "substantially comparable in the aggregate," plus assumption of PAREXEL's existing
  severance guidelines for qualifying terminations during that period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Employee Matters"]
- As of the date of the proxy statement, none of PAREXEL, Parent, or Merger Sub had
  entered into any post-merger employment agreements with PAREXEL's executive officers,
  though certain executives had had or might have discussions regarding continued
  employment or equity participation with Parent/Merger Sub and their affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "New Management Arrangements"]
- For six years after the effective time, Parent and the surviving corporation agreed to
  indemnify current and former PAREXEL directors and officers and to maintain
  directors'-and-officers' liability insurance on terms no less favorable than in effect
  on June 19, 2017 (subject to a 300%-of-current-premium annual cap). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Director and Officer Indemnification and Insurance"]

## Financing structure (what would fund the closing)
- Total funds needed to complete the merger and related transactions were estimated at
  approximately $5.5 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Financing of the Merger"]
- An affiliated Pamplona fund committed equity financing to Parent in an aggregate
  amount not to exceed $2.8 billion pursuant to an equity commitment letter dated June
  19, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Equity Financing"]
- Bank of America, N.A., Merrill Lynch, Pierce, Fenner & Smith Incorporated, and JPMorgan
  Chase Bank, N.A. committed, severally, acquisition debt financing in the aggregate of
  $3.085 billion under a debt commitment letter dated June 19, 2017, consisting of a
  $300 million revolving credit facility, a $2.065 billion senior secured term facility,
  and a senior unsecured credit facility of up to $720 million (with an option to
  replace the latter with up to $720 million of Rule 144A senior unsecured notes). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Debt Financing"]
- Proceeds of the debt financing were designated to fund the merger consideration, repay
  in full the Company's existing credit facility, and fund retirement of the Company's
  3.11% senior notes due 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Debt Financing"]
- Pamplona Fund separately delivered a limited guarantee to PAREXEL, capped at $276
  million plus expenses, guaranteeing payment of the parent termination fee and related
  obligations if due under the Merger Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Limited Guarantee"]

## Deal-era termination-fee and closing-condition context
- PAREXEL agreed to pay Parent a $138 million termination fee in cash under certain
  circumstances; Parent agreed to pay PAREXEL a $276 million "parent termination fee"
  under certain circumstances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A "Termination Fees and Expenses"]
- On September 29, 2017, the merger became effective at 10:32 a.m. Eastern time; each
  outstanding share of common stock (other than excluded shares) was cancelled and
  converted into the right to receive the merger consideration, PAREXEL notified NASDAQ
  and requested a Form 25 filing to remove the stock from listing and registration, and
  PAREXEL became a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, constituting a change in control.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-299711, Form 8-K Items 2.01, 3.01, 5.01]
- On the Closing Date, in connection with consummation of the merger, notes proceeds held
  in escrow were released and used, among other things, to finance the merger and repay
  the Company's existing credit facility and existing notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-299711, Form 8-K Item 1.01/2.03]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A financial sponsor (Pamplona Capital Management, via newly formed Parent and Merger
  Sub entities) acquired the entire Company for $88.10/share cash (~$5.5 billion total
  funding need) in a whole-company going-private merger backed by $2.8 billion of
  committed equity and $3.085 billion of committed debt financing, with contractual
  18-month employee-compensation/benefit continuity and six-year officer/director
  indemnification carried across the ownership boundary, while the Company's public
  listing was terminated at the effective time itself (same-day NASDAQ notification and
  Form 25 request). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258029, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-299711, Form 8-K]
