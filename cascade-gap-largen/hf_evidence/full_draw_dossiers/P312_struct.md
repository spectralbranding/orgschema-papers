# Structural sub-dossier — GCI Liberty, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- GCI Liberty, Inc., an Alaska corporation (then named "General Communication, Inc.,"
  referred to in the drawn filing as "Legacy GCI"), was party to an Agreement and Plan of
  Reorganization dated April 4, 2017 with Liberty Interactive Corporation and a wholly
  owned Liberty Interactive subsidiary (the "GCI reorganization agreement"). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
- On February 2, 2018 GCI Liberty amended its articles to reclassify its capital stock and
  rename from "General Communication, Inc." to "GCI Liberty, Inc."; on March 8, 2018 its
  stock underwent an automatic conversion, and GCI Liberty's Class A-1 Common Stock ceased
  trading on the NASDAQ Global Select Market (Class B-1 ceased OTC quotation). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-016026, Form
  8-K Item 8.01]
- On March 9, 2018 Liberty Interactive contributed assets and liabilities attributed to
  its Ventures Group (after reattributing certain of those assets/liabilities to its QVC
  Group) to GCI Liberty for a controlling equity interest, assumption of the contributed
  liabilities, and cash (the "contribution"); immediately afterward, on the same day,
  Liberty Interactive split off that newly acquired controlling interest to its own Series
  A/B Liberty Ventures shareholders by redeeming their shares for GCI Liberty Class A/B
  Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
- Under the GCI reorganization agreement, GCI Liberty separately agreed to form a wholly
  owned merger subsidiary ("GCI Newco") and, after the split-off, to merge into it solely
  to change GCI Liberty's state of incorporation from Alaska to Delaware; that
  reincorporation merger agreement was executed March 22, 2018 and is the transaction the
  drawn filing puts to a shareholder vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023,
  DEFM14A]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- Product: the operating business — described as the largest Alaska-based communications
  provider by revenue, providing wireless, data, video, voice, and managed services under
  the GCI brand — was the asset inside the entity whose controlling equity interest
  changed hands; its brand and customer base were represented as unchanged through the
  contribution/split-off/reincorporation sequence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023,
  DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: it changed twice — reclassified/renamed in place, then (via the
  reincorporation merger) merged into a newly formed, previously dormant Delaware
  subsidiary carrying the same name forward; the surviving corporation was structured to
  own all assets, succeed to all liabilities, and "continue to conduct the operations and
  businesses of GCI Liberty as they are conducted," with "no changes ... expected to the
  financial presentation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent/imprint: the contribution-then-split-off installed a new controlling
  shareholder base drawn from Liberty Interactive's own Ventures shareholders, overlapping
  with the shareholder bases of several affiliated companies (Liberty Interactive itself,
  to be renamed Qurate Retail Group, plus Liberty Media, Liberty Broadband, Liberty
  TripAdvisor, and Liberty Expedia). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
- People/management: "the current directors and officers of GCI Liberty, some of which
  also serve as directors and officers of Qurate, Liberty Media, Liberty TripAdvisor,
  Liberty Broadband, and Liberty Expedia, will remain the directors and officers of the
  surviving corporation." John C. Malone was to continue as Chairman of GCI Liberty (also
  Chairman/a director at Liberty Media and Liberty Broadband); Gregory B. Maffei was to
  continue as President and CEO of GCI Liberty (also CEO/President/a director at Liberty
  Media, Liberty TripAdvisor, and Liberty Broadband, and Chairman of the Qurate board).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
- Operational processes: "GCI Liberty has continued to operate the Legacy GCI businesses
  consistent with Legacy GCI's past practice" following the split-off and closing — the
  operating processes were represented as continuing unaltered through the
  ownership-boundary changes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]

## Bridging the shared controlling substrate
- No transitional-services or operating agreement bridges a shared substrate; instead the
  surviving corporation's charter was structured with corporate-opportunity renunciation
  provisions acknowledging overlapping directors/officers with Qurate, Liberty Media,
  Liberty Broadband, Liberty TripAdvisor, and Liberty Expedia, and GCI Liberty separately
  discloses it "may enter into transactions" with those affiliates with no assurance such
  terms will be as favorable as an arm's-length transaction. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, a controlling equity interest in the Alaska communications operating
  business was installed via an asset-for-equity contribution immediately followed by a
  split-off to the contributing party's own shareholders, with brand, customer base, and
  day-to-day operating processes represented as continuing unchanged, and with the legal
  vehicle changing state of incorporation (Alaska to Delaware) in a step structured to
  have no substantive effect on operations; the incoming controlling ownership and
  management overlapped with several affiliated companies, bridged at closing by
  charter-level corporate-opportunity renunciation provisions rather than a services
  agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-002023, DEFM14A]
