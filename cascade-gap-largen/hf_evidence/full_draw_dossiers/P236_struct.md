# Structural sub-dossier — Paragon Commercial Corporation merger with TowneBank (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Paragon Commercial Corporation, a North Carolina bank holding company headquartered in
  Raleigh, and its wholly owned subsidiary Paragon Commercial Bank, agreed to combine
  with TowneBank, a Virginia-chartered commercial bank headquartered in Portsmouth,
  Virginia, pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Reorganization dated April 26, 2017
  among TowneBank, TB Acquisition, LLC, Paragon and Paragon Commercial Bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- TB Acquisition, LLC ("Merger Sub"), a Virginia limited liability company and wholly
  owned subsidiary of TowneBank, was organized on April 24, 2017 solely to facilitate
  the merger and had carried on no activities before it other than those incidental to
  its formation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction was structured as a two-step merger: Paragon would merge with and into
  Merger Sub, with Merger Sub as the surviving entity, and immediately thereafter Paragon
  Bank (the NC-chartered bank subsidiary) would merge with and into TowneBank, with
  TowneBank as the surviving bank. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of Paragon common stock was to convert into the right to receive a fixed
  1.7250 shares of TowneBank common stock, representing approximately $59.25 in value
  per Paragon share (approximately $323.6 million in the aggregate) based on TowneBank's
  closing price on the day before announcement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger and bank merger were completed effective January 26, 2018; Paragon merged
  with and into TB Acquisition, LLC (surviving entity), and Paragon Commercial Bank
  immediately thereafter merged with and into TowneBank (surviving entity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-025400, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Paragon's stockholders approved the merger and related matters at a special meeting
  held January 10, 2018; Paragon's board unanimously recommended a vote FOR the
  transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]

## What Paragon comprised at closing (the acquired product/asset tier)
- At closing Paragon Bank operated three full-service banking offices serving the
  Charlotte and Research Triangle (Raleigh) markets in the piedmont region of North
  Carolina, versus TowneBank's pre-deal network of 37 banking offices across Richmond,
  the Greater Hampton Roads area of southeastern Virginia, and northeastern North
  Carolina. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- Following closing, TowneBank stated it would operate in the Raleigh, Charlotte, and
  Cary markets "as Paragon Bank, a division of TowneBank." [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/01/29/1313903/0/en/TowneBank-Completes-Merger-With-Paragon-Commercial-Corporation.html]

## Controlling-intent, culture, and organization/people tier at closing
- Before signing the merger agreement, TowneBank's non-binding letter of interest
  (March 20, 2017) conditioned the proposed merger on entering new employment contracts
  with Paragon's CEO and its "leadership team" (Chief Operating Officer, Chief Credit
  Officer, and two market presidents) prior to signing, and on offering employment
  agreements to additional named individuals prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- Bank leadership from both sides explicitly compared "the business models, market
  areas, and corporate cultures of Paragon and TowneBank" in an in-person meeting on
  March 9, 2017, and TowneBank's leadership separately reviewed "history, corporate
  structure, corporate culture, loan policies, deposit policies, and overall banking
  philosophy" with the Paragon and Paragon Bank boards on April 6, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- Pursuant to the merger agreement, TowneBank appointed two Paragon directors (Robert C.
  Hatley and Howard Jung) to TowneBank's board of directors effective upon closing, and
  established a "TowneBank Raleigh," "TowneBank Cary," and "TowneBank Charlotte" board of
  directors, initially composed of the members of Paragon's existing regional advisory
  boards together with representatives of the Paragon and Paragon Bank boards. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- TowneBank appointed named Paragon executive officers to new TowneBank officer
  positions effective upon closing: Robert C. Hatley (Paragon President/CEO) as
  President of TowneBank North Carolina, and Matthew C. Davis (Paragon Bank EVP/COO) as
  EVP and COO of TowneBank North Carolina; a January 2018 closing press release
  separately described Hatley as continuing "in his role as the President and CEO of the
  Paragon Division." [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/01/29/1313903/0/en/TowneBank-Completes-Merger-With-Paragon-Commercial-Corporation.html]
- New employment-related agreements between TowneBank and Messrs. Hatley, Davis,
  Fielding, Jurney, Reid, and numerous other Paragon/Paragon Bank officers were entered
  into in connection with the merger agreement and, upon consummation, superseded those
  officers' pre-existing Paragon employment and change-in-control agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- Each Paragon director joining the TowneBank board or the new NC advisory boards signed
  an agreement not to engage in activities competitive with TowneBank until the later of
  one year after the merger or the date the individual ceases board service. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy statement's risk factors identified that successful integration would
  require TowneBank to "retain and integrate the appropriate personnel of Paragon Bank
  into the operations of TowneBank, as well as reducing overlapping bank personnel,"
  and warned the integration process "could result in the loss of key employees, the
  disruption of each party's ongoing business, and inconsistencies in standards,
  controls, procedures and policies." [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Paragon Commercial Corporation, the NC holding-company legal entity, ceased to exist
  as a going concern upon merging into Merger Sub; Paragon Commercial Bank's separate
  NC bank charter ceased to exist upon merging into TowneBank, with TowneBank's Virginia
  bank charter continuing as the surviving institution. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-025400, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- In connection with closing, on January 26, 2018 Paragon requested Nasdaq file a Form
  25 notification for removal of Paragon's common stock from listing and registration,
  and Paragon's shares were removed from Nasdaq trading effective that date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-025400, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001354457-18-000017, Form 25-NSE]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a whole-bank, fixed-exchange-ratio stock merger (Paragon into
  a newly formed TowneBank merger subsidiary, then Paragon Bank into TowneBank itself,
  extinguishing Paragon's separate legal and bank charters) with a negotiated,
  pre-signing package of new TowneBank employment agreements and NC-regional advisory/
  board-seat arrangements intended to carry Paragon's named leadership and existing
  regional advisory-board personnel into the combined organization as a "Paragon
  Bank, a division of TowneBank" / "TowneBank North Carolina" operating unit, while the
  transaction's own risk factors flagged a risk of "reducing overlapping bank personnel"
  during integration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001654954-17-011248, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/01/29/1313903/0/en/TowneBank-Completes-Merger-With-Paragon-Commercial-Corporation.html]
