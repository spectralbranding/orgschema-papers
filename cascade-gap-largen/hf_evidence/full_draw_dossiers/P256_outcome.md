# Outcome sub-dossier — Tenneco Inc./Federal-Mogul realized record, ~3-5-year horizon (~2020-2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. Tenneco remained a public SEC filer through this
> window, so its own periodic reports are the outcome record for the acquired business.
> This sub-dossier contains no information about how the transaction was structured at
> closing.

## Disclosed non-completion of the planned post-closing separation
- Tenneco's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2020 disclosed that "the proposed spin-off of the
  Company's aftermarket and ride performance business (the 'Spin-Off') did not occur by
  April 1, 2020," which under the Shareholders Agreement triggered a right for holders of
  Class B Non-Voting Common Stock to convert into Class A Voting Common Stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-21-000004, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Effective April 1, 2020, IEP and its affiliates exercised this right and converted
  3,485,215 shares of Class B Non-Voting Common Stock into Class A Voting Common Stock.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-21-000004, Form 10-K FY2020]

## Continued disclosure of the acquired business as separate reportable segments
- Tenneco's Form 10-K for fiscal year 2021 states that "Tenneco consists of four
  operating segments, Motorparts, Performance Solutions, Clean Air, and Powertrain,"
  with the Motorparts segment described as designing, manufacturing, sourcing,
  marketing, and distributing products in the global vehicle aftermarket and original
  equipment service market under brand names including Monroe, Champion, MOOG, Walker,
  Fel-Pro, Wagner, Ferodo, Rancho, Thrush, National, and Sealed Power, among others. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The same filing shows segment EBITDA (including noncontrolling interests) for the
  Motorparts segment of $375 million (2021), $155 million (2020), and $184 million
  (2019), and for the Powertrain segment of $346 million (2021), $169 million (2020),
  and $257 million (2019) — i.e., both segments derived from Federal-Mogul's pre-closing
  divisional structure continued to be separately disclosed three years after closing,
  each showing a decline in 2020 followed by a partial recovery in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- Company-wide, Tenneco reported net income (loss) of $100 million (2021), $(1,460)
  million (2020), and $(220) million (2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## Goodwill/intangible impairment charges affecting the acquired segments
- The FY2021 10-K reports non-cash goodwill and intangible impairment charges of $383
  million during 2020 ($267 million goodwill, $65 million definite-lived intangibles,
  $51 million indefinite-lived intangibles), attributed to the effects of the COVID-19
  pandemic on projected financial information during the first quarter of 2020; this
  compares to $108 million of goodwill impairment charges recognized in 2019 across
  three reporting units, including a $21 million impairment charge in the Motorparts
  segment and an $18 million impairment charge in the Powertrain segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]
- The Powertrain segment's 2021 EBITDA increase was attributed in part to the
  non-recurrence of a $41 million goodwill impairment charge recognized in that segment
  during 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## A separately structured Aftermarket-side financing entity persisted post-close
- Tenneco's FY2021 10-K exhibit index lists a Fifth Supplemental Indenture and a Sixth
  Supplemental Indenture, each dated September 24, 2020, among Tenneco Inc. and "DRiV
  Automotive Inc." — a distinct legal entity associated with the aftermarket business —
  and the trustee, indicating a separately named financing vehicle for that business
  continued to exist more than a year after the Spin-Off's original 18-month deadline
  had passed without the separation occurring. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021]

## End of Tenneco's own public-reporting status (bounds observability beyond the window)
- On November 17, 2022, Pegasus Holdings III, LLC completed its previously announced
  acquisition of Tenneco pursuant to a Merger Agreement dated February 22, 2022; Merger
  Sub was merged with and into Tenneco, with Tenneco surviving as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent, and each outstanding share of Tenneco Class A voting common
  stock was converted into the right to receive $20.00 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-287303, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the closing, Tenneco requested that trading of its shares on the
  NYSE be suspended the same day and that the NYSE file a Form 25 to remove the shares
  from listing and deregister them under Section 12(b), with Tenneco intending to file a
  Form 15 to terminate its Section 12(g)/13/15(d) reporting obligations thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-287303, Form 8-K]
- No source was located in the reviewed filings separately disclosing net income
  (as opposed to EBITDA) specifically for the Motorparts or Powertrain segments; the
  net-income figures above are Tenneco-wide totals. [UNVERIFIED]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2020-2022 window, Tenneco's own periodic filings show the
  Federal-Mogul-derived Motorparts and Powertrain segments continuing to be separately
  reported three years after closing under their original divisional brand identities,
  a company-wide net loss of $1,460 million in 2020 accompanied by a $383 million
  non-cash goodwill/intangible impairment (including impairments specifically booked in
  the Motorparts and Powertrain segments in 2019-2020), a formally disclosed failure of
  the planned 18-month post-closing separation to occur by its April 1, 2020 deadline,
  and — at the far edge of the window — the end of Tenneco's own public-reporting life
  via a November 17, 2022 going-private merger, after which no further periodic
  disclosure of the acquired business is available on this record. [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-21-000004, Form 10-K FY2020] [SRC: SEC accession 0001024725-22-000005, Form 10-K FY2021] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-22-287303, Form 8-K]
