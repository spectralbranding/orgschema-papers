# Structural sub-dossier — HSN, Inc. combination with Liberty Interactive / QVC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- HSN, Inc. ("HSNi") entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 5, 2017 with
  Liberty Interactive Corporation and its wholly owned merger subsidiary Liberty
  Horizon, Inc., under which the merger subsidiary would merge with and into HSNi,
  HSNi surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Liberty Interactive. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- At signing, Liberty Interactive already indirectly owned approximately 38.2% of
  HSNi's outstanding common stock; the transaction was treated as subject to Rule 13e-3
  of the Exchange Act (the affiliate/going-private disclosure rule), with a joint
  Schedule 13E-3 filed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- Consideration was stock-for-stock: each HSNi share held by unaffiliated stockholders
  converted into 1.650 shares of Liberty Interactive's Series A QVC Group tracking
  stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed December 29, 2017, when Liberty Interactive (later renamed Qurate
  Retail, Inc.) completed acquisition of the remaining approximately 62% of HSNi in an
  all-stock transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-21-001983, Qurate Retail Form
  10-K FY2020]
- HSNi common stock was to be removed from listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market
  and its Exchange Act registration terminated on completion. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed the boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing product/asset that crossed was HSNi's video-commerce retail
  business — its programming, events, and brand portfolio; the acquirer's stated reasons
  included the opportunity "to acquire HSNi's programming and events and leading brands"
  and to extend "top HSNi brands to QVC and zulily." [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- The legal vehicle was a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub merged into HSNi, HSNi
  surviving as the entity, its corporate structure retained rather than dissolved. [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- At signing, HSNi "does not currently have a full-time chief executive officer," and
  the merger agreement itself limited HSNi's ability to fill that position pending
  closing — the people/management tier at the target's top was unsettled going into
  closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- Operating processes were explicitly not integrated at signing: the disclosure states
  the deal "involves the combination of two companies which currently operate as
  independent companies," and lists integration risks including "managing QVC and HSNi
  out of several different locations and integrating personnel from the two companies"
  and "different histories, cultures, regulatory restrictions, markets and customer
  bases." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services or shared-services agreement bridging a carved-out substrate
  was identified in the reviewed filing; this was a whole-company merger of an
  already-affiliated, already-public target into the acquirer's existing operating
  subsidiary (QVC), not a carve-out. [UNVERIFIED — no such agreement located]

## Controlling intent at signing
- The acquirer's stated reasons centered on improving "the QVC Group's competitive
  position" through scale and synergy by combining HSNi with its own pre-existing QVC
  operating unit, targeting approximately $100 million in annual synergies (net of an
  estimated $50 million transaction fees) as a basis for the exchange ratio — an intent
  to fold the target into an existing business, not preserve it standalone. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
- Completion was conditioned on HSR Act antitrust clearance and FCC approvals for
  transfer of control of HSNi's broadcast licenses. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- HSNi, a company in which the acquirer already held roughly a 38.2% stake, was merged
  whole into Liberty Interactive as a wholly owned subsidiary via a reverse triangular
  stock-for-stock merger, its shares removed from trading and its Exchange Act
  registration terminated; at signing the target lacked a full-time CEO and the two
  companies' operations, personnel, and locations were explicitly described as
  unintegrated, with the acquirer's intent being to combine HSNi's product/brand assets
  into its existing QVC operating business rather than run it standalone. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001047469-17-007349, Form DEFM14A]
