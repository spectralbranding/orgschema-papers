# Structural sub-dossier — PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. holding-company reorganization (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-closing outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On September 18, 2018, PennyMac Financial Services, Inc. ("Existing PennyMac") filed a
  proxy statement/prospectus soliciting stockholder approval of a "Reorganization
  Proposal": a Contribution Agreement and Plan of Merger, dated August 2, 2018, creating a
  new holding company above Existing PennyMac, initially named New PennyMac Financial
  Services, Inc. ("New PennyMac"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A]
- The stated purpose was to simplify the corporate structure by eliminating Existing
  PennyMac's "Up-C" structure and moving all equityholders to a single class of common
  stock at the top-level entity, rather than the Class A / Class B structure of Existing
  PennyMac. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A]
- Mechanics: Existing PennyMac's Class A common stock would convert one-for-one (by
  statutory merger, "the Merger") into New PennyMac common stock; Existing PennyMac's
  Class B common stock would be cancelled for no consideration; and holders of Class A
  Units of Private National Mortgage Acceptance Company, LLC ("PNMAC") other than
  Existing PennyMac (45 separate contributor entities/individuals) would voluntarily
  contribute their units to New PennyMac one-for-one for New PennyMac common stock ("the
  Contribution"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A]
- New PennyMac was a newly formed Delaware corporation and, at signing, a direct
  wholly-owned subsidiary of Existing PennyMac; New PennyMac Merger Sub, LLC was a
  Delaware LLC formed as a direct wholly-owned subsidiary of New PennyMac to effect the
  Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A]
- Stockholders approved the proposal, and the Closing occurred on November 1, 2018: New
  PennyMac Merger Sub merged with Existing PennyMac, which survived as a wholly-owned
  subsidiary of New PennyMac (the "Successor"); Class A shares converted 1:1 into
  Successor stock; Class B shares were cancelled; and the 45 contributors exchanged their
  PNMAC Class A units 1:1 for Successor stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- Effective with the Closing, Existing PennyMac was renamed "PNMAC Holdings, Inc." and
  the Successor (New PennyMac) was renamed "PennyMac Financial Services, Inc.," continuing
  the pre-transaction public-company name and NYSE ticker "PFSI" at the new top-level
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Explanatory Note] [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A FAQ 15]
- Prior to the open of trading on November 1, 2018, PNMAC Holdings' (Existing PennyMac's)
  Class A common stock ceased trading on the NYSE; the Successor's common stock began
  trading the same day on the NYSE under the same ticker "PFSI." PNMAC Holdings requested
  the NYSE file a Form 25 to withdraw its Class A common stock from listing and to
  terminate its Section 12(b) registration, and separately filed a Form 15-12B on
  2018-11-13 to suspend its own Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Item 3.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-067965, Form 15-12B]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Controlling intent / imprint: BlackRock Mortgage Ventures, LLC and HC Partners, LLC
  ("Highfields") were disclosed, as of October 1, 2018, as holding approximately 21% and
  26% respectively of the voting power of Existing PennyMac, each with rights to nominate
  board designees; at Closing the parties entered amended and restated Stockholder
  Agreements continuing those arrangements at the Successor level. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Business / operating model: the DEFM14A directly addressed continuity in its FAQ
  section — "Will the management or the businesses of Existing PennyMac or any of its
  subsidiaries change as a result of the Reorganization? ... No. Our management and
  businesses will not change as a result of the Reorganization." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A FAQ 14]
- Legal vehicle: the operating subsidiary, Private National Mortgage Acceptance Company,
  LLC (PNMAC) — described in the DEFM14A as conducting "production and servicing of U.S.
  residential mortgage loans ... and the management of investments related to the U.S.
  mortgage market" — was not itself merged, dissolved, or restructured; at Closing, PNMAC
  Holdings, the Successor, and PNMAC entered a Fifth Amended and Restated Limited
  Liability Company Agreement of PNMAC, and the pre-existing Exchange Agreement (dated
  May 8, 2013) among the prior parent, PNMAC, and PNMAC's owners was terminated because
  all Class A units of PNMAC came to be owned directly or indirectly by the Successor.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A "The Business"] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- People / management: the DEFM14A stated the directors and executive officers of New
  PennyMac following the Reorganization were expected to be the same as those of Existing
  PennyMac immediately prior to it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A "Board of Directors and Executive Officers of New PennyMac Following the Reorganization"]
- Stanford L. Kurland, Existing PennyMac's Executive Chairman and a board member, was
  named among the 45 Contributors exchanging PNMAC Class A units for Successor stock —
  the controlling executive's personal equity interest was carried across the same
  exchange mechanism as the outside institutional holders'. [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A "Interests of Certain Directors and Executive Officers in the Reorganization"]

## Contractual bridging instruments at closing
- At Closing, PNMAC Holdings assigned to the Successor, and the Successor assumed, all
  obligations under existing indemnification agreements with individuals who were
  directors of PNMAC Holdings immediately before Closing, and all existing employment
  agreements and offer letters with each PNMAC Holdings officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K "Assignment and Assumption of Indemnification Agreements and Employment Agreements"]
- PNMAC Holdings and the Successor entered an Amended and Restated Registration Rights
  Agreement under which the Successor assumed PNMAC Holdings' obligations, preserving
  BlackRock's and Highfields' contractual resale-registration rights at the new top-level
  entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K "Amended and Restated Registration Rights Agreement"]
- The Successor assumed PNMAC Holdings' pre-existing equity incentive plan, with
  outstanding awards continuing on their prior terms except that shares issuable under
  each award were replaced with Successor shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K Item 5.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction inserted a new top-level holding company (renamed PennyMac Financial
  Services, Inc., continuing the pre-existing public name and NYSE ticker) above the
  pre-existing public registrant (renamed PNMAC Holdings, Inc., which ceased trading on
  the NYSE and terminated its own Exchange Act reporting) and above the operating
  subsidiary PNMAC, which was not itself merged or reconstituted. Controlling
  shareholders, board composition, executive officers, the operating business, and
  individual director/officer contractual arrangements were each carried across the
  reorganization by explicit contract (amended stockholder agreements, assignment and
  assumption of indemnification/employment agreements, an amended LLC agreement for the
  operating subsidiary) rather than altered, per the DEFM14A's own statement that
  "management and businesses will not change." [SRC: SEC accession 0001047469-18-006280, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-065455, Form 8-K]
