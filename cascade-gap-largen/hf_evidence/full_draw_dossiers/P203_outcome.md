## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- Following the merger's closing on March 14, 2019, Esterline Technologies Corporation
  filed a Form 15-12B on March 25, 2019, certifying termination/suspension of its
  registration and reporting obligations under Sections 12(g), 13, and 15(d) of the
  Exchange Act, with approximately 1 holder of record as of the certification date. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000015, Form 15-12B, filed 2019-03-25]
- No Form 10-K or other periodic report has been filed by Esterline Technologies
  Corporation (CIK 33619) after its March 2019 deregistration; consequently there is no
  independent, audited public financial record for the acquired business as a
  standalone reporting entity for the ~2022-2024 outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000015, Form 15-12B, filed 2019-03-25]
- TransDigm's own periodic filings (10-K) do not report Esterline as a distinct
  reportable segment; TransDigm's segment structure (Power & Control, Airframe,
  Non-aviation) folds acquired businesses, including former Esterline operating units,
  into its existing segments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-22-000065, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2022 (filed 2022-11-10), Note 2 and MD&A]

## Esterline-attributed facts disclosed within TransDigm's in-window filings
- TransDigm's FY2022 Form 10-K (filed November 10, 2022, within the outcome window)
  states that "in the case of larger acquisitions that consist of multiple operating
  units (such as the Esterline acquisition), we may pursue opportunities to divest
  certain acquired operating units that are not in line with our long-term acquisition
  strategy," and its Note 2 (Acquisitions and Divestitures) identifies five specific
  operating businesses that TransDigm divested after having acquired them as part of
  the Esterline transaction: (1) Souriau-Sunbank Connection Technologies, divested
  December 20, 2019 to Eaton Corporation plc for approximately $920 million, reported
  as discontinued operations; (2) Avista, Inc., divested November 17, 2020 to Belcan,
  LLC for approximately $8 million; (3) Racal Acoustics, divested January 29, 2021 to
  Invisio Communications AB for approximately $20 million; (4) Technical Airborne
  Components, divested April 27, 2021 to Searchlight Capital Partners for approximately
  $40 million; and (5) ScioTeq and TREALITY Simulation Visual Systems, divested June
  30, 2021 to OpenGate Capital for approximately $200 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-22-000065, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2022, Note 2 "Acquisitions and Divestitures"]
- TransDigm's FY2023 Form 10-K (filed November 9, 2023, within the outcome window)
  repeats and confirms the same five ex-Esterline divestitures and their disclosed sale
  prices without material change. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-23-000081, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2023, Note 2 "Acquisitions and Divestitures"]
- TransDigm's FY2022 Form 10-K discloses a pension settlement charge of approximately
  $22 million for the fiscal year ended September 30, 2022, attributed specifically to
  the "Esterline Retirement Plan" (ERP), which TransDigm states it terminated effective
  June 30, 2021 in accordance with regulatory requirements, with obligations
  distributed through approximately $107 million in lump-sum payments to eligible
  participants (using existing plan assets) and the purchase of a group annuity
  contract. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-22-000065, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2022, MD&A and Note 13 "Retirement Plans"]
- TransDigm's FY2023 Form 10-K discloses a further, related item for the ERP: a $9
  million cash refund received in fiscal 2023 upon finalizing the group annuity
  purchase funding associated with the ERP settlement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-23-000081, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2023, MD&A and Note 13 "Retirement Plans"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Because Esterline Technologies Corporation deregistered from SEC reporting within
  weeks of closing, there is no independent public outcome record for it as a
  standalone entity; the case is accordingly coded as having an uncertain/limited
  public outcome record on any measure requiring a standalone Esterline financial
  trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0000033619-19-000015, Form 15-12B, filed 2019-03-25]
- TransDigm does not separately report an "Esterline" business line, but its own
  in-window 10-K filings do disclose specific, dated, dollar-quantified facts tied
  expressly to the acquired Esterline business: five identified operating units
  acquired via the Esterline transaction were divested between December 2019 and June
  2021 for disclosed proceeds ranging from approximately $8 million to approximately
  $920 million, and the Esterline-specific retirement plan was terminated in June 2021
  with a $22 million settlement charge recognized in fiscal 2022 and a $9 million
  related cash refund recognized in fiscal 2023. [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-22-000065, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2022, Note 2 and Note 13] [SRC: SEC accession 0001260221-23-000081, TransDigm Group Incorporated Form 10-K for FY2023, Note 2 and Note 13]
