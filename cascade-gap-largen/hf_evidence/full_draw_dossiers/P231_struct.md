# Structural sub-dossier — SUPERVALU INC. merger with United Natural Foods, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- SUPERVALU INC., a Delaware corporation, was described in its own definitive merger
  proxy as "the largest public company grocery wholesaler in the United States,"
  organized into two reportable segments, Wholesale and Retail, with three Retail
  banners in continuing operations at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- Pursuant to an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 25, 2018, among SUPERVALU,
  SUPERVALU Enterprises, Inc. (a wholly owned SUPERVALU subsidiary), United Natural
  Foods, Inc. ("UNFI"), and Jedi Merger Sub, Inc. (a wholly owned UNFI subsidiary,
  "Merger Sub"), Merger Sub was to merge with and into the Company, with the Company
  surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of UNFI. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- Holders of SUPERVALU common stock were entitled to receive $32.50 per share in cash,
  without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- A separate, contingent "holding company merger" (SUPERVALU into SUPERVALU
  Enterprises, via an internal Holding Company Merger Sub) could be effected before
  closing, in which case SUPERVALU Enterprises rather than SUPERVALU would be the
  merger counterparty; SUPERVALU agreed not to implement this reorganization before
  November 25, 2018 without UNFI's consent past a cost threshold. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on October 22, 2018; Merger Sub's separate existence ceased and
  SUPERVALU survived as a wholly owned subsidiary of UNFI. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
- At closing SUPERVALU repaid in full and terminated all commitments under its Term
  Loan Agreement ($698 million outstanding) and ABL Agreement ($344.5 million
  outstanding), releasing the associated guarantees and liens. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]

## What crossed at closing (business model / product tier)
- SUPERVALU's core Wholesale business "distributes grocery and other products and
  provides services to retailers across the United States," with an "industry-leading
  portfolio of private brands and unique ethnic and specialty products." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- SUPERVALU had, since 2016, been "undergoing a strategic transformation... to become
  the wholesale supplier of choice," growing Wholesale (acquisitions of Unified
  Grocers and Associated Grocers of Florida; new distribution centers) while exiting
  its Farm Fresh Retail banner and pursuing sale of its Shop 'n Save (St. Louis) and
  Shop 'n Save East retail operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- During negotiations, UNFI's March 30, 2018 proposed structure contemplated that
  SUPERVALU's wholesale business and net operating losses would be included in the
  transaction while its retail business, legacy corporate technology/systems/software
  tied to retail, and legacy pension liabilities tied to retail would be excluded. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- SUPERVALU's board rejected UNFI's June 19, 2018 proposal on retail divestitures as
  "not acceptable"; the merger agreement as executed on July 25, 2018 was a
  whole-company transaction, with both the Wholesale and Retail segments crossing
  into UNFI ownership rather than the earlier wholesale-only carve-out structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- Between signing and closing, SUPERVALU agreed it would not divest retail business
  assets or wind down retail stores ("a retail change") without UNFI's prior written
  consent (subject to a narrower standard for changes to the Cub banner), keeping
  UNFI able to shape the disposition of the Retail tier before the deal closed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]

## Legal vehicle
- The transaction was structured as a reverse triangular merger: Merger Sub, a newly
  formed wholly owned Delaware subsidiary of UNFI, merged with and into SUPERVALU (or
  SUPERVALU Enterprises, if the holding company merger occurred first), with the
  Company surviving as a wholly owned UNFI subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]

## People / management / organization tier
- At the Effective Time, all nine incumbent SUPERVALU directors (Donald R. Chappel,
  Irwin Cohen, Philip L. Francis, Mark Gross, Eric Johnson, Mathew M. Pendo, Francesca
  Ruiz De Luzuriaga, Frank A. Savage, and Mary Winston) ceased to be directors; Sean
  F. Griffin and Jill E. Sutton (both installed by UNFI) became the Company's sole
  directors. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
- Mark Gross (CEO) and Rob N. Woseth (CFO) ceased to be executive officers at the
  Effective Time; Sean Griffin became principal executive officer and Michael
  Zechmeister principal financial officer of the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
- Concurrent with closing, Gross and other named executive officers (Woseth,
  Weidenheimer, Stigers, Burdick) agreed to amend their employment/severance/equity
  arrangements to prohibit them from engaging in activities relating to the grocery
  wholesale or SUPERVALU grocery retail businesses, and extended these and other
  restrictive covenants from 12 to 24 months post-termination. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
- At signing, the proxy disclosed that SUPERVALU's executive officers "may have
  ongoing discussions with UNFI regarding such officers' roles at UNFI following the
  closing date," but "there is no assurance that any discussions will result in any
  new agreements or arrangements." [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]
- For non-executive "continuing employees," UNFI contractually agreed, for at least
  one year post-closing, to provide base pay/bonus opportunities and benefits no less
  favorable than pre-closing, to recognize prior service for benefit purposes, and to
  provide severance at least equal to the greater of the Company's or UNFI's plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle / listing changes at closing
- On the Closing Date, SUPERVALU requested that the NYSE suspend trading of its
  common stock and remove it from listing (Form 25) before market open, and stated
  its intention to file a Form 15 to terminate its SEC reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
- At the Effective Time, SUPERVALU's Certificate of Incorporation and, immediately
  after, its Bylaws were amended and restated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash merger via a reverse-triangular
  merger-sub structure: both of SUPERVALU's segments (Wholesale and Retail) crossed
  into UNFI ownership together, after UNFI's earlier wholesale-only carve-out
  proposal was rejected during negotiations. At the same time, the people/management
  tier was structured narrowly and asymmetrically: the entire pre-close board and the
  CEO/CFO were replaced at the Effective Time and bound by broadened non-competes,
  other named executives' post-close involvement was left an open, non-binding
  discussion, while UNFI contractually bridged the non-executive workforce's pay,
  benefits, and service credit for a fixed one-year transition period rather than
  leaving the broader organization structurally untouched. [SRC: SEC accession 0001140361-18-038679, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063194, Form 8-K]
