# Structural sub-dossier — Enterprise Products / TEPPCO Jonah Gas Gathering System expansion joint venture (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## The drawn filing (what it establishes)
- The drawn accession is a Form 8-K filed February 17, 2006 by Enterprise Products
  Partners L.P., reporting under Item 1.01 (Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement)
  that on February 13, 2006 TEPPCO Partners, L.P. and Enterprise Products Operating L.P.
  entered into a letter of intent related to an additional expansion of the Jonah Gas
  Gathering System. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The instrument attached (Exhibit 99.1) is a letter of intent, not a definitive
  agreement: it "merely establish[es] a framework for future negotiations," and, except
  for enumerated binding sections, none of its provisions bind the parties until a
  definitive Joint Venture Agreement is executed and delivered. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Section 13)]
- Exhibit 99.2 is the Enterprise press release dated February 17, 2006 announcing the
  planned joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 9.01 / Exhibit 99.2]

## Parties, transaction type, and controlling intent at closing
- The transaction is structured as a joint venture to own and construct an expansion of
  the Jonah Gas Gathering System, between TEPPCO (through its Jonah subsidiary) and
  Enterprise Products Operating L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.2]
- The two parties are affiliates under common control: the general partner of TEPPCO and
  2,500,000 TEPPCO common units are owned by an affiliate of Enterprise under the common
  control of Dan L. Duncan, Chairman of the Board of Enterprise Products GP, LLC.
  Enterprise Products Operating L.P. is the operating partnership of Enterprise Products
  Partners L.P. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What the underlying asset is, and where its operations sit at closing
- The Jonah Gas Gathering System, located in the Green River Basin of southwestern
  Wyoming, gathers and transports natural gas from the Jonah and Pinedale fields to
  processing plants and interstate pipelines; it is part of TEPPCO's midstream business
  segment and is owned and operated by Jonah Gas Gathering Company, an indirect
  wholly-owned subsidiary of TEPPCO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.2]
- The expansion (the customer-facing capacity being created) consists of approximately
  90,000 horsepower of gas turbine compression at a new compression station plus related
  piping and facilities, intended to increase system capacity from 1.5 Bcf/d to 2.0 Bcf/d.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.2]
- The existing operating organization is to remain in place: TEPPCO agrees to cause Jonah
  to operate its business in the ordinary manner, "preserve intact its present business
  organization, keep available the services of its present officers and employees, and
  preserve its relationships with customers, suppliers, jobbers, distributors and others."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Section 4)]
- Construction of the expansion is assigned to Enterprise: Enterprise "will be responsible
  for all activities relating to the construction of the Jonah Expansion," subject to
  advance notice to TEPPCO. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Section 2)]

## How the ownership boundary is drawn and bridged at closing
- Legal vehicle is left open at the letter stage: the Joint Venture Agreement may be
  effected either by amending Jonah's existing partnership agreement or by forming a new
  entity ("Newco"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Section 1(a))]
- Enterprise funds the expansion by advancing all amounts necessary (anticipated at
  approximately $200 million) as a "Subscription" for an equity interest in the joint
  venture; the relative ownership and revenue-sharing interests are to be set forth in the
  Joint Venture Agreement (i.e., not fixed at the letter stage). [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Sections 1(b), 1(d))]
- Until the Joint Venture Agreement is effective, Enterprise has no interest, ownership,
  revenue participation, distributions, or voting rights in the joint venture or Jonah.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.1 (Section 1(b))]
- The funding co-owner (Enterprise) thus finances and builds the new asset while the
  operating processes and personnel that run the system remain with Jonah/TEPPCO; the
  asset's economic ownership is bridged by the Subscription mechanism rather than by any
  transfer of the operating organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1 (Sections 1(b), 2, 4)]
- A reimbursement/adjustment mechanism absorbs the mismatch: TEPPCO has the option to
  return up to 100% of the Subscription, with the parties' relative interests adjusted
  accordingly, and the letter terminates without liability if TEPPCO returns 100%.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.1 (Section 1(c))]
- If the definitive Joint Venture Agreement is not consummated before completion, the
  parties' relative rights, ownership interests, and cost/revenue-sharing are to be fixed
  by binding arbitration (single arbitrator, AAA, Houston), and mutual indemnification
  provisions apply. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.1 (Sections 1(e), 3, 10)]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At the letter-of-intent stage the transaction co-owns an expansion asset (new gas
  compression capacity, ~$200 million) across two commonly-controlled affiliates, with one
  party (Enterprise) financing and constructing the asset and the other party's subsidiary
  (Jonah/TEPPCO) retaining ownership and operation of the existing system and its
  workforce; the ownership split is deferred to a definitive Joint Venture Agreement and
  the funding/ownership mismatch is bridged by a Subscription, a TEPPCO reimbursement
  option, and an arbitration fallback. [SRC: SEC accession 0001061219-06-000015, Form 8-K Item 1.01 / Exhibit 99.1]
