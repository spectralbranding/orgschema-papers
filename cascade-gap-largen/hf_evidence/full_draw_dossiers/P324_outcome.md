## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The acquired entity filed a Form 15 on December 18, 2017 certifying termination of its
  registration under Section 12(g) of the Exchange Act; its last periodic filing (a Form
  10-Q) was dated November 13, 2017, and no further periodic reports were filed by it
  after the closing-related Form 8-K and Form 15 of December 18, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-17-073717, Form 15-12G]
- Consequently there is no independent, audited public financial record for the acquired
  business as a standalone registrant for any post-closing period; the facts below are
  drawn instead from the acquirer's (Jacobs Engineering Group Inc. / later Jacobs
  Solutions Inc., CIK 0000052988) own subsequent Forms 10-K, to the extent those filings
  separately disclose figures or events specific to the acquired business rather than
  only consolidated totals. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-18-000036, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Acquirer's separate disclosure of the acquired business, near-term (fiscal 2018-2019)
- From the acquisition date through the end of fiscal 2018, the acquired business
  ("CH2M") contributed approximately $3.8 billion in revenue and $185.9 million in
  pretax income included in the acquirer's consolidated statement of earnings, which
  included approximately $99.3 million in pre-tax restructuring and transaction costs
  attributable to the acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-18-000036, Form 10-K FY2018, Note "Business Combinations"]
- In its fiscal 2018 Form 10-K, the acquirer's management identified a material weakness
  in internal control over financial reporting related to the completeness and accuracy
  of deferred taxes and the recoverability of deferred taxes associated with the CH2M
  acquisition; the related adjustments revised previously reported results by $(0.10)
  per share for the fourth quarter and $(0.11) per share for fiscal 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-18-000036, Form 10-K FY2018, "Controls and Procedures"]
- In its fiscal 2019 Form 10-K, the acquirer reported that it had remediated the
  previously identified material weakness through enhanced deferred-income-tax control
  procedures, including procedures relating to the recoverability of deferred taxes
  associated with acquired businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-19-000069, Form 10-K FY2019, "Controls and Procedures"]
- During fiscal 2019 the acquirer realigned its reporting segments; CH2M's businesses
  were folded into the acquirer's "Critical Mission Solutions" (CMS) and "People &
  Places Solutions" (PPS) reportable segments rather than continuing to be reported as a
  standalone segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-19-000069, Form 10-K FY2019, MD&A]
- In the first quarter of fiscal 2019 the acquirer elected to discontinue the CH2M Hill
  Retiree Medical Plan (effective December 31, 2018), recording a $35.0 million pre-tax
  gain on settlement in fiscal 2019; integration-related restructuring and transaction
  costs associated with the CH2M acquisition "continued through fiscal 2019." [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-19-000069, Form 10-K FY2019, Notes]

## Acquirer's separate disclosure within the fixed outcome window (fiscal 2020-2022)
- The acquirer's fiscal 2020 Form 10-K (covering the year ended October 2, 2020) contains
  no narrative discussion of CH2M; the only appearances of "CH2M" in that filing's
  machine-readable data are XBRL tags referencing the acquisition-date debt facility
  (tagged to the December 15, 2017 closing date) and an inherited "CH2M Retiree Medical
  and Ireland" pension-plan tag, not narrative disclosure of a separately tracked CH2M
  business or trajectory. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-20-000070, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The acquirer's fiscal 2021 Form 10-K discloses a $(2.1) million loss on settlement of
  "the CH2M portion of the U.S. pension plan" for the year ended October 2, 2020, and
  continues to carry a litigation footnote (see next item) referencing pre-acquisition
  CH2M contingencies. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-21-000065, Form 10-K FY2021, Notes]
- That footnote discloses that in 2012, CH2M HILL Australia Pty Limited (a CH2M
  subsidiary) had entered a 50/50 integrated joint venture with Australian contractor
  UGL Infrastructure Pty Limited; the joint venture's Consortium (with General Electric
  and GE Electrical International Inc.) was awarded a subcontract by JKC Australia LNG
  Pty Limited for a power plant at Blaydin Point, Darwin, Australia; the subcontract was
  terminated in January 2017 and the Consortium commenced arbitration around August
  2017 — pre-dating the Jacobs acquisition's closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-22-000111, Form 10-K FY2022, Note "Contractual Guarantees, Litigation, Investigations and Insurance"]
- On April 12, 2022, JKC and the Consortium entered a confidential Settlement Agreement
  (the "Legacy CH2M Matter"); under its terms, CH2M — as guarantor of CH2M Australia PTY
  Limited's obligations under the subcontract — made a cash payment to JKC in April 2022
  of AUD 640 million (approximately $475 million at mid-April 2022 exchange rates). [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-22-000111, Form 10-K FY2022, Note "Contractual Guarantees, Litigation, Investigations and Insurance"]
- As a result of that settlement, the acquirer (by then renamed Jacobs Solutions Inc.)
  recorded $91.3 million in additional pre-tax charges for the year ended September 30,
  2022, over amounts previously reserved, net of approximately $27 million recorded as
  third-party recoveries reducing selling, general & administrative expense. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-22-000111, Form 10-K FY2022, MD&A / Note "Restructuring and Other Charges"]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The acquired entity itself generated no post-closing public record: it deregistered
  under the Exchange Act three days after the merger closed, and no periodic report by
  it exists for any part of the fixed outcome window. The acquirer separately disclosed
  CH2M-specific facts through fiscal 2019 (a first partial-year revenue/earnings
  contribution alongside a since-remediated material weakness in deferred-tax controls
  tied to the acquisition, and dissolution of CH2M's own retiree medical plan), then
  discontinued narrative CH2M-specific disclosure by fiscal 2020 as CH2M's operations
  were folded into the acquirer's realigned reportable segments. Within the fixed
  outcome window itself, the only further separately disclosed CH2M-specific item was a
  pre-acquisition legal contingency — a 2012 joint-venture subcontract dispute in
  Australia that predated the merger — which reached a confidential settlement in April
  2022 requiring a cash payment of approximately $475 million and an additional $91.3
  million pre-tax charge recorded by the acquirer in fiscal 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-18-000036, Form 10-K FY2018] [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-19-000069, Form 10-K FY2019] [SRC: SEC accession 0000052988-22-000111, Form 10-K FY2022]
