# Outcome sub-dossier — Time Inc. realized record after the 2014 separation (~3–5-year horizon)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-separation record. It contains no information about how the transaction was
> structured at closing. The fixed outcome window is the ~3–5 years following the ~2014
> separation; the entity's independent public reporting record ends within that window.

## Public-reporting status across the window
- Time Inc. filed annual reports as an independent registrant for fiscal years 2014, 2015,
  and 2016; its Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2016 was filed February 27, 2017.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- On November 26, 2017, Time Inc. entered into a merger agreement with Meredith Corporation
  and Gotham Merger Sub, Inc.; on January 31, 2018 Meredith completed its acquisition of
  Time Inc. by consummating the merger, with Time Inc. continuing as the surviving
  corporation and a wholly owned subsidiary of Meredith. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-027482, Form 8-K filed 2018-01-31]
- In connection with the merger, the company requested that the NYSE suspend trading and
  delist the shares, and it filed a Form 15-12B on February 12, 2018 to deregister its
  common stock under the Exchange Act; consequently no independent Time Inc. Form 10-K
  exists for fiscal years after 2016 (i.e. for the deeper 4–5-year portion of the window).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038803, Form 15-12B filed 2018-02-12]

## Realized financial record while independently reporting (FY2014–FY2016)
- Time Inc. reported Total revenues of $3,281 million (2014), $3,103 million (2015), and
  $3,076 million (2016) — a continued year-over-year decline (revenues down 5% in 2015 vs
  2014 and down 1% in 2016 vs 2015). [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Net income (loss) was $87 million (2014), $(881) million (2015), and $(48) million
  (2016); operating income (loss) was $180 million (2014), $(823) million (2015), and $2
  million (2016). [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The 2015 net loss reflected an operating loss driven by a Goodwill impairment charge of
  $952 million, associated with the sale of the Blue Fin Building, a decline in the
  publicly traded share price, and trends in Advertising and Circulation revenues. [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- A separate Goodwill impairment charge of $26 million was recorded in connection with the
  sale of the Mexico business (GEX). [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]

## Documented organizational and operational disruption over time
- The company undertook restructuring plans and realignment programs that included
  streamlining its organizational structure "to enhance operational flexibility, speed
  decision making, and spur the development of new cross-brand products and services," and
  to "achieve operational efficiencies, including through global sourcing of staff." [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- The company disclosed that its restructuring and cost management initiatives in several
  functions "were disruptive to our business," and that realignment was expected to enable
  the organization "to more efficiently work across all brands as an integrated platform."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)]
- Workforce declined over the window: as of December 31, 2016 the company had approximately
  7,450 employees (approximately 4,950 in the United States, approximately 1,500 in the
  United Kingdom), down from the approximately 7,900 reported at separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-089381, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Record after acquisition (deeper window)
- After the January 31, 2018 acquisition and February 2018 deregistration, Time Inc.'s
  results were absorbed into Meredith Corporation and no separate Time Inc. public audited
  outcome record exists for the deeper 4–5-year portion of the window; the acquirer's
  consolidated results are not attributed to Time Inc. here. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038803, Form 15-12B filed 2018-02-12]
- Any Time Inc.-specific financial trajectory for the post-acquisition portion of the
  window is therefore not publicly observable from a separate primary filing. [UNVERIFIED]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the roughly three years for which Time Inc. reported independently after the ~2014
  separation, revenues declined each year (from $3,281M in 2014 to $3,076M in 2016); the
  entity posted a $(881)M net loss in 2015 driven by a $952M goodwill impairment, narrowing
  to a $(48)M net loss in 2016; it ran successive restructuring/realignment programs it
  described as disruptive, and reduced its workforce; and it was then acquired by Meredith
  Corporation (closed January 31, 2018) and deregistered, ending the separate public record
  within the outcome window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001591517-17-000010, Form 10-K (FY2016)] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-027482, Form 8-K filed 2018-01-31] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-038803, Form 15-12B filed 2018-02-12]
