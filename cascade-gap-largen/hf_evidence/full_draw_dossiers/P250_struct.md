# Structural sub-dossier — Buffalo Wild Wings, Inc. merger with Arby's Restaurant Group, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Buffalo Wild Wings, Inc. ("the company"), a Minnesota corporation, describes itself
  as "an established and growing owner, operator, and franchisor of restaurants
  featuring a variety of boldly-flavored, crave-able menu items, including our
  Buffalo, New York-style chicken wings." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Arby's Restaurant Group, Inc. ("Arby's"/"Parent"), a Delaware corporation, is
  described as "the parent company, owner-operator, and franchisor of the Arby's
  brand," majority owned by funds advised by Roark Capital Management LLC ("Roark").
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- IB Merger Sub I Corporation ("Merger Sub"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Arby's,
  was formed on November 22, 2017 "solely for the purpose of entering into the merger
  agreement and consummating the transactions contemplated by the merger agreement,"
  and had "no other business or operations." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated November 27, 2017, Merger Sub was to
  merge with and into the company, with the company "continuing as the surviving
  corporation and becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Arby's." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Each share of company common stock was to convert into the right to receive $157.00
  in cash, without interest, less applicable withholding taxes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Financing was structured as: (1) a debt commitment letter with Barclays Bank plc and
  other institutions for up to $2.21 billion of borrowings, (2) equity financing of up
  to $783 million from a fund advised by Roark, and (3) cash on hand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Shareholders approved the merger at a special meeting on February 2, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000016, Form 8-K]
- The merger closed on February 5, 2018, with aggregate consideration paid by Arby's
  for all equity securities of the company of "approximately $2.5 billion," funded
  through new senior notes, new senior secured credit facilities, new equity from
  Roark-advised funds, and cash on hand. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]
- At closing, the company's own pre-existing credit agreement (dated October 6, 2016,
  with Wells Fargo Bank as administrative agent) was terminated and all obligations
  under it paid off. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Legal vehicle: the company continued in existence as a Minnesota corporation and
  wholly owned subsidiary of Arby's; its articles of incorporation were "amended and
  restated in their entirety to be identical to the articles of incorporation of
  Merger Sub," and Merger Sub's bylaws became the company's bylaws, with references to
  Merger Sub's name replaced by "Buffalo Wild Wings, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]
- Business model/product: the company's own description of itself as an "owner,
  operator, and franchisor" of restaurants is unchanged in the merger documents; Arby's
  is itself described as an owner-operator-franchisor of a separate restaurant brand.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- People/organization: at the effective time, all nine then-serving directors of the
  company (Scott O. Bergren, Cindy L. Davis, Andre J. Fernandez, Janice L. Fields,
  Harry A. Lawton, Richard T. McGuire III, Jerry R. Rose, Sam B. Rovit, and Harmit J.
  Singh) ceased to be directors, and the three directors of Merger Sub (Paul J. Brown,
  Nils H. Okeson, and J. David Pipes) became directors of the company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]
- Prior to signing, then-Chief Executive Officer and President Sally J. Smith "announced
  her intention to retire as the company's chief executive officer when her successor
  was named," with a board search for a new chief executive officer ongoing during the
  period the merger was negotiated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger agreement provided that Arby's would honor all company benefit plans,
  "including employment agreements with our executives," except as otherwise provided
  in the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- Pre-closing retention agreements with cash retention bonuses were approved for named
  executive officers (Alexander H. Ware, Emily C. Decker, Andrew D. Block, Santiago
  Abraham, and an additional named officer), with the proxy specifying the arrangements
  applied to "all of our current executive officers, except Sally J. Smith." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]
- No transition-services agreement, shared-services arrangement, or comparable
  bridging contract for an operating substrate was identified in the reviewed merger
  agreement or proxy statement; the transaction was structured as an outright
  whole-company cash acquisition rather than a carve-out. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-380642, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- Following completion of the merger, the company requested that The Nasdaq Stock
  Market LLC withdraw its common stock from listing on the Nasdaq Global Select Market
  prior to the market open on February 5, 2018 and file a Form 25 notification of
  removal from listing and registration; the company's common shares thereafter ceased
  trading on any national securities exchange that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]
- Each unexercised stock option and each restricted stock unit award outstanding at the
  effective time was cancelled in exchange for a cash amount calculated by reference to
  the $157.00 merger consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash merger: Merger Sub merged with and into the
  company, with the company surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Arby's (majority
  owned by Roark-advised funds), financed by new debt and Roark equity rather than by
  any rollover of target equity. The company's legal vehicle, name, and self-described
  franchisor business model continued intact into the new ownership structure, while
  its entire pre-merger board was replaced at closing by Merger Sub's directors and its
  chief executive was already mid-succession-search before signing (and excluded from
  the pre-closing executive retention arrangements). No transition-services or
  shared-substrate bridging agreement was identified — the whole company, rather than a
  carved-out tier of it, crossed the ownership boundary at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001062449-18-000022, Form 8-K]
