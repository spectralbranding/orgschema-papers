## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The separated company continued as an independent SEC registrant under the name Rayonier
  Advanced Materials Inc. (Commission File Number 001-36285, Jacksonville, FL), filing an
  annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2018, with common stock
  listed on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 cover]
- A separate public 3-5-year outcome record therefore exists (the case is not uncertain on
  the outcome); annual reports on Form 10-K were filed for FY2017 (accession
  0001597672-18-000006), FY2018 (accession 0001597672-19-000008), and FY2019 (accession
  0001597672-20-000005), all within the fixed window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Best-documented realized scalar
- For fiscal year 2018 the company reported net income of $128 million on net sales of
  $2,134 million; the selected financial data show net income of $128M (2018), $325M
  (2017), $73M (2016), $55M (2015), and $32M (2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 Selected Financial Data]
- Net sales rose to $2,134 million in FY2018 from $961 million in FY2017, reflecting the
  addition of the acquired operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018 Selected Financial Data]
- The elevated FY2017 net income of $325 million reflected a non-cash gain on bargain
  purchase recognized on the Tembec acquisition (primarily from eliminating Tembec's
  deferred-tax valuation allowance); a further $20 million increase to that gain was
  recognized during 2018 from tax-related adjustments finalizing the purchase-price
  allocation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Post-transaction trajectory at the product/process and process/organization interfaces
- On November 17, 2017 the company completed the acquisition of Tembec Inc., which
  manufactures cellulose specialties, commodity products, forest products, and pulp and
  paper; the combination created a company with positions across the cellulose-specialties
  product spectrum plus additional product lines. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Following the acquisition the company reorganized into four reportable segments — High
  Purity Cellulose, Forest Products, Pulp, and Paper — expanding beyond the single-product
  cellulose-specialties operation, with paperboard produced at the Temiscaming plant in
  Quebec and operations in the United States, Canada and France. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- The company disclosed integration risk at the process interface: "the inability to
  effectively integrate the Tembec acquisition ... may affect our results," and noted that
  some facilities acquired in the 2017 Tembec acquisition had deferred capital expenditures
  due to Tembec's cash-flow limitations in recent years, which may require additional
  investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Documented labor/organization pressure in-window: as of December 31, 2018, collective
  bargaining agreements covering approximately 800 unionized employees had expired, and
  four collective bargaining agreements representing approximately 24 percent of employees
  had either expired in 2018 without renewal or were scheduled to expire in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~3-5-year window the separated company continued as an independent NYSE-
  listed registrant, remained profitable (FY2018 net income $128 million on net sales
  $2,134 million), and grew substantially through the November 2017 Tembec acquisition into
  a four-segment, multi-country producer; the record also shows disclosed integration risk,
  deferred-capital exposure at acquired facilities, and multiple lapsed collective
  bargaining agreements (~800 employees, ~24 percent of the workforce) at year-end 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001597672-19-000008, Form 10-K FY2018]
