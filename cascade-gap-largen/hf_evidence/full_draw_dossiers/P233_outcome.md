## Financial performance record (FY2018-FY2019, the last audited public record)
- Covia's net loss attributable to the company increased $1.0 billion to a loss of $1.3
  billion for fiscal year 2019, compared to a loss of $270.5 million for fiscal year
  2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Covia recorded non-cash goodwill and other asset impairment charges of $1.4 billion in
  2019 and $267.0 million in 2018, both attributed to a "dynamic shift in Energy
  markets" (the proppant/hydraulic-fracturing-linked segment). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Of the 2018 impairment, $217.1 million was a full write-off of goodwill in the Energy
  reporting unit, triggered by declining market conditions and a decline in Covia's share
  price; goodwill in the Industrial reporting unit was not found impaired in any tested
  period through the FY2019 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Adjusted EBITDA decreased $188.2 million, from $331.2 million in 2018 to $143.0 million
  in 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]
- Between the third quarter of 2018 and the end of 2019, Covia idled mining operations at
  multiple Northern White sand facilities (Shakopee MN, Brewer MO, Wexford MI, Kasota MN,
  Maiden Rock WI) and several resin-coating facilities, reducing Energy-segment nameplate
  annual capacity by 15 million tons through March 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]

## Chapter 11 reorganization (~2 years post-closing)
- On June 29, 2020, Covia Holdings Corporation and certain subsidiaries entered into a
  Restructuring Support Agreement with a majority of lenders under its June 1,
  2018-dated Term Loan Agreement, contemplating a prearranged plan of reorganization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-031311, Form 8-K dated 2020-06-30 reporting event of 2020-06-29]
- On the same date, the Company Parties filed voluntary Chapter 11 cases in the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of Texas, jointly administered as In re:
  Covia Holdings Corporation, et al.; the Company's international subsidiaries in Canada,
  Mexico, and Denmark were not included in the filing and continued operating in the
  ordinary course. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-031311, Form 8-K dated 2020-06-30]
- The commencement of the Chapter 11 cases constituted an event of default that
  accelerated obligations under Covia's debt instruments; the Company cautioned that
  common-stock holders could experience a significant or complete loss on their
  investment depending on the case outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-031311, Form 8-K dated 2020-06-30]
- On December 14, 2020, the Bankruptcy Court entered an order confirming the Modified
  First Amended Joint Chapter 11 Plan of Reorganization. In the Debtors' most recent
  pre-confirmation monthly operating report (filed with the Bankruptcy Court on November
  30, 2020, unaudited), total assets were approximately $2.0 billion against total
  liabilities of approximately $2.4 billion as of October 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-057036, Form 8-K dated 2020-12-15 reporting event of 2020-12-14]
- Under the confirmed Plan: existing Covia common and preferred stock (158,195,156
  shares outstanding as of November 6, 2020) was to be cancelled with no distribution to
  holders; lenders were to receive their pro rata share of a new $825 million term loan
  and 100% of the pro forma equity in the reorganized company (subject to dilution by a
  management incentive plan); a new $135 million asset-based exit facility was to be
  entered into; and a $39.75 million cash pool was established for general unsecured
  claims. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-057036, Form 8-K dated 2020-12-15]
- The reorganized company's post-emergence equity (Class A and Class B common units) was
  to be issued without Securities Act registration, in reliance on Bankruptcy Code
  section 1145 and Securities Act exemptions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-057036, Form 8-K dated 2020-12-15]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within roughly two years of the June 2018 closing, the combined company recorded a
  $1.3 billion net loss and $1.4 billion of impairment charges for fiscal year 2019 (on
  top of a $270.5 million net loss and $267.0 million of impairment in 2018), concentrated
  in its Energy (proppant) segment, and by mid-2020 filed for Chapter 11 reorganization,
  emerging in December 2020 with its pre-petition common and preferred stock cancelled
  and no recovery for existing shareholders, alongside a pre-confirmation balance sheet
  showing total liabilities (~$2.4 billion) exceeding total assets (~$2.0 billion). [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-011206, Form 10-K FY2019]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001564590-20-057036, Form 8-K dated 2020-12-15]
- The company's SEC registration terminated at the end of 2020, before the nominal
  3-5-year (~2021-2023) outcome window opens, so no further public periodic-reporting
  record exists for this business within that window; the record above (through
  emergence from Chapter 11) is the latest and most complete public documentation
  available. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-20-330391, Form 15-12B]
