# Structural sub-dossier — Panera Bread Company acquisition by JAB Holdings (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On April 4, 2017 the Company entered into a merger agreement with JAB Holdings B.V.
  (a private Netherlands limited liability company, "JAB"), Rye Parent Corp. (a
  Delaware corporation, "Parent"), and Rye Merger Sub, Inc. (a Delaware corporation and
  wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, "Merger Sub"); the agreement provides for the
  acquisition by JAB of the Company through the merger of Merger Sub with and into the
  Company, with the Company as the surviving corporation. JAB is described as "a
  privately held company focused on long-term investments in companies with premium
  brands in the consumer goods category." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]
- Parent and Merger Sub were newly formed for the transaction: prior to the effective
  time, each "will have engaged in no other business activities and will have incurred
  no liabilities or obligations other than those contemplated by the merger and the
  merger agreement"; upon completion, Merger Sub ceases to exist and the Company
  continues as the surviving corporation, becoming a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent
  and an indirect subsidiary of JAB. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]

## The business as structured at signing
- At signing the Company was a national bakery-cafe concept with 2,042 Company-owned
  and franchise-operated bakery-cafe locations in 46 states, the District of Columbia,
  and Ontario, Canada, operating three business segments: Company-owned bakery-cafe
  operations (910 locations), franchise operations (1,132 locations), and fresh dough
  and other product operations (24 fresh dough facilities supplying fresh dough and
  other products daily to most Company-owned and franchise-operated bakery-cafes). The
  customer-facing product/asset, the supplying process (fresh dough operations), and
  the franchise contractual relationships all remained within the single surviving
  corporation at closing — the merger agreement does not carve out or separate any
  segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]

## Consideration and financing
- Each outstanding share of common stock converts into the right to receive $315.00 in
  cash, without interest and less applicable withholding taxes (excluded and dissenting
  shares aside). JAB expected to fund the acquisition and repay certain outstanding
  Company indebtedness through existing cash resources and new indebtedness; Merger Sub
  obtained binding financing commitments of an aggregate principal amount of $3 billion,
  and receipt of that debt financing was not a condition to completion of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]

## Controlling-principal / management-tier evidence at closing
- Concurrently with signing, Company stockholder Ronald M. Shaich (Chairman and Chief
  Executive Officer) and related Shaich trusts/entities entered into a voting agreement
  with Parent and Merger Sub agreeing to vote shares representing approximately 15.5% of
  outstanding voting power in favor of the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]
- For one year following the effective time, Parent agreed to provide each continuing
  employee with compensation, severance, other benefits, and 401(k) benefits "at least
  substantially comparable in the aggregate" to what each received immediately before
  the effective time. Named executive officers held "double-trigger" change-in-control
  arrangements (payable only upon both closing and a qualifying termination); the
  Company's executive officers as a group would receive approximately $55,001,148 upon
  a qualifying termination under their non-competition agreements, per the golden
  parachute compensation disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]

## Closing-time listing change
- The proxy statement provides that if the merger is completed, the Company's common
  stock will be removed from listing on the NASDAQ and deregistered under the Exchange
  Act, after which the Company will no longer file periodic reports with the SEC on
  account of that stock. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- As structured, the transaction was a whole-company cash acquisition (reverse
  triangular merger) in which the Company's existing business segments, franchise
  network, and fresh-dough supply operations passed intact into the surviving
  corporation with no separated tier or transitional-services carve-out; continuity for
  employees (one year of comparable benefits) and officers (change-in-control payments)
  was addressed contractually, while board/governance control passed to the acquirer at
  closing and the stock was to be removed from public listing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-189582, DEFM14A]
