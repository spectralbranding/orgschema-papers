## Public-reporting status over the outcome window
- The registrant continued as a separately SEC-registered public company through the
  outcome window, filing its Form 10-K annual report for the fiscal year ended December
  31, 2018 (accession 0001609253-19-000058, filed 2019-02-27), with common stock listed
  on the New York Stock Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]
- As of December 31, 2018 the registrant had approximately 1,500 employees, compared to
  approximately 1,450 as of December 31, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- Net income (loss) attributable to common stock over the window and the surrounding years,
  as reported in the FY2018 selected financial data, was: $328 million (2018), $(266)
  million (2017), $279 million (2016), $(3,554) million (2015), and $(1,434) million
  (2014). [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]
- Long-term debt stood at $5,251 million as of December 31, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]
- The registrant characterized itself as having "significant indebtedness that could limit
  our financial and operating flexibility and make us more vulnerable in economic
  downturns." [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Realized operations at the asset/operations interface
- The registrant operated its oil and gas asset base and its own producing operations as an
  integrated standalone concern through the window; its largest producing asset, the Elk
  Hills field, was operated with company facilities including natural gas processing
  (cryogenic) plants, a power plant, and an advanced central control facility with remote
  automation on over 95% of relevant operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]
- In April 2018 the registrant acquired Chevron's interest in the former Elk Hills unit
  (the "Elk Hills transaction") for approximately $518 million (including $7 million of
  assumed liabilities relating to asset retirement obligations), after which it held all of
  the working, surface, and mineral interests in the former Elk Hills unit; Chevron sold
  all of the CRC common stock it acquired in the transaction during 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the fixed outcome window the registrant remained an operating, separately reporting
  public company running its own oil and gas assets and production processes; the salient
  realized-record feature was the carried financial leverage (long-term debt of $5,251
  million at year-end 2018 and disclosed "significant indebtedness") against a volatile
  earnings trajectory that swung from a $(3,554) million loss in 2015 to $328 million of
  net income attributable to common stock in 2018, with the asset base consolidated during
  the window through the ~$518 million April 2018 Elk Hills acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001609253-19-000058, Form 10-K FY2018]
