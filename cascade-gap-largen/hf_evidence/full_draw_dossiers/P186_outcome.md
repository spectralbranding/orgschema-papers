## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The combined enterprise continued as an active SEC registrant under the successor
  entity Linde plc (CIK 0001707925), which filed Form 10-K annual reports covering
  fiscal years 2020, 2021, 2022, and 2023, including the Form 10-K for fiscal year
  2022 (filed 2023-02-28) and the Form 10-K for fiscal year 2023 (filed 2024-02-28).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Realized financial trajectory
- Net Income attributable to Linde plc was $2,501 million for FY2020, $3,826 million
  for FY2021, $4,147 million for FY2022, and $6,199 million for FY2023, on reported
  sales of $30,793 million (FY2021), $33,364 million (FY2022, +8% year over year),
  and $32,854 million (FY2023, -2% year over year). [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]
- The company's non-GAAP reconciliations continued, through at least FY2022, to
  separately identify and exclude "Purchase accounting impacts - Linde AG," stating
  this treatment is used because, among other reasons, "the business combination was
  a merger of equals in an all-stock merger transaction, with no cash consideration"
  and because purchase-accounting adjustments affect the reportable segments
  unevenly. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Post-close integration and other charges disclosed over the window
- The FY2022 Form 10-K disclosed "Merger-related costs and other charges" of $139
  million ($93 million after tax) for the year ended December 31, 2022, described as
  "primarily related to severance actions within the Engineering segment...the
  impairment of an equity method investment in the EMEA segment, and the sale of the
  GIST business completed on September 30, 2022." [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The same FY2022 filing separately disclosed Russia-related deconsolidation and
  other charges totaling $890 million pre-tax for FY2022 (comprising $787 million of
  Russia deconsolidation charges and $103 million of other Russia-related charges),
  in addition to the merger-related charges above. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022]
- By the FY2023 Form 10-K, the equivalent disclosure line item ("Other Charges") had
  fallen to $40 million for the year ended December 31, 2023, described as "primarily
  related to severance in the Engineering segment and expenses incurred due to the
  intercompany reorganization," with no continuing reference to "merger-related"
  costs in that year's charge description. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Leadership and board composition documented in the window
- The FY2022 Form 10-K (signed February 28, 2023) and the FY2023 Form 10-K (signed
  February 28, 2024) were each signed by Stephen F. Angel in the capacity of
  Chairman and by Sanjiv Lamba in the capacity of Chief Executive Officer and
  Director. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]
- Sanjiv Lamba was appointed Chief Executive Officer effective March 1, 2022; he had
  previously served as Chief Operating Officer beginning January 2021 and as
  Executive Vice President, APAC, beginning October 2018, after having been a member
  of Linde AG's Executive Board since 2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The directors signing the FY2023 Form 10-K, in addition to Angel and Lamba, were
  Ann-Kristin Achleitner, Robert L. Wood, Thomas Enders, Josef Kaeser, Victoria
  Ossadnik, Alberto Weisser, Martin Richenhagen, and Hugh Grant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed ~2021-2023 post-combination window, the successor registrant
  Linde plc continued to report to the SEC and recorded rising net income each year
  ($2,501 million FY2020 to $6,199 million FY2023) alongside sales that grew through
  FY2022 and then declined slightly in FY2023; the filings continued to carry a
  distinct "merger-related" and "purchase accounting impacts - Linde AG" reporting
  line through FY2022, including $139 million of merger-related charges that year,
  before that specific "merger-related" language dropped out of the FY2023 charge
  disclosure; separately, FY2022 also carried $890 million of Russia-related
  deconsolidation charges. Leadership of the combined company as reported in both the
  FY2022 and FY2023 filings placed the former Praxair chief executive in the
  Chairman role and a career Linde AG/BOC executive in the Chief Executive Officer
  role. [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-23-005434, Form 10-K FY2022] [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-24-007424, Form 10-K FY2023]
