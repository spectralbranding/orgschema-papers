# Outcome sub-dossier — Corteva, Inc. realized record, ~3-4 years after separation (FY2022)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> post-transaction trajectory over the years following the transaction. It contains no
> information about how the transaction was structured at closing. The outcome window is
> fixed to the company's fiscal 2022 annual report (~3.5 years after the June 2019
> separation).

## Realized financial trajectory (fixed window)
- Net sales were $17,455 million in fiscal 2022, compared with $15,655 million in fiscal
  2021 and $14,217 million in fiscal 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY ended 2022-12-31]
- Net income (loss) attributable to Corteva was $1,147 million in 2022, $1,759 million in
  2021, and $681 million in 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Seed net sales were $8,979 million in 2022, up 7 percent from $8,402 million in 2021;
  crop protection net sales were $8,476 million in 2022, up 17 percent from $7,253 million
  in 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net income attributable to the company of $1,147 million
  in fiscal 2022, the third consecutive year of positive net income following separation
  ($681 million in 2020, $1,759 million in 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Documented operating-structure and portfolio actions over the period
- The company launched "2022 Restructuring Actions" "in connection with the company's
  shift to a global business unit model," under which it "assessed its business priorities
  and operational struct[ure]," targeting up to approximately $220 million of savings on a
  run-rate basis by 2025. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Restructuring and asset-related charges - net were $363 million in 2022, an increase
  from $289 million for the year ended December 31, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- A separate "Execute to Win Productivity Program" approved in the first quarter of 2020
  was "designed to improve productivity through optimizing certain operational and
  organizational structures"; the company recorded net pre-tax restructuring charges of
  $173 million from inception-to-date under that program. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- Portfolio/exit actions in the period included a "Seed sale associated with Russia Exit"
  and a loss on exit of a non-strategic asset, plus a loss of $(53) million and a gain of
  $27 million relating to the sale of the La Porte site (agreement signed 2020, closed in
  the first quarter of 2021). [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Organization / workforce over the period
- As of December 31, 2022, the company globally employed approximately 21,000 employees,
  described as a "strong employee base... a key element to the success of its business."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
- The 2022 shift to a global business unit model was the stated occasion for reassessing
  the operational structure (see 2022 Restructuring Actions, above), indicating a
  reorganization of how the operations were managed over time. [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the roughly 3-4 years after separation the company recorded rising net sales
  ($14,217M -> $15,655M -> $17,455M for 2020-2022) and positive net income attributable to
  the company each year (peaking at $1,759 million in 2021, $1,147 million in 2022), while
  running successive productivity/restructuring programs (the 2020 "Execute to Win"
  Productivity Program and the 2022 Restructuring Actions tied to a shift to a global
  business unit model) and executing portfolio exits (Russia seed-business exit; La Porte
  site sale). [SRC: SEC accession 0001755672-23-000005, Form 10-K FY2022]
