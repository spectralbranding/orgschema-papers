# Structural sub-dossier — Cabela's Incorporated acquisition by Bass Pro Group, LLC (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Cabela's Incorporated, a Delaware corporation and "a leading specialty retailer and
  the world's largest direct marketer of hunting, fishing, camping and related outdoor
  merchandise," founded in 1961, agreed to be acquired by Bass Pro Group, LLC, a
  privately-held Delaware limited liability company that serves as the holding company
  for the operations of Bass Pro Shops, White River Marine Group and Big Cedar Lodge.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- The parties entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated October 3, 2016, later
  amended by an Amendment to Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 17, 2017, under
  which Prairie Merger Sub, Inc. (a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent) would merge with
  and into the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and
  becoming a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- At the effective time, each outstanding share of Company Class A common stock would be
  cancelled and converted into the right to receive $61.50 in cash per share, subject to
  certain adjustments tied to the financial-services carve-out described below. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- The special meeting to adopt the merger agreement was scheduled for July 11, 2017 at
  the Company's corporate headquarters, One Cabela Drive, Sidney, Nebraska; the merger
  agreement's outside date (absent extension) was October 3, 2017. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- The merger closed on September 26, 2017, at which point Cabela's became part of Bass
  Pro Shops; contemporaneous reporting described the transaction as a roughly $4 billion
  merger bringing Cabela's 82 U.S./Canada stores together with Bass Pro's own 95 stores.
  [SRC: https://www.ksmu.org/business-and-the-economy/2017-09-26/cabelas-now-officially-part-of-bass-pro-shops-after-merger]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Controlling intent / governance: the merger agreement provided that, effective as of
  and immediately following the effective time, the board of directors of the surviving
  corporation would consist of the members of the board of directors of Sub (Parent's
  merger vehicle) immediately prior to the effective time, and the officers of Sub at
  the effective time would become the officers of the surviving corporation — full
  governance succession to Parent's designees rather than retention of the Company's
  pre-closing board/officer slate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- Legal vehicle: the Company itself (Cabela's Incorporated) survived the merger as a
  wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, preserving the corporate entity rather than
  dissolving it into Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product/asset: the retail and direct-marketing outdoor-merchandise
  business (stores and the "well-established direct business") was the asset acquired
  by Parent, whose existing operations already comprised Bass Pro Shops, White River
  Marine Group and Big Cedar Lodge — i.e., the acquirer was an operator in the same
  specialty-retail/outdoor category. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- People/organization continuity commitment: Parent's press-release statement, quoted in
  the proxy's voting-agreement annex, stated that "Bass Pro Shops intends to continue to
  maintain important bases of operations in Sidney and Lincoln and hopes to continue the
  very favorable connections to those communities and the Cabela's team members residing
  there" — a stated (non-binding, as disclosed) intent rather than a contractual
  headcount or facility guarantee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- Employee benefit plans/severance arrangements: the merger agreement provided that
  Parent would cause the surviving corporation to assume, honor and continue the
  Company's and its subsidiaries' existing employment, severance, retention and
  termination plans, policies, programs and agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]

## Financial-services business carved out at closing (product/process separation)
- The merger could not close until the Company's financial-services business — World's
  Foremost Bank ("WFB"), which operated the Cabela's CLUB co-branded credit card program
  — had been sold and a related bank framework agreement had become effective; the
  merger agreement and the bank sale agreements were interlocking conditions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- Under the bank sale agreements (a Bank Framework Agreement, a Synovus bank asset
  purchase agreement, and a Capital One bank asset purchase agreement, all dated April
  17, 2017), Synovus Bank agreed to acquire substantially all of WFB's deposit-related
  assets and liabilities, while Capital One Bank (USA), National Association agreed to
  acquire the assets and liabilities relating to the Cabela's CLUB co-branded credit
  card accounts and equity interests in certain securitization funding vehicles — i.e.,
  the credit-card operating business was routed to Capital One and deposits to Synovus
  in a structured three-way split, separately from the retail-business merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- This carve-out meant the credit-card program that had been integrated with the
  Company's retail/loyalty operations did NOT cross to Parent as part of the merged
  retail business; it was routed to different acquirers (Synovus/Capital One) under
  separate asset purchase agreements executed contemporaneously with the merger
  amendment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
- The bank sale agreements were subject to their own closing conditions, including
  approval by the Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve of a Bank Merger Act
  application filed by Synovus. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]

## Financing structure at closing
- Parent's obligation to fund the aggregate merger consideration (approximately $4.584
  billion based on shares outstanding as of May 17, 2017) was to be met through up to
  approximately $5.145 billion of debt financing and up to approximately $2.383 billion
  of preferred equity financing, per debt and preferred financing commitment letters each
  dated October 3, 2016; directors collectively holding approximately 18.52% of shares
  outstanding as of that date entered into voting agreements obligating them to vote in
  favor of the merger agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction structured a whole-company acquisition of the surviving retail/direct
  legal entity (Cabela's Incorporated) into Parent, with full governance succession to
  Parent's designated board/officers, while contractually severing the customer-facing
  credit-card financial-services business (WFB / Cabela's CLUB) from the retail business
  and routing it to two separate acquirers (Synovus, Capital One) under interlocking
  bank sale agreements that were a closing condition of the merger itself, and preserving
  existing employment/benefit arrangements by assumption rather than renegotiation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-193609, Form DEFM14A]
