## Public-reporting status after the transaction (feasibility-relevant)
- The surviving public filer (SEC CIK 0000109177) continued reporting after the
  transaction under the name "Spectrum Brands Holdings, Inc." (adopted July 2, 2018),
  having previously been named "HRG Group, Inc." (2015-2018), "Harbinger Group Inc."
  (2009-2015) and "Zapata Corp" (to 2009); it remained listed on the NYSE under ticker
  "SPB." [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000109177.json]
- Annual reports covering the fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window were filed as Form 10-K
  for fiscal year ended September 30, 2021 (filed 2021-11-23) and fiscal year ended
  September 30, 2022 (filed 2022-11-22). [SRC: SEC accession 0000109177-21-000058, Form
  10-K; SEC accession 0000109177-22-000034, Form 10-K]

## Realized business composition and consolidated results
- As of the FY2021 10-K, the Company managed its business in three vertically
  integrated, product-focused segments: Home and Personal Care (HPC), Global Pet Care
  (GPC), and Home and Garden (H&G), after its Hardware and Home Improvement (HHI)
  segment was reclassified to discontinued operations (see below). [SRC: SEC accession
  0000109177-21-000058, Form 10-K]
- Consolidated net income attributable to controlling interest was $494.5 million (FY
  2019), $97.8 million (FY2020), $189.6 million (FY2021) and $71.6 million (FY2022); net
  income (loss) from continuing operations attributable to controlling interest was
  $(303.0) million (FY2019), $(52.7) million (FY2020) and $15.1 million (FY2021). [SRC:
  SEC accession 0000109177-21-000058, Form 10-K; SEC accession 0000109177-22-000034,
  Form 10-K]

## Documented post-close divestitures and disruption
- On January 2, 2019 — within roughly six months of the July 13, 2018 closing — the
  Company completed the sale of its Global Batteries and Lighting ("GBL") business to
  Energizer Holdings, Inc. for cash proceeds of $1,956.2 million, recognizing a pre-tax
  gain on sale of $989.8 million; the transaction's results were recognized as
  discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000109177-21-000058, Form 10-K, Note 3 —
  Divestitures]
- On September 8, 2021, the Company entered a definitive Asset and Stock Purchase
  Agreement with ASSA ABLOY AB to sell its Hardware and Home Improvement (HHI) segment
  for cash proceeds of $4.3 billion; HHI's assets and liabilities were classified as
  held for sale and its operations reported as discontinued operations for all periods
  presented, described as a planned disposition representing a strategic shift with a
  major effect on the Company's operations and financial results. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000109177-21-000058, Form 10-K]
- On September 15, 2022, the U.S. Department of Justice issued a petition to enjoin and
  block the HHI-ASSA ABLOY transaction; the Company and ASSA stated disagreement with
  the DOJ's concerns and proposed remedies (including dispositions of ASSA's Emtek
  business and its U.S./Canada smart residential business), and the Company continued to
  classify HHI as held for sale while stating it expected the parties would obtain the
  required governmental clearances and close the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession
  0000109177-22-000034, Form 10-K]
- The FY2022 10-K also discloses a February 18, 2022 acquisition of a home appliances
  and cookware products business from Tristar Products, Inc. folded into the HPC
  segment, together with a stated plan to separate the HPC business into an independent
  "Global Appliances" business and to create a "pure play" consumer products company
  composed of the GPC and H&G businesses — a further reorganization layered on top of
  the pending HHI sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0000109177-22-000034, Form 10-K]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed ~2021-2023 outcome window, the surviving public filer's own annual
  reports show it continued to operate essentially the full pre-transaction Spectrum
  business rather than any divestiture flowing from the HRG/Spectrum combination itself:
  the two major portfolio changes recorded in the window — the completed sale of the
  Global Batteries and Lighting business (January 2019, $1,956.2 million) and the
  announced-but-not-yet-closed sale of the Hardware and Home Improvement segment to ASSA
  ABLOY ($4.3 billion, agreement dated September 2021) — both trace to a divestiture
  process that was already pending before the Merger's closing, and the HHI sale
  encountered a documented obstacle within the window in the form of a DOJ enjoinment
  petition filed September 15, 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000109177-21-000058, Form
  10-K; SEC accession 0000109177-22-000034, Form 10-K]
- Consolidated net income attributable to controlling interest declined from $189.6
  million (FY2021) to $71.6 million (FY2022) within the window, while net income (loss)
  from continuing operations moved from a loss in FY2019-FY2020 to modest positive
  figures by FY2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0000109177-21-000058, Form 10-K; SEC accession
  0000109177-22-000034, Form 10-K]
