## Transaction and parties at closing (deal-terms source)
- On January 10, 2018, IRM Data Centers Expansion LLC ("Buyer"), an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of Iron Mountain Incorporated, completed its acquisition of IO Data Centers,
  LLC ("IODC"), a colocation data center services provider, pursuant to a Purchase
  Agreement dated December 11, 2017; Iron Mountain acted as guarantor with respect to
  specified articles of that agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- At closing the Buyer acquired, directly or indirectly, all of the outstanding equity
  interests of IODC, purchasing the United States operations of IODC and its United States
  subsidiaries, for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $1.34 billion, subject to
  working-capital and other customary adjustments, plus a portion of $60 million in cash
  payable to the sellers based on future performance. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- Customer-facing asset: the acquisition transferred the land and buildings associated with
  four data centers — in Phoenix and Scottsdale, Arizona; Edison, New Jersey; and Columbus,
  Ohio — totaling 728,000 square feet of existing data center space, 62 MW of capacity,
  with expansion potential of an additional 77 MW in Arizona and New Jersey. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- Customer relationships crossed with the asset: the closing coincided with a customer
  expansion at the Phoenix campus by Cyxtera, and the additional purchase consideration was
  tied to company performance since signing, "including the Cyxtera lease." [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- Legal vehicle / operating processes: because the transaction acquired all outstanding
  equity interests of IODC and its U.S. subsidiaries (an equity purchase of the operating
  entity) rather than isolated assets, the operating company and the facilities' operations
  transferred together as a going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- People / organization: the acquirer's SVP and general manager of Iron Mountain Data
  Centers characterized the deal as bringing "our expanded team of experienced and highly
  skilled professionals who we welcome to the Iron Mountain family," indicating the
  workforce running the facilities moved with the operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Financing and contractual bridging at closing
- The transaction was accompanied by an equity offering: on January 10, 2018 Iron Mountain
  completed the sale of an additional 2,175,000 shares of common stock via the full
  exercise of the underwriters' option under a December 12, 2017 underwriting agreement, at
  a public offering price of $37.00 per share. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- No transitional-services, operating, or shared-services agreement between IODC's prior
  owners and the Buyer is disclosed in the retrieved closing filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K]
- The required financial statements of the acquired business and the pro forma financial
  information were to be furnished by amendment to the Form 8-K within 71 calendar days.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 9.01]

## Serial-acquisition context at closing
- The IODC purchase was one of a sequence of data center acquisitions: it followed the
  September 2017 acquisition of the FORTRUST data center and the October 2017 announced
  purchase of two Credit Suisse data centers in the London and Singapore markets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Iron Mountain acquired the U.S. operating company IODC as a going concern through an
  indirect subsidiary — all outstanding equity interests, four owned data-center facilities,
  an existing anchor customer lease, and the operating workforce transferring together — for
  approximately $1.34 billion plus performance-based consideration, financed in part by a
  concurrent common-stock offering, with no transitional-services arrangement disclosed in
  the closing filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K Item 2.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-001664, Form 8-K EX-99.1]
