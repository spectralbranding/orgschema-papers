## Realized trajectory within the window
- On January 28, 2019, Versum Materials, Inc. ("Versum") and Entegris, Inc. ("Entegris") issued a joint press release announcing their entry into an agreement and plan of merger between Versum and Entegris. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-017837, Form 8-K]
- On April 12, 2019, prior to entering a new merger agreement, Versum delivered to Entegris a written notice terminating the previously announced Entegris merger agreement (dated January 27, 2019); Versum paid Entegris a termination fee of $140 million, and Versum's special meeting of stockholders scheduled for April 26, 2019 was cancelled. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-104578, Form 8-K]
- Also on April 12, 2019, Versum entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with Merck KGaA, Darmstadt, Germany ("Parent"), and EMD Performance Materials Holding, Inc. ("Merger Sub"), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Parent, under which each outstanding Versum share (subject to customary exceptions) would be converted into the right to receive $53.00 per share in cash, without interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-104578, Form 8-K]
- The Merck merger agreement provided a termination fee of $235 million payable by Versum under specified circumstances (including a change of board recommendation or termination to accept a Superior Proposal) and an outside date of April 13, 2020, extendable to July 13, 2020 in certain circumstances to obtain regulatory clearances. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-104578, Form 8-K]

## Realized terminal outcome (best-documented scalar)
- On October 7, 2019, pursuant to the April 12, 2019 Merger Agreement, Merger Sub merged with and into Versum, with Versum surviving as the surviving corporation and a wholly-owned indirect subsidiary of Merck KGaA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]
- At the effective time on October 7, 2019, each outstanding share of Versum common stock (subject to customary exceptions and appraisal rights) was automatically converted into the right to receive $53.00 per share in cash, without interest; the merger consideration was funded with cash on hand and Merck term-loan facilities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the completion, all outstanding obligations under Versum's Credit Agreement dated September 30, 2016 were repaid and its commitments terminated, and Versum's 5.500% Senior Notes due 2024 were satisfied and discharged (redemption noticed for October 16, 2019). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]

## Documented disruption at the organization/management interface
- At the effective time, all directors then in office — Guillermo Novo, Jacques Croisetière, Seifi Ghasemi, Dr. Yi Hyon Paik, Thomas J. Riordan, Susan C. Schnabel, and Ambassador Alejandro D. Wolff — resigned from the board and its committees, and were replaced by five individuals designated in connection with the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]
- Immediately following the effective time, certain officers — Guillermo Novo, George G. Bitto, Michael Valente, and Edward C. Shober — ceased to serve in their roles; Versum appointed John J. (Jeff) White, who had served as Senior Vice President of the Delivery Systems & Services segment since 2016, as President, and Monica Elliott, an executive of the acquirer, as Chief Financial Officer. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]
- The bylaws of Versum were amended and restated in their entirety at the effective time to become the bylaws of the former Merger Sub. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]

## Delisting and deregistration
- On October 7, 2019, Versum notified the New York Stock Exchange of the completion of the merger and requested suspension of trading and the filing of a Form 25 to delist and deregister the common stock under Section 12(b); the NYSE halted trading prior to market open on October 7, 2019, and Versum stated it intended to file a Form 15 to suspend its reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-263471, Form 8-K]
- Versum filed a Form 15-12B on October 18, 2019 to deregister and suspend its Securities Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-269927, Form 15-12B]
