## Realized fate of the transaction
- The Asset Purchase Agreement was not consummated. On May 19, 2010, the Seller (Orleans
  Homebuilders, Inc. and affiliates), without the Company's consent, withdrew its motion
  requesting that the Bankruptcy Court approve the Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-052294, Form 8-K Item 1.02 (Termination of a Material Definitive Agreement)]
- The Seller publicly announced plans to file a plan of reorganization rather than
  continuing with an auction process to sell the Assets under Section 363 of the U.S.
  Bankruptcy Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-052294, Form 8-K Item 1.02]
- Because the Bankruptcy Court's approval was a condition to effectiveness and the Seller
  withdrew its approval motion, no ownership boundary was crossed under this transaction:
  the land-under-development / work-in-process asset base of the Seller did not transfer to
  the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-052294, Form 8-K Item 1.02]

## Realized record over the fixed outcome window
- NVR, Inc.'s Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2013 (within the fixed
  window) contains no reference to "Orleans" in its business description, segment
  disclosures, or financial statements — i.e., the proposed acquisition produced no
  acquired-business line, goodwill, impairment, or divestiture in NVR's consolidated
  results for the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-060606, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]
- NVR remained a going concern operating its own homebuilding business over the window:
  consolidated revenues for the year ended December 31, 2013 totaled $4,211,267 thousand,
  an increase of 32% from $3,184,650 thousand in 2012, and net income for 2013 was
  $266,477 thousand, an increase of 48% from the prior year (diluted earnings per share of
  $54.81). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-060606, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013 (dollars in thousands)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- The proposed asset acquisition did not close: after NVR entered a stalking-horse Asset
  Purchase Agreement on April 13, 2010, the Seller withdrew its Bankruptcy Court approval
  motion without NVR's consent on May 19, 2010 and announced it would pursue a stand-alone
  plan of reorganization rather than a Section 363 auction; NVR's in-window FY2013 annual
  report shows no Orleans-related business within its consolidated operations, consistent
  with a transaction that was never consummated, while NVR continued as a profitable
  going concern. [SRC: SEC accession 0000950123-10-052294, Form 8-K Item 1.02] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-060606, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 31, 2013]
