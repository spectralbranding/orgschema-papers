## Outcome window
- The fixed outcome window is the registrant's own realized record 3–5 years after the closing-era filing; the primary source is its Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2021, filed under the same CIK (0001327978) and I.R.S. Employer Identification No. (30-0309068) as the closing-era filing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]

## Change of name and external manager over the window
- By the end of the window the registrant is named Ares Real Estate Income Trust Inc.; the filing's auditor report describes it as "Ares Real Estate Income Trust Inc. (formerly known as Black Creek Diversified Property Fund Inc.)." [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- Ares acquired Black Creek Group's U.S. real estate investment advisory and distribution business in July 2021; as a result of the closing of that transaction (effective July 1, 2021) Ares Commercial Real Estate Management LLC became the Company's new advisor, replacing the former advisor, Black Creek Diversified Property Advisors LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- The filing notes Ares did not acquire the Company's former sponsor, Black Creek Diversified Property Advisors Group LLC; the advisor of record is Black Creek Diversified Property Advisors LLC for periods prior to July 1, 2021 and Ares Commercial Real Estate Management LLC for periods thereafter. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- The organizational form is unchanged: the Company remains an externally managed UPREIT with "no employees," holding substantially all of its assets through AREIT Operating Partnership LP, of which it is the sole general partner. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized financial trajectory
- GAAP net income (loss) attributable to common stockholders was $30,754 thousand for 2021, compared with a net loss of $(14,914) thousand for 2020 and net income of $142,551 thousand for 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- Reported total assets were $2,990,971 thousand as of December 31, 2021, versus $2,111,560 thousand as of December 31, 2020. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]

## Realized portfolio trajectory (continued serial acquisition)
- As of December 31, 2021 the consolidated real property portfolio consisted of 67 properties (including two classified as held for sale), totaling approximately 13.6 million square feet located in 31 markets throughout the U.S. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- Over the window the Company continued to buy and sell: from 2019 to 2021 it disposed of approximately $0.7 billion of properties and acquired approximately $1.7 billion of properties, generally selling higher-yielding properties and acquiring properties with higher income potential. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- During 2021 alone the Company acquired 12 industrial properties, three residential properties and one grocery-anchored retail property (comprising approximately 3.2 million square feet) for an aggregate contractual purchase price of $891.1 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]

## Documented interface / continuity facts over the period
- The customer-facing asset base shifted in composition over the window toward industrial and residential (12 industrial and three residential of the 16 properties acquired in 2021), a change effected through the external advisor's acquisition/disposition process rather than by any in-house operating organization, consistent with the Company continuing to have no employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- The filing flags integration exposure at the advisory/organizational interface: it states that "the integration of Black Creek Group into the business of Ares could present challenges," identifying the July 2021 change of advisor as a source of transition risk. [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the outcome window the registrant remained a non-listed, externally managed UPREIT with no employees, continued to assemble its portfolio by serial acquisition (67 properties / ~13.6 million square feet / 31 markets by December 31, 2021, up from the closing-era footprint), changed its external advisor to an Ares affiliate effective July 1, 2021, and adopted the name Ares Real Estate Income Trust Inc. (formerly Black Creek Diversified Property Fund Inc.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: GAAP net income attributable to common stockholders of $30,754 thousand for FY2021, recovering from a net loss of $(14,914) thousand in FY2020; total assets rose to $2,990,971 thousand at December 31, 2021 (from $2,111,560 thousand a year earlier). [SRC: SEC accession 0001558370-22-003455, 10-K FY2021]
