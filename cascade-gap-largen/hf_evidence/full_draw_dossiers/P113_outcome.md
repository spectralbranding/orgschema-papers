# Outcome sub-dossier — The Hertz Corporation / Advantage Rent A Car business, realized record ~3–5 years after 2009

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY the realized
> record over the fixed 3–5-year outcome window after the April 2009 acquisition (i.e.,
> ~2012–2014); it contains no information about how that acquisition was structured at
> closing. The outcome entity fixed at construction is the registrant, The Hertz
> Corporation, and its Advantage business. Facts are drawn from the registrant's annual
> report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012.

## Reporting status within the outcome window
- The registrant remained an SEC reporting company through the window and filed a Form
  10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2012 under Commission File No. 001-07541,
  giving a primary, audited public record of the realized outcome. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Realized outcome scalar (best-documented)
- On December 12, 2012 — within the 3–5-year window (~3.7 years after the April 2009
  acquisition) — the registrant completed the sale of Simply Wheelz LLC, the wholly owned
  subsidiary that owned and operated the Advantage Rent A Car business (the "Advantage
  divestiture"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The subsidiary was sold to Adreca Holdings Corp., a subsidiary of Macquarie Capital,
  which was expected to be operated by Franchise Services of North America Inc. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Sale consideration was approximately $16.0 million, plus the value of current assets as
  of the closing date of approximately $3.6 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- As a result of the divestiture, the registrant realized a loss (before income taxes) of
  approximately $31.4 million as of December 31, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Why the business exited (controlling-context of the realized record)
- The divestiture was undertaken pursuant to a consent agreement with the Federal Trade
  Commission (entered November 19, 2012) in connection with the registrant's acquisition
  of Dollar Thrifty Automotive Group, requiring the registrant to divest the Advantage
  brand and to secure for the buyer certain Dollar Thrifty on-airport rental concessions.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Immediately prior to the divestiture, Advantage was operating at 62 U.S. locations,
  including 35 on-airport locations where Advantage held concessions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Documented process/organization-interface bridging at the exit
- The divested business could not be handed over as a standalone going concern: as part of
  the sale the registrant agreed to provide the buyer financial support of $17.0 million
  over three years from the closing date (present value $15.6 million as of December 31,
  2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- To assist the buyer in securing alternative fleet financing, the registrant entered a
  senior note credit agreement to loan Simply Wheelz up to $45.0 million over five years
  (2.5-year weighted average life) at interest rates up to 13%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- The registrant further agreed to sublease vehicles to the buyer to continue operating
  Advantage for a period no longer than two years from the closing date, and stated it
  would have "significant continuing involvement" in the operations of the disposed
  business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
- Because of that continuing involvement, the operating results of the Advantage business
  continued to be classified within continuing operations for all periods presented, and
  Simply Wheelz — previously a Guarantor Subsidiary of the registrant's debt — was removed
  as a guarantor upon the divestiture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed 3–5-year window, the Advantage brand business acquired in April 2009 was
  not retained: it was divested on December 12, 2012 at a realized pre-tax loss of about
  $31.4 million, for roughly $16.0 million plus ~$3.6 million of current assets, as a
  regulatory condition of a larger acquisition, and the buyer required continuing fleet
  financing, vehicle subleasing, and financial support from the registrant — leaving the
  registrant with "significant continuing involvement" rather than a clean separation.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-13-000457, Form 10-K (FY2012)]
