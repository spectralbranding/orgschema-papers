# Structural sub-dossier — CF Corporation business combination with Fidelity & Guaranty Life (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- CF Corporation ("CF Corp.") was a blank check company incorporated in the Cayman
  Islands on February 26, 2016 for the purpose of effecting a merger, capital share
  exchange, asset acquisition, share purchase, reorganization or similar business
  combination with one or more businesses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- CF Corp. consummated its initial public offering of units on May 25, 2016 (60,000,000
  units), followed by an underwriters' overallotment exercise on June 29, 2016 (9,000,000
  additional units), with approximately $691 million of the net proceeds placed in a
  trust account. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- On May 24, 2017, CF Corp., FGL US Holdings Inc. ("Parent," an indirect wholly owned
  subsidiary of CF Corp.), FGL Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub," a direct wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent) and Fidelity & Guaranty Life ("FGL," a Delaware corporation)
  entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger under which Merger Sub would merge with
  and into FGL, with FGL surviving as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of CF Corp.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- FGL was a Des Moines, Iowa-based provider of primarily fixed indexed annuity products,
  multi-year guaranty annuity products and index universal life products, with common
  stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "FGL"; approximately 80%
  of FGL's outstanding common stock was owned indirectly by HRG Group, Inc. (NYSE: HRG).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- CF Corp. agreed to pay an aggregate purchase price of approximately $1.84 billion, plus
  assumption or refinancing of approximately $405 million of existing FGL debt; each
  outstanding share of FGL common stock would be converted into the right to receive
  $31.10 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- In a related transaction under a separate Share Purchase Agreement dated May 24, 2017,
  Parent agreed to purchase all outstanding shares of FS Re ("FSR"), a life and annuity
  reinsurance services provider, from an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of HRG for $65
  million in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- The combined entity was to be renamed "FGL Holdings" at closing, replacing "CF
  Corporation." [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- EDGAR's registrant record for CIK 0001668428 confirms the entity operated under the
  name "CF Corp" from 2016-03-09 through 2017-11-20, consistent with the closing and
  renaming occurring in November 2017. [SRC: https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001668428.json]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing and what did not, by tier
- Controlling intent / legal vehicle: FGL's approximately 80%-owning parent, HRG, and its
  subsidiary FS Holdco II Ltd. ("FS Holdco") delivered a written consent approving the
  Merger Agreement; the acquiring vehicle was a special purpose acquisition company (CF
  Corp.) that itself converted from a cash shell into the operating holding company by
  virtue of the combination, taking on FGL's name. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product: the annuity and life-insurance product lines FGL had
  underwritten (fixed indexed annuities, multi-year guaranty annuities, index universal
  life) crossed into the new structure as FGL continued as the surviving, indirect wholly
  owned operating subsidiary. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- People / management: following the closing, FGL was to "continue to be led by certain
  members of its current management team"; FGL's then-current directors were to resign
  at closing, while Christopher J. Littlefield (FGL's President since October 2014 and
  CEO since May 2015), Eric L. Marhoun and Dennis R. Vigneau — each then-current members
  of FGL's management team — were to join CF Corp. as executive officers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- CF Corp. committed that, for twelve months following closing, each FGL employee would
  retain base salary, bonus and long-term-incentive opportunities and benefits no less
  favorable than immediately prior to closing, and would receive severance no less
  favorable than under FGL's pre-closing severance policies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- CF Corp.'s own controlling/sponsor layer: officers Chinh Chu and William P. Foley II
  (a private-equity-oriented CF Corp. management team, distinct from FGL's operating
  management) were to serve as Co-Executive Chairmen post-closing, sitting above the
  continuing FGL operating management. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- Operational/asset-management processes: FGL's insurance subsidiary, Fidelity &
  Guaranty Life Insurance Company ("FGLIC"), agreed to enter into a new Investment
  Management Agreement, subject to regulatory approval, appointing a newly formed,
  wholly owned subsidiary of Blackstone as investment manager of FGL's general account,
  with discretionary authority over the account's investment and reinvestment and a
  management fee of 0.30% per annum of average month-end net asset value. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- FGL agreed, prior to closing, to conduct its business in the ordinary course
  consistent with past practice, to use reasonable best efforts to preserve intact its
  business organization, keep available the services of its current officers, employees
  and consultants, and preserve its relationships with customers, reinsurers, agents and
  service providers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- Financing: CF Corp. obtained a debt commitment letter, dated as of May 31, 2017 (as
  amended and restated), for up to an aggregate principal amount of $425 million from
  Royal Bank of Canada, RBC Capital Markets and Bank of America / BofA Merrill Lynch, to
  help fund the cash consideration and refinance existing FGL debt. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- Public-shareholder mechanics of the acquiring vehicle: as a Cayman Islands blank check
  company, CF Corp.'s charter gave its public shareholders the right to redeem their
  public shares for a pro rata share of the trust account (approximately $10.02 per
  share as of March 31, 2017) in connection with the closing, independent of how they
  voted on the Business Combination Proposal. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the transaction at closing
- The Investment Management Agreement described above is the principal closing-time
  arrangement bridging a shared operational substrate: it replaces whatever
  asset-management arrangement predated the transaction with a newly created,
  sponsor-affiliated (Blackstone) investment-management function for FGL's general
  account, rather than an intact pre-existing function transferring unchanged. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- HRG, FS Holdco, CF Corp. and Parent separately agreed that FS Holdco could elect a
  joint Section 338(h)(10) tax election with respect to the business combination and the
  deemed purchases of FGL's subsidiaries, with cross-payment obligations between FS
  Holdco and Parent depending on the resulting tax cost or savings to FGL. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger Agreement's employee-matters provisions (twelve-month compensation and
  benefits protection, service-credit recognition, and pre-existing-condition/deductible
  carryover) function as the transitional bridge for the people/organization tier,
  contractually preserving pre-closing terms for FGL employees for a fixed post-closing
  period rather than leaving continuity unaddressed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined a cash-shell acquisition vehicle (CF Corp., a blank check
  company holding no operating business of its own) with an established, publicly listed
  operating insurer (FGL) via a reverse-merger-style structure: FGL's customer-facing
  product lines and much of its operating management were retained and continued inside
  the renamed surviving structure, while the controlling/sponsor layer (Chu, Foley, and
  CF Corp.'s board) and a newly created, sponsor-affiliated investment-management
  arrangement (Blackstone) were layered on top of the continuing FGL operating
  organization, bridged contractually through the Investment Management Agreement and
  the Merger Agreement's employee-matters provisions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001571049-17-006940, Form DEFM14A]
