# Structural sub-dossier — MPG Office Trust disposition and new joint venture with a Beacon Capital Partners affiliate (closing structure)

## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn document is a Form 8-K furnished under Item 7.01 (Regulation FD), reporting
  that on March 30, 2012 MPG Office Trust, Inc. issued a press release announcing that
  the previously announced transactions between the Company, Charter Hall Office REIT and
  affiliates of Beacon Capital Partners, LLC had been completed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Item 7.01]
- The 8-K states net proceeds from the transactions to the Company are expected to total
  approximately $44 million, to be used for general corporate purposes; the press release
  is attached as Exhibit 99.1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Item 7.01 and Exhibit 99.1]
- The completion press release is titled "MPG OFFICE TRUST COMPLETES DISPOSITION OF
  NON-CORE ASSETS and ENTERS INTO NEW JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT," dated March 30, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Parties and transaction type as structured
- The transaction terms were set by a Framework Agreement dated October 28, 2011 among
  MPG Office, L.P. (with affiliate entities, the "Company"), Macquarie Office II LLC
  ("Charter Hall Office REIT"), and BCSP VI Portfolio Acquisition LLC (an affiliate of
  Beacon Capital Partners, LLC, "Beacon"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01 and Exhibit 99.1]
- The structure combined (a) a disposition of non-core assets and (b) entry into a new
  joint venture agreement between the Company and a Beacon affiliate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The mechanism: Charter Hall Office REIT transferred its 80% interest in Maguire
  Macquarie Office, LLC (the existing "joint venture") to Beacon, with the Company's
  consent, and the Framework Agreement specified the transactions occurring on the
  closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing vs. what was retained
- Property/asset tier — crossed to Beacon affiliates: at closing the existing joint
  venture sold its interests in Wells Fargo Center (Denver, Colorado) and San Diego Tech
  Center (San Diego, California); the Company additionally sold its development rights and
  an adjacent land parcel at San Diego Tech Center. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The selling and buying legal vehicles were named as Maguire Properties–Denver Center,
  LLC / BCSP Denver Property LLC and Maguire Properties–San Diego Tech Center, LLC / BCSP
  SDTC Property LLC in the purchase and sale agreements filed as Exhibits 99.2 and 99.3. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 9.01 exhibit list]
- Property/asset tier — retained in the new joint venture: the new joint venture between
  the Company and the Beacon affiliate continued to own interests in One California Plaza
  (Downtown Los Angeles), Cerritos Corporate Center (Cerritos, California), and Stadium
  Gateway (Anaheim, California), unless Stadium Gateway is sold in an ongoing marketing
  effort. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- Ownership interest retained: the Company continued to maintain a 20% interest in the
  new joint venture following the closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The Company also received a lump-sum payment in consideration for terminating its right
  to receive certain fees following the closing date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Operating processes and people tier (what was NOT re-assigned at closing)
- The Company remained the operator: it is described at closing as "the largest owner and
  operator of Class A office properties in the Los Angeles central business district ...
  a full-service real estate company with substantial in-house expertise and resources in
  property management, marketing, leasing and financing." [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The completion 8-K was signed by David L. Weinstein, President and Chief Executive
  Officer of MPG Office Trust; no transfer of the Company's management organization to a
  counterparty is described in the closing documents. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K signature block]
- The transferred stake was Charter Hall Office REIT's 80% joint-venture interest moving
  to Beacon; the Company's continuing 20% interest and operating relationship in the
  retained properties persisted through the restructuring rather than moving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- The new joint venture agreement provides for a three-year lockout period, during which
  neither partner has the right to exercise the marketing rights under the joint venture
  agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]
- The Framework Agreement also requires the Company, Charter Hall Office REIT and Beacon
  to jointly market Stadium Gateway for sale to third parties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Closing was subject to customary closing conditions, including obtaining lender and
  other third-party consents; the transactions were expected to close in the first
  quarter of 2012 and were reported completed on March 30, 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing an outside party's controlling (80%) stake in an existing property joint
  venture was transferred to a new partner (Beacon), two properties and certain
  development rights were sold out of the structure, and the Company retained a 20%
  interest in a newly documented joint venture over three remaining Class A office
  properties while continuing as the in-house owner/operator, with a three-year marketing
  lockout and a joint-marketing obligation governing the retained substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-12-000018, Form 8-K Exhibit 99.1] [SRC: SEC accession 0001204560-11-000089, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
