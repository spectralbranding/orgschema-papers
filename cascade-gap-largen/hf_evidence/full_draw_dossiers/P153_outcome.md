## Outcome window and source
- The fixed outcome record is the registrant's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  December 31, 2020, filed February 16, 2021. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020 cover]

## Status of the acquired plants within the window (retention / disruption)
- As of the FY2020 annual report the three plants acquired in September 2016 remained in the
  registrant's owned ethanol-plant portfolio: Madison, Illinois (90 million gallons/year),
  Mount Vernon, Indiana (90 mgy) and York, Nebraska (50 mgy), each listed with a September
  2016 acquisition date and Vogelbusch process technology. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020, properties/plant table]
- The Mount Vernon, Indiana facility was designated (with Obion, Tennessee) for construction
  and deployment of the registrant's Ultra-High Protein technology, indicating continued
  investment in rather than divestiture of that acquired plant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
- Within the window the registrant divested OTHER, separately acquired plants — the
  Hereford, Texas plant (December 2020) and an agreement dated January 25, 2021 to sell the
  Ord, Nebraska plant (65 mgy nameplate) to GreenAmerica Biofuels Ord LLC — none of which
  are the three 2016 Abengoa-sourced plants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
- The registrant reported no material impairments of its long-lived assets (which consist of
  property, plant and equipment) in the reviewed disclosure; the FY2020 goodwill impairment
  and loss on sale of assets are not attributed in the filing to the three 2016-acquired
  plants specifically. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020, impairment discussion]

## Best-documented outcome scalar (consolidated)
- Net loss attributable to Green Plains for fiscal year 2020 was $49.6 million (reported as
  $(49,630) thousand), following net losses attributable to Green Plains of $(34,486)
  thousand (FY2019), $(8,214) thousand (FY2018) and $(16,445) thousand (FY2017). [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020, selected financial data]
- FY2020 results included a $24.1 million noncash goodwill impairment charge and a $22.4
  million loss on sale of assets, net, at the consolidated level. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020, adjusted-EBITDA reconciliation]
- Management described a "continued sustained period of unprofitable operations" and stated
  liquidity was expected to improve from the announced January 2021 sale of the Ord,
  Nebraska plant. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020, liquidity discussion]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~2019-2021 window the registrant remained a public, going-concern SEC filer; the
  three ethanol plants acquired in the September 2016 transaction were retained and operating
  (with the Mount Vernon plant targeted for new protein-technology investment) and not
  impaired or divested within the window, even as the registrant recorded consolidated net
  losses each year (FY2020 net loss attributable to Green Plains of $49.6 million, alongside
  a $24.1 million goodwill impairment) and sold other, unrelated plants. [SRC: SEC accession 0001309402-21-000014, Form 10-K FY2020]
