# Outcome sub-dossier — WABCO Holdings Inc. realized record, fixed ~2010–2012 window

> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window is fixed at the registrant's

> fiscal year ended December 31, 2011. This sub-dossier contains no information about how

## Public-reporting status in the outcome window
- The registrant continued as a stand-alone SEC-reporting public company through the outcome
  window, filing an annual report on Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2011,
  with common stock listed on the New York Stock Exchange; principal executive offices in
  Brussels, Belgium and Piscataway, New Jersey. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]

## Best-documented realized outcome scalars
- Sales for 2011 were $2.8 billion, an increase of 28.4% (22.3% excluding foreign-currency
  translation) from $2.2 billion in 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]
- The registrant recorded net income including noncontrolling interests of $368.2 million for
  2011, compared with a net loss including noncontrolling interests of $214.2 million for
  2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]
- The 2010 net loss was driven by an expense of $400.4 million for an EC Fine indemnification,
  which constituted the majority of $402.6 million of other non-operating expense in 2010;
  absent that expense in 2011, other non-operating results were income. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]

## Documented cross-boundary disruption over time
- The EC Fine indemnification arose from a European Commission proceeding against American
  Standard and certain European subsidiaries engaged in the Bath and Kitchen business for
  alleged infringements of EU competition rules on the distribution of bathroom fixtures — a
  legacy matter of the former parent's other business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]
- Pursuant to the Indemnification and Cooperation Agreement, WABCO Europe BVBA (an indirect
  wholly-owned subsidiary of the registrant) was responsible for, and indemnified, American
  Standard (renamed Trane) and Ideal Standard International for this liability; the realized
  charge landed on the separated registrant rather than the businesses to which the conduct
  related. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]

## Operations and organization in the window
- The registrant maintained an integrated engineering and manufacturing organization, with
  approximately 1,557 employees dedicated to developing new products, components and
  manufacturing processes as of the FY2011 report. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]
- Customer concentration was documented (Daimler ~12% of 2011 sales; Volvo ~11%; top ten
  customers ~52%), with Europe representing approximately 62% of 2011 sales; no divestiture or
  break-up of the core business is reported in the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed outcome window the separated business operated as a going concern at global
  scale, growing sales to $2.8 billion in 2011 and returning to positive net income ($368.2
  million) after a 2010 net loss ($214.2 million) that was driven principally by a $400.4
  million indemnification for a legacy liability of the former parent's Bath and Kitchen
  business absorbed through the closing Indemnification and Cooperation Agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001445305-12-000350, Form 10-K FY2011]
