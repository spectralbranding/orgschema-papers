## Transaction and parties at closing
- On April 23, 2018, Mitel Networks Corporation, a Canadian corporation, entered into an Arrangement Agreement with MLN AcquisitionCo ULC, a British Columbia unlimited liability company ("Purchaser"), and MLN TopCo Ltd., a Cayman Islands exempted company ("Parent"), each formed by funds advised by Searchlight Capital Partners, L.P., providing for Purchaser to acquire all outstanding common shares of the Company pursuant to a plan of arrangement to be approved by the Ontario Superior Court of Justice under Section 192 of the Canada Business Corporations Act (CBCA). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- Purchaser and Parent were newly formed entities that had not engaged in any business except in furtherance of the arrangement; Purchaser was a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, and Parent was owned and controlled by funds affiliated with Searchlight, a private equity investment group, together with certain co-investors (the "sponsors"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- Holders of Mitel common shares were entitled to receive $11.15 in cash per share (without interest, less withholding taxes); outstanding options, RSUs, and PSUs were to be cashed out on a corresponding basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- As a result of the arrangement, Mitel would become a privately held corporation; all its equity would be indirectly held through Parent by the sponsors, who would be the sole beneficiaries of future earnings/growth and the sole persons entitled to vote on corporate matters. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- The arrangement was completed on November 30, 2018 (the "Closing Date"), effective at 9:30 a.m. Eastern time; the total funds used by Parent to complete the arrangement, including repayment of the Company's net debt, were approximately $2.0 billion, funded through a combination of new debt financing, sponsor equity commitments, and Company/subsidiary cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
- Following the Effective Time, Mitel was continued under the Business Corporations Act (British Columbia) and amalgamated with Purchaser, with the resulting entity ("Amalco") adopting Purchaser's articles. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]

## The acquired business (customer-facing product/asset tier)
- Mitel described itself as a global provider of cloud and On-Site business communications and collaboration solutions, serving more than 70 million end users including more than a million cloud-based Unified Communications as a Service subscribers; On-Site solutions were generally sold for a fixed upfront fee with maintenance-driven recurring revenue, while cloud-based solutions were sold under a recurring monthly-fee billing model with three-to-five-year initial contracts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- The whole company and its product lines (both On-Site and cloud) crossed to the acquirer as a single, intact operating entity via the share acquisition; the drawn filing does not describe any carve-out, spin-off, or partial-asset transfer — 100% of outstanding common shares were acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]

## Board recommendation and stated rationale (controlling-intent / business-model context)
- The Board's stated reasons for recommending the arrangement included increasing competition and consolidation in the unified communications industry against larger competitors, and risks associated with executing Mitel's strategy as an independent public company, including "the uncertainty and volatility related to the transition from premise to cloud and the integration of ShoreTel's businesses with Mitel's businesses." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- The $11.15 per-share consideration represented a 9.7% premium over the closing price on the date the arrangement agreement was signed and approximately 24% over the 90-day volume-weighted average price prior to signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]

## Financing structure at closing (legal-vehicle tier)
- The proxy statement disclosed anticipated debt financing of up to $1.38 billion plus a committed $100 million revolving facility, and equity commitments from the sponsors of up to $700 million; completion of the arrangement was not conditioned on Parent/Purchaser's receipt of financing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, "the Borrower" (MLN US HoldCo LLC, a Delaware LLC) entered a First Lien Credit Agreement providing a $1,120.0 million first lien term loan (7-year maturity) and a $100.0 million revolving credit facility (5-year maturity), and a Second Lien Credit Agreement providing a $260.0 million second lien term loan (7-year maturity), each with Credit Suisse AG, Cayman Islands Branch as administrative agent; proceeds were used to fund the acquisition, including repayment in full of Mitel's pre-existing credit facility. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339026, Form 8-K]
- The closing legal structure comprised MLN TopCo Ltd. ("Holdings"), MLN UK HoldCo Limited ("Intermediate Holdings," a private limited company incorporated in England and Wales), MLN US TopCo Inc. ("U.S. Holdings," Delaware), and MLN US HoldCo LLC ("the Borrower," Delaware), with the credit facility obligations guaranteed by Holdings, Intermediate Holdings, and U.S. Holdings and secured by pledges of capital stock at each tier plus substantially all Borrower/subsidiary assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339026, Form 8-K]

## What did NOT move intact: governance/board tier vs. operating-management tier
- In connection with completion of the arrangement, all eight then-serving directors (Richard D. McBee, Terence H. Matthews, Benjamin H. Ball, Martha H. Bejar, Peter D. Charbonneau, John P. McHugh, Sudhakar Ramakrishna, and David M. Williams) resigned from the board; following the amalgamation, three individuals (Gregory Hiscock, Stavros Pethakas, Steven Spooner) were appointed as the new directors of Amalco — a complete board turnover at the governance tier. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
- By contrast, at the operating-management tier: each of the named executive officers (Richard D. McBee, Steven E. Spooner, Graham Bevington, Todd Abbott, and Robert D. Agnes) resigned his officer title with "the Company" but immediately continued to be employed by the Company or an affiliate under an amended and restated employment agreement "providing for substantially the same terms and conditions of employment as in effect prior to the completion of the Arrangement," with only limited changes (a prorated-bonus provision and accelerated RSU-related payment vesting on qualifying termination). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
- Employment agreements for these officers separately provided change-of-control severance protection (12 to 24 months' salary and bonus continuation depending on the executive, plus full acceleration of unvested equity) triggered only if employment were terminated without cause or for good reason within 12 months of the change of control; aggregate potential cash severance for the three then-most-recently-disclosed named executive officers was estimated at approximately $1,377,791, contingent on a qualifying termination that the drawn filing does not report as having occurred at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]
- The drawn filing and the closing 8-Ks contain no transition-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or comparable bridging instrument; this is consistent with the transaction being a whole-company share acquisition (not a carve-out or spin-off) in which the acquired entity's own operating substrate crossed intact, while only the outer holding/governance layer and financing structure were newly imposed. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A]

## Legal-vehicle and listing changes at closing
- On the Closing Date, Mitel notified the Nasdaq and the Toronto Stock Exchange (TSX) that the articles of arrangement had been filed and a certificate of arrangement issued; Mitel requested that both exchanges remove the common shares from listing after market close on November 30, 2018, with trading on the TSX ceasing that day and trading on the Nasdaq suspended prior to the opening on December 3, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
- Mitel requested that Nasdaq file a Form 25 notification of removal from listing and registration to effect removal from trading and deregistration of the common shares under Section 12(b) of the Exchange Act; it was intended that Amalco would separately file a Form 15 to terminate registration under Section 12(g) and suspend reporting obligations under Sections 13 and 15(d). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
- At the Effective Time, holders of common shares ceased to have any rights as shareholders other than the right to receive the cash consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, all-cash acquisition of 100% of Mitel's outstanding common shares by a newly formed acquisition vehicle controlled by Searchlight-affiliated funds, structured as a Canadian plan of arrangement and closed with approximately $1.48 billion of new first- and second-lien debt plus sponsor equity layered atop a new multi-tier holding structure (Holdings/Intermediate Holdings/U.S. Holdings/Borrower). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-187310, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339026, Form 8-K]
- The acquired business — both its customer-facing product lines (On-Site and cloud) and its operating management — crossed to the new ownership as a single intact unit with no disclosed carve-out or transition-services bridge; the discontinuity at closing was confined to the outer governance tier, where the entire pre-closing board resigned and was replaced, while the operating executives continued in place under employment agreements on substantially unchanged terms. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-339023, Form 8-K]
