## Realized financial trajectory (fixed outcome window: fiscal years 2010–2012)
- Net sales grew across the window: $2,884,308 thousand (2010), $3,069,356 thousand
  (2011) and $3,266,739 thousand (2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY ended 2012-12-31, Selected Financial Data]
- Net income grew across the window: $98,523 thousand (2010), $123,070 thousand (2011)
  and $130,675 thousand (2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- The five-year Selected Financial Data reports asset impairments of zero for each of
  2009 through 2012, in contrast to a large asset-impairment charge of approximately
  $3.14 billion reported for 2008. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012, Selected Financial Data]
- HSNi employed approximately 6,700 employees as of early 2013; no employees were
  represented by unions. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]

## Realized business/segment structure over time
- The company continued to operate through two operating segments, HSN and Cornerstone;
  both were retained through the window with no whole-company divestiture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- HSN segment net sales were $2,115,918 thousand (2010), $2,160,341 thousand (2011) and
  $2,265,026 thousand (2012); HSN net sales increased 2% in 2011 and 5% in 2012, driven
  by growth in home design and related categories. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- Cornerstone segment net sales were $768,390 thousand (2010), $909,015 thousand (2011)
  and $1,001,713 thousand (2012); Cornerstone net sales rose 18% in 2011 and 10% in 2012. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]

## Documented portfolio adjustments within the window
- Cornerstone acquired Chasing Fireflies, a direct-to-consumer children's and family
  lifestyle brand, in April 2012, and divested Smith+Noble (window treatments) in May
  2012 and The Territory Ahead (casual apparel) in July 2012, receiving $6.6 million in
  proceeds from the two divestitures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- As reported in this filing, the Cornerstone portfolio comprised Ballard Designs,
  Chasing Fireflies, Frontgate, Garnet Hill, Grandin Road, Improvements and TravelSmith. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- Capital expenditures were primarily at the HSN segment and were directed to information
  and digital technology and warehouse improvements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Over the ~3–5 years after the separation (fiscal 2010–2012) the standalone company
  recorded consecutive years of rising net sales and rising net income, with no asset
  impairments in the window, both operating segments retained and growing, and only
  small brand-level portfolio adjustments within the Cornerstone segment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
- Best-documented outcome scalar: net income of $130,675 thousand (FY2012), up from
  $123,070 thousand (FY2011) and $98,523 thousand (FY2010), on net sales of
  $3,266,739 thousand (FY2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0001434729-13-000004, Form 10-K FY2012]
