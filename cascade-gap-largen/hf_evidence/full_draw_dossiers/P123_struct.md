## The drawn filing — what it does and does not establish
- The drawn current report is not the transaction's deal-terms document. It reports (i) entry on April 26, 2014 into a Third Amendment to the Agreement of Limited Partnership of the registrant's operating partnership (Griffin-American Healthcare REIT II Holdings, LP), amending the calculation of distributions payable to the registrant's external advisor upon a sale of partnership assets, a listing event, termination of the advisory agreement, or a merger; and (ii) a Certificate of Correction filed May 1, 2014 to the registrant's charter correcting the wording of a defined term. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000027, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 5.03]
- It establishes that the registrant is externally managed: it acts as sole general partner of its operating partnership, and subordinated distributions run to Griffin-American Healthcare REIT Advisor, LLC (the "Advisor"), acknowledged and agreed by the Advisor. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000027, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On August 5, 2014 the registrant (Griffin-American Healthcare REIT II, Inc.) and its operating partnership entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger with NorthStar Realty Finance Corp. ("Parent"), NRF Healthcare Subsidiary, LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, "Merger Sub"), and NRF OP Healthcare Subsidiary, LLC (a wholly owned subsidiary of Merger Sub, "Partnership Merger Sub"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-294736, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The transaction is structured as a whole-company merger: the registrant will merge with and into Merger Sub, with the registrant's separate corporate existence ceasing; and the Partnership Merger Sub will merge with and into the operating partnership, with the operating partnership surviving. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-294736, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Merger consideration per share of registrant common stock: $7.75 in cash plus stock consideration in Parent common stock equal to $3.75 divided by the Average Parent Closing Price, with the exchange ratio collared between 0.2344 and 0.1859 shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-294736, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The merger was not subject to any financing condition; termination provisions set a $102 million termination fee payable by the registrant, a $153 million termination payment payable by Parent for failure to consummate, and a $35 million alternative termination payment. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-294736, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Registrant stockholders approved the merger at a Special Meeting reported November 28, 2014; as of the October 14, 2014 record date there were 293,399,469.342 shares of common stock entitled to vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000106, Form 8-K]

## The customer-facing asset that crossed the ownership boundary
- The acquired business is a diversified healthcare real-estate portfolio operating through five reportable business segments: medical office buildings, hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, senior housing, and senior housing-RIDEA. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
- As of June 30, 2014 the registrant had completed 75 acquisitions comprising 289 buildings and approximately 11,277,000 square feet of gross leasable area. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]

## The managing organization tier — what did NOT transfer as an intact operating substrate
- The registrant has no employees; its executive officers are all employees of affiliates of the Advisor. Effective January 7, 2012 the registrant became externally advised by Griffin-American Healthcare REIT Advisor, LLC, sub-advised by an entity jointly owned by American Healthcare Investors LLC and Griffin Capital Corporation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
- The acquirer (NorthStar Realty Finance Corp.) is itself externally managed and advised by an affiliate of NSAM (NorthStar Asset Management Group Inc.), effective June 30, 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
- Upon the merger the registrant's advisory relationship is terminated rather than assumed: the sub-advisor (jointly owned by American Healthcare Investors and Griffin Capital, entities in which the registrant's own executive officers and directors hold interests) is entitled to a Merger Termination Amount, estimated at approximately $43,730,632. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
- The transaction is described as expanding the acquirer's relationships with tenants and operators within the registrant's existing healthcare network. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]

## Bridging of the shared substrate at closing
- The reviewed closing-era filings describe termination of the registrant's external advisory agreement with a contractual Merger Termination Amount, not a transition-services or continuing-management agreement carrying the registrant's advisor/sub-advisor forward. [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
- Whether any successor property- or asset-management arrangement was put in place to run the acquired portfolio after closing is not established in the reviewed filings. [UNVERIFIED]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company merger absorbed an externally advised, employee-less healthcare-real-estate portfolio (289 buildings; ~11,277,000 sq ft; five property segments) into an also-externally-managed acquirer for cash-plus-stock consideration, with the target's external advisory organization terminated and paid a contractual termination amount rather than transferred alongside the assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-294736, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001455271-14-000076, DEFM14A]
