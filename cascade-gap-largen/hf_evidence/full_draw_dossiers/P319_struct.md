# Structural sub-dossier — CalAtlantic Group, Inc. acquisition by Lennar Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties
- On October 29, 2017, CalAtlantic Group, Inc. ("CalAtlantic") entered into an
  Agreement and Plan of Merger with Lennar Corporation ("Lennar") and Cheetah Cub Group
  Corp., a newly formed Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Lennar
  ("Merger Sub"), under which CalAtlantic would merge with and into Merger Sub, with
  Merger Sub continuing as the surviving corporation, a wholly owned subsidiary of
  Lennar, renamed "CalAtlantic Group, Inc." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A cover letter / "The Merger (See Page 38)"]
- CalAtlantic, headquartered in Arlington, Virginia, built homes across the homebuilding
  spectrum from entry level to luxury in over 40 metropolitan statistical areas spanning
  17 states, and also provided mortgage, title and escrow services; its common stock
  traded on the NYSE under the symbol "CAA." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Information About CalAtlantic (See Page 103)"]
- Each outstanding share of CalAtlantic common stock (other than excluded shares) would
  convert into the right to receive 0.885 shares of Lennar Class A common stock and
  0.0177 shares of Lennar Class B common stock, with an option to elect $48.26 per share
  in cash instead, subject to an aggregate cash cap of $1,162,250,000 (limiting cash
  elections to 24,083,091 shares); the merger consideration was described as intended
  to qualify as a tax-free reorganization to the extent stock was received. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A cover letter]
- Upon completion, former Lennar Class A and Class B stockholders together would own
  approximately 74% of the combined company and former CalAtlantic stockholders
  approximately 26%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Q&A" p.6]
- The transaction was described by the companies as creating "the largest homebuilder
  in the United States" and expected to generate operating and administrative cost
  savings, projected at approximately $100 million in fiscal year 2018 and $365 million
  per year in fiscal year 2019 and subsequent years, attributed in part to "compatible
  product strategies" between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A cover letter] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "CalAtlantic Board Recommendation and Its Reasons for the Merger"]

## Financing structure
- Completion of the Merger was not conditioned on Lennar obtaining financing; in
  anticipation of cash-election payments, Lennar had already sold $300 million of 2.95%
  Senior Notes due 2020 and $900 million of 4.75% Senior Notes due 2027 in a private
  offering that closed November 29, 2017, with the intent to use the net proceeds to
  fund the cash consideration, merger-related expenses, and general corporate purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Financing Related to the Merger (See Page 83)"]

## What crossed at closing, per tier
- Legal vehicle: CalAtlantic's entire corporate structure merged with and into Merger
  Sub, which succeeded to "all the Company's assets, liabilities and operations" — the
  whole entity, not a carved-out business unit, was the transaction's subject; the
  filing does not describe a transitional-services or shared-services agreement
  bridging a divided operating substrate, consistent with a whole-company transfer
  rather than a partial separation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042625, Form 8-K Item 5.01]
- Customer-facing product/asset: CalAtlantic's homebuilding business (entry-level
  through luxury homes across its metropolitan markets) together with its mortgage,
  title and escrow services transferred in full as part of the merged entity; no
  carve-out of specific product lines or geographies from the transaction is described. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Information About CalAtlantic"]
- Operational processes: the filing frames much of the transaction's expected benefit
  as depending on Lennar's ability to apply "Lennar's homebuilding techniques" and cost
  saving methods to CalAtlantic's operations following completion, rather than
  CalAtlantic's processes continuing unchanged under Lennar ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Reasons for the Merger — Negative Aspects"]
- People/organization: from the effective time until November 30, 2018, Lennar
  committed to provide continuing CalAtlantic employees compensation and benefits "not
  less favorable in aggregate" than pre-closing (or comparable to similarly situated
  Lennar employees), service credit for benefit eligibility/vesting, and honoring of
  existing change-in-control and severance agreements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Employee Benefits Matters"]
- Senior management/controlling intent: in an early-stage conversation described in the
  "Background of the Merger" narrative, CalAtlantic's CEO understood from Lennar's
  representative that Lennar anticipated "there would be no role for CalAtlantic's
  senior management team with the combined company." [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Background of the Merger"]
- Consistent with that understanding, the disclosed change-in-control/severance
  arrangements for CalAtlantic's named executive officers (CEO, Executive Chairman, and
  others) were quantified on the assumption that each executive officer would be
  terminated without "cause" or resign for "good reason" in connection with the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "Interests of CalAtlantic Directors and Executive Officers in the Merger — Certain Assumptions"]
- One CalAtlantic director, Scott Stowell, was disclosed as becoming a director of
  Lennar following the Merger; the filing did not disclose other CalAtlantic directors
  or senior officers continuing on the combined company's board or executive team. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "The MP CA Homes Voting and Cash Election Agreement"]

## Closing conditions and termination fees
- If the merger agreement were terminated due to a change of board recommendation or
  acceptance of a superior proposal, the party in that position would owe the other a
  termination fee of $178.7 million; a party whose stockholders failed to approve the
  necessary proposals would instead owe expense reimbursement of up to $30.0 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "What will happen if the Merger is not completed?"]

## Stockholder approval and completion
- CalAtlantic's board of directors unanimously approved the merger agreement on October
  29, 2017 and recommended stockholders vote to adopt it. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A "CalAtlantic Board Recommendation"]
- On February 12, 2018, the Merger was completed: CalAtlantic was acquired by Lennar,
  merging with and into Merger Sub, with Merger Sub continuing as the surviving
  corporation and a subsidiary of Lennar; as a result, Merger Sub succeeded to all of
  CalAtlantic's assets, liabilities and operations, giving Lennar sole control. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042625, Form 8-K Item 2.01 / Item 5.01]
- Stockholders receiving cash totaled only 391,893 shares by election, but CalAtlantic's
  largest stockholder, MP CA Homes LLC, was deemed to have elected cash for the
  remaining shares up to the cap, so total merger consideration paid was 82,731,943
  shares of Lennar Class A common stock, 1,654,639 shares of Lennar Class B common
  stock, and $1,162,249,972 in cash. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042625, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- On February 12, 2018, CalAtlantic notified the NYSE that the Merger would be
  effective that day; after the close of trading, the NYSE filed a Form 25 with the SEC
  to remove CalAtlantic's common stock from listing on the exchange and withdraw its
  Section 12(b) registration, and trading of CalAtlantic common stock on the NYSE
  ceased before the opening of trading on February 13, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042625, Form 8-K Item 3.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- A whole-company, stock-and-cash merger transferred CalAtlantic's entire corporate
  entity — its homebuilding, mortgage, title and escrow business, together with all
  assets, liabilities and operations — into a Lennar-owned surviving subsidiary, with no
  disclosed carve-out or transitional-services agreement dividing the operating
  substrate. Continuity of rank-and-file employee compensation and benefits was
  contractually guaranteed through a fixed post-closing date, but CalAtlantic's own
  senior management team was understood by its own CEO, during deal negotiations, not
  to have a role in the combined company, and the disclosed executive severance
  arrangements were quantified on a termination-at-closing assumption; only one
  CalAtlantic director was disclosed as joining Lennar's board. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-003465, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-042625, Form 8-K]
