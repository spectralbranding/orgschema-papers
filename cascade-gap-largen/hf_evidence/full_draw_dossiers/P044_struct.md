# Structural sub-dossier — Rayonier performance-fibers separation into Rayonier Holding Company / SpinCo (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- Rayonier Inc. announced plans to separate its performance-fibers business from its
  forest-resources and real-estate businesses; the separation was structured as a
  spin-off of a newly formed company, Rayonier Holding Company ("SpinCo"), which would
  contain Rayonier Inc.'s Performance Fibers segment, while Rayonier Inc. continued as
  the existing publicly traded company holding forest resources and real estate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1 information statement]
- The separation was to be effected by a pro rata distribution of 100% of SpinCo's
  outstanding common stock to holders of Rayonier common shares; no shareholder vote,
  payment, or surrender of shares was required, and the distribution was intended to be
  tax-free to Rayonier shareholders. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The information statement was a preliminary document subject to completion, dated
  January 29, 2014, filed under a Registration Statement on Form 10. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at the tier level
- The customer-facing product line that separated into the standalone company was
  cellulose specialties (high-purity specialty cellulose fibers), categorized into three
  product lines — acetate, high-value, and commodity products — used in products such as
  cigarette filters, LCDs, food thickeners, pharmaceuticals, personal care, cosmetics,
  and food casings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The producing operations moved with the product: SpinCo's two production facilities,
  located in Jesup, Georgia and Fernandina Beach, Florida, with a combined annual
  production capacity of approximately 675,000 metric tons (Jesup ~520,000; Fernandina
  Beach ~155,000), were part of the separated business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The production processes crossed with the assets: the facilities utilize kraft and
  sulfite manufacturing processes, proprietary bleaching sequences and specialized cold
  caustic processes ("the SpinCo Recipe") developed over 85 years, and SpinCo owns
  numerous patents, trademarks and trade secrets and developed know-how relating to
  production of purified cellulose. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The management/people ran with the business: SpinCo's management team, led by Paul G.
  Boynton (previously an officer of Rayonier) as Chairman, President and Chief Executive
  Officer after the separation, was described as possessing deep, extensive experience in
  the performance-fibers business. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- Employees allocated to the separated business ("SpinCo allocated employees") moved with
  it; SpinCo's hourly employees are covered by collective bargaining agreements
  represented by several labor unions, and SpinCo agreed to credit prior Rayonier service
  for benefit purposes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## What did NOT transfer as an intact substrate
- Shared corporate functions historically provided by Rayonier did not cross as a
  standalone operating substrate: financial statements were prepared including allocations
  from Rayonier for medical costs of active/retired employees, workers' compensation,
  general liability and property insurance, salaried payroll costs, equity-based
  compensation, and a pro-rata share of direct corporate administration expense. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- A number of contracts were to be assigned from Rayonier to SpinCo, but many required
  counterparty consent to the assignment; if consents were not obtained, SpinCo might be
  unable to obtain some intended benefits, assets, and contractual commitments. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Contractual arrangements bridging the shared substrate at closing
- The parties would enter a separation (and distribution) agreement plus related
  agreements: a transition services agreement, a tax matters agreement, an employee
  matters agreement, and an intellectual property agreement, allocating assets and
  liabilities and governing the post-separation relationship. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The transition services agreement provided that Rayonier and SpinCo would provide each
  other, on an interim transitional basis, various services including information
  technology, accounts payable, payroll, and other financial and administrative services,
  generally terminating up to two years following the distribution (with per-service early
  termination on 30 days' notice subject to a 90-day minimum). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The employee matters agreement allocated employment, compensation and benefit
  liabilities between the companies; SpinCo agreed to maintain welfare arrangements
  substantially similar in aggregate through at least December 31, 2014 and certain
  retirement/severance arrangements through at least December 31, 2015, and to establish
  its own tax-qualified pension and savings plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The intellectual property agreement licensed SpinCo to use specified "Rayonier"
  trademarks and corporate names in a limited field of use relating to SpinCo's business
  (with the trademark license terminating automatically if the Rayonier name were not used
  in active commerce for 12 consecutive months), and cross-licensed certain copyrights and
  trade secrets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
- The tax matters agreement governed tax rights and obligations and restricted SpinCo, for
  a two-year period after the distribution, from actions (e.g., disposing of a substantial
  portion of its assets or stock) that could disqualify the distribution as tax-free. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction separated a vertically self-contained product operation — cellulose-
  specialties product lines together with the two producing mills, the associated
  proprietary processes/know-how/IP, and the management and employees that run them — from
  Rayonier's shared corporate service substrate (IT, accounts payable, payroll, financial
  and administrative functions, insurance, tax, and corporate administration), with that
  shared substrate bridged at closing by a transition services agreement (up to two years)
  and by the tax, employee, and intellectual-property matters agreements rather than
  transferred whole; certain contracts required counterparty consent to assign. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-14-025522, Form 10-12B EX-99.1]
