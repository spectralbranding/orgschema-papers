# Outcome sub-dossier — MEMC / SunEdison and the SMP Ltd. polysilicon joint venture, realized ~3–5-year record (window ~2014–2016)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. The outcome window was fixed at construction as the
> registrant's (MEMC Electronic Materials, later renamed SunEdison, Inc.) record roughly
> three to five years after the ~2011 closing. This sub-dossier contains no information
> about how the transaction was structured at closing.

## Realized operating record of the joint venture
- The joint venture was formed as SMP Ltd. ("SMP") with Samsung Fine Chemicals Co. Ltd.
  ("SFC") for the construction and operation of a new facility in Ulsan, South Korea to
  produce high-purity polysilicon, expected to have an initial annual production capacity
  of approximately 10,000 metric tons. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- In September 2011 the registrant executed a Supply and License Agreement with SMP under
  which it licenses and sells to SMP certain technology and related equipment used for
  producing polysilicon, and it received proceeds based on milestones achieved throughout
  the construction, installation, and testing of the equipment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- Construction of the SMP polysilicon facility was recently completed as of the FY2014
  annual report; the facility was "in the initial stages of polysilicon production" but
  had "not reached full commercial capabilities at this time," and SunEdison Semiconductor
  Ltd. ("SSL") was "not yet purchasing polysilicon from the facility."
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]

## Realized ownership restructuring of the joint venture
- Prior to May 28, 2014, the registrant's ownership interest in SMP was 50% and SFC owned
  the other 50%. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- On May 28, 2014, the registrant acquired from SFC an approximate 35% interest in SMP for
  a cash purchase price of $140.7 million ($71.2 million net of cash acquired); on a
  consolidated basis it then owned an approximate 85% interest in SMP, effectively
  controlled SMP's operations, and consolidated SMP's results from May 28, 2014 onward.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- The acquired interest was contributed to SunEdison Semiconductor Ltd. (SSL) in connection
  with SSL's initial public offering; SSL held an approximate 35% interest in SMP Ltd.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)]

## Realized financial record of the registrant (best-documented scalar)
- The registrant reported a net loss attributable to SunEdison stockholders of $1,180.4
  million for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2014 (compared with $586.7 million for
  2013 and $150.6 million for 2012). [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014), Exhibit 13]

## Realized disruption and terminal events
- On February 16, 2016, the registrant's Board approved a restructuring of its Solar
  Materials operations that included closing its granular polysilicon production facility
  in Pasadena, Texas and selling the assets of its Kuching, Malaysia wafering facility
  (a restructuring of the registrant's own polysilicon/materials operations, distinct from
  the SMP joint-venture facility). [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-16-000321, Form 8-K]
- On April 21, 2016, SunEdison, Inc. and certain of its subsidiaries filed a voluntary
  petition for relief under Chapter 11 of the United States Bankruptcy Code in the United
  States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New York. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, Form 8-K Item 1.03]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the outcome window the joint venture's Ulsan plant reached completion but only
  early-stage production (not full commercial capability, no offtake yet by the affiliated
  buyer); the venture's ownership was restructured in May 2014 (registrant buying an
  additional ~35% from SFC for $140.7 million, taking consolidated control at ~85%, then
  contributing a ~35% stake into the SSL IPO). Over the same window the registrant reported
  a $1,180.4 million FY2014 net loss, moved in February 2016 to close its Pasadena granular
  polysilicon facility and exit certain materials operations, and on April 21, 2016 filed
  for Chapter 11 bankruptcy. [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-15-000049, Form 10-K (FY2014)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000945436-16-000321, Form 8-K] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-16-559806, Form 8-K Item 1.03]
