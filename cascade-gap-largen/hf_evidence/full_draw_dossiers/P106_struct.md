# Structural sub-dossier — Nexeo Solutions public business combination (closing structure)

## The drawn filing (described truthfully for what it establishes)
- The drawn 8-K is a Current Report under Item 8.01 that attaches a press release announcing
  the June 9, 2016 completion of the combination; it is an announcement/reference filing, not
  the deal-terms document. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K Item 8.01]
- In connection with completion, stockholders approved a charter amendment allowing the
  registrant to continue as a corporation with perpetual existence. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K Item 8.01]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- WL Ross Holding Corp. (NASDAQ: WLRH), a special purpose acquisition (blank-check) company
  sponsored by WL Ross Sponsor LLC (affiliate of WL Ross & Co. LLC) that completed a ~$500 million
  IPO in June 2014, completed its acquisition of Nexeo Solutions Holdings, LLC and renamed itself
  Nexeo Solutions, Inc., expecting to trade under NXEO/NXEOU/NXEOW from June 10, 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- The deal terms are set by the Agreement and Plan of Merger dated March 21, 2016 (Annex A to the
  proxy), among the Company, Neon Acquisition Company LLC ("Blocker Merger Sub"), Neon Holding
  Company LLC ("Company Merger Sub"), Nexeo Solutions Holdings, LLC ("Nexeo"), TPG Accolade
  Delaware, L.P. ("Blocker"), and Nexeo Holdco, LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]
- Structure: two mergers — Company Merger Sub merged with and into Nexeo, with Nexeo surviving;
  immediately thereafter Blocker Merger Sub merged with and into Blocker, with Blocker surviving —
  collectively the "Business Combination." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]
- Aggregate purchase price expected ~$1,575.0 million: ~$298 million equity consideration to
  Selling Equityholders, ~$420 million cash consideration at closing, assumption or refinancing of
  Nexeo net debt of $811 million (as of Dec 31, 2015), and ~$46 million deferred cash consideration.
  Consideration funded through a mix of stock and cash (trust-account proceeds plus a PIPE Investment
  of up to $225 million). [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]
- Selling Equityholders' aggregate stock ownership was capped at 35% of the post-combination
  company's common stock ("35% Cap"); public stockholders (incl. PIPE investors) ~55%; the Sponsor
  ~10%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the boundary at the tier level
- Controlling intent / imprint: WL Ross & Co. LLC (Wilbur L. Ross, Jr.) entered as a new controlling
  partner; the acquirer stated the combined company was positioned "to grow its market share and roll
  up the fragmented chemicals and plastics distribution space," a stated forward growth strategy.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- The acquiring vehicle contributed capital and a public listing, not operations: it is described as a
  "development stage blank check company" with "no operating history" and no operations other than
  holding IPO trust proceeds. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]
- Customer-facing product / asset: the surviving business (Nexeo) is a global chemical and plastics
  distributor offering over 23,000 products across North America, EMEA and Asia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- Operational processes: Nexeo's logistics capabilities, in-depth market knowledge, dedicated technical
  expertise and Environmental Services crossed as part of the surviving operating entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]
- People / management: as a private company Nexeo employed ~2,450 employees connecting ~1,300 suppliers
  with ~27,500 customers; management continued (David Bradley continued as President and CEO) and TPG
  retained an ongoing ownership stake (~35% Cap). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release]

## Contractual arrangement over a shared substrate (closing-time)
- A Tax Receivable Agreement dated June 9, 2016 was entered among the Company, Nexeo Holdco, LLC and
  TPG-affiliated entities, under which 85% of certain cash tax savings are payable to the Selling
  Equityholders and the Company retains the remaining 15%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The operating business — its customer-facing product (23,000+ distributed chemicals/plastics), the
  processes that deliver it (logistics, technical, environmental services), and the people/management
  that run them (~2,450 employees; continuing CEO) — crossed together as the surviving entity, while the
  counterparty contributed public-market capital, a listing and a new controlling principal (WL Ross &
  Co.) rather than any operating substrate. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-16-126429, Form 8-K EX-99.1 press release] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-16-100057, DEFM14A]
