## Public-reporting status and entity transformation within the window
- The registrant that filed the closing-era report (Crosstex Energy, L.P., CIK 1179060)
  remained an SEC-reporting, publicly traded entity through the outcome window, but was
  transformed: on March 7, 2014 it consummated a Contribution Agreement (dated October 21,
  2013) under which subsidiaries of Devon Energy contributed a 50% limited-partner interest
  in Midstream Holdings and the equity of its general partner in exchange for 120,542,441
  newly issued units, and the entity now reports as EnLink Midstream Partners, LP (NYSE:
  ENLK). [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Because the same registrant continued to file audited annual reports, a public 3-5-year
  outcome record exists and is used here; the outcome facts below are drawn from primary
  SEC filings. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Realized record of the co-owned Permian processing asset
- The co-ownership of the Deadwood processing facility did not persist through the window:
  "Prior to November 2015, we co-owned the Deadwood natural gas processing plant with a
  subsidiary of Apache Corporation." On November 16, 2015 the registrant "acquired Apache's
  50% ownership interest in the Deadwood natural gas processing facility for approximately
  $40.0 million, all of which is considered property, plant and equipment," accounting for
  it under the acquisition method. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Direct transaction costs of the buyout were less than $0.1 million; for the period from
  November 16, 2015 to December 31, 2015 the registrant recognized $3.5 million of revenues
  and $1.3 million of net income related to the acquired (formerly co-owned) assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- As of the FY2015 report the Deadwood facility, then wholly owned, is described with a
  total capacity of 58 MMcf/d and total processing throughput that averaged 61,500 MMBtu/d,
  supported by acreage dedication from a major producer in the Permian Basin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Best-documented outcome scalar (entity level, FY2015)
- For the year ended December 31, 2015 the registrant reported a net loss attributable to
  EnLink Midstream Partners, LP of $(1,377.8) million, compared with net income of $310.5
  million for 2014 and $115.5 million for 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The 2015 result reflected a goodwill impairment totaling $1,328.2 million across the
  registrant's Texas, Louisiana and Crude and Condensate segments, plus a $223.1 million
  impairment of intangible assets in the Crude and Condensate segment, "primarily as a
  result of the further decline in commodity prices and the public trading price of our
  common units." [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- Total revenues were $4,452.1 million for 2015, versus $3,507.8 million for 2014 and
  $2,295.9 million for 2013. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed window the registrant persisted as a public reporting entity but under a
  transformed identity (EnLink Midstream Partners, LP), and the specific co-ownership
  arrangement documented at closing was ended by the registrant's November 16, 2015 buyout
  of its counterparty's 50% interest, consolidating the asset under sole ownership and
  operation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- At the entity level the same year recorded a $(1,377.8) million net loss driven by more
  than $1.5 billion of goodwill and intangible-asset impairments attributed to commodity-
  price and unit-price declines. [SRC: SEC accession 0001179060-16-000036, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
