## Reporting status within the window
- Throughout the window, Sadara Chemical Company remained a 35-percent-owned
  nonconsolidated affiliate of the Company, accounted for as an equity-method
  investment rather than consolidated. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-15-000011, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- As of the FY2014 10-K, Sadara was still described as "a development-stage Saudi
  Arabian company that will manufacture chlorine, ethylene and propylene for internal
  consumption and will produce and sell high-value added chemical products and
  performance plastics." [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-15-000011, Form 10-K (FY2014)]
- The identical "development-stage" characterization was repeated in the FY2015 10-K. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015)]

## Realized financial trajectory (best-documented scalars)
- The Company's equity in earnings of nonconsolidated affiliates fell across the
  window: $1,034 million in 2013, to $835 million in 2014, to $674 million in 2015. The
  FY2014 10-K attributes part of the 2014 decline to "increased losses at Sadara
  Chemical Company." [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-15-000011, Form 10-K (FY2014), MD&A]
- The FY2015 10-K likewise attributes part of the further 2015 decline to "increased
  equity losses from Sadara Chemical Company," even as earnings improved elsewhere in
  the affiliate portfolio. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015), MD&A]
- In 2015, the Company's equity earnings were further reduced by "a loss recognized by
  Sadara related to the write-off of design engineering work for an Epoxy Plant, of
  which Dow's share was $27 million." [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015), MD&A special items]
- During 2015, the Company's investing cash outflow included "investments in and loans
  made to nonconsolidated affiliates, primarily with Sadara," indicating continuing
  parent-company funding of the venture within the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015), Liquidity and Capital Resources]
- The FY2015 10-K's forward outlook stated that equity earnings were "expected to be
  lower due to increased start-up expenses at Sadara." [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-15-000011, Form 10-K (FY2014), Outlook]

## Operational milestone within the window
- On December 8, 2015, the Company announced that Sadara "had achieved its first
  polyethylene production." The FY2015 10-K describes the complex's 26 manufacturing
  assets as undergoing "a sequenced start-up process, beginning with the polyolefins
  envelope ... followed by ethylene oxide/propylene oxide and their derivatives," and
  states the assets "remain on schedule" for that sequenced start-up. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
- The FY2015 10-K's outlook for the following year anticipated volume increases "due to
  the start-up of the first Sadara polyethylene facility in December 2015 and the
  remaining Sadara polyethylene and olefins production facilities in 2016." [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015), Outlook]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across fiscal years 2013-2015, the venture remained in "development stage" from the
  Company's disclosure perspective, had not yet reached full operation, and
  contributed a growing drag on the Company's equity earnings from nonconsolidated
  affiliates each year, including a specific $27 million (Dow's share) write-off of
  design-engineering work for one of its plants in 2015 and continuing parent-company
  cash investment. The venture's first production milestone (polyethylene) was
  publicly announced in December 2015, described by the Company as on schedule for a
  multi-year, sequenced start-up continuing into 2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-15-000011, Form 10-K (FY2014)] [SRC: SEC accession 0000029915-16-000066, Form 10-K (FY2015)]
