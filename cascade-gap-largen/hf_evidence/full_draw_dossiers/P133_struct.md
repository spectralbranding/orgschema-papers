# Structural sub-dossier — RadioShack Corporation Chapter 11 §363 asset sale (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On February 5, 2015 (the "Petition Date"), RadioShack Corporation ("the Company") and
  its direct and indirect domestic subsidiaries filed voluntary petitions for relief
  under chapter 11 of the Bankruptcy Code in the U.S. Bankruptcy Court for the District
  of Delaware; the jointly administered case is styled In re RadioShack Corp., No.
  15-10197. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 1.03]
- The Company's operating subsidiaries in Mexico and Asia were not debtors under the
  Chapter 11 cases. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Also on February 5, 2015, the Company entered into an Asset Purchase Agreement with
  General Wireless Inc. ("General Wireless") for the sale of between 1,500 and 2,400
  Company-owned stores, the number to be elected by General Wireless before the auction.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The sale was structured as a sale of assets under section 363 of the Bankruptcy Code
  through a Court-approved bidding and public-auction process, with General Wireless as
  the initial (stalking-horse) bidder and the agreement subject to higher or better
  offers; a $6 million breakup fee plus expense reimbursement was payable to General
  Wireless if the Company consummated an alternative transaction. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company simultaneously executed a Debtor-in-Possession (DIP) Credit Agreement
  providing a facility of approximately $285 million among the Company, the prepetition
  lenders and Cantor Fitzgerald Securities as administrative agent. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01, EX-10.1]

## Pricing / consideration mechanics (as structured)
- The per-store purchase price was $3,000 plus 85% of the value of eligible inventory and
  petty cash at those stores, less any assumed consumer liabilities; the price was to be
  paid by a combination of cash and a credit bid of secured debt held by affiliates of
  General Wireless. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary — the acquired assets
- The "Acquired Assets" under the Asset Purchase Agreement comprised: all inventory
  located at any acquired store immediately prior to closing; all equipment located at
  any acquired store; all assigned agreements and specified avoidance actions against
  suppliers, vendors or landlords party to an assigned agreement; all petty cash; all
  permits and pending applications; all pre-paid expenses; "all goodwill associated with
  the Acquired Assets"; documents (to the extent available and permitted); claims arising
  out of any acquired store or acquired asset; the sellers' rights under non-disclosure,
  non-compete and non-solicitation agreements relating to the business or acquired stores;
  and any other assets listed on a schedule. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358,
  Form 8-K EX-10.2 §2.1]
- "Acquired Stores" were defined as the retail stores on a preliminary Acquired Store
  Schedule that General Wireless provided to the sellers on February 4, 2015, plus any
  additional U.S. stores the buyer elected, in its sole discretion, to add within 30 days
  after the effective date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K EX-10.2
  §1.1 (definition of "Acquired Stores")]

## What did NOT cross — excluded assets and retained items
- The "Excluded Assets" expressly excluded, among other things: all cash and cash
  equivalents other than petty cash, together with all bank and securities accounts;
  documents prepared in connection with the agreement or primarily relating to the
  bankruptcy case, all minute books, corporate records and organizational documents, tax
  returns and tax work papers, and all documents not related to the business, the acquired
  stores, the acquired assets or the transferred employees; any contract that was not an
  assigned agreement; tax refunds; and specified claims and avoidance actions. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K EX-10.2 §2.2]
- All employees of the sellers other than "Transferred Employees," and all employee
  benefit plans and their funding vehicles and assets, were excluded from the acquired
  assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K EX-10.2 §2.2(h)]

## People / management (as structured)
- Employees did not transfer automatically: the buyer "may offer employment effective as
  of the Closing Date to such employees as Buyer shall determine in its sole discretion,
  at such salary and/or wage levels, with such benefits and under such other terms and
  conditions as Buyer shall determine in its sole discretion"; an employee who accepted
  such an offer on or within 30 days after closing was a "Transferred Employee." [SRC:
  SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K EX-10.2 §7.7 (employees)]

## Operating / co-branding arrangement bridging a shared substrate (closing-time)
- General Wireless's obligation to consummate the acquisition was conditioned on, among
  other things, its entering into definitive agreements with Sprint Solutions Inc. under
  a term sheet to establish co-branded stores (the "Sprint Term Sheet"). [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Sprint Term Sheet contemplated co-branded RadioShack-Sprint stores for the sale of
  RadioShack retail products, warranties, services and accessories together with
  Sprint-branded mobile devices operating on the Sprint wireless network, using a
  "store-within-a-store" format. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K
  EX-99.1 (Proposed Terms for Commercial Relationship), "General"]
- Under that format, Sprint would have the right to use up to 600 contiguous usable square
  feet in each Sprint store-within-a-store, generally located in the front right of the
  store. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K EX-99.1]

## Consummation of the sale (closing)
- On April 1, 2015 the Company completed the sale of 1,743 Company-owned stores and
  inventory to General Wireless Inc. and Sprint Solutions Inc. pursuant to the Asset
  Purchase Agreement; General Wireless and Sprint were declared the winning bidders and
  the Bankruptcy Court approved the section 363 sale on March 31, 2015. [SRC: SEC
  accession 0001193125-15-117783, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
- The aggregate consideration received by the Company in the sale was approximately
  $160.7 million, consisting of approximately $47.6 million in cash and approximately
  $113.0 million in the form of a credit bid of certain secured claims held by, or subject
  to participations in favor of, General Wireless or its affiliates. [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-15-117783, Form 8-K Item 2.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- Under a section 363 process, a customer-facing retail asset base (store inventory,
  equipment, petty cash, permits, assigned supplier/landlord agreements and associated
  goodwill for 1,743 stores) was sold out of a Chapter 11 estate to General Wireless for
  approximately $160.7 million (cash plus a credit bid), while corporate cash, bank
  accounts, corporate records, tax attributes, non-assigned contracts, employee benefit
  plans, and all employees other than those the buyer elected to re-hire were retained in
  the estate; the buyer's obligation was conditioned on a co-branded store-within-a-store
  operating arrangement with Sprint. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-15-044358, Form 8-K
  Items 1.01/1.03, EX-10.2 §§2.1-2.2, §7.7, EX-99.1] [SRC: SEC accession
  0001193125-15-117783, Form 8-K Item 2.01]
