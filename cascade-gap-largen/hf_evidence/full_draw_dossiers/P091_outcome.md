## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The realized-record window is fixed as the registrant's fiscal years 2010-2012 (~3-5 years after the ~2007 closing era); the figures below are drawn from the company's Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended December 31, 2011, which reports selected five-year data covering 2007-2011. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]

## Realized outcome scalar (net loss)
- Net income (loss) attributable to the Company (amounts in thousands as reported): $(316,253) for 2011, $(176,431) for 2010, $(397,960) for 2009 and $(365,178) for 2008 — i.e., reported net losses in each of the four fiscal years leading into and through the outcome window, versus net income of $55,922 in 2007. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011 (selected financial data)]
- Net income (loss) per common share, basic and diluted, was $(0.37) for 2011 and $(0.21) for 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011 (selected financial data)]
- Total income was $1,323,151 thousand for 2011 and $1,186,894 thousand for 2010; reported total assets were $10,919,190 thousand at December 31, 2011, down from $11,391,502 thousand at December 31, 2010. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011 (selected financial data)]

## Realized impairments (write-downs during the window)
- For the year ended December 31, 2011 the company recorded a provision for asset impairment of $105,795 thousand for continuing operations and $57,846 thousand for discontinued operations "to reduce the book value of certain of our investment properties to their fair values," after identifying properties with a reduced expected holding period. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- Impairment of investment in unconsolidated entities was $113,621 thousand in 2011 (and $11,239 thousand in 2010); impairment reflected in equity in earnings of unconsolidated entities was $16,739 thousand in 2011 ($10,710 thousand in 2010). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- For the year ended December 31, 2011 the company recorded an impairment charge of $24,356 thousand on securities (offset by a $6,125 thousand gain on sale of impaired securities). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- Impairment of notes receivable was $111,896 thousand in 2010 and $74,136 thousand in 2009; a provision for goodwill impairment of $26,676 thousand was recorded in 2009. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- For the year ended December 31, 2011 the company reported losses of $13 million and impairments net of gains of $106 million associated with its unconsolidated ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]

## Realized portfolio actions and operating structure during the window
- During 2011, "as part of our strategy to realign our asset segments with higher performing assets," the company sold 26 properties for a gross disposition price of $242.3 million, including fourteen retail properties and six midscale lodging properties. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- Many properties impaired in 2010 and 2009 had been disposed of by December 31, 2011, and the related 2010/2009 impairment charges of $44,349 thousand and $32,934 thousand, respectively, are reflected in discontinued operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
- Operations continued to be run by the external business manager during the window: for the year ended December 31, 2011 the company paid a business management fee of $40 million (~0.35% of average invested assets) plus an investment advisory fee of approximately $1.6 million, and it disclosed that the business manager had at times, in its sole discretion, elected to forgo or defer part of that fee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Across the fixed 2010-2012 outcome window the registrant reported successive net losses (net loss of $316.3 million in 2011 following losses of $176.4 million in 2010 and $398.0 million in 2009), large real-estate and investment write-downs (2011 asset-impairment provisions of $105.8 million continuing plus $57.8 million discontinued, and $113.6 million of unconsolidated-entity impairment), and active portfolio realignment through dispositions (26 properties sold for $242.3 million in 2011), while day-to-day operations continued to be conducted by the external business manager for a $40 million 2011 fee. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-12-104200, 10-K FY2011]
