## Outcome window (fixed at construction)
- The outcome window is the acquirer's fiscal years ending December 28, 2014, December 27,
  2015, and their reported comparatives, as recorded in the Form 10-K for the fiscal year
  ended December 27, 2015 (filed March 11, 2016). This is the acquirer's own annual report
  covering the ~3-5-year horizon after the ~2011 acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K for fiscal year ended December 27, 2015]

## Realized disposition of the acquired business
- On August 21, 2015 the Company completed the sale of the U.S. and U.K. operations of its
  Electronic Products Division to Ultra Electronics Holdings plc and Ultra Electronics
  Defense Inc. (the "Buyer"), selling all of the issued and outstanding capital stock of its
  wholly-owned subsidiary Herley Industries, Inc. and certain of Herley's subsidiaries —
  including Herley-CTI, Inc., EW Simulation Technology, Ltd. and Stapor Research, Inc. (the
  "Herley Entities"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The sale price was $260.0 million in cash plus $5.0 million for taxes incurred as part of
  the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The Company recognized a gain on the sale of the Herley Entities of $80.8 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The Herley Entities were reported in discontinued operations in the Consolidated Financial
  Statements for all periods presented. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Best-documented outcome scalars
- The single best-documented realized outcome is this divestiture: the acquired business,
  purchased in 2011, was sold to a third-party acquirer on August 21, 2015 (within
  approximately four and a half years) for $260.0 million, producing an $80.8 million gain on
  sale. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
- In the five-year selected financial data (fiscal 2011 through 2015), income (loss) from
  discontinued operations was $53.0 million for fiscal 2015 (which includes the gain on the
  Herley Entities sale) versus $(2.3) million for fiscal 2014; loss from continuing operations
  was $(33.2) million for fiscal 2015; and total net income (loss) was $19.8 million for
  fiscal 2015 versus $(78.0) million for fiscal 2014. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Use of disposition proceeds
- In connection with the sale, on August 21, 2015 the Company paid down the $41.0 million
  outstanding on its $110.0 million Credit and Security Agreement dated May 14, 2014, and on
  September 22, 2015 repurchased $175.0 million of its notes. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Facts bearing on the operations/organization interface over time
- The 10-K notes the Company incurred acquisition-related expenses of $12.5 million in fiscal
  2011 and $0.2 million in fiscal 2014, and, in 2012, recorded an impairment of goodwill and
  intangible assets of $96.6 million (a company-wide charge, not attributed in this line to a
  single acquired entity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
- The Company states that in connection with the sale of the Herley Entities it took, and
  "may engage in other," cost-reduction restructuring activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed outcome window, the business acquired in 2011 did not remain within the
  acquirer: it was divested in its entirety to a third party (Ultra Electronics) on August 21,
  2015 for $260.0 million, with an $80.8 million gain, reported as discontinued operations,
  and the proceeds applied to debt reduction — the acquired product operation, its producing
  processes, and its people leaving the acquirer together as a whole subsidiary rather than
  being retained and integrated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001069258-16-000059, Form 10-K FY2015]
