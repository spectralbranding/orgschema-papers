# Structural sub-dossier — Francisco Partners-led acquisition of VeriFone Systems (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- VeriFone Systems, Inc. (the Company), a Delaware corporation, agreed to be acquired by
  an investor group led by the private equity investment firm Francisco Partners.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A]
- The acquisition vehicle was Vertex Holdco LLC (Parent/"Holdco"), a Delaware limited
  liability company, and its wholly owned subsidiary Vertex Merger Sub LLC, a Delaware
  limited liability company; Vertex Holdco LLC and Vertex Merger Sub LLC were owned by
  the investor group led by Francisco Partners and including British Columbia Investment
  Management Corporation (BCI). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A]
- Pursuant to the merger agreement, Vertex Merger Sub LLC would be merged with and into
  the Company, with the Company continuing as the surviving corporation and a wholly
  owned subsidiary of Vertex Holdco LLC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A]
- The Company described itself as built on a 35-year history of security, with
  approximately 30 million devices and terminals deployed worldwide and clients and
  partners in more than 150 countries, including well-known retail brands, financial
  institutions and payment providers; its common stock traded on the New York Stock
  Exchange. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A]
- The transaction represented a total consideration of approximately $3.4 billion,
  including the Company's net debt. [SRC: https://www.franciscopartners.com/media/verifone-to-be-acquired-by-francisco-partners-for-34-billion]

## Closing mechanics
- The merger closed and became effective on August 20, 2018: Merger Sub merged with and
  into the Company on that date, with the Company surviving the merger as a wholly owned
  subsidiary of Parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K, Item 2.01]
- In connection with the closing, the Company requested that the New York Stock
  Exchange suspend trading of the Company's common stock, remove it from listing, and
  file a Form 25 to report the change and effect deregistration under Section 12(b) of
  the Exchange Act; the Company also stated its intent to file a Form 15 to terminate
  registration and suspend reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K, Item 3.01]

## Consideration and financing
- At the effective time, each share of Company common stock (with limited exceptions)
  was converted into the right to receive $23.04 in cash, without interest —
  representing a premium of approximately 54% to the closing price on April 9, 2018,
  the last trading day before public announcement — and the aggregate cash
  consideration paid by Parent to Company stockholders in the merger was approximately
  $2.6 billion. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K, Items 2.01 and 5.01]
- The investor group provided an equity commitment letter and a limited guarantee in
  favor of the Company, with the balance of the purchase price funded through committed
  debt financing; at closing, Parent, Merger Sub, the Company and certain subsidiaries
  entered into First and Second Lien Credit Agreements providing an aggregate $2.2
  billion in borrowings (a seven-year first-priority term loan, a five-year
  first-priority revolving facility, and an eight-year second-priority term loan),
  fully funded at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K, Item 1.01]
- The merger agreement provided for a Company-payable termination fee of $33.3 million
  or $86.6 million depending on the circumstances and timing of termination.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A]

## Management and governance arrangements at closing
- At the effective time, all members of the Company's board immediately prior to
  closing (other than Paul Galant) ceased to be directors, and Jason Brein, Peter
  Christodoulo, Dave Hong, Blake Fizzard and Mike Pulli became directors; Paul Galant
  resigned as Chief Executive Officer at the effective time and Mike Pulli, previously
  a Company officer, was appointed Chief Executive Officer.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K, Item 5.02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company, all-cash go-private acquisition of a listed
  payments-technology company by a private-equity-led investor group (Francisco
  Partners with co-investor BCI) through a newly formed holding company and merger
  subsidiary, closing August 20, 2018, at $23.04 per share (~54% premium; ~$2.6 billion
  aggregate cash consideration; ~$3.4 billion total consideration including net debt),
  funded by committed equity plus $2.2 billion in new secured credit facilities, with the
  operating business (product, processes, and — apart from a CEO/board reconstitution
  drawn substantially from existing personnel — organization) continuing together as a
  private subsidiary, and the Company's stock removed from NYSE listing with Section
  12(b) registration deregistered at closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-169570, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-253049, Form 8-K]
