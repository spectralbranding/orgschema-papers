# Structural sub-dossier — Cypress Semiconductor / SK hynix system ic NAND joint venture (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On October 23, 2018, Cypress Semiconductor Corporation (the "Company") entered into a joint venture agreement with SK hynix system ic Inc. ("SKHYSI"), a company organized under the laws of the Republic of Korea and a wholly-owned subsidiary of SK hynix Inc. ("SKH"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The stated principal purpose of the joint venture is the development, manufacture, sale, and distribution of NAND products and other related products and services using Large Scale NAND Wafers supplied by SKH. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The joint venture agreement has an initial term of five years unless earlier terminated or extended. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The new joint-venture entity is structured as a subsidiary of SK hynix system ic, with the Company holding a minority position. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K EX-99.1 Investor Presentation]

## Legal vehicle and ownership contributed at closing
- SKHYSI agreed to contribute $3.6 million in cash for a 60% ownership interest in the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company agreed to contribute $2.4 million and certain tools and machinery (aggregate book value of less than $55,000) for a 40% ownership interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company was entitled to nominate a minority of the joint venture's board of directors and to hold limited special approval rights over its operations; the Company expected to account for the joint venture under the equity method. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The agreement provided reciprocal put/call rights (including SKHYSI's right to acquire an additional 10% interest above a stated wafer-volume threshold, and to acquire all of the Company's interest upon a change of control of the Company to a competitor) with a purchase price set at book value of the joint venture's shareholder equity times the interest acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Customer-facing product and the customers at closing
- The customer-facing product line placed into the joint venture is the NAND flash business; the Company agreed to use commercially reasonable efforts to transition its current NAND customers to the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Each party agreed that, subject to certain exceptions, neither it nor its controlled affiliates would sell NAND or MCP products consisting of NAND and DRAM in competition with the joint venture for the term of the agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Producing / delivering processes at closing (product vs process split)
- The producing/delivering processes were divided rather than moved intact with the product: front-end wafer supply was to come from SKH under a NAND supply agreement to be entered between the joint venture and SKH, while back-end services for the joint venture were to be provided by the Company under a back-end manufacturing agreement between the joint venture and the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K EX-99.1 Investor Presentation]
- Launch of the joint venture was conditioned on receipt of antitrust and other regulatory approvals (including review and clearance from the Committee on Foreign Investment in the United States), entry into the NAND supply agreement, and entry into the back-end manufacturing agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Upon launch, the pre-existing NAND Supply Agreement dated as of October 1, 2016 between SKH and the Company was to be terminated. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## People / organization crossing the boundary at closing
- It was expected that up to fifteen current employees of the Company would transfer to the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K EX-99.1 Investor Presentation]

## Bridging / transitional arrangements at closing
- The Company agreed to provide limited transition services and design services to the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Company was entitled to a quarterly special dividend on the joint venture's profit for a defined post-launch period, tied to sales made using up to 15,000 wafers per quarter, plus a share of profit on the associated wafer inventory through a stated date. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The full description in the 8-K was qualified by reference to the joint venture agreement to be filed later as an exhibit to the Company's Annual Report on Form 10-K; an investor presentation providing additional detail was furnished under Item 7.01 as Exhibit 99.1. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Items 1.01 and 7.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction placed the Company's NAND flash product line and its current NAND customers into a newly formed entity owned 60% by SK hynix system ic and 40% by the Company, with the front-end (wafer supply) and back-end (manufacturing) processes contractually split between the two parents, up to fifteen Company employees transferring, and the residual shared substrate bridged at closing by a NAND supply agreement, a back-end manufacturing agreement, and Company-provided transition and design services. [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K Item 1.01] [SRC: SEC accession 0001104659-18-063951, Form 8-K EX-99.1 Investor Presentation]
