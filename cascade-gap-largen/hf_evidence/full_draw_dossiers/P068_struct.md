# Structural sub-dossier — Sotheby's / Beijing GeHua equity joint venture (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the

## Note on the drawn filing
- The drawn filing (accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02) is an
  AMENDMENT/commencement report; it reports the amendment of, and Chinese-government
  approval of, an equity joint venture agreement whose original terms were reported in an
  earlier Form 8-K (accession 0000823094-12-000011, filed 2012-09-06). Both are used
  below to describe the transaction as structured. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On September 3, 2012, Sotheby's entered into a 10-year equity joint venture agreement
  with Beijing GeHua Art Company, described as a Chinese State-Owned Enterprise that is
  part of GeHua Cultural and Development Group ("GeHua"). [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- The joint venture entity was Sotheby's (Beijing) Auction Co., Ltd., with the joint
  venture contract executed between GeHua Art Company and Sotheby's Hong Kong Limited.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-17-000009, Form 10-K exhibit list items 10.25/10.26]
- The transaction was structured as a joint venture; it is a foreign-invested enterprise
  under Chinese law, so all changes in shareholding and constitution are subject to
  Chinese-government approval. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing (by tier)
- Controlling interest / intent: upon Chinese-government approval, Sotheby's would invest
  $1.2 million for an 80% ownership interest; GeHua would invest $0.3 million for a 20%
  interest. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Governance: the joint venture's board comprises five members, four of whom (including
  the Chairman) are appointed by Sotheby's; structural changes require a unanimous board
  vote and all other decisions a two-thirds super-majority. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Operational processes / management: Sotheby's is responsible for the day-to-day
  management of the joint venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Business / value-capture model: the venture is a platform for art-related auctions and
  private selling exhibitions of non-cultural relics, travelling exhibitions, and
  educational activities. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Physical platform contributed by the counterparty: the venue is a planned free port that
  GeHua is developing within the Tianzhu Free Trade Zone in Beijing (the "Beijing Free
  Port"), intended to serve as a tax-advantaged storage location; completion timing and
  scope of activities were stated to be uncertain at signing. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- The customer-facing product (Sotheby's-branded auction/selling activity) is carried into
  the venture under a trademark license from Sotheby's to the joint venture; Sotheby's
  right to terminate that trademark license is enumerated among its termination triggers.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Sotheby's existing China-facing operations were NOT folded in: Sotheby's stated it was
  not restricted by the agreement from holding auctions in China through separate
  subsidiaries and that it currently served the Chinese art market through its existing
  operations in Hong Kong. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]

## Exclusivity, exit, and termination terms (closing-time)
- Exclusivity: GeHua would not cooperate with any other company to conduct auctions, or
  specified competitors to conduct commission sales, within its authorized areas, and was
  restricted from partnering with specified competitors elsewhere in the PRC. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]
- Exit: Sotheby's holds an option to put its ownership interest to GeHua at any time during
  the term at a price in excess of its initial investment. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]
- Termination: the agreement may be immediately terminated by either party on enumerated
  events (e.g., unauthorized share transfer, non-compliance with anti-corruption law
  including the U.S. FCPA, material breach, change of control) and, on 30 days' notice, on
  other events (e.g., Sotheby's loss of control, the venture losing more than 20,000,000
  RMB (~$3.1 million), or insolvency). [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06]

## Formation / commencement events
- On September 26, 2012, in response to comments from the Beijing Commerce Commission, the
  parties amended the joint venture agreement (revised exclusivity, dissolution, and
  transfer-of-interest provisions; other material terms unchanged). [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]
- On September 26, 2012, the Beijing Commerce Commission approved the agreement and issued
  the foreign-investment approval letter, foreign-investment certificate, and auction
  license; the Beijing Administration for Industry and Commerce issued the business license
  to commence operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]
- On September 27, 2012, the pre-auction recordal was completed and the joint venture's
  first auction occurred later that day. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- At closing, a Sotheby's-controlled (80% equity, four of five board seats, day-to-day
  management) equity joint venture was formed with a Chinese state-owned counterparty that
  contributed the local platform (a bonded free port and the associated regulatory
  authorizations), with the customer-facing branded product carried in by a trademark
  license rather than by a transfer of Sotheby's existing China/Hong Kong operations, which
  remained outside the venture. [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000011, Form 8-K filed 2012-09-06] [SRC: SEC accession 0000823094-12-000016, Form 8-K filed 2012-10-02]
