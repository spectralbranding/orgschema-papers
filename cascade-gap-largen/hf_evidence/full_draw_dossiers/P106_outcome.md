## Public-reporting status across the outcome window (feasibility-relevant)
- The registrant's last standalone annual report was a Form 10-K for the fiscal year ended
  September 30, 2018, filed December 6, 2018 (which predates the fixed ~2019-2021 window).
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001628280-18-014867, Form 10-K FY2018]
- On February 28, 2019 Univar Inc. completed its acquisition of Nexeo Solutions, Inc. pursuant to
  an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated September 17, 2018, via a two-step merger: Pilates Merger
  Sub I Corp merged with and into Nexeo (Nexeo surviving as a wholly owned Univar subsidiary), then
  Nexeo merged with and into Pilates Merger Sub II LLC, which survived as a wholly owned subsidiary
  of Univar. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, Form 8-K]
- Merger consideration: each Nexeo common share was converted into the right to receive $3.02 in cash
  plus 0.305 of a share of Univar common stock; the stock portion aggregated to approximately 28 million
  Univar shares (~16% of Univar's outstanding common stock after the transaction). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, Form 8-K]
- In connection with the mergers the Tax Receivable Agreement dated June 9, 2016 was terminated.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, Form 8-K]
- Nexeo's securities ceased trading on Nasdaq and were delisted as of the opening of market on
  March 1, 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-059190, Form 8-K Item 3.01]
- A Form 15 (Rule 12g-4 / 12h-3) was filed March 12, 2019 deregistering the common stock, warrants and
  units; as of the certification date there was one holder of record — Univar, as successor by merger.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-19-071988, Form 15-12B]
