# Structural sub-dossier — Phillips Edison Value Added Grocery Venture, LLC formation (closing structure)

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On March 22, 2016, Phillips Edison Grocery Center REIT II, Inc. (the "Company") entered
  into a joint venture through its indirect wholly-owned subsidiary PE OP II Value Added
  Grocery, LLC (the "REIT Member"), with a limited partnership affiliated with TPG Real
  Estate (the "Investor Member") and with PECO Value Added Grocery Manager, LLC (the "PECO
  Member"), a wholly-owned subsidiary of Phillips Edison Limited Partnership and an
  affiliate of the Company's advisor (Phillips Edison NTR II LLC) and property manager
  (Phillips Edison & Company Ltd.). [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The venture was formed pursuant to the Limited Liability Company Agreement (the "Joint
  Venture Agreement") of Phillips Edison Value Added Grocery Venture, LLC (the "Joint
  Venture"). [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Initial equity interests: the REIT Member 20%, the Investor Member 80%; committed
  contributions of up to $50 million and $200 million, respectively, with expected use of
  leverage. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Joint Venture's stated focus was investments outside the Company's core focus —
  more opportunistic and value-add properties — with a right of first offer in favor of
  the Joint Venture until the earlier of March 22, 2019 or investment of all of the Joint
  Venture's capital. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- The Joint Venture's term is seven years after the date of the Joint Venture Agreement,
  unless extended by the members. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary at closing, by tier
- Customer-facing asset: simultaneously with the Joint Venture Agreement, the REIT Member
  entered a Contribution Agreement contributing to the Joint Venture its ownership
  interests in six grocery-anchored shopping center properties (the "Initial Properties"),
  valued at approximately $94.3 million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Operating processes: the Joint Venture separately entered a Master Property Management,
  Leasing and Construction Management Agreement with Phillips Edison & Company Ltd. (the
  "Property Manager"), under which the Property Manager — not the new venture as an
  in-house operating capability — has responsibility for the day-to-day management,
  operation and maintenance of the Initial Properties, for a monthly fee of 4.0% of gross
  revenues plus leasing/construction management fees at market rates and expense
  reimbursements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Controlling intent / management: PECO Member (a Phillips Edison affiliate) manages and
  conducts the day-to-day operations and affairs of the Joint Venture, subject to major
  decisions requiring majority or unanimous member consent; the REIT Member has customary
  approval rights but does not have the right to cause or prohibit material transactions
  (acquisitions, dispositions, financings, significant leasing, distributions, significant
  capital expenditures, or litigation actions). [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Removal right: the Investor Member (TPG) has the ability to remove PECO Member as
  manager under certain circumstances, including a default by PECO Member. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- Economics of the manager's role: PECO Member is entitled to a promote (with respect to
  the REIT Member's investment, 15% of net operating cash flow distributions after a 10%
  return, then 22.5% after a 15% return) and a quarterly asset management fee (0.5% of
  aggregate investment value until $917,500 has been paid, then 1.0% thereafter). [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Contractual arrangements allocating the shared substrate (closing-time)
- A First Amendment to the Advisory Agreement (dated December 3, 2015) was entered into
  among the Company, its Operating Partnership, and the Advisor, so that acquisition,
  asset-management, and disposition fees on Joint-Venture-owned assets would be paid by
  the Joint Venture rather than directly by the Company or Operating Partnership; the
  amendment also provides Advisor indemnification of the Company for losses from certain
  PECO Member actions as manager. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
- A Second Amendment to the Operating Partnership's Amended and Restated Agreement of
  Limited Partnership similarly reallocated Class B asset-management fees so they would not
  be paid with respect to Joint-Venture-owned assets. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Related-party / control observation
- An independent director of the Company, Paul J. Massey, Jr., is President, New York
  Investment Sales of Cushman & Wakefield, which is indirectly owned and controlled by TPG
  Real Estate; the Joint Venture Agreement was presented to the independent directors and
  its terms were determined fair and reasonable and substantially the same as those
  received by the other joint venturers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction moved six operating shopping-center properties (valued ~$94.3 million)
  from a wholly-owned subsidiary into a new 20/80 venture majority-funded by an outside
  investor, while the operating processes that run those properties remained with the same
  external Phillips Edison manager under a separate management agreement, and day-to-day
  control was allocated to the Phillips Edison affiliate rather than to the majority
  economic owner, with fee-reallocation amendments bridging the shared advisory substrate.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001581405-16-000076, Form 8-K Item 1.01]
