# Structural sub-dossier — Fairmount Santrol Holdings Inc. combination with Unimin Corporation to form Covia Holdings Corporation (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties at closing
- On December 11, 2017, Fairmount Santrol Holdings Inc. entered into an Agreement and
  Plan of Merger with SCR-Sibelco NV (a privately-owned Belgian company), Unimin
  Corporation (a Delaware corporation and wholly owned subsidiary of Sibelco), Bison
  Merger Sub, Inc. (a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Unimin), and Bison Merger Sub I,
  LLC (a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Unimin), providing for a merger of Merger Sub
  into Fairmount Santrol, with Fairmount Santrol continuing as the surviving corporation
  and a direct wholly owned subsidiary of Unimin. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- Immediately after this first merger, Fairmount Santrol (as surviving corporation) was
  to be merged with and into Merger Sub LLC (the "Second Merger"), with Merger Sub LLC
  continuing as the surviving entity and Fairmount Santrol ceasing to exist as a separate
  corporate entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon closing, Unimin was to become the parent holding company for the combined group
  and, following the Merger, become a publicly traded company with its common stock
  listed on the NYSE; the combined company would operate under a new name to be selected
  by Unimin and Fairmount Santrol prior to closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- The Merger and Second Merger were completed on June 1, 2018; Fairmount Santrol Holdings
  Inc. became a wholly owned subsidiary of Covia Holdings Corporation (Unimin's new name
  upon closing), and Fairmount Santrol was then merged into Bison Merger Sub I, LLC
  (Covia's new name for the surviving entity). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06 reporting event of 2018-06-01]

## Merger consideration and ownership split
- Each share of Fairmount Santrol common stock was converted into the right to receive
  0.20 shares of combined company common stock (with cash paid in lieu of fractional
  shares) plus approximately $0.73 in cash; the total number of combined-company shares
  estimated to be issued to Fairmount Santrol stockholders was 48,275,651. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06]
- Immediately following the effective time, Sibelco was to own, directly or indirectly,
  approximately 65% of combined company common stock, with former Fairmount Santrol
  stockholders (including certain equity-award holders) owning the remaining
  approximately 35%. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- Separately, at closing Unimin expected to redeem shares of its common stock held by
  Sibelco for cash of approximately $660 million (plus accrued interest from June 30,
  2017) less $170 million (the "Cash Redemption"), funded in part through a $1.65
  billion seven-year term loan and a $200 million revolving credit facility committed by
  Barclays Bank PLC and BNP Paribas. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- The combined company was structured as a "controlled company" within the meaning of
  NYSE rules as a result of Sibelco's post-closing ownership stake. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]

## What crossed into the combined company, and what was carved out
- Fairmount Santrol's entire pre-merger operating business — described in the proxy as a
  leading provider of high-performance sand and sand-based product solutions for oil and
  gas exploration/production customers and for foundry, building-products, water
  filtration, glass, and sports/recreation end markets, with an associated logistics
  network of distribution terminals and railcars — was the asset that crossed into the
  combined company via the whole-company merger structure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- Unimin's own global high-purity-quartz business (its Electronics segment) did NOT
  cross into the combined company: as a pre-closing condition, Unimin contributed those
  assets to a newly formed subsidiary ("HPQ Co"), then distributed 100% of HPQ Co's stock
  to Sibelco in exchange for Unimin shares held by Sibelco (together, the "HPQ
  Carveout"); the HPQ Carveout also included certain personnel and assets historically
  part of Unimin supporting coatings/polymers sales and research, together with related
  intellectual property. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- At closing, the combined company and Sibelco were to enter into Distribution
  Agreements under which each would act as exclusive distributor of certain of the
  other's products in defined territories/industries (20-year terms, terminating early if
  Sibelco's ownership fell to 50% or below), and Agency Agreements providing exclusive
  agency services for certain other product lines. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- The proxy states that, apart from the Distribution Agreement and Agency Agreement (and
  such other arrangements as separately agreed), "Sibelco will have no obligation to
  provide the combined company with any assistance or services" following closing —
  i.e., no general transition-services agreement was contracted beyond these specific
  commercial arrangements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]

## Governance, management, and organizational continuity at closing
- The Combined Company Board was to comprise 11 directors: six Unimin-nominated
  directors (initially Kurt Decat, Jean-Luc Deleersnyder, Michel Delloye, Jean-Pierre
  Labroue, Olivier Lambrechts, plus one additional independent director), four
  Fairmount-nominated directors (initially William E. Conway, Charles D. Fowler, Matthew
  F. LeBaron, plus one additional independent director), and the Chief Executive Officer
  of the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- Jenniffer D. Deckard — Fairmount Santrol's President, CEO, and director since 2013 —
  was designated to become President, Chief Executive Officer, and a director of the
  combined company upon closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- At the consummation of the Second Merger, each of Fairmount Santrol's other directors
  (William E. Conway, Michael G. Fisch, Charles D. Fowler, Stephen J. Hadden, Michael C.
  Kearney, William P. Kelly, Matthew F. LeBaron, Michael E. Sand, and Lawrence N.
  Schultz) ceased to be directors of Fairmount Santrol, and each of Fairmount Santrol's
  other executive officers (Michael F. Biehl, Gerald L. Clancey, Brian J. Richardson,
  George W. Magaud, Robert B. Larson, Daniel N. Gerber, and David J. Crandall — alongside
  Ms. Deckard, whose role continued at the combined-company level) ceased to be
  executive officers of Fairmount Santrol. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06]
- Kurt Decat (Sibelco's Chief Financial Officer) became President and Secretary of Bison
  Merger Sub I, LLC, the entity surviving the Second Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06]
- With respect to Fairmount Santrol employees covered by collective bargaining
  agreements, Unimin agreed to comply with the terms of each applicable agreement; for
  other continuing employees, the Merger Agreement provided for service-credit
  continuity and immediate eligibility in the combined company's new benefit plans. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]

## Listing status of the acquired entity at closing
- Fairmount Santrol common stock, previously listed on the NYSE under symbol "FMSA," was
  to have its listing removed and its Exchange Act registration terminated upon closing
  of the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- On June 1, 2018, in connection with completion of the Merger, Fairmount Santrol
  notified the NYSE that trading in its common stock should be suspended and that the
  listing of its common stock on the NYSE should be removed; the NYSE informed Fairmount
  Santrol the same day that it had filed the corresponding Form 25 with the SEC. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06]
- The combined company's common stock (Unimin's, renamed) was separately approved for
  listing on the NYSE in connection with the Merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined two industrial-minerals/proppant producers into a single new
  publicly traded holding company (Covia) via a forward merger of Fairmount Santrol into
  a Unimin subsidiary, with Sibelco (Unimin's parent) retaining approximately 65%
  ownership and controlled-company status, while Fairmount Santrol's entire operating
  business crossed intact into the combined company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- A distinct product/asset line on the Unimin side (the high-purity-quartz Electronics
  business, with associated personnel) was carved out to Sibelco rather than crossing
  into the combined company, and the shared substrate between the combined company and
  Sibelco going forward was bridged narrowly, by fixed-term commercial Distribution and
  Agency Agreements rather than a general transition-services agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
- At the management tier, only Fairmount Santrol's CEO continued into the combined
  company's senior leadership (as President/CEO); the remainder of Fairmount Santrol's
  pre-merger board and executive officers ceased their roles at closing, while the
  post-closing Combined Company Board was split by nomination rights (six
  Unimin-nominated, four Fairmount-nominated, plus the CEO). [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-185726, Form 8-K dated 2018-06-06]; [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-134923, Form DEFM14A]
