# Structural sub-dossier — ARRIS International plc acquisition by CommScope Holding Company, Inc. (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Parties and transaction type at closing
- ARRIS International plc ("ARRIS") is a public limited company with its registered
  office at Victoria Road, Saltaire, West Yorkshire, England BD18 3LF, incorporated in
  England and Wales under company number 9551763; its operating headquarters was in
  Suwanee, Georgia. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- On November 8, 2018, ARRIS entered into a Bid Conduct Agreement with CommScope Holding
  Company, Inc. ("CommScope"), under which CommScope agreed to acquire all issued and
  to-be-issued ARRIS ordinary shares for $31.75 in cash per share, a whole-company cash
  acquisition. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- The acquisition was structured to be effected by a court-sanctioned scheme of
  arrangement under Part 26 of the UK Companies Act 2006 (with the parties reserving the
  right to switch to a contractual takeover offer under section 974 of that Act in
  certain circumstances) — i.e., a UK statutory scheme rather than a US-style merger-sub
  reverse triangular merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- Upon and with effect from the Effective Time, the Scheme Shares could be acquired by
  (i) CommScope, (ii) a newly referenced acquiring vehicle styled "CommScope BidCo," or
  (iii) a depositary-receipt nominee, at CommScope's election. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- CommScope estimated the total funds required for the Acquisition and related fees at
  approximately $8.3 billion, to be funded from cash on hand plus equity financing (an
  Investment Agreement with Carlyle Partners VII S1 Holdings, L.P.) and debt financing
  under a Debt Commitment Letter; there was no financing condition to the Acquisition.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- The Acquisition Agreement provided for an ARRIS termination fee of $58 million (or $29
  million under certain circumstances) and a CommScope reverse termination fee of $250
  million. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- ARRIS shareholders approved the Court Scheme Proposal at a Court Meeting held February
  1, 2019, requiring approval by a majority in number representing 75% or more in value
  of shares voting. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]

## Customer-facing product/asset tier
- At the time of the transaction, ARRIS described itself as "a global entertainment,
  communications, and networking technology and solutions provider," operating in three
  business segments — Customer Premises Equipment (CPE), Network & Cloud, and Enterprise
  Networks — enabling cable, telephone, and digital broadcast satellite operators and
  media programmers to deliver media, voice, IP data, and Wi-Fi services to subscribers,
  and enabling enterprises to have wired/wireless connectivity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- In describing the strategic rationale, CommScope characterized the intended combination
  as one that would strengthen "end-to-end broadband access, small cell and enterprise
  solutions." [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- The ARRIS Board's stated reasoning for recommending the transaction included its belief
  that, "given the complimentary nature of the products and services provided by ARRIS
  and CommScope," the combined company would continue to serve ARRIS customers, with
  limited product-line overlap between the two companies. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]

## Controlling-intent / legal-vehicle facts at the Effective Time (closing 8-K)
- The Scheme became effective on April 4, 2019, when the Court order was registered with
  the Registrar of Companies in the UK. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]
- At the Effective Time, ARRIS became an indirect, wholly-owned subsidiary of CommScope,
  and a change in control of ARRIS occurred. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]
- Each issued and outstanding ARRIS ordinary share (other than shares owned by CommScope
  or ARRIS wholly-owned subsidiaries) was automatically transferred to CommScope (or its
  nominee) in exchange for the Per Share Consideration; holders ceased to have any
  shareholder rights other than the right to that consideration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]
- ARRIS requested that trading in its ordinary shares (symbol "ARRS") be halted beginning
  April 4, 2019, and that the shares be removed from listing on the NASDAQ Global Select
  Market before the opening of trading April 5, 2019, with NASDAQ filing a Form 25 to
  that effect and ARRIS intending to follow with a Form 15 to suspend/deregister its
  Exchange Act reporting obligations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]

## People / organization / management tier at closing
- At the Effective Time, in accordance with the Bid Conduct Agreement, all of the
  officers and directors of ARRIS ceased to serve as officers and directors of ARRIS;
  Michael D. Coppin and Neil Shankland — both existing CommScope personnel — became the
  directors of ARRIS. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]
- Separately, the Acquisition Agreement provided that for one year following the
  Effective Time, each continuing ARRIS employee would receive the same base salary/wage
  and bonus opportunity, and benefits no less favorable in the aggregate, as immediately
  before the Effective Time — subject to CommScope's discretion, from January 1, 2020
  until the first anniversary of the Acquisition, to replace those benefits with ones
  substantially comparable to CommScope's own employee benefits. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- ARRIS's named executive officers each had pre-existing employment agreements providing
  enhanced change-in-control severance ("golden parachute") benefits if terminated
  without cause or for good reason within a defined post-closing period, and post-
  employment non-competition/non-solicitation covenants of 12-36 months depending on the
  executive. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]
- In advance of the Board's approval of the Acquisition, CEO Bruce McClelland disclosed
  that CommScope had expressed interest in the possibility that he would work for the
  combined company as a senior officer following the Acquisition, though no substantive
  discussion of that possibility had taken place as of the disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A]

## Bridging arrangements
- No transitional-services agreement, shared-services agreement, or supply/operating
  agreement bridging a retained shared substrate between ARRIS and CommScope was located
  in the reviewed filings; this is consistent with a whole-company cash acquisition
  (rather than a carve-out) in which the entire legal entity, including its officer/
  director layer, transferred at the Effective Time. [UNVERIFIED]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction was a whole-company cash acquisition of ARRIS International plc (a UK
  public limited company) by CommScope, effected through a UK court-sanctioned scheme of
  arrangement (not a US merger-sub reverse triangular merger) for $31.75 cash per share
  (~$8.3 billion in aggregate financing). At the April 4, 2019 Effective Time, ARRIS's
  entire officer and director layer was replaced by CommScope personnel and its shares
  were removed from NASDAQ listing the same day, while ARRIS's product/customer-facing
  business (CPE, Network & Cloud, Enterprise Networks) and its rank-and-file workforce
  were carried into the combined company with a contractual one-year compensation/
  benefits continuity floor for continuing employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-18-065399, Form DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001144204-19-018122, Form 8-K]
