## Outcome window fixed at construction
- The outcome window is the acquired China weight-management business's realized record over
  the fiscal 2014 to fiscal 2016 period (approximately three to five years after the April
  2011 acquisition), as reported in the acquirer's annual report on Form 10-K for fiscal
  2016 (period ended December 31, 2016), which presents fiscal 2014, 2015, and 2016
  comparatives. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01)]

## Realized record of the acquired business
- The China business was CLOSED. In fiscal 2014 the Company recognized a $2.4 million (about
  $0.04 per fully diluted share) net tax benefit "related to an intercompany loan write-off
  in connection with the closure of our China business," partially offset by recognition of a
  valuation allowance related to foreign-loss tax benefits not expected to be realized. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01), MD&A "Net Tax Benefit"]
- The closure appears as a discrete "Loss on closure of China" reconciling item in the
  Company's effective-tax-rate reconciliation, affecting the effective tax rate by
  (2.1) percentage points in the fiscal 2014 column and 0.0 in fiscal 2015 and fiscal 2016 —
  indicating the closure event was recognized in fiscal 2014 and did not recur in the two
  later years of the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01), Note "Income Taxes" effective-tax-rate reconciliation (F-28)]
- In year-over-year results discussion the Company again attributes to fiscal 2014 an $0.04
  net tax benefit "associated with the closure of our China business," alongside broader
  fiscal 2014 restructuring charges. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01), MD&A results-of-operations discussion]

## Documented context in the outcome window
- The Company recorded organization-restructuring charges of $11.8 million ($7.2 million
  after tax, $0.13 per fully diluted share) in fiscal 2014 and $8.4 million ($5.1 million
  after tax, $0.09 per fully diluted share) in fiscal 2015. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01), MD&A restructuring discussion]
- After fiscal 2014, the China business is no longer presented as an ongoing operation in the
  fiscal 2016 Form 10-K; the only references to it in that report describe its closure and the
  associated fiscal 2014 tax effect, with no fiscal 2015 or fiscal 2016 China revenue,
  segment, or operating disclosure. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the fixed fiscal 2014-2016 window, the acquired China weight-management operation —
  taken to full ownership in April 2011 — was wound down: the Company closed the China business
  in fiscal 2014, recognizing a $2.4 million net tax benefit tied to an intercompany loan
  write-off on closure and a (2.1)-percentage-point "Loss on closure of China" reconciling item
  in that year, with no further China operating presence disclosed in fiscal 2015 or fiscal
  2016. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-065504, Form 10-K (fiscal 2016, filed 2017-03-01), MD&A "Net Tax Benefit" and Income-Taxes note (F-28)]
