# Structural sub-dossier — NorthStar Real Estate Income II combination into Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate (closing structure)

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what

## Transaction and parties at closing
- NorthStar Real Estate Income II, Inc. ("NorthStar II"), a Maryland corporation and
  publicly registered non-traded REIT, together with NorthStar Real Estate Income Trust,
  Inc. ("NorthStar I"), entered a master combination agreement (dated August 25, 2017, as
  amended and restated November 20, 2017) with subsidiaries of Colony NorthStar, Inc.
  ("CLNS") and with a newly formed entity, Colony NorthStar Credit Real Estate, Inc. (the
  "Company"), under which CLNS subsidiaries contributed a select portfolio of assets and
  liabilities to the Company and NorthStar I and NorthStar II each merged, in an
  all-stock merger, into the Company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- The Company was a Maryland corporation and, at the time of the agreement, a wholly
  owned subsidiary of CLNS formed in August 2017 solely to facilitate the combination; it
  had not carried on any activities or operations prior to the transaction. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- NorthStar II merged with and into the Company under the Maryland General Corporation
  Law, with the Company surviving; each share of NorthStar II common stock converted into
  the right to receive 0.3511 shares of Company Class A common stock (or, on the
  IPO-contingent path, 0.0527 Company Class B-1 and 0.2984 Company Class B-2 shares), plus
  cash in lieu of fractional shares. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- The merger closed January 31, 2018, and Colony NorthStar Credit issued approximately
  40.4 million Class A shares to former NorthStar II stockholders and approximately 42.1
  million Class A shares to former NorthStar I stockholders, alongside approximately 44.4
  million Class B-3 shares issued to a CLNS subsidiary for its contributed portfolio; as
  of closing CLNS and its affiliates held approximately 37%, former NorthStar II
  stockholders approximately 31%, and former NorthStar I stockholders approximately 32%
  of the Company on a fully diluted basis. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]

## What crossed the ownership boundary and what did not
- The customer-facing asset that crossed was NorthStar II's investment portfolio: a
  diversified book of commercial real estate (CRE) debt, equity and securities
  investments — first mortgage loans, subordinate mortgage and mezzanine loans and
  participations, preferred equity interests, direct and indirect real-estate equity
  positions, and CRE securities including commercial-mortgage-backed securities — held,
  directly or indirectly, through its operating partnership. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- NorthStar II had no employees prior to closing; it was externally managed by CNI NSII
  Advisors, LLC, a CLNS subsidiary, under an advisory agreement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- At and after closing the combined Company likewise had no employees and was externally
  managed by CLNC Manager, LLC, a subsidiary of CLNS's operating partnership, under a new
  management agreement with an initial three-year term; the Company's executive officers
  were employees of the Manager or its affiliates rather than of the Company itself.
  [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- Under Accounting Standards Codification Topic 805, the combination was structured such
  that the Company (via its CLNS-contributed predecessor operations) was designated the
  accounting acquirer of NorthStar I and NorthStar II: factors cited included that CLNS
  initiated the transaction, CLNS affiliates would hold the largest voting interest
  post-closing, CLNS executives would manage the Company under the new management
  agreement, and CLNS's contributed entities would represent the largest share of net
  assets and net income among the combining entities. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]

## Contractual/organizational bridge and listing status at closing
- The operational substrate was bridged not by a transition-services agreement but by
  continuity of external management within the same corporate family: NorthStar II's
  pre-closing external manager and the Company's post-closing external manager were both
  CLNS subsidiaries, so day-to-day investment management continued under CLNS-affiliated
  personnel across the boundary rather than passing through an independent operating
  organization. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A]
- NorthStar II common stock itself was not, and had never been, listed on a national
  securities exchange; the Company's Class A common stock, by contrast, was approved for
  listing and began trading on the New York Stock Exchange under the symbol "CLNC"
  effective the opening of trading on February 1, 2018, the day immediately following the
  mergers. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction combined NorthStar II's investment portfolio and legal entity into a
  newly formed, previously non-operating company alongside a parallel merger of NorthStar
  I and a separate contribution of assets from CLNS; the customer-facing asset (the CRE
  debt/equity portfolio) and the legal vehicle changed hands, but the operating
  organization did not change in substance because both the pre- and post-closing
  external managers were CLNS-affiliated entities and neither predecessor REIT carried
  its own employees. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-362943, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-18-029397, Form 8-K dated 2018-02-01]
