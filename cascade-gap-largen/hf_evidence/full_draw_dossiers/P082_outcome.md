# Outcome sub-dossier — PT Freeport Indonesia ownership/joint-venture realized record, ~2017-2019 horizon

> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written

## The realized transaction announced and closed in this window
- On September 27, 2018, Freeport-McMoRan Inc. ("FCX"), International Support LLC, PT
  Freeport Indonesia ("PT-FI"), PT Indocopper Investama ("PT-II"), and PT Indonesia
  Asahan Aluminium (Persero) ("Inalum," an Indonesian state-owned enterprise) entered a
  Divestment Agreement under which Inalum would acquire, for $3.85 billion cash, all of
  Rio Tinto plc's interests in its joint venture with PT-FI and 100 percent of FCX's
  interest in PT-II (owner of 9.36 percent of PT-FI); the Rio Tinto interests were to be
  merged into PT-FI for a 40 percent share issuance, with expected resulting ownership of
  51.2 percent Inalum / 48.8 percent FCX, and cash proceeds at closing of $3.5 billion to
  Rio Tinto and $350 million to FCX. Closing was then expected by Q1 2019, subject to
  conditions including extension of PT-FI's mining rights through 2041 and Indonesian
  regulatory approvals. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-18-000032, Form 8-K (event date 2018-09-27)]
- The transaction completed on December 21, 2018 on those terms: of the $3.85 billion,
  Rio Tinto received $3.5 billion and FCX $350 million, and Rio Tinto separately paid FCX
  $107 million for its share of 2018 joint-venture cash flows. Resulting ownership was PT
  Inalum 26.24 percent and PTI (renamed from PT Indocopper Investama) 25.00 percent —
  51.24 percent combined — versus FCX's 48.76 percent, with FCX's retained economic
  interest (via carried-over revenue/cost-sharing) expected to approximate 81 percent from
  2019 through 2022. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Governance, legal-vehicle, and control facts documented at/after closing
- FCX, PT-FI, PTI and Inalum entered a PT-FI Shareholders Agreement at closing governing
  PT-FI's governance and management; its terms transfer control over management of PT-FI's
  mining operations to an operating committee controlled by FCX, and FCX concluded it
  retained control and would continue to consolidate PT-FI in its financial statements —
  citing SEC Regulation S-X Rule 3A-02(a), covering consolidation despite a lack of
  majority record ownership where a parent-subsidiary relationship exists by means other
  than voting-stock ownership. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- Concurrent with closing, the Indonesian government granted PT-FI a new special mining
  license ("IUPK") replacing its former Contract of Work, extending mining rights through
  2031 with a right to extend through 2041 subject to conditions. FCX accounted for PT-FI's
  acquisition of the former Rio Tinto joint-venture interests as an asset acquisition (a
  single asset, substantially all fair value concentrated in mineral reserves and related
  long-lived mining assets). [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018)]
- The divestment agreement provides FCX will indemnify PT Inalum and PTI for certain losses
  from disclosed PT-FI tax disputes, limited to PTI's proportionate share when a loss is
  finally incurred, with any net settlement payable on December 21, 2025. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Workforce/operations continuity documented in the window
- At December 31, 2018, FCX reported approximately 23,400 employees at the Grasberg
  minerals district in Indonesia (vs. 8,800 in North America, 5,900 in South America, and
  700 in Europe/other); 5,010 PT-FI-Indonesia employees were union-represented across 2
  unions, with the applicable agreement expiring September 2019. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018)]

## Outcome summary (realized record only)
- Within the ~2017-2019 window, the Indonesia ownership/joint-venture arrangement
  underwent a documented realignment: the Rio Tinto joint-venture interest was
  extinguished and merged into direct PT-FI share ownership, an Indonesian state entity
  (with an FCX-affiliated share) became the nominal majority shareholder of PT-FI (51.24
  percent vs. FCX's 48.76 percent), a new government mining license (IUPK) replaced the
  former Contract of Work, and $3.85 billion cash changed hands ($3.5 billion to Rio
  Tinto, $350 million to FCX) — while FCX documented that it retained operating control
  via a shareholders' agreement, continued to consolidate PT-FI, and continued to report
  Grasberg-area employment of approximately 23,400 as of the close of the window. [SRC: SEC accession 0000831259-19-000009, Form 10-K (FY2018); SEC accession 0000831259-18-000032, Form 8-K]
