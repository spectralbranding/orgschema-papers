# Structural sub-dossier — Bankrate, Inc. acquisition by Red Ventures Holdco, LP (closing structure)

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership boundary and what
> did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## Transaction and parties as structured

- Bankrate, Inc., a Delaware corporation whose common stock traded on the NYSE under the
  symbol "RATE," entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated July 2, 2017 with Red
  Ventures Holdco, LP, a North Carolina limited partnership, and Baton Merger Corp., a
  Delaware corporation and indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Red Ventures. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- The agreement structures the transaction so Merger Sub merges with and into Bankrate,
  with Bankrate — the pre-existing operating company, not merely selected assets —
  continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming an indirect wholly owned subsidiary
  of Red Ventures: a whole-company acquisition, not a carve-out or spin-off. Merger Sub
  "was formed solely for the purpose of acquiring" Bankrate and had not carried on any
  activities before the proxy statement date except those incidental to its formation. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- Consideration: each share of Bankrate common stock (other than certain excluded shares)
  converts into the right to receive $14.00 in cash without interest, a premium of
  approximately 31% over the three-month average closing price and approximately 9% over
  the $12.85 closing price on June 30, 2017, the last trading day before the announcement. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- Financing: Red Ventures obtained a debt commitment letter under which Bank of America,
  Merrill Lynch, Barclays Bank PLC, MUFG, and PNC Bank committed to lend Red Ventures $2.4
  billion toward the merger consideration and related refinancing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- A stockholder holding approximately 42.0% of outstanding shares (Ben Holding S.à r.l.)
  entered a voting agreement with Red Ventures agreeing to vote in favor of the merger,
  with the shares subject to the agreement capped at no more than 39.9% of shares
  outstanding and entitled to vote. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]

## What crossed the boundary at the tier level

- Customer-facing product: Bankrate is described as "a leading online publisher,
  aggregator and distributor of personal finance content," with flagship sites
  CreditCards.com, Bankrate.com and Caring.com, additional owned sites/apps including
  NextAdvisor.com, The Points Guy, Interest.com, Quizzle.com and Walla.by, content/tool/
  web-service arrangements with more than 100 online partners (including MSN,
  Realtor.com, MarketWatch and Bloomberg), and editorial-content licensing to Yahoo! and
  Tribune News Service — this content and audience-aggregation operation is the asset
  being acquired. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- People / management: Bankrate's executive officers are parties to employment agreements
  or retention letter agreements providing severance benefits upon a "qualifying
  termination" within a defined window following the change in control that the merger
  constitutes; one named officer's retention letter agreement covered a qualifying
  termination occurring before June 5, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- Controlling intent: Red Ventures is described as "a leading digital consumer choice
  platform" headquartered in Fort Mill, South Carolina, with more than 2,700 employees,
  entering the transaction as the new controlling parent; Red Ventures itself is
  characterized as contributing brand-partnership and consumer-facing-asset capabilities
  rather than being merged into Bankrate's operations. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]

## Interim arrangements bridging signing and closing

- Pending completion, Bankrate agreed to conduct its business in the ordinary course and
  was restricted from actions including material unscheduled changes to employee
  compensation or benefit plans, entering new lines of business, or announcing facility
  closures, layoffs or reductions in force sufficient in number to trigger advance-notice
  legal requirements. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- Bankrate was required to afford Red Ventures and Merger Sub reasonable access, during
  normal business hours, to Bankrate's officers, employees, properties, offices, books and
  records between signing and closing. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
- No dedicated transition-services, shared-services, or operating agreement bridging a
  divided substrate is described in the reviewed proxy statement; the document instead
  describes the ordinary-course and access covenants above as the mechanism preserving
  continuity between signing and the not-yet-occurred closing, consistent with a
  whole-company structure in which the acquired entity itself (not a carved-out unit)
  continues operating. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]

## Closing-time change to public status (as structured)

- The proxy statement states that if the merger is completed, Bankrate common stock will
  be removed from listing on the New York Stock Exchange and deregistered under the
  Securities Exchange Act of 1934, and Bankrate will no longer be a publicly traded
  company. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)

- As structured, the transaction carries the customer-facing content/audience-aggregation
  product, the surviving legal entity, and its existing management (subject to
  change-in-control severance arrangements) forward together as a subsidiary of the new
  controlling parent, bridged between signing and closing by ordinary-course and access
  covenants rather than a dedicated transition-services agreement, with the closing
  contemplated to remove the target's stock from public listing and registration. [SRC: SEC accession 0001193125-17-258576, DEFM14A]
