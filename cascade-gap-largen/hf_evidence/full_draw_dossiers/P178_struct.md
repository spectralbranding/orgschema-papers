# Structural sub-dossier — Sensata Technologies cross-border merger (Netherlands-to-UK reincorporation), closing structure

> EVIDENTIARY STANDARD: every factual line ends with a `[SRC: <SEC accession / URL>]`
> tag pointing to a public primary source. A fact that cannot be so sourced is written
> `[UNVERIFIED]` and is never guessed. This sub-dossier describes ONLY how the
> transaction was structured at closing — what crossed the ownership/legal boundary and
> what did not. It contains no post-transaction outcome information.

## What this filing documents
- This Form DEFM14A is a proxy statement/prospectus by which Sensata Technologies
  Holding N.V. ("Sensata-Netherlands"), a public company incorporated in the Netherlands
  (NYSE: ST), sought shareholder approval for a cross-border merger changing its parent
  company's place of incorporation from the Netherlands to the United Kingdom. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- The filing does not document Sensata acquiring, or being acquired by, an unrelated
  third-party operating business; it documents a corporate reorganization in which
  Sensata-Netherlands would merge into a newly formed public limited company, Sensata
  Technologies Holding plc ("Sensata-UK"), incorporated under the laws of England and
  Wales, with Sensata-Netherlands as the disappearing entity and Sensata-UK as the
  surviving entity. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]

## Mechanics and consideration
- Shareholders were to receive, as consideration, one ordinary share of Sensata-UK for
  each ordinary share of Sensata-Netherlands held immediately before the merger, with all
  assets and liabilities of Sensata-Netherlands transferring to Sensata-UK by universal
  succession of title. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Sensata-Netherlands shareholders' rights were governed by Dutch law and the
  Sensata-Netherlands articles of association pre-merger; post-merger, shareholders'
  rights would be governed by the laws of England and Wales and the Sensata-UK articles
  of association (drafted under the UK Companies Act 2006). [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- A dissenting shareholder who voted against the merger had a withdrawal right to elect
  not to become a Sensata-UK shareholder and to seek compensation under the Dutch Civil
  Code. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Sensata-UK's ordinary shares were expected to be listed and traded on the NYSE under
  the same symbol, "ST." [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- At an Extraordinary Meeting of Shareholders held February 16, 2018, shareholders
  approved the articles-of-association amendment (160,453,149 votes for; 5,123 against)
  and the cross-border merger itself (160,449,585 votes for; 8,586 against). [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000032, Form 8-K Item 5.07]

## What crossed the boundary at closing and what did not
- Legal vehicle: the top public holding entity changed from a Dutch N.V. to an English
  plc; all subsidiary companies previously owned by Sensata-Netherlands continued to be
  owned, directly or indirectly, by Sensata-UK after the merger. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Business/operations: the filing states Sensata-UK, together with its subsidiaries,
  "will continue to conduct our business in substantially the same manner as is currently
  being conducted by Sensata-Netherlands and its subsidiaries." [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Headquarters: principal executive offices were stated to remain in Attleboro,
  Massachusetts. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Employee benefit/equity plans: Sensata-UK was to assume, by operation of law, all
  employee benefit and equity-compensation plans currently sponsored by
  Sensata-Netherlands. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- Operating costs: the company stated it did not expect the merger to have a material
  effect on operating costs, including selling, general and administrative expenses. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]
- No transitional-services or shared-services agreement bridging a separated operating
  substrate is described in the filing; the document instead affirmatively states that
  subsidiaries, operations, employees, and management continue unchanged under the new
  parent, consistent with a change of the parent's place of incorporation rather than a
  separation of a business unit from a parent. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]

## Stated closing-time rationale (board's identified reasons)
- The Sensata-Netherlands board identified several potential benefits: incorporation in a
  jurisdiction (UK) where the company already had significant operations, citing that its
  October 2014 Schrader Electronics and December 2015 CST sensing-business acquisitions
  had increased UK headcount from 19 to 1,515 employees while Netherlands headcount was
  260; a UK legal environment viewed by investors as more shareholder-friendly (permitting
  derivative actions, a lower ownership threshold to call a general meeting, and the
  absence of a Dutch "Stichting" anti-takeover structure); and greater capital-allocation
  flexibility, since the UK, unlike the Netherlands, does not levy withholding tax on
  share buybacks. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A]

## Structural summary (closing only)
- The transaction structured at closing was a cross-border reincorporation merger in
  which the existing operating group (subsidiaries, business, employees, management,
  headquarters) continued unchanged under a newly formed English parent holding company
  that assumed all rights, obligations, and equity-plan sponsorship of the disappearing
  Dutch parent by operation of law, effected via a one-for-one share exchange approved by
  shareholder vote on February 16, 2018. [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000012, DEFM14A] [SRC: SEC accession 0001477294-18-000032, Form 8-K Item 5.07]
