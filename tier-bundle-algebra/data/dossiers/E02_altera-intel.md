## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Intel Corporation ("Parent") acquiring via 615 Corporation ("Acquisition Sub"), a wholly owned subsidiary of Intel. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376; SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/768251/000119312515414631/d102231d8k.htm]`
- Target: Altera Corporation (a Delaware corporation, San Jose, CA), a provider of programmable logic devices (PLDs) and field-programmable gate arrays (FPGAs). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated May 31, 2015; acquisition completed December 28, 2015. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376; SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/768251/000119312515414631/d102231d8k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: All-cash, $54.00 per share (an approximately 56% premium to the pre-announcement closing price), a transaction valued at approximately $16.7 billion. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Statutory reverse-triangular merger — 615 Corporation merged with and into Altera, with Altera surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Intel; each share (excluding rolled/appraisal shares) was converted into the right to receive $54.00 cash. Altera was delisted (Form 15-12G filed 2016-01-11). `[SRC: SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/768251/000119312515414631/d102231d8k.htm; SEC 15-12G accession 0001193125-16-426652]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Altera's standalone-public-company controlling intent (independent FPGA supplier) exited; Intel's imprint replaced it (FPGA capability folded into Intel's strategy of coupling its manufacturing process with programmable logic). This imprint was later reversed — see section 3. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The PLD/FPGA product-and-licensing value model transferred and was absorbed into Intel; the strategic rationale was integration of Altera's FPGA technology with Intel's leading-edge manufacturing. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal entity was acquired: Altera survived the merger as a wholly owned Intel subsidiary and its public listing was terminated; the business was later re-established as an independent company in 2025. `[SRC: SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/768251/000119312515414631/d102231d8k.htm; https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-acceptance layer transferred whole: Altera's FPGA and PLD product families and the customers they served moved into Intel. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376]`
- **T5 Process** — Altera's design/development operations transferred, with the stated intent to leverage Intel's manufacturing process technology. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-15-300376]`
- **T6 Organization** — Altera's people transferred into Intel and were operated as an Intel business unit (subsequently the Programmable Solutions Group). `[SRC: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): Reversal of the acquisition — on April 14, 2025 (roughly 9.3 years after close) Intel agreed to sell a 51% majority stake in Altera to Silver Lake in a transaction valuing Altera at approximately $8.75 billion, about half the approximately $16.7 billion Intel paid in 2015; Intel had first spun Altera back out as an independent company earlier in 2025. `[SRC: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): The re-valuation on divestiture (about half the purchase price after ~9 years) and the decision to spin Altera back out indicate a mixed-to-disappointing integration outcome; the sourced material attributes the sale to Intel sharpening focus and strengthening its balance sheet, without an explicit statement quantifying underperformance. `[SRC: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/intel-sells-majority-stake-altera-140131721.html]`
