## 1. Deal identification
- Parties: Novartis AG (Basel) and GlaxoSmithKline plc (GSK). `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- Structure: a simultaneous three-legged transaction — (a) Novartis acquires GSK's oncology products + two pipeline compounds; (b) GSK acquires Novartis's non-influenza Vaccines business; (c) the two firms pool their consumer divisions into a Consumer Healthcare joint venture. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- Announced / closed dates: announced April 2014 (Novartis global portfolio transformation); completed March 2, 2015. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- Disclosed deal value: (a) GSK oncology acquired for aggregate cash consideration of USD 16 billion, up to USD 1.5 billion contingent on development milestones; (b) Novartis non-influenza Vaccines divested to GSK for up to USD 7.1 billion (USD 5.25 billion on completion + up to USD 1.8 billion future milestones) plus royalties; (c) consumer JV owned GSK 63.5% / Novartis 36.5%. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- Legal form: portfolio/asset carve-out transfers (oncology, vaccines) exchanged in cash-plus-milestone deals, combined with a newly created jointly-owned consumer-health entity. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Replaced per portfolio. Each transferred business came under its acquirer's strategic imprint: Novartis sharpened focus on innovative pharmaceuticals, eye care and generics; the acquired GSK oncology came under Novartis Oncology's strategy, and the Novartis vaccines came under GSK's. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- **T2 Business Model** — Re-slotted / new for consumer. Oncology and vaccines portfolios were folded into each acquirer's existing pharma model; the consumer leg created a NEW shared value model — a standalone global consumer-health JV spanning Wellness, Oral Health, Nutrition and Skin Health. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Mixed. The oncology and vaccines legs transferred product portfolios/assets (not whole standalone legal entities named in the release); the consumer leg established a distinct jointly-owned entity ("GSK Consumer Healthcare") with an 11-seat board (Novartis holding four seats), minority rights and pre-defined market-based exit rights. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- **T4 Product** — Swapped both directions (core content). To Novartis: Tafinlar (BRAF inhibitor), Mekinist (MEK inhibitor), Votrient (renal cell carcinoma), Promacta, Tykerb, Arzerra — expanding Novartis Oncology to 22 oncology/hematology medicines. To GSK: the Novartis non-influenza vaccines portfolio (including meningitis-B vaccine Bexsero). `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- **T5 Process** — Transferred with the products. Each acquirer took over the commercial/operational base for the acquired portfolio; Novartis stated it would leverage Novartis Oncology R&D and commercial capabilities to optimize the acquired therapies. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- **T6 Organization** — Transferred with the business; retention flagged as a risk. The release's forward-looking disclosures explicitly note "uncertainty that GSK's oncology business will be integrated successfully into Novartis Oncology and that key personnel will be retained" — evidence that associated people transferred and retention was a live integration concern. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- Portfolio metrics at close: sales of the acquired GSK oncology products in 2014 were ~USD 2.0 billion and grew ~32% in local currency versus 2013; Novartis Oncology's portfolio expanded to 22 medicines treating >25 conditions. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- Financial impact: Novartis expected the transactions to immediately lift core margins; a substantial exceptional gain from the vaccines divestment and JV creation was to be recorded with 2015 Q1 results. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- 3-5 year outcome (consumer JV): Novartis exercised its contractual exit right and completed the sale of its 36.5% stake in the consumer-healthcare JV to GSK for USD 13.0 billion on June 1, 2018 — a clean, profitable exit from the JV leg. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-completes-sale-stake-consumer-healthcare-joint-venture-gsk-usd130-billion]`
- Narrative outcome: succeeded / strategic-focus-sharpening. The swap executed as planned across all three legs; both companies emerged more focused (Novartis on innovative pharma/eye care/generics; GSK on vaccines and consumer health), and Novartis monetized the JV stake at USD 13.0 billion three years later. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]` `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-completes-sale-stake-consumer-healthcare-joint-venture-gsk-usd130-billion]`

## 4. Failure-pathway evidence (parent-paper P1-P7)
- P4 Product-Process disruption (T4<->T5): none documented as a failure; each acquirer absorbed both product and commercial process for its acquired portfolio. Integration/retention risks were disclosed prospectively but no realized P4 failure is evidenced in the sources reviewed. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- P5 Process-Organization fracture (T5<->T6): key-personnel retention was flagged as a forward-looking risk but no realized fracture is documented in the sources reviewed. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- T1-archetype incoherence (non-cascade channel): none — portfolios moved into strategically aligned acquirers (oncology to an oncology leader, vaccines to a vaccines leader). `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
- T2-model incompatibility (non-cascade channel): none evidenced; the consumer JV functioned until Novartis's scheduled 2018 exit. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-completes-sale-stake-consumer-healthcare-joint-venture-gsk-usd130-billion]`

## 5. Cascade-gap-mitigation evidence (Amendment 3)
- The consumer JV was governed by a defined operating structure (11-seat board, Novartis minority + exit rights, pre-defined market-based exit-pricing mechanism), which contractually pre-absorbed the eventual separation — Novartis's 2018 exit executed against that pre-agreed mechanism rather than a renegotiation. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]` `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-completes-sale-stake-consumer-healthcare-joint-venture-gsk-usd130-billion]`

## 6. Collapse-state evidence
- No Tier-1=Tier-4 founder-identity fusion — these are large-corporate product-portfolio transfers, not founder-embodied firms. Product brands (Tafinlar, Bexsero, etc.) transferred as conveyable assets with no non-transferable principal-identity. `[SRC: https://www.novartis.com/news/media-releases/novartis-announces-completion-transactions-gsk]`
