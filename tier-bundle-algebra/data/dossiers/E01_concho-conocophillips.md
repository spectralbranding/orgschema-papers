## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: ConocoPhillips (a Delaware corporation) acquiring via Falcon Merger Sub Corp., a wholly owned subsidiary of ConocoPhillips. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692]`
- Target: Concho Resources Inc. (a Delaware corporation; NYSE: CXO), an independent oil and natural gas company focused on the Permian Basin of West Texas and Southeast New Mexico. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated October 18, 2020; merger completed January 15, 2021. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692; SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1358071/000110465921004795/tm213218d6_8k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: All-stock; each Concho share converted into 1.46 shares of ConocoPhillips common stock, a per-share value of approximately $49.30 at announcement, for total equity value of approximately $9.7 billion and total enterprise value of approximately $13.3 billion. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692; https://jpt.spe.org/conocophillips-completes-acquisition-of-permian-producer-concho-resources]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Statutory reverse-triangular merger — Falcon Merger Sub merged with and into Concho, with Concho surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of ConocoPhillips; Concho common stock was delisted (Form 25-NSE filed 2021-01-19; Form 15-12B filed 2021-01-29). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692; SEC 15-12B accession 0001104659-21-009083]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Concho's independent-public-company controlling intent exited; ConocoPhillips' imprint (stated aim: extend its position as the largest independent oil and gas company and consolidate a Permian pure-play into an integrated global E&P) replaced it. Partial leadership continuity: Concho CEO Tim Leach joined the ConocoPhillips board and was named executive vice president, Lower 48. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692; https://jpt.spe.org/conocophillips-completes-acquisition-of-permian-producer-concho-resources]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The upstream oil-and-gas exploration-and-production value model transferred and was absorbed as complementary; ConocoPhillips cited anticipated revenue and cost savings from combining the businesses. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal entity was acquired (not an asset carve-out): Concho survived the merger as a wholly owned ConocoPhillips subsidiary and its public listing was terminated. `[SRC: SEC 8-K https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1358071/000110465921004795/tm213218d6_8k.htm; SEC 15-12B accession 0001104659-21-009083]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-facing asset base transferred whole: approximately 550,000 net Permian acres and roughly 200,000 barrels-per-day of production folded into ConocoPhillips' Permian portfolio (bringing total position to ~1.5 million acres). `[SRC: https://jpt.spe.org/conocophillips-completes-acquisition-of-permian-producer-concho-resources]`
- **T5 Process** — Concho's operations were absorbed into ConocoPhillips' Lower 48 business unit; the DEFM14A frames integration to achieve the anticipated revenue and cost savings. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692; https://jpt.spe.org/conocophillips-completes-acquisition-of-permian-producer-concho-resources]`
- **T6 Organization** — People transferred into the combined organization with partial senior-leadership retention (Tim Leach to the board and Lower 48 EVP); the DEFM14A lists "inability to successfully integrate the businesses of Concho into ConocoPhillips" as a risk factor. `[SRC: https://jpt.spe.org/conocophillips-completes-acquisition-of-permian-producer-concho-resources; SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-20-005692]`
