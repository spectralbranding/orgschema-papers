## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: Medtronic, Inc. (NYSE: MDT) agreed to acquire Covidien plc (NYSE: COV); on completion both were combined under a new holding company, Medtronic plc. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
- Target: Covidien plc, an Ireland-domiciled global healthcare/medical-device products company (2013 revenue $10.2 billion; more than 38,000 employees in more than 70 countries). `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]`
- Announced / closed dates: Announced June 15, 2014; completed January 26, 2015 (Medtronic plc shares began trading on the NYSE under "MDT" on January 27, 2015). `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
- Disclosed deal value: Announced at approximately $42.9 billion ($93.22 per Covidien share, based on Medtronic's June 13, 2014 close of $60.70); at completion the cash-and-stock transaction was valued at approximately $49.9 billion, based on Medtronic's January 26, 2015 closing price of $75.59 per share. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Scheme-of-arrangement merger under Irish Takeover Rules creating a new parent, Medtronic plc, incorporated in Ireland. Each Covidien ordinary share converted into $35.19 in cash plus 0.956 of a Medtronic plc ordinary share; each Medtronic, Inc. share was exchanged for one Medtronic plc share. Covidien shareholders ended up owning ~30% of the combined company. The transaction was expected to be taxable for U.S. federal income-tax purposes to shareholders of both companies. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Control of the combined enterprise passed to Medtronic's leadership: Medtronic plc was led by Omar Ishrak (Medtronic Chairman/CEO), with Medtronic shareholders holding ~70% and Covidien shareholders ~30% of the new parent; Covidien's prior standalone controlling intent was subsumed. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The combined company pursued Medtronic's three stated strategies (therapy innovation, globalization, economic value), merging Covidien's complementary medical-device/supply portfolio into Medtronic's; combined revenue ~$27 billion (including $3.7 billion from emerging markets). Product overlap was limited, so the two models were largely complementary rather than substitutive. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The defining tier of the deal: a NEW legal parent, Medtronic plc, was created, incorporated in Ireland (where Covidien was already domiciled), with operational headquarters remaining in Minneapolis — a corporate inversion / redomiciliation. Both legacy Medtronic, Inc. and Covidien plc were combined under this new Irish-incorporated entity. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
- **T4 Product** — Covidien's product portfolio transferred whole into the combined company, described as complementary to Medtronic's and broadening clinical coverage; the combined portfolio spanned a comprehensive medical-technology range across more than 150-160 countries. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
- **T5 Process** — Operations were integrated to capture at least $850 million of annual pre-tax cost synergies by the end of fiscal 2018 via optimizing global back-office, manufacturing and supply-chain infrastructure and eliminating redundant public-company costs. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]`
- **T6 Organization** — The combined organization brought together Medtronic and Covidien's people: ~87,000 employees at announcement (Covidien contributing ~38,000) across more than 150 countries; at completion the merged company was described as more than 85,000 employees in more than 160 countries. `[SRC: https://news.medtronic.com/2014-06-15-Medtronic-to-Acquire-Covidien-for-42-9-billion-in-Cash-and-Stock]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): Integration proceeded on plan. By August 2017 (~2.5 years post-close) Medtronic had generated around $600 million of the targeted $850 million in Covidien cost synergies and was on track to reach the ~$850 million target by end of fiscal 2018, with completed initiatives including the integration of Covidien's Peripheral Vascular segment (~$60 million synergies) and consolidation of more than 100 non-operations facilities (~$100 million synergies). `[SRC: https://marketrealist.com/2017/08/how-medtronic-is-delivering-on-its-covidien-synergies/]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Generally regarded as a successful, largely complementary combination — Medtronic delivered on its committed cost synergies and advanced its therapy-innovation/globalization/economic-value strategies, with the new Irish-domiciled Medtronic plc trading as MDT from January 27, 2015. `[SRC: https://marketrealist.com/2017/08/how-medtronic-is-delivering-on-its-covidien-synergies/]` `[SRC: https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2015/01/26/1891740/0/en/Medtronic-Completes-Acquisition-of-Covidien.html]`
