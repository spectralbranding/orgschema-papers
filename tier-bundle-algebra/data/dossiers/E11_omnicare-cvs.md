## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: CVS Pharmacy, Inc. (acquirer), a Rhode Island corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of CVS Health Corporation, acting through Tree Merger Sub, Inc., a Delaware corporation and wholly-owned subsidiary of CVS Pharmacy. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`
- Target: Omnicare, Inc., a Delaware corporation formed in 1981; a healthcare-services company operating two segments — Long-Term Care Group (the nation's largest provider of pharmaceuticals and related pharmacy services to long-term-care and chronic-care facilities) and Specialty Care Group. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Definitive merger agreement dated May 20, 2015; merger completed on the Closing Date, August 18, 2015. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0000353230-15-000039, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000035323015000039/form8-kcvsclosing.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: $98.00 in cash, without interest, per share of Omnicare common stock; approximately $10.6 billion in cash payable by CVS Pharmacy as merger consideration, and CVS Pharmacy also assumed Omnicare debt. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0000353230-15-000039, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000035323015000039/form8-kcvsclosing.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular merger — Tree Merger Sub merged with and into Omnicare, with Omnicare surviving as a wholly-owned subsidiary of CVS Pharmacy; an all-cash entity (share) acquisition. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0000353230-15-000039, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000035323015000039/form8-kcvsclosing.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Omnicare's prior board/charter control exited; strategic intent was replaced by CVS Health's imprint, positioning long-term-care pharmacy as an adjacent extension of CVS's retail/pharmacy platform. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0000353230-15-000039, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000035323015000039/form8-kcvsclosing.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The long-term-care and specialty-pharmacy value model transferred into CVS; adjacent to but distinct from CVS's core retail-pharmacy model. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Entity acquired via reverse triangular merger; Omnicare, Inc. survived as a wholly-owned subsidiary of CVS Pharmacy. `[SRC: SEC 8-K accession 0000353230-15-000039, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000035323015000039/form8-kcvsclosing.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — The customer-acceptance layer transferred whole: pharmaceutical dispensing and related pharmacy services to long-term-care/chronic-care facilities (Long-Term Care Group) and specialty pharmacy/commercialization services (Specialty Care Group). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Omnicare's pharmacy-fulfillment and distribution operations transferred with the entity as a going concern. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — Omnicare's workforce transferred with the surviving subsidiary. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001047469-15-006208, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353230/000104746915006208/a2225332zdefm14a.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): Material write-down at the 3-year horizon. CVS recorded a ~$3.9 billion ($3,921 million) pre-tax goodwill impairment charge related to the long-term-care (LTC) reporting unit within the Retail/LTC segment in the three months ended June 30, 2018 — the reporting unit into which the acquired Omnicare business was placed. `[SRC: SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1, accession 0000064803-19-000036, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64803/000006480319000036/cvs_ex99x1.htm]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Mixed-to-adverse on the sourced record — the entity and its pharmacy franchises were absorbed as a going concern, but the LTC reporting unit sustained a multi-billion-dollar goodwill impairment (~$3.9 billion) within three years of close, indicating the acquired business underperformed the goodwill carried for it. `[SRC: SEC 8-K exhibit 99.1, accession 0000064803-19-000036, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/64803/000006480319000036/cvs_ex99x1.htm]`
