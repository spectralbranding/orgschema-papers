## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: A Brookfield Asset Management Inc. (NYSE: BAM) real-estate-investment fund, acting through Antlia Holdings LLC ("Parent") and its wholly owned subsidiary Antlia Merger Sub Inc. ("Merger Sub"), acquired Forest City Realty Trust, Inc. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]`
- Target: Forest City Realty Trust, Inc. ("Forest City"), a Maryland corporation (REIT) headquartered at Key Tower, 127 Public Square, Cleveland, Ohio (NYSE: FCEA). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated July 30, 2018; special meeting of Forest City stockholders held November 15, 2018; acquisition completed December 7, 2018 (Forest City common stock delisted from the NYSE at the close of business that day). `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]` `[SRC: SEC 8-K (Forest City) EX-99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518345012/d666358dex991.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: $25.35 per share in cash for each share of Forest City Class A common stock (subject to reduction for certain pre-closing dividends/distributions); the all-cash transaction was valued at approximately $11.4 billion including Forest City's proportionate share of consolidated and unconsolidated debt. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]` `[SRC: web-search-surfaced Brookfield press release / Sullivan & Cromwell, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/12/07/1663936/0/en/Brookfield-Completes-Acquisition-of-Forest-City-Realty-Trust.html]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): All-cash statutory take-private merger; Forest City survived the merger and became a subsidiary of Parent (Antlia Holdings LLC), a Brookfield fund vehicle. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]`

- **T2 Business Model** — Public-REIT diversified-real-estate ownership model transferred into a private Brookfield fund structure (take-private); the underlying rent/asset-operation model of the portfolio carried forward. `[SRC: SEC DEFM14A accession 0001193125-18-298438, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518298438/d576562ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Forest City Realty Trust, Inc. survived the merger as a subsidiary of Parent (Antlia Holdings LLC); its NYSE listing was terminated, moving the entity from public to private ownership. `[SRC: SEC 8-K (Forest City) EX-99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518345012/d666358dex991.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — The income-producing real-estate portfolio transferred: 6.3 million sq ft of office space, 2.3 million sq ft of life-sciences assets, 2.2 million sq ft of retail, and 18,500 multifamily units, plus five large-scale development projects (New York Metro, San Francisco, Washington DC). `[SRC: SEC 8-K (Forest City) EX-99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518345012/d666358dex991.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Property-operating processes transferred into Brookfield's real-estate operating platform (Brookfield cited synergies with its existing operations); specific operating-integration detail beyond that framing not located in the effort budget. `[SRC: SEC 8-K (Forest City) EX-99.1, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1647509/000119312518345012/d666358dex991.htm]`
