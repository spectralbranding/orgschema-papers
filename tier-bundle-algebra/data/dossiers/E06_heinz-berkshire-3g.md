## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: An investment consortium comprised of Berkshire Hathaway Inc. (a Delaware corporation; NYSE: BRK.A/BRK.B) and 3G Special Situations Fund III, L.P. (an affiliate of 3G Capital Partners Ltd.), acting through Hawk Acquisition Holding Corporation ("Parent," a Delaware corporation formed to effect the acquisition) and its wholly owned subsidiary Hawk Acquisition Sub, Inc. ("Merger Sub," a Pennsylvania corporation). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- Target: H. J. Heinz Company, a Pennsylvania corporation (NYSE: HNZ), which manufactures and markets an extensive line of food products worldwide (ketchup, condiments and sauces, frozen food, soups, beans and pasta meals, infant nutrition and other foods); incorporated in Pennsylvania in 1900, successor to a business founded 1869 by Henry J. Heinz. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Merger agreement dated February 13, 2013; acquisition first publicly announced February 14, 2013; transaction closed and became effective June 7, 2013. `[SRC: Heinz completion news release, 8-K accession 0001193125-13-258009, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504dex991.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: $72.50 in cash per share of Heinz common stock; transaction valued at $28 billion including assumption of Heinz's outstanding debt. The proxy states approximately $24.27 billion payable to shareholders/equity-holders (as of January 27, 2013) plus repayment of approximately $4.19 billion of Heinz indebtedness at closing. `[SRC: Heinz completion news release, 8-K accession 0001193125-13-258009, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504dex991.htm; DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular cash merger — Merger Sub (Hawk Acquisition Sub, Inc.) merged with and into Heinz, with Heinz surviving as a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, which is controlled by Berkshire Hathaway and 3G Capital; Heinz common stock ceased NYSE trading after June 7, 2013. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm; Heinz completion news release, 8-K accession 0001193125-13-258009, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504dex991.htm]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Heinz's prior public-shareholder principal exited; the asset came under the consortium's control (Parent controlled by Berkshire Hathaway and 3G Capital). The parties agreed to preserve Heinz's Pittsburgh headquarters as the surviving corporation's headquarters and the "global home of the flagship 'Heinz' brand," to keep the surviving corporation named H. J. Heinz Company, to preserve Heinz's heritage/philanthropic commitments, and to honor the Heinz Field naming-rights agreement. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T2 Business Model** — The global packaged-food value model transferred intact under new private ownership; 3G Capital is described as a firm focused on "maximizing the potential of brands and businesses" with a history of "generating value through operational excellence." New management was installed at close (Bernardo Hees became CEO — Heinz's seventh in its 144-year history — effective immediately). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm; Heinz completion news release, 8-K accession 0001193125-13-258009, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504dex991.htm]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — Legal entity survived as a private subsidiary: Heinz continued as the surviving corporation, retained the name H. J. Heinz Company, and became a wholly owned subsidiary of Parent; it was delisted from the NYSE. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm; Heinz completion news release, 8-K accession 0001193125-13-258009, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513258009/d555504dex991.htm]`
- **T4 Product** — Customer-facing product franchise transferred whole: the Heinz global food portfolio and the flagship Heinz brand, with the merger agreement expressly designating Pittsburgh as the brand's "global home." `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T5 Process** — Operating processes (manufacturing, R&D innovation centers, supply chain) transferred with the going concern; the deal was widely characterized as a platform for 3G's operational-improvement/cost-management approach under new leadership. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]`
- **T6 Organization** — People and culture transferred as a going concern with new top management installed at close; the parties agreed to preserve Heinz's heritage and Pittsburgh community commitments. Specific transferred-headcount figure not located in the filings fetched this session. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001193125-13-128058, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/46640/000119312513128058/d491866ddefm14a.htm]` `[UNVERIFIED: exact transferred employee count]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture — the Heinz asset was retained and, in July 2015 (~25 months after close), Heinz merged with Kraft Foods Group to form The Kraft Heinz Company (controlled by Berkshire Hathaway and 3G Capital), the public successor. In Q4 2018 (~5.5 years after the 2013 close) Kraft Heinz recorded non-cash impairment losses of approximately $15.4 billion ($7.1 billion goodwill + $8.3 billion indefinite-lived intangibles), with the largest brand impairments on Kraft and Oscar Mayer (brands that entered via the 2015 Kraft merger, not the original 1869 Heinz brand); the disclosure accompanied a dividend cut and an SEC subpoena. Kraft Heinz stock fell ~27% on February 22, 2019. `[SRC: Kraft Heinz Form 8-K (Item 2.06 Material Impairments), accession 0001637459-19-000020, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000163745919000020/february2019form8-k.htm]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Mixed-to-negative at the corporate-successor level — the 2013 take-private was executed cleanly (retained entity, brand, HQ) and became the platform for the 2015 Kraft merger, but the 3G operating model applied through the combined Kraft Heinz produced a large 2019 brand/goodwill impairment, dividend cut, and SEC investigation, widely attributed in press coverage to aggressive cost-cutting and brand under-investment. The impaired brands were principally Kraft-side, not the legacy Heinz brand. `[SRC: Kraft Heinz Form 8-K accession 0001637459-19-000020, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1637459/000163745919000020/february2019form8-k.htm; CNBC, https://www.cnbc.com/2019/02/22/kraft-heinz-doubt-mounts-after-kraft-oscar-meyer-write-down.html]`

## 7. Builder notes
- Deal structure, consideration, and post-closing covenants are anchored on the target DEFM14A (accession 0001193125-13-128058) and the completion news release filed as an 8-K exhibit (accession 0001193125-13-258009). Financing per the proxy: $4.12bn common equity from 3G; $12.12bn from Berkshire ($4.12bn common + $8bn preferred with warrants); $9.5bn term loans; $3.1bn second-lien notes; reverse termination fee $1.4bn.
- Important attribution caveat for coders: the $15.4bn 2019 impairment was recorded by the *combined* Kraft Heinz Company (post the July 2015 Kraft merger), and the largest brand impairments were on Kraft-side brands (Kraft, Oscar Mayer, Philadelphia, Velveeta), NOT the original Heinz brand. It is included here as the 3-5yr-horizon corporate-successor outcome of the 2013 take-private, not as a write-down of the 2013 Heinz asset per se.
