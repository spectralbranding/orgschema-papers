## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: No acquirer. This is not an acquisition or sale but a voluntary closure/wind-down: the restaurant "served its final dish to the public and is closing its doors for the last time," with no successor operator taking it over as a going concern. `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]`
- Target: elBulli (El Bulli), the restaurant near Roses (Cala Montjoi, Costa Brava), Catalonia, Spain, run by chef Ferran Adrià and holder of three Michelin stars. `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Bulli]`
- Announced / closed dates: Final public dinner service on 30 July 2011, ending a 27-year run as a restaurant. `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]` `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/ferran-adria-elbulli-riesgo-libertad-creatividad-2/]`
- Disclosed deal value: Not applicable — no transaction/consideration; the restaurant was closed and its activity redirected into a non-profit foundation rather than sold. `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Not a transfer. The restaurant was wound down as a going concern and "relaunched as the El Bulli Foundation," a private foundation and creativity centre — the operation was repurposed, not conveyed to a buyer. `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]` `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Bulli]`

## 2. Tier-level transfer content (evidence for the bundle signature)
For each tier, what crossed the ownership boundary and under what direction.
- **T1 Owner Intent** — Retained by the same controlling principals; no ownership boundary was crossed. Ferran Adrià (with partner Juli Soler) chose to close the restaurant and redirect intent toward a research/creativity mission: "El Bulli is not closing, it is transforming itself, because its soul is going to remain." `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]` `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/ferran-adria-elbulli-riesgo-libertad-creatividad-2/]`
- **T2 Business Model** — Changed, not transferred: the fine-dining commercial model (a 50-seat, ~€250 set-menu restaurant open six months a year that "never turned a profit" and "operated at a loss since 2000," with profit coming from books and lectures) was discontinued in favour of a foundation/creativity-centre model. `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]` `[SRC: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Bulli]`
- **T3 Business Entity** — The legal/organizational vehicle persisted and was repurposed, not dissolved or sold: the restaurant "relaunched as the El Bulli Foundation," and the elBullifoundation is "a private, family-run foundation, promoted by Ferran Adrià and Juli Soler." `[SRC: https://www.bbc.com/news/world-europe-14352973]` `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/ferran-adria-elbulli-riesgo-libertad-creatividad-2/]`
- **T4 Product** — The customer-acceptance layer (the dining experience) was discontinued rather than transferred; it was later re-presented read-only/archival form. The historic restaurant building is "now preserved in its entirety" and reopened on 15 June 2023 as the elBulli1846 museum, "the museum of the restaurant that changed everything." `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/elbulli1846-abre-como-museo/]`
- **T5 Process** — The restaurant's operating processes ceased with closure; the underlying creative/knowledge process was carried forward into foundation projects (Bullipedia, elBulliDNA, exhibitions). `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/elbulli1846-abre-como-museo/]`
- **T6 Organization** — People/culture retained under the same leadership rather than transferred to a buyer: the leaders Ferran Adrià, Juli Soler and Albert Adrià carried forward into the foundation. `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/where-we-come-from/]` `[SRC: https://elbullifoundation.com/en/proyecto/ferran-adria-elbulli-riesgo-libertad-creatividad-2/]`
