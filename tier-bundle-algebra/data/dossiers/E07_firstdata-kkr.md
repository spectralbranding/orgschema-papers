## 1. Deal identification
- Acquirer / parties: New Omaha Holdings L.P. ("Parent," a Delaware limited partnership) and its subsidiary Omaha Acquisition Corporation ("Merger Sub"/"Sub"), affiliates of and controlled by investment funds sponsored by Kohlberg Kravis Roberts & Co. L.P. ("KKR"). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001047469-07-005202, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883980/000104746907005202/a2178563zdefm14a.htm]`
- Target: First Data Corporation, a Delaware corporation (NYSE: FDC), which operates electronic commerce, payment services and customer account management businesses (headquartered in Greenwood Village, Colorado). `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001047469-07-005202, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883980/000104746907005202/a2178563zdefm14a.htm]`
- Announced / closed dates: Agreement and Plan of Merger dated April 1, 2007 (announced April 2, 2007); merger completed September 24, 2007; First Data delisted from the NYSE effective September 24, 2007 (Form 15 filed October 11, 2007). `[SRC: First Data Form 8-K (Introductory Note), accession 0001104659-07-072107, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883980/000110465907072107/a07-24621_18k.htm]`
- Disclosed deal value: $34.00 in cash per share of First Data common stock; transaction valued at approximately $29 billion, including approximately $26.1 billion payable to stockholders and holders of other equity-based interests. `[SRC: DEFM14A accession 0001047469-07-005202, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883980/000104746907005202/a2178563zdefm14a.htm]`
- Legal form (share deal / asset deal / merger / etc.): Reverse triangular cash merger / leveraged buyout — Omaha Acquisition Corporation merged into First Data, with First Data continuing as the surviving corporation and becoming wholly owned by New Omaha Holdings Corporation, a subsidiary of Parent, controlled by KKR-affiliated funds. `[SRC: First Data Form 8-K, accession 0001104659-07-072107, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/883980/000110465907072107/a07-24621_18k.htm]`

## 3. Post-deal performance at the 3-5 year horizon
- CAR / share-price reaction, OR write-down magnitude as fraction of deal value, OR divestiture timing (months from close): No divestiture — First Data remained a KKR portfolio company. The buyout was financed with roughly $22-24 billion of debt and closed immediately before the 2008 financial crisis. At the 3-5yr horizon (circa 2010-2012), First Data's revenue growth stalled and cash flow declined amid falling card-transaction volumes and merchant attrition (reported loss of roughly 15% of merchant customers per year to lower-priced competitors), leaving the company burdened by high-coupon debt (a reported ~$10 billion tranche with a ~10.2% weighted-average coupon). KKR ultimately held First Data for about eight years, taking it public again in an October 2015 IPO. `[SRC: Fortune, https://fortune.com/2015/10/21/first-data-ipo/; TheStreet, https://www.thestreet.com/opinion/first-data-continues-to-be-a-burden-for-private-equity-giant-kkr-13555410]`
- Narrative outcome (integration succeeded / failed / mixed): Mixed-to-strained — the operating franchise survived intact, but the highly leveraged capital structure (T3-level financial obligation) collided with the post-crisis demand environment, producing years of debt-service pressure and refinancing before the 2015 re-IPO; there was no operational tier-transfer failure, the strain was capital-structure driven. `[SRC: Fortune, https://fortune.com/2015/10/21/first-data-ipo/; TheStreet, https://www.thestreet.com/opinion/first-data-continues-to-be-a-burden-for-private-equity-giant-kkr-13555410]`
