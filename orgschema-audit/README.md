## Abstract

This paper introduces the OrgSchema Audit, a structured diagnostic protocol that evaluates organizational specification maturity across six cascading levels: experience contracts, signal requirements, process contracts, procedures, input specifications, and sourcing requirements. The protocol parallels the Spectral Audit developed for brand perception but targets organizational operations rather than perceptual allocation. Each audit level defines what to examine, what a healthy specification looks like, what failure modes indicate, and what corrective actions restore specification integrity. A worked example using a specialty coffee operation demonstrates the full protocol. The paper advances two propositions -- cascade-position prioritization and bidirectional traceability completeness -- that distinguish the OrgSchema Audit from existing diagnostic frameworks and are testable through field research.

## Propositions

| ID | Statement | Status |
|----|-----------|--------|
| P1 | Cascade-Position Prioritization: higher-level failures are more consequential than lower-level failures | Proposed |
| P2 | Bidirectional Traceability Completeness: untraced parameters are waste; untraced goals are debt | Proposed |
